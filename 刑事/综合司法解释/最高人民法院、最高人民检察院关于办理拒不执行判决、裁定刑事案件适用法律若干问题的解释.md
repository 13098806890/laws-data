# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕13号  
**公布日期**：2024-10-30  
**生效日期**：2024-12-01

> （2024年1月8日最高人民法院审判委员会第1911次会议、2024年7月23日最高人民检察院第十四届检察委员会第三十四次会议通过，自2024年12月1日起施行） 为依法惩治拒不执行判决、裁定犯罪，确保人民法院判决、裁定依法执行，切实维护当事人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国行政诉讼法》等法律规定，现就办理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题解释如下： 第一条　被执行人、协助执行义务人、担保人等负有执行义务的人，对人民法院的判决、裁定有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的，应当依照刑法第三百一十三条的规定，以拒不执行判决、裁定罪处罚。 本解释所称负有执行义务的人，包括自然人和单位。 第二条　刑法第三百一十三条规定的“人民法院的判决、裁定”，是指人民法院依法作出的具有执行内容并已发生法律效力的判决、裁定。人民法院为依法执行支付令、生效的调解书、仲裁裁决、公证债权文书等所作的裁定属于该条规定的裁定。 第三条　负有执行义务的人有能力执行而拒不执行，且具有下列情形之一，应当认定为全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于刑法第三百一十三条的解释中规定的“其他有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的情形”： （一）以放弃债权、放弃债权担保等方式恶意无偿处分财产权益，或者恶意延长到期债权的履行期限，或者以虚假和解、虚假转让等方式处分财产权益，致使判决、裁定无法执行的； （二）实施以明显不合理的高价受让他人财产、为他人的债务提供担保等恶意减损责任财产的行为，致使判决、裁定无法执行的； （三）伪造、毁灭、隐匿有关履行能力的重要证据，以暴力、威胁、贿买方法阻止他人作证或者指使、贿买、胁迫他人作伪证，妨碍人民法院查明负有执行义务的人财产情况，致使判决、裁定无法执行的； （四）具有拒绝报告或者虚假报告财产情况、违反人民法院限制消费令等拒不执行行为，经采取罚款、拘留等强制措施后仍拒不执行的； （五）经采取罚款、拘留等强制措施后仍拒不交付法律文书指定交付的财物、票证或者拒不迁出房屋、退出土地，致使判决、裁定无法执行的； （六）经采取罚款、拘留等强制措施后仍拒不履行协助行使人身权益等作为义务，致使判决、裁定无法执行，情节恶劣的； （七）经采取罚款、拘留等强制措施后仍违反人身安全保护令、禁止从事相关职业决定等不作为义务，造成被害人轻微伤以上伤害或者严重影响被害人正常的工作生活的； （八）以恐吓、辱骂、聚众哄闹、威胁等方法或者以拉拽、推搡等消极抗拒行为，阻碍执行人员进入执行现场，致使执行工作无法进行，情节恶劣的； （九）毁损、抢夺执行案件材料、执行公务车辆和其他执行器械、执行人员服装以及执行公务证件，致使执行工作无法进行的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　被执行人、协助执行义务人、担保人等负有执行义务的人，对人民法院的判决、裁定有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的，应当依照[刑法第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)的规定，以拒不执行判决、裁定罪处罚。  
本解释所称负有执行义务的人，包括自然人和单位。

**Article 1** A person who has an obligation to execute, such as a judgment debtor, a person with a duty to assist in execution, or a surety, who has the ability to execute a judgment or ruling of a People's Court but refuses to do so, and where the circumstances are serious, shall be punished for the crime of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling in accordance with the provisions of Article 313 of the Criminal Law.  
For the purposes of this Interpretation, a person who has an obligation to execute includes natural persons and entities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[刑法第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)规定的“人民法院的判决、裁定”，是指人民法院依法作出的具有执行内容并已发生法律效力的判决、裁定。人民法院为依法执行支付令、生效的调解书、仲裁裁决、公证债权文书等所作的裁定属于该条规定的裁定。

**Article 2** The term "judgments and rulings of a People's Court" as provided in Article 313 of the Criminal Law refers to judgments and rulings that are lawfully made by a People's Court, have enforcement content, and have taken legal effect. Rulings made by a People's Court for the lawful enforcement of a payment order, a legally effective mediation document, an arbitration award, a notarized instrument of creditor's rights, or similar instruments, shall fall within the category of rulings as provided in that Article.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　负有执行义务的人有能力执行而拒不执行，且具有下列情形之一，应当认定为全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于[刑法第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)的解释中规定的“其他有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的情形”：  
（一）以放弃债权、放弃债权担保等方式恶意无偿处分财产权益，或者恶意延长到期债权的履行期限，或者以虚假和解、虚假转让等方式处分财产权益，致使判决、裁定无法执行的；  
（二）实施以明显不合理的高价受让他人财产、为他人的债务提供担保等恶意减损责任财产的行为，致使判决、裁定无法执行的；  
（三）伪造、毁灭、隐匿有关履行能力的重要证据，以暴力、威胁、贿买方法阻止他人作证或者指使、贿买、胁迫他人作伪证，妨碍人民法院查明负有执行义务的人财产情况，致使判决、裁定无法执行的；  
（四）具有拒绝报告或者虚假报告财产情况、违反人民法院限制消费令等拒不执行行为，经采取罚款、拘留等强制措施后仍拒不执行的；  
（五）经采取罚款、拘留等强制措施后仍拒不交付法律文书指定交付的财物、票证或者拒不迁出房屋、退出土地，致使判决、裁定无法执行的；  
（六）经采取罚款、拘留等强制措施后仍拒不履行协助行使人身权益等作为义务，致使判决、裁定无法执行，情节恶劣的；  
（七）经采取罚款、拘留等强制措施后仍违反人身安全保护令、禁止从事相关职业决定等不作为义务，造成被害人轻微伤以上伤害或者严重影响被害人正常的工作生活的；  
（八）以恐吓、辱骂、聚众哄闹、威胁等方法或者以拉拽、推搡等消极抗拒行为，阻碍执行人员进入执行现场，致使执行工作无法进行，情节恶劣的；  
（九）毁损、抢夺执行案件材料、执行公务车辆和其他执行器械、执行人员服装以及执行公务证件，致使执行工作无法进行的；  
（十）其他有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的情形。

**Article 3** A person who has an obligation to execute and has the ability to execute but refuses to do so, and who falls under any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed to have committed the "other circumstances where a person has the ability to execute but refuses to do so, and the circumstances are serious" as specified in the Interpretation of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Article 313 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) maliciously and without consideration disposing of property interests by waiving claims, waiving security for claims, or other means, or maliciously extending the performance period of due claims, or disposing of property interests by means of false settlements, false transfers, etc., thereby rendering the judgment or ruling unenforceable;  
(2) engaging in malicious acts that reduce liable property, such as acquiring another person's property at a manifestly unreasonable high price, or providing security for another person's debts, thereby rendering the judgment or ruling unenforceable;  
(3) forging, destroying, or concealing important evidence concerning the ability to perform, or preventing others from testifying by violence, threats, or bribery, or instructing, bribing, or coercing others to give false testimony, thereby obstructing the People's Court from ascertaining the property situation of the person who has an obligation to execute, and rendering the judgment or ruling unenforceable;  
(4) engaging in conduct of refusing to execute, such as refusing to report property or making false reports, or violating a People's Court's restriction on consumption order, and after compulsory measures such as a fine or detention have been taken, still refusing to execute;  
(5) after compulsory measures such as a fine or detention have been taken, still refusing to deliver property or negotiable instruments specified to be delivered by legal documents, or still refusing to vacate premises or withdraw from land, thereby rendering the judgment or ruling unenforceable;  
(6) after compulsory measures such as a fine or detention have been taken, still refusing to perform obligations of action such as assistance in exercising personal rights, thereby rendering the judgment or ruling unenforceable, and the circumstances are flagrant;  
(7) after compulsory measures such as a fine or detention have been taken, still violating obligations of inaction such as a personal safety protection order or a decision prohibiting engagement in relevant occupations, thereby causing more than minor injury to the victim or seriously affecting the victim's normal work or life;  
(8) by means of intimidation, verbal abuse, gathering a crowd to create a disturbance, threats, or negative resistance acts such as pulling or pushing, obstructing enforcement personnel from entering the enforcement site, thereby rendering enforcement work impossible, and the circumstances are flagrant;  
(9) damaging or forcibly seizing materials of an enforcement case, enforcement vehicles, other enforcement equipment, enforcement personnel's uniforms, or enforcement service credentials, thereby rendering enforcement work impossible;  
(10) other circumstances where a person has the ability to execute but refuses to do so, and the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　负有执行义务的人有能力执行而拒不执行，且具有下列情形之一，应当认定属于“情节特别严重”的情形：  
（一）通过虚假诉讼、虚假仲裁、虚假公证等方式妨害执行，致使判决、裁定无法执行的；  
（二）聚众冲击执行现场，致使执行工作无法进行的；  
（三）以围攻、扣押、殴打等暴力方法对执行人员进行人身攻击，致使执行工作无法进行的；  
（四）因拒不执行，致使申请执行人自杀、自残或者造成其他严重后果的；  
（五）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 4** A person who has an obligation to execute and has the ability to execute but refuses to do so, and who falls under any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed to constitute a case where the circumstances are "particularly serious":  
(1) obstructing execution by means of false litigation, false arbitration, false notarization, etc., thereby rendering the judgment or ruling unenforceable;  
(2) gathering a crowd to storm the execution site, thereby rendering enforcement work impossible;  
(3) personally attacking enforcement personnel by violent means such as surrounding, detaining, or beating, thereby rendering enforcement work impossible;  
(4) as a result of the refusal to execute, causing the applicant for execution to commit suicide, self-harm, or other serious consequences;  
(5) other circumstances where the circumstances are particularly serious.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　有能力执行是指负有执行义务的人有全部执行或者部分执行给付财产义务或履行特定行为义务的能力。  
在认定负有执行义务的人的执行能力时，应当扣除负有执行义务的人及其所扶养家属的生活必需费用。

**Article 5** Having the ability to execute means that the person who has an obligation to execute has the ability to fully or partially perform the obligation to pay property or the obligation to perform a specific act.  
When determining the execution ability of a person who has an obligation to execute, the necessary living expenses of that person and their dependents shall be deducted.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　行为人为逃避执行义务，在诉讼开始后、裁判生效前实施隐藏、转移财产等行为，在判决、裁定生效后经查证属实，要求其执行而拒不执行的，可以认定其有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重，以拒不执行判决、裁定罪追究刑事责任。  
前款所指诉讼开始后，一般是指被告接到人民法院应诉通知后。

**Article 6** Where an actor, for the purpose of avoiding the obligation to execute, commits acts such as concealing or transferring property after the commencement of litigation but before the judgment or ruling takes effect, and after the judgment or ruling takes effect, it is verified that he or she has the ability to execute but refuses to do so upon being required to execute, it may be determined that he or she has the ability to execute but refuses to do so, and the circumstances are serious, and criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling.  
The phrase "after the commencement of litigation" in the preceding paragraph generally refers to the time after the defendant has received the notice to respond from the People's Court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于[刑法第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)的解释和本解释中规定的“致使判决、裁定无法执行”，一般是指人民法院依据法律及相关规定采取执行措施后仍无法执行的情形，包括判决、裁定全部无法执行，也包括部分无法执行。

**Article 7** The term "rendering the judgment or ruling unenforceable" as provided in the Interpretation of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Article 313 of the Criminal Law and in this Interpretation generally refers to a situation where the People's Court is still unable to enforce the judgment or ruling after taking enforcement measures in accordance with the law and relevant regulations, including cases where the judgment or ruling is entirely unenforceable as well as cases where it is partially unenforceable.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　案外人明知负有执行义务的人有能力执行而拒不执行人民法院的判决、裁定，与其通谋，协助实施隐藏、转移财产等拒不执行行为，致使判决、裁定无法执行的，以拒不执行判决、裁定罪的共犯论处。

**Article 8** Where a person not a party to the case, knowing that a person who has an obligation to execute has the ability to execute but refuses to execute a judgment or ruling of a People's Court, colludes with that person and assists in committing acts of refusal to execute such as concealing or transferring property, thereby rendering the judgment or ruling unenforceable, such person shall be handled as an accomplice to the crime of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　负有执行义务的人有能力执行而拒不执行人民法院的判决、裁定，同时构成拒不执行判决、裁定罪，妨害公务罪，袭警罪，非法处置查封、扣押、冻结的财产罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Where a person who has an obligation to execute has the ability to execute but refuses to execute a judgment or ruling of a People's Court, and this act also constitutes the crime of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling, the crime of obstructing official business, the crime of assaulting a police officer, the crime of illegally disposing of sealed-up, distrained, or frozen property, or other crimes, such person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision providing for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　拒不执行支付赡养费、扶养费、抚养费、抚恤金、医疗费用、劳动报酬等判决、裁定，构成犯罪的，应当依法从重处罚。

**Article 10** Where a person refuses to execute a judgment or ruling on payment of alimony, child support, maintenance, pensions, medical expenses, labor remuneration, or similar payments, and the act constitutes a crime, such person shall be given a heavier punishment according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　实施[刑法第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)规定的拒不执行判决、裁定行为，情节显著轻微危害不大的，不认为是犯罪；在提起公诉前，履行全部或者部分执行义务，犯罪情节轻微的，可以依法不起诉。在一审宣告判决前，履行全部或者部分执行义务，犯罪情节轻微的，可以依法从轻或者免除处罚。

**Article 11** Where a person commits an act of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling as provided in Article 313 of the Criminal Law, and the circumstances are clearly minor and the harm is not great, the act shall not be considered a crime. If, before public prosecution is initiated, the person performs all or part of the execution obligation and the circumstances of the crime are minor, the person may not be prosecuted according to law. If, before a judgment of first instance is pronounced, the person performs all or part of the execution obligation and the circumstances of the crime are minor, the person may be given a lighter or exempted punishment according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对被告人以拒不执行判决、裁定罪追诉时，对其故意毁损、无偿处分、以明显不合理价格处分、虚假转让等方式违法处置的财产，应当依法予以追缴或者责令退赔，交由执行法院依法处置。

**Article 12** When a defendant is prosecuted for the crime of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling, the property that the defendant has illegally disposed of by means such as intentional destruction, disposition without consideration, disposition at a manifestly unreasonable price, or false transfer shall, according to law, be confiscated or the defendant shall be ordered to make restitution, and the property shall be turned over to the enforcement court for lawful disposition.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民检察院应当结合侦查移送情况对涉案财产进行审查，在提起公诉时对涉案财产提出明确处理意见。人民法院应当依法作出判决，对涉案财产作出处理。

**Article 13** The People's Procuratorate shall, in light of the investigation and transfer, examine the property involved in the case and, when initiating a public prosecution, provide clear opinions on the handling of the involved property. The People's Court shall, according to law, render a judgment and dispose of the involved property.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请执行人有证据证明同时具有下列情形，人民法院认为符合[刑事诉讼法第二百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-210)第三项规定的，以自诉案件立案审理：  
（一）负有执行义务的人拒不执行判决、裁定，侵犯了申请执行人的人身、财产权利，应当依法追究刑事责任的；  
（二）申请执行人曾经提出控告，而公安机关或者人民检察院对负有执行义务的人不予追究刑事责任的。  
自诉人在判决宣告前，可以同被告人自行和解或者撤回自诉。

**Article 14** Where an applicant for execution has evidence proving that the following circumstances concurrently exist, and the People's Court considers that the provisions of Article 210, item (3) of the Criminal Procedure Law are satisfied, the case shall be docketed and tried as a private prosecution case:  
(1) the person who has an obligation to execute refuses to execute a judgment or ruling, thereby infringing upon the personal or property rights of the applicant for execution, and criminal liability shall be investigated according to law;  
(2) the applicant for execution has previously filed an accusation, but the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate has not investigated the criminal liability of the person who has an obligation to execute.  
Before the judgment is pronounced, the private prosecutor may voluntarily reach a settlement with the defendant or withdraw the private prosecution.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件，一般由执行法院所在地人民法院管辖。

**Article 15** Criminal cases of refusing to execute a judgment or ruling shall generally be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court in the locality of the enforcement court.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本解释自2024年12月1日起施行。《最高人民法院关于审理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2015〕16号）同时废止。最高人民法院、最高人民检察院此前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 16** This Interpretation shall come into force on December 1, 2024. The Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Refusing to Execute Judgments or Rulings (Fa Shi [2015] No. 16) shall be abolished simultaneously. Where judicial interpretations and regulatory documents previously issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
