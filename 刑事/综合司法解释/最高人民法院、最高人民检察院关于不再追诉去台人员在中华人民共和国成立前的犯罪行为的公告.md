# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于不再追诉去台人员在中华人民共和国成立前的犯罪行为的公告

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：1988-03-14  
**生效日期**：1988-03-14

---

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院    最高人民检察院  
关于不再追诉去台人员在中华人民共和国  
成立前的犯罪行为的公告  
（1988年3月14日）  
台湾同胞来祖国大陆探亲旅游的日益增多，这对于促进海峡两岸的“三通”和实现祖国和平统一大业将起到积极的作用。为此，对去台人员中在中华人民共和国成立前在大陆犯有罪行的，根据[《中华人民共和国刑法》第七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-76)关于对犯罪追诉时效的规定的精神，决定对其当时所犯罪行不再追诉。  
来祖国大陆的台湾同胞应遵守国家的法律，其探亲、旅游、贸易、投资等正当活动，均受法律保护。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court Supreme People's Procuratorate  
Announcement on No Longer Prosecuting the Criminal Acts Committed by Persons Who Went to Taiwan Before the Founding of the People's Republic of China  
(March 14, 1988)  
  
The number of Taiwanese compatriots coming to mainland China for visiting relatives and tourism is increasing day by day, which shall play a positive role in promoting the "three direct links" across the Taiwan Strait and realizing the great cause of peaceful reunification of the motherland. For this reason, for those who went to Taiwan and committed crimes on the mainland before the founding of the People's Republic of China, in accordance with the spirit of the provisions of Article 76 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China regarding the statute of limitations for the prosecution of crimes, it is decided that no prosecution shall be instituted for the crimes they committed at that time.  
Taiwanese compatriots coming to mainland China shall abide by the laws of the state, and their legitimate activities such as visiting relatives, tourism, trade, and investment shall be protected by law.
