# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释(三)

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2020-08-31  
**生效日期**：2020-09-14

> （2020年8月31日最高人民法院审判委员会第1811次会议、2020年8月21日最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第48次会议通过 2020年9月12日最高人民法院、最高人民检察院公告公布 自2020年9月14日起施行 法释〔2020〕10号) 为依法惩治侵犯知识产权犯罪，维护社会主义市场经济秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等有关规定，现就办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为刑法第二百一十三条规定的“与其注册商标相同的商标”： （一）改变注册商标的字体、字母大小写或者文字横竖排列，与注册商标之间基本无差别的； （二）改变注册商标的文字、字母、数字等之间的间距，与注册商标之间基本无差别的； （三）改变注册商标颜色，不影响体现注册商标显著特征的； （四）在注册商标上仅增加商品通用名称、型号等缺乏显著特征要素，不影响体现注册商标显著特征的； （五）与立体注册商标的三维标志及平面要素基本无差别的； （六）其他与注册商标基本无差别、足以对公众产生误导的商标。 第二条　在刑法第二百一十七条规定的作品、录音制品上以通常方式署名的自然人、法人或者非法人组织，应当推定为著作权人或者录音制作者，且该作品、录音制品上存在着相应权利，但有相反证明的除外。 在涉案作品、录音制品种类众多且权利人分散的案件中，有证据证明涉案复制品系非法出版、复制发行，且出版者、复制发行者不能提供获得著作权人、录音制作者许可的相关证据材料的，可以认定为刑法第二百一十七条规定的“未经著作权人许可”“未经录音制作者许可”。但是，有证据证明权利人放弃权利、涉案作品的著作权或者录音制品的有关权利不受我国著作权法保护、权利保护期限已经届满的除外。 第三条　采取非法复制、未经授权或者超越授权使用计算机信息系统等方式窃取商业秘密的，应当认定为刑法第二百一十九条第一款第一项规定的“盗窃”。 以贿赂、欺诈、电子侵入等方式获取权利人的商业秘密的，应当认定为刑法第二百一十九条第一款第一项规定的“其他不正当手段”。 第四条　实施刑法第二百一十九条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“给商业秘密的权利人造成重大损失”： （一）给商业秘密的权利人造成损失数额或者因侵犯商业秘密违法所得数额在三十万元以上的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“与其注册商标相同的商标”：  
（一）改变注册商标的字体、字母大小写或者文字横竖排列，与注册商标之间基本无差别的；  
（二）改变注册商标的文字、字母、数字等之间的间距，与注册商标之间基本无差别的；  
（三）改变注册商标颜色，不影响体现注册商标显著特征的；  
（四）在注册商标上仅增加商品通用名称、型号等缺乏显著特征要素，不影响体现注册商标显著特征的；  
（五）与立体注册商标的三维标志及平面要素基本无差别的；  
（六）其他与注册商标基本无差别、足以对公众产生误导的商标。

**Article 1** Where any of the following circumstances exists, it may be determined as a 'trademark identical to its registered trademark' as provided for in Article 213 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) changing the font, letter case, or horizontal or vertical arrangement of the registered trademark, resulting in no substantial difference from the registered trademark;  
(2) changing the spacing between characters, letters, numbers, etc. of the registered trademark, resulting in no substantial difference from the registered trademark;  
(3) changing the color of the registered trademark, without affecting the reflection of the distinctive features of the registered trademark;  
(4) only adding generic names, models, or other elements lacking distinctive features to the registered trademark, without affecting the reflection of the distinctive features of the registered trademark;  
(5) having no substantial difference from the three-dimensional signs and planar elements of a three-dimensional registered trademark;  
(6) other trademarks that are substantially identical to the registered trademark and sufficient to mislead the public.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的作品、录音制品上以通常方式署名的自然人、法人或者非法人组织，应当推定为著作权人或者录音制作者，且该作品、录音制品上存在着相应权利，但有相反证明的除外。  
在涉案作品、录音制品种类众多且权利人分散的案件中，有证据证明涉案复制品系非法出版、复制发行，且出版者、复制发行者不能提供获得著作权人、录音制作者许可的相关证据材料的，可以认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“未经著作权人许可”“未经录音制作者许可”。但是，有证据证明权利人放弃权利、涉案作品的著作权或者录音制品的有关权利不受我国著作权法保护、权利保护期限已经届满的除外。

**Article 2** A natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization that is named in the usual manner on a work or sound recording as specified in Article 217 of the Criminal Law shall be presumed to be the copyright owner or sound recording producer, and the corresponding rights exist on such work or sound recording, unless there is evidence to the contrary.  
In cases where the types of works or sound recordings involved are numerous and the rights holders are scattered, if there is evidence that the reproductions involved are illegally published, reproduced, or distributed, and the publisher, reproducer, or distributor cannot provide relevant evidence materials of obtaining permission from the copyright owner or sound recording producer, it may be determined as 'without permission of the copyright owner' or 'without permission of the sound recording producer' as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law. However, this does not apply if there is evidence that the rights holder has waived the rights, the copyright of the work involved or the relevant rights of the sound recording are not protected by the copyright law of China, or the protection period has expired.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　采取非法复制、未经授权或者超越授权使用计算机信息系统等方式窃取商业秘密的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)第一款第一项规定的“盗窃”。  
以贿赂、欺诈、电子侵入等方式获取权利人的商业秘密的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)第一款第一项规定的“其他不正当手段”。

**Article 3** Stealing trade secrets by means such as illegal reproduction, unauthorized use or exceeding authorized use of computer information systems shall be determined as 'theft' as provided in Item (1) of Paragraph 1 of Article 219 of the Criminal Law.  
Obtaining trade secrets of the right holder by means such as bribery, fraud, or electronic intrusion shall be determined as 'other improper means' as provided in Item (1) of Paragraph 1 of Article 219 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　实施[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“给商业秘密的权利人造成重大损失”：  
（一）给商业秘密的权利人造成损失数额或者因侵犯商业秘密违法所得数额在三十万元以上的；  
（二）直接导致商业秘密的权利人因重大经营困难而破产、倒闭的；  
（三）造成商业秘密的权利人其他重大损失的。  
给商业秘密的权利人造成损失数额或者因侵犯商业秘密违法所得数额在二百五十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的“造成特别严重后果”。

**Article 4** Where any of the following circumstances exists in the commission of the act specified in Article 219 of the Criminal Law, it shall be determined as 'causing significant losses to the right holder of trade secrets':  
(1) the amount of losses caused to the right holder of trade secrets or the amount of illegal gains from infringement of trade secrets reaches 300,000 RMB or more;  
(2) directly causing the right holder of trade secrets to go bankrupt or close down due to major operational difficulties;  
(3) causing other significant losses to the right holder of trade secrets.  
Where the amount of losses caused to the right holder of trade secrets or the amount of illegal gains from infringement of trade secrets reaches 2.5 million RMB or more, it shall be determined as 'causing particularly serious consequences' as provided in Article 219 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　实施[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的行为造成的损失数额或者违法所得数额，可以按照下列方式认定：  
（一）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密，尚未披露、使用或者允许他人使用的，损失数额可以根据该项商业秘密的合理许可使用费确定；  
（二）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密后，披露、使用或者允许他人使用的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成销售利润的损失确定，但该损失数额低于商业秘密合理许可使用费的，根据合理许可使用费确定；  
（三）违反约定、权利人有关保守商业秘密的要求，披露、使用或者允许他人使用其所掌握的商业秘密的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成销售利润的损失确定；  
（四）明知商业秘密是不正当手段获取或者是违反约定、权利人有关保守商业秘密的要求披露、使用、允许使用，仍获取、使用或者披露的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成销售利润的损失确定；  
（五）因侵犯商业秘密行为导致商业秘密已为公众所知悉或者灭失的，损失数额可以根据该项商业秘密的商业价值确定。商业秘密的商业价值，可以根据该项商业秘密的研究开发成本、实施该项商业秘密的收益综合确定；  
（六）因披露或者允许他人使用商业秘密而获得的财物或者其他财产性利益，应当认定为违法所得。  
前款第二项、第三项、第四项规定的权利人因被侵权造成销售利润的损失，可以根据权利人因被侵权造成销售量减少的总数乘以权利人每件产品的合理利润确定；销售量减少的总数无法确定的，可以根据侵权产品销售量乘以权利人每件产品的合理利润确定；权利人因被侵权造成销售量减少的总数和每件产品的合理利润均无法确定的，可以根据侵权产品销售量乘以每件侵权产品的合理利润确定。商业秘密系用于服务等其他经营活动的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权而减少的合理利润确定。  
商业秘密的权利人为减轻对商业运营、商业计划的损失或者重新恢复计算机信息系统安全、其他系统安全而支出的补救费用，应当计入给商业秘密的权利人造成的损失。

**Article 5** The amount of losses or the amount of illegal gains caused by the act specified in Article 219 of the Criminal Law may be determined in the following ways:  
(1) if the trade secret of the right holder is obtained by improper means but has not yet been disclosed, used, or allowed to be used by others, the amount of losses may be determined based on the reasonable licensing fee for the trade secret;  
(2) if, after obtaining the trade secret of the right holder by improper means, it is disclosed, used, or allowed to be used by others, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of sales profits caused to the right holder by the infringement; however, if such amount of losses is lower than the reasonable licensing fee for the trade secret, it shall be determined based on the reasonable licensing fee;  
(3) if the trade secret in one's possession is disclosed, used, or allowed to be used by others in violation of an agreement or the right holder's requirements on protecting the trade secret, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of sales profits caused to the right holder by the infringement;  
(4) if a person who knows that the trade secret was obtained by improper means or was disclosed, used, or allowed to be used in violation of an agreement or the right holder's requirements on protecting the trade secret still obtains, uses, or discloses it, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of sales profits caused to the right holder by the infringement;  
(5) if the trade secret has become known to the public or has been lost due to the infringement, the amount of losses may be determined based on the commercial value of the trade secret. The commercial value of the trade secret may be determined comprehensively based on the research and development costs of the trade secret and the income from implementing the trade secret;  
(6) property or other property interests obtained from disclosing or allowing others to use the trade secret shall be determined as illegal gains.  
The loss of sales profits caused to the right holder by the infringement as specified in Items (2), (3), and (4) of the preceding paragraph may be determined based on the total reduction in sales volume caused to the right holder by the infringement multiplied by the reasonable profit per product of the right holder; if the total reduction in sales volume cannot be determined, it may be determined based on the sales volume of infringing products multiplied by the reasonable profit per product of the right holder; if neither the total reduction in sales volume caused to the right holder by the infringement nor the reasonable profit per product of the right holder can be determined, it may be determined based on the sales volume of infringing products multiplied by the reasonable profit per infringing product. If the trade secret is used for services or other business activities, the amount of losses may be determined based on the reduction in reasonable profit of the right holder caused by the infringement.  
The remedial expenses incurred by the right holder of the trade secret to mitigate losses to business operations or business plans, or to restore the security of computer information systems or other systems, shall be included in the losses caused to the right holder of the trade secret.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在刑事诉讼程序中，当事人、辩护人、诉讼代理人或者案外人书面申请对有关商业秘密或者其他需要保密的商业信息的证据、材料采取保密措施的，应当根据案件情况采取组织诉讼参与人签署保密承诺书等必要的保密措施。  
违反前款有关保密措施的要求或者法律法规规定的保密义务的，依法承担相应责任。擅自披露、使用或者允许他人使用在刑事诉讼程序中接触、获取的商业秘密，符合[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 6** In the course of criminal proceedings, if a party, defender, litigation agent, or person not involved in the case applies in writing to take confidentiality measures for evidence or materials concerning trade secrets or other commercial information that needs to be kept confidential, necessary confidentiality measures such as requiring litigation participants to sign a confidentiality commitment shall be taken according to the circumstances of the case.  
Any person who violates the requirements of the confidentiality measures in the preceding paragraph or the confidentiality obligations provided by laws or regulations shall bear corresponding liability according to law. If a person without authorization discloses, uses, or allows others to use trade secrets accessed or obtained during the criminal proceedings, and such conduct meets the provisions of Article 219 of the Criminal Law, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　除特殊情况外，假冒注册商标的商品、非法制造的注册商标标识、侵犯著作权的复制品、主要用于制造假冒注册商标的商品、注册商标标识或者侵权复制品的材料和工具，应当依法予以没收和销毁。  
上述物品需要作为民事、行政案件的证据使用的，经权利人申请，可以在民事、行政案件终结后或者采取取样、拍照等方式对证据固定后予以销毁。

**Article 7** Except under special circumstances, goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks, illegally manufactured registered trademark labels, reproductions infringing copyright, and materials and tools mainly used for manufacturing goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks, registered trademark labels, or infringing reproductions shall be confiscated and destroyed according to law.  
If the above-mentioned articles need to be used as evidence in civil or administrative cases, upon application by the right holder, they may be destroyed after the conclusion of the civil or administrative case or after the evidence is fixed by means such as sampling or photographing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　具有下列情形之一的，可以酌情从重处罚，一般不适用缓刑：  
（一）主要以侵犯知识产权为业的；  
（二）因侵犯知识产权被行政处罚后再次侵犯知识产权构成犯罪的；  
（三）在重大自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件期间，假冒抢险救灾、防疫物资等商品的注册商标的；  
（四）拒不交出违法所得的。

**Article 8** Where any of the following circumstances exists, a severe punishment may be imposed at discretion, and probation shall generally not be applied:  
(1) mainly engaging in infringement of intellectual property rights as a profession;  
(2) having been subjected to administrative penalties for infringement of intellectual property rights and then committing a crime of infringing intellectual property rights again;  
(3) during major natural disasters, accidents, or public health events, counterfeiting registered trademarks of goods such as emergency rescue, disaster relief, or epidemic prevention materials;  
(4) refusing to surrender illegal gains.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　具有下列情形之一的，可以酌情从轻处罚：  
（一）认罪认罚的；  
（二）取得权利人谅解的；  
（三）具有悔罪表现的；  
（四）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密后尚未披露、使用或者允许他人使用的。

**Article 9** Where any of the following circumstances exists, a light punishment may be imposed at discretion:  
(1) pleading guilty and accepting punishment;  
(2) obtaining forgiveness from the right holder;  
(3) showing repentance;  
(4) having obtained the trade secret of the right holder by improper means but not yet disclosed, used, or allowed others to use it.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对于侵犯知识产权犯罪的，应当综合考虑犯罪违法所得数额、非法经营数额、给权利人造成的损失数额、侵权假冒物品数量及社会危害性等情节，依法判处罚金。  
罚金数额一般在违法所得数额的一倍以上五倍以下确定。违法所得数额无法查清的，罚金数额一般按照非法经营数额的百分之五十以上一倍以下确定。违法所得数额和非法经营数额均无法查清，判处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者单处罚金的，一般在三万元以上一百万元以下确定罚金数额；判处三年以上有期徒刑的，一般在十五万元以上五百万元以下确定罚金数额。

**Article 10** For crimes of infringing intellectual property rights, a fine shall be imposed according to law, taking into comprehensive consideration the amount of illegal gains from the crime, the amount of illegal business operations, the amount of losses caused to the right holder, the quantity of infringing and counterfeit goods, and the degree of social harm.  
The amount of the fine shall generally be determined at not less than one time and not more than five times the amount of illegal gains. If the amount of illegal gains cannot be ascertained, the amount of the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 50% and not more than one time of the amount of illegal business operations. If neither the amount of illegal gains nor the amount of illegal business operations can be ascertained, and a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of less than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or a fine alone is imposed, the amount of the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 30,000 RMB and not more than 1,000,000 RMB; if a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of three years or more is imposed, the amount of the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 150,000 RMB and not more than 5,000,000 RMB.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本解释发布施行后，之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 11** After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, if previously promulgated judicial interpretations and normative documents are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本解释自2020年9月14日起施行。

**Article 12** This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 14, 2020.
