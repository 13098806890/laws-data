# 中华人民共和国尘肺病防治条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：1987-12-03

> (1987年12月3日国务院发布) 第一章　总则 第一条　为保护职工健康，消除粉尘危害，防止发生尘肺病，促进生产发展，制定本条例。 第二条　本条例适用于所有有粉尘作业的企业、事业单位。 第三条　尘肺病系指在生产活动中吸入粉尘而发生的肺组织纤维化为主的疾病。 第四条　地方各级人民政府要加强对尘肺病防治工作的领导。在制定本地区国民经济和社会发展计划时，要统筹安排尘肺病防治工作。 第五条　企业、事业单位的主管部门应当根据国家卫生等有关标准，结合实际情况，制定所属企业的尘肺病防治规划，并督促其施行。 乡镇企业主管部门，必须指定专人负责乡镇企业尘肺病的防治工作，建立监督检查制度，并指导乡镇企业对尘肺病的防治工作。 第六条　企业、事业单位的负责人，对本单位的尘肺病防治工作负有直接责任，应采取有效措施使本单位的粉尘作业场所达到国家卫生标准。 第二章　防尘 第七条　凡有粉尘作业的企业、事业单位应采取综合防尘措施和无尘或低尘的新技术、新工艺、新设备，使作业场所的粉尘浓度不超过国家卫生标准。 第八条　尘肺病诊断标准由卫生行政部门制定，粉尘浓度卫生标准由卫生行政部门会同劳动等有关部门联合制定。 第九条　防尘设施的鉴定和定型制度，由劳动部门会同卫生行政部门制定。任何企业、事业单位除特殊情况外，未经上级主管部门批准，不得停止运行或者拆除防尘设施。 第十条　防尘经费应当纳入基本建设和技术改造经费计划，专款专用，不得挪用。 第十一条　严禁任何企业、事业单位将粉尘作业转嫁、外包或以联营的形式给没有防尘设施的乡镇、街道企业或个体工商户。

---

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为保护职工健康，消除粉尘危害，防止发生尘肺病，促进生产发展，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated to protect the health of workers, eliminate dust hazards, prevent the occurrence of pneumoconiosis, and promote the development of production.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本条例适用于所有有粉尘作业的企业、事业单位。

**Article 2** These Regulations shall apply to all enterprises and institutions with dust operations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　尘肺病系指在生产活动中吸入粉尘而发生的肺组织纤维化为主的疾病。

**Article 3** Pneumoconiosis refers to a disease mainly characterized by pulmonary fibrosis resulting from the inhalation of dust during production activities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　地方各级人民政府要加强对尘肺病防治工作的领导。在制定本地区国民经济和社会发展计划时，要统筹安排尘肺病防治工作。

**Article 4** Local people's governments at all levels shall strengthen leadership over the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis. In formulating the local plans for national economic and social development, they shall make overall arrangements for the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　企业、事业单位的主管部门应当根据国家卫生等有关标准，结合实际情况，制定所属企业的尘肺病防治规划，并督促其施行。  
乡镇企业主管部门，必须指定专人负责乡镇企业尘肺病的防治工作，建立监督检查制度，并指导乡镇企业对尘肺病的防治工作。

**Article 5** The competent departments of enterprises and institutions shall, in accordance with the national health and other relevant standards and in light of the actual conditions, formulate plans for the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis of enterprises under their jurisdiction, and supervise their implementation.  
The competent departments of township enterprises must designate specialized personnel to be responsible for the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis in township enterprises, establish a supervision and inspection system, and guide the township enterprises in the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业、事业单位的负责人，对本单位的尘肺病防治工作负有直接责任，应采取有效措施使本单位的粉尘作业场所达到国家卫生标准。

**Article 6** The persons in charge of enterprises and institutions shall bear direct responsibility for the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis in their units, and shall take effective measures to ensure that the dust operation sites in their units meet the national health standards.

### 第二章　防尘

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　凡有粉尘作业的企业、事业单位应采取综合防尘措施和无尘或低尘的新技术、新工艺、新设备，使作业场所的粉尘浓度不超过国家卫生标准。

**Article 7** All enterprises and institutions with dust operations shall adopt comprehensive dust prevention measures and new technologies, new processes, and new equipment that are dust-free or low-dust, so that the dust concentration at the operation sites does not exceed the national health standards.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　尘肺病诊断标准由卫生行政部门制定，粉尘浓度卫生标准由卫生行政部门会同劳动等有关部门联合制定。

**Article 8** The diagnostic criteria for pneumoconiosis shall be formulated by the administrative department of health, and the health standards for dust concentration shall be jointly formulated by the administrative department of health together with the labor and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　防尘设施的鉴定和定型制度，由劳动部门会同卫生行政部门制定。任何企业、事业单位除特殊情况外，未经上级主管部门批准，不得停止运行或者拆除防尘设施。

**Article 9** The system for appraisal and finalization of dust prevention facilities shall be formulated by the labor department in conjunction with the administrative department of health. Except under special circumstances, no enterprise or institution shall, without the approval of the competent higher authority, stop the operation or dismantle the dust prevention facilities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　防尘经费应当纳入基本建设和技术改造经费计划，专款专用，不得挪用。

**Article 10** The funds for dust prevention shall be included in the plan for capital construction and technical renovation funds, used for specific purposes, and shall not be misappropriated.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　严禁任何企业、事业单位将粉尘作业转嫁、外包或以联营的形式给没有防尘设施的乡镇、街道企业或个体工商户。  
中、小学校各类校办的实习工厂或车间，禁止从事有粉尘的作业。

**Article 11** It is strictly prohibited for any enterprise or institution to transfer, subcontract, or in the form of joint operation, assign dust operations to township or street enterprises or individual industrial and commercial households without dust prevention facilities.  
All kinds of school-run practice factories or workshops in primary and secondary schools are prohibited from engaging in dust operations.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　职工使用的防止粉尘危害的防护用品，必须符合国家的有关标准。企业、事业单位应当建立严格的管理制度，并教育职工按规定和要求使用。  
对初次从事粉尘作业的职工，由其所在单位进行防尘知识教育和考核，考试合格后方可从事粉尘作业。  
不满十八周岁的未成年人，禁止从事粉尘作业。

**Article 12** The protective equipment used by workers to prevent dust hazards must comply with the relevant national standards. Enterprises and institutions shall establish strict management systems and educate workers to use such equipment in accordance with the provisions and requirements.  
For workers who engage in dust operations for the first time, the unit they work for shall provide education on dust prevention knowledge and conduct an examination; only after passing the examination may they engage in dust operations.  
Minors under the age of 18 are prohibited from engaging in dust operations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　新建、改建、扩建、续建有粉尘作业的工程项目，防尘设施必须与主体工程同时设计、同时施工、同时投产。设计任务书，必须经当地卫生行政部门、劳动部门和工会组织审查同意后，方可施工。竣工验收，应由当地卫生行政部门、劳动部门和工会组织参加，凡不符合要求的，不得投产。

**Article 13** For newly constructed, reconstructed, expanded, or continued construction projects with dust operations, the dust prevention facilities must be designed, constructed, and put into operation simultaneously with the main project. The design task document must be reviewed and approved by the local administrative department of health, the labor department, and the trade union organization before construction may commence. For completion acceptance, the local administrative department of health, the labor department, and the trade union organization shall participate; if the requirements are not met, the project shall not be put into operation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　作业场所的粉尘浓度超过国家卫生标准，又未积极治理，严重影响职工安全健康时，职工有权拒绝操作。

**Article 14** When the dust concentration at the operation site exceeds the national health standards, no active control measures are taken, and the safety and health of workers are seriously affected, workers shall have the right to refuse to operate.

### 第三章　监督和监测

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　卫生行政部门、劳动部门和工会组织分工协作，互相配合，对企业、事业单位的尘肺病防治工作进行监督。

**Article 15** The administrative department of health, the labor department, and the trade union organization shall divide their work, cooperate, and supervise the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis in enterprises and institutions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　卫生行政部门负责卫生标准的监测；劳动部门负责劳动卫生工程技术标准的监测。  
工会组织负责组织职工群众对本单位的尘肺病防治工作进行监督，并教育职工遵守操作规程与防尘制度。

**Article 16** The administrative department of health shall be responsible for the monitoring of health standards; the labor department shall be responsible for the monitoring of labor health engineering and technical standards.  
The trade union organization shall be responsible for organizing the workers to supervise the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis in their units, and for educating workers to comply with the operating procedures and dust prevention systems.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　凡有粉尘作业的企业、事业单位，必须定期测定作业场所的粉尘浓度。测尘结果必须向主管部门和当地卫生行政部门、劳动部门和工会组织报告，并定期向职工公布。  
从事粉尘作业的单位必须建立测尘资料档案。

**Article 17** All enterprises and institutions with dust operations must regularly measure the dust concentration at the operation sites. The measurement results must be reported to the competent department and the local administrative department of health, the labor department, and the trade union organization, and be published to workers on a regular basis.  
Units engaged in dust operations must establish archives of dust measurement data.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　卫生行政部门和劳动部门，要对从事粉尘作业的企业、事业单位的测尘机构加强业务指导，并对测尘人员加强业务指导和技术培训。

**Article 18** The administrative department of health and the labor department shall strengthen professional guidance to the dust measurement institutions of enterprises and institutions engaged in dust operations, and shall strengthen professional guidance and technical training to the dust measurement personnel.

### 第四章　健康管理

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　各企业、事业单位对新从事粉尘作业的职工，必须进行健康检查。对在职和离职的从事粉尘作业的职工，必须定期进行健康检查。检查的内容、期限和尘肺病诊断标准，按卫生行政部门有关职业病管理的规定执行。

**Article 19** All enterprises and institutions must conduct health examinations for workers newly engaged in dust operations. For workers who are currently or have formerly been engaged in dust operations, regular health examinations must be conducted. The contents, time limits, and diagnostic criteria for pneumoconiosis shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the administrative department of health on occupational disease management.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　各企业、事业单位必须贯彻执行职业病报告制度，按期向当地卫生行政部门、劳动部门、工会组织和本单位的主管部门报告职工尘肺病发生和死亡情况。

**Article 20** All enterprises and institutions must implement the occupational disease reporting system, and report on the incidence and deaths from pneumoconiosis among workers to the local administrative department of health, the labor department, the trade union organization, and the competent department of their units within the prescribed time limit.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　各企业、事业单位对已确诊为尘肺病的职工，必须调离粉尘作业岗位，并给予治疗或疗养。尘肺病患者的社会保险待遇，按国家有关规定办理。

**Article 21** All enterprises and institutions, for workers who have been definitively diagnosed with pneumoconiosis, must transfer them from dust operation posts and provide them with treatment or convalescence. The social insurance benefits for pneumoconiosis patients shall be handled in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

### 第五章　奖励和处罚

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对在尘肺病防治工作中做出显著成绩的单位和个人，由其上级主管部门给予奖励。

**Article 22** Units and individuals that have made outstanding achievements in the prevention and treatment of pneumoconiosis shall be rewarded by their competent higher authorities.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　凡违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，卫生行政部门和劳动部门，可视其情节轻重，给予警告、限期治理、罚款和停业整顿的处罚。但停业整顿的处罚，需经当地人民政府同意。  
(一)作业场所粉尘浓度超过国家卫生标准，逾期不采取措施的；  
(二)任意拆除防尘设施，致使粉尘危害严重的；  
(三)挪用防尘措施经费的；  
(四)工程设计和竣工验收未经卫生行政部门、劳动部门和工会组织审查同意，擅自施工、投产的；  
(五)将粉尘作业转嫁、外包或以联营的形式给没有防尘设施的乡镇、街道企业或个体工商户的；  
(六)不执行健康检查制度和测尘制度的；  
(七)强令尘肺病患者继续从事粉尘作业的；  
(八)假报测尘结果或尘肺病诊断结果的；  
(九)安排未成年人从事粉尘作业的。

**Article 23** Whoever violates the provisions of these Regulations and commits any of the following acts, the administrative department of health and the labor department may, depending on the severity of the circumstances, impose penalties including a warning, an order to rectify within a time limit, a fine, and an order to suspend operations for rectification. However, the penalty of suspension of operations for rectification shall require the consent of the local people's government.  
(1) The dust concentration at the operation site exceeds the national health standards and no measures are taken within the prescribed time limit;  
(2) Arbitrarily dismantling dust prevention facilities, resulting in serious dust hazards;  
(3) Misappropriating funds for dust prevention measures;  
(4) Commencing construction or operation without the review and approval of the administrative department of health, the labor department, and the trade union organization for the engineering design and completion acceptance;  
(5) Transferring, subcontracting, or assigning dust operations in the form of joint operation to township or street enterprises or individual industrial and commercial households without dust prevention facilities;  
(6) Failing to implement the health examination system and the dust measurement system;  
(7) Compelling pneumoconiosis patients to continue engaging in dust operations;  
(8) Falsely reporting dust measurement results or pneumoconiosis diagnosis results;  
(9) Arranging for minors to engage in dust operations.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　当事人对处罚不服的，可在接到处罚通知之日起十五日内，向作出处理的部门的上级机关申请复议。但是，对停业整顿的决定应当立即执行。上级机关应当在接到申请之日起三十日内作出答复。对答复不服的，可以在接到答复之日起十五日内，向人民法院起诉。

**Article 24** If a party disagrees with the penalty, it may, within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the penalty notice, apply to the higher authority of the department that made the decision for reconsideration. However, the decision to suspend operations for rectification shall be executed immediately. The higher authority shall make a reply within thirty days from the date of receipt of the application. If the party disagrees with the reply, it may file a prosecution with the People's Court within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the reply.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　企业、事业单位负责人和监督、监测人员玩忽职守，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受损失，情节轻微的，由其主管部门给予行政处分；造成重大损失，构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究直接责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 25** If the persons in charge of enterprises and institutions, or the supervision and monitoring personnel, neglect their duties, causing losses to public property, the State, and the interests of the people, where the circumstances are minor, they shall be given administrative sanctions by their competent departments; where major losses are caused and a crime is constituted, the judicial organs shall, according to law, pursue the criminal liability of the persons directly responsible.

### 第六章　附则

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本条例由国务院卫生行政部门和劳动部门联合进行解释。

**Article 26** These Regulations shall be jointly interpreted by the administrative department of health and the labor department of the State Council.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　各省、自治区、直辖市人民政府应当结合当地实际情况，制定本条例的实施办法。

**Article 27** The people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government shall, in light of local actual conditions, formulate implementing measures for these Regulations.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本条例自发布之日起施行。

**Article 28** These Regulations shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
