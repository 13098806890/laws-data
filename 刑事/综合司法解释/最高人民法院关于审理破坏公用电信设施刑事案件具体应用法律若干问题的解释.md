# 最高人民法院关于审理破坏公用电信设施刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕21号  
**公布日期**：2004-12-30  
**生效日期**：2005-01-11

> 《最高人民法院关于审理破坏公用电信设施刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2004年8月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1322次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年1月11日起施行。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　采用截断通信线路、损毁通信设备或者删除、修改、增加电信网计算机信息系统中存储、处理或者传输的数据和应用程序等手段，故意破坏正在使用的公用电信设施，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)规定的“危害公共安全”，依照[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)第一款规定，以破坏公用电信设施罪处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：  


**Article 1** Where a person, by means of cutting off communication lines, damaging communication equipment, or deleting, modifying, or adding data and application programs stored, processed, or transmitted in the computer information system of a telecommunications network, intentionally damages public telecommunications facilities in use, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "endangering public security" as stipulated in Article 124 of the Criminal Law, and the person shall, in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 124 of the Criminal Law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years for the crime of damaging public telecommunications facilities: (1) causing interruption or serious obstruction of communications for fire alarm, police alarm, medical emergency, traffic accident alarm, disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, etc., and thereby delaying rescue, treatment, disaster relief, emergency rescue, etc., resulting in the death of one person, serious injury to three or more persons, or causing property losses of not less than 300,000 yuan; (2) causing interruption of communications to not less than 2,000 but less than 10,000 users for more than one hour, or to 10,000 or more users for less than one hour; (3) within the scope of one local network, full blockage of inter-network communication, complete interruption of all connections from a gateway office to a certain office, or complete interruption of a certain inter-network service for less than two hours, or the scope of direct impact being less than 50,000 (user × hours); (4) causing serious obstruction of inter-network communication for a cumulative period of not less than two hours but less than twelve hours within one day; or (5) other circumstances that endanger public security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)第一款规定的“严重后果”，以破坏公用电信设施罪处七年以上有期徒刑：  


**Article 2** Where a person commits the act specified in Article 1 of this Interpretation and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious consequences" as stipulated in paragraph 1 of Article 124 of the Criminal Law, and the person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years for the crime of damaging public telecommunications facilities: (1) causing interruption or serious obstruction of communications for fire alarm, police alarm, medical emergency, traffic accident alarm, disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, etc., and thereby delaying rescue, treatment, disaster relief, emergency rescue, etc., resulting in the death of two or more persons, serious injury to six or more persons, or causing property losses of not less than 600,000 yuan; (2) causing interruption of communications to 10,000 or more users for more than one hour; (3) within the scope of one local network, full blockage of inter-network communication, complete interruption of all connections from a gateway office to a certain office, or complete interruption of a certain inter-network service for two hours or more, or the scope of direct impact being 50,000 (user × hours) or more; (4) causing serious obstruction of inter-network communication for a cumulative period of twelve hours or more within one day; or (5) causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　故意破坏正在使用的公用电信设施尚未危害公共安全，或者故意毁坏尚未投入使用的公用电信设施，造成财物损失，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第二百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-275)规定，以故意毁坏财物罪定罪处罚。  


**Article 3** Where a person intentionally damages public telecommunications facilities in use but does not endanger public security, or intentionally destroys public telecommunications facilities not yet put into use, causing property losses and constituting a crime, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of intentionally destroying property in accordance with Article 275 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person steals public telecommunications facilities of relatively small value but the act constitutes a crime of endangering public security, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 124 of the Criminal Law; where the act of stealing public telecommunications facilities simultaneously constitutes the crime of theft and the crime of damaging public telecommunications facilities, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision that imposes a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　指使、组织、教唆他人实施本解释规定的故意犯罪行为的，按照共犯定罪处罚。


**Article 4** A person who directs, organizes, or instigates another person to commit the intentional criminal acts specified in this Interpretation shall be convicted and punished as an accomplice.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　本解释中规定的公用电信设施的范围、用户数、通信中断和严重障碍的标准和时间长度，依据国家电信行业主管部门的有关规定确定。


**Article 5** The scope of public telecommunications facilities, the number of users, and the standards and durations for communication interruption and serious obstruction as specified in this Interpretation shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent national telecommunications industry authority.
