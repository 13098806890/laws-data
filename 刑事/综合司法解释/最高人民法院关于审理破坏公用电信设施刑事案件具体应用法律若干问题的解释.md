# 最高人民法院关于审理破坏公用电信设施刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕21号  
**公布日期**：2004-12-30  
**生效日期**：2005-01-11

> 《最高人民法院关于审理破坏公用电信设施刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2004年8月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1322次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年1月11日起施行。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　采用截断通信线路、损毁通信设备或者删除、修改、增加电信网计算机信息系统中存储、处理或者传输的数据和应用程序等手段，故意破坏正在使用的公用电信设施，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)规定的“危害公共安全”，依照[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)第一款规定，以破坏公用电信设施罪处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：  

**Article 1** Where any person intentionally damages a public telecommunications facility in service by severing communication lines, damaging communication equipment, or deleting, modifying, or adding data or application programs stored, processed, or transmitted in a telecommunications network computer system, and such act falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute "endangering public security" as provided for in Article 124 of the Criminal Law. In accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 124 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years for the crime of damaging public telecommunications facilities:
(1) Causing communication interruption or serious obstruction for fire alarm, police alarm, medical emergency, traffic accident reporting, disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, or similar services, thereby delaying rescue, medical treatment, disaster relief, or emergency response, and resulting in the death of one person, serious injury to three or more persons, or property losses of 300,000 yuan or more;
(2) Causing communication interruption for more than one hour affecting not less than 2,000 but fewer than 10,000 users, or communication interruption for less than one hour affecting 10,000 or more users;
(3) Within the scope of one local network, causing complete blockage of inter-network communication, complete interruption of all connections from a gateway office to a certain office direction, or complete interruption of a certain inter-network service for less than two hours, or directly affecting fewer than 50,000 (user × hours);
(4) Causing serious obstruction to inter-network communication, with a cumulative duration of not less than two hours but less than twelve hours within one day;
(5) Other circumstances that endanger public security.
（一）造成火警、匪警、医疗急救、交通事故报警、救灾、抢险、防汛等通信中断或者严重障碍，并因此贻误救助、救治、救灾、抢险等，致使人员死亡一人、重伤三人以上或者造成财产损失三十万元以上的；  
（二）造成二千以上不满一万用户通信中断一小时以上，或者一万以上用户通信中断不满一小时的；  
（三）在一个本地网范围内，网间通信全阻、关口局至某一局向全部中断或网间某一业务全部中断不满二小时或者直接影响范围不满五万（用户×小时）的；  
（四）造成网间通信严重障碍，一日内累计二小时以上不满十二小时的；  
（五）其他危害公共安全的情形。

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)第一款规定的“严重后果”，以破坏公用电信设施罪处七年以上有期徒刑：  

**Article 2** Where any person commits the acts specified in Article 1 of this Interpretation, and such acts fall under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute "serious consequences" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 124 of the Criminal Law, and the person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years for the crime of damaging public telecommunications facilities:
(1) Causing communication interruption or serious obstruction for fire alarm, police alarm, medical emergency, traffic accident reporting, disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, or similar services, thereby delaying rescue, medical treatment, disaster relief, or emergency response, and resulting in the death of two or more persons, serious injury to six or more persons, or property losses of 600,000 yuan or more;
(2) Causing communication interruption for more than one hour affecting 10,000 or more users;
(3) Within the scope of one local network, causing complete blockage of inter-network communication, complete interruption of all connections from a gateway office to a certain office direction, or complete interruption of a certain inter-network service for two hours or more, or directly affecting 50,000 (user × hours) or more;
(4) Causing serious obstruction to inter-network communication, with a cumulative duration of twelve hours or more within one day;
(5) Causing other serious consequences.
（一）造成火警、匪警、医疗急救、交通事故报警、救灾、抢险、防汛等通信中断或者严重障碍，并因此贻误救助、救治、救灾、抢险等，致使人员死亡二人以上、重伤六人以上或者造成财产损失六十万元以上的；  
（二）造成一万以上用户通信中断一小时以上的；  
（三）在一个本地网范围内，网间通信全阻、关口局至某一局向全部中断或网间某一业务全部中断二小时以上或者直接影响范围五万（用户×小时）以上的；  
（四）造成网间通信严重障碍，一日内累计十二小时以上的；  
（五）造成其他严重后果的。

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　故意破坏正在使用的公用电信设施尚未危害公共安全，或者故意毁坏尚未投入使用的公用电信设施，造成财物损失，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第二百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-275)规定，以故意毁坏财物罪定罪处罚。  

**Article 3** Where any person intentionally damages a public telecommunications facility in service but such act does not yet endanger public security, or intentionally destroys a public telecommunications facility not yet put into service, causing property damage and constituting a crime, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of intentionally damaging property in accordance with Article 275 of the Criminal Law.
Where any person steals a public telecommunications facility of relatively small value, but the act constitutes a crime of endangering public security, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 124 of the Criminal Law; where the theft of a public telecommunications facility simultaneously constitutes both the crime of theft and the crime of damaging public telecommunications facilities, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision providing for the heavier punishment.
盗窃公用电信设施价值数额不大，但是构成危害公共安全犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)的规定定罪处罚；盗窃公用电信设施同时构成盗窃罪和破坏公用电信设施罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　指使、组织、教唆他人实施本解释规定的故意犯罪行为的，按照共犯定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Any person who directs, organizes, or instigates another person to commit the intentional criminal acts specified in this Interpretation shall be convicted and punished as an accomplice.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　本解释中规定的公用电信设施的范围、用户数、通信中断和严重障碍的标准和时间长度，依据国家电信行业主管部门的有关规定确定。

**Article 5** The scope of public telecommunications facilities, number of users, and the standards and duration for communication interruption and serious obstruction as specified in this Interpretation shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the competent national telecommunications industry authorities.
