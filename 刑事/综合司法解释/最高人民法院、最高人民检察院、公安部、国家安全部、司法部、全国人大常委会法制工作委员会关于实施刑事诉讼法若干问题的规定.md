# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于实施刑事诉讼法若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2012-12-26  
**生效日期**：2013-01-01

> （2012年12月26日发布 自2013年1月1日起施行） 一、管 辖 1．公安机关侦查刑事案件涉及人民检察院管辖的贪污贿赂案件时，应当将贪污贿赂案件移送人民检察院；人民检察院侦查贪污贿赂案件涉及公安机关管辖的刑事案件，应当将属于公安机关管辖的刑事案件移送公安机关。在上述情况中，如果涉嫌主罪属于公安机关管辖，由公安机关为主侦查，人民检察院予以配合；如果涉嫌主罪属于人民检察院管辖，由人民检察院为主侦查，公安机关予以配合。 2．刑事诉讼法第二十四条中规定：“刑事案件由犯罪地的人民法院管辖。”刑事诉讼法规定的“犯罪地”，包括犯罪的行为发生地和结果发生地。 3．具有下列情形之一的，人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关可以在其职责范围内并案处理： （一）一人犯数罪的； （二）共同犯罪的； （三）共同犯罪的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人还实施其他犯罪的； （四）多个犯罪嫌疑人、被告人实施的犯罪存在关联，并案处理有利于查明案件事实的。 二、辩护与代理 4．人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、国家安全机关、监狱的现职人员，人民陪审员，外国人或者无国籍人，以及与本案有利害关系的人，不得担任辩护人。但是，上述人员系犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的监护人或者近亲属，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人委托其担任辩护人的，可以准许。无行为能力或者限制行为能力的人，不得担任辩护人。 一名辩护人不得为两名以上的同案犯罪嫌疑人、被告人辩护，不得为两名以上的未同案处理但实施的犯罪存在关联的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人辩护。 5．刑事诉讼法第三十四条、第二百六十七条、第二百八十六条对法律援助作了规定。对于人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关根据上述规定，通知法律援助机构指派律师提供辩护或者法律帮助的，法律援助机构应当在接到通知后三日以内指派律师，并将律师的姓名、单位、联系方式书面通知人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关。 6．刑事诉讼法第三十六条规定：“辩护律师在侦查期间可以为犯罪嫌疑人提供法律帮助；代理申诉、控告；申请变更强制措施；向侦查机关了解犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌的罪名和案件有关情况，提出意见。”根据上述规定，辩护律师在侦查期间可以向侦查机关了解犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌的罪名及当时已查明的该罪的主要事实，犯罪嫌疑人被采取、变更、解除强制措施的情况，侦查机关延长侦查羁押期限等情况。 7．刑事诉讼法第三十七条第二款规定：“辩护律师持律师执业证书、律师事务所证明和委托书或者法律援助公函要求会见在押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的，看守所应当及时安排会见，至迟不得超过四十八小时。”根据上述规定，辩护律师要求会见在 押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的，看守所应当及时安排会见，保证辩护律师在四十八小时以内见到在押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、管 辖  
1．公安机关侦查刑事案件涉及人民检察院管辖的贪污贿赂案件时，应当将贪污贿赂案件移送人民检察院；人民检察院侦查贪污贿赂案件涉及公安机关管辖的刑事案件，应当将属于公安机关管辖的刑事案件移送公安机关。在上述情况中，如果涉嫌主罪属于公安机关管辖，由公安机关为主侦查，人民检察院予以配合；如果涉嫌主罪属于人民检察院管辖，由人民检察院为主侦查，公安机关予以配合。  
2．[刑事诉讼法第二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-24)中规定：“刑事案件由犯罪地的人民法院管辖。”刑事诉讼法规定的“犯罪地”，包括犯罪的行为发生地和结果发生地。  
3．具有下列情形之一的，人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关可以在其职责范围内并案处理：  
（一）一人犯数罪的；  
（二）共同犯罪的；  
（三）共同犯罪的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人还实施其他犯罪的；  
（四）多个犯罪嫌疑人、被告人实施的犯罪存在关联，并案处理有利于查明案件事实的。

**Article 1** I. Jurisdiction  
1. Where a public security organ, during the investigation of a criminal case, discovers that the case involves an embezzlement or bribery case under the jurisdiction of a People's Procuratorate, it shall transfer the embezzlement or bribery case to the People's Procuratorate; where a People's Procuratorate, during the investigation of an embezzlement or bribery case, discovers that the case involves a criminal case under the jurisdiction of a public security organ, it shall transfer the criminal case under the jurisdiction of the public security organ to the public security organ. In the above circumstances, if the suspected principal crime is under the jurisdiction of the public security organ, the public security organ shall take the lead in the investigation, and the People's Procuratorate shall cooperate; if the suspected principal crime is under the jurisdiction of the People's Procuratorate, the People's Procuratorate shall take the lead in the investigation, and the public security organ shall cooperate.  
2. Article 24 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "Criminal cases shall be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court of the place where the crime was committed." The "place where the crime was committed" as provided for in the Criminal Procedure Law includes the place where the criminal act occurred and the place where the result occurred.  
3. A People's Court, a People's Procuratorate, and a public security organ may, within the scope of their functions and powers, consolidate cases for handling under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Where one person commits several crimes;  
(2) Where the crime is a joint crime;  
(3) Where a criminal suspect or defendant of a joint crime also commits other crimes;  
(4) Where the crimes committed by multiple criminal suspects or defendants are related, and consolidation facilitates the ascertainment of the facts of the case.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、辩护与代理  
4．人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、国家安全机关、监狱的现职人员，人民陪审员，外国人或者无国籍人，以及与本案有利害关系的人，不得担任辩护人。但是，上述人员系犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的监护人或者近亲属，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人委托其担任辩护人的，可以准许。无行为能力或者限制行为能力的人，不得担任辩护人。  
一名辩护人不得为两名以上的同案犯罪嫌疑人、被告人辩护，不得为两名以上的未同案处理但实施的犯罪存在关联的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人辩护。  
5．[刑事诉讼法第三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-34)、第二百六十七条、第二百八十六条对法律援助作了规定。对于人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关根据上述规定，通知法律援助机构指派律师提供辩护或者法律帮助的，法律援助机构应当在接到通知后三日以内指派律师，并将律师的姓名、单位、联系方式书面通知人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关。  
6．[刑事诉讼法第三十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-36)规定：“辩护律师在侦查期间可以为犯罪嫌疑人提供法律帮助；代理申诉、控告；申请变更强制措施；向侦查机关了解犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌的罪名和案件有关情况，提出意见。”根据上述规定，辩护律师在侦查期间可以向侦查机关了解犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌的罪名及当时已查明的该罪的主要事实，犯罪嫌疑人被采取、变更、解除强制措施的情况，侦查机关延长侦查羁押期限等情况。  
7．[刑事诉讼法第三十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-37)第二款规定：“辩护律师持律师执业证书、律师事务所证明和委托书或者法律援助公函要求会见在押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的，看守所应当及时安排会见，至迟不得超过四十八小时。”根据上述规定，辩护律师要求会见在 押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的，看守所应当及时安排会见，保证辩护律师在四十八小时以内见到在押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人。  
8．[刑事诉讼法第四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-41)第一款规定：“辩护律师经证人或者其他有关单位和个人同意，可以向他们收集与本案有关的材料，也可以申请人民检察院、人民法院收集、调取证据，或者申请人民法院通知证人出庭作证。”对于辩护律师申请人民检察院、人民法院收集、调取证据，人民检察院、人民法院认为需要调查取证的，应当由人民检察院、人民法院收集、调取证据，不得向律师签发准许调查决定书，让律师收集、调取证据。  
9．[刑事诉讼法第四十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-42)第二款中规定：“违反前款规定的，应当依法追究法律责任，辩护人涉嫌犯罪的，应当由办理辩护人所承办案件的侦查机关以外的侦查机关办理。”根据上述规定，公安机关、人民检察院发现辩护人涉嫌犯罪，或者接受报案、控告、举报、有关机关的移送，依照侦查管辖分工进行审查后认为符合立案条件的，应当按照规定报请办理辩护人所承办案件的侦查机关的上一级侦查机关指定其他侦查机关立案侦查，或者由上一级侦查机关立案侦查。不得指定办理辩护人所承办案件的侦查机关的下级侦查机关立案侦查  
10．[刑事诉讼法第四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-47)规定：“辩护人、诉讼代理人认为公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院及其工作人员阻碍其依法行使诉讼权利的，有权向同级或者上一级人民检察院申诉或者控告。人民检察院对申诉或者控告应当及时进行审查，情况属实的，通知有关机关予以纠正。”人民检察院受理辩护人、诉讼代理人的申诉或者控告后，应当在十日以内将处理情况书面答复提出申诉或者控告的辩护人、诉讼代理人。

**Article 2** II. Defense and Representation  
4. Incumbent personnel of People's Courts, People's Procuratorates, public security organs, state security organs, and prisons, people's assessors, foreigners or stateless persons, and persons who have an interest in the case shall not serve as defenders. However, where the aforementioned persons are guardians or close relatives of a criminal suspect or defendant and the criminal suspect or defendant entrusts them to serve as defenders, this may be permitted. Persons with no capacity or limited capacity for civil conduct shall not serve as defenders.  
No defender shall serve as defender for more than two criminal suspects or defendants in the same case, nor shall he or she serve as defender for more than two criminal suspects or defendants who are not jointly dealt with but whose committed crimes are related.  
5. Articles 34, 267, and 286 of the Criminal Procedure Law contain provisions on legal aid. Where a People's Court, a People's Procuratorate, or a public security organ, in accordance with the above provisions, notifies a legal aid institution to assign a lawyer to provide defense or legal assistance, the legal aid institution shall assign a lawyer within three days after receiving the notice, and shall notify the People's Court, People's Procuratorate, or public security organ in writing of the lawyer's name, unit, and contact information.  
6. Article 36 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "During the investigation period, a defense lawyer may provide legal assistance to a criminal suspect, file petitions and complaints on his or her behalf, apply for alteration of compulsory measures, and inquire of the investigation organ about the crime suspected of being committed by the criminal suspect and the relevant information of the case, and provide opinions." According to the above provisions, during the investigation period, a defense lawyer may inquire of the investigation organ about the crime suspected of being committed by the criminal suspect and the main facts of that crime that have been ascertained up to that time, about the circumstances under which compulsory measures were taken, altered, or lifted against the criminal suspect, and about the extension of the period of custody for investigation by the investigation organ.  
7. Paragraph 2 of Article 37 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "Where a defense lawyer, holding a lawyer's practice certificate, a certificate from a law firm, and a letter of entrustment or a legal aid letter, requests to meet with a criminal suspect or defendant in custody, the detention house shall arrange the meeting in a timely manner, and the meeting shall in no case be delayed for more than 48 hours." According to the above provisions, where a defense lawyer requests to meet with a criminal suspect or defendant in custody, the detention house shall arrange the meeting in a timely manner and ensure that the defense lawyer meets with the criminal suspect or defendant in custody within 48 hours.  
8. Paragraph 1 of Article 41 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "With the consent of a witness or any other relevant entity or individual, a defense lawyer may collect from them materials related to the case, may also apply to the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court for the collection or retrieval of evidence, or may apply to the People's Court for a witness to testify in court." Where a defense lawyer applies to a People's Procuratorate or a People's Court for collection or retrieval of evidence, and the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court considers that investigation and evidence collection are necessary, the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court shall collect or retrieve the evidence themselves, and shall not issue a permit for investigation to the lawyer for the lawyer to collect or retrieve the evidence.  
9. Paragraph 2 of Article 42 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "Whoever violates the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be investigated for legal liability according to law. Where a defender is suspected of committing a crime, the case shall be handled by an investigation organ other than the one handling the case undertaken by the defender." According to the above provisions, where a public security organ or a People's Procuratorate discovers that a defender is suspected of committing a crime, or after receiving a report, complaint, tip-off, or transfer from a relevant organ, reviews the matter in accordance with the division of investigatory jurisdiction and considers that the conditions for filing a case are met, it shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions, report to the investigation organ at the next higher level over the investigation organ handling the case undertaken by the defender, requesting that it designate another investigation organ to file the case and conduct investigation, or the higher-level investigation organ itself shall file the case and conduct investigation. No lower-level investigation organ of the investigation organ handling the case undertaken by the defender may be designated to file the case and conduct investigation.  
10. Article 47 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "Where a defender or agent ad litem considers that a public security organ, a People's Procuratorate, a People's Court, or any of their staff members obstructs his or her exercise of litigation rights according to law, he or she has the right to file a petition or complaint with the People's Procuratorate at the same level or at the next higher level. The People's Procuratorate shall promptly review the petition or complaint; if the facts are verified, it shall notify the relevant organ to rectify the matter." After a People's Procuratorate accepts a petition or complaint from a defender or agent ad litem, it shall, within ten days, reply in writing to the defender or agent ad litem who filed the petition or complaint regarding the outcome of the handling.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、证 据  
11．[刑事诉讼法第五十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-56)第一款规定：“法庭审理过程中，审判人员认为可能存在本法第五十四条规定的以非法方法收集证据情形的，应当对证据收集的合法性进行法庭调查。”法庭经对当事人及其辩护人、诉讼代理人提供的相关线索或者材料进行审查后，认为可能存在[刑事诉讼法第五十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-54)规定的以非法方法收集证据情形的，应当对证据收集的合法性进行法庭调查。法庭调查的顺序由法庭根据案件审理情况确定。  
12．[刑事诉讼法第六十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-62)规定，对证人、鉴定人、被害人可以采取“不公开真实姓名、住址和工作单位等个人信息”的保护措施。人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关依法决定不公开证人、鉴定人、被害人的真实姓名、住址和工作单位等个人信息的，可以在判决书、裁定书、起诉书、询问笔录等法律文书、证据材料中使用化名等代替证人、鉴定人、被害人的个人信息。但是，应当书面说明使用化名的情况并标明密级，单独成卷。辩护律师经法庭许可，查阅对证人、鉴定人、被害人使用化名情况的，应当签署保密承诺书。

**Article 3** III. Evidence  
11. Paragraph 1 of Article 56 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "During the court hearing, if the judicial personnel consider that there may be circumstances of illegally collecting evidence as provided for in Article 54 of this Law, they shall conduct a court investigation into the legality of the evidence collection." After examining the relevant clues or materials provided by the parties and their defenders or agents ad litem, if the court considers that there may be circumstances of illegally collecting evidence as provided for in Article 54 of the Criminal Procedure Law, it shall conduct a court investigation into the legality of the evidence collection. The sequence of the court investigation shall be determined by the court based on the circumstances of the case trial.  
12. Article 62 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates that protective measures such as "not disclosing personal information such as the true name, address, and work unit" may be taken for witnesses, expert evaluators, and victims. Where a People's Court, a People's Procuratorate, or a public security organ decides according to law not to disclose the true name, address, work unit, or other personal information of a witness, expert evaluator, or victim, they may use pseudonyms or the like to replace the personal information of the witness, expert evaluator, or victim in legal documents and evidentiary materials such as judgments, rulings, indictments, and interrogation records. However, they shall provide a written explanation of the use of pseudonyms, indicate the classification level, and maintain separate files. If a defense lawyer, with permission from the court, reviews the use of pseudonyms for witnesses, expert evaluators, or victims, he or she shall sign a confidentiality commitment.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、强制措施  
13．被取保候审、监视居住的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人无正当理由不得离开所居住的市、县或者执行监视居住的处所，有正当理由需要离开所居住的市、县或者执行监视居住的处所，应当经执行机关批准。如果取保候审、监视居住是由人民检察院、人民法院决定的，执行机关在批准犯罪嫌疑人、被告人离开所居住的市、县或者执行监视居住的处所前，应当征得决定机关同意。  
14．对取保候审保证人是否履行了保证义务，由公安机关认定，对保证人的罚款决定，也由公安机关作出。  
15．指定居所监视居住的，不得要求被监视居住人支付费用。  
16．刑事诉讼法规定，拘留由公安机关执行。对于人民检察院直接受理的案件，人民检察院作出的拘留决定，应当送达公安机关执行，公安机关应当立即执行，人民检察院可以协助公安机关执行。  
17．对于人民检察院批准逮捕的决定，公安机关应当立即执行，并将执行回执及时送达批准逮捕的人民检察院。如果未能执行，也应当将回执送达人民检察院，并写明未能执行的原因。对于人民检察院决定不批准逮捕的，公安机关在收到不批准逮捕决定书后，应当立即释放在押的犯罪嫌疑人或者变更强制措施，并将执行回执在收到不批准逮捕决定书后的三日内送达作出不批准逮捕决定的人民检察院。

**Article 4** IV. Compulsory Measures  
13. A criminal suspect or defendant released on bail pending investigation or trial or under residential surveillance shall not leave the city or county where he or she resides or the place where residential surveillance is executed without justifiable reasons. If there is a justifiable reason to leave the city or county where he or she resides or the place where residential surveillance is executed, approval shall be obtained from the executing organ. If the release on bail or residential surveillance is decided by a People's Procuratorate or a People's Court, the executing organ shall obtain the consent of the deciding organ before approving the criminal suspect or defendant's departure from the city or county or the place of residential surveillance execution.  
14. The determination of whether a surety for release on bail has fulfilled the suretyship obligations shall be made by the public security organ, and the decision to impose a fine on the surety shall also be made by the public security organ.  
15. Where residential surveillance is executed at a designated location, the person under surveillance shall not be required to pay any expenses.  
16. The Criminal Procedure Law provides that detention shall be executed by a public security organ. For cases directly accepted by a People's Procuratorate, the detention decision made by the People's Procuratorate shall be served on the public security organ for execution, the public security organ shall execute it immediately, and the People's Procuratorate may assist the public security organ in the execution.  
17. A public security organ shall immediately execute a decision by a People's Procuratorate to approve an arrest, and shall promptly deliver the execution receipt to the People's Procuratorate that approved the arrest. If the execution cannot be carried out, the receipt shall still be delivered to the People's Procuratorate, stating the reasons for the failure to execute. Where a People's Procuratorate decides not to approve an arrest, the public security organ shall, upon receipt of the written decision not to approve the arrest, immediately release the criminal suspect in custody or alter the compulsory measure, and shall deliver the execution receipt within three days after receiving the written decision not to approve the arrest to the People's Procuratorate that made the decision not to approve the arrest.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、立 案  
18．[刑事诉讼法第一百一十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-111)规定：“人民检察院认为公安机关对应当立案侦查的案件而不立案侦查的，或者被害人认为公安机关对应当立案侦查的案件而不立案侦查，向人民检察院提出的，人民检察院应当要求公安机关说明不立案的理由。人民检察院认为公安机关不立案理由不能成立的，应当通知公安机关立案，公安机关接到通知后应当立案。”根据上述规定，公安机关收到人民检察院要求说明不立案理由通知书后，应当在七日内将说明情况书面答复人民检察院。人民检察院认为公安机关不立案理由不能成立，发出通知立案书时，应当将有关证明应当立案的材料同时移送公安机关。公安机关收到通知立案书后，应当在十五日内决定立案，并将立案决定书送达人民检察院。

**Article 5** V. Filing of a Case  
18. Article 111 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "If a People's Procuratorate considers that a public security organ should file a case for investigation but fails to do so, or if a victim considers that a public security organ should file a case for investigation but fails to do so and raises the matter with the People's Procuratorate, the People's Procuratorate shall require the public security organ to provide a written explanation of the reasons for not filing the case. If the People's Procuratorate considers that the reasons given by the public security organ for not filing the case are untenable, it shall notify the public security organ to file the case, and upon receipt of the notice, the public security organ shall file the case." According to the above provisions, after receiving the notice from the People's Procuratorate requesting an explanation of the reasons for not filing the case, the public security organ shall, within seven days, reply in writing to the People's Procuratorate with its explanation. When the People's Procuratorate considers that the reasons given by the public security organ for not filing the case are untenable and issues a notice to file the case, it shall simultaneously transfer the relevant materials proving that the case should be filed to the public security organ. After receiving the notice to file the case, the public security organ shall decide to file the case within 15 days and serve the written decision to file the case on the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、侦 查  
19．[刑事诉讼法第一百二十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-121)第一款规定：“侦查人员在讯问犯罪嫌疑人的时候，可以对讯问过程进行录音或者录像；对于可能判处无期徒刑、死刑的案件或者其他重大犯罪案件，应当对讯问过程进行录音或者录像。”侦查人员对讯问过程进行录音或者录像的，应当在讯问笔录中注明。人民检察院、人民法院可以根据需要调取讯问犯罪嫌疑人的录音或者录像，有关机关应当及时提供。  
20．[刑事诉讼法第一百四十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-149)中规定：“批准决定应当根据侦查犯罪的需要，确定采取技术侦查措施的种类和适用对象。”采取技术侦查措施收集的材料作为证据使用的，批准采取技术侦查措施的法律文书应当附卷，辩护律师可以依法查阅、摘抄、复制，在审判过程中可以向法庭出示。  
21．公安机关对案件提请延长羁押期限的，应当在羁押期限届满七日前提出，并书面呈报延长羁押期限案件的主要案情和延长羁押期限的具体理由，人民检察院应当在羁押期限届满前作出决定。  
22．[刑事诉讼法第一百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-158)第一款规定：“在侦查期间，发现犯罪嫌疑人另有重要罪行的，自发现之日起依照本法第一百五十四条的规定重新计算侦查羁押期限。”公安机关依照上述规定重新计算侦查羁押期限的，不需要经人民检察院批准，但应当报人民检察院备案，人民检察院可以进行监督。

**Article 6** VI. Investigation  
19. Paragraph 1 of Article 121 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "When interrogating a criminal suspect, investigators may make an audio or video recording of the interrogation process; for cases where the criminal suspect may be sentenced to life imprisonment, the death penalty, or for other major criminal cases, an audio or video recording of the interrogation process shall be made." Where investigators make an audio or video recording of the interrogation process, they shall indicate this in the interrogation record. A People's Procuratorate or a People's Court may, as needed, request the retrieval of audio or video recordings of the interrogation of a criminal suspect, and the relevant organ shall provide them in a timely manner.  
20. Article 149 of the Criminal Procedure Law provides: "The approval decision shall, based on the needs of criminal investigation, determine the types of technical investigation measures and the objects to which they apply." Where materials collected through technical investigation measures are used as evidence, the legal documents approving the adoption of technical investigation measures shall be attached to the case file, and the defense lawyer may, according to law, review, extract, or copy them, and may present them to the court during the trial.  
21. Where a public security organ requests an extension of the custody period for a case, it shall submit the request at least seven days before the custody period expires, and shall submit in writing the main facts of the case for which the custody period is to be extended and the specific reasons for the extension; the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision before the custody period expires.  
22. Paragraph 1 of Article 158 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "If, during the investigation period, it is discovered that the criminal suspect has committed another major crime, the period of custody for investigation shall be recalculated from the date of such discovery in accordance with the provisions of Article 154 of this Law." Where a public security organ recalculates the period of custody for investigation in accordance with the above provisions, it does not need approval from the People's Procuratorate, but shall report it to the People's Procuratorate for filing; the People's Procuratorate may exercise supervision.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、提起公诉  
23．上级公安机关指定下级公安机关立案侦查的案件，需要逮捕犯罪嫌疑人的，由侦查该案件的公安机关提请同级人民检察院审查批准；需要提起公诉的，由侦查该案件的公安机关移送同级人民检察院审查起诉。  
人民检察院对于审查起诉的案件，按照刑事诉讼法的管辖规定，认为应当由上级人民检察院或者同级其他人民检察院起诉的，应当将案件移送有管辖权的人民检察院。人民检察院认为需要依照刑事诉讼法的规定指定审判管辖的，应当协商同级人民法院办理指定管辖有关事宜。  
24．人民检察院向人民法院提起公诉时，应当将案卷材料和全部证据移送人民法院，包括犯罪嫌疑人、被告人翻供的材料，证人改变证言的材料，以及对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有利的其他证据材料。

**Article 7** VII. Initiation of Public Prosecution  
23. Where a case is filed for investigation by a lower-level public security organ designated by a higher-level public security organ, and an arrest of a criminal suspect is necessary, the public security organ investigating the case shall submit the matter to the People's Procuratorate at the same level for review and approval; where public prosecution is necessary, the public security organ investigating the case shall transfer the case to the People's Procuratorate at the same level for review and prosecution.  
When handling a case transferred for review and prosecution, if a People's Procuratorate considers, in accordance with the jurisdictional provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law, that the case should be prosecuted by a higher-level People's Procuratorate or another People's Procuratorate at the same level, it shall transfer the case to the People's Procuratorate with jurisdiction. If a People's Procuratorate considers that it is necessary to designate trial jurisdiction in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law, it shall consult with the People's Court at the same level regarding the relevant matters of designating jurisdiction.  
24. When a People's Procuratorate initiates a public prosecution with a People's Court, it shall transfer all case files and all evidence to the People's Court, including materials in which a criminal suspect or defendant retracts his or her confession, materials in which a witness changes his or her testimony, and other evidentiary materials favorable to the criminal suspect or defendant.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、审 判  
25．[刑事诉讼法第一百八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-181)规定：“人民法院对提起公诉的案件进行审查后，对于起诉书中有明确的指控犯罪事实的，应当决定开庭审判。”对于人民检察院提起公诉的案件，人民法院都应当受理。人民法院对提起公诉的案件进行审查后，对于起诉书中有明确的指控犯罪事实并且附有案卷材料、证据的，应当决定开庭审判，不得以上述材料不充足为由而不开庭审判。如果人民检察院移送的材料中缺少上述材料的，人民法院可以通知人民检察院补充材料，人民检察院应当自收到通知之日起三日内补送。  
人民法院对提起公诉的案件进行审查的期限计入人民法院的审理期限。  
26．人民法院开庭审理公诉案件时，出庭的检察人员和辩护人需要出示、宣读、播放已移交人民法院的证据的，可以申请法庭出示、宣读、播放。  
27．[刑事诉讼法第三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-39)规定：“辩护人认为在侦查、审查起诉期间公安机关、人民检察院收集的证明犯罪嫌疑人、被告人无罪或者罪轻的证据材料未提交的，有权申请人民检察院、人民法院调取。”第一百九十一条第一款规定：“法庭审理过程中，合议庭对证据有疑问的，可以宣布休庭，对证据进行调查核实。”第一百九十二条第一款规定：“法庭审理过程中，当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人有权申请通知新的证人到庭，调取新的物证，申请重新鉴定或者勘验。”根据上述规定，自案件移送审查起诉之日起，人民检察院可以根据辩护人的申请，向公安机关调取未提交的证明犯罪嫌疑人、被告人无罪或者罪轻的证据材料。在法庭审理过程中，人民法院可以根据辩护人的申请，向人民检察院调取未提交的证明被告人无罪或者罪轻的证据材料，也可以向人民检察院调取需要调查核实的证据材料。公安机关、人民检察院应当自收到要求调取证据材料决定书后三日内移交。  
28．人民法院依法通知证人、鉴定人出庭作证的，应当同时将证人、鉴定人出庭通知书送交控辩双方，控辩双方应当予以配合。  
29．[刑事诉讼法第一百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-187)第三款规定：“公诉人、当事人或者辩护人、诉讼代理人对鉴定意见有异议，人民法院认为鉴定人有必要出庭的，鉴定人应当出庭作证。经人民法院通知，鉴定人拒不出庭作证的，鉴定意见不得作为定案的根据。”根据上述规定，依法应当出庭的鉴定人经人民法院通知未出庭作证的，鉴定意见不得作为定案的根据。鉴定人由于不能抗拒的原因或者有其他正当理由无法出庭的，人民法院可以根据案件审理情况决定延期审理。  
30．人民法院审理公诉案件，发现有新的事实，可能影响定罪的，人民检察院可以要求补充起诉或者变更起诉，人民法院可以建议人民检察院补充起诉或者变更起诉。人民法院建议人民检察院补充起诉或者变更起诉的，人民检察院应当在七日以内回复意见。  
31．法庭审理过程中，被告人揭发他人犯罪行为或者提供重要线索，人民检察院认为需要进行查证的，可以建议补充侦查。  
32．[刑事诉讼法第二百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-203)规定：“人民检察院发现人民法院审理案件违反法律规定的诉讼程序，有权向人民法院提出纠正意见。”人民检察院对违反法定程序的庭审活动提出纠正意见，应当由人民检察院在庭审后提出。

**Article 8** VIII. Trial  
25. Article 181 of the Criminal Procedure Law stipulates: "After a People's Court has reviewed a case in which a public prosecution has been initiated, if the indictment contains clear facts of the alleged crime, it shall decide to hold a court session for trial." A People's Court shall accept all cases in which a public prosecution has been initiated by a People's Procuratorate. After reviewing a case in which a public prosecution has been initiated, if the indictment contains clear facts of the alleged crime and is accompanied by case files and evidence, the People's Court shall decide to hold a court session for trial, and shall not refuse to hold a court trial on the ground that the above materials are insufficient. If the materials transferred by the People's Procuratorate lack the above materials, the People's Court may notify the People's Procuratorate to supplement the materials, and the People's Procuratorate shall, within three days from receipt of the notice, supplement and submit them.  
The period for the People's Court to review a case in which a public prosecution has been initiated shall be included in the trial period of the People's Court.  
26. When a People's Court holds a court session to hear a public prosecution case, the procuratorial personnel and defenders appearing in court who need to present, read aloud, or play evidence that has already been transferred to the People's Court may request the court to present, read aloud, or play such evidence.  
27. Article 39 of the Criminal Procedure Law provides: "If a defender considers that, during the investigation or review and prosecution period, a public security organ or a People's Procuratorate has collected but not submitted evidentiary materials proving that a criminal suspect or defendant is innocent or bears a lighter criminal responsibility, he or she has the right to apply to the People's Procuratorate or the People's Court for retrieval of such materials." Paragraph 1 of Article 191 provides: "During the court hearing, if the collegial panel has doubts about the evidence, it may announce an adjournment and conduct investigation and verification of the evidence." Paragraph 1 of Article 192 provides: "During the court hearing, the parties and their defenders or agents ad litem have the right to apply for summoning new witnesses to appear in court, retrieving new material evidence, and applying for a new expert evaluation or inspection." According to the above provisions, from the date the case is transferred for review and prosecution, a People's Procuratorate may, upon the application of a defender, retrieve from a public security organ evidentiary materials that have not been submitted but prove that a criminal suspect or defendant is innocent or bears a lighter criminal responsibility. During the court hearing, a People's Court may, upon the application of a defender, retrieve from a People's Procuratorate evidentiary materials that have not been submitted but prove that the defendant is innocent or bears a lighter criminal responsibility, and may also retrieve from the People's Procuratorate evidentiary materials requiring investigation and verification. The public security organ or the People's Procuratorate shall, within three days after receiving the decision to request the retrieval of evidentiary materials, transfer such materials.  
28. Where a People's Court notifies a witness or expert evaluator to appear in court to testify according to law, it shall simultaneously serve the notice of appearance in court on both the prosecution and the defense, and the prosecution and the defense shall cooperate accordingly.  
29. Paragraph 3 of Article 187 of the Criminal Procedure Law provides: "If the public prosecutor, a party, or the defender or agent ad litem has objections to the expert opinion, and the People's Court considers that it is necessary for the expert evaluator to appear in court, the expert evaluator shall appear in court to testify. Where, after being notified by the People's Court, the expert evaluator refuses to appear in court to testify, the expert opinion shall not be used as a basis for deciding the case." According to the above provisions, if an expert evaluator who should appear in court according to law fails to appear in court to testify after being notified by the People's Court, the expert opinion shall not be used as a basis for deciding the case. Where an expert evaluator is unable to appear in court due to irresistible causes or other justifiable reasons, the People's Court may, based on the circumstances of the case trial, decide to postpone the trial.  
30. Where a People's Court, during the trial of a public prosecution case, discovers new facts that may affect the conviction, the People's Procuratorate may request a supplementary prosecution or an alteration of the prosecution, and the People's Court may suggest that the People's Procuratorate make a supplementary prosecution or an alteration of the prosecution. Where the People's Court suggests that the People's Procuratorate make a supplementary prosecution or an alteration of the prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall reply with its opinion within seven days.  
31. During the court hearing, if a defendant reveals the criminal acts of another person or provides important leads, and the People's Procuratorate considers that verification is necessary, it may suggest supplementary investigation.  
32. Article 203 of the Criminal Procedure Law provides: "If a People's Procuratorate discovers that a People's Court, in trying a case, has violated the litigation procedures prescribed by law, it has the power to present its corrective opinions to the People's Court." Corrective opinions of a People's Procuratorate regarding a court trial activity that violates statutory procedures shall be presented by the People's Procuratorate after the court session.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、执 行  
33．[刑事诉讼法第二百五十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-254)第五款中规定：“在交付执行前，暂予监外执行由交付执行的人民法院决定”。对于被告人可能被判处拘役、有期徒刑、无期徒刑，符合暂予监外执行条件的，被告人及其辩护人有权向人民法院提出暂予监外执行的申请，看守所可以将有关情况通报人民法院。人民法院应当进行审查，并在交付执行前作出是否暂予监外执行的决定。  
34．[刑事诉讼法第二百五十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-257)第三款规定：“不符合暂予监外执行条件的罪犯通过贿赂等非法手段被暂予监外执行的，在监外执行的期间不计入执行刑期。罪犯在暂予监外执行期间脱逃的，脱逃的期间不计入执行刑期。”对于人民法院决定暂予监外执行的罪犯具有上述情形的，人民法院在决定予以收监的同时，应当确定不计入刑期的期间。对于监狱管理机关或者公安机关决定暂予监外执行的罪犯具有上述情形的，罪犯被收监后，所在监狱或者看守所应当及时向所在地的中级人民法院提出不计入执行刑期的建议书，由人民法院审核裁定。  
35．被决定收监执行的社区矫正人员在逃的，社区矫正机构应当立即通知公安机关，由公安机关负责追捕。

**Article 9** IX. Enforcement  
33. Paragraph 5 of Article 254 of the Criminal Procedure Law provides: "Before a criminal sentence is executed, the decision on temporary execution of a sentence outside prison shall be made by the People's Court that delivers the criminal for execution." For a defendant who may be sentenced to criminal detention, fixed-term imprisonment, or life imprisonment, and who meets the conditions for temporary execution of a sentence outside prison, the defendant and his or her defender have the right to submit an application to the People's Court for temporary execution of the sentence outside prison, and the detention house may notify the People's Court of the relevant circumstances. The People's Court shall review the application and, before the criminal is delivered for execution, make a decision on whether to grant temporary execution of the sentence outside prison.  
34. Paragraph 3 of Article 257 of the Criminal Procedure Law provides: "If a convict who does not meet the conditions for temporary execution of a sentence outside prison is granted temporary execution outside prison by illegal means such as bribery, the period of execution outside prison shall not be counted as part of the executed sentence. If a convict escapes during the period of temporary execution of the sentence outside prison, the period of escape shall not be counted as part of the executed sentence." Where a convict granted temporary execution of a sentence outside prison by a decision of a People's Court falls under the above circumstances, the People's Court shall, at the same time it decides to place the convict in prison, determine the period that shall not be counted towards the executed sentence. Where a convict granted temporary execution of a sentence outside prison by a decision of a prison administration authority or a public security organ falls under the above circumstances, after the convict is taken into custody, the prison or detention house shall promptly submit a proposal to the Intermediate People's Court at the place where the convict is located regarding the period not to be counted towards the executed sentence, and the People's Court shall review and render a ruling.  
35. Where a community correction participant who has been ordered to be placed in prison is at large, the community correction institution shall immediately notify the public security organ, and the public security organ shall be responsible for pursuing and capturing the participant.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、涉案财产的处理  
36．对于依照刑法规定应当追缴的违法所得及其他涉案财产，除依法返还被害人的财物以及依法销毁的违禁品外，必须一律上缴国库。查封、扣押的涉案财产，依法不移送的，待人民法院作出生效判决、裁定后，由人民法院通知查封、扣押机关上缴国库，查封、扣押机关应当向人民法院送交执行回单；冻结在金融机构的违法所得及其他涉案财产，待人民法院作出生效判决、裁定后，由人民法院通知有关金融机构上缴国库，有关金融机构应当向人民法院送交执行回单。  
对于被扣押、冻结的债券、股票、基金份额等财产，在扣押、冻结期间权利人申请出售，经扣押、冻结机关审查，不损害国家利益、被害人利益，不影响诉讼正常进行的，以及扣押、冻结的汇票、本票、支票的有效期即将届满的，可以在判决生效前依法出售或者变现，所得价款由扣押、冻结机关保管，并及时告知当事人或者其近亲属。  
37．[刑事诉讼法第一百四十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-142)第一款中规定：“人民检察院、公安机关根据侦查犯罪的需要，可以依照规定查询、冻结犯罪嫌疑人的存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产。”根据上述规定，人民检察院、公安机关不能扣划存款、汇款、债券、股票、基金份额等财产。对于犯罪嫌疑人、被告人死亡，依照刑法规定应当追缴其违法所得及其他涉案财产的，适用刑事诉讼法第五编第三章规定的程序，由人民检察院向人民法院提出没收违法所得的申请。  
38．犯罪嫌疑人、被告人死亡，现有证据证明存在违法所得及其他涉案财产应当予以没收的，公安机关、人民检察院可以进行调查。公安机关、人民检察院进行调查，可以依法进行查封、扣押、查询、冻结。  
人民法院在审理案件过程中，被告人死亡的，应当裁定终止审理；被告人脱逃的，应当裁定中止审理。人民检察院可以依法另行向人民法院提出没收违法所得的申请。  
39．对于人民法院依法作出的没收违法所得的裁定，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的近亲属和其他利害关系人或者人民检察院可以在五日内提出上诉、抗诉。

**Article 10** X. Disposal of Property Involved in the Case  
36. All illegal gains and other property involved in a case that should be recovered in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law, except for property returned to the victim according to law and contraband destroyed according to law, shall be turned over to the state treasury. For sealed or seized property involved in the case that is not transferred according to law, after the People's Court renders an effective judgment or ruling, the People's Court shall notify the sealing or seizing organ to turn it over to the state treasury, and the sealing or seizing organ shall submit an execution receipt to the People's Court; for illegal gains and other property involved in the case that are frozen in a financial institution, after the People's Court renders an effective judgment or ruling, the People's Court shall notify the relevant financial institution to turn it over to the state treasury, and the relevant financial institution shall submit an execution receipt to the People's Court.  
With respect to property such as bonds, stocks, and fund shares that are seized or frozen, if during the period of seizure or freezing, the right holder applies for sale, and upon review by the seizing or freezing organ, it is determined that such sale does not harm the interests of the state or the victim and does not affect the normal progress of the proceedings, or if the validity period of the seized or frozen bills of exchange, promissory notes, or checks is about to expire, such property may be sold or liquidated according to law before the judgment takes effect, and the proceeds shall be kept by the seizing or freezing organ and the party or his or her close relatives shall be informed in a timely manner.  
37. Paragraph 1 of Article 142 of the Criminal Procedure Law provides: "A People's Procuratorate or a public security organ may, based on the needs of criminal investigation, inquire into or freeze a criminal suspect's deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, fund shares, or other property in accordance with relevant provisions." According to the above provisions, a People's Procuratorate or a public security organ may not deduct or transfer deposits, remittances, bonds, stocks, fund shares, or other property. Where a criminal suspect or defendant dies, and in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law, his or her illegal gains and other property involved in the case should be recovered, the procedures provided for in Section 3 of Part V of the Criminal Procedure Law shall apply, and the People's Procuratorate shall submit an application to the People's Court for confiscation of illegal gains.  
38. Where a criminal suspect or defendant dies and existing evidence proves that there are illegal gains and other property involved in the case that should be confiscated, the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate may conduct an investigation. When conducting an investigation, the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate may, according to law, seal up, seize, inquire into, or freeze property.  
If, during the trial of a case, the defendant dies, the People's Court shall rule to terminate the trial; if the defendant escapes, the People's Court shall rule to suspend the trial. The People's Procuratorate may, according to law, separately submit an application to the People's Court for confiscation of illegal gains.  
39. With respect to a ruling on confiscation of illegal gains made by a People's Court according to law, the close relatives of the criminal suspect or defendant and other interested parties, or the People's Procuratorate, may file an appeal or a protest within five days.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、其 他  
40．[刑事诉讼法第一百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-147)规定：“对犯罪嫌疑人作精神病鉴定的期间不计入办案期限。”根据上述规定，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人在押的案件，除对犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的精神病鉴定期间不计入办案期限外，其他鉴定期间都应当计入办案期限。对于因鉴定时间较长，办案期限届满仍不能终结的案件，自期限届满之日起，应当对被羁押的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人变更强制措施，改为取保候审或者监视居住。  
国家安全机关依照法律规定，办理危害国家安全的刑事案件，适用本规定中有关公安机关的规定。  
本规定自2013年1月1日起施行。1998年1月19日发布的《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部、全国人大常委会法制工作委员会关于刑事诉讼法实施中若干问题的规定》同时废止。

**Article 11** XI. Miscellaneous  
40. Article 147 of the Criminal Procedure Law provides: "The period during which a criminal suspect undergoes a psychiatric evaluation shall not be counted as part of the case-handling time limit." According to the above provision, for cases where the criminal suspect or defendant is in custody, except for the period of psychiatric evaluation of the criminal suspect or defendant, which shall not be counted as part of the case-handling time limit, all other periods for evaluation shall be counted as part of the case-handling time limit. For cases where, due to the length of the evaluation time, the case-handling time limit expires but the case cannot be concluded, beginning from the date of expiration of the time limit, the compulsory measures against the detained criminal suspect or defendant shall be altered to release on bail pending investigation or trial or to residential surveillance.  
State security organs, when handling criminal cases of endangering national security in accordance with the provisions of the law, shall apply the provisions of these Provisions concerning public security organs.  
These Provisions shall come into force on January 1, 2013. The "Provisions of the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of State Security, the Ministry of Justice, and the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of the Criminal Procedure Law" issued on January 19, 1998 shall be abolished simultaneously.
