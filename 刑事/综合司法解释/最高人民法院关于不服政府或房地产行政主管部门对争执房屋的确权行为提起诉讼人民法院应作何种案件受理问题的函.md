# 最高人民法院关于不服政府或房地产行政主管部门对争执房屋的确权行为提起诉讼人民法院应作何种案件受理问题的函

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法函〔1993〕33号  
**公布日期**：1993-04-17  
**生效日期**：1993-04-17

---

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
关于不服政府或房地产行政主管部门对争执  
房屋的确权行为提起诉讼人民法院  
应作何种案件受理问题的函  
1993年4月17日      法函〔1993〕33号  
四川省高级人民法院：  
你院川高法〔1993〕27号关于不服政府或房地产行政主管部门对争执房屋的确权行为提起诉讼，人民法院应作何种案件受理的请示收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
当事人对人民政府或房地产行政主管部门关于房屋产权争议的确权决定不服而提起诉讼的，人民法院应作为行政案件受理。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Letter on the Issue of What Type of Case the People's Court Shall Accept for a Lawsuit against the Act of Confirming Rights over a Disputed House by the Government or Real Estate Administrative Authority  
April 17, 1993 Fa Han [1993] No. 33  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your request, Chuan Gao Fa [1993] No. 27, regarding what type of case the People's Court shall accept for a lawsuit against the act of confirming rights over a disputed house by the government or real estate administrative authority, has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Where a party initiates a lawsuit against the decision of the people's government or real estate administrative authority on the confirmation of rights over a house property dispute, the People's Court shall accept the case as an administrative case.
