# 人民检察院办理行政执法机关移送涉嫌犯罪案件的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民检察院  
**发文字号**：高检发释字〔2001〕4号  
**公布日期**：2001-12-03  
**生效日期**：2001-12-03

> 为依法办理行政执法机关移送的涉嫌犯罪案件，充分履行检察职责，有效惩治犯罪，维护国家安定和社会稳定，保护公民合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则》的有关规定，结合2001 年7 月9 日国务院颁布的《行政执法机关移送涉嫌犯罪案件的规定》，最高人民检察院制定了《人民检察院办理行政执法机关移送涉嫌犯罪案件的规定》，现印发给你们，请遵照执行。 各地将执行中遇到的问题及时层报最高人民检察院。 最高人民检察院 2001年12月3日 人民检察院办理行政执法机关 移送涉嫌犯罪案件的规定 （2001年9月10日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会 第九十六次会议讨论通过）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、对于行政执法机关移送检察机关的涉嫌犯罪案件，统一由人民检察院控告检察部门受理。  
人民检察院控告检察部门受理行政执法机关移送的涉嫌犯罪案件后，应当登记，并指派二名以上检察人员进行初步审查。

**Article 1** . For suspected criminal cases transferred by administrative law enforcement organs to procuratorial organs, the complaint and accusation department of the People's Procuratorate shall uniformly accept them.  
After accepting a suspected criminal case transferred by an administrative law enforcement organ, the complaint and accusation department of the People's Procuratorate shall register it and assign two or more prosecutors to conduct a preliminary review.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、人民检察院控告检察部门审查行政执法机关移送的涉嫌犯罪案件，应当根据不同情况，提出移送有关部门的处理意见，三日内报主管副检察长或者检察长批准，并通知移送的行政执法机关：  
（一）对于不属于检察机关管辖的案件，移送其他有管辖权的机关处理；  
（二）对于属于检察机关管辖，但不属于本院管辖的案件，移送有管辖权的人民检察院办理；  
（三）对于属于本院管辖的案件，转本院反贪、渎职侵权检察部门办理。  
对于性质不明、难以归口办理的案件，可以先由控告检察部门进行必须的调查。

**Article 2** . When reviewing a suspected criminal case transferred by an administrative law enforcement organ, the complaint and accusation department of the People's Procuratorate shall, based on different circumstances, propose handling opinions for transfer to the relevant department, report to the deputy chief procurator or chief procurator in charge for approval within three days, and notify the transferring administrative law enforcement organ:  
(1) For cases not under the jurisdiction of procuratorial organs, transfer them to other competent organs for handling;  
(2) For cases under the jurisdiction of procuratorial organs but not under the jurisdiction of this procuratorate, transfer them to the People's Procuratorate with jurisdiction for handling;  
(3) For cases under the jurisdiction of this procuratorate, transfer them to the anti-corruption and duty crime investigation departments of this procuratorate for handling.  
For cases with unclear nature or difficult to assign to a specific department, the complaint and accusation department may first conduct necessary investigations.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、对于不属于本院管辖但又必须采取紧急措施的案件，人民检察院控告检察部门在报经主管副检察长或者检察长批准后，应当先采取紧急措施，再行移送。

**Article 3** . For cases that are not under the jurisdiction of this procuratorate but for which emergency measures must be taken, the complaint and accusation department of the People's Procuratorate, after reporting to and obtaining approval from the deputy chief procurator or chief procurator in charge, shall first take emergency measures before transferring.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、对于行政执法机关移送的涉嫌犯罪案件，人民检察院反贪、渎职侵权检察部门应当审查是否附有下列材料：  
（一）涉嫌犯罪案件移送书；  
（二）涉嫌犯罪案件情况的调查报告；  
（三）涉案物品清单；  
（四）有关检验报告或者鉴定结论；  
（五）其他有关涉嫌犯罪的材料。  
人民检察院可以要求移送案件的行政执法机关补充上述材料和证据。

**Article 4** . For suspected criminal cases transferred by administrative law enforcement organs, the anti-corruption and duty crime investigation departments of the People's Procuratorate shall review whether the following materials are attached:  
(1) the letter of transfer for a suspected criminal case;  
(2) the investigation report on the circumstances of the suspected criminal case;  
(3) the list of items involved in the case;  
(4) relevant inspection reports or expert conclusions;  
(5) other materials related to the suspected crime.  
The People's Procuratorate may request the administrative law enforcement organ transferring the case to supplement the above materials and evidence.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、对于行政执法机关移送的涉嫌犯罪案件，人民检察院经审查，认为符合立案条件的，应当及时作出立案决定，并通知移送的行政执法机关。

**Article 5** . For suspected criminal cases transferred by administrative law enforcement organs, if the People's Procuratorate, upon review, considers that the conditions for case filing are met, it shall promptly make a decision to file the case and notify the transferring administrative law enforcement organ.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、对于行政执法机关移送的涉嫌犯罪案件，人民检察院经审查，认为不符合立案条件的，可以作出不立案决定；对于需要给予有关责任人员行政处分、行政处罚或者没收违法所得的，可以提出检察意见，移送有关主管部门处理，并通知移送的行政执法机关。

**Article 6** . For suspected criminal cases transferred by administrative law enforcement organs, if the People's Procuratorate, upon review, considers that the conditions for case filing are not met, it may make a decision not to file the case; if it is necessary to impose administrative sanctions, administrative penalties, or confiscation of illegal gains on relevant responsible persons, it may propose procuratorial opinions, transfer the case to the relevant competent authority for handling, and notify the transferring administrative law enforcement organ.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、对于人民检察院的不立案决定，移送涉嫌犯罪案件的行政执法机关可以在收到不立案决定书后五日内要求作出不立案决定的人民检察院复议。人民检察院刑事申诉检察部门应当指派专人进行审查，并在收到行政执法机关要求复议意见书后七日内作出复议决定。  
行政执法机关对复议决定不服的，可以在收到人民检察院复议决定书后五日内向上一级人民检察院提请复核。上一级人民检察院应当在收到行政执法机关提请复核意见书后十五日内作出复核决定。对于原不立案决定错误的，应当及时纠正，并通知作出不立案决定的下级人民检察院执行。

**Article 7** . For a decision of non-filing of a case by the People's Procuratorate, the administrative law enforcement organ that transferred the suspected criminal case may, within five days of receiving the written decision of non-filing, request a review from the People's Procuratorate that made the decision. The criminal complaint department of the People's Procuratorate shall assign a dedicated person to conduct a review and shall make a review decision within seven days of receiving the written request for review from the administrative law enforcement organ.  
If the administrative law enforcement organ is dissatisfied with the review decision, it may, within five days of receiving the written review decision of the People's Procuratorate, petition the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for a re-examination. The People's Procuratorate at the next higher level shall make a re-examination decision within fifteen days of receiving the written request for re-examination from the administrative law enforcement organ. If the original decision of non-filing was erroneous, it shall be promptly corrected, and the lower-level People's Procuratorate that made the decision shall be notified to enforce the correction.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、对于人民检察院决定立案侦查的案件，办理案件的人民检察院应当将立案决定和案件的办理结果及时通知移送案件的行政执法机关。

**Article 8** . For cases in which the People's Procuratorate decides to file a case for investigation, the People's Procuratorate handling the case shall promptly notify the transferring administrative law enforcement organ of the case filing decision and the case handling result.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、移送涉嫌犯罪案件的行政执法机关对公安机关不予立案决定或者不予立案的复议决定有异议，建议人民检察院依法进行立案监督的，统一由人民检察院侦查监督部门办理。

**Article 9** . Where the administrative law enforcement organ that transferred a suspected criminal case objects to the public security organ's decision not to file a case or its review decision not to file a case, and recommends that the People's Procuratorate conduct supervision over case filing according to law, such matters shall be uniformly handled by the investigation supervision department of the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、人民检察院应当依法对公安机关办理行政执法机关移送涉嫌犯罪案件进行立案监督。对于具有下列情形之一的，人民检察院应当要求公安机关在收到人民检察院《要求说明不立案理由通知书》后七日内将关于不立案理由的说明书面答复人民检察院：  
（一）人民检察院认为公安机关对应当立案侦查的案件而不立案侦查的；  
（二）被害人认为公安机关对应当立案侦查的案件而不立案侦查，向人民检察院提出的；  
（三）移送涉嫌犯罪案件的行政执法机关对公安机关不予立案决定或者不予立案的复议决定有异议，建议人民检察院依法进行立案监督的。  
人民检察院认为公安机关不立案理由不能成立，应当通知公安机关在收到《通知立案书》后十五日内决定立案，并将立案决定书送达人民检察院。

**Article 10** . The People's Procuratorate shall, according to law, conduct supervision over case filing by the public security organ in handling suspected criminal cases transferred by administrative law enforcement organs. For any of the following circumstances, the People's Procuratorate shall require the public security organ to provide a written explanation of the reasons for non-filing to the People's Procuratorate within seven days of receiving the People's Procuratorate's 'Notice Requiring Explanation of Reasons for Non-Filing':  
(1) The People's Procuratorate considers that the public security organ has not filed a case for investigation when it should have;  
(2) The victim considers that the public security organ has not filed a case for investigation when it should have, and has raised the matter to the People's Procuratorate;  
(3) The administrative law enforcement organ that transferred the suspected criminal case objects to the public security organ's decision not to file a case or its review decision not to file a case, and recommends that the People's Procuratorate conduct supervision over case filing according to law.  
If the People's Procuratorate considers that the reasons for non-filing given by the public security organ are not valid, it shall notify the public security organ to decide to file the case within fifteen days of receiving the 'Notice to File a Case', and the decision to file a case shall be served to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、对于人民检察院认为公安机关不立案理由成立的，或者认为公安机关的不立案理由不成立而通知公安机关立案，公安机关已经立案的，人民检察院应当及时通知提出立案监督建议的行政执法机关。

**Article 11** . Where the People's Procuratorate considers that the reasons for non-filing given by the public security organ are valid, or where the People's Procuratorate considers that the reasons for non-filing are not valid and has notified the public security organ to file the case, and the public security organ has already filed the case, the People's Procuratorate shall promptly notify the administrative law enforcement organ that proposed the supervision suggestion.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、各级人民检察院对行政执法机关不移送涉嫌犯罪案件，具有下列情形之一的，可以提出检察意见：  
（一）检察机关发现行政执法机关应当移送的涉嫌犯罪案件而不移送的；  
（二）有关单位和个人举报的行政执法机关应当移送的涉嫌犯罪案件而不移送的；  
（三）隐匿、销毁涉案物品或者私分涉案财物的；  
（四）以行政处罚代替刑事追究而不移送的。  
有关行政执法人员涉嫌犯罪的，依照刑法的有关规定，追究刑事责任。

**Article 12** . In any of the following circumstances where an administrative law enforcement organ fails to transfer a suspected criminal case, the People's Procuratorate at each level may issue procuratorial opinions:  
(1) The procuratorial organ discovers that the administrative law enforcement organ should have transferred a suspected criminal case but fails to do so;  
(2) Relevant entities or individuals report that the administrative law enforcement organ should have transferred a suspected criminal case but fails to do so;  
(3) Concealing, destroying items involved in the case, or illegally distributing property involved in the case;  
(4) Substituting administrative penalties for criminal prosecution and failing to transfer.  
If relevant administrative law enforcement personnel are suspected of committing a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、各级人民检察院对公安机关不接受行政执法机关移送的涉嫌犯罪案件，或者逾期不作出立案或者不予立案决定，在检察机关依法实施立案监督后，仍不接受或者不作出决定的，可以向公安机关提出检察意见。  
有关公安人员涉嫌犯罪的，依照刑法的有关规定，追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** . If the public security organ at any level does not accept a suspected criminal case transferred by an administrative law enforcement organ, or fails to make a decision to file or not to file a case within the time limit, and after the procuratorial organ conducts supervision over case filing according to law, still does not accept it or make a decision, the People's Procuratorate at each level may issue procuratorial opinions to the public security organ.  
If relevant public security personnel are suspected of committing a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、最高人民检察院对地方各级人民检察院，上级人民检察院对下级人民检察院办理的行政执法机关移送的涉嫌犯罪案件，应加强指导和监督，对不依法办理以及办理过程中的违法违纪问题，要依照有关规定严肃处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 14** . The Supreme People's Procuratorate shall strengthen guidance and supervision over the handling of suspected criminal cases transferred by administrative law enforcement organs by local People's Procuratorates at all levels, and the higher-level People's Procuratorate shall do so over the lower-level People's Procuratorate. For failure to handle cases according to law and for illegal or disciplinary issues during handling, such matters shall be seriously dealt with in accordance with relevant provisions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>十五、各级人民检察院对于其他机关和部门移送的涉嫌犯罪案件，依照本规定办理。

**Article 15** . The People's Procuratorate at each level shall handle suspected criminal cases transferred by other organs and departments in accordance with these Provisions.
