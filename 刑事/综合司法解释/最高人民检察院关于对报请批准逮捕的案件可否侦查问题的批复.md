# 最高人民检察院关于对报请批准逮捕的案件可否侦查问题的批复

> 关于对报请批准逮捕的案件可否侦查问题的批复 （1998年5月7日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会 第二次会议通过）

---

<a id="art-1"></a>（1998年5月7日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会  
第二次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔1998〕2号  
海南省人民检察院：  
你院琼检发刑捕字〔1998〕1号《关于执行＜关于刑事诉讼法实施中若干问题的规定＞有关问题的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
人民检察院审查公安机关提请逮捕的案件，经审查，应当作出批准或者不批准逮捕的决定，对报请批准逮捕的案件不另行侦查。人民检察院在审查批捕中如果认为报请批准逮捕的证据存有疑问的，可以复核有关证据，讯问犯罪嫌疑人、询问证人，以保证批捕案件的质量，防止错捕或漏捕。  
最高人民检察院  
1998年5月12日

**Article 1** (Adopted at the Second Session of the Ninth Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on May 7, 1998)  
Gao Jian Fa Shi Zi [1998] No. 2  
People's Procuratorate of Hainan Province:  
Your request, Qiong Jian Fa Xing Bu Zi [1998] No. 1, the "Request for Instructions on Issues Concerning the Implementation of the Provisions on Several Issues in the Implementation of the Criminal Procedure Law," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
When the People's Procuratorate reviews a case for which a public security organ requests approval of arrest, it shall, after review, make a decision to approve or disapprove the arrest, and shall not conduct additional investigation into the case for which approval of arrest is requested. If, during the review of the arrest approval, the People's Procuratorate believes that the evidence submitted for arrest approval is questionable, it may re-examine the relevant evidence, interrogate the criminal suspect, and question witnesses, so as to ensure the quality of arrest approval cases and prevent wrongful arrests or omissions of arrests.  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
May 12, 1998
