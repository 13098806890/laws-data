# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于推进以审判为中心的刑事诉讼制度改革的意见

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法发〔2016〕18号  
**公布日期**：2016-07-20

> （2016年7月20日发布 法发〔2016〕18号） 为贯彻落实《中共中央关于全面推进依法治国若干重大问题的决定》的有关要求，推进以审判为中心的刑事诉讼制度改革，依据宪法法律规定，结合司法工作实际，制定本意见。 一、未经人民法院依法判决，对任何人都不得确定有罪。人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关办理刑事案件，应当分工负责，互相配合，互相制约，保证准确、及时地查明犯罪事实，正确应用法律，惩罚犯罪分子，保障无罪的人不受刑事追究。 二、严格按照法律规定的证据裁判要求，没有证据不得认定犯罪事实。侦查机关侦查终结，人民检察院提起公诉，人民法院作出有罪判决，都应当做到犯罪事实清楚，证据确实、充分。 侦查机关、人民检察院应当按照裁判的要求和标准收集、固定、审查、运用证据，人民法院应当按照法定程序认定证据，依法作出裁判。 人民法院作出有罪判决，对于证明犯罪构成要件的事实，应当综合全案证据排除合理怀疑，对于量刑证据存疑的，应当作出有利于被告人的认定。 三、建立健全符合裁判要求、适应各类案件特点的证据收集指引。探索建立命案等重大案件检查、搜查、辨认、指认等过程录音录像制度。完善技术侦查证据的移送、审查、法庭调查和使用规则以及庭外核实程序。 统一司法鉴定标准和程序。完善见证人制度。 四、侦查机关应当全面、客观、及时收集与案件有关的证据。 侦查机关应当依法收集证据。对采取刑讯逼供、暴力、威胁等非法方法收集的言词证据，应当依法予以排除。侦查机关收集物证、书证不符合法定程序，可能严重影响司法公正，不能补正或者作出合理解释的，应当依法予以排除。 对物证、书证等实物证据，一般应当提取原物、原件，确保证据的真实性。需要鉴定的，应当及时送检。证据之间有矛盾的，应当及时查证。所有证据应当妥善保管，随案移送。 五、完善讯问制度，防止刑讯逼供，不得强迫任何人证实自己有罪。严格按照有关规定要求，在规范的讯问场所讯问犯罪嫌疑人。严格依照法律规定对讯问过程全程同步录音录像，逐步实行对所有案件的讯问过程全程同步录音录像。 探索建立重大案件侦查终结前对讯问合法性进行核查制度。对公安机关、国家安全机关和人民检察院侦查的重大案件，由人民检察院驻看守所检察人员询问犯罪嫌疑人，核查是否存在刑讯逼供、非法取证情形，并同步录音录像。经核查，确有刑讯逼供、非法取证情形的，侦查机关应当及时排除非法证据，不得作为提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉的根据。 六、在案件侦查终结前，犯罪嫌疑人提出无罪或者罪轻的辩解，辩护律师提出犯罪嫌疑人无罪或者依法不应追究刑事责任的意见，侦查机关应当依法予以核实。 七、完善补充侦查制度。进一步明确退回补充侦查的条件，建立人民检察院退回补充侦查引导和说理机制，明确补充侦查方向、标准和要求。规范补充侦查行为，对于确实无法查明的事项，公安机关、国家安全机关应当书面向人民检察院说明理由。对于二次退回补充侦查后，仍然证据不足、不符合起诉条件的，依法作出不起诉决定。 八、进一步完善公诉机制，被告人有罪的举证责任，由人民检察院承担。对被告人不认罪的，人民检察院应当强化庭前准备和当庭讯问、举证、质证。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、未经人民法院依法判决，对任何人都不得确定有罪。人民法院、人民检察院和公安机关办理刑事案件，应当分工负责，互相配合，互相制约，保证准确、及时地查明犯罪事实，正确应用法律，惩罚犯罪分子，保障无罪的人不受刑事追究。

**Article 1** I. No person may be found guilty without a verdict rendered by the People's Court according to law. The People's Court, the People's Procuratorate and the public security organ handling criminal cases shall divide their responsibilities, cooperate with each other and check each other to ensure the accurate and timely ascertainment of criminal facts, correct application of law, punishment of criminals, and protection of innocent persons from being investigated for criminal liability.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、严格按照法律规定的证据裁判要求，没有证据不得认定犯罪事实。侦查机关侦查终结，人民检察院提起公诉，人民法院作出有罪判决，都应当做到犯罪事实清楚，证据确实、充分。  
侦查机关、人民检察院应当按照裁判的要求和标准收集、固定、审查、运用证据，人民法院应当按照法定程序认定证据，依法作出裁判。  
人民法院作出有罪判决，对于证明犯罪构成要件的事实，应当综合全案证据排除合理怀疑，对于量刑证据存疑的，应当作出有利于被告人的认定。

**Article 2** II. The determination of criminal facts shall be strictly based on the requirements of evidentiary adjudication prescribed by law; no criminal facts may be determined without evidence. Where the investigation organ concludes the investigation, the People's Procuratorate initiates a public prosecution, or the People's Court renders a guilty verdict, the criminal facts shall be clear and the evidence shall be credible and sufficient.  
The investigation organ and the People's Procuratorate shall collect, preserve, examine and utilize evidence in accordance with the requirements and standards of adjudication, and the People's Court shall determine evidence in accordance with legal procedures and render adjudication according to law.  
When the People's Court renders a guilty verdict, it shall comprehensively review all evidence in the case to exclude reasonable doubt regarding the facts that prove the constitutive elements of the crime; where the evidence for sentencing is in doubt, it shall make a determination favorable to the defendant.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、建立健全符合裁判要求、适应各类案件特点的证据收集指引。探索建立命案等重大案件检查、搜查、辨认、指认等过程录音录像制度。完善技术侦查证据的移送、审查、法庭调查和使用规则以及庭外核实程序。 统一司法鉴定标准和程序。完善见证人制度。

**Article 3** III. Establish and improve evidence collection guidelines that meet the requirements of adjudication and are suited to the characteristics of various types of cases. Explore and establish a system of audio and video recording of the processes of inspection, search, identification and pointing-out in major cases such as homicide cases. Improve the rules for the transfer, examination, courtroom investigation and use of evidence obtained through technical investigation, as well as the procedures for out-of-court verification. Unify the standards and procedures for forensic expert evaluation. Improve the system of eyewitnesses.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、侦查机关应当全面、客观、及时收集与案件有关的证据。  
侦查机关应当依法收集证据。对采取刑讯逼供、暴力、威胁等非法方法收集的言词证据，应当依法予以排除。侦查机关收集物证、书证不符合法定程序，可能严重影响司法公正，不能补正或者作出合理解释的，应当依法予以排除。  
对物证、书证等实物证据，一般应当提取原物、原件，确保证据的真实性。需要鉴定的，应当及时送检。证据之间有矛盾的，应当及时查证。所有证据应当妥善保管，随案移送。

**Article 4** IV. The investigation organ shall collect evidence related to the case comprehensively, objectively, and promptly.  
The investigation organ shall collect evidence according to law. Verbal evidence collected by illegal methods such as extortion of confessions by torture, violence, threats, etc., shall be excluded according to law. Where physical evidence or documentary evidence collected by the investigation organ does not comply with statutory procedures and may seriously affect judicial fairness, and cannot be supplemented or reasonably explained, it shall be excluded according to law.  
For physical evidence such as objects and documents, the original objects or original documents shall generally be extracted to ensure the authenticity of the evidence. Where expert evaluation is required, the items shall be submitted for evaluation in a timely manner. Where there are contradictions among the evidence, timely verification shall be conducted. All evidence shall be properly preserved and transferred with the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、完善讯问制度，防止刑讯逼供，不得强迫任何人证实自己有罪。严格按照有关规定要求，在规范的讯问场所讯问犯罪嫌疑人。严格依照法律规定对讯问过程全程同步录音录像，逐步实行对所有案件的讯问过程全程同步录音录像。  
探索建立重大案件侦查终结前对讯问合法性进行核查制度。对公安机关、国家安全机关和人民检察院侦查的重大案件，由人民检察院驻看守所检察人员询问犯罪嫌疑人，核查是否存在刑讯逼供、非法取证情形，并同步录音录像。经核查，确有刑讯逼供、非法取证情形的，侦查机关应当及时排除非法证据，不得作为提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉的根据。

**Article 5** V. Improve the interrogation system to prevent extortion of confessions by torture, and no person shall be compelled to prove his or her own guilt. Strictly in accordance with the requirements of relevant regulations, interrogate criminal suspects in standardized interrogation premises. Strictly, in accordance with legal provisions, make simultaneous audio and video recordings of the entire interrogation process, and gradually implement simultaneous audio and video recording of the entire interrogation process for all cases.  
Explore the establishment of a system for verifying the legality of interrogations before the conclusion of investigation in major cases. For major cases investigated by the public security organ, the state security organ and the People's Procuratorate, the procuratorial personnel stationed in the detention center shall question the criminal suspect to verify whether there is extortion of confessions by torture or illegal evidence collection, and make simultaneous audio and video recordings. If upon verification it is indeed found that there is extortion of confessions by torture or illegal evidence collection, the investigation organ shall promptly exclude the illegal evidence and shall not use it as the basis for submitting a request for approval of arrest or for transfer for prosecution.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、在案件侦查终结前，犯罪嫌疑人提出无罪或者罪轻的辩解，辩护律师提出犯罪嫌疑人无罪或者依法不应追究刑事责任的意见，侦查机关应当依法予以核实。

**Article 6** VI. Before the conclusion of the investigation of a case, if the criminal suspect raises a defense of innocence or lesser guilt, or if the defense lawyer submits an opinion that the criminal suspect is innocent or should not be investigated for criminal liability according to law, the investigation organ shall verify the matter according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、完善补充侦查制度。进一步明确退回补充侦查的条件，建立人民检察院退回补充侦查引导和说理机制，明确补充侦查方向、标准和要求。规范补充侦查行为，对于确实无法查明的事项，公安机关、国家安全机关应当书面向人民检察院说明理由。对于二次退回补充侦查后，仍然证据不足、不符合起诉条件的，依法作出不起诉决定。

**Article 7** VII. Improve the system of supplementary investigation. Further clarify the conditions for returning a case for supplementary investigation, establish a mechanism for the People's Procuratorate to provide guidance and reasoning when returning a case for supplementary investigation, and specify the direction, standards and requirements for supplementary investigation. Standardize the conduct of supplementary investigation; for matters that truly cannot be ascertained, the public security organ or the state security organ shall provide a written explanation of the reasons to the People's Procuratorate. For cases where, after being returned twice for supplementary investigation, there is still insufficient evidence and the conditions for prosecution are not met, a decision not to prosecute shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、进一步完善公诉机制，被告人有罪的举证责任，由人民检察院承担。对被告人不认罪的，人民检察院应当强化庭前准备和当庭讯问、举证、质证。

**Article 8** VIII. Further improve the public prosecution mechanism; the burden of adducing evidence for the defendant's guilt shall be borne by the People's Procuratorate. Where the defendant does not plead guilty, the People's Procuratorate shall strengthen pre-trial preparation and in-court interrogation, adduction of evidence, and cross-examination.

<a id="art-9"></a>九、完善不起诉制度，对未达到法定证明标准的案件，人民检察院应当依法作出不起诉决定，防止事实不清、证据不足的案件进入审判程序。完善撤回起诉制度，规范撤回起诉的条件和程序。

**Article 9** IX. Improve the system of non-prosecution; for cases that do not meet the statutory standard of proof, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision not to prosecute according to law to prevent cases where the facts are unclear and the evidence is insufficient from entering the adjudication procedure. Improve the system of withdrawal of prosecution, and standardize the conditions and procedures for withdrawal of prosecution.

<a id="art-10"></a>十、完善庭前会议程序，对适用普通程序审理的案件，健全庭前证据展示制度，听取出庭证人名单、非法证据排除等方面的意见。

**Article 10** X. Improve the procedure for pre-trial conferences; for cases subject to ordinary procedure, improve the system of pre-trial evidence disclosure, and hear opinions regarding the list of witnesses to appear in court, exclusion of illegal evidence, and other aspects.

<a id="art-11"></a>十一、规范法庭调查程序，确保诉讼证据出示在法庭、案件事实查明在法庭。证明被告人有罪或者无罪、罪轻或者罪重的证据，都应当在法庭上出示，依法保障控辩双方的质证权利。对定罪量刑的证据，控辩双方存在争议的，应当单独质证；对庭前会议中控辩双方没有异议的证据，可以简化举证、质证。

**Article 11** XI. Standardize the procedure of court investigation to ensure that litigation evidence is presented in court and that the facts of the case are ascertained in court. Evidence proving the defendant's guilt or innocence, or the severity or leniency of the crime, shall all be presented in court, and the right of the prosecution and the defense to cross-examine shall be guaranteed according to law. Where there is a dispute between the prosecution and the defense over evidence for conviction or sentencing, a separate cross-examination shall be conducted; for evidence on which both the prosecution and the defense have no objection in the pre-trial conference, the adduction and cross-examination of such evidence may be simplified.

<a id="art-12"></a>十二、完善对证人、鉴定人的法庭质证规则。落实证人、鉴定人、侦查人员出庭作证制度，提高出庭作证率。公诉人、当事人或者辩护人、诉讼代理人对证人证言有异议，人民法院认为该证人证言对案件定罪量刑有重大影响的，证人应当出庭作证。  
健全证人保护工作机制，对因作证面临人身安全等危险的人员依法采取保护措施。建立证人、鉴定人等作证补助专项经费划拨机制。完善强制证人到庭制度。

**Article 12** XII. Improve the rules for court cross-examination of witnesses and expert evaluators. Implement the system for witnesses, expert evaluators, and investigative personnel to testify in court, and increase the rate of testifying in court. Where the public prosecutor, a party, the defender, or the litigation agent objects to the testimony of a witness, and the People's Court considers that such testimony has a significant impact on the conviction and sentencing of the case, the witness shall testify in court.  
Improve the working mechanism for protecting witnesses; adopt protective measures according to law for persons who face dangers to personal safety, etc., due to testifying. Establish a special funding allocation mechanism for subsidies to witnesses, expert evaluators, and others who testify. Improve the system for compelling witnesses to appear in court.

<a id="art-13"></a>十三、完善法庭辩论规则，确保控辩意见发表在法庭。法庭辩论应当围绕定罪、量刑分别进行，对被告人认罪的案件，主要围绕量刑进行。法庭应当充分听取控辩双方意见，依法保障被告人及其辩护人的辩论辩护权。

**Article 13** XIII. Improve the rules for court debate to ensure that the opinions of the prosecution and the defense are presented in court. The court debate shall proceed separately on conviction and sentencing; for cases where the defendant pleads guilty, it shall mainly focus on sentencing. The court shall fully hear the opinions of both the prosecution and the defense, and guarantee the debate and defense rights of the defendant and his or her defender according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>十四、完善当庭宣判制度，确保裁判结果形成在法庭。适用速裁程序审理的案件，除附带民事诉讼的案件以外，一律当庭宣判；适用简易程序审理的案件一般应当当庭宣判；适用普通程序审理的案件逐步提高当庭宣判率。规范定期宣判制度。

**Article 14** XIV. Improve the system of pronouncing judgment in court to ensure that the adjudicative outcome is formed in court. For cases subject to expedited procedure, except for cases with incidental civil actions, the judgment shall be pronounced in court in all cases; for cases subject to summary procedure, the judgment shall generally be pronounced in court; for cases subject to ordinary procedure, the rate of pronouncing judgment in court shall be gradually increased. Regulate the system of adjourned pronouncement of judgment.

<a id="art-15"></a>十五、严格依法裁判。人民法院经审理，对案件事实清楚，证据确实、充分，依据法律认定被告人有罪的，应当作出有罪判决。依据法律规定认定被告人无罪的，应当作出无罪判决。证据不足，不能认定被告人有罪的，应当按照疑罪从无原则，依法作出无罪判决。

**Article 15** XV. Render judgments strictly according to law. Where the People's Court, after trial, finds that the facts of the case are clear, the evidence is credible and sufficient, and the defendant is determined to be guilty according to law, it shall render a guilty verdict. Where it determines that the defendant is innocent according to legal provisions, it shall render a verdict of not guilty. If the evidence is insufficient and the defendant cannot be found guilty, a verdict of not guilty shall be rendered according to law in accordance with the principle of in dubio pro reo (when in doubt, acquit).

<a id="art-16"></a>十六、完善人民检察院对侦查活动和刑事审判活动的监督机制。建立健全对强制措施的监督机制。加强人民检察院对逮捕后羁押必要性的审查，规范非羁押性强制措施的适用。进一步规范和加强人民检察院对人民法院确有错误的刑事判决和裁定的抗诉工作，保证刑事抗诉的及时性、准确性和全面性。

**Article 16** XVI. Improve the supervision mechanism of the People's Procuratorate over investigation activities and criminal adjudication activities. Establish and improve the supervision mechanism over compulsory measures. Strengthen the People's Procuratorate's review of the necessity of custody after arrest, and standardize the application of compulsory measures not involving custody. Further regulate and strengthen the People's Procuratorate's work of protesting against criminal judgments and rulings of the People's Court that are indeed erroneous, ensuring the timeliness, accuracy and comprehensiveness of criminal protests.

<a id="art-17"></a>十七、健全当事人、辩护人和其他诉讼参与人的权利保障制度。  
依法保障当事人和其他诉讼参与人的知情权、陈述权、辩论辩护权、申请权、申诉权。犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有权获得辩护，人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关、国家安全机关有义务保证犯罪嫌疑人、被告人获得辩护。  
依法保障辩护人会见、阅卷、收集证据和发问、质证、辩论辩护等权利，完善便利辩护人参与诉讼的工作机制。

**Article 17** XVII. Improve the system for protecting the rights of the parties, defenders and other participants in proceedings.  
Guarantee according to law the rights of the parties and other participants in proceedings to be informed, to make statements, to debate and defend, to apply, and to petition. A criminal suspect or defendant has the right to defense, and the People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, the public security organ and the state security organ have the obligation to ensure that the criminal suspect or defendant obtains defense.  
Guarantee according to law the rights of the defender to meet, to review the case files, to collect evidence, to ask questions, to cross-examine, and to debate and defend, and improve the working mechanism to facilitate the participation of defenders in proceedings.

<a id="art-18"></a>十八、辩护人或者其他任何人，不得帮助犯罪嫌疑人、被告人隐匿、毁灭、伪造证据或者串供，不得威胁、引诱证人作伪证以及进行其他干扰司法机关诉讼活动的行为。对于实施上述行为的，应当依法追究法律责任。

**Article 18** XVIII. A defender or any other person shall not assist a criminal suspect or defendant in concealing, destroying or falsifying evidence, or colluding in making confessions; shall not threaten or induce a witness to give false testimony; or engage in other acts that interfere with the litigation activities of judicial organs. Any person who commits any of the above acts shall be investigated for legal liability according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>十九、当事人、诉讼参与人和旁听人员在庭审活动中应当服从审判长或独任审判员的指挥，遵守法庭纪律。对扰乱法庭秩序、危及法庭安全等违法行为，应当依法处理；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 19** XIX. The parties, participants in proceedings and observers in court activities shall obey the directions of the presiding judge or the sole judge and observe court discipline. Acts that disrupt court order or endanger court safety shall be dealt with according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>二十、建立法律援助值班律师制度，法律援助机构在看守所、人民法院派驻值班律师，为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律帮助。  
完善法律援助制度，健全依申请法律援助工作机制和办案机关通知辩护工作机制。对未履行通知或者指派辩护职责的办案人员，严格实行责任追究。

**Article 20** XX. Establish a system of duty lawyers for legal aid; legal aid institutions shall station duty lawyers in detention centers and People's Courts to provide legal assistance to criminal suspects and defendants.  
Improve the legal aid system; improve the working mechanism of legal aid upon application and the working mechanism for case-handling organs to notify defense counsel. Strictly hold accountable the case-handling personnel who fail to perform the duty of notifying or assigning defense counsel.

<a id="art-21"></a>二十一、推进案件繁简分流，优化司法资源配置。完善刑事案件速裁程序和认罪认罚从宽制度，对案件事实清楚、证据充分的轻微刑事案件，或者犯罪嫌疑人、被告人自愿认罪认罚的，可以适用速裁程序、简易程序或者普通程序简化审理。

**Article 21** XXI. Promote the diversion of cases based on complexity to optimize the allocation of judicial resources. Improve the expedited procedure for criminal cases and the system of leniency for admitting guilt and accepting punishment; for minor criminal cases where the facts are clear and the evidence is sufficient, or where the criminal suspect or defendant voluntarily admits guilt and accepts punishment, the expedited procedure, the summary procedure, or a simplified ordinary procedure may be applied.
