# 国务院关于在对外公务活动中赠送和接受礼品的规定

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：1993-12-05  
**生效日期**：1993-12-05

> (1993年12月5日中华人民共和国国务院令第133号发布　自发布之日起施行) 第一条　为了加强对国家行政机关工作人员在对外公务活动中赠送和接受礼品的管理，严肃外事纪律，保持清廉，制定本规定。 第二条　本规定所称的礼品，是指礼物、礼金、有价证券。 第三条　根据国际惯例和对外工作需要，必要时可以对外赠送礼物。礼物的金额标准另行规定。 第四条　对外赠送礼物必须贯彻节约、从简的原则。礼物应当以具有民族特色的纪念品、传统手工艺品和实用物品为主。 第五条　对来访的外宾，不主动赠送礼物。外宾向我方赠送礼物的，可以适当回赠礼物。 第六条　对外赠送礼物或者回赠礼物，必须经国务院所属部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，或者由其授权的机关批准。审批时，应当从严掌握。 第七条　在对外公务活动中接受的礼物，应当妥善处理。价值按我国市价折合人民币二百元以上的，自接受之日起(在国外接受礼物的，自回国之日起)一个月内填写礼品申报单并将应上缴的礼物上缴礼品管理部门或者受礼人所在单位；不满二百元的，归受礼人本人或者受礼人所在单位。 在对外公务活动中，对方赠送礼金、有价证券时，应当予以谢绝；确实难以谢绝的，所收礼金、有价证券必须一律上缴国库。 第八条　在对外公务活动中，不得私相授受礼品，不得以明示或者暗示的方式索取礼品。 第九条　国务院机关事务管理局负责保管、处理国务院各部门上缴的礼品。 县级以上地方各级人民政府指定专门单位负责保管、处理该级人民政府各部门上缴的礼品。 第十条　礼品管理部门及有关部门对于收缴的礼品，应当登记造册，妥善保管，及时处理。礼品保管部门应当每年向受礼单位通报礼品处理情况。受礼单位应当将礼品处理情况告知受礼人。 第十一条　国家行政监察机关按照有关规定负责对对外赠送和接受礼品的情况进行监督、检查。 第十二条　国家行政机关工作人员违反本规定的，对负直接责任的机关有关领导人和直接责任人，给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强对国家行政机关工作人员在对外公务活动中赠送和接受礼品的管理，严肃外事纪律，保持清廉，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated to strengthen the management of the giving and acceptance of gifts by staff members of State administrative organs in foreign official activities, to tighten foreign affairs discipline, and to maintain integrity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　本规定所称的礼品，是指礼物、礼金、有价证券。

**Article 2** The term "gifts" as used in these Provisions refers to presents, cash gifts, and marketable securities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　根据国际惯例和对外工作需要，必要时可以对外赠送礼物。礼物的金额标准另行规定。

**Article 3** In accordance with international practice and the needs of foreign work, gifts may be given to foreign parties when necessary. The value standard of gifts shall be separately prescribed.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对外赠送礼物必须贯彻节约、从简的原则。礼物应当以具有民族特色的纪念品、传统手工艺品和实用物品为主。

**Article 4** The giving of gifts to foreign parties must adhere to the principle of thrift and simplicity. Gifts shall mainly be souvenirs with national characteristics, traditional handicrafts, and practical items.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对来访的外宾，不主动赠送礼物。外宾向我方赠送礼物的，可以适当回赠礼物。

**Article 5** Gifts shall not be proactively given to visiting foreign guests. If foreign guests give gifts to our side, gifts may be appropriately given in return.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对外赠送礼物或者回赠礼物，必须经国务院所属部门或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府批准，或者由其授权的机关批准。审批时，应当从严掌握。

**Article 6** The giving of gifts to foreign parties or the giving of return gifts must be approved by a department under the State Council or by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government, or by an organ authorized thereby. When examining and approving, strict control shall be exercised.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　在对外公务活动中接受的礼物，应当妥善处理。价值按我国市价折合人民币二百元以上的，自接受之日起(在国外接受礼物的，自回国之日起)一个月内填写礼品申报单并将应上缴的礼物上缴礼品管理部门或者受礼人所在单位；不满二百元的，归受礼人本人或者受礼人所在单位。  
在对外公务活动中，对方赠送礼金、有价证券时，应当予以谢绝；确实难以谢绝的，所收礼金、有价证券必须一律上缴国库。

**Article 7** Gifts accepted in foreign official activities shall be properly handled. Those valued at RMB 200 or more at the prevailing Chinese market price shall be reported by filing a gift declaration form and turned over to the gift management department or the recipient's unit within one month from the date of acceptance (or from the date of return to China if accepted abroad); those valued at less than RMB 200 shall belong to the recipient or the recipient's unit.  
In foreign official activities, when the other party offers cash gifts or marketable securities, they shall be declined; if it is truly difficult to decline, all cash gifts and marketable securities received must be turned over to the State treasury.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在对外公务活动中，不得私相授受礼品，不得以明示或者暗示的方式索取礼品。

**Article 8** In foreign official activities, gifts shall not be privately given or accepted, and gifts shall not be solicited by express or implied means.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　国务院机关事务管理局负责保管、处理国务院各部门上缴的礼品。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府指定专门单位负责保管、处理该级人民政府各部门上缴的礼品。

**Article 9** The Government Offices Administration of the State Council shall be responsible for the custody and disposal of gifts turned over by various departments of the State Council.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall designate specialized units to be responsible for the custody and disposal of gifts turned over by the departments of the respective governments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　礼品管理部门及有关部门对于收缴的礼品，应当登记造册，妥善保管，及时处理。礼品保管部门应当每年向受礼单位通报礼品处理情况。受礼单位应当将礼品处理情况告知受礼人。

**Article 10** The gift management department and relevant departments shall register, keep properly, and promptly dispose of the confiscated gifts. The gift custody department shall notify the recipient units of the gift disposal status each year. The recipient unit shall inform the recipient of the gift disposal status.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　国家行政监察机关按照有关规定负责对对外赠送和接受礼品的情况进行监督、检查。

**Article 11** The State administrative supervisory organs shall, in accordance with relevant provisions, be responsible for supervising and inspecting the giving and acceptance of gifts in foreign official activities.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家行政机关工作人员违反本规定的，对负直接责任的机关有关领导人和直接责任人，给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，由司法机关依法追究刑事责任。  
对国家行政机关工作人员的行政处分，按照干部管理权限和规定程序办理。

**Article 12** Where staff members of State administrative organs violate these Provisions, the leaders of the organ who bear direct responsibility and the persons directly responsible shall be given administrative sanctions; if the conduct constitutes a crime, the judicial organs shall investigate criminal liability according to law.  
Administrative sanctions against staff members of State administrative organs shall be handled in accordance with the authority over cadre management and the prescribed procedures.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　国家行政机关工作人员在公务活动中向华侨和香港、澳门、台湾地区的居民赠送礼品和接受其礼品，依照本规定执行。

**Article 13** Where staff members of State administrative organs give gifts to or accept gifts from overseas Chinese and residents of the Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan regions in official activities, these Provisions shall apply.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本规定由国务院办公厅负责解释。

**Article 14** These Provisions shall be interpreted by the General Office of the State Council.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本规定自发布之日起施行。

**Article 15** These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of promulgation.
