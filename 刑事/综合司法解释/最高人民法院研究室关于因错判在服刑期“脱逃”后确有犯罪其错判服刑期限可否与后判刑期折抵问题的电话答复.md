# 最高人民法院研究室关于因错判在服刑期“脱逃”后确有犯罪其错判服刑期限可否与后判刑期折抵问题的电话答复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：1983-08-31  
**生效日期**：1983-08-31

---

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院研究室  
关于因错判在服刑期“脱逃”后确有犯罪  
其错判服刑期限可否与后判刑期折抵  
问题的电话答复  
（1983年8月31日）  
湖北省高级人民法院：  
你院1983年8月12日鄂法研字〔83〕第19号对《因错判在服刑期“脱逃”后确有犯罪其错判服刑期限可否与后判刑期折抵的请示》已收悉。我们同意你院报告中所提出的意见，即：对被错判徒刑的在服刑期间“脱逃”的行为，可不以脱逃论罪判刑；但在脱逃期间犯罪的，应依法定罪判刑；对被错判已服刑的日期与后来犯罪所判处的刑期不宜折抵，可在量刑时酌情考虑从轻或减轻处罚。  
附：  
湖北省高级人民法院  
对因错判在服刑期“脱逃”后确有犯罪其错  
判服刑期限可否与后判刑期折抵的请示  
1983年8月12日      鄂法研字〔83〕第19号  
最高人民法院：  
武汉市中级人民法院为陈会群抢劫案刑期折抵问题请示我院。经我院研究后，认为没有把握答复，特请示如下：  
陈会群于1976年3月30日因抢劫一案经武昌县法院判处有期徒刑十年。1980年5月服刑期间脱逃，被沙洋人民法院加刑一年。于1982年3月13日又脱逃，持刀拦路抢劫，被武汉市中级法院以脱逃罪判处有期徒刑三年，以抢劫罪判处有期徒刑十五年，加上前罪尚未执行完的刑期，决定执行有期徒刑二十年。上诉后，因事实不清，被发回重审。武汉中院指令武昌县院对陈的抢劫前案进行再审。经武昌县院再审后撤销了1976年以抢劫罪判处陈有期徒刑十年的判决，宣告陈无罪。武汉中院根据上述情况，除认定陈犯“脱逃”罪已失去前提，不能成立外，但陈在“脱逃”后的行为确已构成抢劫罪，故判处陈有期徒刑八年。但陈因前案错判，已服刑六年四个月又七天，后来的犯罪与前案的错判也有一定的关系，因此，该院请示可否在这次判处的有期徒刑中如数折抵。如不能折抵其原错判而执行了的刑期应如何处理。  
经我们研究，根据最高人民法院〔81〕法研字第14号文的规定，我们认为，陈会群原被错判服刑与后来犯罪的行为并非同一行为，因此其刑期不宜折抵。但考虑到陈原因错判服刑而“脱逃”又犯罪的这一事实，在量刑时可相应酌情从轻或减轻。  
以上意见妥否，请批示。

**Article 1** Research Office of the Supreme People's Court  
Telephone Reply on Whether the Period of Wrongfully Imposed Fixed-Term Imprisonment Served Can Be Offset Against the Sentence for a Subsequent Crime Committed After "Escape" During the Period of Wrongful Imprisonment  
(August 31, 1983)  
  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province:  
  
Your Request for Instructions No. E Fa Yan Zi [83] 19 of August 12, 1983, on "Whether the Period of Wrongfully Imposed Fixed-Term Imprisonment Served Can Be Offset Against the Sentence for a Subsequent Crime Committed After 'Escape' During the Period of Wrongful Imprisonment" has been received. We agree with the opinion set forth in your report, namely: the act of "escape" during the period of service of a wrongfully imposed fixed-term imprisonment sentence shall not be convicted and punished as the crime of escape; however, if a crime is committed during the period of escape, the person shall be convicted and punished according to law; the period of wrongful imprisonment already served shall not be offset against the sentence imposed for the subsequent crime, but may be considered as a circumstance for a lighter or mitigated punishment at sentencing.  
  
Appendix:  
  
Higher People's Court of Hubei Province  
Request for Instructions on Whether the Period of Wrongfully Imposed Fixed-Term Imprisonment Served Can Be Offset Against the Sentence for a Subsequent Crime Committed After "Escape" During the Period of Wrongful Imprisonment  
  
August 12, 1983 E Fa Yan Zi [83] No. 19  
  
Supreme People's Court:  
  
Wuhan Intermediate People's Court submitted a request to our court regarding the offset of the sentence in the robbery case of Chen Huiqun. After study, our court finds itself unable to reply with certainty and hereby requests instructions as follows:  
  
Chen Huiqun was sentenced to ten years of fixed-term imprisonment by Wuchang County People's Court on March 30, 1976, for a robbery case. In May 1980, he escaped during service of his sentence and was given an additional one year of imprisonment by Shayang People's Court. On March 13, 1982, he escaped again, committed a robbery with a weapon on a road, and was sentenced by Wuhan Intermediate People's Court to three years of fixed-term imprisonment for escape and fifteen years of fixed-term imprisonment for robbery, with a combined sentence of twenty years of fixed-term imprisonment, taking into account the unserved portion of the previous sentence. After appeal, the case was remanded for retrial due to unclear facts. Wuhan Intermediate People's Court instructed Wuchang County People's Court to conduct a retrial of Chen's previous robbery case. After the retrial, Wuchang County People's Court revoked the 1976 judgment sentencing Chen to ten years of fixed-term imprisonment for robbery and declared Chen not guilty. Based on the above circumstances, Wuhan Intermediate People's Court held that the premise for convicting Chen of the crime of "escape" no longer existed and such conviction could not stand. However, Chen's conduct after the "escape" indeed constituted the crime of robbery. Accordingly, the court sentenced Chen to eight years of fixed-term imprisonment. Nevertheless, Chen had already served six years, four months, and seven days of the wrongfully imposed sentence, and his subsequent crime was related to the wrongful sentence. Therefore, the court requested instructions on whether this period could be fully offset against the newly imposed sentence of fixed-term imprisonment, and if not, how to handle the sentence already served due to the wrongful conviction.  
  
After our study, and in accordance with the provisions of Supreme People's Court Document [81] Fa Yan Zi No. 14, we are of the opinion that the period of wrongful imprisonment served by Chen Huiqun and the act of his subsequent crime are not the same act. Hence, the sentence should not be offset. However, considering the fact that Chen escaped due to the wrongful conviction and then committed a crime, a lighter or mitigated punishment may be accordingly considered at sentencing.  
  
The above opinion is submitted for your approval and instructions.
