# 最高人民法院关于在审理经济纠纷案件中涉及经济犯罪嫌疑若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：2020-12-29  
**生效日期**：2021-01-01

> （1998年4月9日最高人民法院审判委员会第974次会议通过，根据2020年12月23日最高人民法院审判委员会第1823次会议通过的《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用《中华人民共和国工会法》若干问题的解释〉等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定》修正） 根据《中华人民共和国民法典》《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等有关规定，对审理经济纠纷案件中涉及经济犯罪嫌疑问题作以下规定： 第一条　同一自然人、法人或非法人组织因不同的法律事实，分别涉及经济纠纷和经济犯罪嫌疑的，经济纠纷案件和经济犯罪嫌疑案件应当分开审理。 第二条　单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，以为单位骗取财物为目的，采取欺骗手段对外签订经济合同，骗取的财物被该单位占有、使用或处分构成犯罪的，除依法追究有关人员的刑事责任，责令该单位返还骗取的财物外，如给被害人造成经济损失的，单位应当承担赔偿责任。 第三条　单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，以该单位的名义对外签订经济合同，将取得的财物部分或全部占为己有构成犯罪的，除依法追究行为人的刑事责任外，该单位对行为人因签订、履行该经济合同造成的后果，依法应当承担民事责任。 第四条　个人借用单位的业务介绍信、合同专用章或者盖有公章的空白合同书，以出借单位名义签订经济合同，骗取财物归个人占有、使用、处分或者进行其他犯罪活动，给对方造成经济损失构成犯罪的，除依法追究借用人的刑事责任外，出借业务介绍信、合同专用章或者盖有公章的空白合同书的单位，依法应当承担赔偿责任。但是，有证据证明被害人明知签订合同对方当事人是借用行为，仍与之签订合同的除外。 第五条　行为人盗窃、盗用单位的公章、业务介绍信、盖有公章的空白合同书，或者私刻单位的公章签订经济合同，骗取财物归个人占有、使用、处分或者进行其他犯罪活动构成犯罪的，单位对行为人该犯罪行为所造成的经济损失不承担民事责任。 行为人私刻单位公章或者擅自使用单位公章、业务介绍信、盖有公章的空白合同书以签订经济合同的方法进行的犯罪行为，单位有明显过错，且该过错行为与被害人的经济损失之间具有因果关系的，单位对该犯罪行为所造成的经济损失，依法应当承担赔偿责任。 第六条　企业承包、租赁经营合同期满后，企业按规定办理了企业法定代表人的变更登记，而企业法人未采取有效措施收回其公章、业务介绍信、盖有公章的空白合同书，或者没有及时采取措施通知相对人，致原企业承包人、租赁人得以用原承包、租赁企业的名义签订经济合同，骗取财物占为己有构成犯罪的，该企业对被害人的经济损失，依法应当承担赔偿责任。但是，原承包人、承租人利用擅自保留的公章、业务介绍信、盖有公章的空白合同书以原承包、租赁企业的名义签订经济合同，骗取财物占为己有构成犯罪的，企业一般不承担民事责任。 单位聘用的人员被解聘后，或者受单位委托保管公章的人员被解除委托后，单位未及时收回其公章，行为人擅自利用保留的原单位公章签订经济合同，骗取财物占为己有构成犯罪，如给被害人造成经济损失的，单位应当承担赔偿责任。 第七条　单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，将单位进行走私或其他犯罪活动所得财物以签订经济合同的方法予以销售，买方明知或者应当知道的，如因此造成经济损失，其损失由买方自负。但是，如果买方不知该经济合同的标的物是犯罪行为所得财物而购买的，卖方对买方所造成的经济损失应当承担民事责任。 第八条　根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第一百零一条第一款的规定，被害人或其法定代理人、近亲属对本规定第二条因单位犯罪行为造成经济损失的，对第四条、第五条第一款、第六条应当承担刑事责任的被告人未能返还财物而遭受经济损失提起附带民事诉讼的，受理刑事案件的人民法院应当依法一并审理。被害人或其法定代理人、近亲属因被害人遭受经济损失也有权对单位另行提起民事诉讼。若被害人或其法定代理人、近亲属另行提起民事诉讼的，有管辖权的人民法院应当依法受理。 第九条　被害人请求保护其民事权利的诉讼时效在公安机关、检察机关查处经济犯罪嫌疑期间中断。如果公安机关决定撤销涉嫌经济犯罪案件或者检察机关决定不起诉的，诉讼时效从撤销案件或决定不起诉之次日起重新计算。 第十条　人民法院在审理经济纠纷案件中，发现与本案有牵连，但与本案不是同一法律关系的经济犯罪嫌疑线索、材料，应将犯罪嫌疑线索、材料移送有关公安机关或检察机关查处，经济纠纷案件继续审理。 第十一条　人民法院作为经济纠纷受理的案件，经审理认为不属经济纠纷案件而有经济犯罪嫌疑的，应当裁定驳回起诉，将有关材料移送公安机关或检察机关。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　同一自然人、法人或非法人组织因不同的法律事实，分别涉及经济纠纷和经济犯罪嫌疑的，经济纠纷案件和经济犯罪嫌疑案件应当分开审理。

**Article 1** Where the same natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization is involved, respectively, in economic disputes and economic crime suspicions due to different legal facts, the economic dispute case and the economic crime suspicion case shall be tried separately.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，以为单位骗取财物为目的，采取欺骗手段对外签订经济合同，骗取的财物被该单位占有、使用或处分构成犯罪的，除依法追究有关人员的刑事责任，责令该单位返还骗取的财物外，如给被害人造成经济损失的，单位应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 2** Where the directly responsible person in charge of an entity and other directly responsible persons, for the purpose of defrauding property for the entity, use deceptive means to enter into an economic contract with a third party, and the defrauded property is possessed, used, or disposed of by the entity, constituting a crime, in addition to investigating the criminal liability of the relevant persons according to law and ordering the entity to return the defrauded property, if economic losses are caused to the victim, the entity shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，以该单位的名义对外签订经济合同，将取得的财物部分或全部占为己有构成犯罪的，除依法追究行为人的刑事责任外，该单位对行为人因签订、履行该经济合同造成的后果，依法应当承担民事责任。

**Article 3** Where the directly responsible person in charge of an entity and other directly responsible persons, in the name of the entity, enter into an economic contract with a third party and take the obtained property partially or wholly into their own possession, constituting a crime, in addition to investigating the actor's criminal liability according to law, the entity shall bear civil liability according to law for the consequences caused by the actor in entering into and performing the economic contract.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　个人借用单位的业务介绍信、合同专用章或者盖有公章的空白合同书，以出借单位名义签订经济合同，骗取财物归个人占有、使用、处分或者进行其他犯罪活动，给对方造成经济损失构成犯罪的，除依法追究借用人的刑事责任外，出借业务介绍信、合同专用章或者盖有公章的空白合同书的单位，依法应当承担赔偿责任。但是，有证据证明被害人明知签订合同对方当事人是借用行为，仍与之签订合同的除外。

**Article 4** Where an individual borrows the entity's business introduction letter, special contract seal, or blank contract form bearing the official seal, enters into an economic contract in the name of the lending entity, defrauds property for personal possession, use, or disposal, or engages in other criminal activities, causing economic losses to the other party and constituting a crime, in addition to investigating the borrower's criminal liability according to law, the entity that lent the business introduction letter, special contract seal, or blank contract form bearing the official seal shall bear compensation liability according to law. However, this does not apply if there is evidence that the victim knew that the other party entering into the contract was acting by way of borrowing and nevertheless entered into the contract.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　行为人盗窃、盗用单位的公章、业务介绍信、盖有公章的空白合同书，或者私刻单位的公章签订经济合同，骗取财物归个人占有、使用、处分或者进行其他犯罪活动构成犯罪的，单位对行为人该犯罪行为所造成的经济损失不承担民事责任。  
行为人私刻单位公章或者擅自使用单位公章、业务介绍信、盖有公章的空白合同书以签订经济合同的方法进行的犯罪行为，单位有明显过错，且该过错行为与被害人的经济损失之间具有因果关系的，单位对该犯罪行为所造成的经济损失，依法应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 5** Where an actor steals or wrongfully uses the entity's official seal, business introduction letter, or blank contract form bearing the official seal, or forges the entity's official seal to enter into an economic contract, defrauds property for personal possession, use, or disposal, or engages in other criminal activities, constituting a crime, the entity shall not bear civil liability for the economic losses caused by the actor's criminal act.  
Where the actor uses the forged official seal of the entity or without authorization uses the entity's official seal, business introduction letter, or blank contract form bearing the official seal to commit a crime by entering into an economic contract, and the entity has obvious fault, and there is a causal relationship between such fault and the victim's economic losses, the entity shall bear compensation liability according to law for the economic losses caused by the criminal act.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　企业承包、租赁经营合同期满后，企业按规定办理了企业法定代表人的变更登记，而企业法人未采取有效措施收回其公章、业务介绍信、盖有公章的空白合同书，或者没有及时采取措施通知相对人，致原企业承包人、租赁人得以用原承包、租赁企业的名义签订经济合同，骗取财物占为己有构成犯罪的，该企业对被害人的经济损失，依法应当承担赔偿责任。但是，原承包人、承租人利用擅自保留的公章、业务介绍信、盖有公章的空白合同书以原承包、租赁企业的名义签订经济合同，骗取财物占为己有构成犯罪的，企业一般不承担民事责任。  
单位聘用的人员被解聘后，或者受单位委托保管公章的人员被解除委托后，单位未及时收回其公章，行为人擅自利用保留的原单位公章签订经济合同，骗取财物占为己有构成犯罪，如给被害人造成经济损失的，单位应当承担赔偿责任。

**Article 6** After the term of an enterprise contract for operation under lease expires, if the enterprise has handled the registration of change of the enterprise's legal representative according to regulations, but the enterprise legal person has not taken effective measures to retrieve its official seal, business introduction letter, or blank contract form bearing the official seal, or has not timely taken measures to notify the counterparty, thereby enabling the original enterprise contractor or leaseholder to enter into an economic contract in the name of the original contracted or leased enterprise and defraud property for personal possession, constituting a crime, the enterprise shall bear compensation liability according to law for the victim's economic losses. However, if the original contractor or leaseholder uses the official seal, business introduction letter, or blank contract form bearing the official seal retained without authorization to enter into an economic contract in the name of the original contracted or leased enterprise and defrauds property for personal possession, constituting a crime, the enterprise generally shall not bear civil liability.  
Where, after a person employed by an entity is dismissed, or after the person entrusted by the entity to keep the official seal is relieved of the entrustment, the entity fails to retrieve the official seal in a timely manner, and the actor without authorization uses the retained original official seal of the entity to enter into an economic contract and defrauds property for personal possession, constituting a crime, if economic losses are caused to the victim, the entity shall bear compensation liability.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，将单位进行走私或其他犯罪活动所得财物以签订经济合同的方法予以销售，买方明知或者应当知道的，如因此造成经济损失，其损失由买方自负。但是，如果买方不知该经济合同的标的物是犯罪行为所得财物而购买的，卖方对买方所造成的经济损失应当承担民事责任。

**Article 7** Where the directly responsible person in charge of an entity and other directly responsible persons sell, by entering into an economic contract, the property obtained by the entity from smuggling or other criminal activities, and the buyer knows or should have known thereof, if economic losses are thus caused, the losses shall be borne by the buyer. However, if the buyer purchases without knowing that the subject matter of the economic contract is property obtained from a criminal act, the seller shall bear civil liability for the economic losses caused to the buyer.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　根据[《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第一百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-101)第一款的规定，被害人或其法定代理人、近亲属对[本规定第二条](#art-2)因单位犯罪行为造成经济损失的，对第四条、第五条第一款、第六条应当承担刑事责任的被告人未能返还财物而遭受经济损失提起附带民事诉讼的，受理刑事案件的人民法院应当依法一并审理。被害人或其法定代理人、近亲属因被害人遭受经济损失也有权对单位另行提起民事诉讼。若被害人或其法定代理人、近亲属另行提起民事诉讼的，有管辖权的人民法院应当依法受理。

**Article 8** According to Article 101, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, where the victim, or the victim's statutory agent or close relatives, file an incidental civil action with respect to the economic losses caused by the entity's criminal act under Article 2 of these Provisions, or against the defendant who, under Articles 4, 5(1), and 6, shall bear criminal liability but fails to return the property, resulting in economic losses, the People's Court accepting the criminal case shall try them together according to law. The victim, or the victim's statutory agent or close relatives, also have the right to separately file a civil action against the entity for the economic losses suffered by the victim. If the victim, or the victim's statutory agent or close relatives, separately file a civil action, the People's Court with jurisdiction shall accept it according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　被害人请求保护其民事权利的诉讼时效在公安机关、检察机关查处经济犯罪嫌疑期间中断。如果公安机关决定撤销涉嫌经济犯罪案件或者检察机关决定不起诉的，诉讼时效从撤销案件或决定不起诉之次日起重新计算。

**Article 9** The limitation of action for the victim to request protection of his or her civil rights shall be interrupted during the period when the public security organ or the procuratorial organ investigates and handles the economic crime suspicion. If the public security organ decides to withdraw the suspected economic crime case, or the procuratorial organ decides not to prosecute, the limitation of action shall be recalculated from the day following the date of withdrawal of the case or the decision not to prosecute.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民法院在审理经济纠纷案件中，发现与本案有牵连，但与本案不是同一法律关系的经济犯罪嫌疑线索、材料，应将犯罪嫌疑线索、材料移送有关公安机关或检察机关查处，经济纠纷案件继续审理。

**Article 10** Where, in the course of trying an economic dispute case, a People's Court discovers clues and materials of economic crime suspicion that are connected with the case but do not involve the same legal relationship as the case, it shall transfer such clues and materials to the relevant public security organ or procuratorial organ for investigation and handling, and the economic dispute case shall continue to be tried.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院作为经济纠纷受理的案件，经审理认为不属经济纠纷案件而有经济犯罪嫌疑的，应当裁定驳回起诉，将有关材料移送公安机关或检察机关。

**Article 11** Where a case accepted by a People's Court as an economic dispute is, after trial, found not to be an economic dispute case but rather to involve an economic crime suspicion, the court shall rule to dismiss the action and transfer the relevant materials to the public security organ or the procuratorial organ.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民法院已立案审理的经济纠纷案件，公安机关或检察机关认为有经济犯罪嫌疑，并说明理由附有关材料函告受理该案的人民法院的，有关人民法院应当认真审查。经过审查，认为确有经济犯罪嫌疑的，应当将案件移送公安机关或检察机关，并书面通知当事人，退还案件受理费；如认为确属经济纠纷案件的，应当依法继续审理，并将结果函告有关公安机关或检察机关。

**Article 12** Where a public security organ or a procuratorial organ believes that a case which has been docketed and tried by a People's Court as an economic dispute involves an economic crime suspicion, and informs the People's Court handling the case of such suspicion by letter stating the reasons and attaching relevant materials, the relevant People's Court shall conduct a careful review. Upon review, if it finds that there is indeed an economic crime suspicion, it shall transfer the case to the public security organ or the procuratorial organ, notify the parties in writing, and refund the case acceptance fee; if it finds that the case indeed belongs to an economic dispute, it shall continue to try it according to law and inform the relevant public security organ or procuratorial organ of the result by letter.
