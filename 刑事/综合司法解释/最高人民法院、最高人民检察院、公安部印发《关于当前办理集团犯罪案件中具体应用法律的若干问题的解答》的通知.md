# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部印发《关于当前办理集团犯罪案件中具体应用法律的若干问题的解答》的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：1984-06-15  
**生效日期**：1984-06-15

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、怎样办理团伙犯罪的案件？  
办理团伙犯罪的重大案件，应当在党的方针政策指导下，依照刑法和《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于严惩严重危害社会治安的犯罪分子的决定》的有关规定执行。鉴于在刑法和全国人大常务会的有关决定中，只有共同犯罪和犯罪集团的规定，在法律文书中，应当统一使用法律规定的提法。即：  
办理团伙犯罪案件，凡其中符合刑事犯罪集团基本特征的，应按犯罪集团处理；不符合犯罪集团基本特征的，就按一般共同犯罪处理，并根据其共同犯罪的事实和情节，该重判的重判，该轻判的轻判。  
对犯罪团伙既要坚决打击，又必须打准。不要把三人以上共同犯罪，但罪行较轻，危害较小的案件当作犯罪团伙，进而当作“犯罪集团”来严厉打击。

**Article 1** 1. How to handle cases of gang crimes?  
Major cases of gang crimes shall be handled under the guidance of the Party's policies and in accordance with the Criminal Law and the relevant provisions of the "Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Severely Punishing Criminals Who Seriously Endanger Public Security". Given that only provisions on joint crimes and criminal groups exist in the Criminal Law and the relevant decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, uniform legal terminology prescribed by law shall be used in legal documents, i.e.:  
When handling gang crime cases, those that meet the basic characteristics of a criminal group shall be dealt with as a criminal group; those that do not meet the basic characteristics of a criminal group shall be dealt with as a general joint crime, and, based on the facts and circumstances of the joint crime, those deserving heavier punishment shall be severely punished, and those deserving lighter punishment shall be leniently punished.  
Criminal gangs shall be resolutely cracked down upon, but the crackdown must be accurate. Cases involving three or more persons committing a joint crime but with minor offenses and lesser harm shall not be treated as criminal gangs, and further, shall not be severely cracked down upon as "criminal groups".

<a id="art-2"></a>二、在办案实践中怎样认定刑事犯罪集团？  
刑事犯罪集团一般应具备下列基本特征：（1）人数较多（三人以上），重要成员固定或基本固定。（2）经常纠集一起进行一种或数种严重的刑事犯罪活动。（3）有明显的首要分子。有的首要分子是在纠集过程中形成的，有的首要分子在纠集开始时就是组织者和领导者。（4）有预谋地实施犯罪活动。（5）不论作案次数多少，对社会造成的危害或其具有的危险性都很严重。  
刑事犯罪集团的首要分子，是指在该集团中起组织、策划、指挥作用的犯罪分子（见[刑法第二十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-23)、第八十六条）。首要分子可以是一名，也可以不只一名。首要分子应对该集团经过预谋、有共同故意的全部罪行负责。集团的其他成员，应按其地位和作用，分别对其参与实施的具体罪行负责。如果某个成员实施了该集团共同故意犯罪范围以外的其他犯罪，则应由他个人负责。  
对单一的犯罪集团，应按其所犯的罪定性；对一个犯罪集团犯多种罪的，应按其主罪定性；犯罪集团成员或一般共同犯罪的共犯，犯数罪的，分别按数罪并罚的原则处罚。

**Article 2** 2. How to identify a criminal group in practice?  
A criminal group shall generally have the following basic characteristics: (1) a relatively large number of persons (three or more), with core members fixed or basically fixed; (2) regularly gathering together to commit one or more serious criminal activities; (3) having an obvious principal culprit. Some principal culprits are formed during the process of gathering, while others are organizers and leaders from the beginning of the gathering; (4) carrying out criminal activities with premeditation; (5) regardless of the number of crimes committed, the harm caused to society or the dangerousness thereof is grave.  
The principal culprit of a criminal group refers to the criminal who organizes, plans, and directs the activities within the group (see Articles 23 and 86 of the Criminal Law). The principal culprit may be one or more than one. The principal culprit shall be liable for all crimes of the group that have been premeditated and intentionally committed with common intent. Other members of the group shall be liable for the specific crimes they participated in, according to their status and role. If a member commits other crimes beyond the scope of the group's common intent, that member shall bear personal liability.  
Where there is a single criminal group, it shall be characterized according to the crime it committed; where a criminal group commits multiple crimes, it shall be characterized according to its principal crime; where members of a criminal group or accomplices in a general joint crime commit multiple crimes, they shall be punished separately in accordance with the principle of cumulative punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、为什么对共同犯罪的案件必须坚持全案审判？  
办理共同犯罪案件特别是集团犯罪案件，除对其中已逃跑的成员可以另案处理外，一定要把全案的事实查清，然后对应当追究刑事责任的同案人，全案起诉，全案判处。切不要全案事实还没有查清，就急于杀掉首要分子或主犯，或者把案件拆散，分开处理。这样做，不仅可能造成定罪不准，量刑失当，而且会造成死无对证，很容易漏掉同案成员的罪行，甚至漏掉罪犯，难以做到依法“从重从快，一网打尽”。

**Article 3** 3. Why must joint crime cases be adjudicated in their entirety?  
When handling joint crime cases, especially group crime cases, except where any member who has escaped may be dealt with in a separate case, the facts of the entire case must be ascertained, and then all co-defendants subject to criminal liability shall be prosecuted and sentenced in the entire case. One must not, before the facts of the entire case have been ascertained, hastily execute the principal culprit or the primary offender, or split the case and handle it separately. Doing so may not only lead to inaccurate conviction and improper sentencing, but also result in "death without confrontation," easily omitting the crimes of co-defendants, or even missing offenders, making it difficult to achieve the goal of "severely and swiftly punishing according to law and catching all in one net."

<a id="art-4"></a>四、办理犯罪集团和一般共同犯罪中的重大案件，怎样执行党的政策，做到区别对待？  
办理上述两类案件，应根据犯罪分子在犯罪活动中的地位、作用及危害大小，依照党的政策和刑法、全国人大常委会有关决定的规定，实行区别对待。  
对犯罪集团的首要分子和其他主犯，一般共同犯罪中的重大案件的主犯，应依法从重严惩，其中罪行特别严重、不杀不足以平民愤的，应依法判处死刑。  
上述两类案件的从犯，应根据其不同的犯罪情节，比照主犯依法从轻、减轻或者免除刑罚。对于胁从犯，应比照从犯依法减轻处罚或免除处罚。犯罪情节轻微，不需要追究刑事责任的，可以免予起诉或由公安部门作其他处理。  
对于同犯罪集团成员有一般来往，而无犯罪行为的人，不要株连。

**Article 4** 4. In handling major cases involving criminal groups and general joint crimes, how to implement the Party's policy and achieve differentiated treatment?  
When handling the two types of cases mentioned above, differentiated treatment shall be applied according to the status, role, and degree of harm of the criminals in the criminal activities, in accordance with the Party's policies, the Criminal Law, and the relevant decisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.  
The principal culprit and other primary offenders of a criminal group, and the primary offenders in major cases of general joint crimes, shall be severely punished according to law; where the crimes are exceptionally serious and not imposing the death penalty would fail to satisfy public indignation, the death penalty shall be imposed according to law.  
Accomplices in the two types of cases mentioned above shall, according to the different circumstances of their crimes, be given a lighter or mitigated punishment or be exempted from punishment by reference to the primary offenders. For coerced accomplices, punishment shall be mitigated or exempted by reference to the accomplices according to law. Where the criminal circumstances are minor and criminal liability need not be investigated, the case may be exempted from prosecution or otherwise dealt with by the public security department.  
Persons who have only ordinary contacts with members of a criminal group but have committed no criminal acts shall not be implicated.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、有些犯罪分子参加几起共同犯罪活动，应如何办理这些案件？  
对这类案件，应分案判处，不能凑合成一案处理。某罪犯主要参加那个案件的共同犯罪活动，就列入那个案件去处理（在该犯参加的其他案件中可注明该犯已另案处理）。

**Article 5** 5. How should cases be handled where some criminals have participated in several joint criminal activities?  
Such cases shall be adjudicated separately and shall not be combined into one case. An offender shall be placed in the case in which he primarily participated in the joint criminal activity (in other cases in which he participated, it may be noted that the offender has been dealt with in another case).
