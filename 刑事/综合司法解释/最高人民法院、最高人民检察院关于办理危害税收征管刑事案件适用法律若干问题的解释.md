# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害税收征管刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2024〕4号  
**公布日期**：2024-03-15  
**生效日期**：2024-03-20

> （2024年1月8日最高人民法院审判委员会第1911次会议、2024年2月22日最高人民检察院第十四届检察委员会第二十五次会议通过，自2024年3月20日起施行） 为依法惩治危害税收征管犯罪，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　纳税人进行虚假纳税申报，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百零一条第一款规定的“欺骗、隐瞒手段”： （一）伪造、变造、转移、隐匿、擅自销毁账簿、记账凭证或者其他涉税资料的； （二）以签订“阴阳合同”等形式隐匿或者以他人名义分解收入、财产的； （三）虚列支出、虚抵进项税额或者虚报专项附加扣除的； （四）提供虚假材料，骗取税收优惠的； （五）编造虚假计税依据的； （六）为不缴、少缴税款而采取的其他欺骗、隐瞒手段。 具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百零一条第一款规定的“不申报”： （一）依法在登记机关办理设立登记的纳税人，发生应税行为而不申报纳税的； （二）依法不需要在登记机关办理设立登记或者未依法办理设立登记的纳税人，发生应税行为，经税务机关依法通知其申报而不申报纳税的； （三）其他明知应当依法申报纳税而不申报纳税的。 扣缴义务人采取第一、二款所列手段，不缴或者少缴已扣、已收税款，数额较大的，依照刑法第二百零一条第一款的规定定罪处罚。扣缴义务人承诺为纳税人代付税款，在其向纳税人支付税后所得时，应当认定扣缴义务人“已扣、已收税款”。 第二条　纳税人逃避缴纳税款十万元以上、五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为刑法第二百零一条第一款规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　纳税人进行虚假纳税申报，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的“欺骗、隐瞒手段”：  
（一）伪造、变造、转移、隐匿、擅自销毁账簿、记账凭证或者其他涉税资料的；  
（二）以签订“阴阳合同”等形式隐匿或者以他人名义分解收入、财产的；  
（三）虚列支出、虚抵进项税额或者虚报专项附加扣除的；  
（四）提供虚假材料，骗取税收优惠的；  
（五）编造虚假计税依据的；  
（六）为不缴、少缴税款而采取的其他欺骗、隐瞒手段。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的“不申报”：  
（一）依法在登记机关办理设立登记的纳税人，发生应税行为而不申报纳税的；  
（二）依法不需要在登记机关办理设立登记或者未依法办理设立登记的纳税人，发生应税行为，经税务机关依法通知其申报而不申报纳税的；  
（三）其他明知应当依法申报纳税而不申报纳税的。  
扣缴义务人采取第一、二款所列手段，不缴或者少缴已扣、已收税款，数额较大的，依照[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款的规定定罪处罚。扣缴义务人承诺为纳税人代付税款，在其向纳税人支付税后所得时，应当认定扣缴义务人“已扣、已收税款”。

**Article 1** Where a taxpayer makes a false tax declaration and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as the "means of deception or concealment" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) falsifying, altering, transferring, concealing, or destroying without authorization account books, accounting vouchers, or other tax-related materials;  
(2) concealing income or property by entering into "yin-yang contracts" or similar forms, or decomposing income or property in the name of another person;  
(3) falsely listing expenditures, falsely deducting input tax, or falsely reporting special additional deductions;  
(4) providing false materials to defraud tax preferences;  
(5) fabricating false tax bases;  
(6) other means of deception or concealment adopted for the purpose of not paying or underpaying taxes.  
Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "non-declaration" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) a taxpayer that has registered for establishment with the registration authority according to law fails to declare and pay tax after a taxable act occurs;  
(2) a taxpayer that is not required to register for establishment with the registration authority according to law or has not registered for establishment according to law fails to declare and pay tax after a taxable act occurs, despite being notified by the tax authority according to law to declare and pay tax;  
(3) other cases where the taxpayer knowingly fails to declare and pay tax that shall be declared and paid according to law.  
Where a withholding agent, by adopting the means listed in paragraphs 1 and 2, fails to pay or underpays taxes that have been withheld or collected, and the amount is relatively large, it shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law. Where a withholding agent undertakes to pay taxes on behalf of a taxpayer and, when paying after-tax income to the taxpayer, it shall be deemed that the withholding agent has "withheld or collected taxes".

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　纳税人逃避缴纳税款十万元以上、五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”。  
扣缴义务人不缴或者少缴已扣、已收税款“数额较大”、“数额巨大”的认定标准，依照前款规定。

**Article 2** Where a taxpayer evades tax payments of 100,000 yuan or more, or 500,000 yuan or more, such amounts shall be respectively deemed as "relatively large amount" and "huge amount" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law.  
The standards for determining "relatively large amount" and "huge amount" for a withholding agent's failure to pay or underpayment of taxes that have been withheld or collected shall be pursuant to the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　纳税人有[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的逃避缴纳税款行为，在公安机关立案前，经税务机关依法下达追缴通知后，在规定的期限或者批准延缓、分期缴纳的期限内足额补缴应纳税款，缴纳滞纳金，并全部履行税务机关作出的行政处罚决定的，不予追究刑事责任。但是，五年内因逃避缴纳税款受过刑事处罚或者被税务机关给予二次以上行政处罚的除外。  
纳税人有逃避缴纳税款行为，税务机关没有依法下达追缴通知的，依法不予追究刑事责任。

**Article 3** Where a taxpayer has committed tax evasion as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law, and before the case is filed by a public security organ, the taxpayer fully pays the payable taxes, pays the surcharge for overdue payment, and fully performs the administrative penalty decision made by the tax authority within the prescribed time limit or within the approved extended or installment payment period after the tax authority issues a recovery notice according to law, criminal liability shall not be investigated. However, this shall not apply where the taxpayer has been subject to criminal punishment for tax evasion or has been given two or more administrative penalties by the tax authority within the preceding five years.  
Where a taxpayer has committed tax evasion but the tax authority has not issued a recovery notice according to law, criminal liability shall not be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的“逃避缴纳税款数额”，是指在确定的纳税期间，不缴或者少缴税务机关负责征收的各税种税款的总额。  
[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的“应纳税额”，是指应税行为发生年度内依照税收法律、行政法规规定应当缴纳的税额，不包括海关代征的增值税、关税等及纳税人依法预缴的税额。  
[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的“逃避缴纳税款数额占应纳税额的百分比”，是指行为人在一个纳税年度中的各税种逃税总额与该纳税年度应纳税总额的比例；不按纳税年度确定纳税期的，按照最后一次逃税行为发生之日前一年中各税种逃税总额与该年应纳税总额的比例确定。纳税义务存续期间不足一个纳税年度的，按照各税种逃税总额与实际发生纳税义务期间应纳税总额的比例确定。  
逃税行为跨越若干个纳税年度，只要其中一个纳税年度的逃税数额及百分比达到[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的标准，即构成逃税罪。各纳税年度的逃税数额应当累计计算，逃税额占应纳税额百分比应当按照各逃税年度百分比的最高值确定。  
[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第三款规定的“未经处理”，包括未经行政处理和刑事处理。

**Article 4** The "amount of tax evaded" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law means the total amount of taxes of various types levied by the tax authority that are not paid or underpaid within the determined tax period.  
The "tax payable" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law means the tax amount that shall be paid according to tax laws and administrative regulations within the year in which the taxable act occurs, excluding the value-added tax and customs duties collected on behalf of the customs, and the taxes prepaid by the taxpayer according to law.  
The "percentage of tax evaded to tax payable" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law means the ratio of the total amount of taxes evaded by the actor in a tax year to the total tax payable for that tax year; if the tax period is not determined by tax year, the ratio shall be determined based on the total amount of taxes evaded in the year preceding the date of the last tax evasion act to the total tax payable for that year. If the duration of the tax liability is less than one tax year, the ratio shall be determined based on the total amount of taxes evaded to the total tax payable during the period when the tax liability actually arises.  
Where a tax evasion act spans several tax years, as long as the amount and percentage of tax evaded in any one tax year meet the standards prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law, the crime of tax evasion shall be constituted. The amounts of tax evaded in each tax year shall be calculated cumulatively, and the percentage of tax evaded to tax payable shall be determined based on the highest percentage among the tax evasion years.  
The term "not having been dealt with" as prescribed in paragraph 3 of Article 201 of the Criminal Law includes not having been dealt with administratively or criminally.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　以暴力、威胁方法拒不缴纳税款，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-202)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）聚众抗税的首要分子；  
（二）故意伤害致人轻伤的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施抗税行为致人重伤、死亡，符合[刑法第二百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-234)或者第二百三十二条规定的，以故意伤害罪或者故意杀人罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where a taxpayer refuses to pay taxes by means of violence or threats and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in Article 202 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) being a ringleader of a crowd resisting tax;  
(2) intentionally causing injury resulting in minor injury;  
(3) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where an act of tax resistance causes serious injury or death to a person, and it conforms to the provisions of Article 234 or Article 232 of the Criminal Law, it shall be convicted and punished as the crime of intentional injury or the crime of intentional homicide.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　纳税人欠缴应纳税款，为逃避税务机关追缴，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-203)规定的“采取转移或者隐匿财产的手段”：  
（一）放弃到期债权的；  
（二）无偿转让财产的；  
（三）以明显不合理的价格进行交易的；  
（四）隐匿财产的；  
（五）不履行税收义务并脱离税务机关监管的；  
（六）以其他手段转移或者隐匿财产的。

**Article 6** Where a taxpayer owes taxes payable and, for the purpose of evading recovery by the tax authority, falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "adopting the means of transferring or concealing property" as prescribed in Article 203 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) waiving due claims;  
(2) transferring property without consideration;  
(3) conducting transactions at a clearly unreasonable price;  
(4) concealing property;  
(5) failing to perform tax obligations and escaping the supervision of the tax authority;  
(6) transferring or concealing property by other means.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)第一款规定的“假报出口或者其他欺骗手段”：  
（一）使用虚开、非法购买或者以其他非法手段取得的增值税专用发票或者其他可以用于出口退税的发票申报出口退税的；  
（二）将未负税或者免税的出口业务申报为已税的出口业务的；  
（三）冒用他人出口业务申报出口退税的；  
（四）虽有出口，但虚构应退税出口业务的品名、数量、单价等要素，以虚增出口退税额申报出口退税的；  
（五）伪造、签订虚假的销售合同，或者以伪造、变造等非法手段取得出口报关单、运输单据等出口业务相关单据、凭证，虚构出口事实申报出口退税的；  
（六）在货物出口后，又转入境内或者将境外同种货物转入境内循环进出口并申报出口退税的；  
（七）虚报出口产品的功能、用途等，将不享受退税政策的产品申报为退税产品的；  
（八）以其他欺骗手段骗取出口退税款的。

**Article 7** Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "falsely reporting exports or other deceptive means" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 204 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) using falsely issued, illegally purchased, or otherwise illegally obtained special VAT invoices or other invoices that can be used for export tax refund to declare export tax refund;  
(2) declaring an export transaction that is not taxed or is exempt from tax as a taxed export transaction;  
(3) using another person's export transaction to declare export tax refund;  
(4) even though there is an export, fabricating elements such as the product name, quantity, or unit price of the taxable export transaction to inflate the export tax refund amount and declaring export tax refund;  
(5) forging or entering into false sales contracts, or obtaining export documents such as customs declaration forms, transport documents, or other related documents or vouchers by forging, altering, or other illegal means, fabricating the fact of export and declaring export tax refund;  
(6) after the goods are exported, bringing them back into the territory or importing the same type of goods from abroad to cycle imports and exports and declaring export tax refund;  
(7) falsely reporting the function or use of export products, declaring products that do not enjoy tax refund policy as refundable products;  
(8) fraudulently obtaining export tax refund by other deceptive means.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　骗取国家出口退税款数额十万元以上、五十万元以上、五百万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)第一款规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)第一款规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）两年内实施虚假申报出口退税行为三次以上，且骗取国家税款三十万元以上的；  
（二）五年内因骗取国家出口退税受过刑事处罚或者二次以上行政处罚，又实施骗取国家出口退税行为，数额在三十万元以上的；  
（三）致使国家税款被骗取三十万元以上并且在提起公诉前无法追回的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)第一款规定的“其他特别严重情节”：  
（一）两年内实施虚假申报出口退税行为五次以上，或者以骗取出口退税为主要业务，且骗取国家税款三百万元以上的；  
（二）五年内因骗取国家出口退税受过刑事处罚或者二次以上行政处罚，又实施骗取国家出口退税行为，数额在三百万元以上的；  
（三）致使国家税款被骗取三百万元以上并且在提起公诉前无法追回的；  
（四）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 8** Where the amount of fraudulently obtained export tax refund is 100,000 yuan or more, 500,000 yuan or more, or 5,000,000 yuan or more, such amounts shall be respectively deemed as "relatively large amount," "huge amount," and "especially huge amount" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 204 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 204 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) committing the act of falsely declaring export tax refund three times or more within two years, and fraudulently obtaining State tax of 300,000 yuan or more;  
(2) having been subject to criminal punishment or two or more administrative penalties for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund within five years, and again committing the act of fraudulently obtaining export tax refund, with the amount being 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) causing State tax of 300,000 yuan or more to be fraudulently obtained and unable to be recovered before the initiation of public prosecution;  
(4) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "other especially serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 204 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) committing the act of falsely declaring export tax refund five times or more within two years, or taking the fraudulent obtaining of export tax refund as a main business, and fraudulently obtaining State tax of 3,000,000 yuan or more;  
(2) having been subject to criminal punishment or two or more administrative penalties for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund within five years, and again committing the act of fraudulently obtaining export tax refund, with the amount being 3,000,000 yuan or more;  
(3) causing State tax of 3,000,000 yuan or more to be fraudulently obtained and unable to be recovered before the initiation of public prosecution;  
(4) other circumstances of an especially serious nature.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　实施骗取国家出口退税行为，没有实际取得出口退税款的，可以比照既遂犯从轻或者减轻处罚。  
从事货物运输代理、报关、会计、税务、外贸综合服务等中介组织及其人员违反国家有关进出口经营规定，为他人提供虚假证明文件，致使他人骗取国家出口退税款，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 9** Where a person commits an act of fraudulently obtaining export tax refund but does not actually obtain the export tax refund, the person may be given a lighter or mitigated punishment by reference to the completed offender.  
Where intermediary organizations such as those engaged in freight forwarding, customs declaration, accounting, taxation, or foreign trade comprehensive services, or their personnel, violate the relevant State regulations on import and export operations, provide false supporting documents to others, thereby causing others to fraudulently obtain export tax refund, and the circumstances are serious, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with Article 229 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)第一款规定的“虚开增值税专用发票或者虚开用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票”：  
（一）没有实际业务，开具增值税专用发票、用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票的；  
（二）有实际应抵扣业务，但开具超过实际应抵扣业务对应税款的增值税专用发票、用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票的；  
（三）对依法不能抵扣税款的业务，通过虚构交易主体开具增值税专用发票、用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票的；  
（四）非法篡改增值税专用发票或者用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票相关电子信息的；  
（五）违反规定以其他手段虚开的。  
为虚增业绩、融资、贷款等不以骗抵税款为目的，没有因抵扣造成税款被骗损失的，不以本罪论处，构成其他犯罪的，依法以其他犯罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 10** Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "falsely issuing special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 205 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) issuing special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction without actual business transactions;  
(2) having actual deductible business, but issuing special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction exceeding the tax amount corresponding to the actual deductible business;  
(3) for business that cannot be deducted according to law, issuing special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction through fictitious transaction subjects;  
(4) illegally tampering with electronic information of special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction;  
(5) falsely issuing by other means in violation of regulations.  
Where the issuance is for purposes such as inflating performance, financing, or obtaining loans, without the intent to defraud or deduct tax, and no loss of tax is caused by deduction, the act shall not be punished under this crime; if it constitutes another crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law under the other crime.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　虚开增值税专用发票、用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票，税款数额在十万元以上的，应当依照[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)的规定定罪处罚；虚开税款数额在五十万元以上、五百万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)第一款规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)第一款规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）在提起公诉前，无法追回的税款数额达到三十万元以上的；  
（二）五年内因虚开发票受过刑事处罚或者二次以上行政处罚，又虚开增值税专用发票或者虚开用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票，虚开税款数额在三十万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)第一款规定的“其他特别严重情节”：  
（一）在提起公诉前，无法追回的税款数额达到三百万元以上的；  
（二）五年内因虚开发票受过刑事处罚或者二次以上行政处罚，又虚开增值税专用发票或者虚开用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票，虚开税款数额在三百万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。  
以同一购销业务名义，既虚开进项增值税专用发票、用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票，又虚开销项的，以其中较大的数额计算。  
以伪造的增值税专用发票进行虚开，达到本条规定标准的，应当以虚开增值税专用发票罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 11** Where the amount of tax involved in falsely issuing special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction is 100,000 yuan or more, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 205 of the Criminal Law; where the amount of tax falsely issued is 500,000 yuan or more, or 5,000,000 yuan or more, such amounts shall be respectively deemed as "relatively large amount" and "huge amount" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 205 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 205 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of tax that cannot be recovered before the initiation of public prosecution reaches 300,000 yuan or more;  
(2) having been subject to criminal punishment or two or more administrative penalties for falsely issuing invoices within five years, and again falsely issuing special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction, with the falsely issued tax amount being 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "other especially serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 205 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of tax that cannot be recovered before the initiation of public prosecution reaches 3,000,000 yuan or more;  
(2) having been subject to criminal punishment or two or more administrative penalties for falsely issuing invoices within five years, and again falsely issuing special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction, with the falsely issued tax amount being 3,000,000 yuan or more;  
(3) other circumstances of an especially serious nature.  
Where, in the name of the same purchase or sale business, both input special VAT invoices or other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction and output invoices are falsely issued, the calculation shall be based on the larger amount.  
Where a forged special VAT invoice is used to falsely issue, and the standard prescribed in this Article is met, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of falsely issuing special VAT invoices.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)之一第一款规定的“虚开[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)规定以外的其他发票”：  
（一）没有实际业务而为他人、为自己、让他人为自己、介绍他人开具发票的；  
（二）有实际业务，但为他人、为自己、让他人为自己、介绍他人开具与实际业务的货物品名、服务名称、货物数量、金额等不符的发票的；  
（三）非法篡改发票相关电子信息的；  
（四）违反规定以其他手段虚开的。

**Article 12** Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "falsely issuing invoices other than those prescribed in Article 205 of the Criminal Law" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 205a of the Criminal Law:  
(1) issuing invoices for others, for oneself, or requesting others to issue for oneself, or introducing others to issue invoices, without actual business transactions;  
(2) having actual business transactions, but issuing invoices for others, for oneself, or requesting others to issue for oneself, or introducing others to issue invoices, which do not match the actual business in terms of the name of goods, service name, quantity of goods, amount, etc.;  
(3) illegally tampering with electronic information of invoices;  
(4) falsely issuing by other means in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)之一第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）虚开发票票面金额五十万元以上的；  
（二）虚开发票一百份以上且票面金额三十万元以上的；  
（三）五年内因虚开发票受过刑事处罚或者二次以上行政处罚，又虚开发票，票面金额达到第一、二项规定的标准60%以上的。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-205)之一第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）虚开发票票面金额二百五十万元以上的；  
（二）虚开发票五百份以上且票面金额一百五十万元以上的；  
（三）五年内因虚开发票受过刑事处罚或者二次以上行政处罚，又虚开发票，票面金额达到第一、二项规定的标准60%以上的。  
以伪造的发票进行虚开，达到本条第一款规定的标准的，应当以虚开发票罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 13** Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 205a of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the face amount of falsely issued invoices is 500,000 yuan or more;  
(2) 100 or more invoices are falsely issued and the face amount is 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) having been subject to criminal punishment or two or more administrative penalties for falsely issuing invoices within five years, and again falsely issuing invoices, with the face amount reaching 60% or more of the standard prescribed in items (1) or (2).  
Where a taxpayer falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 205a of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the face amount of falsely issued invoices is 2,500,000 yuan or more;  
(2) 500 or more invoices are falsely issued and the face amount is 1,500,000 yuan or more;  
(3) having been subject to criminal punishment or two or more administrative penalties for falsely issuing invoices within five years, and again falsely issuing invoices, with the face amount reaching 60% or more of the standard prescribed in items (1) or (2).  
Where a forged invoice is used to falsely issue and the standard prescribed in paragraph 1 of this Article is met, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of falsely issuing invoices.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第二百零六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-206)的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）票面税额十万元以上的；  
（二）伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票十份以上且票面税额六万元以上的；  
（三）违法所得一万元以上的。  
伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票票面税额五十万元以上的，或者五十份以上且票面税额三十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-206)第一款规定的“数量较大”。  
五年内因伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票受过刑事处罚或者二次以上行政处罚，又实施伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票行为，票面税额达到本条第二款规定的标准60%以上的，或者违法所得五万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-206)第一款规定的“其他严重情节”。  
伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票票面税额五百万元以上的，或者五百份以上且票面税额三百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-206)第一款规定的“数量巨大”。  
五年内因伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票受过刑事处罚或者二次以上行政处罚，又实施伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票行为，票面税额达到本条第四款规定的标准60%以上的，或者违法所得五十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-206)第一款规定的“其他特别严重情节”。  
伪造并出售同一增值税专用发票的，以伪造、出售伪造的增值税专用发票罪论处，数量不重复计算。  
变造增值税专用发票的，按照伪造增值税专用发票论处。

**Article 14** Where a person forges or sells forged special VAT invoices and falls under any of the following circumstances, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 206 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the tax amount on the face is 100,000 yuan or more;  
(2) 10 or more forged special VAT invoices are forged or sold and the tax amount on the face is 60,000 yuan or more;  
(3) the illegal income is 10,000 yuan or more.  
Where the tax amount on the face of forged or sold forged special VAT invoices is 500,000 yuan or more, or 50 or more invoices are forged or sold and the tax amount on the face is 300,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "relatively large quantity" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 206 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person, having been subject to criminal punishment or two or more administrative penalties for forging or selling forged special VAT invoices within five years, again commits the act of forging or selling forged special VAT invoices, and the tax amount on the face reaches 60% or more of the standard prescribed in paragraph 2 of this Article, or the illegal income is 50,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 206 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the tax amount on the face of forged or sold forged special VAT invoices is 5,000,000 yuan or more, or 500 or more invoices are forged or sold and the tax amount on the face is 3,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "huge quantity" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 206 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person, having been subject to criminal punishment or two or more administrative penalties for forging or selling forged special VAT invoices within five years, again commits the act of forging or selling forged special VAT invoices, and the tax amount on the face reaches 60% or more of the standard prescribed in paragraph 4 of this Article, or the illegal income is 500,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "other especially serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 206 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person both forges and sells the same forged special VAT invoice, the person shall be punished for the crime of forging and selling forged special VAT invoices, and the quantity shall not be counted repeatedly.  
Where a person alters a special VAT invoice, it shall be treated as forging a special VAT invoice.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　非法出售增值税专用发票的，依照[本解释第十四条](#art-14)的定罪量刑标准定罪处罚。

**Article 15** Where a person illegally sells special VAT invoices, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the standards for conviction and sentencing prescribed in Article 14 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　非法购买增值税专用发票或者购买伪造的增值税专用发票票面税额二十万元以上的，或者二十份以上且票面税额十万元以上的，应当依照[刑法第二百零八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-208)第一款的规定定罪处罚。  
非法购买真、伪两种增值税专用发票的，数额累计计算，不实行数罪并罚。  
购买伪造的增值税专用发票又出售的，以出售伪造的增值税专用发票罪定罪处罚；非法购买增值税专用发票用于骗取抵扣税款或者骗取出口退税款，同时构成非法购买增值税专用发票罪与虚开增值税专用发票罪、骗取出口退税罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 16** Where a person illegally purchases special VAT invoices or purchases forged special VAT invoices, and the tax amount on the face is 200,000 yuan or more, or 20 or more invoices are purchased and the tax amount on the face is 100,000 yuan or more, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 208 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person illegally purchases both genuine and forged special VAT invoices, the amounts shall be calculated cumulatively, and combined punishment for multiple crimes shall not be imposed.  
Where a person purchases forged special VAT invoices and then sells them, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of selling forged special VAT invoices; where a person illegally purchases special VAT invoices for the purpose of fraudulently obtaining tax deduction or export tax refund, and the act simultaneously constitutes the crime of illegally purchasing special VAT invoices and the crime of falsely issuing special VAT invoices or the crime of fraudulently obtaining export tax refund, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造的用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第二百零九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-209)第一款的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）票面可以退税、抵扣税额十万元以上的；  
（二）伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造的发票十份以上且票面可以退税、抵扣税额六万元以上的；  
（三）违法所得一万元以上的。  
伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造的可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票票面可以退税、抵扣税额五十万元以上的，或者五十份以上且票面可以退税、抵扣税额三十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-209)第一款规定的“数量巨大”；伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造的可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票票面可以退税、抵扣税额五百万元以上的，或者五百份以上且票面可以退税、抵扣税额三百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-209)第一款规定的“数量特别巨大”。  
伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造[刑法第二百零九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-209)第二款规定的发票，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照该款的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）票面金额五十万元以上的；  
（二）伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造发票一百份以上且票面金额三十万元以上的；  
（三）违法所得一万元以上的。  
伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造[刑法第二百零九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-209)第二款规定的发票，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“情节严重”：  
（一）票面金额二百五十万元以上的；  
（二）伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造发票五百份以上且票面金额一百五十万元以上的；  
（三）违法所得五万元以上的。  
非法出售用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票的，定罪量刑标准依照本条第一、二款的规定执行。  
非法出售增值税专用发票、用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票以外的发票的，定罪量刑标准依照本条第三、四款的规定执行。

**Article 17** Where a person forges, manufactures without authorization, or sells forged or unauthorized manufactured other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 209 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of tax refund or tax deduction that can be obtained from the face of the invoices is 100,000 yuan or more;  
(2) 10 or more invoices are forged, manufactured without authorization, or sold and the amount of tax refund or tax deduction that can be obtained from the face is 60,000 yuan or more;  
(3) the illegal income is 10,000 yuan or more.  
Where the amount of tax refund or tax deduction that can be obtained from the face of forged, unauthorized manufactured, or sold other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction is 500,000 yuan or more, or 50 or more invoices are involved and the amount is 300,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "huge quantity" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 209 of the Criminal Law; where the amount is 5,000,000 yuan or more, or 500 or more invoices are involved and the amount is 3,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "especially huge quantity" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 209 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person forges, manufactures without authorization, or sells forged or unauthorized manufactured invoices as prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 209 of the Criminal Law, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with that paragraph:  
(1) the face amount is 500,000 yuan or more;  
(2) 100 or more invoices are forged, manufactured without authorization, or sold and the face amount is 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) the illegal income is 10,000 yuan or more.  
Where a person forges, manufactures without authorization, or sells forged or unauthorized manufactured invoices as prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 209 of the Criminal Law, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances":  
(1) the face amount is 2,500,000 yuan or more;  
(2) 500 or more invoices are forged, manufactured without authorization, or sold and the face amount is 1,500,000 yuan or more;  
(3) the illegal income is 50,000 yuan or more.  
Where a person illegally sells other invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction, the standards for conviction and sentencing shall be implemented in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article.  
Where a person illegally sells invoices other than special VAT invoices and invoices for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction, the standards for conviction and sentencing shall be implemented in accordance with paragraphs 3 and 4 of this Article.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-210)之一第一款规定的“数量较大”：  
（一）持有伪造的增值税专用发票或者可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票票面税额五十万元以上的；或者五十份以上且票面税额二十五万元以上的；  
（二）持有伪造的前项规定以外的其他发票票面金额一百万元以上的，或者一百份以上且票面金额五十万元以上的。  
持有的伪造发票数量、票面税额或者票面金额达到前款规定的标准五倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-210)之一第一款规定的“数量巨大”。

**Article 18** Where a person falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "relatively large quantity" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 210a of the Criminal Law:  
(1) possessing forged special VAT invoices or other invoices that can be used for fraudulently obtaining export tax refund or tax deduction, with a face tax amount of 500,000 yuan or more, or with 50 or more such invoices and a face tax amount of 250,000 yuan or more;  
(2) possessing forged invoices other than those specified in the preceding item, with a face amount of 1,000,000 yuan or more, or with 100 or more such invoices and a face amount of 500,000 yuan or more.  
Where the quantity, face tax amount, or face amount of possessed forged invoices reaches five times the standard prescribed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be deemed as "huge quantity" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 210a of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　明知他人实施危害税收征管犯罪而仍为其提供账号、资信证明或者其他帮助的，以相应犯罪的共犯论处。

**Article 19** Where a person, knowing that others are committing crimes harmful to tax collection and management, still provides them with accounts, credit certificates, or other assistance, the person shall be punished as an accomplice to the corresponding crime.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　单位实施危害税收征管犯罪的定罪量刑标准，依照本解释规定的标准执行。

**Article 20** The standards for conviction and sentencing of a unit that commits a crime harmful to tax collection and management shall be implemented in accordance with the standards prescribed in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　实施危害税收征管犯罪，造成国家税款损失，行为人补缴税款、挽回税收损失，有效合规整改的，可以从宽处罚；犯罪情节轻微不需要判处刑罚的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。  
对于实施本解释规定的相关行为被不起诉或者免予刑事处罚，需要给予行政处罚、政务处分或者其他处分的，依法移送有关主管机关处理。有关主管机关应当将处理结果及时通知人民检察院、人民法院。

**Article 21** Where a person commits a crime harmful to tax collection and management, causing losses to State tax revenue, and the actor pays the overdue tax, recovers the tax loss, and conducts effective compliance rectification, the person may be given a lenient punishment; if the circumstances of the crime are minor and a criminal punishment is not necessary, the person may not be prosecuted or may be exempted from criminal punishment; if the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not serious, the act shall not be treated as a crime.  
Where a person is not prosecuted or is exempted from criminal punishment for committing the relevant acts prescribed in this Interpretation, and administrative penalties, government disciplinary sanctions, or other sanctions need to be imposed, the matter shall be transferred to the relevant competent authority for handling according to law. The relevant competent authority shall promptly notify the People's Procuratorate and the People's Court of the handling result.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　本解释自2024年3月20日起施行。《最高人民法院关于适用〈全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于惩治虚开、伪造和非法出售增值税专用发票犯罪的决定〉的若干问题的解释》（法发〔1996〕30号）、《最高人民法院关于审理骗取出口退税刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2002〕30号）、《最高人民法院关于审理偷税、抗税刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2002〕33号）同时废止；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 22** This Interpretation shall come into force on March 20, 2024. The Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Application of the "Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Punishing Crimes of Falsely Issuing, Forging, and Illegally Selling Special VAT Invoices" (Fa Fa [1996] No. 30), the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Fraudulently Obtaining Export Tax Refund (Fa Shi [2002] No. 30), and the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Tax Evasion and Tax Resistance (Fa Shi [2002] No. 33) shall be abolished simultaneously; where judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
