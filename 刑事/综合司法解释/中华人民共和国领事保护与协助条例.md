# 中华人民共和国领事保护与协助条例

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2023-07-09  
**生效日期**：2023-09-01

> 第五条　外交部建立公开的热线电话和网络平台，驻外外交机构对外公布办公地址和联系方式，受理涉及领事保护与协助的咨询和求助。 中国公民、法人、非法人组织请求领事保护与协助时，应当向驻外外交机构提供能够证明其身份的文件或者相关信息。 第六条　在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织可以在外交部或者驻外外交机构建立的信息登记平台上预先登记基本信息，便于驻外外交机构对其提供领事保护与协助。 国务院有关部门、驻外外交机构根据领事保护与协助的需要依法共享在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织有关信息，并做好信息保护工作。 第七条　驻外外交机构应当在履责区域内履行领事保护与协助职责；特殊情况下，经驻在国同意，可以临时在履责区域外执行领事保护与协助职责；经第三国同意，可以在该第三国执行领事保护与协助职责。 第八条　在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织因正当权益被侵犯向驻外外交机构求助的，驻外外交机构应当根据相关情形向其提供维护自身正当权益的渠道和建议，向驻在国有关部门核实情况，敦促依法公正妥善处理，并提供协助。 第九条　获知在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织因涉嫌违法犯罪被驻在国采取相关措施的，驻外外交机构应当根据相关情形向驻在国有关部门了解核实情况，要求依法公正妥善处理。 前款中的中国公民被拘留、逮捕、监禁或者以其他方式被驻在国限制人身自由的，驻外外交机构应当根据相关情形，按照驻在国法律和我国与驻在国缔结或者共同参加的国际条约对其进行探视或者与其联络，了解其相关需求，要求驻在国有关部门给予该中国公民人道主义待遇和公正待遇。 第十条　获知驻在国审理涉及中国公民、法人、非法人组织的案件的，驻外外交机构可以按照驻在国法律和我国与驻在国缔结或者共同参加的国际条约进行旁听，并要求驻在国有关部门根据驻在国法律保障其诉讼权利。 第十一条　获知在国外的中国公民需要监护但生活处于无人照料状态的，驻外外交机构应当向驻在国有关部门通报情况，敦促依法妥善处理。情况紧急的，驻外外交机构应当协调有关方面给予必要的临时生活照料。 驻外外交机构应当将有关情况及时通知该中国公民的亲属或者国内住所地省级人民政府。接到通知的省级人民政府应当将有关情况及时逐级通知到该中国公民住所地的居民委员会、村民委员会或者民政部门。驻外外交机构和地方人民政府应当为有关人员或者组织履行监护职责提供协助。 第十二条　在国外的中国公民因基本生活保障出现困难向驻外外交机构求助的，驻外外交机构应当为其联系亲友、获取救济等提供协助。 第十三条　在国外的中国公民下落不明，其亲属向驻外外交机构求助的，驻外外交机构应当提供当地报警方式及其他获取救助的信息。 驻在国警方立案的，驻外外交机构应当敦促驻在国警方及时妥善处理。 第十四条　获知在国外的中国公民因治安刑事案件、自然灾害、意外事故等受伤的，驻外外交机构应当根据相关情形向驻在国有关部门了解核实情况，敦促开展紧急救助和医疗救治，要求依法公正妥善处理。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织可以在外交部或者驻外外交机构建立的信息登记平台上预先登记基本信息，便于驻外外交机构对其提供领事保护与协助。  
国务院有关部门、驻外外交机构根据领事保护与协助的需要依法共享在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织有关信息，并做好信息保护工作。

**Article 6** Chinese citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations abroad may pre-register basic information on the information registration platform established by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the diplomatic missions, so as to facilitate the diplomatic missions in providing them with consular protection and assistance.  
The relevant departments of the State Council and the diplomatic missions shall, according to the need for consular protection and assistance, share information concerning Chinese citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations abroad in accordance with the law, and shall protect such information.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　驻外外交机构应当在履责区域内履行领事保护与协助职责；特殊情况下，经驻在国同意，可以临时在履责区域外执行领事保护与协助职责；经第三国同意，可以在该第三国执行领事保护与协助职责。

**Article 7** The diplomatic missions shall perform the duties of consular protection and assistance within their respective areas of responsibility; under special circumstances and with the consent of the host country, they may temporarily perform the duties of consular protection and assistance outside their areas of responsibility; and with the consent of a third country, they may perform the duties of consular protection and assistance in that third country.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织因正当权益被侵犯向驻外外交机构求助的，驻外外交机构应当根据相关情形向其提供维护自身正当权益的渠道和建议，向驻在国有关部门核实情况，敦促依法公正妥善处理，并提供协助。

**Article 8** Where a Chinese citizen, legal person, or unincorporated organization abroad seeks assistance from the diplomatic missions because its legitimate rights and interests have been infringed upon, the diplomatic missions shall, according to the relevant circumstances, provide them with channels and suggestions for safeguarding their legitimate rights and interests, verify the situation with the relevant departments of the host country, urge them to handle the matter fairly and properly in accordance with the law, and provide assistance.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　获知在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织因涉嫌违法犯罪被驻在国采取相关措施的，驻外外交机构应当根据相关情形向驻在国有关部门了解核实情况，要求依法公正妥善处理。  
前款中的中国公民被拘留、逮捕、监禁或者以其他方式被驻在国限制人身自由的，驻外外交机构应当根据相关情形，按照驻在国法律和我国与驻在国缔结或者共同参加的国际条约对其进行探视或者与其联络，了解其相关需求，要求驻在国有关部门给予该中国公民人道主义待遇和公正待遇。

**Article 9** Where the diplomatic missions learn that Chinese citizens, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations abroad have been subjected to relevant measures by the host country on suspicion of committing illegal acts or crimes, the diplomatic missions shall, according to the relevant circumstances, inquire about and verify the situation with the relevant departments of the host country, and request that the matter be handled fairly and properly in accordance with the law.  
Where a Chinese citizen mentioned in the preceding paragraph is detained, arrested, imprisoned, or otherwise restricted in personal freedom by the host country, the diplomatic missions shall, according to the relevant circumstances, visit or communicate with such Chinese citizen in accordance with the laws of the host country and the international treaties concluded between China and the host country or to which both are parties, ascertain their relevant needs, and request the relevant departments of the host country to grant such Chinese citizen humanitarian treatment and fair treatment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　获知驻在国审理涉及中国公民、法人、非法人组织的案件的，驻外外交机构可以按照驻在国法律和我国与驻在国缔结或者共同参加的国际条约进行旁听，并要求驻在国有关部门根据驻在国法律保障其诉讼权利。

**Article 10** Where the diplomatic missions learn that the host country is trying a case involving Chinese citizens, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations, the diplomatic missions may, in accordance with the laws of the host country and the international treaties concluded between China and the host country or to which both are parties, attend the trial as observers, and request the relevant departments of the host country to guarantee their litigation rights in accordance with the laws of the host country.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　获知在国外的中国公民需要监护但生活处于无人照料状态的，驻外外交机构应当向驻在国有关部门通报情况，敦促依法妥善处理。情况紧急的，驻外外交机构应当协调有关方面给予必要的临时生活照料。  
驻外外交机构应当将有关情况及时通知该中国公民的亲属或者国内住所地省级人民政府。接到通知的省级人民政府应当将有关情况及时逐级通知到该中国公民住所地的居民委员会、村民委员会或者民政部门。驻外外交机构和地方人民政府应当为有关人员或者组织履行监护职责提供协助。

**Article 11** Where the diplomatic missions learn that a Chinese citizen abroad needs guardianship but is in a state of no one taking care of him or her, the diplomatic missions shall inform the relevant departments of the host country of the situation and urge them to handle the matter properly in accordance with the law. In an emergency, the diplomatic missions shall coordinate relevant parties to provide necessary temporary life care.  
The diplomatic missions shall promptly notify the relatives of such Chinese citizen or the people's government of the province where his or her domicile in China is located of the relevant situation. The provincial people's government that receives the notification shall promptly notify the residents' committee, villagers' committee, or civil affairs department of the place where such Chinese citizen's domicile is located, at each level in turn. The diplomatic missions and local people's governments shall provide assistance to relevant persons or organizations in performing guardianship duties.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在国外的中国公民因基本生活保障出现困难向驻外外交机构求助的，驻外外交机构应当为其联系亲友、获取救济等提供协助。

**Article 12** Where a Chinese citizen abroad seeks assistance from the diplomatic missions because of difficulties in basic living security, the diplomatic missions shall provide assistance in contacting relatives or obtaining relief for him or her.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　在国外的中国公民下落不明，其亲属向驻外外交机构求助的，驻外外交机构应当提供当地报警方式及其他获取救助的信息。  
驻在国警方立案的，驻外外交机构应当敦促驻在国警方及时妥善处理。

**Article 13** Where a Chinese citizen abroad is missing and his or her relatives seek assistance from the diplomatic missions, the diplomatic missions shall provide information on local police reporting methods and other means of obtaining assistance.  
Where the police of the host country have opened a case, the diplomatic missions shall urge the police of the host country to handle the matter promptly and properly.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　获知在国外的中国公民因治安刑事案件、自然灾害、意外事故等受伤的，驻外外交机构应当根据相关情形向驻在国有关部门了解核实情况，敦促开展紧急救助和医疗救治，要求依法公正妥善处理。  
中国公民因前款所列情形死亡的，驻外外交机构应当为死者近亲属按照驻在国有关规定处理善后事宜提供协助，告知死者近亲属当地关于遗体、遗物处理时限等规定，要求驻在国有关部门依法公正处理并妥善保管遗体、遗物。

**Article 14** Where the diplomatic missions learn that a Chinese citizen abroad is injured due to criminal cases, natural disasters, accidents, or other causes, the diplomatic missions shall, according to the relevant circumstances, inquire about and verify the situation with the relevant departments of the host country, urge them to carry out emergency rescue and medical treatment, and request that the matter be handled fairly and properly in accordance with the law.  
Where a Chinese citizen dies due to the circumstances listed in the preceding paragraph, the diplomatic missions shall provide assistance to the close relatives of the deceased in handling the funeral and other related matters in accordance with the relevant provisions of the host country, inform the close relatives of the deceased of the local regulations on the time limits for handling the remains and belongings, and request the relevant departments of the host country to handle the matter in accordance with the law and properly preserve the remains and belongings.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　驻在国发生战争、武装冲突、暴乱、严重自然灾害、重大事故灾难、重大传染病疫情、恐怖袭击等重大突发事件，在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织因人身财产安全受到威胁需要帮助的，驻外外交机构应当及时核实情况，敦促驻在国采取有效措施保护中国公民、法人、非法人组织的人身财产安全，并根据相关情形提供协助。  
确有必要且条件具备的，外交部和驻外外交机构应当联系、协调驻在国及国内有关方面为在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织提供有关协助，有关部门和地方人民政府应当积极履行相应职责。

**Article 15** Where major emergencies such as war, armed conflict, riot, severe natural disaster, major accident disaster, major infectious disease epidemic, or terrorist attack occur in the host country, and Chinese citizens, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations abroad need assistance because their personal and property safety is threatened, the diplomatic missions shall promptly verify the situation, urge the host country to take effective measures to protect the personal and property safety of such Chinese citizens, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations, and provide assistance according to the relevant circumstances.  
Where it is truly necessary and conditions permit, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the diplomatic missions shall contact and coordinate with relevant parties of the host country and domestic parties to provide relevant assistance to Chinese citizens, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations abroad, and the relevant departments and local people's governments shall actively perform their corresponding duties.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　驻外外交机构应当了解驻在国当地法律服务、翻译、医疗、殡葬等机构的信息，在中国公民、法人、非法人组织需要时提供咨询。

**Article 16** Diplomatic missions abroad shall keep themselves informed of information on local legal services, translation, medical care, funeral and interment institutions in the host country, and shall provide consultation when Chinese citizens, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations need it.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织因与中介机构、旅游经营者、运输机构等产生纠纷向驻外外交机构求助的，驻外外交机构应当根据具体情况向其提供依法维护自身正当权益的有关信息和建议。

**Article 17** Where Chinese citizens, legal persons, or unincorporated organizations abroad seek assistance from a diplomatic mission abroad due to disputes arising from intermediaries, tour operators, transport agencies, or similar entities, the diplomatic mission abroad shall, based on the specific circumstances, provide them with relevant information and advice on safeguarding their legitimate rights and interests according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　驻外外交机构应当结合当地安全形势、法律环境、风俗习惯等情况，建立领事保护与协助工作安全预警和应急处置机制，开展安全风险评估，对履责区域内的中国公民、法人、非法人组织进行安全宣传，指导其开展突发事件应对、日常安全保护等工作。  
在国外的中国法人、非法人组织应当根据所在国的安全形势，建立安全防范和应急处置机制，保障有关经费，加强安全防范教育和应急知识培训，根据需要设立专门安全管理机构、配备人员。

**Article 18** Diplomatic missions abroad shall, taking into account the local security situation, legal environment, customs, and other relevant factors, establish a security early warning and emergency response mechanism for consular protection and assistance, conduct security risk assessments, carry out safety publicity among Chinese citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations within their areas of responsibility, and guide them in handling emergencies and conducting routine safety protection.  
Chinese legal persons and unincorporated organizations abroad shall establish security prevention and emergency response mechanisms based on the security situation of the host country, ensure relevant funding, strengthen safety prevention education and emergency knowledge training, and, where necessary, set up dedicated security management bodies and assign personnel.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　外交部和驻外外交机构应当密切关注有关国家和地区社会治安、自然灾害、事故灾难、传染病疫情等安全形势，根据情况公开发布国外安全提醒。国外安全提醒的级别划分和发布程序，由外交部制定。  
国务院文化和旅游主管部门会同外交部建立国外旅游目的地安全风险提示机制，根据国外安全提醒，公开发布旅游目的地安全风险提示。  
国务院有关部门和地方人民政府结合国外安全提醒，根据各自职责提醒有关中国公民、法人、非法人组织在当地做好安全防范、避免前往及驻留高风险国家或者地区。

**Article 19** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and diplomatic missions abroad shall closely monitor the security situation of the relevant countries and regions, including social order, natural disasters, accidents, and infectious disease epidemics, and shall, based on the circumstances, publicly issue overseas security alerts. The classification levels and issuance procedures for overseas security alerts shall be formulated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.  
The competent department of culture and tourism under the State Council shall, jointly with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, establish a security risk alert mechanism for overseas tourist destinations, and, based on overseas security alerts, publicly issue security risk alerts for tourist destinations.  
The relevant departments under the State Council and local people's governments shall, in light of overseas security alerts and within their respective responsibilities, remind the relevant Chinese citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations to take safety precautions locally and to avoid traveling to or staying in high-risk countries or regions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　国务院有关部门根据各自职责开展国外安全的宣传、教育培训工作，提高相关行业和人员国外安全风险防范水平，着重提高在国外留学、旅游、经商、务工等人员的安全意识和风险防范能力。  
地方人民政府结合本地区在国外的中国公民、法人、非法人组织状况，加强对重点地区和群体的安全宣传及对有关人员的培训。

**Article 20** The relevant departments under the State Council shall, within their respective responsibilities, carry out publicity, education, and training on overseas security, enhance the level of risk prevention in relevant industries and among relevant personnel, and focus on improving the safety awareness and risk prevention capabilities of persons studying, traveling, conducting business, or working abroad.  
Local people's governments shall, taking into account the situation of Chinese citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations from their region who are abroad, strengthen safety publicity for key areas and groups, as well as training for relevant personnel.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　有关中国公民、法人、非法人组织应当积极关注安全提醒，根据安全提醒要求，在当地做好安全防范、避免前往及驻留高风险国家或者地区。  
经营出国旅游业务的旅行社应当关注国外安全提醒和旅游目的地安全风险提示，通过出行前告知等方式，就目的地国家或者地区存在的安全风险，向旅游者作出真实说明和明确提示；通过网络平台销售的，应当在显著位置标明有关风险。

**Article 21** The relevant Chinese citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations shall actively pay attention to security alerts and, in accordance with the requirements of such alerts, take safety precautions locally and avoid traveling to or staying in high-risk countries or regions.  
Travel agencies operating outbound tourism business shall pay attention to overseas security alerts and security risk alerts for tourist destinations, and shall, through pre-trip notifications or other means, make truthful explanations and clear warnings to tourists regarding the security risks existing in the destination country or region; where selling through online platforms, they shall clearly indicate the relevant risks in a prominent position.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　国家为领事保护与协助工作提供人员、资金等保障。  
地方人民政府参与领事保护与协助相关工作的经费纳入预算管理。  
有外派人员的国内企业用于国外安全保障的投入纳入企业成本费用。

**Article 22** The State shall provide personnel, funding, and other support for consular protection and assistance.  
The expenses incurred by local people's governments in participating in work related to consular protection and assistance shall be included in budgetary management.  
The investments made by domestic enterprises with dispatched personnel for overseas security protection shall be included in the enterprise's costs and expenses.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　驻外外交机构根据领事保护与协助工作实际需要，经外交部批准，可以聘用人员从事辅助性工作。  
外交部和驻外外交机构根据工作职责要求，对从事领事保护与协助工作的驻外外交人员及其他人员进行培训。

**Article 23** Diplomatic missions abroad may, based on the actual needs of consular protection and assistance work and with the approval of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, employ personnel to perform auxiliary tasks.  
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and diplomatic missions abroad shall, according to the requirements of their work duties, provide training to diplomatic personnel abroad and other personnel engaged in consular protection and assistance work.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　国家鼓励有关组织和个人为领事保护与协助工作提供志愿服务。  
国家鼓励和支持保险公司、紧急救援机构、律师事务所等社会力量参与领事保护与协助相关工作。

**Article 24** The State encourages relevant organizations and individuals to provide volunteer services for consular protection and assistance.  
The State encourages and supports social forces such as insurance companies, emergency rescue agencies, and law firms to participate in work related to consular protection and assistance.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　对在领事保护与协助工作中作出突出贡献的组织和个人，按照国家有关规定给予表彰、奖励。

**Article 25** Organizations and individuals that make outstanding contributions in consular protection and assistance work shall be commended and rewarded in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　中国公民、法人、非法人组织在领事保护与协助过程中，得到第三方提供的食宿、交通、医疗等物资和服务的，应当支付应由其自身承担的费用。

**Article 26** Chinese citizens, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that receive materials and services such as accommodation, transportation, and medical care provided by a third party during consular protection and assistance shall pay the expenses that shall be borne by themselves.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　本条例自2023年9月1日起施行。

**Article 27** These Regulations shall come into force as of September 1, 2023.
