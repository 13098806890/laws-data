# 外国人在中华人民共和国收养子女登记办法

**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2024-12-06  
**生效日期**：2025-01-20

> （1999年5月12日国务院批准　1999年5月25日民政部令第15号发布　根据2024年12月6日《国务院关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定》修订） 第一条　为了规范涉外收养登记行为，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》（以下简称民法典），制定本办法。 第二条　外国人在中华人民共和国境内收养子女（以下简称外国人在华收养子女），应当依照本办法办理登记。 收养人夫妻一方为外国人，在华收养子女，也应当依照本办法办理登记。 第三条　外国人在华收养子女，应当符合中国有关收养法律的规定，并应当符合收养人所在国有关收养法律的规定；因收养人所在国法律的规定与中国法律的规定不一致而产生的问题，由两国政府有关部门协商处理。 第四条　外国人在华收养子女，应当通过所在国政府或者政府委托的收养组织（以下简称外国收养组织）向中国政府委托的收养组织（以下简称中国收养组织）转交收养申请并提交收养人的家庭情况报告和证明。 前款规定的收养人的收养申请、家庭情况报告和证明，是指由其所在国有权机构出具，经其所在国外交机关或者外交机关授权的机构认证，并经中华人民共和国驻该国使馆或者领馆认证的，或者履行中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约规定的证明手续的下列文件： （一）跨国收养申请书； （二）出生证明； （三）婚姻状况证明； （四）职业、经济收入和财产状况证明； （五）身体健康检查证明； （六）有无受过刑事处罚的证明； （七）收养人所在国主管机关同意其跨国收养子女的证明； （八）家庭情况报告，包括收养人的身份、收养的合格性和适当性、家庭状况和病史、收养动机以及适合于照顾儿童的特点等。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了规范涉外收养登记行为，根据《中华人民共和国民法典》（以下简称民法典），制定本办法。

**Article 1** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the Civil Code) for the purpose of regulating the registration of foreign-related adoption.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　外国人在中华人民共和国境内收养子女（以下简称外国人在华收养子女），应当依照本办法办理登记。  
收养人夫妻一方为外国人，在华收养子女，也应当依照本办法办理登记。

**Article 2** Aliens who adopt children within the territory of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as foreigners adopting children in China) shall go through the registration formalities in accordance with these Measures.  
Where one of the spouses of the adopter is an alien and the spouses adopt a child in China, they shall also go through the registration formalities in accordance with these Measures.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　外国人在华收养子女，应当符合中国有关收养法律的规定，并应当符合收养人所在国有关收养法律的规定；因收养人所在国法律的规定与中国法律的规定不一致而产生的问题，由两国政府有关部门协商处理。

**Article 3** Foreigners adopting children in China shall comply with the provisions of the relevant Chinese laws on adoption and shall also comply with the provisions of the relevant laws of the adopter's home country on adoption; issues arising from inconsistencies between the provisions of the laws of the adopter's home country and the provisions of Chinese laws shall be resolved through consultation between the relevant departments of the two governments.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　外国人在华收养子女，应当通过所在国政府或者政府委托的收养组织（以下简称外国收养组织）向中国政府委托的收养组织（以下简称中国收养组织）转交收养申请并提交收养人的家庭情况报告和证明。  
前款规定的收养人的收养申请、家庭情况报告和证明，是指由其所在国有权机构出具，经其所在国外交机关或者外交机关授权的机构认证，并经中华人民共和国驻该国使馆或者领馆认证的，或者履行中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约规定的证明手续的下列文件：  
（一）跨国收养申请书；  
（二）出生证明；  
（三）婚姻状况证明；  
（四）职业、经济收入和财产状况证明；  
（五）身体健康检查证明；  
（六）有无受过刑事处罚的证明；  
（七）收养人所在国主管机关同意其跨国收养子女的证明；  
（八）家庭情况报告，包括收养人的身份、收养的合格性和适当性、家庭状况和病史、收养动机以及适合于照顾儿童的特点等。  
在华工作或者学习连续居住一年以上的外国人在华收养子女，应当提交前款规定的除身体健康检查证明以外的文件，并应当提交在华所在单位或者有关部门出具的婚姻状况证明，职业、经济收入或者财产状况证明，有无受过刑事处罚证明以及县级以上医疗机构出具的身体健康检查证明。

**Article 4** Foreigners adopting children in China shall submit the adoption application and the family report and certificates of the adopter to the adoption organization entrusted by the Chinese government (hereinafter referred to as the China Adoption Organization) through the government of the adopter's home country or the adoption organization entrusted by such government (hereinafter referred to as the foreign adoption organization).  
The adoption application, family report and certificates of the adopter as prescribed in the preceding paragraph refer to the following documents which are issued by the competent authorities of the adopter's home country, authenticated by the foreign affairs authority of the adopter's home country or an institution authorized by the said foreign affairs authority, and further authenticated by the embassy or consulate of the People's Republic of China in that country, or which comply with the certification procedures provided for in international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China:  
(1) an application form for intercountry adoption;  
(2) a birth certificate;  
(3) a certificate of marital status;  
(4) certificates of occupation, income and property status;  
(5) a certificate of physical health examination;  
(6) a certificate of no criminal record;  
(7) a certificate from the competent authority of the adopter's home country approving the intercountry adoption of the child;  
(8) a family report, including the identity of the adopter, the eligibility and appropriateness of the adoption, the family situation and medical history, the adoption motive, and the characteristics suitable for caring for children.  
Where a foreigner who has lived continuously in China for more than one year for work or study adopts a child in China, he or she shall submit the documents specified in the preceding paragraph except the certificate of physical health examination, and shall also submit a certificate of marital status, certificates of occupation, income or property status, a certificate of no criminal record issued by the unit where he or she works or studies in China or relevant authorities, and a certificate of physical health examination issued by a medical institution at or above the county level.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　送养人应当向省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门提交本人的居民户口簿和居民身份证（社会福利机构作送养人的，应当提交其负责人的身份证件）、被收养人的户籍证明等情况证明，并根据不同情况提交下列有关证明材料：  
（一）被收养人的生父母（包括已经离婚的）为送养人的，应当提交生父母有特殊困难无力抚养的证明和生父母双方同意送养的书面意见；其中，被收养人的生父或者生母因丧偶或者一方下落不明，由单方送养的，并应当提交配偶死亡或者下落不明的证明以及死亡的或者下落不明的配偶的父母不行使优先抚养权的书面声明；  
（二）被收养人的父母均不具备完全民事行为能力，由被收养人的其他监护人作送养人的，应当提交被收养人的父母不具备完全民事行为能力且对被收养人有严重危害的证明以及监护人有监护权的证明；  
（三）被收养人的父母均已死亡，由被收养人的监护人作送养人的，应当提交其生父母的死亡证明、监护人实际承担监护责任的证明，以及其他有抚养义务的人同意送养的书面意见；  
（四）由社会福利机构作送养人的，应当提交弃婴、儿童被遗弃和发现的情况证明以及查找其父母或者其他监护人的情况证明；被收养人是孤儿的，应当提交孤儿父母的死亡或者宣告死亡证明，以及有抚养孤儿义务的其他人同意送养的书面意见。  
送养残疾儿童的，还应当提交县级以上医疗机构出具的该儿童的残疾证明。

**Article 5** The person placing out the child for adoption shall submit his or her own residence booklet and resident identity card (where a social welfare institution acts as the person placing out the child for adoption, it shall submit the identity document of its responsible person), the child's household registration certificate and other certificates, and shall also submit the following relevant certifying documents according to different circumstances:  
(1) where the child's birth parents (including those who have been divorced) are the persons placing out the child for adoption, they shall submit a certificate proving that the birth parents have special difficulties and are unable to raise the child, and a written opinion of both birth parents consenting to the placement; where the child's birth father or mother unilaterally places out the child for adoption due to the death of the spouse or the whereabouts of the other spouse being unknown, a certificate of the spouse's death or whereabouts being unknown and a written declaration by the parents of the deceased or missing spouse that they will not exercise the right of priority in raising the child shall also be submitted;  
(2) where both parents of the child do not have full capacity for civil conduct and another guardian of the child acts as the person placing out the child for adoption, a certificate proving that the child's parents do not have full capacity for civil conduct and pose a serious danger to the child, and a certificate proving the guardian's guardianship rights shall be submitted;  
(3) where both parents of the child have died and the child's guardian acts as the person placing out the child for adoption, a death certificate of the child's birth parents, a certificate proving that the guardian actually assumes the guardianship responsibility, and a written opinion of other persons having the obligation to raise the child consenting to the placement shall be submitted;  
(4) where a social welfare institution acts as the person placing out the child for adoption, a certificate proving the circumstances of the abandonment and discovery of the abandoned infant or child and a certificate proving the search for his or her parents or other guardians shall be submitted; where the child to be adopted is an orphan, a death certificate or certificate of declaration of death of the orphan's parents, and a written opinion of other persons having the obligation to raise the orphan consenting to the placement shall be submitted.  
Where a disabled child is placed out for adoption, a certificate of disability of the child issued by a medical institution at or above the county level shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门应当对送养人提交的证件和证明材料进行审查，对查找不到生父母的弃婴和儿童公告查找其生父母；认为被收养人、送养人符合民法典规定条件的，将符合民法典规定的被收养人、送养人名单通知中国收养组织，同时转交下列证件和证明材料：  
（一）送养人的居民户口簿和居民身份证（社会福利机构作送养人的，为其负责人的身份证件）复制件；  
（二）被收养人是弃婴或者孤儿的证明、户籍证明、成长情况报告和身体健康检查证明的复制件及照片。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门查找弃婴或者儿童生父母的公告应当在省级地方报纸上刊登。自公告刊登之日起满60日，弃婴和儿童的生父母或者其他监护人未认领的，视为查找不到生父母的弃婴和儿童。

**Article 6** The civil affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government shall examine the certificates and certifying documents submitted by the person placing out the child for adoption, and shall issue a public notice to search for the birth parents of an abandoned infant or child whose birth parents cannot be found; if it considers that the child to be adopted and the person placing out the child for adoption meet the conditions prescribed in the Civil Code, it shall notify the China Adoption Organization of the list of the children to be adopted and the persons placing out the children for adoption that meet the conditions prescribed in the Civil Code, and shall at the same time transfer the following certificates and certifying documents:  
(1) copies of the residence booklet and resident identity card of the person placing out the child for adoption (where a social welfare institution acts as the person placing out the child for adoption, the identity document of its responsible person);  
(2) copies of the certificate proving that the child to be adopted is an abandoned infant or orphan, the household registration certificate, the growth report, and the certificate of physical health examination, as well as photographs.  
The public notice issued by the civil affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to search for the birth parents of an abandoned infant or child shall be published in a provincial-level local newspaper. If the birth parents or other guardians of the abandoned infant or child do not claim the child after the expiry of 60 days from the date of publication of the public notice, the abandoned infant or child shall be deemed to be an abandoned infant or child whose birth parents cannot be found.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　中国收养组织对外国收养人的收养申请和有关证明进行审查后，应当在省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门报送的符合民法典规定条件的被收养人中，参照外国收养人的意愿，选择适当的被收养人，并将该被收养人及其送养人的有关情况通过外国政府或者外国收养组织送交外国收养人。外国收养人同意收养的，中国收养组织向其发出来华收养子女通知书，同时通知有关的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门向送养人发出被收养人已被同意收养的通知。

**Article 7** After examining the adoption application and relevant certificates of the foreign adopter, the China Adoption Organization shall, among the children to be adopted submitted by the civil affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government that meet the conditions prescribed in the Civil Code, select an appropriate child to be adopted with reference to the wishes of the foreign adopter, and shall send the relevant information of the child to be adopted and the person placing out the child for adoption to the foreign adopter through the foreign government or foreign adoption organization. If the foreign adopter agrees to the adoption, the China Adoption Organization shall issue a notice of adoption of a child in China to him or her, and shall at the same time notify the relevant civil affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government to issue a notice to the person placing out the child for adoption that the child to be adopted has been approved for adoption.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　外国人来华收养子女，应当亲自来华办理登记手续。夫妻共同收养的，应当共同来华办理收养手续；一方因故不能来华的，应当书面委托另一方。委托书应当经所在国公证和认证。中华人民共和国缔结或者参加的国际条约另有规定的，按照国际条约规定的证明手续办理。  
收养人对外国主管机关依据[本办法第四条](#art-4)第二款和前款提及的国际条约出具的证明文书的真实性负责，签署书面声明，并承担相应法律责任。

**Article 8** Foreigners adopting children in China shall come to China in person to go through the registration formalities. Where the spouses jointly adopt a child, they shall come to China jointly to go through the adoption formalities; if one of the spouses cannot come to China for some reason, he or she shall give a written power of attorney to the other spouse. The power of attorney shall be notarized and authenticated in the adopter's home country. Where there are other provisions in the international treaties concluded or acceded to by the People's Republic of China, the certification procedures provided for in the international treaties shall be followed.  
The adopter shall be responsible for the authenticity of the certifying documents issued by the competent foreign authority in accordance with the international treaties referred to in the second paragraph of Article 4 of these Measures and the preceding paragraph, shall sign a written declaration, and shall bear the corresponding legal liability.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　外国人来华收养子女，应当与送养人订立书面收养协议。协议一式三份，收养人、送养人各执一份，办理收养登记手续时收养登记机关收存一份。  
书面协议订立后，收养关系当事人应当共同到被收养人常住户口所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门办理收养登记。

**Article 9** Foreigners adopting children in China shall conclude a written adoption agreement with the person placing out the child for adoption. The agreement shall be made in triplicate, with one copy held by the adopter, one copy by the person placing out the child for adoption, and one copy deposited with the adoption registration authority when going through the adoption registration formalities.  
After the written agreement is concluded, the parties to the adoption relationship shall jointly go to the civil affairs department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the child to be adopted has his or her permanent residence to complete the adoption registration.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　收养关系当事人办理收养登记时，应当填写外国人来华收养子女登记申请书并提交收养协议，同时分别提供有关材料。  
收养人应当提供下列材料：  
（一）中国收养组织发出的来华收养子女通知书；  
（二）收养人的身份证件和照片。  
送养人应当提供下列材料：  
（一）省、自治区、直辖市人民政府民政部门发出的被收养人已被同意收养的通知；  
（二）送养人的居民户口簿和居民身份证（社会福利机构作送养人的，为其负责人的身份证件）、被收养人的照片。

**Article 10** When going through the adoption registration, the parties to the adoption relationship shall fill out a registration application form for foreigners adopting children in China and submit the adoption agreement, and shall also provide relevant materials respectively.  
The adopter shall provide the following materials:  
(1) the notice of adoption of a child in China issued by the China Adoption Organization;  
(2) the identity document and photographs of the adopter.  
The person placing out the child for adoption shall provide the following materials:  
(1) the notice issued by the civil affairs department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government that the child to be adopted has been approved for adoption;  
(2) the residence booklet and resident identity card of the person placing out the child for adoption (where a social welfare institution acts as the person placing out the child for adoption, the identity document of its responsible person), and photographs of the child to be adopted.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　收养登记机关收到外国人来华收养子女登记申请书和收养人、被收养人及其送养人的有关材料后，应当自次日起7日内进行审查，对符合[本办法第十条](#art-10)规定的，为当事人办理收养登记，发给收养登记证书。收养关系自登记之日起成立。  
收养登记机关应当将登记结果通知中国收养组织。

**Article 11** After receiving the registration application form for foreigners adopting children in China and the relevant materials of the adopter, the child to be adopted and the person placing out the child for adoption, the adoption registration authority shall conduct an examination within 7 days from the following day; if the requirements prescribed in Article 10 of these Measures are met, it shall complete the adoption registration for the parties and issue an adoption registration certificate. The adoption relationship shall be established as of the date of registration.  
The adoption registration authority shall notify the China Adoption Organization of the registration result.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　收养关系当事人办理收养登记后，各方或者一方要求办理收养公证的，应当到收养登记地的具有办理涉外公证资格的公证机构办理收养公证。

**Article 12** After the parties to the adoption relationship have completed the adoption registration, if any party or both parties request notarization of the adoption, they shall go to a notary institution with the qualification to handle foreign-related notarization at the place of adoption registration to handle the notarization of the adoption.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　被收养人出境前，收养人应当凭收养登记证书到收养登记地的公安机关为被收养人办理出境手续。

**Article 13** Before the adopted child leaves the country, the adopter shall, by presenting the adoption registration certificate, go to the public security organ at the place of adoption registration to go through the exit formalities for the adopted child.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　外国人在华收养子女，应当向登记机关交纳登记费。登记费的收费标准按照国家有关规定执行。  
中国收养组织是非营利性公益事业单位，为外国收养人提供收养服务，可以收取服务费。服务费的收费标准按照国家有关规定执行。  
为抚养在社会福利机构生活的弃婴和儿童，国家鼓励外国收养人、外国收养组织向社会福利机构捐赠。受赠的社会福利机构必须将捐赠财物全部用于改善所抚养的弃婴和儿童的养育条件，不得挪作它用，并应当将捐赠财物的使用情况告知捐赠人。受赠的社会福利机构还应当接受有关部门的监督，并应当将捐赠的使用情况向社会公布。

**Article 14** Foreigners adopting children in China shall pay the registration fee to the registration authority. The fee standard for registration shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
The China Adoption Organization is a non-profit public welfare institution; it may charge a service fee for providing adoption services to foreign adopters. The fee standard for services shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the State.  
For the purpose of raising abandoned infants and children living in social welfare institutions, the State encourages foreign adopters and foreign adoption organizations to make donations to social welfare institutions. The social welfare institutions that receive donations shall use all the donated money and goods for improving the raising conditions of the abandoned infants and children under their care, and shall not use them for other purposes; they shall also inform the donors of the use of the donated money and goods. The social welfare institutions that receive donations shall also accept supervision by relevant departments, and shall make public the use of the donations.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　中国收养组织的活动受国务院民政部门监督。

**Article 15** The activities of the China Adoption Organization shall be subject to the supervision of the civil affairs department of the State Council.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本办法自发布之日起施行。1993年11月3日国务院批准，1993年11月10日司法部、民政部发布的《外国人在中华人民共和国收养子女实施办法》同时废止。

**Article 16** These Measures shall come into force as of the date of promulgation. The Measures for the Implementation of Adoption of Children by Foreigners in the People's Republic of China approved by the State Council on November 3, 1993 and promulgated by the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Civil Affairs on November 10, 1993 shall be abolished simultaneously.
