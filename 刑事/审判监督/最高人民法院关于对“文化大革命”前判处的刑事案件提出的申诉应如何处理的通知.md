# 最高人民法院关于对“文化大革命”前判处的刑事案件提出的申诉应如何处理的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1980-02-15  
**生效日期**：1980-02-15

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、对“文化大革命”前判处的刑事案件当事人提出申诉的，或者有关单位提出要复查的，或者司法人员自己发现可能判错提出复查的，由人民法院作为一项经常工作，认真负责地审查处理。但对“文化大革命”前判处的刑事案件不进行全面复查。

**Article 1** I. Where a party to a criminal case adjudicated before the "Cultural Revolution" files a petition, or a relevant unit requests a review, or judicial personnel themselves discover a possible error and propose a review, the People's Court shall, as a regular task, conscientiously examine and handle the matter. However, no comprehensive review shall be conducted for criminal cases adjudicated before the "Cultural Revolution."

<a id="art-2"></a>二、处理对“文化大革命”前判处的刑事案件提出的申诉，除了党的十一届三中全会以来，中央对解决历史遗留问题有专门规定的，应按规定复查纠正外，对其他申诉案件，要根据判决时中央的政策和国家法律来衡量原判是否适当，不能以现在的政策和法律去处理过去判处的案件。

**Article 2** II. In handling petitions regarding criminal cases adjudicated before the "Cultural Revolution," except where the Central Committee has made specific provisions since the Third Plenary Session of the 11th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China for resolving historical legacy issues, which shall be reviewed and corrected in accordance with such provisions, for other petition cases, the appropriateness of the original judgment shall be measured according to the Central Committee's policies and national laws in force at the time of the judgment, and the present policies and laws shall not be applied to cases adjudicated in the past.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、对于具体的案件，主要事实或者基本性质认定错了，或者按照当时中央的政策和国家法律不构成犯罪、不该判刑而定罪判刑的，要改判纠正。对于主要事实或者基本性质不错的，一般可不改判。对量刑畸重，仍在服刑的，可酌情改判；如已刑满释放，一般可再不改判。

**Article 3** III. For specific cases, if the main facts or basic nature are wrongly determined, or if a conviction and sentence are imposed for acts that, according to the Central Committee's policies and national laws at the time, did not constitute a crime and should not have been sentenced, the judgment shall be altered and corrected. If the main facts or basic nature are not erroneous, generally the judgment may not be altered. Where the sentence is manifestly excessive and the person is still serving the sentence, the judgment may be altered as appropriate; if the sentence has already been fully served, generally the judgment may no longer be altered.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、向原审人民法院提出申诉的，由原审人民法院处理；向上级人民法院提出申诉的，一般也可转交原审人民法院处理。重大的、疑难的，或者多次申诉又确有理由而未得到妥善处理的，可由上一级人民法院或高级人民法院处理。  
对最高人民法院前各大区分院判处的案件提出申诉的，委托有关地区高级人民法院查清事实，提出处理意见后，由最高人民法院处理。

**Article 4** IV. Where a petition is filed with the original trial People's Court, it shall be handled by the original trial People's Court; where a petition is filed with a higher People's Court, it may generally be referred to the original trial People's Court for handling. For major or difficult cases, or cases where repeated petitions have been filed with good cause yet have not been properly handled, the case may be handled by the next higher People's Court or the Higher People's Court.  
For petitions concerning cases adjudicated by the former branch courts of the Supreme People's Court, the relevant Higher People's Court shall be entrusted to ascertain the facts and submit its proposed handling opinion, after which the matter shall be handled by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、对于错判案件改判后的善后工作，可参照中共中央1979年第96号文件的规定办理。改判纠正后善后工作的实际问题，要着重解决在基层，上级人民法院改判的案件，可委托当事人所在地的人民法院或有关的人民法院协助办理。  
处理“文化大革命”前判处的刑事案件的申诉，要持慎重态度。遇有重大问题和其他难以解决的问题时，应请示党委解决。

**Article 5** V. For the follow-up work after the alteration of judgments in wrongly decided cases, reference shall be made to the provisions of Document No. 96 of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China in 1979. Practical issues in the follow-up work after the alteration and correction of judgments shall be primarily resolved at the grassroots level; for cases where the judgment is altered by a higher People's Court, the court may entrust the People's Court at the location of the party concerned or the relevant People's Court to assist in handling.  
The handling of petitions concerning criminal cases adjudicated before the "Cultural Revolution" shall be approached with caution. When major issues or other difficult problems arise, the matter shall be reported to the Party committee for resolution.
