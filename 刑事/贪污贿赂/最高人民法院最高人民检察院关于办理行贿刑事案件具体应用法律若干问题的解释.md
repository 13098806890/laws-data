# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理行贿刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕22号  
**公布日期**：2012-05-14  
**生效日期**：2013-01-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理行贿刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2012年5月14日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1547次会议、2012年8月21日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第77次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年1月1日起施行。 2012年12月26日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理行贿刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2012〕22号 为依法惩治行贿犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理行贿刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   为谋取不正当利益，向国家工作人员行贿，数额在一万元以上的，应当依照刑法第三百九十条的规定追究刑事责任。 第二条   因行贿谋取不正当利益，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百九十条第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）行贿数额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的； （二）行贿数额在十万元以上不满二十万元，并具有下列情形之一的： 1. 向三人以上行贿的； 2. 将违法所得用于行贿的； 3. 为实施违法犯罪活动，向负有食品、药品、安全生产、环境保护等监督管理职责的国家工作人员行贿，严重危害民生、侵犯公众生命财产安全的； 4. 向行政执法机关、司法机关的国家工作人员行贿，影响行政执法和司法公正的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   为谋取不正当利益，向国家工作人员行贿，数额在一万元以上的，应当依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 1** Whoever, for the purpose of obtaining illegitimate benefits, offers bribes to a state employee, and the amount involved is 10,000 yuan or more, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   因行贿谋取不正当利益，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）行贿数额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的；  
（二）行贿数额在十万元以上不满二十万元，并具有下列情形之一的：  
1. 向三人以上行贿的；  
2. 将违法所得用于行贿的；  
3. 为实施违法犯罪活动，向负有食品、药品、安全生产、环境保护等监督管理职责的国家工作人员行贿，严重危害民生、侵犯公众生命财产安全的；  
4. 向行政执法机关、司法机关的国家工作人员行贿，影响行政执法和司法公正的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** Where a person offers bribes to obtain illegitimate benefits and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) The bribery amount is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 1,000,000 yuan;  
(2) The bribery amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and falls under any of the following circumstances:  
(a) Bribes are offered to three or more persons;  
(b) Illegal gains are used for offering bribes;  
(c) For the purpose of carrying out illegal or criminal activities, bribes are offered to state employees who are responsible for supervision and administration of food, drugs, work safety, environmental protection, etc., thereby seriously endangering people's livelihood and infringing upon the safety of life and property of the public;  
(d) Bribes are offered to state employees of administrative law enforcement organs or judicial organs, thereby affecting administrative law enforcement and judicial fairness;  
(3) Other circumstances that are serious.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   因行贿谋取不正当利益，造成直接经济损失数额在一百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“使国家利益遭受重大损失”。

**Article 3** Where a person offers bribes to obtain illegitimate benefits, causing direct economic losses of 1,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as “causing major losses to the interests of the state” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   因行贿谋取不正当利益，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）行贿数额在一百万元以上的；  
（二）行贿数额在五十万元以上不满一百万元，并具有下列情形之一的：  
1. 向三人以上行贿的；  
2. 将违法所得用于行贿的；  
3. 为实施违法犯罪活动，向负有食品、药品、安全生产、环境保护等监督管理职责的国家工作人员行贿，严重危害民生、侵犯公众生命财产安全的；  
4. 向行政执法机关、司法机关的国家工作人员行贿，影响行政执法和司法公正的；  
（三）造成直接经济损失数额在五百万元以上的；  
（四）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 4** Where a person offers bribes to obtain illegitimate benefits and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as “extremely serious circumstances” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) The bribery amount is 1,000,000 yuan or more;  
(2) The bribery amount is 500,000 yuan or more but less than 1,000,000 yuan, and falls under any of the following circumstances:  
(a) Bribes are offered to three or more persons;  
(b) Illegal gains are used for offering bribes;  
(c) For the purpose of carrying out illegal or criminal activities, bribes are offered to state employees who are responsible for supervision and administration of food, drugs, work safety, environmental protection, etc., thereby seriously endangering people's livelihood and infringing upon the safety of life and property of the public;  
(d) Bribes are offered to state employees of administrative law enforcement organs or judicial organs, thereby affecting administrative law enforcement and judicial fairness;  
(3) Direct economic losses of 5,000,000 yuan or more are caused;  
(4) Other circumstances that are extremely serious.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   多次行贿未经处理的，按照累计行贿数额处罚。

**Article 5** Where acts of offering bribes have been committed multiple times and have not been dealt with, the punishment shall be imposed based on the accumulated amount of bribes.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   行贿人谋取不正当利益的行为构成犯罪的，应当与行贿犯罪实行数罪并罚。

**Article 6** Where the act of the bribe-offerer in obtaining illegitimate benefits constitutes a crime, it shall be subject to combined punishment for multiple crimes together with the bribery crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   因行贿人在被追诉前主动交待行贿行为而破获相关受贿案件的，对行贿人不适用[刑法第六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-68)关于立功的规定，依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款的规定，可以减轻或者免除处罚。  
单位行贿的，在被追诉前，单位集体决定或者单位负责人决定主动交待单位行贿行为的，依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款的规定，对单位及相关责任人员可以减轻处罚或者免除处罚；受委托直接办理单位行贿事项的直接责任人员在被追诉前主动交待自己知道的单位行贿行为的，对该直接责任人员可以依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款的规定减轻处罚或者免除处罚。

**Article 7** Where, because the bribe-offerer voluntarily confesses the bribery act before being prosecuted, the relevant bribery-accepting case is solved, the provisions of Article 68 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China regarding meritorious service shall not apply to the bribe-offerer, and in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the punishment may be mitigated or the offender may be exempted from punishment.  
Where a unit offers bribes and, before being prosecuted, the unit collectively decides or the person in charge of the unit decides to voluntarily confess the unit's bribery act, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the unit and the relevant responsible persons may be given mitigated punishment or exempted from punishment; where the directly responsible person entrusted to directly handle the unit's bribery matters voluntarily confesses the unit's bribery act within his or her knowledge before being prosecuted, that directly responsible person may be given mitigated punishment or exempted from punishment in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   行贿人被追诉后如实供述自己罪行的，依照[刑法第六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-67)第三款的规定，可以从轻处罚；因其如实供述自己罪行，避免特别严重后果发生的，可以减轻处罚。

**Article 8** Where a bribe-offerer truthfully confesses his or her own crime after being prosecuted, in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 67 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, a light punishment may be imposed; if, because of the truthful confession, particularly serious consequences are avoided, the punishment may be mitigated.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   行贿人揭发受贿人与其行贿无关的其他犯罪行为，查证属实的，依照[刑法第六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-68)关于立功的规定，可以从轻、减轻或者免除处罚。

**Article 9** Where a bribe-offerer exposes other criminal acts of the bribe-taker that are unrelated to the bribery, and the exposure is verified, in accordance with the provisions of Article 68 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China regarding meritorious service, a light punishment may be imposed, or the punishment may be mitigated or the offender may be exempted from punishment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   实施行贿犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，一般不适用缓刑和免予刑事处罚：  
（一）向三人以上行贿的；  
（二）因行贿受过行政处罚或者刑事处罚的；  
（三）为实施违法犯罪活动而行贿的；  
（四）造成严重危害后果的；  
（五）其他不适用缓刑和免予刑事处罚的情形。  
具有[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款规定的情形的，不受前款规定的限制。

**Article 10** Where a bribery crime is committed and falls under any of the following circumstances, probation and exemption from criminal punishment shall generally not be applied:  
(1) Bribes are offered to three or more persons;  
(2) The person has previously received administrative penalty or criminal punishment for bribery;  
(3) Bribes are offered for the purpose of carrying out illegal or criminal activities;  
(4) Serious harmful consequences are caused;  
(5) Other circumstances where probation and exemption from criminal punishment are not applicable.  
Where the circumstances provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China exist, the restrictions in the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   行贿犯罪取得的不正当财产性利益应当依照[刑法第六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-64)的规定予以追缴、责令退赔或者返还被害人。  
因行贿犯罪取得财产性利益以外的经营资格、资质或者职务晋升等其他不正当利益，建议有关部门依照相关规定予以处理。

**Article 11** The illegitimate pecuniary benefits obtained from a bribery crime shall be recovered, ordered to be returned or compensated, or returned to the victim in accordance with Article 64 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where other illegitimate benefits such as business qualifications, certifications, or job promotions, other than pecuniary benefits, are obtained through a bribery crime, it is recommended that the relevant departments handle the matter in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   行贿犯罪中的“谋取不正当利益”，是指行贿人谋取的利益违反法律、法规、规章、政策规定，或者要求国家工作人员违反法律、法规、规章、政策、行业规范的规定，为自己提供帮助或者方便条件。  
违背公平、公正原则，在经济、组织人事管理等活动中，谋取竞争优势的，应当认定为“谋取不正当利益”。

**Article 12** “Obtaining illegitimate benefits” in bribery crimes means that the benefits sought by the bribe-offerer violate the provisions of laws, regulations, rules, or policies, or that the bribe-offerer requests a state employee to violate the provisions of laws, regulations, rules, policies, or industry standards to provide assistance or favorable conditions for the bribe-offerer.  
Where, in economic, organizational, personnel management, or other activities, a person seeks competitive advantages in violation of the principles of fairness and impartiality, it shall be deemed as “obtaining illegitimate benefits”.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条   [刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款规定的“被追诉前”，是指检察机关对行贿人的行贿行为刑事立案前。

**Article 13** The term “before being prosecuted” as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China means before the procuratorial authority initiates a criminal case into the bribery act of the bribe-offerer.
