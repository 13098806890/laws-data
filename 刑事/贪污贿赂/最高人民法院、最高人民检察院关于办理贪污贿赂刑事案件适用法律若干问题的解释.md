# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕9号  
**公布日期**：2016-03-28  
**生效日期**：2016-04-18

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2016年3月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1680次会议、2016年3月25日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第50次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年4月18日起施行。 2016年4月18日 最高人民法院最高人民检察院 关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2016〕9号 为依法惩治贪污贿赂犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　贪污或者受贿数额在三万元以上不满二十万元的，应当认定为刑法第三百八十三条第一款规定的“数额较大”，依法判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金。 贪污数额在一万元以上不满三万元，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百八十三条第一款规定的“其他较重情节”，依法判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金： （一）贪污救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济、防疫、社会捐助等特定款物的； （二）曾因贪污、受贿、挪用公款受过党纪、行政处分的； （三）曾因故意犯罪受过刑事追究的； （四）赃款赃物用于非法活动的； （五）拒不交待赃款赃物去向或者拒不配合追缴工作，致使无法追缴的； （六）造成恶劣影响或者其他严重后果的。 受贿数额在一万元以上不满三万元，具有前款第二项至第六项规定的情形之一，或者具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百八十三条第一款规定的“其他较重情节”，依法判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　贪污或者受贿数额在三万元以上不满二十万元的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“数额较大”，依法判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金。  
贪污数额在一万元以上不满三万元，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“其他较重情节”，依法判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金：  
（一）贪污救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济、防疫、社会捐助等特定款物的；  
（二）曾因贪污、受贿、挪用公款受过党纪、行政处分的；  
（三）曾因故意犯罪受过刑事追究的；  
（四）赃款赃物用于非法活动的；  
（五）拒不交待赃款赃物去向或者拒不配合追缴工作，致使无法追缴的；  
（六）造成恶劣影响或者其他严重后果的。  
受贿数额在一万元以上不满三万元，具有前款第二项至第六项规定的情形之一，或者具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“其他较重情节”，依法判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金：  
（一）多次索贿的；  
（二）为他人谋取不正当利益，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受损失的；  
（三）为他人谋取职务提拔、调整的。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/贪污贿赂/最高人民检察院关于贪污养老、医疗等社会保险基金能否适用《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第一条第二款第一项规定的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民检察院关于贪污养老、医疗等社会保险基金能否适用《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第一条第二款第一项规定的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../刑事/贪污贿赂/最高人民检察院关于贪污养老、医疗等社会保险基金能否适用《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第一条第二款第一项规定的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民检察院关于贪污养老、医疗等社会保险基金能否适用《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第一条第二款第一项规定的批复》第1条引用">[2]</a></sup>

**Article 1** Where the amount of embezzlement or acceptance of bribes is 30,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, it shall be deemed as "relatively large" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined.  
If the amount of embezzlement is 10,000 yuan or more but less than 30,000 yuan, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "other relatively serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined:  
(1) embezzlement of specific funds or materials for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for disabled servicemen and family members of revolutionary martyrs, poverty alleviation, resettlement of immigrants, relief, epidemic prevention, social donations, etc;  
(2) having previously received disciplinary or administrative sanctions by the Party or the government for embezzlement, acceptance of bribes, or misappropriation of public funds;  
(3) having previously been subject to criminal investigation for intentional crimes;  
(4) using the proceeds of crime and illegal gains for illegal activities;  
(5) refusing to account for the whereabouts of the proceeds of crime and illegal gains, or refusing to cooperate with the recovery efforts, making recovery impossible;  
(6) causing adverse effects or other serious consequences.  
If the amount of acceptance of bribes is 10,000 yuan or more but less than 30,000 yuan, and falls under any of the circumstances specified in items (2) to (6) of the preceding paragraph, or under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "other relatively serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined:  
(1) repeatedly soliciting bribes;  
(2) seeking illegitimate interests for others, causing losses to public property, the state, and the interests of the people;  
(3) seeking promotion or adjustment of posts for others.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　贪污或者受贿数额在二十万元以上不满三百万元的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“数额巨大”，依法判处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
贪污数额在十万元以上不满二十万元，具有[本解释第一条](#art-1)第二款规定的情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“其他严重情节”，依法判处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
受贿数额在十万元以上不满二十万元，具有[本解释第一条](#art-1)第三款规定的情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“其他严重情节”，依法判处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。

**Article 2** Where the amount of embezzlement or acceptance of bribes is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 3 million yuan, it shall be deemed as "huge" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated.  
If the amount of embezzlement is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and falls under any of the circumstances specified in paragraph 2 of Article 1 of this Interpretation, it shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated.  
If the amount of acceptance of bribes is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and falls under any of the circumstances specified in paragraph 3 of Article 1 of this Interpretation, it shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　贪污或者受贿数额在三百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“数额特别巨大”，依法判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑或者死刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
贪污数额在一百五十万元以上不满三百万元，具有[本解释第一条](#art-1)第二款规定的情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“其他特别严重情节”，依法判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑或者死刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
受贿数额在一百五十万元以上不满三百万元，具有[本解释第一条](#art-1)第三款规定的情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-383)第一款规定的“其他特别严重情节”，依法判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑或者死刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。

**Article 3** Where the amount of embezzlement or acceptance of bribes is 3 million yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "especially huge" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, life imprisonment, or the death penalty, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated.  
If the amount of embezzlement is 1.5 million yuan or more but less than 3 million yuan, and falls under any of the circumstances specified in paragraph 2 of Article 1 of this Interpretation, it shall be deemed as "other especially serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, life imprisonment, or the death penalty, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated.  
If the amount of acceptance of bribes is 1.5 million yuan or more but less than 3 million yuan, and falls under any of the circumstances specified in paragraph 3 of Article 1 of this Interpretation, it shall be deemed as "other especially serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 383 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, life imprisonment, or the death penalty, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　贪污、受贿数额特别巨大，犯罪情节特别严重、社会影响特别恶劣、给国家和人民利益造成特别重大损失的，可以判处死刑。  
符合前款规定的情形，但具有自首，立功，如实供述自己罪行、真诚悔罪、积极退赃，或者避免、减少损害结果的发生等情节，不是必须立即执行的，可以判处死刑缓期二年执行。  
符合第一款规定情形的，根据犯罪情节等情况可以判处死刑缓期二年执行，同时裁判决定在其死刑缓期执行二年期满依法减为无期徒刑后，终身监禁，不得减刑、假释。

**Article 4** If the amount of embezzlement or acceptance of bribes is especially huge, the circumstances of the crime are especially serious, the social impact is especially adverse, and particularly heavy losses are caused to the interests of the state and the people, the death penalty may be imposed.  
If the circumstances specified in the preceding paragraph are met, but there are circumstances such as voluntary surrender, meritorious service, truthful confession of one's crimes, sincere repentance, active restitution of illicit gains, or avoidance or reduction of the occurrence of harmful consequences, and immediate execution is not necessary, a death sentence with a two-year reprieve may be imposed.  
If the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 are met, depending on the circumstances of the crime, a death sentence with a two-year reprieve may be imposed, and at the same time, upon the expiration of the two-year reprieve and if the sentence is commuted to life imprisonment according to law, the offender shall be imprisoned for life without commutation of punishment or parole.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　挪用公款归个人使用，进行非法活动，数额在三万元以上的，应当依照[刑法第三百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-384)的规定以挪用公款罪追究刑事责任；数额在三百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-384)第一款规定的“数额巨大”。具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-384)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）挪用公款数额在一百万元以上的；  
（二）挪用救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济特定款物，数额在五十万元以上不满一百万元的；  
（三）挪用公款不退还，数额在五十万元以上不满一百万元的；  
（四）其他严重的情节。

**Article 5** If a person misappropriates public funds for personal use, engages in illegal activities, and the amount is 30,000 yuan or more, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of misappropriation of public funds in accordance with Article 384 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China; if the amount is 3 million yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "huge" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 384 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China. If any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 384 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the amount of misappropriated public funds is 1 million yuan or more;  
(2) misappropriation of specific funds or materials for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for disabled servicemen and family members of revolutionary martyrs, poverty alleviation, resettlement of immigrants, relief, with an amount of 500,000 yuan or more but less than 1 million yuan;  
(3) misappropriation of public funds that is not repaid, with an amount of 500,000 yuan or more but less than 1 million yuan;  
(4) other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　挪用公款归个人使用，进行营利活动或者超过三个月未还，数额在五万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-384)第一款规定的“数额较大”；数额在五百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-384)第一款规定的“数额巨大”。具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-384)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）挪用公款数额在二百万元以上的；  
（二）挪用救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济特定款物，数额在一百万元以上不满二百万元的；  
（三）挪用公款不退还，数额在一百万元以上不满二百万元的；  
（四）其他严重的情节。

**Article 6** If a person misappropriates public funds for personal use for profit-making activities or if the funds are not repaid within three months, and the amount is 50,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "relatively large" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 384 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China; if the amount is 5 million yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "huge" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 384 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China. If any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 384 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the amount of misappropriated public funds is 2 million yuan or more;  
(2) misappropriation of specific funds or materials for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for disabled servicemen and family members of revolutionary martyrs, poverty alleviation, resettlement of immigrants, relief, with an amount of 1 million yuan or more but less than 2 million yuan;  
(3) misappropriation of public funds that is not repaid, with an amount of 1 million yuan or more but less than 2 million yuan;  
(4) other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　为谋取不正当利益，向国家工作人员行贿，数额在三万元以上的，应当依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)的规定以行贿罪追究刑事责任。  
行贿数额在一万元以上不满三万元，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)的规定以行贿罪追究刑事责任：  
（一）向三人以上行贿的；  
（二）将违法所得用于行贿的；  
（三）通过行贿谋取职务提拔、调整的；  
（四）向负有食品、药品、安全生产、环境保护等监督管理职责的国家工作人员行贿，实施非法活动的；  
（五）向司法工作人员行贿，影响司法公正的；  
（六）造成经济损失数额在五十万元以上不满一百万元的。

**Article 7** If a person gives a bribe to a functionary of a state organ for the purpose of seeking illegitimate interests, and the amount is 30,000 yuan or more, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of offering bribes in accordance with Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If the amount of the bribe is 10,000 yuan or more but less than 30,000 yuan, and any of the following circumstances exists, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of offering bribes in accordance with Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) giving bribes to three or more persons;  
(2) using illegal gains to give bribes;  
(3) seeking promotion or adjustment of posts through bribery;  
(4) giving bribes to state functionaries who are responsible for supervision and management of food, pharmaceuticals, production safety, environmental protection, etc., and carrying out illegal activities;  
(5) giving bribes to judicial officers, affecting judicial fairness;  
(6) causing economic losses of 500,000 yuan or more but less than 1 million yuan.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　犯行贿罪，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）行贿数额在一百万元以上不满五百万元的；  
（二）行贿数额在五十万元以上不满一百万元，并具有[本解释第七条](#art-7)第二款第一项至第五项规定的情形之一的；  
（三）其他严重的情节。  
为谋取不正当利益，向国家工作人员行贿，造成经济损失数额在一百万元以上不满五百万元的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“使国家利益遭受重大损失”。

**Article 8** If a person commits the crime of offering bribes and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the amount of the bribe is 1 million yuan or more but less than 5 million yuan;  
(2) the amount of the bribe is 500,000 yuan or more but less than 1 million yuan, and any of the circumstances specified in items (1) to (5) of paragraph 2 of Article 7 of this Interpretation exists;  
(3) other serious circumstances.  
If a person gives a bribe to a functionary of a state organ for the purpose of seeking illegitimate interests, causing economic losses of 1 million yuan or more but less than 5 million yuan, it shall be deemed as "causing major losses to the interests of the state" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　犯行贿罪，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）行贿数额在五百万元以上的；  
（二）行贿数额在二百五十万元以上不满五百万元，并具有[本解释第七条](#art-7)第二款第一项至第五项规定的情形之一的；  
（三）其他特别严重的情节。  
为谋取不正当利益，向国家工作人员行贿，造成经济损失数额在五百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“使国家利益遭受特别重大损失”。

**Article 9** If a person commits the crime of offering bribes and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the amount of the bribe is 5 million yuan or more;  
(2) the amount of the bribe is 2.5 million yuan or more but less than 5 million yuan, and any of the circumstances specified in items (1) to (5) of paragraph 2 of Article 7 of this Interpretation exists;  
(3) other especially serious circumstances.  
If a person gives a bribe to a functionary of a state organ for the purpose of seeking illegitimate interests, causing economic losses of 5 million yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "causing especially major losses to the interests of the state" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[刑法第三百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-388)之一规定的利用影响力受贿罪的定罪量刑适用标准，参照本解释关于受贿罪的规定执行。  
[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)之一规定的对有影响力的人行贿罪的定罪量刑适用标准，参照本解释关于行贿罪的规定执行。  
单位对有影响力的人行贿数额在二十万元以上的，应当依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)之一的规定以对有影响力的人行贿罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 10** The standards for conviction and sentencing of the crime of influence peddling as prescribed in Article 388 bis of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be implemented with reference to the provisions of this Interpretation on the crime of acceptance of bribes.  
The standards for conviction and sentencing of the crime of offering bribes to a person with influence as prescribed in Article 390 bis of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be implemented with reference to the provisions of this Interpretation on the crime of offering bribes.  
If a unit gives a bribe to a person with influence in an amount of 200,000 yuan or more, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of offering bribes to a person with influence in accordance with Article 390 bis of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　[刑法第一百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-163)规定的非国家工作人员受贿罪、第二百七十一条规定的职务侵占罪中的“数额较大”“数额巨大”的数额起点，按照本解释关于受贿罪、贪污罪相对应的数额标准规定的二倍、五倍执行。  
[刑法第二百七十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-272)规定的挪用资金罪中的“数额较大”“数额巨大”以及“进行非法活动”情形的数额起点，按照本解释关于挪用公款罪“数额较大”“情节严重”以及“进行非法活动”的数额标准规定的二倍执行。  
[刑法第一百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-164)第一款规定的对非国家工作人员行贿罪中的“数额较大”“数额巨大”的数额起点，按照[本解释第七条](#art-7)、第八条第一款关于行贿罪的数额标准规定的二倍执行。

**Article 11** The thresholds for "relatively large" and "huge" in the crime of acceptance of bribes by a non-state functionary as prescribed in Article 163 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China and the crime of embezzlement of duty as prescribed in Article 271 shall be implemented at two times and five times the corresponding amount standards provided in this Interpretation for the crimes of acceptance of bribes and embezzlement.  
The thresholds for "relatively large", "huge", and the circumstances of "engaging in illegal activities" in the crime of misappropriation of funds as prescribed in Article 272 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be implemented at two times the amount standards provided in this Interpretation for "relatively large", "serious circumstances", and "engaging in illegal activities" in the crime of misappropriation of public funds.  
The thresholds for "relatively large" and "huge" in the crime of offering bribes to a non-state functionary as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 164 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be implemented at two times the amount standards provided in Articles 7 and 8, paragraph 1 of this Interpretation for the crime of offering bribes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　贿赂犯罪中的“财物”，包括货币、物品和财产性利益。财产性利益包括可以折算为货币的物质利益如房屋装修、债务免除等，以及需要支付货币的其他利益如会员服务、旅游等。后者的犯罪数额，以实际支付或者应当支付的数额计算。

**Article 12** "Property" in bribery crimes includes currency, articles, and property interests. Property interests include material interests that can be converted into currency, such as house decoration and debt forgiveness, as well as other interests that require payment of currency, such as member services and travel. The amount of the crime for the latter shall be calculated based on the amount actually paid or that shall be paid.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“为他人谋取利益”，构成犯罪的，应当依照刑法关于受贿犯罪的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）实际或者承诺为他人谋取利益的；  
（二）明知他人有具体请托事项的；  
（三）履职时未被请托，但事后基于该履职事由收受他人财物的。  
国家工作人员索取、收受具有上下级关系的下属或者具有行政管理关系的被管理人员的财物价值三万元以上，可能影响职权行使的，视为承诺为他人谋取利益。

**Article 13** If any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "seeking interests for others", and if a crime is constituted, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China on the crime of acceptance of bribes:  
(1) actually or promising to seek interests for others;  
(2) knowing that others have specific matters of entrustment;  
(3) not being entrusted during the performance of duties, but accepting property from others after the fact based on such performance of duties.  
If a state functionary solicits or accepts property from a subordinate with whom there is a superior-subordinate relationship or from a person under management with whom there is an administrative management relationship, and the value of the property is 30,000 yuan or more, and it may affect the exercise of authority, it shall be deemed as promising to seek interests for others.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　根据行贿犯罪的事实、情节，可能被判处三年有期徒刑以下刑罚的，可以认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款规定的“犯罪较轻”。  
根据犯罪的事实、情节，已经或者可能被判处十年有期徒刑以上刑罚的，或者案件在本省、自治区、直辖市或者全国范围内有较大影响的，可以认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款规定的“重大案件”。  
具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款规定的“对侦破重大案件起关键作用”：  
（一）主动交待办案机关未掌握的重大案件线索的；  
（二）主动交待的犯罪线索不属于重大案件的线索，但该线索对于重大案件侦破有重要作用的；  
（三）主动交待行贿事实，对于重大案件的证据收集有重要作用的；  
（四）主动交待行贿事实，对于重大案件的追逃、追赃有重要作用的。

**Article 14** If, based on the facts and circumstances of the bribery crime, the offender may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of less than three years, it may be deemed as "relatively minor crime" as prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If, based on the facts and circumstances of the crime, the offender has been or may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, or if the case has a relatively significant influence within the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or nationwide, it may be deemed as "major case" as prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, it may be deemed as "playing a key role in the detection of a major case" as prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) voluntarily confessing leads of major cases not known to the case-handling authorities;  
(2) voluntarily confessing criminal leads that are not leads of major cases, but such leads play an important role in the detection of major cases;  
(3) voluntarily confessing the facts of bribery, which play an important role in the collection of evidence for major cases;  
(4) voluntarily confessing the facts of bribery, which play an important role in the pursuit of fugitives and recovery of illicit gains in major cases.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对多次受贿未经处理的，累计计算受贿数额。  
国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益前后多次收受请托人财物，受请托之前收受的财物数额在一万元以上的，应当一并计入受贿数额。

**Article 15** If multiple acts of acceptance of bribes have not been dealt with, the amounts shall be accumulated and calculated.  
If a state functionary, before and after using his position to seek interests for the person making the request, accepts property from such person on multiple occasions, and the amount of property accepted before the request is 10,000 yuan or more, such amount shall be included in the total amount of bribes accepted.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　国家工作人员出于贪污、受贿的故意，非法占有公共财物、收受他人财物之后，将赃款赃物用于单位公务支出或者社会捐赠的，不影响贪污罪、受贿罪的认定，但量刑时可以酌情考虑。  
特定关系人索取、收受他人财物，国家工作人员知道后未退还或者上交的，应当认定国家工作人员具有受贿故意。

**Article 16** If a state functionary, with the intention of embezzlement or acceptance of bribes, unlawfully takes possession of public property or accepts property from others, and after that, uses the illicit gains for official expenses of the unit or social donations, this shall not affect the determination of the crime of embezzlement or acceptance of bribes, but may be considered as a discretionary circumstance at sentencing.  
If a specific related person solicits or accepts property from others, and the state functionary, after knowing this, does not return or surrender it, the state functionary shall be deemed to have the intention to accept bribes.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　国家工作人员利用职务上的便利，收受他人财物，为他人谋取利益，同时构成受贿罪和刑法分则第三章第三节、第九章规定的渎职犯罪的，除刑法另有规定外，以受贿罪和渎职犯罪数罪并罚。

**Article 17** If a state functionary, by taking advantage of his position, accepts property from others and seeks interests for others, and concurrently constitutes the crime of acceptance of bribes and a crime of dereliction of duty as prescribed in Section 3 of Chapter III and Chapter IX of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, unless otherwise provided by the Criminal Law, the offender shall be punished for both the crime of acceptance of bribes and the crime of dereliction of duty in accordance with the principle of combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　贪污贿赂犯罪分子违法所得的一切财物，应当依照[刑法第六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-64)的规定予以追缴或者责令退赔，对被害人的合法财产应当及时返还。对尚未追缴到案或者尚未足额退赔的违法所得，应当继续追缴或者责令退赔。

**Article 18** All property obtained illegally by criminals of embezzlement or bribery shall be recovered or ordered to be returned in accordance with Article 64 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the lawful property of the victims shall be returned promptly. If the illegal gains have not yet been recovered or fully returned, they shall continue to be recovered or the offender shall be ordered to return them.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对贪污罪、受贿罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役的，应当并处十万元以上五十万元以下的罚金；判处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑的，应当并处二十万元以上犯罪数额二倍以下的罚金或者没收财产；判处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑的，应当并处五十万元以上犯罪数额二倍以下的罚金或者没收财产。  
对刑法规定并处罚金的其他贪污贿赂犯罪，应当在十万元以上犯罪数额二倍以下判处罚金。

**Article 19** If a person is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention for the crime of embezzlement or acceptance of bribes, a fine of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than 500,000 yuan shall also be imposed; if a person is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, a fine of not less than 200,000 yuan but not more than two times the amount of the crime shall also be imposed, or property shall be confiscated; if a person is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, a fine of not less than 500,000 yuan but not more than two times the amount of the crime shall also be imposed, or property shall be confiscated.  
For other crimes of embezzlement or bribery that are subject to a fine as provided by the Criminal Law, a fine shall be imposed in an amount of not less than 100,000 yuan but not more than two times the amount of the crime.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本解释自2016年4月18日起施行。最高人民法院、最高人民检察院此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 20** This Interpretation shall come into force on April 18, 2016. If any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
