# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理渎职刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（一）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕18号  
**公布日期**：2012-07-09  
**生效日期**：2013-01-09

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理渎职刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（一）》已于2012年7月9日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1552次会议、2012年9月12日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第79次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年1月9日起施行。 2012年12月7日 最高人民法院　最高人民检察院 关于办理渎职刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释（一）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)规定的“致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失”：  
（一）造成死亡1人以上，或者重伤3人以上，或者轻伤9人以上，或者重伤2人、轻伤3人以上，或者重伤1人、轻伤6人以上的；  
（二）造成经济损失30万元以上的；  
（三）造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（四）其他致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）造成伤亡达到前款第（一）项规定人数3倍以上的；  
（二）造成经济损失150万元以上的；  
（三）造成前款规定的损失后果，不报、迟报、谎报或者授意、指使、强令他人不报、迟报、谎报事故情况，致使损失后果持续、扩大或者抢救工作延误的；  
（四）造成特别恶劣社会影响的；  
（五）其他特别严重的情节。

**Article 1** Where a functionary of a State organ abuses his power or neglects his duty, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as “causing heavy losses to public property or the interests of the State and the people” as prescribed in Article 397 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing death of one or more persons, or serious injury to three or more persons, or minor injury to nine or more persons, or serious injury to two persons and minor injury to three or more persons, or serious injury to one person and minor injury to six or more persons;  
(2) causing economic losses of 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) causing a vile social impact;  
(4) other circumstances causing heavy losses to public property or the interests of the State and the people.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as “the circumstances being especially serious” as prescribed in Article 397 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing casualties reaching three times or more the number of persons specified in item (1) of the preceding paragraph;  
(2) causing economic losses of 1.5 million yuan or more;  
(3) causing the loss consequences as specified in the preceding paragraph and failing to report, delaying to report, making false reports, or instigating, instructing, or compelling others not to report, delay reporting, or make false reports on the accident, resulting in the continuation or expansion of the loss consequences or delay in rescue work;  
(4) causing a particularly vile social impact;  
(5) other circumstances of especially serious nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   国家机关工作人员实施滥用职权或者玩忽职守犯罪行为，触犯刑法分则第九章第三百九十八条至第四百一十九条规定的，依照该规定定罪处罚。  
国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，因不具备徇私舞弊等情形，不符合刑法分则第九章第三百九十八条至第四百一十九条的规定，但依法构成第三百九十七条规定的犯罪的，以滥用职权罪或者玩忽职守罪定罪处罚。

**Article 2** Where a functionary of a State organ commits a crime of abusing his power or neglecting his duty and violates the provisions of Articles 398 through 419 of Chapter IX of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with those provisions.  
Where a functionary of a State organ abuses his power or neglects his duty, but does not meet the requirements of the provisions of Articles 398 through 419 of Chapter IX of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law due to the absence of such circumstances as engaging in malpractices for personal gain, and yet his act constitutes a crime under Article 397 according to law, he shall be convicted and punished with the crime of abuse of power or the crime of neglect of duty.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   国家机关工作人员实施渎职犯罪并收受贿赂，同时构成受贿罪的，除刑法另有规定外，以渎职犯罪和受贿罪数罪并罚。

**Article 3** Where a functionary of a State organ commits a crime of dereliction of duty and accepts bribes, which also constitutes the crime of accepting bribes, unless otherwise provided in the Criminal Law, he shall be punished for the crime of dereliction of duty and the crime of accepting bribes in accordance with the rule of combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   国家机关工作人员实施渎职行为，放纵他人犯罪或者帮助他人逃避刑事处罚，构成犯罪的，依照渎职罪的规定定罪处罚。  
国家机关工作人员与他人共谋，利用其职务行为帮助他人实施其他犯罪行为，同时构成渎职犯罪和共谋实施的其他犯罪共犯的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
国家机关工作人员与他人共谋，既利用其职务行为帮助他人实施其他犯罪，又以非职务行为与他人共同实施该其他犯罪行为，同时构成渎职犯罪和其他犯罪的共犯的，依照数罪并罚的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where a functionary of a State organ commits an act of dereliction of duty, indulges another person's crime or helps another person to evade criminal punishment, and the act constitutes a crime, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions on crimes of dereliction of duty.  
Where a functionary of a State organ conspires with another person, uses his official act to help the other person to commit another crime, and the act concurrently constitutes a crime of dereliction of duty and being an accomplice in the other crime committed through conspiracy, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions on the heavier punishment.  
Where a functionary of a State organ conspires with another person, both uses his official act to help the other person to commit another crime and jointly commits that other crime with the other person through a non-official act, and the act concurrently constitutes a crime of dereliction of duty and being an accomplice in the other crime, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   国家机关负责人员违法决定，或者指使、授意、强令其他国家机关工作人员违法履行职务或者不履行职务，构成刑法分则第九章规定的渎职犯罪的，应当依法追究刑事责任。  
以“集体研究”形式实施的渎职犯罪，应当依照刑法分则第九章的规定追究国家机关负有责任的人员的刑事责任。对于具体执行人员，应当在综合认定其行为性质、是否提出反对意见、危害结果大小等情节的基础上决定是否追究刑事责任和应当判处的刑罚。

**Article 5** Where a person in charge of a State organ makes an illegal decision, or instigates, instructs, or compels other functionaries of the State organ to perform their duties illegally or fail to perform their duties, and the act constitutes a crime of dereliction of duty as prescribed in Chapter IX of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
For a crime of dereliction of duty committed in the form of “collective study,” criminal liability shall be investigated against the responsible personnel of the State organ in accordance with the provisions of Chapter IX of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law. With respect to the specific executing personnel, whether to investigate criminal liability and the sentence to be imposed shall be determined on the basis of a comprehensive assessment of circumstances such as the nature of their act, whether they raised objections, and the extent of harmful consequences.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   以危害结果为条件的渎职犯罪的追诉期限，从危害结果发生之日起计算；有数个危害结果的，从最后一个危害结果发生之日起计算。

**Article 6** The limitation of action for prosecution of a crime of dereliction of duty that requires a harmful consequence as a condition shall be calculated from the date on which the harmful consequence occurs; where there are several harmful consequences, it shall be calculated from the date on which the last harmful consequence occurs.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   依法或者受委托行使国家行政管理职权的公司、企业、事业单位的工作人员，在行使行政管理职权时滥用职权或者玩忽职守，构成犯罪的，应当依照《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于〈中华人民共和国刑法〉第九章渎职罪主体适用问题的解释》的规定，适用渎职罪的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 7** Where a staff member of a company, enterprise, or institution that exercises State administrative powers according to law or by entrustment, abuses his power or neglects his duty while exercising such administrative powers, and the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated by applying the provisions on crimes of dereliction of duty in accordance with the Interpretation of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the Application of the Subject of Crimes of Dereliction of Duty in Chapter IX of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   本解释规定的“经济损失”，是指渎职犯罪或者与渎职犯罪相关联的犯罪立案时已经实际造成的财产损失，包括为挽回渎职犯罪所造成损失而支付的各种开支、费用等。立案后至提起公诉前持续发生的经济损失，应一并计入渎职犯罪造成的经济损失。  
债务人经法定程序被宣告破产，债务人潜逃、去向不明，或者因行为人的责任超过诉讼时效等，致使债权已经无法实现的，无法实现的债权部分应当认定为渎职犯罪的经济损失。  
渎职犯罪或者与渎职犯罪相关联的犯罪立案后，犯罪分子及其亲友自行挽回的经济损失，司法机关或者犯罪分子所在单位及其上级主管部门挽回的经济损失，或者因客观原因减少的经济损失，不予扣减，但可以作为酌定从轻处罚的情节。

**Article 8** The term “economic losses” as provided in this Interpretation means the property losses actually caused at the time of filing a case concerning a crime of dereliction of duty or a crime related to dereliction of duty, including various expenses and costs paid to recover the losses caused by the crime of dereliction of duty. The economic losses continuously incurred after the filing of the case and before the initiation of a public prosecution shall be included in the economic losses caused by the crime of dereliction of duty.  
Where a debtor is declared bankrupt through legal procedures, or the debtor absconds or his whereabouts are unknown, or due to the actor's responsibility the limitation of action is exceeded, resulting in the inability to realize the creditor's rights, the part of the creditor's rights that cannot be realized shall be deemed as the economic losses of the crime of dereliction of duty.  
After the filing of a case concerning a crime of dereliction of duty or a crime related to dereliction of duty, the economic losses recovered by the criminal himself or his relatives and friends, the economic losses recovered by the judicial organs or the unit where the criminal works or its superior competent department, or the economic losses reduced due to objective reasons shall not be deducted, but may be taken as a circumstance for discretionary light punishment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   负有监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，致使不符合安全标准的食品、有毒有害食品、假药、劣药等流入社会，对人民群众生命、健康造成严重危害后果的，依照渎职罪的规定从严惩处。

**Article 9** Where a functionary of a State organ with supervisory and administrative duties abuses his power or neglects his duty, resulting in the circulation of food that does not meet safety standards, toxic or harmful food, counterfeit drugs, inferior drugs, etc. into society, causing serious harm to the life and health of the people, he shall be severely punished in accordance with the provisions on crimes of dereliction of duty.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   最高人民法院、最高人民检察院此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 10** Where the judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
