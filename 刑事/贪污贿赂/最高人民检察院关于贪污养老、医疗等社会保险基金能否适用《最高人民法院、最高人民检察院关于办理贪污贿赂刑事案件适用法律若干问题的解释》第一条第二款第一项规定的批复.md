各省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院，解放军军事检察院，新疆生产建设兵团人民检察院：  
近来，一些地方人民检察院就贪污养老、医疗等社会保险基金能否适用[《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第一条](../../刑事/贪污贿赂/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-1)第二款第一项规定请示我院。经研究，批复如下：  
养老、医疗、工伤、失业、生育等社会保险基金可以认定为[《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》第一条](../../刑事/贪污贿赂/最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理贪污贿赂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释.md#art-1)第二款第一项规定的“特定款物”。  
根据刑法和有关司法解释规定，贪污罪和挪用公款罪中的“特定款物”的范围有所不同，实践中应注意区分，依法适用。  
此复。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
July 26, 2017  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
Reply on Whether the Embezzlement of Old-Age Insurance, Medical Insurance, and Other Social Insurance Funds Shall Be Subject to Item (I) of Paragraph 2 of Article 1 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Handling of Criminal Cases of Embezzlement and Bribery  
(Adopted at the 67th Session of the Twelfth Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on July 19, 2017)  
Gao Jian Fa Shi Zi [2017] No. 1  
To the People's Procuratorates of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; the People's Procuratorate of the People's Liberation Army; and the People's Procuratorate of Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps:  
  
Recently, some local people's procuratorates have petitioned our Procuratorate for instructions on whether the embezzlement of old-age insurance, medical insurance, and other social insurance funds shall be subject to the provisions of Item (I) of Paragraph 2 of Article 1 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Handling of Criminal Cases of Embezzlement and Bribery. Upon deliberation, the reply is as follows:  
  
Old-age insurance, medical insurance, work-related injury insurance, unemployment insurance, maternity insurance, and other social insurance funds may be identified as "specific funds and materials" as provided for in Item (I) of Paragraph 2 of Article 1 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Handling of Criminal Cases of Embezzlement and Bribery.  
  
In accordance with the Criminal Law and relevant judicial interpretations, the scope of "specific funds and materials" in the crime of embezzlement and the crime of misappropriation of public funds differs. In practice, attention shall be paid to distinguishing them and applying the law accordingly.  
  
It is hereby replied.  
  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
July 26, 2017
