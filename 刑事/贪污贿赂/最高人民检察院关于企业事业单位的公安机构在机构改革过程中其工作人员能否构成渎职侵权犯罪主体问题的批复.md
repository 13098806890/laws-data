最高人民检察院关于企业事业单位的公安机构在机构改革过程中其工作人员能否构成渎职  
侵权犯罪主体问题的批复  
（2002年4月24日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会  
第一百零七次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔2002〕3号  
陕西省人民检察院：  
你院陕检发研〔2001〕159号《关于对企业事业单位的公安机构在机构改革过程中其工作人员能否构成渎职侵权犯罪主体问题的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
企业事业单位的公安机构在机构改革过程中虽尚未列入公安机关建制，其工作人员在行使侦查职责时，实施渎职侵权行为的，可以成为渎职侵权犯罪的主体。  
此复。  
最高人民检察院  
2002年7月9日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
April 29, 2002  
Reply of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Whether the Staff Members of Public Security Organs in Enterprises and Institutions May Constitute the Subjects of Crimes of Dereliction of Duty and Infringement of Rights during the Institutional Reform Process  
(Adopted by the 107th Session of the Judicial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on April 24, 2002)  
Gao Jian Fa Shi Zi [2002] No. 3  
Shaanxi Provincial People's Procuratorate:  
Your petition, Shan Jian Fa Yan [2001] No. 159, titled "Request for Instructions on Whether the Staff Members of Public Security Organs in Enterprises and Institutions May Constitute the Subjects of Crimes of Dereliction of Duty and Infringement of Rights during the Institutional Reform Process," has been received. Upon deliberation, the reply is as follows:  
Although the public security organs in enterprises and institutions have not yet been incorporated into the establishment of public security organs during the institutional reform process, their staff members, when performing investigation duties and committing acts of dereliction of duty or infringement of rights, may constitute the subjects of crimes of dereliction of duty and infringement of rights.  
This reply is hereby given.  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
July 9, 2002
