# 人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2002-03-25

> （2002年3月25日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会第一百零五次会议通过　2006年12月28日最高人民检察院第十届检察委员会第六十八次会议第一次修订　2013年12月19日最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第十四次会议第二次修订） 第一章　总　则 第一条　为了切实保障未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人和未成年罪犯的合法权益，正确履行检察职责，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国未成年人保护法》、《中华人民共和国预防未成年人犯罪法》、《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则(试行)》等有关规定，结合人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件工作实际，制定本规定。 第二条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，实行教育、感化、挽救的方针，坚持教育为主、惩罚为辅和特殊保护的原则。在严格遵守法律规定的前提下，按照最有利于未成年人和适合未成年人身心特点的方式进行，充分保障未成年人合法权益。 第三条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当保障未成年人依法行使其诉讼权利，保障未成年人得到法律帮助。 第四条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当在依照法定程序和保证办案质量的前提下，快速办理，减少刑事诉讼对未成年人的不利影响。 第五条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当依法保护涉案未成年人的名誉，尊重其人格尊严，不得公开或者传播涉案未成年人的姓名、住所、照片、图像及可能推断出该未成年人的资料。 人民检察院办理刑事案件，应当依法保护未成年被害人、证人以及其他与案件有关的未成年人的合法权益。 第六条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当加强与公安机关、人民法院以及司法行政机关的联系，注意工作各环节的衔接和配合，共同做好对涉案未成年人的教育、感化、挽救工作。 人民检察院应当加强同政府有关部门、共青团、妇联、工会等人民团体，学校、基层组织以及未成年人保护组织的联系和配合，加强对违法犯罪的未成年人的教育和挽救，共同做好未成年人犯罪预防工作。 第七条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，发现有关单位或者部门在预防未成年人违法犯罪等方面制度不落实、不健全，存在管理漏洞的，可以采取检察建议等方式向有关单位或者部门提出预防违法犯罪的意见和建议。 第八条　省级、地市级人民检察院和未成年人刑事案件较多的基层人民检察院，应当设立独立的未成年人刑事检察机构。地市级人民检察院也可以根据当地实际，指定一个基层人民检察院设立独立机构，统一办理辖区范围内的未成年人刑事案件；条件暂不具备的，应当成立专门办案组或者指定专人办理。对于专门办案组或者专人，应当保证其集中精力办理未成年人刑事案件，研究未成年人犯罪规律，落实对涉案未成年人的帮教措施等工作。 各级人民检察院应当选任经过专门培训，熟悉未成年人身心特点，具有犯罪学、社会学、心理学、教育学等方面知识的检察人员承办未成年人刑事案件，并加强对办案人员的培训和指导。 第九条　人民检察院根据情况可以对未成年犯罪嫌疑人的成长经历、犯罪原因、监护教育等情况进行调查，并制作社会调查报告，作为办案和教育的参考。 人民检察院开展社会调查，可以委托有关组织和机构进行。开展社会调查应当尊重和保护未成年人名誉，避免向不知情人员泄露未成年犯罪嫌疑人的涉罪信息。

---

### 第一章　总　则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了切实保障未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人和未成年罪犯的合法权益，正确履行检察职责，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国未成年人保护法》、《中华人民共和国预防未成年人犯罪法》、《人民检察院刑事诉讼规则(试行)》等有关规定，结合人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件工作实际，制定本规定。

**Article 1** : These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Minors, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention of Juvenile Delinquency, the Criminal Procedure Rules of the People's Procuratorate (for Trial Implementation), and other relevant regulations, and in light of the actual work of the People's Procuratorate in handling criminal cases involving minors, for the purpose of effectively safeguarding the legitimate rights and interests of juvenile criminal suspects, defendants, and juvenile offenders, and correctly performing prosecutorial duties.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，实行教育、感化、挽救的方针，坚持教育为主、惩罚为辅和特殊保护的原则。在严格遵守法律规定的前提下，按照最有利于未成年人和适合未成年人身心特点的方式进行，充分保障未成年人合法权益。

**Article 2** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, the People's Procuratorate shall implement the policy of education, persuasion, and redemption, adhere to the principles of focusing on education, supplementing punishment, and providing special protection. Under the precondition of strictly complying with legal provisions, it shall proceed in a manner that is most beneficial to minors and suitable for their physical and mental characteristics, and fully safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of minors.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当保障未成年人依法行使其诉讼权利，保障未成年人得到法律帮助。

**Article 3** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, the People's Procuratorate shall ensure that minors exercise their litigation rights according to law and guarantee that minors receive legal assistance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当在依照法定程序和保证办案质量的前提下，快速办理，减少刑事诉讼对未成年人的不利影响。

**Article 4** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, the People's Procuratorate shall, on the premise of following statutory procedures and ensuring case handling quality, handle the case promptly to reduce the adverse impact of criminal proceedings on minors.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当依法保护涉案未成年人的名誉，尊重其人格尊严，不得公开或者传播涉案未成年人的姓名、住所、照片、图像及可能推断出该未成年人的资料。  
人民检察院办理刑事案件，应当依法保护未成年被害人、证人以及其他与案件有关的未成年人的合法权益。

**Article 5** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, the People's Procuratorate shall protect the reputation of the minors involved according to law, respect their human dignity, and shall not disclose or disseminate the name, domicile, photographs, images, or any information that could lead to the identification of the minors involved.  
When handling criminal cases, the People's Procuratorate shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of minor victims, witnesses, and other minors related to the case according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当加强与公安机关、人民法院以及司法行政机关的联系，注意工作各环节的衔接和配合，共同做好对涉案未成年人的教育、感化、挽救工作。  
人民检察院应当加强同政府有关部门、共青团、妇联、工会等人民团体，学校、基层组织以及未成年人保护组织的联系和配合，加强对违法犯罪的未成年人的教育和挽救，共同做好未成年人犯罪预防工作。

**Article 6** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, the People's Procuratorate shall strengthen contact with public security organs, People's Courts, and judicial administrative organs, pay attention to the connection and coordination of various work stages, and jointly carry out the work of education, persuasion, and redemption of the minors involved.  
The People's Procuratorate shall strengthen contact and coordination with relevant government departments, the Communist Youth League, the Women's Federation, trade unions, and other people's organizations, schools, grassroots organizations, and organizations for the protection of minors, strengthen the education and redemption of minors who have committed illegal acts, and jointly carry out the work of preventing juvenile delinquency.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，发现有关单位或者部门在预防未成年人违法犯罪等方面制度不落实、不健全，存在管理漏洞的，可以采取检察建议等方式向有关单位或者部门提出预防违法犯罪的意见和建议。

**Article 7** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, if the People's Procuratorate discovers that relevant units or departments fail to implement or have incomplete systems in preventing juvenile delinquency or other aspects, and there are management loopholes, it may submit opinions and suggestions on crime prevention to such units or departments by means of procuratorial suggestions.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　省级、地市级人民检察院和未成年人刑事案件较多的基层人民检察院，应当设立独立的未成年人刑事检察机构。地市级人民检察院也可以根据当地实际，指定一个基层人民检察院设立独立机构，统一办理辖区范围内的未成年人刑事案件；条件暂不具备的，应当成立专门办案组或者指定专人办理。对于专门办案组或者专人，应当保证其集中精力办理未成年人刑事案件，研究未成年人犯罪规律，落实对涉案未成年人的帮教措施等工作。  
各级人民检察院应当选任经过专门培训，熟悉未成年人身心特点，具有犯罪学、社会学、心理学、教育学等方面知识的检察人员承办未成年人刑事案件，并加强对办案人员的培训和指导。

**Article 8** : Provincial, prefectural and municipal People's Procuratorates, and grassroots People's Procuratorates that handle a large number of criminal cases involving minors, shall establish independent juvenile criminal prosecutorial organs. Prefectural and municipal People's Procuratorates may also, based on local conditions, designate a grassroots People's Procuratorate to establish an independent organ to uniformly handle criminal cases involving minors within its jurisdiction; if conditions are temporarily unavailable, a special case-handling team shall be established or a designated person shall be assigned to handle such cases. For the special case-handling team or designated person, it shall be ensured that they concentrate their efforts on handling criminal cases involving minors, studying the patterns of juvenile delinquency, and implementing measures for assistance and education of the minors involved.  
People's Procuratorates at all levels shall select and appoint prosecutorial personnel who have undergone special training, are familiar with the physical and mental characteristics of minors, and possess knowledge of criminology, sociology, psychology, education, and other relevant fields to handle criminal cases involving minors, and shall strengthen the training and guidance of case-handling personnel.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民检察院根据情况可以对未成年犯罪嫌疑人的成长经历、犯罪原因、监护教育等情况进行调查，并制作社会调查报告，作为办案和教育的参考。  
人民检察院开展社会调查，可以委托有关组织和机构进行。开展社会调查应当尊重和保护未成年人名誉，避免向不知情人员泄露未成年犯罪嫌疑人的涉罪信息。  
人民检察院应当对公安机关移送的社会调查报告进行审查，必要时可以进行补充调查。  
提起公诉的案件，社会调查报告应当随案移送人民法院。

**Article 9** : The People's Procuratorate may, depending on the circumstances, investigate the growth experience, causes of the crime, guardianship and education, and other aspects of a juvenile criminal suspect, and produce a social investigation report to serve as a reference for case handling and education.  
When conducting a social investigation, the People's Procuratorate may entrust relevant organizations and institutions to carry it out. The social investigation shall respect and protect the reputation of the minor, and shall avoid disclosing to uninformed persons information about the criminal involvement of the juvenile criminal suspect.  
The People's Procuratorate shall review the social investigation report submitted by the public security organ, and may conduct supplementary investigation if necessary.  
For cases where a public prosecution is initiated, the social investigation report shall be transferred to the People's Court together with the case files.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，可以应犯罪嫌疑人家属、被害人及其家属的要求，告知其审查逮捕、审查起诉的进展情况，并对有关情况予以说明和解释。

**Article 10** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, the People's Procuratorate may, upon the request of the criminal suspect's family members, the victim, or his or her family members, inform them of the progress of the review of arrest and review for prosecution, and provide explanations and clarifications on relevant matters.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民检察院受理案件后，应当向未成年犯罪嫌疑人及其法定代理人了解其委托辩护人的情况，并告知其有权委托辩护人。  
未成年犯罪嫌疑人没有委托辩护人的，人民检察院应当书面通知法律援助机构指派律师为其提供辩护。

**Article 11** : After accepting a case, the People's Procuratorate shall inquire with the juvenile criminal suspect and his or her legal representative about the status of retaining a defender, and shall inform them of the right to retain a defender.  
If the juvenile criminal suspect does not retain a defender, the People's Procuratorate shall notify the legal aid authority in writing to assign a lawyer to provide defense for him or her.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件，应当注重矛盾化解，认真听取被害人的意见，做好释法说理工作。对于符合和解条件的，要发挥检调对接平台作用，积极促使双方当事人达成和解。  
人民检察院应当充分维护未成年被害人的合法权益。对于符合条件的被害人，应当及时启动刑事被害人救助程序，对其进行救助。对于未成年被害人，可以适当放宽救助条件、扩大救助的案件范围。  
人民检察院根据需要，可以对未成年犯罪嫌疑人、未成年被害人进行心理疏导。必要时，经未成年犯罪嫌疑人及其法定代理人同意，可以对未成年犯罪嫌疑人进行心理测评。  
在办理未成年人刑事案件时，人民检察院应当加强办案风险评估预警工作，主动采取适当措施，积极回应和引导社会舆论，有效防范执法办案风险。

**Article 12** : When handling criminal cases involving minors, the People's Procuratorate shall focus on conflict resolution, earnestly listen to the opinions of the victim, and carry out the work of explaining the law and reasoning. Where conditions for reconciliation are met, the platform for connection between prosecution and mediation shall be utilized to actively promote reconciliation between the parties.  
The People's Procuratorate shall fully safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of minor victims. For victims who meet the conditions, the procedure for providing assistance to criminal victims shall be promptly initiated to provide them with assistance. For minor victims, the conditions for assistance may be appropriately relaxed and the scope of cases eligible for assistance may be expanded.  
The People's Procuratorate may, as needed, provide psychological counseling to juvenile criminal suspects and minor victims. When necessary, with the consent of the juvenile criminal suspect and his or her legal representative, a psychological assessment may be conducted on the juvenile criminal suspect.  
When handling criminal cases involving minors, the People's Procuratorate shall strengthen the work of risk assessment and early warning for case handling, proactively take appropriate measures, actively respond to and guide public opinion, and effectively prevent risks in law enforcement and case handling.

### 第二章　未成年人刑事案件的审查逮捕

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民检察院办理未成年犯罪嫌疑人审查逮捕案件，应当根据未成年犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌犯罪的事实、主观恶性、有无监护与社会帮教条件等，综合衡量其社会危险性，严格限制适用逮捕措施，可捕可不捕的不捕。

**Article 13** : When handling cases of reviewing arrest of a juvenile criminal suspect, the People's Procuratorate shall comprehensively assess his or her social dangerousness based on the facts of the alleged crime, subjective malignancy, and whether there are conditions of guardianship and social assistance and education, strictly restrict the application of arrest measures, and shall not arrest where arrest may or may not be necessary.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　审查逮捕未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当重点审查其是否已满十四、十六、十八周岁。  
对犯罪嫌疑人实际年龄难以判断，影响对该犯罪嫌疑人是否应当负刑事责任认定的，应当不批准逮捕。需要补充侦查的，同时通知公安机关。

**Article 14** : When reviewing the arrest of a juvenile criminal suspect, it shall focus on examining whether he or she has reached the ages of fourteen, sixteen, or eighteen.  
If the actual age of the criminal suspect is difficult to determine and affects the determination of whether the criminal suspect shall bear criminal liability, approval of the arrest shall be denied. If supplementary investigation is necessary, the public security organ shall be notified at the same time.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　审查逮捕未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当审查公安机关依法提供的证据和社会调查报告等材料。公安机关没有提供社会调查报告的，人民检察院根据案件情况可以要求公安机关提供，也可以自行或者委托有关组织和机构进行调查。

**Article 15** : When reviewing the arrest of a juvenile criminal suspect, the materials such as evidence and social investigation reports provided by the public security organ according to law shall be examined. If the public security organ fails to provide a social investigation report, the People's Procuratorate may, based on the circumstances of the case, request the public security organ to provide one, or may conduct the investigation itself or entrust relevant organizations and institutions to do so.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　审查逮捕未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当注意是否有被胁迫、引诱的情节，是否存在成年人教唆犯罪、传授犯罪方法或者利用未成年人实施犯罪的情况。

**Article 16** : When reviewing the arrest of a juvenile criminal suspect, attention shall be paid to whether there are circumstances of coercion or inducement, and whether there exists a situation where an adult instigates the crime, imparts criminal methods, or uses a minor to commit a crime.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　人民检察院办理未成年犯罪嫌疑人审查逮捕案件，应当讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人，听取辩护律师的意见，并制作笔录附卷。  
讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当根据该未成年人的特点和案件情况，制定详细的讯问提纲，采取适宜该未成年人的方式进行，讯问用语应当准确易懂。  
讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当告知其依法享有的诉讼权利，告知其如实供述案件事实的法律规定和意义，核实其是否有自首、立功、坦白等情节，听取其有罪的供述或者无罪、罪轻的辩解。  
讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当通知其法定代理人到场，告知法定代理人依法享有的诉讼权利和应当履行的义务。无法通知、法定代理人不能到场或者法定代理人是共犯的，也可以通知未成年犯罪嫌疑人的其他成年亲属，所在学校、单位或者居住地的村民委员会、居民委员会、未成年人保护组织的代表等合适成年人到场，并将有关情况记录在案。到场的法定代理人可以代为行使未成年犯罪嫌疑人的诉讼权利，行使时不得侵犯未成年犯罪嫌疑人的合法权益。  
未成年犯罪嫌疑人明确拒绝法定代理人以外的合适成年人到场，人民检察院可以准许，但应当另行通知其他合适成年人到场。  
到场的法定代理人或者其他人员认为办案人员在讯问中侵犯未成年犯罪嫌疑人合法权益的，可以提出意见。讯问笔录应当交由到场的法定代理人或者其他人员阅读或者向其宣读，并由其在笔录上签字、盖章或者捺指印确认。  
讯问女性未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当有女性检察人员参加。  
询问未成年被害人、证人，适用本条第四款至第七款的规定。

**Article 17** : When handling a case of reviewing the arrest of a juvenile criminal suspect, the People's Procuratorate shall interrogate the juvenile criminal suspect, hear the opinions of the defense lawyer, and make a record to be attached to the case file.  
When interrogating a juvenile criminal suspect, a detailed interrogation outline shall be formulated based on the characteristics of the minor and the circumstances of the case, and the interrogation shall be conducted in a manner suitable for the minor; the language used in interrogation shall be accurate and easy to understand.  
When interrogating a juvenile criminal suspect, he or she shall be informed of the procedural rights he or she enjoys according to law, informed of the legal provisions and significance of making a truthful confession of the facts of the case, and it shall be verified whether there are circumstances such as voluntary surrender, meritorious service, or confession; his or her confession of guilt or defense of innocence or lesser guilt shall be heard.  
When interrogating a juvenile criminal suspect, his or her legal representative shall be notified to be present, and the legal representative shall be informed of the procedural rights he or she enjoys according to law and the obligations he or she shall perform. If it is impossible to notify, the legal representative cannot be present, or the legal representative is an accomplice, other adult relatives of the juvenile criminal suspect, representatives from his or her school, work unit, or the villagers' committee, residents' committee of his or her place of residence, or an organization for the protection of minors, and other suitable adults may be notified to be present, and the relevant circumstances shall be recorded. The legal representative present may exercise the procedural rights of the juvenile criminal suspect on his or her behalf, but shall not infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of the juvenile criminal suspect when exercising such rights.  
If the juvenile criminal suspect explicitly refuses the presence of a suitable adult other than the legal representative, the People's Procuratorate may permit such refusal, but shall separately notify another suitable adult to be present.  
If the legal representative or other person present believes that the case-handling personnel have infringed upon the legitimate rights and interests of the juvenile criminal suspect during the interrogation, he or she may raise an opinion. The interrogation record shall be given to the legal representative or other person present to read or shall be read out to him or her, and such person shall sign, affix his or her seal, or affix his or her fingerprint on the record for confirmation.  
When interrogating a female juvenile criminal suspect, female prosecutorial personnel shall participate.  
When questioning a minor victim or witness, the provisions of paragraphs 4 to 7 of this Article shall apply.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人一般不得使用械具。对于确有人身危险性，必须使用械具的，在现实危险消除后，应当立即停止使用。

**Article 18** : Instruments of restraint shall generally not be used during the interrogation of a juvenile criminal suspect. If there is indeed personal dangerousness and the use of instruments of restraint is necessary, such use shall cease immediately after the actual danger is eliminated.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　对于罪行较轻，具备有效监护条件或者社会帮教措施，没有社会危险性或者社会危险性较小，不逮捕不致妨害诉讼正常进行的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当不批准逮捕。  
对于罪行比较严重，但主观恶性不大，有悔罪表现，具备有效监护条件或者社会帮教措施，具有下列情形之一，不逮捕不致妨害诉讼正常进行的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，可以不批准逮捕：  
(一)初次犯罪、过失犯罪的；  
(二)犯罪预备、中止、未遂的；  
(三)有自首或者立功表现的；  
(四)犯罪后如实交待罪行，真诚悔罪，积极退赃，尽力减少和赔偿损失，被害人谅解的；  
(五)不属于共同犯罪的主犯或者集团犯罪中的首要分子的；  
(六)属于已满十四周岁不满十六周岁的未成年人或者系在校学生的；  
(七)其他可以不批准逮捕的情形。  
对于不予批准逮捕的案件，应当说明理由，连同案卷材料送达公安机关执行。需要补充侦查的，应当同时通知公安机关。必要时可以向被害方作说明解释。

**Article 19** : For a juvenile criminal suspect whose crime is relatively minor, who has effective guardianship conditions or social assistance and education measures, has no or low social dangerousness, and whose non-arrest would not impede the normal conduct of proceedings, the arrest shall not be approved.  
For a juvenile criminal suspect whose crime is relatively serious, but whose subjective malignancy is not great, who shows repentance, has effective guardianship conditions or social assistance and education measures, and falls under any of the following circumstances, and whose non-arrest would not impede the normal conduct of proceedings, the arrest may not be approved:  
(1) First-time offense or negligent offense;  
(2) Preparation for crime, discontinuation of crime, or attempted crime;  
(3) Voluntary surrender or meritorious service;  
(4) Truthfully confessing the crime after committing it, sincerely repenting, actively returning illicit gains, making every effort to reduce and compensate for losses, and obtaining the victim's forgiveness;  
(5) Not being a principal offender in a joint crime or a principal in a gang crime;  
(6) Being a minor who has reached the age of fourteen but not yet the age of sixteen, or being a student;  
(7) Other circumstances where arrest may not be approved.  
For cases where arrest is not approved, the reasons shall be stated, and the decision together with the case files shall be served on the public security organ for execution. If supplementary investigation is necessary, the public security organ shall be notified at the same time. When necessary, explanations may be provided to the victim.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　适用[本规定第十九条](#art-19)的规定，在作出不批准逮捕决定前，应当审查其监护情况，参考其法定代理人、学校、居住地公安派出所及居民委员会、村民委员会的意见，并在审查逮捕意见书中对未成年犯罪嫌疑人是否具备有效监护条件或者社会帮教措施进行具体说明。

**Article 20** : Before making a decision not to approve arrest under Article 19 of these Provisions, the guardianship status shall be examined, reference shall be made to the opinions of the legal representative, the school, the local public security sub-bureau, and the residents' committee or villagers' committee, and the review opinion on arrest shall specifically explain whether the juvenile criminal suspect has effective guardianship conditions or social assistance and education measures.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　对未成年犯罪嫌疑人作出批准逮捕决定后，应当依法进行羁押必要性审查。对不需要继续羁押的，应当及时建议予以释放或者变更强制措施。

**Article 21** : After making a decision to approve the arrest of a juvenile criminal suspect, a review of the necessity of custody shall be conducted according to law. If continued custody is not necessary, a suggestion for release or alteration of compulsory measures shall be promptly made.

### 第三章　未成年人刑事案件的审查起诉与出庭支持公诉

#### 第一节　审查

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民检察院审查起诉未成年人刑事案件，自收到移送审查起诉的案件材料之日起三日以内，应当告知被害人及其法定代理人或者其近亲属、附带民事诉讼的当事人及其法定代理人有权委托诉讼代理人。  
对未成年被害人或者其法定代理人提出聘请律师意向，但因经济困难或者其他原因没有委托诉讼代理人的，应当帮助其申请法律援助。  
未成年犯罪嫌疑人被羁押的，人民检察院应当审查是否有必要继续羁押。对不需要继续羁押的，应当予以释放或者变更强制措施。  
审查起诉未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当听取其父母或者其他法定代理人、辩护人、被害人及其法定代理人的意见。

**Article 22** : When reviewing for prosecution a criminal case involving a minor, within three days from the date of receiving the case materials transferred for review for prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall inform the victim and his or her legal representative or close relatives, and the parties to the incidental civil action and their legal representatives, of their right to retain a litigation agent.  
If a minor victim or his or her legal representative expresses the intention to retain a lawyer but fails to retain a litigation agent due to financial difficulties or other reasons, the People's Procuratorate shall assist him or her in applying for legal aid.  
If the juvenile criminal suspect is in custody, the People's Procuratorate shall review whether continued custody is necessary. If continued custody is not necessary, the suspect shall be released or compulsory measures shall be altered.  
When reviewing for prosecution a juvenile criminal suspect, the opinions of his or her parents or other legal representatives, defender, the victim, and the victim's legal representative shall be heard.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民检察院审查起诉未成年人刑事案件，应当讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人。讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人适用[本规定第十七条](#art-17)、第十八条的规定。

**Article 23** : When reviewing for prosecution a criminal case involving a minor, the People's Procuratorate shall interrogate the juvenile criminal suspect. The provisions of Articles 17 and 18 of these Provisions shall apply to the interrogation of a juvenile criminal suspect.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　移送审查起诉的案件具备以下条件之一，且其法定代理人、近亲属等与本案无牵连的，经公安机关同意，检察人员可以安排在押的未成年犯罪嫌疑人与其法定代理人、近亲属等进行会见、通话：  
(一)案件事实已基本查清，主要证据确实、充分，安排会见、通话不会影响诉讼活动正常进行；  
(二)未成年犯罪嫌疑人有认罪、悔罪表现，或者虽尚未认罪、悔罪，但通过会见、通话有可能促使其转化，或者通过会见、通话有利于社会、家庭稳定；  
(三)未成年犯罪嫌疑人的法定代理人、近亲属对其犯罪原因、社会危害性以及后果有一定的认识，并能配合司法机关进行教育。

**Article 24** : Where a case transferred for review for prosecution meets any of the following conditions, and the legal representative, close relatives, etc. of the juvenile criminal suspect are not implicated in the case, with the consent of the public security organ, the prosecutorial personnel may arrange for the juvenile criminal suspect in custody to meet or communicate by telephone with his or her legal representative, close relatives, etc.:  
(1) The facts of the case have been basically established, the main evidence is reliable and sufficient, and arranging a meeting or telephone call will not affect the normal conduct of proceedings;  
(2) The juvenile criminal suspect has shown confession or repentance, or although not yet confessed or repented, the meeting or telephone call may promote transformation, or the meeting or telephone call is conducive to social and family stability;  
(3) The legal representative or close relatives of the juvenile criminal suspect have a certain understanding of the cause of the crime, social harmfulness, and consequences, and are able to cooperate with judicial organs in education.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　在押的未成年犯罪嫌疑人同其法定代理人、近亲属等进行会见、通话时，检察人员应当告知其会见、通话不得有串供或者其他妨碍诉讼的内容。会见、通话时检察人员可以在场。会见、通话结束后，检察人员应当将有关内容及时整理并记录在案。

**Article 25** : When a juvenile criminal suspect in custody meets or communicates by telephone with his or her legal representative, close relatives, etc., the prosecutorial personnel shall inform them that the meeting or telephone call shall not involve collusion of confessions or other content that obstructs the proceedings. The prosecutorial personnel may be present during the meeting or telephone call. After the meeting or telephone call concludes, the prosecutorial personnel shall promptly organize the relevant content and record it in the case file.

#### 第二节　不起诉

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　对于犯罪情节轻微，具有下列情形之一，依照刑法规定不需要判处刑罚或者免除刑罚的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，一般应当依法作出不起诉决定：  
(一)被胁迫参与犯罪的；  
(二)犯罪预备、中止、未遂的；  
(三)在共同犯罪中起次要或者辅助作用的；  
(四)系又聋又哑的人或者盲人的；  
(五)因防卫过当或者紧急避险过当构成犯罪的；  
(六)有自首或者立功表现的；  
(七)其他依照刑法规定不需要判处刑罚或者免除刑罚的情形。

**Article 26** : For a juvenile criminal suspect whose criminal circumstances are minor and who falls under any of the following circumstances, and for whom, according to the provisions of criminal law, no punishment is required or a penalty may be exempted, the People's Procuratorate shall generally make a decision not to prosecute according to law:  
(1) Participating in a crime under coercion;  
(2) Preparation for crime, discontinuation of crime, or attempted crime;  
(3) Playing a secondary or auxiliary role in a joint crime;  
(4) Being a deaf-mute or a blind person;  
(5) Constituting a crime due to excessive self-defense or excessive act of rescue;  
(6) Having voluntary surrender or meritorious service;  
(7) Other circumstances where, according to the provisions of criminal law, no punishment is required or a penalty may be exempted.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对于未成年人实施的轻伤害案件、初次犯罪、过失犯罪、犯罪未遂的案件以及被诱骗或者被教唆实施的犯罪案件等，情节轻微，犯罪嫌疑人确有悔罪表现，当事人双方自愿就民事赔偿达成协议并切实履行或者经被害人同意并提供有效担保，符合[刑法第三十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-37)规定的，人民检察院可以依照[刑事诉讼法第一百七十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-173)第二款的规定作出不起诉决定，并可以根据案件的不同情况，予以训诫或者责令具结悔过、赔礼道歉、赔偿损失，或者由主管部门予以行政处罚。

**Article 27** : For cases of minor injury committed by a minor, first-time offenses, negligent offenses, attempted crimes, and cases of crimes committed under inducement or instigation, where the circumstances are minor, the criminal suspect genuinely shows repentance, both parties voluntarily reach an agreement on civil compensation and actually perform it, or the victim agrees and provides effective guarantee, and the conditions set forth in Article 37 of the Criminal Law are met, the People's Procuratorate may make a decision not to prosecute in accordance with the provisions of Article 173, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law, and may, depending on the circumstances of the case, administer a reprimand or order the suspect to sign a statement of repentance, apologize, or compensate for losses, or may refer the matter to the competent authority for administrative sanctions.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　不起诉决定书应当向被不起诉的未成年人及其法定代理人宣布，并阐明不起诉的理由和法律依据。  
不起诉决定书应当送达公安机关，被不起诉的未成年人及其法定代理人、辩护人，被害人或者其近亲属及其诉讼代理人。  
送达时，应当告知被害人或者其近亲属及其诉讼代理人，如果对不起诉决定不服，可以自收到不起诉决定书后七日以内向上一级人民检察院申诉，也可以不经申诉，直接向人民法院起诉；告知被不起诉的未成年人及其法定代理人，如果对不起诉决定不服，可以自收到不起诉决定书后七日以内向人民检察院申诉。

**Article 28** : The decision not to prosecute shall be announced to the minor who is not prosecuted and his or her legal representative, and the reasons and legal basis for the non-prosecution shall be explained.  
The decision not to prosecute shall be served on the public security organ, the minor not prosecuted and his or her legal representative and defender, and the victim or his or her close relatives and their litigation agent.  
Upon service, the victim or his or her close relatives and their litigation agent shall be informed that if they refuse to accept the decision not to prosecute, they may file a petition with the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level within seven days from receipt of the decision, or may directly file a lawsuit with the People's Court without filing a petition; the minor not prosecuted and his or her legal representative shall be informed that if they refuse to accept the decision not to prosecute, they may file a petition with the People's Procuratorate within seven days from receipt of the decision.

#### 第三节　附条件不起诉

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　对于犯罪时已满十四周岁不满十八周岁的未成年人，同时符合下列条件的，人民检察院可以作出附条件不起诉决定：  
(一)涉嫌刑法分则第四章、第五章、第六章规定的犯罪；  
(二)根据具体犯罪事实、情节，可能被判处一年有期徒刑以下刑罚；  
(三)犯罪事实清楚，证据确实、充分，符合起诉条件；  
(四)具有悔罪表现。

**Article 29** : For a minor who has reached the age of fourteen but not yet the age of eighteen at the time of committing the crime, and who meets all of the following conditions, the People's Procuratorate may make a decision to attach conditions to non-prosecution:  
(1) Allegedly committing a crime as provided for in Chapters IV, V, and VI of the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law;  
(2) Based on the specific facts and circumstances of the crime, the likely punishment would be a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of one year or less;  
(3) The facts of the crime are clear, the evidence is reliable and sufficient, and the conditions for prosecution are met;  
(4) Showing repentance.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民检察院在作出附条件不起诉的决定以前，应当听取公安机关、被害人、未成年犯罪嫌疑人的法定代理人、辩护人的意见，并制作笔录附卷。被害人是未成年人的，还应当听取被害人的法定代理人、诉讼代理人的意见。

**Article 30** : Before making a decision to attach conditions to non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall hear the opinions of the public security organ, the victim, the legal representative of the juvenile criminal suspect, and the defender, and shall make a record to be attached to the case file. If the victim is a minor, the opinions of the victim's legal representative and litigation agent also shall be heard.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　公安机关或者被害人对附条件不起诉有异议或争议较大的案件，人民检察院可以召集侦查人员、被害人及其法定代理人、诉讼代理人、未成年犯罪嫌疑人及其法定代理人、辩护人举行不公开听证会，充分听取各方的意见和理由。  
对于决定附条件不起诉可能激化矛盾或者引发不稳定因素的，人民检察院应当慎重适用。

**Article 31** : In cases where the public security organ or the victim objects to or has significant disputes over the conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate may convene a closed hearing with investigators, the victim and his or her legal representative and litigation agent, the juvenile criminal suspect and his or her legal representative, and the defender, to fully hear the opinions and reasons of all parties.  
If a decision to attach conditions to non-prosecution may escalate conflicts or trigger instability, the People's Procuratorate shall exercise caution in applying it.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　适用附条件不起诉的审查意见，应当由办案人员在审查起诉期限届满十五日前提出，并根据案件的具体情况拟定考验期限和考察方案，连同案件审查报告、社会调查报告等，经部门负责人审核，报检察长或者检察委员会决定。

**Article 32** : The review opinion on the application of conditional non-prosecution shall be submitted by the case-handling personnel no later than fifteen days before the expiration of the time limit for review for prosecution, and the probation period and supervision plan shall be drafted based on the specific circumstances of the case. Together with the case review report, social investigation report, etc., it shall be reviewed by the head of the department and submitted to the Chief Prosecutor or the Prosecutorial Committee for decision.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　人民检察院作出附条件不起诉的决定后，应当制作附条件不起诉决定书，并在三日以内送达公安机关、被害人或者其近亲属及其诉讼代理人、未成年犯罪嫌疑人及其法定代理人、辩护人。  
送达时，应当告知被害人或者其近亲属及其诉讼代理人，如果对附条件不起诉决定不服，可以自收到附条件不起诉决定书后七日以内向上一级人民检察院申诉。  
人民检察院应当当面向未成年犯罪嫌疑人及其法定代理人宣布附条件不起诉决定，告知考验期限、在考验期内应当遵守的规定和违反规定应负的法律责任，以及可以对附条件不起诉决定提出异议，并制作笔录附卷。

**Article 33** : After making a decision to attach conditions to non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall produce a written decision on conditional non-prosecution and, within three days, serve it on the public security organ, the victim or his or her close relatives and their litigation agent, and the juvenile criminal suspect and his or her legal representative and defender.  
Upon service, the victim or his or her close relatives and their litigation agent shall be informed that if they refuse to accept the decision of conditional non-prosecution, they may file a petition with the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level within seven days from receipt of the written decision on conditional non-prosecution.  
The People's Procuratorate shall announce the decision of conditional non-prosecution in person to the juvenile criminal suspect and his or her legal representative, inform them of the probation period, the obligations to be complied with during the probation period and the legal liability for violating such obligations, and the right to raise objections to the decision of conditional non-prosecution, and shall make a record to be attached to the case file.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　未成年犯罪嫌疑人在押的，作出附条件不起诉决定后，人民检察院应当作出释放或者变更强制措施的决定。

**Article 34** : If the juvenile criminal suspect is in custody, after making the decision of conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision to release or alter compulsory measures.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　公安机关认为附条件不起诉决定有错误，要求复议的，人民检察院未成年人刑事检察机构应当另行指定检察人员进行审查并提出审查意见，经部门负责人审核，报请检察长或者检察委员会决定。  
人民检察院应当在收到要求复议意见书后的三十日以内作出复议决定，通知公安机关。

**Article 35** : If the public security organ believes that the decision of conditional non-prosecution is erroneous and requests reconsideration, the juvenile criminal prosecutorial organ of the People's Procuratorate shall designate a different prosecutorial personnel to review the case and submit a review opinion, which shall be examined by the head of the department and submitted to the Chief Prosecutor or the Prosecutorial Committee for decision.  
The People's Procuratorate shall make a reconsideration decision within thirty days from receipt of the written request for reconsideration and notify the public security organ.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　上一级人民检察院收到公安机关对附条件不起诉决定提请复核的意见书后，应当交由未成年人刑事检察机构办理。未成年人刑事检察机构应当指定检察人员进行审查并提出审查意见，经部门负责人审核，报请检察长或者检察委员会决定。  
上一级人民检察院应当在收到提请复核意见书后的三十日以内作出决定，制作复核决定书送交提请复核的公安机关和下级人民检察院。经复核改变下级人民检察院附条件不起诉决定的，应当撤销下级人民检察院作出的附条件不起诉决定，交由下级人民检察院执行。

**Article 36** : After receiving the written opinion requesting review of the conditional non-prosecution decision submitted by the public security organ, the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level shall refer the matter to the juvenile criminal prosecutorial organ for handling. The juvenile criminal prosecutorial organ shall designate a prosecutorial personnel to conduct a review and submit a review opinion, which shall be examined by the head of the department and submitted to the Chief Prosecutor or the Prosecutorial Committee for decision.  
The People's Procuratorate at the next higher level shall make a decision within thirty days from receipt of the written request for review, and produce a written decision on review to be served on the public security organ that requested the review and the lower-level People's Procuratorate. If the review results in a change to the lower-level People's Procuratorate's decision of conditional non-prosecution, the decision of conditional non-prosecution made by the lower-level People's Procuratorate shall be revoked, and the matter shall be referred to the lower-level People's Procuratorate for execution.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　被害人不服附条件不起诉决定，在收到附条件不起诉决定书后七日以内申诉的，由作出附条件不起诉决定的人民检察院的上一级人民检察院未成年人刑事检察机构立案复查。  
被害人向作出附条件不起诉决定的人民检察院提出申诉的，作出决定的人民检察院应当将申诉材料连同案卷一并报送上一级人民检察院受理。  
被害人不服附条件不起诉决定，在收到附条件不起诉决定书七日后提出申诉的，由作出附条件不起诉决定的人民检察院未成年人刑事检察机构另行指定检察人员审查后决定是否立案复查。  
未成年人刑事检察机构复查后应当提出复查意见，报请检察长决定。  
复查决定书应当送达被害人、被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人及其法定代理人和作出附条件不起诉决定的人民检察院。  
上级人民检察院经复查作出起诉决定的，应当撤销下级人民检察院的附条件不起诉决定，由下级人民检察院提起公诉，并将复查决定抄送移送审查起诉的公安机关。

**Article 37** : If the victim, refusing to accept the decision of conditional non-prosecution, files a petition within seven days from receipt of the written decision on conditional non-prosecution, the juvenile criminal prosecutorial organ of the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level of the People's Procuratorate that made the decision shall open a case for review.  
If the victim files a petition with the People's Procuratorate that made the decision of conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate that made the decision shall submit the petition materials together with the case files to the People's Procuratorate at the next higher level for acceptance.  
If the victim, refusing to accept the decision of conditional non-prosecution, files a petition after seven days from receipt of the written decision on conditional non-prosecution, the juvenile criminal prosecutorial organ of the People's Procuratorate that made the decision shall designate a different prosecutorial personnel to review the case and determine whether to open a case for review.  
After review, the juvenile criminal prosecutorial organ shall submit a review opinion to the Chief Prosecutor for decision.  
The written decision on review shall be served on the victim, the juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution and his or her legal representative, and the People's Procuratorate that made the decision of conditional non-prosecution.  
If, upon review, the People's Procuratorate at the higher level decides to initiate a prosecution, it shall revoke the decision of conditional non-prosecution made by the lower-level People's Procuratorate, and the lower-level People's Procuratorate shall initiate a public prosecution, and a copy of the review decision shall be sent to the public security organ that transferred the case for review for prosecution.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　未成年犯罪嫌疑人及其法定代理人对人民检察院决定附条件不起诉有异议的，人民检察院应当作出起诉的决定。

**Article 38** : If the juvenile criminal suspect and his or her legal representative object to the People's Procuratorate's decision of conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision to initiate a prosecution.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　人民检察院在作出附条件不起诉决定后，应当在十日内将附条件不起诉决定书报上级人民检察院主管部门备案。  
上级人民检察院认为下级人民检察院作出的附条件不起诉决定不适当的，应当及时撤销下级人民检察院作出的附条件不起诉决定，下级人民检察院应当执行。

**Article 39** : After making a decision of conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall, within ten days, submit the written decision on conditional non-prosecution to the competent department of the People's Procuratorate at the higher level for recordation.  
If the People's Procuratorate at the higher level considers that the decision of conditional non-prosecution made by the lower-level People's Procuratorate is inappropriate, it shall promptly revoke the decision of conditional non-prosecution made by the lower-level People's Procuratorate, and the lower-level People's Procuratorate shall comply.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　人民检察院决定附条件不起诉的，应当确定考验期。考验期为六个月以上一年以下，从人民检察院作出附条件不起诉的决定之日起计算。考验期不计入案件审查起诉期限。  
考验期的长短应当与未成年犯罪嫌疑人所犯罪行的轻重、主观恶性的大小和人身危险性的大小、一贯表现及帮教条件等相适应，根据未成年犯罪嫌疑人在考验期的表现，可以在法定期限范围内适当缩短或者延长。

**Article 40** : When the People's Procuratorate decides to attach conditions to non-prosecution, a probation period shall be determined. The probation period shall be not less than six months and not more than one year, calculated from the date of the People's Procuratorate's decision of conditional non-prosecution. The probation period shall not be counted in the time limit for review for prosecution.  
The length of the probation period shall be commensurate with the severity of the crime committed by the juvenile criminal suspect, the degree of subjective malignancy, the degree of personal dangerousness, the suspect's past conduct, and the conditions of assistance and education, etc. Based on the performance of the juvenile criminal suspect during the probation period, the period may be appropriately shortened or extended within the statutory limits.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当遵守下列规定：  
(一)遵守法律法规，服从监督；  
(二)按照考察机关的规定报告自己的活动情况；  
(三)离开所居住的市、县或者迁居，应当报经考察机关批准；  
(四)按照考察机关的要求接受矫治和教育。

**Article 41** : A juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution shall comply with the following provisions:  
(1) Comply with laws and regulations and submit to supervision;  
(2) Report his or her activities in accordance with the provisions of the supervisory organ;  
(3) Leaving the city or county where he or she resides or changing residence shall be subject to the approval of the supervisory organ;  
(4) Accept correction and education as required by the supervisory organ.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　人民检察院可以要求被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人接受下列矫治和教育：  
(一)完成戒瘾治疗、心理辅导或者其他适当的处遇措施；  
(二)向社区或者公益团体提供公益劳动；  
(三)不得进入特定场所，与特定的人员会见或者通信，从事特定的活动；  
(四)向被害人赔偿损失、赔礼道歉等；  
(五)接受相关教育；  
(六)遵守其他保护被害人安全以及预防再犯的禁止性规定。

**Article 42** : The People's Procuratorate may require a juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution to accept the following correction and education:  
(1) Complete addiction treatment, psychological counseling, or other appropriate treatment measures;  
(2) Provide community service or charitable work to the community or public welfare organizations;  
(3) Not enter specific places, meet or communicate with specific persons, or engage in specific activities;  
(4) Compensate the victim for losses, apologize, etc.;  
(5) Receive relevant education;  
(6) Comply with other prohibitive provisions to protect the safety of the victim and prevent recidivism.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　在附条件不起诉的考验期内，人民检察院应当对被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人进行监督考察。未成年犯罪嫌疑人的监护人应当对未成年犯罪嫌疑人加强管教，配合人民检察院做好监督考察工作。  
人民检察院可以会同未成年犯罪嫌疑人的监护人、所在学校、单位、居住地的村民委员会、居民委员会、未成年人保护组织等的有关人员定期对未成年犯罪嫌疑人进行考察、教育，实施跟踪帮教。

**Article 43** : During the probation period of conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall supervise and assess the juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution. The guardian of the juvenile criminal suspect shall strengthen discipline over the juvenile criminal suspect and cooperate with the People's Procuratorate in carrying out the supervision and assessment work.  
The People's Procuratorate may, together with the guardian of the juvenile criminal suspect, the school, work unit, relevant personnel from the villagers' committee, residents' committee of his or her place of residence, and organizations for the protection of minors, regularly conduct assessments and education of the juvenile criminal suspect and implement follow-up assistance and education.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　未成年犯罪嫌疑人经批准离开所居住的市、县或者迁居，作出附条件不起诉决定的人民检察院可以要求迁入地的人民检察院协助进行考察，并将考察结果函告作出附条件不起诉决定的人民检察院。

**Article 44** : If the juvenile criminal suspect, with approval, leaves the city or county where he or she resides or changes residence, the People's Procuratorate that made the decision of conditional non-prosecution may request the People's Procuratorate in the place to which he or she moves to assist in the assessment, and the assessment results shall be communicated by letter to the People's Procuratorate that made the decision of conditional non-prosecution.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　考验期届满，办案人员应当制作附条件不起诉考察意见书，提出起诉或者不起诉的意见，经部门负责人审核，报请检察长决定。  
人民检察院应当在审查起诉期限内作出起诉或者不起诉的决定。  
作出附条件不起诉决定的案件，审查起诉期限自人民检察院作出附条件不起诉决定之日起中止计算，自考验期限届满之日起或者人民检察院作出撤销附条件不起诉决定之日起恢复计算。

**Article 45** : Upon expiration of the probation period, the case-handling personnel shall produce a written opinion on the assessment of conditional non-prosecution, put forward an opinion on whether to initiate prosecution or not to prosecute, which shall be examined by the head of the department and submitted to the Chief Prosecutor for decision.  
The People's Procuratorate shall make a decision to initiate prosecution or not to prosecute within the time limit for review for prosecution.  
For cases where a decision of conditional non-prosecution has been made, the time limit for review for prosecution shall be suspended from the date of the People's Procuratorate's decision of conditional non-prosecution, and shall resume from the date of expiration of the probation period or from the date of the People's Procuratorate's decision to revoke the conditional non-prosecution.

<a id="art-46"></a>第四十六条　被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，在考验期内有下列情形之一的，人民检察院应当撤销附条件不起诉的决定，提起公诉：  
(一)实施新的犯罪的；  
(二)发现决定附条件不起诉以前还有其他犯罪需要追诉的；  
(三)违反治安管理规定，造成严重后果，或者多次违反治安管理规定的；  
(四)违反考察机关有关附条件不起诉的监督管理规定，造成严重后果，或者多次违反考察机关有关附条件不起诉的监督管理规定的。

**Article 46** : If a juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution, during the probation period, falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Procuratorate shall revoke the decision of conditional non-prosecution and initiate a public prosecution:  
(1) Committing a new crime;  
(2) It is discovered that there are other crimes that need to be prosecuted before the decision of conditional non-prosecution;  
(3) Violating public security administration regulations, causing serious consequences, or repeatedly violating public security administration regulations;  
(4) Violating the supervision and management regulations of the supervisory organ concerning conditional non-prosecution, causing serious consequences, or repeatedly violating the supervision and management regulations of the supervisory organ concerning conditional non-prosecution.

<a id="art-47"></a>第四十七条　对于未成年犯罪嫌疑人在考验期内实施新的犯罪或者在决定附条件不起诉以前还有其他犯罪需要追诉的，人民检察院应当移送侦查机关立案侦查。

**Article 47** : If a juvenile criminal suspect commits a new crime during the probation period or there are other crimes that need to be prosecuted before the decision of conditional non-prosecution, the People's Procuratorate shall transfer the case to an investigation organ to open a case for investigation.

<a id="art-48"></a>第四十八条　被附条件不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，在考验期内没有[本规定第四十六条](#art-46)规定的情形，考验期满的，人民检察院应当作出不起诉的决定。

**Article 48** : If a juvenile criminal suspect subject to conditional non-prosecution has no circumstances specified in Article 46 of these Provisions during the probation period and the probation period expires, the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision not to prosecute.

<a id="art-49"></a>第四十九条　对于附条件不起诉的案件，不起诉决定宣布后六个月内，办案人员可以对被不起诉的未成年人进行回访，巩固帮教效果，并做好相关记录。

**Article 49** : For cases of conditional non-prosecution, within six months after the announcement of the decision not to prosecute, the case-handling personnel may conduct follow-up visits to the minor who is not prosecuted to consolidate the effect of assistance and education, and shall keep relevant records.

<a id="art-50"></a>第五十条　对人民检察院依照[刑事诉讼法第一百七十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-173)第二款规定作出的不起诉决定和经附条件不起诉考验期满不起诉的，在向被不起诉的未成年人及其法定代理人宣布不起诉决定书时，应当充分阐明不起诉的理由和法律依据，并结合社会调查，围绕犯罪行为对被害人、对本人及家庭、对社会等造成的危害，导致犯罪行为发生的原因及应当吸取的教训等，对被不起诉的未成年人开展必要的教育。如果侦查人员、合适成年人、辩护人、社工等参加有利于教育被不起诉未成年人的，经被不起诉的未成年人及其法定代理人同意，可以邀请他们参加，但要严格控制参与人范围。  
对于犯罪事实清楚，但因未达刑事责任年龄不起诉、年龄证据存疑而不起诉的未成年犯罪嫌疑人，参照上述规定举行不起诉宣布教育仪式。

**Article 50** : When announcing the decision not to prosecute to the minor not prosecuted and his or her legal representative, in cases where the People's Procuratorate makes a decision not to prosecute under Article 173, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law or where non-prosecution results from the expiration of the probation period for conditional non-prosecution, the reasons and legal basis for the non-prosecution shall be fully explained, and based on the social investigation, necessary education shall be provided to the minor not prosecuted, focusing on the harm caused by the criminal act to the victim, to the minor himself or herself, to the family, and to society, the causes of the criminal act, and the lessons to be learned. If the participation of investigators, suitable adults, defenders, social workers, etc., is beneficial to the education of the minor not prosecuted, with the consent of the minor not prosecuted and his or her legal representative, they may be invited to participate, but the scope of participants shall be strictly controlled.  
For juvenile criminal suspects who are not prosecuted because the facts of the crime are clear but they have not reached the age of criminal responsibility, or because the age evidence is doubtful, the ceremony of announcing the non-prosecution decision with education shall be conducted with reference to the above provisions.

#### 第四节　提起公诉

<a id="art-51"></a>第五十一条　人民检察院审查未成年人与成年人共同犯罪案件，一般应当将未成年人与成年人分案起诉。但是具有下列情形之一的，可以不分案起诉：  
(一)未成年人系犯罪集团的组织者或者其他共同犯罪中的主犯的；  
(二)案件重大、疑难、复杂，分案起诉可能妨碍案件审理的；  
(三)涉及刑事附带民事诉讼，分案起诉妨碍附带民事诉讼部分审理的；  
(四)具有其他不宜分案起诉情形的。  
对分案起诉至同一人民法院的未成年人与成年人共同犯罪案件，由未成年人刑事检察机构一并办理更为适宜的，经检察长决定，可以由未成年人刑事检察机构一并办理。  
分案起诉的未成年人与成年人共同犯罪案件，由不同机构分别办理的，应当相互了解案件情况，提出量刑建议时，注意全案的量刑平衡。

**Article 51** : When a People's Procuratorate examines a joint crime case involving a minor and an adult, it shall generally initiate separate prosecutions against the minor and the adult. However, in any of the following circumstances, it may not initiate separate prosecutions:  
(1) The minor is an organizer of a criminal group or a principal offender in other joint crimes;  
(2) The case is significant, difficult, complex, and separate prosecutions may impede the adjudication of the case;  
(3) The case involves civil actions collateral to criminal proceedings, and separate prosecutions impede the adjudication of the civil action collateral to the criminal proceedings;  
(4) There are other circumstances unsuitable for separate prosecutions.  
Where it is more appropriate for the juvenile criminal prosecution authorities to handle jointly the joint crime case of a minor and an adult that has been separately prosecuted to the same People's Court, with the decision of the chief procurator, the juvenile criminal prosecution authorities may handle it jointly.  
For a joint crime case of a minor and an adult that is separately prosecuted and handled by different authorities, the authorities shall keep each other informed of the case situation, and when making sentencing recommendations, they shall pay attention to the overall sentencing balance of the entire case.

<a id="art-52"></a>第五十二条　对于分案起诉的未成年人与成年人共同犯罪案件，一般应当同时移送人民法院。对于需要补充侦查的，如果补充侦查事项不涉及未成年犯罪嫌疑人所参与的犯罪事实，不影响对未成年犯罪嫌疑人提起公诉的，应当对未成年犯罪嫌疑人先予提起公诉。

**Article 52** : For a joint crime case of a minor and an adult that is separately prosecuted, the case shall generally be transferred to the People's Court at the same time. If supplementary investigation is needed, and the matters for supplementary investigation do not involve the criminal facts in which the juvenile criminal suspect participated and do not affect the initiation of a public prosecution against the juvenile criminal suspect, the juvenile criminal suspect shall first be prosecuted.

<a id="art-53"></a>第五十三条　对于分案起诉的未成年人与成年人共同犯罪案件，在审查起诉过程中可以根据全案情况制作一个审结报告，起诉书以及出庭预案等应当分别制作。

**Article 53** : For a joint crime case of a minor and an adult that is separately prosecuted, during the review for prosecution, one case conclusion report may be prepared based on the entire case situation, but the indictment and the plan for appearing in court, etc., shall be prepared separately.

<a id="art-54"></a>第五十四条　人民检察院对未成年人与成年人共同犯罪案件分别提起公诉后，在诉讼过程中出现不宜分案起诉情形的，可以建议人民法院并案审理。

**Article 54** : After a People's Procuratorate separately initiates public prosecutions against a minor and an adult in a joint crime case, if circumstances unsuitable for separate prosecutions arise during the proceedings, it may suggest to the People's Court to conduct a joint adjudication.

<a id="art-55"></a>第五十五条　对于符合适用简易程序审理条件的未成年人刑事案件，人民检察院应当在提起公诉时向人民法院提出适用简易程序审理的建议。

**Article 55** : For a juvenile criminal case that meets the conditions for trial under summary procedures, the People's Procuratorate shall, at the time of initiating the public prosecution, make a suggestion to the People's Court to apply summary procedures for trial.

<a id="art-56"></a>第五十六条　对提起公诉的未成年人刑事案件，应当认真做好下列出席法庭的准备工作：  
(一)掌握未成年被告人的心理状态，并对其进行接受审判的教育，必要时，可以再次讯问被告人；  
(二)与未成年被告人的法定代理人、合适成年人、辩护人交换意见，共同做好教育、感化工作；  
(三)进一步熟悉案情，深入研究本案的有关法律政策问题，根据案件性质，结合社会调查情况，拟定讯问提纲、询问被害人、证人、鉴定人提纲、举证提纲、答辩提纲、公诉意见书和针对未成年被告人进行法制教育的书面材料。

**Article 56** : For a juvenile criminal case for which a public prosecution has been initiated, the following preparations for appearing in court shall be earnestly carried out:  
(1) Grasp the psychological state of the juvenile defendant, and educate him/her on accepting trial; if necessary, the defendant may be interrogated again;  
(2) Exchange views with the legal representative, suitable adult, and defender of the juvenile defendant, and jointly carry out education and rehabilitative work;  
(3) Further familiarize with the case facts, deeply study the relevant legal and policy issues in this case, and based on the nature of the case and in combination with social investigation information, prepare an interrogation outline, an outline for questioning the victim, witnesses, and expert evaluators, an outline for presenting evidence, a defense outline, a written opinion of the public prosecutor, and written materials for legal education targeted at the juvenile defendant.

<a id="art-57"></a>第五十七条　公诉人出席未成年人刑事审判法庭，应当遵守公诉人出庭行为规范要求，发言时应当语调温和，并注意用语文明、准确，通俗易懂。  
公诉人一般不提请未成年证人、被害人出庭作证。确有必要出庭作证的，应当建议人民法院采取相应的保护措施。

**Article 57** : When a public prosecutor appears in court for juvenile criminal trials, he/she shall comply with the behavioral norms for public prosecutors appearing in court, shall speak in a gentle tone, and shall pay attention to using civilized, accurate, and understandable language.  
A public prosecutor shall generally not request a juvenile witness or victim to testify in court. If it is truly necessary for them to testify in court, he/she shall suggest that the People's Court take corresponding protective measures.

<a id="art-58"></a>第五十八条　在法庭审理过程中，公诉人的讯问、询问、辩论等活动，应当注意未成年人的身心特点。对于未成年被告人情绪严重不稳定，不宜继续接受审判的，公诉人可以建议法庭休庭。

**Article 58** : During court proceedings, the interrogation, questioning, and debate activities of the public prosecutor shall pay attention to the physical and psychological characteristics of the minor. If a juvenile defendant is severely emotionally unstable and unsuitable to continue standing trial, the public prosecutor may suggest that the court adjourn the trial.

<a id="art-59"></a>第五十九条　对于具有下列情形之一，依法可能判处拘役、三年以下有期徒刑，有悔罪表现，宣告缓刑对所居住社区没有重大不良影响，具备有效监护条件或者社会帮教措施、适用缓刑确实不致再危害社会的未成年被告人，人民检察院应当建议人民法院适用缓刑：  
(一)犯罪情节较轻，未造成严重后果的；  
(二)主观恶性不大的初犯或者胁从犯、从犯；  
(三)被害人同意和解或者被害人有明显过错的；  
(四)其他可以适用缓刑的情节。  
建议宣告缓刑，可以根据犯罪情况，同时建议禁止未成年被告人在缓刑考验期限内从事特定活动，进入特定区域、场所，接触特定的人。  
人民检察院提出对未成年被告人适用缓刑建议的，应当将未成年被告人能够获得有效监护、帮教的书面材料于判决前移送人民法院。

**Article 59** : For a juvenile defendant who meets any of the following circumstances, who according to law may be sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, who shows repentance, whose probation would not have a significant adverse impact on the community where he/she resides, who has effective guardianship conditions or social assistance and education measures, and whose probation would truly not cause further harm to society, the People's Procuratorate shall suggest to the People's Court that it apply probation:  
(1) The circumstances of the crime are relatively minor and no serious consequences have been caused;  
(2) It is a first-time offender, coerced accomplice, or accessory with relatively low subjective culpability;  
(3) The victim agrees to conciliation or the victim is clearly at fault;  
(4) Other circumstances under which probation may be applied.  
When suggesting a suspended sentence, the People's Procuratorate may, based on the circumstances of the crime, also suggest that the juvenile defendant be prohibited from engaging in specific activities, entering specific areas or places, or contacting specific persons during the probation period.  
If the People's Procuratorate makes a suggestion to apply probation for a juvenile defendant, it shall transfer to the People's Court before the judgment is rendered written materials demonstrating that the juvenile defendant can obtain effective guardianship and assistance/education.

<a id="art-60"></a>第六十条　公诉人在依法指控犯罪的同时，要剖析未成年被告人犯罪的原因、社会危害性，适时进行法制教育，促使其深刻反省，吸取教训。

**Article 60** : While accusing the crime according to law, the public prosecutor shall analyze the causes of the juvenile defendant's crime and the social harmfulness, and shall conduct legal education at appropriate times to urge him/her to deeply reflect and learn lessons.

<a id="art-61"></a>第六十一条　人民检察院派员出席未成年人刑事案件二审法庭适用本节的相关规定。

**Article 61** : When a People's Procuratorate sends personnel to appear in court for the second instance trial of a juvenile criminal case, the relevant provisions of this Section shall apply.

<a id="art-62"></a>第六十二条　犯罪的时候不满十八周岁，被判处五年有期徒刑以下刑罚的，人民检察院应当在收到人民法院生效判决后，对犯罪记录予以封存。  
对于二审案件，上级人民检察院封存犯罪记录时，应当通知下级人民检察院对相关犯罪记录予以封存。

**Article 62** : Where a person is under the age of 18 at the time of committing the crime and is sentenced to a punishment of not more than five years of fixed-term imprisonment, the People's Procuratorate shall, after receiving the effective judgment of the People's Court, seal the criminal record.  
For a case of second instance, when the superior People's Procuratorate seals the criminal record, it shall notify the lower People's Procuratorate to seal the relevant criminal record.

<a id="art-63"></a>第六十三条　人民检察院应当将拟封存的未成年人犯罪记录、卷宗等相关材料装订成册，加密保存，不予公开，并建立专门的未成年人犯罪档案库，执行严格的保管制度。

**Article 63** : The People's Procuratorate shall bind into volumes the juvenile criminal records, case files, and other relevant materials to be sealed, keep them encrypted, not disclose them, and establish a specialized juvenile criminal record archive with a strict storage system.

<a id="art-64"></a>第六十四条　除司法机关为办案需要或者有关单位根据国家规定进行查询的以外，人民检察院不得向任何单位和个人提供封存的犯罪记录，并不得提供未成年人有犯罪记录的证明。  
司法机关或者有关单位需要查询犯罪记录的，应当向封存犯罪记录的人民检察院提出书面申请，人民检察院应当在七日以内作出是否许可的决定。

**Article 64** : Except where judicial authorities need to handle cases or relevant entities inquire in accordance with national regulations, the People's Procuratorate shall not provide sealed criminal records to any unit or individual, and shall not provide a certificate that a minor has a criminal record.  
If a judicial authority or relevant entity needs to inquire about a criminal record, it shall submit a written application to the People's Procuratorate that sealed the criminal record, and the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision on whether to permit the inquiry within seven days.

<a id="art-65"></a>第六十五条　对被封存犯罪记录的未成年人，符合下列条件之一的，应当对其犯罪记录解除封存：  
(一)实施新的犯罪，且新罪与封存记录之罪数罪并罚后被决定执行五年有期徒刑以上刑罚的；  
(二)发现漏罪，且漏罪与封存记录之罪数罪并罚后被决定执行五年有期徒刑以上刑罚的。

**Article 65** : For a minor whose criminal record has been sealed, if he/she meets one of the following conditions, his/her criminal record shall be unsealed:  
(1) He/She commits a new crime, and after combined punishment for multiple crimes of the new crime and the crime for which the record was sealed, the decided penalty is fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years;  
(2) An undiscovered crime is found, and after combined punishment for multiple crimes of the undiscovered crime and the crime for which the record was sealed, the decided penalty is fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years.

<a id="art-66"></a>第六十六条　人民检察院对未成年犯罪嫌疑人作出不起诉决定后，应当对相关记录予以封存。具体程序参照[本规定第六十二条](#art-62)至第六十五条规定办理。

**Article 66** : After the People's Procuratorate makes a decision not to prosecute a juvenile criminal suspect, it shall seal the relevant records. The specific procedures shall be handled by reference to Articles 62 through 65 of these Provisions.

### 第四章　未成年人刑事案件的法律监督

<a id="art-67"></a>第六十七条　人民检察院审查批准逮捕、审查起诉未成年犯罪嫌疑人，应当同时依法监督侦查活动是否合法，发现有下列违法行为的，应当提出纠正意见；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任：  
(一)违法对未成年犯罪嫌疑人采取强制措施或者采取强制措施不当的；  
(二)未依法实行对未成年犯罪嫌疑人与成年犯罪嫌疑人分别关押、管理的；  
(三)对未成年犯罪嫌疑人采取刑事拘留、逮捕措施后，在法定时限内未进行讯问，或者未通知其家属的；  
(四)讯问未成年犯罪嫌疑人或者询问未成年被害人、证人时，未依法通知其法定代理人或者合适成年人到场的；  
(五)讯问或者询问女性未成年人时，没有女性检察人员参加；  
(六)未依法告知未成年犯罪嫌疑人有权委托辩护人的；  
(七)未依法通知法律援助机构指派律师为未成年犯罪嫌疑人提供辩护的；  
(八)对未成年犯罪嫌疑人威胁、体罚、侮辱人格、游行示众，或者刑讯逼供、指供、诱供的；  
(九)利用未成年人认知能力低而故意制造冤、假、错案的；  
(十)对未成年被害人、证人以暴力、威胁、诱骗等非法手段收集证据或者侵害未成年被害人、证人的人格尊严及隐私权等合法权益的；  
(十一)违反羁押和办案期限规定的；  
(十二)已作出不批准逮捕、不起诉决定，公安机关不立即释放犯罪嫌疑人的；  
(十三)在侦查中有其他侵害未成年人合法权益行为的。

**Article 67** : When reviewing an approval of arrest or reviewing for prosecution for a juvenile criminal suspect, the People's Procuratorate shall simultaneously supervise, according to law, whether the investigative activities are lawful, and if it finds any of the following illegal acts, it shall issue a corrective opinion; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Illegally applying compulsory measures against a juvenile criminal suspect or applying inappropriate compulsory measures;  
(2) Failing to separately detain and manage juvenile criminal suspects from adult criminal suspects according to law;  
(3) After applying criminal detention or arrest measures to a juvenile criminal suspect, failing to interrogate him/her within the statutory time limit or failing to notify his/her family;  
(4) When interrogating a juvenile criminal suspect or questioning a juvenile victim or witness, failing to notify his/her legal representative or a suitable adult to be present according to law;  
(5) When interrogating or questioning a female minor, having no female procuratorial personnel participate;  
(6) Failing to inform the juvenile criminal suspect according to law of his/her right to entrust a defender;  
(7) Failing to notify a legal aid institution according to law to assign a lawyer to provide defense for the juvenile criminal suspect;  
(8) Threatening, physically punishing, humiliating, parading in public, or extorting confessions by torture, inducing the suspect to confess, or tricking the suspect into confessing to a juvenile criminal suspect;  
(9) Taking advantage of a minor's low cognitive ability to deliberately fabricate unjust, false, or wrong cases;  
(10) Using violence, threats, deception, or other illegal means to collect evidence against a juvenile victim or witness, or infringing upon the personal dignity, privacy rights, or other lawful rights and interests of a juvenile victim or witness;  
(11) Violating the provisions on the time limits for custody and case handling;  
(12) After a decision not to approve arrest or not to prosecute has been made, the public security organ fails to immediately release the criminal suspect;  
(13) Other acts during the investigation that infringe upon the lawful rights and interests of minors.

<a id="art-68"></a>第六十八条　对依法不应当公开审理的未成年人刑事案件公开审理的，人民检察院应当在开庭前提出纠正意见。  
公诉人出庭支持公诉时，发现法庭审判有下列违反法律规定的诉讼程序的情形之一的，应当在休庭后及时向本院检察长报告，由人民检察院向人民法院提出纠正意见：  
(一)开庭或者宣告判决时未通知未成年被告人的法定代理人到庭的；  
(二)人民法院没有给聋、哑或者不通晓当地通用的语言文字的未成年被告人聘请或者指定翻译人员的；  
(三)未成年被告人在审判时没有辩护人的；对未成年被告人及其法定代理人依照法律和有关规定拒绝辩护人为其辩护，合议庭未另行通知法律援助机构指派律师的；  
(四)法庭未告知未成年被告人及其法定代理人依法享有的申请回避、辩护、提出新的证据、申请重新鉴定或者勘验、最后陈述、提出上诉等诉讼权利的；  
(五)其他违反法律规定的诉讼程序的情形。

**Article 68** : If a juvenile criminal case that shall not be tried in public according to law is tried in public, the People's Procuratorate shall issue a corrective opinion before the trial begins.  
When a public prosecutor appears in court to support the public prosecution and finds any of the following circumstances in the court trial that violate the legally prescribed procedural rules, he/she shall promptly report to the chief procurator of his/her procuratorate after the court adjourns, and the People's Procuratorate shall issue a corrective opinion to the People's Court:  
(1) Failing to notify the legal representative of the juvenile defendant to appear when the trial begins or when the judgment is announced;  
(2) The People's Court fails to retain or appoint an interpreter for a juvenile defendant who is deaf, mute, or does not understand the local commonly used language or script;  
(3) The juvenile defendant has no defender during the trial; where the juvenile defendant and his/her legal representative refuse the defender's defense in accordance with law and relevant provisions, and the collegial panel fails to separately notify a legal aid institution to assign a lawyer;  
(4) The court fails to inform the juvenile defendant and his/her legal representative of the litigation rights they enjoy according to law, such as applying for withdrawal, defense, presenting new evidence, applying for renewed expert evaluation or inspection, making final statements, and filing appeals;  
(5) Other circumstances that violate the legally prescribed procedural rules.

<a id="art-69"></a>第六十九条　人民检察院发现有关机关对未成年人犯罪记录应当封存而未封存的，不应当允许查询而允许查询的或者不应当提供犯罪记录而提供的，应当依法提出纠正意见。

**Article 69** : If a People's Procuratorate finds that a relevant authority has failed to seal a juvenile criminal record that should have been sealed, has allowed inquiries that should not have been allowed, or has provided a criminal record that should not have been provided, it shall issue a corrective opinion according to law.

<a id="art-70"></a>第七十条　人民检察院依法对未成年犯管教所实行驻所检察。在刑罚执行监督中，发现关押成年罪犯的监狱收押未成年罪犯的，未成年犯管教所违法收押成年罪犯的，或者对年满十八周岁时余刑在二年以上的罪犯留在未成年犯管教所执行剩余刑期的，应当依法提出纠正意见。

**Article 70** : The People's Procuratorate shall, according to law, station procurators at juvenile correctional facilities for supervision. During the supervision of execution of criminal punishments, if it finds that a prison detaining adult offenders has taken in a juvenile offender, a juvenile correctional facility has illegally taken in an adult offender, or a juvenile offender who has reached the age of 18 with a remaining sentence of more than two years has been kept in the juvenile correctional facility to serve the remaining sentence, it shall issue a corrective opinion according to law.

<a id="art-71"></a>第七十一条　人民检察院在看守所检察中，发现没有对未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人与成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人分别关押、管理或者对未成年犯留所执行刑罚的，应当依法提出纠正意见。

**Article 71** : When conducting supervision in detention houses, if a People's Procuratorate finds that juvenile criminal suspects or defendants have not been separately detained and managed from adult criminal suspects or defendants, or that juvenile offenders have been kept in the detention house to serve their sentences, it shall issue a corrective opinion according to law.

<a id="art-72"></a>第七十二条　人民检察院应当加强对未成年犯管教所、看守所监管未成年罪犯活动的监督，依法保障未成年罪犯的合法权益，维护监管改造秩序和教学、劳动、生活秩序。  
人民检察院配合未成年犯管教所、看守所加强对未成年罪犯的政治、法律、文化教育，促进依法、科学、文明监管。

**Article 72** : The People's Procuratorate shall strengthen supervision over the activities of juvenile correctional facilities and detention houses in supervising juvenile offenders, shall protect the lawful rights and interests of juvenile offenders according to law, and shall maintain the order of supervision and reform, as well as the order of education, labor, and daily life.  
The People's Procuratorate shall cooperate with juvenile correctional facilities and detention houses to strengthen political, legal, and cultural education for juvenile offenders, and promote lawful, scientific, and civilized supervision.

<a id="art-73"></a>第七十三条　人民检察院依法对未成年人的社区矫正进行监督，发现有下列情形之一的，应当依法向公安机关、人民法院、监狱、社区矫正机构等有关部门提出纠正意见：  
(一)没有将未成年人的社区矫正与成年人分开进行的；  
(二)对实行社区矫正的未成年人脱管、漏管或者没有落实帮教措施的；  
(三)没有对未成年社区矫正人员给予身份保护，其矫正宣告公开进行，矫正档案未进行保密，公开或者传播其姓名、住所、照片等可能推断出该未成年人的其他资料以及矫正资料等情形的；  
(四)未成年社区矫正人员的矫正小组没有熟悉青少年成长特点的人员参加的；  
(五)没有针对未成年人的年龄、心理特点和身心发育需要等特殊情况采取相应的监督管理和教育矫正措施的；  
(六)其他违法情形。

**Article 73** : The People's Procuratorate shall supervise community corrections for minors according to law. If it finds any of the following circumstances, it shall issue a corrective opinion to the relevant authorities, such as the public security organ, the People's Court, the prison, or the community corrections institution:  
(1) Community corrections for minors are not carried out separately from those for adults;  
(2) Minors subject to community corrections are not supervised or are missed, or assistance and education measures are not implemented;  
(3) Identity protection is not provided to minors receiving community corrections; the correction pronouncement is conducted publicly, the correction files are not kept confidential, or their names, addresses, photographs, or other information that may identify them are publicly disclosed or disseminated;  
(4) The correction team for the minor receiving community corrections does not include personnel familiar with the growth characteristics of adolescents;  
(5) Corresponding supervision, management, and educational correction measures are not taken in light of the special circumstances such as the minor's age, psychological characteristics, and physical and mental development needs;  
(6) Other illegal circumstances.

<a id="art-74"></a>第七十四条　人民检察院依法对未成年犯的减刑、假释、暂予监外执行等活动实行监督。对符合减刑、假释、暂予监外执行法定条件的，应当建议执行机关向人民法院、监狱管理机关或者公安机关提请；发现提请或者裁定、决定不当的，应当依法提出纠正意见；对徇私舞弊减刑、假释、暂予监外执行等构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 74** : The People's Procuratorate shall supervise, according to law, commutation of punishment, parole, and temporary service of sentence outside prison for juvenile offenders. If the statutory conditions for commutation, parole, or temporary service of sentence outside prison are met, it shall suggest that the executing authority submit a request to the People's Court, the prison administration authority, or the public security organ; if it finds that the request, ruling, or decision is inappropriate, it shall issue a corrective opinion according to law; and if the act of engaging in fraud for personal gain in commutation, parole, or temporary service of sentence outside prison constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第五章　未成年人案件的刑事申诉检察

<a id="art-75"></a>第七十五条　人民检察院依法受理未成年人及其法定代理人提出的刑事申诉案件和国家赔偿案件。  
人民检察院对未成年人刑事申诉案件和国家赔偿案件，应当指定专人及时办理。

**Article 75** : The People's Procuratorate shall accept, according to law, criminal petition cases and state compensation cases filed by a minor and his/her legal representative.  
The People's Procuratorate shall designate specific personnel to handle juvenile criminal petition cases and state compensation cases promptly.

<a id="art-76"></a>第七十六条　人民检察院复查未成年人刑事申诉案件，应当直接听取未成年人及其法定代理人的陈述或者辩解，认真审核、查证与案件有关的证据和线索，查清案件事实，依法作出处理。  
案件复查终结作出处理决定后，应当向未成年人及其法定代理人当面送达法律文书，做好释法说理和教育工作。

**Article 76** : When the People's Procuratorate reviews a juvenile criminal petition case, it shall directly hear the statements or defenses of the minor and his/her legal representative, seriously examine and verify the evidence and leads related to the case, clarify the facts of the case, and make a decision according to law.  
After the review is concluded and a decision is made, the legal documents shall be served in person to the minor and his/her legal representative, and legal interpretation and reasoning as well as education work shall be properly carried out.

<a id="art-77"></a>第七十七条　对已复查纠正的未成年人刑事申诉案件，应当配合有关部门做好善后工作。

**Article 77** : For juvenile criminal petition cases that have been reviewed and corrected, the People's Procuratorate shall cooperate with relevant authorities to properly carry out follow-up work.

<a id="art-78"></a>第七十八条　人民检察院办理未成年人国家赔偿案件，应当充分听取未成年人及其法定代理人的意见，对于依法应当赔偿的案件，应当及时作出和执行赔偿决定。

**Article 78** : When handling juvenile state compensation cases, the People's Procuratorate shall fully hear the opinions of the minor and his/her legal representative, and for cases where compensation shall be paid according to law, it shall promptly make and execute compensation decisions.

### 第六章　附　则

<a id="art-79"></a>第七十九条　本规定所称未成年人刑事案件，是指犯罪嫌疑人、被告人实施涉嫌犯罪行为时已满十四周岁、未满十八周岁的刑事案件，但在有关未成年人诉讼权利和体现对未成年人程序上特殊保护的条文中所称的未成年人，是指在诉讼过程中未满十八周岁的人。犯罪嫌疑人实施涉嫌犯罪行为时未满十八周岁，在诉讼过程中已满十八周岁的，人民检察院可以根据案件的具体情况适用本规定。

**Article 79** : For the purposes of these Provisions, a "juvenile criminal case" refers to a criminal case in which the criminal suspect or defendant has reached the age of 14 but is under the age of 18 at the time of committing the alleged criminal act. However, the term "minor" as used in the provisions concerning litigation rights of minors and the special procedural protection for minors refers to a person who is under the age of 18 during the proceedings. If a criminal suspect was under the age of 18 at the time of committing the alleged criminal act but has reached the age of 18 during the proceedings, the People's Procuratorate may apply these Provisions based on the specific circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-80"></a>第八十条　实施犯罪行为的年龄，一律按公历的年、月、日计算。从周岁生日的第二天起，为已满××周岁。

**Article 80** : The age at the time of committing the criminal act shall be calculated according to the calendar year, month, and day. Commencing from the day after the birthday, the person shall be deemed to have reached the age of XX.

<a id="art-81"></a>第八十一条　未成年人刑事案件的法律文书和工作文书，应当注明未成年人的出生年月日、法定代理人或者到场的合适成年人、辩护人基本情况。  
对未成年犯罪嫌疑人、被告人、未成年罪犯的有关情况和办案人员开展教育感化工作的情况，应当记录在卷，随案移送。

**Article 81** : In legal documents and work documents for juvenile criminal cases, the date of birth of the minor, and the basic information of the legal representative or the suitable adult present, and the defender shall be indicated.  
The relevant information about the juvenile criminal suspect, defendant, or juvenile offender, and the circumstances under which case-handling personnel carry out education and rehabilitation work, shall be recorded in the case file and transferred along with the case.

<a id="art-82"></a>第八十二条　本规定由最高人民检察院负责解释。

**Article 82** : The Supreme People's Procuratorate shall be responsible for the interpretation of these Provisions.

<a id="art-83"></a>第八十三条　本规定自发布之日起施行，最高人民检察院 2007年1月9日发布的《人民检察院办理未成年人刑事案件的规定》同时废止。

**Article 83** : These Provisions shall come into force as of the date of issuance, and the Provisions of the People's Procuratorate on Handling Juvenile Criminal Cases issued by the Supreme People's Procuratorate on January 9, 2007 shall be simultaneously abolished.
