# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理非法采供血液等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕12号  
**公布日期**：2008-02-18  
**生效日期**：2008-09-23

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理非法采供血液等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2008年2月18日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1444次会议、2008年5月8日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第1次会议通过，现予公布，自2008年9月23日起施行。 2008年9月22日 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院 关于办理非法采供血液等刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2008〕12号 为保障公民的身体健康和生命安全，依法惩处非法采供血液等犯罪，根据刑法有关规定，现对办理此类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  对未经国家主管部门批准或者超过批准的业务范围，采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的，应认定为刑法第三百三十四条第一款规定的“非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品”。 第二条  对非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为刑法第三百三十四条第一款规定的“不符合国家规定的标准，足以危害人体健康”，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金： （一）采集、供应的血液含有艾滋病病毒、乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体等病原微生物的； （二）制作、供应的血液制品含有艾滋病病毒、乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体等病原微生物，或者将含有上述病原微生物的血液用于制作血液制品的； （三）使用不符合国家规定的药品、诊断试剂、卫生器材，或者重复使用一次性采血器材采集血液，造成传染病传播危险的； （四）违反规定对献血者、供血浆者超量、频繁采集血液、血浆，足以危害人体健康的； （五）其他不符合国家有关采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的规定标准，足以危害人体健康的。 第三条  对非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为刑法第三百三十四条第一款规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  对未经国家主管部门批准或者超过批准的业务范围，采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第一款规定的“非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品”。

**Article 1** Any act of collecting or supplying blood or making or supplying blood products without the approval of the competent state authority or beyond the approved scope of business shall be determined as "illegally collecting or supplying blood or making or supplying blood products" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 334 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  对非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第一款规定的“不符合国家规定的标准，足以危害人体健康”，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金：  
（一）采集、供应的血液含有艾滋病病毒、乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体等病原微生物的；  
（二）制作、供应的血液制品含有艾滋病病毒、乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体等病原微生物，或者将含有上述病原微生物的血液用于制作血液制品的；  
（三）使用不符合国家规定的药品、诊断试剂、卫生器材，或者重复使用一次性采血器材采集血液，造成传染病传播危险的；  
（四）违反规定对献血者、供血浆者超量、频繁采集血液、血浆，足以危害人体健康的；  
（五）其他不符合国家有关采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的规定标准，足以危害人体健康的。

**Article 2** Any conduct of illegally collecting or supplying blood or making or supplying blood products that falls under any of the following circumstances shall be determined as "failing to meet the standards prescribed by the state and being sufficient to endanger human health" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 334 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention and shall also be fined:  
(1) the collected or supplied blood contains pathogenic microorganisms such as HIV, hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, or Treponema pallidum;  
(2) the made or supplied blood products contain pathogenic microorganisms such as HIV, hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, or Treponema pallidum, or blood containing the aforesaid pathogenic microorganisms is used for making blood products;  
(3) medicines, diagnostic reagents, or sanitary materials that do not comply with state provisions are used, or disposable blood collection equipment is reused for blood collection, causing a risk of transmission of infectious diseases;  
(4) in violation of regulations, blood or plasma is collected from blood donors or plasma donors in excessive quantities or at excessive frequency, sufficient to endanger human health;  
(5) other circumstances where the conduct fails to meet the relevant state standards for collecting or supplying blood or making or supplying blood products and is sufficient to endanger human health.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  对非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第一款规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）造成献血者、供血浆者、受血者感染乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体或者其他经血液传播的病原微生物的；  
（二）造成献血者、供血浆者、受血者重度贫血、造血功能障碍或者其他器官组织损伤导致功能障碍等身体严重危害的；  
（三）对人体健康造成其他严重危害的。

**Article 3** Any conduct of illegally collecting or supplying blood or making or supplying blood products that falls under any of the following circumstances shall be determined as "causing serious harm to human health" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 334 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years and shall also be fined:  
(1) causing blood donors, plasma donors, or blood recipients to be infected with hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, Treponema pallidum, or other pathogen microorganisms transmitted through blood;  
(2) causing blood donors, plasma donors, or blood recipients to suffer from severe anemia, hematopoietic dysfunction, or other serious bodily harm such as dysfunction due to organ or tissue damage;  
(3) causing other serious harm to human health.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  对非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第一款规定的“造成特别严重后果”，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）因血液传播疾病导致人员死亡或者感染艾滋病病毒的；  
（二）造成五人以上感染乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体或者其他经血液传播的病原微生物的；  
（三）造成五人以上重度贫血、造血功能障碍或者其他器官组织损伤导致功能障碍等身体严重危害的；  
（四）造成其他特别严重后果的。

**Article 4** Any conduct of illegally collecting or supplying blood or making or supplying blood products that falls under any of the following circumstances shall be determined as "causing especially serious consequences" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 334 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated:  
(1) causing death or infection with HIV due to blood-borne diseases;  
(2) causing five or more persons to be infected with hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, Treponema pallidum, or other pathogen microorganisms transmitted through blood;  
(3) causing five or more persons to suffer from severe anemia, hematopoietic dysfunction, or other serious bodily harm such as dysfunction due to organ or tissue damage;  
(4) causing other especially serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  对经国家主管部门批准采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的部门，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第二款规定的“不依照规定进行检测或者违背其他操作规定”：  
（一）血站未用两个企业生产的试剂对艾滋病病毒抗体、乙型肝炎病毒表面抗原、丙型肝炎病毒抗体、梅毒抗体进行两次检测的；  
（二）单采血浆站不依照规定对艾滋病病毒抗体、乙型肝炎病毒表面抗原、丙型肝炎病毒抗体、梅毒抗体进行检测的；  
（三）血液制品生产企业在投料生产前未用主管部门批准和检定合格的试剂进行复检的；  
（四）血站、单采血浆站和血液制品生产企业使用的诊断试剂没有生产单位名称、生产批准文号或者经检定不合格的；  
（五）采供血机构在采集检验标本、采集血液和成分血分离时，使用没有生产单位名称、生产批准文号或者超过有效期的一次性注射器等采血器材的；  
（六）不依照国家规定的标准和要求包装、储存、运输血液、原料血浆的；  
（七）对国家规定检测项目结果呈阳性的血液未及时按照规定予以清除的；  
（八）不具备相应资格的医务人员进行采血、检验操作的；  
（九）对献血者、供血浆者超量、频繁采集血液、血浆的；  
（十）采供血机构采集血液、血浆前，未对献血者或供血浆者进行身份识别，采集冒名顶替者、健康检查不合格者血液、血浆的；  
（十一）血站擅自采集原料血浆，单采血浆站擅自采集临床用血或者向医疗机构供应原料血浆的；  
（十二）重复使用一次性采血器材的；  
（十三）其他不依照规定进行检测或者违背操作规定的。

**Article 5** Any department that has been approved by the competent state authority to collect or supply blood or make or supply blood products that falls under any of the following circumstances shall be determined as "failing to carry out testing in accordance with the provisions or violating other operational regulations" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 334 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) a blood station fails to use reagents produced by two enterprises to conduct two tests for HIV antibodies, hepatitis B virus surface antigens, hepatitis C virus antibodies, and syphilis antibodies;  
(2) a plasma collection station fails to test for HIV antibodies, hepatitis B virus surface antigens, hepatitis C virus antibodies, and syphilis antibodies in accordance with the provisions;  
(3) a blood product production enterprise fails to perform re-testing using reagents that have been approved by the competent authority and verified as qualified before putting materials into production;  
(4) the diagnostic reagents used by blood stations, plasma collection stations, and blood product production enterprises have no name of the production unit, no production approval document number, or are verified as unqualified;  
(5) when collecting test specimens, collecting blood, or separating blood components, a blood collection and supply institution uses disposable syringes or other blood collection equipment that have no name of the production unit, no production approval document number, or have exceeded their validity period;  
(6) failing to package, store, or transport blood or source plasma in accordance with the standards and requirements prescribed by the state;  
(7) failing to promptly eliminate blood that tests positive for items required to be tested by the state in accordance with the regulations;  
(8) medical personnel who lack the relevant qualifications carry out blood collection or testing operations;  
(9) collecting blood or plasma from blood donors or plasma donors in excessive quantities or at excessive frequency;  
(10) before collecting blood or plasma, a blood collection and supply institution fails to identify the identity of the blood donor or plasma donor and collects blood or plasma from persons acting under false identities or persons who have failed health checks;  
(11) a blood station collects source plasma without authorization, or a plasma collection station collects blood for clinical use or supplies source plasma to medical institutions without authorization;  
(12) reusing disposable blood collection equipment;  
(13) other circumstances where testing is not carried out in accordance with the provisions or operational regulations are violated.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  对经国家主管部门批准采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的部门，不依照规定进行检测或者违背其他操作规定，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第二款规定的“造成危害他人身体健康后果”，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役：  
（一）造成献血者、供血浆者、受血者感染艾滋病病毒、乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体或者其他经血液传播的病原微生物的；  
（二）造成献血者、供血浆者、受血者重度贫血、造血功能障碍或者其他器官组织损伤导致功能障碍等身体严重危害的；  
（三）造成其他危害他人身体健康后果的。

**Article 6** Where a department that has been approved by the competent state authority to collect or supply blood or make or supply blood products fails to carry out testing in accordance with the provisions or violates other operational regulations, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "causing consequences harmful to the physical health of others" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 334 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the entity shall be fined, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention:  
(1) causing blood donors, plasma donors, or blood recipients to be infected with HIV, hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, Treponema pallidum, or other pathogen microorganisms transmitted through blood;  
(2) causing blood donors, plasma donors, or blood recipients to suffer from severe anemia, hematopoietic dysfunction, or other serious bodily harm such as dysfunction due to organ or tissue damage;  
(3) causing other consequences harmful to the physical health of others.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  经国家主管部门批准的采供血机构和血液制品生产经营单位，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第二款规定的“经国家主管部门批准采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的部门”。

**Article 7** A blood collection and supply institution or a blood product production and operation unit that has been approved by the competent state authority shall be determined as "a department that has been approved by the competent state authority to collect or supply blood or make or supply blood products" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 334 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  本解释所称“血液”，是指全血、成分血和特殊血液成分。  
本解释所称“血液制品”，是指各种人血浆蛋白制品。  
本解释所称“采供血机构”，包括血液中心、中心血站、中心血库、脐带血造血干细胞库和国家卫生行政主管部门根据医学发展需要批准、设置的其他类型血库、单采血浆站。

**Article 8** As used in this Interpretation, "blood" means whole blood, blood components, and special blood components.  
As used in this Interpretation, "blood products" means various human plasma protein products.  
As used in this Interpretation, "blood collection and supply institution" includes blood centers, central blood stations, central blood banks, umbilical cord blood hematopoietic stem cell banks, and other types of blood banks and plasma collection stations approved and established by the competent health administrative department of the state based on the needs of medical development.
