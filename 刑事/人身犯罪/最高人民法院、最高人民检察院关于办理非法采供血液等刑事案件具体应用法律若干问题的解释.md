# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理非法采供血液等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**发文字号**：法释〔2008〕12号

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　对未经国家主管部门批准或者超过批准的业务范围，采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第一款规定的“非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品”。

**Article 1** Whoever collects or supplies blood or manufactures or supplies blood products without approval from the competent State authority or beyond the approved scope of business shall be determined as "illegally collecting or supplying blood or manufacturing or supplying blood products" as provided in Article 334(1) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　对非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第一款规定的“不符合国家规定的标准，足以危害人体健康”，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金：  
（一）采集、供应的血液含有艾滋病病毒、乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体等病原微生物的；  
（二）制作、供应的血液制品含有艾滋病病毒、乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体等病原微生物，或者将含有上述病原微生物的血液用于制作血液制品的；  
（三）使用不符合国家规定的药品、诊断试剂、卫生器材，或者重复使用一次性采血器材采集血液，造成传染病传播危险的；  
（四）违反规定对献血者、供血浆者超量、频繁采集血液、血浆，足以危害人体健康的；  
（五）其他不符合国家有关采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的规定标准，足以危害人体健康的。

**Article 2** Where the illegal collection or supply of blood or the manufacture or supply of blood products falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "failing to meet the standards prescribed by the State, sufficient to endanger human health" as provided in Article 334(1) of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined:  
(1) the collected or supplied blood contains pathogenic microorganisms such as HIV, hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, or Treponema pallidum;  
(2) the manufactured or supplied blood products contain pathogenic microorganisms such as HIV, hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, or Treponema pallidum, or blood containing the aforesaid pathogenic microorganisms is used in the manufacture of blood products;  
(3) the use of pharmaceuticals, diagnostic reagents, or sanitary equipment that do not meet State regulations, or the repeated use of disposable blood collection equipment in collecting blood, creates a risk of transmission of infectious diseases;  
(4) in violation of regulations, blood or plasma is collected from blood donors or plasma donors in excessive quantities or at excessive frequency, sufficient to endanger human health;  
(5) any other circumstance where the collection or supply of blood or the manufacture or supply of blood products fails to meet the relevant standards prescribed by the State, sufficient to endanger human health.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第一款规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）造成献血者、供血浆者、受血者感染乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体或者其他经血液传播的病原微生物的；  
（二）造成献血者、供血浆者、受血者重度贫血、造血功能障碍或者其他器官组织损伤导致功能障碍等身体严重危害的；  
（三）对人体健康造成其他严重危害的。

**Article 3** Where the illegal collection or supply of blood or the manufacture or supply of blood products falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "causing serious harm to human health" as provided in Article 334(1) of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined:  
(1) causing blood donors, plasma donors, or blood recipients to be infected with hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, Treponema pallidum, or other blood-borne pathogenic microorganisms;  
(2) causing blood donors, plasma donors, or blood recipients to suffer severe anemia, hematopoietic dysfunction, or other organ or tissue damage leading to dysfunction or other serious physical harm;  
(3) causing other serious harm to human health.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对非法采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第一款规定的“造成特别严重后果”，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）因血液传播疾病导致人员死亡或者感染艾滋病病毒的；  
（二）造成五人以上感染乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体或者其他经血液传播的病原微生物的；  
（三）造成五人以上重度贫血、造血功能障碍或者其他器官组织损伤导致功能障碍等身体严重危害的；  
（四）造成其他特别严重后果的。

**Article 4** Where the illegal collection or supply of blood or the manufacture or supply of blood products falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "causing particularly serious consequences" as provided in Article 334(1) of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated:  
(1) causing death or HIV infection due to blood-borne diseases;  
(2) causing five or more persons to be infected with hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, Treponema pallidum, or other blood-borne pathogenic microorganisms;  
(3) causing five or more persons to suffer severe anemia, hematopoietic dysfunction, or other organ or tissue damage leading to dysfunction or other serious physical harm;  
(4) causing other particularly serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对经国家主管部门批准采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的部门，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第二款规定的“不依照规定进行检测或者违背其他操作规定”：  
（一）血站未用两个企业生产的试剂对艾滋病病毒抗体、乙型肝炎病毒表面抗原、丙型肝炎病毒抗体、梅毒抗体进行两次检测的；  
（二）单采血浆站不依照规定对艾滋病病毒抗体、乙型肝炎病毒表面抗原、丙型肝炎病毒抗体、梅毒抗体进行检测的；  
（三）血液制品生产企业在投料生产前未用主管部门批准和检定合格的试剂进行复检的；  
（四）血站、单采血浆站和血液制品生产企业使用的诊断试剂没有生产单位名称、生产批准文号或者经检定不合格的；  
（五）采供血机构在采集检验标本、采集血液和成分血分离时，使用没有生产单位名称、生产批准文号或者超过有效期的一次性注射器等采血器材的；  
（六）不依照国家规定的标准和要求包装、储存、运输血液、原料血浆的；  
（七）对国家规定检测项目结果呈阳性的血液未及时按照规定予以清除的；  
（八）不具备相应资格的医务人员进行采血、检验操作的；  
（九）对献血者、供血浆者超量、频繁采集血液、血浆的；  
（十）采供血机构采集血液、血浆前，未对献血者或供血浆者进行身份识别，采集冒名顶替者、健康检查不合格者血液、血浆的；  
（十一）血站擅自采集原料血浆，单采血浆站擅自采集临床用血或者向医疗机构供应原料血浆的；  
（十二）重复使用一次性采血器材的；  
（十三）其他不依照规定进行检测或者违背操作规定的。

**Article 5** Where a department approved by the competent State authority to collect or supply blood or to manufacture or supply blood products falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "failing to carry out testing in accordance with regulations or violating other operational rules" as provided in Article 334(2) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) a blood station fails to conduct two tests for HIV antibody, hepatitis B virus surface antigen, hepatitis C virus antibody, and syphilis antibody using reagents produced by two different manufacturers;  
(2) a plasma collection station fails to test for HIV antibody, hepatitis B virus surface antigen, hepatitis C virus antibody, and syphilis antibody in accordance with regulations;  
(3) a blood product manufacturer fails to perform re-testing with reagents approved and certified as qualified by the competent authority before putting materials into production;  
(4) the diagnostic reagents used by a blood station, plasma collection station, or blood product manufacturer lack the name of the manufacturer, the production approval number, or have been tested as unqualified;  
(5) a blood collection and supply institution uses disposable syringes or other blood collection equipment lacking the name of the manufacturer, the production approval number, or beyond the expiration date when collecting test specimens, collecting blood, or separating blood components;  
(6) failing to package, store, or transport blood or source plasma in accordance with the standards and requirements prescribed by the State;  
(7) failing to promptly discard blood that tests positive for items required by the State for testing in accordance with regulations;  
(8) medical personnel who do not possess the requisite qualifications perform blood collection or testing procedures;  
(9) collecting blood or plasma from blood donors or plasma donors in excessive quantities or at excessive frequencies;  
(10) before collecting blood or plasma, failing to verify the identity of blood donors or plasma donors, and collecting blood or plasma from impostors or persons who fail health examinations;  
(11) a blood station collects source plasma without authorization, or a plasma collection station collects blood for clinical use without authorization or supplies source plasma to medical institutions;  
(12) reusing disposable blood collection equipment;  
(13) any other failure to carry out testing in accordance with regulations or violation of operational rules.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对经国家主管部门批准采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的部门，不依照规定进行检测或者违背其他操作规定，具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第二款规定的“造成危害他人身体健康后果”，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役：  
（一）造成献血者、供血浆者、受血者感染艾滋病病毒、乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒、梅毒螺旋体或者其他经血液传播的病原微生物的；  
（二）造成献血者、供血浆者、受血者重度贫血、造血功能障碍或者其他器官组织损伤导致功能障碍等身体严重危害的；  
（三）造成其他危害他人身体健康后果的。

**Article 6** Where a department approved by the competent State authority to collect or supply blood or to manufacture or supply blood products fails to carry out testing in accordance with regulations or violates other operational rules, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "causing consequences harmful to the health of others" as provided in Article 334(2) of the Criminal Law; the entity shall be fined, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention:  
(1) causing blood donors, plasma donors, or blood recipients to be infected with HIV, hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, Treponema pallidum, or other blood-borne pathogenic microorganisms;  
(2) causing blood donors, plasma donors, or blood recipients to suffer severe anemia, hematopoietic dysfunction, or other organ or tissue damage leading to dysfunction or other serious physical harm;  
(3) causing other consequences harmful to the health of others.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　经国家主管部门批准的采供血机构和血液制品生产经营单位，应认定为[刑法第三百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-334)第二款规定的“经国家主管部门批准采集、供应血液或者制作、供应血液制品的部门”。

**Article 7** Blood collection and supply institutions and blood product production and operation units approved by the competent State authority shall be determined as "departments approved by the competent State authority to collect or supply blood or to manufacture or supply blood products" as provided in Article 334(2) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本解释所称“血液”，是指全血、成分血和特殊血液成分。  
本解释所称“血液制品”，是指各种人血浆蛋白制品。  
本解释所称“采供血机构”，包括血液中心、中心血站、中心血库、脐带血造血干细胞库和国家卫生行政主管部门根据医学发展需要批准、设置的其他类型血库、单采血浆站。

**Article 8** The term "blood" as used in this Interpretation means whole blood, blood components, and special blood components.  
The term "blood products" as used in this Interpretation means various human plasma protein products.  
The term "blood collection and supply institutions" as used in this Interpretation includes blood centers, central blood stations, central blood banks, cord blood hematopoietic stem cell banks, and other types of blood banks and plasma collection stations approved and established by the competent national health administrative department in accordance with the needs of medical development.
