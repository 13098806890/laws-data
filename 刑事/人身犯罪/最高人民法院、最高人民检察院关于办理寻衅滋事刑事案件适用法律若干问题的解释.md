# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理寻衅滋事刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕18号  
**公布日期**：2013-07-15  
**生效日期**：2013-07-22

> （2013年5月27日最高人民法院审判委员会第1579次会议、2013年4月28日最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第5次会议通过　2013年7月15日最高人民法院、最高人民检察院公告公布　自2013年7月22日起施行） 为依法惩治寻衅滋事犯罪，维护社会秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关规定，现就办理寻衅滋事刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　行为人为寻求刺激、发泄情绪、逞强耍横等，无事生非，实施刑法第二百九十三条规定的行为的，应当认定为“寻衅滋事”。 行为人因日常生活中的偶发矛盾纠纷，借故生非，实施刑法第二百九十三条规定的行为的，应当认定为“寻衅滋事”，但矛盾系由被害人故意引发或者被害人对矛盾激化负有主要责任的除外。 行为人因婚恋、家庭、邻里、债务等纠纷，实施殴打、辱骂、恐吓他人或者损毁、占用他人财物等行为的，一般不认定为“寻衅滋事”，但经有关部门批评制止或者处理处罚后，继续实施前列行为，破坏社会秩序的除外。 第二条　随意殴打他人，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百九十三条第一款第一项规定的“情节恶劣”： （一）致一人以上轻伤或者二人以上轻微伤的； （二）引起他人精神失常、自杀等严重后果的； （三）多次随意殴打他人的； （四）持凶器随意殴打他人的； （五）随意殴打精神病人、残疾人、流浪乞讨人员、老年人、孕妇、未成年人，造成恶劣社会影响的； （六）在公共场所随意殴打他人，造成公共场所秩序严重混乱的； （七）其他情节恶劣的情形。 第三条　追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓他人，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百九十三条第一款第二项规定的“情节恶劣”： （一）多次追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓他人，造成恶劣社会影响的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　行为人为寻求刺激、发泄情绪、逞强耍横等，无事生非，实施[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)规定的行为的，应当认定为“寻衅滋事”。  
行为人因日常生活中的偶发矛盾纠纷，借故生非，实施[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)规定的行为的，应当认定为“寻衅滋事”，但矛盾系由被害人故意引发或者被害人对矛盾激化负有主要责任的除外。  
行为人因婚恋、家庭、邻里、债务等纠纷，实施殴打、辱骂、恐吓他人或者损毁、占用他人财物等行为的，一般不认定为“寻衅滋事”，但经有关部门批评制止或者处理处罚后，继续实施前列行为，破坏社会秩序的除外。

**Article 1** Where an actor, for the purpose of seeking stimulation, venting emotions, acting tough, etc., creates trouble without cause and commits the acts specified in Article 293 of the Criminal Law, it shall be deemed as "creating disturbances".  
Where an actor, due to occasional conflicts and disputes in daily life, picks a quarrel for no reason and commits the acts specified in Article 293 of the Criminal Law, it shall be deemed as "creating disturbances", except where the conflict is intentionally provoked by the victim or the victim bears primary responsibility for the escalation of the conflict.  
Where an actor commits acts such as beating, abusing, threatening others, or damaging or occupying others' property due to disputes over marriage, family, neighborhood, debts, etc., it shall generally not be deemed as "creating disturbances", except where the actor continues to commit the above acts after being criticized, stopped, or punished by the relevant departments, thereby disrupting social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　随意殴打他人，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第一款第一项规定的“情节恶劣”：  
（一）致一人以上轻伤或者二人以上轻微伤的；  
（二）引起他人精神失常、自杀等严重后果的；  
（三）多次随意殴打他人的；  
（四）持凶器随意殴打他人的；  
（五）随意殴打精神病人、残疾人、流浪乞讨人员、老年人、孕妇、未成年人，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（六）在公共场所随意殴打他人，造成公共场所秩序严重混乱的；  
（七）其他情节恶劣的情形。

**Article 2** Where a person arbitrarily beats others, disrupting social order, and has any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as specified in item (1) of the first paragraph of Article 293 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing one or more persons to suffer minor injuries or two or more persons to suffer slight injuries;  
(2) causing serious consequences such as mental disorder or suicide to others;  
(3) arbitrarily beating others on multiple occasions;  
(4) arbitrarily beating others with a lethal weapon;  
(5) arbitrarily beating mentally ill persons, disabled persons, vagrants and beggars, elderly persons, pregnant women, or minors, causing adverse social impact;  
(6) arbitrarily beating others in a public place, causing serious disorder in the public place;  
(7) other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓他人，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第一款第二项规定的“情节恶劣”：  
（一）多次追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓他人，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（二）持凶器追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓他人的；  
（三）追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓精神病人、残疾人、流浪乞讨人员、老年人、孕妇、未成年人，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（四）引起他人精神失常、自杀等严重后果的；  
（五）严重影响他人的工作、生活、生产、经营的；  
（六）其他情节恶劣的情形。

**Article 3** Where a person chases, intercepts, abuses, or threatens others, disrupting social order, and has any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as specified in item (2) of the first paragraph of Article 293 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) chasing, intercepting, abusing, or threatening others on multiple occasions, causing adverse social impact;  
(2) chasing, intercepting, abusing, or threatening others with a lethal weapon;  
(3) chasing, intercepting, abusing, or threatening mentally ill persons, disabled persons, vagrants and beggars, elderly persons, pregnant women, or minors, causing adverse social impact;  
(4) causing serious consequences such as mental disorder or suicide to others;  
(5) seriously affecting others' work, life, production, or business operations;  
(6) other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　强拿硬要或者任意损毁、占用公私财物，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第一款第三项规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）强拿硬要公私财物价值一千元以上，或者任意损毁、占用公私财物价值二千元以上的；  
（二）多次强拿硬要或者任意损毁、占用公私财物，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（三）强拿硬要或者任意损毁、占用精神病人、残疾人、流浪乞讨人员、老年人、孕妇、未成年人的财物，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（四）引起他人精神失常、自杀等严重后果的；  
（五）严重影响他人的工作、生活、生产、经营的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 4** Where a person forcibly takes or demands, or arbitrarily damages or occupies public or private property, disrupting social order, and has any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as specified in item (3) of the first paragraph of Article 293 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) forcibly taking or demanding public or private property worth more than 1,000 yuan, or arbitrarily damaging or occupying public or private property worth more than 2,000 yuan;  
(2) forcibly taking or demanding, or arbitrarily damaging or occupying public or private property on multiple occasions, causing adverse social impact;  
(3) forcibly taking or demanding, or arbitrarily damaging or occupying the property of mentally ill persons, disabled persons, vagrants and beggars, elderly persons, pregnant women, or minors, causing adverse social impact;  
(4) causing serious consequences such as mental disorder or suicide to others;  
(5) seriously affecting others' work, life, production, or business operations;  
(6) other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在车站、码头、机场、医院、商场、公园、影剧院、展览会、运动场或者其他公共场所起哄闹事，应当根据公共场所的性质、公共活动的重要程度、公共场所的人数、起哄闹事的时间、公共场所受影响的范围与程度等因素，综合判断是否“造成公共场所秩序严重混乱”。

**Article 5** Where a person creates a disturbance in a station, wharf, airport, hospital, shopping mall, park, theater, exhibition center, sports ground, or other public place, it shall be comprehensively determined whether it "causes serious disorder in the public place" based on factors such as the nature of the public place, the importance of the public activity, the number of people in the public place, the time of the disturbance, and the scope and degree of influence on the public place.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　纠集他人三次以上实施寻衅滋事犯罪，未经处理的，应当依照[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第二款的规定处罚。

**Article 6** Where a person gathers others three or more times to commit the crime of creating disturbances, which have not been dealt with, the punishment shall be imposed in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 293 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　实施寻衅滋事行为，同时符合寻衅滋事罪和故意杀人罪、故意伤害罪、故意毁坏财物罪、敲诈勒索罪、抢夺罪、抢劫罪等罪的构成要件的，依照处罚较重的犯罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Where an act of creating disturbances simultaneously meets the constitutive requirements of the crime of creating disturbances and the crimes of intentional homicide, intentional injury, intentional destruction of property, extortion, snatching, robbery, etc., the conviction and punishment shall be in accordance with the crime that imposes a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　行为人认罪、悔罪，积极赔偿被害人损失或者取得被害人谅解的，可以从轻处罚；犯罪情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。

**Article 8** Where an actor pleads guilty and shows repentance, and actively compensates the victim for losses or obtains the victim's understanding, the punishment may be light; if the circumstances of the crime are minor, the prosecution may be exempted or the actor may be exempted from criminal punishment.
