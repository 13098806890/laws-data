### 正文

三、怎样理解《决定》第一条第一款第（三）项关于“奸淫被拐卖的妇女”的规定？  
《决定》第一条第一款第（三）项所规定的“奸淫被拐卖的妇女”，是指拐卖妇女的犯罪分子在拐卖过程中，与被害妇女发生性关系的行为。不论行为人是否使用了暴力或者胁迫手段，也不论被害妇女是否有反抗行为，都应当按照该款规定处罚。

**Article None** 3. How to understand the provision in Article 1, Paragraph 1, Subparagraph (3) of the Decision concerning 'rape of an abducted or trafficked woman'?  
The 'rape of an abducted or trafficked woman' as provided in Article 1, Paragraph 1, Subparagraph (3) of the Decision means the act of a criminal who engages in sexual relations with the victim woman during the process of abducting or trafficking in women. Regardless of whether the perpetrator uses violence or coercive means, and regardless of whether the victim woman resists, the punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of that subparagraph.

四、怎样理解《决定》第一条第一款第（五）项关于“造成被拐卖的妇女、儿童或者其亲属重伤、死亡或者其他严重后果”的规定？  
《决定》第一条第一款第（五）项所规定的“造成被拐卖的妇女、儿童或者其亲属重伤、死亡或者其他严重后果”，是指由于犯罪分子拐卖妇女、儿童的行为，直接、间接造成被拐卖的妇女、儿童或者其亲属重伤、死亡或者其他严重后果的。例如：由于犯罪分子采取拘禁、捆绑、虐待等手段，致使被害人重伤、死亡或者造成其他严重后果的；由于犯罪分子的拐卖行为以及拐卖中的侮辱、殴打等行为引起的被害人或者其亲属自杀、精神失常或者其他严重后果的，等等。  
对被拐卖的妇女、儿童进行故意杀害、伤害的，应当以故意杀人罪或者故意伤害罪与拐卖妇女、儿童罪实行并罚。

**Article None** 4. How to understand the provision in Article 1, Paragraph 1, Subparagraph (5) of the Decision concerning 'causing serious injury, death, or other serious consequences to the abducted or trafficked woman, child, or their relatives'?  
The 'causing serious injury, death, or other serious consequences to the abducted or trafficked woman, child, or their relatives' as provided in Article 1, Paragraph 1, Subparagraph (5) of the Decision means the direct or indirect causation of serious injury, death, or other serious consequences to the abducted or trafficked woman, child, or their relatives as a result of the criminal's acts of abducting or trafficking in women or children. For example: where the criminal uses means such as detention, binding, or maltreatment, resulting in serious injury, death, or other serious consequences to the victim; where the criminal's abduction or trafficking acts and acts of humiliation, beating, etc. during the abduction or trafficking cause the victim or their relatives to commit suicide, become mentally ill, or suffer other serious consequences, and so forth.  
If a person intentionally kills or injures an abducted or trafficked woman or child, the crime of intentional homicide or intentional injury shall be punished concurrently with the crime of abducting or trafficking in women or children.

五、如何区分绑架妇女、儿童罪和绑架勤索罪？  
（一）根据《决定》第二条的规定，绑架妇女、儿童罪，是指以出卖为目的，使用暴力、胁迫或者麻醉方法，劫持妇女、儿童的行为。绑架勒家罪，是指以勒索财物为目的，使用暴力、胁迫或者麻醉方法，劫持他人的行为。两罪的主要区别在于：1.犯罪目的不同。前者是以出卖为目的，后者则是以勒索财物为目的。2.犯罪侵犯的客体不同。前者侵犯的是被绑架人的人身权利，后者侵犯的不仅是被绑架人的人身权利，还侵犯了他们的财产权利。3.犯罪对象不同。前者的绑架对象仅指妇女、儿童，后者则是指包括妇女、儿童在内的一切人。  
（二）根据《决定》第二条第二款的规定，以出卖为目的，偷盗婴、幼儿的，以绑架儿童罪定罪，并依照《决定》第二条第一款的规定处罚。  
（三）以勒索财物为目的，偷盗婴、幼儿的，以绑架勒索罪定罪，并依照《决定》第二条第一款的规定处罚。  
（四）以索债为目的，非法剥夺他人人身自由的，定非法拘禁罪，不能定绑架勒索罪。

**Article None** 5. How to distinguish between the crime of kidnapping women or children and the crime of kidnapping for extortion?  
(1) According to Article 2 of the Decision, the crime of kidnapping women or children means the act of using violence, coercion, or anaesthetic methods to abduct a woman or child for the purpose of selling. The crime of kidnapping for extortion means the act of using violence, coercion, or anaesthetic methods to abduct another person for the purpose of extorting property. The main distinctions between the two crimes are as follows: 1. The purpose of the crime is different. The former has the purpose of selling, while the latter has the purpose of extorting property. 2. The object infringed upon by the crime is different. The former infringes upon the personal rights of the kidnapped person, while the latter infringes not only upon the personal rights of the kidnapped person but also upon their property rights. 3. The target of the crime is different. The target of kidnapping in the former is limited to women or children, while the target in the latter includes all persons, including women and children.  
(2) According to the provisions of Article 2, Paragraph 2 of the Decision, if a person steals an infant or toddler for the purpose of selling, the person shall be convicted of the crime of kidnapping children and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 2, Paragraph 1 of the Decision.  
(3) If a person steals an infant or toddler for the purpose of extorting property, the person shall be convicted of the crime of kidnapping for extortion and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 2, Paragraph 1 of the Decision.  
(4) If a person unlawfully deprives another person of their personal freedom for the purpose of recovering debts, the person shall be convicted of the crime of unlawful detention, and shall not be convicted of the crime of kidnapping for extortion.

六、怎样认定拐卖妇女、儿童罪和绑架妇女、儿童罪中“情节特别严重”的行为？  
拐卖妇女、儿童罪中的“情节特别严重”，是指《决定》第一条第一款所列六项情形中特别严重的情节。在具体执行中，不应在这六项情形之外扩大范围。  
绑架妇女、儿童罪中的“情节特别严重”，主要是指绑架妇女、儿童犯罪集团的首要分子情节特别严重的；绑架手段极其残忍、恶劣的；造成被害人或者其家属重伤、死亡或者其他严重后果情节特别严重的；绑架妇女、儿童多人具有极大社会危害性的，等等。

**Article None** 6. How to determine 'particularly serious circumstances' in the crime of abducting or trafficking in women or children and the crime of kidnapping women or children?  
'Particularly serious circumstances' in the crime of abducting or trafficking in women or children refers to the particularly serious circumstances among the six types of circumstances listed in Article 1, Paragraph 1 of the Decision. In specific enforcement, the scope shall not be expanded beyond these six types of circumstances.  
'Particularly serious circumstances' in the crime of kidnapping women or children mainly refers to circumstances where the principal offender of a criminal group that kidnaps women or children has particularly serious circumstances; where the means of kidnapping are extremely cruel or heinous; where serious injury, death, or other serious consequences are caused to the victim or their family members and the circumstances are particularly serious; where a large number of women or children are kidnapped, causing a great social harm, and so forth.

七、怎样认定收买被拐卖、绑架的妇女、儿童罪？  
根据《决定》第三条第一款的规定，收买被拐卖、绑架的妇女、儿童罪，是指不以出卖为目的，收买被拐卖、绑架的妇女、儿童的行为。以出卖为目的，收买被拐卖、绑架的妇女、儿童的，以拐卖妇女、儿童罪论处。  
（一）收买人必须明知是被拐卖、绑架的妇女、儿童而予以收买的，才能构成本罪。  
（二）共同参与了收买被拐卖、绑架的妇女、儿童犯罪行为的（例如，有些收买行为是全体家庭成员或者亲属朋友共同商量决定的等），对于其中的主犯，应当追究刑事责任；对于其他参与者，如果是情节显著轻微危害不大的，不认为是犯罪，不追究刑事责任。  
（三）被买妇女与收买人已成婚，并愿意留在当地共同生活的，对收买人可以视为“按照被买妇女的意愿，不阻碍其返回原居住地”，不追究刑事责任。

**Article None** 7. How to determine the crime of buying an abducted, trafficked, or kidnapped woman or child?  
According to Article 3, Paragraph 1 of the Decision, the crime of buying an abducted, trafficked, or kidnapped woman or child means the act of buying an abducted, trafficked, or kidnapped woman or child without the purpose of selling. If a person buys an abducted, trafficked, or kidnapped woman or child for the purpose of selling, the person shall be dealt with as the crime of abducting or trafficking in women or children.  
(1) Only if the buyer knows that the woman or child has been abducted, trafficked, or kidnapped and still buys the person, shall the crime be constituted.  
(2) If a person jointly participates in the criminal act of buying an abducted, trafficked, or kidnapped woman or child (for example, some buying acts are decided jointly by all family members or relatives and friends), the principal offender among them shall be investigated for criminal liability; for other participants, if the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not great, the act shall not be considered a crime and criminal liability shall not be investigated.  
(3) If the bought woman has married the buyer and is willing to stay and live together locally, the buyer may be deemed to have 'not obstructed her return to her original place of residence in accordance with the wishes of the bought woman' and criminal liability shall not be investigated.

八、怎样划分婴儿、幼儿、儿童的年龄界限？  
《决定》和本解答中所说的“儿童”，是指不满十四岁的人。其中，不满一岁的为婴儿，一岁以上不满六岁的为幼儿。

**Article None** 8. How to define the age limits for infants, toddlers, and children?  
'Children' as referred to in the Decision and this Interpretation means persons under 14 years of age. Among them, those under one year old are infants, and those one year old or older but under six years old are toddlers.

九、如何理解《决定》的时效问题？  
（一）对在《决定》公布施行后发生的案件，依照《决定》的规定办理。对在《决定》公布施行前发生、公布施行后尚未处理或者正在处理的案件，依照刑法第九条规定的原则办理。  
（二）本解答发布后，对应当按照《决定》处理的案件，适用本解答。《决定》公布施行前已处理的案件和本解答发布前已按《决定》处理的案件，不再适用本解答。  
（三）本解答发布前有关办理拐卖、绑架妇女、儿童案件的司法解释，与本解答不一致的，不再适用。

**Article None** 9. How to understand the issue of the temporal effect of the Decision?  
(1) Cases occurring after the promulgation and coming into force of the Decision shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Decision. Cases occurring before the promulgation and coming into force of the Decision but not yet handled or being handled after the promulgation and coming into force shall be handled in accordance with the principles provided in Article 9 of the Criminal Law.  
(2) After the issuance of this Interpretation, it shall apply to cases that shall be handled in accordance with the Decision. This Interpretation shall no longer apply to cases already handled before the promulgation and coming into force of the Decision or cases already handled in accordance with the Decision before the issuance of this Interpretation.  
(3) Judicial interpretations concerning the handling of cases of abducting, trafficking in, or kidnapping women or children that were issued before the issuance of this Interpretation and are inconsistent with this Interpretation shall no longer apply.
