# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理寻衅滋事刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕18号  
**公布日期**：2013-05-27  
**生效日期**：2013-07-22

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理寻衅滋事刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年5月27日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1579次会议、2013年4月28日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第5次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年7月22日起施行。 2013年7月15日 最高人民法院   最高人民检察院 关于办理寻衅滋事刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕18号 为依法惩治寻衅滋事犯罪，维护社会秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关规定，现就办理寻衅滋事刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   行为人为寻求刺激、发泄情绪、逞强耍横等，无事生非，实施刑法第二百九十三条规定的行为的，应当认定为“寻衅滋事”。 行为人因日常生活中的偶发矛盾纠纷，借故生非，实施刑法第二百九十三条规定的行为的，应当认定为“寻衅滋事”，但矛盾系由被害人故意引发或者被害人对矛盾激化负有主要责任的除外。 行为人因婚恋、家庭、邻里、债务等纠纷，实施殴打、辱骂、恐吓他人或者损毁、占用他人财物等行为的，一般不认定为“寻衅滋事”，但经有关部门批评制止或者处理处罚后，继续实施前列行为，破坏社会秩序的除外。 第二条   随意殴打他人，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百九十三条第一款第一项规定的“情节恶劣”： （一）致一人以上轻伤或者二人以上轻微伤的； （二）引起他人精神失常、自杀等严重后果的； （三）多次随意殴打他人的； （四）持凶器随意殴打他人的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   行为人为寻求刺激、发泄情绪、逞强耍横等，无事生非，实施[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)规定的行为的，应当认定为“寻衅滋事”。  
行为人因日常生活中的偶发矛盾纠纷，借故生非，实施[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)规定的行为的，应当认定为“寻衅滋事”，但矛盾系由被害人故意引发或者被害人对矛盾激化负有主要责任的除外。  
行为人因婚恋、家庭、邻里、债务等纠纷，实施殴打、辱骂、恐吓他人或者损毁、占用他人财物等行为的，一般不认定为“寻衅滋事”，但经有关部门批评制止或者处理处罚后，继续实施前列行为，破坏社会秩序的除外。

**Article 1** An actor who, for the purpose of seeking stimulation, venting emotions, showing off, etc., creates trouble without cause and engages in conduct prescribed in Article 293 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be deemed as picking quarrels and provoking trouble.  
If an actor, due to accidental disputes or conflicts in daily life, seizes on a pretext to create trouble, and engages in conduct prescribed in Article 293 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, such conduct shall be deemed as picking quarrels and provoking trouble, unless the conflict was intentionally provoked by the victim or the victim bears primary responsibility for the escalation of the conflict.  
If an actor engages in conduct such as beating, insulting, intimidating others, or damaging or occupying others' property due to disputes over marriage, family, neighborhood, debts, etc., such conduct shall generally not be deemed as picking quarrels and provoking trouble, unless the actor continues to commit the aforesaid conduct after being criticized, stopped, or punished by the relevant authorities, thereby undermining social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   随意殴打他人，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第一款第一项规定的“情节恶劣”：  
（一）致一人以上轻伤或者二人以上轻微伤的；  
（二）引起他人精神失常、自杀等严重后果的；  
（三）多次随意殴打他人的；  
（四）持凶器随意殴打他人的；  
（五）随意殴打精神病人、残疾人、流浪乞讨人员、老年人、孕妇、未成年人，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（六）在公共场所随意殴打他人，造成公共场所秩序严重混乱的；  
（七）其他情节恶劣的情形。

**Article 2** Where a person arbitrarily beats others, thereby undermining social order, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as flagrant circumstances as prescribed in subparagraph (1) of the first paragraph of Article 293 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causing minor injuries to one or more persons or slight injuries to two or more persons;  
(2) Causing serious consequences such as mental disorder or suicide of others;  
(3) Repeatedly arbitrarily beating others;  
(4) Arbitrarily beating others with lethal weapons;  
(5) Arbitrarily beating mentally ill persons, disabled persons, vagrants and beggars, elderly persons, pregnant women, or minors, thereby causing adverse social impact;  
(6) Arbitrarily beating others in a public place, thereby causing serious chaos in public order;  
(7) Other circumstances where the circumstances are flagrant.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓他人，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第一款第二项规定的“情节恶劣”：  
（一）多次追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓他人，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（二）持凶器追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓他人的；  
（三）追逐、拦截、辱骂、恐吓精神病人、残疾人、流浪乞讨人员、老年人、孕妇、未成年人，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（四）引起他人精神失常、自杀等严重后果的；  
（五）严重影响他人的工作、生活、生产、经营的；  
（六）其他情节恶劣的情形。

**Article 3** Where a person pursues, intercepts, insults, or intimidates others, thereby undermining social order, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as flagrant circumstances as prescribed in subparagraph (2) of the first paragraph of Article 293 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Repeatedly pursuing, intercepting, insulting, or intimidating others, thereby causing adverse social impact;  
(2) Pursuing, intercepting, insulting, or intimidating others with lethal weapons;  
(3) Pursuing, intercepting, insulting, or intimidating mentally ill persons, disabled persons, vagrants and beggars, elderly persons, pregnant women, or minors, thereby causing adverse social impact;  
(4) Causing serious consequences such as mental disorder or suicide of others;  
(5) Seriously affecting the work, life, production, or business of others;  
(6) Other circumstances where the circumstances are flagrant.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   强拿硬要或者任意损毁、占用公私财物，破坏社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第一款第三项规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）强拿硬要公私财物价值一千元以上，或者任意损毁、占用公私财物价值二千元以上的；  
（二）多次强拿硬要或者任意损毁、占用公私财物，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（三）强拿硬要或者任意损毁、占用精神病人、残疾人、流浪乞讨人员、老年人、孕妇、未成年人的财物，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（四）引起他人精神失常、自杀等严重后果的；  
（五）严重影响他人的工作、生活、生产、经营的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 4** Where a person forcibly takes or demands, or wantonly damages or occupies public or private property, thereby undermining social order, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as serious circumstances as prescribed in subparagraph (3) of the first paragraph of Article 293 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Forcibly taking or demanding public or private property worth 1,000 yuan or more, or wantonly damaging or occupying public or private property worth 2,000 yuan or more;  
(2) Repeatedly forcibly taking or demanding, or wantonly damaging or occupying public or private property, thereby causing adverse social impact;  
(3) Forcibly taking or demanding, or wantonly damaging or occupying property of mentally ill persons, disabled persons, vagrants and beggars, elderly persons, pregnant women, or minors, thereby causing adverse social impact;  
(4) Causing serious consequences such as mental disorder or suicide of others;  
(5) Seriously affecting the work, life, production, or business of others;  
(6) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   在车站、码头、机场、医院、商场、公园、影剧院、展览会、运动场或者其他公共场所起哄闹事，应当根据公共场所的性质、公共活动的重要程度、公共场所的人数、起哄闹事的时间、公共场所受影响的范围与程度等因素，综合判断是否“造成公共场所秩序严重混乱”。

**Article 5** Where a person stirs up trouble at a station, wharf, airport, hospital, shopping mall, park, theater, exhibition center, stadium, or other public place, the determination of whether it "causes serious chaos in public order at the public place" shall be made comprehensively based on factors such as the nature of the public place, the importance of the public activity, the number of persons in the public place, the time of stirring up trouble, and the scope and degree of impact on the public place.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   纠集他人三次以上实施寻衅滋事犯罪，未经处理的，应当依照[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第二款的规定处罚。

**Article 6** Where a person gathers others to commit the crime of picking quarrels and provoking trouble three or more times, and such acts have not been dealt with, the person shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 293 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   实施寻衅滋事行为，同时符合寻衅滋事罪和故意杀人罪、故意伤害罪、故意毁坏财物罪、敲诈勒索罪、抢夺罪、抢劫罪等罪的构成要件的，依照处罚较重的犯罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Where conduct that constitutes picking quarrels and provoking trouble simultaneously meets the constituent elements of the crime of picking quarrels and provoking trouble and other crimes such as intentional homicide, intentional injury, intentional destruction of property, extortion, forcible seizure, robbery, etc., the actor shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the crime that imposes the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   行为人认罪、悔罪，积极赔偿被害人损失或者取得被害人谅解的，可以从轻处罚；犯罪情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。

**Article 8** Where an actor pleads guilty and shows remorse, actively compensates the victim for losses, or obtains the victim's forgiveness, the actor may be given a light punishment; if the circumstances of the crime are minor, the actor may not be prosecuted or may be exempted from criminal punishment.
