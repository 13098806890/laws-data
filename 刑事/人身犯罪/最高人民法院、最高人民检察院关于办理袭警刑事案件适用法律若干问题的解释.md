# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理袭警刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：高检发释字〔2025〕1号  
**公布日期**：2025-01-15  
**生效日期**：2025-01-18

> （2024年11月4日最高人民法院审判委员会第1930次会议、2024年6月27日最高人民检察院第十四届检察委员会第三十二次会议通过，自2025年1月18日起施行） 高检发释字〔2025〕1号 为依法惩治袭警犯罪，保障人民警察依法执行职务，保护人民警察人身安全，维护国家法律权威和社会秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》和《中华人民共和国人民警察法》等法律规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　袭击正在依法执行职务的人民警察，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百七十七条第五款规定的“暴力袭击”： （一）实施撕咬、掌掴、踢打、抱摔、投掷物品等行为，造成轻微伤以上后果的； （二）实施打砸、毁坏、抢夺人民警察乘坐的车辆、使用的警械等行为，足以危及人身安全的。 与人民警察发生轻微肢体冲突，或者为摆脱抓捕、约束实施甩手、挣脱、蹬腿等一般性抗拒行为，危害不大的，或者仅实施辱骂、讽刺等言语攻击行为的，不属于刑法第二百七十七条第五款规定的“暴力袭击”。 第二条　暴力袭击正在依法执行职务的人民警察，具有下列情形之一，足以致人重伤或者死亡的，应当认定为刑法第二百七十七条第五款规定的“严重危及其人身安全”： （一）使用枪支、管制刀具或者其他具有杀伤力的工具的； （二）驾驶机动车撞击人民警察或者其乘坐的车辆的； （三）其他严重暴力袭击行为。 第三条　实施袭警犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚： （一）造成人民警察轻伤的； （二）致使人民警察不能正常执行职务，造成他人伤亡、犯罪嫌疑人脱逃、关键证据灭失或者公私财产重大损失等严重后果的； （三）纠集或者煽动多人袭警的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　袭击正在依法执行职务的人民警察，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第五款规定的“暴力袭击”：  
（一）实施撕咬、掌掴、踢打、抱摔、投掷物品等行为，造成轻微伤以上后果的；  
（二）实施打砸、毁坏、抢夺人民警察乘坐的车辆、使用的警械等行为，足以危及人身安全的。  
与人民警察发生轻微肢体冲突，或者为摆脱抓捕、约束实施甩手、挣脱、蹬腿等一般性抗拒行为，危害不大的，或者仅实施辱骂、讽刺等言语攻击行为的，不属于[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第五款规定的“暴力袭击”。

**Article 1** : Where a person assaults a People's Police officer who is lawfully performing duties, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "violent assault" as provided in paragraph 5 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) committing acts such as biting, slapping, kicking, throwing, projecting objects, etc., resulting in consequences of minor injury or above;  
(2) committing acts such as smashing, destroying, or seizing the vehicle used by the People's Police officer or the police equipment used, which is sufficient to endanger personal safety.  
Minor physical conflicts with a People's Police officer, or general resisting acts such as shaking off, breaking free, or kicking legs to escape arrest or restraint, where the harm is minor, or merely verbal attacks such as abusive language or sarcasm, shall not constitute "violent assault" as provided in paragraph 5 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　暴力袭击正在依法执行职务的人民警察，具有下列情形之一，足以致人重伤或者死亡的，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第五款规定的“严重危及其人身安全”：  
（一）使用枪支、管制刀具或者其他具有杀伤力的工具的；  
（二）驾驶机动车撞击人民警察或者其乘坐的车辆的；  
（三）其他严重暴力袭击行为。

**Article 2** : Where a person violently assaults a People's Police officer who is lawfully performing duties, and any of the following circumstances exists, which is sufficient to cause serious injury or death, it shall be deemed as "seriously endangering his personal safety" as provided in paragraph 5 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) using firearms, controlled knives, or other lethal tools;  
(2) driving a motor vehicle to ram into a People's Police officer or the vehicle in which he is riding;  
(3) other acts of serious violent assault.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　实施袭警犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）造成人民警察轻伤的；  
（二）致使人民警察不能正常执行职务，造成他人伤亡、犯罪嫌疑人脱逃、关键证据灭失或者公私财产重大损失等严重后果的；  
（三）纠集或者煽动多人袭警的；  
（四）袭击人民警察二人以上的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 3** : Where a person commits a crime of assaulting a police officer, and any of the following circumstances exists, a severe punishment shall be imposed:  
(1) causing minor injury to a People's Police officer;  
(2) rendering a People's Police officer unable to perform duties normally, causing serious consequences such as casualties of others, escape of criminal suspects, loss of key evidence, or major losses of public or private property;  
(3) gathering or inciting multiple persons to assault a police officer;  
(4) assaulting two or more People's Police officers;  
(5) other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　对于人民警察执法活动存在过错，在认定行为人暴力袭击行为是否构成袭警罪时，应当综合考虑行为人的暴力程度、危害后果及执法过错程度等因素，依法妥当处理。  
人民警察执法活动存在严重过错的，对行为人一般不作为犯罪处理。执法过错较大，袭击行为暴力程度较轻、危害不大的，可以不作为犯罪处理。袭击行为造成严重后果，确需追究刑事责任的，应当依法从宽处理。

**Article 4** : Where there is fault in the law enforcement activities of a People's Police officer, in determining whether the violent assault conduct of the actor constitutes the crime of assaulting a police officer, factors such as the degree of violence, harmful consequences, and the degree of fault in law enforcement shall be comprehensively considered, and proper handling shall be carried out according to law.  
Where there is serious fault in the law enforcement activities of a People's Police officer, the actor shall generally not be treated as committing a crime. Where the fault in law enforcement is relatively significant, and the assault conduct is of a minor degree of violence with little harm, it may not be treated as a crime. Where the assault conduct causes serious consequences and it is truly necessary to investigate criminal liability, lenient treatment shall be given according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　醉酒的人实施袭警犯罪的，应当负刑事责任。

**Article 5** : A person who commits a crime of assaulting a police officer while intoxicated shall bear criminal liability.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　教唆、煽动他人实施袭警犯罪或者明知他人实施袭警犯罪而提供工具或者其他帮助，情节严重的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 6** : Any person who instigates or incites another to commit a crime of assaulting a police officer, or who, knowing that another is committing a crime of assaulting a police officer, provides tools or other assistance, if the circumstances are serious, shall be punished as a joint crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　行为人阻碍人民警察依法执行职务，但未实施暴力袭击行为的，不构成袭警罪；符合[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第一款规定的，以妨害公务罪定罪处罚。  
对在非工作时间遇有紧急情况依法履行职责的人民警察实施暴力袭击，符合[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第五款规定的，以袭警罪定罪处罚。  
对非执行职务的人民警察实施报复性暴力袭击的，不构成袭警罪；符合[刑法第二百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-234)、第二百三十二条等规定的，依照相应犯罪从重处罚。

**Article 7** : Where an actor obstructs a People's Police officer from lawfully performing duties but does not commit a violent assault, the crime of assaulting a police officer shall not be established; if the conduct falls within the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law, the actor shall be convicted and punished for the crime of obstructing public service.  
Where a person commits a violent assault against a People's Police officer who is lawfully performing duties in an emergency situation outside working hours, and the conduct falls within the provisions of paragraph 5 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law, the actor shall be convicted and punished for the crime of assaulting a police officer.  
Where a person commits a retaliatory violent assault against a People's Police officer who is not performing duties, the crime of assaulting a police officer shall not be established; if the conduct falls within the provisions of Articles 234, 232, etc. of the Criminal Law, the actor shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with the corresponding crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　暴力袭击正在依法配合人民警察执行职务的警务辅助人员的，不构成袭警罪；符合[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第一款规定的，以妨害公务罪定罪处罚。  
同时暴力袭击正在依法执行职务的人民警察和配合人民警察执行职务的警务辅助人员，符合[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第五款规定的，以袭警罪从重处罚。

**Article 8** : Where a person violently assaults a police auxiliary officer who is lawfully assisting a People's Police officer in performing duties, the crime of assaulting a police officer shall not be established; if the conduct falls within the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law, the actor shall be convicted and punished for the crime of obstructing public service.  
Where a person simultaneously violently assaults a People's Police officer who is lawfully performing duties and a police auxiliary officer who is assisting the People's Police officer in performing duties, and the conduct falls within the provisions of paragraph 5 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law, the actor shall be given a severe punishment for the crime of assaulting a police officer.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　实施本解释规定的行为，同时构成袭警罪、妨害公务罪和故意杀人罪、故意伤害罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 9** : Where an act prescribed in this Interpretation concurrently constitutes the crime of assaulting a police officer, the crime of obstructing public service, and crimes such as intentional homicide, intentional injury, etc., the actor shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision that imposes a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　实施本解释规定的行为，综合考虑行为人认罪悔罪表现、赔偿损失情况、行为手段、危害后果等情形，认为犯罪情节较轻的，可以从宽处罚；犯罪情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 10** : Where a person commits an act prescribed in this Interpretation, if after comprehensively considering factors such as the actor's confession and repentance, compensation for losses, means of conduct, harmful consequences, etc., the criminal circumstances are considered relatively minor, a lighter punishment may be imposed; if the criminal circumstances are minor, the case may be not prosecuted or the actor may be exempted from criminal punishment; if the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not significant, it shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于实施本解释规定的相关行为被不起诉或者免予刑事处罚的行为人，需要给予行政处罚、政务处分或者其他处分的，依法移送有关主管机关处理。

**Article 11** : Where an actor who has committed relevant acts prescribed in this Interpretation and is not prosecuted or is exempted from criminal punishment requires an administrative penalty, disciplinary sanction, or other sanction, the case shall be transferred to the relevant competent authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对于[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第五款规定的“人民警察”，依照人民警察法的相关规定认定，包括公安机关、国家安全机关、监狱等部门的人民警察和人民法院、人民检察院的司法警察。

**Article 12** : The term "People's Police officer" as provided in paragraph 5 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the People's Police Law, including People's Police officers of public security organs, state security organs, prisons, and other departments, as well as judicial police officers of the People's Courts and People's Procuratorates.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本解释自2025年1月18日起施行。本解释施行后，之前发布的规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 13** : This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 18, 2025. After this Interpretation comes into force, if any previously issued normative documents are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
