# 最高人民法院关于办理未成年人刑事案件适用法律的若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1995〕9号  
**公布日期**：1995-05-02

### 正文

一、关于确定未成年人刑事责任的年龄  
未成年人犯罪，是指已满14岁不满18岁的人实施了法律规定的犯罪行为。实施犯罪时的年龄，一律按照公历的年、月、日计算。过了周岁生日，从第二天起，为已满××周岁．  
审理未成年人刑事案件，应当把被告人犯罪时的年龄作为重要事实予以查清。法律文书中要写明未成年被告人出生年、月、日。对于未成年被告人犯罪时的年龄没有查清，而又关系到应否追究刑事责任和判处何种刑罚的公诉案件，应当退回人民检察院补充侦查。

**Article None** I. Determination of the Age for Criminal Liability of Minors  
A "minor crime" refers to an act committed by a person who has reached the age of 14 but not yet the age of 18 that constitutes a crime under the law. The age at the time of commission of the crime shall be calculated according to the Gregorian calendar year, month, and day. After the birthday, from the following day, the person shall be deemed to have reached the full age of XX years.  
In the trial of a criminal case involving a minor, the age of the defendant at the time of the crime shall be ascertained as an important fact. The legal documents shall specify the year, month, and day of the minor defendant's birth. In a public prosecution case where the age of the minor defendant at the time of the crime has not been ascertained and which concerns whether criminal liability shall be investigated and what punishment shall be imposed, the case shall be returned to the People's Procuratorate for supplementary investigation.

二、关于刑法第十四条第二款的适用  
根据刑法第十四条第二款规定，已满14岁不满16岁的人，犯杀人、重伤、抢劫、放火、惯窃罪或者其他严重破坏社会秩序罪，应当负刑事责任。具体确定已满14岁不满16岁被告人的刑事责任，应根据案件情况慎重考虑。  
（一）已满14岁不满16岁的人被胁迫、诱骗参与犯罪，被教唆犯罪，或者属于犯罪预备、中止、未遂，情节一般的，可以免除处罚或者不认为是犯罪。  
（二）以下情形，可以不认为是犯罪：  
1、已满14岁不满16岁的人出于以大欺小，以强凌弱，使用语言威胁或者使用轻微暴力强行索要其他未成年人的生活、学习用品或者钱财的；  
2、已满14岁不满16岁的人盗窃财物，数额刚达到或者略过“数额巨大”标准，而其他情节轻微，又系初犯或者偶犯的；盗窃近亲属的财物，其亲属不要求对被告人定罪处罚的；  
3、已满14岁不满16岁的人偶尔与幼女发生性行为，情节轻微、尚未造成严重后果的。  
（三）未成年人在年满14岁以前和已满14岁不满16岁期间都实施了刑法第十四条第二款规定的犯罪行为，应当对其已满14岁不满16岁期间实施的行为追究刑事责任，而不应将年满14岁以前实施的行为作为犯罪一并追究。  
未成年人在年满16岁前后都实施了刑法第十四条第二款规定以外的其他犯罪行为，应当对其年满16岁以后的行为追究刑事责任，而不应把年满16岁以前实施的行为作为犯罪一并追究．

**Article None** II. Application of Paragraph 2 of Article 14 of the Criminal Law  
According to the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 14 of the Criminal Law, persons who have reached the age of 14 but not the age of 16 and commit crimes of homicide, causing serious injury, robbery, arson, habitual theft, or other crimes seriously undermining the social order shall bear criminal liability. The specific determination of the criminal liability of a defendant aged 14 but under 16 shall be made cautiously after considering the circumstances of the case.  
(A) A person aged 14 but under 16 who was coerced, induced to participate in a crime, instigated to commit a crime, or whose act constitutes preparation for a crime, discontinuance of a crime, or attempted crime, where the circumstances are ordinary, may be exempted from punishment or not be deemed to have committed a crime.  
(B) The following circumstances may not be deemed as a crime:  
1. Where a person aged 14 but under 16, out of bullying the weak or oppressing the small, uses verbal threats or minor violence to forcibly extort daily necessities, school supplies, or money from other minors;  
2. Where a person aged 14 but under 16 steals property, and the amount just reaches or slightly exceeds the standard of “huge amount”, but other circumstances are minor, and the person is a first-time or occasional offender; or where the person steals property of close relatives and the relatives do not request that the defendant be convicted and punished;  
3. Where a person aged 14 but under 16 occasionally engages in sexual conduct with a young girl, and the circumstances are minor and no serious consequences have been caused.  
(C) Where a minor commits any of the criminal acts specified in Paragraph 2 of Article 14 of the Criminal Law before reaching the age of 14 and also during the period between the ages of 14 and 16, criminal liability shall be investigated only for the acts committed during the period between the ages of 14 and 16, and the acts committed before the age of 14 shall not be prosecuted as crimes together.  
Where a minor commits other criminal acts not specified in Paragraph 2 of Article 14 of the Criminal Law both before and after reaching the age of 16, criminal liability shall be investigated only for the acts committed after reaching the age of 16, and the acts committed before the age of 16 shall not be prosecuted as crimes together.

三、关于对未成年罪犯刑罚的适用  
对未成年罪犯适用刑罚应当坚持“教育为主，惩罚为辅”的原则。  
（一）剥夺政治权利刑的适用  
对犯严重破坏社会秩序罪的未成年罪犯，除依法判处无期徒刑、死刑缓期执行的以外，一般不附加判处剥夺政治权利刑。  
对于未成年罪犯，不应单独适用剥夺政治权利刑。  
（二）从轻、减轻处罚的适用  
对未成年罪犯依法从轻处罚，应当在法定刑范围内判处相对较轻的刑种或者相对较短的刑期；依法减轻处罚，应当在法定最低刑以下判处刑罚。  
在具体量刑时，不但要根据犯罪事实、犯罪性质和危害社会的程度，还要充分考虑未成年人犯罪的动机、犯罪时的年龄，是否初犯、偶犯或者惯犯，在共同犯罪中的地位和作用等情节，以及犯罪后有无悔罪、个人一贯表现等情况，决定对其适用从轻还是减轻处罚和从轻或者减轻处罚的幅度，使判处的刑罚有利于未成年罪犯的改过自新和健康成长。  
（三）缓刑的适用对于被判处拘役、三年以下有期徒刑的未成年罪犯，犯罪后有悔罪表现，家庭有监护条件或者社会帮教措施能够落实，认为适用缓刑确实不致再危害社会的，应当适用缓刑。  
有下列情形之一的，一般不宜适用缓刑：惯犯、有前科或者被劳动教养二次以上的；共同犯罪中情节严重的主犯；犯罪后拒不认罪的。  
（四）免予刑事处分的适用  
未成年罪犯中的初犯、偶犯，如果罪行较轻，悔罪表现好，并具有下列情形之一的，一般应适用刑法第三十二条的规定免予刑事处分：预备犯、中止犯、防卫过当、避险过当、共同犯罪中的从犯、胁从犯，以及犯罪后自首或者有立功表现的。对免予刑事处分的，可予以训诫或者责令具结悔过、赔礼道歉、赔偿损失，或者建议有关主管部门给予行政处分。  
（五）对未成年罪犯的减刑、假释  
1、未成年罪犯认罪服判，遵守教育改造规范，积极学习文化和生产技能，可以视为“确有悔改表现”。未成年罪犯确有悔改或者立功表现的，人民法院应当及时予以减刑；被判处拘役、有期徒刑宣告缓刑的未成年罪犯，认罪悔罪，并有真诚悔罪的实际行动，也可予以减刑，同时相应地缩减缓刑考验期。  
2、被判处死刑缓期执行的未成年罪犯，二年期满，符合减刑条件的，应即减为十五年以上二十年以下有期徒刑。  
被判处无期徒刑的未成年罪犯，在服刑期间确有悔改或者立功表现的，可以减为十五年以上十八年以下有期徒刑；对确有悔改并有立功表现的，可以减为十年以上十五年以下有期徒刑。  
被判处有期徒刑的未成年罪犯，在服刑期间确有悔改或者立功表现，一般一次可以减一年六个月以下有期徒刑；如果确有悔改并有立功表现，一般一次可以减二年六个月以下有期徒刑。对确有悔改并有重大立功表现的，可以不受上述减刑期限的限制。  
对未成年罪犯减刑时，原判附加剥夺政治权利的刑期可以一并酌减，但酌减后的剥夺政治权利的期限，最短不得少于六个月。  
3、对被判处无期徒刑的未成年罪犯，一般执行一年六个月以上方可减刑；对被判处有期徒刑的未成年罪犯，一般执行一年以上方可减刑；两次减刑之间一般以间隔六个月以上为宜。对有重大立功表现的，可以不受上述规定时间的限制。对未成年罪犯减刑后，符合假释条件的，可予以假释。  
4、未成年罪犯在服刑期间确有悔改表现，不致再危害社会，接受教育改造表现突出的，可以适用刑法第七十三条关于“特殊情节”的规定予以假释。但对犯罪集团的首要分子、主犯、惯犯、累犯和罪行特别严重的未成年罪犯假释．应当从严掌握。  
5、被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚的未成年罪犯，在服刑期间已成年，但依照《中华人民共和国监狱法》第七十六条的规定，因余刑不满二年继续留在未成年犯管教所执行刑罚的，对其减刑、假释，仍然可以适用对未成年罪犯的从宽标准。

**Article None** III. Application of Penalties to Minor Offenders  
In applying penalties to minor offenders, the principle of "education as the primary means and punishment as supplementary" shall be adhered to.  
(1) Application of Deprivation of Political Rights  
For minor offenders who commit crimes seriously undermining social order, generally, no additional sentence of deprivation of political rights shall be imposed, except where life imprisonment or the death penalty with a two-year reprieve is legally imposed.  
For minor offenders, the penalty of deprivation of political rights shall not be imposed as a standalone punishment.  
(2) Application of Light Punishment and Mitigated Punishment  
Where a minor offender is given a light punishment according to law, a relatively lighter type of punishment or a relatively shorter term of sentence shall be imposed within the statutory range of punishment; where a mitigated punishment is given according to law, a punishment below the statutory minimum sentence shall be imposed.  
In the specific determination of the sentence, not only the facts of the crime, the nature of the crime, and the degree of harm to society shall be considered, but also full consideration shall be given to the minor's motive for committing the crime, the age at the time of the crime, whether it is a first offense, occasional offense, or habitual offense, the role and status in a joint crime, as well as whether there is remorse after the crime and the minor's general conduct, in order to decide whether to apply a light punishment or a mitigated punishment and the extent thereof, so that the sentence imposed facilitates the minor offender's reform and healthy growth.  
(3) Application of Probation  
For a minor offender sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years who shows remorse after the crime, and where family guardianship conditions exist or social assistance and education measures can be implemented, and it is deemed that the application of probation would indeed not cause further harm to society, probation shall be applied.  
Under any of the following circumstances, probation shall generally not be applied: habitual offenders; persons with a criminal record or who have been subjected to re-education through labor twice or more; principal offenders with serious circumstances in a joint crime; persons who refuse to admit guilt after the crime.  
(4) Application of Exemption from Criminal Punishment  
For minor offenders who are first-time or occasional offenders, if the crime is relatively minor, the remorse is shown, and one of the following circumstances exists, the provisions of Article 32 of the Criminal Law shall generally be applied to exempt them from criminal punishment: preparators, discontinuers, excessive self-defense, excessive necessity, accomplices in a joint crime, coerced accomplices, and those who voluntarily surrender or perform meritorious services after the crime. Where exemption from criminal punishment is granted, a reprimand may be given, or the offender may be ordered to make a statement of repentance, offer an apology, compensate for losses, or a suggestion may be made to the relevant competent authority for administrative sanctions.  
(5) Commutation of Punishment and Parole for Minor Offenders  
1. Where a minor offender accepts the verdict, complies with the norms of education and reform, and actively studies culture and productive skills, this may be deemed as "genuinely demonstrating signs of repentance." Where a minor offender genuinely demonstrates signs of repentance or performs meritorious service, the People's Court shall promptly commute the sentence; where a minor offender sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment with a suspended sentence admits guilt and repents and takes concrete actions of sincere repentance, the sentence may also be commuted, and the probation period shall be correspondingly shortened.  
2. For a minor offender sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, upon expiry of the two-year period and satisfaction of the conditions for commutation, the sentence shall be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 15 years but not more than 20 years.  
For a minor offender sentenced to life imprisonment, if during the term of imprisonment the person genuinely demonstrates signs of repentance or performs meritorious service, the sentence may be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 15 years but not more than 18 years; if the person genuinely demonstrates signs of repentance and performs meritorious service, the sentence may be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 10 years but not more than 15 years.  
For a minor offender sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, if during the term of imprisonment the person genuinely demonstrates signs of repentance or performs meritorious service, the sentence may generally be commuted once by not more than one year and six months of fixed-term imprisonment; if the person genuinely demonstrates signs of repentance and performs meritorious service, the sentence may generally be commuted once by not more than two years and six months of fixed-term imprisonment. For those who genuinely demonstrate signs of repentance and perform significant meritorious service, the above limits on commutation periods shall not apply.  
When commuting the sentence of a minor offender, if the original judgment imposed an additional penalty of deprivation of political rights, the term of deprivation of political rights may be similarly reduced; however, the term of deprivation of political rights after reduction shall not be less than six months at minimum.  
3. For a minor offender sentenced to life imprisonment, commutation shall generally not be applied until at least one year and six months of the sentence have been served; for a minor offender sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, commutation shall generally not be applied until at least one year of the sentence have been served; the interval between two commutations shall generally be no less than six months. For those who perform significant meritorious service, the above time restrictions shall not apply. Where a minor offender, after commutation, meets the conditions for parole, parole may be granted.  
4. Where a minor offender, during the term of imprisonment, genuinely demonstrates signs of repentance and does not pose a risk of further harm to society, and shows outstanding performance in accepting education and reform, the provisions of Article 73 of the Criminal Law regarding "special circumstances" may be applied to grant parole. However, for minor offenders who are ringleaders of criminal groups, principal offenders, habitual offenders, recidivists, or whose crimes are particularly serious, parole shall be applied strictly.  
5. Where a minor offender sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier penalty has reached adulthood during the term of imprisonment, but continues to serve the sentence in a juvenile correctional facility because the remaining term is less than two years in accordance with the provisions of Article 76 of the Prison Law of the People's Republic of China, the lenient standards for minor offenders may still be applied in determining commutation and parole.

四、关于未成年人刑事案件附带民事诉讼的赔偿范围、原则  
未成年人刑事案件附带民事诉讼的赔偿范围、原则与成年人刑事案件相同。赔偿责任一般应当由未成年被告人的监护人承担。未成年被告人有个人财产的，应当由本人承担赔偿责任，不足部分由监护人予以赔偿，但单位担任监护人的除外。

**Article None** IV. Scope and Principles of Compensation in Civil Actions Collateral to Criminal Cases Involving Minors  
The scope and principles of compensation in civil actions collateral to criminal cases involving minors shall be the same as those in criminal cases involving adults. The liability for compensation shall generally be borne by the guardian of the minor defendant. Where the minor defendant has personal property, the minor defendant shall bear the liability for compensation; the guardian shall compensate for any shortfall, except where the guardian is an organization.

五、关于《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于处理逃跑或者重新犯罪的劳改犯和劳教人员的决定》（以下简称《决定》）的适用问题  
已满14岁不满18岁的未成年罪犯，逃跑后或者刑满释放后重新犯罪时已满18岁的，应当适用《决定》处罚；重新犯罪时不满18岁的，一般不适用《决定》。  
未成年的劳教人员解除劳动教养后三年内或者逃跑后五年内犯罪，如果犯罪时已满18岁的，应当适用《决定》；如果犯罪时仍不满18岁的，一般不适用《决定》。  
因不满16岁不处罚由政府收容教养的未成年人，在收容教养期间逃跑或者重新犯罪的，不适用《决定》。

**Article None** V. Application of the "Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Handling Escaped or Re-offending Criminals Undergoing Reform through Labor and Persons Undergoing Re-education through Labor" (hereinafter referred to as the "Decision")  
Where a minor offender who has reached the age of 14 but under the age of 18 has reached the age of 18 at the time of re-offending after escape or after release upon completion of the sentence, the Decision shall be applied for punishment; if the person is under the age of 18 at the time of re-offending, the Decision shall generally not be applied.  
Where a minor subjected to re-education through labor commits a crime within three years after the termination of re-education through labor or within five years after escape, if the person has reached the age of 18 at the time of the crime, the Decision shall be applied; if the person is still under the age of 18 at the time of the crime, the Decision shall generally not be applied.  
Where a minor who is not punished due to being under the age of 16 and is placed in custody for rehabilitation by the government escapes or commits a new crime during the period of custody for rehabilitation, the Decision shall not be applied.
