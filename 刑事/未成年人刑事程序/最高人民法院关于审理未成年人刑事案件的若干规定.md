# 最高人民法院关于审理未成年人刑事案件的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕9号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-15  
**生效日期**：2001-04-12

> （2000年11月15日最高人民法院审判委员会第1139次会议通过，自2001年4月12日起施行。） 法释〔2001〕9号 第一章 总则 第一条 为了维护未成年被告人的合法权益，依法惩罚和教育未成年罪犯，保障无罪的未成年人不受刑事追究，逐步建立和完善具有中国特色的未成年人刑事审判制度，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》、《中华人民共和国未成年人保护法》和《中华人民共和国预防未成年人犯罪法》的有关规定，结合我国审判未成年人刑事案件的司法实践，制定本规定。 第二条 审理未成年人刑事案件适用《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、《最高人民法院关于执行〈中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》（以下简称《解释》）。本规定有特别规定的，适用本规定。 第三条 审判未成年人刑事案件，必须以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，坚持教育为主、惩罚为辅的原则，执行教育、感化、挽救的方针，积极参与社会治安综合治理。 第四条 人民法院应当加强同公安机关、人民检察院的联系，坚持分工负责、互相配合、互相制约的原则，以保证公正、及时地审理未成年人刑事案件。 第五条 人民法院要加强同政府有关部门、共青团、妇联、工会等人民团体以及未成年人保护组织等有关社会团体的联系，共同做好未成年罪犯的教育和挽救工作。 第六条 中级人民法院和基层人民法院可以建立未成年人刑事审判庭。条件尚不具备的地方，应当在刑事审判庭内设立未成年人刑事案件合议庭或者由专人负责办理未成年人刑事案件。高级人民法院可以在刑事审判庭内设立未成年人刑事案件合议庭。 未成年人刑事审判庭和未成年人刑事案件合议庭统称少年法庭。 最高人民法院和高级人民法院设立少年法庭指导小组，指导少年法庭的工作，总结和推广未成年人刑事审判工作的经验。少年法庭指导小组应当有专人或者设立办公室负责具体指导工作。 第七条 审判第一审未成年人刑事案件的合议庭，可以由审判员或者由审判员与人民陪审员组成。依照法律规定适用简易程序的案件除外。 第八条 审判未成年人刑事案件合议庭的审判长，应当由熟悉未成年人特点、善于做未成年人思想教育工作的审判员担任，并且应当保持其工作的相对稳定性。 审判未成年人刑事案件的人民陪审员，一般由熟悉未成年人特点，热心于教育、挽救失足未成年人工作，并经过必要培训的共青团、妇联、工会、学校的干部、教师或者离退休人员、未成年人保护组织的工作人员等担任。 第九条 审判未成年人刑事案件，应当注意掌握未成年被告人的生理和心理特点，依法准确、及时地查明起诉指控的案件事实；对于构成犯罪的未成年人，应当帮助其认识犯罪原因和犯罪行为的社会危害性，做到寓教于审，惩教结合。

---

### 第一章 总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 为了维护未成年被告人的合法权益，依法惩罚和教育未成年罪犯，保障无罪的未成年人不受刑事追究，逐步建立和完善具有中国特色的未成年人刑事审判制度，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、《中华人民共和国人民法院组织法》、《中华人民共和国未成年人保护法》和《中华人民共和国预防未成年人犯罪法》的有关规定，结合我国审判未成年人刑事案件的司法实践，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Organic Law of the People's Courts of the People's Republic of China, the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Minors, and the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention of Juvenile Delinquency, and in light of the judicial practice of adjudicating criminal cases involving minors in China, for the purposes of safeguarding the lawful rights and interests of juvenile defendants, punishing and educating juvenile offenders according to law, ensuring that innocent minors shall not be subject to criminal prosecution, and gradually establishing and improving the juvenile criminal adjudication system with Chinese characteristics.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 审理未成年人刑事案件适用《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、《最高人民法院关于执行〈中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》（以下简称《解释》）。本规定有特别规定的，适用本规定。

**Article 2** The Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China and the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Implementation of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the "Interpretation") shall apply to the trial of criminal cases involving minors. Where these Provisions contain special provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 审判未成年人刑事案件，必须以事实为根据，以法律为准绳，坚持教育为主、惩罚为辅的原则，执行教育、感化、挽救的方针，积极参与社会治安综合治理。

**Article 3** In adjudicating criminal cases involving minors, the facts shall be taken as the basis and the law as the criterion, the principle of giving priority to education and supplementing with punishment shall be adhered to, the policy of education, persuasion, and redemption shall be implemented, and active participation in comprehensive management of public security shall be carried out.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 人民法院应当加强同公安机关、人民检察院的联系，坚持分工负责、互相配合、互相制约的原则，以保证公正、及时地审理未成年人刑事案件。

**Article 4** People's Courts shall strengthen contacts with public security organs and People's Procuratorates, adhere to the principle of division of responsibilities, mutual coordination, and mutual checks, so as to ensure fair and timely adjudication of criminal cases involving minors.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院要加强同政府有关部门、共青团、妇联、工会等人民团体以及未成年人保护组织等有关社会团体的联系，共同做好未成年罪犯的教育和挽救工作。

**Article 5** People's Courts shall strengthen contacts with relevant government departments, people's organizations such as the Communist Youth League, the Women's Federation, and trade unions, as well as relevant social organizations such as juvenile protection organizations, and jointly do a good job in educating and redeeming juvenile offenders.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 中级人民法院和基层人民法院可以建立未成年人刑事审判庭。条件尚不具备的地方，应当在刑事审判庭内设立未成年人刑事案件合议庭或者由专人负责办理未成年人刑事案件。高级人民法院可以在刑事审判庭内设立未成年人刑事案件合议庭。  
未成年人刑事审判庭和未成年人刑事案件合议庭统称少年法庭。  
最高人民法院和高级人民法院设立少年法庭指导小组，指导少年法庭的工作，总结和推广未成年人刑事审判工作的经验。少年法庭指导小组应当有专人或者设立办公室负责具体指导工作。

**Article 6** Intermediate People's Courts and Primary People's Courts may establish juvenile criminal adjudication tribunals. Where conditions are not yet available, a collegiate bench for juvenile criminal cases shall be established within the criminal adjudication tribunal, or a special person shall be designated to handle juvenile criminal cases. Higher People's Courts may establish a collegiate bench for juvenile criminal cases within the criminal adjudication tribunal.  
Juvenile criminal adjudication tribunals and collegiate benches for juvenile criminal cases are collectively referred to as juvenile courts.  
The Supreme People's Court and Higher People's Courts shall establish a juvenile court guidance group to guide the work of juvenile courts, and summarize and disseminate the experience of juvenile criminal adjudication. The juvenile court guidance group shall have a special person or establish an office responsible for specific guidance work.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 审判第一审未成年人刑事案件的合议庭，可以由审判员或者由审判员与人民陪审员组成。依照法律规定适用简易程序的案件除外。

**Article 7** The collegiate bench for adjudicating first-instance criminal cases involving minors may be composed of judges or judges and people's assessors, except for cases subject to summary procedure in accordance with the provisions of the law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 审判未成年人刑事案件合议庭的审判长，应当由熟悉未成年人特点、善于做未成年人思想教育工作的审判员担任，并且应当保持其工作的相对稳定性。  
审判未成年人刑事案件的人民陪审员，一般由熟悉未成年人特点，热心于教育、挽救失足未成年人工作，并经过必要培训的共青团、妇联、工会、学校的干部、教师或者离退休人员、未成年人保护组织的工作人员等担任。

**Article 8** The presiding judge of the collegiate bench for adjudicating criminal cases involving minors shall be a judge who is familiar with the characteristics of minors and skilled in ideological education work for minors, and the relative stability of his or her work shall be maintained.  
People's assessors for adjudicating criminal cases involving minors shall generally be cadres, teachers, or retired personnel from the Communist Youth League, the Women's Federation, trade unions, schools, or staff of juvenile protection organizations, who are familiar with the characteristics of minors, enthusiastic about the education and redemption of delinquent minors, and have undergone necessary training.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 审判未成年人刑事案件，应当注意掌握未成年被告人的生理和心理特点，依法准确、及时地查明起诉指控的案件事实；对于构成犯罪的未成年人，应当帮助其认识犯罪原因和犯罪行为的社会危害性，做到寓教于审，惩教结合。

**Article 9** In adjudicating criminal cases involving minors, attention shall be paid to understanding the physiological and psychological characteristics of juvenile defendants, and the facts of the case as charged in the prosecution shall be accurately and timely ascertained according to law; for minors who have committed crimes, they shall be helped to understand the causes of the crime and the social harmfulness of the criminal act, so as to combine education with adjudication and integrate punishment with education.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 少年法庭受理案件的范围：  
（一）被告人在实施被指控的犯罪时不满十八周岁的案件；  
（二）被告人在实施被指控的犯罪时不满十八周岁，并被指控为首要分子或者主犯的共同犯罪案件。  
其他共同犯罪案件有未成年被告人的，或者其他涉及未成年人的刑事案件是否由少年法庭审理，由人民法院院长根据少年法庭工作的实际情况决定。

**Article 10** The scope of cases accepted by juvenile courts:  
(1) Cases where the defendant was under the age of 18 at the time of committing the alleged crime;  
(2) Joint crime cases where the defendant was under the age of 18 at the time of committing the alleged crime and is charged as the ringleader or principal offender.  
For other joint crime cases involving juvenile defendants, or other criminal cases involving minors, whether they shall be tried by a juvenile court shall be decided by the president of the People's Court based on the actual situation of the juvenile court's work.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 对在开庭审理时不满十六周岁的未成年人刑事案件，一律不公开审理。  
对在开庭审理时不满十八周岁的未成年人刑事案件，一般也不公开审理。如果有必要公开审理的，必须经过本院院长批准，并且应适当限制旁听人数和范围。

**Article 11** Criminal cases involving minors who are under the age of 16 at the time of court hearing shall not be heard in public.  
Criminal cases involving minors who are under the age of 18 at the time of court hearing shall generally not be heard in public. If it is necessary to hear the case in public, approval must be obtained from the president of the court, and the number and scope of observers shall be appropriately restricted.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 未成年人刑事案件的证人是未成年人的，除法律规定外，经人民法院准许，可以不出庭。

**Article 12** Where a witness in a criminal case involving a minor is a minor, the witness may, with the permission of the People's Court, not appear in court, except as otherwise provided by law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 未成年人刑事案件判决前，审判人员不得向外界披露该未成年人的姓名、住所、照片及可能推断出该未成年人的资料。  
未成年人刑事案件的诉讼案卷材料，除依法查阅、摘抄、复制以外，未经本院院长批准，不得查询和摘录，并不得公开和传播。

**Article 13** Before a verdict is rendered in a criminal case involving a minor, judicial personnel shall not disclose to the outside world the name, domicile, photograph, or any information that may lead to the identification of the minor.  
The litigation file materials of criminal cases involving minors shall not be accessed or excerpted without the approval of the president of the court, and shall not be disclosed or disseminated, except for inspection, excerpting, and copying according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 未成年被告人的法定代理人在诉讼中享有申请回避、辩护、发问、提出新的证据、要求重新鉴定或者勘验、提出上诉等诉讼权利。在未成年被告人最后陈述后，经审判长许可，法定代理人可以发表意见。

**Article 14** The legal representative of a juvenile defendant shall enjoy the litigation rights such as applying for withdrawal, defense, questioning, adducing new evidence, requesting a new expert evaluation or inspection, and filing an appeal during the proceedings. After the juvenile defendant makes the final statement, with the permission of the presiding judge, the legal representative may express opinions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 人民法院应当依法保证未成年被告人获得辩护。  
开庭审理时不满十八周岁的未成年被告人没有委托辩护人的，人民法院应当指定承担法律援助义务的律师为其提供辩护。  
在审判过程中，未成年被告人及其法定代理人可以拒绝辩护人为他辩护。

**Article 15** People's Courts shall ensure according to law that juvenile defendants obtain defense.  
Where a juvenile defendant who is under the age of 18 at the time of court hearing has not entrusted a defender, the People's Court shall designate a lawyer who undertakes the obligation of legal aid to provide defense for him or her.  
During the trial, the juvenile defendant and his or her legal representative may refuse the defender to defend him or her.

### 第二章 开庭前的准备工作

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 对于人民检察院提起公诉的未成年人刑事案件，人民法院除依照《解释》的有关规定进行审查外，还应当查明是否附有被告人年龄的有效证明材料。对于没有附送被告人年龄的有效证明材料的，应当通知人民检察院在三日内补送。

**Article 16** For criminal cases involving minors that are prosecuted by the People's Procuratorate, the People's Court shall, in addition to conducting examination in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Interpretation, also ascertain whether valid evidentiary materials concerning the defendant's age are attached. Where no valid evidentiary materials concerning the defendant's age are attached, the People's Procuratorate shall be notified to supply them within three days.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 向未成年被告人送达起诉书副本时，应当向其讲明被指控的罪行和有关法律条款；并告知诉讼的程序及有关的诉讼权利、义务，消除未人民法院成年被告人的紧张情绪。

**Article 17** When serving a copy of the indictment on a juvenile defendant, the charges and relevant legal provisions shall be explained to him or her; and the procedures, relevant litigation rights, and obligations shall be informed, so as to alleviate the nervousness of the juvenile defendant.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 人民法院在向未成年被告人的法定代理人送达起诉书副本时，应当告知其诉讼权利、义务和在开庭审判中应当注意的有关事项。

**Article 18** When the People's Court serves a copy of the indictment on the legal representative of a juvenile defendant, it shall inform the legal representative of the litigation rights, obligations, and relevant matters that should be noted during the court trial.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 开庭审理前，应当通知未成年被告人的法定代理人出庭。法定代理人无法出庭或者确实不适宜出庭的，应另行通知其他监护人或者其他成年近亲属出庭。经通知，其他监护人或者成年近亲属不到庭的，人民法院应当记录在卷。

**Article 19** Before the court hearing, the legal representative of the juvenile defendant shall be notified to appear in court. Where the legal representative is unable to appear or is truly unsuitable to appear, other guardians or other adult close relatives shall be notified separately to appear in court. Where, after notification, other guardians or adult close relatives fail to appear, the People's Court shall record this in the file.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 开庭审理前，审判未成年人刑事案件的审判长认为有必要的，可以安排法定代理人或者其他成年近亲属、教师等人员与未成年被告人会见。

**Article 20** Before the court hearing, where the presiding judge adjudicating the criminal case involving a minor deems it necessary, he or she may arrange for the legal representative, other adult close relatives, teachers, or other persons to meet with the juvenile defendant.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 开庭审理前，控辩双方可以分别就未成年被告人性格特点、家庭情况、社会交往、成长经历以及实施被指控的犯罪前后的表现等情况进行调查，并制作书面材料提交合议庭。必要时，人民法院也可以委托有关社会团体组织就上述情况进行调查或者自行进行调查。

**Article 21** Before the court hearing, the prosecution and defense may respectively investigate the juvenile defendant's personality traits, family circumstances, social contacts, upbringing, and behavior before and after committing the alleged crime, and prepare written materials to submit to the collegiate bench. When necessary, the People's Court may also entrust relevant social organizations to investigate the above circumstances or conduct the investigation itself.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 人民法院应当为辩护律师查阅、摘抄、复制所指控的犯罪事实的材料，以及同在押未成年被告人会见和通信提供便利条件。经人民法院许可，其他辩护人也可以查阅、摘抄、复制上述材料，同在押的未成年被告人会见和通信。

**Article 22** People's Courts shall provide convenience for defense lawyers to inspect, excerpt, and copy the materials concerning the alleged criminal facts, as well as to meet and correspond with the juvenile defendant in custody. With the permission of the People's Court, other defenders may also inspect, excerpt, and copy the above materials, and meet and correspond with the juvenile defendant in custody.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 少年法庭应当将开庭前的准备工作和活动记录存卷。

**Article 23** The juvenile court shall record the preparatory work and activities before the court hearing and keep them in the file.

### 第三章 审判

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 人民法院应当在辩护台靠近旁听区一侧为未成年被告人的法定代理人设置席位。

**Article 24** People's Courts shall set up seats for the legal representative of the juvenile defendant on the side of the defense bench near the observer area.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 在法庭上不得对未成年被告人使用戒具。未成年被告人在法庭上可以坐着接受法庭调查、询问，在回答审判人员的提问、宣判时应当起立。

**Article 25** No restraints shall be used on a juvenile defendant in court. A juvenile defendant may sit to accept court investigation and questioning in court, and shall stand when answering questions from judicial personnel or when the judgment is pronounced.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 未成年被告人或者其法定代理人当庭拒绝委托的辩护人进行辩护，要求另行委托或者人民法院为其另行指定辩护人、辩护律师的，合议庭应当同意并宣布延期审理。  
未成年被告人或者其法定代理人当庭拒绝由人民法院指定的辩护律师进行辩护，要求另行委托辩护人的，合议庭应当同意并宣布延期审理。未成年被告人或者其法定代理人当庭拒绝人民法院指定的辩护律师为其辩护，如确有正当理由，合议庭应当同意并宣布延期审理，人民法院应当为未成年被告人另行指定辩护律师。  
重新开庭后，未成年被告人或者其法定代理人再次当庭拒绝重新委托的辩护人或者由人民法院指定的辩护律师进行辩护的，一般不予准许。如果重新开庭时被告人已满十八周岁的，应当准许，但不得再行委托或者由人民法院再行指定辩护人、辩护律师。上述情况应当记录在卷。

**Article 26** Where a juvenile defendant or his or her legal representative refuses the defense by the entrusted defender in court and requests to entrust another defender or to have the People's Court designate another defender or defense lawyer, the collegiate bench shall grant the request and announce an adjournment of the trial.  
Where a juvenile defendant or his or her legal representative refuses the defense by the defense lawyer designated by the People's Court in court and requests to entrust another defender, the collegiate bench shall grant the request and announce an adjournment of the trial. If the juvenile defendant or his or her legal representative refuses the defense by the defense lawyer designated by the People's Court in court and there are justifiable reasons, the collegiate bench shall grant the request and announce an adjournment of the trial, and the People's Court shall designate another defense lawyer for the juvenile defendant.  
After the trial is resumed, if the juvenile defendant or his or her legal representative again refuses the defense by the re-entrusted defender or the defense lawyer designated by the People's Court in court, such refusal shall generally not be permitted. If the defendant has reached the age of 18 at the time of the resumed trial, the refusal shall be permitted, but no further entrustment or designation of defenders or defense lawyers by the People's Court shall be allowed. The above circumstances shall be recorded in the file.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 法庭审理时，审判人员应当注意未成年被告人的智力发育程度和心理状态，要态度严肃、和蔼，用语准确、通俗易懂。发现有对未成年被告人诱供、训斥、讽刺或者威胁的情形时，应当及时制止。

**Article 27** During court trial, judicial personnel shall pay attention to the intellectual development level and psychological state of the juvenile defendant, maintain a serious and kind attitude, and use accurate and easy-to-understand language. Where any situation of inducing, reprimanding, ridiculing, or threatening the juvenile defendant is discovered, it shall be promptly stopped.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 法庭调查时，审判人员应当核实未成年被告人在实施被指控的行为时的年龄。同时还应当查明未成年被告人实施被指控的行为时的主观和客观原因。

**Article 28** During court investigation, judicial personnel shall verify the age of the juvenile defendant at the time of committing the alleged act. At the same time, they shall also ascertain the subjective and objective reasons for the juvenile defendant's commission of the alleged act.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条 法庭审理时，控辩双方向法庭提出从轻判处未成年被告人管制、拘役宣告缓刑或者有期徒刑宣告缓刑、免予刑事处罚等适用刑罚建议的，应当提供有关未成年被告人能够获得监护、帮教的书面材料。

**Article 29** During court trial, where the prosecution and defense propose to the court a lenient punishment such as public surveillance, criminal detention with probation, fixed-term imprisonment with probation, or exemption from criminal punishment for a juvenile defendant, they shall provide written materials concerning the juvenile defendant's ability to obtain guardianship and assistance.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条 休庭时，可以允许法定代理人或者其他成年近亲属、教师等人员会见被告人。

**Article 30** During an adjournment, the legal representative, other adult close relatives, teachers, or other persons may be allowed to meet with the defendant.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条 对未成年人刑事案件宣告判决应当公开进行，但不得采取召开大会等形式。

**Article 31** The pronouncement of judgment in a criminal case involving a minor shall be conducted in public, but forms such as holding mass meetings shall not be adopted.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条 定期宣告判决的，合议庭应当通知公诉人、未成年被告人的法定代理人及其他诉讼参与人到庭。  
法定代理人不到庭或者确实无法到庭的，也可以通知其他成年近亲属到庭，并在宣判后向其送达判决书副本。

**Article 32** Where the judgment is scheduled to be pronounced, the collegiate bench shall notify the public prosecutor, the legal representative of the juvenile defendant, and other participants in the proceedings to appear in court.  
Where the legal representative does not appear or is truly unable to appear, other adult close relatives may also be notified to appear, and a copy of the judgment shall be served on them after the pronouncement.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条 人民法院判决未成年被告人有罪的，宣判后，由合议庭组织到庭的诉讼参与人对未成年被告人进行教育。如果未成年被告人的法定代理人以外的其他成年近亲属或者教师、公诉人等参加有利于教育、感化未成年被告人的，合议庭可以邀请其参加宣判后的教育。  
对未成年被告人的教育可以围绕下列内容进行：  
（一）犯罪行为对社会的危害和应当受刑罚处罚的必要性；  
（二）导致犯罪行为发生的主观、客观原因及应当吸取的教训；  
（三）正确对待人民法院的裁判。

**Article 33** Where the People's Court finds a juvenile defendant guilty, after the judgment is pronounced, the collegiate bench shall organize the participants in the proceedings present in court to educate the juvenile defendant. If the participation of other adult close relatives other than the legal representative of the juvenile defendant, or teachers, public prosecutors, etc., is beneficial to the education and persuasion of the juvenile defendant, the collegiate bench may invite them to participate in the education after the pronouncement.  
The education of the juvenile defendant may focus on the following contents:  
(1) The harm of the criminal act to society and the necessity of being subject to criminal punishment;  
(2) The subjective and objective causes leading to the criminal act and the lessons to be learned;  
(3) Correctly accepting the judgment of the People's Court.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条 开庭审理的上诉和抗诉案件，参照上述规定进行。

**Article 34** Cases of appeal and protest tried in open court shall be handled by reference to the above provisions.

### 第四章 简易程序

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条 少年法庭应当根据[刑事诉讼法第一百七十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-174)及《解释》的有关规定，确定未成年人刑事案件是否适用简易程序。

**Article 35** A juvenile court shall determine whether a criminal case involving a minor is subject to summary procedure in accordance with Article 174 of the Criminal Procedure Law and the relevant provisions of the Interpretation.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条 适用简易程序审理的案件，应当通知未成年被告人的法定代理人、辩护人出庭。

**Article 36** In cases tried under summary procedure, the legal representative and defender of the juvenile defendant shall be notified to appear in court.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条 适用简易程序审理的案件，对未成年被告人进行法庭教育适用本《规定》第三十三条的规定。

**Article 37** In cases tried under summary procedure, the court education of the juvenile defendant shall be governed by Article 33 of these Provisions.

### 第五章 执行

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条 对于判决、裁定已经发生法律效最高人民法院力并应当收监服刑的未成年罪犯，少年法庭应当填写结案登记表并附送有关未成年罪犯的调查材料及其在案件审理中的表现材料，连同起诉书副本、判决书或者裁定书副本、执行通知书，一并送达执行机关。

**Article 38** For juvenile offenders whose judgments or rulings have taken legal effect and who shall be committed to prison to serve their sentences, the juvenile court shall fill out a case-closing registration form and attach the relevant investigation materials concerning the juvenile offender and materials on his or her behavior during the trial, together with a copy of the indictment, a copy of the judgment or ruling, and the enforcement notice, and shall serve them all on the enforcement authority.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条 少年法庭可以通过多种形式与未成年犯管教所等未成年罪犯服刑场所建立联系，了解未成年罪犯的改造情况，协助做好帮教、改造工作；并可以对正在服刑的未成年罪犯进行回访考察。

**Article 39** Juvenile courts may establish contacts with juvenile offender correctional institutions and other places where juvenile offenders serve their sentences through various forms, learn about the rehabilitation situation of juvenile offenders, assist in the work of assistance and rehabilitation; and may conduct return visits and investigations on juvenile offenders currently serving their sentences.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条 少年法庭认为有必要时，可以敦促被收监服刑的未成年罪犯的父母或者其他监护人及时探视，以使未成年罪犯获得家庭和社会的关怀，增强改造的信心。

**Article 40** Where a juvenile court deems it necessary, it may urge the parents or other guardians of a juvenile offender who is serving a sentence in prison to visit him or her promptly, so that the juvenile offender may receive care from the family and society and enhance confidence in rehabilitation.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条 对于判处管制、拘役宣告缓刑或者有期徒刑宣告缓刑、免予刑事处罚等的未成年罪犯，少年法庭可以协助公安机关同其所在学校、单位、街道、居民委员会、村民委员会、监护人等制定帮教措施。

**Article 41** For juvenile offenders sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention with probation, fixed-term imprisonment with probation, or exemption from criminal punishment, the juvenile court may assist the public security organ in formulating assistance and education measures with the offender's school, work unit, neighborhood, residents' committee, villagers' committee, guardians, etc.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条 少年法庭可以适时走访被判处管制、拘役宣告缓刑或者有期徒刑宣告缓刑、免予刑事处罚等的未成年罪犯及其家庭，了解对未成年罪犯的管理和教育情况，以引导未成年罪犯的家庭正确地承担管教责任，为未成年罪犯改过自新创造良好的环境。

**Article 42** Juvenile courts may timely visit juvenile offenders sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention with probation, fixed-term imprisonment with probation, or exemption from criminal punishment, and their families, to learn about the management and education of the juvenile offenders, so as to guide the families of juvenile offenders to correctly assume the responsibility of discipline and education and create a favorable environment for the juvenile offenders to reform themselves.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条 对于判处管制、拘役宣告缓刑或者有期徒刑宣告缓刑、免予刑事处罚等的未成年罪犯具备就学就业条件的，人民法院可以就其安置问题向有关部门提出司法建议，并且附送必要的材料。

**Article 43** Where juvenile offenders sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention with probation, fixed-term imprisonment with probation, or exemption from criminal punishment meet the conditions for school attendance or employment, the People's Court may submit judicial suggestions to the relevant departments regarding their placement, and attach necessary materials.

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条 对于执行机关依法提出给未成年罪犯减刑或者假释的书面意见，人民法院应当及时予以审核、裁定。

**Article 44** Where an enforcement authority submits a written opinion on commutation of punishment or parole for a juvenile offender according to law, the People's Court shall promptly review and render a ruling thereon.
