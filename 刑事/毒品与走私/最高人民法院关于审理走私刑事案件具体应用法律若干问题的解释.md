# 最高人民法院关于审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕30号  
**公布日期**：2000-09-20  
**生效日期**：2000-10-08

> （2000年9月20日最高人民法院审判委员会第1131次会议通过，自2000年10月8日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕30号 为严惩走私犯罪活动，根据刑法分则第三章第二节的规定，现就审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 根据刑法第一百五十一条第一款的规定，具有下列情节之一的，属于走私武器、弹药罪“情节较轻”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金： （一）走私军用子弹十发以上不满五十发的； （二）走私非军用枪支二支以上不满五支或者非军用子弹一百发以上不满五百发的； （三）走私武器、弹药虽未达到上述数量标准，但具有走私的武器、弹药被用于实施其他犯罪等恶劣情节的。 走私武器、弹药，具有下列情节之一的，处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产： （一）走私军用枪支一支或者军用子弹五十发以上不满一百发的； （二）走私非军用枪支五支以上不满十支或者非军用子弹五百发以上不满一千发的； （三）走私武器、弹药达到本条第一款规定的数量标准，并具有其他恶劣情节的。 具有下列情节之一的，属于走私武器、弹药罪“情节特别严重”，处无期徒刑或者死刑，并处没收财产： （一）走私军用枪支二支以上或者军用子弹一百发以上的； （二）走私非军用枪支十支以上或者非军用子弹一千发以上的； （三）犯罪集团的首要分子或者使用特种车，走私武器、弹药达到本条第二款规定的数量标准的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 根据[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款的规定，具有下列情节之一的，属于走私武器、弹药罪“情节较轻”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）走私军用子弹十发以上不满五十发的；  
（二）走私非军用枪支二支以上不满五支或者非军用子弹一百发以上不满五百发的；  
（三）走私武器、弹药虽未达到上述数量标准，但具有走私的武器、弹药被用于实施其他犯罪等恶劣情节的。  
走私武器、弹药，具有下列情节之一的，处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）走私军用枪支一支或者军用子弹五十发以上不满一百发的；  
（二）走私非军用枪支五支以上不满十支或者非军用子弹五百发以上不满一千发的；  
（三）走私武器、弹药达到本条第一款规定的数量标准，并具有其他恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情节之一的，属于走私武器、弹药罪“情节特别严重”，处无期徒刑或者死刑，并处没收财产：  
（一）走私军用枪支二支以上或者军用子弹一百发以上的；  
（二）走私非军用枪支十支以上或者非军用子弹一千发以上的；  
（三）犯罪集团的首要分子或者使用特种车，走私武器、弹药达到本条第二款规定的数量标准的；  
（四）走私武器、弹药达到本条第二款规定的数量标准，并具有其他恶劣情节的。  
走私其他武器、弹药的，参照本条各款规定的量刑标准处罚。  
走私成套枪支散件的，以走私相应数量的枪支计；走私非成套枪支散件的，以每三十件为一套枪支散件计。  
走私管制刀具、仿真枪支构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)的规定定罪处罚。  
[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“武器、弹药”的种类，参照《中华人民共和国海关进口税则》及《中华人民共和国禁止进出境物品表》的有关规定确定。

**Article 1** In accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law, those who fall under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to have committed the crime of smuggling weapons or ammunition under "relatively minor circumstances" and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined:  
(1) smuggling ten rounds but less than fifty rounds of military bullets;  
(2) smuggling two but less than five non-military firearms or one hundred rounds but less than five hundred rounds of non-military bullets;  
(3) although the quantity of smuggled weapons or ammunition does not reach the above quantitative standards, there are such vile circumstances as the smuggled weapons or ammunition being used to commit other crimes.  
Those who smuggle weapons or ammunition under any of the following circumstances shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years, and shall also be fined or have their property confiscated:  
(1) smuggling one military firearm or fifty rounds but less than one hundred rounds of military bullets;  
(2) smuggling five but less than ten non-military firearms or five hundred rounds but less than one thousand rounds of non-military bullets;  
(3) smuggling weapons or ammunition reaching the quantitative standards set forth in paragraph 1 of this Article and involving other vile circumstances.  
Those who fall under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to have committed the crime of smuggling weapons or ammunition under "particularly serious circumstances" and shall be sentenced to life imprisonment or death penalty, and shall also have their property confiscated:  
(1) smuggling two or more military firearms or one hundred rounds or more of military bullets;  
(2) smuggling ten or more non-military firearms or one thousand rounds or more of non-military bullets;  
(3) being the principal culprit of a criminal group or using special vehicles, smuggling weapons or ammunition reaching the quantitative standards set forth in paragraph 2 of this Article;  
(4) smuggling weapons or ammunition reaching the quantitative standards set forth in paragraph 2 of this Article and involving other vile circumstances.  
Those who smuggle other weapons or ammunition shall be punished by referring to the sentencing standards set forth in each paragraph of this Article.  
Those who smuggle complete sets of firearm parts shall be counted as smuggling the corresponding number of firearms; those who smuggle incomplete sets of firearm parts shall be counted as one complete set of firearm parts for every thirty pieces.  
Whoever smuggles controlled knives or imitation firearms which constitutes a crime shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 153 of the Criminal Law.  
The types of "weapons and ammunition" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law shall be determined by referring to the relevant provisions of the Customs Import and Export Tariff of the People's Republic of China and the List of Prohibited Articles for Entry and Exit of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 [刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“货币”，是指可在国内市场流通或者兑换的人民币、境外货币。  
走私伪造的货币，总面额二千元以上不足二万元或者币量二百张（枚）以上不足二千张（枚）的，属于走私假币罪“情节较轻”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
走私伪造的货币，具有下列情节之一的，处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）走私伪造的货币，总面额二万元以上不足二十万元或者币量二千张（枚）以上不足二万张（枚）的；  
（二）走私伪造的货币并流入市场，面额达到本条第二款规定的数量标准的。  
具有下列情节之一的，属于走私假币罪“情节特别严重”，处无期徒刑或者死刑，并处没收财产：  
（一）走私伪造的货币，总面额二十万元以上或者币量二万张（枚）以上的；  
（二）走私伪造的货币并流入市场，面额达到本条第三款第（一）项规定的数量标准的；  
（三）走私伪造的货币达到本条第三款规定的数量标准，并具有是犯罪集团的首要分子或者使用特种车进行走私等严重情节的。  
货币面额以人民币计。走私伪造的境外货币的，其面额以案发时国家外汇管理机关公布的外汇牌价折合人民币计算。

**Article 2** "Currency" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law means Renminbi or foreign currencies that may circulate or be exchanged on the domestic market.  
Where the total face value of smuggled counterfeit currency is not less than 2,000 yuan but less than 20,000 yuan, or the quantity is not less than 200 pieces (sheets) but less than 2,000 pieces (sheets), this shall be deemed as "relatively minor circumstances" of the crime of smuggling counterfeit currency, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined.  
Whoever smuggles counterfeit currency under any of the following circumstances shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated:  
(1) the total face value of the smuggled counterfeit currency is not less than 20,000 yuan but less than 200,000 yuan, or the quantity is not less than 2,000 pieces (sheets) but less than 20,000 pieces (sheets);  
(2) the smuggled counterfeit currency has been put into circulation, and the face value reaches the quantitative standard provided for in paragraph 2 of this Article.  
Whoever falls under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to have committed the crime of smuggling counterfeit currency under "particularly serious circumstances" and shall be sentenced to life imprisonment or death penalty, and shall also have his property confiscated:  
(1) the total face value of the smuggled counterfeit currency is 200,000 yuan or more, or the quantity is 20,000 pieces (sheets) or more;  
(2) the smuggled counterfeit currency has been put into circulation, and the face value reaches the quantitative standard provided for in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 3 of this Article;  
(3) the smuggled counterfeit currency reaches the quantitative standard provided for in paragraph 3 of this Article, and there are such serious circumstances as being the principal culprit of a criminal group or using special vehicles for smuggling.  
The face value of currency shall be calculated in Renminbi. Where foreign counterfeit currency is smuggled, its face value shall be converted into Renminbi according to the foreign exchange rate published by the state foreign exchange administration authority at the time the case is discovered.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 走私国家禁止出口的三级文物二件以下的，属于走私文物罪“情节较轻”，处五年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
走私文物，具有下列情节之一的，处五年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）走私国家禁止出口的二级文物二件以下或者三级文物三件以上八件以下的；  
（二）走私国家禁止出口的文物达到本条第一款规定的数量标准，并具有造成该文物严重毁损或者无法追回等恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情节之一的，属于走私文物罪“情节特别严重”，处无期徒刑或者死刑，并处没收财产：  
（一）走私国家禁止出口的一级文物一件以上或者二级文物三件以上或者三级文物九件以上的；  
（二）走私国家禁止出口的文物达到本条第二款规定的数量标准，并造成该文物严重毁损或者无法追回的；  
（三）走私国家禁止出口的文物达到本条第二款规定的数量标准，并具有是犯罪集团的首要分子或者使用特种车进行走私等严重情节的.

**Article 3** Where the quantity of smuggled Class III cultural relics whose export is prohibited by the State is less than two pieces, this shall be deemed as "relatively minor circumstances" of the crime of smuggling cultural relics, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years and shall also be fined.  
Whoever smuggles cultural relics under any of the following circumstances shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years and shall also be fined:  
(1) smuggling not more than two pieces of Class II cultural relics whose export is prohibited by the State, or not less than three but not more than eight pieces of Class III cultural relics;  
(2) smuggling cultural relics whose export is prohibited by the State reaching the quantitative standard provided for in paragraph 1 of this Article, and involving such vile circumstances as causing serious damage to the cultural relics or making them irretrievable.  
Whoever falls under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to have committed the crime of smuggling cultural relics under "particularly serious circumstances" and shall be sentenced to life imprisonment or death penalty, and shall also have his property confiscated:  
(1) smuggling one or more pieces of Class I cultural relics, or three or more pieces of Class II cultural relics, or nine or more pieces of Class III cultural relics whose export is prohibited by the State;  
(2) smuggling cultural relics whose export is prohibited by the State reaching the quantitative standard provided for in paragraph 2 of this Article and causing serious damage to the cultural relics or making them irretrievable;  
(3) smuggling cultural relics whose export is prohibited by the State reaching the quantitative standard provided for in paragraph 2 of this Article, and having such serious circumstances as being the principal culprit of a criminal group or using special vehicles for smuggling.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 [刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“珍贵动物”，是指列入《国家重点保护野生动物名录》中的国家一、二级保护野生动物和列入《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》附录一、附录二中的野生动物以及驯养繁殖的上述物种。  
走私国家二级保护动物未达到本解释附表中（一）规定的数量标准或者走私珍贵动物制品价值十万元以下的，属于走私珍贵动物、珍贵动物制品罪“情节较轻”，处五年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
走私珍贵动物及其制品，具有下列情节之一的，处五年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）走私国家一、二级保护动物达到本解释附表中（一）规定的数量标准的；  
（二）走私珍贵动物制品价值十万元以上不满二十万元的；  
（三）走私国家一、二级保护动物虽未达到本款规定的数量标准，但具有造成该珍贵动物死亡或者无法追回等恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于走私珍贵动物、珍贵动物制品罪“情节特别严重”，处无期徒刑或者死刑，并处没收财产：  
（一）走私国家一、二级保护动物达到本解释附表中（二）规定的数量标准的；  
（二）走私珍贵动物制品价值二十万元以上的；  
（三）走私国家一、二级保护动物达到本解释附表中（一）规定的数量标准，并造成该珍贵动物死亡或者无法追回的；  
（四）走私国家一、二级保护动物达到本解释附表中（一）规定的数量标准，并具有是犯罪集团的首要分子或者使用特种车进行走私等严重情节的。  
走私《濒危动植物种国际贸易公约》附录一、附录二中的动物及其制品的，参照本解释附表中规定的同属或者同科动物的定罪量刑标准执行。

**Article 4** "Precious animals" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law mean the wild animals under Grade I and Grade II State protection listed in the List of Wild Animals under Key State Protection and the wild animals listed in Appendix I and Appendix II of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora, as well as the artificially bred and propagated species of the above-mentioned animals.  
Where the smuggling of Grade II State-protected animals does not reach the quantitative standard provided for in item (1) of the appendix to this Interpretation, or the value of smuggled precious animal products is less than 100,000 yuan, this shall be deemed as "relatively minor circumstances" of the crime of smuggling precious animals or precious animal products, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years and shall also be fined.  
Whoever smuggles precious animals or their products under any of the following circumstances shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years and shall also be fined:  
(1) smuggling Grade I and Grade II State-protected animals reaching the quantitative standard provided for in item (1) of the appendix to this Interpretation;  
(2) the value of smuggled precious animal products is not less than 100,000 yuan but less than 200,000 yuan;  
(3) although the quantity of smuggled Grade I and Grade II State-protected animals does not reach the quantitative standard provided for in this paragraph, there are such vile circumstances as causing the death of the precious animals or making them irretrievable.  
Whoever falls under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to have committed the crime of smuggling precious animals or precious animal products under "particularly serious circumstances" and shall be sentenced to life imprisonment or death penalty, and shall also have his property confiscated:  
(1) smuggling Grade I and Grade II State-protected animals reaching the quantitative standard provided for in item (2) of the appendix to this Interpretation;  
(2) the value of smuggled precious animal products is 200,000 yuan or more;  
(3) smuggling Grade I and Grade II State-protected animals reaching the quantitative standard provided for in item (1) of the appendix to this Interpretation and causing the death of the precious animals or making them irretrievable;  
(4) smuggling Grade I and Grade II State-protected animals reaching the quantitative standard provided for in item (1) of the appendix to this Interpretation and having such serious circumstances as being the principal culprit of a criminal group or using special vehicles for smuggling.  
Where animals listed in Appendix I and Appendix II of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora or their products are smuggled, the conviction and sentencing standards for animals of the same genus or family provided for in the appendix to this Interpretation shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 [刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)规定的“其他淫秽物品”，是指除淫秽的影片、录像带、录音带、图片、书刊以外的，通过文字、声音、形象等形式表现淫秽内容的影碟、音碟、电子出版物等物品。  
走私淫秽物品达到下列数量之一的，属于走私淫秽物品罪“情节较轻”，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制，并处罚金：  
（一）走私淫秽录像带、影碟五十盘（张）以上至一百盘（张）的；  
（二）走私淫秽录音带、音碟一百盘（张）以上至二百盘（张）的；  
（三）走私淫秽扑克、书刊、画册一百副（册）以上至二百副（册）的；  
（四）走私淫秽照片、画片五百张以上至一千张的；  
（五）走私其他淫秽物品相当于上述数量的。  
走私淫秽物品在本条第二款规定的最高数量以上不满最高数量五倍的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
走私淫秽物品在本条第二款规定的最高数量五倍以上，或者虽不满最高数量五倍，但具有是犯罪集团的首要分子或者使用特种车进行走私等严重情节的，属于走私淫秽物品罪“情节严重”，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
走私非淫秽的影片、影碟、录像带、录音带、音碟、图片、书刊、电子出版物等物品的，依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 5** "Other pornographic articles" as provided for in Article 152 of the Criminal Law mean articles other than pornographic films, videotapes, audio tapes, pictures, and books, which express pornographic content through forms such as text, sound, or image, including video discs, audio discs, and electronic publications.  
Where the smuggling of pornographic articles reaches any of the following quantities, it shall be deemed as "relatively minor circumstances" of the crime of smuggling pornographic articles, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance, and shall also be fined:  
(1) smuggling not less than fifty but not more than one hundred tapes (discs) of pornographic videotapes or video discs;  
(2) smuggling not less than one hundred but not more than two hundred tapes (discs) of pornographic audio tapes or audio discs;  
(3) smuggling not less than one hundred but not more than two hundred decks (volumes) of pornographic playing cards, books, or picture albums;  
(4) smuggling not less than five hundred but not more than one thousand pornographic photos or pictures;  
(5) smuggling other pornographic articles equivalent to the above quantities.  
Where the smuggled pornographic articles are at or above the maximum quantity provided for in paragraph 2 of this Article but less than five times that maximum quantity, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined.  
Where the smuggled pornographic articles are five times or more the maximum quantity provided for in paragraph 2 of this Article, or although less than five times that quantity but there are such serious circumstances as being the principal culprit of a criminal group or using special vehicles for smuggling, this shall be deemed as the crime of smuggling pornographic articles under "serious circumstances", and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and shall also be fined or have his property confiscated.  
Whoever smuggles non-pornographic films, video discs, videotapes, audio tapes, audio discs, pictures, books, or electronic publications shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 153 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 [刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)规定的“应缴税额”，是指进出口货物、物品应当缴纳的进出口关税和进口环节海关代征税的税额。  
走私货物、物品所偷逃的应缴税额，应当以走私行为案发时所适用的税则、税率、汇率和海关审定的完税价格计算，并以海关出具的证明为准。  
[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第三款规定的“对多次走私未经处理的”，是指对多次走私未经行政处罚处理的。

**Article 6** "Amount of tax payable" as provided for in Article 153 of the Criminal Law means the amount of import or export duties and the taxes collected by the customs on behalf of other authorities at the time of importation that shall be paid on the imported or exported goods or articles.  
The amount of tax evaded by smuggling goods or articles shall be calculated based on the tariff schedule, tax rate, exchange rate, and the duty-paid value verified by the customs applicable at the time the smuggling act was discovered, and the certificate issued by the customs shall prevail.  
"Where multiple smuggling acts have not been dealt with" as provided for in paragraph 3 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law means that the multiple smuggling acts have not been subject to administrative penalty.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 [刑法第一百五十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-154)规定的“保税货物”，是指经海关批准，未办理纳税手续进境，在境内储存、加工、装配后应予复运出境的货物。保税货物包括通过加工贸易、补偿贸易等方式进口的货物，以及在保税仓库、保税工厂、保税区或者免税商店内等储存、加工、寄售的货物。

**Article 7** "Bonded goods" as provided for in Article 154 of the Criminal Law mean goods that have entered the country with the approval of the customs without going through the tax payment procedures and are to be re-exported after storage, processing, or assembly within the country. Bonded goods include goods imported through processing trade, compensation trade, or other means, as well as goods stored, processed, or consigned in bonded warehouses, bonded factories, bonded zones, or duty-free shops.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 [刑法第一百五十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-155)规定的“直接向走私人非法收购走私进口的其他货物、物品，数额较大的”，是指明知是走私行为人而向其非法收购走私进口的其他货物、物品，应缴税额为五万元以上的。  
直接向走私人非法收购国家禁止进口物品的，或者在内海、领海运输、收购、贩卖国家禁止进出口物品的，应当按照走私物品的种类，分别适用[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)、第一百五十二条、第三百四十七条的规定定罪处罚。  
直接向走私人非法收购走私进口的国家非禁止进口货物、物品，数额较大的，或者在内海、领海运输、收购、贩卖国家限制进出口货物、物品，数额较大，没有合法证明的，应当适用[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)的规定定罪处罚。  
[刑法第一百五十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-155)第二项规定的“内海”，包括内河的入海口水域。

**Article 8** "Directly purchasing from smugglers other smuggled imported goods or articles in a relatively large amount" as provided for in Article 155 of the Criminal Law means knowingly purchasing from a smuggler other smuggled imported goods or articles, where the amount of tax payable is 50,000 yuan or more.  
Whoever directly purchases from smugglers articles whose import is prohibited by the State or transports, purchases, or sells articles whose import or export is prohibited by the State in the inland sea or territorial waters shall be convicted and punished by applying the provisions of Articles 151, 152, and 347 of the Criminal Law respectively according to the type of the smuggled articles.  
Whoever directly purchases from smugglers goods or articles whose import is not prohibited by the State in a relatively large amount, or transports, purchases, or sells goods or articles whose import or export is restricted by the State in the inland sea or territorial waters in a relatively large amount without lawful certificates shall be convicted and punished by applying the provisions of Article 153 of the Criminal Law.  
"Inland sea" as provided for in item (2) of Article 155 of the Criminal Law includes the waters at the mouths of inland rivers.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 [刑法第一百五十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-155)第（三）项规定的“固体废物”，是指国家禁止进口的固体废物和国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物。国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物的具体种类，按照《国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物目录》执行。  
走私国家禁止进口的固体废物不满十吨，或者走私国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物偷逃应缴税额在五万元以上不满十五万元的，依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第一款第（三）项规定处罚。  
走私国家禁止进口的固体废物十吨以上不满一百吨，或者走私国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物偷逃应缴税额十五万元以上不满五十万元的，依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第一款第（二）项规定处罚。  
走私国家禁止进口的固体废物一百吨以上，或者走私国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物偷逃应缴税额五十万元以上的，依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第一款第（一）项规定处罚。

**Article 9** "Solid waste" as provided for in item (3) of Article 155 of the Criminal Law means solid waste whose import is prohibited by the State and solid waste whose import is restricted by the State and which may be used as raw materials. The specific types of solid waste whose import is restricted by the State and which may be used as raw materials shall be determined in accordance with the Catalogue of Solid Waste Whose Import Is Restricted by the State and Which May Be Used as Raw Materials.  
Whoever smuggles less than ten tons of solid waste whose import is prohibited by the State, or smuggles solid waste whose import is restricted by the State and which may be used as raw materials with evaded tax of not less than 50,000 yuan but less than 150,000 yuan, shall be punished in accordance with item (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law.  
Whoever smuggles not less than ten tons but less than one hundred tons of solid waste whose import is prohibited by the State, or smuggles solid waste whose import is restricted by the State and which may be used as raw materials with evaded tax of not less than 150,000 yuan but less than 500,000 yuan, shall be punished in accordance with item (2) of paragraph 1 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law.  
Whoever smuggles one hundred tons or more of solid waste whose import is prohibited by the State, or smuggles solid waste whose import is restricted by the State and which may be used as raw materials with evaded tax of 500,000 yuan or more, shall be punished in accordance with item (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 单位犯[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)、第一百五十二条规定的各罪以及走私国家禁止进口的固体废物的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，分别依照本解释的有关规定处罚。  
单位犯走私普通货物、物品罪以及走私国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物的，偷逃应缴税额在二十五万元以上不满七十五万元的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；偷逃应缴税额在七十五万元以上不满二百五十万元的，属于情节严重，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑；偷逃应缴税额在二百五十万元以上的，属于情节特别严重，处十年以上有期徒刑。

**Article 10** Where a unit commits any of the crimes provided for in Articles 151 and 152 of the Criminal Law or smuggles solid waste whose import is prohibited by the State, the unit shall be fined, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be punished respectively in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.  
Where a unit commits the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles or smuggles solid waste whose import is restricted by the State and which may be used as raw materials, if the evaded tax is not less than 250,000 yuan but less than 750,000 yuan, the unit shall be fined, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention; if the evaded tax is not less than 750,000 yuan but less than 2,500,000 yuan, this shall be deemed as serious circumstances, and the said persons shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years; if the evaded tax is 2,500,000 yuan or more, this shall be deemed as particularly serious circumstances, and the said persons shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years.
