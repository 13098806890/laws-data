### 最高人民法院关于已满十四岁不满十六岁的人犯走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品罪应当如何适用法律问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院关于已满十四岁不满十六岁  
  
的人犯走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品罪  
  
应当如何适用法律问题的批复  
  
法复〔1992〕3号  
  
云南省高级人民法院：  
  
你院云法研字〔1992〕第 5号《关于已满十四岁不满十六岁的人犯走私毒品罪能否适用[刑法第十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-14)第二款规定的请示报告》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
已满十四岁不满十六岁的人走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品，且具有《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于禁毒的决定》（以下简称《决定》）第二条第一款和第二款规定的情形之一的，属于[刑法第十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-14)第二款中规定的“其他严重破坏社会秩序罪”，应当负刑事责任。但是，在处理具体案件时，应当注意根据案件的不同情况区别对待，对于被利用、教唆、胁迫、诱骗参加上述毒品犯罪活动的已满十四岁不满十六岁的人，一般可以不追究其刑事责任，依照[刑法第十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-14)第四款的规定处理。  
  
1992年5月18日

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court on How the Law Shall Be Applied to Persons Who Have Reached the Age of Fourteen but Not the Age of Sixteen Who Commit the Crimes of Smuggling, Trafficking, Transporting, and Manufacturing Narcotic Drugs  
Fa Fu [1992] No. 3  
Higher People's Court of Yunnan Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions, Yun Fa Yan Zi [1992] No. 5, titled 'On Whether the Provisions of Article 14, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law May Be Applied to Persons Who Have Reached the Age of Fourteen but Not the Age of Sixteen Who Commit the Crime of Smuggling Narcotic Drugs'. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
Persons who have reached the age of fourteen but not the age of sixteen who smuggle, traffic in, transport, or manufacture narcotic drugs and who fall under any of the circumstances provided for in Article 2, Paragraph 1 and Paragraph 2 of the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Prohibition of Narcotic Drugs (hereinafter referred to as the 'Decision') shall be regarded as committing 'other crimes seriously undermining social order' as provided in Article 14, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law, and shall bear criminal liability. However, when handling specific cases, care shall be taken to treat them differently based on the different circumstances of the case. For persons who have reached the age of fourteen but not the age of sixteen who were used, instigated, coerced, or induced to participate in the aforesaid narcotic drug criminal activities, generally their criminal liability may not be investigated, and they shall be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of Article 14, Paragraph 4 of the Criminal Law.  
May 18, 1992
