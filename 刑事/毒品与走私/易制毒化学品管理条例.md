**分类**：行政法规  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2018-09-18  
**生效日期**：2005-11-01

### 第一章　总则

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了加强易制毒化学品管理，规范易制毒化学品的生产、经营、购买、运输和进口、出口行为，防止易制毒化学品被用于制造毒品，维护经济和社会秩序，制定本条例。

**Article 1** These Regulations are formulated for the purposes of strengthening the administration of precursor chemicals, regulating the production, operation, purchase, transportation, import and export of precursor chemicals, preventing precursor chemicals from being used in the manufacture of drugs, and maintaining economic and social order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　国家对易制毒化学品的生产、经营、购买、运输和进口、出口实行分类管理和许可制度。  
易制毒化学品分为三类。第一类是可以用于制毒的主要原料，第二类、第三类是可以用于制毒的化学配剂。易制毒化学品的具体分类和品种，由本条例附表列示。  
易制毒化学品的分类和品种需要调整的，由国务院公安部门会同国务院药品监督管理部门、安全生产监督管理部门、商务主管部门、卫生主管部门和海关总署提出方案，报国务院批准。  
省、自治区、直辖市人民政府认为有必要在本行政区域内调整分类或者增加本条例规定以外的品种的，应当向国务院公安部门提出，由国务院公安部门会同国务院有关行政主管部门提出方案，报国务院批准。

**Article 2** The State applies classified administration and a licensing system to the production, operation, purchase, transportation, import and export of precursor chemicals.  
Precursor chemicals are divided into three categories. The first category consists of the main materials that may be used in drug manufacture, and the second and third categories consist of the chemical agents that may be used in drug manufacture. The specific classification and varieties of precursor chemicals are set forth in the Schedule to these Regulations.  
Where adjustment to the classification or varieties of precursor chemicals is necessary, the public security department of the State Council shall, together with the drug regulatory department, the department of work safety, the competent commerce department, the competent health department of the State Council and the General Administration of Customs, develop a plan and submit it to the State Council for approval.  
Where the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government deems it necessary to adjust the classification within its administrative region or to add varieties not specified in these Regulations, it shall file a request with the public security department of the State Council, and the public security department of the State Council shall, together with the relevant administrative departments of the State Council, develop a plan and submit it to the State Council for approval.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　国务院公安部门、药品监督管理部门、安全生产监督管理部门、商务主管部门、卫生主管部门、海关总署、价格主管部门、铁路主管部门、交通主管部门、市场监督管理部门、生态环境主管部门在各自的职责范围内，负责全国的易制毒化学品有关管理工作；县级以上地方各级人民政府有关行政主管部门在各自的职责范围内，负责本行政区域内的易制毒化学品有关管理工作。  
县级以上地方各级人民政府应当加强对易制毒化学品管理工作的领导，及时协调解决易制毒化学品管理工作中的问题。

**Article 3** The public security department, the drug regulatory department, the department of work safety, the competent commerce department, the competent health department, the General Administration of Customs, the competent pricing department, the competent railway department, the competent transport department, the competent market regulation department and the competent ecology and environment department of the State Council shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the administration of precursor chemicals nationwide; the relevant administrative departments of the local people's governments at or above the county level shall, within their respective functions and duties, be responsible for the administration of precursor chemicals within their respective administrative regions.  
Local people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen their leadership over the administration of precursor chemicals and shall, in a timely manner, coordinate and resolve problems arising in the administration of precursor chemicals.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　易制毒化学品的产品包装和使用说明书，应当标明产品的名称(含学名和通用名)、化学分子式和成分。

**Article 4** The product packaging and user instructions of precursor chemicals shall indicate the name (including scientific name and common name), chemical formula and composition of the product.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　易制毒化学品的生产、经营、购买、运输和进口、出口，除应当遵守本条例的规定外，属于药品和危险化学品的，还应当遵守法律、其他行政法规对药品和危险化学品的有关规定。  
禁止走私或者非法生产、经营、购买、转让、运输易制毒化学品。  
禁止使用现金或者实物进行易制毒化学品交易。但是，个人合法购买第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品药品制剂和第三类易制毒化学品的除外。  
生产、经营、购买、运输和进口、出口易制毒化学品的单位，应当建立单位内部易制毒化学品管理制度。

**Article 5** In the production, operation, purchase, transportation, import and export of precursor chemicals, in addition to complying with the provisions of these Regulations, where such chemicals are drugs or dangerous chemicals, the relevant provisions of laws and other administrative regulations on drugs and dangerous chemicals shall also be complied with.  
Smuggling or illegal production, operation, purchase, transfer or transportation of precursor chemicals is prohibited.  
The use of cash or goods in kind for transactions of precursor chemicals is prohibited, except where an individual legally purchases pharmaceutical preparations of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals or precursor chemicals of the third category.  
Entities that produce, operate, purchase, transport, import or export precursor chemicals shall establish an internal management system for precursor chemicals.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　国家鼓励向公安机关等有关行政主管部门举报涉及易制毒化学品的违法行为。接到举报的部门应当为举报者保密。对举报属实的，县级以上人民政府及有关行政主管部门应当给予奖励。

**Article 6** The State encourages the reporting of illegal acts involving precursor chemicals to the public security organs and other relevant administrative departments. The department receiving a report shall maintain the confidentiality of the informant. Where a report is verified to be true, the people's government at or above the county level and the relevant administrative department shall give a reward.

### 第二章　生产、经营管理

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　申请生产第一类易制毒化学品，应当具备下列条件，并经[本条例第八条](#art-8)规定的行政主管部门审批，取得生产许可证后，方可进行生产：  
(一)属依法登记的化工产品生产企业或者药品生产企业；  
(二)有符合国家标准的生产设备、仓储设施和污染物处理设施；  
(三)有严格的安全生产管理制度和环境突发事件应急预案；  
(四)企业法定代表人和技术、管理人员具有安全生产和易制毒化学品的有关知识，无毒品犯罪记录；  
(五)法律、法规、规章规定的其他条件。  
申请生产第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品，还应当在仓储场所等重点区域设置电视监控设施以及与公安机关联网的报警装置。

**Article 7** To apply for the production of the first category of precursor chemicals, the following conditions shall be satisfied, and the production may only be carried out after obtaining a production license upon examination and approval by the administrative department specified in Article 8 of these Regulations:  
(1) being an enterprise lawfully registered for the production of chemical products or for the production of pharmaceuticals;  
(2) having production equipment, storage facilities and pollutant treatment facilities that meet the national standards;  
(3) having a strict work safety management system and an emergency response plan for environmental emergencies;  
(4) the legal representative and the technical and managerial personnel of the enterprise having relevant knowledge on work safety and precursor chemicals, and having no record of drug-related crimes; and  
(5) other conditions as prescribed by laws, regulations and rules.  
Where an application is made for the production of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals, television surveillance facilities and alarm devices connected with the public security organ shall also be installed in key areas such as storage premises.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　申请生产第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门审批；申请生产第一类中的非药品类易制毒化学品的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门审批。  
前款规定的行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起60日内，对申请人提交的申请材料进行审查。对符合规定的，发给生产许可证，或者在企业已经取得的有关生产许可证件上标注；不予许可的，应当书面说明理由。  
审查第一类易制毒化学品生产许可申请材料时，根据需要，可以进行实地核查和专家评审。

**Article 8** An application for the production of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals shall be examined and approved by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; an application for the production of the first category of non-drug-type precursor chemicals shall be examined and approved by the work safety department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The administrative department specified in the preceding paragraph shall, within 60 days from the date of receipt of the application, examine the application materials submitted by the applicant. If the materials conform to the provisions, a production license shall be issued, or the relevant production license already obtained by the enterprise shall be endorsed; if the license is not granted, the reasons shall be given in writing.  
When examining the application materials for a production license for the first category of precursor chemicals, on-site verification and expert evaluation may be conducted as necessary.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　申请经营第一类易制毒化学品，应当具备下列条件，并经[本条例第十条](#art-10)规定的行政主管部门审批，取得经营许可证后，方可进行经营：  
(一)属依法登记的化工产品经营企业或者药品经营企业；  
(二)有符合国家规定的经营场所，需要储存、保管易制毒化学品的，还应当有符合国家技术标准的仓储设施；  
(三)有易制毒化学品的经营管理制度和健全的销售网络；  
(四)企业法定代表人和销售、管理人员具有易制毒化学品的有关知识，无毒品犯罪记录；  
(五)法律、法规、规章规定的其他条件。

**Article 9** To apply for the operation of the first category of precursor chemicals, the following conditions shall be satisfied, and the operation may only be carried out after obtaining an operation license upon examination and approval by the administrative department specified in Article 10 of these Regulations:  
(1) being an enterprise lawfully registered for the operation of chemical products or for the operation of pharmaceuticals;  
(2) having business premises that meet the national requirements, and, where storage and safekeeping of precursor chemicals are necessary, storage facilities that meet the national technical standards;  
(3) having an operation management system for precursor chemicals and a sound sales network;  
(4) the legal representative and the sales and managerial personnel of the enterprise having relevant knowledge on precursor chemicals, and having no record of drug-related crimes; and  
(5) other conditions as prescribed by laws, regulations and rules.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　申请经营第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门审批；申请经营第一类中的非药品类易制毒化学品的，由省、自治区、直辖市人民政府安全生产监督管理部门审批。  
前款规定的行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起30日内，对申请人提交的申请材料进行审查。对符合规定的，发给经营许可证，或者在企业已经取得的有关经营许可证件上标注；不予许可的，应当书面说明理由。  
审查第一类易制毒化学品经营许可申请材料时，根据需要，可以进行实地核查。

**Article 10** An application for the operation of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals shall be examined and approved by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government; an application for the operation of the first category of non-drug-type precursor chemicals shall be examined and approved by the work safety department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government.  
The administrative department specified in the preceding paragraph shall, within 30 days from the date of receipt of the application, examine the application materials submitted by the applicant. If the materials conform to the provisions, an operation license shall be issued, or the relevant operation license already obtained by the enterprise shall be endorsed; if the license is not granted, the reasons shall be given in writing.  
When examining the application materials for an operation license for the first category of precursor chemicals, on-site verification may be conducted as necessary.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　取得第一类易制毒化学品生产许可或者依照[本条例第十三条](#art-13)第一款规定已经履行第二类、第三类易制毒化学品备案手续的生产企业，可以经销自产的易制毒化学品。但是，在厂外设立销售网点经销第一类易制毒化学品的，应当依照本条例的规定取得经营许可。  
第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品药品单方制剂，由麻醉药品定点经营企业经销，且不得零售。

**Article 11** A production enterprise that has obtained a production license for the first category of precursor chemicals or has completed the filing procedures for the second or third category of precursor chemicals in accordance with Paragraph 1 of Article 13 of these Regulations may distribute the precursor chemicals produced by itself. However, where a sales outlet is set up outside the plant to distribute the first category of precursor chemicals, an operation license shall be obtained in accordance with these Regulations.  
Single-ingredient pharmaceutical preparations of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals shall be distributed by designated enterprises for the distribution of narcotic drugs, and shall not be sold at retail.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　取得第一类易制毒化学品生产、经营许可的企业，应当凭生产、经营许可证到市场监督管理部门办理经营范围变更登记。未经变更登记，不得进行第一类易制毒化学品的生产、经营。  
第一类易制毒化学品生产、经营许可证被依法吊销的，行政主管部门应当自作出吊销决定之日起5日内通知市场监督管理部门；被吊销许可证的企业，应当及时到市场监督管理部门办理经营范围变更或者企业注销登记。

**Article 12** An enterprise that has obtained a production or operation license for the first category of precursor chemicals shall, by presenting such production or operation license, undergo modification registration of its business scope with the market regulation department. Without such modification registration, the enterprise shall not engage in the production or operation of the first category of precursor chemicals.  
Where a production or operation license for the first category of precursor chemicals is lawfully revoked, the administrative department shall notify the market regulation department within five days from the date of the decision on revocation; the enterprise whose license has been revoked shall promptly undergo modification registration of its business scope or enterprise deregistration with the market regulation department.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　生产第二类、第三类易制毒化学品的，应当自生产之日起30日内，将生产的品种、数量等情况，向所在地的设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门备案。  
经营第二类易制毒化学品的，应当自经营之日起30日内，将经营的品种、数量、主要流向等情况，向所在地的设区的市级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门备案；经营第三类易制毒化学品的，应当自经营之日起30日内，将经营的品种、数量、主要流向等情况，向所在地的县级人民政府安全生产监督管理部门备案。  
前两款规定的行政主管部门应当于收到备案材料的当日发给备案证明。

**Article 13** Any entity that produces the second or third category of precursor chemicals shall, within 30 days from the date of production, report the varieties and quantities produced to the work safety department of the people's government of the city divided into districts where it is located for filing.  
Any entity that operates the second category of precursor chemicals shall, within 30 days from the date of operation, report the varieties, quantities and main distribution flows to the work safety department of the people's government of the city divided into districts where it is located for filing; any entity that operates the third category of precursor chemicals shall, within 30 days from the date of operation, report the varieties, quantities and main distribution flows to the work safety department of the people's government of the county where it is located for filing.  
The administrative department specified in the preceding two paragraphs shall issue a filing certificate on the same day it receives the filing materials.

### 第三章　购买管理

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　申请购买第一类易制毒化学品，应当提交下列证件，经[本条例第十五条](#art-15)规定的行政主管部门审批，取得购买许可证：  
(一)经营企业提交企业营业执照和合法使用需要证明；  
(二)其他组织提交登记证书(成立批准文件)和合法使用需要证明。

**Article 14** To apply for the purchase of the first category of precursor chemicals, the following documents shall be submitted, and a purchase license shall be obtained upon examination and approval by the administrative department specified in Article 15 of these Regulations:  
(1) an operating enterprise shall submit its business license and a certificate of lawful use need; and  
(2) any other organization shall submit its registration certificate (document of approval for establishment) and a certificate of lawful use need.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　申请购买第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品的，由所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府药品监督管理部门审批；申请购买第一类中的非药品类易制毒化学品的，由所在地的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府公安机关审批。  
前款规定的行政主管部门应当自收到申请之日起10日内，对申请人提交的申请材料和证件进行审查。对符合规定的，发给购买许可证；不予许可的，应当书面说明理由。  
审查第一类易制毒化学品购买许可申请材料时，根据需要，可以进行实地核查。

**Article 15** An application for the purchase of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals shall be examined and approved by the drug regulatory department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located; an application for the purchase of the first category of non-drug-type precursor chemicals shall be examined and approved by the public security organ of the people's government of the province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government where the applicant is located.  
The administrative department specified in the preceding paragraph shall, within 10 days from the date of receipt of the application, examine the application materials and documents submitted by the applicant. If they conform to the provisions, a purchase license shall be issued; if the license is not granted, the reasons shall be given in writing.  
When examining the application materials for a purchase license for the first category of precursor chemicals, on-site verification may be conducted as necessary.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　持有麻醉药品、第一类精神药品购买印鉴卡的医疗机构购买第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品的，无须申请第一类易制毒化学品购买许可证。  
个人不得购买第一类、第二类易制毒化学品。

**Article 16** A medical institution that holds a purchase seal card for narcotic drugs and first-category psychotropic substances may purchase the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals without applying for a purchase license for the first category of precursor chemicals.  
Individuals shall not purchase the first or second category of precursor chemicals.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　购买第二类、第三类易制毒化学品的，应当在购买前将所需购买的品种、数量，向所在地的县级人民政府公安机关备案。个人自用购买少量高锰酸钾的，无须备案。

**Article 17** Any entity that purchases the second or third category of precursor chemicals shall, prior to purchase, report the varieties and quantities to be purchased to the public security organ of the people's government of the county where it is located for filing. Where an individual purchases a small quantity of potassium permanganate for personal use, no filing is required.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　经营单位销售第一类易制毒化学品时，应当查验购买许可证和经办人的身份证明。对委托代购的，还应当查验购买人持有的委托文书。  
经营单位在查验无误、留存上述证明材料的复印件后，方可出售第一类易制毒化学品；发现可疑情况的，应当立即向当地公安机关报告。

**Article 18** When selling the first category of precursor chemicals, an operating entity shall examine the purchase license and the identity certificate of the handler. Where the purchase is made by an agent, the power of attorney held by the purchaser shall also be examined.  
An operating entity may sell the first category of precursor chemicals only after verifying the above documents and retaining copies of them; if any suspicious circumstances are discovered, it shall immediately report to the local public security organ.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　经营单位应当建立易制毒化学品销售台账，如实记录销售的品种、数量、日期、购买方等情况。销售台账和证明材料复印件应当保存2年备查。  
第一类易制毒化学品的销售情况，应当自销售之日起5日内报当地公安机关备案；第一类易制毒化学品的使用单位，应当建立使用台账，并保存2年备查。  
第二类、第三类易制毒化学品的销售情况，应当自销售之日起30日内报当地公安机关备案。

**Article 19** An operating entity shall establish a sales ledger for precursor chemicals, truthfully recording the varieties, quantities, dates, purchasers and other particulars. The copies of the sales ledger and supporting documents shall be kept for two years for reference.  
The sales of the first category of precursor chemicals shall be reported to the local public security organ for filing within five days from the date of sale; the entity using the first category of precursor chemicals shall establish a usage ledger and keep it for two years for reference.  
The sales of the second and third categories of precursor chemicals shall be reported to the local public security organ for filing within 30 days from the date of sale.

### 第四章　运输管理

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　跨设区的市级行政区域(直辖市为跨市界)或者在国务院公安部门确定的禁毒形势严峻的重点地区跨县级行政区域运输第一类易制毒化学品的，由运出地的设区的市级人民政府公安机关审批；运输第二类易制毒化学品的，由运出地的县级人民政府公安机关审批。经审批取得易制毒化学品运输许可证后，方可运输。  
运输第三类易制毒化学品的，应当在运输前向运出地的县级人民政府公安机关备案。公安机关应当于收到备案材料的当日发给备案证明。

**Article 20** Where the transportation of the first category of precursor chemicals crosses the administrative region of a city divided into districts (crossing the boundary of a municipality directly under the Central Government) or crosses a county-level administrative region within a key area identified by the public security department of the State Council as having a severe drug control situation, the transportation shall be examined and approved by the public security organ of the people's government of the city divided into districts where the consignor is located; where the transportation is of the second category of precursor chemicals, it shall be examined and approved by the public security organ of the people's government of the county where the consignor is located. Such transportation may only be carried out after obtaining a transportation license for precursor chemicals upon examination and approval.  
Where the transportation is of the third category of precursor chemicals, the consignor shall report to the public security organ of the people's government of the county where the consignor is located for filing prior to transportation. The public security organ shall issue a filing certificate on the same day it receives the filing materials.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　申请易制毒化学品运输许可，应当提交易制毒化学品的购销合同，货主是企业的，应当提交营业执照；货主是其他组织的，应当提交登记证书(成立批准文件)；货主是个人的，应当提交其个人身份证明。经办人还应当提交本人的身份证明。  
公安机关应当自收到第一类易制毒化学品运输许可申请之日起10日内，收到第二类易制毒化学品运输许可申请之日起3日内，对申请人提交的申请材料进行审查。对符合规定的，发给运输许可证；不予许可的，应当书面说明理由。  
审查第一类易制毒化学品运输许可申请材料时，根据需要，可以进行实地核查。

**Article 21** To apply for a transportation license for precursor chemicals, the purchase and sale contract for precursor chemicals shall be submitted; if the consignor is an enterprise, its business license shall be submitted; if the consignor is any other organization, its registration certificate (document of approval for establishment) shall be submitted; if the consignor is an individual, his/her personal identity certificate shall be submitted. The handler shall also submit his/her personal identity certificate.  
The public security organ shall, within 10 days from the date of receipt of an application for a transportation license for the first category of precursor chemicals, and within three days from the date of receipt of an application for a transportation license for the second category of precursor chemicals, examine the application materials submitted by the applicant. If the materials conform to the provisions, a transportation license shall be issued; if the license is not granted, the reasons shall be given in writing.  
When examining the application materials for a transportation license for the first category of precursor chemicals, on-site verification may be conducted as necessary.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对许可运输第一类易制毒化学品的，发给一次有效的运输许可证。  
对许可运输第二类易制毒化学品的，发给3个月有效的运输许可证；6个月内运输安全状况良好的，发给12个月有效的运输许可证。  
易制毒化学品运输许可证应当载明拟运输的易制毒化学品的品种、数量、运入地、货主及收货人、承运人情况以及运输许可证种类。

**Article 22** For the approved transportation of the first category of precursor chemicals, a single-use transportation license shall be issued.  
For the approved transportation of the second category of precursor chemicals, a transportation license valid for three months shall be issued; if the transportation safety condition is good within six months, a transportation license valid for 12 months shall be issued.  
A transportation license for precursor chemicals shall specify the varieties and quantities of precursor chemicals to be transported, the place of destination, the consignor, the consignee, the carrier and the type of transportation license.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　运输供教学、科研使用的100克以下的麻黄素样品和供医疗机构制剂配方使用的小包装麻黄素以及医疗机构或者麻醉药品经营企业购买麻黄素片剂6万片以下、注射剂1.5万支以下，货主或者承运人持有依法取得的购买许可证明或者麻醉药品调拨单的，无须申请易制毒化学品运输许可。

**Article 23** Where the transportation involves ephedrine samples not exceeding 100 grams for teaching or scientific research purposes, or small-packaged ephedrine for the formulation of pharmaceutical preparations by medical institutions, or the purchase by a medical institution or a narcotic drug operating enterprise of ephedrine tablets not exceeding 60,000 tablets or injections not exceeding 15,000 ampoules, and the consignor or carrier holds a lawfully obtained purchase permit certificate or a narcotic drug allocation order, no application for a transportation license for precursor chemicals is required.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　接受货主委托运输的，承运人应当查验货主提供的运输许可证或者备案证明，并查验所运货物与运输许可证或者备案证明载明的易制毒化学品品种等情况是否相符；不相符的，不得承运。  
运输易制毒化学品，运输人员应当自启运起全程携带运输许可证或者备案证明。公安机关应当在易制毒化学品的运输过程中进行检查。  
运输易制毒化学品，应当遵守国家有关货物运输的规定。

**Article 24** Where a carrier accepts a transportation mandate from the consignor, the carrier shall examine the transportation license or filing certificate provided by the consignor, and verify whether the goods to be transported are consistent with the varieties of precursor chemicals specified in the transportation license or filing certificate; if they are inconsistent, the carrier shall not accept the consignment.  
When transporting precursor chemicals, the transport personnel shall carry the transportation license or filing certificate throughout the entire journey from the point of departure. The public security organ shall conduct inspections during the transportation of precursor chemicals.  
The transportation of precursor chemicals shall comply with the provisions of the State on the transportation of goods.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　因治疗疾病需要，患者、患者近亲属或者患者委托的人凭医疗机构出具的医疗诊断书和本人的身份证明，可以随身携带第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品药品制剂，但是不得超过医用单张处方的最大剂量。  
医用单张处方最大剂量，由国务院卫生主管部门规定、公布。

**Article 25** Where necessary for the treatment of a disease, a patient, a close relative of the patient or a person authorized by the patient may, by presenting a medical diagnosis issued by a medical institution and his/her personal identity certificate, carry with him/her the pharmaceutical preparations of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals, provided that the maximum dosage of a single medical prescription shall not be exceeded.  
The maximum dosage of a single medical prescription shall be prescribed and published by the competent health department of the State Council.

### 第五章　进口、出口管理

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　申请进口或者出口易制毒化学品，应当提交下列材料，经国务院商务主管部门或者其委托的省、自治区、直辖市人民政府商务主管部门审批，取得进口或者出口许可证后，方可从事进口、出口活动：  
(一)对外贸易经营者备案登记证明复印件；  
(二)营业执照副本；  
(三)易制毒化学品生产、经营、购买许可证或者备案证明；  
(四)进口或者出口合同(协议)副本；  
(五)经办人的身份证明。  
申请易制毒化学品出口许可的，还应当提交进口方政府主管部门出具的合法使用易制毒化学品的证明或者进口方合法使用的保证文件。

**Article 26** To apply for the import or export of precursor chemicals, the following materials shall be submitted, and the import or export activities may only be carried out after obtaining an import or export license upon examination and approval by the competent commerce department of the State Council or its entrusted competent commerce department of the people's government of a province, autonomous region or municipality directly under the Central Government:  
(1) a copy of the filing certificate for the registration of a foreign trade operator;  
(2) a duplicate of the business license;  
(3) a production, operation or purchase license or filing certificate for precursor chemicals;  
(4) a copy of the import or export contract (agreement); and  
(5) the identity certificate of the handler.  
Where an application is made for an export license for precursor chemicals, a certificate issued by the competent government department of the importing party certifying the lawful use of precursor chemicals or a guarantee document of lawful use by the importing party shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　受理易制毒化学品进口、出口申请的商务主管部门应当自收到申请材料之日起20日内，对申请材料进行审查，必要时可以进行实地核查。对符合规定的，发给进口或者出口许可证；不予许可的，应当书面说明理由。  
对进口第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品的，有关的商务主管部门在作出许可决定前，应当征得国务院药品监督管理部门的同意。

**Article 27** The competent commerce department that accepts an application for the import or export of precursor chemicals shall, within 20 days from the date of receipt of the application materials, examine the materials and may conduct on-site verification if necessary. If the materials conform to the provisions, an import or export license shall be issued; if the license is not granted, the reasons shall be given in writing.  
Where the import of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals is concerned, the relevant competent commerce department shall, before making a licensing decision, obtain the consent of the drug regulatory department of the State Council.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　麻黄素等属于重点监控物品范围的易制毒化学品，由国务院商务主管部门会同国务院有关部门核定的企业进口、出口。

**Article 28** Precursor chemicals that fall within the scope of key monitoring items, such as ephedrine, shall be imported and exported by enterprises designated by the competent commerce department of the State Council together with the relevant departments of the State Council.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　国家对易制毒化学品的进口、出口实行国际核查制度。易制毒化学品国际核查目录及核查的具体办法，由国务院商务主管部门会同国务院公安部门规定、公布。  
国际核查所用时间不计算在许可期限之内。  
对向毒品制造、贩运情形严重的国家或者地区出口易制毒化学品以及本条例规定品种以外的化学品的，可以在国际核查措施以外实施其他管制措施，具体办法由国务院商务主管部门会同国务院公安部门、海关总署等有关部门规定、公布。

**Article 29** The State implements an international verification system for the import and export of precursor chemicals. The catalogue of precursor chemicals subject to international verification and the specific measures for verification shall be prescribed and published by the competent commerce department of the State Council together with the public security department of the State Council.  
The time taken for international verification shall not be counted within the licensing period.  
Where precursor chemicals or chemicals other than the varieties specified in these Regulations are exported to a country or region where the manufacture or trafficking of drugs is severe, other control measures may be implemented in addition to international verification measures; the specific measures shall be prescribed and published by the competent commerce department of the State Council together with the public security department of the State Council, the General Administration of Customs and other relevant departments.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　进口、出口或者过境、转运、通运易制毒化学品的，应当如实向海关申报，并提交进口或者出口许可证。海关凭许可证办理通关手续。  
易制毒化学品在境外与保税区、出口加工区等海关特殊监管区域、保税场所之间进出的，适用前款规定。  
易制毒化学品在境内与保税区、出口加工区等海关特殊监管区域、保税场所之间进出的，或者在上述海关特殊监管区域、保税场所之间进出的，无须申请易制毒化学品进口或者出口许可证。  
进口第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品，还应当提交药品监督管理部门出具的进口药品通关单。

**Article 30** Where precursor chemicals are imported, exported, or are in transit, transshipment or through-shipment, a truthful declaration shall be made to the Customs and an import or export license shall be submitted. The Customs shall handle the clearance procedures based on the license.  
The provisions of the preceding paragraph apply to the movement of precursor chemicals between the territory outside the country and specially supervised Customs zones such as bonded areas and export processing zones, and bonded premises.  
Where precursor chemicals move between the domestic territory and specially supervised Customs zones such as bonded areas and export processing zones, or between the aforesaid specially supervised Customs zones and bonded premises, no application for an import or export license for precursor chemicals is required.  
For the import of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals, an import drug clearance notice issued by the drug regulatory department shall also be submitted.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　进出境人员随身携带第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品药品制剂和高锰酸钾，应当以自用且数量合理为限，并接受海关监管。  
进出境人员不得随身携带前款规定以外的易制毒化学品。

**Article 31** Personnel entering or leaving the country may carry with them pharmaceutical preparations of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals and potassium permanganate only to the extent of personal use and in reasonable quantities, and shall be subject to Customs supervision.  
Personnel entering or leaving the country shall not carry with them precursor chemicals other than those specified in the preceding paragraph.

### 第六章　监督检查

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条　县级以上人民政府公安机关、负责药品监督管理的部门、安全生产监督管理部门、商务主管部门、卫生主管部门、价格主管部门、铁路主管部门、交通主管部门、市场监督管理部门、生态环境主管部门和海关，应当依照本条例和有关法律、行政法规的规定，在各自的职责范围内，加强对易制毒化学品生产、经营、购买、运输、价格以及进口、出口的监督检查；对非法生产、经营、购买、运输易制毒化学品，或者走私易制毒化学品的行为，依法予以查处。  
前款规定的行政主管部门在进行易制毒化学品监督检查时，可以依法查看现场、查阅和复制有关资料、记录有关情况、扣押相关的证据材料和违法物品；必要时，可以临时查封有关场所。  
被检查的单位或者个人应当如实提供有关情况和材料、物品，不得拒绝或者隐匿。

**Article 32** The public security organs, the drug regulatory departments, the work safety departments, the competent commerce departments, the competent health departments, the competent pricing departments, the competent railway departments, the competent transport departments, the competent market regulation departments, the competent ecology and environment departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, and the Customs, shall, in accordance with these Regulations and the provisions of relevant laws and administrative regulations, strengthen supervision and inspection of the production, operation, purchase, transportation, pricing, import and export of precursor chemicals within their respective functions and duties; and shall lawfully investigate and deal with acts of illegal production, operation, purchase or transportation of precursor chemicals, or smuggling of precursor chemicals.  
The administrative departments specified in the preceding paragraph may, when conducting supervision and inspection of precursor chemicals, lawfully inspect the premises, consult and duplicate relevant materials, record relevant information, and seize relevant evidence and illegal articles; and may, when necessary, temporarily seal up relevant premises.  
The entity or individual being inspected shall truthfully provide relevant information, materials and articles, and shall not refuse or conceal them.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条　对依法收缴、查获的易制毒化学品，应当在省、自治区、直辖市或者设区的市级人民政府公安机关、海关或者生态环境主管部门的监督下，区别易制毒化学品的不同情况进行保管、回收，或者依照环境保护法律、行政法规的有关规定，由有资质的单位在生态环境主管部门的监督下销毁。其中，对收缴、查获的第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品，一律销毁。  
易制毒化学品违法单位或者个人无力提供保管、回收或者销毁费用的，保管、回收或者销毁的费用在回收所得中开支，或者在有关行政主管部门的禁毒经费中列支。

**Article 33** Precursor chemicals that are lawfully collected or seized shall, under the supervision of the public security organ, the Customs or the competent ecology and environment department of the people's government of the province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government or city divided into districts, be stored, reclaimed, or destroyed by a qualified entity under the supervision of the competent ecology and environment department in accordance with the relevant provisions of environmental protection laws and administrative regulations, depending on the circumstances of the precursor chemicals. Among them, the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals that are collected or seized shall be destroyed in all cases.  
Where the illegal entity or individual in respect of precursor chemicals is unable to bear the costs of storage, reclamation or destruction, such costs shall be defrayed from the proceeds of reclamation, or from the anti-drug funds of the relevant administrative department.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条　易制毒化学品丢失、被盗、被抢的，发案单位应当立即向当地公安机关报告，并同时报告当地的县级人民政府负责药品监督管理的部门、安全生产监督管理部门、商务主管部门或者卫生主管部门。接到报案的公安机关应当及时立案查处，并向上级公安机关报告；有关行政主管部门应当逐级上报并配合公安机关的查处。

**Article 34** Where precursor chemicals are lost, stolen or robbed, the entity where the incident occurs shall immediately report to the local public security organ, and at the same time report to the local drug regulatory department, the work safety department, the competent commerce department or the competent health department of the people's government at the county level. The public security organ that receives the report shall promptly file a case for investigation and handling, and report to the higher-level public security organ; the relevant administrative departments shall report to the higher levels level by level and cooperate with the public security organ in the investigation and handling.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条　有关行政主管部门应当将易制毒化学品许可以及依法吊销许可的情况通报有关公安机关和市场监督管理部门；市场监督管理部门应当将生产、经营易制毒化学品企业依法变更或者注销登记的情况通报有关公安机关和行政主管部门。

**Article 35** The relevant administrative departments shall notify the relevant public security organs and market regulation departments of the licensing of precursor chemicals and the lawful revocation of licenses; the market regulation departments shall notify the relevant public security organs and administrative departments of the lawful modification or deregistration of enterprises that produce or operate precursor chemicals.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　生产、经营、购买、运输或者进口、出口易制毒化学品的单位，应当于每年3月31日前向许可或者备案的行政主管部门和公安机关报告本单位上年度易制毒化学品的生产、经营、购买、运输或者进口、出口情况；有条件的生产、经营、购买、运输或者进口、出口单位，可以与有关行政主管部门建立计算机联网，及时通报有关经营情况。

**Article 36** An entity that produces, operates, purchases, transports, imports or exports precursor chemicals shall, before March 31 of each year, report to the administrative department that granted the license or filed the record and to the public security organ on its production, operation, purchase, transportation, import or export of precursor chemicals in the preceding year; entities that have the capability may establish computer networking with the relevant administrative departments to promptly report relevant operational information.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条　县级以上人民政府有关行政主管部门应当加强协调合作，建立易制毒化学品管理情况、监督检查情况以及案件处理情况的通报、交流机制。

**Article 37** The relevant administrative departments of the people's governments at or above the county level shall strengthen coordination and cooperation, and establish a mechanism for the notification and exchange of information on the administration, supervision and inspection of precursor chemicals, and on the handling of cases.

### 第七章　法律责任

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条　违反本条例规定，未经许可或者备案擅自生产、经营、购买、运输易制毒化学品，伪造申请材料骗取易制毒化学品生产、经营、购买或者运输许可证，使用他人的或者伪造、变造、失效的许可证生产、经营、购买、运输易制毒化学品的，由公安机关没收非法生产、经营、购买或者运输的易制毒化学品、用于非法生产易制毒化学品的原料以及非法生产、经营、购买或者运输易制毒化学品的设备、工具，处非法生产、经营、购买或者运输的易制毒化学品货值10倍以上20倍以下的罚款，货值的20倍不足1万元的，按1万元罚款；有违法所得的，没收违法所得；有营业执照的，由市场监督管理部门吊销营业执照；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。  
对有前款规定违法行为的单位或者个人，有关行政主管部门可以自作出行政处罚决定之日起3年内，停止受理其易制毒化学品生产、经营、购买、运输或者进口、出口许可申请。

**Article 38** Where, in violation of these Regulations, precursor chemicals are produced, operated, purchased or transported without a license or filing, or a production, operation, purchase or transportation license for precursor chemicals is obtained by fraud through forged application materials, or a license of another person, or a forged, altered or invalid license is used to produce, operate, purchase or transport precursor chemicals, the public security organ shall confiscate the precursor chemicals illegally produced, operated, purchased or transported, the raw materials used for the illegal production of precursor chemicals, and the equipment and tools used for the illegal production, operation, purchase or transportation of precursor chemicals; impose a fine of not less than 10 times but not more than 20 times the value of the precursor chemicals illegally produced, operated, purchased or transported; where 20 times the value is less than 10,000 yuan, a fine of 10,000 yuan shall be imposed; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated; if there is a business license, the market regulation department shall revoke the business license; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.  
For an entity or individual that has committed an illegal act as specified in the preceding paragraph, the relevant administrative department may, within three years from the date of the decision on administrative penalty, cease to accept its or his/her application for a production, operation, purchase, transportation, import or export license for precursor chemicals.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条　违反本条例规定，走私易制毒化学品的，由海关没收走私的易制毒化学品；有违法所得的，没收违法所得，并依照海关法律、行政法规给予行政处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 39** Where, in violation of these Regulations, precursor chemicals are smuggled, the Customs shall confiscate the smuggled precursor chemicals; if there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated, and an administrative penalty shall be imposed in accordance with the Customs laws and administrative regulations; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条　违反本条例规定，有下列行为之一的，由负有监督管理职责的行政主管部门给予警告，责令限期改正，处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款；对违反规定生产、经营、购买的易制毒化学品可以予以没收；逾期不改正的，责令限期停产停业整顿；逾期整顿不合格的，吊销相应的许可证：  
(一)易制毒化学品生产、经营、购买、运输或者进口、出口单位未按规定建立安全管理制度的；  
(二)将许可证或者备案证明转借他人使用的；  
(三)超出许可的品种、数量生产、经营、购买易制毒化学品的；  
(四)生产、经营、购买单位不记录或者不如实记录交易情况、不按规定保存交易记录或者不如实、不及时向公安机关和有关行政主管部门备案销售情况的；  
(五)易制毒化学品丢失、被盗、被抢后未及时报告，造成严重后果的；  
(六)除个人合法购买第一类中的药品类易制毒化学品药品制剂以及第三类易制毒化学品外，使用现金或者实物进行易制毒化学品交易的；  
(七)易制毒化学品的产品包装和使用说明书不符合本条例规定要求的；  
(八)生产、经营易制毒化学品的单位不如实或者不按时向有关行政主管部门和公安机关报告年度生产、经销和库存等情况的。  
企业的易制毒化学品生产经营许可被依法吊销后，未及时到市场监督管理部门办理经营范围变更或者企业注销登记的，依照前款规定，对易制毒化学品予以没收，并处罚款。

**Article 40** Where, in violation of these Regulations, any of the following acts is committed, the administrative department responsible for supervision and administration shall give a warning, order a correction within a specified time limit, and impose a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; the precursor chemicals produced, operated or purchased in violation of the provisions may be confiscated; if correction is not made within the time limit, a suspension of production or business operation for rectification within a specified time limit shall be ordered; if the rectification is not qualified after the specified time limit, the corresponding license shall be revoked:  
(1) an entity producing, operating, purchasing, transporting, importing or exporting precursor chemicals fails to establish a safety management system as required;  
(2) a license or filing certificate is transferred or lent to another person for use;  
(3) the production, operation or purchase of precursor chemicals exceeds the licensed varieties or quantities;  
(4) an entity producing, operating or purchasing fails to record or truthfully record transaction information, fails to keep transaction records as required, or fails to truthfully or promptly report the sales to the public security organ and the relevant administrative department for filing;  
(5) after precursor chemicals are lost, stolen or robbed, failure to report in a timely manner, causing serious consequences;  
(6) using cash or goods in kind to transact precursor chemicals, except where an individual legally purchases pharmaceutical preparations of the first category of drug-type precursor chemicals and precursor chemicals of the third category;  
(7) the product packaging and user instructions of precursor chemicals do not meet the requirements of these Regulations; or  
(8) an entity producing or operating precursor chemicals fails to truthfully or timely report the annual production, distribution and inventory to the relevant administrative department and the public security organ.  
Where, after the production or operation license for precursor chemicals of an enterprise is lawfully revoked, the enterprise fails to promptly undergo modification registration of its business scope or enterprise deregistration with the market regulation department, the precursor chemicals shall be confiscated and a fine shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条　运输的易制毒化学品与易制毒化学品运输许可证或者备案证明载明的品种、数量、运入地、货主及收货人、承运人等情况不符，运输许可证种类不当，或者运输人员未全程携带运输许可证或者备案证明的，由公安机关责令停运整改，处5000元以上5万元以下的罚款；有危险物品运输资质的，运输主管部门可以依法吊销其运输资质。  
个人携带易制毒化学品不符合品种、数量规定的，没收易制毒化学品，处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款。

**Article 41** Where the precursor chemicals being transported are inconsistent with the varieties, quantities, destination, consignor, consignee, carrier or other particulars specified in the transportation license or filing certificate, or the type of transportation license is inappropriate, or the transport personnel fail to carry the transportation license or filing certificate throughout the entire journey, the public security organ shall order a stop of transportation and rectification, and impose a fine of not less than 5,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan; if the carrier has a qualification for the transport of dangerous goods, the competent transport department may lawfully revoke such qualification.  
Where an individual carries precursor chemicals that do not conform to the provisions on varieties or quantities, the precursor chemicals shall be confiscated and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条　生产、经营、购买、运输或者进口、出口易制毒化学品的单位或者个人拒不接受有关行政主管部门监督检查的，由负有监督管理职责的行政主管部门责令改正，对直接负责的主管人员以及其他直接责任人员给予警告；情节严重的，对单位处1万元以上5万元以下的罚款，对直接负责的主管人员以及其他直接责任人员处1000元以上5000元以下的罚款；有违反治安管理行为的，依法给予治安管理处罚；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 42** Where an entity or individual that produces, operates, purchases, transports, imports or exports precursor chemicals refuses to accept supervision and inspection by the relevant administrative department, the administrative department responsible for supervision and administration shall order a correction, and give a warning to the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons; if the circumstances are serious, a fine of not less than 10,000 yuan but not more than 50,000 yuan shall be imposed on the entity, and a fine of not less than 1,000 yuan but not more than 5,000 yuan shall be imposed on the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons; if there is an act violating public security administration, a public security administration penalty shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-43"></a>第四十三条　易制毒化学品行政主管部门工作人员在管理工作中有应当许可而不许可、不应当许可而滥许可，不依法受理备案，以及其他滥用职权、玩忽职守、徇私舞弊行为的，依法给予行政处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 43** Where any staff member of the administrative department for precursor chemicals commits any of the following acts in the course of administration: failing to grant a license that should be granted, granting a license that should not be granted, failing to accept filings according to law, or other acts of abuse of power, dereliction of duty, or engaging in malpractices for personal gain, an administrative sanction shall be imposed according to law; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

### 第八章　附则

<a id="art-44"></a>第四十四条　易制毒化学品生产、经营、购买、运输和进口、出口许可证，由国务院有关行政主管部门根据各自的职责规定式样并监制。

**Article 44** The forms and supervision of production, operation, purchase, transportation, import and export licenses for precursor chemicals shall be prescribed and supervised by the relevant administrative departments of the State Council in accordance with their respective functions and duties.

<a id="art-45"></a>第四十五条　本条例自2005年11月1日起施行。  
本条例施行前已经从事易制毒化学品生产、经营、购买、运输或者进口、出口业务的，应当自本条例施行之日起6个月内，依照本条例的规定重新申请许可。
