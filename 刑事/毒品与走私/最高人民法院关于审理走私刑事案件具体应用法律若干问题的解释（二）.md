# 最高人民法院关于审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2006〕9号  
**公布日期**：2006-07-31  
**生效日期**：2006-11-16

> 《最高人民法院关于审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》已于2006年7月31日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1396次会议通过，现予公布，自2006年11月16日起施行。 2006年11月14日 最高人民法院 关于审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律 若干问题的解释（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  走私各种口径在六十毫米以下常规炮弹、手榴弹或者枪榴弹等分别或者合计不满五枚的，属于[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“情节较轻”，以走私弹药罪判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
走私各种口径在六十毫米以下常规炮弹、手榴弹或者枪榴弹等分别或者合计达到五枚以上不满十枚，或者走私各种口径超过六十毫米以上常规炮弹合计不满五枚的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定，以走私弹药罪判处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
走私本条第二款规定的各种弹药，数量超过该款规定的数量标准，或者走私具有巨大杀伤力的非常规炮弹一枚以上的，属于[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第四款规定的“情节特别严重”，以走私弹药罪判处无期徒刑或者死刑，并处没收财产。

**Article 1** : Whoever smuggles conventional artillery shells, hand grenades, or rifle grenades of various calibers below 60 millimeters, respectively or in total, less than five, shall fall under the "relatively minor circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years for the crime of smuggling ammunition, and shall also be fined.  
Whoever smuggles conventional artillery shells, hand grenades, or rifle grenades of various calibers below 60 millimeters, respectively or in total, reaching five but less than ten, or smuggles conventional artillery shells of various calibers exceeding 60 millimeters in total less than five, shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years for the crime of smuggling ammunition, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated.  
Whoever smuggles various types of ammunition as specified in paragraph 2 of this Article, where the quantity exceeds the quantity standard set forth in that paragraph, or smuggles one or more unconventional artillery shells with huge lethal power, shall fall under the "extremely serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 4 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to life imprisonment or death penalty for the crime of smuggling ammunition, and shall also have property confiscated.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  走私各种弹药的弹头、弹壳，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定，以走私弹药罪定罪处罚。  
走私报废或者无法组装并使用的各种弹药的弹头、弹壳，构成犯罪的，以走私普通货物、物品罪定罪处罚；经国家有关技术部门鉴定为废物的，以走私废物罪定罪处罚。  
对走私的各种弹药的弹头、弹壳是否属于“报废或者无法组装并使用”的，可由国家有关技术部门进行鉴定。

**Article 2** : Whoever smuggles warheads or cartridge cases of various types of ammunition, which constitutes a crime, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling ammunition in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law.  
Whoever smuggles warheads or cartridge cases of various types of ammunition that are obsolete or cannot be assembled and used, which constitutes a crime, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles; if identified as waste by the relevant state technical departments, such person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling waste.  
Whether the smuggled warheads or cartridge cases of various types of ammunition fall under "obsolete or cannot be assembled and used" may be subject to expert evaluation by the relevant state technical departments.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  走私各种炮弹、手榴弹、枪榴弹的弹头、弹壳的定罪量刑数量标准，按照[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的定罪量刑数量标准的五倍执行。  
走私军用子弹、非军用子弹的弹头、弹壳的定罪量刑数量标准，按照最高人民法院法释〔2000〕30号《关于审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》第一条规定的关于走私军用子弹或者非军用子弹的定罪量刑数量标准的五倍执行。

**Article 3** : The quantitative standard for conviction and sentencing of smuggling warheads or cartridge cases of various artillery shells, hand grenades, or rifle grenades shall be implemented at five times the quantitative standard for conviction and sentencing as provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation.  
The quantitative standard for conviction and sentencing of smuggling warheads or cartridge cases of military bullets or non-military bullets shall be implemented at five times the quantitative standard for conviction and sentencing of smuggling military bullets or non-military bullets as provided in Article 1 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Smuggling (Fa Shi [2000] No. 30).

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条规定的走私犯罪行为，符合最高人民法院法释〔2000〕30号《关于审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》第一条的第一款第（三）项、第二款第（三）项、第三款第（三）项和第（四）项规定的相应情形的，按照该解释有关规定的处罚原则处理。

**Article 4** : Where the smuggling criminal acts as provided in Articles 1 and 2 of this Interpretation meet the corresponding circumstances under subparagraph (3) of paragraph 1, subparagraph (3) of paragraph 2, and subparagraphs (3) and (4) of paragraph 3 of Article 1 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Smuggling (Fa Shi [2000] No. 30), the penalty principles under the relevant provisions of that Interpretation shall be applied.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  对在走私的普通货物、物品或者废物中藏匿[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)、第一百五十二条、第三百四十七条、第三百五十条规定的货物、物品，构成犯罪的，以实际走私的货物、物品定罪处罚；构成数罪的，实行数罪并罚。

**Article 5** : Where goods or articles as provided in Articles 151, 152, 347, and 350 of the Criminal Law are concealed in smuggled ordinary goods, articles, or waste, which constitutes a crime, the conviction and punishment shall be based on the goods or articles actually smuggled; where several crimes are constituted, combined punishment for multiple crimes shall be imposed.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  逃避海关监管，走私国家禁止进口的废物或者国家限制进口的可用作原料的废物，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)第二款规定的“情节严重”，以走私废物罪判处五年以下有期徒刑，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）走私国家禁止进口的危险性固体废物、液态废物分别或者合计达到一吨以上不满五吨的；  
（二）走私国家禁止进口的非危险性固体废物、液态废物分别或者合计达到五吨以上不满二十五吨的；  
（三）未经许可，走私国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物、液态废物分别或者合计达到二十吨以上不满一百吨的；  
（四）走私国家禁止进口的废物并造成重大环境污染事故。

**Article 6** : Whoever evades customs supervision and smuggles waste whose import is prohibited by the State or waste that is limited by the State for import as raw materials, under any of the following circumstances, shall fall under the "serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 152 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years for the crime of smuggling waste, and shall also or only be fined:  
(1) The dangerous solid waste or liquid waste whose import is prohibited by the State, separately or in total, reaches one ton or more but less than five tons;  
(2) The non-dangerous solid waste or liquid waste whose import is prohibited by the State, separately or in total, reaches five tons or more but less than twenty-five tons;  
(3) Without permission, the solid waste or liquid waste that is limited by the State for import as raw materials, separately or in total, reaches twenty tons or more but less than one hundred tons;  
(4) Smuggling waste whose import is prohibited by the State and causing a major environmental pollution accident.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  走私国家禁止进口的废物或者国家限制进口的可用作原料的废物的数量，超过[本解释第六条](#art-6)规定的数量标准，或者达到了规定的数量标准并造成重大环境污染事故，或者虽未达到规定的数量标准但造成重大环境污染事故且后果特别严重的，属于[刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)第二款规定的“情节特别严重”，以走私废物罪判处五年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金。

**Article 7** : Where the quantity of smuggled waste whose import is prohibited by the State or waste that is limited by the State for import as raw materials exceeds the quantity standard provided in Article 6 of this Interpretation, or reaches the prescribed quantity standard and causes a major environmental pollution accident, or although not reaching the prescribed quantity standard, causes a major environmental pollution accident with particularly serious consequences, such circumstances shall fall under the "extremely serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 152 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years for the crime of smuggling waste, and shall also be fined.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  经许可进口国家限制进口的可用作原料的废物时，偷逃应缴税额，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)规定，以走私普通货物罪定罪处罚；既未经许可，又偷逃应缴税额，同时构成走私废物罪和走私普通货物罪的，应当按照刑法处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
虽经许可，但超过许可数量进口国家限制进口的可用作原料的废物，超过部分以未经许可论。

**Article 8** : Where, when importing waste that is limited by the State for import as raw materials with permission, a person evades the payable tax, which constitutes a crime, such person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling ordinary goods in accordance with the provisions of Article 153 of the Criminal Law; where a person imports without permission and also evades the payable tax, which simultaneously constitutes the crime of smuggling waste and the crime of smuggling ordinary goods, the conviction and punishment shall be in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law that impose a heavier penalty.  
Where a person imports waste that is limited by the State for import as raw materials with permission but exceeds the permitted quantity, the excess portion shall be deemed as having been imported without permission.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  走私置于容器中的气态废物的，参照本解释规定的有关固体废物、液态废物的定罪数量标准和处罚原则处理。  
国家限制进口的可用作原料的废物的具体种类，按照国家有关部门规定执行。

**Article 9** : Where gaseous waste placed in containers is smuggled, the matter shall be handled by reference to the quantitative standards for conviction and the penalty principles concerning solid waste and liquid waste as provided in this Interpretation.  
The specific categories of waste that is limited by the State for import as raw materials shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the relevant state departments.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  本解释施行后，最高人民法院法释〔2000〕30号《关于审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》中有关走私固体废物犯罪的规定不再执行。

**Article 10** : After this Interpretation comes into force, the provisions concerning the crime of smuggling solid waste in the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Smuggling (Fa Shi [2000] No. 30) shall no longer be enforced.
