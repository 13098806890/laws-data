# 最高人民检察院关于《非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录》能否作为认定毒品依据的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2019-04-29  
**生效日期**：2019-04-30

> 《最高人民检察院关于<非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录>能否作为认定毒品依据的批复》已于2018年12月12日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第十一次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年4月30日起施行。 最 高 人 民 检 察 院 2019年4月29日 最高人民检察院关于《非药用类麻醉药品和 精神药品管制品种增补目录》能否作为认定 毒品依据的批复 （2018年12月12日  最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会 第十一次会议通过）

（2018年12月12日  最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会  
第十一次会议通过）  
河南省人民检察院：  
你院《关于<非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录>能否作为认定毒品的依据的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
根据[《中华人民共和国刑法》第三百五十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-357)和[《中华人民共和国禁毒法》第二条](../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国禁毒法.md#art-2)的规定，毒品是指鸦片、海洛因、甲基苯丙胺（冰毒）、吗啡、大麻、可卡因以及国家规定管制的其他能够使人形成瘾癖的麻醉药品和精神药品。  
2015年10月1日起施行的公安部、国家食品药品监督管理总局、国家卫生和计划生育委员会、国家禁毒委员会办公室《非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品列管办法》及其附表《非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录》，是根据国务院[《麻醉药品和精神药品管理条例》第三条](../../行政与公法/行政法规/卫生医药/麻醉药品和精神药品管理条例.md#art-3)第二款授权制定的，《非药用类麻醉药品和精神药品管制品种增补目录》可以作为认定毒品的依据。  
此复。  
最 高 人 民 检 察 院

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
April 29, 2019  
Official Reply of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Whether the Supplemental List of Controlled Varieties of Non-Pharmaceutical Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Can Serve as a Basis for Identifying Drugs  
(Adopted at the 11th Meeting of the 13th Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on December 12, 2018)  
  
People's Procuratorate of Henan Province:  
  
We have received your Request for Instructions on Whether the Supplemental List of Controlled Varieties of Non-Pharmaceutical Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Can Serve as a Basis for Identifying Drugs. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
  
Pursuant to the provisions of Article 357 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 2 of the Narcotics Control Law of the People's Republic of China, drugs refer to opium, heroin, methamphetamine (ice), morphine, cannabis, cocaine, and other narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances that are controlled by the state and can cause addiction.  
  
The Measures on the Listing and Control of Non-Pharmaceutical Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (effective as of October 1, 2015) and its attached Supplemental List of Controlled Varieties of Non-Pharmaceutical Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances, issued by the Ministry of Public Security, the former State Food and Drug Administration, the former National Health and Family Planning Commission, and the Office of the National Narcotics Control Commission, were formulated pursuant to the authorization under paragraph 2 of Article 3 of the Regulations on the Administration of Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances of the State Council. The Supplemental List of Controlled Varieties of Non-Pharmaceutical Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances may serve as a basis for identifying drugs.  
  
This is our reply.
