# 最高人民法院关于审理毒品犯罪案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕8号  
**公布日期**：2016-01-25  
**生效日期**：2016-04-11

> 最高人民法院《关于审理毒品犯罪案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2016年1月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1676次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年4月11日起施行。 2016年4月6日 最高人民法院 关于审理毒品犯罪案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2016〕8号 为依法惩治毒品犯罪，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关规定，现就审理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条    走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有下列毒品，应当认定为刑法第三百四十七条第二款第一项、第三百四十八条规定的“其他毒品数量大”： （一）  可卡因五十克以上； （二） 3，4 -亚甲二氧基甲基苯丙胺（MDMA）等苯丙胺类毒品（甲基苯丙胺除外）、吗啡一百克以上； （三）芬太尼一百二十五克以上； （四）甲卡西酮二百克以上； （五）二氢埃托啡十毫克以上； （六）哌替啶（度冷丁）二百五十克以上； （七）氯胺酮五百克以上； （八）美沙酮一千克以上；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条    走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有下列毒品，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第二款第一项、第三百四十八条规定的“其他毒品数量大”：  
（一）  可卡因五十克以上；  
（二） 3，4 -亚甲二氧基甲基苯丙胺（MDMA）等苯丙胺类毒品（甲基苯丙胺除外）、吗啡一百克以上；  
（三）芬太尼一百二十五克以上；  
（四）甲卡西酮二百克以上；  
（五）二氢埃托啡十毫克以上；  
（六）哌替啶（度冷丁）二百五十克以上；  
（七）氯胺酮五百克以上；  
（八）美沙酮一千克以上；  
（九）曲马多、γ-羟丁酸二千克以上；  
（十）大麻油五千克、大麻脂十千克、大麻叶及大麻烟一百五十千克以上；  
（十一）可待因、丁丙诺啡五千克以上；  
（十二）三唑仑、安眠酮五十千克以上；  
（十三）阿普唑仑、恰特草一百千克以上；  
（十四）咖啡因、罂粟壳二百千克以上；  
（十五）巴比妥、苯巴比妥、安钠咖、尼美西泮二百五十千克以上；  
（十六）氯氮卓、艾司唑仑、地西泮、溴西泮五百千克以上；  
（十七）上述毒品以外的其他毒品数量大的。  
国家定点生产企业按照标准规格生产的麻醉药品或者精神药品被用于毒品犯罪的，根据药品中毒品成分的含量认定涉案毒品数量。

**Article 1** . The smuggling, trafficking, transportation, manufacture, or illegal possession of the following narcotic drugs shall be deemed as "large quantities of other narcotic drugs" as provided for in Item (1) of Paragraph 2 of Article 347 and Article 348 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Fifty grams or more of cocaine;  
(2) One hundred grams or more of amphetamine-type drugs (excluding methamphetamine) such as 3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine (MDMA), or morphine;  
(3) One hundred twenty-five grams or more of fentanyl;  
(4) Two hundred grams or more of methcathinone;  
(5) Ten milligrams or more of dihydroetorphine;  
(6) Two hundred fifty grams or more of pethidine (meperidine);  
(7) Five hundred grams or more of ketamine;  
(8) One kilogram or more of methadone;  
(9) Two kilograms or more of tramadol or gamma-hydroxybutyric acid;  
(10) Five kilograms or more of cannabis oil, ten kilograms or more of cannabis resin, or one hundred fifty kilograms or more of cannabis leaves and cannabis cigarettes;  
(11) Five kilograms or more of codeine or buprenorphine;  
(12) Fifty kilograms or more of triazolam or methaqualone;  
(13) One hundred kilograms or more of alprazolam or khat;  
(14) Two hundred kilograms or more of caffeine or poppy shells;  
(15) Two hundred fifty kilograms or more of barbital, phenobarbital, caffeine sodium benzoate, or nimetazepam;  
(16) Five hundred kilograms or more of chlordiazepoxide, estazolam, diazepam, or bromazepam;  
(17) Large quantities of other narcotic drugs not listed above.  
Where narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances produced by state-designated manufacturers in accordance with standard specifications are used in drug crimes, the quantity of drugs involved in the case shall be determined based on the content of the drug components in the substances.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有下列毒品，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第三款、第三百四十八条规定的“其他毒品数量较大”：  
（一）  可卡因十克以上不满五十克；  
（二） 3，4- 亚甲二氧基甲基苯丙胺（MDMA）等苯丙胺类毒品（甲基苯丙胺除外）、吗啡二十克以上不满一百克；  
（三）芬太尼二十五克以上不满一百二十五克；  
（四）甲卡西酮四十克以上不满二百克；  
（五）二氢埃托啡二毫克以上不满十毫克；  
（六）哌替啶（度冷丁）五十克以上不满二百五十克；  
（七）氯胺酮一百克以上不满五百克；  
（八）美沙酮二百克以上不满一千克；  
（九）曲马多、γ-羟丁酸四百克以上不满二千克；  
（十）大麻油一千克以上不满五千克、大麻脂二千克以上不满十千克、大麻叶及大麻烟三十千克以上不满一百五十千克；  
（十一）可待因、丁丙诺啡一千克以上不满五千克；  
（十二）三唑仑、安眠酮十千克以上不满五十千克；  
（十三）阿普唑仑、恰特草二十千克以上不满一百千克；  
（十四）咖啡因、罂粟壳四十千克以上不满二百千克；  
（十五）巴比妥、苯巴比妥、安钠咖、尼美西泮五十千克以上不满二百五十千克；  
（十六）氯氮卓、艾司唑仑、地西泮、溴西泮一百千克以上不满五百千克；  
（十七）上述毒品以外的其他毒品数量较大的。

**Article 2** . The smuggling, trafficking, transportation, manufacture, or illegal possession of the following narcotic drugs shall be deemed as "relatively large quantities of other narcotic drugs" as provided for in Paragraph 3 of Article 347 and Article 348 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Ten grams or more but less than fifty grams of cocaine;  
(2) Twenty grams or more but less than one hundred grams of amphetamine-type drugs (excluding methamphetamine) such as 3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine (MDMA), or morphine;  
(3) Twenty-five grams or more but less than one hundred twenty-five grams of fentanyl;  
(4) Forty grams or more but less than two hundred grams of methcathinone;  
(5) Two milligrams or more but less than ten milligrams of dihydroetorphine;  
(6) Fifty grams or more but less than two hundred fifty grams of pethidine (meperidine);  
(7) One hundred grams or more but less than five hundred grams of ketamine;  
(8) Two hundred grams or more but less than one kilogram of methadone;  
(9) Four hundred grams or more but less than two kilograms of tramadol or gamma-hydroxybutyric acid;  
(10) One kilogram or more but less than five kilograms of cannabis oil, two kilograms or more but less than ten kilograms of cannabis resin, or thirty kilograms or more but less than one hundred fifty kilograms of cannabis leaves and cannabis cigarettes;  
(11) One kilogram or more but less than five kilograms of codeine or buprenorphine;  
(12) Ten kilograms or more but less than fifty kilograms of triazolam or methaqualone;  
(13) Twenty kilograms or more but less than one hundred kilograms of alprazolam or khat;  
(14) Forty kilograms or more but less than two hundred kilograms of caffeine or poppy shells;  
(15) Fifty kilograms or more but less than two hundred fifty kilograms of barbital, phenobarbital, caffeine sodium benzoate, or nimetazepam;  
(16) One hundred kilograms or more but less than five hundred kilograms of chlordiazepoxide, estazolam, diazepam, or bromazepam;  
(17) Relatively large quantities of other narcotic drugs not listed above.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条    在实施走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品犯罪的过程中，携带枪支、弹药或者爆炸物用于掩护的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第二款第三项规定的“武装掩护走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品”。  
枪支、弹药、爆炸物种类的认定，依照相关司法解释的规定执行。  
在实施走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品犯罪的过程中，以暴力抗拒检查、拘留、逮捕，造成执法人员死亡、重伤、多人轻伤或者具有其他严重情节的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第二款第四项规定的“以暴力抗拒检查、拘留、逮捕，情节严重”。

**Article 3** . Carrying firearms, ammunition, or explosives for the purpose of cover during the commission of crimes of smuggling, trafficking, transportation, or manufacture of narcotic drugs shall be deemed as "armed cover for smuggling, trafficking, transportation, or manufacture of narcotic drugs" as provided for in Item (3) of Paragraph 2 of Article 347 of the Criminal Law.  
The determination of the types of firearms, ammunition, and explosives shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of relevant judicial interpretations.  
During the commission of crimes of smuggling, trafficking, transportation, or manufacture of narcotic drugs, violently resisting inspection, detention, or arrest, causing the death, serious injury, or multiple minor injuries of law enforcement personnel, or having other serious circumstances, shall be deemed as "resisting inspection, detention, or arrest by violence with serious circumstances" as provided for in Item (4) of Paragraph 2 of Article 347 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条    走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第四款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）向多人贩卖毒品或者多次走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的；  
（二）在戒毒场所、监管场所贩卖毒品的；  
（三）向在校学生贩卖毒品的；  
（四）组织、利用残疾人、严重疾病患者、怀孕或者正在哺乳自己婴儿的妇女走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的；  
（五）国家工作人员走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 4** . Smuggling, trafficking, transportation, or manufacture of narcotic drugs under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 4 of Article 347 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Trafficking drugs to multiple persons or committing smuggling, trafficking, transportation, or manufacture of drugs on multiple occasions;  
(2) Trafficking drugs in drug rehabilitation facilities or correctional facilities;  
(3) Trafficking drugs to enrolled students;  
(4) Organizing or using disabled persons, persons suffering from serious diseases, or pregnant women or women nursing their own infants to smuggle, traffic, transport, or manufacture drugs;  
(5) State functionaries smuggling, trafficking, transporting, or manufacturing drugs;  
(6) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条    非法持有毒品达到[刑法第三百四十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-348)或者[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定的“数量较大”标准，且具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-348)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）在戒毒场所、监管场所非法持有毒品的；  
（二）利用、教唆未成年人非法持有毒品的；  
（三）国家工作人员非法持有毒品的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 5** . Illegal possession of narcotic drugs reaching the standard of "relatively large quantities" as provided for in Article 348 of the Criminal Law or Article 2 of this Interpretation, and under any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 348 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Illegally possessing drugs in drug rehabilitation facilities or correctional facilities;  
(2) Using or instigating minors to illegally possess drugs;  
(3) State functionaries illegally possessing drugs;  
(4) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条    包庇走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的犯罪分子，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-349)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）被包庇的犯罪分子依法应当判处十五年有期徒刑以上刑罚的；  
（二）包庇多名或者多次包庇走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的犯罪分子的；  
（三）严重妨害司法机关对被包庇的犯罪分子实施的毒品犯罪进行追究的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
为走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的犯罪分子窝藏、转移、隐瞒毒品或者毒品犯罪所得的财物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-349)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）为犯罪分子窝藏、转移、隐瞒毒品达到[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第二款第一项或者[本解释第一条](#art-1)第一款规定的“数量大”标准的；  
（二）为犯罪分子窝藏、转移、隐瞒毒品犯罪所得的财物价值达到五万元以上的；  
（三）为多人或者多次为他人窝藏、转移、隐瞒毒品或者毒品犯罪所得的财物的；  
（四）严重妨害司法机关对该犯罪分子实施的毒品犯罪进行追究的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
包庇走私、贩卖、运输、制造毒品的近亲属，或者为其窝藏、转移、隐瞒毒品或者毒品犯罪所得的财物，不具有本条前两款规定的“情节严重”情形，归案后认罪、悔罪、积极退赃，且系初犯、偶犯，犯罪情节轻微不需要判处刑罚的，可以免予刑事处罚。

**Article 6** . Harboring criminals who smuggle, traffic, transport, or manufacture narcotic drugs under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 1 of Article 349 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The harbored criminal shall, according to law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of fifteen years or more;  
(2) Harboring multiple persons or repeatedly harboring criminals who smuggle, traffic, transport, or manufacture drugs;  
(3) Seriously obstructing judicial authorities from investigating the drug crimes committed by the harbored criminal;  
(4) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.  
Concealing, transferring, or hiding drugs or the proceeds of drug crimes for criminals who smuggle, traffic, transport, or manufacture drugs under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 1 of Article 349 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Concealing, transferring, or hiding drugs for the criminal reaching the standard of "large quantities" as provided for in Item (1) of Paragraph 2 of Article 347 of the Criminal Law or Paragraph 1 of Article 1 of this Interpretation;  
(2) The value of the drugs or proceeds of drug crimes concealed, transferred, or hidden for the criminal reaches 50,000 yuan or more;  
(3) Concealing, transferring, or hiding drugs or the proceeds of drug crimes for multiple persons or on multiple occasions;  
(4) Seriously obstructing judicial authorities from investigating the drug crimes committed by that criminal;  
(5) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.  
Where a person harbors a close relative who smuggles, traffics, transports, or manufactures drugs, or conceals, transfers, or hides drugs or the proceeds of drug crimes for such a close relative, and the circumstances do not constitute "serious circumstances" as provided for in the preceding two paragraphs of this Article, and after being brought to justice, the person confesses, shows repentance, actively returns the illicit gains, and is a first-time offender or occasional offender, and the circumstances of the crime are minor so that no criminal punishment is necessary, the person may be granted exemption from criminal punishment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条    违反国家规定，非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品，达到下列数量标准的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-350)第一款规定的“情节较重”：  
（一）麻黄碱（麻黄素）、伪麻黄碱（伪麻黄素）、消旋麻黄碱（消旋麻黄素）一千克以上不满五千克；  
（二）1-苯基-2-丙酮、1-苯基-2-溴-1-丙酮、3，4-亚甲基二氧苯基-2-丙酮、羟亚胺二千克以上不满十千克；  
（三）3-氧-2-苯基丁腈、邻氯苯基环戊酮、去甲麻黄碱（去甲麻黄素）、甲基麻黄碱（甲基麻黄素）四千克以上不满二十千克；  
（四）醋酸酐十千克以上不满五十千克；  
（五）麻黄浸膏、麻黄浸膏粉、胡椒醛、黄樟素、黄樟油、异黄樟素、麦角酸、麦角胺、麦角新碱、苯乙酸二十千克以上不满一百千克；  
（六）N-乙酰邻氨基苯酸、邻氨基苯甲酸、三氯甲烷、乙醚、哌啶五十千克以上不满二百五十千克；  
（七）甲苯、丙酮、甲基乙基酮、高锰酸钾、硫酸、盐酸一百千克以上不满五百千克；  
（八）其他制毒物品数量相当的。  
违反国家规定，非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品，达到前款规定的数量标准最低值的百分之五十，且具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-350)第一款规定的“情节较重”：  
（一）曾因非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）二年内曾因非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品受过行政处罚的；  
（三）一次组织五人以上或者多次非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品，或者在多个地点非法生产制毒物品的；  
（四）利用、教唆未成年人非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品的；  
（五）国家工作人员非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品的；  
（六）严重影响群众正常生产、生活秩序的；  
（七）其他情节较重的情形。  
易制毒化学品生产、经营、购买、运输单位或者个人未办理许可证明或者备案证明，生产、销售、购买、运输易制毒化学品，确实用于合法生产、生活需要的，不以制毒物品犯罪论处。

**Article 7** . Violating state regulations by illegally producing, trading, transporting, or smuggling precursor chemicals, reaching the following quantitative standards, shall be deemed as "relatively serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 1 of Article 350 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) One kilogram or more but less than five kilograms of ephedrine (ephedra alkaloid), pseudoephedrine (pseudoephedrine alkaloid), or racemic ephedrine (racemic ephedra alkaloid);  
(2) Two kilograms or more but less than ten kilograms of 1-phenyl-2-propanone, 1-phenyl-2-bromo-1-propanone, 3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl-2-propanone, or hydroxyimine;  
(3) Four kilograms or more but less than twenty kilograms of 3-oxo-2-phenylbutanenitrile, o-chlorophenylcyclopentanone, norephedrine (norephedrine alkaloid), or methylephedrine (methylephedrine alkaloid);  
(4) Ten kilograms or more but less than fifty kilograms of acetic anhydride;  
(5) Twenty kilograms or more but less than one hundred kilograms of ephedra extract, ephedra extract powder, piperonal, safrole, sassafras oil, isosafrole, lysergic acid, ergotamine, ergonovine, or phenylacetic acid;  
(6) Fifty kilograms or more but less than two hundred fifty kilograms of N-acetylanthranilic acid, anthranilic acid, chloroform, diethyl ether, or piperidine;  
(7) One hundred kilograms or more but less than five hundred kilograms of toluene, acetone, methyl ethyl ketone, potassium permanganate, sulfuric acid, or hydrochloric acid;  
(8) Other precursor chemicals in equivalent quantities.  
Violating state regulations by illegally producing, trading, transporting, or smuggling precursor chemicals, reaching 50% of the minimum quantitative standard specified in the preceding paragraph, and having any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "relatively serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 1 of Article 350 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Having previously received criminal punishment for illegally producing, trading, transporting, or smuggling precursor chemicals;  
(2) Having received administrative penalty for illegally producing, trading, transporting, or smuggling precursor chemicals within the previous two years;  
(3) Organizing five or more persons at one time or repeatedly illegally producing, trading, transporting, or smuggling precursor chemicals, or illegally producing precursor chemicals at multiple locations;  
(4) Using or instigating minors to illegally produce, trade, transport, or smuggle precursor chemicals;  
(5) State functionaries illegally producing, trading, transporting, or smuggling precursor chemicals;  
(6) Seriously affecting the normal production and living order of the public;  
(7) Other circumstances where the circumstances are relatively serious.  
Where a unit or individual engaged in the production, operation, purchase, or transportation of precursor chemicals, without obtaining a permit or filing a record, produces, sells, purchases, or transports precursor chemicals that are indeed used for legitimate production or living needs, the act shall not be treated as a precursor chemical crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条    违反国家规定，非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-350)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）制毒物品数量在[本解释第七条](#art-7)第一款规定的最高数量标准以上，不满最高数量标准五倍的；  
（二）达到[本解释第七条](#art-7)第一款规定的数量标准，且具有[本解释第七条](#art-7)第二款第三项至第六项规定的情形之一的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
违反国家规定，非法生产、买卖、运输制毒物品、走私制毒物品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-350)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）制毒物品数量在[本解释第七条](#art-7)第一款规定的最高数量标准五倍以上的；  
（二）达到前款第一项规定的数量标准，且具有[本解释第七条](#art-7)第二款第三项至第六项规定的情形之一的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 8** . Violating state regulations by illegally producing, trading, transporting, or smuggling precursor chemicals under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 1 of Article 350 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The quantity of precursor chemicals is at or above the maximum quantitative standard specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 7 of this Interpretation, but less than five times that maximum standard;  
(2) Reaching the quantitative standard specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 7 of this Interpretation, and meeting any of the circumstances specified in Items (3) through (6) of Paragraph 2 of Article 7 of this Interpretation;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.  
Violating state regulations by illegally producing, trading, transporting, or smuggling precursor chemicals under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 1 of Article 350 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The quantity of precursor chemicals is five times or more the maximum quantitative standard specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 7 of this Interpretation;  
(2) Reaching the quantitative standard specified in Item (1) of the preceding paragraph, and meeting any of the circumstances specified in Items (3) through (6) of Paragraph 2 of Article 7 of this Interpretation;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are especially serious.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条    非法种植毒品原植物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-351)第一款第一项规定的“数量较大”：  
（一）非法种植大麻五千株以上不满三万株的；  
（二）非法种植罂粟二百平方米以上不满一千二百平方米、大麻二千平方米以上不满一万二千平方米，尚未出苗的；  
（三）非法种植其他毒品原植物数量较大的。  
非法种植毒品原植物，达到前款规定的最高数量标准的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-351)第二款规定的“数量大”。

**Article 9** . Illegally cultivating mother plants of narcotic drugs under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "relatively large quantities" as provided for in Item (1) of Paragraph 1 of Article 351 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Illegally cultivating five thousand or more but less than thirty thousand cannabis plants;  
(2) Illegally cultivating poppy in an area of two hundred square meters or more but less than one thousand two hundred square meters, or cannabis in an area of two thousand square meters or more but less than twelve thousand square meters, where the seedlings have not yet emerged;  
(3) Illegally cultivating other mother plants of narcotic drugs in relatively large quantities.  
Illegally cultivating mother plants of narcotic drugs reaching the maximum quantitative standard specified in the preceding paragraph shall be deemed as "large quantities" as provided for in Paragraph 2 of Article 351 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条    非法买卖、运输、携带、持有未经灭活的毒品原植物种子或者幼苗，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-352)规定的“数量较大”：  
（一）罂粟种子五十克以上、罂粟幼苗五千株以上的；  
（二）大麻种子五十千克以上、大麻幼苗五万株以上的；  
（三）其他毒品原植物种子或者幼苗数量较大的。

**Article 10** . Illegally trading, transporting, carrying, or possessing non-viable seeds or seedlings of mother plants of narcotic drugs under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "relatively large quantities" as provided for in Article 352 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Poppy seeds of fifty grams or more, or poppy seedlings of five thousand or more;  
(2) Cannabis seeds of fifty kilograms or more, or cannabis seedlings of fifty thousand or more;  
(3) Other seeds or seedlings of mother plants of narcotic drugs in relatively large quantities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条    引诱、教唆、欺骗他人吸食、注射毒品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-353)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）引诱、教唆、欺骗多人或者多次引诱、教唆、欺骗他人吸食、注射毒品的；  
（二）对他人身体健康造成严重危害的；  
（三）导致他人实施故意杀人、故意伤害、交通肇事等犯罪行为的；  
（四）国家工作人员引诱、教唆、欺骗他人吸食、注射毒品的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 11** . Inducing, instigating, or deceiving others into ingesting or injecting narcotic drugs under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 1 of Article 353 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Inducing, instigating, or deceiving multiple persons or repeatedly inducing, instigating, or deceiving others into ingesting or injecting drugs;  
(2) Causing serious harm to the physical health of others;  
(3) Causing others to commit crimes such as intentional homicide, intentional injury, or causing traffic accidents;  
(4) State functionaries inducing, instigating, or deceiving others into ingesting or injecting drugs;  
(5) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条    容留他人吸食、注射毒品，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百五十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-354)的规定，以容留他人吸毒罪定罪处罚：  
（一）一次容留多人吸食、注射毒品的；  
（二）二年内多次容留他人吸食、注射毒品的；  
（三）二年内曾因容留他人吸食、注射毒品受过行政处罚的；  
（四）容留未成年人吸食、注射毒品的；  
（五）以牟利为目的容留他人吸食、注射毒品的；  
（六）容留他人吸食、注射毒品造成严重后果的；  
（七）其他应当追究刑事责任的情形。  
向他人贩卖毒品后又容留其吸食、注射毒品，或者容留他人吸食、注射毒品并向其贩卖毒品，符合前款规定的容留他人吸毒罪的定罪条件的，以贩卖毒品罪和容留他人吸毒罪数罪并罚。  
容留近亲属吸食、注射毒品，情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理；需要追究刑事责任的，可以酌情从宽处罚。

**Article 12** . Providing premises for others to ingest or inject narcotic drugs under any of the following circumstances shall be convicted and punished as the crime of providing premises for others to ingest drugs in accordance with Article 354 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Providing premises for multiple persons to ingest or inject drugs at one time;  
(2) Repeatedly providing premises for others to ingest or inject drugs within two years;  
(3) Having received administrative penalty for providing premises for others to ingest or inject drugs within the previous two years;  
(4) Providing premises for minors to ingest or inject drugs;  
(5) Providing premises for others to ingest or inject drugs for the purpose of seeking profits;  
(6) Causing serious consequences by providing premises for others to ingest or inject drugs;  
(7) Other circumstances where criminal liability shall be investigated.  
Where a person traffics drugs to another person and subsequently provides premises for that person to ingest or inject the drugs, or provides premises for another person to ingest or inject drugs and traffics drugs to that person, and the conditions for conviction of the crime of providing premises for others to ingest drugs as specified in the preceding paragraph are met, the person shall be punished for both the crime of trafficking drugs and the crime of providing premises for others to ingest drugs, and the punishments shall be combined.  
Where a person provides premises for a close relative to ingest or inject drugs, and the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not serious, the act shall not be treated as a crime; if criminal liability is required, the punishment may be lenient as appropriate.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条    依法从事生产、运输、管理、使用国家管制的麻醉药品、精神药品的人员，违反国家规定，向吸食、注射毒品的人提供国家规定管制的能够使人形成瘾癖的麻醉药品、精神药品，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百五十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-355)第一款的规定，以非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品达到[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第三款或者[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定的“数量较大”标准最低值的百分之五十，不满“数量较大”标准的；  
（二）二年内曾因非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品受过行政处罚的；  
（三）向多人或者多次非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品的；  
（四）向吸食、注射毒品的未成年人非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品的；  
（五）非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品造成严重后果的；  
（六）其他应当追究刑事责任的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-355)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品达到[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第三款或者[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定的“数量较大”标准的；  
（二）非法提供麻醉药品、精神药品达到前款第一项规定的数量标准，且具有前款第三项至第五项规定的情形之一的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 13** . Persons who, according to law, are engaged in the production, transportation, management, or use of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances under state control, violating state regulations by providing to persons who ingest or inject drugs narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances that are under state control and capable of causing addiction, under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of illegally providing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances in accordance with Paragraph 1 of Article 355 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The quantity of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances illegally provided reaches 50% of the minimum standard for "relatively large quantities" as provided for in Paragraph 3 of Article 347 of the Criminal Law or Article 2 of this Interpretation, but is less than the "relatively large quantities" standard;  
(2) Having received administrative penalty for illegally providing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances within the previous two years;  
(3) Illegally providing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances to multiple persons or on multiple occasions;  
(4) Illegally providing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances to minors who ingest or inject drugs;  
(5) Causing serious consequences by illegally providing narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances;  
(6) Other circumstances where criminal liability shall be investigated.  
Under any of the following circumstances, the act shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Paragraph 1 of Article 355 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The quantity of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances illegally provided reaches the "relatively large quantities" standard as provided for in Paragraph 3 of Article 347 of the Criminal Law or Article 2 of this Interpretation;  
(2) The quantity of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances illegally provided reaches the quantitative standard specified in Item (1) of the preceding paragraph, and the act meets any of the circumstances specified in Items (3) through (5) of the preceding paragraph;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条    利用信息网络，设立用于实施传授制造毒品、非法生产制毒物品的方法，贩卖毒品，非法买卖制毒物品或者组织他人吸食、注射毒品等违法犯罪活动的网站、通讯群组，或者发布实施前述违法犯罪活动的信息，情节严重的，应当依照[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一的规定，以非法利用信息网络罪定罪处罚。  
实施[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一、第二百八十七条之二规定的行为，同时构成贩卖毒品罪、非法买卖制毒物品罪、传授犯罪方法罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 14** . Where a person uses an information network to establish websites or communication groups for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities such as teaching methods of manufacturing drugs, illegally producing precursor chemicals, trafficking drugs, illegally trading precursor chemicals, or organizing others to ingest or inject drugs, or publishes information for carrying out the aforementioned illegal or criminal activities, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished as the crime of illegally using an information network in accordance with Article 287-1 of the Criminal Law.  
Where an act committed under Article 287-1 or Article 287-2 of the Criminal Law also constitutes crimes such as the crime of trafficking drugs, the crime of illegally trading precursor chemicals, or the crime of teaching criminal methods, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision that imposes the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条    本解释自2016年4月11日起施行。  
最高人民法院《关于审理毒品案件定罪量刑标准有关问题的解释》（法释〔2000〕13号）同时废止；之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 15** . This Interpretation shall come into force on April 11, 2016.  
The Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Issues Concerning the Standards for Conviction and Sentencing in Drug Cases (Fa Shi [2000] No. 13) shall be abolished simultaneously; where any previously issued judicial interpretation or normative document is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
