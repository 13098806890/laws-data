# 最高人民法院关于审理毒品案件定罪量刑标准有关问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕13号  
**公布日期**：2000-04-20

> （2000年4月20日最高人民法院审判委员会第1110次会议通过，自2000年6月100起施行。） 法释〔2000〕13号 为依法严惩毒品犯罪，根据刑法分则第六章第七节的规定，现就审理毒品案件定罪量刑标准有关问题解释如下： 第一条 走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有下列毒品，应当认定为刑法第三百四十七条第二款第（一）项、第三百四十八条规定的“其他毒品数量大”： （一）苯丙胺类毒品（甲基苯丙胺除外）一百克以上； （二）大麻油五千克、大麻脂十千克、大麻叶及大麻烟一百五十千克以上； （三）可卡因五十克以上； （四）吗啡一百克以上； （五）度冷丁（杜冷丁）二百五十克以上（针剂100mg/支规格的二千五百支以上，50mg/支规格的五千支以上；片剂25mg/片规格的一万片以上，50mg/片规格的五千片以上）； （六）盐酸二氢埃托啡十毫克以上（针剂或者片剂20μg/支、片规格的五百支、片以上）； （七）咖啡因二百千克以上； （八）罂粟壳二百千克以上； （九）上述毒品以外的其他毒品数量大的。 第二条 走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有下列毒品，应当认定为刑法第三百四十七条第三款、第三百四十八条规定的“其他毒品数盆较大”： （一）苯丙胺类毒品（甲基苯丙胺除外）二十克以上不满一百克；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有下列毒品，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第二款第（一）项、第三百四十八条规定的“其他毒品数量大”：  
（一）苯丙胺类毒品（甲基苯丙胺除外）一百克以上；  
（二）大麻油五千克、大麻脂十千克、大麻叶及大麻烟一百五十千克以上；  
（三）可卡因五十克以上；  
（四）吗啡一百克以上；  
（五）度冷丁（杜冷丁）二百五十克以上（针剂100mg/支规格的二千五百支以上，50mg/支规格的五千支以上；片剂25mg/片规格的一万片以上，50mg/片规格的五千片以上）；  
（六）盐酸二氢埃托啡十毫克以上（针剂或者片剂20μg/支、片规格的五百支、片以上）；  
（七）咖啡因二百千克以上；  
（八）罂粟壳二百千克以上；  
（九）上述毒品以外的其他毒品数量大的。

**Article 1** The smuggling, trafficking, transportation, manufacture, and illegal possession of the following drugs shall be determined as the "large quantities of other drugs" as stipulated in Article 347(2)(1) and Article 348 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) One hundred grams or more of amphetamine-type drugs (excluding methamphetamine);  
(2) Five kilograms or more of cannabis oil, ten kilograms or more of cannabis resin, and one hundred fifty kilograms or more of cannabis leaves and cannabis tobacco;  
(3) Fifty grams or more of cocaine;  
(4) One hundred grams or more of morphine;  
(5) Two hundred fifty grams or more of pethidine (dolantin) (two thousand five hundred or more injections at 100mg/injection, five thousand or more injections at 50mg/injection; ten thousand or more tablets at 25mg/tablet, five thousand or more tablets at 50mg/tablet);  
(6) Ten milligrams or more of dihydroetorphine hydrochloride (five hundred or more injections or tablets at 20μg/injection or tablet);  
(7) Two hundred kilograms or more of caffeine;  
(8) Two hundred kilograms or more of poppy shells;  
(9) Large quantities of other drugs other than the above-mentioned drugs.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 走私、贩卖、运输、制造、非法持有下列毒品，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第三款、第三百四十八条规定的“其他毒品数盆较大”：  
（一）苯丙胺类毒品（甲基苯丙胺除外）二十克以上不满一百克；  
（二）大麻油一千克以上不满五千克，大麻脂二千克以上不满十千克，大麻叶及大麻烟三十千克以上不满一百五十千克；  
（三）可卡因十克以上不满五十克；  
（四）吗啡二十克以上不满一百克；  
（五）度冷丁（杜冷丁）五十克以上不满二百五十克（针剂100mg/支规格的五百支以上不满二千五百支，50mg/支规格的一千支以上不满五千支；片剂25mg/片规格的二千片以上不满一万片，50mg/片规格的一千片以上不满五千片）；  
（六）盐酸二氢埃托啡二毫克以上不满十毫克（针剂或者片剂20μ g/支、片规格的一百支、片以上不满五百支、片）；  
（七）咖啡因五十千克以上不满二百千克；  
（八）罂粟壳五十千克以上不满二百千克；  
（九）上述毒品以外的其他毒品数盆较大的。

**Article 2** The smuggling, trafficking, transportation, manufacture, and illegal possession of the following drugs shall be determined as the "relatively large quantities of other drugs" as stipulated in Article 347(3) and Article 348 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Twenty grams or more but less than one hundred grams of amphetamine-type drugs (excluding methamphetamine);  
(2) One kilogram or more but less than five kilograms of cannabis oil, two kilograms or more but less than ten kilograms of cannabis resin, and thirty kilograms or more but less than one hundred fifty kilograms of cannabis leaves and cannabis tobacco;  
(3) Ten grams or more but less than fifty grams of cocaine;  
(4) Twenty grams or more but less than one hundred grams of morphine;  
(5) Fifty grams or more but less than two hundred fifty grams of pethidine (dolantin) (five hundred or more but less than two thousand five hundred injections at 100mg/injection, one thousand or more but less than five thousand injections at 50mg/injection; two thousand or more but less than ten thousand tablets at 25mg/tablet, one thousand or more but less than five thousand tablets at 50mg/tablet);  
(6) Two milligrams or more but less than ten milligrams of dihydroetorphine hydrochloride (one hundred or more but less than five hundred injections or tablets at 20μg/injection or tablet);  
(7) Fifty kilograms or more but less than two hundred kilograms of caffeine;  
(8) Fifty kilograms or more but less than two hundred kilograms of poppy shells;  
(9) Relatively large quantities of other drugs other than the above-mentioned drugs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第三百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-347)第四款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）走私、贩卖、运输、制造鸦片一百四十克以上不满二百克、海洛因或者甲基苯丙胺七克以上不满十克或者其他数量相当毒品的；  
（二）国家工作人员走私、制造、运输、贩卖毒品；  
（三）在戒毒监管场所贩卖毒品的；  
（四）向多人贩毒或者多次贩毒的；  
（五）其他情节严重的行为。

**Article 3** In any of the following circumstances, it may be determined as the "serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 347(4) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling, trafficking, transportation, or manufacture of one hundred forty grams or more but less than two hundred grams of opium, seven grams or more but less than ten grams of heroin or methamphetamine, or other drugs in equivalent quantities;  
(2) State functionaries smuggling, manufacturing, transporting, or trafficking drugs;  
(3) Trafficking drugs in drug rehabilitation supervision sites;  
(4) Trafficking drugs to multiple persons or trafficking drugs multiple times;  
(5) Other acts with serious circumstances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 违反国家规定，非法运输、携带进出境或在境内非法买卖醋酸酐、乙醚、三氯甲烷或者其他用于制造毒品的原料或者配剂达到下列数量标准的，依照[刑法第三百五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-350)第一款的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）麻黄碱、伪麻黄碱及其盐类和单方制剂五千克以上不满五十千克；麻黄浸膏、麻黄浸膏粉一百千克以上不满一千千克；  
（二）醋酸酐、三氯甲烷二百千克以上不满二千千克；  
（三）乙醚四百千克以上不满三千千克；  
（四）上述原料或者配剂以外其他相当数量的用于制造毒品的原料或者配剂。  
违反国家规定，非法运输、携带进出境或者在境内非法买卖用于制造毒品的原料或者配剂，超过前款所列数量标准的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-350)第一款规定的“数量大”。

**Article 4** Whoever, in violation of state regulations, illegally transports or carries into or out of the territory, or illegally sells within the territory, acetic anhydride, ether, chloroform, or other raw materials or preparations for manufacturing drugs that meet the following quantity standards, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 350(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Five kilograms or more but less than fifty kilograms of ephedrine, pseudoephedrine, their salts and single-ingredient preparations; one hundred kilograms or more but less than one thousand kilograms of ephedra extract and ephedra extract powder;  
(2) Two hundred kilograms or more but less than two thousand kilograms of acetic anhydride and chloroform;  
(3) Four hundred kilograms or more but less than three thousand kilograms of ether;  
(4) Other raw materials or preparations for manufacturing drugs in equivalent quantities other than the above-mentioned raw materials or preparations.  
Where a person, in violation of state regulations, illegally transports or carries into or out of the territory, or illegally sells within the territory, raw materials or preparations for manufacturing drugs, exceeding the quantity standards listed in the preceding paragraph, such act shall be determined as the "large quantities" as stipulated in Article 350(1) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 非法种植大麻五千株以上不满三万株，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-351)第一款第（一）项规定的非法种植大麻“数量较大”；非法种植大麻三万株以上，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-351)第二款规定的非法种植大麻“数量大”。

**Article 5** Illegal cultivation of five thousand or more but less than thirty thousand cannabis plants shall be determined as the "relatively large quantities" of illegal cultivation of cannabis as stipulated in Article 351(1)(1) of the Criminal Law; illegal cultivation of thirty thousand or more cannabis plants shall be determined as the "large quantities" of illegal cultivation of cannabis as stipulated in Article 351(2) of the Criminal Law.
