# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理走私刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕10号  
**公布日期**：2014-02-24  
**生效日期**：2014-09-10

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理走私刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2014年2月24日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1608次会议、2014年6月13日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第23次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年9月10日起施行。 2014年8月12日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理走私刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2014〕10号 为依法惩治走私犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理走私刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 走私武器、弹药，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为刑法第一百五十一条第一款规定的“情节较轻”： （一）走私以压缩气体等非火药为动力发射枪弹的枪支二支以上不满五支的； （二）走私气枪铅弹五百发以上不满二千五百发，或者其他子弹十发以上不满五十发的； （三）未达到上述数量标准，但属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者走私的武器、弹药被用于实施犯罪等情形的； （四）走私各种口径在六十毫米以下常规炮弹、手榴弹或者枪榴弹等分别或者合计不满五枚的。 具有下列情形之一的，依照刑法第一百五十一条第一款的规定处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产： （一）走私以火药为动力发射枪弹的枪支一支，或者以压缩气体等非火药为动力发射枪弹的枪支五支以上不满十支的； （二）走私第一款第二项规定的弹药，数量在该项规定的最高数量以上不满最高数量五倍的； （三）走私各种口径在六十毫米以下常规炮弹、手榴弹或者枪榴弹等分别或者合计达到五枚以上不满十枚，或者各种口径超过六十毫米以上常规炮弹合计不满五枚的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 走私武器、弹药，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“情节较轻”：  
（一）走私以压缩气体等非火药为动力发射枪弹的枪支二支以上不满五支的；  
（二）走私气枪铅弹五百发以上不满二千五百发，或者其他子弹十发以上不满五十发的；  
（三）未达到上述数量标准，但属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者走私的武器、弹药被用于实施犯罪等情形的；  
（四）走私各种口径在六十毫米以下常规炮弹、手榴弹或者枪榴弹等分别或者合计不满五枚的。  
具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款的规定处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）走私以火药为动力发射枪弹的枪支一支，或者以压缩气体等非火药为动力发射枪弹的枪支五支以上不满十支的；  
（二）走私第一款第二项规定的弹药，数量在该项规定的最高数量以上不满最高数量五倍的；  
（三）走私各种口径在六十毫米以下常规炮弹、手榴弹或者枪榴弹等分别或者合计达到五枚以上不满十枚，或者各种口径超过六十毫米以上常规炮弹合计不满五枚的；  
（四）达到第一款第一、二、四项规定的数量标准，且属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者走私的武器、弹药被用于实施犯罪等情形的。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）走私第二款第一项规定的枪支，数量超过该项规定的数量标准的；  
（二）走私第一款第二项规定的弹药，数量在该项规定的最高数量标准五倍以上的;  
（三）走私第二款第三项规定的弹药，数量超过该项规定的数量标准，或者走私具有巨大杀伤力的非常规炮弹一枚以上的；  
（四）达到第二款第一项至第三项规定的数量标准，且属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者走私的武器、弹药被用于实施犯罪等情形的。  
走私其他武器、弹药，构成犯罪的，参照本条各款规定的标准处罚。

**Article 1** Where smuggling of weapons or ammunition falls under any of the following circumstances, it may be deemed as 'relatively minor circumstances' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of two or more but less than five guns that fire projectiles by power of compressed gas or other non-explosive propellants;  
(2) Smuggling of five hundred or more but less than two thousand five hundred air gun pellets, or ten or more but less than fifty other bullets;  
(3) Failing to meet the above quantitative standards, but the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or the smuggled weapons or ammunition are used to commit crimes, or other such circumstances;  
(4) Smuggling of conventional shells, hand grenades, or rifle grenades of various calibers below 60 mm, respectively or in total less than five pieces.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years shall be imposed, and a fine or confiscation of property shall also be imposed, in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of one gun that fires projectiles by power of explosive propellants, or five or more but less than ten guns that fire projectiles by power of compressed gas or other non-explosive propellants;  
(2) Smuggling of ammunition specified in subparagraph (2) of the first paragraph, the quantity of which is not less than the maximum quantity specified in that subparagraph but less than five times the maximum quantity;  
(3) Smuggling of conventional shells, hand grenades, or rifle grenades of various calibers below 60 mm, respectively or in total reaching five or more but less than ten pieces, or conventional shells of various calibers above 60 mm respectively or in total less than five pieces;  
(4) Meeting the quantitative standards specified in subparagraphs (1), (2) and (4) of the first paragraph, and the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or the smuggled weapons or ammunition are used to commit crimes, or other such circumstances.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as 'especially serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of guns specified in subparagraph (1) of the second paragraph, the quantity of which exceeds the quantitative standard specified in that subparagraph;  
(2) Smuggling of ammunition specified in subparagraph (2) of the first paragraph, the quantity of which is more than five times the maximum quantitative standard specified in that subparagraph;  
(3) Smuggling of ammunition specified in subparagraph (3) of the second paragraph, the quantity of which exceeds the quantitative standard specified in that subparagraph, or smuggling of one or more unconventional shells of enormous lethality;  
(4) Meeting the quantitative standards specified in subparagraphs (1) to (3) of the second paragraph, and the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or the smuggled weapons or ammunition are used to commit crimes, or other such circumstances.  
Where smuggling of other weapons or ammunition constitutes a crime, punishment shall be imposed with reference to the standards provided in each paragraph of this Article.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 [刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“武器、弹药”的种类，参照《中华人民共和国进口税则》及《中华人民共和国禁止进出境物品表》的有关规定确定。

**Article 2** The types of 'weapons and ammunition' provided in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law shall be determined with reference to the relevant provisions of the Import Tariff of the People's Republic of China and the List of Articles Prohibited from Entry and Exit of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 走私枪支散件，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款的规定，以走私武器罪定罪处罚。成套枪支散件以相应数量的枪支计，非成套枪支散件以每三十件为一套枪支散件计。

**Article 3** Where smuggling of firearm parts and components constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling weapons in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law. Complete sets of firearm parts and components shall be counted as the corresponding number of firearms, and incomplete sets of firearm parts and components shall be counted as one set for every thirty pieces.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 走私各种弹药的弹头、弹壳，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款的规定，以走私弹药罪定罪处罚。具体的定罪量刑标准，按照[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的数量标准的五倍执行。  
走私报废或者无法组装并使用的各种弹药的弹头、弹壳，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)的规定，以走私普通货物、物品罪定罪处罚；属于废物的，依照[刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)第二款的规定，以走私废物罪定罪处罚。  
弹头、弹壳是否属于前款规定的“报废或者无法组装并使用”或者“废物”，由国家有关技术部门进行鉴定。

**Article 4** Where smuggling of bullet heads or cartridge cases of various kinds of ammunition constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling ammunition in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law. The specific standards for conviction and sentencing shall be implemented at five times the quantitative standards provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation.  
Where smuggling of bullet heads or cartridge cases of various kinds of ammunition that are scrapped or cannot be assembled and used constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles in accordance with Article 153 of the Criminal Law; if they are waste, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling waste in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 152 of the Criminal Law.  
Whether bullet heads or cartridge cases fall under 'scrapped or cannot be assembled and used' or 'waste' as provided in the preceding paragraph shall be subject to expert evaluation by the relevant state technical authorities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 走私国家禁止或者限制进出口的仿真枪、管制刀具，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第三款的规定，以走私国家禁止进出口的货物、物品罪定罪处罚。具体的定罪量刑标准，适用[本解释第十一条](#art-11)第一款第六、七项和第二款的规定。  
走私的仿真枪经鉴定为枪支，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款的规定，以走私武器罪定罪处罚。不以牟利或者从事违法犯罪活动为目的，且无其他严重情节的，可以依法从轻处罚；情节轻微不需要判处刑罚的，可以免予刑事处罚。

**Article 5** Where smuggling of imitation guns or controlled knives that are prohibited or restricted by the State from import or export constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling goods or articles prohibited from import or export by the State in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law. The specific standards for conviction and sentencing shall apply the provisions of subparagraphs (6) and (7) of paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 of Article 11 of this Interpretation.  
Where smuggled imitation guns are identified as firearms through expert evaluation and constitute a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling weapons in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law. If the act is not for the purpose of profit-making or engaging in illegal or criminal activities, and there are no other serious circumstances, a lighter punishment may be imposed according to law; if the circumstances are minor and no criminal punishment is necessary, the offender may be exempted from criminal punishment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 走私伪造的货币，数额在二千元以上不满二万元，或者数量在二百张（枚）以上不满二千张（枚）的，可以认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“情节较轻”。  
具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款的规定处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）走私数额在二万元以上不满二十万元，或者数量在二千张（枚）以上不满二万张（枚）的；  
（二）走私数额或者数量达到第一款规定的标准，且具有走私的伪造货币流入市场等情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）走私数额在二十万元以上，或者数量在二万张（枚）以上的；  
（二）走私数额或者数量达到第二款第一项规定的标准，且属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者走私的伪造货币流入市场等情形的。

**Article 6** Where smuggling of counterfeit currency, with an amount of not less than 2,000 yuan but less than 20,000 yuan, or a quantity of not less than 200 pieces (coins) but less than 2,000 pieces (coins), may be deemed as 'relatively minor circumstances' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years shall be imposed, and a fine or confiscation of property shall also be imposed, in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of counterfeit currency with an amount of not less than 20,000 yuan but less than 200,000 yuan, or a quantity of not less than 2,000 pieces (coins) but less than 20,000 pieces (coins);  
(2) The amount or quantity of smuggling reaches the standard specified in the first paragraph, and there are circumstances such as the smuggled counterfeit currency entering the market.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as 'especially serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of counterfeit currency with an amount of 200,000 yuan or more, or a quantity of 20,000 pieces (coins) or more;  
(2) The amount or quantity of smuggling reaches the standard specified in subparagraph (1) of the second paragraph, and the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or the smuggled counterfeit currency enters the market, or other such circumstances.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 [刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第一款规定的“货币”，包括正在流通的人民币和境外货币。伪造的境外货币数额，折合成人民币计算。

**Article 7** 'Currency' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law includes Renminbi in circulation as well as foreign currency. The amount of counterfeit foreign currency shall be converted into Renminbi for calculation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　走私国家禁止出口的三级文物二件以下的，可以认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“情节较轻”。  
具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款的规定处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）走私国家禁止出口的二级文物不满三件，或者三级文物三件以上不满九件的；  
（二）走私国家禁止出口的三级文物不满三件，且具有造成文物严重毁损或者无法追回等情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）走私国家禁止出口的一级文物一件以上，或者二级文物三件以上，或者三级文物九件以上的；  
（二）走私国家禁止出口的文物达到第二款第一项规定的数量标准，且属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者造成文物严重毁损、无法追回等情形的。

**Article 8** Where smuggling of two or fewer pieces of Grade Three cultural relics prohibited from export by the State may be deemed as 'relatively minor circumstances' as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years shall be imposed, and a fine shall also be imposed, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of fewer than three pieces of Grade Two cultural relics prohibited from export by the State, or three or more but fewer than nine pieces of Grade Three cultural relics;  
(2) Smuggling of fewer than three pieces of Grade Three cultural relics prohibited from export by the State, and there are circumstances such as causing serious damage to the cultural relics or making them irretrievable.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as 'especially serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of one or more pieces of Grade One cultural relics prohibited from export by the State, or three or more pieces of Grade Two cultural relics, or nine or more pieces of Grade Three cultural relics;  
(2) The quantity of smuggled cultural relics prohibited from export by the State reaches the standard specified in subparagraph (1) of the second paragraph, and the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or causes serious damage to the cultural relics or makes them irretrievable, or other such circumstances.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 走私国家一、二级保护动物未达到本解释附表中（一）规定的数量标准，或者走私珍贵动物制品数额不满二十万元的，可以认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“情节较轻”。  
具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款的规定处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）走私国家一、二级保护动物达到本解释附表中（一）规定的数量标准的；  
（二）走私珍贵动物制品数额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的；  
（三）走私国家一、二级保护动物未达到本解释附表中（一）规定的数量标准，但具有造成该珍贵动物死亡或者无法追回等情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）走私国家一、二级保护动物达到本解释附表中（二）规定的数量标准的；  
（二）走私珍贵动物制品数额在一百万元以上的；  
（三）走私国家一、二级保护动物达到本解释附表中（一）规定的数量标准，且属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者造成该珍贵动物死亡、无法追回等情形的。  
不以牟利为目的，为留作纪念而走私珍贵动物制品进境，数额不满十万元的，可以免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 9** Where smuggling of Class I or Class II protected animals under the State fails to meet the quantitative standard specified in (1) of the Appendix to this Interpretation, or smuggling of products of precious animals with an amount of less than 200,000 yuan, may be deemed as 'relatively minor circumstances' as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years shall be imposed, and a fine shall also be imposed, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of Class I or Class II protected animals under the State reaches the quantitative standard specified in (1) of the Appendix to this Interpretation;  
(2) Smuggling of products of precious animals with an amount of not less than 200,000 yuan but less than 1,000,000 yuan;  
(3) Smuggling of Class I or Class II protected animals under the State fails to meet the quantitative standard specified in (1) of the Appendix to this Interpretation, but there are circumstances such as causing the death of the precious animal or making it irretrievable.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as 'especially serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of Class I or Class II protected animals under the State reaches the quantitative standard specified in (2) of the Appendix to this Interpretation;  
(2) Smuggling of products of precious animals with an amount of 1,000,000 yuan or more;  
(3) Smuggling of Class I or Class II protected animals under the State reaches the quantitative standard specified in (1) of the Appendix to this Interpretation, and the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or causes the death of the precious animal or makes it irretrievable, or other such circumstances.  
Where the importation of products of precious animals by smuggling is not for the purpose of profit-making but for retention as a souvenir, and the amount is less than 100,000 yuan, the offender may be exempted from criminal punishment; if the circumstances are obviously minor, the act shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  [刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“珍贵动物”，包括列入《国家重点保护野生动物名录》中的国家一、二级保护野生动物，《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》附录Ⅰ、附录Ⅱ中的野生动物，以及驯养繁殖的上述动物。  
走私本解释附表中未规定的珍贵动物的，参照附表中规定的同属或者同科动物的数量标准执行。  
走私本解释附表中未规定珍贵动物的制品的，按照最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、国家林业局、公安部、海关总署《关于破坏野生动物资源刑事案件中涉及的CITES附录Ⅰ和附录Ⅱ所列陆生野生动物制品价值核定问题的通知》（林濒发〔2012〕239号）的有关规定核定价值。

**Article 10** 'Precious animals' as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law include Class I and Class II protected wild animals listed in the List of Key Protected Wild Animals of the State, wild animals listed in Appendix I and Appendix II of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora, and the aforementioned animals that are domesticated and bred.  
Where smuggling of precious animals not specified in the Appendix to this Interpretation, the quantitative standards for animals of the same genus or family specified in the Appendix shall apply.  
Where smuggling of products of precious animals not specified in the Appendix to this Interpretation, the value shall be determined in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Notice of the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the State Forestry Administration, the Ministry of Public Security, and the General Administration of Customs on the Verification of the Value of Terrestrial Wild Animal Products Listed in CITES Appendix I and Appendix II Involved in Criminal Cases of Damaging Wildlife Resources (Lin Bin Fa [2012] No. 239).

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 走私国家禁止进出口的货物、物品，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第三款的规定处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）走私国家一级保护野生植物五株以上不满二十五株，国家二级保护野生植物十株以上不满五十株，或者珍稀植物、珍稀植物制品数额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的；  
（二）走私重点保护古生物化石或者未命名的古生物化石不满十件，或者一般保护古生物化石十件以上不满五十件的；  
（三）走私禁止进出口的有毒物质一吨以上不满五吨，或者数额在二万元以上不满十万元的；  
（四）走私来自境外疫区的动植物及其产品五吨以上不满二十五吨，或者数额在五万元以上不满二十五万元的；  
（五）走私木炭、硅砂等妨害环境、资源保护的货物、物品十吨以上不满五十吨，或者数额在十万元以上不满五十万元的；  
（六）走私旧机动车、切割车、旧机电产品或者其他禁止进出口的货物、物品二十吨以上不满一百吨，或者数额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的；  
（七）数量或者数额未达到本款第一项至第六项规定的标准，但属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，造成环境严重污染，或者引起甲类传染病传播、重大动植物疫情等情形的。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第三款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）走私数量或者数额超过前款第一项至第六项规定的标准的；  
（二）达到前款第一项至第六项规定的标准，且属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，造成环境严重污染，或者引起甲类传染病传播、重大动植物疫情等情形的。

**Article 11** Where smuggling of goods or articles prohibited from import or export by the State falls under any of the following circumstances, a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention shall be imposed, and a fine or a single fine shall be imposed, in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of five or more but fewer than twenty-five wild plants under Class I State protection, ten or more but fewer than fifty wild plants under Class II State protection, or rare plants or products of rare plants with an amount of not less than 200,000 yuan but less than 1,000,000 yuan;  
(2) Smuggling of fewer than ten key protected paleontological fossils or unnamed paleontological fossils, or ten or more but fewer than fifty generally protected paleontological fossils;  
(3) Smuggling of one ton or more but fewer than five tons of toxic substances prohibited from import or export, or with an amount of not less than 20,000 yuan but less than 100,000 yuan;  
(4) Smuggling of five tons or more but fewer than twenty-five tons of animals, plants and their products from overseas epidemic areas, or with an amount of not less than 50,000 yuan but less than 250,000 yuan;  
(5) Smuggling of ten tons or more but fewer than fifty tons of goods or articles such as charcoal and silica sand that hinder environmental protection or resource protection, or with an amount of not less than 100,000 yuan but less than 500,000 yuan;  
(6) Smuggling of twenty tons or more but fewer than one hundred tons of used motor vehicles, cut-up cars, used mechanical and electrical products or other goods or articles prohibited from import or export, or with an amount of not less than 200,000 yuan but less than 1,000,000 yuan;  
(7) The quantity or amount fails to meet the standards specified in subparagraphs (1) to (6) of this paragraph, but the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, causes serious environmental pollution, or causes the spread of a Class A infectious disease or a major animal or plant epidemic, or other such circumstances.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as 'serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 3 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The quantity or amount smuggled exceeds the standards specified in subparagraphs (1) to (6) of the preceding paragraph;  
(2) The standards specified in subparagraphs (1) to (6) of the preceding paragraph are met, and the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, causes serious environmental pollution, or causes the spread of a Class A infectious disease or a major animal or plant epidemic, or other such circumstances.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 [刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第三款规定的“珍稀植物”，包括列入《国家重点保护野生植物名录》、《国家重点保护野生药材物种名录》、《国家珍贵树种名录》中的国家一、二级保护野生植物、国家重点保护的野生药材、珍贵树木，《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》附录I、附录Ⅱ中的野生植物，以及人工培育的上述植物。  
本解释规定的“古生物化石”，按照《古生物化石保护条例》的规定予以认定。走私具有科学价值的古脊椎动物化石、古人类化石，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款的规定，以走私文物罪定罪处罚。

**Article 12** 'Rare plants' as provided in paragraph 3 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law include Class I and Class II protected wild plants listed in the List of Key Protected Wild Plants of the State, the List of Key Protected Wild Medicinal Species of the State, the List of Precious Tree Species of the State, key protected wild medicinal materials of the State, precious trees, wild plants listed in Appendix I and Appendix II of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora, and the aforementioned plants that are artificially cultivated.  
'Paleontological fossils' as provided in this Interpretation shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of the Regulations on the Protection of Paleontological Fossils. Where smuggling of scientifically valuable fossilized vertebrates or fossilized human remains constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling cultural relics in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 以牟利或者传播为目的，走私淫秽物品，达到下列数量之一的，可以认定为[刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)第一款规定的“情节较轻”：  
（一）走私淫秽录像带、影碟五十盘（张）以上不满一百盘（张）的；  
（二）走私淫秽录音带、音碟一百盘（张）以上不满二百盘（张）的；  
（三）走私淫秽扑克、书刊、画册一百副（册）以上不满二百副（册）的；  
（四）走私淫秽照片、画片五百张以上不满一千张的；  
（五）走私其他淫秽物品相当于上述数量的。  
走私淫秽物品在前款规定的最高数量以上不满最高数量五倍的，依照[刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)第一款的规定处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
走私淫秽物品在第一款规定的最高数量五倍以上，或者在第一款规定的最高数量以上不满五倍，但属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动等情形的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)第一款规定的“情节严重”。

**Article 13** Where smuggling of pornographic articles for the purpose of profit-making or dissemination, and reaching one of the following quantities, may be deemed as 'relatively minor circumstances' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 152 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of fifty or more but fewer than one hundred pornographic video tapes or video discs;  
(2) Smuggling of one hundred or more but fewer than two hundred pornographic audio tapes or audio discs;  
(3) Smuggling of one hundred or more but fewer than two hundred decks (volumes) of pornographic playing cards, books, or picture albums;  
(4) Smuggling of five hundred or more but fewer than one thousand pornographic photos or pictures;  
(5) Smuggling of other pornographic articles equivalent to the above quantities.  
Where the quantity of smuggled pornographic articles is not less than the maximum quantity specified in the preceding paragraph but less than five times the maximum quantity, a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years shall be imposed, and a fine shall also be imposed, in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 152 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the quantity of smuggled pornographic articles is five times or more the maximum quantity specified in the first paragraph, or is not less than the maximum quantity specified in the first paragraph but less than five times, but the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or other such circumstances, it shall be deemed as 'serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 152 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 走私国家禁止进口的废物或者国家限制进口的可用作原料的废物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)第二款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）走私国家禁止进口的危险性固体废物、液态废物分别或者合计达到一吨以上不满五吨的；  
（二）走私国家禁止进口的非危险性固体废物、液态废物分别或者合计达到五吨以上不满二十五吨的；  
（三）走私国家限制进口的可用作原料的固体废物、液态废物分别或者合计达到二十吨以上不满一百吨的；  
（四）未达到上述数量标准，但属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者造成环境严重污染等情形的。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-152)第二款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）走私数量超过前款规定的标准的；  
（二）达到前款规定的标准，且属于犯罪集团的首要分子，使用特种车辆从事走私活动，或者造成环境严重污染等情形的；  
（三）未达到前款规定的标准，但造成环境严重污染且后果特别严重的。走私置于容器中的气态废物，构成犯罪的，参照前两款规定的标准处罚。

**Article 14** Where smuggling of waste prohibited from import by the State or waste restricted by the State for import as raw materials falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as 'serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 152 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Smuggling of hazardous solid waste or liquid waste prohibited from import by the State, respectively or in total reaching one ton or more but less than five tons;  
(2) Smuggling of non-hazardous solid waste or liquid waste prohibited from import by the State, respectively or in total reaching five tons or more but less than twenty-five tons;  
(3) Smuggling of solid waste or liquid waste restricted by the State for import as raw materials, respectively or in total reaching twenty tons or more but less than one hundred tons;  
(4) Failing to meet the above quantitative standards, but the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or causes serious environmental pollution, or other such circumstances.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as 'especially serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 152 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The quantity smuggled exceeds the standards specified in the preceding paragraph;  
(2) The standards specified in the preceding paragraph are met, and the offender is a principal offender of a criminal group, uses special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities, or causes serious environmental pollution, or other such circumstances;  
(3) Failing to meet the standards specified in the preceding paragraph, but causing serious environmental pollution and particularly serious consequences.  
Where smuggling of gaseous waste contained in containers constitutes a crime, punishment shall be imposed with reference to the standards provided in the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 国家限制进口的可用作原料的废物的具体种类，参照国家有关部门的规定确定。

**Article 15** The specific categories of waste restricted by the State for import as raw materials shall be determined with reference to the provisions of the relevant state departments.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 走私普通货物、物品，偷逃应缴税额在十万元以上不满五十万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第一款规定的“偷逃应缴税额较大”；偷逃应缴税额在五十万元以上不满二百五十万元的，应当认定为“偷逃应缴税额巨大”；偷逃应缴税额在二百五十万元以上的，应当认定为“偷逃应缴税额特别巨大”。  
走私普通货物、物品，具有下列情形之一，偷逃应缴税额在三十万元以上不满五十万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第一款规定的“其他严重情节”；偷逃应缴税额在一百五十万元以上不满二百五十万元的，应当认定为“其他特别严重情节”：  
（一）犯罪集团的首要分子；  
（二）使用特种车辆从事走私活动的；  
（三）为实施走私犯罪，向国家机关工作人员行贿的；  
（四）教唆、利用未成年人、孕妇等特殊人群走私的；  
（五）聚众阻挠缉私的。

**Article 16** Where smuggling of ordinary goods or articles, with the amount of duties evaded being not less than 100,000 yuan but less than 500,000 yuan, shall be deemed as 'relatively large amount of duties evaded' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law; where the amount of duties evaded is not less than 500,000 yuan but less than 2,500,000 yuan, shall be deemed as 'large amount of duties evaded'; where the amount of duties evaded is 2,500,000 yuan or more, shall be deemed as 'especially large amount of duties evaded'.  
Where smuggling of ordinary goods or articles falls under any of the following circumstances, and the amount of duties evaded is not less than 300,000 yuan but less than 500,000 yuan, shall be deemed as 'other serious circumstances' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law; where the amount of duties evaded is not less than 1,500,000 yuan but less than 2,500,000 yuan, shall be deemed as 'other especially serious circumstances':  
(1) Being a principal offender of a criminal group;  
(2) Using special vehicles to engage in smuggling activities;  
(3) Bribing state functionaries to commit smuggling crimes;  
(4) Instigating or using minors, pregnant women or other special groups to commit smuggling;  
(5) Gathering a crowd to obstruct anti-smuggling operations.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 [刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第一款规定的“一年内曾因走私被给予二次行政处罚后又走私”中的“一年内”，以因走私第一次受到行政处罚的生效之日与“又走私”行为实施之日的时间间隔计算确定；“被给予二次行政处罚”的走私行为，包括走私普通货物、物品以及其他货物、物品；“又走私”行为仅指走私普通货物、物品。

**Article 17** 'Within one year' in the phrase 'having been given two administrative penalties for smuggling within one year and then smuggling again' as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law shall be calculated based on the time interval between the effective date of the first administrative penalty for smuggling and the date of commission of the 'smuggling again' act. 'Having been given two administrative penalties' refers to smuggling acts, including smuggling of ordinary goods or articles as well as other goods or articles. 'Smuggling again' refers only to smuggling of ordinary goods or articles.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 [刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)规定的“应缴税额”，包括进出口货物、物品应当缴纳的进出口关税和进口环节海关代征税的税额。应缴税额以走私行为实施时的税则、税率、汇率和完税价格计算；多次走私的，以每次走私行为实施时的税则、税率、汇率和完税价格逐票计算；走私行为实施时间不能确定的，以案发时的税则、税率、汇率和完税价格计算。  
[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第三款规定的“多次走私未经处理”，包括未经行政处理和刑事处理。

**Article 18** 'Amount of duties payable' as provided in Article 153 of the Criminal Law includes the import and export customs duties and the taxes collected by the Customs on behalf of other authorities at the time of importation on the goods or articles. The amount of duties payable shall be calculated based on the tariff schedule, tax rate, exchange rate, and dutiable value in effect at the time of the smuggling act; where there are multiple smuggling acts, it shall be calculated for each shipment based on the tariff schedule, tax rate, exchange rate, and dutiable value in effect at the time of each smuggling act; if the time of the smuggling act cannot be determined, it shall be calculated based on the tariff schedule, tax rate, exchange rate, and dutiable value in effect at the time of the case.  
'Multiple smuggling acts not yet handled' as provided in paragraph 3 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law includes those not yet subject to administrative handling or criminal handling.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 [刑法第一百五十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-154)规定的“保税货物”，是指经海关批准，未办理纳税手续进境，在境内储存、加工、装配后应予复运出境的货物，包括通过加工贸易、补偿贸易等方式进口的货物，以及在保税仓库、保税工厂、保税区或者免税商店内等储存、加工、寄售的货物。

**Article 19** 'Bonded goods' as provided in Article 154 of the Criminal Law refer to goods that are imported with the approval of the Customs without going through the duty payment procedures and that shall be re-exported after being stored, processed or assembled within the country, including goods imported through processing trade, compensation trade, or other methods, as well as goods stored, processed, or consigned in bonded warehouses, bonded factories, bonded zones, or duty-free shops.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 直接向走私人非法收购走私进口的货物、物品，在内海、领海、界河、界湖运输、收购、贩卖国家禁止进出口的物品，或者没有合法证明，在内海、领海、界河、界湖运输、收购、贩卖国家限制进出口的货物、物品，构成犯罪的，应当按照走私货物、物品的种类，分别依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)、第一百五十二条、第一百五十三条、第三百四十七条、第三百五十条的规定定罪处罚。  
[刑法第一百五十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-155)第二项规定的“内海”，包括内河的入海口水域。

**Article 20** Where a person illegally purchases from smugglers goods or articles that have been smuggled into the country, or transports, purchases, or sells articles prohibited from import or export by the State in the inland sea, territorial sea, boundary rivers, or boundary lakes, or without lawful certificates transports, purchases, or sells goods or articles restricted from import or export by the State in the inland sea, territorial sea, boundary rivers, or boundary lakes, and such conduct constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Articles 151, 152, 153, 347, and 350 of the Criminal Law respectively, based on the type of smuggled goods or articles.  
'Inland sea' as provided in subparagraph (2) of Article 155 of the Criminal Law includes the estuarine waters of inland rivers.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 未经许可进出口国家限制进出口的货物、物品，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)、第一百五十二条的规定，以走私国家禁止进出口的货物、物品罪等罪名定罪处罚；偷逃应缴税额，同时又构成走私普通货物、物品罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
取得许可，但超过许可数量进出口国家限制进出口的货物、物品，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)的规定，以走私普通货物、物品罪定罪处罚。  
租用、借用或者使用购买的他人许可证，进出口国家限制进出口的货物、物品的，适用本条第一款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 21** Where a person imports or exports goods or articles restricted from import or export by the State without permission, and such conduct constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling goods or articles prohibited from import or export by the State, and other such charges, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 151 and 152 of the Criminal Law; if the act also constitutes the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles for evading duties payable, the offense shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that carry a heavier punishment.  
Where a person has obtained permission but imports or exports goods or articles restricted from import or export by the State in excess of the permitted quantity, and such conduct constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles in accordance with Article 153 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person rents, borrows, or uses a purchased license of another person to import or export goods or articles restricted from import or export by the State, the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article shall apply for conviction and punishment.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 在走私的货物、物品中藏匿[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)、第一百五十二条、第三百四十七条、第三百五十条规定的货物、物品，构成犯罪的，以实际走私的货物、物品定罪处罚；构成数罪的，实行数罪并罚。

**Article 22** Where goods or articles specified in Articles 151, 152, 347, and 350 of the Criminal Law are concealed among the smuggled goods or articles, and such conduct constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished based on the goods or articles actually smuggled; if multiple crimes are constituted, the offender shall be sentenced for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 实施走私犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为犯罪既遂：  
（一）在海关监管现场被查获的；  
（二）以虚假申报方式走私，申报行为实施完毕的；  
（三）以保税货物或者特定减税、免税进口的货物、物品为对象走私，在境内销售的，或者申请核销行为实施完毕的。

**Article 23** Where any person commits a smuggling crime, under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as a completed crime:  
(1) being discovered at the customs supervision site;  
(2) where the smuggling is conducted by means of false declaration, the act of declaration has been completed;  
(3) where the smuggling targets bonded goods, or imported goods or articles subject to specific tax reduction or exemption, and the goods or articles are sold within the territory, or the act of applying for cancellation has been completed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 单位犯[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)、第一百五十二条规定之罪，依照本解释规定的标准定罪处罚。  
单位犯走私普通货物、物品罪，偷逃应缴税额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的，应当依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第二款的规定，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；偷逃应缴税额在一百第二十三条实施走私犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为犯罪既遂：  
（一）在海关监管现场被查获的；  
（二）以虚假申报方式走私，申报行为实施完毕的；  
（三）以保税货物或者特定减税、免税进口的货物、物品为对象走私，在境内销售的，或者申请核销行为实施完毕的。

**Article 24** Where a unit commits a crime as provided for in Articles 151 and 152 of the Criminal Law, it shall be convicted and punished according to the standards stipulated in this Interpretation.  
Where a unit commits the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles, and the amount of evaded payable tax is not less than 200,000 yuan but less than 1,000,000 yuan, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law, be fined, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention; where the amount of evaded payable tax is not less than 1,000,000 yuan... (1) being discovered at the customs supervision site;  
(2) where the smuggling is conducted by means of false declaration, the act of declaration has been completed;  
(3) where the smuggling targets bonded goods, or imported goods or articles subject to specific tax reduction or exemption, and the goods or articles are sold within the territory, or the act of applying for cancellation has been completed.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 单位犯[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)、第一百五十二条规定之罪，依照本解释规定的标准定罪处罚。  
单位犯走私普通货物、物品罪，偷逃应缴税额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的，应当依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)第二款的规定，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役；偷逃应缴税额在一百万元以上不满五百万元的，应当认定为“情节严重”；偷逃应缴税额在五百万元以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”。

**Article 24** Where a unit commits a crime as provided for in Articles 151 and 152 of the Criminal Law, it shall be convicted and punished according to the standards stipulated in this Interpretation.  
Where a unit commits the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles, and the amount of evaded payable tax is not less than 200,000 yuan but less than 1,000,000 yuan, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law, be fined, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention; where the amount of evaded payable tax is not less than 1,000,000 yuan... **Article 24** Where a unit commits a crime as provided for in Articles 151 and 152 of the Criminal Law, it shall be convicted and punished according to the standards stipulated in this Interpretation.  
Where a unit commits the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles, and the amount of evaded payable tax is not less than 200,000 yuan but less than 1,000,000 yuan, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 153 of the Criminal Law, be fined, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention; where the amount of evaded payable tax is not less than 1,000,000 yuan but less than 5,000,000 yuan, it shall be deemed as 'serious circumstances'; where the amount of evaded payable tax is not less than 5,000,000 yuan, it shall be deemed as 'especially serious circumstances'.
