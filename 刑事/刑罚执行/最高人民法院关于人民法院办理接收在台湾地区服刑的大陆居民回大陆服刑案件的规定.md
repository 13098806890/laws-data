# 最高人民法院关于人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民回大陆服刑案件的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕11号  
**公布日期**：2016-04-27  
**生效日期**：2016-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民回大陆服刑案件的规定》已于2015年6月2日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1653次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年5月1日起施行。 2016年4月27日 最高人民法院 关于人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的 大陆居民回大陆服刑案件的规定 法释〔2016〕11号 为落实《海峡两岸共同打击犯罪及司法互助协议》，保障接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民回大陆服刑工作顺利进行，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等有关法律，制定本规定。 第一条　人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民（以下简称被判刑人）回大陆服刑案件（以下简称接收被判刑人案件），应当遵循一个中国原则，遵守国家法律的基本原则，秉持人道和互惠原则，不得违反社会公共利益。 第二条　接收被判刑人案件由最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。 第三条　申请机关向人民法院申请接收被判刑人回大陆服刑，应当同时提交以下材料： （一）申请机关制作的接收被判刑人申请书，其中应当载明： 1.台湾地区法院认定的被判刑人实施的犯罪行为及判决依据的具体条文内容； 2.该行为在大陆依据刑法也构成犯罪、相应的刑法条文、罪名及该行为未进入大陆刑事诉讼程序的说明； 3.建议转换的具体刑罚； 4.其他需要说明的事项。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院办理接收在台湾地区服刑的大陆居民（以下简称被判刑人）回大陆服刑案件（以下简称接收被判刑人案件），应当遵循一个中国原则，遵守国家法律的基本原则，秉持人道和互惠原则，不得违反社会公共利益。

**Article 1** When a People's Court handles a case of receiving a mainland resident serving a sentence in the Taiwan region (hereinafter referred to as the sentenced person) returning to the mainland to serve the sentence (hereinafter referred to as a case of receiving a sentenced person), it shall adhere to the One-China principle, comply with the basic principles of national laws, uphold the principles of humanity and reciprocity, and shall not violate the public interest.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　接收被判刑人案件由最高人民法院指定的中级人民法院管辖。

**Article 2** Cases of receiving a sentenced person shall be under the jurisdiction of an Intermediate People's Court designated by the Supreme People's Court.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　申请机关向人民法院申请接收被判刑人回大陆服刑，应当同时提交以下材料：  
（一）申请机关制作的接收被判刑人申请书，其中应当载明：  
1.台湾地区法院认定的被判刑人实施的犯罪行为及判决依据的具体条文内容；  
2.该行为在大陆依据刑法也构成犯罪、相应的刑法条文、罪名及该行为未进入大陆刑事诉讼程序的说明；  
3.建议转换的具体刑罚；  
4.其他需要说明的事项。  
（二）被判刑人系大陆居民的身份证明；  
（三）台湾地区法院对被判刑人定罪处刑的裁判文书、生效证明和执行文书；  
（四）被判刑人或其法定代理人申请或者同意回大陆服刑的书面意见，且法定代理人与被判刑人的意思表示一致；  
（五）被判刑人或其法定代理人所作的关于被判刑人在台湾地区接受公正审判的权利已获得保障的书面声明；  
（六）两岸有关业务主管部门均同意被判刑人回大陆服刑的书面意见；  
（七）台湾地区业务主管部门出具的有关刑罚执行情况的说明，包括被判刑人交付执行前的羁押期、已服刑期、剩余刑期，被判刑人服刑期间的表现、退赃退赔情况，被判刑人的健康状况、疾病与治疗情况；  
（八）根据案件具体情况需要提交的其他材料。  
申请机关提交材料齐全的，人民法院应当在七日内立案。提交材料不全的，应当通知申请机关在十五日内补送，至迟不能超过两个月；逾期未补送的，不予立案，并于七日内书面告知申请机关。

**Article 3** The applying authority shall submit the following materials simultaneously when applying to the People's Court for receiving a sentenced person to return to the mainland for service of sentence:  
(1) The application for receiving the sentenced person prepared by the applying authority, which shall include:  
1. The criminal acts committed by the sentenced person as determined by the Taiwan region court and the specific provisions of the law on which the judgment is based;  
2. A statement that such acts also constitute a crime under the Criminal Law of the mainland, the corresponding provisions of the Criminal Law, the name of the crime, and that the acts have not entered criminal proceedings in the mainland;  
3. The specific penalty recommended for conversion;  
4. Other matters that need to be explained.  
(2) The identity certificate proving that the sentenced person is a resident of the mainland;  
(3) The judgment documents, certification of validity, and enforcement documents of the Taiwan region court convicting and sentencing the sentenced person;  
(4) Written opinion of the sentenced person or his/her legal representative applying for or agreeing to return to the mainland for service of sentence, and the legal representative's expression of intention shall be consistent with that of the sentenced person;  
(5) A written statement made by the sentenced person or his/her legal representative that the right to a fair trial of the sentenced person in the Taiwan region has been guaranteed;  
(6) Written opinions from the competent authorities concerned on both sides of the strait agreeing to the sentenced person's return to the mainland for service of sentence;  
(7) A statement issued by the competent authority of the Taiwan region concerning the status of enforcement of the penalty, including the period of custody prior to the delivery for enforcement, the period of sentence already served, the remaining sentence, the performance of the sentenced person during the service of sentence, the restitution of stolen property or compensation for losses, the health status, disease and treatment of the sentenced person;  
(8) Other materials that need to be submitted according to the specific circumstances of the case.  
If the materials submitted by the applying authority are complete, the People's Court shall file the case within seven days. If the materials are incomplete, it shall notify the applying authority to supplement them within fifteen days, which shall not exceed two months at the latest; if the supplementation is not made within the time limit, the case shall not be filed, and the applying authority shall be notified in writing within seven days.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院应当组成合议庭审理接收被判刑人案件。

**Article 4** A People's Court shall form a collegial panel to hear a case of receiving a sentenced person.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院应当在立案后一个月内就是否准予接收被判刑人作出裁定，情况复杂、特殊的，可以延长一个月。  
人民法院裁定准予接收的，应当依据台湾地区法院判决认定的事实并参考其所定罪名，根据刑法就相同或者最相似犯罪行为规定的法定刑，按照下列原则对台湾地区法院确定的无期徒刑或者有期徒刑予以转换：  
（一）原判处刑罚未超过刑法规定的最高刑，包括原判处刑罚低于刑法规定的最低刑的，以原判处刑罚作为转换后的刑罚；  
（二）原判处刑罚超过刑法规定的最高刑的，以刑法规定的最高刑作为转换后的刑罚；  
（三）转换后的刑罚不附加适用剥夺政治权利。  
前款所称的最高刑，如台湾地区法院认定的事实依据刑法应当认定为一个犯罪的，是指刑法对该犯罪规定的最高刑；如应当认定为多个犯罪的，是指刑法对数罪并罚规定的最高刑。  
对人民法院立案前，台湾地区有关业务主管部门对被判刑人在服刑期间作出的减轻刑罚决定，人民法院应当一并予以转换，并就最终应当执行的刑罚作出裁定。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall render a ruling within one month after filing the case on whether to approve the reception of the sentenced person; if the circumstances are complex or special, the period may be extended by one month.  
If the People's Court rules to approve the reception, it shall, based on the facts determined by the judgment of the Taiwan region court and with reference to the crime determined therein, convert the life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment determined by the Taiwan region court in accordance with the statutory penalties prescribed by the Criminal Law for the same or the most similar criminal act, pursuant to the following principles:  
(1) If the originally imposed penalty does not exceed the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law, including cases where the originally imposed penalty is lower than the minimum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law, the originally imposed penalty shall be taken as the converted penalty;  
(2) If the originally imposed penalty exceeds the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law, the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law shall be taken as the converted penalty;  
(3) The converted penalty shall not be supplemented by deprivation of political rights.  
The maximum penalty referred to in the preceding paragraph means, if the facts determined by the Taiwan region court should be deemed as a single crime under the Criminal Law, the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law for that crime; if they should be deemed as multiple crimes, the maximum penalty prescribed by the Criminal Law for combined punishment for multiple crimes.  
With respect to the decision to reduce the penalty of the sentenced person made by the competent authority of the Taiwan region during the service of sentence before the People's Court files the case, the People's Court shall convert such decision together and render a ruling on the final penalty to be executed.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　被判刑人被接收回大陆服刑前被实际羁押的期间，应当以一日折抵转换后的刑期一日。

**Article 6** The period during which the sentenced person is actually held in custody before being received back to the mainland to serve the sentence shall be offset against the converted term of sentence on a one-day-for-one-day basis.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　被判刑人被接收回大陆前已在台湾地区被假释或保外就医的，或者被判刑人或其法定代理人在申请或者同意回大陆服刑的书面意见中同时申请暂予监外执行的，人民法院应当根据刑法、刑事诉讼法的规定一并审查，并作出是否假释或者暂予监外执行的决定。

**Article 7** If the sentenced person has been paroled or released on medical parole in the Taiwan region before being received back to the mainland, or if the sentenced person or his/her legal representative also applies for temporary service of sentence outside prison in the written opinion applying for or consenting to return to the mainland to serve the sentence, the People's Court shall review the matter together pursuant to the provisions of the Criminal Law and the Criminal Procedure Law, and make a decision on whether to grant parole or temporary service of sentence outside prison.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院作出裁定后，应当在七日内送达申请机关。裁定一经送达，立即生效。

**Article 8** After making a ruling, the People's Court shall serve it on the applying authority within seven days. The ruling shall take effect immediately upon service.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　被判刑人回大陆服刑后，有关减刑、假释、暂予监外执行、赦免等事项，适用刑法、刑事诉讼法及相关司法解释的规定。

**Article 9** After the sentenced person returns to the mainland to serve the sentence, matters such as commutation of punishment, parole, temporary service of sentence outside prison, and pardon shall be governed by the provisions of the Criminal Law, the Criminal Procedure Law, and relevant judicial interpretations.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　被判刑人回大陆服刑后，对其在台湾地区已被判处刑罚的行为，人民法院不再审理。

**Article 10** After the sentenced person returns to the mainland to serve the sentence, the People's Court shall not hear the acts for which the sentenced person has already been sentenced to punishment in the Taiwan region.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本规定自2016年5月1日起施行。

**Article 11** These Provisions shall come into force on May 1, 2016.
