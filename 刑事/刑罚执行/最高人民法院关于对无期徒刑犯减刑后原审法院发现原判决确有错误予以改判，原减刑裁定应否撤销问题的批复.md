### 最高人民法院关于对无期徒刑犯减刑后原审法院发现原判决确有错误予以改判，原减刑裁定应否撤销问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院批复  
  
关于对无期徒刑犯减刑后原审法院发现原判决  
  
确有错误予以改判，原减刑裁定应否撤销问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔 1989〕2号  
  
江西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 88）赣法研二字第 3号请示报告收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
被判处无期徒刑的罪犯由服刑地的高级人民法院依法裁定减刑后，原审人民法院发现原判决确有错误，并按照审判监督程序改判为有期徒刑的，应当将改判的判决书送达罪犯所在的劳改部门和罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院，根据改判的刑期执行，并由罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院裁定撤销原减刑裁定。如果罪犯在原判执行期间确有悔改或者立功表现，还需要依法减刑的，应当依照法定程序另行作出裁定。  
  
1989年1月3日

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court  
Concerning the Issue of Whether the Original Commutation Ruling Shall Be Revoked When the Original Trial Court Finds a Genuine Error in the Original Judgment and Revises It After Commutation of Punishment for a Prisoner Sentenced to Life Imprisonment  
Reply  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1989] No. 2  
Jiangxi Higher People's Court:  
We have received your Request Report No. (88) Gan Fa Yan Er Zi No. 3. Upon deliberation, we reply as follows:  
After a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment has been granted commutation of punishment by a ruling of the Higher People's Court of the place where the sentence is being served according to law, if the original trial court finds that the original judgment indeed contains an error and revises it to a fixed-term imprisonment through the trial supervision procedure, it shall deliver the revised judgment document to the labor reform department where the criminal is located and the Higher People's Court of the place where the criminal is serving the sentence; the sentence shall be executed in accordance with the revised term; and the Higher People's Court of the place where the criminal is serving the sentence shall rule to revoke the original commutation ruling. If the criminal has genuinely demonstrated repentance or meritorious service during the execution of the original sentence and still needs to be granted commutation of punishment according to law, a separate ruling shall be made in accordance with legal procedures.  
January 3, 1989
