### 最高人民法院关于撤销缓刑时罪犯在宣告缓刑前羁押的时间能否折抵刑期问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于撤销缓刑时罪犯在宣告缓刑前羁押的时间能否折抵刑期问题的批复  
  
（2002年4月8日最高人民法院审判委员会第1220次会议通过，自2002年4月18日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2002〕11号  
  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院，解放军军事法院，新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院生产建设兵团分院：  
  
最近，有的法院反映，关于在撤销缓刑时罪犯在宣告缓刑前羁押的时间能否折抵刑期的问题不明确。经研究，批复如下：  
  
根据[刑法第七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-77)的规定，对被宣告缓刑的犯罪分子撤销缓刑执行原判刑罚的，对其在宣告缓刑前羁押的时间应当折抵刑期。  
  
2002年4月10日

**Article 1** Interpretation No. 11 [2002] of the Supreme People's Court  
Higher People's Courts of various provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; the Military Court of the People's Liberation Army; and the Production and Construction Corps Branch of the Higher People's Court of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region:  
Recently, some courts have reported that the issue of whether the period of custody of a criminal before the pronouncement of probation may be offset against the term of imprisonment when the probation is revoked is unclear. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 77 of the Criminal Law, where a criminal who has been sentenced to probation has the probation revoked and the original sentence of punishment is enforced, the period of custody before the pronouncement of probation shall be offset against the term of imprisonment.  
April 10, 2002
