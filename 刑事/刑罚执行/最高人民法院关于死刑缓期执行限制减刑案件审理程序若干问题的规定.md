# 最高人民法院关于死刑缓期执行限制减刑案件审理程序若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕8号  
**公布日期**：2011-04-20  
**生效日期**：2011-05-01

> 最高人民法院《关于死刑缓期执行限制减刑案件审理程序若干问题的规定》已于2011年4月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1519次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年5月1日起施行。 2011年4月25日 最高人民法院 关于死刑缓期执行限制减刑案件审理程序若干问题的规定 法释〔2011〕8号 为正确适用《中华人民共和国刑法修正案（八）》关于死刑缓期执行限制减刑的规定，根据刑事诉讼法的有关规定，结合审判实践，现就相关案件审理程序的若干问题规定如下： 第一条  根据刑法第五十条第二款的规定，对被判处死刑缓期执行的累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪被判处死刑缓期执行的犯罪分子，人民法院根据犯罪情节、人身危险性等情况，可以在作出裁判的同时决定对其限制减刑。 第二条  被告人对第一审人民法院作出的限制减刑判决不服的，可以提出上诉。被告人的辩护人和近亲属，经被告人同意，也可以提出上诉。 第三条  高级人民法院审理或者复核判处死刑缓期执行并限制减刑的案件，认为原判对被告人判处死刑缓期执行适当，但判决限制减刑不当的，应当改判，撤销限制减刑。 第四条  高级人民法院审理判处死刑缓期执行没有限制减刑的上诉案件，认为原判事实清楚、证据充分，但应当限制减刑的，不得直接改判，也不得发回重新审判。确有必要限制减刑的，应当在第二审判决、裁定生效后，按照审判监督程序重新审判。 高级人民法院复核判处死刑缓期执行没有限制减刑的案件，认为应当限制减刑的，不得以提高审级等方式对被告人限制减刑。 第五条  高级人民法院审理判处死刑的第二审案件，对被告人改判死刑缓期执行的，如果符合刑法第五十条第二款的规定，可以同时决定对其限制减刑。 高级人民法院复核判处死刑后没有上诉、抗诉的案件，认为应当改判死刑缓期执行并限制减刑的，可以提审或者发回重新审判。 第六条  最高人民法院复核死刑案件，认为对被告人可以判处死刑缓期执行并限制减刑的，应当裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判。 一案中两名以上被告人被判处死刑，最高人民法院复核后，对其中部分被告人改判死刑缓期执行的，如果符合刑法第五十条第二款的规定，可以同时决定对其限制减刑。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  根据[刑法第五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-50)第二款的规定，对被判处死刑缓期执行的累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪被判处死刑缓期执行的犯罪分子，人民法院根据犯罪情节、人身危险性等情况，可以在作出裁判的同时决定对其限制减刑。

**Article 1** In accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 50 of the Criminal Law, for a recidivist who is sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve, or for a criminal who is sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve for intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, disseminating dangerous substances, or organized violent crimes, the People's Court may, based on the circumstances of the crime and the degree of personal danger, decide at the same time as rendering the judgment to restrict commutation of punishment for such person.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  被告人对第一审人民法院作出的限制减刑判决不服的，可以提出上诉。被告人的辩护人和近亲属，经被告人同意，也可以提出上诉。

**Article 2** If a defendant refuses to accept a judgment rendered by a People's Court of first instance on restricting commutation of punishment, he may file an appeal. The defendant's defender and close relatives may also file an appeal with the defendant's consent.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  高级人民法院审理或者复核判处死刑缓期执行并限制减刑的案件，认为原判对被告人判处死刑缓期执行适当，但判决限制减刑不当的，应当改判，撤销限制减刑。

**Article 3** When the Higher People's Court adjudicates or reviews a case involving a death sentence with a two-year reprieve and restriction on commutation of punishment, and finds that the original judgment imposing a death sentence with a two-year reprieve on the defendant is appropriate but the decision to restrict commutation of punishment is improper, it shall modify the judgment and revoke the restriction on commutation of punishment.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  高级人民法院审理判处死刑缓期执行没有限制减刑的上诉案件，认为原判事实清楚、证据充分，但应当限制减刑的，不得直接改判，也不得发回重新审判。确有必要限制减刑的，应当在第二审判决、裁定生效后，按照审判监督程序重新审判。  
高级人民法院复核判处死刑缓期执行没有限制减刑的案件，认为应当限制减刑的，不得以提高审级等方式对被告人限制减刑。

**Article 4** When the Higher People's Court adjudicates an appealed case involving a death sentence with a two-year reprieve without restriction on commutation of punishment, and finds that the facts of the original judgment are clear and the evidence is sufficient, but that restriction on commutation of punishment shall be imposed, it shall neither directly modify the judgment nor remand the case for retrial. If restriction on commutation of punishment is truly necessary, it shall, after the judgment or ruling of the second instance takes effect, conduct a retrial in accordance with the procedure for adjudication supervision.  
When the Higher People's Court reviews a case involving a death sentence with a two-year reprieve without restriction on commutation of punishment, and finds that restriction on commutation of punishment shall be imposed, it shall not restrict the defendant's commutation of punishment by means such as raising the level of trial.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  高级人民法院审理判处死刑的第二审案件，对被告人改判死刑缓期执行的，如果符合[刑法第五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-50)第二款的规定，可以同时决定对其限制减刑。  
高级人民法院复核判处死刑后没有上诉、抗诉的案件，认为应当改判死刑缓期执行并限制减刑的，可以提审或者发回重新审判。

**Article 5** When the Higher People's Court adjudicates a case of second instance where a death sentence has been imposed and it modifies the defendant's sentence to death with a two-year reprieve, if the conditions set forth in paragraph 2 of Article 50 of the Criminal Law are met, it may simultaneously decide to restrict commutation of punishment for such defendant.  
When the Higher People's Court reviews a case where a death sentence has been imposed and there is no appeal or protest, and it finds that the sentence should be modified to death with a two-year reprieve with restriction on commutation of punishment, it may either bring the case up for trial or remand it for retrial.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  最高人民法院复核死刑案件，认为对被告人可以判处死刑缓期执行并限制减刑的，应当裁定不予核准，并撤销原判，发回重新审判。  
一案中两名以上被告人被判处死刑，最高人民法院复核后，对其中部分被告人改判死刑缓期执行的，如果符合[刑法第五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-50)第二款的规定，可以同时决定对其限制减刑。

**Article 6** When the Supreme People's Court reviews a death penalty case and finds that the defendant may be sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve with restriction on commutation of punishment, it shall rule to disapprove the death sentence, vacate the original judgment, and remand the case for retrial.  
Where two or more defendants in a single case are sentenced to death, and after review by the Supreme People's Court, some of them are given modified sentences of death with a two-year reprieve, if the conditions set forth in paragraph 2 of Article 50 of the Criminal Law are met, the Court may simultaneously decide to restrict commutation of punishment for such defendants.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  人民法院对被判处死刑缓期执行的被告人所作的限制减刑决定，应当在判决书主文部分单独作为一项予以宣告。

**Article 7** The People's Court's decision to restrict commutation of punishment for a defendant sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve shall be declared as a separate item in the main body of the judgment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  死刑缓期执行限制减刑案件审理程序的其他事项，依照刑事诉讼法和有关司法解释的规定执行。

**Article 8** Other matters concerning the adjudication procedure for cases involving death sentence with a two-year reprieve and restriction on commutation of punishment shall be executed in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law and relevant judicial interpretations.
