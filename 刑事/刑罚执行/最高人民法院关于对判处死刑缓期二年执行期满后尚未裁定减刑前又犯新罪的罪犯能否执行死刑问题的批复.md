### 最高人民法院关于对判处死刑缓期二年执行期满后尚未裁定减刑前又犯新罪的罪犯能否执行死刑问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对判处死刑缓期二年执行期满后尚未裁定减刑前  
  
又犯新罪的罪犯能否执行死刑问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1987〕15号  
  
山东省、新疆维吾尔自治区高级人民法院：  
  
你们请示的“关于对判处死刑缓期二年执行期满后，尚未裁定减刑前又犯新罪的罪犯能否执行死刑问题”，经我们研究，同意你们的意见，即：依照[刑法第四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-43)、第四十七条的规定，死刑缓期执行的期间，应自判决确定之日起计算，二年期满。二年缓期执行期间又犯新罪的，当然应视为是在死刑缓期执行期间犯罪。二年期满以后，尚未裁定减刑以前又犯新罪的，不能视为是在死刑缓期执行期间犯罪，对这种罪犯，应依照[刑法第四十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-46)、[刑事诉讼法第一百五十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-153)的规定予以减刑，然后对其所犯新罪另行起诉、审判，作出判决，并按照[刑法第六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-66)的规定，决定执行的刑罚。对新罪判处死刑的，才能执行死刑。  
  
对死缓犯的减刑，应严格依法办事。在死刑缓期二年执行期满以后，符合法定减刑条件的，应及时依法减刑。今后应切实抓紧关于死缓期满依法减刑的工作，务必避免二年期满后，迟迟不依法裁定减刑的情况发生。  
  
1987年5月12日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether a Criminal Sentenced to Death with a Two-Year Reprieve Who Commits a New Crime After the Expiration of the Two-Year Reprieve Period but Before a Ruling on Commutation of Punishment May Be Executed  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1987] No. 15  
The Higher People's Courts of Shandong Province and Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region:  
With respect to the issue you have submitted for instructions, i.e., "whether a criminal sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve who commits a new crime after the expiration of the two-year reprieve period but before a ruling on commutation of punishment may be executed," upon our study, we agree with your opinion, namely: In accordance with the provisions of Articles 43 and 47 of the Criminal Law, the period of a death sentence with a reprieve shall be calculated from the date on which the judgment becomes final, and the two-year period shall expire. If a new crime is committed during the two-year reprieve period, it shall certainly be deemed a crime committed during the period of the death sentence with a reprieve. If a new crime is committed after the expiration of the two-year period but before a ruling on commutation of punishment, it shall not be deemed a crime committed during the period of the death sentence with a reprieve. For such a criminal, commutation of punishment shall be granted in accordance with the provisions of Article 46 of the Criminal Law and Article 153 of the Criminal Procedure Law, and then a separate prosecution and adjudication of the new crime shall be conducted, a judgment shall be rendered, and the penalty to be executed shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Article 66 of the Criminal Law. Only if the death penalty is imposed for the new crime may the death penalty be executed.  
The commutation of punishment for a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve shall be handled strictly in accordance with law. After the expiration of the two-year reprieve period of the death sentence, if the statutory conditions for commutation of punishment are met, commutation shall be made promptly according to law. From now on, the work of legally commuting punishment upon the expiration of the death sentence with a reprieve shall be effectively strengthened, and the situation where the two-year period expires and the ruling on commutation of punishment is delayed without legal basis shall be avoided.  
May 12, 1987
