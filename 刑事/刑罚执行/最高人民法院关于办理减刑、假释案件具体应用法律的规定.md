# 最高人民法院关于办理减刑、假释案件具体应用法律的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕23号  
**公布日期**：2016-09-19  
**生效日期**：2017-01-01

> 最高人民法院《关于办理减刑、假释案件具体应用法律的规定》已于2016年9月19日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1693次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年1月1日起施行。 2016年11月14日 最高人民法院 关于办理减刑、假释案件具体应用法律的规定 法释〔2016〕23号 为确保依法公正办理减刑、假释案件，依据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国监狱法》和其他法律规定，结合司法实践，制定本规定。 第一条  减刑、假释是激励罪犯改造的刑罚制度，减刑、假释的适用应当贯彻宽严相济刑事政策，最大限度地发挥刑罚的功能，实现刑罚的目的。 第二条  对于罪犯符合刑法第七十八条第一款规定“可以减刑”条件的案件，在办理时应当综合考察罪犯犯罪的性质和具体情节、社会危害程度、原判刑罚及生效裁判中财产性判项的履行情况、交付执行后的一贯表现等因素。 第三条  “确有悔改表现”是指同时具备以下条件： （一）认罪悔罪； （二）遵守法律法规及监规，接受教育改造； （三）积极参加思想、文化、职业技术教育； （四）积极参加劳动，努力完成劳动任务。 对职务犯罪、破坏金融管理秩序和金融诈骗犯罪、组织（领导、参加、包庇、纵容）黑社会性质组织犯罪等罪犯，不积极退赃、协助追缴赃款赃物、赔偿损失，或者服刑期间利用个人影响力和社会关系等不正当手段意图获得减刑、假释的，不认定其“确有悔改表现”。 罪犯在刑罚执行期间的申诉权利应当依法保护，对其正当申诉不能不加分析地认为是不认罪悔罪。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  减刑、假释是激励罪犯改造的刑罚制度，减刑、假释的适用应当贯彻宽严相济刑事政策，最大限度地发挥刑罚的功能，实现刑罚的目的。

**Article 1** Commutation of punishment and parole are penal systems that encourage the reform of criminals. The application of commutation of punishment and parole shall implement the criminal policy of combining leniency with severity, maximize the function of penalty, and achieve the purpose of penalty.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  对于罪犯符合[刑法第七十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-78)第一款规定“可以减刑”条件的案件，在办理时应当综合考察罪犯犯罪的性质和具体情节、社会危害程度、原判刑罚及生效裁判中财产性判项的履行情况、交付执行后的一贯表现等因素。

**Article 2** For a case where a criminal meets the conditions for "may be granted commutation of punishment" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 78 of the Criminal Law, when handling the case, the nature and specific circumstances of the crime, the degree of social harm, the original sentence, the performance of property-related judgments in the effective judgment, and the consistent behavior after being delivered for execution, among other factors, shall be comprehensively examined.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  “确有悔改表现”是指同时具备以下条件：  
（一）认罪悔罪；  
（二）遵守法律法规及监规，接受教育改造；  
（三）积极参加思想、文化、职业技术教育；  
（四）积极参加劳动，努力完成劳动任务。  
对职务犯罪、破坏金融管理秩序和金融诈骗犯罪、组织（领导、参加、包庇、纵容）黑社会性质组织犯罪等罪犯，不积极退赃、协助追缴赃款赃物、赔偿损失，或者服刑期间利用个人影响力和社会关系等不正当手段意图获得减刑、假释的，不认定其“确有悔改表现”。  
罪犯在刑罚执行期间的申诉权利应当依法保护，对其正当申诉不能不加分析地认为是不认罪悔罪。

**Article 3** "Truly showing repentance and reform" means simultaneously meeting the following conditions:  
(1) admitting guilt and showing repentance;  
(2) abiding by laws, regulations, and prison rules, and accepting educational reform;  
(3) actively participating in ideological, cultural, and vocational technical education;  
(4) actively participating in labor and striving to complete labor tasks.  
For criminals who commit duty crimes, crimes undermining the order of financial management and financial fraud, crimes of organizing (leading, participating in, harboring, or conniving at) organizations of a mafia nature, if they do not actively return illicit gains, assist in recovering stolen money and goods, compensate for losses, or during the period of serving their sentence, use improper means such as personal influence and social relations to seek commutation of punishment or parole, they shall not be deemed to have "truly shown repentance and reform."  
The right of a criminal to file a petition during the execution of a criminal penalty shall be protected according to law, and a legitimate petition shall not be summarily considered as a failure to admit guilt and show repentance.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为有“立功表现”：  
（一）阻止他人实施犯罪活动的；  
（二）检举、揭发监狱内外犯罪活动，或者提供重要的破案线索，经查证属实的；  
（三）协助司法机关抓捕其他犯罪嫌疑人的；  
（四）在生产、科研中进行技术革新，成绩突出的；  
（五）在抗御自然灾害或者排除重大事故中，表现积极的；  
（六）对国家和社会有其他较大贡献的。  
第（四）项、第（六）项中的技术革新或者其他较大贡献应当由罪犯在刑罚执行期间独立或者为主完成，并经省级主管部门确认。

**Article 4** If one of the following circumstances exists, it may be deemed as having "meritorious performance":  
(1) preventing another person from committing a criminal activity;  
(2) reporting or exposing criminal activities inside or outside prison, or providing important leads for solving a case, which have been verified as true;  
(3) assisting judicial authorities in arresting other criminal suspects;  
(4) making technological innovations in production or scientific research with outstanding results;  
(5) performing actively in resisting natural disasters or eliminating major accidents;  
(6) making other relatively significant contributions to the state and society.  
The technological innovations or other relatively significant contributions in subparagraphs (4) and (6) shall be independently or predominantly completed by the criminal during the execution of the criminal penalty and shall be confirmed by the provincial competent department.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为有“重大立功表现”：  
（一）阻止他人实施重大犯罪活动的；  
（二）检举监狱内外重大犯罪活动，经查证属实的；  
（三）协助司法机关抓捕其他重大犯罪嫌疑人的；  
（四）有发明创造或者重大技术革新的；  
（五）在日常生产、生活中舍己救人的；  
（六）在抗御自然灾害或者排除重大事故中，有突出表现的；  
（七）对国家和社会有其他重大贡献的。  
第（四）项中的发明创造或者重大技术革新应当是罪犯在刑罚执行期间独立或者为主完成并经国家主管部门确认的发明专利，且不包括实用新型专利和外观设计专利；第（七）项中的其他重大贡献应当由罪犯在刑罚执行期间独立或者为主完成，并经国家主管部门确认。

**Article 5** If one of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as having "major meritorious performance":  
(1) preventing another person from committing a major criminal activity;  
(2) reporting or exposing major criminal activities inside or outside prison, which have been verified as true;  
(3) assisting judicial authorities in arresting other major criminal suspects;  
(4) making inventions or major technological innovations;  
(5) sacrificing oneself to save others in daily production or life;  
(6) performing outstandingly in resisting natural disasters or eliminating major accidents;  
(7) making other major contributions to the state and society.  
The inventions or major technological innovations in subparagraph (4) shall be invention patents independently or predominantly completed by the criminal during the execution of the criminal penalty and confirmed by the national competent department, and shall not include utility model patents or design patents; the other major contributions in subparagraph (7) shall be independently or predominantly completed by the criminal during the execution of the criminal penalty and confirmed by the national competent department.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  被判处有期徒刑的罪犯减刑起始时间为：不满五年有期徒刑的，应当执行一年以上方可减刑；五年以上不满十年有期徒刑的，应当执行一年六个月以上方可减刑；十年以上有期徒刑的，应当执行二年以上方可减刑。有期徒刑减刑的起始时间自判决执行之日起计算。  
确有悔改表现或者有立功表现的，一次减刑不超过九个月有期徒刑；确有悔改表现并有立功表现的，一次减刑不超过一年有期徒刑；有重大立功表现的，一次减刑不超过一年六个月有期徒刑；确有悔改表现并有重大立功表现的，一次减刑不超过二年有期徒刑。  
被判处不满十年有期徒刑的罪犯，两次减刑间隔时间不得少于一年；被判处十年以上有期徒刑的罪犯，两次减刑间隔时间不得少于一年六个月。减刑间隔时间不得低于上次减刑减去的刑期。  
罪犯有重大立功表现的，可以不受上述减刑起始时间和间隔时间的限制。

**Article 6** The starting time for commutation of punishment for a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment is as follows: if the fixed-term imprisonment is less than five years, at least one year shall have been served before commutation may be granted; if the fixed-term imprisonment is five years or more but less than ten years, at least one year and six months shall have been served; if the fixed-term imprisonment is ten years or more, at least two years shall have been served. The starting time for commutation of punishment of fixed-term imprisonment shall be calculated from the date on which the sentence begins to be executed.  
If the criminal truly shows repentance and reform or has meritorious performance, one-time commutation shall not exceed nine months of fixed-term imprisonment; if the criminal truly shows repentance and reform and has meritorious performance, one-time commutation shall not exceed one year of fixed-term imprisonment; if the criminal has major meritorious performance, one-time commutation shall not exceed one year and six months of fixed-term imprisonment; if the criminal truly shows repentance and reform and has major meritorious performance, one-time commutation shall not exceed two years of fixed-term imprisonment.  
For a criminal sentenced to less than ten years of fixed-term imprisonment, the interval between two commutations shall not be less than one year; for a criminal sentenced to ten years or more of fixed-term imprisonment, the interval between two commutations shall not be less than one year and six months. The interval between commutations shall not be less than the term of sentence commuted in the previous commutation.  
If a criminal has major meritorious performance, the above restrictions on the starting time and interval of commutation may not apply.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  对符合减刑条件的职务犯罪罪犯，破坏金融管理秩序和金融诈骗犯罪罪犯，组织、领导、参加、包庇、纵容黑社会性质组织犯罪罪犯，危害国家安全犯罪罪犯，恐怖活动犯罪罪犯，毒品犯罪集团的首要分子及毒品再犯，累犯，确有履行能力而不履行或者不全部履行生效裁判中财产性判项的罪犯，被判处十年以下有期徒刑的，执行二年以上方可减刑，减刑幅度应当比照[本规定第六条](#art-6)从严掌握，一次减刑不超过一年有期徒刑，两次减刑之间应当间隔一年以上。  
对被判处十年以上有期徒刑的前款罪犯，以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪被判处十年以上有期徒刑的罪犯，数罪并罚且其中两罪以上被判处十年以上有期徒刑的罪犯，执行二年以上方可减刑，减刑幅度应当比照[本规定第六条](#art-6)从严掌握，一次减刑不超过一年有期徒刑，两次减刑之间应当间隔一年六个月以上。  
罪犯有重大立功表现的，可以不受上述减刑起始时间和间隔时间的限制。

**Article 7** For a criminal who meets the conditions for commutation of punishment and is a criminal guilty of duty crimes, crimes undermining the order of financial management and financial fraud, crimes of organizing, leading, participating in, harboring, or conniving at organizations of a mafia nature, crimes endangering national security, terrorist activities, principal criminals of drug crime groups and drug recidivists, recidivists, or criminals who have the ability to perform but fail to perform or fully perform the property-related judgments in effective judgments, if sentenced to less than ten years of fixed-term imprisonment, at least two years shall have been served before commutation may be granted. The extent of commutation shall be strictly controlled by reference to Article 6 of these Provisions, with one-time commutation not exceeding one year of fixed-term imprisonment, and the interval between two commutations shall not be less than one year.  
For a criminal mentioned in the preceding paragraph who is sentenced to ten years or more of fixed-term imprisonment, and for a criminal sentenced to ten years or more of fixed-term imprisonment for intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, release of dangerous substances, or organized violent crimes, or for a criminal sentenced to ten years or more of fixed-term imprisonment for combined punishment for multiple crimes where two or more of the crimes are punishable by ten years or more of fixed-term imprisonment, at least two years shall have been served before commutation may be granted. The extent of commutation shall be strictly controlled by reference to Article 6 of these Provisions, with one-time commutation not exceeding one year of fixed-term imprisonment, and the interval between two commutations shall not be less than one year and six months.  
If a criminal has major meritorious performance, the above restrictions on the starting time and interval of commutation may not apply.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  被判处无期徒刑的罪犯在刑罚执行期间，符合减刑条件的，执行二年以上，可以减刑。减刑幅度为：确有悔改表现或者有立功表现的，可以减为二十二年有期徒刑；确有悔改表现并有立功表现的，可以减为二十一年以上二十二年以下有期徒刑；有重大立功表现的，可以减为二十年以上二十一年以下有期徒刑；确有悔改表现并有重大立功表现的，可以减为十九年以上二十年以下有期徒刑。无期徒刑罪犯减为有期徒刑后再减刑时，减刑幅度依照[本规定第六条](#art-6)的规定执行。两次减刑间隔时间不得少于二年。  
罪犯有重大立功表现的，可以不受上述减刑起始时间和间隔时间的限制。

**Article 8** A criminal sentenced to life imprisonment, during the execution of the criminal penalty, if meeting the conditions for commutation of punishment, may be granted commutation after serving at least two years. The extent of commutation is as follows: if the criminal truly shows repentance and reform or has meritorious performance, the sentence may be commuted to 22 years of fixed-term imprisonment; if the criminal truly shows repentance and reform and has meritorious performance, the sentence may be commuted to a term of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 21 years but not more than 22 years; if the criminal has major meritorious performance, the sentence may be commuted to a term of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 20 years but not more than 21 years; if the criminal truly shows repentance and reform and has major meritorious performance, the sentence may be commuted to a term of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 19 years but not more than 20 years. When a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment is further commuted after being commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, the extent of commutation shall be implemented in accordance with Article 6 of these Provisions. The interval between two commutations shall not be less than two years.  
If a criminal has major meritorious performance, the above restrictions on the starting time and interval of commutation may not apply.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  对被判处无期徒刑的职务犯罪罪犯，破坏金融管理秩序和金融诈骗犯罪罪犯，组织、领导、参加、包庇、纵容黑社会性质组织犯罪罪犯，危害国家安全犯罪罪犯，恐怖活动犯罪罪犯，毒品犯罪集团的首要分子及毒品再犯，累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪的罪犯，确有履行能力而不履行或者不全部履行生效裁判中财产性判项的罪犯，数罪并罚被判处无期徒刑的罪犯，符合减刑条件的，执行三年以上方可减刑，减刑幅度应当比照[本规定第八条](#art-8)从严掌握，减刑后的刑期最低不得少于二十年有期徒刑；减为有期徒刑后再减刑时，减刑幅度比照[本规定第六条](#art-6)从严掌握，一次不超过一年有期徒刑，两次减刑之间应当间隔二年以上。  
罪犯有重大立功表现的，可以不受上述减刑起始时间和间隔时间的限制。

**Article 9** For a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment who is a criminal guilty of duty crimes, crimes undermining the order of financial management and financial fraud, crimes of organizing, leading, participating in, harboring, or conniving at organizations of a mafia nature, crimes endangering national security, terrorist activities, principal criminals of drug crime groups and drug recidivists, recidivists, or criminals guilty of intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, release of dangerous substances, or organized violent crimes, or criminals who have the ability to perform but fail to perform or fully perform the property-related judgments in effective judgments, or criminals sentenced to life imprisonment for combined punishment for multiple crimes, if meeting the conditions for commutation of punishment, at least three years shall have been served before commutation may be granted. The extent of commutation shall be strictly controlled by reference to Article 8 of these Provisions, and the term after commutation shall be not less than 20 years of fixed-term imprisonment; when further commutation is granted after commutation to fixed-term imprisonment, the extent of commutation shall be strictly controlled by reference to Article 6 of these Provisions, with one-time commutation not exceeding one year of fixed-term imprisonment, and the interval between two commutations shall not be less than two years.  
If a criminal has major meritorious performance, the above restrictions on the starting time and interval of commutation may not apply.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯减为无期徒刑后，符合减刑条件的，执行三年以上方可减刑。减刑幅度为：确有悔改表现或者有立功表现的，可以减为二十五年有期徒刑；确有悔改表现并有立功表现的，可以减为二十四年以上二十五年以下有期徒刑；有重大立功表现的，可以减为二十三年以上二十四年以下有期徒刑；确有悔改表现并有重大立功表现的，可以减为二十二年以上二十三年以下有期徒刑。  
被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯减为有期徒刑后再减刑时，比照[本规定第八条](#art-8)的规定办理。

**Article 10** After a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve has his sentence commuted to life imprisonment, if he meets the conditions for commutation of punishment, at least three years shall have been served before commutation may be granted. The extent of commutation is as follows: if the criminal truly shows repentance and reform or has meritorious performance, the sentence may be commuted to 25 years of fixed-term imprisonment; if the criminal truly shows repentance and reform and has meritorious performance, the sentence may be commuted to a term of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 24 years but not more than 25 years; if the criminal has major meritorious performance, the sentence may be commuted to a term of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 23 years but not more than 24 years; if the criminal truly shows repentance and reform and has major meritorious performance, the sentence may be commuted to a term of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 22 years but not more than 23 years.  
When a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve is further commuted after being commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, it shall be handled by reference to Article 8 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  对被判处死刑缓期执行的职务犯罪罪犯，破坏金融管理秩序和金融诈骗犯罪罪犯，组织、领导、参加、包庇、纵容黑社会性质组织犯罪罪犯，危害国家安全犯罪罪犯，恐怖活动犯罪罪犯，毒品犯罪集团的首要分子及毒品再犯，累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪的罪犯，确有履行能力而不履行或者不全部履行生效裁判中财产性判项的罪犯，数罪并罚被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯，减为无期徒刑后，符合减刑条件的，执行三年以上方可减刑，一般减为二十五年有期徒刑,有立功表现或者重大立功表现的，可以比照[本规定第十条](#art-10)减为二十三年以上二十五年以下有期徒刑；减为有期徒刑后再减刑时，减刑幅度比照[本规定第六条](#art-6)从严掌握，一次不超过一年有期徒刑，两次减刑之间应当间隔二年以上。

**Article 11** For a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve who is a criminal guilty of duty crimes, crimes undermining the order of financial management and financial fraud, crimes of organizing, leading, participating in, harboring, or conniving at organizations of a mafia nature, crimes endangering national security, terrorist activities, principal criminals of drug crime groups and drug recidivists, recidivists, or criminals guilty of intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, release of dangerous substances, or organized violent crimes, or criminals who have the ability to perform but fail to perform or fully perform the property-related judgments in effective judgments, or criminals sentenced to death with a reprieve for combined punishment for multiple crimes, after the sentence is commuted to life imprisonment, if they meet the conditions for commutation of punishment, at least three years shall have been served before commutation may be granted. Generally, the sentence shall be commuted to 25 years of fixed-term imprisonment; if there is meritorious performance or major meritorious performance, it may be commuted, by reference to Article 10 of these Provisions, to a term of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 23 years but not more than 25 years. When further commutation is granted after commutation to fixed-term imprisonment, the extent of commutation shall be strictly controlled by reference to Article 6 of these Provisions, with one-time commutation not exceeding one year of fixed-term imprisonment, and the interval between two commutations shall not be less than two years.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯经过一次或者几次减刑后，其实际执行的刑期不得少于十五年，死刑缓期执行期间不包括在内。  
死刑缓期执行罪犯在缓期执行期间不服从监管、抗拒改造，尚未构成犯罪的，在减为无期徒刑后再减刑时应当适当从严。

**Article 12** After a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve undergoes one or more commutations, the actual term of the sentence executed shall not be less than 15 years, excluding the period of serving the death sentence with a reprieve.  
If a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve, during the period of reprieve, fails to submit to supervision, resists reform, but has not yet committed a crime, when further commutation is granted after the sentence is commuted to life imprisonment, the conditions shall be appropriately stricter.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  被限制减刑的死刑缓期执行罪犯，减为无期徒刑后，符合减刑条件的，执行五年以上方可减刑。减刑间隔时间和减刑幅度依照[本规定第十一条](#art-11)的规定执行。

**Article 13** For a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve whose commutation of punishment is restricted, after the sentence is commuted to life imprisonment, if he meets the conditions for commutation of punishment, at least five years shall have been served before commutation may be granted. The interval and extent of commutation shall be implemented in accordance with Article 11 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  被限制减刑的死刑缓期执行罪犯，减为有期徒刑后再减刑时，一次减刑不超过六个月有期徒刑，两次减刑间隔时间不得少于二年。有重大立功表现的，间隔时间可以适当缩短，但一次减刑不超过一年有期徒刑。

**Article 14** For a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve whose commutation of punishment is restricted, when further commutation is granted after commutation to fixed-term imprisonment, one-time commutation shall not exceed six months of fixed-term imprisonment, and the interval between two commutations shall not be less than two years. If there is major meritorious performance, the interval may be appropriately shortened, but one-time commutation shall not exceed one year of fixed-term imprisonment.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  对被判处终身监禁的罪犯，在死刑缓期执行期满依法减为无期徒刑的裁定中，应当明确终身监禁，不得再减刑或者假释。

**Article 15** For a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment without parole, in the ruling that the death sentence with a reprieve is commuted to life imprisonment upon expiration of the reprieve period according to law, it shall be clearly specified that the punishment is life imprisonment without parole, and no further commutation of punishment or parole shall be granted.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  被判处管制、拘役的罪犯，以及判决生效后剩余刑期不满二年有期徒刑的罪犯，符合减刑条件的，可以酌情减刑，减刑起始时间可以适当缩短，但实际执行的刑期不得少于原判刑期的二分之一。

**Article 16** For a criminal sentenced to public surveillance or criminal detention, and for a criminal whose remaining term after the judgment takes effect is less than two years of fixed-term imprisonment, if the conditions for commutation of punishment are met, commutation may be granted at the court's discretion, and the starting time for commutation may be appropriately shortened; however, the actual term of the sentence executed shall not be less than one-half of the originally imposed sentence.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  被判处有期徒刑罪犯减刑时，对附加剥夺政治权利的期限可以酌减。酌减后剥夺政治权利的期限，不得少于一年。  
被判处死刑缓期执行、无期徒刑的罪犯减为有期徒刑时，应当将附加剥夺政治权利的期限减为七年以上十年以下，经过一次或者几次减刑后，最终剥夺政治权利的期限不得少于三年。

**Article 17** When a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment is granted commutation of punishment, the term of the supplementary deprivation of political rights may be appropriately reduced. After such reduction, the term of deprivation of political rights shall not be less than one year.  
When a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve or life imprisonment has his sentence commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, the term of the supplementary deprivation of political rights shall be reduced to not less than seven years but not more than ten years. After one or more commutations, the final term of deprivation of political rights shall not be less than three years.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  被判处拘役或者三年以下有期徒刑，并宣告缓刑的罪犯，一般不适用减刑。  
前款规定的罪犯在缓刑考验期内有重大立功表现的，可以参照[刑法第七十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-78)的规定予以减刑，同时应当依法缩减其缓刑考验期。缩减后，拘役的缓刑考验期限不得少于二个月，有期徒刑的缓刑考验期限不得少于一年。

**Article 18** For a criminal sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years and placed on probation, commutation of punishment generally shall not apply.  
If a criminal specified in the preceding paragraph has major meritorious performance during the probation period, commutation of punishment may be granted by reference to Article 78 of the Criminal Law, and at the same time, the probation period shall be shortened according to law. After shortening, the probation period for criminal detention shall not be less than two months, and the probation period for fixed-term imprisonment shall not be less than one year.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  对在报请减刑前的服刑期间不满十八周岁，且所犯罪行不属于[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)第二款规定情形的罪犯，认罪悔罪，遵守法律法规及监规，积极参加学习、劳动，应当视为确有悔改表现。  
对上述罪犯减刑时，减刑幅度可以适当放宽，或者减刑起始时间、间隔时间可以适当缩短，但放宽的幅度和缩短的时间不得超过本规定中相应幅度、时间的三分之一。

**Article 19** For a criminal who, at the time of the request for commutation of punishment, is under the age of 18 and whose crime does not fall under the circumstances specified in paragraph 2 of Article 81 of the Criminal Law, if he admits guilt, shows repentance, abides by laws, regulations, and prison rules, and actively participates in study and labor, he shall be deemed to have truly shown repentance and reform.  
When granting commutation of punishment to the above-mentioned criminal, the extent of commutation may be appropriately lenient, or the starting time and interval of commutation may be appropriately shortened; however, the extent of leniency and the time shortened shall not exceed one-third of the corresponding extent or time provided in these Provisions.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  老年罪犯、患严重疾病罪犯或者身体残疾罪犯减刑时，应当主要考察其认罪悔罪的实际表现。  
对基本丧失劳动能力，生活难以自理的上述罪犯减刑时，减刑幅度可以适当放宽，或者减刑起始时间、间隔时间可以适当缩短，但放宽的幅度和缩短的时间不得超过本规定中相应幅度、时间的三分之一。

**Article 20** When granting commutation of punishment to an elderly criminal, a criminal suffering from a serious disease, or a physically disabled criminal, the actual performance in admitting guilt and showing repentance shall be mainly examined.  
When granting commutation of punishment to the above-mentioned criminal who has basically lost the ability to work and is unable to take care of himself in daily life, the extent of commutation may be appropriately lenient, or the starting time and interval of commutation may be appropriately shortened; however, the extent of leniency and the time shortened shall not exceed one-third of the corresponding extent or time provided in these Provisions.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  被判处有期徒刑、无期徒刑的罪犯在刑罚执行期间又故意犯罪，新罪被判处有期徒刑的，自新罪判决确定之日起三年内不予减刑；新罪被判处无期徒刑的，自新罪判决确定之日起四年内不予减刑。  
罪犯在死刑缓期执行期间又故意犯罪，未被执行死刑的，死刑缓期执行的期间重新计算，减为无期徒刑后，五年内不予减刑。  
被判处死刑缓期执行罪犯减刑后，在刑罚执行期间又故意犯罪的，依照第一款规定处理。

**Article 21** If a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or life imprisonment commits another intentional crime during the execution of the criminal penalty, and the new crime is punishable by fixed-term imprisonment, no commutation of punishment shall be granted within three years from the date on which the judgment for the new crime becomes final; if the new crime is punishable by life imprisonment, no commutation of punishment shall be granted within four years from the date on which the judgment for the new crime becomes final.  
If a criminal commits another intentional crime during the period of serving a death sentence with a reprieve and is not executed, the period of the death sentence with a reprieve shall be recalculated, and after the sentence is commuted to life imprisonment, no commutation of punishment shall be granted within five years.  
If a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve commits another intentional crime during the execution of the criminal penalty after commutation of punishment, it shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  办理假释案件，认定“没有再犯罪的危险”，除符合[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)规定的情形外，还应当根据犯罪的具体情节、原判刑罚情况，在刑罚执行中的一贯表现，罪犯的年龄、身体状况、性格特征，假释后生活来源以及监管条件等因素综合考虑。

**Article 22** When handling a parole case and determining that there is "no risk of committing another crime," in addition to meeting the circumstances specified in Article 81 of the Criminal Law, comprehensive consideration shall be given to factors such as the specific circumstances of the crime, the original sentence, the consistent behavior during the execution of the criminal penalty, the criminal's age, physical condition, personality characteristics, sources of livelihood after parole, and supervision conditions.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  被判处有期徒刑的罪犯假释时，执行原判刑期二分之一的时间，应当从判决执行之日起计算，判决执行以前先行羁押的，羁押一日折抵刑期一日。  
被判处无期徒刑的罪犯假释时，刑法中关于实际执行刑期不得少于十三年的时间，应当从判决生效之日起计算。判决生效以前先行羁押的时间不予折抵。  
被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯减为无期徒刑或者有期徒刑后，实际执行十五年以上，方可假释，该实际执行时间应当从死刑缓期执行期满之日起计算。死刑缓期执行期间不包括在内，判决确定以前先行羁押的时间不予折抵。

**Article 23** When a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment is granted parole, the period of serving one-half of the originally imposed sentence shall be calculated from the date on which the sentence begins to be executed. If there has been pre-judgment custody before the execution of the sentence, one day of such custody shall offset one day of the sentence.  
When a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment is granted parole, the period of actual execution of not less than 13 years as required by the Criminal Law shall be calculated from the date on which the judgment takes effect. The period of pre-judgment custody before the judgment takes effect shall not be offset.  
After a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve has his sentence commuted to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment, parole may be granted only after the actual execution of more than 15 years. The period of actual execution shall be calculated from the date on which the period of serving the death sentence with a reprieve expires. The period of serving the death sentence with a reprieve shall not be included, and the period of pre-judgment custody before the judgment is determined shall not be offset.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  [刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)第一款规定的“特殊情况”，是指有国家政治、国防、外交等方面特殊需要的情况。

**Article 24** The "special circumstances" specified in paragraph 1 of Article 81 of the Criminal Law refer to circumstances where there are special needs in national politics, national defense, diplomacy, or other aspects.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  对累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪被判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑的罪犯，不得假释。  
因前款情形和犯罪被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯，被减为无期徒刑、有期徒刑后，也不得假释。

**Article 25** Parole shall not be granted to a recidivist or a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment for intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, release of dangerous substances, or organized violent crimes.  
A criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve due to the circumstances and crimes specified in the preceding paragraph, after the sentence is commuted to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment, also shall not be granted parole.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  对下列罪犯适用假释时可以依法从宽掌握：  
（一）过失犯罪的罪犯、中止犯罪的罪犯、被胁迫参加犯罪的罪犯；  
（二）因防卫过当或者紧急避险过当而被判处有期徒刑以上刑罚的罪犯；  
（三）犯罪时未满十八周岁的罪犯；  
（四）基本丧失劳动能力、生活难以自理，假释后生活确有着落的老年罪犯、患严重疾病罪犯或者身体残疾罪犯；  
（五）服刑期间改造表现特别突出的罪犯；  
（六）具有其他可以从宽假释情形的罪犯。  
罪犯既符合法定减刑条件，又符合法定假释条件的，可以优先适用假释。

**Article 26** Parole may be applied leniently according to law for the following criminals:  
(1) criminals guilty of negligent crimes, criminals who desist from crime, or criminals who participate in a crime under duress;  
(2) criminals sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or a heavier penalty for excessive self-defense or excessive act of rescue;  
(3) criminals who were under the age of 18 at the time of committing the crime;  
(4) elderly criminals, criminals suffering from a serious disease, or physically disabled criminals who have basically lost the ability to work, are unable to take care of themselves in daily life, and have a definite source of livelihood after parole;  
(5) criminals who have demonstrated particularly outstanding reform performance during the period of serving their sentence;  
(6) criminals who have other circumstances for which parole may be granted leniently.  
Where a criminal meets both the statutory conditions for commutation of punishment and the statutory conditions for parole, parole may be applied preferentially.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  对于生效裁判中有财产性判项，罪犯确有履行能力而不履行或者不全部履行的，不予假释。

**Article 27** For a criminal who has the ability to perform but fails to perform or fully perform the property-related judgments in an effective judgment, parole shall not be granted.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  罪犯减刑后又假释的，间隔时间不得少于一年；对一次减去一年以上有期徒刑后，决定假释的，间隔时间不得少于一年六个月。  
罪犯减刑后余刑不足二年，决定假释的，可以适当缩短间隔时间。

**Article 28** If a criminal is granted parole after commutation of punishment, the interval shall not be less than one year; if, after a one-time commutation of more than one year of fixed-term imprisonment, parole is decided, the interval shall not be less than one year and six months.  
If the remaining term after commutation is less than two years and parole is decided, the interval may be appropriately shortened.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  罪犯在假释考验期内违反法律、行政法规或者国务院有关部门关于假释的监督管理规定的，作出假释裁定的人民法院，应当在收到报请机关或者检察机关撤销假释建议书后及时审查，作出是否撤销假释的裁定，并送达报请机关，同时抄送人民检察院、公安机关和原刑罚执行机关。  
罪犯在逃的，撤销假释裁定书可以作为对罪犯进行追捕的依据。

**Article 29** If a criminal violates laws, administrative regulations, or the provisions on supervision and administration of parole issued by the relevant departments of the State Council during the probation period for parole, the people's court that made the parole ruling shall promptly review the matter upon receiving the proposal for revocation of parole submitted by the reporting authority or the procuratorate, and shall make a ruling on whether to revoke the parole, and shall serve the ruling on the reporting authority, with copies to the People's Procuratorate, the public security organ, and the original penal execution organ.  
If the criminal is at large, the ruling to revoke parole may serve as the basis for pursuing and arresting the criminal.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条  依照[刑法第八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-86)规定被撤销假释的罪犯，一般不得再假释。但依照该条第二款被撤销假释的罪犯，如果罪犯对漏罪曾作如实供述但原判未予认定，或者漏罪系其自首，符合假释条件的，可以再假释。  
被撤销假释的罪犯，收监后符合减刑条件的，可以减刑，但减刑起始时间自收监之日起计算。

**Article 30** A criminal whose parole has been revoked in accordance with Article 86 of the Criminal Law generally shall not be granted parole again. However, if a criminal whose parole has been revoked pursuant to paragraph 2 of that Article has truthfully confessed to a missing crime but the original judgment did not recognize it, or the missing crime is voluntarily surrendered by the criminal, and the conditions for parole are met, parole may be granted again.  
For a criminal whose parole has been revoked, if, after being taken back into custody, he meets the conditions for commutation of punishment, commutation may be granted, but the starting time for commutation shall be calculated from the date of being taken back into custody.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条  年满八十周岁、身患疾病或者生活难以自理、没有再犯罪危险的罪犯，既符合减刑条件，又符合假释条件的，优先适用假释；不符合假释条件的，参照[本规定第二十条](#art-20)有关的规定从宽处理。

**Article 31** For a criminal who has reached the age of 80, suffers from illness, or is unable to take care of himself in daily life, and has no risk of committing another crime, if he meets both the conditions for commutation of punishment and the conditions for parole, parole shall be applied preferentially; if he does not meet the conditions for parole, lenient treatment shall be granted by reference to the relevant provisions of Article 20 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-32"></a>第三十二条  人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审理的案件，裁定维持原判决、裁定的，原减刑、假释裁定继续有效。  
再审裁判改变原判决、裁定的，原减刑、假释裁定自动失效，执行机关应当及时报请有管辖权的人民法院重新作出是否减刑、假释的裁定。重新作出减刑裁定时，不受本规定有关减刑起始时间、间隔时间和减刑幅度的限制。重新裁定时应综合考虑各方面因素，减刑幅度不得超过原裁定减去的刑期总和。  
再审改判为死刑缓期执行或者无期徒刑的，在新判决减为有期徒刑之时，原判决已经实际执行的刑期一并扣减。  
再审裁判宣告无罪的，原减刑、假释裁定自动失效。

**Article 32** If a people's court, when retrying a case in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, rules to affirm the original judgment or ruling, the original rulings on commutation of punishment or parole shall remain valid.  
If the retrial judgment changes the original judgment or ruling, the original rulings on commutation of punishment or parole shall automatically cease to have effect, and the executing organ shall promptly request a people's court with jurisdiction to make a new ruling on whether to grant commutation of punishment or parole. When making a new commutation ruling, it shall not be bound by the provisions of these Provisions regarding the starting time, interval, and extent of commutation. When making a new ruling, comprehensive consideration shall be given to various factors, and the extent of commutation shall not exceed the total term of sentence commuted in the original ruling.  
If the retrial judgment changes the sentence to death with a reprieve or life imprisonment, at the time when the new sentence is commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, the term of the original sentence that has already been actually executed shall be deducted together.  
If the retrial judgment declares the criminal not guilty, the original rulings on commutation of punishment or parole shall automatically cease to have effect.

<a id="art-33"></a>第三十三条  罪犯被裁定减刑后，刑罚执行期间因故意犯罪而数罪并罚时，经减刑裁定减去的刑期不计入已经执行的刑期。原判死刑缓期执行减为无期徒刑、有期徒刑，或者无期徒刑减为有期徒刑的裁定继续有效。

**Article 33** After a criminal has been ruled to have his punishment commuted, if, during the execution of the criminal penalty, he commits an intentional crime and is subject to combined punishment for multiple crimes, the term of sentence commuted through the commutation ruling shall not be included in the term already executed. The ruling that the original death sentence with a reprieve is commuted to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment, or that life imprisonment is commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, shall remain valid.

<a id="art-34"></a>第三十四条  罪犯被裁定减刑后，刑罚执行期间因发现漏罪而数罪并罚的，原减刑裁定自动失效。如漏罪系罪犯主动交代的，对其原减去的刑期，由执行机关报请有管辖权的人民法院重新作出减刑裁定，予以确认；如漏罪系有关机关发现或者他人检举揭发的，由执行机关报请有管辖权的人民法院，在原减刑裁定减去的刑期总和之内，酌情重新裁定。

**Article 34** After a criminal has been ruled to have his punishment commuted, if, during the execution of the criminal penalty, a missing crime is discovered and he is subject to combined punishment for multiple crimes, the original commutation ruling shall automatically cease to have effect. If the missing crime was voluntarily confessed by the criminal, the executing organ shall request a people's court with jurisdiction to make a new commutation ruling to confirm the term originally commuted. If the missing crime was discovered by a relevant authority or reported by another person, the executing organ shall request a people's court with jurisdiction to make a new ruling at its discretion within the total term of sentence commuted in the original commutation ruling.

<a id="art-35"></a>第三十五条  被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯，在死刑缓期执行期内被发现漏罪，依据[刑法第七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-70)规定数罪并罚，决定执行死刑缓期执行的，死刑缓期执行期间自新判决确定之日起计算，已经执行的死刑缓期执行期间计入新判决的死刑缓期执行期间内，但漏罪被判处死刑缓期执行的除外。

**Article 35** If, during the period of serving a death sentence with a reprieve, a missing crime is discovered, and the criminal is subject to combined punishment for multiple crimes in accordance with Article 70 of the Criminal Law, and it is decided to execute a death sentence with a reprieve, the period of serving the death sentence with a reprieve shall be calculated from the date on which the new judgment becomes final, and the period of the death sentence with a reprieve already served shall be included in the period of the death sentence with a reprieve under the new judgment, unless the missing crime is also punishable by death with a reprieve.

<a id="art-36"></a>第三十六条　被  判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯，在死刑缓期执行期满后被发现漏罪，依据[刑法第七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-70)规定数罪并罚，决定执行死刑缓期执行的，交付执行时对罪犯实际执行无期徒刑，死缓考验期不再执行，但漏罪被判处死刑缓期执行的除外。  
在无期徒刑减为有期徒刑时，前罪死刑缓期执行减为无期徒刑之日起至新判决生效之日止已经实际执行的刑期，应当计算在减刑裁定决定执行的刑期以内。  
原减刑裁定减去的刑期依照[本规定第三十四条](#art-34)处理。

**Article 36** : Where a criminal who has been sentenced to death with a two-year reprieve is found, after the expiration of the reprieve period, to have committed an undiscovered crime, and where, pursuant to Article 70 of the Criminal Law, concurrent punishment for multiple crimes is imposed and a death sentence with a two-year reprieve is decided to be executed, the criminal shall, at the time of delivery for execution, actually undergo life imprisonment, and the reprieve period for the death sentence with a two-year reprieve shall no longer be executed, except where the undiscovered crime is itself punishable by death with a two-year reprieve.  
When life imprisonment is commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, the term of sentence that has actually been served from the date on which the death sentence with a two-year reprieve for the previous crime was commuted to life imprisonment until the date on which the new judgment takes effect shall be counted within the term of sentence decided to be executed by the commutation ruling.  
The term of sentence deducted by the original commutation ruling shall be handled in accordance with Article 34 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-37"></a>第三十七条  被判处无期徒刑的罪犯在减为有期徒刑后因发现漏罪，依据[刑法第七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-70)规定数罪并罚，决定执行无期徒刑的，前罪无期徒刑生效之日起至新判决生效之日止已经实际执行的刑期，应当在新判决的无期徒刑减为有期徒刑时，在减刑裁定决定执行的刑期内扣减。  
无期徒刑罪犯减为有期徒刑后因发现漏罪判处三年有期徒刑以下刑罚，数罪并罚决定执行无期徒刑的，在新判决生效后执行一年以上，符合减刑条件的，可以减为有期徒刑，减刑幅度依照[本规定第八条](#art-8)、第九条的规定执行。  
原减刑裁定减去的刑期依照[本规定第三十四条](#art-34)处理。

**Article 37** : Where a criminal who has been sentenced to life imprisonment, after having been commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, is found to have committed an undiscovered crime, and where, pursuant to Article 70 of the Criminal Law, concurrent punishment for multiple crimes is imposed and life imprisonment is decided to be executed, the term of sentence that has actually been served from the date on which the life imprisonment for the previous crime took effect until the date on which the new judgment takes effect shall, when the life imprisonment under the new judgment is commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, be deducted from the term of sentence decided to be executed by the commutation ruling.  
Where a criminal serving life imprisonment, after having been commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, is found to have committed an undiscovered crime and is sentenced to a punishment of less than three years of fixed-term imprisonment, and concurrent punishment for multiple crimes results in a decision to execute life imprisonment, if, after the new judgment takes effect, the criminal has served more than one year and meets the conditions for commutation, the sentence may be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, and the extent of commutation shall be executed in accordance with Articles 8 and 9 of these Provisions.  
The term of sentence deducted by the original commutation ruling shall be handled in accordance with Article 34 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-38"></a>第三十八条  人民法院作出的刑事判决、裁定发生法律效力后，在依照[刑事诉讼法第二百五十三条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-253)、第二百五十四条的规定将罪犯交付执行刑罚时，如果生效裁判中有财产性判项，人民法院应当将反映财产性判项执行、履行情况的有关材料一并随案移送刑罚执行机关。罪犯在服刑期间本人履行或者其亲属代为履行生效裁判中财产性判项的，应当及时向刑罚执行机关报告。刑罚执行机关报请减刑时应随案移送以上材料。  
人民法院办理减刑、假释案件时，可以向原一审人民法院核实罪犯履行财产性判项的情况。原一审人民法院应当出具相关证明。  
刑罚执行期间，负责办理减刑、假释案件的人民法院可以协助原一审人民法院执行生效裁判中的财产性判项。

**Article 38** : After a criminal judgment or ruling rendered by a People's Court has taken legal effect, when the criminal is delivered for execution of punishment in accordance with Articles 253 and 254 of the Criminal Procedure Law, if the effective judgment or ruling contains property-related sentencing items, the People's Court shall transfer the relevant materials reflecting the execution and performance of the property-related sentencing items together with the case file to the penal enforcement organ. If, during the service of sentence, the criminal personally performs or his or her relatives perform on his or her behalf the property-related sentencing items in the effective judgment or ruling, they shall report to the penal enforcement organ in a timely manner. When the penal enforcement organ submits a request for commutation of punishment, it shall transfer the above materials together with the case file.  
When a People's Court handles a commutation or parole case, it may verify with the original court of first instance the situation regarding the criminal's performance of the property-related sentencing items. The original court of first instance shall issue relevant certifications.  
During the period of penal enforcement, the People's Court responsible for handling the commutation or parole case may assist the original court of first instance in executing the property-related sentencing items in the effective judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-39"></a>第三十九条  本规定所称“老年罪犯”，是指报请减刑、假释时年满六十五周岁的罪犯。  
本规定所称“患严重疾病罪犯”，是指因患有重病，久治不愈，而不能正常生活、学习、劳动的罪犯。  
本规定所称“身体残疾罪犯”，是指因身体有肢体或者器官残缺、功能不全或者丧失功能，而基本丧失生活、学习、劳动能力的罪犯，但是罪犯犯罪后自伤致残的除外。  
对刑罚执行机关提供的证明罪犯患有严重疾病或者有身体残疾的证明文件，人民法院应当审查，必要时可以委托有关单位重新诊断、鉴定。

**Article 39** : For the purposes of these Provisions, an "elderly criminal" means a criminal who has reached the age of sixty-five at the time of the request for commutation or parole.  
For the purposes of these Provisions, a "criminal suffering from a serious disease" means a criminal who, due to suffering from a severe illness that has not been cured after prolonged treatment, is unable to live, study, or work normally.  
For the purposes of these Provisions, a "physically disabled criminal" means a criminal who, due to a physical impairment such as loss or dysfunction of limbs or organs, has essentially lost the ability to live, study, or work, except where the criminal intentionally caused self-harm after committing the crime.  
The People's Court shall examine the documentary evidence provided by the penal enforcement organ that proves the criminal suffers from a serious disease or physical disability, and where necessary, may entrust a relevant unit to conduct a new diagnosis or expert evaluation.

<a id="art-40"></a>第四十条  本规定所称“判决执行之日”，是指罪犯实际送交刑罚执行机关之日。  
本规定所称“减刑间隔时间”，是指前一次减刑裁定送达之日起至本次减刑报请之日止的期间。

**Article 40** : For the purposes of these Provisions, the "date of execution of the judgment" means the date on which the criminal is actually delivered to the penal enforcement organ.  
For the purposes of these Provisions, the "interval between commutations" means the period from the date on which the previous commutation ruling is served to the date on which the current commutation is requested.

<a id="art-41"></a>第四十一条  本规定所称“财产性判项”是指判决罪犯承担的附带民事赔偿义务判项，以及追缴、责令退赔、罚金、没收财产等判项。

**Article 41** : For the purposes of these Provisions, "property-related sentencing items" means the sentencing items in the judgment requiring the criminal to bear incidental civil compensation obligations, as well as sentencing items such as recovery of illegal proceeds, an order for restitution, a fine, and confiscation of property.

<a id="art-42"></a>第四十二条  本规定自2017年1月1日起施行。以前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 42** : These Provisions shall come into force on January 1, 2017. Where any previously issued judicial interpretations are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
