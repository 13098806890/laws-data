# 人民检察院办理减刑、假释案件规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2014-07-21

> （2014年7月21日最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第二十五次会议通过） 第一条　为了进一步加强和规范减刑、假释法律监督工作，确保刑罚变更执行合法、公正，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》和《中华人民共和国监狱法》等有关规定，结合检察工作实际，制定本规定。 第二条　人民检察院依法对减刑、假释案件的提请、审理、裁定等活动是否合法实行法律监督。 第三条　人民检察院办理减刑、假释案件，应当按照下列情形分别处理： （一）对减刑、假释案件提请活动的监督，由对执行机关承担检察职责的人民检察院负责； （二）对减刑、假释案件审理、裁定活动的监督，由人民法院的同级人民检察院负责；同级人民检察院对执行机关不承担检察职责的，可以根据需要指定对执行机关承担检察职责的人民检察院派员出席法庭；下级人民检察院发现减刑、假释裁定不当的，应当及时向作出减刑、假释裁定的人民法院的同级人民检察院报告。 第四条　人民检察院办理减刑、假释案件，依照规定实行统一案件管理和办案责任制。 第五条　人民检察院收到执行机关移送的下列减刑、假释案件材料后，应当及时进行审查： （一）执行机关拟提请减刑、假释意见； （二）终审法院裁判文书、执行通知书、历次减刑裁定书； （三）罪犯确有悔改表现、立功表现或者重大立功表现的证明材料； （四）罪犯评审鉴定表、奖惩审批表 （五）其他应当审查的案件材料。 对拟提请假释案件，还应当审查社区矫正机构或者基层组织关于罪犯假释后对所居住社区影响的调查评估报告。 第六条　具有下列情形之一的，人民检察院应当进行调查核实：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为了进一步加强和规范减刑、假释法律监督工作，确保刑罚变更执行合法、公正，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》和《中华人民共和国监狱法》等有关规定，结合检察工作实际，制定本规定。

**Article 1** These Provisions are formulated in accordance with the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the Prison Law of the People's Republic of China and other relevant provisions, and in light of the actual work of the procuratorial organs, for the purpose of further strengthening and regulating the legal supervision over commutation of punishment and parole, and ensuring the legality and fairness of the execution of modified sentences.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民检察院依法对减刑、假释案件的提请、审理、裁定等活动是否合法实行法律监督。

**Article 2** A People's Procuratorate shall, according to law, exercise legal supervision over whether the submission, adjudication, ruling and other activities in cases of commutation of punishment and parole are lawful.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民检察院办理减刑、假释案件，应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）对减刑、假释案件提请活动的监督，由对执行机关承担检察职责的人民检察院负责；  
（二）对减刑、假释案件审理、裁定活动的监督，由人民法院的同级人民检察院负责；同级人民检察院对执行机关不承担检察职责的，可以根据需要指定对执行机关承担检察职责的人民检察院派员出席法庭；下级人民检察院发现减刑、假释裁定不当的，应当及时向作出减刑、假释裁定的人民法院的同级人民检察院报告。

**Article 3** A People's Procuratorate handling a case of commutation of punishment or parole shall handle it according to the following circumstances:  
(1) The supervision over the submission activities of a case of commutation of punishment or parole shall be the responsibility of the People's Procuratorate that performs the procuratorial duties for the executing organ;  
(2) The supervision over the adjudication and ruling activities of a case of commutation of punishment or parole shall be the responsibility of the People's Procuratorate at the same level as the People's Court. If the People's Procuratorate at the same level does not perform the procuratorial duties for the executing organ, it may, as needed, designate the People's Procuratorate that performs the procuratorial duties for the executing organ to send personnel to appear in court. If a People's Procuratorate at a lower level discovers that a ruling on commutation of punishment or parole is improper, it shall promptly report to the People's Procuratorate at the same level as the People's Court that made the ruling on commutation of punishment or parole.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民检察院办理减刑、假释案件，依照规定实行统一案件管理和办案责任制。

**Article 4** A People's Procuratorate handling a case of commutation of punishment or parole shall implement unified case management and case-handling responsibility system in accordance with the provisions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民检察院收到执行机关移送的下列减刑、假释案件材料后，应当及时进行审查：  
（一）执行机关拟提请减刑、假释意见；  
（二）终审法院裁判文书、执行通知书、历次减刑裁定书；  
（三）罪犯确有悔改表现、立功表现或者重大立功表现的证明材料；  
（四）罪犯评审鉴定表、奖惩审批表  
（五）其他应当审查的案件材料。  
对拟提请假释案件，还应当审查社区矫正机构或者基层组织关于罪犯假释后对所居住社区影响的调查评估报告。

**Article 5** After receiving the following case materials for commutation of punishment or parole transferred by the executing organ, the People's Procuratorate shall promptly conduct a review:  
(1) The opinions of the executing organ on the proposed submission of commutation of punishment or parole;  
(2) The written judgment of the court of final instance, enforcement notice, and all previous written rulings on commutation of punishment;  
(3) The evidentiary materials showing that the criminal has demonstrated genuine repentance, meritorious service, or major meritorious service;  
(4) The criminal's evaluation and appraisal form, and approval form for rewards and punishments;  
(5) Other case materials that shall be reviewed.  
For cases of proposed submission of parole, the investigation and assessment report issued by the community correction institution or grass-roots organization on the impact of the criminal's parole on the community where he or she shall reside shall also be reviewed.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　具有下列情形之一的，人民检察院应当进行调查核实：  
（一）拟提请减刑、假释罪犯系职务犯罪罪犯，破坏金融管理秩序和金融诈骗犯罪罪犯，黑社会性质组织犯罪罪犯，严重暴力恐怖犯罪罪犯，或者其他在社会上有重大影响、社会关注度高的罪犯；  
（二）因罪犯有立功表现或者重大立功表现拟提请减刑的；  
（三）拟提请减刑、假释罪犯的减刑幅度大、假释考验期长、起始时间早、间隔时间短或者实际执行刑期短的；  
（四）拟提请减刑、假释罪犯的考核计分高、专项奖励多或者鉴定材料、奖惩记录有疑点的；  
（五）收到控告、举报的；  
（六）其他应当进行调查核实的。

**Article 6** The People's Procuratorate shall conduct investigation and verification under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) The criminal for whom commutation of punishment or parole is proposed is a criminal who committed a duty crime, a criminal who committed a crime of undermining the order of financial management or a financial fraud crime, a criminal who committed a crime by an organization of a triad nature, a criminal who committed a serious violent terrorist crime, or other criminal who has a major social impact or attracts high public attention;  
(2) Commutation of punishment is proposed because the criminal has demonstrated meritorious service or major meritorious service;  
(3) The criminal for whom commutation of punishment or parole is proposed is to receive a large reduction in sentence, to serve a long parole probation period, to receive the reduction early, to have a short interval between reductions, or to have a short actual sentence served;  
(4) The criminal for whom commutation of punishment or parole is proposed has a high assessment score, receives many special rewards, or there are doubts about the evaluation materials or reward and punishment records;  
(5) Complaints or reports are received;  
(6) Other circumstances that shall be investigated and verified.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民检察院可以采取调阅复制有关材料、重新组织诊断鉴别、进行文证鉴定、召开座谈会、个别询问等方式，对下列情况进行调查核实：  
（一）拟提请减刑、假释罪犯在服刑期间的表现情况；  
（二）拟提请减刑、假释罪犯的财产刑执行、附带民事裁判履行、退赃退赔等情况；  
（三）拟提请减刑罪犯的立功表现、重大立功表现是否属实，发明创造、技术革新是否系罪犯在服刑期间独立完成并经有关主管机关确认；  
（四）拟提请假释罪犯的身体状况、性格特征、假释后生活来源和监管条件等影响再犯罪的因素；  
（五）其他应当进行调查核实的情况。

**Article 7** A People's Procuratorate may investigate and verify the following circumstances by means such as accessing and reproducing relevant materials, reorganizing diagnosis and identification, conducting documentary evidence evaluation, holding symposiums, or conducting individual inquiries:  
(1) The performance of the criminal for whom commutation of punishment or parole is proposed during the period of serving his or her sentence;  
(2) The execution of property-related penalties, fulfillment of incidental civil judgments, return of illicit gains and restitution, etc., of the criminal for whom commutation of punishment or parole is proposed;  
(3) Whether the meritorious service or major meritorious service of the criminal for whom commutation of punishment is proposed is truthful, and whether the invention or technological innovation was independently completed by the criminal during the period of serving his or her sentence and confirmed by the competent authority;  
(4) The physical condition, personality characteristics, and factors affecting recidivism such as sources of livelihood and supervision conditions after parole, of the criminal for whom parole is proposed;  
(5) Other circumstances that shall be investigated and verified.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民检察院可以派员列席执行机关提请减刑、假释评审会议，了解案件有关情况，根据需要发表意见。

**Article 8** A People's Procuratorate may send personnel to attend the review meeting of the executing organ on the submission of commutation of punishment or parole as non-voting participants, learn about the relevant circumstances of the case, and express opinions as needed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民检察院发现罪犯符合减刑、假释条件，但是执行机关未提请减刑、假释的，可以建议执行机关提请减刑、假释。

**Article 9** If a People's Procuratorate discovers that a criminal meets the conditions for commutation of punishment or parole but the executing organ has not submitted a request for commutation of punishment or parole, it may suggest that the executing organ submit such a request.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民检察院收到执行机关抄送的减刑、假释建议书副本后，应当逐案进行审查，可以向人民法院提出书面意见。发现减刑、假释建议不当或者提请减刑、假释违反法定程序的，应当在收到建议书副本后十日以内，依法向审理减刑、假释案件的人民法院提出书面意见，同时将检察意见书副本抄送执行机关。案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长十日。

**Article 10** After receiving a copy of the proposal for commutation of punishment or parole submitted by the executing organ, the People's Procuratorate shall review each case and may submit written opinions to the People's Court. If it discovers that the proposal for commutation of punishment or parole is improper or that the submission of the request for commutation of punishment or parole violates statutory procedures, it shall, within ten days after receiving the copy of the proposal, submit written opinions according to law to the People's Court adjudicating the case of commutation of punishment or parole, and simultaneously send a copy of the written procuratorial opinions to the executing organ. If the case is complex or special, the time limit may be extended by ten days.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院开庭审理减刑、假释案件的，人民检察院应当指派检察人员出席法庭，发表检察意见，并对法庭审理活动是否合法进行监督。

**Article 11** Where a People's Court opens a court session to adjudicate a case of commutation of punishment or parole, the People's Procuratorate shall assign procuratorial personnel to appear in court, present procuratorial opinions, and supervise whether the court adjudication activities are lawful.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　出席法庭的检察人员不得少于二人，其中至少一人具有检察官职务。

**Article 12** The procuratorial personnel appearing in court shall be no less than two persons, and at least one of them shall hold the post of a prosecutor.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　检察人员应当在庭审前做好下列准备工作：  
（一）全面熟悉案情，掌握证据情况，拟定法庭调查提纲和出庭意见；  
（二）对执行机关提请减刑、假释有异议的案件，应当收集相关证据，可以建议人民法院通知相关证人出庭作证。

**Article 13** Procuratorial personnel shall, before the court session, make the following preparations:  
(1) Fully understand the case, grasp the evidence situation, and draft an outline for the court investigation and opinions for appearing in court;  
(2) For cases where there are objections to the executing organ's submission of commutation of punishment or parole, they shall collect relevant evidence and may suggest that the People's Court notify relevant witnesses to testify in court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　庭审开始后，在执行机关代表宣读减刑、假释建议书并说明理由之后，检察人员应当发表检察意见。

**Article 14** After the court session begins and the representative of the executing organ reads out the proposal for commutation of punishment or parole and explains the reasons, the procuratorial personnel shall present the procuratorial opinions.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　庭审过程中，检察人员对执行机关提请减刑、假释有疑问的，经审判长许可，可以出示证据，申请证人出庭作证，要求执行机关代表出示证据或者作出说明，向被提请减刑、假释的罪犯及证人提问并发表意见。

**Article 15** During the court session, if procuratorial personnel have doubts about the executing organ's submission of commutation of punishment or parole, they may, with the permission of the presiding judge, present evidence, apply for witnesses to testify in court, request the representative of the executing organ to present evidence or provide explanations, question the criminal for whom commutation of punishment or parole is proposed and witnesses, and express opinions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　法庭调查结束时，在被提请减刑、假释罪犯作最后陈述之前，经审判长许可，检察人员可以发表总结性意见。

**Article 16** At the conclusion of the court investigation, before the criminal for whom commutation of punishment or parole is proposed makes a final statement, the procuratorial personnel may, with the permission of the presiding judge, present a summative opinion.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　庭审过程中，检察人员认为需要进一步调查核实案件事实、证据，需要补充鉴定或者重新鉴定，或者需要通知新的证人到庭的，应当建议休庭。

**Article 17** During the court session, if procuratorial personnel consider it necessary to further investigate and verify the facts and evidence of the case, to supplement or re-conduct an evaluation, or to notify a new witness to appear in court, they shall suggest an adjournment.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　检察人员发现法庭审理活动违反法律规定的，应当在庭审后及时向本院检察长报告，依法向人民法院提出纠正意见。

**Article 18** If procuratorial personnel discover that the court adjudication activities violate legal provisions, they shall promptly report to the chief procurator of their own procuratorate after the court session and submit corrective opinions to the People's Court according to law.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　人民检察院收到人民法院减刑、假释裁定书副本后，应当及时审查下列内容：  
（一）人民法院对罪犯裁定予以减刑、假释，以及起始时间、间隔时间、实际执行刑期、减刑幅度或者假释考验期是否符合有关规定；  
（二）人民法院对罪犯裁定不予减刑、假释是否符合有关规定；  
（三）人民法院审理、裁定减刑、假释的程序是否合法；  
（四）按照有关规定应当开庭审理的减刑、假释案件，人民法院是否开庭审理；  
（五）人民法院减刑、假释裁定书是否依法送达执行并向社会公布。

**Article 19** After receiving a copy of the written ruling on commutation of punishment or parole from the People's Court, the People's Procuratorate shall promptly review the following content:  
(1) Whether the People's Court's ruling to grant commutation of punishment or parole to the criminal, and the starting time, interval, actual sentence served, extent of reduction, or parole probation period comply with the relevant provisions;  
(2) Whether the People's Court's ruling not to grant commutation of punishment or parole to the criminal complies with the relevant provisions;  
(3) Whether the procedures for the People's Court's adjudication and ruling on commutation of punishment or parole are lawful;  
(4) Whether the People's Court opened a court session for cases of commutation of punishment or parole that shall be tried in court according to relevant provisions;  
(5) Whether the written ruling on commutation of punishment or parole from the People's Court was served to the executing organ according to law and made public to society.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民检察院经审查认为人民法院减刑、假释裁定不当的，应当在收到裁定书副本后二十日以内，依法向作出减刑、假释裁定的人民法院提出书面纠正意见。

**Article 20** If a People's Procuratorate, after review, considers that the People's Court's ruling on commutation of punishment or parole is improper, it shall, within twenty days after receiving a copy of the written ruling, submit written corrective opinions according to law to the People's Court that made the ruling on commutation of punishment or parole.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　人民检察院对人民法院减刑、假释裁定提出纠正意见的，应当监督人民法院在收到纠正意见后一个月以内重新组成合议庭进行审理并作出最终裁定。

**Article 21** Where a People's Procuratorate submits corrective opinions on a People's Court's ruling on commutation of punishment or parole, it shall supervise the People's Court to reconstitute a collegial panel to conduct adjudication and make a final ruling within one month after receiving the corrective opinions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　人民检察院发现人民法院已经生效的减刑、假释裁定确有错误的，应当向人民法院提出书面纠正意见，提请人民法院按照审判监督程序依法另行组成合议庭重新审理并作出裁定。

**Article 22** If a People's Procuratorate discovers that a People's Court's ruling on commutation of punishment or parole that has already taken effect is indeed erroneous, it shall submit written corrective opinions to the People's Court and request that the People's Court reconstitute a collegial panel according to law under the adjudication supervision procedure to retry the case and make a ruling.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　人民检察院收到控告、举报或者发现司法工作人员在办理减刑、假释案件中涉嫌违法的，应当依法进行调查，并根据情况，向有关单位提出纠正违法意见，建议更换办案人，或者建议予以纪律处分；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 23** If a People's Procuratorate receives a complaint or report, or discovers that a judicial officer is suspected of violating the law in handling a case of commutation of punishment or parole, it shall conduct an investigation according to law, and based on the circumstances, submit opinions on correcting the violation to the relevant unit, propose replacing the case handler, or propose imposing disciplinary sanctions; if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　人民检察院办理职务犯罪罪犯减刑、假释案件，按照有关规定实行备案审查。

**Article 24** Where a People's Procuratorate handles a case of commutation of punishment or parole for a criminal who committed a duty crime, it shall implement the filing review system in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本规定自发布之日起施行。最高人民检察院以前发布的有关规定与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 25** These Provisions shall come into force from the date of publication. Any provisions previously issued by the Supreme People's Procuratorate that are inconsistent with these Provisions shall cease to apply, and these Provisions shall prevail.
