### 最高人民法院关于被判处拘役或者三年以下有期徒刑宣告缓刑的罪犯减刑的管辖和处理程序的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于被判处拘役或者三年以下有期徒刑  
  
宣告缓刑的罪犯减刑的管辖和处理程序的批复  
  
法（研）复〔 1988〕1号  
  
福建省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于缓刑犯减刑的管辖和处理程序问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
对于被判处拘役或者三年以下有期徒刑、宣告缓刑的犯罪分子，在缓刑考验期内确有悔改或者立功表现，决定对原判刑罚予以减刑，同时相应地缩减其缓刑考验期限的，应由公安派出所会同负责考察罪犯的所在单位或者基层组织提出书面意见，报县以上公安机关审查后，对被判处有期徒刑宣告缓刑的罪犯的减刑，提请当地中级人民法院裁定；对被判处拘役宣告缓刑的罪犯的减刑，提请当地基层人民法院裁定。人民法院应将裁定书副本同时送同级人民检察院。  
  
此复。  
  
1988年1月5日

**Article 1** Reply on the Jurisdiction and Procedure for Commutation of Punishment of Criminals Sentenced to Criminal Detention or Fixed-term Imprisonment of Not More Than Three Years with Probation  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1988] No. 1  
Higher People's Court of Fujian Province:  
Your request for instructions on the jurisdiction and procedure for commutation of punishment of criminals on probation has been received. After study, we reply as follows:  
  
For a criminal who is sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years and is granted probation, if during the probation period the criminal truly shows repentance or performs meritorious service, and a decision is made to commute the original punishment while correspondingly reducing the probation period, the public security police station shall, together with the unit or grassroots organization responsible for supervising the criminal, submit a written opinion. After review by the public security organ at or above the county level, the commutation of punishment for a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment with probation shall be submitted to the local Intermediate People's Court for a ruling; the commutation of punishment for a criminal sentenced to criminal detention with probation shall be submitted to the local Primary People's Court for a ruling. The People's Court shall at the same time send a copy of the written ruling to the People's Procuratorate at the same level.  
  
This is our reply.  
January 5, 1988
