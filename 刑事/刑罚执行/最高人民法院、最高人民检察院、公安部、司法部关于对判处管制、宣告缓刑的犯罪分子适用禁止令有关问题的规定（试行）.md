### 正文

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　人民法院可以根据犯罪情况，禁止判处管制、宣告缓刑的犯罪分子在管制执行期间、缓刑考验期限内接触以下一类或者几类人员：  
（一）未经对方同意，禁止接触被害人及其法定代理人、近亲属；  
（二）未经对方同意，禁止接触证人及其法定代理人、近亲属；  
（三）未经对方同意，禁止接触控告人、批评人、举报人及其法定代理人、近亲属；  
（四）禁止接触同案犯；  
（五）禁止接触其他可能遭受其侵害、滋扰的人或者可能诱发其再次危害社会的人。

**Article 5** The People's Court may, according to the circumstances of the crime, prohibit a criminal sentenced to public surveillance or granted probation from contacting the following categories of persons during the period of execution of public surveillance or during the probation period:  
(1) without the consent of the other party, contacting the victim and his/her legal representative or close relatives;  
(2) without the consent of the other party, contacting the witness and his/her legal representative or close relatives;  
(3) without the consent of the other party, contacting the accuser, critic, or informant and their legal representatives or close relatives;  
(4) contacting the co-offender;  
(5) contacting any other person who may suffer harm or harassment from the criminal or who may induce the criminal to harm society again.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　禁止令的期限，既可以与管制执行、缓刑考验的期限相同，也可以短于管制执行、缓刑考验的期限，但判处管制的，禁止令的期限不得少于三个月，宣告缓刑的，禁止令的期限不得少于二个月。  
判处管制的犯罪分子在判决执行以前先行羁押以致管制执行的期限少于三个月的，禁止令的期限不受前款规定的最短期限的限制。  
禁止令的执行期限，从管制、缓刑执行之日起计算。

**Article 6** The term of a restraining order may be the same as the term of execution of public surveillance or the probation period, or may be shorter than the term of execution of public surveillance or the probation period. However, where public surveillance is imposed, the term of the restraining order shall not be less than three months; where probation is granted, the term of the restraining order shall not be less than two months.  
Where a criminal sentenced to public surveillance is detained pending enforcement of the judgment such that the term of execution of public surveillance is less than three months, the term of the restraining order shall not be subject to the minimum term restriction provided in the preceding paragraph.  
The execution term of the restraining order shall be calculated from the date of enforcement of the public surveillance or probation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　人民检察院在提起公诉时，对可能判处管制、宣告缓刑的被告人可以提出宣告禁止令的建议。当事人、辩护人、诉讼代理人可以就应否对被告人宣告禁止令提出意见，并说明理由。  
公安机关在移送审查起诉时，可以根据犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌犯罪的情况，就应否宣告禁止令及宣告何种禁止令，向人民检察院提出意见。

**Article 7** When initiating a public prosecution, the People's Procuratorate may propose the issuance of a restraining order against a defendant who may be sentenced to public surveillance or granted probation. The parties, defenders, and litigation agents may present opinions on whether a restraining order should be issued against the defendant and state their reasons.  
When transferring the case for examination and prosecution, the public security organ may, based on the circumstances of the crime of which the criminal suspect is suspected, present opinions to the People's Procuratorate on whether a restraining order should be issued and the type of restraining order to be issued.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院对判处管制、宣告缓刑的被告人宣告禁止令的，应当在裁判文书主文部分单独作为一项予以宣告。

**Article 8** Where the People's Court issues a restraining order against a defendant sentenced to public surveillance or granted probation, it shall declare the restraining order as a separate item in the main text of the judgment document.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　禁止令由司法行政机关指导管理的社区矫正机构负责执行。

**Article 9** The restraining order shall be enforced by the community correction institution under the guidance and management of the judicial administrative authority.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　人民检察院对社区矫正机构执行禁止令的活动实行监督。发现有违反法律规定的情况，应当通知社区矫正机构纠正。

**Article 10** The People's Procuratorate shall supervise the activities of the community correction institution in enforcing the restraining order. If it discovers any violation of the law, it shall notify the community correction institution to rectify it.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　判处管制的犯罪分子违反禁止令，或者被宣告缓刑的犯罪分子违反禁止令尚不属情节严重的，由负责执行禁止令的社区矫正机构所在地的公安机关依照[《中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法》第六十条](../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国治安管理处罚法.md#art-60)的规定处罚。

**Article 11** Where a criminal sentenced to public surveillance violates the restraining order, or a criminal granted probation violates the restraining order but the circumstances are not serious, the public security organ at the location of the community correction institution responsible for enforcing the restraining order shall impose a penalty in accordance with Article 60 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Public Security Administration Punishments.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被宣告缓刑的犯罪分子违反禁止令，情节严重的，应当撤销缓刑，执行原判刑罚。原作出缓刑裁判的人民法院应当自收到当地社区矫正机构提出的撤销缓刑建议书之日起一个月内依法作出裁定。人民法院撤销缓刑的裁定一经作出，立即生效。  
违反禁止令，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“情节严重”：  
（一）三次以上违反禁止令的；  
（二）因违反禁止令被治安管理处罚后，再次违反禁止令的；  
（三）违反禁止令，发生较为严重危害后果的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 12** Where a criminal granted probation violates the restraining order and the circumstances are serious, the probation shall be revoked and the original sentence shall be executed. The People's Court that originally rendered the probation decision shall, within one month from the date of receipt of the recommendation for revocation of probation submitted by the local community correction institution, make a ruling according to law. Once made, the ruling of the People's Court to revoke probation shall take effect immediately.  
Violation of the restraining order under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances":  
(1) violating the restraining order three times or more;  
(2) violating the restraining order again after having been subjected to a public security administrative penalty for violating it;  
(3) violating the restraining order, resulting in relatively serious harmful consequences;  
(4) other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　被宣告禁止令的犯罪分子被依法减刑时，禁止令的期限可以相应缩短，由人民法院在减刑裁定中确定新的禁止令期限。

**Article 13** Where a criminal against whom a restraining order has been issued is granted a commutation of punishment according to law, the term of the restraining order may be shortened accordingly, and the People's Court shall determine the new term of the restraining order in the commutation ruling.
