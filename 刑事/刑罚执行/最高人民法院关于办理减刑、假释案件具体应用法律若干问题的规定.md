# 最高人民法院关于办理减刑、假释案件具体应用法律若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕2号  
**公布日期**：2011-11-21  
**生效日期**：2012-07-01

> 最高人民法院 《关于办理减刑、假释案件具体应用法律若干问题的规定》已于2011年11月21日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1532次会议通过，现予公布，自2012年7月1日起施行。 2012年1月17日 最高人民法院 关于办理减刑、假释案件具体应用法律 若干问题的规定 法释〔2012〕2号 为正确适用刑法、刑事诉讼法，依法办理减刑、假释案件，根据刑法、刑事诉讼法和有关法律的规定，制定本规定。 第一条  根据刑法第七十八条第一款的规定，被判处管制、拘役、有期徒刑、无期徒刑的犯罪分子，在执行期间，认真遵守监规，接受教育改造，确有悔改表现的，或者有立功表现的，可以减刑；有重大立功表现的，应当减刑。 第二条  “确有悔改表现”是指同时具备以下四个方面情形：认罪悔罪；认真遵守法律法规及监规，接受教育改造；积极参加思想、文化、职业技术教育；积极参加劳动，努力完成劳动任务。 对罪犯在刑罚执行期间提出申诉的，要依法保护其申诉权利，对罪犯申诉不应不加分析地认为是不认罪悔罪。 罪犯积极执行财产刑和履行附带民事赔偿义务的，可视为有认罪悔罪表现，在减刑、假释时可以从宽掌握；确有执行、履行能力而不执行、不履行的，在减刑、假释时应当从严掌握。 第三条  具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为有“立功表现”： （一）阻止他人实施犯罪活动的； （二）检举、揭发监狱内外犯罪活动，或者提供重要的破案线索，经查证属实的； （三）协助司法机关抓捕其他犯罪嫌疑人（包括同案犯）的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  根据[刑法第七十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-78)第一款的规定，被判处管制、拘役、有期徒刑、无期徒刑的犯罪分子，在执行期间，认真遵守监规，接受教育改造，确有悔改表现的，或者有立功表现的，可以减刑；有重大立功表现的，应当减刑。

**Article 1** According to the provisions of Article 78, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, criminals sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, fixed-term imprisonment, or life imprisonment who, during the period of enforcement, earnestly observe prison regulations, accept education and reform, and truly show repentance or have meritorious service, may have their punishment commuted; those with major meritorious service shall have their punishment commuted.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  “确有悔改表现”是指同时具备以下四个方面情形：认罪悔罪；认真遵守法律法规及监规，接受教育改造；积极参加思想、文化、职业技术教育；积极参加劳动，努力完成劳动任务。  
对罪犯在刑罚执行期间提出申诉的，要依法保护其申诉权利，对罪犯申诉不应不加分析地认为是不认罪悔罪。  
罪犯积极执行财产刑和履行附带民事赔偿义务的，可视为有认罪悔罪表现，在减刑、假释时可以从宽掌握；确有执行、履行能力而不执行、不履行的，在减刑、假释时应当从严掌握。

**Article 2** "Truly showing repentance" means concurrently meeting the following four aspects: admitting guilt and repenting; earnestly observing laws, regulations, and prison regulations, and accepting education and reform; actively participating in ideological, cultural, and vocational technical education; and actively participating in labor and striving to complete labor tasks.  
For criminals who file petitions during the period of enforcement of punishment, their right to petition shall be protected according to law, and a criminal's petition shall not be indiscriminately regarded as not admitting guilt or repenting.  
If a criminal actively executes property-related punishment and fulfills supplementary civil compensation obligations, it may be deemed as showing admission of guilt and repentance, and may be handled leniently when granting commutation or parole; if a criminal has the ability to execute or fulfill but fails to do so, it shall be handled strictly when granting commutation or parole.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为有“立功表现”：  
（一）阻止他人实施犯罪活动的；  
（二）检举、揭发监狱内外犯罪活动，或者提供重要的破案线索，经查证属实的；  
（三）协助司法机关抓捕其他犯罪嫌疑人（包括同案犯）的；  
（四）在生产、科研中进行技术革新，成绩突出的；  
（五）在抢险救灾或者排除重大事故中表现突出的；  
（六）对国家和社会有其他贡献的。

**Article 3** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as having "meritorious service":  
(1) preventing others from committing criminal activities;  
(2) reporting or exposing criminal activities inside or outside prison, or providing important case-solving clues, which are verified as true;  
(3) assisting judicial authorities in arresting other criminal suspects (including accomplices);  
(4) making technological innovations in production or scientific research with outstanding achievements;  
(5) showing outstanding performance in emergency rescue, disaster relief, or eliminating major accidents;  
(6) making other contributions to the state and society.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为有“重大立功表现”：  
（一）阻止他人实施重大犯罪活动的；  
（二）检举监狱内外重大犯罪活动，经查证属实的；  
（三）协助司法机关抓捕其他重大犯罪嫌疑人（包括同案犯）的；  
（四）有发明创造或者重大技术革新的；  
（五）在日常生产、生活中舍己救人的；  
（六）在抗御自然灾害或者排除重大事故中，有特别突出表现的；  
（七）对国家和社会有其他重大贡献的。

**Article 4** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as having "major meritorious service":  
(1) preventing others from committing major criminal activities;  
(2) reporting major criminal activities inside or outside prison, which are verified as true;  
(3) assisting judicial authorities in arresting other major criminal suspects (including accomplices);  
(4) making inventions or major technological innovations;  
(5) sacrificing oneself to save others in daily production or life;  
(6) showing particularly outstanding performance in resisting natural disasters or eliminating major accidents;  
(7) making other major contributions to the state and society.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  有期徒刑罪犯在刑罚执行期间，符合减刑条件的，减刑幅度为：确有悔改表现，或者有立功表现的，一次减刑一般不超过一年有期徒刑；确有悔改表现并有立功表现，或者有重大立功表现的，一次减刑一般不超过二年有期徒刑。

**Article 5** For criminals sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, during the period of enforcement, if the conditions for commutation are met, the scope of commutation shall be: if they truly show repentance or have meritorious service, the commutation at one time shall generally not exceed one year of fixed-term imprisonment; if they truly show repentance and have meritorious service, or have major meritorious service, the commutation at one time shall generally not exceed two years of fixed-term imprisonment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  有期徒刑罪犯的减刑起始时间和间隔时间为：被判处五年以上有期徒刑的罪犯，一般在执行一年六个月以上方可减刑，两次减刑之间一般应当间隔一年以上。被判处不满五年有期徒刑的罪犯，可以比照上述规定，适当缩短起始和间隔时间。  
确有重大立功表现的，可以不受上述减刑起始和间隔时间的限制。  
有期徒刑的减刑起始时间自判决执行之日起计算。

**Article 6** The starting time and interval for commutation of punishment for criminals sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment shall be: for criminals sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of five years or more, commutation may generally be granted only after more than one year and six months of enforcement, and the interval between two commutations shall generally be at least one year. For criminals sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of less than five years, the starting time and interval may be appropriately shortened by reference to the above provisions.  
If a criminal has major meritorious service, the restrictions on the starting time and interval for commutation may be waived.  
The starting time for commutation of fixed-term imprisonment shall be calculated from the date on which the judgment is enforced.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  无期徒刑罪犯在刑罚执行期间，确有悔改表现，或者有立功表现的，服刑二年以后，可以减刑。减刑幅度为：确有悔改表现，或者有立功表现的，一般可以减为二十年以上二十二年以下有期徒刑；有重大立功表现的，可以减为十五年以上二十年以下有期徒刑。

**Article 7** If a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment, during the period of enforcement, truly shows repentance or has meritorious service, after serving two years of imprisonment, their punishment may be commuted. The scope of commutation shall be: if they truly show repentance or have meritorious service, it may generally be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 20 years but not more than 22 years; if they have major meritorious service, it may be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than 15 years but not more than 20 years.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　无期徒刑罪犯经过一次或几次减刑后，其实际执行的刑期不能少于十三年，起始时间应当自无期徒刑判决确定之日起计算。

**Article 8** After a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment undergoes commutation once or several times, the actual term of imprisonment served shall not be less than 13 years, and the starting time shall be calculated from the date on which the life imprisonment judgment is confirmed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  死刑缓期执行罪犯减为无期徒刑后，确有悔改表现，或者有立功表现的，服刑二年以后可以减为二十五年有期徒刑；有重大立功表现的，服刑二年以后可以减为二十三年有期徒刑。  
死刑缓期执行罪犯经过一次或几次减刑后，其实际执行的刑期不能少于十五年，死刑缓期执行期间不包括在内。  
死刑缓期执行罪犯在缓期执行期间抗拒改造，尚未构成犯罪的，此后减刑时可以适当从严。

**Article 9** After a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve has their punishment commuted to life imprisonment, if they truly show repentance or have meritorious service, after serving two years of imprisonment, their punishment may be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of 25 years; if they have major meritorious service, after serving two years of imprisonment, their punishment may be commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of 23 years.  
After a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve undergoes commutation once or several times, the actual term of imprisonment served shall not be less than 15 years, and the period of reprieve shall not be included.  
If a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve resists reform during the reprieve period, which does not constitute a crime, the commutation may be appropriately strictly handled thereafter.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  被限制减刑的死刑缓期执行罪犯，缓期执行期满后依法被减为无期徒刑的，或者因有重大立功表现被减为二十五年有期徒刑的，应当比照未被限制减刑的死刑缓期执行罪犯在减刑的起始时间、间隔时间和减刑幅度上从严掌握。

**Article 10** For a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve whose commutation is restricted, if after the expiration of the reprieve period, the punishment is commuted according to law to life imprisonment, or commuted to fixed-term imprisonment of 25 years due to major meritorious service, the starting time, interval, and scope of commutation shall be handled strictly by reference to criminals sentenced to death with a reprieve whose commutation is not restricted.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  判处管制、拘役的罪犯，以及判决生效后剩余刑期不满一年有期徒刑的罪犯，符合减刑条件的，可以酌情减刑，其实际执行的刑期不能少于原判刑期的二分之一。

**Article 11** For criminals sentenced to public surveillance or criminal detention, and criminals whose remaining term of fixed-term imprisonment is less than one year after the judgment takes effect, if the conditions for commutation are met, commutation may be granted at discretion, and the actual term of imprisonment served shall not be less than one-half of the originally imposed term of imprisonment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  有期徒刑罪犯减刑时，对附加剥夺政治权利的期限可以酌减。酌减后剥夺政治权利的期限，不能少于一年。

**Article 12** When commutation is granted to a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, the term of the supplementary punishment of deprivation of political rights may be appropriately reduced. The term of deprivation of political rights after reduction shall not be less than one year.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  判处拘役或者三年以下有期徒刑并宣告缓刑的罪犯，一般不适用减刑。  
前款规定的罪犯在缓刑考验期限内有重大立功表现的，可以参照[刑法第七十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-78)的规定，予以减刑，同时应依法缩减其缓刑考验期限。拘役的缓刑考验期限不能少于二个月，有期徒刑的缓刑考验期限不能少于一年。

**Article 13** Criminals sentenced to criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years and granted probation shall generally not be eligible for commutation.  
If a criminal as provided in the preceding paragraph has major meritorious service during the probation period, commutation may be granted by reference to the provisions of Article 78 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and at the same time, the probation period shall be shortened according to law. The probation period for criminal detention shall not be less than two months, and the probation period for fixed-term imprisonment shall not be less than one year.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  被判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑的罪犯在刑罚执行期间又犯罪，被判处有期徒刑以下刑罚的，自新罪判决确定之日起二年内一般不予减刑；新罪被判处无期徒刑的，自新罪判决确定之日起三年内一般不予减刑。

**Article 14** If a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment commits another crime during the period of enforcement and is sentenced to a punishment below fixed-term imprisonment, commutation shall generally not be granted within two years from the date on which the judgment for the new crime is confirmed; if the new crime is sentenced to life imprisonment, commutation shall generally not be granted within three years from the date on which the judgment for the new crime is confirmed.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  办理假释案件，判断“没有再犯罪的危险”，除符合[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)规定的情形外，还应根据犯罪的具体情节、原判刑罚情况，在刑罚执行中的一贯表现，罪犯的年龄、身体状况、性格特征，假释后生活来源以及监管条件等因素综合考虑。

**Article 15** When handling parole cases, to determine "no risk of re-offending", in addition to meeting the circumstances provided in Article 81 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, comprehensive consideration shall also be given to factors such as the specific circumstances of the crime, the originally imposed punishment, the consistent behavior during enforcement, the criminal's age, physical condition, personality traits, sources of livelihood after parole, and supervision conditions.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  有期徒刑罪犯假释，执行原判刑期二分之一以上的起始时间，应当从判决执行之日起计算，判决执行以前先行羁押的，羁押一日折抵刑期一日。

**Article 16** For parole of a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, the starting time for having served more than one-half of the originally imposed term of imprisonment shall be calculated from the date on which the judgment is enforced; if the criminal was placed in custody before enforcement, one day in custody shall be offset against one day of the term of imprisonment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条  [刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)第一款规定的“特殊情况”，是指与国家、社会利益有重要关系的情况。

**Article 17** The "special circumstances" provided in Article 81, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China refer to circumstances that have an important relationship with the interests of the state and society.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条  对累犯以及因故意杀人、强奸、抢劫、绑架、放火、爆炸、投放危险物质或者有组织的暴力性犯罪被判处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑的罪犯，不得假释。  
因前款情形和犯罪被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯，被减为无期徒刑、有期徒刑后，也不得假释。

**Article 18** Criminals who are recidivists or who, due to intentional homicide, rape, robbery, kidnapping, arson, explosion, releasing hazardous substances, or organized violent crimes, have been sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, shall not be granted parole.  
Criminals sentenced to death with a reprieve due to the circumstances and crimes in the preceding paragraph, after their punishment is commuted to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment, shall also not be granted parole.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条  未成年罪犯的减刑、假释，可以比照成年罪犯依法适当从宽。  
未成年罪犯能认罪悔罪，遵守法律法规及监规，积极参加学习、劳动的，应视为确有悔改表现，减刑的幅度可以适当放宽，起始时间、间隔时间可以相应缩短。符合[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)第一款规定的，可以假释。  
前两款所称未成年罪犯，是指减刑时不满十八周岁的罪犯。

**Article 19** For juvenile criminals, commutation and parole may be appropriately lenient by reference to adult criminals according to law.  
If a juvenile criminal can admit guilt and repent, abide by laws, regulations, and prison regulations, and actively participate in study and labor, it shall be deemed as truly showing repentance, the scope of commutation may be appropriately relaxed, and the starting time and interval may be correspondingly shortened. If the conditions in Article 81, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China are met, parole may be granted.  
The juvenile criminals referred to in the preceding two paragraphs mean criminals who are under the age of 18 at the time of commutation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条  老年、身体残疾（不含自伤致残）、患严重疾病罪犯的减刑、假释，应当主要注重悔罪的实际表现。  
基本丧失劳动能力、生活难以自理的老年、身体残疾、患严重疾病的罪犯，能够认真遵守法律法规及监规，接受教育改造，应视为确有悔改表现，减刑的幅度可以适当放宽，起始时间、间隔时间可以相应缩短。假释后生活确有着落的，除法律和本解释规定不得假释的情形外，可以依法假释。  
对身体残疾罪犯和患严重疾病罪犯进行减刑、假释，其残疾、疾病程度应由法定鉴定机构依法作出认定。

**Article 20** For elderly, physically disabled (excluding self-inflicted disability), or seriously ill criminals, commutation and parole shall mainly focus on the actual manifestation of repentance.  
If an elderly, physically disabled, or seriously ill criminal who has basically lost the ability to work and has difficulty in self-care can earnestly abide by laws, regulations, and prison regulations, and accept education and reform, it shall be deemed as truly showing repentance, the scope of commutation may be appropriately relaxed, and the starting time and interval may be correspondingly shortened. If there is a definite source of livelihood after parole, except for circumstances where parole is not permitted by law or this Interpretation, parole may be granted according to law.  
When granting commutation or parole to physically disabled or seriously ill criminals, the degree of disability or disease shall be determined by a statutory expert evaluation institution according to law.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  对死刑缓期执行罪犯减为无期徒刑或者有期徒刑后，符合[刑法第八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-81)第一款和[本规定第九条](#art-9)第二款、第十八条规定的，可以假释。

**Article 21** After a criminal sentenced to death with a reprieve has their punishment commuted to life imprisonment or fixed-term imprisonment, if the conditions in Article 81, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China and Article 9, paragraph 2 and Article 18 of these Provisions are met, parole may be granted.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条  罪犯减刑后又假释的间隔时间，一般为一年；对一次减去二年有期徒刑后，决定假释的，间隔时间不能少于二年。  
罪犯减刑后余刑不足二年，决定假释的，可以适当缩短间隔时间。

**Article 22** The interval between commutation and subsequent parole shall generally be one year; if parole is decided after a commutation of two years of fixed-term imprisonment at one time, the interval shall not be less than two years.  
If the remaining term of imprisonment after commutation is less than two years and parole is decided, the interval may be appropriately shortened.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条  人民法院按照审判监督程序重新审理的案件，维持原判决、裁定的，原减刑、假释裁定效力不变；改变原判决、裁定的，应由刑罚执行机关依照再审裁判情况和原减刑、假释情况，提请有管辖权的人民法院重新作出减刑、假释裁定。

**Article 23** For a case retried by the People's Court according to the adjudication supervision procedure, if the original judgment or ruling is affirmed, the original commutation or parole ruling shall remain effective; if the original judgment or ruling is modified, the penal enforcement authority shall, based on the retrial judgment or ruling and the original commutation or parole circumstances, request the People's Court with jurisdiction to make a new commutation or parole ruling.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条  人民法院受理减刑、假释案件，应当审查执行机关是否移送下列材料：  
（一）减刑或者假释建议书；  
（二）终审法院的裁判文书、执行通知书、历次减刑裁定书的复制件；  
（三）罪犯确有悔改或者立功、重大立功表现的具体事实的书面证明材料；  
（四）罪犯评审鉴定表、奖惩审批表等；  
（五）其他根据案件的审理需要移送的材料。  
提请假释的，应当附有社区矫正机构关于罪犯假释后对所居住社区影响的调查评估报告。  
人民检察院对提请减刑、假释案件提出的检察意见，应当一并移送受理减刑、假释案件的人民法院。  
经审查，如果前三款规定的材料齐备的，应当立案；材料不齐备的，应当通知提请减刑、假释的执行机关补送。

**Article 24** When a People's Court accepts a commutation or parole case, it shall review whether the enforcement authority has submitted the following materials:  
(1) a proposal for commutation or parole;  
(2) copies of the judgment document of the court of final instance, enforcement notice, and previous commutation rulings;  
(3) written evidentiary materials on the specific facts showing the criminal's true repentance or meritorious service or major meritorious service;  
(4) criminal evaluation forms, reward and punishment approval forms, etc.;  
(5) other materials that need to be submitted according to the case proceedings.  
If parole is proposed, it shall be accompanied by an investigation and evaluation report from the community correction institution on the impact of the criminal's parole on the community where he or she will reside.  
The procuratorial opinions submitted by the People's Procuratorate on the proposed commutation or parole case shall also be submitted together to the People's Court that accepts the commutation or parole case.  
Upon review, if the materials specified in the preceding three paragraphs are complete, the case shall be filed; if the materials are incomplete, the enforcement authority that proposed the commutation or parole shall be notified to supplement them.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条  人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，应当一律予以公示。公示地点为罪犯服刑场所的公共区域。有条件的地方，应面向社会公示，接受社会监督。公示应当包括下列内容：  
（一）罪犯的姓名；  
（二）原判认定的罪名和刑期；  
（三）罪犯历次减刑情况；  
（四）执行机关的减刑、假释建议和依据；  
（五）公示期限；  
（六）意见反馈方式等。

**Article 25** When a People's Court tries a commutation or parole case, it shall publicly announce it. The place of announcement shall be the public area of the criminal's place of confinement. Where conditions permit, it shall be announced to the public to accept public supervision. The announcement shall include the following contents:  
(1) the criminal's name;  
(2) the crime and term of imprisonment determined in the original judgment;  
(3) the criminal's previous commutation circumstances;  
(4) the enforcement authority's proposal and basis for commutation or parole;  
(5) the announcement period;  
(6) channels for feedback, etc.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条  人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，可以采用书面审理的方式。但下列案件，应当开庭审理：  
（一）因罪犯有重大立功表现提请减刑的；  
（二）提请减刑的起始时间、间隔时间或者减刑幅度不符合一般规定的；  
（三）在社会上有重大影响或社会关注度高的；  
（四）公示期间收到投诉意见的；  
（五）人民检察院有异议的；  
（六）人民法院认为有开庭审理必要的。

**Article 26** When a People's Court tries a commutation or parole case, it may adopt the method of written adjudication. However, the following cases shall be tried open in court:  
(1) commutation is proposed because the criminal has major meritorious service;  
(2) the starting time, interval, or scope of commutation proposed does not comply with general provisions;  
(3) there is a major social impact or high public concern;  
(4) complaints or opinions are received during the announcement period;  
(5) the People's Procuratorate objects;  
(6) the People's Court considers an open session necessary.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条  在人民法院作出减刑、假释裁定前，执行机关书面提请撤回减刑、假释建议的，是否准许，由人民法院决定。

**Article 27** Before the People's Court makes a commutation or parole ruling, if the enforcement authority submits a written request to withdraw the commutation or parole proposal, whether to permit it shall be decided by the People's Court.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条  减刑、假释的裁定，应当在裁定作出之日起七日内送达有关执行机关、人民检察院以及罪犯本人。

**Article 28** The ruling on commutation or parole shall be served on the relevant enforcement authority, the People's Procuratorate, and the criminal himself within seven days from the date on which the ruling is made.

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条  人民法院发现本院或者下级人民法院已经生效的减刑、假释裁定确有错误，应当依法重新组成合议庭进行审理并作出裁定。

**Article 29** If the People's Court discovers that an effective commutation or parole ruling made by itself or a lower People's Court is indeed erroneous, it shall form a new collegial panel according to law to conduct a trial and make a ruling.
