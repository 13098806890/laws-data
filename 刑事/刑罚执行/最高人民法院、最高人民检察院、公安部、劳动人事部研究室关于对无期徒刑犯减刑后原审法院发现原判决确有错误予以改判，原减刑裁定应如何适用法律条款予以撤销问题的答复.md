关于对无期徒刑犯减刑后原审法院发现原判决  
确有错误予以改判，原减刑裁定应如何适用  
法律条款予以撤销问题的答复  
（1994年11月7日）  
江西省高级人民法院：  
你院赣高法〔1994〕110号《关于撤销减刑裁定应当如何适用法律条款的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
被判处无期徒刑的罪犯由服刑地的高级人民法院依法裁定减刑后，原审人民法院发一原判决确有错误并依照审判监督程序改判为有期徒刑的，应当依照我院法（研）复〔1989〕2号批复撤销原减刑裁定。鉴于原减刑裁定是在无期徒刑基础上的减刑，既然原判无期徒刑已被认定为错判，那么原减刑裁定在认定事实和适用法律上亦应视为确有错误。由此，由罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院根据[刑事诉讼法第一百四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-149)第一款的规定，按照审判监督程序撤销原减刑裁定是适宜的。

1994年9月21日      赣高法〔1994〕110号  
最高人民法院：  
本院曾以（88）赣法研二字第3号请示报告向你院请示“关于对无期徒刑犯减刑后又改判原减刑裁定应否撤销”的问题，你院1989年1月3日以法（研）复（1989）8号批复对此问题作出了答复。我们在执行上述批复的过程中，对此类情况下撤销减刑裁定应当如何适用法律条款有不同认识。一种意见认为，被判处无期徒刑的罪犯经依法裁定减刑后，发现原审判决确有错误依法改判为有期徒刑，应当撤销原减刑裁定。撤销减刑裁定的原因是原减刑的基础已不存在，可以视为原减刑裁定认定事实有误。因此，撤销减刑的裁定书应当适用[刑事诉讼法第一百四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-149)的规定。另一种意见认为，因原判无期徒刑确有错误被改判为有期徒刑而撤销减刑裁定，并非原减刑有错误。司法机关根据罪犯在服刑中的表现作出的减刑裁定本身没有错误，之所以要撤销减刑裁定，是因为原判刑罚已经改判，该减刑裁定不具有实际意义了，但撤销减刑裁定不属于适用审判监督程序的范畴，不必在撤销减刑裁定书中引用[刑事诉讼法第一百四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-149)，目前刑事诉讼法对此类情况的撤销减刑裁定未作规定，今后修改法律时应予规定。  
对撤销减刑裁定如何适用法律条款的问题我们把握不准，特向你院请示。望予批复。

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court Supreme People's Procuratorate  
Ministry of Public Security Research Office of the Ministry of Labor and Personnel  
Reply on How the Legal Provisions Shall Be Applied  
to Revoke the Original Commutation Ruling When the Original Trial Court Discovers  
That the Original Judgment Is Indeed Erroneous and Amends It by Adjudication  
After Commutation of Punishment for a Prisoner Sentenced to Life Imprisonment  
(November 7, 1994)  
Jiangxi Higher People's Court:  
We have received your court's Jiang Gao Fa [1994] No. 110 "Request for Instructions on How the Legal Provisions Shall Be Applied to Revoke the Commutation Ruling". Upon study, we reply as follows:  
Where a prisoner sentenced to life imprisonment has, after the commutation of his punishment by a ruling lawfully made by the Higher People's Court of the place where the sentence is executed, the original trial court discovers that the original judgment is indeed erroneous and amends it to fixed-term imprisonment in accordance with the adjudication supervision procedure, the original commutation ruling shall be revoked in accordance with the Reply of this Court Fa (Yan) Fu [1989] No. 2. In view that the original commutation ruling was made on the basis of life imprisonment, since the original life imprisonment has been recognized as an erroneous judgment, the original commutation ruling shall also be deemed to be indeed erroneous in the finding of facts and application of law. Therefore, it is appropriate for the Higher People's Court of the place where the sentence is executed to revoke the original commutation ruling in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 149 of the Criminal Procedure Law and by following the adjudication supervision procedure.

September 21, 1994 Jiang Gao Fa [1994] No. 110  
Supreme People's Court:  
Previously, this court submitted a request for instructions to your court by (88) Gan Fa Yan Er Zi No. 3, inquiring about the issue "whether the original commutation ruling shall be revoked when a prisoner sentenced to life imprisonment has his punishment commuted and then the original judgment is amended". Your court replied to this issue by Fa (Yan) Fu (1989) No. 8 Reply on January 3, 1989. In the process of implementing the above Reply, we have different understandings on how the legal provisions shall be applied to revoke the commutation ruling under such circumstances. One opinion holds that after the commutation of punishment by a ruling lawfully made for a prisoner sentenced to life imprisonment, if it is discovered that the original trial judgment is indeed erroneous and the judgment is amended to fixed-term imprisonment according to law, the original commutation ruling shall be revoked. The reason for revoking the commutation ruling is that the basis for commutation no longer exists, and it may be deemed that the original commutation ruling had erroneous findings of fact. Therefore, the ruling to revoke the commutation shall apply the provisions of Article 149 of the Criminal Procedure Law. Another opinion holds that revoking the commutation ruling because the original life imprisonment judgment was indeed erroneous and amended to fixed-term imprisonment does not mean that the commutation itself was erroneous. The commutation ruling made by the judicial authority based on the prisoner's performance during sentence execution is not erroneous in itself. The reason for revoking the commutation ruling is that the original judgment has been amended, and the commutation ruling no longer has practical significance. However, revoking the commutation ruling does not fall within the scope of applying the adjudication supervision procedure, and it is unnecessary to cite Article 149 of the Criminal Procedure Law in the ruling to revoke the commutation. Currently, the Criminal Procedure Law does not provide for the revocation of the commutation ruling under such circumstances, and such provision shall be made when the law is amended in the future.  
We are not certain about how the legal provisions shall be applied to revoke the commutation ruling, and we specially request instructions from your court. We look forward to your reply.
