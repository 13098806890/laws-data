# 最高人民法院关于减刑、假释案件审理程序的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕5号  
**公布日期**：2014-04-10  
**生效日期**：2014-06-01

> 最高人民法院《关于减刑、假释案件审理程序的规定》已于2014年4月10日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1611次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年6月1日起施行。 2014年4月23日 最高人民法院 关于减刑、假释案件审理程序的规定 法释〔2014〕5号 为进一步规范减刑、假释案件的审理程序，确保减刑、假释案件审理的合法、公正，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》有关规定，结合减刑、假释案件审理工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条   对减刑、假释案件，应当按照下列情形分别处理： （一）对被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯的减刑，由罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院在收到同级监狱管理机关审核同意的减刑建议书后一个月内作出裁定； （二）对被判处无期徒刑的罪犯的减刑、假释，由罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院在收到同级监狱管理机关审核同意的减刑、假释建议书后一个月内作出裁定，案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长一个月； （三）对被判处有期徒刑和被减为有期徒刑的罪犯的减刑、假释，由罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院在收到执行机关提出的减刑、假释建议书后一个月内作出裁定，案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长一个月； （四）对被判处拘役、管制的罪犯的减刑，由罪犯服刑地中级人民法院在收到同级执行机关审核同意的减刑、假释建议书后一个月内作出裁定。 对暂予监外执行罪犯的减刑，应当根据情况，分别适用前款的有关规定。 第二条   人民法院受理减刑、假释案件，应当审查执行机关移送的下列材料： （一）减刑或者假释建议书； （二）终审法院裁判文书、执行通知书、历次减刑裁定书的复印件；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   对减刑、假释案件，应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）对被判处死刑缓期执行的罪犯的减刑，由罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院在收到同级监狱管理机关审核同意的减刑建议书后一个月内作出裁定；  
（二）对被判处无期徒刑的罪犯的减刑、假释，由罪犯服刑地的高级人民法院在收到同级监狱管理机关审核同意的减刑、假释建议书后一个月内作出裁定，案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长一个月；  
（三）对被判处有期徒刑和被减为有期徒刑的罪犯的减刑、假释，由罪犯服刑地的中级人民法院在收到执行机关提出的减刑、假释建议书后一个月内作出裁定，案情复杂或者情况特殊的，可以延长一个月；  
（四）对被判处拘役、管制的罪犯的减刑，由罪犯服刑地中级人民法院在收到同级执行机关审核同意的减刑、假释建议书后一个月内作出裁定。  
对暂予监外执行罪犯的减刑，应当根据情况，分别适用前款的有关规定。

**Article 1** Cases of commutation of punishment and parole shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) For commutation of punishment of a criminal sentenced to death sentence with a two-year reprieve, the Higher People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall make a ruling within one month after receiving the commutation proposal submitted by the prison administration authority at the same level after examination and approval;  
(2) For commutation of punishment or parole of a criminal sentenced to life imprisonment, the Higher People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall make a ruling within one month after receiving the commutation or parole proposal submitted by the prison administration authority at the same level after examination and approval; if the case is complex or the circumstances are special, the time limit may be extended by one month;  
(3) For commutation of punishment or parole of a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment or whose sentence has been commuted to fixed-term imprisonment, the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall make a ruling within one month after receiving the commutation or parole proposal submitted by the executing authority; if the case is complex or the circumstances are special, the time limit may be extended by one month;  
(4) For commutation of punishment of a criminal sentenced to criminal detention or public surveillance, the Intermediate People's Court in the place where the criminal serves his sentence shall make a ruling within one month after receiving the commutation or parole proposal submitted by the executing authority at the same level after examination and approval.  
The commutation of punishment of a criminal who is temporarily serving his sentence outside prison shall be handled by applying the relevant provisions of the preceding paragraph according to the circumstances.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   人民法院受理减刑、假释案件，应当审查执行机关移送的下列材料：  
（一）减刑或者假释建议书；  
（二）终审法院裁判文书、执行通知书、历次减刑裁定书的复印件；  
（三）罪犯确有悔改或者立功、重大立功表现的具体事实的书面证明材料；  
（四）罪犯评审鉴定表、奖惩审批表等；  
（五）其他根据案件审理需要应予移送的材料。  
报请假释的，应当附有社区矫正机构或者基层组织关于罪犯假释后对所居住社区影响的调查评估报告。  
人民检察院对报请减刑、假释案件提出检察意见的，执行机关应当一并移送受理减刑、假释案件的人民法院。  
经审查，材料齐备的，应当立案；材料不齐的，应当通知执行机关在三日内补送，逾期未补送的，不予立案。

**Article 2** When accepting a case of commutation of punishment or parole, the People's Court shall review the following materials transferred by the executing authority:  
(1) The proposal for commutation of punishment or parole;  
(2) Copies of the judgment document of the court of final instance, the execution notice, and all previous commutation rulings;  
(3) Written evidentiary materials concerning the specific facts showing the criminal's genuine repentance or meritorious or major meritorious service;  
(4) The criminal's evaluation and assessment forms, approval forms for rewards and punishments, etc.;  
(5) Other materials that shall be transferred as required for the trial of the case.  
If parole is proposed, a survey and evaluation report prepared by the community correction institution or grassroots organization on the impact of the criminal's parole on the community where he resides shall be attached.  
If the People's Procuratorate has submitted procuratorial opinions on the case of commutation of punishment or parole, the executing authority shall transfer such opinions together to the People's Court accepting the case.  
Upon review, if the materials are complete, the case shall be placed on file; if the materials are incomplete, the executing authority shall be notified to supplement them within three days; if no supplementation is made within the time limit, the case shall not be placed on file.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，应当在立案后五日内将执行机关报请减刑、假释的建议书等材料依法向社会公示。  
公示内容应当包括罪犯的个人情况、原判认定的罪名和刑期、罪犯历次减刑情况、执行机关的建议及依据。  
公示应当写明公示期限和提出意见的方式。公示期限为五日。

**Article 3** When trying a case of commutation of punishment or parole, the People's Court shall, within five days after placing the case on file, publicly announce the proposal for commutation of punishment or parole submitted by the executing authority and other materials according to law.  
The content of the announcement shall include the criminal's personal information, the crime and sentence as determined in the original judgment, the criminal's previous commutation history, the proposal of the executing authority and its basis.  
The announcement shall specify the period for public notice and the method for submitting opinions. The period for public notice shall be five days.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，应当依法由审判员或者由审判员和人民陪审员组成合议庭进行。

**Article 4** When trying a case of commutation of punishment or parole, the People's Court shall form a collegial panel composed of judges or of judges and people's assessors according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，除应当审查罪犯在执行期间的一贯表现外，还应当综合考虑犯罪的具体情节、原判刑罚情况、财产刑执行情况、附带民事裁判履行情况、罪犯退赃退赔等情况。  
人民法院审理假释案件，除应当审查第一款所列情形外，还应当综合考虑罪犯的年龄、身体状况、性格特征、假释后生活来源以及监管条件等影响再犯罪的因素。  
执行机关以罪犯有立功表现或重大立功表现为由提出减刑的，应当审查立功或重大立功表现是否属实。涉及发明创造、技术革新或者其他贡献的，应当审查该成果是否系罪犯在执行期间独立完成，并经有关主管机关确认。

**Article 5** When trying a case of commutation of punishment or parole, the People's Court shall, in addition to examining the criminal's consistent performance during the execution of the sentence, comprehensively consider the specific circumstances of the crime, the original sentence, the execution of property-related penalties, the performance of incidental civil judgments, and the criminal's restitution and return of illegal gains, among others.  
When trying a case of parole, the People's Court shall, in addition to examining the circumstances listed in the first paragraph, comprehensively consider factors affecting the likelihood of re-offending, such as the criminal's age, physical condition, personality traits, source of livelihood after parole, and supervision conditions.  
If the executing authority proposes commutation of punishment on the ground that the criminal has performed meritorious or major meritorious service, it shall examine whether such meritorious or major meritorious service is true. If it involves an invention, creation, technical innovation, or other contribution, it shall examine whether the achievement was independently completed by the criminal during the execution of the sentence and has been confirmed by the competent authority concerned.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，可以采取开庭审理或者书面审理的方式。但下列减刑、假释案件，应当开庭审理：  
（一）因罪犯有重大立功表现报请减刑的；  
（二）报请减刑的起始时间、间隔时间或者减刑幅度不符合司法解释一般规定的；  
（三）公示期间收到不同意见的；  
（四）人民检察院有异议的；  
（五）被报请减刑、假释罪犯系职务犯罪罪犯，组织（领导、参加、包庇、纵容）黑社会性质组织犯罪罪犯，破坏金融管理秩序和金融诈骗犯罪罪犯及其他在社会上有重大影响或社会关注度高的；  
（六）人民法院认为其他应当开庭审理的。

**Article 6** When trying a case of commutation of punishment or parole, the People's Court may conduct a hearing in court or conduct a written trial. However, the following cases of commutation of punishment or parole shall be heard in court:  
(1) Where commutation is proposed because the criminal has performed major meritorious service;  
(2) Where the starting time, interval, or extent of commutation proposed does not comply with the general provisions of judicial interpretations;  
(3) Where dissenting opinions are received during the period of public notice;  
(4) Where the People's Procuratorate has objections;  
(5) Where the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed is a criminal of duty crimes, a criminal who organizes (leads, participates in, harbors, or connives at) crimes of a mafia-type organization, a criminal of crimes that disrupt the order of financial management or of financial fraud, or any other criminal who has a significant social impact or attracts high public attention;  
(6) Other cases that the People's Court considers should be heard in court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   人民法院开庭审理减刑、假释案件，应当通知人民检察院、执行机关及被报请减刑、假释罪犯参加庭审。  
人民法院根据需要，可以通知证明罪犯确有悔改表现或者立功、重大立功表现的证人，公示期间提出不同意见的人，以及鉴定人、翻译人员等其他人员参加庭审。

**Article 7** When hearing a case of commutation of punishment or parole in court, the People's Court shall notify the People's Procuratorate, the executing authority, and the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed to participate in the trial.  
As needed, the People's Court may notify witnesses who can prove the criminal's genuine repentance or meritorious or major meritorious service, persons who raised dissenting opinions during the period of public notice, and other persons such as expert evaluators and interpreters to participate in the trial.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   开庭审理应当在罪犯刑罚执行场所或者人民法院确定的场所进行。有条件的人民法院可以采取视频开庭的方式进行。  
在社区执行刑罚的罪犯因重大立功被报请减刑的，可以在罪犯服刑地或者居住地开庭审理。

**Article 8** A hearing in court shall be conducted at the place where the criminal serves his sentence or at a place designated by the People's Court. A People's Court that has the necessary conditions may conduct the hearing by video.  
If a criminal serving his sentence in the community is proposed for commutation of punishment because of major meritorious service, the hearing may be conducted at the place where the criminal serves his sentence or at his place of residence.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   人民法院对于决定开庭审理的减刑、假释案件，应当在开庭三日前将开庭的时间、地点通知人民检察院、执行机关、被报请减刑、假释罪犯和有必要参加庭审的其他人员，并于开庭三日前进行公告。

**Article 9** For a case of commutation of punishment or parole that a People's Court has decided to hear in court, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate, the executing authority, the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed, and any other persons whose participation in the trial is necessary, of the time and place of the hearing three days before the hearing, and shall make a public announcement three days before the hearing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   减刑、假释案件的开庭审理由审判长主持，应当按照以下程序进行：  
（一）审判长宣布开庭，核实被报请减刑、假释罪犯的基本情况；  
（二）审判长宣布合议庭组成人员、检察人员、执行机关代表及其他庭审参加人；  
（三）执行机关代表宣读减刑、假释建议书，并说明主要理由；  
（四）检察人员发表检察意见；  
（五）法庭对被报请减刑、假释罪犯确有悔改表现或立功表现、重大立功表现的事实以及其他影响减刑、假释的情况进行调查核实；  
（六）被报请减刑、假释罪犯作最后陈述；  
（七）审判长对庭审情况进行总结并宣布休庭评议。

**Article 10** The hearing of a case of commutation of punishment or parole in court shall be presided over by the presiding judge and shall proceed according to the following procedures:  
(1) The presiding judge announces the opening of the hearing and verifies the basic information of the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed;  
(2) The presiding judge announces the members of the collegial panel, the procuratorial personnel, the representatives of the executing authority, and other participants in the trial;  
(3) The representative of the executing authority reads the proposal for commutation of punishment or parole and explains the main reasons;  
(4) The procuratorial personnel state the procuratorial opinions;  
(5) The court investigates and verifies the facts showing the criminal's genuine repentance or meritorious or major meritorious service, and other circumstances affecting commutation of punishment or parole;  
(6) The criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed makes a final statement;  
(7) The presiding judge summarizes the hearing and announces an adjournment for deliberation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   庭审过程中，合议庭人员对报请理由有疑问的，可以向被报请减刑、假释罪犯、证人、执行机关代表、检察人员提问。  
庭审过程中，检察人员对报请理由有疑问的，在经审判长许可后，可以出示证据，申请证人到庭，向被报请减刑、假释罪犯及证人提问并发表意见。被报请减刑、假释罪犯对报请理由有疑问的，在经审判长许可后，可以出示证据，申请证人到庭，向证人提问并发表意见。

**Article 11** During the trial, if a member of the collegial panel has doubts about the reasons for the proposal, he or she may question the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed, witnesses, representatives of the executing authority, and procuratorial personnel.  
During the trial, if a procuratorial personnel has doubts about the reasons for the proposal, he or she may, with the permission of the presiding judge, present evidence, apply for witnesses to appear in court, question the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed and witnesses, and express opinions. If the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed has doubts about the reasons for the proposal, he or she may, with the permission of the presiding judge, present evidence, apply for witnesses to appear in court, question witnesses, and express opinions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   庭审过程中，合议庭对证据有疑问需要进行调查核实，或者检察人员、执行机关代表提出申请的，可以宣布休庭。

**Article 12** During the trial, if the collegial panel has doubts about the evidence and needs to investigate and verify, or if the procuratorial personnel or representatives of the executing authority make an application, the panel may announce an adjournment.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条   人民法院开庭审理减刑、假释案件，能够当庭宣判的应当当庭宣判；不能当庭宣判的，可以择期宣判。

**Article 13** When hearing a case of commutation of punishment or parole in court, the People's Court shall pronounce a judgment in court if it is able to do so; if it cannot pronounce a judgment in court, it may pronounce a judgment on a later date.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条   人民法院书面审理减刑、假释案件，可以就被报请减刑、假释罪犯是否符合减刑、假释条件进行调查核实或听取有关方面意见。

**Article 14** When conducting a written trial of a case of commutation of punishment or parole, the People's Court may investigate and verify whether the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed meets the conditions for commutation or parole, or may solicit opinions from relevant parties.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条   人民法院书面审理减刑案件，可以提讯被报请减刑罪犯；书面审理假释案件，应当提讯被报请假释罪犯。

**Article 15** When conducting a written trial of a commutation case, the People's Court may interrogate the criminal for whom commutation is proposed; when conducting a written trial of a parole case, the People's Court shall interrogate the criminal for whom parole is proposed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条   人民法院审理减刑、假释案件，应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）被报请减刑、假释罪犯符合法律规定的减刑、假释条件的，作出予以减刑、假释的裁定；  
（二）被报请减刑的罪犯符合法律规定的减刑条件，但执行机关报请的减刑幅度不适当的，对减刑幅度作出相应调整后作出予以减刑的裁定；  
（三）被报请减刑、假释罪犯不符合法律规定的减刑、假释条件的，作出不予减刑、假释的裁定。  
在人民法院作出减刑、假释裁定前，执行机关书面申请撤回减刑、假释建议的，是否准许，由人民法院决定。

**Article 16** When trying a case of commutation of punishment or parole, the People's Court shall handle the case separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed meets the conditions for commutation or parole prescribed by law, a ruling shall be made to grant commutation or parole;  
(2) If the criminal for whom commutation is proposed meets the conditions for commutation prescribed by law, but the extent of commutation proposed by the executing authority is inappropriate, a ruling shall be made to grant commutation after making corresponding adjustments to the extent of commutation;  
(3) If the criminal for whom commutation or parole is proposed does not meet the conditions for commutation or parole prescribed by law, a ruling shall be made to deny commutation or parole.  
Before the People's Court makes a ruling on commutation or parole, if the executing authority applies in writing to withdraw the proposal for commutation or parole, the People's Court shall decide whether to permit such withdrawal.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条   减刑、假释裁定书应当写明罪犯原判和历次减刑情况，确有悔改表现或者立功、重大立功表现的事实和理由，以及减刑、假释的法律依据。  
裁定减刑的，应当注明刑期的起止时间；裁定假释的，应当注明假释考验期的起止时间。  
裁定调整减刑幅度或者不予减刑、假释的，应当在裁定书中说明理由。

**Article 17** A ruling on commutation of punishment or parole shall specify the criminal's original sentence and previous commutation history, the facts and reasons showing genuine repentance or meritorious or major meritorious service, and the legal basis for the commutation or parole.  
If the ruling grants commutation, it shall indicate the start and end dates of the term of imprisonment; if the ruling grants parole, it shall indicate the start and end dates of the parole supervision period.  
If the ruling adjusts the extent of commutation or denies commutation or parole, the reasons shall be stated in the ruling.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条   人民法院作出减刑、假释裁定后，应当在七日内送达报请减刑、假释的执行机关、同级人民检察院以及罪犯本人。作出假释裁定的，还应当送达社区矫正机构或者基层组织。

**Article 18** After the People's Court makes a ruling on commutation of punishment or parole, it shall serve the ruling on the executing authority that proposed the commutation or parole, the People's Procuratorate at the same level, and the criminal himself within seven days. If the ruling grants parole, it shall also be served on the community correction institution or grassroots organization.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条   减刑、假释裁定书应当通过互联网依法向社会公布。

**Article 19** A ruling on commutation of punishment or parole shall be published to the public via the Internet according to law.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条   人民检察院认为人民法院减刑、假释裁定不当，在法定期限内提出书面纠正意见的，人民法院应当在收到纠正意见后另行组成合议庭审理，并在一个月内作出裁定。

**Article 20** If the People's Procuratorate considers that a People's Court's ruling on commutation or parole is improper and submits a written corrective opinion within the statutory time limit, the People's Court shall, after receiving the corrective opinion, form a new collegial panel to try the case and shall make a ruling within one month.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条   人民法院发现本院已经生效的减刑、假释裁定确有错误的，应当依法重新组成合议庭进行审理并作出裁定；上级人民法院发现下级人民法院已经生效的减刑、假释裁定确有错误的，应当指令下级人民法院另行组成合议庭审理，也可以自行依法组成合议庭进行审理并作出裁定。

**Article 21** If a People's Court discovers that a commutation or parole ruling made by it which has already taken effect is indeed erroneous, it shall form a new collegial panel according to law to conduct a trial and make a ruling; if the People's Court at a higher level discovers that a commutation or parole ruling made by a People's Court at a lower level which has already taken effect is indeed erroneous, it shall instruct the lower People's Court to form a new collegial panel to conduct a trial, or it may itself form a collegial panel according to law to conduct a trial and make a ruling.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条   最高人民法院以前发布的司法解释和规范性文件，与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 22** If any judicial interpretations or regulatory documents previously issued by the Supreme People's Court are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
