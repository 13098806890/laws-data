# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2025〕13号  
**公布日期**：2025-08-24  
**生效日期**：2025-08-26

> （2023年3月24日最高人民法院审判委员会第1881次会议、2024年3月29日最高人民检察院第十四届检察委员会第二十八次会议通过，自2025年8月26日起施行） 为依法惩治掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益犯罪活动，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》的规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　刑法第三百一十二条规定的“犯罪所得”，是指通过犯罪得到的赃款、赃物或其他财产性利益；“犯罪所得收益”，是指通过犯罪所得获取的孳息等财产性利益。 刑法第三百一十二条规定的“其他方法”，包括任何足以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的行为手段，如居间介绍买卖，收受，持有，使用，加工，提供资金账户，将财产转换为现金、金融票据、有价证券，通过转账或者其他支付结算方式转移资金，跨境转移资产等。 第二条　刑法第三百一十二条规定的“明知”，包括知道或者应当知道。应当根据行为人所接触、接收的信息，经手他人犯罪所得及其收益的情况，犯罪所得及其收益的种类、数额，犯罪所得及其收益的转移、转换方式，交易行为、资金账户的异常情况，结合行为人的职业经历、与上游犯罪人之间的关系以及其供述和辩解等综合审查判断。 第三条　办理掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益刑事案件，应当综合考虑上游犯罪的性质和社会危害，掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的情节、后果和妨害司法秩序的程度等，依法定罪处罚。 第四条　掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益，行为人认罪认罚并积极配合追缴犯罪所得及其收益，且具有下列情形之一，情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚： （一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的； （二）为近亲属掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益，且系初犯、偶犯的； （三）配合司法机关追查上游犯罪起较大作用的； （四）其他情节轻微、危害不大的。 第五条　上游犯罪为非法采矿罪等定罪量刑数额标准相对较高的犯罪，掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的数额达到五百万元以上，且具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为刑法第三百一十二条第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）多次实施掩饰、隐瞒行为的； （二）明知掩饰、隐瞒的犯罪所得及其收益系电力设备、交通设施、广播电视设施、公用电信设施、军事设施或者救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、移民、救济、防疫、社会捐助、社会保险基金等特定款物，仍实施掩饰、隐瞒行为的； （三）拒不配合财物追缴，致使赃款、赃物无法追缴的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的“犯罪所得”，是指通过犯罪得到的赃款、赃物或其他财产性利益；“犯罪所得收益”，是指通过犯罪所得获取的孳息等财产性利益。  
[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的“其他方法”，包括任何足以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的行为手段，如居间介绍买卖，收受，持有，使用，加工，提供资金账户，将财产转换为现金、金融票据、有价证券，通过转账或者其他支付结算方式转移资金，跨境转移资产等。

**Article 1** The "proceeds of crime" as provided for in Article 312 of the Criminal Law shall refer to the illicit money, goods, or other property interests obtained through crime; the "income derived from proceeds of crime" shall refer to property interests such as the fruits obtained from the proceeds of crime.  
The "other methods" as provided for in Article 312 of the Criminal Law shall include any means sufficient to conceal or disguise the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, such as introducing or arranging for sale and purchase, accepting, possessing, using, processing, providing capital accounts, converting property into cash, financial instruments, or negotiable securities, transferring funds through transfer or other payment settlement methods, cross-border transfer of assets, etc.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的“明知”，包括知道或者应当知道。应当根据行为人所接触、接收的信息，经手他人犯罪所得及其收益的情况，犯罪所得及其收益的种类、数额，犯罪所得及其收益的转移、转换方式，交易行为、资金账户的异常情况，结合行为人的职业经历、与上游犯罪人之间的关系以及其供述和辩解等综合审查判断。

**Article 2** The "knowingly" as provided for in Article 312 of the Criminal Law shall include knowing or should have known. A determination shall be made based on a comprehensive review and assessment of the information the actor contacts or receives, the circumstances under which the actor handles the proceeds of crime and income derived therefrom of others, the types and amounts of the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, the methods of transfer or conversion of the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, the abnormal circumstances of transactions and capital accounts, combined with the actor's professional experience, the relationship with the upstream offender, and the actor's statements and defenses, etc.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　办理掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益刑事案件，应当综合考虑上游犯罪的性质和社会危害，掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的情节、后果和妨害司法秩序的程度等，依法定罪处罚。

**Article 3** In handling criminal cases of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, the nature and social harm of the upstream crime, the circumstances and consequences of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, and the degree of obstruction of judicial order shall be comprehensively considered, and a conviction and punishment shall be made according to law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益，行为人认罪认罚并积极配合追缴犯罪所得及其收益，且具有下列情形之一，情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚：  
（一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的；  
（二）为近亲属掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益，且系初犯、偶犯的；  
（三）配合司法机关追查上游犯罪起较大作用的；  
（四）其他情节轻微、危害不大的。

**Article 4** Where an actor who conceals or disguises the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom pleads guilty and accepts punishment, actively cooperates in the recovery of such proceeds and income, and the circumstances are minor, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the actor may be exempted from prosecution or exempted from criminal punishment:  
(1) Having statutory circumstances for lenient punishment;  
(2) Concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom for close relatives, and being a first-time or occasional offender;  
(3) Having played a significant role in cooperating with the judicial authorities in investigating the upstream crime;  
(4) Other circumstances where the circumstances are minor and the harm is not great.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　上游犯罪为非法采矿罪等定罪量刑数额标准相对较高的犯罪，掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的数额达到五百万元以上，且具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）多次实施掩饰、隐瞒行为的；  
（二）明知掩饰、隐瞒的犯罪所得及其收益系电力设备、交通设施、广播电视设施、公用电信设施、军事设施或者救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、移民、救济、防疫、社会捐助、社会保险基金等特定款物，仍实施掩饰、隐瞒行为的；  
（三）拒不配合财物追缴，致使赃款、赃物无法追缴的；  
（四）造成损失二百五十万元以上的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
上游犯罪为前款规定以外的其他犯罪，掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的数额达到五十万元以上，且具有前款规定第（一）（二）（三）（五）项情形之一，或者造成损失二十五万元以上的，可以认定为[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)第一款规定的“情节严重”。  
认定“情节严重”，应当注意与上游犯罪保持量刑均衡。

**Article 5** Where the upstream crime is a crime with a relatively high threshold of amount for conviction and sentencing, such as the crime of illegal mining, and the amount of the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom concealing or disguising reaches RMB 5 million or more, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it may be determined as "serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 312 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Having committed the concealing or disguising acts multiple times;  
(2) Knowing that the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom being concealed or disguised are specific goods or funds such as electric power equipment, transportation facilities, radio and television facilities, public telecommunications facilities, military facilities, or relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care, resettlement, disaster relief, epidemic prevention, social donations, social insurance funds, and still committing the concealing or disguising acts;  
(3) Refusing to cooperate with the recovery of property, resulting in the inability to recover the illicit money and goods;  
(4) Causing losses of RMB 2.5 million or more;  
(5) Other circumstances of serious circumstances.  
Where the upstream crime is a crime other than those specified in the preceding paragraph, and the amount of the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom concealing or disguising reaches RMB 500,000 or more, and falls under any of the circumstances specified in items (1), (2), (3), or (5) of the preceding paragraph, or causes losses of RMB 250,000 or more, it may be determined as "serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 312 of the Criminal Law.  
When determining "serious circumstances", attention shall be paid to maintaining a balanced sentencing with the upstream crime.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的数额，应当以实施掩饰、隐瞒行为时为准。收购或者代为销售财物的价格高于其实际价值的，以收购或者代为销售的价格计算。  
多次实施掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益行为，未经行政处罚，依法应当追诉的，犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益的数额应当累计计算。

**Article 6** The amount of the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom that are concealed or disguised shall be determined at the time of committing the concealing or disguising act. Where the price for purchasing or selling on behalf of property is higher than its actual value, the price for purchase or sale on behalf shall be used for calculation.  
Where the acts of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom are committed multiple times, and if no administrative penalty has been imposed and prosecution shall be initiated according to law, the amounts of the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　事前与盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺等行为人通谋，掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益，构成犯罪的，分别以盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺等犯罪的共犯论处。

**Article 7** Where any person colludes in advance with the perpetrator of theft, robbery, fraud, snatch, or other crimes, and conceals or disguises the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, which constitutes a crime, the person shall be convicted and punished as an accomplice to the crime of theft, robbery, fraud, snatch, or other crimes, as the case may be.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对犯罪所得及其收益实施盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺等行为，构成犯罪的，分别以盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺等犯罪定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Where any person commits theft, robbery, fraud, snatch, or other acts against the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, which constitutes a crime, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of theft, robbery, fraud, snatch, or other crimes, as the case may be.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　明知是犯罪所得及其收益而予以掩饰、隐瞒，构成掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪，同时构成其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Where any person, knowing that property is the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, conceals or disguises it, which constitutes the crime of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, and also constitutes another crime, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision that imposes a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　认定掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪，以上游犯罪事实存在为前提。  
上游犯罪事实经查证属实，但行为人尚未到案的，或者因行为人死亡、不具有刑事责任能力等原因依法不予追究刑事责任的，不影响掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪的认定。

**Article 10** The determination of the crime of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom shall be premised on the existence of the facts of the upstream crime.  
Where the facts of the upstream crime have been verified to be true, but the actor has not yet been brought to justice, or where criminal liability is not pursued according to law because the actor has died or lacks the capacity for criminal liability, this shall not affect the determination of the crime of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　单位实施掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益犯罪的，依照本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚。  
盗用单位名义实施掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益行为，违法所得由行为人私分的，依照刑法和司法解释有关自然人犯罪的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 11** Where an entity commits a crime of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, a fine shall be imposed on the entity in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards for the corresponding crime committed by a natural person as provided for in this Interpretation, and the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished.  
Where any person, under the guise of an entity's name, commits acts of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, and the illegal gains are distributed privately by the actor, the actor shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law and judicial interpretations concerning crimes committed by natural persons.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本解释自2025年8月26日起施行。《最高人民法院关于审理掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2015〕11号）及《最高人民法院关于修改〈关于审理掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释〉的决定》（法释〔2021〕8号）同时废止。最高人民法院、最高人民检察院此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 12** This Interpretation shall come into force on August 26, 2025. The Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Concealing or Disguising the Proceeds of Crime and the Income Derived Therefrom (Fa Shi [2015] No. 11) and the Decision of the Supreme People's Court on Amending the Interpretation on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Concealing or Disguising the Proceeds of Crime and the Income Derived Therefrom (Fa Shi [2021] No. 8) shall be abolished simultaneously. Where any judicial interpretations previously issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
