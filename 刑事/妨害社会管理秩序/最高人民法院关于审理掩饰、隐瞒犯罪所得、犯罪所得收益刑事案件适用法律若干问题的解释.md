# 最高人民法院关于审理掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕11号  
**公布日期**：2015-05-11  
**生效日期**：2015-06-01

> 最高人民法院《关于审理掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2015年5月11日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1651次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年6月1日起施行。 2015年5月29日 最高人民法院 关于审理掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2015〕11号 为依法惩治掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，结合人民法院刑事审判工作实际，现就审理此类案件具体适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　明知是犯罪所得及其产生的收益而予以窝藏、转移、收购、代为销售或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照刑法第三百一十二条第一款的规定，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪定罪处罚： （一）掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益价值三千元至一万元以上的； （二）一年内曾因掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为受过行政处罚，又实施掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为的； （三）掩饰、隐瞒的犯罪所得系电力设备、交通设施、广播电视设施、公用电信设施、军事设施或者救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济款物的； （四）掩饰、隐瞒行为致使上游犯罪无法及时查处，并造成公私财物损失无法挽回的； （五）实施其他掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为，妨害司法机关对上游犯罪进行追究的。 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以根据本地区经济社会发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在本条第一款第（一）项规定的数额幅度内，确定本地执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院备案。 司法解释对掩饰、隐瞒涉及计算机信息系统数据、计算机信息系统控制权的犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为构成犯罪已有规定的，审理此类案件依照该规定。 依照全国人民代表大会常务委员会《关于〈中华人民共和国刑法〉第三百四十一条、第三百一十二条的解释》,明知是非法狩猎的野生动物而收购，数量达到五十只以上的，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得罪定罪处罚。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　明知是犯罪所得及其产生的收益而予以窝藏、转移、收购、代为销售或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)第一款的规定，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪定罪处罚：  
（一）掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益价值三千元至一万元以上的；  
（二）一年内曾因掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为受过行政处罚，又实施掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为的；  
（三）掩饰、隐瞒的犯罪所得系电力设备、交通设施、广播电视设施、公用电信设施、军事设施或者救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济款物的；  
（四）掩饰、隐瞒行为致使上游犯罪无法及时查处，并造成公私财物损失无法挽回的；  
（五）实施其他掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为，妨害司法机关对上游犯罪进行追究的。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以根据本地区经济社会发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在本条第一款第（一）项规定的数额幅度内，确定本地执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院备案。  
司法解释对掩饰、隐瞒涉及计算机信息系统数据、计算机信息系统控制权的犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为构成犯罪已有规定的，审理此类案件依照该规定。  
依照全国人民代表大会常务委员会《关于〈中华人民共和国刑法〉第三百四十一条、第三百一十二条的解释》,明知是非法狩猎的野生动物而收购，数量达到五十只以上的，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得罪定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Whoever knowingly harbors, transfers, purchases, sells on commission, or disguises or conceals by other means the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom, in any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom in accordance with the provisions of Article 312, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The value of the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom that are disguised or concealed is 3,000 yuan or more but less than 10,000 yuan;  
(2) Having received an administrative penalty within one year for the act of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom, the person again commits the act of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom;  
(3) The proceeds of crime disguised or concealed are funds or property for electric power equipment, transportation facilities, radio and television facilities, public telecommunications facilities, military facilities, or for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care, poverty alleviation, resettlement, or relief;  
(4) The act of disguising or concealing causes the predicate offense to be unable to be promptly investigated and prosecuted, and results in irreparable losses to public or private property;  
(5) Committing other acts of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom that obstruct the judicial authorities from pursuing the predicate offense.  
The Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the economic and social development of their respective regions and taking into account the public security situation, determine the specific amount standard for local enforcement within the range of amounts specified in item (1) of paragraph 1 of this Article, and shall report it to the Supreme People's Court for record.  
Where judicial interpretations already contain provisions on the act of disguising or concealing proceeds of crime and benefits derived therefrom involving computer information system data or computer information system control rights as constituting a crime, the adjudication of such cases shall be governed by those provisions.  
In accordance with the Interpretation of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Articles 341 and 312 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, whoever knowingly purchases illegally hunted wildlife, with the number reaching 50 or more, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为符合[本解释第一条](#art-1)的规定，认罪、悔罪并退赃、退赔，且具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，免予刑事处罚：  
（一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的；  
（二）为近亲属掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益，且系初犯、偶犯的；  
（三）有其他情节轻微情形的。  
行为人为自用而掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得，财物价值刚达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)第一款第（一）项规定的标准，认罪、悔罪并退赃、退赔的，一般可不认为是犯罪；依法追究刑事责任的，应当酌情从宽。

**Article 2** Where the act of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom meets the provisions of Article 1 of this Interpretation, and the perpetrator confesses guilt, shows remorse, returns the stolen goods and compensates for losses, and has any of the following circumstances, the crime may be deemed as minor and the perpetrator may be granted exemption from criminal punishment:  
(1) Having a statutory circumstance for lenient punishment;  
(2) Disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom for a close relative, and being a first-time offender or occasional offender;  
(3) Having other circumstances of minor nature.  
Where a person disguises or conceals proceeds of crime for personal use, and the value of the property just reaches the standard specified in item (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 1 of this Interpretation, and the person confesses guilt, shows remorse, returns the stolen goods and compensates for losses, it generally may not be deemed a crime; if criminal liability is investigated according to law, leniency shall be considered as appropriate.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益价值总额达到十万元以上的；  
（二）掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益十次以上，或者三次以上且价值总额达到五万元以上的；  
（三）掩饰、隐瞒的犯罪所得系电力设备、交通设施、广播电视设施、公用电信设施、军事设施或者救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济款物，价值总额达到五万元以上的；  
（四）掩饰、隐瞒行为致使上游犯罪无法及时查处，并造成公私财物重大损失无法挽回或其他严重后果的；  
（五）实施其他掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为，严重妨害司法机关对上游犯罪予以追究的。  
司法解释对掩饰、隐瞒涉及机动车、计算机信息系统数据、计算机信息系统控制权的犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为认定“情节严重”已有规定的，审理此类案件依照该规定。

**Article 3** Disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as provided in Article 312, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The total value of the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom that are disguised or concealed reaches 100,000 yuan or more;  
(2) The proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom are disguised or concealed ten times or more, or three times or more and the total value reaches 50,000 yuan or more;  
(3) The proceeds of crime disguised or concealed are funds or property for electric power equipment, transportation facilities, radio and television facilities, public telecommunications facilities, military facilities, or for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care, poverty alleviation, resettlement, or relief, and the total value reaches 50,000 yuan or more;  
(4) The act of disguising or concealing causes the predicate offense to be unable to be promptly investigated and prosecuted, and results in major irreparable losses to public or private property or other serious consequences;  
(5) Committing other acts of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom that seriously obstruct the judicial authorities from pursuing the predicate offense.  
Where judicial interpretations already contain provisions on the act of disguising or concealing proceeds of crime and benefits derived therefrom involving motor vehicles, computer information system data, or computer information system control rights as constituting “serious circumstances”, the adjudication of such cases shall be governed by those provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益的数额，应当以实施掩饰、隐瞒行为时为准。收购或者代为销售财物的价格高于其实际价值的，以收购或者代为销售的价格计算。  
多次实施掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为，未经行政处罚，依法应当追诉的，犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益的数额应当累计计算。

**Article 4** The amount of the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom that are disguised or concealed shall be determined as of the time when the act of disguising or concealing is committed. Where the price at which the property is purchased or sold on commission is higher than its actual value, the calculation shall be based on the purchase or commission sale price.  
Where multiple acts of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom have been committed and, without having been subjected to administrative penalty, shall be prosecuted according to law, the amounts of the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　事前与盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺等犯罪分子通谋，掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益的，以盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺等犯罪的共犯论处。

**Article 5** Whoever, in prior collusion with criminals committing theft, robbery, fraud, snatching, or other such crimes, disguises or conceals the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom, shall be punished as an accomplice in the crime of theft, robbery, fraud, snatching, or the like.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　对犯罪所得及其产生的收益实施盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺等行为，构成犯罪的，分别以盗窃罪、抢劫罪、诈骗罪、抢夺罪等定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Whoever commits theft, robbery, fraud, snatching, or other acts against the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be convicted and punished respectively for the crime of theft, robbery, fraud, snatching, or the like.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　明知是犯罪所得及其产生的收益而予以掩饰、隐瞒，构成[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的犯罪，同时构成其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Whoever knowingly disguises or conceals the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom, if the act constitutes the crime provided in Article 312 of the Criminal Law and also constitutes another crime, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision providing for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　认定掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪，以上游犯罪事实成立为前提。上游犯罪尚未依法裁判，但查证属实的，不影响掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪的认定。  
上游犯罪事实经查证属实，但因行为人未达到刑事责任年龄等原因依法不予追究刑事责任的，不影响掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪的认定。

**Article 8** The conviction of the crime of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom shall be based on the establishment of the facts of the predicate offense. Where the predicate offense has not yet been adjudicated according to law, but the facts are verified, it shall not affect the conviction of the crime of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom.  
Where the facts of the predicate offense are verified, but criminal liability is not investigated according to law due to reasons such as the actor not having reached the age of criminal responsibility, it shall not affect the conviction of the crime of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　盗用单位名义实施掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其产生的收益行为，违法所得由行为人私分的，依照刑法和司法解释有关自然人犯罪的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Where a person usurps the name of a unit to commit the act of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom, and the illegal gains from such act are distributed among the actors in private, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law and judicial interpretations on crimes committed by natural persons.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　通过犯罪直接得到的赃款、赃物，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的“犯罪所得”。上游犯罪的行为人对犯罪所得进行处理后得到的孳息、租金等，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的“犯罪所得产生的收益”。  
明知是犯罪所得及其产生的收益而采取窝藏、转移、收购、代为销售以外的方法，如居间介绍买卖，收受，持有，使用，加工，提供资金账户，协助将财物转换为现金、金融票据、有价证券，协助将资金转移、汇往境外等，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的“其他方法”。

**Article 10** The illicit money and goods directly obtained through the commission of a crime shall be deemed as “proceeds of crime” as provided in Article 312 of the Criminal Law. The fruits, rent, and the like obtained by the actor of the predicate offense after processing the proceeds of crime shall be deemed as “benefits derived from the proceeds of crime” as provided in Article 312 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person, knowing that the property is proceeds of crime and benefits derived therefrom, adopts methods other than harboring, transferring, purchasing, or selling on commission, such as introducing purchase or sale, accepting, possessing, using, processing, providing a fund account, assisting in converting the property into cash, financial bills, or negotiable securities, or assisting in transferring or remitting funds abroad, such methods shall be deemed as “other methods” as provided in Article 312 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪是选择性罪名，审理此类案件，应当根据具体犯罪行为及其指向的对象，确定适用的罪名。

**Article 11** The crime of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom is a selective charge. In adjudicating such cases, the applicable charge shall be determined based on the specific criminal act and the object thereof.
