# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理破坏黑土地资源刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2025〕7号  
**公布日期**：2025-04-30  
**生效日期**：2025-05-06

> （2025年3月25日最高人民法院审判委员会第1946次会议、2025年4月11日最高人民检察院第十四届检察委员会第五十一次会议通过，自2025年5月6日起施行） 为依法保护黑土地，惩治破坏黑土地资源犯罪，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国黑土地保护法》等法律的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　违反土地管理法规，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百四十二条规定的非法占用耕地，改变被占用耕地用途，造成耕地毁坏： （一）非法占用黑土地实施建房、建窑、建坟、挖湖造景等工程建设的； （二）非法占用黑土地实施挖砂、采石、采矿、取土等活动的； （三）非法占用黑土地排放污染物、堆放废弃物等造成黑土地被严重污染的； （四）其他非法占用黑土地，改变被占用黑土地用途，造成黑土地毁坏的情形。 实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百四十二条规定的“数量较大，造成耕地、林地等农用地大量毁坏”： （一）非法占用并毁坏属于永久基本农田的黑土地三亩以上，或者非法占用并毁坏永久基本农田以外的黑土地六亩以上的； （二）非法在属于永久基本农田的黑土地上取黑土五百立方米以上，或者非法在永久基本农田以外的黑土地上取黑土一千立方米以上的； （三）非法占用并毁坏黑土地，或者非法在黑土地上取黑土，数量虽未分别达到第一项、第二项规定标准，但按相应标准的比例折算合计达到标准的； （四）二年内曾因非法占用农用地受过二次以上行政处罚，又非法占用并毁坏黑土地，数量达到第一项至第三项规定标准一半以上的。 第二条　违反矿产资源法的规定，非法开采属于矿产资源的黑土，符合刑法第三百四十三条第一款规定的，以非法采矿罪定罪处罚。 第三条　违反国家规定，排放、倾倒或者处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质或者其他有害物质，严重污染黑土地，符合刑法第三百三十八条第一款规定的，以污染环境罪定罪处罚。 实施前款行为，致使属于永久基本农田的黑土地六亩以上或者永久基本农田以外的黑土地十二亩以上基本功能丧失或者遭受永久性破坏的，应当认定为刑法第三百三十八条第一款规定的“情节严重”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　违反土地管理法规，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-342)规定的非法占用耕地，改变被占用耕地用途，造成耕地毁坏：  
（一）非法占用黑土地实施建房、建窑、建坟、挖湖造景等工程建设的；  
（二）非法占用黑土地实施挖砂、采石、采矿、取土等活动的；  
（三）非法占用黑土地排放污染物、堆放废弃物等造成黑土地被严重污染的；  
（四）其他非法占用黑土地，改变被占用黑土地用途，造成黑土地毁坏的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-342)规定的“数量较大，造成耕地、林地等农用地大量毁坏”：  
（一）非法占用并毁坏属于永久基本农田的黑土地三亩以上，或者非法占用并毁坏永久基本农田以外的黑土地六亩以上的；  
（二）非法在属于永久基本农田的黑土地上取黑土五百立方米以上，或者非法在永久基本农田以外的黑土地上取黑土一千立方米以上的；  
（三）非法占用并毁坏黑土地，或者非法在黑土地上取黑土，数量虽未分别达到第一项、第二项规定标准，但按相应标准的比例折算合计达到标准的；  
（四）二年内曾因非法占用农用地受过二次以上行政处罚，又非法占用并毁坏黑土地，数量达到第一项至第三项规定标准一半以上的。

**Article 1** Where anyone violates laws and regulations on land administration and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as illegal occupation of cultivated land, changing the use of the occupied cultivated land, and causing damage to cultivated land as prescribed in Article 342 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally occupying black soil land to carry out construction projects such as building houses, kilns, tombs, or creating lakes and landscapes;  
(2) illegally occupying black soil land to carry out activities such as sand excavation, quarrying, mining, or earth extraction;  
(3) illegally occupying black soil land to discharge pollutants, pile up waste, etc., causing severe pollution to black soil land;  
(4) other circumstances of illegally occupying black soil land, changing the use of the occupied black soil land, and causing damage to black soil land.  
Where any act specified in the preceding paragraph falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "a relatively large quantity, causing extensive damage to cultivated land, forest land, and other agricultural land" as prescribed in Article 342 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally occupying and damaging three mu or more of black soil land that is permanent basic farmland, or illegally occupying and damaging six mu or more of black soil land other than permanent basic farmland;  
(2) illegally extracting five hundred cubic meters or more of black soil from black soil land that is permanent basic farmland, or illegally extracting one thousand cubic meters or more of black soil from black soil land other than permanent basic farmland;  
(3) illegally occupying and damaging black soil land, or illegally extracting black soil from black soil land, where the quantity does not separately reach the standards specified in subparagraphs (1) and (2), but the total converted according to the proportions of the corresponding standards reaches the standard;  
(4) having been subjected to two or more administrative penalties for illegally occupying agricultural land within the past two years, and again illegally occupying and damaging black soil land, with the quantity reaching more than half of the standards specified in subparagraphs (1) to (3).

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　违反矿产资源法的规定，非法开采属于矿产资源的黑土，符合[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款规定的，以非法采矿罪定罪处罚。

**Article 2** Where anyone violates the provisions of the Mineral Resources Law and illegally mines black soil that belongs to mineral resources, and meets the requirements of paragraph 1 of Article 343 of the Criminal Law, it shall be convicted and punished as the crime of illegal mining.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　违反国家规定，排放、倾倒或者处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质或者其他有害物质，严重污染黑土地，符合[刑法第三百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-338)第一款规定的，以污染环境罪定罪处罚。  
实施前款行为，致使属于永久基本农田的黑土地六亩以上或者永久基本农田以外的黑土地十二亩以上基本功能丧失或者遭受永久性破坏的，应当认定为[刑法第三百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-338)第一款规定的“情节严重”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
实施第一款行为，致使属于永久基本农田的黑土地三十亩以上基本功能丧失或者遭受永久性破坏的，应当处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金。

**Article 3** Where anyone violates state regulations by discharging, dumping, or disposing of radioactive waste, waste containing infectious disease pathogens, toxic substances, or other harmful substances, causing severe pollution to black soil land, and meets the requirements of paragraph 1 of Article 338 of the Criminal Law, it shall be convicted and punished as the crime of environmental pollution.  
Where the act specified in the preceding paragraph causes the loss of basic functions or permanent damage to six mu or more of black soil land that is permanent basic farmland, or to twelve mu or more of black soil land other than permanent basic farmland, it shall be determined as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 338 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined.  
Where the act specified in the first paragraph causes the loss of basic functions or permanent damage to thirty mu or more of black soil land that is permanent basic farmland, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years, and shall also be fined.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条或者第三条规定的行为，同时构成非法占用农用地罪、非法采矿罪或者污染环境罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where any act specified in Article 1, Article 2, or Article 3 of this Interpretation simultaneously constitutes the crime of illegal occupation of agricultural land, the crime of illegal mining, or the crime of environmental pollution, or other crimes, it shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions providing for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　明知是犯罪所得的黑土及其产生的收益，而予以窝藏、转移、收购、代为销售或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒，符合[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪妥当定罪量刑。  
实施前款规定的犯罪行为，事前通谋的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 5** Where anyone, knowing that black soil and its proceeds are obtained from crimes, harbors, transfers, purchases, sells on commission, or uses other methods to conceal or hide them, and meets the requirements of Article 312 of the Criminal Law, it shall be properly convicted and sentenced as the crime of concealing or hiding criminal proceeds and proceeds from crimes.  
Where any criminal act specified in the preceding paragraph is committed with prior collusion, it shall be dealt with as a joint crime.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　实施破坏黑土地资源犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）在行政主管部门作出责令停止违法行为、限期改正等行政处理决定后继续实施相关行为的；  
（二）暴力抗拒、阻碍国家机关工作人员依法执行职务的；  
（三）向国家工作人员行贿的。  
实施前款第二项、第三项规定的行为，同时构成妨害公务罪、袭警罪、行贿罪等其他犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。

**Article 6** Where any crime of destroying black soil resources is committed with any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment shall be imposed:  
(1) continuing to commit the relevant acts after the competent administrative department has made an administrative decision to order the cessation of the illegal act or to make corrections within a time limit;  
(2) violently resisting or obstructing state functionaries from performing their duties according to law;  
(3) bribing state functionaries.  
Where any act specified in subparagraphs (2) and (3) of the preceding paragraph simultaneously constitutes the crime of obstructing public service, the crime of assaulting police, the crime of bribery, or other crimes, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions on joinder of penalties for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　组织他人实施破坏黑土地资源犯罪的，应当按照其组织实施的全部罪行处罚。  
对于受雇佣为破坏黑土地资源犯罪提供劳务的人员，除曾因破坏土地资源受过刑事处罚的以外，一般不以犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Where anyone organizes others to commit a crime of destroying black soil resources, he shall be punished according to all the crimes he organizes and implements.  
For a person hired to provide labor services for the crime of destroying black soil resources, unless he has previously received criminal punishment for destroying land resources, he shall generally not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　单位实施破坏黑土地资源犯罪的，依照本解释规定的定罪量刑标准，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。

**Article 8** Where a unit commits a crime of destroying black soil resources, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished according to the standards for conviction and sentencing prescribed in this Interpretation, and the unit shall be fined.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　多次实施破坏黑土地资源犯罪，依法应当追诉的，或者二年内多次实施破坏黑土地资源行为未经处理的，数量、数额累计计算。

**Article 9** Where a crime of destroying black soil resources is committed multiple times and shall be prosecuted according to law, or where acts of destroying black soil resources are committed multiple times within two years without being dealt with, the quantities and amounts shall be cumulatively calculated.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　实施破坏黑土地资源的行为，在认定是否构成犯罪以及裁量刑罚时，应当综合考虑行为动机目的、对黑土地资源的破坏程度、是否积极履行生态环境修复责任等情节，综合评估社会危害性，准确认定是否构成犯罪，妥当裁量刑罚，确保罪责刑相适应。

**Article 10** Where an act of destroying black soil resources is committed, in determining whether a crime is constituted and in sentencing, the motives and purposes of the act, the degree of damage to black soil resources, whether the ecological environment restoration responsibility is actively performed, and other circumstances shall be comprehensively considered, the social harmfulness shall be comprehensively assessed, whether a crime is constituted shall be accurately determined, and the punishment shall be properly measured to ensure that the crime, responsibility, and penalty are proportionate.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于实施破坏黑土地资源的相关行为被不起诉、宣告无罪或者免予刑事处罚的行为人，依法应当给予行政处罚、政务处分或者其他处分的，依法移送有关主管机关处理。

**Article 11** Where a person who has committed relevant acts of destroying black soil resources is not prosecuted, is acquitted, or is exempted from criminal punishment, and administrative penalty, disciplinary sanction, or other sanctions shall be imposed according to law, the case shall be transferred to the relevant competent authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对于涉案黑土地类型、面积，涉案黑土体积、数量、价值等专门性事实问题，可以依据鉴定机构出具的鉴定意见，或者农业农村、自然资源、水行政、生态环境等主管部门出具的认定意见，或者价格认证机构、国务院相关主管部门指定或推荐的机构、地市级以上人民政府相关主管部门出具的报告，结合其他证据作出认定。

**Article 12** For specialized factual issues such as the type and area of black soil land involved, the volume, quantity, and value of black soil involved, etc., determination may be made based on the expert evaluation opinion issued by an expert evaluation institution, or the determination opinion issued by the competent departments of agriculture and rural affairs, natural resources, water administration, ecological environment, etc., or the report issued by a price certification institution, an institution designated or recommended by the relevant competent department of the State Council, or the relevant competent department of the people's government at or above the prefectural level, combined with other evidence.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本解释所称“黑土地”，是指[《中华人民共和国黑土地保护法》第二条](../../行政与公法/生态环境/中华人民共和国黑土地保护法.md#art-2)第二款所规定的黑龙江省、吉林省、辽宁省、内蒙古自治区的相关区域范围内具有黑色或者暗黑色腐殖质表土层，性状好、肥力高的耕地。  
盗挖、滥挖、非法买卖前款规定相关区域的林地、草原、湿地、河湖等范围内农用地的黑土，参照本解释的规定定罪量刑。

**Article 13** For the purpose of this Interpretation, "black soil land" means cultivated land with black or dark black humus topsoil, good properties, and high fertility within the relevant regions of Heilongjiang Province, Jilin Province, Liaoning Province, and Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region as specified in paragraph 2 of Article 2 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Black Soil.  
For the acts of stealing, overmining, or illegally trading black soil from agricultural land within forest land, grassland, wetland, rivers, and lakes within the regions specified in the preceding paragraph, conviction and sentencing shall be made by reference to the provisions of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　本解释自2025年5月6日起施行。

**Article 14** This Interpretation shall come into force as of May 6, 2025.
