# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理非法利用信息网络、帮助信息网络犯罪活动等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理非法利用信息网络、帮助信息网络犯罪活动等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2019 年6月3 日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1771 次会议、2019 年9 月4 日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第二十三次会议通过，现予公布，自2019 年11 月1 日起施行。 2019 年10 月21 日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理非法利用信息网络、帮助信息网络犯罪活动等 刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2019〕15 号 为依法惩治拒不履行信息网络安全管理义务、非法利用信息网络、帮助信息网络犯罪活动等犯罪，维护正常网络秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条     提供下列服务的单位和个人， 应当认定为刑法第二百八十六条之一第一款规定的“网络服务提供者”： （一）网络接入、域名注册解析等信息网络接入、计算、存储、传输服务； （二）信息发布、搜索引擎、即时通讯、网络支付、网络预约、网络购物、网络游戏、网络直播、网站建设、安全防护、广告推广、应用商店等信息网络应用服务； （三）利用信息网络提供的电子政务、通信、能源、交通、水利、金融、教育、医疗等公共服务。 第二条     刑法第二百八十六条之一第一款规定的“ 监管部门责令采取改正措施”，是指网信、电信、公安等依照法律、行政法规的规定承担信息网络安全监管职责的部门，以责令整改通知书或者其他文书形式，责令网络服务提供者采取改正措施。 认定“经监管部门责令采取改正措施而拒不改正”，应当综合考虑监管部门责令改正是否具有法律、行政法规依据，改正措施及期限要求是否明确、合理，网络服务提供者是否具有按照要求采取改正措施的能力等因素进行判断。 第三条     拒不履行信息网络安全管理义务，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百八十六条之一第一款第一项规定的“致使违法信息大量传播”： （一）致使传播违法视频文件二百个以上的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条     提供下列服务的单位和个人， 应当认定为[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)之一第一款规定的“网络服务提供者”：  
（一）网络接入、域名注册解析等信息网络接入、计算、存储、传输服务；  
（二）信息发布、搜索引擎、即时通讯、网络支付、网络预约、网络购物、网络游戏、网络直播、网站建设、安全防护、广告推广、应用商店等信息网络应用服务；  
（三）利用信息网络提供的电子政务、通信、能源、交通、水利、金融、教育、医疗等公共服务。

**Article 1** : The units and individuals that provide the following services shall be deemed as "network service providers" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 286-1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Network access, domain name registration and resolution, and other information network access, computing, storage, and transmission services;  
(2) Information publishing, search engines, instant messaging, online payment, online booking, online shopping, online games, live streaming, website construction, security protection, advertising promotion, application stores, and other information network application services;  
(3) Electronic government affairs, communication, energy, transportation, water conservancy, finance, education, medical care, and other public services provided by using information networks.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条     [刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)之一第一款规定的“ 监管部门责令采取改正措施”，是指网信、电信、公安等依照法律、行政法规的规定承担信息网络安全监管职责的部门，以责令整改通知书或者其他文书形式，责令网络服务提供者采取改正措施。  
认定“经监管部门责令采取改正措施而拒不改正”，应当综合考虑监管部门责令改正是否具有法律、行政法规依据，改正措施及期限要求是否明确、合理，网络服务提供者是否具有按照要求采取改正措施的能力等因素进行判断。

**Article 2** : The "regulatory authority orders the adoption of corrective measures" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 286-1 of the Criminal Law refers to the departments responsible for information network security supervision in accordance with laws and administrative regulations, such as the Cyberspace Administration, the telecommunications authority, and the public security authority, ordering the network service provider to adopt corrective measures in the form of a notice ordering rectification or other documents.  
To determine "failing to correct despite being ordered by the regulatory authority to adopt corrective measures", comprehensive consideration shall be given to factors such as whether the regulatory order has a legal or administrative basis, whether the corrective measures and time limits are clear and reasonable, and whether the network service provider has the ability to adopt corrective measures as required.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条     拒不履行信息网络安全管理义务，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)之一第一款第一项规定的“致使违法信息大量传播”：  
（一）致使传播违法视频文件二百个以上的；  
（二）致使传播违法视频文件以外的其他违法信息二千个以上的；  
（三）致使传播违法信息，数量虽未达到第一项、第二项规定标准，但是按相应比例折算合计达到有关数量标准的；  
（四）致使向二千个以上用户账号传播违法信息的；  
（五）致使利用群组成员账号数累计三千以上的通讯群组或者关注人员账号数累计三万以上的社交网络传播违法信息的；  
（六）致使违法信息实际被点击数达到五万以上的；  
（七）其他致使违法信息大量传播的情形。

**Article 3** : Refusing to perform information network security management obligations, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "causing illegal information to be disseminated in large quantities" as prescribed in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 286-1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing the dissemination of 200 or more illegal video files;  
(2) Causing the dissemination of 2,000 or more items of illegal information other than illegal video files;  
(3) Causing the dissemination of illegal information in quantities that, although not reaching the standards set forth in subparagraphs (1) and (2), when converted proportionally, reach the relevant quantity standards;  
(4) Causing the dissemination of illegal information to 2,000 or more user accounts;  
(5) Causing the dissemination of illegal information through communication groups with a total of 3,000 or more group member accounts, or through social networks with a total of 30,000 or more follower accounts;  
(6) Causing the actual number of clicks on illegal information to reach 50,000 or more;  
(7) Other circumstances causing illegal information to be disseminated in large quantities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条     拒不履行信息网络安全管理义务，致使用户信息泄露，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)之一第一款第二项规定的“造成严重后果”：  
（一）致使泄露行踪轨迹信息、通信内容、征信信息、财产信息五百条以上的；  
（二）致使泄露住宿信息、通信记录、健康生理信息、交易信息等其他可能影响人身、财产安全的用户信息五千条以上的；  
（三）致使泄露第一项、第二项规定以外的用户信息五万条以上的；  
（四）数量虽未达到第一项至第三项规定标准，但是按相应比例折算合计达到有关数量标准的；  
（五）造成他人死亡、重伤、精神失常或者被绑架等严重后果的；  
（六）造成重大经济损失的；  
（七）严重扰乱社会秩序的；  
（八）造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 4** : Refusing to perform information network security management obligations, causing the disclosure of user information, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "causing serious consequences" as prescribed in subparagraph (2) of paragraph 1 of Article 286-1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing the disclosure of 500 or more pieces of whereabouts tracking information, communication content, credit information, or property information;  
(2) Causing the disclosure of 5,000 or more pieces of user information such as accommodation information, communication records, health and physiological information, transaction information, or other information that may affect personal or property safety;  
(3) Causing the disclosure of 50,000 or more pieces of user information other than those specified in subparagraphs (1) and (2);  
(4) Where the quantities, although not reaching the standards set forth in subparagraphs (1) to (3), when converted proportionally, reach the relevant quantity standards;  
(5) Causing serious consequences such as death, serious injury, mental disorder, or kidnapping of others;  
(6) Causing significant economic losses;  
(7) Seriously disrupting social order;  
(8) Causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条     拒不履行信息网络安全管理义务，致使影响定罪量刑的刑事案件证据灭失，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)之一第一款第三项规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）造成危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质组织犯罪、贪污贿赂犯罪案件的证据灭失的；  
（二）造成可能判处五年有期徒刑以上刑罚犯罪案件的证据灭失的；  
（三）多次造成刑事案件证据灭失的；  
（四）致使刑事诉讼程序受到严重影响的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 5** : Refusing to perform information network security management obligations, causing the loss of evidence in criminal cases that affects conviction and sentencing, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in subparagraph (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 286-1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing the loss of evidence in cases of crimes endangering national security, terrorist activities, organized crimes of a mafia nature, or embezzlement and bribery;  
(2) Causing the loss of evidence in criminal cases that may result in a sentence of five years or more of fixed-term imprisonment;  
(3) Repeatedly causing the loss of evidence in criminal cases;  
(4) Seriously affecting criminal proceedings;  
(5) Other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条     拒不履行信息网络安全管理义务，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)之一第一款第四项规定的“有其他严重情节”：  
（一）对绝大多数用户日志未留存或者未落实真实身份信息认证义务的；  
（二）二年内经多次责令改正拒不改正的；  
（三）致使信息网络服务被主要用于违法犯罪的；  
（四）致使信息网络服务、网络设施被用于实施网络攻击，严重影响生产、生活的；  
（五）致使信息网络服务被用于实施危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质组织犯罪、贪污贿赂犯罪或者其他重大犯罪的；  
（六）致使国家机关或者通信、能源、交通、水利、金融、教育、医疗等领域提供公共 服务的信息网络受到破坏，严重影响生产、生活的；  
（七）其他严重违反信息网络安全管理义务的情形。

**Article 6** : Refusing to perform information network security management obligations, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "having other serious circumstances" as prescribed in subparagraph (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 286-1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Failing to retain logs for the vast majority of users or failing to fulfill the obligation of real-name identity information authentication;  
(2) Having been repeatedly ordered to correct within two years but refusing to correct;  
(3) Causing information network services to be mainly used for illegal or criminal activities;  
(4) Causing information network services or network facilities to be used for launching cyber attacks, seriously affecting production or daily life;  
(5) Causing information network services to be used for committing crimes endangering national security, terrorist activities, organized crimes of a mafia nature, embezzlement and bribery, or other major crimes;  
(6) Causing damage to information networks that provide public services in state organs or in fields such as communication, energy, transportation, water conservancy, finance, education, medical care, seriously affecting production or daily life;  
(7) Other circumstances that seriously violate information network security management obligations.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条     [刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一规定的“违法犯罪”，包括犯罪行为和属于刑法分则规定的行为类型但尚未构成犯罪的违法行为。

**Article 7** : "Illegal or criminal activities" as prescribed in Article 287-1 of the Criminal Law includes criminal acts and illegal acts that fall under the types of conduct stipulated in the Specific Provisions of the Criminal Law but have not yet constituted a crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条     以实施违法犯罪活动为目的而设立或者设立后主要用于实施违法犯罪活动的网站、通讯群组，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一第一款第一项规定的  “用于实施诈骗、传授犯罪方法、制作或者销售违禁物品、管制物品等违法犯罪活动的网站、通讯群组”。

**Article 8** : Websites or communication groups established for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities, or mainly used for committing illegal or criminal activities after establishment, shall be deemed as "websites or communication groups used for committing illegal or criminal activities such as fraud, imparting criminal methods, producing or selling prohibited articles, controlled articles, etc." as prescribed in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 287-1 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条     利用信息网络提供信息的链接、截屏、二维码、访问账号密码及其他指引访问服务的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一第一款第二项、第三项规定的“发布信息”。

**Article 9** : Providing links, screenshots, QR codes, access account passwords, or other referral access services for information by using information networks shall be deemed as "publishing information" as prescribed in subparagraphs (2) and (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 287-1 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条     非法利用信息网络，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）假冒国家机关、金融机构名义，设立用于实施违法犯罪活动的网站的；  
（二）设立用于实施违法犯罪活动的网站，数量达到三个以上或者注册账号数累计达到二千以上的；  
（三）设立用于实施违法犯罪活动的通讯群组，数量达到五个以上或者群组成员账号数累计达到一千以上的；  
（四）发布有关违法犯罪的信息或者为实施违法犯罪活动发布信息，具有下列情形之一的：  
1.在网站上发布有关信息一百条以上的；  
2.向二千个以上用户账号发送有关信息的；  
3.向群组成员数累计达到三千以上的通讯群组发送有关信息的；  
4.利用关注人员账号数累计达到三万以上的社交网络传播有关信息的；  
（五）违法所得一万元以上的；  
（六）二年内曾因非法利用信息网络、帮助信息网络犯罪活动、危害计算机信息系统安全受过行政处罚，又非法利用信息网络的；  
（七）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 10** : Illegally using information networks, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 287-1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Establishing a website in the name of a state organ or financial institution for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities;  
(2) Establishing three or more websites, or websites with a total of 2,000 or more registered accounts, for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities;  
(3) Establishing five or more communication groups, or communication groups with a total of 1,000 or more group member accounts, for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities;  
(4) Publishing information about illegal or criminal activities, or publishing information for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities, in any of the following circumstances:  
1. Publishing 100 or more items of relevant information on a website;  
2. Sending relevant information to 2,000 or more user accounts;  
3. Sending relevant information to communication groups with a total of 3,000 or more group members;  
4. Disseminating relevant information through social networks with a total of 30,000 or more follower accounts;  
(5) Illegally obtaining 10,000 RMB or more in proceeds;  
(6) Having received an administrative penalty within two years for illegally using information networks, aiding information network criminal activities, or endangering computer information system security, and again illegally using information networks;  
(7) Other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条     为他人实施犯罪提供技术支持或者帮助，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定行为人明知他人利用信息网络实施犯罪，但是有相反证据的除外：  
（一）经监管部门告知后仍然实施有关行为的；  
（二）接到举报后不履行法定管理职责的；  
（三）交易价格或者方式明显异常的；  
（四）提供专门用于违法犯罪的程序、工具或者其他技术支持、帮助的；  
（五）频繁采用隐蔽上网、加密通信、销毁数据等措施或者使用虚假身份，逃避监管或者规避调查的；  
（六）为他人逃避监管或者规避调查提供技术支持、帮助的；  
（七）其他足以认定行为人明知的情形。

**Article 11** : Where a person provides technical support or assistance to another person for the commission of a crime, in any of the following circumstances, it may be determined that the actor knows that the other person is using an information network to commit a crime, unless there is evidence to the contrary:  
(1) Still carrying out the relevant act after being informed by the regulatory authority;  
(2) Failing to perform statutory management duties after receiving a report;  
(3) The transaction price or method is obviously abnormal;  
(4) Providing programs, tools, or other technical support or assistance specifically for illegal or criminal purposes;  
(5) Frequently using measures such as covert browsing, encrypted communication, data destruction, or using false identities to evade supervision or investigation;  
(6) Providing technical support or assistance to others to evade supervision or investigation;  
(7) Other circumstances sufficient to determine that the actor knows.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条     明知他人利用信息网络实施犯罪，为其犯罪提供帮助，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之二第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）为三个以上对象提供帮助的；  
（二）支付结算金额二十万元以上的；  
（三）以投放广告等方式提供资金五万元以上的；  
（四）违法所得一万元以上的；  
（五）二年内曾因非法利用信息网络、帮助信息网络犯罪活动、危害计算机信息系统安全受过行政处罚，又帮助信息网络犯罪活动的；  
（六）被帮助对象实施的犯罪造成严重后果的；  
（七）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，确因客观条件限制无法查证被帮助对象是否达到犯罪的程度，但相关数额总计达到前款第二项至第四项规定标准五倍以上，或者造成特别严重后果的，应当以帮助信息网络犯罪活动罪追究行为人的刑事责任。

**Article 12** : Where a person knowingly uses an information network to commit a crime and provides assistance for the commission of the crime, in any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 287-2 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Providing assistance to three or more targets;  
(2) The amount of payment settlement is 200,000 RMB or more;  
(3) Providing funds of 50,000 RMB or more by means such as placing advertisements;  
(4) Illegally obtaining 10,000 RMB or more in proceeds;  
(5) Having received an administrative penalty within two years for illegally using information networks, aiding information network criminal activities, or endangering computer information system security, and again aiding information network criminal activities;  
(6) The crime committed by the aided target causes serious consequences;  
(7) Other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph is committed, and it is indeed impossible to verify whether the aided target has reached the level of a crime due to objective conditions, but the total relevant amount reaches five times the standards set forth in subparagraphs (2) to (4) of the preceding paragraph, or particularly serious consequences are caused, the actor shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of aiding information network criminal activities.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条     被帮助对象实施的犯罪行为可以确认，但尚未到案、尚未依法裁判或者因未达到刑事责任年龄等原因依法未予追究刑事责任的，不影响帮助信息网络犯罪活动罪的认定。

**Article 13** : If the criminal act committed by the aided target can be confirmed, but the target has not yet been brought to justice, has not yet been adjudicated according to law, or has not been investigated for criminal liability due to reasons such as not having reached the age of criminal responsibility according to law, this shall not affect the determination of the crime of aiding information network criminal activities.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条     单位实施本解释规定的犯罪的，依照本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。

**Article 14** : Where a unit commits a crime prescribed in this Interpretation, the directly responsible managers and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards for the corresponding natural person crimes prescribed in this Interpretation, and the unit shall be fined.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条     综合考虑社会危害程度、认罪悔罪态度等情节，认为犯罪情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不以犯罪论处。

**Article 15** : Where, after comprehensively considering the degree of social harm, the attitude of confessing guilt and repentance, and other circumstances, it is considered that the criminal circumstances are minor, the case may be exempted from prosecution or criminal punishment; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not great, it shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条     多次拒不履行信息网络安全管理义务、非法利用信息网络、帮助信息网络犯罪活动构成犯罪，依法应当追诉的， 或者二年内多次实施前述行为未经处理的，数量或者数额累计计算。

**Article 16** : Where a person repeatedly refuses to perform information network security management obligations, illegally uses information networks, or aids information network criminal activities, constituting a crime and shall be prosecuted according to law, or where the person repeatedly commits the aforementioned acts within two years without being dealt with, the quantity or amount shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条     对于实施本解释规定的犯罪被判处刑罚的，可以根据犯罪情况和预防再犯罪的需要，依法宣告职业禁止；被判处管制、宣告缓刑的，可以根据犯罪情况， 依法宣告禁止令。

**Article 17** : For a person sentenced to a penalty for committing a crime prescribed in this Interpretation, a professional prohibition may be declared according to law based on the circumstances of the crime and the need to prevent recidivism; where the person is sentenced to public surveillance or declared probation, a prohibition order may be declared according to law based on the circumstances of the crime.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条     对于实施本解释规定的犯罪的，应当综合考虑犯罪的危害程度、违法所得数额以及被告人的前科情况、认罪悔罪态度等，依法判处罚金。

**Article 18** : For a person committing a crime prescribed in this Interpretation, a fine shall be imposed according to law after comprehensive consideration of the degree of harm of the crime, the amount of illegal proceeds, the defendant's previous criminal record, attitude of confessing guilt and repentance, and other factors.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条     本解释自2019 年11 月1 日起施行。

**Article 19** : This Interpretation shall come into force on November 1, 2019.
