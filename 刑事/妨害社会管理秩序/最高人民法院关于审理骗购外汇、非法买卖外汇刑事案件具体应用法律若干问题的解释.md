# 最高人民法院关于审理骗购外汇、非法买卖外汇刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1998〕20号  
**公布日期**：1998-08-28  
**生效日期**：1998-09-01

> （1998年8月28日最高人民法院审判委员会第1018次会议通过，自1998年9月1日起施行） 为依法惩处骗购外汇、非法买卖外汇的犯罪行为，根据刑法的有关规定，现对审理骗购外汇、非法买卖外汇案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 以进行走私、逃汇、洗钱、骗税等犯罪活动为目的，使用虚假、无效的凭证、商业单据或者采取其他手段向外汇指定银行骗购外汇的，应当分别按照刑法分则第三章第二节、第一百九十条、第一百九十一条和第二百零四条等规定定罪处罚。 非国有公司、企业或者其他单位，与国有公司、企业或者其他国有单位勾结逃汇的，以逃汇罪的共犯处罚。 第二条 伪造、变造、买卖海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理机关的核准件等凭证或者购买伪造、变造的上述凭证的，按照刑法第二百八十条第一款的规定定罪处罚。 第三条 在外汇指定银行和中国外汇交易中心及其分中心以外买卖外汇，扰乱金融市场秩序，具有下列情形之一的，按照刑法第二百二十五条第（三）项的规定定罪处罚： （一）非法买卖外汇二十万美元以上的； （二）违法所得五万元人民币以上的。 第四条 公司、企业或者其他单位，违反有关外贸代理业务的规定，采用非法手段，或者明知是伪造、变造的凭证、商业单据，为他人向外汇指定银行骗购外汇，数额在五百万美元以上或者违法所得五十万元人民币以上的，按照刑法第二百二十五条第（三）项的规定定罪处罚。 居间介绍骗购外汇一百万美元以上或者违法所得十万元人民币以上的，按照刑法第二百二十五条第（三）项的规定定罪处罚。 第五条 海关、银行、外汇管理机关工作人员与骗购外汇的行为人通谋，为其提供购买外汇的有关凭证，或者明知是伪造、变造的凭证和商业单据而出售外汇，构成犯罪的，按照刑法的有关规定从重处罚。 第六条 实施本解释规定的行为，同时触犯二个以上罪名的，择一重罪从重处罚。 第七条 根据刑法第六十四条规定，骗购外汇、非法买卖外汇的，其违法所得予以追缴，用于骗购外汇、非法买卖外汇的资金予以没收，上缴国库。 第八条 骗购、非法买卖不同币种的外汇的，以案发时国家外汇管理机关制定的统一折算率折合后依照本解释处罚。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 以进行走私、逃汇、洗钱、骗税等犯罪活动为目的，使用虚假、无效的凭证、商业单据或者采取其他手段向外汇指定银行骗购外汇的，应当分别按照刑法分则第三章第二节、第一百九十条、第一百九十一条和第二百零四条等规定定罪处罚。  
非国有公司、企业或者其他单位，与国有公司、企业或者其他国有单位勾结逃汇的，以逃汇罪的共犯处罚。

**Article 1** Any person who, for the purpose of committing criminal activities such as smuggling, evasion of foreign exchange, money laundering, or tax fraud, uses false or invalid certificates or commercial documents, or adopts other means to fraudulently purchase foreign exchange from designated foreign exchange banks, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Section 2 of Chapter III, Article 190, Article 191, and Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, respectively.  
Where a non-state-owned company, enterprise, or other unit colludes with a state-owned company, enterprise, or other state-owned unit to evade foreign exchange, it shall be punished as an accomplice in the crime of evasion of foreign exchange.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 伪造、变造、买卖海关签发的报关单、进口证明、外汇管理机关的核准件等凭证或者购买伪造、变造的上述凭证的，按照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)第一款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 2** Any person who forges, alters, buys or sells certificates such as customs declaration forms, import certificates, or approval documents issued by foreign exchange administration authorities, or purchases the forged or altered certificates as mentioned above, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 280, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 在外汇指定银行和中国外汇交易中心及其分中心以外买卖外汇，扰乱金融市场秩序，具有下列情形之一的，按照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（三）项的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）非法买卖外汇二十万美元以上的；  
（二）违法所得五万元人民币以上的。

**Article 3** Any person who buys or sells foreign exchange outside designated foreign exchange banks and the China Foreign Exchange Trade Center and its sub-centers, thereby disrupting the order of the financial market, and who falls under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 225, Item (3) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Illegally buying or selling foreign exchange amounting to more than 200,000 US dollars;  
(2) Illegally obtaining proceeds amounting to more than 50,000 RMB.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 公司、企业或者其他单位，违反有关外贸代理业务的规定，采用非法手段，或者明知是伪造、变造的凭证、商业单据，为他人向外汇指定银行骗购外汇，数额在五百万美元以上或者违法所得五十万元人民币以上的，按照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（三）项的规定定罪处罚。  
居间介绍骗购外汇一百万美元以上或者违法所得十万元人民币以上的，按照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（三）项的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where a company, enterprise, or other unit, in violation of the provisions on foreign trade agency business, adopts illegal means, or knowingly uses forged or altered certificates or commercial documents to fraudulently purchase foreign exchange from designated foreign exchange banks for another person, and the amount involved is more than 5 million US dollars or the illegal proceeds are more than 500,000 RMB, it shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 225, Item (3) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a person introduces and brokers the fraudulent purchase of foreign exchange in an amount of more than 1 million US dollars or with illegal proceeds of more than 100,000 RMB, such person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 225, Item (3) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 海关、银行、外汇管理机关工作人员与骗购外汇的行为人通谋，为其提供购买外汇的有关凭证，或者明知是伪造、变造的凭证和商业单据而出售外汇，构成犯罪的，按照刑法的有关规定从重处罚。

**Article 5** Where any staff member of customs, a bank, or a foreign exchange administration authority conspires with a person who fraudulently purchases foreign exchange to provide relevant certificates for purchasing foreign exchange, or knowingly sells foreign exchange using forged or altered certificates and commercial documents, and such conduct constitutes a crime, such staff member shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 实施本解释规定的行为，同时触犯二个以上罪名的，择一重罪从重处罚。

**Article 6** Where any act stipulated in this Interpretation simultaneously violates two or more charges, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the heavier crime and shall be given a severe punishment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 根据[刑法第六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-64)规定，骗购外汇、非法买卖外汇的，其违法所得予以追缴，用于骗购外汇、非法买卖外汇的资金予以没收，上缴国库。

**Article 7** In accordance with the provisions of Article 64 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with respect to fraudulent purchase of foreign exchange or illegal purchase and sale of foreign exchange, the illegal proceeds shall be recovered, and the funds used for fraudulent purchase of foreign exchange or illegal purchase and sale of foreign exchange shall be confiscated and turned over to the State Treasury.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 骗购、非法买卖不同币种的外汇的，以案发时国家外汇管理机关制定的统一折算率折合后依照本解释处罚。

**Article 8** Where foreign exchange of different currencies is fraudulently purchased or illegally bought or sold, the amount shall be converted according to the unified conversion rate formulated by the state foreign exchange administration authority at the time of the case, and the punishment shall be imposed in accordance with this Interpretation.
