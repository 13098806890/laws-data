# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕11号  
**公布日期**：2004-09-01  
**生效日期**：2004-09-06

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2004年9月1日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1323次会议、2004年9月2日由最高人民检察院第十届检察委员会第26次会议通过，现予公布，自2004年9月6日起施行。 2004年9月3日 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院 关于办理利用互联网、移动通讯 终端、声讯台制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播 淫秽电子信息刑事案件具体应用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2004〕11号 为依法惩治利用互联网、移动通讯终端制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息、通过声讯台传播淫秽语音信息等犯罪活动，维护公共网络、通讯的正常秩序，保障公众的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于维护互联网安全的决定》的规定，现对办理该类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 以牟利为目的，利用互联网、移动通讯终端制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息，具有下列情形之一的，依照刑法第三百六十三条第一款的规定，以制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚： （一）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电影、表演、动画等视频文件二十个以上的； （二）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽音频文件一百个以上的； （三）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子刊物、图片、文章、短信息等二百件以上的； （四）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播的淫秽电子信息，实际被点击数达到一万次以上的； （五）以会员制方式出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息，注册会员达二百人以上的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 以牟利为目的，利用互联网、移动通讯终端制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款的规定，以制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚：  
（一）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电影、表演、动画等视频文件二十个以上的；  
（二）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽音频文件一百个以上的；  
（三）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子刊物、图片、文章、短信息等二百件以上的；  
（四）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播的淫秽电子信息，实际被点击数达到一万次以上的；  
（五）以会员制方式出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息，注册会员达二百人以上的；  
（六）利用淫秽电子信息收取广告费、会员注册费或者其他费用，违法所得一万元以上的；  
（七）数量或者数额虽未达到第（一）项至第（六）项规定标准，但分别达到其中两项以上标准一半以上的；  
（八）造成严重后果的。  
利用聊天室、论坛、即时通信软件、电子邮件等方式，实施第一款规定行为的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款的规定，以制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Anyone who, for the purpose of profit, produces, reproduces, publishes, sells, or disseminates pornographic electronic information through the internet or mobile communication terminals, under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials for profit in accordance with the provisions of Article 363(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating twenty or more video files of pornographic movies, performances, animations, or the like;  
(2) producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating one hundred or more audio files of pornographic content;  
(3) producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating two hundred or more pieces of pornographic electronic publications, pictures, articles, short messages, or the like;  
(4) the actual number of clicks on the pornographic electronic information produced, reproduced, published, sold, or disseminated reaches ten thousand or more;  
(5) publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic electronic information through a membership system, with two hundred or more registered members;  
(6) collecting advertising fees, membership registration fees, or other fees by using pornographic electronic information, with illegal gains of ten thousand RMB or more;  
(7) the quantity or amount does not reach the standards specified in items (1) to (6), but reaches more than half of the standards of two or more of the said items respectively;  
(8) causing serious consequences.  
Whoever commits the acts specified in the preceding paragraph by means of chat rooms, forums, instant messaging software, e-mails, or the like, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials for profit in accordance with the provisions of Article 363(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 实施第一条规定的行为，数量或者数额达到第一条第一款第（一）项至第（六）项规定标准五倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款规定的“情节严重”；达到规定标准二十五倍以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”。

**Article 2** Where the quantity or amount of the acts specified in Article 1 reaches five times or more the standards specified in items (1) to (6) of paragraph 1 of Article 1, it shall be deemed as 'serious circumstances' as provided for in Article 363(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China; where it reaches twenty-five times or more the prescribed standards, it shall be deemed as 'particularly serious circumstances'.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 不以牟利为目的，利用互联网或者移动通讯终端传播淫秽电子信息，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-364)第一款的规定，以传播淫秽物品罪定罪处罚：  
（一）数量达到第一条第一款第（一）项至第（五）项规定标准二倍以上的；  
（二）数量分别达到第一条第一款第（一）项至第（五）项两项以上标准的；  
（三）造成严重后果的。  
利用聊天室、论坛、即时通信软件、电子邮件等方式，实施第一款规定行为的，依照[刑法第三百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-364)第一款的规定，以传播淫秽物品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 3** Anyone who, without the purpose of profit, disseminates pornographic electronic information through the internet or mobile communication terminals, under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of disseminating pornographic materials in accordance with the provisions of Article 364(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the quantity reaches two times or more the standards specified in items (1) to (5) of paragraph 1 of Article 1;  
(2) the quantity respectively reaches the standards of two or more items among items (1) to (5) of paragraph 1 of Article 1;  
(3) causing serious consequences.  
Whoever commits the acts specified in the preceding paragraph by means of chat rooms, forums, instant messaging software, e-mails, or the like, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of disseminating pornographic materials in accordance with the provisions of Article 364(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 明知是淫秽电子信息而在自己所有、管理或者使用的网站或者网页上提供直接链接的，其数量标准根据所链接的淫秽电子信息的种类计算。

**Article 4** Where a person knowingly provides a direct link to pornographic electronic information on a website or webpage owned, managed, or used by such person, the quantity standard shall be calculated based on the type of pornographic electronic information linked to.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 以牟利为目的，通过声讯台传播淫秽语音信息，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款的规定，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员以传播淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚：  
（一）向一百人次以上传播的；  
（二）违法所得一万元以上的；  
（三）造成严重后果的。  
实施前款规定行为，数量或者数额达到前款第（一）项至第（二）项规定标准五倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款规定的“情节严重”；达到规定标准二十五倍以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”。

**Article 5** Anyone who, for the purpose of profit, disseminates pornographic voice information through a voice message service platform, under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of disseminating pornographic materials for profit in accordance with the provisions of Article 363(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible:  
(1) disseminating to one hundred person-times or more;  
(2) illegal gains of ten thousand RMB or more;  
(3) causing serious consequences.  
Where the quantity or amount of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph reaches five times or more the standards specified in items (1) to (2) of the preceding paragraph, it shall be deemed as 'serious circumstances' as provided for in Article 363(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China; where it reaches twenty-five times or more the prescribed standards, it shall be deemed as 'particularly serious circumstances'.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 实施本解释前五条规定的犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款、第三百六十四条第一款的规定从重处罚：  
（一）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播具体描绘不满十八周岁未成年人性行为的淫秽电子信息的；  
（二）明知是具体描绘不满十八周岁的未成年人性行为的淫秽电子信息而在自己所有、管理或者使用的网站或者网页上提供直接链接的；  
（三）向不满十八周岁的未成年人贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息和语音信息的；  
（四）通过使用破坏性程序、恶意代码修改用户计算机设置等方法，强制用户访问、下载淫秽电子信息的。

**Article 6** Where any crime specified in the preceding five Articles of this Interpretation is committed under any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 363(1) and Article 364(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic electronic information that specifically depicts sexual acts of minors under the age of eighteen;  
(2) knowingly providing a direct link on a website or webpage owned, managed, or used by the person to pornographic electronic information that specifically depicts sexual acts of minors under the age of eighteen;  
(3) selling or disseminating pornographic electronic information or voice information to minors under the age of eighteen;  
(4) forcing users to access or download pornographic electronic information by using destructive programs, malicious codes to modify user computer settings, or other means.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 明知他人实施制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息犯罪，为其提供互联网接入、服务器托管、网络存储空间、通讯传输通道、费用结算等帮助的，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Where a person knowingly provides assistance, such as internet access, server hosting, network storage space, communication transmission channels, or fee settlement, to another person who commits the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic electronic information, the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible shall be punished as joint offenders.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台贩卖、传播淫秽书刊、影片、录像带、录音带等以实物为载体的淫秽物品的，依照《最高人民法院关于审理非法出版物刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》的有关规定定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Whoever sells or disseminates pornographic materials in physical forms, such as pornographic books, films, video tapes, and audio tapes, through the internet, mobile communication terminals, or voice message service platforms, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Illegal Publications.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 [刑法第三百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-367)第一款规定的“其他淫秽物品”，包括具体描绘性行为或者露骨宣扬色情的诲淫性的视频文件、音频文件、电子刊物、图片、文章、短信息等互联网、移动通讯终端电子信息和声讯台语音信息。  
有关人体生理、医学知识的电子信息和声讯台语音信息不是淫秽物品。包含色情内容的有艺术价值的电子文学、艺术作品不视为淫秽物品。

**Article 9** The 'other pornographic materials' as provided for in Article 367(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall include video files, audio files, electronic publications, pictures, articles, short messages, and other internet or mobile communication terminal electronic information and voice message service platform voice information that specifically depict sexual acts or explicitly promote pornography and are obscene.  
Electronic information and voice message service platform voice information relating to human physiology or medical knowledge shall not be pornographic materials. Electronic literary or artistic works of artistic value that contain pornographic content shall not be regarded as pornographic materials.
