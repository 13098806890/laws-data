# 最高人民法院	最高人民检察院关于办理组织考试作弊等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理组织考试作弊等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2019 年4 月8 日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1765 次会议、2019 年6 月28 日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第二十次会议通过，现予公布，自2019 年9 月4 日起施行。 2019 年9 月2 日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理组织考试作弊等刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2019〕13 号 为依法惩治组织考试作弊、非法出售、提供试题、答案、代替考试等犯罪，维护考试公平与秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑 法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定， 现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  刑法第二百八十四条之一规定的“法律规定的国家考试”，仅限于全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定的法律所规定的考试。 根据有关法律规定，下列考试属于“法律规定的国家考试”： （一）普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、高等教育自学考试、成人高等学校招生考试等国家教育考试； （二）中央和地方公务员录用考试； （三）国家统一法律职业资格考试、国家教师资格考试、注册会计师全国统一考试、会计专业技术资格考试、资产评估师资格考试、医师资格考试、执业药师职业资格考试、注册建筑师考试、建造师执业资格考试等专业技术资格考试； （四）其他依照法律由中央或者地方主管部门以及行业组织的国家考试。 前款规定的考试涉及的特殊类型招生、特殊技能测试、面试等考试，属于“法律规定的国家考试”。 第二条  在法律规定的国家考试中，组织作弊，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百八十四条之一第一款规定的“情节严重”：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  [刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一规定的“法律规定的国家考试”，仅限于全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定的法律所规定的考试。  
根据有关法律规定，下列考试属于“法律规定的国家考试”：  
（一）普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、高等教育自学考试、成人高等学校招生考试等国家教育考试；  
（二）中央和地方公务员录用考试；  
（三）国家统一法律职业资格考试、国家教师资格考试、注册会计师全国统一考试、会计专业技术资格考试、资产评估师资格考试、医师资格考试、执业药师职业资格考试、注册建筑师考试、建造师执业资格考试等专业技术资格考试；  
（四）其他依照法律由中央或者地方主管部门以及行业组织的国家考试。  
前款规定的考试涉及的特殊类型招生、特殊技能测试、面试等考试，属于“法律规定的国家考试”。

**Article 1** The "national examinations prescribed by law" as referred to in Article 284(1) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be limited to examinations prescribed by laws enacted by the National People's Congress and its Standing Committee.  
According to relevant legal provisions, the following examinations fall within the scope of "national examinations prescribed by law":  
(1) national education examinations such as the general college entrance examination, postgraduate entrance examination, higher education self-study examination, and adult college entrance examination;  
(2) central and local civil service recruitment examinations;  
(3) professional technical qualification examinations such as the unified national legal professional qualification examination, national teacher qualification examination, national unified certified public accountant examination, accounting professional technical qualification examination, asset appraiser qualification examination, medical practitioner qualification examination, licensed pharmacist professional qualification examination, registered architect examination, and constructor professional qualification examination;  
(4) other national examinations organized by central or local competent authorities or industries in accordance with law.  
Special types of enrollment, special skill tests, interviews and other examinations involved in the examinations prescribed in the preceding paragraph shall be considered "national examinations prescribed by law".

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  在法律规定的国家考试中，组织作弊，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）在普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、公务员录用考试中组织考试作弊的；  
（二）导致考试推迟、取消或者启用备用试题的；  
（三）考试工作人员组织考试作弊的；  
（四）组织考生跨省、自治区、直辖市作弊的；  
（五）多次组织考试作弊的；  
（六）组织三十人次以上作弊的；  
（七）提供作弊器材五十件以上的；  
（八）违法所得三十万元以上的；  
（九）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** In organizing cheating in national examinations prescribed by law, any of the following circumstances shall be found as "serious circumstances" as referred to in paragraph 1 of Article 284(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) organizing cheating in the general college entrance examination, postgraduate entrance examination, or civil service recruitment examination;  
(2) causing the examination to be postponed, canceled, or requiring the use of backup examination questions;  
(3) examination staff organizing cheating;  
(4) organizing examinees to cheat across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(5) repeatedly organizing cheating;  
(6) organizing cheating involving 30 person-times or more;  
(7) providing 50 pieces or more of cheating equipment;  
(8) illegally obtaining 300,000 yuan or more;  
(9) other circumstances of serious nature.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  具有避开或者突破考场防范作弊的安全管理措施，获取、记录、传递、接收、存储考试试题、答案等功能的程序、工具，以及专门设计用于作弊的程序、工具， 应当认定为[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第二款规定的“作弊器材”。  
对于是否属于[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第二款规定的“作弊器材”难以确定的， 依据省级以上公安机关或者考试主管部门出具的报告，结合其他证据作出认定；涉及专用间谍器材、窃听、窃照专用器材、“伪基 站”等器材的，依照相关规定作出认定。

**Article 3** Programs and tools that have the functions of evading or breaking through security management measures for preventing cheating in examination venues, and of obtaining, recording, transmitting, receiving, or storing examination questions or answers, as well as programs and tools specially designed for cheating, shall be found as "cheating equipment" as referred to in paragraph 2 of Article 284(1) of the Criminal Law.  
Where it is difficult to determine whether an item constitutes "cheating equipment" as referred to in paragraph 2 of Article 284(1) of the Criminal Law, the determination shall be made based on reports issued by provincial-level or higher public security organs or examination competent authorities, in combination with other evidence; where the equipment involves specialized espionage equipment, specialized eavesdropping or covert photography equipment, "pseudo base stations" and other equipment, the determination shall be made in accordance with relevant provisions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  组织考试作弊，在考试开始之前被查获,但已经非法获取考试试题、答案或者具有其他严重扰乱考试秩序情形的， 应当认定为组织考试作弊罪既遂。

**Article 4** Where an organizer of examination cheating is discovered before the examination begins, but has already illegally obtained examination questions or answers or has other circumstances that severely disrupt the examination order, the act shall be found as the completed offense of the crime of organizing examination cheating.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  为实施考试作弊行为，非法出售或者提供法律规定的国家考试的试题、答案，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第三款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法出售或者提供普通高等学校招生考试、研究生招生考试、公务员录用考试的试题、答案的；  
（二）导致考试推迟、取消或者启用备用试题的;  
（三）考试工作人员非法出售或者提供试题、答案的；  
（四）多次非法出售或者提供试题、答案的；  
（五）向三十人次以上非法出售或者提供试题、答案的；  
（六）违法所得三十万元以上的；  
（七）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 5** Where, for the purpose of committing examination cheating, a person illegally sells or provides the questions or answers of national examinations prescribed by law, any of the following circumstances shall be found as "serious circumstances" as referred to in paragraph 3 of Article 284(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally selling or providing the questions or answers of the general college entrance examination, postgraduate entrance examination, or civil service recruitment examination;  
(2) causing the examination to be postponed, canceled, or requiring the use of backup examination questions;  
(3) examination staff illegally selling or providing questions or answers;  
(4) repeatedly illegally selling or providing questions or answers;  
(5) illegally selling or providing questions or answers to 30 person-times or more;  
(6) illegally obtaining 300,000 yuan or more;  
(7) other circumstances of serious nature.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  为实施考试作弊行为，向他人非法出售或者提供法律规定的国家考试的试题、答案，试题不完整或者答案与标准答案不完全一致的，不影响非法出售、提供试题、答案罪的认定。

**Article 6** Where, for the purpose of committing examination cheating, a person illegally sells or provides to another person the questions or answers of a national examination prescribed by law, the fact that the questions are incomplete or the answers are not completely consistent with the standard answers shall not affect the determination of the crime of illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  代替他人或者让他人代替自己参加法律规定的国家考试的，应当依照[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一第四款的规定， 以代替考试罪定罪处罚。  
对于行为人犯罪情节较轻，确有悔罪表现，综合考虑行为人替考情况以及考试类型等因素，认为符合缓刑适用条件的，可以宣告缓刑；犯罪情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不以犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Where a person takes a national examination prescribed by law in place of another person or causes another person to take such examination in his or her place, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of taking an examination by surrogate in accordance with paragraph 4 of Article 284(1) of the Criminal Law.  
Where the circumstances of the crime are relatively minor, the offender truly shows repentance, and upon comprehensive consideration of factors such as the circumstances of the surrogate taking and the type of examination, it is deemed that the conditions for applying probation are met, probation may be declared; where the circumstances of the crime are minor, the offender may be exempted from prosecution or criminal punishment; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not significant, the act shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  单位实施组织考试作弊、非法出售、提供试题、答案等行为的，依照本解 释规定的相应定罪量刑标准，追究组织者、策划者、实施者的刑事责任。

**Article 8** Where a unit commits acts such as organizing examination cheating, illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers, the criminal liability of the organizers, planners, and implementers shall be investigated in accordance with the corresponding conviction and sentencing standards prescribed in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   以窃取、刺探、收买方法非法获取法律规定的国家考试的试题、答案，又组织考试作弊或者非法出售、提供试题、答案，分别符合[刑法第二百八十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-282)和[刑法第二百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-284)之一规定的，以非法获取国家秘密罪和组织考试作弊罪或者非法出售、提供试题、答案罪数罪并罚。

**Article 9** Where a person illegally obtains the questions or answers of a national examination prescribed by law by means of theft, espionage, or purchase, and then organizes examination cheating or illegally sells or provides the questions or answers, and such acts respectively meet the provisions of Article 282 of the Criminal Law and Article 284(1) of the Criminal Law, the person shall be punished for multiple crimes, namely the crime of illegally obtaining state secrets and the crime of organizing examination cheating or the crime of illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers, with combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  在法律规定的国家考试以外的其他考试中，组织作弊，为他人组织作弊提供作弊器材或者其他帮助，或者非法出售、提供试题、答案，符合非法获取国家秘密罪、非法生产、销售窃听、窃照专用器材罪、非法使用窃听、窃照专用器材罪、非法利用信息网络罪、扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序罪等犯罪构成要件的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 10** Where, in examinations other than the national examinations prescribed by law, a person organizes cheating, provides cheating equipment or other assistance to others for organizing cheating, or illegally sells or provides examination questions or answers, and the conduct meets the constitutive elements of the crime of illegally obtaining state secrets, the crime of illegally producing or selling specialized eavesdropping or covert photography equipment, the crime of illegally using specialized eavesdropping or covert photography equipment, the crime of illegally using information networks, the crime of disrupting the order of radio communication management, or other crimes, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   设立用于实施考试作弊的网站、通讯群组或者发布有关考试作弊的信息，情节严重的，应当依照[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一的规定，以非法利用信息网络罪定罪处罚；同时构成组织考试作弊罪、非法出售、提供试题、答案罪、非法获取国家秘密罪等其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 11** Where a person establishes a website or communication group for committing examination cheating or publishes information concerning examination cheating, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally using information networks in accordance with Article 287(1) of the Criminal Law; where the act simultaneously constitutes other crimes such as the crime of organizing examination cheating, the crime of illegally selling or providing examination questions or answers, or the crime of illegally obtaining state secrets, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  对于实施本解释规定的犯罪被判处刑罚的，可以根据犯罪情况和预防再犯罪的需要，依法宣告职业禁止；被判处管制、宣告缓刑的，可以根据犯罪情况， 依法宣告禁止令。

**Article 12** Where a person is sentenced to criminal punishment for committing a crime prescribed in this Interpretation, a professional prohibition may be declared according to law based on the circumstances of the crime and the need to prevent re-offending; where a person is sentenced to public surveillance or declared probation, a restraining order may be declared according to law based on the circumstances of the crime.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  对于实施本解释规定的行为构成犯罪的，应当综合考虑犯罪的危害程度、违法所得数额以及被告人的前科情况、认罪悔罪态度等，依法判处罚金。

**Article 13** Where the conduct prescribed in this Interpretation constitutes a crime, the court shall comprehensively consider the degree of harm of the crime, the amount of illegal gains, and the defendant's criminal record, admission of guilt and repentance, and other factors, and shall impose a fine according to law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  本解释自2019 年9 月4 日起施行。

**Article 14** This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 4, 2019.
