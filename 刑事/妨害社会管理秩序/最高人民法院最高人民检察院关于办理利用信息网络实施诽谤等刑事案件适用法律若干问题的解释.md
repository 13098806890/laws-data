# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理利用信息网络实施诽谤等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕21号  
**公布日期**：2013-09-05  
**生效日期**：2013-09-10

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理利用信息网络实施诽谤等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年9月5日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1589次会议、2013年9月2日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第9次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年9月10日起施行。 2013年9月6日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理利用信息网络实施诽谤等刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕21号 为保护公民、法人和其他组织的合法权益，维护社会秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于维护互联网安全的决定》等规定，对办理利用信息网络实施诽谤、寻衅滋事、敲诈勒索、非法经营等刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百四十六条第一款规定的“捏造事实诽谤他人”： （一）捏造损害他人名誉的事实，在信息网络上散布，或者组织、指使人员在信息网络上散布的； （二）将信息网络上涉及他人的原始信息内容篡改为损害他人名誉的事实，在信息网络上散布，或者组织、指使人员在信息网络上散布的。 明知是捏造的损害他人名誉的事实，在信息网络上散布，情节恶劣的，以“捏造事实诽谤他人”论。 第二条   利用信息网络诽谤他人，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百四十六条第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）同一诽谤信息实际被点击、浏览次数达到五千次以上，或者被转发次数达到五百次以上的； （二）造成被害人或者其近亲属精神失常、自残、自杀等严重后果的； （三）二年内曾因诽谤受过行政处罚，又诽谤他人的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百四十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-246)第一款规定的“捏造事实诽谤他人”：  
（一）捏造损害他人名誉的事实，在信息网络上散布，或者组织、指使人员在信息网络上散布的；  
（二）将信息网络上涉及他人的原始信息内容篡改为损害他人名誉的事实，在信息网络上散布，或者组织、指使人员在信息网络上散布的。  
明知是捏造的损害他人名誉的事实，在信息网络上散布，情节恶劣的，以“捏造事实诽谤他人”论。

**Article 1** Any of the following circumstances shall be identified as "fabricating facts to defame others" as stipulated in Article 246(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) fabricating facts that damage the reputation of others and disseminating them on the information network, or organizing or instructing persons to disseminate them on the information network;  
(2) altering the original information content concerning others on the information network into facts that damage the reputation of others and disseminating them on the information network, or organizing or instructing persons to disseminate them on the information network.  
Where a person, knowing that it is fabricated facts that damage the reputation of others, disseminates them on the information network, and the circumstances are egregious, the person shall be treated as "fabricating facts to defame others".

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   利用信息网络诽谤他人，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百四十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-246)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）同一诽谤信息实际被点击、浏览次数达到五千次以上，或者被转发次数达到五百次以上的；  
（二）造成被害人或者其近亲属精神失常、自残、自杀等严重后果的；  
（三）二年内曾因诽谤受过行政处罚，又诽谤他人的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** Where a person defames others through the information network, any of the following circumstances shall be identified as "serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 246(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the same defamatory information is actually clicked on or viewed five thousand times or more, or is forwarded five hundred times or more;  
(2) causing serious consequences such as mental disorder, self-harm, or suicide of the victim or his close relatives;  
(3) having previously received an administrative penalty for defamation within two years and again defaming others;  
(4) other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   利用信息网络诽谤他人，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百四十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-246)第二款规定的“严重危害社会秩序和国家利益”：  
（一）引发群体性事件的；  
（二）引发公共秩序混乱的；  
（三）引发民族、宗教冲突的；  
（四）诽谤多人，造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（五）损害国家形象，严重危害国家利益的；  
（六）造成恶劣国际影响的；  
（七）其他严重危害社会秩序和国家利益的情形。

**Article 3** Where a person defames others through the information network, any of the following circumstances shall be identified as "seriously endangering social order and national interests" as stipulated in Article 246(2) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing mass incidents;  
(2) causing public order chaos;  
(3) causing ethnic or religious conflicts;  
(4) defaming multiple persons, causing adverse social impact;  
(5) damaging the national image, seriously endangering national interests;  
(6) causing adverse international impact;  
(7) other circumstances that seriously endanger social order and national interests.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   一年内多次实施利用信息网络诽谤他人行为未经处理，诽谤信息实际被点击、浏览、转发次数累计计算构成犯罪的，应当依法定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where a person commits multiple acts of defamation through the information network within one year that have not been dealt with, and the cumulative number of actual clicks, views, or forwards of the defamatory information constitutes a crime, the person shall be convicted and punished according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   利用信息网络辱骂、恐吓他人，情节恶劣，破坏社会秩序的，依照[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第一款第（二）项的规定，以寻衅滋事罪定罪处罚。  
编造虚假信息，或者明知是编造的虚假信息，在信息网络上散布，或者组织、指使人员在信息网络上散布，起哄闹事，造成公共秩序严重混乱的，依照[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)第一款第（四）项的规定，以寻衅滋事罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where a person insults or intimidates others through the information network, with egregious circumstances, disrupting social order, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of picking quarrels and provoking trouble in accordance with Article 293(1)(ii) of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person fabricates false information, or knowing it is fabricated false information, disseminates it on the information network, or organizes or instructs persons to disseminate it on the information network, creating a disturbance, causing serious disorder of public order, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of picking quarrels and provoking trouble in accordance with Article 293(1)(iv) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   以在信息网络上发布、删除等方式处理网络信息为由，威胁、要挟他人，索取公私财物，数额较大，或者多次实施上述行为的，依照[刑法第二百七十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-274)的规定，以敲诈勒索罪定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where a person threatens or blackmails others on the ground of handling network information by means of posting or deleting on the information network, extorting public or private property, and the amount is relatively large, or commits the above acts multiple times, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of extortion in accordance with Article 274 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   违反国家规定，以营利为目的，通过信息网络有偿提供删除信息服务，或者明知是虚假信息，通过信息网络有偿提供发布信息等服务，扰乱市场秩序，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节严重”，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（四）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚：  
（一）个人非法经营数额在五万元以上，或者违法所得数额在二万元以上的；  
（二）单位非法经营数额在十五万元以上，或者违法所得数额在五万元以上的。  
实施前款规定的行为，数额达到前款规定的数额五倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节特别严重”。

**Article 7** Where a person, in violation of state regulations and for profit, provides paid deletion of information services through the information network, or, knowing it is false information, provides paid services such as publishing information through the information network, disrupting market order, and having any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute "serious circumstances" of illegal business operations, and the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with Article 225(4) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the individual's illegal business turnover is more than 50,000 yuan, or the illegal income is more than 20,000 yuan;  
(2) the unit's illegal business turnover is more than 150,000 yuan, or the illegal income is more than 50,000 yuan.  
Where the amount of the act under the preceding paragraph reaches more than five times the amount specified in the preceding paragraph, it shall be identified as "especially serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 225 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   明知他人利用信息网络实施诽谤、寻衅滋事、敲诈勒索、非法经营等犯罪，为其提供资金、场所、技术支持等帮助的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 8** Where a person, knowing that others are committing crimes such as defamation, picking quarrels and provoking trouble, extortion, or illegal business operations through the information network, provides them with assistance such as funds, venues, or technical support, the person shall be treated as a joint offender.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   利用信息网络实施诽谤、寻衅滋事、敲诈勒索、非法经营犯罪，同时又构成[刑法第二百二十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-221)规定的损害商业信誉、商品声誉罪，第二百七十八条规定的煽动暴力抗拒法律实施罪，第二百九十一条之一规定的编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Where a person commits crimes of defamation, picking quarrels and provoking trouble, extortion, or illegal business operations through the information network, and at the same time constitutes crimes such as the crime of damaging commercial credit and commodity reputation as stipulated in Article 221 of the Criminal Law, the crime of inciting violence to resist the implementation of law as stipulated in Article 278, or the crime of fabricating and intentionally disseminating false terrorist information as stipulated in Article 291a, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision imposing the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   本解释所称信息网络，包括以计算机、电视机、固定电话机、移动电话机等电子设备为终端的计算机互联网、广播电视网、固定通信网、移动通信网等信息网络，以及向公众开放的局域网络。

**Article 10** The term "information network" as used in this Interpretation includes information networks such as the computer internet, broadcast television network, fixed communication network, and mobile communication network, with electronic devices such as computers, televisions, fixed telephones, and mobile telephones as terminals, as well as local area networks open to the public.
