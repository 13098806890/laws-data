# 最高人民法院关于审理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕16号  
**公布日期**：2015-07-06  
**生效日期**：2015-07-22

> 最高人民法院《关于审理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2015年7月6日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1657次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年7月22日起施行。 2015年7月20日 最高人民法院 关于审理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2015〕16号 为依法惩治拒不执行判决、裁定犯罪，确保人民法院判决、裁定依法执行，切实维护当事人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，就审理拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件适用法律若干问题，解释如下： 第一条　被执行人、协助执行义务人、担保人等负有执行义务的人对人民法院的判决、裁定有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的，应当依照刑法第三百一十三条的规定，以拒不执行判决、裁定罪处罚。 第二条　负有执行义务的人有能力执行而实施下列行为之一的，应当认定为全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于刑法第三百一十三条的解释中规定的“其他有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的情形”： （一）具有拒绝报告或者虚假报告财产情况、违反人民法院限制高消费及有关消费令等拒不执行行为，经采取罚款或者拘留等强制措施后仍拒不执行的； （二）伪造、毁灭有关被执行人履行能力的重要证据，以暴力、威胁、贿买方法阻止他人作证或者指使、贿买、胁迫他人作伪证，妨碍人民法院查明被执行人财产情况，致使判决、裁定无法执行的； （三）拒不交付法律文书指定交付的财物、票证或者拒不迁出房屋、退出土地，致使判决、裁定无法执行的； （四）与他人串通，通过虚假诉讼、虚假仲裁、虚假和解等方式妨害执行，致使判决、裁定无法执行的； （五）以暴力、威胁方法阻碍执行人员进入执行现场或者聚众哄闹、冲击执行现场，致使执行工作无法进行的； （六）对执行人员进行侮辱、围攻、扣押、殴打，致使执行工作无法进行的； （七）毁损、抢夺执行案件材料、执行公务车辆和其他执行器械、执行人员服装以及执行公务证件，致使执行工作无法进行的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　被执行人、协助执行义务人、担保人等负有执行义务的人对人民法院的判决、裁定有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的，应当依照[刑法第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)的规定，以拒不执行判决、裁定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Where a person subject to enforcement, a person obliged to assist in enforcement, a surety, or any other person with an obligation to enforce has the ability to enforce a judgment or ruling of a People's Court but refuses to do so, and the circumstances are serious, he or she shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 313 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, be punished for the crime of refusing to enforce a judgment or ruling.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　负有执行义务的人有能力执行而实施下列行为之一的，应当认定为全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于[刑法第三百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-313)的解释中规定的“其他有能力执行而拒不执行，情节严重的情形”：  
（一）具有拒绝报告或者虚假报告财产情况、违反人民法院限制高消费及有关消费令等拒不执行行为，经采取罚款或者拘留等强制措施后仍拒不执行的；  
（二）伪造、毁灭有关被执行人履行能力的重要证据，以暴力、威胁、贿买方法阻止他人作证或者指使、贿买、胁迫他人作伪证，妨碍人民法院查明被执行人财产情况，致使判决、裁定无法执行的；  
（三）拒不交付法律文书指定交付的财物、票证或者拒不迁出房屋、退出土地，致使判决、裁定无法执行的；  
（四）与他人串通，通过虚假诉讼、虚假仲裁、虚假和解等方式妨害执行，致使判决、裁定无法执行的；  
（五）以暴力、威胁方法阻碍执行人员进入执行现场或者聚众哄闹、冲击执行现场，致使执行工作无法进行的；  
（六）对执行人员进行侮辱、围攻、扣押、殴打，致使执行工作无法进行的；  
（七）毁损、抢夺执行案件材料、执行公务车辆和其他执行器械、执行人员服装以及执行公务证件，致使执行工作无法进行的；  
（八）拒不执行法院判决、裁定，致使债权人遭受重大损失的。

**Article 2** Where a person with an obligation to enforce has the ability to enforce but commits any of the following acts, it shall be deemed as "other circumstances where a person has the ability to enforce but refuses to do so and the circumstances are serious" as provided in the Interpretation of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Article 313 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) refusing to report property, or making false reports on property, violating the People's Court's order on restricting high consumption and related consumption orders, or other acts of refusal to enforce, and after compulsory measures such as fine or detention have been taken, still refusing to enforce;  
(2) forging or destroying important evidence concerning the enforcement capability of the person subject to enforcement, using violence, threats, or bribery to prevent others from testifying, or instructing, bribing, or coercing others to give false testimony, thereby obstructing the People's Court from ascertaining the property status of the person subject to enforcement, and causing the judgment or ruling to be unenforceable;  
(3) refusing to deliver property or certificates designated for delivery by a legal document, or refusing to vacate a house or relinquish land, thereby causing the judgment or ruling to be unenforceable;  
(4) conspiring with others to obstruct enforcement through false litigation, false arbitration, false settlement, or other means, thereby causing the judgment or ruling to be unenforceable;  
(5) using violence or threats to obstruct enforcement personnel from entering the enforcement site, or gathering a crowd to create a disturbance or storm the enforcement site, thereby causing the enforcement work to be impossible;  
(6) insulting, besieging, detaining, or beating enforcement personnel, thereby causing the enforcement work to be impossible;  
(7) damaging or seizing enforcement case materials, enforcement official vehicles, other enforcement equipment, enforcement personnel uniforms, or enforcement official documents, thereby causing the enforcement work to be impossible;  
(8) refusing to enforce a court judgment or ruling, thereby causing significant losses to the creditor.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　申请执行人有证据证明同时具有下列情形，人民法院认为符合[刑事诉讼法第二百零四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-204)第三项规定的，以自诉案件立案审理：  
（一）负有执行义务的人拒不执行判决、裁定，侵犯了申请执行人的人身、财产权利，应当依法追究刑事责任的；  
（二）申请执行人曾经提出控告，而公安机关或者人民检察院对负有执行义务的人不予追究刑事责任的。

**Article 3** Where the person applying for enforcement has evidence proving that both of the following circumstances exist, and the People's Court considers that it meets the provisions of paragraph (3) of Article 204 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, the case shall be accepted and heard as a private prosecution case:  
(1) the person with an obligation to enforce refuses to enforce a judgment or ruling, infringing upon the personal or property rights of the person applying for enforcement, and criminal liability shall be investigated according to law; and  
(2) the person applying for enforcement has previously lodged an accusation, but the public security organ or the People's Procuratorate has not investigated the criminal liability of the person with an obligation to enforce.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[本解释第三条](#art-3)规定的自诉案件，依照[刑事诉讼法第二百零六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-206)的规定，自诉人在宣告判决前，可以同被告人自行和解或者撤回自诉。

**Article 4** For a private prosecution case as provided in Article 3 of this Interpretation, the private prosecutor may, in accordance with the provisions of Article 206 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, voluntarily reach a settlement with the defendant or withdraw the private prosecution before a judgment is pronounced.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　拒不执行判决、裁定刑事案件，一般由执行法院所在地人民法院管辖。

**Article 5** Criminal cases of refusing to enforce a judgment or ruling shall generally be under the jurisdiction of the People's Court in the place where the enforcement court is located.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　拒不执行判决、裁定的被告人在一审宣告判决前，履行全部或部分执行义务的，可以酌情从宽处罚。

**Article 6** Where the defendant who refuses to enforce a judgment or ruling performs all or part of the enforcement obligation before a judgment is pronounced in the first instance, he or she may be given a lighter punishment as deemed appropriate.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　拒不执行支付赡养费、扶养费、抚育费、抚恤金、医疗费用、劳动报酬等判决、裁定的，可以酌情从重处罚。

**Article 7** Where a person refuses to enforce a judgment or ruling concerning the payment of alimony, support, maintenance, pensions, medical expenses, labor remuneration, etc., he or she may be given a harsher punishment as deemed appropriate.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　本解释自发布之日起施行。此前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 8** This Interpretation shall come into force as of the date of issuance. Any previous judicial interpretations and normative documents that are inconsistent with this Interpretation shall be subject to this Interpretation.
