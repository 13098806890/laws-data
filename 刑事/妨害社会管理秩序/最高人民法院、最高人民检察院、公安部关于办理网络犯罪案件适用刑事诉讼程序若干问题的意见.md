### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>二、关于网络犯罪案件的管辖  
2.网络犯罪案件由犯罪地公安机关立案侦查。必要时，可以由犯罪嫌疑人居住地公安机关立案侦查。  
网络犯罪案件的犯罪地包括用于实施犯罪行为的网站服务器所在地，网络接入地，网站建立者、管理者所在地，被侵害的计算机信息系统或其管理者所在地，犯罪嫌疑人、被害人使用的计算机信息系统所在地，被害人被侵害时所在地，以及被害人财产遭受损失地等。  
涉及多个环节的网络犯罪案件，犯罪嫌疑人为网络犯罪提供帮助的，其犯罪地或者居住地公安机关可以立案侦查。  
3.有多个犯罪地的网络犯罪案件，由最初受理的公安机关或者主要犯罪地公安机关立案侦查。有争议的，按照有利于查清犯罪事实、有利于诉讼的原则，由共同上级公安机关指定有关公安机关立案侦查。需要提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉、提起公诉的，由该公安机关所在地的人民检察院、人民法院受理。  
4.具有下列情形之一的，有关公安机关可以在其职责范围内并案侦查，需要提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉、提起公诉的，由该公安机关所在地的人民检察院、人民法院受理：  
（1）一人犯数罪的；  
（2）共同犯罪的；  
（3）共同犯罪的犯罪嫌疑人、被告人还实施其他犯罪的；  
（4）多个犯罪嫌疑人、被告人实施的犯罪存在关联，并案处理有利于查明案件事实的。  
5.对因网络交易、技术支持、资金支付结算等关系形成多层级链条、跨区域的网络犯罪案件，共同上级公安机关可以按照有利于查清犯罪事实、有利于诉讼的原则，指定有关公安机关一并立案侦查，需要提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉、提起公诉的，由该公安机关所在地的人民检察院、人民法院受理。  
6.具有特殊情况，由异地公安机关立案侦查更有利于查清犯罪事实、保证案件公正处理的跨省（自治区、直辖市）重大网络犯罪案件，可以由公安部商最高人民检察院和最高人民法院指定管辖。  
7.人民检察院对于公安机关移送审查起诉的网络犯罪案件，发现犯罪嫌疑人还有犯罪被其他公安机关立案侦查的，应当通知移送审查起诉的公安机关。  
人民法院受理案件后，发现被告人还有犯罪被其他公安机关立案侦查的，可以建议人民检察院补充侦查。人民检察院经审查，认为需要补充侦查的，应当通知移送审查起诉的公安机关。  
经人民检察院通知，有关公安机关根据案件具体情况，可以对犯罪嫌疑人所犯其他犯罪并案侦查。  
8.为保证及时结案，避免超期羁押，人民检察院对于公安机关提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉的网络犯罪案件，第一审人民法院对于已经受理的网络犯罪案件，经审查发现没有管辖权的，可以依法报请共同上级人民检察院、人民法院指定管辖。  
9.部分犯罪嫌疑人在逃，但不影响对已到案共同犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的犯罪事实认定的网络犯罪案件，可以依法先行追究已到案共同犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的刑事责任。在逃的共同犯罪嫌疑人、被告人归案后，可以由原公安机关、人民检察院、人民法院管辖其所涉及的案件。

**Article 1** II. Jurisdiction Over Cybercrime Cases  
2. A cybercrime case shall be investigated and placed on file by the public security organ of the place where the crime was committed. When necessary, it may be investigated and placed on file by the public security organ of the place where the criminal suspect resides.  
The place where a cybercrime was committed includes the location of the website server used to commit the criminal acts, the network access point, the location of the website founder or administrator, the location of the victimized computer information system or its administrator, the location of the computer information system used by the criminal suspect or victim, the location of the victim at the time of victimization, and the place where the victim's property suffered loss, among others.  
For a cybercrime case involving multiple links, if a criminal suspect provides assistance for the cybercrime, the public security organ of the place where the crime was committed or of the suspect's residence may investigate and place the case on file.  
3. For a cybercrime case with multiple crime locations, the investigation and case filing shall be conducted by the public security organ that first accepted the case or the public security organ of the principal crime location. In case of dispute, the common higher-level public security organ shall designate the relevant public security organ to investigate and place the case on file in accordance with the principles of facilitating the ascertainment of criminal facts and facilitating litigation. Where an application for approval of arrest, referral for prosecution, or initiation of public prosecution is required, it shall be accepted by the People's Procuratorate and the People's Court of the place where the public security organ is located.  
4. In any of the following circumstances, the relevant public security organ may consolidate the cases for investigation within its scope of duties. Where an application for approval of arrest, referral for prosecution, or initiation of public prosecution is required, it shall be accepted by the People's Procuratorate and the People's Court of the place where the public security organ is located:  
(1) one person commits multiple crimes;  
(2) joint crimes;  
(3) a criminal suspect or defendant in a joint crime also commits other crimes;  
(4) the crimes committed by multiple criminal suspects or defendants are related, and consolidated handling facilitates the ascertainment of the facts of the case.  
5. For a multi-level chain, cross-regional cybercrime case formed through relationships such as network transactions, technical support, or fund payment settlement, the common higher-level public security organ may, in accordance with the principles of facilitating the ascertainment of criminal facts and facilitating litigation, designate the relevant public security organ to investigate and place the case on file. Where an application for approval of arrest, referral for prosecution, or initiation of public prosecution is required, it shall be accepted by the People's Procuratorate and the People's Court of the place where the public security organ is located.  
6. For a major cross-provincial (autonomous region, municipality) cybercrime case in which special circumstances exist and investigation and case filing by a public security organ in a different locality would be more conducive to ascertaining the criminal facts and ensuring fair handling of the case, the Ministry of Public Security may, after consulting with the Supreme People's Procuratorate and the Supreme People's Court, designate the jurisdiction.  
7. Where a People's Procuratorate, in reviewing a cybercrime case referred for prosecution by a public security organ, finds that the criminal suspect has also committed other crimes that are being investigated and placed on file by another public security organ, it shall notify the public security organ that referred the case for prosecution.  
After accepting a case, if a People's Court finds that the defendant has also committed other crimes that are being investigated and placed on file by another public security organ, it may recommend that the People's Procuratorate conduct supplementary investigation. If the People's Procuratorate, upon review, deems supplementary investigation necessary, it shall notify the public security organ that referred the case for prosecution.  
Upon notification by the People's Procuratorate, the relevant public security organ may, based on the specific circumstances of the case, consolidate the investigation of the other crimes committed by the criminal suspect.  
8. To ensure timely case closure and avoid prolonged custody, if a People's Procuratorate, in handling a cybercrime case for which the public security organ has applied for approval of arrest or referred for prosecution, or a first-instance People's Court, in handling a cybercrime case that has been accepted, finds upon review that it has no jurisdiction, it may, according to law, report to the common higher-level People's Procuratorate or People's Court for designation of jurisdiction.  
9. For a cybercrime case in which some criminal suspects are at large but this does not affect the determination of the criminal facts of the joint criminal suspects or defendants who have been brought to justice, the criminal liability of the joint criminal suspects or defendants who have been brought to justice may be first pursued according to law. After the joint criminal suspects or defendants who are at large have been brought to justice, the cases involving them may be under the jurisdiction of the original public security organ, People's Procuratorate, and People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>三、关于网络犯罪案件的初查  
10.对接受的案件或者发现的犯罪线索，在审查中发现案件事实或者线索不明，需要经过调查才能够确认是否达到犯罪追诉标准的，经办案部门负责人批准，可以进行初查。  
初查过程中，可以采取询问、查询、勘验、检查、鉴定、调取证据材料等不限制初查对象人身、财产权利的措施，但不得对初查对象采取强制措施和查封、扣押、冻结财产。

**Article 2** III. Preliminary Investigation of Cybercrime Cases  
10. For a case that has been accepted or a criminal clue that has been discovered, if during review it is found that the facts of the case or the clue are unclear and it is necessary to conduct investigation to confirm whether the standard for criminal prosecution is met, a preliminary investigation may be conducted with the approval of the head of the case-handling department.  
During the preliminary investigation, measures that do not restrict the personal or property rights of the subject of the preliminary investigation may be taken, such as inquiry, query, inspection, examination, expert evaluation, and obtaining evidentiary materials. However, compulsory measures and sealing, seizure, or freezing of property shall not be applied to the subject of the preliminary investigation.

<a id="art-3"></a>四、关于网络犯罪案件的跨地域取证  
11.公安机关跨地域调查取证的，可以将办案协作函和相关法律文书及凭证电传或者通过公安机关信息化系统传输至协作地公安机关。协作地公安机关经审查确认，在传来的法律文书上加盖本地公安机关印章后，可以代为调查取证。  
12.询（讯）问异地证人、被害人以及与案件有关联的犯罪嫌疑人的，可以由办案地公安机关通过远程网络视频等方式进行询（讯）问并制作笔录。  
远程询（讯）问的，应当由协作地公安机关事先核实被询（讯）问人的身份。办案地公安机关应当将询（讯）问笔录传输至协作地公安机关。询（讯）问笔录经被询（讯）问人确认并逐页签名、捺指印后，由协作地公安机关协作人员签名或者盖章，并将原件提供给办案地公安机关。询（讯）问人员收到笔录后，应当在首页右上方写明“于某年某月某日收到”，并签名或者盖章。  
远程询（讯）问的，应当对询（讯）问过程进行录音录像，并随案移送。  
异地证人、被害人以及与案件有关联的犯罪嫌疑人亲笔书写证词、供词的，参照本条第二款规定执行。

**Article 3** IV. Cross-Regional Evidence Collection in Cybercrime Cases  
11. Where a public security organ conducts cross-regional investigation and evidence collection, it may transmit the case-handling cooperation letter and relevant legal documents and vouchers by fax or through the public security organs' informatization system to the cooperating public security organ. After review and confirmation, the cooperating public security organ may, after affixing its seal on the transmitted legal documents, conduct the investigation and evidence collection on its behalf.  
12. Where inquiry (interrogation) of a witness, victim, or criminal suspect related to the case located in another locality is to be conducted, the case-handling public security organ may conduct the inquiry (interrogation) via remote network video or other means and make a record.  
For remote inquiry (interrogation), the cooperating public security organ shall verify the identity of the person being inquired (interrogated) in advance. The case-handling public security organ shall transmit the record of inquiry (interrogation) to the cooperating public security organ. After the record of inquiry (interrogation) is confirmed by the person being inquired (interrogated) and signed and fingerprinted page by page, the cooperating personnel of the cooperating public security organ shall sign or seal it and provide the original to the case-handling public security organ. Upon receiving the record, the inquirer (interrogator) shall write on the top right of the first page "Received on ____ year ____ month ____ day" and sign or seal it.  
For remote inquiry (interrogation), the process of inquiry (interrogation) shall be audio-video recorded and transferred with the case.  
Where a witness, victim, or criminal suspect related to the case located in another locality writes a testimony or confession in his/her own handwriting, the provisions of the second paragraph of this Article shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-4"></a>五、关于电子数据的取证与审查  
13.收集、提取电子数据，应当由二名以上具备相关专业知识的侦查人员进行。取证设备和过程应当符合相关技术标准，并保证所收集、提取的电子数据的完整性、客观性。  
14.收集、提取电子数据，能够获取原始存储介质的，应当封存原始存储介质，并制作笔录，记录原始存储介质的封存状态，由侦查人员、原始存储介质持有人签名或者盖章；持有人无法签名或者拒绝签名的，应当在笔录中注明，由见证人签名或者盖章。有条件的，侦查人员应当对相关活动进行录像。  
15.具有下列情形之一，无法获取原始存储介质的，可以提取电子数据，但应当在笔录中注明不能获取原始存储介质的原因、原始存储介质的存放地点等情况，并由侦查人员、电子数据持有人、提供人签名或者盖章；持有人、提供人无法签名或者拒绝签名的，应当在笔录中注明，由见证人签名或者盖章；有条件的，侦查人员应当对相关活动进行录像：  
（1）原始存储介质不便封存的；  
（2）提取计算机内存存储的数据、网络传输的数据等不是存储在存储介质上的电子数据的；  
（3）原始存储介质位于境外的；  
（4）其他无法获取原始存储介质的情形。  
16.收集、提取电子数据应当制作笔录，记录案由、对象、内容，收集、提取电子数据的时间、地点、方法、过程，电子数据的清单、规格、类别、文件格式、完整性校验值等，并由收集、提取电子数据的侦查人员签名或者盖章。远程提取电子数据的，应当说明原因，有条件的，应当对相关活动进行录像。通过数据恢复、破解等方式获取被删除、隐藏或者加密的电子数据的，应当对恢复、破解过程和方法作出说明。  
17.收集、提取的原始存储介质或者电子数据，应当以封存状态随案移送，并制作电子数据的复制件一并移送。  
对文档、图片、网页等可以直接展示的电子数据，可以不随案移送电子数据打印件，但应当附有展示方法说明和展示工具；人民法院、人民检察院因设备等条件限制无法直接展示电子数据的，公安机关应当随案移送打印件。  
对侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的程序、工具以及计算机病毒等无法直接展示的电子数据，应当附有电子数据属性、功能等情况的说明。  
对数据统计数量、数据同一性等问题，公安机关应当出具说明。  
18.对电子数据涉及的专门性问题难以确定的，由司法鉴定机构出具鉴定意见，或者由公安部指定的机构出具检验报告。

**Article 4** V. Collection and Examination of Electronic Data  
13. The collection and extraction of electronic data shall be conducted by at least two investigators possessing relevant professional knowledge. The equipment and process for evidence collection shall comply with relevant technical standards and ensure the integrity and objectivity of the collected and extracted electronic data.  
14. When collecting and extracting electronic data, if the original storage medium can be obtained, the original storage medium shall be sealed and a record shall be made, documenting the sealing status of the original storage medium. The record shall be signed or sealed by the investigators and the holder of the original storage medium. If the holder is unable to sign or refuses to sign, this shall be noted in the record and the witness shall sign or seal it. Where conditions permit, investigators shall video-record the relevant activities.  
15. In any of the following circumstances, if the original storage medium cannot be obtained, the electronic data may be extracted, but the record shall specify the reason why the original storage medium cannot be obtained and the storage location of the original storage medium, among others, and the record shall be signed or sealed by the investigators, the holder of the electronic data, and the provider; if the holder or provider is unable to sign or refuses to sign, this shall be noted in the record, and the witness shall sign or seal it; where conditions permit, investigators shall video-record the relevant activities:  
(1) The original storage medium is inconvenient to seal;  
(2) Extracting data stored in computer memory, data transmitted over networks, or other electronic data not stored on a storage medium;  
(3) The original storage medium is located abroad;  
(4) Other circumstances where the original storage medium cannot be obtained.  
16. The collection and extraction of electronic data shall be documented in a record, including the case cause, object, content, time, location, method, and process of collection and extraction, the list, specifications, category, file format, and integrity check value of the electronic data, and shall be signed or sealed by the investigators who collected and extracted the electronic data. For remote extraction of electronic data, the reason shall be stated, and where conditions permit, the relevant activities shall be video-recorded. Where electronic data that has been deleted, hidden, or encrypted is obtained through data recovery, decryption, or other means, the process and method of recovery or decryption shall be explained.  
17. The collected and extracted original storage medium or electronic data shall be transferred with the case in a sealed state, and a copy of the electronic data shall be made and transferred together.  
For electronic data that can be directly displayed, such as documents, pictures, and webpages, printed copies of the electronic data need not be transferred with the case, but a description of the display method and display tools shall be attached; if the People's Court or People's Procuratorate cannot directly display the electronic data due to equipment or other constraints, the public security organ shall transfer printed copies with the case.  
For electronic data that cannot be directly displayed, such as programs, tools for intruding into or illegally controlling computer information systems, and computer viruses, a description of the attributes and functions of the electronic data shall be attached.  
Regarding issues such as the quantity of data statistics and data identity, the public security organ shall issue a statement.  
18. Where specialized issues involving electronic data are difficult to determine, an expert opinion shall be issued by a judicial authentication institution, or an inspection report shall be issued by an institution designated by the Ministry of Public Security.

<a id="art-5"></a>六、关于网络犯罪案件的其他问题  
19.采取技术侦查措施收集的材料作为证据使用的，应当随案移送批准采取技术侦查措施的法律文书和所收集的证据材料。使用有关证据材料可能危及有关人员的人身安全，或者可能产生其他严重后果的，应当采取不暴露有关人员身份、技术方法等保护措施，必要时，可以由审判人员在庭外进行核实。  
20.对针对或者组织、教唆、帮助不特定多数人实施的网络犯罪案件，确因客观条件限制无法逐一收集相关言词证据的，可以根据记录被害人数、被侵害的计算机信息系统数量、涉案资金数额等犯罪事实的电子数据、书证等证据材料，在慎重审查被告人及其辩护人所提辩解、辩护意见的基础上，综合全案证据材料，对相关犯罪事实作出认定。

**Article 5** VI. Other Issues Concerning Cybercrime Cases  
19. Where materials collected through technical investigation measures are used as evidence, the legal documents approving the adoption of technical investigation measures and the collected evidentiary materials shall be transferred with the case. If the use of relevant evidentiary materials may endanger the personal safety of relevant persons or may cause other serious consequences, protective measures such as not disclosing the identity of relevant persons and technical methods shall be taken; where necessary, the materials may be verified outside the courtroom by the adjudicators.  
20. For a cybercrime case committed against or through organizing, instigating, or assisting an unspecified majority of persons, if it is truly impossible to collect the relevant testimonial evidence one by one due to objective conditions, the determination of the relevant criminal facts may be made based on the electronic data, documentary evidence, and other evidentiary materials recording the number of victims, the number of victimized computer information systems, the amount of funds involved, and other criminal facts, and on the basis of careful examination of the defenses and opinions presented by the defendant and his/her defender, and by comprehensively considering all evidentiary materials in the case.
