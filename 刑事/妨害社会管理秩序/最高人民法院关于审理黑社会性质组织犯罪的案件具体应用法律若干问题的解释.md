# 最高人民法院关于审理黑社会性质组织犯罪的案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕42号  
**公布日期**：2000-12-04  
**生效日期**：2000-12-10

> （2000年12月4日最高人民法院审判委员会第1148次会议通过，自2000年12月10日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕42号 为依法惩治黑社会性质组织的犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现就审理黑社会性质组织的犯罪案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 刑法第二百九十四条规定的“黑社会性质的组织”，一般应具备以下特征： （一）组织结构比较紧密，人数较多，有比较明确的组织者、领导者，骨干成员基本固定，有较为严格的组织纪律； （二）通过违法犯罪活动或者其他手段获取经济利益，具有一定的经济实力； （三）通过贿赂、威胁等手段，引诱、逼迫国家工作人员参加黑社会性质组织活动，或者为其提供非法保护； （四）在一定区域或者行业范围内，以暴力、威胁、滋扰等手段，大肆进行敲诈勒索、欺行霸市、聚众斗殴、寻衅滋事、故意伤害等违法犯罪活动，严重破坏经济、社会生活秩序。 第二条 刑法第二百九十四条第二款规定的“发展组织成员”，是指将境内、外人员吸收为该黑社会组织成员的行为。对黑社会组织成员进行内部调整等行为，可视为“发展组织成员”。 港、澳、台黑社会组织到内地发展组织成员的，适用刑法第二百九十四条第二款的规定定罪处罚。 第三条 组织、领导、参加黑社会性质的组织又有其他犯罪行为的，根据刑法第二百九十四条第三款的规定，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚；对于黑社会性质组织的组织者、领导者，应当按照其所组织、领导的黑社会性质组织所犯的全部罪行处罚；对于黑社会性质组织的参加者，应当按照其所参与的犯罪处罚。 对于参加黑社会性质的组织，没有实施其他违法犯罪活动的，或者受蒙蔽、胁迫参加黑社会性质的组织，情节轻微的，可以不作为犯罪处理。 第四条 国家机关工作人员组织、领导、参加黑社会性质组织的，从重处罚。 第五条 刑法第二百九十四条第四款规定的“包庇”，是指国家机关工作人员为使黑社会性质组织及其成员逃避查禁，而通风报信，隐匿、毁灭、伪造证据，阻止他人作证、检举揭发，指使他人作伪证，帮助逃匿，或者阻挠其他国家机关工作人员依法查禁等行为。 刑法第二百九十四条第四款规定的“纵容”，是指国家机关工作人员不依法履行职责，放纵黑社会性质组织进行违法犯罪活动的行为。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 [刑法第二百九十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-294)规定的“黑社会性质的组织”，一般应具备以下特征：  
（一）组织结构比较紧密，人数较多，有比较明确的组织者、领导者，骨干成员基本固定，有较为严格的组织纪律；  
（二）通过违法犯罪活动或者其他手段获取经济利益，具有一定的经济实力；  
（三）通过贿赂、威胁等手段，引诱、逼迫国家工作人员参加黑社会性质组织活动，或者为其提供非法保护；  
（四）在一定区域或者行业范围内，以暴力、威胁、滋扰等手段，大肆进行敲诈勒索、欺行霸市、聚众斗殴、寻衅滋事、故意伤害等违法犯罪活动，严重破坏经济、社会生活秩序。

**Article 1** The term "organization of a mafia-like nature" as provided for in Article 294 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall generally possess the following characteristics:  
(1) a relatively tight organizational structure with a relatively large number of members, relatively clear organizers and leaders, basically fixed core members, and relatively strict organizational discipline;  
(2) obtaining economic benefits through illegal or criminal activities or other means, and possessing a certain economic strength;  
(3) inducing or coercing state functionaries to participate in the activities of the organization of a mafia-like nature, or providing illegal protection for it, through bribery, threats, or other means;  
(4) within a certain region or industry, engaging in violent, threatening, harassing or other means to commit extortion, market bullying, affray, picking quarrels and provoking trouble, intentional injury, and other illegal or criminal activities on a large scale, seriously disrupting the economic and social living order.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 [刑法第二百九十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-294)第二款规定的“发展组织成员”，是指将境内、外人员吸收为该黑社会组织成员的行为。对黑社会组织成员进行内部调整等行为，可视为“发展组织成员”。  
港、澳、台黑社会组织到内地发展组织成员的，适用[刑法第二百九十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-294)第二款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 2** The term "developing members of the organization" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 294 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China refers to the act of recruiting persons from within or outside the country as members of the mafia organization. Acts such as internal adjustments among members of the mafia organization may be regarded as "developing members of the organization."  
Where a mafia organization from Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan comes to the mainland to develop members of the organization, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 294 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall apply for conviction and punishment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 组织、领导、参加黑社会性质的组织又有其他犯罪行为的，根据[刑法第二百九十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-294)第三款的规定，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚；对于黑社会性质组织的组织者、领导者，应当按照其所组织、领导的黑社会性质组织所犯的全部罪行处罚；对于黑社会性质组织的参加者，应当按照其所参与的犯罪处罚。  
对于参加黑社会性质的组织，没有实施其他违法犯罪活动的，或者受蒙蔽、胁迫参加黑社会性质的组织，情节轻微的，可以不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 3** Where a person organizes, leads, or participates in an organization of a mafia-like nature and also commits other criminal acts, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 294 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes; the organizer or leader of the organization of a mafia-like nature shall be punished for all the crimes committed by the organization of a mafia-like nature under his or her organization or leadership; a participant in the organization of a mafia-like nature shall be punished for the crimes in which he or she participated.  
Where a person participates in an organization of a mafia-like nature but has not committed other illegal or criminal activities, or is deceived or coerced into participating in an organization of a mafia-like nature, if the circumstances are minor, the act may not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 国家机关工作人员组织、领导、参加黑社会性质组织的，从重处罚。

**Article 4** Where a state functionary organizes, leads, or participates in an organization of a mafia-like nature, he or she shall be given a severe punishment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 [刑法第二百九十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-294)第四款规定的“包庇”，是指国家机关工作人员为使黑社会性质组织及其成员逃避查禁，而通风报信，隐匿、毁灭、伪造证据，阻止他人作证、检举揭发，指使他人作伪证，帮助逃匿，或者阻挠其他国家机关工作人员依法查禁等行为。  
[刑法第二百九十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-294)第四款规定的“纵容”，是指国家机关工作人员不依法履行职责，放纵黑社会性质组织进行违法犯罪活动的行为。

**Article 5** The term "harboring" as provided for in paragraph 4 of Article 294 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China refers to the acts of a state functionary who, in order to enable an organization of a mafia-like nature or its members to evade investigation and prohibition, leaks information, conceals, destroys, or forges evidence, prevents others from testifying or reporting, instructs others to give false testimony, assists in escape, or obstructs other state functionaries from carrying out investigations and prohibitions according to law.  
The term "conniving at" as provided for in paragraph 4 of Article 294 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China refers to the act of a state functionary who fails to perform his or her duties according to law and allows an organization of a mafia-like nature to engage in illegal or criminal activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 国家机关工作人员包庇、纵容黑社会性质的组织，有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百九十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-294)第四款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）包庇、纵容黑社会性质组织跨境实施违法犯罪活动的；  
（二）包庇、纵容境外黑社会组织在境内实施违法犯罪活动的；  
（三）多次实施包庇、纵容行为的；  
（四）致使某一区域或者行业的经济、社会生活秩序遭受黑社会性质组织特别严重破坏的；  
（五）致使黑社会性质组织的组织者、领导者逃匿，或者致使对黑社会性质组织的查禁工作严重受阻的；  
（六）具有其他严重情节的。

**Article 6** Where a state functionary harbors or connives at an organization of a mafia-like nature and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute "serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 4 of Article 294 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) harboring or conniving at an organization of a mafia-like nature in carrying out illegal or criminal activities across borders;  
(2) harboring or conniving at an overseas mafia organization in carrying out illegal or criminal activities within the country;  
(3) committing acts of harboring or conniving at multiple times;  
(4) causing particularly serious disruption to the economic and social living order of a certain region or industry by the organization of a mafia-like nature;  
(5) causing the organizer or leader of the organization of a mafia-like nature to escape, or causing serious obstruction to the investigation and prohibition of the organization of a mafia-like nature;  
(6) having other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 对黑社会性质组织和组织、领导、参加黑社会性质组织的犯罪分子聚敛的财物及其收益，以及用于犯罪的工具等，应当依法追缴、没收。

**Article 7** The property and proceeds accumulated by the organization of a mafia-like nature and by the criminals who organize, lead, or participate in the organization of a mafia-like nature, as well as the tools used for crimes, shall be recovered and confiscated according to law.
