# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理非法从事资金支付结算业务、非法买卖外汇刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2019〕1号  
**公布日期**：2018-09-17

> 最高人民法院最高人民检察院《关于办理非法从事资金支付结算业务、非法买卖外汇刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2018年9月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1749次会议、2018年12月12日最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第十一次会议通过，现予公布，自2019年2月1日起施行。 2019年1月31日 最高人民法院    最高人民检察院 关于办理非法从事资金支付结算业务、 非法买卖外汇刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2019〕1号 为依法惩治非法从事资金支付结算业务、非法买卖外汇犯罪活动，维护金融市场秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定，现就办理非法从事资金支付结算业务、非法买卖外汇刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　违反国家规定，具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第二百二十五条第三项规定的“非法从事资金支付结算业务”： （一）使用受理终端或者网络支付接口等方法，以虚构交易、虚开价格、交易退款等非法方式向指定付款方支付货币资金的； （二）非法为他人提供单位银行结算账户套现或者单位银行结算账户转个人账户服务的； （三）非法为他人提供支票套现服务的； （四）其他非法从事资金支付结算业务的情形。 第二条　违反国家规定，实施倒买倒卖外汇或者变相买卖外汇等非法买卖外汇行为，扰乱金融市场秩序，情节严重的，依照刑法第二百二十五条第四项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。 第三条　非法从事资金支付结算业务或者非法买卖外汇，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为非法经营行为“情节严重”：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　违反国家规定，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第三项规定的“非法从事资金支付结算业务”：  
（一）使用受理终端或者网络支付接口等方法，以虚构交易、虚开价格、交易退款等非法方式向指定付款方支付货币资金的；  
（二）非法为他人提供单位银行结算账户套现或者单位银行结算账户转个人账户服务的；  
（三）非法为他人提供支票套现服务的；  
（四）其他非法从事资金支付结算业务的情形。

**Article 1** Whoever violates state regulations and falls under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to have "illegally engaged in fund payment and settlement business" as stipulated in Item (3) of Article 225 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) using methods such as acceptance terminals or network payment interfaces to pay monetary funds to designated payees in illegal ways such as fabricating transactions, inflating prices, or refunding transactions;  
(2) illegally providing cash-out services for corporate bank settlement accounts or services for transferring corporate bank settlement accounts to personal accounts for others;  
(3) illegally providing check cash-out services for others;  
(4) other circumstances of illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　违反国家规定，实施倒买倒卖外汇或者变相买卖外汇等非法买卖外汇行为，扰乱金融市场秩序，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第四项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。

**Article 2** Whoever violates state regulations by engaging in illegal foreign exchange trading such as buying and selling foreign exchange or disguised foreign exchange trading, thereby disrupting the financial market order, if the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operation in accordance with Item (4) of Article 225 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　非法从事资金支付结算业务或者非法买卖外汇，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为非法经营行为“情节严重”：  
（一）非法经营数额在五百万元以上的；  
（二）违法所得数额在十万元以上的。  
非法经营数额在二百五十万元以上，或者违法所得数额在五万元以上，且具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为非法经营行为“情节严重”：  
（一）曾因非法从事资金支付结算业务或者非法买卖外汇犯罪行为受过刑事追究的；  
（二）二年内因非法从事资金支付结算业务或者非法买卖外汇违法行为受过行政处罚的；  
（三）拒不交代涉案资金去向或者拒不配合追缴工作，致使赃款无法追缴的；  
（四）造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 3** Illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trading foreign exchange, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as having "serious circumstances" of illegal business operation:  
(1) the amount of illegal business operation is five million yuan or more;  
(2) the amount of illegal gains is one hundred thousand yuan or more.  
Where the amount of illegal business operation is 2.5 million yuan or more, or the amount of illegal gains is fifty thousand yuan or more, and in any of the following circumstances, it may be deemed as having "serious circumstances" of illegal business operation:  
(1) having previously been subject to criminal punishment for illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trading foreign exchange;  
(2) having been subject to administrative penalty within two years for illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trading foreign exchange;  
(3) refusing to account for the whereabouts of the funds involved or refusing to cooperate with recovery efforts, rendering the illegal proceeds unrecoverable;  
(4) causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　非法从事资金支付结算业务或者非法买卖外汇，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为非法经营行为“情节特别严重”：  
（一）非法经营数额在二千五百万元以上的；  
（二）违法所得数额在五十万元以上的。  
非法经营数额在一千二百五十万元以上，或者违法所得数额在二十五万元以上，且具有[本解释第三条](#art-3)第二款规定的四种情形之一的，可以认定为非法经营行为“情节特别严重”。

**Article 4** Illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trading foreign exchange, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as having "particularly serious circumstances" of illegal business operation:  
(1) the amount of illegal business operation is 25 million yuan or more;  
(2) the amount of illegal gains is 500,000 yuan or more.  
Where the amount of illegal business operation is 12.5 million yuan or more, or the amount of illegal gains is 250,000 yuan or more, and falls under any of the four circumstances provided in Paragraph 2 of Article 3 of this Interpretation, it may be deemed as having "particularly serious circumstances" of illegal business operation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　非法从事资金支付结算业务或者非法买卖外汇，构成非法经营罪，同时又构成[刑法第一百二十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-120)之一规定的帮助恐怖活动罪或者第一百九十一条规定的洗钱罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trading foreign exchange constitutes the crime of illegal business operation, and at the same time constitutes the crime of assisting terrorist activities as provided in Article 120-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China or the crime of money laundering as provided in Article 191, the conviction and punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provision prescribing the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　二次以上非法从事资金支付结算业务或者非法买卖外汇，依法应予行政处理或者刑事处理而未经处理的，非法经营数额或者违法所得数额累计计算。  
同一案件中，非法经营数额、违法所得数额分别构成情节严重、情节特别严重的，按照处罚较重的数额定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where a person illegally engages in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trades foreign exchange two or more times, and those acts should have been subject to administrative or criminal handling according to law but have not been handled, the amount of illegal business operation or the amount of illegal gains shall be calculated cumulatively.  
Where in the same case, the amount of illegal business operation and the amount of illegal gains respectively constitute serious circumstances and particularly serious circumstances, conviction and punishment shall be based on the amount that results in the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　非法从事资金支付结算业务或者非法买卖外汇违法所得数额难以确定的，按非法经营数额的千分之一认定违法所得数额，依法并处或者单处违法所得一倍以上五倍以下罚金。

**Article 7** Where the amount of illegal gains from illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trading foreign exchange is difficult to determine, the amount of illegal gains shall be determined as one-thousandth of the amount of illegal business operation, and a fine of not less than one time but not more than five times the amount of illegal gains shall be imposed concurrently or separately according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　符合[本解释第三条](#art-3)规定的标准，行为人如实供述犯罪事实，认罪悔罪，并积极配合调查，退缴违法所得的，可以从轻处罚；其中犯罪情节轻微的，可以依法不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。  
符合刑事诉讼法规定的认罪认罚从宽适用范围和条件的，依照刑事诉讼法的规定处理。

**Article 8** Where the standards provided in Article 3 of this Interpretation are met, and the actor truthfully confesses the criminal facts, admits guilt and shows repentance, and actively cooperates with the investigation and returns the illegal gains, a lighter punishment may be imposed; where the criminal circumstances are minor, the actor may, according to law, be not prosecuted or be exempted from criminal punishment.  
Where the scope and conditions for leniency based on admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment as stipulated in the Criminal Procedure Law are met, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　单位实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条规定的非法从事资金支付结算业务、非法买卖外汇行为，依照本解释规定的定罪量刑标准，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Where a unit commits the acts of illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trading foreign exchange as provided in Article 1 and Article 2 of this Interpretation, the unit shall be fined in accordance with the standards for conviction and sentencing provided in this Interpretation, and the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　非法从事资金支付结算业务、非法买卖外汇刑事案件中的犯罪地，包括犯罪嫌疑人、被告人用于犯罪活动的账户开立地、资金接收地、资金过渡账户开立地、资金账户操作地，以及资金交易对手资金交付和汇出地等。

**Article 10** The place of crime in criminal cases of illegally engaging in fund payment and settlement business or illegally trading foreign exchange includes the place where the criminal suspect or defendant opened accounts used for criminal activities, the place where funds are received, the place where fund transit accounts are opened, the place where fund accounts are operated, and the place where the counterparty delivers and remits funds, among others.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　涉及外汇的犯罪数额，按照案发当日中国外汇交易中心或者中国人民银行授权机构公布的人民币对该货币的中间价折合成人民币计算。中国外汇交易中心或者中国人民银行授权机构未公布汇率中间价的境外货币，按照案发当日境内银行人民币对该货币的中间价折算成人民币，或者该货币在境内银行、国际外汇市场对美元汇率，与人民币对美元汇率中间价进行套算。

**Article 11** The amount of crime involving foreign exchange shall be calculated by converting it into RMB according to the central parity rate of RMB against that currency published by the China Foreign Exchange Trade System or an institution authorized by the People's Bank of China on the date of the incident. For foreign currencies for which the central parity rate is not published by the China Foreign Exchange Trade System or an institution authorized by the People's Bank of China, the amount shall be converted into RMB according to the central parity rate of RMB against that currency published by domestic banks on the date of the incident, or by cross calculation of the exchange rate of that currency against the US dollar in domestic banks or the international foreign exchange market with the central parity rate of RMB against the US dollar.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本解释自2019年2月1日起施行。《最高人民法院关于审理骗购外汇、非法买卖外汇刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔1998〕20号）与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 12** This Interpretation shall come into force on February 1, 2019. Where the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Fraudulently Purchasing Foreign Exchange and Illegally Trading Foreign Exchange (Fa Shi [1998] No. 20) is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
