# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理妨害国（边）境管理刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　领导、策划、指挥他人偷越国（边）境或者在首要分子指挥下，实施拉拢、引诱、介绍他人偷越国（边）境等行为的，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-318)规定的“组织他人偷越国（边）境”。  
组织他人偷越国（边）境人数在十人以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-318)第一款第（二）项规定的“人数众多”；违法所得数额在二十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-318)第一款第（六）项规定的“违法所得数额巨大”。  
以组织他人偷越国（边）境为目的，招募、拉拢、引诱、介绍、培训偷越国（边）境人员，策划、安排偷越国（边）境行为，在他人偷越国（边）境之前或者偷越国（边）境过程中被查获的，应当以组织他人偷越国（边）境罪（未遂）论处；具有[刑法第三百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-318)第一款规定的情形之一的，应当在相应的法定刑幅度基础上，结合未遂犯的处罚原则量刑。

**Article 1** : Leading, planning, or directing others to illegally cross the national border (frontier), or under the direction of a principal, committing acts such as soliciting, luring, or introducing others to illegally cross the national border (frontier), shall be deemed as "organizing others to illegally cross the national border (frontier)" as provided in Article 318 of the Criminal Law.  
If the number of persons organized to illegally cross the national border (frontier) reaches ten or more, it shall be deemed as "a large number of persons" as provided in subparagraph (2) of paragraph 1 of Article 318 of the Criminal Law; if the amount of illegal gains reaches 200,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "a huge amount of illegal gains" as provided in subparagraph (6) of paragraph 1 of Article 318 of the Criminal Law.  
If a person, for the purpose of organizing others to illegally cross the national border (frontier), recruits, solicits, lures, introduces, or trains persons to illegally cross the national border (frontier), or plans or arranges acts of illegally crossing the national border (frontier), and is discovered before the illegal crossing or during the process, the person shall be punished for the crime of organizing others to illegally cross the national border (frontier) (attempted); if any of the circumstances provided in paragraph 1 of Article 318 of the Criminal Law exists, the sentence shall be determined within the corresponding statutory punishment range, taking into account the sentencing principles for attempted offenders.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　为组织他人偷越国（边）境，编造出境事由、身份信息或者相关的境外关系证明的，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-319)第一款规定的“弄虚作假”。  
[刑法第三百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-319)第一款规定的“出境证件”，包括护照或者代替护照使用的国际旅行证件，中华人民共和国海员证，中华人民共和国出入境通行证，中华人民共和国旅行证，中国公民往来香港、澳门、台湾地区证件，边境地区出入境通行证，签证、签注，出国（境）证明、名单，以及其他出境时需要查验的资料。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-319)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）骗取出境证件五份以上的；  
（二）非法收取费用三十万元以上的；  
（三）明知是国家规定的不准出境的人员而为其骗取出境证件的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** : If, for the purpose of organizing others to illegally cross the national border (frontier), a person fabricates the purpose of exit, identity information, or relevant proof of overseas relations, it shall be deemed as "practicing fraud" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 319 of the Criminal Law.  
The "exit documents" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 319 of the Criminal Law include: passports or international travel documents used in lieu of passports; the People's Republic of China Seaman's Book; the People's Republic of China Exit-Entry Permit; the People's Republic of China Travel Permit; documents for Chinese citizens traveling to and from Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan; border area exit-entry permits; visas and endorsements; certificates or lists for leaving the country (border); and other materials required for inspection upon exit.  
Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 319 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Obtaining five or more exit documents by fraud;  
(2) Illegally collecting fees of 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) Knowingly obtaining exit documents by fraud for a person who is prohibited from leaving the country as stipulated by state regulations;  
(4) Other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　[刑法第三百二十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-320)规定的“出入境证件”，包括[本解释第二条](#art-2)第二款所列的证件以及其他入境时需要查验的资料。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-320)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）为他人提供伪造、变造的出入境证件或者出售出入境证件五份以上的；  
（二）非法收取费用三十万元以上的；  
（三）明知是国家规定的不准出入境的人员而为其提供伪造、变造的出入境证件或者向其出售出入境证件的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 3** : The "exit-entry documents" as provided in Article 320 of the Criminal Law include the documents listed in paragraph 2 of Article 2 of this Interpretation and other materials required for inspection upon entry.  
Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 320 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Providing forged or altered exit-entry documents to others or selling five or more exit-entry documents;  
(2) Illegally collecting fees of 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) Knowingly providing forged or altered exit-entry documents to a person who is prohibited from entering or leaving the country as stipulated by state regulations, or selling exit-entry documents to such person;  
(4) Other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　运送他人偷越国（边）境人数在十人以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-321)第一款第（一）项规定的“人数众多”；违法所得数额在二十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-321)第一款第（三）项规定的“违法所得数额巨大”。

**Article 4** : If the number of persons transported to illegally cross the national border (frontier) reaches ten or more, it shall be deemed as "a large number of persons" as provided in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 321 of the Criminal Law; if the amount of illegal gains reaches 200,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "a huge amount of illegal gains" as provided in subparagraph (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 321 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　偷越国（边）境，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-322)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）在境外实施损害国家利益行为的；  
（二）偷越国（边）境三次以上或者三人以上结伙偷越国（边）境的；  
（三）拉拢、引诱他人一起偷越国（边）境的；  
（四）勾结境外组织、人员偷越国（边）境的；  
（五）因偷越国（边）境被行政处罚后一年内又偷越国（边）境的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 5** : If a person illegally crosses the national border (frontier) under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 322 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Committing acts that harm national interests abroad;  
(2) Illegally crossing the national border (frontier) three times or more, or three or more persons acting in concert to illegally cross the national border (frontier);  
(3) Soliciting or luring others to illegally cross the national border (frontier) together;  
(4) Colluding with overseas organizations or persons to illegally cross the national border (frontier);  
(5) Having been subjected to administrative penalty for illegally crossing the national border (frontier) and then illegally crossing again within one year;  
(6) Other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第六章第三节规定的“偷越国（边）境”行为：  
（一）没有出入境证件出入国（边）境或者逃避接受边防检查的；  
（二）使用伪造、变造、无效的出入境证件出入国（边）境的；  
（三）使用他人出入境证件出入国（边）境的；  
（四）使用以虚假的出入境事由、隐瞒真实身份、冒用他人身份证件等方式骗取的出入境证件出入国（边）境的；  
（五）采用其他方式非法出入国（边）境的。

**Article 6** : Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as an act of "illegally crossing the national border (frontier)" as provided in Section 3 of Chapter VI of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Entering or leaving the national border (frontier) without exit-entry documents or evading border inspection;  
(2) Entering or leaving the national border (frontier) by using forged, altered, or invalid exit-entry documents;  
(3) Entering or leaving the national border (frontier) by using another person's exit-entry documents;  
(4) Entering or leaving the national border (frontier) by using exit-entry documents obtained through fraud, such as false reasons for entry/exit, concealing true identity, or using another person's identity documents;  
(5) Illegally entering or leaving the national border (frontier) by other means.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　以单位名义或者单位形式组织他人偷越国（边）境、为他人提供伪造、变造的出入境证件或者运送他人偷越国（边）境的，应当依照[刑法第三百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-318)、第三百二十条、第三百二十一条的规定追究直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 7** : If a person, in the name of a unit or in the form of a unit, organizes others to illegally cross the national border (frontier), provides others with forged or altered exit-entry documents, or transports others to illegally cross the national border (frontier), the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with Articles 318, 320, and 321 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　实施组织他人偷越国（边）境犯罪，同时构成骗取出境证件罪、提供伪造、变造的出入境证件罪、出售出入境证件罪、运送他人偷越国（边）境罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 8** : If a crime of organizing others to illegally cross the national border (frontier) is committed and at the same time constitutes the crime of fraudulently obtaining exit documents, the crime of providing forged or altered exit-entry documents, the crime of selling exit-entry documents, or the crime of transporting others to illegally cross the national border (frontier), the person shall be convicted and punished according to the provision with the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对跨地区实施的不同妨害国（边）境管理犯罪，符合并案处理要求，有关地方公安机关依照法律和相关规定一并立案侦查，需要提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉、提起公诉的，由该公安机关所在地的同级人民检察院、人民法院依法受理。

**Article 9** : For different crimes of obstructing national border (frontier) administration committed across regions, if the requirements for consolidated case handling are met, the relevant local public security organs shall, in accordance with the law and relevant regulations, place the case on file and conduct investigation together. If a request for approval of arrest, referral for review and prosecution, or initiation of public prosecution is required, the case shall be accepted according to law by the People's Procuratorate and the People's Court at the same level as the place where the public security organ is located.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本解释发布实施后，《最高人民法院关于审理组织、运送他人偷越国（边）境等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2002〕3号）不再适用。

**Article 10** : After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into effect, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Organizing or Transporting Others to Illegally Cross the National Border (Frontier) (Fa Shi [2002] No. 3) shall cease to apply.
