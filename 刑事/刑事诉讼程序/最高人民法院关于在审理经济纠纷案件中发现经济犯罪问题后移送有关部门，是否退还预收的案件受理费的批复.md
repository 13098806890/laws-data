### 最高人民法院关于在审理经济纠纷案件中发现经济犯罪问题后移送有关部门，是否退还预收的案件受理费的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于在审理经济纠纷案件中发现经济犯罪问题后  
  
移送有关部门，是否退还预收的案件受理费的批复  
  
法（司）复〔 1986〕29号  
  
贵州省高级人民法院：  
  
你院黔法〔 1986〕17号“关于在审理经济纠纷案件中发现经济犯罪移送有关部门后，是否退还预收的案件受理费的请示报告”收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
第一审人民法院在审理经济纠纷案件中，发现经济犯罪，移送有关部门依法处理时，对当事人所预交的案件受理费，应分别不同情况进行处理：案件全部移送的，预收的案件受理费应全部退还；案件部分移送，即只移送经济犯罪部分，经济纠纷仍需继续审理的，预收的受理费不予退还。至于实际应收取多少，待案件审结后，按核定的数额计算。  
  
第二审人民法院在审理经济纠纷上诉案件中，发现经济犯罪移送有关部门处理时，对所预收的受理费的处理方法与第一审相同。  
  
此复  
  
一九八六年八月二十八日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether to Refund Prepaid Case Acceptance Fees When Economic Crimes Are Discovered During Trial of Economic Dispute Cases and  
Transferred to Relevant Departments  
Fa (Si) Fu [1986] No. 29  
Guizhou Higher People's Court:  
Your Court's Qian Fa [1986] No. 17 "Request for Instructions on Whether to Refund Prepaid Case Acceptance Fees When Economic Crimes Are Discovered During Trial of Economic Dispute Cases and Transferred to Relevant Departments" has been received and studied. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
When a court of first instance discovers an economic crime during the trial of an economic dispute case and transfers the case to the relevant department for handling according to law, the case acceptance fee prepaid by the party shall be handled according to different circumstances: If the entire case is transferred, the prepaid case acceptance fee shall be fully refunded; if part of the case is transferred, i.e., only the economic crime part is transferred and the economic dispute still needs to be tried, the prepaid acceptance fee shall not be refunded. As for the actual amount that should be charged, it shall be calculated based on the verified amount after the case is concluded.  
When a court of second instance discovers an economic crime during the trial of an economic dispute appeal case and transfers it to the relevant department for handling, the method of handling the prepaid acceptance fee shall be the same as that for the first instance.  
This reply is issued.  
August 28, 1986
