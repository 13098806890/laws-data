# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部关于规范量刑程序若干问题的意见

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法发〔2020〕38号  
**公布日期**：0000-00-00  
**生效日期**：2020-11-06

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　人民法院审理刑事案件，在法庭审理中应当保障量刑程序的相对独立性。  
人民检察院在审查起诉中应当规范量刑建议。

**Article 1** When a People's Court adjudicates a criminal case, it shall guarantee the relative independence of the sentencing procedure during court adjudication.  
The People's Procuratorate shall standardize sentencing recommendations during prosecution review.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　侦查机关、人民检察院应当依照法定程序，全面收集、审查、移送证明犯罪嫌疑人、被告人犯罪事实、量刑情节的证据。  
对于法律规定并处或者单处财产刑的案件，侦查机关应当根据案件情况对被告人的财产状况进行调查，并向人民检察院移送相关证据材料。人民检察院应当审查并向人民法院移送相关证据材料。  
人民检察院在审查起诉时发现侦查机关应当收集而未收集量刑证据的，可以退回侦查机关补充侦查，也可以自行侦查。人民检察院退回补充侦查的，侦查机关应当按照人民检察院退回补充侦查提纲的要求及时收集相关证据。

**Article 2** Investigation authorities and the People's Procuratorate shall, in accordance with legal procedures, comprehensively collect, review, and transfer evidence proving the criminal facts and sentencing circumstances of the criminal suspect or defendant.  
For cases where the law provides for a concurrent or separate pecuniary punishment, the investigation authority shall, based on the circumstances of the case, investigate the property status of the defendant and transfer the relevant evidence materials to the People's Procuratorate. The People's Procuratorate shall review and transfer the relevant evidence materials to the People's Court.  
If, during prosecution review, the People's Procuratorate discovers that the investigation authority should have collected but failed to collect sentencing evidence, it may return the case to the investigation authority for supplementary investigation, or it may conduct its own investigation. Where the People's Procuratorate returns the case for supplementary investigation, the investigation authority shall promptly collect the relevant evidence in accordance with the requirements of the supplementary investigation outline issued by the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对于可能判处管制、缓刑的案件，侦查机关、人民检察院、人民法院可以委托社区矫正机构或者有关社会组织进行调查评估，提出意见，供判处管制、缓刑时参考。  
社区矫正机构或者有关社会组织收到侦查机关、人民检察院或者人民法院调查评估的委托后，应当根据委托机关的要求依法进行调查，形成评估意见，并及时提交委托机关。  
对于没有委托进行调查评估或者判决前没有收到调查评估报告的，人民法院经审理认为被告人符合管制、缓刑适用条件的，可以依法判处管制、宣告缓刑。

**Article 3** For cases where public surveillance or probation may be imposed, the investigation authority, the People's Procuratorate, and the People's Court may entrust community correction institutions or relevant social organizations to conduct investigation and assessment and provide opinions for reference when imposing public surveillance or probation.  
Upon receiving the entrustment for investigation and assessment from the investigation authority, the People's Procuratorate, or the People's Court, the community correction institution or relevant social organization shall conduct the investigation according to law in accordance with the requirements of the entrusting authority, form an assessment opinion, and promptly submit it to the entrusting authority.  
Where no investigation and assessment are entrusted or no investigation and assessment report is received before judgment, if the People's Court, upon trial, considers that the defendant meets the conditions for the application of public surveillance or probation, it may, according to law, impose public surveillance or pronounce probation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　侦查机关在移送审查起诉时，可以根据犯罪嫌疑人涉嫌犯罪的情况，就宣告禁止令和从业禁止向人民检察院提出意见。  
人民检察院在提起公诉时，可以提出宣告禁止令和从业禁止的建议。被告人及其辩护人、被害人及其诉讼代理人可以就是否对被告人宣告禁止令和从业禁止提出意见，并说明理由。  
人民法院宣告禁止令和从业禁止，应当根据被告人的犯罪原因、犯罪性质、犯罪手段、悔罪表现、个人一贯表现等，充分考虑与被告人所犯罪行的关联程度，有针对性地决定禁止从事特定的职业、活动，进入特定区域、场所，接触特定的人等。

**Article 4** When transferring a case for prosecution review, the investigation authority may, based on the circumstances of the suspected crime of the criminal suspect, provide opinions to the People's Procuratorate on the issuance of a prohibition order and occupational prohibition.  
When initiating a public prosecution, the People's Procuratorate may propose the issuance of a prohibition order and occupational prohibition. The defendant and his defender, and the victim and his litigation agent may provide opinions on whether to issue a prohibition order and occupational prohibition against the defendant, and shall state the reasons.  
When issuing a prohibition order and occupational prohibition, the People's Court shall, based on the defendant's criminal motive, nature of the crime, means of crime, expression of remorse, consistent personal conduct, etc., fully consider the degree of relevance to the crime committed by the defendant, and make a targeted decision to prohibit engaging in specific occupations or activities, entering specific areas or venues, contacting specific persons, etc.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　符合下列条件的案件，人民检察院提起公诉时可以提出量刑建议；被告人认罪认罚的，人民检察院应当提出量刑建议：  
（一）犯罪事实清楚，证据确实、充分；  
（二）提出量刑建议所依据的法定从重、从轻、减轻或者免除处罚等量刑情节已查清；  
（三）提出量刑建议所依据的酌定从重、从轻处罚等量刑情节已查清。

**Article 5** For cases meeting the following conditions, the People's Procuratorate may propose sentencing recommendations when initiating a public prosecution; where the defendant admits guilt and accepts punishment, the People's Procuratorate shall propose sentencing recommendations:  
(1) The criminal facts are clear, and the evidence is reliable and sufficient;  
(2) The statutory sentencing circumstances for a heavier, lighter, mitigated, or exempted punishment, which form the basis for the sentencing recommendation, have been ascertained;  
(3) The discretionary sentencing circumstances for a heavier or lighter punishment, which form the basis for the sentencing recommendation, have been ascertained.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　量刑建议包括主刑、附加刑、是否适用缓刑等。主刑可以具有一定的幅度，也可以根据案件具体情况，提出确定刑期的量刑建议。建议判处财产刑的，可以提出确定的数额。

**Article 6** Sentencing recommendations include the principal punishment, supplementary punishment, whether probation shall be applied, etc. The principal punishment may have a certain range, or a sentencing recommendation with a definite term of punishment may be proposed based on the specific circumstances of the case. Where a pecuniary punishment is recommended, a definite amount may be proposed.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对常见犯罪案件，人民检察院应当按照量刑指导意见提出量刑建议。对新类型、不常见犯罪案件，可以参照相关量刑规范提出量刑建议。提出量刑建议，应当说明理由和依据。

**Article 7** For common criminal cases, the People's Procuratorate shall propose sentencing recommendations in accordance with the sentencing guidelines. For new types or uncommon criminal cases, sentencing recommendations may be proposed by reference to relevant sentencing norms. When proposing sentencing recommendations, the reasons and basis shall be stated.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民检察院指控被告人犯有数罪的，应当对指控的个罪分别提出量刑建议，并依法提出数罪并罚后决定执行的刑罚的量刑建议。  
对于共同犯罪案件，人民检察院应当根据各被告人在共同犯罪中的地位、作用以及应当承担的刑事责任分别提出量刑建议。

**Article 8** Where the People's Procuratorate charges the defendant with multiple crimes, it shall propose sentencing recommendations for each crime charged separately, and shall, according to law, propose a sentencing recommendation for the punishment to be executed after combined punishment for multiple crimes.  
For joint crime cases, the People's Procuratorate shall propose sentencing recommendations separately based on each defendant's status, role, and criminal liability to be borne in the joint crime.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民检察院提出量刑建议，可以制作量刑建议书，与起诉书一并移送人民法院；对于案情简单、量刑情节简单的适用速裁程序的案件，也可以在起诉书中写明量刑建议。  
量刑建议书中应当写明人民检察院建议对被告人处以的主刑、附加刑、是否适用缓刑等及其理由和依据。  
人民检察院以量刑建议书方式提出量刑建议的，人民法院在送达起诉书副本时，应当将量刑建议书一并送达被告人。

**Article 9** When proposing sentencing recommendations, the People's Procuratorate may prepare a sentencing recommendation letter and transfer it together with the indictment to the People's Court; for cases with simple facts and simple sentencing circumstances applying the expedited procedures, the sentencing recommendations may also be stated in the indictment.  
The sentencing recommendation letter shall state the principal punishment, supplementary punishment, whether probation shall be applied, etc., as recommended by the People's Procuratorate to be imposed on the defendant, as well as the reasons and basis.  
Where the People's Procuratorate proposes sentencing recommendations in the form of a sentencing recommendation letter, the People's Court shall, when serving the copy of the indictment, serve the sentencing recommendation letter together to the defendant.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　在刑事诉讼中，自诉人、被告人及其辩护人、被害人及其诉讼代理人可以提出量刑意见，并说明理由，人民检察院、人民法院应当记录在案并附卷。

**Article 10** In a criminal action, the private prosecutor, the defendant and his defender, and the victim and his litigation agent may propose sentencing opinions and state the reasons; the People's Procuratorate and the People's Court shall record them in the case file and attach them to the case.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院、人民检察院、侦查机关应当告知犯罪嫌疑人、被告人申请法律援助的权利，对符合法律援助条件的，依法通知法律援助机构指派律师为其提供辩护或者法律帮助。

**Article 11** The People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, and the investigation authority shall inform the criminal suspect or defendant of the right to apply for legal aid; for those who meet the conditions for legal aid, they shall, according to law, notify the legal aid institution to assign a lawyer to provide defense or legal assistance.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　适用速裁程序审理的案件，在确认被告人认罪认罚的自愿性和认罪认罚具结书内容的真实性、合法性后，一般不再进行法庭调查、法庭辩论，但在判决宣告前应当听取辩护人的意见和被告人的最后陈述意见。  
适用速裁程序审理的案件，应当当庭宣判。

**Article 12** For cases tried under the expedited procedures, after confirming the voluntariness of the defendant's admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment and the authenticity and legality of the content of the admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment affidavit, court investigation and court debate shall generally not be conducted, but before the judgment is pronounced, the opinions of the defender and the defendant's final statement shall be heard.  
Cases tried under the expedited procedures shall be pronounced in court.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　适用简易程序审理的案件，在确认被告人对起诉书指控的犯罪事实和罪名没有异议，自愿认罪且知悉认罪的法律后果后，法庭审理可以直接围绕量刑进行，不再区分法庭调查、法庭辩论，但在判决宣告前应当听取被告人的最后陈述意见。  
适用简易程序审理的案件，一般应当当庭宣判。

**Article 13** For cases tried under the summary procedures, after confirming that the defendant has no objection to the criminal facts and charges alleged in the indictment, voluntarily admits guilt and is aware of the legal consequences of admission, the court trial may proceed directly around sentencing, without distinguishing between court investigation and court debate, but before the judgment is pronounced, the defendant's final statement shall be heard.  
Cases tried under the summary procedures shall generally be pronounced in court.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　适用普通程序审理的被告人认罪案件，在确认被告人了解起诉书指控的犯罪事实和罪名，自愿认罪且知悉认罪的法律后果后，法庭审理主要围绕量刑和其他有争议的问题进行，可以适当简化法庭调查、法庭辩论程序。

**Article 14** For cases where the defendant admits guilt and the ordinary procedures are applied, after confirming that the defendant understands the criminal facts and charges alleged in the indictment, voluntarily admits guilt, and is aware of the legal consequences of admission, the court trial shall mainly focus on sentencing and other disputed issues, and the procedures for court investigation and court debate may be appropriately simplified.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对于被告人不认罪或者辩护人做无罪辩护的案件，法庭调查和法庭辩论分别进行。  
在法庭调查阶段，应当在查明定罪事实的基础上，查明有关量刑事实，被告人及其辩护人可以出示证明被告人无罪或者罪轻的证据，当庭发表质证意见。  
在法庭辩论阶段，审判人员引导控辩双方先辩论定罪问题。在定罪辩论结束后，审判人员告知控辩双方可以围绕量刑问题进行辩论，发表量刑建议或者意见，并说明依据和理由。被告人及其辩护人参加量刑问题的调查的，不影响作无罪辩解或者辩护。

**Article 15** For cases where the defendant does not admit guilt or the defender makes a defense of innocence, court investigation and court debate shall be conducted separately.  
During the court investigation stage, on the basis of ascertaining the facts concerning conviction, the relevant facts concerning sentencing shall be ascertained; the defendant and his defender may present evidence proving the defendant's innocence or lighter guilt, and present opinions on cross-examination in court.  
During the court debate stage, the judicial personnel shall guide the prosecution and defense to debate the conviction issue first. After the conviction debate concludes, the judicial personnel shall inform the prosecution and defense that they may debate around the sentencing issue, present sentencing recommendations or opinions, and state the basis and reasons. The participation of the defendant and his defender in the investigation of sentencing issues shall not affect the making of a defense of innocence or mitigation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　在法庭调查中，公诉人可以根据案件的不同种类、特点和庭审的实际情况，合理安排和调整举证顺序。定罪证据和量刑证据分开出示的，应当先出示定罪证据，后出示量刑证据。  
对于有数起犯罪事实的案件的量刑证据，可以在对每起犯罪事实举证时分别出示，也可以对同类犯罪事实一并出示；涉及全案综合量刑情节的证据，一般应当在举证阶段最后出示。

**Article 16** During court investigation, the public prosecutor may, based on the different types and characteristics of the case and the actual circumstances of the court trial, reasonably arrange and adjust the order of presenting evidence. Where evidence for conviction and evidence for sentencing are presented separately, the evidence for conviction shall be presented first, followed by the evidence for sentencing.  
For cases involving multiple criminal facts, the sentencing evidence may be presented separately when presenting evidence for each criminal fact, or may be presented together for similar criminal facts; evidence involving comprehensive sentencing circumstances of the entire case shall generally be presented last during the evidence presentation stage.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　在法庭调查中，人民法院应当查明对被告人适用具体法定刑幅度的犯罪事实以及法定或者酌定量刑情节。

**Article 17** During court investigation, the People's Court shall ascertain the criminal facts that determine the application of the specific statutory punishment range to the defendant, as well as the statutory or discretionary sentencing circumstances.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　人民法院、人民检察院、侦查机关或者辩护人委托有关方面制作涉及未成年人的社会调查报告的，调查报告应当在法庭上宣读，并进行质证。

**Article 18** Where the People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, the investigation authority, or the defender entrusts a relevant party to prepare a social investigation report involving a minor, the report shall be read out in court and subject to cross-examination.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在法庭审理中，审判人员对量刑证据有疑问的，可以宣布休庭，对证据进行调查核实，必要时也可以要求人民检察院补充调查核实。人民检察院补充调查核实有关证据，必要时可以要求侦查机关提供协助。  
对于控辩双方补充的证据，应当经过庭审质证才能作为定案的根据。但是，对于有利于被告人的量刑证据，经庭外征求意见，控辩双方没有异议的除外。

**Article 19** During court trial, if the judicial personnel have doubts about the sentencing evidence, they may announce an adjournment to investigate and verify the evidence, and when necessary, may also request the People's Procuratorate to supplement investigation and verification. When the People's Procuratorate supplements investigation and verification of relevant evidence, it may, when necessary, request the investigation authority to provide assistance.  
Evidence supplemented by the prosecution and defense shall be subject to court cross-examination before it can be used as the basis for deciding the case. However, this does not apply to sentencing evidence favorable to the defendant if, after obtaining opinions outside court, the prosecution and defense have no objection.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　被告人及其辩护人、被害人及其诉讼代理人申请人民法院调取在侦查、审查起诉阶段收集的量刑证据材料，人民法院认为确有必要的，应当依法调取；人民法院认为不需要调取的，应当说明理由。

**Article 20** Where the defendant and his defender, or the victim and his litigation agent, apply to the People's Court to retrieve sentencing evidence materials collected during the investigation and prosecution review stages, if the People's Court considers it truly necessary, it shall retrieve them according to law; if the People's Court considers retrieval unnecessary, it shall state the reasons.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在法庭辩论中，量刑辩论按照以下顺序进行：  
（一）公诉人发表量刑建议，或者自诉人及其诉讼代理人发表量刑意见；  
（二）被害人及其诉讼代理人发表量刑意见；  
（三）被告人及其辩护人发表量刑意见。

**Article 21** During court debate, the sentencing debate shall proceed in the following order:  
(1) The public prosecutor presents sentencing recommendations, or the private prosecutor and his litigation agent present sentencing opinions;  
(2) The victim and his litigation agent present sentencing opinions;  
(3) The defendant and his defender present sentencing opinions.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　在法庭辩论中，出现新的量刑事实，需要进一步调查的，应当恢复法庭调查，待事实查清后继续法庭辩论。

**Article 22** If, during court debate, new sentencing facts emerge that require further investigation, the court investigation shall be resumed, and after the facts are ascertained, the court debate shall continue.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　对于人民检察院提出的量刑建议，人民法院应当依法审查。对于事实清楚，证据确实、充分，指控的罪名准确，量刑建议适当的，人民法院应当采纳。  
人民法院经审理认为，人民检察院的量刑建议不当的，可以告知人民检察院。人民检察院调整量刑建议的，应当在法庭审理结束前提出。人民法院认为人民检察院调整后的量刑建议适当的，应当予以采纳；人民检察院不调整量刑建议或者调整量刑建议后仍不当的，人民法院应当依法作出判决。

**Article 23** The People's Court shall, according to law, review the sentencing recommendations proposed by the People's Procuratorate. Where the facts are clear, the evidence is reliable and sufficient, the charges are accurate, and the sentencing recommendations are appropriate, the People's Court shall adopt them.  
If, upon trial, the People's Court considers that the sentencing recommendations of the People's Procuratorate are improper, it may inform the People's Procuratorate. If the People's Procuratorate adjusts the sentencing recommendations, it shall propose them before the conclusion of the court trial. If the People's Court considers the adjusted sentencing recommendations appropriate, it shall adopt them; if the People's Procuratorate does not adjust the sentencing recommendations or the adjusted recommendations remain improper, the People's Court shall render a judgment according to law.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　有下列情形之一，被告人当庭认罪，愿意接受处罚的，人民法院应当根据审理查明的事实，就定罪和量刑听取控辩双方意见，依法作出裁判：  
（一）被告人在侦查、审查起诉阶段认罪认罚，但人民检察院没有提出量刑建议的；  
（二）被告人在侦查、审查起诉阶段没有认罪认罚的；  
（三）被告人在第一审程序中没有认罪认罚，在第二审程序中认罪认罚的；  
（四）被告人在庭审过程中不同意量刑建议的。

**Article 24** Under any of the following circumstances, where the defendant admits guilt in court and is willing to accept punishment, the People's Court shall, based on the facts ascertained upon trial, hear the opinions of both the prosecution and defense on conviction and sentencing, and render a judgment according to law:  
(1) The defendant admitted guilt and accepted punishment during the investigation and prosecution review stages, but the People's Procuratorate did not propose sentencing recommendations;  
(2) The defendant did not admit guilt and accept punishment during the investigation and prosecution review stages;  
(3) The defendant did not admit guilt and accept punishment during the first instance proceedings, but admits guilt and accepts punishment in the second instance proceedings;  
(4) The defendant does not agree with the sentencing recommendations during the court trial.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　人民法院应当在刑事裁判文书中说明量刑理由。量刑说理主要包括：  
（一）已经查明的量刑事实及其对量刑的影响；  
（二）是否采纳公诉人、自诉人、被告人及其辩护人、被害人及其诉讼代理人发表的量刑建议、意见及理由；  
（三）人民法院判处刑罚的理由和法律依据。  
对于适用速裁程序审理的案件，可以简化量刑说理。

**Article 25** The People's Court shall state the reasons for sentencing in the criminal judgment document. The reasoning for sentencing mainly includes:  
(1) The sentencing facts that have been ascertained and their impact on sentencing;  
(2) Whether the sentencing recommendations, opinions, and reasons presented by the public prosecutor, private prosecutor, defendant and his defender, and victim and his litigation agent are adopted;  
(3) The reasons and legal basis for the punishment imposed by the People's Court.  
For cases tried under the expedited procedures, the reasoning for sentencing may be simplified.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　开庭审理的二审、再审案件的量刑程序，依照有关法律规定进行。法律没有规定的，参照本意见进行。  
对于不开庭审理的二审、再审案件，审判人员在阅卷、讯问被告人、听取自诉人、辩护人、被害人及其诉讼代理人的意见时，应当注意审查量刑事实和证据。

**Article 26** The sentencing procedures for cases of second instance and retrial tried in open court shall be conducted in accordance with relevant legal provisions. Where the law has no provisions, reference may be made to these Opinions.  
For cases of second instance and retrial tried without holding court sessions, when the judicial personnel review the case files, interrogate the defendant, and hear the opinions of the private prosecutor, defender, victim, and his litigation agent, they shall pay attention to reviewing the sentencing facts and evidence.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　对于认罪认罚案件量刑建议的提出、采纳与调整等，适用最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部《关于适用认罪认罚从宽制度的指导意见》的有关规定。

**Article 27** For the proposal, adoption, and adjustment of sentencing recommendations in cases where the defendant admits guilt and accepts punishment, the relevant provisions of the "Guiding Opinions on Applying the System of Leniency for Admission of Guilt and Acceptance of Punishment" of the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of State Security, and the Ministry of Justice shall apply.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本意见自2020年11月6日起施行。2010年9月13日最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部《印发〈关于规范量刑程序若干问题的意见（试行）〉的通知》（法发〔2010〕35号）同时废止。

**Article 28** These Opinions shall come into force as of November 6, 2020. The "Notice of the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of State Security, and the Ministry of Justice on Printing and Distributing the 'Opinions on Several Issues concerning Standardizing Sentencing Procedures (Trial Implementation)'" (Fa Fa [2010] No. 35) issued on September 13, 2010 shall be abolished simultaneously.
