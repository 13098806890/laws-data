### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>四、人民检察院对于海警机构移送起诉的海上刑事案件，按照刑事诉讼法、司法解释以及本通知的有关规定进行审查后，认为应当由其他人民检察院起诉的，应当将案件移送有管辖权的人民检察院。  
需要按照刑事诉讼法、司法解释以及本通知的有关规定指定审判管辖的，海警机构应当在移送起诉前向人民检察院通报，由人民检察院协商同级人民法院办理指定管辖有关事宜。

**Article 1** 4. Where, after reviewing a maritime criminal case transferred by a coast guard authority for prosecution in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law, judicial interpretations, and the relevant provisions of this Notice, a People's Procuratorate determines that the case shall be prosecuted by another People's Procuratorate, it shall transfer the case to the People's Procuratorate with jurisdiction.  
Where the adjudication jurisdiction needs to be designated in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Law, judicial interpretations, and the relevant provisions of this Notice, the coast guard authority shall notify the People's Procuratorate before transferring the case for prosecution, and the People's Procuratorate shall consult with the People's Court at the same level to handle the matters concerning the designation of jurisdiction.

<a id="art-2"></a>五、对人民检察院提起公诉的海上刑事案件，人民法院经审查认为符合刑事诉讼法、司法解释以及本通知有关规定的，应当依法受理。

**Article 2** 5. Where, after reviewing a maritime criminal case initiated by a People's Procuratorate for public prosecution, the People's Court determines that it complies with the Criminal Procedure Law, judicial interpretations, and the relevant provisions of this Notice, it shall accept the case according to law.

<a id="art-3"></a>六、海警机构办理刑事案件应当主动接受检察机关监督，与检察机关建立信息共享平台，定期向检察机关通报行政执法与刑事司法衔接，刑事立案、破案，采取强制措施等情况。  
海警机构所在地的人民检察院依法对海警机构的刑事立案、侦查活动实行监督。  
海警机构办理重大、疑难、复杂的刑事案件，可以商请人民检察院介入侦查活动，并听取人民检察院的意见和建议。人民检察院认为确有必要时，可以派员介入海警机构的侦查活动，对收集证据、适用法律提出意见，监督侦查活动是否合法，海警机构应当予以配合。  
本通知自印发之日起施行。各地接本通知后，请认真贯彻执行。执行中遇到的问题，请及时分别报告最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、中国海警局。

**Article 3** 6. When handling criminal cases, a coast guard authority shall proactively accept supervision by the procuratorial authority, establish an information-sharing platform with the procuratorial authority, and periodically report to the procuratorial authority on the interface between administrative law enforcement and criminal justice, as well as the status of criminal docketing, case solving, and adoption of compulsory measures.  
The People's Procuratorate at the locality of the coast guard authority shall, according to law, exercise supervision over the coast guard authority's criminal docketing and investigation activities.  
When handling major, difficult, or complex criminal cases, the coast guard authority may request the People's Procuratorate to participate in the investigation activities and solicit opinions and suggestions from the People's Procuratorate. Where the People's Procuratorate deems it truly necessary, it may send personnel to participate in the coast guard authority's investigation activities, provide opinions on evidence collection and application of law, and supervise whether the investigation activities are lawful, and the coast guard authority shall cooperate.  
This Notice shall come into force as of the date of issuance. Upon receipt of this Notice, all localities shall earnestly implement it. For issues encountered during implementation, reports shall be timely submitted to the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the China Coast Guard respectively.
