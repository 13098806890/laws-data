# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理受贿刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法发〔2007〕22号  
**公布日期**：2007-07-08

> 根据《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理赌博刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》第七条规定，国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，通过赌博方式收受请托人财物的，构成受贿。 实践中应注意区分贿赂与赌博活动、娱乐活动的界限。具体认定时，主要应当结合以下因素进行判断：（1）赌博的背景、场合、时间、次数；（2）赌资来源；（3）其他赌博参与者有无事先通谋；（4）输赢钱物的具体情况和金额大小。 六、关于特定关系人“挂名”领取薪酬问题 国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，要求或者接受请托人以给特定关系人安排工作为名，使特定关系人不实际工作却获取所谓薪酬的，以受贿论处。 七、关于由特定关系人收受贿赂问题 国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，授意请托人以本意见所列形式，将有关财物给予特定关系人的，以受贿论处。 特定关系人与国家工作人员通谋，共同实施前款行为的，对特定关系人以受贿罪的共犯论处。特定关系人以外的其他人与国家工作人员通谋，由国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，收受请托人财物后双方共同占有的，以受贿罪的共犯论处。 八、关于收受贿赂物品未办理权属变更问题 国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，收受请托人房屋、汽车等物品，未变更权属登记或者借用他人名义办理权属变更登记的，不影响受贿的认定。 认定以房屋、汽车等物品为对象的受贿，应注意与借用的区分。具体认定时，除双方交代或者书面协议之外，主要应当结合以下因素进行判断：（1）有无借用的合理事由；（2）是否实际使用；（3）借用时间的长短；（4）有无归还的条件；（5）有无归还的意思表示及行为。 九、关于收受财物后退还或者上交问题 国家工作人员收受请托人财物后及时退还或者上交的，不是受贿。 国家工作人员受贿后，因自身或者与其受贿有关联的人、事被查处，为掩饰犯罪而退还或者上交的，不影响认定受贿罪。 十、关于在职时为请托人谋利，离职后收受财物问题 国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益之前或者之后，约定在其离职后收受请托人财物，并在离职后收受的，以受贿论处。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>六、关于特定关系人“挂名”领取薪酬问题  
国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，要求或者接受请托人以给特定关系人安排工作为名，使特定关系人不实际工作却获取所谓薪酬的，以受贿论处。

**Article 1** VI. Issue of a Specific Related Person Receiving Salary in Name Only  
Where a functionary of the State takes advantage of his position to seek benefits for an entrusting person, and requests or accepts that the entrusting person, under the pretext of arranging a job for a specific related person, causes the specific related person to obtain so-called salary without actually working, it shall be dealt with as acceptance of bribes.

<a id="art-2"></a>七、关于由特定关系人收受贿赂问题  
国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，授意请托人以本意见所列形式，将有关财物给予特定关系人的，以受贿论处。  
特定关系人与国家工作人员通谋，共同实施前款行为的，对特定关系人以受贿罪的共犯论处。特定关系人以外的其他人与国家工作人员通谋，由国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，收受请托人财物后双方共同占有的，以受贿罪的共犯论处。

**Article 2** VII. Issue of Acceptance of Bribes by a Specific Related Person  
Where a functionary of the State takes advantage of his position to seek benefits for an entrusting person, and instructs the entrusting person to give relevant property to a specific related person in the forms listed in this Opinion, it shall be dealt with as acceptance of bribes.  
Where a specific related person colludes with the functionary of the State to jointly commit the act set forth in the preceding paragraph, the specific related person shall be dealt with as an accomplice to the crime of accepting bribes. Where a person other than a specific related person colludes with a functionary of the State, and the functionary of the State takes advantage of his position to seek benefits for the entrusting person, and after accepting property from the entrusting person, both parties jointly possess the property, the said person shall be dealt with as an accomplice to the crime of accepting bribes.

<a id="art-3"></a>八、关于收受贿赂物品未办理权属变更问题  
国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，收受请托人房屋、汽车等物品，未变更权属登记或者借用他人名义办理权属变更登记的，不影响受贿的认定。  
认定以房屋、汽车等物品为对象的受贿，应注意与借用的区分。具体认定时，除双方交代或者书面协议之外，主要应当结合以下因素进行判断：（1）有无借用的合理事由；（2）是否实际使用；（3）借用时间的长短；（4）有无归还的条件；（5）有无归还的意思表示及行为。

**Article 3** VIII. Issue of Acceptance of Bribery Items Without Registration of Ownership Change  
Where a functionary of the State takes advantage of his position to seek benefits for an entrusting person, and accepts items such as a house or a car from the entrusting person without undergoing registration of ownership change or by undergoing registration of ownership change under another person's name, the determination of bribery shall not be affected.  
When determining bribery the object of which is items such as a house or a car, attention shall be paid to distinguishing it from borrowing. In making a specific determination, besides statements of both parties or written agreements, judgment shall mainly be made by taking into consideration the following factors: (1) whether there is a reasonable reason for borrowing; (2) whether actual use is made; (3) the length of the borrowing period; (4) whether there are conditions for return; (5) whether there is an expression of intention to return and the act of return.

<a id="art-4"></a>九、关于收受财物后退还或者上交问题  
国家工作人员收受请托人财物后及时退还或者上交的，不是受贿。  
国家工作人员受贿后，因自身或者与其受贿有关联的人、事被查处，为掩饰犯罪而退还或者上交的，不影响认定受贿罪。

**Article 4** IX. Issue of Returning or Surrendering Property After Acceptance  
Where a functionary of the State returns or surrenders the property accepted from an entrusting person in a timely manner, it shall not constitute acceptance of bribes.  
Where a functionary of the State, after accepting bribes, returns or surrenders the property in order to conceal the crime because himself or persons or matters related to his acceptance of bribes are being investigated, the determination of the crime of accepting bribes shall not be affected.

<a id="art-5"></a>十、关于在职时为请托人谋利，离职后收受财物问题  
国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益之前或者之后，约定在其离职后收受请托人财物，并在离职后收受的，以受贿论处。  
国家工作人员利用职务上的便利为请托人谋取利益，离职前后连续收受请托人财物的，离职前后收受部分均应计入受贿数额。

**Article 5** X. Issue of Seeking Benefits for an Entrusting Person While in Office and Accepting Property After Resignation  
Where a functionary of the State, before or after taking advantage of his position to seek benefits for an entrusting person, agrees to accept property from the entrusting person after his resignation and actually accepts it after resignation, it shall be dealt with as acceptance of bribes.  
Where a functionary of the State takes advantage of his position to seek benefits for an entrusting person, and continuously accepts property from the entrusting person before and after resignation, both the part accepted before resignation and the part accepted after resignation shall be included in the amount of bribes.

<a id="art-6"></a>十一、关于“特定关系人”的范围  
本意见所称“特定关系人”，是指与国家工作人员有近亲属、情妇（夫）以及其他共同利益关系的人。

**Article 6** XI. Scope of a "Specific Related Person"  
The "specific related person" referred to in this Opinion means a person who has a relationship with the functionary of the State as a close relative, a mistress (lover), or any other person with a common interest.

<a id="art-7"></a>十二、关于正确贯彻宽严相济刑事政策的问题  
依照本意见办理受贿刑事案件，要根据刑法关于受贿罪的有关规定和受贿罪权钱交易的本质特征，准确区分罪与非罪、此罪与彼罪的界限，惩处少数，教育多数。在从严惩处受贿犯罪的同时，对于具有自首、立功等情节的，依法从轻、减轻或者免除处罚。

**Article 7** XII. Issue of Correctly Implementing the Criminal Policy of Combining Leniency with Severity  
When handling criminal cases of bribery in accordance with this Opinion, it shall be necessary, based on the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law on the crime of accepting bribes and the essential feature of power-for-money deals in the crime of accepting bribes, to accurately distinguish the boundaries between crime and non-crime and between this crime and other crimes, punish the minority, and educate the majority. While imposing severe punishment on bribery crimes, for those who have circumstances such as voluntary surrender or meritorious service, a lighter or mitigated punishment shall be imposed, or the punishment shall be exempted according to law.
