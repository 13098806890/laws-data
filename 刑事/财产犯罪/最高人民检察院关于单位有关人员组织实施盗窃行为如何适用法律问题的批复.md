2002年8月9日  
最高人民检察院关于单位有关人员  
组织实施盗窃行为如何适用法律问题的批复  
（2002年7月8日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会  
第一百一十二次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔2002〕5号  
各省、自治区、直辖市人民检察院，军事检察院，新疆生产建设兵团人民检察院：  
近来，一些省人民检察院就单位有关人员为谋取单位利益组织实施盗窃行为如何适用法律问题向我院请示。根据刑法有关规定，现批复如下：  
单位有关人员为谋取单位利益组织实施盗窃行为，情节严重的，应当依照[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)的规定以盗窃罪追究直接责任人员的刑事责任。  
此复。  
最高人民检察院  
2002年8月9日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
August 9, 2002  
Reply of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on How to Apply the Law to the Organizing and Carrying out of Theft by Relevant Personnel of a Unit  
(Adopted by the 112th Session of the Ninth Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on July 8, 2002)  
Gao Jian Fa Shi Zi [2002] No. 5  
People's Procuratorates of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; Military Procuratorates; and the People's Procuratorate of Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps:  
Recently, some provincial people's procuratorates have submitted requests to our Procuratorate concerning how to apply the law to the organizing and carrying out of theft by relevant personnel of a unit for the benefit of the unit. In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law, the reply is as follows:  
Where relevant personnel of a unit organize and carry out theft for the benefit of the unit, and the circumstances are serious, criminal liability of the directly responsible persons shall be pursued in accordance with the provisions of Article 264 of the Criminal Law for the crime of theft.  
This is the reply.  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
August 9, 2002
