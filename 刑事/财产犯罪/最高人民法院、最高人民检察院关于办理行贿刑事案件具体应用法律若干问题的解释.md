# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理行贿刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕22号  
**公布日期**：2012-12-26  
**生效日期**：2013-01-01

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　为谋取不正当利益，向国家工作人员行贿，数额在一万元以上的，应当依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 1** Where, for the purpose of seeking illegitimate benefits, a person bribes a State functionary, and the amount is 10,000 yuan or more, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 390 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　因行贿谋取不正当利益，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）行贿数额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的；  
（二）行贿数额在十万元以上不满二十万元，并具有下列情形之一的：  
1.向三人以上行贿的；  
2.将违法所得用于行贿的；  
3.为实施违法犯罪活动，向负有食品、药品、安全生产、环境保护等监督管理职责的国家工作人员行贿，严重危害民生、侵犯公众生命财产安全的；  
4.向行政执法机关、司法机关的国家工作人员行贿，影响行政执法和司法公正的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** Where, through bribery, a person seeks illegitimate benefits, and any of the following circumstances is present, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of the bribe is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 1,000,000 yuan;  
(2) the amount of the bribe is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and any of the following circumstances is present:  
(a) bribing three or more persons;  
(b) using illegal gains for bribery;  
(c) for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities, bribing a State functionary who is responsible for supervision and administration over such areas as food, drugs, production safety, and environmental protection, thereby seriously endangering people's livelihood and infringing upon the safety of life and property of the public; or  
(d) bribing a State functionary of an administrative law enforcement organ or a judicial organ, thereby affecting administrative law enforcement and judicial fairness; and  
(3) other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　因行贿谋取不正当利益，造成直接经济损失数额在一百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“使国家利益遭受重大损失”。

**Article 3** Where, through bribery, a person seeks illegitimate benefits and causes direct economic losses of 1,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "causing heavy losses to the interests of the State" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　因行贿谋取不正当利益，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）行贿数额在一百万元以上的；  
（二）行贿数额在五十万元以上不满一百万元，并具有下列情形之一的：  
1.向三人以上行贿的；  
2.将违法所得用于行贿的；  
3.为实施违法犯罪活动，向负有食品、药品、安全生产、环境保护等监督管理职责的国家工作人员行贿，严重危害民生、侵犯公众生命财产安全的；  
4.向行政执法机关、司法机关的国家工作人员行贿，影响行政执法和司法公正的；  
（三）造成直接经济损失数额在五百万元以上的；  
（四）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 4** Where, through bribery, a person seeks illegitimate benefits, and any of the following circumstances is present, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of the bribe is 1,000,000 yuan or more;  
(2) the amount of the bribe is 500,000 yuan or more but less than 1,000,000 yuan, and any of the following circumstances is present:  
(a) bribing three or more persons;  
(b) using illegal gains for bribery;  
(c) for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities, bribing a State functionary who is responsible for supervision and administration over such areas as food, drugs, production safety, and environmental protection, thereby seriously endangering people's livelihood and infringing upon the safety of life and property of the public; or  
(d) bribing a State functionary of an administrative law enforcement organ or a judicial organ, thereby affecting administrative law enforcement and judicial fairness;  
(3) causing direct economic losses of 5,000,000 yuan or more; and  
(4) other circumstances where the circumstances are especially serious.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　多次行贿未经处理的，按照累计行贿数额处罚。

**Article 5** Where a person commits bribery multiple times without being dealt with, the punishment shall be imposed according to the cumulative amount of bribes.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　行贿人谋取不正当利益的行为构成犯罪的，应当与行贿犯罪实行数罪并罚。

**Article 6** Where the act of the briber in seeking illegitimate benefits constitutes a crime, cumulative punishment for several crimes shall be imposed together with the crime of bribery.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　因行贿人在被追诉前主动交待行贿行为而破获相关受贿案件的，对行贿人不适用[刑法第六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-68)关于立功的规定，依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款的规定，可以减轻或者免除处罚。  
单位行贿的，在被追诉前，单位集体决定或者单位负责人决定主动交待单位行贿行为的，依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款的规定，对单位及相关责任人员可以减轻处罚或者免除处罚；受委托直接办理单位行贿事项的直接责任人员在被追诉前主动交待自己知道的单位行贿行为的，对该直接责任人员可以依照[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款的规定减轻处罚或者免除处罚。

**Article 7** Where, because the briber voluntarily confesses the bribery act before being prosecuted, a relevant bribery acceptance case is solved, the provisions of Article 68 of the Criminal Law concerning meritorious service shall not apply to the briber, and in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law, the punishment may be mitigated or the briber may be exempted from punishment.  
Where a unit commits bribery, if before being prosecuted, the unit, by a collective decision or a decision of the person in charge of the unit, voluntarily confesses the unit's bribery act, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law, the punishment for the unit and the relevant responsible persons may be mitigated or they may be exempted from punishment; if the directly responsible person who is entrusted to directly handle the unit's bribery matters voluntarily confesses, before being prosecuted, the unit's bribery act of which he or she is aware, then in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law, the punishment for that directly responsible person may be mitigated or he or she may be exempted from punishment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　行贿人被追诉后如实供述自己罪行的，依照[刑法第六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-67)第三款的规定，可以从轻处罚；因其如实供述自己罪行，避免特别严重后果发生的，可以减轻处罚。

**Article 8** Where, after being prosecuted, the briber truthfully confesses his or her own crime, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 67 of the Criminal Law, a light punishment may be given; where, because of the truthful confession of his or her own crime, especially serious consequences are avoided, the punishment may be mitigated.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　行贿人揭发受贿人与其行贿无关的其他犯罪行为，查证属实的，依照[刑法第六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-68)关于立功的规定，可以从轻、减轻或者免除处罚。

**Article 9** Where the briber discloses other criminal acts of the bribery acceptor that are unrelated to the bribery, and the disclosure is verified to be true, in accordance with the provisions of Article 68 of the Criminal Law concerning meritorious service, a light punishment may be given, the punishment may be mitigated, or the briber may be exempted from punishment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　实施行贿犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，一般不适用缓刑和免予刑事处罚：  
（一）向三人以上行贿的；  
（二）因行贿受过行政处罚或者刑事处罚的；  
（三）为实施违法犯罪活动而行贿的；  
（四）造成严重危害后果的；  
（五）其他不适用缓刑和免予刑事处罚的情形。  
具有[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款规定的情形的，不受前款规定的限制。

**Article 10** Where a person commits the crime of bribery and any of the following circumstances is present, probation and exemption from criminal punishment shall generally not be applied:  
(1) bribing three or more persons;  
(2) having previously received an administrative penalty or criminal punishment for bribery;  
(3) bribing for the purpose of committing illegal or criminal activities;  
(4) causing serious harmful consequences; or  
(5) other circumstances where probation and exemption from criminal punishment shall not be applied.  
Where the circumstances provided in paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law are present, the restriction in the preceding paragraph shall not apply.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　行贿犯罪取得的不正当财产性利益应当依照[刑法第六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-64)的规定予以追缴、责令退赔或者返还被害人。  
因行贿犯罪取得财产性利益以外的经营资格、资质或者职务晋升等其他不正当利益，建议有关部门依照相关规定予以处理。

**Article 11** The illegitimate property interests obtained through the crime of bribery shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 64 of the Criminal Law, be recovered, ordered to make restitution, or returned to the victim.  
Where illegitimate interests other than property interests, such as business qualifications, accreditations, or job promotions, are obtained through the crime of bribery, it is recommended that the relevant authorities handle them in accordance with the relevant provisions.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　行贿犯罪中的“谋取不正当利益”，是指行贿人谋取的利益违反法律、法规、规章、政策规定，或者要求国家工作人员违反法律、法规、规章、政策、行业规范的规定，为自己提供帮助或者方便条件。  
违背公平、公正原则，在经济、组织人事管理等活动中，谋取竞争优势的，应当认定为“谋取不正当利益”。

**Article 12** "Seeking illegitimate benefits" in the crime of bribery means that the benefits sought by the briber violate the provisions of laws, regulations, rules, or policies, or that the briber requires a State functionary to violate the provisions of laws, regulations, rules, policies, or industry norms to provide assistance or favorable conditions for the briber.  
If a person, in violation of the principles of fairness and justice, seeks competitive advantages in economic, organizational, or personnel management activities, it shall be deemed as "seeking illegitimate benefits."

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　[刑法第三百九十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-390)第二款规定的“被追诉前”，是指检察机关对行贿人的行贿行为刑事立案前。

**Article 13** "Before being prosecuted" as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 390 of the Criminal Law means before the procuratorial organ files a criminal case regarding the briber's act of bribery.
