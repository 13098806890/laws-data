# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部关于办理盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的意见

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**公布日期**：2018-09-28  
**生效日期**：2018-09-28

> （最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部于2018年9月28日公布 自公布之日起施行 法发〔2018〕18号） 为依法惩治盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等犯罪，维护公共安全、能源安全和生态安全，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》和《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》等法律、司法解释的规定，结合工作实际，制定本意见。 一、关于危害公共安全的认定 在实施盗窃油气等行为过程中，破坏正在使用的油气设备，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百一十八条规定的“危害公共安全”： （一）采用切割、打孔、撬砸、拆卸手段的，但是明显未危害公共安全的除外； （二）采用开、关等手段，足以引发火灾、爆炸等危险的。 二、关于盗窃油气未遂的刑事责任 着手实施盗窃油气行为，由于意志以外的原因未得逞，具有下列情形之一的，以盗窃罪（未遂）追究刑事责任： （一）以数额巨大的油气为盗窃目标的； （二）已将油气装入包装物或者运输工具，达到“数额较大”标准三倍以上的； （三）携带盗油卡子、手摇钻、电钻、电焊枪等切割、打孔、撬砸、拆卸工具的； （四）其他情节严重的情形。 三、关于共犯的认定 在共同盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等犯罪中，实际控制、为主出资或者组织、策划、纠集、雇佣、指使他人参与犯罪的，应当依法认定为主犯；对于其他人员，在共同犯罪中起主要作用的，也应当依法认定为主犯。 在输油输气管道投入使用前擅自安装阀门，在管道投入使用后将该阀门提供给他人盗窃油气的，以盗窃罪、破坏易燃易爆设备罪等有关犯罪的共同犯罪论处。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、关于危害公共安全的认定  
在实施盗窃油气等行为过程中，破坏正在使用的油气设备，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-118)规定的“危害公共安全”：  
（一）采用切割、打孔、撬砸、拆卸手段的，但是明显未危害公共安全的除外；  
（二）采用开、关等手段，足以引发火灾、爆炸等危险的。

**Article 1** I. Determination of Endangering Public Security  
In the process of committing theft of oil and gas, where the destroying of oil and gas equipment in use has any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as 'endangering public security' as stipulated in Article 118 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Using means of cutting, drilling, prying, smashing, or dismantling, except where it obviously does not endanger public security;  
(2) Using means such as opening or closing, which is sufficient to cause dangers such as fire or explosion.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、关于盗窃油气未遂的刑事责任  
着手实施盗窃油气行为，由于意志以外的原因未得逞，具有下列情形之一的，以盗窃罪（未遂）追究刑事责任：  
（一）以数额巨大的油气为盗窃目标的；  
（二）已将油气装入包装物或者运输工具，达到“数额较大”标准三倍以上的；  
（三）携带盗油卡子、手摇钻、电钻、电焊枪等切割、打孔、撬砸、拆卸工具的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** II. Criminal Liability for Attempted Theft of Oil and Gas  
Where a person has commenced the act of stealing oil and gas but fails to succeed due to reasons beyond his or her will, and has any of the following circumstances, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of theft (attempt):  
(1) Having oil and gas of a huge amount as the target of theft;  
(2) Having already loaded the oil and gas into packaging or onto a means of transport, reaching more than three times the standard for 'relatively large amount';  
(3) Carrying tools such as oil-theft clamps, hand drills, electric drills, or welding torches for cutting, drilling, prying, smashing, or dismantling;  
(4) Other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、关于共犯的认定  
在共同盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等犯罪中，实际控制、为主出资或者组织、策划、纠集、雇佣、指使他人参与犯罪的，应当依法认定为主犯；对于其他人员，在共同犯罪中起主要作用的，也应当依法认定为主犯。  
在输油输气管道投入使用前擅自安装阀门，在管道投入使用后将该阀门提供给他人盗窃油气的，以盗窃罪、破坏易燃易爆设备罪等有关犯罪的共同犯罪论处。

**Article 3** III. Determination of Joint Offenders  
In crimes such as joint theft of oil and gas or destruction of oil and gas equipment, those who actually control, primarily fund, or organize, plan, gather, hire, or instigate others to participate in the crime shall be determined as principal offenders according to law; for other persons who play a major role in the joint crime, they shall also be determined as principal offenders according to law.  
Where a person installs a valve without authorization before an oil or gas pipeline is put into use, and provides the valve to others for stealing oil or gas after the pipeline is put into use, the person shall be dealt with as a joint offender in the crime of theft, the crime of destroying flammable and explosive equipment, or other related crimes.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、关于内外勾结盗窃油气行为的处理  
行为人与油气企业人员勾结共同盗窃油气，没有利用油气企业人员职务便利，仅仅是利用其易于接近油气设备、熟悉环境等方便条件的，以盗窃罪的共同犯罪论处。  
实施上述行为，同时构成破坏易燃易爆设备罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 4** IV. Handling of Theft of Oil and Gas Involving Collusion Between Insiders and Outsiders  
Where a perpetrator colludes with a staff member of an oil and gas enterprise to jointly steal oil and gas, without utilizing the staff member's official convenience but merely taking advantage of his or her easy access to oil and gas equipment or familiarity with the environment, the perpetrator shall be dealt with as a joint offender in the crime of theft.  
Where the above act also constitutes the crime of destroying flammable and explosive equipment, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions providing for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、关于窝藏、转移、收购、加工、代为销售被盗油气行为的处理  
明知是犯罪所得的油气而予以窝藏、转移、收购、加工、代为销售或者以其他方式掩饰、隐瞒，符合[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得罪追究刑事责任。  
“明知”的认定，应当结合行为人的认知能力、所得报酬、运输工具、运输路线、收购价格、收购形式、加工方式、销售地点、仓储条件等因素综合考虑。  
实施第一款规定的犯罪行为，事前通谋的，以盗窃罪、破坏易燃易爆设备罪等有关犯罪的共同犯罪论处。

**Article 5** V. Handling of Concealing, Transferring, Purchasing, Processing, or Selling on Commission of Stolen Oil and Gas  
Where a person knowingly conceals, transfers, purchases, processes, sells on commission, or otherwise disguises or conceals oil and gas that is criminal proceeds, and the act meets the provisions of Article 312 of the Criminal Law, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of disguising or concealing criminal proceeds.  
The determination of 'knowingly' shall be made by comprehensively considering factors such as the actor's cognitive ability, remuneration received, means of transport, transport route, purchase price, purchase method, processing method, sales location, and storage conditions.  
Where a person commits the criminal act specified in the first paragraph and has prior collusion, the person shall be dealt with as a joint offender in the crime of theft, the crime of destroying flammable and explosive equipment, or other related crimes.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、关于直接经济损失的认定  
《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》第二条第三项规定的“直接经济损失”包括因实施盗窃油气等行为直接造成的油气损失以及采取抢修堵漏等措施所产生的费用。  
对于直接经济损失数额，综合油气企业提供的证据材料、犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其辩护人所提辩解、辩护意见等认定；难以确定的，依据价格认证机构出具的报告，结合其他证据认定。  
油气企业提供的证据材料，应当有工作人员签名和企业公章。

**Article 6** VI. Determination of Direct Economic Loss  
'Direct economic loss' as stipulated in Item (3) of Article 2 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Theft of Oil and Gas and Destruction of Oil and Gas Equipment includes the loss of oil and gas directly caused by the commission of theft of oil and gas, as well as the expenses incurred in taking measures such as emergency repair and plugging.  
The amount of direct economic loss shall be determined by comprehensively considering the evidence materials provided by the oil and gas enterprise, the explanations and defense opinions put forward by the criminal suspect, defendant, and their defenders; where it is difficult to determine, it shall be determined based on the report issued by a price certification authority, in combination with other evidence.  
The evidence materials provided by the oil and gas enterprise shall bear the signature of the staff member and the official seal of the enterprise.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、关于专门性问题的认定  
对于油气的质量、标准等专门性问题，综合油气企业提供的证据材料、犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其辩护人所提辩解、辩护意见等认定；难以确定的，依据司法鉴定机构出具的鉴定意见或者国务院公安部门指定的机构出具的报告，结合其他证据认定。  
油气企业提供的证据材料，应当有工作人员签名和企业公章。

**Article 7** VII. Determination of Specialized Issues  
For specialized issues such as the quality and standards of oil and gas, the determination shall be made by comprehensively considering the evidence materials provided by the oil and gas enterprise, the explanations and defense opinions put forward by the criminal suspect, defendant, and their defenders; where it is difficult to determine, it shall be determined based on the expert evaluation opinion issued by a judicial appraisal institution or the report issued by an institution designated by the public security department of the State Council, in combination with other evidence.  
The evidence materials provided by the oil and gas enterprise shall bear the signature of the staff member and the official seal of the enterprise.
