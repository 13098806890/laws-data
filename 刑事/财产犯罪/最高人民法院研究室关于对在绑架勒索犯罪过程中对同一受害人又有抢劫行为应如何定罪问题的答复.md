<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院研究室  
关于对在绑架勒索犯罪过程中对同一受害人  
又有抢劫行为应如何定罪问题的答复  
（1995年5月30日）  
江西省高级人民法院：  
你院赣高法〔1995〕54号《关于在绑架勒索犯罪过程中又有抢劫行为是否数罪并罚的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
行为人在绑架勒索犯罪过程中，又抢劫同一被害人财物的，应以绑架勒索罪定罪，从重处罚；同时又抢劫他人财物的，应分别以绑架勒索罪、抢劫罪定罪，实行数罪并罚。  
附：  
江西省高级人民法院  
关于在绑架勒索犯罪过程中又有抢劫  
行为是否数罪并罚的请示  
1995年4月20日      赣高法〔1995〕54号  
最高人民法院：  
本院在审理唐胜平、石自房、石远彬绑架勒索案（案情见本院审理报告）时，对该案是以绑架勒索一罪处罚还是以绑架勒索罪、抢劫罪并罚，审判委员会讨论意见不一，把握不准。第一种意见认为：绑架勒索中直接从被绑架人身上劫取钱物，虽然符合抢劫的特征，但它是一种牵连行为，应当依重罪吸收轻罪的原则，以绑架勒索罪从重处罚。第二种意见认为，罪犯实施了绑架勒索行为，又在绑架的同时采取暴力或暴力相威胁，直接从被绑架人身上劫取财物，具有绑架勒索和抢劫的两种故意和行为，构成了两个犯罪，应当数罪并罚。  
此类情况应如何定罪处罚，请批复。

**Article 1** Reply of the Research Office of the Supreme People's Court  
on How to Determine the Crime When a Robbery Is Also Committed  
Against the Same Victim in the Course of Kidnapping for Extortion  
(May 30, 1995)  
  
Higher People's Court of Jiangxi Province:  
  
Your court's Request for Instructions No. 54 [1995] of the Higher People's Court of Jiangxi Province, titled "Request for Instructions on Whether Concurrent Punishment for Multiple Crimes Shall Apply When a Robbery Is Also Committed in the Course of Kidnapping for Extortion," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
Where, in the course of committing the crime of kidnapping for extortion, a perpetrator also robs property from the same victim, the crime shall be determined as kidnapping for extortion, and a severe punishment shall be imposed. Where the perpetrator also robs property from another person, the crimes shall be determined as kidnapping for extortion and robbery respectively, and concurrent punishment for multiple crimes shall be imposed.  
  
Appendix:  
  
Request for Instructions of the Higher People's Court of Jiangxi Province  
on Whether Concurrent Punishment for Multiple Crimes Shall Apply  
When a Robbery Is Also Committed in the Course of  
Kidnapping for Extortion  
  
April 20, 1995 Document No. 54 [1995] of the Higher People's Court of Jiangxi Province  
  
To the Supreme People's Court:  
  
When this court was adjudicating the case of Tang Shengping, Shi Zifang, and Shi Yuanbin for kidnapping for extortion (the facts of the case are detailed in this court's trial report), the Adjudication Committee had differing opinions and could not reach a consensus on whether the case shall be punished as a single crime of kidnapping for extortion or shall be subject to concurrent punishment for the crimes of kidnapping for extortion and robbery. The first opinion held that directly taking money or property from the kidnapped person in the course of kidnapping for extortion, though conforming to the characteristics of robbery, constitutes a connected act. Therefore, in accordance with the principle of the more serious crime absorbing the less serious one, the crime shall be determined as kidnapping for extortion with a severe punishment. The second opinion held that the offender committed the act of kidnapping for extortion and, at the same time, directly took property from the kidnapped person by resorting to violence or threat of violence. Thus, having both the intent and act of kidnapping for extortion and robbery, the offender committed two crimes and shall be subject to concurrent punishment.  
  
Please provide instructions on how to determine and punish such crimes.
