### 最高人民法院关于办理非法制造、买卖、运输、私藏钢珠枪犯罪案件适用法律问题的通知

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于办理非法制造、买卖、运输、私藏  
  
钢珠枪犯罪案件适用法律问题的通知  
  
法发〔1993〕43号  
  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院，解放军军事法院：  
  
近年来，非法制造、买卖、运输、私藏钢珠枪的问题在一些地方十分突出。犯罪分子利用钢珠枪进行犯罪活动的案件明显增多，对社会治安造成严重危害。  
  
钢珠枪是能发射金属弹丸，可致人伤亡的枪支。各级人民法院对非法制造、买卖、运输、私藏钢珠枪，构成犯罪的，应根据案件不同情况，分别依照[刑法第一百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-112)、第一百六十三条的规定，追究刑事责任。  
  
1993年12月17日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Notice on Issues Concerning the Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Illegal Manufacture, Purchase, Sale, Transportation, or Private Possession of Steel Pellet Guns  
Fa Fa [1993] No. 43  
  
Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government; the Military Court of the People's Liberation Army:  
  
In recent years, the problem of illegal manufacture, purchase, sale, transportation, or private possession of steel pellet guns has become very prominent in some localities. Cases in which criminals use steel pellet guns to commit criminal activities have significantly increased, causing serious harm to public security.  
  
A steel pellet gun is a firearm capable of firing metal pellets and causing casualties. Where the illegal manufacture, purchase, sale, transportation, or private possession of steel pellet guns constitutes a crime, the People's Courts at all levels shall, based on the specific circumstances of each case, investigate criminal liability in accordance with Article 112 and Article 163 of the Criminal Law respectively.  
  
December 17, 1993
