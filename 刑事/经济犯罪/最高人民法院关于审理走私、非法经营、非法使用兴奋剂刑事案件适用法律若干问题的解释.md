# 最高人民法院关于审理走私、非法经营、非法使用兴奋剂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2019〕16号  
**公布日期**：2020-01-01  
**生效日期**：2020-01-01

> 《最高人民法院关于审理走私、非法经营、非法使用兴奋剂刑事案件适 用法律若干问题的解释》已于 2019 年 11 月 12 日由最高人民法院审判委 员会第1781次会议通过，现予公布，自2020年1月1日起施行。 2019年11月18日 最高人民法院 关于审理走私、非法经营、非法使用 兴奋剂刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2019〕16号 为依法惩治走私、非法经营、非法使用 兴奋剂犯罪，维护体育竞赛的公平竞争，保 护体育运动参加者的身心健康，根据《中华 人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉 讼法》的规定，制定本解释。 第一条 运动员、运动员辅助人员走 私兴奋剂目录所列物质，或者其他人员以 在体育竞赛中非法使用为目的走私兴奋剂 目录所列物质，涉案物质属于国家禁止进 出口的货物、物品，具有下列情形之一的， 应当依照刑法第一百五十一条第三款的规 定，以走私国家禁止进出口的货物、物品罪 定罪处罚： （一）一年内曾因走私被给予二次以上 行政处罚后又走私的； （二）用于或者准备用于未成年人运动 员、残疾人运动员的； （三）用于或者准备用于国内、国际重 大体育竞赛的； （四）其他造成严重恶劣社会影响的情 形。 实施前款规定的行为，涉案物质不属 于国家禁止进出口的货物、物品，但偷逃应缴税额一万元以上或者一年内曾因走私被给予二次以上行政处罚后又走私的，应当 依照刑法第一百五十三条的规定，以走私 普通货物、物品罪定罪处罚。 对于本条第一款、第二款规定以外的 走私兴奋剂目录所列物质行为，适用《最高 人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理走私 刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释 〔2014〕10号）规定的定罪量刑标准。 第二条 违反国家规定，未经许可经 营兴奋剂目录所列物质，涉案物质属于法 律、行政法规规定的限制买卖的物品，扰乱 市场秩序，情节严重的，应当依照刑法第二 百二十五条的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处 罚。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 运动员、运动员辅助人员走 私兴奋剂目录所列物质，或者其他人员以 在体育竞赛中非法使用为目的走私兴奋剂 目录所列物质，涉案物质属于国家禁止进 出口的货物、物品，具有下列情形之一的， 应当依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第三款的规 定，以走私国家禁止进出口的货物、物品罪 定罪处罚：  
（一）一年内曾因走私被给予二次以上 行政处罚后又走私的；  
（二）用于或者准备用于未成年人运动 员、残疾人运动员的；  
（三）用于或者准备用于国内、国际重 大体育竞赛的；  
（四）其他造成严重恶劣社会影响的情 形。  
实施前款规定的行为，涉案物质不属 于国家禁止进出口的货物、物品，但偷逃应缴税额一万元以上或者一年内曾因走私被给予二次以上行政处罚后又走私的，应当 依照[刑法第一百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-153)的规定，以走私 普通货物、物品罪定罪处罚。  
对于本条第一款、第二款规定以外的 走私兴奋剂目录所列物质行为，适用《最高 人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理走私 刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释 〔2014〕10号）规定的定罪量刑标准。

**Article 1** Where an athlete or an athlete's support personnel smuggles a substance listed in the Prohibited List, or any other person smuggles a substance listed in the Prohibited List for the purpose of illegal use in sports competitions, and the involved substance is a good or article the import or export of which is prohibited by the State, in any of the following circumstances, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law for the crime of smuggling goods or articles whose import or export is prohibited by the State:  
(1) the person has been given an administrative penalty for smuggling twice or more within one year and subsequently smuggles again;  
(2) the substance is used or intended to be used by a minor athlete or a disabled athlete;  
(3) the substance is used or intended to be used in a major domestic or international sports competition; or  
(4) other circumstances that have caused serious adverse social impact.  
Where the act under the preceding paragraph is committed, and the involved substance is not a good or article the import or export of which is prohibited by the State, but the amount of tax evaded is 10,000 yuan or more, or the person has been given an administrative penalty for smuggling twice or more within one year and subsequently smuggles again, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with Article 153 of the Criminal Law for the crime of smuggling ordinary goods or articles.  
For any act of smuggling a substance listed in the Prohibited List other than those specified in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 of this Article, the standards for conviction and sentencing provided in the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues concerning the Application of Law in the Handling of Criminal Cases of Smuggling (Fa Shi [2014] No. 10) shall apply.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 违反国家规定，未经许可经 营兴奋剂目录所列物质，涉案物质属于法 律、行政法规规定的限制买卖的物品，扰乱 市场秩序，情节严重的，应当依照刑法第二 百二十五条的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处 罚。

**Article 2** Where, in violation of State regulations, a person operates a substance listed in the Prohibited List without a license, and the involved substance is an article the sale of which is restricted by laws or administrative regulations, thereby disrupting market order, if the circumstances are serious, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law for the crime of illegal business operation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 对未成年人、残疾人负有监 护、看护职责的人组织未成年人、残疾人在 体育运动中非法使用兴奋剂，具有下列情 形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百六十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-260) 之一规定的“情节恶劣”，以虐待被监护、看 护人罪定罪处罚：  
（一）强迫未成年人、残疾人使用的；  
（二）引诱、欺骗未成年人、残疾人长期 使用的；  
（三）其他严重损害未成年人、残疾人 身心健康的情形。

**Article 3** Where a person who has a duty of guardianship or care over a minor or a disabled person organizes the minor or disabled person to illegally use a prohibited substance in sports activities, in any of the following circumstances, the circumstances shall be deemed as "disgusting" as provided in Article 260 bis of the Criminal Law, and punishment shall be imposed for the crime of maltreating a person under guardianship or care:  
(1) compelling the minor or disabled person to use the substance;  
(2) inducing or deceiving the minor or disabled person into long-term use of the substance; or  
(3) other circumstances that seriously harm the physical or mental health of the minor or disabled person.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 在普通高等学校招生、公务 员录用等法律规定的国家考试涉及的体 育、体能测试等体育运动中，组织考生非法 使用兴奋剂的，应当依照刑法第二百八十 四条之一的规定，以组织考试作弊罪定罪 处罚。  
明知他人实施前款犯罪而为其提供兴 奋剂的，依照前款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where a person organizes examinees to illegally use a prohibited substance in sports activities such as physical tests or fitness tests involved in State examinations prescribed by law, including higher education institution admissions and civil servant recruitment, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with Article 284 bis of the Criminal Law for the crime of organizing cheating on examinations.  
Where a person, knowing that another person commits the crime under the preceding paragraph, provides a prohibited substance to that person, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 生产、销售含有兴奋剂目录 所列物质的食品，符合刑法第一百四十三 条、第一百四十四条规定的，以生产、销售 不符合安全标准的食品罪、生产、销售有 毒、有害食品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where a person produces or sells food containing a substance listed in the Prohibited List, and the act meets the provisions of Article 143 or Article 144 of the Criminal Law, punishment shall be imposed for the crime of producing or selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing or selling toxic or harmful food.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 国家机关工作人员在行使反 兴奋剂管理职权时滥用职权或者玩忽职 守，造成严重兴奋剂违规事件,严重损害国 家声誉或者造成恶劣社会影响，符合刑法 第三百九十七条规定的，以滥用职权罪、玩 忽职守罪定罪处罚。  
依法或者受委托行使反兴奋剂管理职 权的单位的工作人员，在行使反兴奋剂管 理职权时滥用职权或者玩忽职守的，依照 前款规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where a functionary of a State organ, in exercising anti-doping management authority, abuses his or her power or neglects his or her duty, resulting in a serious violation of anti-doping rules, causing serious damage to the reputation of the State or causing adverse social impact, and the act meets the provisions of Article 397 of the Criminal Law, punishment shall be imposed for the crime of abuse of power or the crime of dereliction of duty.  
Where a staff member of a unit that exercises anti-doping management authority according to law or by delegation, in exercising anti-doping management authority, abuses his or her power or neglects his or her duty, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 实施本解释规定的行为，涉 案物质属于毒品、制毒物品等，构成有关犯 罪的，依照相应犯罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Where an act provided in this Interpretation is committed, and the involved substance is a drug, a precursor chemical, or the like, constituting a relevant crime, punishment shall be imposed for the corresponding crime according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 对于是否属于本解释规定的 “兴奋剂”“兴奋剂目录所列物质”“体育运 动”“国内、国际重大体育竞赛”等专门性问 题，应当依据《中华人民共和国体育法》《反 兴奋剂条例》等法律法规，结合国务院体育 主管部门出具的认定意见等证据材料作出 认定。

**Article 8** With respect to whether a matter falls within the scope of "prohibited substance," "substance listed in the Prohibited List," "sports activities," "major domestic or international sports competitions," and other specialized issues as provided in this Interpretation, a determination shall be made in accordance with laws and regulations such as the Sports Law of the People's Republic of China and the Anti-Doping Regulations, and in light of evidence materials including the identification opinion issued by the competent sports authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 本解释自 2020 年 1 月 1 日起 施行。

**Article 9** This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2020.
