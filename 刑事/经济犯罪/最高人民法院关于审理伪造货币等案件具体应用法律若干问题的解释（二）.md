# 最高人民法院关于审理伪造货币等案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕14号  
**公布日期**：2010-10-11  
**生效日期**：2010-11-03

> 最高人民法院《关于审理伪造货币等案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》已于2010年10月11日由最高人民法院审判委员会1498次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年11月3日起施行。 2010年10月20日 最高人民法院 关于审理伪造货币等案件具体应用 法律若干问题的解释（二）

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  仿照真货币的图案、形状、色彩等特征非法制造假币，冒充真币的行为，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-170)规定的“伪造货币”。  
对真货币采用剪贴、挖补、揭层、涂改、移位、重印等方法加工处理，改变真币形态、价值的行为，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-173)规定的“变造货币”。

**Article 1** The act of illegally manufacturing counterfeit currency by imitating the patterns, shapes, colors and other features of genuine currency and passing it off as genuine currency shall be determined as "forgery of currency" as provided for in Article 170 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The act of processing genuine currency by methods such as cutting, pasting, digging, peeling, altering, shifting, reprinting, etc., to change the form or value of genuine currency shall be determined as "alteration of currency" as provided for in Article 173 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  同时采用伪造和变造手段，制造真伪拼凑货币的行为，依照[刑法第一百七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-170)的规定，以伪造货币罪定罪处罚。

**Article 2** The act of manufacturing currency that combines genuine and counterfeit elements by using both forgery and alteration methods shall, in accordance with Article 170 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, be convicted and punished as the crime of forgery of currency.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  以正在流通的境外货币为对象的假币犯罪，依照[刑法第一百七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-170)至第一百七十三条的规定定罪处罚。  
假境外货币犯罪的数额，按照案发当日中国外汇交易中心或者中国人民银行授权机构公布的人民币对该货币的中间价折合成人民币计算。中国外汇交易中心或者中国人民银行授权机构未公布汇率中间价的境外货币，按照案发当日境内银行人民币对该货币的中间价折算成人民币，或者该货币在境内银行、国际外汇市场对美元汇率，与人民币对美元汇率中间价进行套算。

**Article 3** A counterfeit currency crime targeting foreign currency in circulation shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Articles 170 through 173 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The amount of a crime involving counterfeit foreign currency shall be calculated by converting it into RMB at the central parity rate of RMB against such currency published by the China Foreign Exchange Trade System or an institution authorized by the People's Bank of China on the date of the occurrence of the crime. Where the China Foreign Exchange Trade System or an institution authorized by the People's Bank of China has not published a central parity rate for a foreign currency, it shall be converted into RMB at the central parity rate of RMB against such currency published by domestic banks on the date of the occurrence of the crime, or by calculating the cross rate between the exchange rate of such currency against the US dollar in domestic banks or the international foreign exchange market and the central parity rate of RMB against the US dollar.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  以中国人民银行发行的普通纪念币和贵金属纪念币为对象的假币犯罪，依照[刑法第一百七十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-170)至第一百七十三条的规定定罪处罚。  
假普通纪念币犯罪的数额，以面额计算；假贵金属纪念币犯罪的数额，以贵金属纪念币的初始发售价格计算。

**Article 4** A counterfeit currency crime targeting ordinary commemorative coins and precious metal commemorative coins issued by the People's Bank of China shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Articles 170 through 173 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The amount of a crime involving counterfeit ordinary commemorative coins shall be calculated on the basis of the face value; the amount of a crime involving counterfeit precious metal commemorative coins shall be calculated on the basis of the initial issuance price of the precious metal commemorative coins.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  以使用为目的，伪造停止流通的货币，或者使用伪造的停止流通的货币的，依照[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)的规定，以诈骗罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Whoever, for the purpose of use, forges currency that has ceased to circulate, or uses forged currency that has ceased to circulate, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of fraud in accordance with Article 266 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 6** Where any previously issued judicial interpretation is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
