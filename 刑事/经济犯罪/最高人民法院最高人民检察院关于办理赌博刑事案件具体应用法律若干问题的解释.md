### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 以营利为目的，有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第三百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-303)规定的“聚众赌博”：  
（一）组织3人以上赌博，抽头渔利数额累计达到5000元以上的；  
（二）组织3人以上赌博，赌资数额累计达到5万元以上的；  
（三）组织3人以上赌博，参赌人数累计达到20人以上的；  
（四）组织中华人民共和国公民10人以上赴境外赌博，从中收取回扣、介绍费的。

**Article 1** For the purpose of profit, in any of the following circumstances, the act shall constitute "gathering a crowd to gamble" as provided in Article 303 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Organizing gambling among three or more persons, where the cumulative amount of rake-off proceeds reaches 5,000 yuan or more;  
(2) Organizing gambling among three or more persons, where the cumulative amount of gambling funds reaches 50,000 yuan or more;  
(3) Organizing gambling among three or more persons, where the cumulative number of participants reaches 20 or more;  
(4) Organizing ten or more citizens of the People's Republic of China to gamble abroad, and receiving kickbacks or referral fees therefrom.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 以营利为目的，在计算机网络上建立赌博网站，或者为赌博网站担任代理，接受投注的，属于[刑法第三百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-303)规定的“开设赌场”。

**Article 2** For the purpose of profit, establishing a gambling website on a computer network, or acting as an agent for a gambling website and accepting bets, shall constitute "operating a gambling establishment" as provided in Article 303 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 中华人民共和国公民在我国领域外周边地区聚众赌博、开设赌场，以吸引中华人民共和国公民为主要客源，构成赌博罪的，可以依照刑法规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 3** Where a citizen of the People's Republic of China gathers a crowd to gamble or operates a gambling establishment in areas bordering the territory of China, with the primary purpose of attracting citizens of the People's Republic of China as customers, and the act constitutes a gambling crime, criminal liability may be investigated in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 明知他人实施赌博犯罪活动，而为其提供资金、计算机网络、通讯、费用结算等直接帮助的，以赌博罪的共犯论处。

**Article 4** Whoever, knowing that another person is engaging in gambling criminal activities, provides him or her with direct assistance such as funds, computer network, communications, or fee settlement, shall be treated as an accomplice to the gambling crime.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 实施赌博犯罪，有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-303)的规定从重处罚：  
（一）具有国家工作人员身份的；  
（二）组织国家工作人员赴境外赌博的；  
（三）组织未成年人参与赌博，或者开设赌场吸引未成年人参与赌博的。

**Article 5** Where a gambling crime is committed and any of the following circumstances exists, a severe punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 303 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The perpetrator has the status of a state functionary;  
(2) The perpetrator organizes state functionaries to gamble abroad;  
(3) The perpetrator organizes minors to participate in gambling, or operates a gambling establishment to attract minors to participate in gambling.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 未经国家批准擅自发行、销售彩票，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（四）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Whoever issues or sells lottery tickets without state approval, which constitutes a crime, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with the provisions of Article 225, Item (4) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 通过赌博或者为国家工作人员赌博提供资金的形式实施行贿、受贿行为，构成犯罪的，依照刑法关于贿赂犯罪的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Whoever commits bribery or accepts bribes through gambling or by providing funds for gambling of state functionaries, which constitutes a crime, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law on bribery crimes.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 赌博犯罪中用作赌注的款物、换取筹码的款物和通过赌博赢取的款物属于赌资。通过计算机网络实施赌博犯罪的，赌资数额可以按照在计算机网络上投注或者赢取的点数乘以每一点实际代表的金额认定。  
赌资应当依法予以追缴；赌博用具、赌博违法所得以及赌博犯罪分子所有的专门用于赌博的资金、交通工具、通讯工具等，应当依法予以没收。

**Article 8** The money and goods used as stakes, the money and goods used to exchange for chips, and the money and goods won through gambling in gambling crimes shall constitute gambling funds. For gambling crimes committed through computer networks, the amount of gambling funds may be determined by multiplying the points bet or won on the computer network by the actual amount represented by each point.  
Gambling funds shall be recovered according to law; gambling paraphernalia, illegal gains from gambling, and funds, means of transport, communication tools, etc., exclusively used for gambling by the gambling criminals shall be confiscated according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 不以营利为目的，进行带有少量财物输赢的娱乐活动，以及提供棋牌室等娱乐场所只收取正常的场所和服务费用的经营行为等，不以赌博论处。

**Article 9** Entertainment activities involving small amounts of winnings or losses not for the purpose of profit, and business operations such as providing chess and card rooms where only normal venue and service fees are charged, shall not be treated as gambling.
