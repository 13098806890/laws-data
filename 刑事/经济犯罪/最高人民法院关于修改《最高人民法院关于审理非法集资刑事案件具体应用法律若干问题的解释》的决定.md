# 最高人民法院关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理非法集资刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕5号  
**公布日期**：2021-12-30  
**生效日期**：2022-03-01

> 《最高人民法院关于修改〈最高人民法院关于审理非法集资刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释〉的决定》已于2021年12月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1860次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年3月1日起施行。 2022年2月23日 最高人民法院 关于修改《最高人民法院关于审理非法集资刑事 案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》的决定 法释〔2022〕5号 根据刑法修改和司法实践，现决定对《最高人民法院关于审理非法集资刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2010〕18号，以下简称《解释》）作如下修改： 一、将第一条第一款第一项修改为：“未经有关部门依法许可或者借用合法经营的形式吸收资金”，第二项修改为：“通过网络、媒体、推介会、传单、手机信息等途径向社会公开宣传”。 二、将第二条第八项修改为：“以网络借贷、投资入股、虚拟币交易等方式非法吸收资金的”，第九项修改为：“以委托理财、融资租赁等方式非法吸收资金的”，增加一项作为第十项：“以提供‘养老服务'、投资‘养老项目'、销售‘老年产品'等方式非法吸收资金的”，原第十项、第十一项改为第十一项、第十二项。 三、将第三条修改为：“非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，具有下列情形之一的，应当依法追究刑事责任： “（一）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款数额在100万元以上的； “（二）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款对象150人以上的； “（三）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在50万元以上的。 “非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款数额在50万元以上或者给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在25万元以上，同时具有下列情节之一的，应当依法追究刑事责任： “（一）曾因非法集资受过刑事追究的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　违反国家金融管理法律规定，向社会公众（包括单位和个人）吸收资金的行为，同时具备下列四个条件的，除刑法另有规定的以外，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-176)规定的“非法吸收公众存款或者变相吸收公众存款”：  
（一）未经有关部门依法许可或者借用合法经营的形式吸收资金；  
（二）通过网络、媒体、推介会、传单、手机信息等途径向社会公开宣传；  
（三）承诺在一定期限内以货币、实物、股权等方式还本付息或者给付回报；  
（四）向社会公众即社会不特定对象吸收资金。  
未向社会公开宣传，在亲友或者单位内部针对特定对象吸收资金的，不属于非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款。

**Article 1** Any act of absorbing funds from the public (including both units and individuals) in violation of the provisions of the state's financial management laws shall, if meeting all of the following four conditions simultaneously, be deemed as the "illegal absorption of public deposits or disguised absorption of public deposits" as stipulated in Article 176 of the Criminal Law, except as otherwise provided in the Criminal Law:  
(1) Absorbing funds without permission from the relevant departments according to law or by borrowing the form of lawful business operations;  
(2) Making public propaganda through the internet, media, promotional meetings, leaflets, mobile phone messages, or other channels;  
(3) Promising to repay principal and interest or give returns in the form of currency, in-kind goods, equity, etc. within a certain period;  
(4) Absorbing funds from the public, i.e., unspecified targets in society.  
Where funds are absorbed from specific targets among relatives, friends, or within a unit without public publicity, this shall not constitute illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　实施下列行为之一，符合[本解释第一条](#art-1)第一款规定的条件的，应当依照[刑法第一百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-176)的规定，以非法吸收公众存款罪定罪处罚：  
（一）不具有房产销售的真实内容或者不以房产销售为主要目的，以返本销售、售后包租、约定回购、销售房产份额等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（二）以转让林权并代为管护等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（三）以代种植（养殖）、租种植（养殖）、联合种植（养殖）等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（四）不具有销售商品、提供服务的真实内容或者不以销售商品、提供服务为主要目的，以商品回购、寄存代售等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（五）不具有发行股票、债券的真实内容，以虚假转让股权、发售虚构债券等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（六）不具有募集基金的真实内容，以假借境外基金、发售虚构基金等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（七）不具有销售保险的真实内容，以假冒保险公司、伪造保险单据等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（八）以网络借贷、投资入股、虚拟币交易等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（九）以委托理财、融资租赁等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（十）以提供“养老服务”、投资“养老项目”、销售“老年产品”等方式非法吸收资金的；  
（十一）利用民间“会”“社”等组织非法吸收资金的；  
（十二）其他非法吸收资金的行为。

**Article 2** Any person who commits any of the following acts, if meeting the conditions specified in Article 1, paragraph 1 of this Interpretation, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally absorbing public deposits in accordance with Article 176 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Illegally absorbing funds by means of capital return sales, sale-and-leaseback, agreed repurchase, sale of property shares, etc., without genuine content of real estate sales or without taking real estate sales as the main purpose;  
(2) Illegally absorbing funds by means of transferring forest rights and managing them on behalf of others, etc.;  
(3) Illegally absorbing funds by means of substitute planting (breeding), lease planting (breeding), joint planting (breeding), etc.;  
(4) Illegally absorbing funds by means of commodity repurchase, deposit and consignment sales, etc., without genuine content of selling goods or providing services, or without taking selling goods or providing services as the main purpose;  
(5) Illegally absorbing funds by means of falsely transferring equities, issuing fictitious bonds, etc., without genuine content of issuing stocks or bonds;  
(6) Illegally absorbing funds by means of falsely using overseas funds, issuing fictitious funds, etc., without genuine content of raising funds;  
(7) Illegally absorbing funds by means of counterfeiting insurance companies, forging insurance documents, etc., without genuine content of selling insurance;  
(8) Illegally absorbing funds by means of online lending, equity investment, virtual currency transactions, etc.;  
(9) Illegally absorbing funds by means of entrusted wealth management, financial leasing, etc.;  
(10) Illegally absorbing funds by means of providing "elderly care services", investing in "elderly care projects", selling "elderly products", etc.;  
(11) Illegally absorbing funds by using folk organizations such as "associations" or "societies";  
(12) Other acts of illegally absorbing funds.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，具有下列情形之一的，应当依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款数额在100万元以上的；  
（二）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款对象150人以上的；  
（三）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在50万元以上的。  
非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款数额在50万元以上或者给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在25万元以上，同时具有下列情节之一的，应当依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）曾因非法集资受过刑事追究的；  
（二）二年内曾因非法集资受过行政处罚的；  
（三）造成恶劣社会影响或者其他严重后果的。

**Article 3** Where illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits falls under any of the following circumstances, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) The amount of illegally absorbed or disguisedly absorbed public deposits reaches RMB 1 million or more;  
(2) The number of targets of illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits reaches 150 or more;  
(3) The illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits causes direct economic losses to depositors of RMB 500,000 or more.  
Where the amount of illegally absorbed or disguisedly absorbed public deposits reaches RMB 500,000 or more, or the direct economic losses caused to depositors reach RMB 250,000 or more, and at the same time any of the following circumstances exists, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
(1) Having previously been subject to criminal investigation for illegal fundraising;  
(2) Having been subject to administrative penalty for illegal fundraising within the preceding two years;  
(3) Causing adverse social impact or other serious consequences.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-176)规定的“数额巨大或者有其他严重情节”：  
（一）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款数额在500万元以上的；  
（二）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款对象500人以上的；  
（三）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在250万元以上的。  
非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款数额在250万元以上或者给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在150万元以上，同时具有[本解释第三条](#art-3)第二款第三项情节的，应当认定为“其他严重情节”。

**Article 4** Where illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as the "huge amount or has other serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 176 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The amount of illegally absorbed or disguisedly absorbed public deposits reaches RMB 5 million or more;  
(2) The number of targets of illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits reaches 500 or more;  
(3) The illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits causes direct economic losses to depositors of RMB 2.5 million or more.  
Where the amount of illegally absorbed or disguisedly absorbed public deposits reaches RMB 2.5 million or more, or the direct economic losses caused to depositors reach RMB 1.5 million or more, and at the same time the circumstance set forth in Article 3, paragraph 2, subparagraph (3) of this Interpretation exists, it shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances".

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-176)规定的“数额特别巨大或者有其他特别严重情节”：  
（一）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款数额在5000万元以上的；  
（二）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款对象5000人以上的；  
（三）非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在2500万元以上的。  
非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款数额在2500万元以上或者给存款人造成直接经济损失数额在1500万元以上，同时具有[本解释第三条](#art-3)第二款第三项情节的，应当认定为“其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 5** Where illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as the "particularly huge amount or has other particularly serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 176 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The amount of illegally absorbed or disguisedly absorbed public deposits reaches RMB 50 million or more;  
(2) The number of targets of illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits reaches 5,000 or more;  
(3) The illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits causes direct economic losses to depositors of RMB 25 million or more.  
Where the amount of illegally absorbed or disguisedly absorbed public deposits reaches RMB 25 million or more, or the direct economic losses caused to depositors reach RMB 15 million or more, and at the same time the circumstance set forth in Article 3, paragraph 2, subparagraph (3) of this Interpretation exists, it shall be deemed as "other particularly serious circumstances".

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款的数额，以行为人所吸收的资金全额计算。在提起公诉前积极退赃退赔，减少损害结果发生的，可以从轻或者减轻处罚；在提起公诉后退赃退赔的，可以作为量刑情节酌情考虑。  
非法吸收或者变相吸收公众存款，主要用于正常的生产经营活动，能够在提起公诉前清退所吸收资金，可以免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。  
对依法不需要追究刑事责任或者免予刑事处罚的，应当依法将案件移送有关行政机关。

**Article 6** The amount of illegal absorption or disguised absorption of public deposits shall be calculated based on the full amount of funds absorbed by the perpetrator. If the perpetrator actively returns the illegal gains and makes restitution before the initiation of public prosecution, thereby mitigating the harmful consequences, the punishment may be lenient or mitigated; if the return and restitution are made after the initiation of public prosecution, it may be considered as a discretionary circumstance for sentencing.  
If the illegally absorbed or disguisedly absorbed public deposits are mainly used for normal production and business operations, and the absorbed funds can be fully returned before the initiation of public prosecution, the perpetrator may be exempted from criminal punishment; if the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not significant, it shall not be treated as a crime.  
For cases that according to law do not require investigation of criminal liability or are exempted from criminal punishment, the case shall be transferred to the relevant administrative authority according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　以非法占有为目的，使用诈骗方法实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定所列行为的，应当依照[刑法第一百九十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-192)的规定，以集资诈骗罪定罪处罚。  
使用诈骗方法非法集资，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为“以非法占有为目的”：  
（一）集资后不用于生产经营活动或者用于生产经营活动与筹集资金规模明显不成比例，致使集资款不能返还的；  
（二）肆意挥霍集资款，致使集资款不能返还的；  
（三）携带集资款逃匿的；  
（四）将集资款用于违法犯罪活动的；  
（五）抽逃、转移资金、隐匿财产，逃避返还资金的；  
（六）隐匿、销毁账目，或者搞假破产、假倒闭，逃避返还资金的；  
（七）拒不交代资金去向，逃避返还资金的；  
（八）其他可以认定非法占有目的的情形。  
集资诈骗罪中的非法占有目的，应当区分情形进行具体认定。行为人部分非法集资行为具有非法占有目的的，对该部分非法集资行为所涉集资款以集资诈骗罪定罪处罚；非法集资共同犯罪中部分行为人具有非法占有目的，其他行为人没有非法占有集资款的共同故意和行为的，对具有非法占有目的的行为人以集资诈骗罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Any person who, for the purpose of illegal possession, uses fraudulent methods to commit the acts listed in Article 2 of this Interpretation shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fundraising fraud in accordance with Article 192 of the Criminal Law.  
If illegal fundraising is carried out using fraudulent methods, and any of the following circumstances exists, it may be deemed as "for the purpose of illegal possession":  
(1) Not using the raised funds for production and business operations after fundraising, or using them for such activities in a proportion obviously disproportionate to the scale of the raised funds, resulting in the inability to repay the funds;  
(2) Lavishly squandering the raised funds, resulting in the inability to repay them;  
(3) Absconding with the raised funds;  
(4) Using the raised funds for illegal or criminal activities;  
(5) Secretly withdrawing or transferring funds, concealing assets, or evading the return of funds;  
(6) Concealing or destroying accounts, or engaging in false bankruptcy or false closure to evade the return of funds;  
(7) Refusing to disclose the whereabouts of the funds to evade the return of funds;  
(8) Other circumstances that may establish the purpose of illegal possession.  
The purpose of illegal possession in the crime of fundraising fraud shall be specifically determined based on the circumstances. If part of the illegal fundraising acts by the perpetrator involves the purpose of illegal possession, the funds involved in that part shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fundraising fraud; in a joint crime of illegal fundraising, if some perpetrators have the purpose of illegal possession while others do not share the common intent and conduct of illegally possessing the funds, those with the purpose of illegal possession shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fundraising fraud.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　集资诈骗数额在10万元以上的，应当认定为“数额较大”；数额在100万元以上的，应当认定为“数额巨大”。  
集资诈骗数额在50万元以上，同时具有[本解释第三条](#art-3)第二款第三项情节的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-192)规定的“其他严重情节”。  
集资诈骗的数额以行为人实际骗取的数额计算，在案发前已归还的数额应予扣除。行为人为实施集资诈骗活动而支付的广告费、中介费、手续费、回扣，或者用于行贿、赠与等费用，不予扣除。行为人为实施集资诈骗活动而支付的利息，除本金未归还可予折抵本金以外，应当计入诈骗数额。

**Article 8** Where the amount of fundraising fraud is RMB 100,000 or more, it shall be deemed as "a relatively large amount"; where the amount is RMB 1 million or more, it shall be deemed as "a huge amount".  
Where the amount of fundraising fraud is RMB 500,000 or more, and at the same time the circumstance set forth in Article 3, paragraph 2, subparagraph (3) of this Interpretation exists, it shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 192 of the Criminal Law.  
The amount of fundraising fraud shall be calculated based on the amount actually defrauded by the perpetrator, and the amount already returned before the case is discovered shall be deducted. Expenses paid by the perpetrator for carrying out the fundraising fraud, such as advertising fees, agency fees, handling fees, kickbacks, or bribes, gifts, etc., shall not be deducted. Interest paid by the perpetrator for carrying out the fundraising fraud shall be included in the fraudulent amount, except that if the principal has not been repaid, the interest may be offset against the principal.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　犯非法吸收公众存款罪，判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金的，处五万元以上一百万元以下罚金；判处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑的，并处十万元以上五百万元以下罚金；判处十年以上有期徒刑的，并处五十万元以上罚金。  
犯集资诈骗罪，判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑的，并处十万元以上五百万元以下罚金；判处七年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑的，并处五十万元以上罚金或者没收财产。

**Article 9** Where a person commits the crime of illegally absorbing public deposits and is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, a fine of not less than RMB 50,000 but not more than RMB 1 million shall be imposed concurrently or solely; where the sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 5 million shall be imposed concurrently; where the sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed concurrently.  
Where a person commits the crime of fundraising fraud and is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, a fine of not less than RMB 100,000 but not more than RMB 5 million shall be imposed concurrently; where the sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years or life imprisonment, a fine of not less than RMB 500,000 shall be imposed concurrently or confiscation of property shall be imposed.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　未经国家有关主管部门批准，向社会不特定对象发行、以转让股权等方式变相发行股票或者公司、企业债券，或者向特定对象发行、变相发行股票或者公司、企业债券累计超过200人的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-179)规定的“擅自发行股票或者公司、企业债券”。构成犯罪的，以擅自发行股票、公司、企业债券罪定罪处罚。

**Article 10** Where, without approval from the relevant competent state authority, stocks or corporate bonds are issued to unspecified targets in society, or disguisedly issued by means of equity transfer, etc., or stocks or corporate bonds are issued or disguisedly issued to specific targets cumulatively exceeding 200 persons, this shall be deemed as the "unauthorized issuance of stocks or corporate bonds" as stipulated in Article 179 of the Criminal Law. If a crime is constituted, the perpetrator shall be convicted and punished for the crime of unauthorized issuance of stocks, corporate bonds.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　违反国家规定，未经依法核准擅自发行基金份额募集基金，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。

**Article 11** Where, in violation of state regulations and without approval according to law, fund shares are issued without authorization to raise funds, and the circumstances are serious, the perpetrator shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operation in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　广告经营者、广告发布者违反国家规定，利用广告为非法集资活动相关的商品或者服务作虚假宣传，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第二百二十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-222)的规定，以虚假广告罪定罪处罚：  
（一）违法所得数额在10万元以上的；  
（二）造成严重危害后果或者恶劣社会影响的；  
（三）二年内利用广告作虚假宣传，受过行政处罚二次以上的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
明知他人从事欺诈发行证券，非法吸收公众存款，擅自发行股票、公司、企业债券，集资诈骗或者组织、领导传销活动等集资犯罪活动，为其提供广告等宣传的，以相关犯罪的共犯论处。

**Article 12** Where an advertising operator or advertising publisher, in violation of state regulations, uses advertisements to make false publicity for goods or services related to illegal fundraising activities, and any of the following circumstances exists, the perpetrator shall be convicted and punished for the crime of false advertising in accordance with Article 222 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The amount of illegal gains is RMB 100,000 or more;  
(2) Causing serious harmful consequences or adverse social impact;  
(3) Having been subject to administrative penalty twice or more for false publicity through advertisements within the preceding two years;  
(4) Other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a person knowingly provides advertising or other publicity for others engaged in fundraising crimes such as fraudulent issuance of securities, illegal absorption of public deposits, unauthorized issuance of stocks or corporate bonds, fundraising fraud, or organizing or leading pyramid schemes, the person shall be dealt with as an accomplice in the relevant crime.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　通过传销手段向社会公众非法吸收资金，构成非法吸收公众存款罪或者集资诈骗罪，同时又构成组织、领导传销活动罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 13** Where funds are illegally absorbed from the public by means of pyramid schemes, constituting the crime of illegally absorbing public deposits or the crime of fundraising fraud, and at the same time constituting the crime of organizing or leading pyramid scheme activities, the perpetrator shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision imposing the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　单位实施非法吸收公众存款、集资诈骗犯罪的，依照本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚。

**Article 14** Where a unit commits the crime of illegally absorbing public deposits or fundraising fraud, the unit shall be sentenced to a fine, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the standards for conviction and sentencing of the corresponding crime committed by a natural person as prescribed in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 15** Where any judicial interpretation previously issued is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
