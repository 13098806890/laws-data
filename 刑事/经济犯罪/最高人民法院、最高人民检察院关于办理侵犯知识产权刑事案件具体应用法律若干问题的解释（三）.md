# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（三）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2020〕10号  
**公布日期**：2020-08-31  
**生效日期**：2020-09-14

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（三）》已于2020年8月31日最高人民法院审判委员会第 1811次会议、2020年 8月 21 日最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第四十八次会议通过，现予公布，自2020年9 月14日起施行。 2020年9月12日 最高人民法院     最高人民检察院 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律 若干问题的解释（三）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“与其注册商标相同的商标”：  
（一）改变注册商标的字体、字母大小写或者文字横竖排列，与注册商标之间基本无差别的；  
（二）改变注册商标的文字、字母、数字等之间的间距，与注册商标之间基本无差别的；  
（三）改变注册商标颜色，不影响体现注册商标显著特征的；  
（四）在注册商标上仅增加商品通用名称、型号等缺乏显著特征要素，不影响体现注册商标显著特征的；  
（五）与立体注册商标的三维标志及平面要素基本无差别的；  
（六）其他与注册商标基本无差别、足以对公众产生误导的商标。

**Article 1** : Under any of the following circumstances, it may be determined as a trademark identical to its registered trademark as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) altering the font, case, or horizontal/vertical arrangement of the registered trademark without substantial difference from the registered trademark;  
(2) altering the spacing between characters, letters, or numbers of the registered trademark without substantial difference from the registered trademark;  
(3) altering the color of the registered trademark without affecting the embodiment of its distinctive features;  
(4) merely adding generic names, models, or other elements lacking distinctive features to the registered trademark without affecting the embodiment of its distinctive features;  
(5) having no substantial difference from the three-dimensional mark and planar elements of a three-dimensional registered trademark;  
(6) other trademarks that are substantially identical to the registered trademark and sufficient to mislead the public.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的作品、录音制品上以通常方式署名的自然人、法人或者非法人组织，应当推定为著作权人或者录音制作者，且该作品、录音制品上存在着相应权利，但有相反证明的除外。  
在涉案作品、录音制品种类众多且权利人分散的案件中，有证据证明涉案复制品系非法出版、复制发行，且出版者、复制发行者不能提供获得著作权人、录音制作者许可的相关证据材料的，可以认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“未经著作权人许可”“未经录音制作者许可”。但是，有证据证明权利人放弃权利、涉案作品的著作权或者录音制品的有关权利不受我国著作权法保护、权利保护期限已经届满的除外。

**Article 2** : A natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization that affixes its name in the usual manner on the works or sound recordings specified in Article 217 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be presumed to be the copyright owner or sound recording producer, and corresponding rights shall exist in such works or sound recordings, unless contrary evidence is provided.  
In cases where the types of works and sound recordings involved are numerous and the right holders are dispersed, if there is evidence proving that the reproductions involved are illegally published, reproduced, or distributed, and the publisher, reproducer, or distributor cannot provide evidence of permission obtained from the copyright owner or sound recording producer, it may be determined as "without permission of the copyright owner" or "without permission of the sound recording producer" as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China. However, this shall not apply where there is evidence proving that the right holder has waived the rights, the copyright of the works involved or the relevant rights of the sound recordings are not protected under the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, or the term of protection has expired.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　采取非法复制、未经授权或者超越授权使用计算机信息系统等方式窃取商业秘密的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)第一款第一项规定的“盗窃”。  
以贿赂、欺诈、电子侵入等方式获取权利人的商业秘密的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)第一款第一项规定的“其他不正当手段”。

**Article 3** : Obtaining trade secrets by means of illegal reproduction, unauthorized use, or use beyond authorization of computer information systems shall be determined as "theft" as provided in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 219 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Obtaining the right holder's trade secrets by means of bribery, fraud, electronic intrusion, or other such means shall be determined as "other illegitimate means" as provided in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 219 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　实施[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“给商业秘密的权利人造成重大损失”：  
（一）给商业秘密的权利人造成损失数额或者因侵犯商业秘密违法所得数额在三十万元以上的；  
（二）直接导致商业秘密的权利人因重大经营困难而破产、倒闭的；  
（三）造成商业秘密的权利人其他重大损失的。  
给商业秘密的权利人造成损失数额或者因侵犯商业秘密违法所得数额在二百五十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的“造成特别严重后果”。

**Article 4** : Where the conduct specified in Article 219 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China is committed, and under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "causing significant losses to the right holder of trade secrets":  
(1) the amount of losses caused to the right holder of trade secrets or the amount of illegal gains from infringing trade secrets is 300,000 RMB or more;  
(2) directly causing the right holder of trade secrets to go bankrupt or close due to significant operational difficulties;  
(3) causing other significant losses to the right holder of trade secrets.  
Where the amount of losses caused to the right holder of trade secrets or the amount of illegal gains from infringing trade secrets is 2.5 million RMB or more, it shall be determined as "causing particularly serious consequences" as provided in Article 219 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　实施[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的行为造成的损失数额或者违法所得数额，可以按照下列方式认定：  
（一）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密，尚未披露、使用或者允许他人使用的，损失数额可以根据该项商业秘密的合理许可使用费确定；  
（二）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密后，披露、使用或者允许他人使用的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成销售利润的损失确定，但该损失数额低于商业秘密合理许可使用费的，根据合理许可使用费确定；  
（三）违反约定、权利人有关保守商业秘密的要求，披露、使用或者允许他人使用其所掌握的商业秘密的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成销售利润的损失确定；  
（四）明知商业秘密是不正当手段获取或者是违反约定、权利人有关保守商业秘密的要求披露、使用、允许使用，仍获取、使用或者披露的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成销售利润的损失确定；  
（五）因侵犯商业秘密行为导致商业秘密已为公众所知悉或者灭失的，损失数额可以根据该项商业秘密的商业价值确定。商业秘密的商业价值，可以根据该项商业秘密的研究开发成本、实施该项商业秘密的收益综合确定；  
（六）因披露或者允许他人使用商业秘密而获得的财物或者其他财产性利益，应当认定为违法所得。  
前款第二项、第三项、第四项规定的权利人因被侵权造成销售利润的损失，可以根据权利人因被侵权造成销售量减少的总数乘以权利人每件产品的合理利润确定；销售量减少的总数无法确定的，可以根据侵权产品销售量乘以权利人每件产品的合理利润确定；权利人因被侵权造成销售量减少的总数和每件产品的合理利润均无法确定的，可以根据侵权产品销售量乘以每件侵权产品的合理利润确定。  
商业秘密系用于服务等其他经营活动的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权而减少的合理利润确定。商业秘密的权利人为减轻对商业运营、商业计划的损失或者重新恢复计算机信息系统安全、其他系统安全而支出的补救费用，应当计入给商业秘密的权利人造成的损失。

**Article 5** : The amount of losses or illegal gains caused by the conduct specified in Article 219 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China may be determined in the following manners:  
(1) Where trade secrets are obtained by illegitimate means but have not been disclosed, used, or permitted to be used by others, the amount of losses may be determined based on the reasonable license fee for such trade secrets;  
(2) Where after obtaining trade secrets by illegitimate means, they are disclosed, used, or permitted to be used by others, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of sales profit suffered by the right holder due to the infringement, but if such loss amount is lower than the reasonable license fee for the trade secret, it shall be determined based on the reasonable license fee;  
(3) Where a person discloses, uses, or permits others to use trade secrets in their possession in violation of an agreement or the right holder's requirements on confidentiality, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of sales profit suffered by the right holder due to the infringement;  
(4) Where a person, knowing that trade secrets were obtained by illegitimate means or disclosed, used, or permitted to be used in violation of an agreement or the right holder's confidentiality requirements, nevertheless obtains, uses, or discloses them, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of sales profit suffered by the right holder due to the infringement;  
(5) Where trade secrets have become known to the public or have been lost due to the infringement, the amount of losses may be determined based on the commercial value of such trade secrets. The commercial value of trade secrets may be determined comprehensively based on the research and development costs of such trade secrets and the profits from implementing them;  
(6) Property or other property interests obtained by disclosing or permitting others to use trade secrets shall be determined as illegal gains.  
The loss of sales profit suffered by the right holder due to the infringement as specified in items (2), (3), and (4) of the preceding paragraph may be determined by multiplying the total reduction in sales volume of the right holder caused by the infringement by the reasonable profit per product of the right holder; if the total reduction in sales volume cannot be determined, it may be determined by multiplying the sales volume of infringing products by the reasonable profit per product of the right holder; if neither the total reduction in sales volume nor the reasonable profit per product of the right holder can be determined, it may be determined by multiplying the sales volume of infringing products by the reasonable profit per product of the infringing products.  
If the trade secret is used for services or other business activities, the amount of losses may be determined based on the reduction in reasonable profits of the right holder caused by the infringement. The remedial expenses incurred by the right holder of trade secrets to mitigate losses to business operations or business plans or to restore the security of computer information systems or other systems shall be included in the losses caused to the right holder of trade secrets.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　在刑事诉讼程序中，当事人、辩护人、诉讼代理人或者案外人书面申请对有关商业秘密或者其他需要保密的商业信息的证据、材料采取保密措施的，应当根据案件情况采取组织诉讼参与人签署保密承诺书等必要的保密措施。  
违反前款有关保密措施的要求或者法律法规规定的保密义务的，依法承担相应责任。擅自披露、使用或者允许他人使用在刑事诉讼程序中接触、获取的商业秘密，符合[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 6** : In criminal proceedings, if a party, defender, litigation agent, or person not involved in the case applies in writing for taking confidentiality measures regarding evidence or materials involving trade secrets or other commercial information that needs to be kept confidential, necessary confidentiality measures such as requiring participants in the proceedings to sign a confidentiality commitment shall be taken based on the circumstances of the case.  
Anyone who violates the requirements of the preceding paragraph concerning confidentiality measures or the confidentiality obligations provided by laws and regulations shall bear corresponding liability according to law. Where anyone, without authorization, discloses, uses, or permits others to use trade secrets accessed or obtained during criminal proceedings, which meets the provisions of Article 219 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　除特殊情况外，假冒注册商标的商品、非法制造的注册商标标识、侵犯著作权的复制品、主要用于制造假冒注册商标的商品、注册商标标识或者侵权复制品的材料和工具，应当依法予以没收和销毁。  
上述物品需要作为民事、行政案件的证据使用的，经权利人申请，可以在民事、行政案件终结后或者采取取样、拍照等方式对证据固定后予以销毁。

**Article 7** : Except under special circumstances, goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks, illegally manufactured registered trademark labels, reproductions infringing copyright, and materials and tools mainly used for manufacturing goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks, registered trademark labels, or infringing reproductions shall be confiscated and destroyed according to law.  
If the above items need to be used as evidence in civil or administrative cases, upon application by the right holder, they may be destroyed after the conclusion of the civil or administrative case or after the evidence is fixed by means such as sampling or photographing.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　具有下列情形之一的，可以酌情从重处罚，一般不适用缓刑：  
（一）主要以侵犯知识产权为业的；  
（二）因侵犯知识产权被行政处罚后再次侵犯知识产权构成犯罪的；  
（三）在重大自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件期间,假冒抢险救灾、防疫物资等商品的注册商标的；  
（四）拒不交出违法所得的。

**Article 8** : Under any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment may be imposed at discretion, and probation shall generally not be applied:  
(1) primarily engaged in infringing intellectual property rights as a profession;  
(2) after having been subject to an administrative penalty for infringing intellectual property rights, commits a crime by infringing intellectual property rights again;  
(3) during major natural disasters, accidents, or public health events, counterfeits registered trademarks of goods such as emergency rescue, disaster relief, and epidemic prevention materials;  
(4) refuses to surrender illegal gains.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　具有下列情形之一的，可以酌情从轻处罚：  
（一）认罪认罚的；  
（二）取得权利人谅解的；  
（三）具有悔罪表现的；  
（四）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密后尚未披露、使用或者允许他人使用的。

**Article 9** : Under any of the following circumstances, a lighter punishment may be imposed at discretion:  
(1) admits guilt and accepts punishment;  
(2) obtains the understanding of the right holder;  
(3) shows repentance;  
(4) has not yet disclosed, used, or permitted others to use trade secrets obtained by illegitimate means.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对于侵犯知识产权犯罪的，应当综合考虑犯罪违法所得数额、非法经营数额、给权利人造成的损失数额、侵权假冒物品数量及社会危害性等情节，依法判处罚金。  
罚金数额一般在违法所得数额的一倍以上五倍以下确定。违法所得数额无法查清的，罚金数额一般按照非法经营数额的百分之五十以上一倍以下确定。违法所得数额和非法经营数额均无法查清，判处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者单处罚金的，一般在三万元以上一百万元以下确定罚金数额；判处三年以上有期徒刑的，一般在十五万元以上五百万元以下确定罚金数额。

**Article 10** : For crimes of infringing intellectual property rights, a fine shall be imposed according to law, taking into account such circumstances as the amount of illegal gains from the crime, the amount of illegal business operations, the amount of losses caused to the right holder, the quantity of infringing and counterfeit goods, and the degree of social harm.  
The amount of the fine shall generally be determined at one to five times the amount of illegal gains. If the amount of illegal gains cannot be ascertained, the amount of the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 50 percent and not more than 100 percent of the amount of illegal business operations. If neither the amount of illegal gains nor the amount of illegal business operations can be ascertained, and the sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or a fine only, the amount of the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 30,000 RMB and not more than 1 million RMB; if the sentence is fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years, the amount of the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 150,000 RMB and not more than 5 million RMB.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　本解释发布施行后，之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 11** : After this Interpretation comes into force, if any previously issued judicial interpretations and normative documents are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本解释自2020年9月14日起施行。

**Article 12** : This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 14, 2020.
