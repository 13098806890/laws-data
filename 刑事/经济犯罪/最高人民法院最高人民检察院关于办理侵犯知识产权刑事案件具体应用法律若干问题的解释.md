# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2004〕19号

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 未经注册商标所有人许可，在同一种商品上使用与其注册商标相同的商标，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“情节严重”，应当以假冒注册商标罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）非法经营数额在五万元以上或者违法所得数额在三万元以上的；  
（二）假冒两种以上注册商标，非法经营数额在三万元以上或者违法所得数额在二万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“情节特别严重”，应当以假冒注册商标罪判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：（一）非法经营数额在二十五万元以上或者违法所得数额在十五万元以上的；  
（二）假冒两种以上注册商标，非法经营数额在十五万元以上或者违法所得数额在十万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 1** Whoever, without permission of the owner of a registered trademark, uses a trademark identical to its registered trademark on the same kind of goods, in any of the following circumstances, falls under the 'relatively serious circumstances' as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and/or shall be fined, for the crime of counterfeiting a registered trademark:  
(1) the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 50,000 yuan or the amount of illegal gains is not less than 30,000 yuan;  
(2) counterfeiting two or more registered trademarks, with the amount of illegal business operations being not less than 30,000 yuan or the amount of illegal gains being not less than 20,000 yuan;  
(3) other circumstances that are relatively serious.  
In any of the following circumstances, it falls under the 'especially serious circumstances' as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall be fined: (1) the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 250,000 yuan or the amount of illegal gains is not less than 150,000 yuan;  
(2) counterfeiting two or more registered trademarks, with the amount of illegal business operations being not less than 150,000 yuan or the amount of illegal gains being not less than 100,000 yuan;  
(3) other circumstances that are especially serious.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 销售明知是假冒注册商标的商品，销售金额在五万元以上的，属于[刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)规定的“数额较大”，应当以销售假冒注册商标的商品罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金。  
销售金额在二十五万元以上的，属于[刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)规定的“数额巨大”，应当以销售假冒注册商标的商品罪判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。

**Article 2** Whoever sells goods that he knows are counterfeited registered trademarks, with the sales amount being not less than 50,000 yuan, falls under the 'relatively large amount' as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and/or shall be fined, for the crime of selling goods bearing counterfeited registered trademarks.  
Where the sales amount is not less than 250,000 yuan, it falls under the 'huge amount' as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall be fined.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 伪造、擅自制造他人注册商标标识或者销售伪造、擅自制造的注册商标标识，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百一十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-215)规定的“情节严重”，应当以非法制造、销售非法制造的注册商标标识罪判处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）伪造、擅自制造或者销售伪造、擅自制造的注册商标标识数量在二万件以上，或者非法经营数额在五万元以上，或者违法所得数额在三万元以上的；  
（二）伪造、擅自制造或者销售伪造、擅自制造两种以上注册商标标识数量在一万件以上，或者非法经营数额在三万元以上，或者违法所得数额在二万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百一十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-215)规定的“情节特别严重”，应当以非法制造、销售非法制造的注册商标标识罪判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）伪造、擅自制造或者销售伪造、擅自制造的注册商标标识数量在十万件以上，或者非法经营数额在二十五万元以上，或者违法所得数额在十五万元以上的；  
（二）伪造、擅自制造或者销售伪造、擅自制造两种以上注册商标标识数量在五万件以上，或者非法经营数额在十五万元以上，或者违法所得数额在十万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 3** Whoever forges or makes without authorization the marks of another person's registered trademark, or sells forged or unauthorized made marks of a registered trademark, in any of the following circumstances, falls under the 'relatively serious circumstances' as provided in Article 215 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance, and/or shall be fined, for the crime of illegally manufacturing or selling illegally manufactured marks of a registered trademark:  
(1) the number of forged or unauthorized made or sold forged or unauthorized made marks of a registered trademark is not less than 20,000 pieces, or the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 50,000 yuan, or the amount of illegal gains is not less than 30,000 yuan;  
(2) the number of forged or unauthorized made or sold forged or unauthorized made marks of two or more registered trademarks is not less than 10,000 pieces, or the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 30,000 yuan, or the amount of illegal gains is not less than 20,000 yuan;  
(3) other circumstances that are relatively serious.  
In any of the following circumstances, it falls under the 'especially serious circumstances' as provided in Article 215 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall be fined:  
(1) the number of forged or unauthorized made or sold forged or unauthorized made marks of a registered trademark is not less than 100,000 pieces, or the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 250,000 yuan, or the amount of illegal gains is not less than 150,000 yuan;  
(2) the number of forged or unauthorized made or sold forged or unauthorized made marks of two or more registered trademarks is not less than 50,000 pieces, or the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 150,000 yuan, or the amount of illegal gains is not less than 100,000 yuan;  
(3) other circumstances that are especially serious.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 假冒他人专利，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百一十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-216)规定的“情节严重”，应当以假冒专利罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）非法经营数额在二十万元以上或者违法所得数额在十万元以上的；  
（二）给专利权人造成直接经济损失五十万元以上的；  
（三）假冒两项以上他人专利，非法经营数额在十万元以上或者违法所得数额在五万元以上的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 4** Whoever counterfeits another person's patent, in any of the following circumstances, falls under the 'relatively serious circumstances' as provided in Article 216 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and/or shall be fined, for the crime of counterfeiting a patent:  
(1) the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 200,000 yuan or the amount of illegal gains is not less than 100,000 yuan;  
(2) causing direct economic losses of not less than 500,000 yuan to the patentee;  
(3) counterfeiting two or more patents of others, with the amount of illegal business operations being not less than 100,000 yuan or the amount of illegal gains being not less than 50,000 yuan;  
(4) other circumstances that are relatively serious.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 以营利为目的，实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)所列侵犯著作权行为之一，违法所得数额在三万元以上的，属于“违法所得数额较大”；具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他严重情节”，应当以侵犯著作权罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）非法经营数额在五万元以上的；  
（二）未经著作权人许可，复制发行其文字作品、音乐、电影、电视、录像作品、计算机软件及其他作品，复制品数量合计在一千张（份）以上的；  
（三）其他严重情节的情形。  
以营利为目的，实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)所列侵犯著作权行为之一，违法所得数额在十五万元以上的，属于“违法所得数额巨大”；具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他特别严重情节”，应当以侵犯著作权罪判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）非法经营数额在二十五万元以上的；  
（二）未经著作权人许可，复制发行其文字作品、音乐、电影、电视、录像作品、计算机软件及其他作品，复制品数量合计在五千张（份）以上的；  
（三）其他特别严重情节的情形。

**Article 5** Whoever, for the purpose of profit, commits any of the acts of copyright infringement listed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law, with the amount of illegal gains being not less than 30,000 yuan, falls under 'a relatively large amount of illegal gains'; in any of the following circumstances, falls under 'other serious circumstances', and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and/or shall be fined, for the crime of copyright infringement:  
(1) the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 50,000 yuan;  
(2) without permission of the copyright owner, reproducing and distributing their written works, music, movies, television, video works, computer software, and other works, with the total number of copies being not less than 1,000 copies (units);  
(3) other circumstances of serious nature.  
Whoever, for the purpose of profit, commits any of the acts of copyright infringement listed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law, with the amount of illegal gains being not less than 150,000 yuan, falls under 'a huge amount of illegal gains'; in any of the following circumstances, falls under 'other particularly serious circumstances', and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall be fined:  
(1) the amount of illegal business operations is not less than 250,000 yuan;  
(2) without permission of the copyright owner, reproducing and distributing their written works, music, movies, television, video works, computer software, and other works, with the total number of copies being not less than 5,000 copies (units);  
(3) other circumstances of particularly serious nature.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 以营利为目的，实施[刑法第二百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-218)规定的行为，违法所得数额在十万元以上的，属于“违法所得数额巨大”，应当以销售侵权复制品罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金。

**Article 6** Whoever, for the purpose of profit, commits the act prescribed in Article 218 of the Criminal Law, with the amount of illegal gains being not less than 100,000 yuan, falls under 'a huge amount of illegal gains', and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and/or shall be fined, for the crime of selling infringing reproductions.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 实施[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的行为之一，给商业秘密的权利人造成损失数额在五十万元以上的，属于“给商业秘密的权利人造成重大损失”，应当以侵犯商业秘密罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金。  
给商业秘密的权利人造成损失数额在二百五十万元以上的，属于[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的“造成特别严重后果”，应当以侵犯商业秘密罪判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。

**Article 7** Whoever commits any of the acts prescribed in Article 219 of the Criminal Law, causing losses to the owner of a trade secret in an amount of not less than 500,000 yuan, falls under 'causing significant losses to the owner of the trade secret', and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention, and/or shall be fined, for the crime of infringing trade secrets.  
Where the losses caused to the owner of the trade secret are not less than 2,500,000 yuan, it falls under 'causing particularly serious consequences' as provided in Article 219 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall be fined.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 [刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“相同的商标”，是指与被假冒的注册商标完全相同，或者与被假冒的注册商标在视觉上基本无差别、足以对公众产生误导的商标。  
[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“使用”，是指将注册商标或者假冒的注册商标用于商品、商品包装或者容器以及产品说明书、商品交易文书，或者将注册商标或者假冒的注册商标用于广告宣传、展览以及其他商业活动等行为。

**Article 8** The 'identical trademark' as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law refers to a trademark that is completely identical to the counterfeited registered trademark, or that is visually indistinguishable from the counterfeited registered trademark to the extent that it is sufficient to mislead the public.  
'Use' as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law refers to the acts of applying the registered trademark or the counterfeited registered trademark to goods, packaging or containers of goods, product specifications, transaction documents of goods, or using the registered trademark or the counterfeited registered trademark in advertising, exhibitions, and other commercial activities.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 [刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)规定的“销售金额”，是指销售假冒注册商标的商品后所得和应得的全部违法收入。具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为属于[刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)规定的“明知”：  
（一）知道自己销售的商品上的注册商标被涂改、调换或者覆盖的；  
（二）因销售假冒注册商标的商品受到过行政处罚或者承担过民事责任、又销售同一种假冒注册商标的商品的；  
（三）伪造、涂改商标注册人授权文件或者知道该文件被伪造、涂改的；  
（四）其他知道或者应当知道是假冒注册商标的商品的情形。

**Article 9** 'Sales amount' as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Law refers to all illegal income obtained and receivable from selling goods bearing counterfeited registered trademarks. In any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as 'knowingly' as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) knowing that the registered trademark on the goods they sell has been altered, replaced, or covered;  
(2) having been subjected to administrative penalties or having borne civil liability for selling goods bearing counterfeited registered trademarks, and then selling the same kind of goods bearing counterfeited registered trademarks again;  
(3) forging or altering the authorization document of the trademark registrant or knowing that such document has been forged or altered;  
(4) other circumstances where the person knows or should know that the goods are bearing counterfeited registered trademarks.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 实施下列行为之一的，属于[刑法第二百一十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-216)规定的“假冒他人专利”的行为：  
（一）未经许可，在其制造或者销售的产品、产品的包装上标注他人专利号的；  
（二）未经许可，在广告或者其他宣传材料中使用他人的专利号，使人将所涉及的技术误认为是他人专利技术的；  
（三）未经许可，在合同中使用他人的专利号，使人将合同涉及的技术误认为是他人专利技术的；  
（四）伪造或者变造他人的专利证书、专利文件或者专利申请文件的。

**Article 10** Any of the following acts falls under the act of 'counterfeiting another person's patent' as provided in Article 216 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) without permission, marking another person's patent number on the products that he manufactures or sells or on the packaging of such products;  
(2) without permission, using another person's patent number in advertisements or other promotional materials, causing people to mistake the technology involved for another person's patented technology;  
(3) without permission, using another person's patent number in a contract, causing people to mistake the technology involved in the contract for another person's patented technology;  
(4) forging or altering another person's patent certificate, patent documents, or patent application documents.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 以刊登收费广告等方式直接或者间接收取费用的情形，属于[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“以营利为目的”。  
[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“未经著作权人许可”，是指没有得到著作权人授权或者伪造、涂改著作权人授权许可文件或者超出授权许可范围的情形。  
通过信息网络向公众传播他人文字作品、音乐、电影、电视、录像作品、计算机软件及其他作品的行为，应当视为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“复制发行”。

**Article 11** The situation of directly or indirectly collecting fees by means such as publishing paid advertisements falls under the 'for the purpose of profit' as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law.  
'Without permission of the copyright owner' as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law refers to the situation where no authorization has been obtained from the copyright owner, or the authorization license document of the copyright owner has been forged or altered, or the scope of authorization has been exceeded.  
The act of communicating to the public through the information network another person's written works, music, movies, television, video works, computer software, and other works shall be deemed as 'reproduction and distribution' as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 本解释所称“非法经营数额”，是指行为人在实施侵犯知识产权行为过程中，制造、储存、运输、销售侵权产品的价值。已销售的侵权产品的价值，按照实际销售的价格计算。制造、储存、运输和未销售的侵权产品的价值，按照标价或者已经查清的侵权产品的实际销售平均价格计算。侵权产品没有标价或者无法查清其实际销售价格的，按照被侵权产品的市场中间价格计算。  
多次实施侵犯知识产权行为，未经行政处理或者刑事处罚的，非法经营数额、违法所得数额或者销售金额累计计算。  
[本解释第三条](#art-3)所规定的“件”，是指标有完整商标图样的一份标识。

**Article 12** For the purpose of this Interpretation, 'amount of illegal business operations' refers to the value of infringing products manufactured, stored, transported, or sold by the actor during the process of committing the intellectual property infringement. The value of already sold infringing products shall be calculated based on the actual selling price. The value of manufactured, stored, transported, and unsold infringing products shall be calculated based on the marked price or the average actual selling price of the infringing products that has been ascertained. Where the infringing products have no marked price or the actual selling price cannot be ascertained, the calculation shall be based on the average market price of the infringed products.  
Where intellectual property infringements are committed multiple times and have not been subject to administrative handling or criminal punishment, the amounts of illegal business operations, illegal gains, or sales amounts shall be calculated cumulatively.  
'Piece' as prescribed in Article 3 of this Interpretation refers to one unit of a mark bearing a complete trademark pattern.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的假冒注册商标犯罪，又销售该假冒注册商标的商品，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)的规定，以假冒注册商标罪定罪处罚。  
实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的假冒注册商标犯罪，又销售明知是他人的假冒注册商标的商品，构成犯罪的，应当实行数罪并罚。

**Article 13** Where a person commits the crime of counterfeiting a registered trademark as prescribed in Article 213 of the Criminal Law, and also sells the goods bearing such counterfeited registered trademark, if the act constitutes a crime, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 213 of the Criminal Law for the crime of counterfeiting a registered trademark.  
Where a person commits the crime of counterfeiting a registered trademark as prescribed in Article 213 of the Criminal Law, and also sells goods that he knows bear a counterfeited registered trademark of another person, if the act constitutes a crime, the punishments for several crimes shall be combined.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的侵犯著作权犯罪，又销售该侵权复制品，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)的规定，以侵犯著作权罪定罪处罚。  
实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的侵犯著作权犯罪，又销售明知是他人的侵权复制品，构成犯罪的，应当实行数罪并罚。

**Article 14** Where a person commits the crime of copyright infringement as prescribed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law, and also sells such infringing reproductions, if the act constitutes a crime, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 217 of the Criminal Law for the crime of copyright infringement.  
Where a person commits the crime of copyright infringement as prescribed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law, and also sells infringing reproductions that he knows belong to another person, if the act constitutes a crime, the punishments for several crimes shall be combined.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 单位实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)至第二百一十九条规定的行为，按照本解释规定的相应个人犯罪的定罪量刑标准的三倍定罪量刑。

**Article 15** Where a unit commits the acts prescribed in Articles 213 through 219 of the Criminal Law, the conviction and sentencing shall be based on three times the standards for conviction and sentencing of the corresponding individual crimes as prescribed in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 明知他人实施侵犯知识产权犯罪，而为其提供贷款、资金、账号、发票 、证 明 、许可证件 ，或者提供生产 、经营场所或者运输 、储存 、代理进出口等便利条件帮助的，以侵犯知识产权犯罪的共犯论处。

**Article 16** Whoever knowingly provides assistance to another person in committing an intellectual property crime by providing loans, funds, bank accounts, invoices, certificates, license documents, or by providing production or business premises, or transportation, storage, agency for import and export, or other convenient conditions, shall be punished as an accomplice in the intellectual property crime.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　以前发布的有关侵犯知识产权犯罪的司法解释，与本解释相抵触的，自本解释施行后不再适用。

**Article 17** Any previous judicial interpretations concerning crimes of intellectual property infringement that conflict with this Interpretation shall cease to apply after this Interpretation comes into force.
