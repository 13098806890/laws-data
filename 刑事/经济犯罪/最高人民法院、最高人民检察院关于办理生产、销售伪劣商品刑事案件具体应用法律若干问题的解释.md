# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理生产、销售伪劣商品刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕10号  
**公布日期**：2001-04-09  
**生效日期**：2001-04-10

> （2001年4月5日最高人民法院审判委员会第1168次会议、2001年3月30日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会第84次会议通过　2001年4月9日最高人民法院、最高人民检察院公告公布　自2001年4月10日起施行） 为依法惩治生产、销售伪劣商品犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　刑法第一百四十条规定的“在产品中掺杂、掺假”，是指在产品中掺入杂质或者异物，致使产品质量不符合国家法律、法规或者产品明示质量标准规定的质量要求，降低、失去应有使用性能的行为。 刑法第一百四十条规定的“以假充真”，是指以不具有某种使用性能的产品冒充具有该种使用性能的产品的行为。 刑法第一百四十条规定的“以次充好”，是指以低等级、低档次产品冒充高等级、高档次产品，或者以残次、废旧零配件组合、拼装后冒充正品或者新产品的行为。 刑法第一百四十条规定的“不合格产品”，是指不符合《中华人民共和国产品质量法》第二十六条第二款规定的质量要求的产品。 对本条规定的上述行为难以确定的，应当委托法律、行政法规规定的产品质量检验机构进行鉴定。 第二条　刑法第一百四十条、第一百四十九条规定的“销售金额”，是指生产者、销售者出售伪劣产品后所得和应得的全部违法收入。 伪劣产品尚未销售，货值金额达到刑法第一百四十条规定的销售金额3倍以上的，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪（未遂）定罪处罚。 货值金额以违法生产、销售的伪劣产品的标价计算；没有标价的，按照同类合格产品的市场中间价格计算。货值金额难以确定的，按照国家计划委员会、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部1997年4月22日联合发布的《扣押、追缴、没收物品估价管理办法》的规定，委托指定的估价机构确定。 多次实施生产、销售伪劣产品行为，未经处理的，伪劣产品的销售金额或者货值金额累计计算。 第三条　经省级以上药品监督管理部门设置或者确定的药品检验机构鉴定，生产、销售的假药具有下列情形之一的，应认定为刑法第一百四十一条规定的“足以严重危害人体健康”： （一）含有超标准的有毒有害物质的； （二）不含所标明的有效成分，可能贻误诊治的； （三）所标明的适应症或者功能主治超出规定范围，可能造成贻误诊治的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)规定的“在产品中掺杂、掺假”，是指在产品中掺入杂质或者异物，致使产品质量不符合国家法律、法规或者产品明示质量标准规定的质量要求，降低、失去应有使用性能的行为。  
[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)规定的“以假充真”，是指以不具有某种使用性能的产品冒充具有该种使用性能的产品的行为。  
[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)规定的“以次充好”，是指以低等级、低档次产品冒充高等级、高档次产品，或者以残次、废旧零配件组合、拼装后冒充正品或者新产品的行为。  
[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)规定的“不合格产品”，是指不符合[《中华人民共和国产品质量法》第二十六条](../../经济、税务与金融/其他经济法规/中华人民共和国产品质量法.md#art-26)第二款规定的质量要求的产品。  
对本条规定的上述行为难以确定的，应当委托法律、行政法规规定的产品质量检验机构进行鉴定。

**Article 1** : The term "adulteration or mixing impurities" as provided in Article 140 of the Criminal Law refers to the act of mixing impurities or foreign substances into a product, thereby causing the product's quality to fail to meet the quality requirements stipulated by national laws, regulations, or the product's specified quality standards, and reducing or losing its due performance.  
  
The term "passing off a fake as genuine" as provided in Article 140 of the Criminal Law refers to the act of passing off a product without a certain performance as a product with that performance.  
  
The term "passing off inferior products as good ones" as provided in Article 140 of the Criminal Law refers to the act of passing off low-grade or low-level products as high-grade or high-level products, or passing off combinations or assemblies of defective or waste parts as genuine or new products.  
  
The term "unqualified products" as provided in Article 140 of the Criminal Law refers to products that fail to meet the quality requirements stipulated in Article 26, Paragraph 2 of the Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China.  
  
If the above-mentioned acts specified in this Article are difficult to determine, an expert evaluation shall be entrusted to a product quality inspection institution established in accordance with laws and administrative regulations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)、第一百四十九条规定的“销售金额”，是指生产者、销售者出售伪劣产品后所得和应得的全部违法收入。  
伪劣产品尚未销售，货值金额达到[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)规定的销售金额3倍以上的，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪（未遂）定罪处罚。  
货值金额以违法生产、销售的伪劣产品的标价计算；没有标价的，按照同类合格产品的市场中间价格计算。货值金额难以确定的，按照国家计划委员会、最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部1997年4月22日联合发布的《扣押、追缴、没收物品估价管理办法》的规定，委托指定的估价机构确定。  
多次实施生产、销售伪劣产品行为，未经处理的，伪劣产品的销售金额或者货值金额累计计算。

**Article 2** : The term "sales amount" as provided in Articles 140 and 149 of the Criminal Law refers to all illegal income obtained or receivable by producers or sellers after selling fake or shoddy products.  
  
If fake or shoddy products have not yet been sold, but the value of the goods reaches three times or more of the sales amount provided in Article 140 of the Criminal Law, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling fake or shoddy products (attempted).  
  
The value of the goods shall be calculated based on the marked price of the illegally produced or sold fake or shoddy products; if there is no marked price, it shall be calculated based on the average market price of similar qualified products. If the value of the goods is difficult to determine, an authorized appraisal agency shall be entrusted to determine it in accordance with the provisions of the Measures for the Administration of Appraisal of Seized, Recovered, and Confiscated Items jointly issued by the State Planning Commission, the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the Ministry of Public Security on April 22, 1997.  
  
If multiple acts of producing or selling fake or shoddy products have been committed and have not been dealt with, the sales amount or value of the goods of the fake or shoddy products shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　经省级以上药品监督管理部门设置或者确定的药品检验机构鉴定，生产、销售的假药具有下列情形之一的，应认定为[刑法第一百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-141)规定的“足以严重危害人体健康”：  
（一）含有超标准的有毒有害物质的；  
（二）不含所标明的有效成分，可能贻误诊治的；  
（三）所标明的适应症或者功能主治超出规定范围，可能造成贻误诊治的；  
（四）缺乏所标明的急救必需的有效成分的。  
生产、销售的假药被使用后，造成轻伤、重伤或者其他严重后果的，应认定为“对人体健康造成严重危害”。  
生产、销售的假药被使用后，致人严重残疾、3人以上重伤、10人以上轻伤或者造成其他特别严重后果的，应认定为“对人体健康造成特别严重危害”。

**Article 3** : Where, upon expert evaluation by a drug inspection institution established or designated by the drug regulatory department at or above the provincial level, the counterfeit drug produced or sold falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "sufficient to seriously endanger human health" as provided in Article 141 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Contains toxic or harmful substances exceeding the standards;  
(2) Does not contain the indicated active ingredients and may delay diagnosis and treatment;  
(3) The indications or functions indicated exceed the prescribed scope and may cause delay in diagnosis and treatment;  
(4) Lacks the necessary active ingredients for emergency as indicated.  
  
Where the counterfeit drug produced or sold, after being used, causes minor injury, serious injury, or other serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "causing serious harm to human health."  
  
Where the counterfeit drug produced or sold, after being used, causes severe disability, serious injury to three or more persons, minor injury to ten or more persons, or other particularly serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "causing particularly serious harm to human health."

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　经省级以上卫生行政部门确定的机构鉴定，食品中含有可能导致严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾患的超标准的有害细菌或者其他污染物的，应认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾患”。  
生产、销售不符合卫生标准的食品被食用后，造成轻伤、重伤或者其他严重后果的，应认定为“对人体健康造成严重危害”。  
生产、销售不符合卫生标准的食品被食用后，致人死亡、严重残疾、3人以上重伤、10人以上轻伤或者造成其他特别严重后果的，应认定为“后果特别严重”。

**Article 4** : Where, upon expert evaluation by an institution established or designated by the health administration department at or above the provincial level, food contains harmful bacteria or other pollutants exceeding the standards that may cause serious food poisoning accidents or other serious foodborne diseases, it shall be deemed as "sufficient to cause serious food poisoning accidents or other serious foodborne diseases" as provided in Article 143 of the Criminal Law.  
  
Where food that does not meet hygiene standards, after being consumed, causes minor injury, serious injury, or other serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "causing serious harm to human health."  
  
Where food that does not meet hygiene standards, after being consumed, causes death, severe disability, serious injury to three or more persons, minor injury to ten or more persons, or other particularly serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "particularly serious consequences."

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　生产、销售的有毒、有害食品被食用后，造成轻伤、重伤或者其他严重后果的，应认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”。  
生产、销售的有毒、有害食品被食用后，致人严重残疾、3人以上重伤、10人以上轻伤或者造成其他特别严重后果的，应认定为“对人体健康造成特别严重危害”。

**Article 5** : Where toxic or harmful food produced or sold, after being consumed, causes minor injury, serious injury, or other serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "causing serious harm to human health" as provided in Article 144 of the Criminal Law.  
  
Where toxic or harmful food produced or sold, after being consumed, causes severe disability, serious injury to three or more persons, minor injury to ten or more persons, or other particularly serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "causing particularly serious harm to human health."

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　生产、销售不符合标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料，致人轻伤或者其他严重后果的，应认定为[刑法第一百四十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-145)规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”。  
生产、销售不符合标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料，造成感染病毒性肝炎等难以治愈的疾病、1人以上重伤、3人以上轻伤或者其他严重后果的，应认定为“后果特别严重”。  
生产、销售不符合标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料，致人死亡、严重残疾、感染艾滋病、3人以上重伤、10人以上轻伤或者造成其他特别严重后果的，应认定为“情节特别恶劣”。  
医疗机构或者个人，知道或者应当知道是不符合保障人体健康的国家标准、行业标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料而购买、使用，对人体健康造成严重危害的，以销售不符合标准的医用器材罪定罪处罚。  
没有国家标准、行业标准的医疗器械，注册产品标准可视为“保障人体健康的行业标准”。

**Article 6** : Where medical devices or medical hygiene materials that do not meet the standards are produced or sold, causing minor injury or other serious consequences to a person, it shall be deemed as "causing serious harm to human health" as provided in Article 145 of the Criminal Law.  
  
Where medical devices or medical hygiene materials that do not meet the standards are produced or sold, causing infection with diseases that are difficult to cure, such as viral hepatitis, serious injury to one or more persons, minor injury to three or more persons, or other serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "particularly serious consequences."  
  
Where medical devices or medical hygiene materials that do not meet the standards are produced or sold, causing death, severe disability, infection with AIDS, serious injury to three or more persons, minor injury to ten or more persons, or other particularly serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "particularly flagrant circumstances."  
  
Where a medical institution or individual, knowing or having reason to know that the medical devices or medical hygiene materials do not meet the national standards or industry standards for protecting human health, purchases and uses them, causing serious harm to human health, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of selling medical equipment that does not meet the standards.  
  
For medical devices for which there are no national standards or industry standards, the registered product standards may be regarded as "industry standards for protecting human health."

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[刑法第一百四十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-147)规定的生产、销售伪劣农药、兽药、化肥、种子罪中“使生产遭受较大损失”，一般以2万元为起点；“重大损失”，一般以10万元为起点；“特别重大损失”，一般以50万元为起点。

**Article 7** : In the crime of producing or selling fake or shoddy pesticides, veterinary drugs, chemical fertilizers, or seeds as provided in Article 147 of the Criminal Law, "causing relatively heavy losses to production" shall generally take 20,000 yuan as the starting point; "heavy losses" shall generally take 100,000 yuan as the starting point; and "especially heavy losses" shall generally take 500,000 yuan as the starting point.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　国家机关工作人员徇私舞弊，对生产、销售伪劣商品犯罪不履行法律规定的查处职责，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第四百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-414)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）放纵生产、销售假药或者有毒、有害食品犯罪行为的；  
（二）放纵依法可能判处2年有期徒刑以上刑罚的生产、销售伪劣商品犯罪行为的；  
（三）对3个以上有生产、销售伪劣商品犯罪行为的单位或者个人不履行追究职责的；  
（四）致使国家和人民利益遭受重大损失或者造成恶劣影响的。

**Article 8** : Where a state functionary, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, fails to perform the statutory duty of investigating and dealing with crimes of producing or selling fake or shoddy commodities, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall fall within the "serious circumstances" provided in Article 414 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Conniving at criminal acts of producing or selling counterfeit drugs or toxic or harmful food;  
(2) Conniving at criminal acts of producing or selling fake or shoddy commodities that, according to law, may be punishable by fixed-term imprisonment of not less than two years;  
(3) Failing to perform the duty of investigation against three or more entities or individuals who have committed crimes of producing or selling fake or shoddy commodities;  
(4) Causing substantial losses to the interests of the state or the people, or causing adverse effects.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　知道或者应当知道他人实施生产、销售伪劣商品犯罪，而为其提供贷款、资金、账号、发票、证明、许可证件，或者提供生产、经营场所或者运输、仓储、保管、邮寄等便利条件，或者提供制假生产技术的，以生产、销售伪劣商品犯罪的共犯论处。

**Article 9** : If a person, knowing or having reason to know that another person is committing a crime of producing or selling fake or shoddy commodities, provides the other person with loans, funds, accounts, invoices, certificates, licenses, or provides production or business premises, or facilitates conditions such as transportation, warehousing, storage, or postal services, or provides production technology for manufacturing counterfeit goods, the person shall be dealt with as an accomplice to the crime of producing or selling fake or shoddy commodities.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　实施生产、销售伪劣商品犯罪，同时构成侵犯知识产权、非法经营等其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 10** : Where the commission of a crime of producing or selling fake or shoddy commodities also constitutes other crimes such as infringement of intellectual property rights or illegal business operations, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision that imposes a heavier penalty.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　实施[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)至第一百四十八条规定的犯罪，又以暴力、威胁方法抗拒查处，构成其他犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。

**Article 11** : Where a person commits a crime as provided in Articles 140 to 148 of the Criminal Law, and also resists investigation and punishment by means of violence or threats, thereby constituting another crime, the person shall be punished in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　国家机关工作人员参与生产、销售伪劣商品犯罪的，从重处罚。

**Article 12** : Where a state functionary participates in a crime of producing or selling fake or shoddy commodities, the person shall be given a severe punishment.
