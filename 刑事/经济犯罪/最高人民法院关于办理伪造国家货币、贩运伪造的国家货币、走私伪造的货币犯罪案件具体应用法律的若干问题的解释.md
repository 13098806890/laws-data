# 最高人民法院关于办理伪造国家货币、贩运伪造的国家货币、走私伪造的货币犯罪案件具体应用法律的若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1994〕20号  
**公布日期**：1994-09-08

---

### 正文

一、关于伪造国家货币罪的认定  
仿照国家货币的图案、形状、色彩等，使用各种方法，非法制造假货币，冒充国家货币的行为，构成《中华人民共和国刑法》第一百二十二条规定的伪造国家货币罪。  
对国家货币采用剪贴、挖补、揭层、涂改等方法加工处理，使国家货币改变形态、升值的变造国家货币行为，以伪造国家货币罪论处。

**Article None** I. Identification of the Crime of Counterfeiting National Currency  
Any act of illegally manufacturing counterfeit currency by imitating the patterns, shapes, colors, etc., of national currency through various methods, and passing it off as national currency, shall constitute the crime of counterfeiting national currency as prescribed in Article 122 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Any act of altering national currency by processing it through methods such as cutting, pasting, gouging, layering, or painting, thereby changing its form and increasing its value, shall be punished as the crime of counterfeiting national currency.

二、关于贩运伪造的国家货币罪的认定  
明知是伪造的国家货币，而予以买卖、携带或者运输的行为，构成《中华人民共和国刑法》第一百二十二条规定的贩运伪造的国家货币罪。

**Article None** II. Identification of the Crime of Trafficking in Counterfeit National Currency  
Any act of knowingly selling, carrying, or transporting counterfeit national currency shall constitute the crime of trafficking in counterfeit national currency as prescribed in Article 122 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

三、关于走私伪造的货币犯罪行为的认定  
明知是伪造、变造的货币，而运输、携带、邮寄进出国（边）境的行为，构成全国人大常委会《关于惩治走私罪的补充规定》第一条规定的走私伪造的货币的行为，按走私罪定罪处罚。  
直接向走私犯罪分子收买其走私的伪造的货币的，以走私罪论处。

**Article None** III. Identification of the Criminal Act of Smuggling Counterfeit Currency  
Any act of knowingly transporting, carrying, or sending counterfeit or altered currency into or out of the national border (frontier) shall constitute the act of smuggling counterfeit currency as prescribed in Article 1 of the Supplementary Provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress Concerning the Punishment of Smuggling Crimes, and shall be convicted and punished as the crime of smuggling.  
Any person who directly purchases from a smuggler the counterfeit currency smuggled by such smuggler shall be punished as the crime of smuggling.

四、关于伪造国家货币罪、贩运伪造的国家货币罪的处罚  
伪造国家货币总面值在500元以上不满15000元或者币量50张以上不满1500张的，依照刑法第一百二十二条第一款的规定处罚。  
贩运伪造的国家货币总面值在1000元以上不满30000元或者币量100张以上不满3000张的，依照刑法第一百二十二条第一款的规定处罚。  
因伪造或者贩运伪造的国家货币受过刑事处罚的，利用职务便利伪造或者贩运伪造的国家货币的，或者具有其他严重情节的，伪造或者贩运伪造的国家货币总面值或币量虽不足本条第一款或者第二款规定的起点数量，也应当依照刑法第一百二十二条第一款的规定处罚。  
行为人具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”，依照刑法第一百二十二条第二款的规定处罚：  
（一）伪造国家货币总面值15000元以上或者币量1500张以上的，或者贩运伪造的国家货币总面值30000元以上或者币量3000张以上的；  
（二）伪造国家货币或者贩运伪造的国家货币犯罪集团的首要分子；  
（三）武装贩运伪造的国家货币的主犯；  
（四）以暴力抗拒检查、拘留、逮捕，情节严重的；  
（五）以机械印刷方法伪造国家货币的；  
（六）金融、财会等人员利用工作之便伪造国家货币、贩运伪造的国家货币总面值10000元以上或者币量1000张以上的；  
（七）伪造国家货币或者贩运伪造的国家货币并投放市场流通，总面值1000元以上或者币量100张以上的；  
（八）因伪造或者贩运伪造的国家货币受过刑事处罚后，又实施伪造或者贩运伪造的国家货币的行为，伪造的货币总面值或币量达到本条第一款或者第二款规定的；  
（九）具有其他特别严重情节的。  
伪造国家货币并贩运该宗伪造的国家货币的，定伪造国家货币罪，从重处罚。

**Article None** IV. Punishment for the Crime of Counterfeiting National Currency and the Crime of Trafficking in Counterfeit National Currency  
Where the total face value of counterfeit national currency counterfeited is 500 yuan or more but less than 15,000 yuan, or the quantity is 50 pieces or more but less than 1,500 pieces, the offender shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 1 of Article 122 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the total face value of counterfeit national currency trafficked is 1,000 yuan or more but less than 30,000 yuan, or the quantity is 100 pieces or more but less than 3,000 pieces, the offender shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 1 of Article 122 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the offender has previously received criminal punishment for counterfeiting or trafficking in counterfeit national currency, or has taken advantage of his or her position to counterfeit or traffic in counterfeit national currency, or where there are other serious circumstances, even if the total face value or quantity of counterfeit national currency counterfeited or trafficked is less than the threshold specified in Paragraph 1 or Paragraph 2 of this Article, the offender shall still be punished in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 1 of Article 122 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the actor falls under any of the following circumstances, the circumstances shall be considered as "especially serious" and the offender shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 122 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The total face value of counterfeit national currency counterfeited is 15,000 yuan or more, or the quantity is 1,500 pieces or more; or the total face value of counterfeit national currency trafficked is 30,000 yuan or more, or the quantity is 3,000 pieces or more;  
(2) Being the ringleader of a criminal group that counterfeits national currency or traffics in counterfeit national currency;  
(3) Being a principal offender who traffics in counterfeit national currency with armed forces;  
(4) Using violence to resist inspection, detention, or arrest, where the circumstances are serious;  
(5) Counterfeiting national currency by mechanical printing methods;  
(6) Where personnel in finance, accounting, or other fields take advantage of their work to counterfeit national currency or traffic in counterfeit national currency, with a total face value of 10,000 yuan or more or a quantity of 1,000 pieces or more;  
(7) Counterfeiting national currency or trafficking in counterfeit national currency and putting it into market circulation, with a total face value of 1,000 yuan or more or a quantity of 100 pieces or more;  
(8) After having received criminal punishment for counterfeiting or trafficking in counterfeit national currency, again committing the act of counterfeiting or trafficking in counterfeit national currency, where the total face value or quantity of counterfeit currency reaches the amount specified in Paragraph 1 or Paragraph 2 of this Article;  
(9) Other especially serious circumstances.  
Where a person counterfeits national currency and also traffics in the same batch of counterfeit national currency, the offense shall be determined as the crime of counterfeiting national currency, and a severe punishment shall be imposed.

五、关于走私伪造的货币的犯罪的处罚  
走私伪造货币的总面值（折合人民币）500元以上不足5000元，或者币量50张以上不足500张的，属子情节较轻，依照全国人大常委会《关于惩治走私罪的补充规是》第一条的规定处七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
走私伪造货币具有下列情节之一的，依照全国人大常委会《关于惩治走私罪的补充规定》第一条的规定处七年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）走私伪造的货币总面值（折合人民币）5000元以上不足50000元，或者币量500张以上不足5000张的；  
（二）走私伪造的货币达到本条第一款规定的数量，并具有暴力抗拒检查、拘留、逮捕情节的；  
（三）走私伪造的货币，并投放市场流通总面值（折合人民币）1000元以上不足10000元或者币量100张以上不足1000张的；  
（四）具有其他严重情节的。  
走私伪造货币具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”，依照全国人大常委会《关于惩治走私罪的补充规定》第一条的规定处无期徒刑或者死刑，并处没收财产：  
（一）走私伪造的货币总面值（折合人民币）50000元以上或者币量5000张以上的；  
（二）走私伪造的货币构成犯罪，并以暴力抗拒检查、拘留、逮捕造成严重后果的；  
（三）冒充司法、海关、工商管理等国家机关工作人员走私伪造的货币总面值（折合人民币）10000元以上或者币量1000张以上的；  
（四）走私伪造的货币，并投放市场流通总面值（折合人民币）10000元以上或者币量1000张以上的；  
（五）与境外不法分子勾结走私伪造的货币情节严重的；  
（六）具有其他特别严重情节的。  
行为人走私伪造的货币后，又在境内贩运该宗走私的伪造的货币，定走私罪，从重处罚。  
走私伪造的外国货币的面值，按查获犯罪当日国家外汇管理局公布的同种货币兑换人民币市场汇价折算。

**Article None** V. Punishment for the Crime of Smuggling Counterfeit Currency  
Where the total face value of smuggled counterfeit currency (converted into Renminbi) is 500 yuan or more but less than 5,000 yuan, or the quantity is 50 pieces or more but less than 500 pieces, the circumstances shall be considered as relatively minor, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than seven years and shall also be fined in accordance with the provisions of Article 1 of the Supplementary Provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress Concerning the Punishment of Smuggling Crimes.  
Where any of the following circumstances exist in the smuggling of counterfeit currency, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years and shall also be fined or have property confiscated in accordance with the provisions of Article 1 of the Supplementary Provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress Concerning the Punishment of Smuggling Crimes:  
(1) The total face value of smuggled counterfeit currency (converted into Renminbi) is 5,000 yuan or more but less than 50,000 yuan, or the quantity is 500 pieces or more but less than 5,000 pieces;  
(2) The quantity of smuggled counterfeit currency reaches the amount specified in Paragraph 1 of this Article, and there are circumstances of using violence to resist inspection, detention, or arrest;  
(3) Smuggling counterfeit currency and putting it into market circulation, with a total face value (converted into Renminbi) of 1,000 yuan or more but less than 10,000 yuan, or a quantity of 100 pieces or more but less than 1,000 pieces;  
(4) Other serious circumstances.  
Where any of the following circumstances exist in the smuggling of counterfeit currency, the circumstances shall be considered as "especially serious", and the offender shall be sentenced to life imprisonment or the death penalty, and shall also have property confiscated in accordance with the provisions of Article 1 of the Supplementary Provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress Concerning the Punishment of Smuggling Crimes:  
(1) The total face value of smuggled counterfeit currency (converted into Renminbi) is 50,000 yuan or more, or the quantity is 5,000 pieces or more;  
(2) The smuggling of counterfeit currency constitutes a crime, and violence is used to resist inspection, detention, or arrest, resulting in serious consequences;  
(3) Impersonating state organ personnel such as judicial officers, customs officers, or industrial and commercial administration officers to smuggle counterfeit currency, with a total face value (converted into Renminbi) of 10,000 yuan or more, or a quantity of 1,000 pieces or more;  
(4) Smuggling counterfeit currency and putting it into market circulation, with a total face value (converted into Renminbi) of 10,000 yuan or more, or a quantity of 1,000 pieces or more;  
(5) Colluding with foreign lawless elements to smuggle counterfeit currency, where the circumstances are serious;  
(6) Other especially serious circumstances.  
Where an actor smuggles counterfeit currency and subsequently traffics in the same batch of smuggled counterfeit currency within the country, the offense shall be determined as the crime of smuggling, and a severe punishment shall be imposed.  
The face value of smuggled counterfeit foreign currency shall be converted according to the market exchange rate of the same currency against Renminbi announced by the State Administration of Exchange Control on the date the crime is discovered.

六、关于故意使用伪造货币的处罚  
伪造国家货币、贩运伪造的国家货币或者走私伪造的货币后又在市场上使用该宗伪造的货币的，分别以伪造国家货币罪、贩运伪造的国家货币罪或者走私罪定罪，从重处罚。  
收取伪造的货币后，故意在市场上使用，数量较大构成犯罪的，以诈骗罪论处。  
1994年9月8日

**Article None** VI. Punishment for Knowingly Using Counterfeit Currency  
Where a person counterfeits national currency, traffics in counterfeit national currency, or smuggles counterfeit currency and subsequently uses the same batch of counterfeit currency in the market, he or she shall be convicted of the crime of counterfeiting national currency, the crime of trafficking in counterfeit national currency, or the crime of smuggling, respectively, and shall be subject to a severe punishment.  
Where a person knowingly uses counterfeit currency in the market after receiving it, and the amount is large enough to constitute a crime, the offense shall be punished as the crime of fraud.  
September 8, 1994
