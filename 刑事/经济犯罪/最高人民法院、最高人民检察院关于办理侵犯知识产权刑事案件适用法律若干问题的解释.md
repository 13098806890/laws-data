# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2025〕5号  
**公布日期**：2025-04-07  
**生效日期**：2025-04-26

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2025年4月7日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1947次会议、2025年4月11日由最高人民检察院第十四届检察委员会第五十一次会议通过，现予公布，自2025年4月26日起施行。 2025年4月23日 最高人民法院　最高人民检察院 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2025〕5号 为依法惩治侵犯知识产权犯罪，维护社会主义市场经济秩序，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等法律的有关规定，结合司法实践，现就办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　未经注册商标所有人许可，在同一种商品、服务上使用与其注册商标相同的商标，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百一十三条规定的“同一种商品、服务”： （一）行为人实际生产销售的商品名称、实际提供的服务名称与权利人注册商标核定使用的商品、服务名称相同的； （二）商品名称不同，但在功能、用途、主要原料、消费对象、销售渠道等方面相同或者基本相同，相关公众一般认为是同种商品的； （三）服务名称不同，但在服务的目的、内容、方式、对象、场所等方面相同或者基本相同，相关公众一般认为是同种服务的。 认定“同一种商品、服务”，应当在权利人注册商标核定使用的商品、服务和行为人实际生产销售的商品、实际提供的服务之间进行比较。 第二条　与被假冒的注册商标完全相同，或者与被假冒的注册商标基本无差别、足以对相关公众产生误导的商标，应当认定为刑法第二百一十三条规定的“与其注册商标相同的商标”。具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为与被假冒的注册商标基本无差别、足以对相关公众产生误导的商标： （一）改变注册商标的字体、字母大小写或者文字横竖排列，与注册商标基本无差别的； （二）改变注册商标的文字、字母、数字等之间的间距，与注册商标基本无差别的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　未经注册商标所有人许可，在同一种商品、服务上使用与其注册商标相同的商标，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“同一种商品、服务”：  
（一）行为人实际生产销售的商品名称、实际提供的服务名称与权利人注册商标核定使用的商品、服务名称相同的；  
（二）商品名称不同，但在功能、用途、主要原料、消费对象、销售渠道等方面相同或者基本相同，相关公众一般认为是同种商品的；  
（三）服务名称不同，但在服务的目的、内容、方式、对象、场所等方面相同或者基本相同，相关公众一般认为是同种服务的。  
认定“同一种商品、服务”，应当在权利人注册商标核定使用的商品、服务和行为人实际生产销售的商品、实际提供的服务之间进行比较。

**Article 1** Where, without permission of the owner of a registered trademark, a person uses a trademark that is identical to the registered trademark on the same type of goods or services, the following circumstances shall be deemed as the "same type of goods or services" as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the name of the goods actually produced or sold by the actor or the name of the services actually provided by the actor is identical to the name of the goods or services approved for use with the registered trademark of the right holder;  
(2) the names of the goods are different, but the goods are identical or substantially identical in terms of function, purpose, main raw materials, consumers, sales channels, etc., and the relevant public generally considers them to be the same type of goods; or  
(3) the names of the services are different, but the services are identical or substantially identical in terms of purpose, content, method, object, place, etc., and the relevant public generally considers them to be the same type of services.  
To determine the "same type of goods or services," a comparison shall be made between the goods or services approved for use with the registered trademark of the right holder and the goods actually produced or sold by the actor or the services actually provided by the actor.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　与被假冒的注册商标完全相同，或者与被假冒的注册商标基本无差别、足以对相关公众产生误导的商标，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“与其注册商标相同的商标”。具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为与被假冒的注册商标基本无差别、足以对相关公众产生误导的商标：  
（一）改变注册商标的字体、字母大小写或者文字横竖排列，与注册商标基本无差别的；  
（二）改变注册商标的文字、字母、数字等之间的间距，与注册商标基本无差别的；  
（三）改变注册商标颜色，不影响体现注册商标显著特征的；  
（四）在注册商标上仅增加商品通用名称、型号等缺乏显著特征要素，不影响体现注册商标显著特征的；  
（五）与立体注册商标的三维标志及平面要素基本无差别的；  
（六）其他与注册商标基本无差别、足以对相关公众产生误导的。

**Article 2** A trademark that is identical to the counterfeit registered trademark, or that is substantially indistinguishable from the counterfeit registered trademark and sufficient to mislead the relevant public, shall be deemed as the "trademark identical to its registered trademark" as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law. The following circumstances shall be deemed as a trademark that is substantially indistinguishable from the counterfeit registered trademark and sufficient to mislead the relevant public:  
(1) changing the font, letter case, or horizontal or vertical arrangement of the registered trademark, resulting in no substantial difference from the registered trademark;  
(2) changing the spacing between characters, letters, numbers, etc., of the registered trademark, resulting in no substantial difference from the registered trademark;  
(3) changing the color of the registered trademark, which does not affect the representation of its distinctive features;  
(4) merely adding elements lacking distinctive features, such as generic names or models of goods, to the registered trademark, which does not affect the representation of its distinctive features;  
(5) being substantially indistinguishable from the three-dimensional mark and planar elements of a three-dimensional registered trademark; or  
(6) other circumstances where the trademark is substantially indistinguishable from the registered trademark and sufficient to mislead the relevant public.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　未经注册商标所有人许可，在同一种商品上使用与其注册商标相同的商标，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）违法所得数额在三万元以上或者非法经营数额在五万元以上的；  
（二）假冒两种以上注册商标，违法所得数额在二万元以上或者非法经营数额在三万元以上的；  
（三）二年内因实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)至第二百一十五条规定的行为受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚后再次实施，违法所得数额在二万元以上或者非法经营数额在三万元以上的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
未经注册商标所有人许可，在同一种服务上使用与其注册商标相同的商标，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）违法所得数额在五万元以上的；  
（二）假冒两种以上注册商标，违法所得数额在三万元以上的；  
（三）二年内因实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)至第二百一十五条规定的行为受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚后再次实施，违法所得数额在三万元以上的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
既假冒商品注册商标，又假冒服务注册商标，假冒商品注册商标的违法所得数额不足本条第一款规定标准，但与假冒服务注册商标的违法所得数额合计达到本条第二款规定标准的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“情节严重”。  
违法所得数额、非法经营数额达到本条前三款相应规定标准十倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的“情节特别严重”。

**Article 3** Where, without permission of the owner of a registered trademark, a person uses a trademark that is identical to the registered trademark on the same type of goods, the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of illegal gains is 30,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover is 50,000 yuan or more;  
(2) counterfeiting two or more registered trademarks, with the amount of illegal gains being 20,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover being 30,000 yuan or more;  
(3) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for committing acts specified in Articles 213 through 215 of the Criminal Law within the past two years, and committing the act again, with the amount of illegal gains being 20,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover being 30,000 yuan or more; or  
(4) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where, without permission of the owner of a registered trademark, a person uses a trademark that is identical to the registered trademark on the same type of services, the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of illegal gains is 50,000 yuan or more;  
(2) counterfeiting two or more registered trademarks, with the amount of illegal gains being 30,000 yuan or more;  
(3) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for committing acts specified in Articles 213 through 215 of the Criminal Law within the past two years, and committing the act again, with the amount of illegal gains being 30,000 yuan or more; or  
(4) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a person counterfeits both registered trademarks of goods and registered trademarks of services, and the amount of illegal gains from counterfeiting registered trademarks of goods does not reach the standard specified in paragraph 1 of this Article but, when combined with the amount of illegal gains from counterfeiting registered trademarks of services, reaches the standard specified in paragraph 2 of this Article, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the amount of illegal gains or the amount of illegal business turnover reaches ten times or more of the corresponding standards specified in the preceding three paragraphs of this Article, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　销售假冒注册商标的商品，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)规定的“明知”，但有证据证明确实不知道的除外：  
（一）知道自己销售的商品上的注册商标被涂改、调换或者覆盖的；  
（二）伪造、涂改商标注册人授权文件或者知道该文件被伪造、涂改的；  
（三）因销售假冒注册商标的商品受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚，又销售同一种假冒注册商标的商品的；  
（四）无正当理由以明显低于市场价格进货或者销售的；  
（五）被行政执法机关、司法机关发现销售假冒注册商标的商品后，转移、销毁侵权商品、会计凭证等证据或者提供虚假证明的；  
（六）其他可以认定为明知是假冒注册商标的商品的情形。

**Article 4** Where a person sells goods bearing a counterfeit registered trademark, the following circumstances may be deemed as "knowingly" as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Law, unless there is evidence proving that the person genuinely did not know:  
(1) the person knows that the registered trademark on the goods he/she sells has been altered, replaced, or covered;  
(2) the person forges or alters the authorization documents of the trademark registrant, or knows that such documents have been forged or altered;  
(3) the person has received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for selling goods bearing a counterfeit registered trademark, and subsequently sells the same type of goods bearing a counterfeit registered trademark;  
(4) the person purchases or sells goods at a price significantly lower than the market price without justifiable reasons;  
(5) after being discovered by administrative law enforcement authorities or judicial authorities for selling goods bearing a counterfeit registered trademark, the person transfers, destroys evidence such as infringing goods or accounting vouchers, or provides false evidence; or  
(6) other circumstances that may be deemed as knowingly selling goods bearing a counterfeit registered trademark.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　销售明知是假冒注册商标的商品，违法所得数额在三万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)规定的“违法所得数额较大”；具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）销售金额在五万元以上的；  
（二）二年内因实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)至第二百一十五条规定的行为受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚后再次实施，违法所得数额在二万元以上或者销售金额在三万元以上的；  
（三）假冒注册商标的商品尚未销售，货值金额达到本款前两项规定的销售金额标准三倍以上，或者已销售商品的销售金额不足本款前两项标准，但与尚未销售商品的货值金额合计达到本款前两项规定的销售金额标准三倍以上的。  
违法所得数额、销售金额、货值金额或者销售金额与货值金额合计达到本条前款相应规定标准十倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)规定的“违法所得数额巨大或者有其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 5** Where a person sells goods that he/she knows bear a counterfeit registered trademark, and the amount of illegal gains is 30,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "a relatively large amount of illegal gains" as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Law; the following circumstances shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the sales amount is 50,000 yuan or more;  
(2) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for committing acts specified in Articles 213 through 215 of the Criminal Law within the past two years, and committing the act again, with the amount of illegal gains being 20,000 yuan or more, or the sales amount being 30,000 yuan or more;  
(3) where the goods bearing a counterfeit registered trademark have not yet been sold, the value of the goods reaches three times or more of the sales amount standards specified in subparagraphs (1) and (2) of this paragraph, or where the sales amount of the goods already sold does not reach the standards in the preceding two subparagraphs but, when combined with the value of the unsold goods, reaches three times or more of the sales amount standards specified in the preceding two subparagraphs.  
Where the amount of illegal gains, the sales amount, the value of goods, or the combined total of the sales amount and the value of goods reaches ten times or more of the corresponding standards specified in the preceding paragraph of this Article, it shall be deemed as "a huge amount of illegal gains or other especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 214 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　伪造、擅自制造他人注册商标标识或者销售伪造、擅自制造的注册商标标识，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-215)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）标识数量在一万件以上，或者违法所得数额在二万元以上，或者非法经营数额在三万元以上的；  
（二）伪造、擅自制造或者销售伪造、擅自制造两种以上注册商标标识，标识数量在五千件以上，或者违法所得数额在一万元以上，或者非法经营数额在二万元以上的；  
（三）二年内因实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)至第二百一十五条规定的行为受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚后再次实施，标识数量在五千件以上，或者违法所得数额在一万元以上，或者非法经营数额在二万元以上的；  
（四）销售他人非法制造的注册商标标识，尚未销售的标识数量达到本款前三项规定标准三倍以上，或者已销售的标识数量不足本款前三项标准，但与尚未销售的标识数量合计达到本款前三项规定标准三倍以上的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
标识数量、违法所得数额、非法经营数额达到本条前款相应规定标准五倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-215)规定的“情节特别严重”。

**Article 6** Where a person forges or manufactures without authorization another person's registered trademark labels, or sells forged or unauthorized manufactured registered trademark labels, the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 215 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the number of labels is 10,000 or more, or the amount of illegal gains is 20,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover is 30,000 yuan or more;  
(2) forging, manufacturing without authorization, or selling forged or unauthorized manufactured registered trademark labels of two or more types, with the number of labels being 5,000 or more, or the amount of illegal gains being 10,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover being 20,000 yuan or more;  
(3) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for committing acts specified in Articles 213 through 215 of the Criminal Law within the past two years, and committing the act again, with the number of labels being 5,000 or more, or the amount of illegal gains being 10,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover being 20,000 yuan or more;  
(4) selling another person's illegally manufactured registered trademark labels, where the number of unsold labels reaches three times or more of the standards specified in subparagraphs (1) through (3) of this paragraph, or where the number of labels already sold does not reach the standards in the preceding three subparagraphs but, when combined with the number of unsold labels, reaches three times or more of the standards specified in the preceding three subparagraphs; or  
(5) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where the number of labels, the amount of illegal gains, or the amount of illegal business turnover reaches five times or more of the corresponding standards specified in the preceding paragraph of this Article, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 215 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本解释所称“两种以上注册商标”，是指识别商品、服务不同来源的两种以上注册商标。虽然注册商标不同，但在同一种商品、服务上使用，均指向同一商品、服务来源的，不应当认定为“两种以上注册商标”。  
本解释所称注册商标标识“件”，一般是指标有完整商标图样的一份标识。对于在一件有形载体上印制数个标识图样，该标识图样不能脱离有形载体单独使用的，应当认定为一件标识。

**Article 7** The term "two or more registered trademarks" as used in this Interpretation refers to two or more registered trademarks that identify different sources of goods or services. Where different registered trademarks are used on the same type of goods or services and all point to the same source of goods or services, they shall not be deemed as "two or more registered trademarks."  
The term "piece" of a registered trademark label as used in this Interpretation generally refers to one label bearing a complete trademark design. Where several trademark designs are printed on one tangible carrier, and such trademark designs cannot be used separately from the tangible carrier, they shall be deemed as one label.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的假冒注册商标犯罪，又销售该假冒注册商标的商品，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)的规定，以假冒注册商标罪定罪处罚。  
实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)规定的假冒注册商标犯罪，又销售明知是他人的假冒注册商标的商品，构成犯罪的，应当实行数罪并罚。

**Article 8** Where a person commits the crime of counterfeiting a registered trademark as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law and also sells the goods bearing the counterfeit registered trademark, which constitutes a crime, he/she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of counterfeiting a registered trademark in accordance with Article 213 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person commits the crime of counterfeiting a registered trademark as provided in Article 213 of the Criminal Law and also sells goods that he/she knows bear a counterfeit registered trademark of another person, which constitutes a crime, he/she shall be subject to cumulative punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-216)规定的“假冒他人专利”：  
（一）伪造或者变造他人的专利证书、专利文件或者专利申请文件的；  
（二）未经许可，在其制造或者销售的产品、产品包装上标注他人专利号的；  
（三）未经许可，在合同、产品说明书或者广告等宣传材料中使用他人的专利号，使人误认为是他人发明、实用新型或者外观设计的。

**Article 9** The following circumstances shall be deemed as "passing off another person's patent" as provided in Article 216 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) forging or altering another person's patent certificate, patent document, or patent application document;  
(2) without permission, marking another person's patent number on the products manufactured or sold by the actor or on the product packaging; or  
(3) without permission, using another person's patent number in contracts, product instructions, or promotional materials such as advertisements, causing others to mistakenly believe that it is another person's invention, utility model, or design.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　假冒他人专利，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-216)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）违法所得数额在十万元以上或者非法经营数额在二十万元以上的；  
（二）给专利权人造成直接经济损失三十万元以上的；  
（三）假冒两项以上他人专利，违法所得数额在五万元以上或者非法经营数额在十万元以上的；  
（四）二年内因实施假冒他人专利的行为受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚后再次实施，违法所得数额在五万元以上或者非法经营数额在十万元以上的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 10** Where a person passes off another person's patent, the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 216 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of illegal gains is 100,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover is 200,000 yuan or more;  
(2) causing direct economic losses of 300,000 yuan or more to the patentee;  
(3) passing off two or more patents of another person, with the amount of illegal gains being 50,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover being 100,000 yuan or more;  
(4) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for passing off another person's patent within the past two years, and committing the act again, with the amount of illegal gains being 50,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover being 100,000 yuan or more; or  
(5) other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　实施侵犯著作权或者与著作权有关的权利的行为，没有取得著作权人、录音录像制作者、表演者授权，或者伪造、涂改授权许可文件，或者超出授权许可范围，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“未经著作权人许可”、“未经录音录像制作者许可”、“未经表演者许可”。  
在[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的作品、录音录像制品上以通常方式署名的自然人、法人或者非法人组织，应当推定为著作权人或者录音录像制作者，且该作品、录音录像制品上存在着相应权利，但有相反证据的除外。  
在涉案作品、录音录像制品种类众多且权利人分散的案件中，有证据证明涉案作品、录音录像制品系非法出版、复制发行、通过信息网络向公众传播，且出版者、复制发行者、信息网络传播者不能提供获得著作权人、录音录像制作者、表演者许可的相关证据材料的，可以认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“未经著作权人许可”、“未经录音录像制作者许可”、“未经表演者许可”。但是，有证据证明权利人放弃权利、涉案作品的著作权或者录音录像制品、表演者的有关权利不受我国著作权法保护、权利保护期限已经届满等情形除外。

**Article 11** Where a person commits an act infringing copyright or rights related to copyright without obtaining authorization from the copyright owner, the sound recording or video recording producer, or the performer, or forges or alters the authorization license document, or exceeds the scope of authorization, it shall be deemed as "without permission of the copyright owner," "without permission of the sound recording or video recording producer," or "without permission of the performer" as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law.  
A natural person, legal person, or unincorporated organization that is named in the usual manner on a work, sound recording, or video recording as specified in Article 217 of the Criminal Law shall be presumed to be the copyright owner or the sound recording or video recording producer, and that the work, sound recording, or video recording is protected by the corresponding rights, unless there is evidence to the contrary.  
In cases involving a large variety of works, sound recordings, or video recordings and scattered right holders, where there is evidence that the involved works, sound recordings, or video recordings were illegally published, reproduced and distributed, or disseminated to the public via information networks, and the publisher, reproducer and distributor, or disseminator via information network cannot provide relevant evidence of obtaining permission from the copyright owner, the sound recording or video recording producer, or the performer, it may be deemed as "without permission of the copyright owner," "without permission of the sound recording or video recording producer," or "without permission of the performer" as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law. However, this does not apply where there is evidence that the right holder has waived the rights, that the copyright of the involved works or the relevant rights of the sound recordings, video recordings, or performers are not protected under the Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, or that the term of protection has expired.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　未经著作权人或者与著作权有关的权利人许可，既复制又发行或者为发行而复制作品、录音录像制品的行为，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“复制发行”。  
未经著作权人或者与著作权有关的权利人许可，以有线或者无线的方式向公众提供，使公众可以在其选定的时间和地点获得作品、录音录像制品、表演的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“通过信息网络向公众传播”。

**Article 12** Without permission of the copyright owner or the owner of rights related to copyright, the act of both reproducing and distributing, or reproducing works, sound recordings, or video recordings for the purpose of distribution, shall be deemed as "reproduction and distribution" as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law.  
Without permission of the copyright owner or the owner of rights related to copyright, providing to the public by wire or wireless means, enabling the public to obtain works, sound recordings, video recordings, or performances at a time and place individually chosen by them, shall be deemed as "dissemination to the public via information networks" as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的侵犯著作权或者与著作权有关的权利的行为，违法所得数额在三万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“违法所得数额较大”；具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）非法经营数额在五万元以上的；  
（二）二年内因实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)、第二百一十八条规定的行为受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚后再次实施，违法所得数额在二万元以上或者非法经营数额在三万元以上的；  
（三）复制发行他人作品或者录音录像制品，复制件数量合计在五百份（张）以上的；  
（四）通过信息网络向公众传播他人作品、录音录像制品或者表演，数量合计在五百件（部）以上的，或者下载数量达到一万次以上的，或者被点击数量达到十万次以上的，或者以会员制方式传播，注册会员数量达到一千人以上的；  
（五）数额或者数量虽未达到本款第一项至第四项规定标准，但分别达到其中两项以上标准一半以上的。  
明知他人实施侵犯著作权犯罪，而向他人提供主要用于避开、破坏技术措施的装置或者部件，或者为他人避开、破坏技术措施提供技术服务，违法所得数额、非法经营数额达到前款规定标准的，应当以侵犯著作权罪追究刑事责任。  
数额、数量达到本条前两款相应规定标准十倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“违法所得数额巨大或者有其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 13** Where a person commits an act infringing copyright or rights related to copyright as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law, and the amount of illegal gains is 30,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "a relatively large amount of illegal gains" as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law; the following circumstances shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of illegal business turnover is 50,000 yuan or more;  
(2) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for committing acts specified in Articles 217 or 218 of the Criminal Law within the past two years, and committing the act again, with the amount of illegal gains being 20,000 yuan or more, or the amount of illegal business turnover being 30,000 yuan or more;  
(3) reproducing and distributing another person's works, sound recordings, or video recordings, with a total number of copies of 500 or more;  
(4) disseminating another person's works, sound recordings, video recordings, or performances to the public via information networks, with a total number of 500 items or more, or with a download count of 10,000 times or more, or with a click count of 100,000 times or more, or disseminating in a membership-based manner with 1,000 or more registered members; or  
(5) where the amount or quantity does not reach the standards set out in subparagraphs (1) through (4) of this paragraph, but reaches at least half of the standards in two or more of the said subparagraphs.  
Where a person, knowing that another person is committing a crime of infringing copyright, provides that other person with devices or components primarily used to circumvent or destroy technological measures, or provides technical services for circumventing or destroying technological measures, and the amount of illegal gains or the amount of illegal business turnover reaches the standards set out in the preceding paragraph, he/she shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of infringing copyright.  
Where the amount or quantity reaches ten times or more of the corresponding standards specified in the preceding two paragraphs of this Article, it shall be deemed as "a huge amount of illegal gains or other especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　销售明知是[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的侵权复制品，违法所得数额在五万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-218)规定的“违法所得数额巨大”；具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-218)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）销售金额在十万元以上的；  
（二）二年内因实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)、第二百一十八条规定的行为受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚后再次实施，违法所得数额在三万元以上或者销售金额在五万元以上的；  
（三）销售他人作品或者录音录像制品，复制件数量合计在一千份（张）以上的；  
（四）侵权复制品尚未销售，货值金额或者侵权复制品数量达到本款前三项规定标准三倍以上，或者已销售侵权复制品的销售金额、数量不足本款前三项标准，但与尚未销售的侵权复制品的货值金额、数量合计达到本款前三项规定标准三倍以上的。

**Article 14** Where a person sells infringing copies that he/she knows are as specified in Article 217 of the Criminal Law, and the amount of illegal gains is 50,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "a huge amount of illegal gains" as provided in Article 218 of the Criminal Law; the following circumstances shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as provided in Article 218 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the sales amount is 100,000 yuan or more;  
(2) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for committing acts specified in Articles 217 or 218 of the Criminal Law within the past two years, and committing the act again, with the amount of illegal gains being 30,000 yuan or more, or the sales amount being 50,000 yuan or more;  
(3) selling another person's works, sound recordings, or video recordings, with a total number of copies of 1,000 or more;  
(4) where the infringing copies have not yet been sold, the value of the goods or the number of infringing copies reaches three times or more of the standards specified in subparagraphs (1) through (3) of this paragraph, or where the sales amount or quantity of infringing copies already sold does not reach the standards in the preceding three subparagraphs but, when combined with the value or quantity of unsold infringing copies, reaches three times or more of the standards specified in the preceding three subparagraphs.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的侵犯著作权犯罪，又销售该侵权复制品，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)的规定，以侵犯著作权罪定罪处罚。  
实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的侵犯著作权犯罪，又销售明知是他人的侵权复制品，构成犯罪的，应当实行数罪并罚。

**Article 15** Where a person commits the crime of infringing copyright as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law and also sells the infringing copies, which constitutes a crime, he/she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of infringing copyright in accordance with Article 217 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person commits the crime of infringing copyright as provided in Article 217 of the Criminal Law and also sells infringing copies that he/she knows belong to another person, which constitutes a crime, he/she shall be subject to cumulative punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　采取非法复制等方式获取商业秘密的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)第一款第一项规定的“盗窃”；未经授权或者超越授权使用计算机信息系统等方式获取商业秘密的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)第一款第一项规定的“电子侵入”。

**Article 16** Obtaining trade secrets by means of illegal reproduction, among others, shall be deemed as "theft" as provided in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 219 of the Criminal Law; obtaining trade secrets by unauthorized or exceeding authorized use of computer information systems, among others, shall be deemed as "electronic intrusion" as provided in subparagraph (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 219 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　侵犯商业秘密，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）给商业秘密的权利人造成损失数额在三十万元以上的；  
（二）因侵犯商业秘密违法所得数额在三十万元以上的；  
（三）二年内因实施[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)、第二百一十九条之一规定的行为受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚后再次实施，造成损失数额或者违法所得数额在十万元以上的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
侵犯商业秘密，直接导致商业秘密的权利人因重大经营困难而破产、倒闭的，或者数额达到本条前款相应规定标准十倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)规定的“情节特别严重”。

**Article 17** Where a person infringes trade secrets, the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 219 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing losses to the right holder of the trade secret in an amount of 300,000 yuan or more;  
(2) the amount of illegal gains from infringing trade secrets is 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for committing acts specified in Articles 219 or 219(1) of the Criminal Law within the past two years, and committing the act again, with the amount of losses or illegal gains being 100,000 yuan or more; or  
(4) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where the infringement of trade secrets directly causes the right holder of the trade secret to go bankrupt or close down due to major operational difficulties, or where the amount reaches ten times or more of the corresponding standards specified in the preceding paragraph of this Article, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 219 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　本解释规定的侵犯商业秘密“损失数额”，按照下列方式予以认定：  
（一）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密，尚未披露、使用或者允许他人使用的，损失数额可以根据该项商业秘密的合理许可使用费确定；  
（二）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密后，披露、使用或者允许他人使用的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成利润的损失确定，但该损失数额低于商业秘密合理许可使用费的，根据合理许可使用费确定；  
（三）违反保密义务或者权利人有关保守商业秘密的要求，披露、使用或者允许他人使用其所掌握的商业秘密的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成利润的损失确定；  
（四）明知商业秘密是不正当手段获取或者是违反保密义务、权利人有关保守商业秘密的要求披露、使用、允许使用，仍获取、披露、使用或者允许他人使用的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权造成利润的损失确定；  
（五）因侵犯商业秘密行为导致商业秘密已为公众所知悉或者灭失的，损失数额可以根据该项商业秘密的商业价值确定。商业秘密的商业价值，可以根据该项商业秘密的研究开发成本、实施该项商业秘密的收益等因素综合确定。  
前款第二项至第四项规定的权利人因被侵权造成利润的损失，可以根据权利人因被侵权造成产品销售量减少的总数乘以权利人每件产品的合理利润确定；产品销售量减少的总数无法确定的，可以根据侵权产品销售量乘以权利人每件产品的合理利润确定。商业秘密系用于服务等其他经营活动的，损失数额可以根据权利人因被侵权而减少的合理利润确定。  
商业秘密的权利人为减轻对商业运营、商业计划的损失或者重新恢复计算机信息系统安全、其他系统安全而支出的补救费用，应当计入给商业秘密的权利人造成的损失。

**Article 18** The "amount of losses" for infringement of trade secrets as provided in this Interpretation shall be determined in the following manner:  
(1) where a trade secret of the right holder is obtained by improper means, and has not yet been disclosed, used, or allowed to be used by others, the amount of losses may be determined based on the reasonable license fee for the use of the trade secret;  
(2) where a trade secret of the right holder is obtained by improper means and subsequently disclosed, used, or allowed to be used by others, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of profits suffered by the right holder due to the infringement; however, if such loss amount is lower than the reasonable license fee for the trade secret, it shall be determined based on the reasonable license fee;  
(3) where a person violates a confidentiality obligation or the right holder's requirements on keeping the trade secret confidential, and discloses, uses, or allows others to use the trade secret in his/her possession, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of profits suffered by the right holder due to the infringement;  
(4) where a person, knowing that a trade secret was obtained by improper means or was disclosed, used, or allowed to be used in violation of a confidentiality obligation or the right holder's requirements on keeping the trade secret confidential, still obtains, discloses, uses, or allows others to use it, the amount of losses may be determined based on the loss of profits suffered by the right holder due to the infringement;  
(5) where the trade secret has become known to the public or has been lost due to the act of infringing the trade secret, the amount of losses may be determined based on the commercial value of the trade secret. The commercial value of the trade secret may be determined by comprehensively considering factors such as the research and development costs of the trade secret and the revenue from implementing the trade secret.  
The loss of profits suffered by the right holder due to infringement as specified in subparagraphs (2) through (4) of the preceding paragraph may be determined by multiplying the total reduction in product sales volume caused by the infringement by the reasonable profit per product of the right holder; if the total reduction in product sales volume cannot be determined, it may be determined by multiplying the sales volume of infringing products by the reasonable profit per product of the right holder. Where the trade secret is used for other business activities such as services, the amount of losses may be determined based on the reduction in reasonable profits of the right holder due to the infringement.  
The remedial expenses incurred by the right holder of the trade secret to mitigate losses to business operations or business plans, or to restore the security of computer information systems or other systems, shall be included in the losses caused to the right holder of the trade secret.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　本解释规定的侵犯商业秘密“违法所得数额”，是指因披露、允许他人使用商业秘密而获得的财物或者其他财产性利益的价值，或者因使用商业秘密所获得的利润。该利润可以根据侵权产品销售量乘以每件侵权产品的合理利润确定。

**Article 19** The "amount of illegal gains" from infringement of trade secrets as provided in this Interpretation refers to the value of property or other pecuniary benefits obtained from disclosing or allowing others to use the trade secret, or the profits obtained from using the trade secret. Such profits may be determined by multiplying the sales volume of infringing products by the reasonable profit per infringing product.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　为境外机构、组织、人员窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供商业秘密，具有[本解释第十七条](#art-17)规定情形的，应当认定为[刑法第二百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-219)之一规定的“情节严重”。

**Article 20** Where a person steals, spies on, buys, or illegally provides trade secrets for overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals, and the circumstances fall under Article 17 of this Interpretation, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in Article 219-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　在刑事诉讼程序中，当事人、辩护人、诉讼代理人或者案外人书面申请对有关商业秘密或者其他需要保密的商业信息的证据、材料采取保密措施的，应当根据案件情况采取组织诉讼参与人签署保密承诺书等必要的保密措施。  
违反前款有关保密措施的要求或者法律法规规定的保密义务的，依法承担相应责任。擅自披露、使用或者允许他人使用在刑事诉讼程序中接触、获取的商业秘密，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 21** In criminal proceedings, where a party, defender, litigation agent, or an outsider to the case submits a written application for taking confidentiality measures with respect to evidence or materials concerning trade secrets or other commercial information requiring confidentiality, the People's Court shall, based on the circumstances of the case, take necessary confidentiality measures such as organizing litigation participants to sign a confidentiality commitment letter.  
Any person who violates the requirements of the confidentiality measures provided in the preceding paragraph or the confidentiality obligations prescribed by laws or regulations shall bear corresponding liability according to law. Where a person, without authorization, discloses, uses, or allows others to use trade secrets obtained or accessed during criminal proceedings, and such conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　明知他人实施侵犯知识产权犯罪，具有下列行为之一的，以共同犯罪论处，但法律和司法解释另有规定的除外：  
（一）提供生产、制造侵权产品的主要原材料、辅助材料、半成品、包装材料、机械设备、标签标识、生产技术、配方等帮助的；  
（二）提供贷款、资金、账号、许可证件、支付结算等服务的；  
（三）提供生产、经营场所或者运输、仓储、保管、快递、邮寄等服务的；  
（四）提供互联网接入、服务器托管、网络存储、通讯传输等技术支持的；  
（五）其他帮助侵犯知识产权犯罪的情形。

**Article 22** Where a person, knowing that another person commits a crime of infringing intellectual property rights, commits any of the following acts, such person shall be deemed as a joint offender, unless otherwise provided by law or judicial interpretations:  
(1) providing assistance such as main raw materials, auxiliary materials, semi-finished products, packaging materials, machinery equipment, labels, production technologies, or formulas for producing or manufacturing infringing products;  
(2) providing services such as loans, funds, accounts, licenses, payment or settlement services;  
(3) providing services such as production or business premises, or transportation, warehousing, custody, express delivery, or postal services;  
(4) providing technical support such as internet access, server hosting, network storage, or communication transmission;  
(5) other circumstances of assisting the crime of infringing intellectual property rights.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　实施侵犯知识产权犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，一般酌情从重处罚：  
（一）主要以侵犯知识产权为业的；  
（二）在重大自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件期间，假冒抢险救灾、防疫物资等商品或者服务注册商标的；  
（三）拒不交出违法所得的。

**Article 23** Where a person commits a crime of infringing intellectual property rights and falls under any of the following circumstances, the person shall generally be given a severe punishment at discretion:  
(1) mainly engaging in the infringement of intellectual property rights as a profession;  
(2) during major natural disasters, accident catastrophes, or public health incidents, counterfeiting registered trademarks of goods or services such as emergency rescue, disaster relief, or epidemic prevention materials;  
(3) refusing to surrender illegal gains.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　实施侵犯知识产权犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，可以依法从轻处罚：  
（一）认罪认罚的；  
（二）取得权利人谅解的；  
（三）以不正当手段获取权利人的商业秘密后尚未披露、使用或者允许他人使用的。  
犯罪情节轻微的，可以依法不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。情节显著轻微危害不大的，不以犯罪论处。

**Article 24** Where a person commits a crime of infringing intellectual property rights and falls under any of the following circumstances, the person may be given a light punishment according to law:  
(1) admitting guilt and accepting punishment;  
(2) obtaining forgiveness from the right holder;  
(3) after obtaining the trade secret of the right holder by improper means, has not yet disclosed, used, or allowed others to use it.  
If the circumstances of the crime are minor, the person may not be prosecuted or may be exempted from criminal punishment according to law. If the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not serious, it shall not be deemed as a crime.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　实施侵犯知识产权犯罪的，应当综合考虑犯罪违法所得数额、非法经营数额、给权利人造成的损失数额、侵权假冒物品数量及社会危害性等情节，依法判处罚金。  
罚金数额一般在违法所得数额的一倍以上十倍以下确定。违法所得数额无法查清的，罚金数额一般按照非法经营数额的百分之五十以上一倍以下确定。违法所得数额和非法经营数额均无法查清，判处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者单处罚金的，一般在三万元以上一百万元以下确定罚金数额；判处三年以上有期徒刑的，一般在十五万元以上五百万元以下确定罚金数额。

**Article 25** Where a person commits a crime of infringing intellectual property rights, the People's Court shall, taking into comprehensive consideration the amount of illegal gains from the crime, the amount of illegal business turnover, the amount of losses caused to the right holder, the quantity of infringing and counterfeit goods, the degree of social harm, and other circumstances, impose a fine according to law.  
The amount of the fine shall generally be determined at not less than one time but not more than ten times the amount of illegal gains. If the amount of illegal gains cannot be ascertained, the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 50% but not more than one time the amount of illegal business turnover. If neither the amount of illegal gains nor the amount of illegal business turnover can be ascertained, and the person is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or a fine only, the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 30,000 yuan but not more than 1,000,000 yuan; if the person is sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years, the fine shall generally be determined at not less than 150,000 yuan but not more than 5,000,000 yuan.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　单位实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)至第二百一十九条之一行为的，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照本解释规定的定罪量刑标准处罚。

**Article 26** Where a unit commits any act under Articles 213 through 219-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the unit shall be sentenced to a fine, and the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards prescribed in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条　除特殊情况外，假冒注册商标的商品、非法制造的注册商标标识、侵犯著作权的复制品、主要用于制造假冒注册商标的商品、注册商标标识或者侵权复制品的材料和工具，应当依法予以没收和销毁。  
上述物品需要作为民事、行政案件的证据使用的，经权利人申请，可以在民事、行政案件终结后或者采取取样、拍照等方式对证据固定后予以销毁。

**Article 27** Except under special circumstances, goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks, illegally manufactured registered trademark labels, reproductions that infringe copyright, and materials and tools mainly used for manufacturing goods bearing counterfeit registered trademarks, registered trademark labels, or infringing reproductions shall be confiscated and destroyed according to law.  
If the articles mentioned above are needed as evidence in civil or administrative cases, upon application by the right holder, they may be destroyed after the conclusion of the civil or administrative case or after the evidence is fixed by means such as sampling or photographing.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条　本解释所称“非法经营数额”，是指行为人在实施侵犯知识产权行为过程中，制造、储存、运输、销售侵权产品的价值。已销售侵权产品的价值，按照实际销售的价格计算。尚未销售侵权产品的价值，按照已经查清的侵权产品实际销售平均价格计算。实际销售平均价格无法查清的，按照侵权产品的标价计算。无法查清实际销售价格或者侵权产品没有标价的，按照被侵权产品的市场中间价格计算。  
本解释所称“货值金额”，依照前款规定的尚未销售的侵犯知识产权的产品价值认定。  
本解释所称“销售金额”，是指行为人在实施侵犯知识产权行为过程中，出售侵权产品后所得和应得的全部违法收入。  
本解释所称“违法所得数额”，是指行为人出售侵犯知识产权的产品后所得和应得的全部违法收入扣除原材料、所售产品的购进价款；提供服务的，扣除该项服务中所使用产品的购进价款。通过收取服务费、会员费或者广告费等方式营利的，收取的费用应当认定为“违法所得”。

**Article 28** The term "illegal business turnover" as used in this Interpretation means the value of infringing products manufactured, stored, transported, or sold by the actor in the course of committing the act of infringing intellectual property rights. The value of infringing products already sold shall be calculated based on the actual selling price. The value of infringing products not yet sold shall be calculated based on the average actual selling price of the infringing products that has been ascertained. If the average actual selling price cannot be ascertained, calculation shall be made based on the marked price of the infringing products. If the actual selling price cannot be ascertained or the infringing product has no marked price, calculation shall be made based on the average market price of the infringed product.  
The term "value of goods" as used in this Interpretation shall be determined in accordance with the value of the products infringing intellectual property rights that have not yet been sold as prescribed in the preceding paragraph.  
The term "sales amount" as used in this Interpretation means the total illegal income obtained and receivable by the actor after selling the infringing products in the course of committing the act of infringing intellectual property rights.  
The term "amount of illegal gains" as used in this Interpretation means the total illegal income obtained and receivable by the actor after selling the products infringing intellectual property rights, minus the cost of raw materials and the purchase price of the products sold; if services are provided, the purchase price of the products used in such services shall be deducted. If the actor makes profits by charging service fees, membership fees, advertising fees, or other similar means, the fees collected shall be deemed as "illegal gains."

<a id="art-29"></a>第二十九条　多次实施侵犯知识产权行为，未经处理且依法应当追诉的，定罪量刑所涉数额、数量等分别累计计算。  
对于已经制作完成但尚未附着或者尚未全部附着假冒注册商标标识的产品，有证据证明该产品将假冒他人注册商标的，其价值计入非法经营数额。

**Article 29** Where a person has repeatedly committed acts of infringing intellectual property rights that have not been dealt with and for which prosecution is lawfully required, the amounts, quantities, and other factors involved in conviction and sentencing shall be calculated cumulatively respectively.  
For products that have been completely manufactured but on which counterfeit registered trademark labels have not yet been affixed or have not been fully affixed, if there is evidence proving that such products will counterfeit another person's registered trademark, their value shall be included in the illegal business turnover.

<a id="art-30"></a>第三十条　人民法院依法受理侵犯知识产权刑事自诉案件，对于当事人因客观原因不能取得的证据，在提起自诉时能够提供有关线索，申请人民法院调取的，人民法院应当依法调取。

**Article 30** Where a People's Court accepts a criminal private prosecution case of infringing intellectual property rights according to law, if a party is unable to obtain evidence due to objective reasons but can provide relevant clues when filing the private prosecution and applies to the People's Court for collection and obtainment of such evidence, the People's Court shall, according to law, collect and obtain the evidence.

<a id="art-31"></a>第三十一条　本解释自2025年4月26日起施行。  
本解释施行后，《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2004〕19号）、《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》（法释〔2007〕6号）、《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（三）》（法释〔2020〕10号）及《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯著作权刑事案件中涉及录音录像制品有关问题的批复》（法释〔2005〕12号）同时废止。  
本解释施行后，之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 31** This Interpretation shall come into force as of April 26, 2025.  
Upon the entry into force of this Interpretation, the "Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Infringement of Intellectual Property Rights" (Fa Shi [2004] No. 19), the "Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Infringement of Intellectual Property Rights (II)" (Fa Shi [2007] No. 6), the "Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Infringement of Intellectual Property Rights (III)" (Fa Shi [2020] No. 10), and the "Reply of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Issues Concerning Audio and Video Recordings in Criminal Cases of Copyright Infringement" (Fa Shi [2005] No. 12) shall be simultaneously abolished.  
After this Interpretation comes into force, if any previously issued judicial interpretations and normative documents are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
