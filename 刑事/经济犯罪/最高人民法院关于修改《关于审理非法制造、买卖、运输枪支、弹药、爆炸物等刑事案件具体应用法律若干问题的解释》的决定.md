# 最高人民法院关于修改《关于审理非法制造、买卖、运输枪支、弹药、爆炸物等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕18号  
**公布日期**：2009-11-09  
**生效日期**：2010-01-01

> 最高人民法院《关于修改〈关于审理非法制造、买卖、运输枪支、弹药、爆炸物等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释〉的决定》已于2009年11月9日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1476次会议通过。现予公布，自2010年1月1日起施行。 2009年11月16日 最高人民法院 关于修改《关于审理非法制造、买卖、运输枪支、 弹药、爆炸物等刑事案件具体应用法律 若干问题的解释》的决定 法释〔2009〕18号 为了依法惩治非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸物犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，并结合审判实践情况，现决定对最高人民法院《关于审理非法制造、买卖、运输枪支、弹药、爆炸物等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（以下简称《解释》）作如下修改: 一、将《解释》第八条第一款修改为：“刑法第一百二十五条第一款规定的‘非法储存’，是指明知是他人非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄的枪支、弹药而为其存放的行为，或者非法存放爆炸物的行为。” 二、增加一条，作为《解释》第九条：“因筑路、建房、打井、整修宅基地和土地等正常生产、生活需要，或者因从事合法的生产经营活动而非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸物，数量达到本《解释》第一条规定标准，没有造成严重社会危害，并确有悔改表现的，可依法从轻处罚；情节轻微的，可以免除处罚。 具有前款情形，数量虽达到本《解释》第二条规定标准的，也可以不认定为刑法第一百二十五条第一款规定的‘情节严重’。 在公共场所、居民区等人员集中区域非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸物，或者因非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸物三年内受到两次以上行政处罚又实施上述行为，数量达到本《解释》规定标准的，不适用前两款量刑的规定。” 三、将《解释》原第九条变更为第十条。 根据本《决定》，将《解释》作相应修改并对条文顺序作相应调整后，重新公布。 关于审理非法制造、买卖、运输枪支、

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 个人或者单位非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-125)第一款的规定，以非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存军用枪支一支以上的；  
（二）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存以火药为动力发射枪弹的非军用枪支一支以上或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支二支以上的；  
（三）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存军用子弹十发以上、气枪铅弹五百发以上或者其他非军用子弹一百发以上的；  
（四）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存手榴弹一枚以上的；  
（五）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸装置的；  
（六）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存炸药、发射药、黑火药一千克以上或者烟火药三千克以上、雷管三十枚以上或者导火索、导爆索三十米以上的；  
（七）具有生产爆炸物品资格的单位不按照规定的品种制造，或者具有销售、使用爆炸物品资格的单位超过限额买卖炸药、发射药、黑火药十千克以上或者烟火药三十千克以上、雷管三百枚以上或者导火索、导爆索三百米以上的；  
（八）多次非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存弹药、爆炸物的；  
（九）虽未达到上述最低数量标准，但具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。  
介绍买卖枪支、弹药、爆炸物的，以买卖枪支、弹药、爆炸物罪的共犯论处。

**Article 1** Where an individual or a unit illegally manufactures, buys, sells, transports, mails, or stores firearms, ammunition, or explosives, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing firearms, ammunition, or explosives in accordance with Article 125(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing one or more military firearms;  
(2) illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing one or more non-military firearms that fire bullets using gunpowder as a propellant, or two or more other non-military firearms that use compressed gas or the like as a propellant;  
(3) illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing ten or more military bullets, 500 or more air gun pellets, or 100 or more other non-military bullets;  
(4) illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing one or more hand grenades;  
(5) illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing explosive devices;  
(6) illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing one kilogram or more of explosives, propellant, or black powder, or three kilograms or more of pyrotechnic powder, or 30 or more detonators, or 30 meters or more of fuse or detonating cord;  
(7) a unit qualified to produce explosive materials fails to manufacture according to the prescribed variety, or a unit qualified to sell or use explosive materials buys or sells ten kilograms or more of explosives, propellant, or black powder, or 30 kilograms or more of pyrotechnic powder, or 300 or more detonators, or 300 meters or more of fuse or detonating cord, in excess of the quota;  
(8) repeatedly illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing ammunition or explosives;  
(9) although the minimum quantity standards above are not met, other egregious circumstances such as causing serious consequences exist.  
Whoever acts as an intermediary in buying or selling firearms, ammunition, or explosives shall be punished as an accomplice to the crime of buying or selling firearms, ammunition, or explosives.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-125)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存枪支、弹药、爆炸物的数量达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)第（一）、（二）、（三）、（六）、（七）项规定的最低数量标准五倍以上的；  
（二）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存手榴弹三枚以上的；  
（三）非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸装置，危害严重的；  
（四）达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。

**Article 2** Where the illegal manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing of firearms, ammunition, or explosives falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute the 'serious circumstances' as provided in Article 125(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the quantity of illegally manufactured, bought, sold, transported, mailed, or stored firearms, ammunition, or explosives reaches five times or more the minimum quantity standards specified in Subparagraphs (1), (2), (3), (6), and (7) of Article 1 of this Interpretation;  
(2) illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing three or more hand grenades;  
(3) illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing explosive devices, causing serious harm;  
(4) meeting the minimum quantity standards specified in Article 1 of this Interpretation and also having other egregious circumstances such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 依法被指定或者确定的枪支制造、销售企业，实施[刑法第一百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-126)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，以违规制造、销售枪支罪定罪处罚：  
（一）违规制造枪支五支以上的；  
（二）违规销售枪支二支以上的；  
（三）虽未达到上述最低数量标准，但具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-126)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）违规制造枪支二十支以上的；  
（二）违规销售枪支十支以上的；  
（三）达到本条第一款规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-126)规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）违规制造枪支五十支以上的；  
（二）违规销售枪支三十支以上的；  
（三）达到本条第二款规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。

**Article 3** Where an enterprise designated or confirmed according to law for the manufacturing or sale of firearms commits any act provided in Article 126 of the Criminal Law, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, the enterprise shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally manufacturing or selling firearms:  
(1) illegally manufacturing five or more firearms;  
(2) illegally selling two or more firearms;  
(3) although the minimum quantity standards above are not met, other egregious circumstances such as causing serious consequences exist.  
Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute the 'serious circumstances' as provided in Article 126 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally manufacturing twenty or more firearms;  
(2) illegally selling ten or more firearms;  
(3) meeting the minimum quantity standards specified in the first paragraph of this Article and also having other egregious circumstances such as causing serious consequences.  
Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute the 'especially serious circumstances' as provided in Article 126 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally manufacturing fifty or more firearms;  
(2) illegally selling thirty or more firearms;  
(3) meeting the minimum quantity standards specified in the second paragraph of this Article and also having other egregious circumstances such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 盗窃、抢夺枪支、弹药、爆炸物，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百二十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-127)第一款的规定，以盗窃、抢夺枪支、弹药、爆炸物罪定罪处罚：  
（一）盗窃、抢夺以火药为动力的发射枪弹非军用枪支一支以上或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支二支以上的；  
（二）盗窃、抢夺军用子弹十发以上、气枪铅弹五百发以上或者其他非军用子弹一百发以上的；  
（三）盗窃、抢夺爆炸装置的；  
（四）盗窃、抢夺炸药、发射药、黑火药一千克以上或者烟火药三千克以上、雷管三十枚以上或者导火索、导爆索三十米以上的；  
（五）虽未达到上述最低数量标准，但具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-127)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）盗窃、抢夺枪支、弹药、爆炸物的数量达到本条第一款规定的最低数量标准五倍以上的；  
（二）盗窃、抢夺军用枪支的；  
（三）盗窃、抢夺手榴弹的；  
（四）盗窃、抢夺爆炸装置，危害严重的；  
（五）达到本条第一款规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。

**Article 4** Where any person steals or forcibly seizes firearms, ammunition, or explosives, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of stealing or forcibly seizing firearms, ammunition, or explosives in accordance with Article 127(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) stealing or forcibly seizing one or more non-military firearms that fire bullets using gunpowder as a propellant, or two or more other non-military firearms that use compressed gas or the like as a propellant;  
(2) stealing or forcibly seizing ten or more military bullets, 500 or more air gun pellets, or 100 or more other non-military bullets;  
(3) stealing or forcibly seizing explosive devices;  
(4) stealing or forcibly seizing one kilogram or more of explosives, propellant, or black powder, or three kilograms or more of pyrotechnic powder, or 30 or more detonators, or 30 meters or more of fuse or detonating cord;  
(5) although the minimum quantity standards above are not met, other egregious circumstances such as causing serious consequences exist.  
Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute the 'serious circumstances' as provided in Article 127(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the quantity of stolen or forcibly seized firearms, ammunition, or explosives reaches five times or more the minimum quantity standards specified in the first paragraph of this Article;  
(2) stealing or forcibly seizing military firearms;  
(3) stealing or forcibly seizing hand grenades;  
(4) stealing or forcibly seizing explosive devices, causing serious harm;  
(5) meeting the minimum quantity standards specified in the first paragraph of this Article and also having other egregious circumstances such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-128)第一款的规定，以非法持有、私藏枪支、弹药罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法持有、私藏军用枪支一支的；  
（二）非法持有、私藏以火药为动力发射枪弹的非军用枪支一支或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支二支以上的；  
（三）非法持有、私藏军用子弹二十发以上，气枪铅弹一千发以上或者其他非军用子弹二百发以上的；  
（四）非法持有、私藏手榴弹一枚以上的；  
（五）非法持有、私藏的弹药造成人员伤亡、财产损失的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-128)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法持有、私藏军用枪支二支以上的；  
（二）非法持有、私藏以火药为动力发射枪弹的非军用枪支二支以上或者以压缩气体等为动力的其他非军用枪支五支以上的；  
（三）非法持有、私藏军用子弹一百发以上，气枪铅弹五千发以上或者其他非军用子弹一千发以上的；  
（四）非法持有、私藏手榴弹三枚以上的；  
（五）达到本条第一款规定的最低数量标准，并具有造成严重后果等其他恶劣情节的。

**Article 5** Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally possessing or privately storing firearms or ammunition in accordance with Article 128(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally possessing or privately storing one military firearm;  
(2) illegally possessing or privately storing one non-military firearm that fires bullets using gunpowder as a propellant, or two or more other non-military firearms that use compressed gas or the like as a propellant;  
(3) illegally possessing or privately storing twenty or more military bullets, 1,000 or more air gun pellets, or 200 or more other non-military bullets;  
(4) illegally possessing or privately storing one or more hand grenades;  
(5) the ammunition illegally possessed or privately stored causes personal injury, death, or property damage.  
Where the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute the 'serious circumstances' as provided in Article 128(1) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) illegally possessing or privately storing two or more military firearms;  
(2) illegally possessing or privately storing two or more non-military firearms that fire bullets using gunpowder as a propellant, or five or more other non-military firearms that use compressed gas or the like as a propellant;  
(3) illegally possessing or privately storing 100 or more military bullets, 5,000 or more air gun pellets, or 1,000 or more other non-military bullets;  
(4) illegally possessing or privately storing three or more hand grenades;  
(5) meeting the minimum quantity standards specified in the first paragraph of this Article and also having other egregious circumstances such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 非法携带枪支、弹药、爆炸物进入公共场所或者公共交通工具，危及公共安全，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百三十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-130)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）携带枪支或者手榴弹的；  
（二）携带爆炸装置的；  
（三）携带炸药、发射药、黑火药五百克以上或者烟火药一千克以上、雷管二十枚以上或者导火索、导爆索二十米以上的；  
（四）携带的弹药、爆炸物在公共场所或者公共交通工具上发生爆炸或者燃烧，尚未造成严重后果的；  
（五）具有其他严重情节的。  
行为人非法携带本条第一款第（三）项规定的爆炸物进入公共场所或者公共交通工具，虽未达到上述数量标准，但拒不交出的，依照[刑法第一百三十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-130)的规定定罪处罚；携带的数量达到最低数量标准，能够主动、全部交出的，可不以犯罪论处。

**Article 6** Where any person illegally carries firearms, ammunition, or explosives into a public place or on a public conveyance, endangering public safety, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall constitute the 'serious circumstances' as provided in Article 130 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) carrying a firearm or a hand grenade;  
(2) carrying an explosive device;  
(3) carrying 500 grams or more of explosives, propellant, or black powder, or one kilogram or more of pyrotechnic powder, or 20 or more detonators, or 20 meters or more of fuse or detonating cord;  
(4) the carried ammunition or explosives explode or ignite in a public place or on a public conveyance, without yet having caused serious consequences;  
(5) having other serious circumstances.  
Where a person illegally carries the explosives specified in Subparagraph (3) of the first paragraph of this Article into a public place or on a public conveyance, and although the quantity standards above are not met, the person refuses to surrender them, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 130 of the Criminal Law; if the quantity carried meets the minimum quantity standard and the person voluntarily and completely surrenders them, the act may not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存、盗窃、抢夺、持有、私藏、携带成套枪支散件的，以相应数量的枪支计；非成套枪支散件以每三十件为一成套枪支散件计。

**Article 7** Where any person illegally manufactures, buys, sells, transports, mails, stores, steals, forcibly seizes, possesses, privately stores, or carries complete sets of firearm parts, the quantity shall be calculated as the corresponding number of firearms; for non-complete sets of firearm parts, every thirty pieces shall be counted as one complete set of firearm parts.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 [刑法第一百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-125)第一款规定的“非法储存”，是指明知是他人非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄的枪支、弹药而为其存放的行为，或者非法存放爆炸物的行为。  
[刑法第一百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-128)第一款规定的“非法持有”，是指不符合配备、配置枪支、弹药条件的人员，违反枪支管理法律、法规的规定，擅自持有枪支、弹药的行为。  
[刑法第一百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-128)第一款规定的“私藏”，是指依法配备、配置枪支、弹药的人员，在配备、配置枪支、弹药的条件消除后，违反枪支管理法律、法规的规定，私自藏匿所配备、配置的枪支、弹药且拒不交出的行为。

**Article 8** The term 'illegal storage' as provided in Article 125(1) of the Criminal Law refers to the act of storing firearms or ammunition for another person while knowing that they are illegally manufactured, bought, sold, transported, or mailed by that other person, or the act of illegally storing explosives.  
The term 'illegal possession' as provided in Article 128(1) of the Criminal Law refers to the act of a person who does not meet the conditions for being equipped or provided with firearms or ammunition, in violation of the provisions of laws and regulations on the administration of firearms, taking possession of firearms or ammunition without authorization.  
The term 'private storage' as provided in Article 128(1) of the Criminal Law refers to the act of a person who is equipped or provided with firearms or ammunition according to law, after the conditions for being equipped or provided with firearms or ammunition cease to exist, in violation of the provisions of laws and regulations on the administration of firearms, privately concealing the firearms or ammunition with which the person is equipped or provided, and refusing to surrender them.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 因筑路、建房、打井、整修宅基地和土地等正常生产、生活需要，或者因从事合法的生产经营活动而非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸物，数量达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定标准，没有造成严重社会危害，并确有悔改表现的，可依法从轻处罚；情节轻微的，可以免除处罚。  
具有前款情形，数量虽达到[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定标准的，也可以不认定为[刑法第一百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-125)第一款规定的“情节严重”。  
在公共场所、居民区等人员集中区域非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸物，或者因非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存爆炸物三年内受到两次以上行政处罚又实施上述行为，数量达到本解释规定标准的，不适用前两款量刑的规定。

**Article 9** Where a person illegally manufactures, buys, sells, transports, mails, or stores explosives due to normal production or living needs such as road construction, building houses, digging wells, or renovating homesteads and land, or due to engaging in lawful production and business activities, and the quantity reaches the standards specified in Article 1 of this Interpretation, but no serious social harm is caused and the person truly shows repentance, a light punishment may be imposed according to law; if the circumstances are minor, punishment may be exempted.  
Where the circumstances in the preceding paragraph exist, and although the quantity reaches the standards specified in Article 2 of this Interpretation, it may also not be determined as 'serious circumstances' under Article 125(1) of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person illegally manufactures, buys, sells, transports, mails, or stores explosives in a public place, residential area, or other densely populated area, or having received two or more administrative penalties within three years for illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, or storing explosives, commits the aforesaid act again, and the quantity reaches the standards specified in this Interpretation, the sentencing provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall not apply.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 实施非法制造、买卖、运输、邮寄、储存、盗窃、抢夺、持有、私藏其他弹药、爆炸物品等行为，参照本解释有关条文规定的定罪量刑标准处罚。

**Article 10** Where any person commits acts such as illegally manufacturing, buying, selling, transporting, mailing, storing, stealing, forcibly seizing, possessing, or privately storing other ammunition or explosive articles, the person shall be punished by reference to the conviction and sentencing standards specified in the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.
