# 最高人民法院关于适用《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于惩治虚开、伪造和非法出售增值税专用发票犯罪的决定》的若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1996-10-17

---

### 正文

一、根据《决定》第一条规定，虚开增值税专用发票的，构成虚开增值税专用发票罪。  
具有下列行为之一的，属于“虚开增值税专用发票”：（1）没有货物购销或者没有提供或接受应税劳务而为他人、为自己、让他人为自己、介绍他人开具增值税专用发票；（2）有货物购销或者提供或接受了应税劳务但为他人、为自己、让他人为自己、介绍他人开具数量或者金额不实的增值税专用发票；（3）进行了实际经营活动，但让他人为自己代开增值税专用发票。  
虚开税款数额1万元以上的或者虚开增值税专用发票致使国家税款被骗取5千元以上的，应当依法定罪处罚。  
虚开税款数额10万元以上的，属于“虚开的税款数额较大”。具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他严重情节”：（1）因虚开增值税专用发票致使国家税款被骗取5万元以上的；（2）曾因虚开增值税专用发票受过刑事处罚的；（3）具有其他严重情节的。  
虚开税款数额50万元以上的，属于“虚开的税款数额巨大”。具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他特别严重情节”：（1）因虚开增值税专用发票致使国家税款被骗取30万元以上的；（2）虚开的税款数额接近巨大并有其他严重情节的；（3）具有其他特别严重情节的。  
利用虚开的增值税专用发票实际抵扣税款或者骗取出口退税100万元以上的，属于“骗取国家税款数额特别巨大”；造成国家税款损失50万元以上并且在侦查终结前仍无法追回的，属于“给国家利益造成特别重大损失”。利用虚开的增值税专用发票骗取国家税款数额特别巨大、给国家利益造成特别重大损失，为“情节特别严重”的基本内容。  
虚开增值税专用发票犯罪分子与骗取税款犯罪分子均应当对虚开的税款数额和实际骗取的国家税款数额承担刑事责任。  
利用虚开的增值税专用发票抵扣税款或者骗取出口退税的，应当依照《决定》第一条的规定定罪处罚；以其他手段骗取国家税款的，仍应依照《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于惩治偷税、抗税犯罪的补充规定》的有关规定定罪处罚。

**Article None** 1. According to the provisions of Article 1 of the Decision, whoever falsely issues special VAT invoices shall constitute the crime of falsely issuing special VAT invoices.  
Any of the following acts shall be deemed as “falsely issuing special VAT invoices”: (1) issuing special VAT invoices for others, for oneself, causing others to issue for oneself, or introducing others to issue, without any purchase or sale of goods or without providing or receiving taxable services; (2) issuing special VAT invoices for others, for oneself, causing others to issue for oneself, or introducing others to issue, with the quantity or amount not corresponding to the actual situation, despite having purchase or sale of goods or having provided or received taxable services; (3) carrying out actual business activities but causing others to issue special VAT invoices on one’s behalf.  
Where the amount of tax falsely issued reaches 10,000 yuan or more, or the falsely issued special VAT invoices cause state tax to be defrauded of 5,000 yuan or more, the offender shall be convicted and punished according to law.  
Where the amount of tax falsely issued reaches 100,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as “a relatively large amount of tax falsely issued.” Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “other serious circumstances”: (1) causing state tax to be defrauded of 50,000 yuan or more due to falsely issuing special VAT invoices; (2) having previously received criminal punishment for falsely issuing special VAT invoices; (3) having other serious circumstances.  
Where the amount of tax falsely issued reaches 500,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as “a huge amount of tax falsely issued.” Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “other especially serious circumstances”: (1) causing state tax to be defrauded of 300,000 yuan or more due to falsely issuing special VAT invoices; (2) the amount of tax falsely issued is close to huge and there are other serious circumstances; (3) having other especially serious circumstances.  
Where the actual tax deduction or fraudulent export tax refund obtained by using falsely issued special VAT invoices reaches 1,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as “an especially huge amount of state tax defrauded”; where the loss of state tax caused is 500,000 yuan or more and cannot be recovered before the conclusion of investigation, it shall be deemed as “causing especially heavy losses to state interests.” Using falsely issued special VAT invoices to defraud state tax of an especially huge amount and causing especially heavy losses to state interests shall be the basic content of “especially serious circumstances.”  
Both the offender who falsely issues special VAT invoices and the offender who defrauds tax shall bear criminal liability for the amount of tax falsely issued and the amount of state tax actually defrauded.  
Where falsely issued special VAT invoices are used to deduct tax or to fraudulently obtain export tax refund, the offender shall be convicted and punished according to the provisions of Article 1 of the Decision; where state tax is defrauded by other means, the offender shall still be convicted and punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Supplementary Provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress concerning the Punishment of Crimes of Tax Evasion and Tax Refusal.

二、根据《决定》第二条规定，伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票的，构成伪造、出售伪造的增值税专用发票罪。  
伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票25份以上或者票面额（千元版以每份1000元，万元版以每份1万元计算，以此类推。下同）累计10万元以上的应当依法定罪处罚。  
伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票100份以上或者票面额累计50万元以上的，属于“数量较大”。具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他严重情节”：（1）违法所得数额在1万元以上的；（2）伪造并出售伪造的增值税专用发票60份以上或者票面额累计30万元以上的；（3）造成严重后果或者具有其他严重情节的。  
伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票500份以上或者票面额累计250万元以上的，属于“数量巨大”。具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他特别严重情节”：（1）违法所得数额在5万元以上的；（2）伪造并出售伪造的增值税专用发票300份以上或者票面额累计200万元以上的；（3）伪造或者出售伪造的增值税专用发票接近“数量巨大”并有其他严重情节的；（4）造成特别严重后果或者具有其他特别严重情节的。  
伪造并出售伪造的增值税专用发票1000份以上或者票面额累计1000万元以上的，属于“伪造并出售伪造的增值税专用发票数量特别巨大”。具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”：（1）违法所得数额在5万元以上的；（2）因伪造、出售伪造的增值税专用发票致使国家税款被骗取100万元以上的；（3）给国家税款造成实际损失50万元以上的；（4）具有其他特别严重情节的。对于伪造并出售伪造的增值税专用发票数量达到特别巨大，又具有特别严重情节，严重破坏经济秩序的，应当依照《决定》第二条第二款的规定处罚。  
伪造并出售同一宗增值税专用发票的，数量或者票面额不重复计算。  
变造增值税专用发票的，按照伪造增值税专用发票行为处理。

**Article None** 2. According to the provisions of Article 2 of the Decision, whoever forges or sells forged special VAT invoices shall constitute the crime of forging or selling forged special VAT invoices.  
Where the number of forged or sold forged special VAT invoices reaches 25 or more, or the cumulative face amount (for the thousand-yuan version, each copy is calculated as 1,000 yuan; for the ten-thousand-yuan version, each copy is calculated as 10,000 yuan; and so on. The same below) reaches 100,000 yuan or more, the offender shall be convicted and punished according to law.  
Where the number of forged or sold forged special VAT invoices reaches 100 or more, or the cumulative face amount reaches 500,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as “a relatively large quantity.” Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “other serious circumstances”: (1) the amount of illegal gains reaches 10,000 yuan or more; (2) forging and selling forged special VAT invoices in a quantity of 60 or more, or with a cumulative face amount of 300,000 yuan or more; (3) causing serious consequences or having other serious circumstances.  
Where the number of forged or sold forged special VAT invoices reaches 500 or more, or the cumulative face amount reaches 2,500,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as “a huge quantity.” Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “other especially serious circumstances”: (1) the amount of illegal gains reaches 50,000 yuan or more; (2) forging and selling forged special VAT invoices in a quantity of 300 or more, or with a cumulative face amount of 2,000,000 yuan or more; (3) forging or selling forged special VAT invoices in a quantity close to “huge quantity” and having other serious circumstances; (4) causing especially serious consequences or having other especially serious circumstances.  
Where the number of forged and sold forged special VAT invoices reaches 1,000 or more, or the cumulative face amount reaches 10,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as “an especially huge quantity of forged and sold forged special VAT invoices.” Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “especially serious circumstances”: (1) the amount of illegal gains reaches 50,000 yuan or more; (2) causing state tax to be defrauded of 1,000,000 yuan or more due to forging or selling forged special VAT invoices; (3) causing actual losses to state tax of 500,000 yuan or more; (4) having other especially serious circumstances. Where the quantity of forged and sold forged special VAT invoices reaches an especially huge level and there are especially serious circumstances that seriously disrupt the economic order, the offender shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 2, paragraph 2 of the Decision.  
Where the same batch of special VAT invoices is both forged and sold, the quantity or face amount shall not be calculated repeatedly.  
Altering special VAT invoices shall be handled as the act of forging special VAT invoices.

三、根据《决定》第三条规定，非法出售增值税专用发票的，构成非法出售增值税专用发票罪。  
非法出售增值税专用发票案件的定罪量刑数量标准按照本解释第二条第二、三、四款的规定执行。

**Article None** 3. According to the provisions of Article 3 of the Decision, whoever illegally sells special VAT invoices shall constitute the crime of illegally selling special VAT invoices.  
The quantitative standards for conviction and sentencing in cases of illegally selling special VAT invoices shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of paragraphs 2, 3, and 4 of Article 2 of this Interpretation.

四、根据《决定》第四条规定，非法购买增值税专用发票或者购买伪造的增值税专用发票的，构成非法购买增值税专用发票、伪造的增值税专用发票罪。  
非法购买增值税专用发票或者购买伪造的增值税专用发票25份以上或者票面额累计10万元以上的，应当依法定罪处罚。  
非法购买真、伪两种增值税专用发票的，数量累计计算，不实行数罪并罚。

**Article None** 4. According to the provisions of Article 4 of the Decision, whoever illegally purchases special VAT invoices or purchases forged special VAT invoices shall constitute the crime of illegally purchasing special VAT invoices or forged special VAT invoices.  
Where the number of illegally purchased special VAT invoices or purchased forged special VAT invoices reaches 25 or more, or the cumulative face amount reaches 100,000 yuan or more, the offender shall be convicted and punished according to law.  
Where both genuine and forged special VAT invoices are illegally purchased, the quantities shall be calculated cumulatively, and combined punishment for multiple crimes shall not be imposed.

五、根据《决定》第五条规定，虚开用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票的，构成虚开专用发票罪，依照《决定》第一条的规定处罚。  
“用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票”是指可以用于申请出口退税、抵扣税款的非增值税专用发票，如运输发票、废旧物品收购发票、农业产品收购发票等。

**Article None** 5. According to the provisions of Article 5 of the Decision, whoever falsely issues other invoices used for fraudulent export tax refund or tax deduction shall constitute the crime of falsely issuing special invoices and shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 1 of the Decision.  
“Other invoices used for fraudulent export tax refund or tax deduction” refers to non-special VAT invoices that can be used to apply for export tax refund or tax deduction, such as transportation invoices, waste materials purchase invoices, agricultural product purchase invoices, etc.

六、根据《决定》第六条规定，伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造的可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票的，构成非法制造专用发票罪或出售非法制造的专用发票罪。  
伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造的可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票50份以上的，应当依法定罪处罚；伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造的可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票200份以上的，属于“数量巨大”；伪造、擅自制造或者出售伪造、擅自制造的可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票1000份以上的，属于“数量特别巨大”。

**Article None** 6. According to the provisions of Article 6 of the Decision, whoever forges, unlawfully manufactures, or sells forged or unlawfully manufactured other invoices that can be used for fraudulent export tax refund or tax deduction shall constitute the crime of unlawfully manufacturing special invoices or the crime of selling unlawfully manufactured special invoices.  
Where the number of forged, unlawfully manufactured, or sold forged or unlawfully manufactured other invoices that can be used for fraudulent export tax refund or tax deduction reaches 50 or more, the offender shall be convicted and punished according to law; where the number reaches 200 or more, it shall be deemed as “a huge quantity”; where the number reaches 1,000 or more, it shall be deemed as “an especially huge quantity.”

七、盗窃增值税专用发票或者可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票25份以上，或者其他发票50份以上的；诈骗增值税专用发票或者可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票50份以上，或者其他发票100份以上的，依照刑法第一百五十一条的规定处罚。  
盗窃增值税专用发票或者可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票250份以上，或者其他发票500份以上的；诈骗增值税专用发票或者可以用于骗取出口退税、抵扣税款的其他发票500份以上，或者其他发票1000份以上的，依照刑法第一百五十二条的规定处罚。  
盗窃增值税专用发票或者其他发票情节特别严重的，依照《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于严惩严重破坏经济的罪犯的决定》第一条第（一）项的规定处罚。  
盗窃、诈骗增值税专用发票或者其他发票后，又实施《决定》规定的虚开、出售等犯罪的，按照其中的重罪定罪处罚，不实行数罪并罚。

**Article None** 7. Whoever steals 25 or more special VAT invoices or other invoices that can be used for fraudulent export tax refund or tax deduction, or 50 or more other invoices; or whoever fraudulently obtains 50 or more special VAT invoices or other invoices that can be used for fraudulent export tax refund or tax deduction, or 100 or more other invoices, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 151 of the Criminal Law.  
Whoever steals 250 or more special VAT invoices or other invoices that can be used for fraudulent export tax refund or tax deduction, or 500 or more other invoices; or whoever fraudulently obtains 500 or more special VAT invoices or other invoices that can be used for fraudulent export tax refund or tax deduction, or 1,000 or more other invoices, shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 152 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the circumstances of stealing special VAT invoices or other invoices are especially serious, the offender shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 1, paragraph (1) of the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress Regarding the Severe Punishment of Criminals Who Seriously Sabotage the Economy.  
After stealing or fraudulently obtaining special VAT invoices or other invoices, if the offender subsequently commits crimes such as falsely issuing or selling as provided in the Decision, the offender shall be convicted and punished according to the heavier crime, and combined punishment for multiple crimes shall not be imposed.
