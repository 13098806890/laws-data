# 最高人民法院关于审理洗钱等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕15号  
**公布日期**：2009-09-21  
**生效日期**：2009-11-11

> 最高人民法院《关于审理洗钱等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2009年9月21日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1474次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年11月11日起施行。 2009年11月4日 最高人民法院 关于审理洗钱等刑事案件具体应用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2009〕15号 为依法惩治洗钱，掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益，资助恐怖活动等犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现就审理此类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 刑法第一百九十一条、第三百一十二条规定的“明知”，应当结合被告人的认知能力，接触他人犯罪所得及其收益的情况，犯罪所得及其收益的种类、数额，犯罪所得及其收益的转换、转移方式以及被告人的供述等主、客观因素进行认定。 具有下列情形之一的，可以认定被告人明知系犯罪所得及其收益，但有证据证明确实不知道的除外： （一）知道他人从事犯罪活动，协助转换或者转移财物的； （二）没有正当理由，通过非法途径协助转换或者转移财物的； （三）没有正当理由，以明显低于市场的价格收购财物的； （四）没有正当理由，协助转换或者转移财物，收取明显高于市场的“手续费”的； （五）没有正当理由，协助他人将巨额现金散存于多个银行账户或者在不同银行账户之间频繁划转的； （六）协助近亲属或者其他关系密切的人转换或者转移与其职业或者财产状况明显不符的财物的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 [刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)、第三百一十二条规定的“明知”，应当结合被告人的认知能力，接触他人犯罪所得及其收益的情况，犯罪所得及其收益的种类、数额，犯罪所得及其收益的转换、转移方式以及被告人的供述等主、客观因素进行认定。  
具有下列情形之一的，可以认定被告人明知系犯罪所得及其收益，但有证据证明确实不知道的除外：  
（一）知道他人从事犯罪活动，协助转换或者转移财物的；  
（二）没有正当理由，通过非法途径协助转换或者转移财物的；  
（三）没有正当理由，以明显低于市场的价格收购财物的；  
（四）没有正当理由，协助转换或者转移财物，收取明显高于市场的“手续费”的；  
（五）没有正当理由，协助他人将巨额现金散存于多个银行账户或者在不同银行账户之间频繁划转的；  
（六）协助近亲属或者其他关系密切的人转换或者转移与其职业或者财产状况明显不符的财物的；  
（七）其他可以认定行为人明知的情形。  
被告人将[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)规定的某一上游犯罪的犯罪所得及其收益误认为[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)规定的上游犯罪范围内的其他犯罪所得及其收益的，不影响[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)规定的“明知”的认定。

**Article 1** : The 'knowing' as prescribed in Articles 191 and 312 of the Criminal Law shall be determined by combining subjective and objective factors such as the defendant's cognitive ability, the circumstances of contacting the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, the type and amount of the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, the method of conversion or transfer of the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, and the defendant's confessions.  
Under any of the following circumstances, it may be determined that the defendant knew that it was proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, unless there is evidence proving that the defendant genuinely did not know:  
(1) Knowing that another person was engaged in criminal activities, assisting in the conversion or transfer of property;  
(2) Without legitimate reasons, assisting in the conversion or transfer of property through illegal means;  
(3) Without legitimate reasons, purchasing property at a price clearly lower than the market price;  
(4) Without legitimate reasons, assisting in the conversion or transfer of property and charging a 'handling fee' clearly higher than the market rate;  
(5) Without legitimate reasons, assisting another person in depositing huge amounts of cash in multiple bank accounts or frequently transferring funds between different bank accounts;  
(6) Assisting a close relative or other person with a close relationship in converting or transferring property that is clearly inconsistent with their occupation or financial status;  
(7) Other circumstances that may establish that the actor knew.  
If the defendant mistakenly believes that the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom from a particular predicate crime under Article 191 of the Criminal Law are the proceeds and income derived from another predicate crime within the scope of the predicate crimes under Article 191, this shall not affect the determination of 'knowing' under Article 191 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)第一款第（五）项规定的“以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益的来源和性质”：  
（一）通过典当、租赁、买卖、投资等方式，协助转移、转换犯罪所得及其收益的；  
（二）通过与商场、饭店、娱乐场所等现金密集型场所的经营收入相混合的方式，协助转移、转换犯罪所得及其收益的；  
（三）通过虚构交易、虚设债权债务、虚假担保、虚报收入等方式，协助将犯罪所得及其收益转换为“合法”财物的；  
（四）通过买卖彩票、奖券等方式，协助转换犯罪所得及其收益的；  
（五）通过赌博方式，协助将犯罪所得及其收益转换为赌博收益的；  
（六）协助将犯罪所得及其收益携带、运输或者邮寄出入境的；  
（七）通过前述规定以外的方式协助转移、转换犯罪所得及其收益的。

**Article 2** : Any of the following circumstances may be determined as 'disguising or concealing the source and nature of proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom by other methods' as prescribed in subparagraph (5) of the first paragraph of Article 191 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Assisting in the transfer or conversion of proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom through methods such as pawn, lease, sale, investment, etc.;  
(2) Assisting in the transfer or conversion of proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom by mixing them with operating income of cash-intensive venues such as shopping malls, restaurants, entertainment venues, etc.;  
(3) Assisting in converting proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom into 'legal' property through methods such as fictitious transactions, fictitious claims and debts, false guarantees, false declarations of income, etc.;  
(4) Assisting in the conversion of proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom through methods such as buying and selling lottery tickets, prize coupons, etc.;  
(5) Assisting in converting proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom into gambling proceeds through gambling methods;  
(6) Assisting in carrying, transporting or mailing proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom into or out of the country;  
(7) Assisting in the transfer or conversion of proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom through methods other than those specified above.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 明知是犯罪所得及其产生的收益而予以掩饰、隐瞒，构成[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的犯罪，同时又构成[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)或者第三百四十九条规定的犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 3** : If a person knowingly disguises or conceals proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom, constituting a crime under Article 312 of the Criminal Law, and at the same time constitutes a crime under Article 191 or Article 349 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision providing for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 [刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)、第三百一十二条、第三百四十九条规定的犯罪，应当以上游犯罪事实成立为认定前提。上游犯罪尚未依法裁判，但查证属实的，不影响[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)、第三百一十二条、第三百四十九条规定的犯罪的审判。  
上游犯罪事实可以确认，因行为人死亡等原因依法不予追究刑事责任的，不影响[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)、第三百一十二条、第三百四十九条规定的犯罪的认定。  
上游犯罪事实可以确认，依法以其他罪名定罪处罚的，不影响[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)、第三百一十二条、第三百四十九条规定的犯罪的认定。  
本条所称“上游犯罪”，是指产生[刑法第一百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-191)、第三百一十二条、第三百四十九条规定的犯罪所得及其收益的各种犯罪行为。

**Article 4** : For the crimes under Articles 191, 312, and 349 of the Criminal Law, the establishment of the predicate crime facts shall be the prerequisite for determination. Where the predicate crime has not yet been adjudicated according to law, but the facts have been verified, this shall not affect the adjudication of the crimes under Articles 191, 312, and 349 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the facts of the predicate crime can be confirmed, but criminal liability is not pursued according to law due to the death of the actor or other reasons, this shall not affect the determination of the crimes under Articles 191, 312, and 349 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the facts of the predicate crime can be confirmed, but the person is convicted and punished under another charge according to law, this shall not affect the determination of the crimes under Articles 191, 312, and 349 of the Criminal Law.  
The term 'predicate crime' in this Article refers to any criminal act that generates the proceeds of crime and the income derived therefrom as prescribed in Articles 191, 312, and 349 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 [刑法第一百二十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-120)之一规定的“资助”，是指为恐怖活动组织或者实施恐怖活动的个人筹集、提供经费、物资或者提供场所以及其他物质便利的行为。  
[刑法第一百二十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-120)之一规定的“实施恐怖活动的个人”，包括预谋实施、准备实施和实际实施恐怖活动的个人。

**Article 5** : 'Funding' as prescribed in Article 120-1 of the Criminal Law refers to the act of raising or providing funds, materials, or providing venues or other material conveniences for a terrorist organization or an individual who commits terrorist activities.  
'Individuals who commit terrorist activities' as prescribed in Article 120-1 of the Criminal Law include individuals who premeditate, prepare for, and actually commit terrorist activities.
