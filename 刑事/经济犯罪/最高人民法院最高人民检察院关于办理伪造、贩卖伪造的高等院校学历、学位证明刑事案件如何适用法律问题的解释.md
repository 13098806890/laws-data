### 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理伪造、贩卖伪造的高等院校学历、学位证明刑事案件如何适用法律问题的解释

证明刑事案件如何适用法律问题的解释

法释〔2001〕22号  
  
为依法惩处伪造、贩卖伪造的高等院校学历、学位证明的犯罪活动，现就办理这类案件适用法律的有关问题解释如下：  
  
对于伪造高等院校印章制作学历、学位证明的行为，应当依照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)第二款的规定，以伪造事业单位印章罪定罪处罚。  
  
明知是伪造高等院校印章制作的学历、学位证明而贩卖的，以伪造事业单位印章罪的共犯论处。

**Article 1** Interpretation No.22 [2001] of the Supreme People's Court  
For the purpose of punishing according to law the criminal activities of forging and selling forged diplomas and degree certificates of institutions of higher education, the following interpretation is hereby given on the relevant issues concerning the application of law in handling such cases:  
Any act of forging seals of institutions of higher education to produce diplomas or degree certificates shall be convicted and punished as the crime of forging seals of public institutions in accordance with the provision of paragraph 2 of Article 280 of the Criminal Law.  
Any person who knowingly sells diplomas or degree certificates produced by forging seals of institutions of higher education shall be punished as an accomplice to the crime of forging seals of public institutions.
