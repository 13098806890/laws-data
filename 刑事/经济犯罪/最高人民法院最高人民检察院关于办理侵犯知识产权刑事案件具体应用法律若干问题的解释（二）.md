# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）

**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕6号  
**公布日期**：2007-04-04

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》已于2007年4月4日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1422次会议、最高人民检察院第十届检察委员会第75次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年4月5日起施行。 2007年4月5日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体 应用法律若干问题的解释（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  以营利为目的，未经著作权人许可，复制发行其文字作品、音乐、电影、电视、录像作品、计算机软件及其他作品，复制品数量合计在五百张（份）以上的，属于[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“有其他严重情节”；复制品数量在二千五百张（份）以上的，属于[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“有其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 1** Where, for the purpose of profit-making, without permission of the copyright owner, a person reproduces and distributes his or her written works, musical works, cinematographic works, television works, video works, computer software or other works, and the total number of copies is 500 (copies) or more, it shall fall under the circumstances of "having other serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 217 of the Criminal Law; where the total number of copies is 2,500 (copies) or more, it shall fall under the circumstances of "having other especially serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 217 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  [刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)侵犯著作权罪中的“复制发行”，包括复制、发行或者既复制又发行的行为。  
侵权产品的持有人通过广告、征订等方式推销侵权产品的，属于[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的“发行”。  
非法出版、复制、发行他人作品，侵犯著作权构成犯罪的，按照侵犯著作权罪定罪处罚。

**Article 2** The term "reproduction and distribution" in the crime of copyright infringement under Article 217 of the Criminal Law includes the acts of reproduction, distribution, or both reproduction and distribution.  
Where a holder of infringing products promotes the sale of such products through advertisements, subscriptions, or other means, it shall constitute "distribution" as stipulated in Article 217 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person illegally publishes, reproduces, or distributes works of others, thereby infringing copyright and constituting a crime, he or she shall be convicted and punished according to the crime of copyright infringement.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  侵犯知识产权犯罪，符合刑法规定的缓刑条件的，依法适用缓刑。有下列情形之一的，一般不适用缓刑：  
（一）因侵犯知识产权被刑事处罚或者行政处罚后，再次侵犯知识产权构成犯罪的；  
（二）不具有悔罪表现的；  
（三）拒不交出违法所得的；  
（四）其他不宜适用缓刑的情形。

**Article 3** For crimes of infringing intellectual property rights, if the conditions for probation as prescribed by the Criminal Law are satisfied, probation shall be granted according to law. In any of the following circumstances, probation shall generally not be granted:  
(1) Where after being subjected to criminal punishment or administrative penalty for infringement of intellectual property rights, the person again infringes intellectual property rights and constitutes a crime;  
(2) Where there is no manifestation of repentance;  
(3) Where the person refuses to surrender the illegal gains;  
(4) Other circumstances where probation is not appropriate.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  对于侵犯知识产权犯罪的，人民法院应当综合考虑犯罪的违法所得、非法经营数额、给权利人造成的损失、社会危害性等情节，依法判处罚金。罚金数额一般在违法所得的一倍以上五倍以下，或者按照非法经营数额的50％以上一倍以下确定。

**Article 4** For crimes of infringing intellectual property rights, the People's Court shall, in accordance with law, impose a fine by comprehensively considering the circumstances such as the illegal income from the crime, the amount of illegal business operations, the losses caused to the right holder, and the degree of social harm. The amount of the fine shall generally be between one and five times the illegal income, or be determined at 50% or more but not exceeding one time the amount of illegal business operations.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  被害人有证据证明的侵犯知识产权刑事案件，直接向人民法院起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理；严重危害社会秩序和国家利益的侵犯知识产权刑事案件，由人民检察院依法提起公诉。

**Article 5** Where a victim has evidence to prove a criminal case of infringement of intellectual property rights and directly brings a lawsuit to the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law; criminal cases of infringement of intellectual property rights that seriously endanger social order and national interests shall be prosecuted by the People's Procuratorate according to law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  单位实施[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)至第二百一十九条规定的行为，按照《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯知识产权刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》和本解释规定的相应个人犯罪的定罪量刑标准定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where a unit commits acts as stipulated in Articles 213 to 219 of the Criminal Law, the unit shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards for corresponding individual crimes as prescribed in the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Infringement of Intellectual Property Rights and this Interpretation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 7** Where any previously issued judicial interpretation is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
