# 最高人民法院关于办理非法制造、买卖、运输非军用枪支、弹药刑事案件适用法律问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1995〕20号  
**公布日期**：1995-09-20

---

### 正文

一、非法制造、买卖、运输非军用枪支、非军用枪支主要零部件或者其专用弹药，构成犯罪的，依照刑法第一百一十二条的规定定罪处罚。  
非军用枪支是指射击运动枪、猎枪、麻醉注射枪、气枪、钢珠枪、催泪枪、电击枪以及其他足以致人伤亡或者使人丧失知觉的枪支。

**Article None** I. Whoever illegally manufactures, purchases, sells, or transports non-military firearms, major components of non-military firearms, or their special ammunition, if the act constitutes a crime, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 112 of the Criminal Law.  
Non-military firearms refer to shooting sports guns, hunting guns, anesthetic injection guns, air guns, steel ball guns, tear gas guns, stun guns, and other guns sufficient to cause casualties or make people lose consciousness.

二、非法制造、买卖、运输非军用枪支、弹药，有下列情形之一的，依照刑法第一百一十二条规定，判处七年以下有期徒刑：  
1、制造非军用枪支1支或者买卖、运输2支以上的；  
2、制造非军用枪支成套散件1套以上或者买卖、运输2套以上的；  
3、制造非军用枪支主要零部件50件以上或者买卖、运输100件以上的；  
4、制造非军用枪支专用子弹500发以上或者买卖、运输1000发以上的；  
5、虽未达到上述各项最低数量标准．但具有其他情形，应依法追究刑事责任的。

**Article None** II. Whoever illegally manufactures, purchases, sells, or transports non-military firearms or ammunition, in any of the following circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than seven years in accordance with Article 112 of the Criminal Law:  
1. Manufacturing one non-military firearm or purchasing, selling, or transporting two or more thereof;  
2. Manufacturing one or more complete sets of non-military firearm components or purchasing, selling, or transporting two or more sets;  
3. Manufacturing 50 or more major components of non-military firearms or purchasing, selling, or transporting 100 or more thereof;  
4. Manufacturing 500 or more special bullets for non-military firearms or purchasing, selling, or transporting 1,000 or more thereof;  
5. Although the minimum quantity standards set forth in the above items have not been reached, if there are other circumstances, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

三、非法制造、买卖、运输非军用枪支、弹药的数量达到本解释第二条规定的各项最低数量标准5倍以上，或者具有其他严重情节的，依照刑法第一百一十二条规定，判处七年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑。

**Article None** III. If the quantity of illegally manufactured, purchased, sold, or transported non-military firearms or ammunition reaches five times or more the minimum quantity standards set forth in Article 2 of this Interpretation, or if there are other serious circumstances, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years or life imprisonment in accordance with Article 112 of the Criminal Law.

四、非法制造、买卖、运输非军用枪支、弹药情节特别严重，或者造成严重后果的，依照全国人大常委会《关于严惩严重危害社会治安的犯罪分子的决定》第一条第四项的规定处罚。  
1995年9月20日

**Article None** IV. If the circumstances of illegally manufacturing, purchasing, selling, or transporting non-military firearms or ammunition are especially serious, or if serious consequences are caused, the offender shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Item 4 of Article 1 of the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Severely Punishing Criminals Who Seriously Endanger Public Security.  
September 20, 1995.
