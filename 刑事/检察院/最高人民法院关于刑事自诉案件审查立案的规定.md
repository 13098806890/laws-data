# 最高人民法院关于刑事自诉案件审查立案的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1993〕25号  
**公布日期**：1993-09-24

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　刑事自诉案件是指刑法规定的告诉才处理和其他不需要进行侦查，由人民法院直接受理的轻徽刑事案件。

**Article 1** Criminal private prosecution cases refer to minor criminal cases that, as provided for in the Criminal Law, are handled only upon complaint and do not require investigation, and are directly accepted by the People's Court.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院受理下列刑事自诉案件：  
（一）[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第一款中有原告和被告，明显属于轻伤害，因果关系清楚，不需要进行侦查的伤害案：  
（二）[刑法第一百四十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-145)中告诉才处理并且不需要侦查的侮辱、诽谤案：  
（三）[刑法第一百七十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-179)第一款暴力干涉婚姻自由案：  
（四）[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)重婚案，但人民检察院提起公诉的除外：  
（五）[刑法第一百八十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-181)破坏现役军人婚姻案：  
（六）刑法第一八十二条第一款虐待案：  
（七）[刑法第一百八十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-183)遗弃案：  
（八）法律规定有人民法院直接受理的其他刑事案件。

**Article 2** The People's Court shall accept the following criminal private prosecution cases:  
(1) Injury cases under Article 134(1) of the Criminal Law where there are a plaintiff and a defendant, obviously involving minor injury, with clear causal relationship, and requiring no investigation;  
(2) Cases of insult and defamation under Article 145 of the Criminal Law that are handled only upon complaint and require no investigation;  
(3) Cases of interference with the freedom of marriage through violence under Article 179(1) of the Criminal Law;  
(4) Cases of bigamy under Article 180 of the Criminal Law, except those initiated by the People's Procuratorate;  
(5) Cases of disrupting marriages of active servicemen under Article 181 of the Criminal Law;  
(6) Cases of maltreatment under Article 182(1) of the Criminal Law;  
(7) Cases of abandonment under Article 183 of the Criminal Law;  
(8) Other criminal cases that are directly accepted by the People's Court as provided by law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　[刑法第一百五十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-157)中抗拒执行判决、裁定案由人民法院直接立案审理。

**Article 3** Cases of resisting enforcement of judgments or orders under Article 157 of the Criminal Law shall be directly filed and adjudicated by the People's Court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　刑事自诉案件由犯罪地的基层人民法院审判，如果由被告人居住地的基层人民法院审判更为适宜的，也可由被告人居住地的人民法院审判。几个法院都有管辖权的，由最先受理的人民法院审判。

**Article 4** Criminal private prosecution cases shall be adjudicated by the Primary People's Court of the place where the crime was committed. If adjudication by the Primary People's Court of the place where the defendant resides is more appropriate, the case may also be adjudicated by the People's Court of the place where the defendant resides. If several People's Courts have jurisdiction, the case shall be adjudicated by the People's Court that first accepted the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　刑事自诉案件的自诉人、被告人一方或双方是外国人的，由犯的中级人民法院审判。  
自诉人是外国人的，应当提供本人国籍的证明。

**Article 5** Where one or both of the private prosecutor and the defendant in a criminal private prosecution case are foreigners, the case shall be adjudicated by the Intermediate People's Court of the place where the crime was committed.  
Where the private prosecutor is a foreigner, he or she shall provide proof of his or her nationality.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　刑事自诉案件的自诉人、被告人一方或双方是港、澳、台同胞的，由犯罪地的基层人民法院审判。  
港、澳、台同胞告诉的，应当出示港、澳、台居民身份证、回乡证或其他能证明本人身份的证明。

**Article 6** Where one or both of the private prosecutor and the defendant in a criminal private prosecution case are compatriots from Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan, the case shall be adjudicated by the Primary People's Court of the place where the crime was committed.  
Where a compatriot from Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan lodges a complaint, he or she shall present his or her Hong Kong, Macao, or Taiwan resident identity card, home visit certificate, or other certificate capable of proving his or her identity.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　刑事自诉案件的自诉人应当是本案的被害人。被害人因受强制、威吓无法告诉的，人民检察院和被害人的近亲属也可以告诉。被害人是未成年人、无行为能力或限制行为能力人以及因年老、患病、聋、哑等不能亲自告诉的，其近亲属可以代为告诉。  
近亲属代为告诉的，应提供与被害人关系的证明，同时提供被害人系未成年人，无行为能力或限制行为能力和聋、哑、患病等证明。

**Article 7** The private prosecutor in a criminal private prosecution case shall be the victim of the case. Where the victim is unable to lodge a complaint because of coercion or intimidation, the People's Procuratorate and the victim's close relatives may also lodge a complaint. Where the victim is a minor, a person with no capacity or limited capacity for civil conduct, or is unable to lodge a complaint in person due to old age, illness, deafness, muteness, etc., his or her close relatives may lodge a complaint on his or her behalf.  
Where close relatives lodge a complaint on behalf of the victim, they shall provide proof of their relationship with the victim, and also provide proof that the victim is a minor, has no capacity or limited capacity for civil conduct, or is deaf, mute, ill, etc.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　刑事自诉案件的被害人由于被告人的犯罪行为而遭受经济损失的，可以同时提起附带民事诉讼。

**Article 8** Where the victim of a criminal private prosecution case suffers economic losses due to the criminal act of the defendant, the victim may simultaneously file an incidental civil action.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　自诉必须在法定的犯罪追诉期限内提起。

**Article 9** A private prosecution must be initiated within the statutory limitation period for criminal prosecution.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　自诉人应当向人民法院提交自诉状；提交自诉状确有困难的，可以口头告诉，由人民法院的审判人员作出笔录，向自诉人宣读，自诉人认为没有错误后，应当签名或者盖章。自诉人提交外文自诉状的应当附中文译本。

**Article 10** The private prosecutor shall submit a private prosecution complaint to the People's Court. If it is truly difficult to submit a written complaint, he or she may lodge an oral complaint, whereupon the judicial officers of the People's Court shall make a record, read it to the private prosecutor, and after the private prosecutor acknowledges that there is no error, shall sign or affix his or her seal. Where the private prosecutor submits a private prosecution complaint in a foreign language, a Chinese translation shall be attached.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　自诉状或告诉笔录应当包括以下内容：  
（一）诉状的名称为《刑事自诉状》。附带民事诉讼的为《刑事附带民事自诉状》。  
（二）自诉人、被告人、代为告诉的近亲属的姓名、性别、年龄、民族、籍贯、职业、工作单位和住址。  
（三）被告人犯罪行为的时间、地点、手段、情节和危害后果等。  
（四）具体的诉讼请求。  
（五）致送人民法院的名称及具状时间。  
（六）物证和书证名称、件数等。

**Article 11** A private prosecution complaint or complaint record shall include the following contents:  
(1) The title of the complaint shall be "Criminal Private Prosecution Complaint." For an incidental civil action, it shall be "Criminal and Incidental Civil Private Prosecution Complaint."  
(2) The name, sex, age, ethnicity, place of origin, occupation, work unit, and address of the private prosecutor, the defendant, and the close relatives lodging the complaint on behalf of the victim.  
(3) The time, place, means, circumstances, and harmful consequences of the defendant's criminal act.  
(4) The specific claims for relief.  
(5) The name of the People's Court to which the complaint is addressed and the date of submission.  
(6) The name and number of items of physical evidence and documentary evidence.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　刑事自诉案件的自诉人，应当向人民法院提供被告人犯罪的证据或证据线索。

**Article 12** The private prosecutor in a criminal private prosecution case shall provide the People's Court with evidence or clues to evidence of the defendant's crime.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　人民法院对自诉案件要认真进行审查，对符合本规定的，应当在收到自诉状或口头告诉之日起十五日内立案，并书面通知自诉人。对不符合立案条件的，应在十五日内书面通知自诉人并说明不予受理的原因。自诉人坚持告诉的，人民法院应当裁定驳回起诉。

**Article 13** The People's Court shall carefully examine cases of private prosecution. For those that comply with these Provisions, it shall file the case within fifteen days from the date of receipt of the private prosecution complaint or oral complaint, and shall notify the private prosecutor in writing. For those that do not meet the conditions for case filing, it shall within fifteen days notify the private prosecutor in writing and explain the reasons for non-acceptance. If the private prosecutor insists on lodging a complaint, the People's Court shall make a ruling to dismiss the prosecution.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院在审查立案时，如认为证据不足，可以限期让自诉人补充证据，人民法院也可以自行调查，收集证据。

**Article 14** When examining a case for filing, if the People's Court considers that the evidence is insufficient, it may set a time limit for the private prosecutor to supplement evidence, and the People's Court may also conduct its own investigation and collect evidence.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　自诉人不得诽谤诬陷被告人。人民法院审查立案时，应当向自诉人说明诽谤诬陷应负的法律责任。

**Article 15** The private prosecutor shall not slander or frame the defendant. When examining a case for filing, the People's Court shall inform the private prosecutor of the legal liability for slander or framing.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　对已决定立案的自诉案件，自诉人应当向人民法院提交与被告人人数相等的自诉状副本。口头告诉的，由自诉人按被告人人数复制告诉笔录。

**Article 16** For a private prosecution case that has been decided to be filed, the private prosecutor shall submit to the People's Court copies of the private prosecution complaint equal in number to the number of defendants. For oral complaints, the private prosecutor shall make copies of the complaint record according to the number of defendants.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　自诉人明知侵害人是二人以上，但只对部分侵害人提出告诉的，人民法院应当受理，并视为对其他侵害人放弃告诉权利。判决宣告后自诉人又对其他侵害人提出告诉的，人民法院不再受理。

**Article 17** Where the private prosecutor knows that there are two or more infringers but only lodges a complaint against some of them, the People's Court shall accept the case and shall deem it as a waiver of the right to lodge a complaint against the other infringers. If after the judgment is pronounced the private prosecutor lodges a complaint against the other infringers, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　共同被害人中只有部分人告诉的，人民法院应当通知其他被害人参加诉讼。被通知人接到通知后，至一审宣判时未提出告诉的，即视为放弃告诉权利。一审宣判后，被通知人就同一事实提出告诉的，人民法院不予受理。

**Article 18** Where only some of the co-victims lodge a complaint, the People's Court shall notify the other victims to participate in the proceedings. If the notified person, after receiving the notification, does not lodge a complaint by the time of first-instance adjudication, he or she shall be deemed to have waived the right to lodge a complaint. After the first-instance judgment is pronounced, if the notified person lodges a complaint about the same facts, the People's Court shall not accept it.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　刑事自诉案件经审查有下列情形之一的，人民法院不予受理：  
（一）犯罪已过刑法规定的追诉时效期限的；  
（二）被告人死亡的；  
（三）被告人下落不明的；  
（四）不属自诉案件范围的；  
（五）自诉人撤诉后，就同一事实又告诉的，但因证据不足而撤诉的除外；  
（六）经人民法院调解结案后，自诉人反悔，就同一事实再行告诉的；  
（七）民事案件结案后，自诉人就同一事实再行提出刑事自诉的。

**Article 19** Where, upon examination, a criminal private prosecution case falls under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall not accept it:  
(1) The crime has exceeded the limitation period for prosecution as prescribed by the Criminal Law;  
(2) The defendant is deceased;  
(3) The defendant's whereabouts are unknown;  
(4) The case does not fall within the scope of private prosecution cases;  
(5) After the private prosecutor withdraws the complaint, he or she lodges another complaint about the same facts, except where the withdrawal was due to insufficient evidence;  
(6) After the case is concluded through mediation by the People's Court, the private prosecutor reneges and lodges another complaint about the same facts;  
(7) After a civil case is concluded, the private prosecutor initiates a criminal private prosecution about the same facts.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　刑事自诉案件由人民法院负责立案工作的审判庭和人民法庭审查立案。

**Article 20** Criminal private prosecution cases shall be examined and filed by the adjudication division and people's tribunals responsible for case filing work of the People's Court.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　本规定自一九九三年十月一日起施行。  
1993年9月24日

**Article 21** These Provisions shall come into force as of October 1, 1993.  
September 24, 1993
