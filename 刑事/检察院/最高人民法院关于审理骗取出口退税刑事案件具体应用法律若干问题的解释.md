# 最高人民法院关于审理骗取出口退税刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕30号  
**公布日期**：2002-09-09  
**生效日期**：2002-09-23

> （2002年9月9日最高人民法院审判委员会第1241次会议通过，自2002年9月23日起施行。） 法释〔2002〕30号 为依法惩治骗取出口退税犯罪活动，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关规定，现就审理骗取出口退税刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 刑法第二百零四条规定的“假报出口”，是指以虚构已税货物出口事实为目的，具有下列情形之一的行为： （一）伪造或者签订虚假的买卖合同； （二）以伪造、变造或者其他非法手段取得出口货物报关单、出口收汇核销单、出口货物专用缴款书等有关出口退税单据、凭证； （三）虚开、伪造、非法购买增值税专用发票或者其他可以用于出口退税的发票； （四）其他虚构已税货物出口事实的行为。 第二条 具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百零四条规定的“其他欺骗手段”： （一）骗取出口货物退税资格的； （二）将未纳税或者免税货物作为已税货物出口的； （三）虽有货物出口，但虚构该出口货物的品名、数量、单价等要素，骗取未实际纳税部分出口退税款的； （四）以其他手段骗取出口退税款的。 第三条 骗取国家出口退税款5万元以上的，为刑法第二百零四条规定的“数额较大”；骗取国家出口退税款50万元以上的，为刑法第二百零四条规定的“数额巨大”；骗取国家出口退税款250万元以上的，为刑法第二百零四条规定的“数额特别巨大”。 第四条 具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第二百零四条规定的“其他严重情节”：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 [刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)规定的“假报出口”，是指以虚构已税货物出口事实为目的，具有下列情形之一的行为：  
（一）伪造或者签订虚假的买卖合同；  
（二）以伪造、变造或者其他非法手段取得出口货物报关单、出口收汇核销单、出口货物专用缴款书等有关出口退税单据、凭证；  
（三）虚开、伪造、非法购买增值税专用发票或者其他可以用于出口退税的发票；  
（四）其他虚构已税货物出口事实的行为。

**Article 1** The "false declaration of export" as provided for in Article 204 of the Criminal Law shall refer to conduct that, for the purpose of fabricating facts of export of taxed goods, falls under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) Forging or signing a false sales contract;  
(2) Obtaining export goods customs declarations, export proceeds verification and cancellation certificates, special payment certificates for export goods, and other documents and vouchers related to export tax refunds by means of forgery, alteration, or other illegal means;  
(3) Falsely issuing, forging, or illegally purchasing special value-added tax invoices or other invoices that may be used for export tax refunds;  
(4) Other acts of fabricating facts of export of taxed goods.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)规定的“其他欺骗手段”：  
（一）骗取出口货物退税资格的；  
（二）将未纳税或者免税货物作为已税货物出口的；  
（三）虽有货物出口，但虚构该出口货物的品名、数量、单价等要素，骗取未实际纳税部分出口退税款的；  
（四）以其他手段骗取出口退税款的。

**Article 2** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as the "other fraudulent means" as provided for in Article 204 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Fraudulently obtaining the qualification for export tax refund on export goods;  
(2) Exporting untaxed or tax-exempt goods as taxed goods;  
(3) Although goods are exported, fabricating elements such as the name, quantity, or unit price of the exported goods to fraudulently obtain export tax refund for the portion on which tax has not actually been paid;  
(4) Obtaining export tax refunds by other means.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 骗取国家出口退税款5万元以上的，为[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)规定的“数额较大”；骗取国家出口退税款50万元以上的，为[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)规定的“数额巨大”；骗取国家出口退税款250万元以上的，为[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)规定的“数额特别巨大”。

**Article 3** Fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund of 50,000 yuan or more shall constitute the "relatively large amount" as provided for in Article 204 of the Criminal Law; fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund of 500,000 yuan or more shall constitute the "large amount" as provided for in Article 204 of the Criminal Law; fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund of 2,500,000 yuan or more shall constitute the "extremely large amount" as provided for in Article 204 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）造成国家税款损失30万元以上并且在第一审判决宣告前无法追回的；  
（二）因骗取国家出口退税行为受过行政处罚，两年内又骗取国家出口退税款数额在30万元以上的；  
（三）情节严重的其他情形。

**Article 4** Any of the following circumstances shall fall under the "other serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 204 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing national tax losses of more than 300,000 yuan that cannot be recovered before the pronouncement of the first-instance judgment;  
(2) Having been subject to an administrative penalty for fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund, and within two years, again fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund of more than 300,000 yuan;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)规定的“其他特别严重情节”：  
（一）造成国家税款损失150万元以上并且在第一审判决宣告前无法追回的；  
（二）因骗取国家出口退税行为受过行政处罚，两年内又骗取国家出口退税款数额在150万元以上的；  
（三）情节特别严重的其他情形。

**Article 5** Any of the following circumstances shall fall under the "other especially serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 204 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing national tax losses of more than 1,500,000 yuan that cannot be recovered before the pronouncement of the first-instance judgment;  
(2) Having been subject to an administrative penalty for fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund, and within two years, again fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund of more than 1,500,000 yuan;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are especially serious.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 有进出口经营权的公司、企业，明知他人意欲骗取国家出口退税款，仍违反国家有关进出口经营的规定，允许他人自带客户、自带货源、自带汇票并自行报关，骗取国家出口退税款的，依照[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)第一款、第二百一十一条的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where a company or enterprise with the right to engage in import and export operations, knowing that another person intends to fraudulently obtain a national export tax refund, still violates the relevant state regulations on import and export operations by allowing such other person to bring their own customers, bring their own sources of goods, bring their own bills of exchange, and handle customs declaration on their own, thereby fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund, such company or enterprise shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 204, paragraph 1, and Article 211 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 实施骗取国家出口退税行为，没有实际取得出口退税款的，可以比照既遂犯从轻或者减轻处罚。

**Article 7** Where a person commits the act of fraudulently obtaining a national export tax refund but does not actually obtain the export tax refund, such person may be given a lighter or mitigated punishment by reference to that for a completed offense.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 国家工作人员参与实施骗取出口退税犯罪活动的，依照[刑法第二百零四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-204)第一款的规定从重处罚。

**Article 8** Where a state functionary participates in the commission of criminal activities of fraudulently obtaining export tax refunds, such person shall be given a severe punishment in accordance with the provisions of Article 204, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 实施骗取出口退税犯罪，同时构成虚开增值税专用发票罪等其他犯罪的，依照刑法处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
2002年9月17日

**Article 9** Where a person commits the crime of fraudulently obtaining export tax refunds and such act concurrently constitutes other crimes such as the crime of falsely issuing special value-added tax invoices, such person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law that prescribe a heavier punishment.  
September 17, 2002
