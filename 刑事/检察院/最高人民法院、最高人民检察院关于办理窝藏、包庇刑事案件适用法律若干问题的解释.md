### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　明知是犯罪的人，为帮助其逃匿，实施下列行为之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-310)第一款的规定，以窝藏罪定罪处罚：  
（一）为犯罪的人提供房屋或者其他可以用于隐藏的处所的；  
（二）为犯罪的人提供车辆、船只、航空器等交通工具，或者提供手机等通讯工具的；  
（三）为犯罪的人提供金钱的；  
（四）其他为犯罪的人提供隐藏处所、财物，帮助其逃匿的情形。  
保证人在犯罪的人取保候审期间，协助其逃匿，或者明知犯罪的人的藏匿地点、联系方式，但拒绝向司法机关提供的，应当依照[刑法第三百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-310)第一款的规定，对保证人以窝藏罪定罪处罚。  
虽然为犯罪的人提供隐藏处所、财物，但不是出于帮助犯罪的人逃匿的目的，不以窝藏罪定罪处罚；对未履行法定报告义务的行为人，依法移送有关主管机关给予行政处罚。

**Article 1** : Where a person knowingly provides for a criminal, with the purpose of assisting the criminal to escape, any of the following acts, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of harboring in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 310 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) providing the criminal with a house or other premises that can be used for concealment;  
(2) providing the criminal with vehicles, vessels, aircraft or other means of transportation, or providing mobile phones or other communication tools;  
(3) providing the criminal with money;  
(4) other circumstances where a hiding place or property is provided for the criminal to assist the criminal to escape.  
If a surety, during the period when the criminal is released on bail pending investigation or trial, assists the criminal to escape, or knowingly knows the criminal's hiding place or contact information but refuses to provide it to the judicial organs, the surety shall be convicted and punished for the crime of harboring in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 310 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a hiding place or property is provided for the criminal, but not for the purpose of assisting the criminal to escape, the person shall not be convicted and punished for the crime of harboring; a person who fails to perform the statutory reporting obligation shall be transferred according to law to the relevant competent authority for administrative penalty.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　明知是犯罪的人，为帮助其逃避刑事追究，或者帮助其获得从宽处罚，实施下列行为之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-310)第一款的规定，以包庇罪定罪处罚：  
（一）故意顶替犯罪的人欺骗司法机关的；  
（二）故意向司法机关作虚假陈述或者提供虚假证明，以证明犯罪的人没有实施犯罪行为，或者犯罪的人所实施行为不构成犯罪的；  
（三）故意向司法机关提供虚假证明，以证明犯罪的人具有法定从轻、减轻、免除处罚情节的；  
（四）其他作假证明包庇的行为。

**Article 2** : Where a person knowingly provides for a criminal, with the purpose of assisting the criminal to evade criminal investigation or to obtain lenient punishment, any of the following acts, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of shielding in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 310 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) intentionally substituting for the criminal to deceive the judicial organs;  
(2) intentionally making false statements or providing false evidence to the judicial organs to prove that the criminal did not commit a criminal act, or that the act committed by the criminal does not constitute a crime;  
(3) intentionally providing false evidence to the judicial organs to prove that the criminal has statutory circumstances for lighter, mitigated, or exempted punishment;  
(4) other acts of making false testimony to shield the criminal.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　明知他人有间谍犯罪或者恐怖主义、极端主义犯罪行为，在司法机关向其调查有关情况、收集有关证据时，拒绝提供，情节严重的，依照[刑法第三百一十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-311)的规定，以拒绝提供间谍犯罪、恐怖主义犯罪、极端主义犯罪证据罪定罪处罚；作假证明包庇的，依照[刑法第三百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-310)的规定，以包庇罪从重处罚。

**Article 3** : Where a person knowingly knows that another person has committed espionage crimes or terrorist or extremist crimes, and refuses to provide relevant information or evidence when the judicial organs investigate the relevant circumstances or collect relevant evidence from the person, if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of refusing to provide evidence of espionage crimes, terrorist crimes, and extremist crimes in accordance with Article 311 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China; if the person makes false testimony to shield the criminal, the person shall be given a severe punishment for the crime of shielding in accordance with Article 310 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　窝藏、包庇犯罪的人，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-310)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）被窝藏、包庇的人可能被判处无期徒刑以上刑罚的；  
（二）被窝藏、包庇的人犯危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖主义或者极端主义犯罪，或者系黑社会性质组织犯罪的组织者、领导者，且可能被判处十年有期徒刑以上刑罚的；  
（三）被窝藏、包庇的人系犯罪集团的首要分子，且可能被判处十年有期徒刑以上刑罚的；  
（四）被窝藏、包庇的人在被窝藏、包庇期间再次实施故意犯罪，且新罪可能被判处五年有期徒刑以上刑罚的；  
（五）多次窝藏、包庇犯罪的人，或者窝藏、包庇多名犯罪的人的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。  
前款所称“可能被判处”刑罚，是指根据被窝藏、包庇的人所犯罪行，在不考虑自首、立功、认罪认罚等从宽处罚情节时应当依法判处的刑罚。

**Article 4** : Where harboring or shielding a criminal falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 310 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the harbored or shielded person may be sentenced to life imprisonment or a heavier penalty;  
(2) the harbored or shielded person commits crimes of endangering national security, terrorist or extremist crimes, or is an organizer or leader of a crime of an organized crime of a gangland nature, and may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years;  
(3) the harbored or shielded person is a principal offender of a criminal group, and may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years;  
(4) the harbored or shielded person again commits an intentional crime during the period of being harbored or shielded, and the new crime may be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years;  
(5) harboring or shielding a criminal multiple times, or harboring or shielding several criminals;  
(6) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
The term "may be sentenced" as used in the preceding paragraph refers to the penalty that shall be imposed according to law based on the crime committed by the harbored or shielded person, without considering the circumstances of lenient punishment such as voluntary surrender, meritorious service, or admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　认定[刑法第三百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-310)第一款规定的“明知”，应当根据案件的客观事实，结合行为人的认知能力，接触被窝藏、包庇的犯罪人的情况，以及行为人和犯罪人的供述等主、客观因素进行认定。  
行为人将犯罪的人所犯之罪误认为其他犯罪的，不影响[刑法第三百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-310)第一款规定的“明知”的认定。  
行为人虽然实施了提供隐藏处所、财物等行为，但现有证据不能证明行为人知道犯罪的人实施了犯罪行为的，不能认定为[刑法第三百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-310)第一款规定的“明知”。

**Article 5** : The determination of "knowingly" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 310 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be made based on the objective facts of the case, taking into account the actor's cognitive ability, the circumstances of contact with the harbored or shielded criminal, and the subjective and objective factors such as the statements of the actor and the criminal.  
If the actor mistakenly believes that the crime committed by the criminal is another crime, such mistake shall not affect the determination of "knowingly" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 310 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If the actor has provided a hiding place, property, or the like, but the existing evidence cannot prove that the actor knew that the criminal had committed a criminal act, the actor shall not be determined as "knowingly" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 310 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　认定窝藏、包庇罪，以被窝藏、包庇的人的行为构成犯罪为前提。  
被窝藏、包庇的人实施的犯罪事实清楚，证据确实、充分，但尚未到案、尚未依法裁判或者因不具有刑事责任能力依法未予追究刑事责任的，不影响窝藏、包庇罪的认定。但是，被窝藏、包庇的人归案后被宣告无罪的，应当依照法定程序宣告窝藏、包庇行为人无罪。

**Article 6** : The determination of the crime of harboring or shielding shall be premised on the fact that the act of the harbored or shielded person constitutes a crime.  
If the facts of the crime committed by the harbored or shielded person are clear and the evidence is credible and sufficient, but the person has not yet been brought to justice, has not yet been adjudicated according to law, or has not been investigated for criminal liability according to law due to lack of criminal capacity, such circumstances shall not affect the determination of the crime of harboring or shielding. However, if the harbored or shielded person is later found not guilty after being brought to justice, the actor who committed the harboring or shielding shall be declared not guilty in accordance with statutory procedures.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　为帮助同一个犯罪的人逃避刑事处罚，实施窝藏、包庇行为，又实施洗钱行为，或者掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得及其收益行为，或者帮助毁灭证据行为，或者伪证行为的，依照处罚较重的犯罪定罪，并从重处罚，不实行数罪并罚。

**Article 7** : Where, for the purpose of assisting the same criminal to evade criminal punishment, a person commits acts of harboring and shielding, and also commits acts of money laundering, or acts of concealing or hiding the proceeds of crime and the benefits derived therefrom, or acts of helping to destroy evidence, or acts of perjury, the person shall be convicted of the crime that carries the heavier penalty and shall be given a severe punishment, and the person shall not be punished for several crimes in combination.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　共同犯罪人之间互相实施的窝藏、包庇行为，不以窝藏、包庇罪定罪处罚，但对共同犯罪以外的犯罪人实施窝藏、包庇行为的，以所犯共同犯罪和窝藏、包庇罪并罚。

**Article 8** : Acts of harboring or shielding among joint offenders against each other shall not be convicted and punished as the crime of harboring or shielding; however, if a joint offender harbors or shields a person who is not a joint offender in the same crime, the joint offender shall be punished for both the joint crime and the crime of harboring or shielding in combination.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本解释自2021年8月11日起施行。

**Article 9** : This Interpretation shall come into force on August 11, 2021.
