# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕11号  
**公布日期**：2017-04-17  
**生效日期**：2017-07-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2017年4月17日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1715次会议、2017年5月25日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第64次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年7月1日起施行。 2017年6月27日 最高人民法院   最高人民检察院 关于办理扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序等刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2017〕11号 为依法惩治扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序犯罪，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下: 第一条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百八十八条第一款规定的“擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自使用无线电频率，干扰无线电通讯秩序”： （一）未经批准设置无线电广播电台（以下简称“黑广播”），非法使用广播电视专用频段的频率的； （二）未经批准设置通信基站（以下简称“伪基站”），强行向不特定用户发送信息，非法使用公众移动通信频率的； （三）未经批准使用卫星无线电频率的； （四）非法设置、使用无线电干扰器的； （五）其他擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自使用无线电频率，干扰无线电通讯秩序的情形。 第二条　违反国家规定，擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自使用无线电频率，干扰无线电通讯秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百八十八条第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）影响航天器、航空器、铁路机车、船舶专用无线电导航、遇险救助和安全通信等涉及公共安全的无线电频率正常使用的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-288)第一款规定的“擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自使用无线电频率，干扰无线电通讯秩序”：  
（一）未经批准设置无线电广播电台（以下简称“黑广播”），非法使用广播电视专用频段的频率的；  
（二）未经批准设置通信基站（以下简称“伪基站”），强行向不特定用户发送信息，非法使用公众移动通信频率的；  
（三）未经批准使用卫星无线电频率的；  
（四）非法设置、使用无线电干扰器的；  
（五）其他擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自使用无线电频率，干扰无线电通讯秩序的情形。

**Article 1** In any of the following circumstances, it shall be found that a person "without authorization sets up or uses a radio station (or stations), or without authorization uses radio frequencies, thereby disrupting the order of radio communications" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 288 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Setting up a radio broadcasting station without approval (hereinafter referred to as a "pirate radio station") and illegally using frequencies of the dedicated frequency band for radio and television broadcasting;  
(2) Setting up a communication base station without approval (hereinafter referred to as a "fake base station"), forcibly sending information to unspecified users, and illegally using frequencies of public mobile communications;  
(3) Using satellite radio frequencies without approval;  
(4) Illegally setting up or using radio jammers;  
(5) Other circumstances of setting up or using a radio station without authorization, or using radio frequencies without authorization, thereby disrupting the order of radio communications.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　违反国家规定，擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自使用无线电频率，干扰无线电通讯秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-288)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）影响航天器、航空器、铁路机车、船舶专用无线电导航、遇险救助和安全通信等涉及公共安全的无线电频率正常使用的；  
（二）自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件、社会安全事件等突发事件期间，在事件发生地使用“黑广播”“伪基站”的；  
（三）举办国家或者省级重大活动期间，在活动场所及周边使用“黑广播”“伪基站”的；  
（四）同时使用三个以上“黑广播”“伪基站”的；  
（五）“黑广播”的实测发射功率五百瓦以上，或者覆盖范围十公里以上的；  
（六）使用“伪基站”发送诈骗、赌博、招嫖、木马病毒、钓鱼网站链接等违法犯罪信息，数量在五千条以上，或者销毁发送数量等记录的；  
（七）雇佣、指使未成年人、残疾人等特定人员使用“伪基站”的；  
（八）违法所得三万元以上的；  
（九）曾因扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序受过刑事处罚，或者二年内曾因扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序受过行政处罚，又实施[刑法第二百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-288)规定的行为的；  
（十）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** Where a person violates State regulations, without authorization sets up or uses a radio station (or stations), or without authorization uses radio frequencies, thereby disrupting the order of radio communications, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be found to constitute "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 288 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Affecting the normal use of radio frequencies involving public safety, such as dedicated radio navigation, distress rescue and safety communications of spacecraft, aircraft, railway locomotives, and ships;  
(2) During periods of emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, or social security incidents, using a "pirate radio station" or a "fake base station" in the place where the incident occurs;  
(3) During the period of a national or provincial major event, using a "pirate radio station" or a "fake base station" at or around the event venue;  
(4) Using three or more "pirate radio stations" or "fake base stations" simultaneously;  
(5) The measured transmission power of the "pirate radio station" is 500 watts or more, or its coverage area is 10 kilometers or more;  
(6) Using a "fake base station" to send information on illegal or criminal activities such as fraud, gambling, solicitation, Trojan viruses, or phishing website links, with the number of such messages being 5,000 or more, or destroying records of the number sent;  
(7) Hiring or directing specific persons such as minors or disabled persons to use a "fake base station";  
(8) The illegal gains amount to 30,000 yuan or more;  
(9) Having previously received criminal punishment for disrupting the order of radio communications management, or having received an administrative penalty for disrupting the order of radio communications management within the previous two years, and again committing the act prescribed in Article 288 of the Criminal Law;  
(10) Other circumstances of serious nature.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　违反国家规定，擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自使用无线电频率，干扰无线电通讯秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-288)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）影响航天器、航空器、铁路机车、船舶专用无线电导航、遇险救助和安全通信等涉及公共安全的无线电频率正常使用，危及公共安全的；  
（二）造成公共秩序混乱等严重后果的；  
（三）自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件和社会安全事件等突发事件期间，在事件发生地使用“黑广播”“伪基站”，造成严重影响的；  
（四）对国家或者省级重大活动造成严重影响的；  
（五）同时使用十个以上“黑广播”“伪基站”的；  
（六）“黑广播”的实测发射功率三千瓦以上，或者覆盖范围二十公里以上的；  
（七）违法所得十五万元以上的；  
（八）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 3** Where a person violates State regulations, without authorization sets up or uses a radio station (or stations), or without authorization uses radio frequencies, thereby disrupting the order of radio communications, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be found to constitute "particularly serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 288 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Affecting the normal use of radio frequencies involving public safety, such as dedicated radio navigation, distress rescue and safety communications of spacecraft, aircraft, railway locomotives, and ships, thereby endangering public safety;  
(2) Causing serious consequences such as public order chaos;  
(3) During periods of emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, or social security incidents, using a "pirate radio station" or a "fake base station" in the place where the incident occurs, causing a severe impact;  
(4) Causing a severe impact on a national or provincial major event;  
(5) Using ten or more "pirate radio stations" or "fake base stations" simultaneously;  
(6) The measured transmission power of the "pirate radio station" is 3,000 watts or more, or its coverage area is 20 kilometers or more;  
(7) The illegal gains amount to 150,000 yuan or more;  
(8) Other circumstances of particularly serious nature.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　非法生产、销售“黑广播”“伪基站”、无线电干扰器等无线电设备，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法生产、销售无线电设备三套以上的；  
（二）非法经营数额五万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，数量或者数额达到前款第一项、第二项规定标准五倍以上，或者具有其他情节特别严重的情形的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节特别严重”。  
在非法生产、销售无线电设备窝点查扣的零件，以组装完成的套数以及能够组装的套数认定；无法组装为成套设备的，每三套广播信号调制器（激励器）认定为一套“黑广播”设备，每三块主板认定为一套“伪基站”设备。

**Article 4** Where a person illegally produces or sells radio equipment such as "pirate radio stations", "fake base stations", or radio jammers, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be found to constitute "serious circumstances" as prescribed in Article 225 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Illegally producing or selling three or more sets of radio equipment;  
(2) The illegal business turnover amounts to 50,000 yuan or more;  
(3) Other circumstances of serious nature.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph, and the quantity or amount reaches five times or more the standard set in subparagraph (1) or (2) of the preceding paragraph, or there are other circumstances of particularly serious nature, it shall be found to constitute "particularly serious circumstances" as prescribed in Article 225 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Parts seized at the site of illegal production or sale of radio equipment shall be determined based on the number of assembled sets and the number of sets that can be assembled; where assembly into complete sets is not possible, every three sets of broadcast signal modulators (exciters) shall be determined as one set of "pirate radio station" equipment, and every three mainboards shall be determined as one set of "fake base station" equipment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　单位犯本解释规定之罪的，对单位判处罚金，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照本解释规定的自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where a unit commits a crime prescribed in this Interpretation, a fine shall be imposed on the unit, and the directly responsible supervisors and other persons directly responsible shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the standards for conviction and sentencing of crimes committed by natural persons as prescribed in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　擅自设置、使用无线电台（站），或者擅自使用无线电频率，同时构成其他犯罪的，按照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
明知他人实施诈骗等犯罪，使用“黑广播”“伪基站”等无线电设备为其发送信息或者提供其他帮助，同时构成其他犯罪的，按照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where a person without authorization sets up or uses a radio station, or without authorization uses radio frequencies, and such act simultaneously constitutes another crime, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision providing for a heavier punishment.  
Where a person, knowing that another person is committing a crime such as fraud, uses radio equipment such as a "pirate radio station" or a "fake base station" to send messages for such person or provide other assistance, and such act simultaneously constitutes another crime, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision providing for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　负有无线电监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，应当依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)的规定，以滥用职权罪或者玩忽职守罪追究刑事责任。  
有查禁扰乱无线电管理秩序犯罪活动职责的国家机关工作人员，向犯罪分子通风报信、提供便利，帮助犯罪分子逃避处罚的，应当依照[刑法第四百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-417)的规定，以帮助犯罪分子逃避处罚罪追究刑事责任；事先通谋的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Where a State functionary responsible for radio supervision and administration abuses his or her powers or neglects his or her duties, thereby causing major losses to public property or the interests of the State and the people, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with Article 397 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of abuse of powers or the crime of dereliction of duty.  
Where a State functionary who has the duty to investigate and suppress criminal activities disrupting the order of radio management tips off criminals, provides convenience, or helps criminals to evade punishment, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with Article 417 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of helping criminals to evade punishment; where there is prior collusion, the person shall be dealt with as an accomplice.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　为合法经营活动,使用“黑广播”“伪基站”或者实施其他扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序的行为，构成扰乱无线电通讯管理秩序罪,但不属于“情节特别严重”，行为人系初犯，并确有悔罪表现的，可以认定为情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；确有必要判处刑罚的，应当从宽处罚。

**Article 8** Where a person, for the purpose of lawful business operations, uses a "pirate radio station", a "fake base station", or commits other acts disrupting the order of radio communications management, thereby constituting the crime of disrupting the order of radio communications management, but such acts do not fall under "particularly serious circumstances", and the actor is a first-time offender and genuinely shows repentance, the circumstances may be found to be minor, and the person may not be prosecuted or may be granted exemption from criminal punishment; where it is truly necessary to impose a penalty, a lenient punishment shall be given.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对案件所涉的有关专门性问题难以确定的，依据司法鉴定机构出具的鉴定意见，或者下列机构出具的报告，结合其他证据作出认定：  
（一）省级以上无线电管理机构、省级无线电管理机构依法设立的派出机构、地市级以上广播电视主管部门就是否系“伪基站”“黑广播”出具的报告；  
（二）省级以上广播电视主管部门及其指定的检测机构就“黑广播”功率、覆盖范围出具的报告；  
（三）省级以上航空、铁路、船舶等主管部门就是否干扰导航、通信等出具的报告。  
对移动终端用户受影响的情况，可以依据相关通信运营商出具的证明，结合被告人供述、终端用户证言等证据作出认定。

**Article 9** Where it is difficult to determine the relevant specialized issues involved in a case, the findings shall be made based on expert evaluation opinions issued by judicial expert evaluation institutions, or reports issued by the following institutions, in conjunction with other evidence:  
(1) Reports issued by radio regulatory authorities at or above the provincial level, dispatched agencies established according to law by provincial radio regulatory authorities, or radio and television regulatory authorities at or above the prefecture level, on whether the equipment constitutes a "fake base station" or a "pirate radio station";  
(2) Reports issued by radio and television regulatory authorities at or above the provincial level and the testing institutions designated by them, on the power and coverage area of the "pirate radio station";  
(3) Reports issued by competent authorities at or above the provincial level for aviation, railways, ships, etc., on whether navigation, communications, etc., are disrupted.  
For the situation of affected mobile terminal users, findings may be made based on certificates issued by relevant communications operators, in conjunction with evidence such as the defendant's confession and testimony of terminal users.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本解释自2017年7月1日起施行。

**Article 10** This Interpretation shall come into force as of July 1, 2017.
