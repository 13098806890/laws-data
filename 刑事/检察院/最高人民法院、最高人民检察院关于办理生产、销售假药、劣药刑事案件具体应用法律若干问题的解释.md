# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理生产、销售假药、劣药刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2009〕9号  
**公布日期**：2009-01-05  
**生效日期**：2009-05-27

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理生产、销售假药、劣药刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2009年1月5日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1461次会议、2009年2月24日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第10次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年5月27日起施行。 2009年5月13日 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院 关于办理生产、销售假药、劣药刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2009〕9号 为依法惩治生产、销售假药、劣药犯罪，保障人民群众生命健康安全，维护药品市场秩序，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  生产、销售的假药具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百四十一条规定的“足以严重危害人体健康”： （一）依照国家药品标准不应含有有毒有害物质而含有，或者含有的有毒有害物质超过国家药品标准规定的； （二）属于麻醉药品、精神药品、医疗用毒性药品、放射性药品、避孕药品、血液制品或者疫苗的； （三）以孕产妇、婴幼儿、儿童或者危重病人为主要使用对象的； （四）属于注射剂药品、急救药品的； （五）没有或者伪造药品生产许可证或者批准文号，且属于处方药的； （六）其他足以严重危害人体健康的情形。 对前款第（一）项、第（六）项规定的情形难以确定的，可以委托省级以上药品监督管理部门设置或者确定的药品检验机构检验。司法机关根据检验结论，结合假药标明的适应病症、对人体健康可能造成的危害程度等情况认定。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  生产、销售的假药具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-141)规定的“足以严重危害人体健康”：  
（一）依照国家药品标准不应含有有毒有害物质而含有，或者含有的有毒有害物质超过国家药品标准规定的；  
（二）属于麻醉药品、精神药品、医疗用毒性药品、放射性药品、避孕药品、血液制品或者疫苗的；  
（三）以孕产妇、婴幼儿、儿童或者危重病人为主要使用对象的；  
（四）属于注射剂药品、急救药品的；  
（五）没有或者伪造药品生产许可证或者批准文号，且属于处方药的；  
（六）其他足以严重危害人体健康的情形。  
对前款第（一）项、第（六）项规定的情形难以确定的，可以委托省级以上药品监督管理部门设置或者确定的药品检验机构检验。司法机关根据检验结论，结合假药标明的适应病症、对人体健康可能造成的危害程度等情况认定。

**Article 1** : Where the counterfeit drugs produced or sold fall under any of the following circumstances, they shall be deemed as "sufficient to cause serious harm to human health" as provided for in Article 141 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) containing toxic or harmful substances that should not be contained under national drug standards, or containing toxic or harmful substances in excess of the limits specified by national drug standards;  
(2) being narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, medicinal toxic drugs, radioactive drugs, contraceptive drugs, blood products or vaccines;  
(3) being mainly intended for use by pregnant women, parturients, infants, young children, children, or critically ill patients;  
(4) being injectable drugs or emergency drugs;  
(5) being prescription drugs without or with forged drug manufacturing licenses or approval numbers;  
(6) other circumstances sufficient to cause serious harm to human health.  
Where it is difficult to determine the circumstances provided for in items (1) and (6) of the preceding paragraph, a drug inspection institution established or designated by the drug regulatory department at or above the provincial level may be entrusted to conduct inspection. Judicial authorities shall make the determination based on the inspection conclusions, taking into account the indications for which the counterfeit drugs are labeled and the degree of possible harm to human health.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  生产、销售的假药被使用后，造成轻伤以上伤害，或者轻度残疾、中度残疾，或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍或者严重功能障碍，或者有其他严重危害人体健康情形的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-141)规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”。  
生产、销售的假药被使用后，造成重度残疾、三人以上重伤、三人以上中度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍、十人以上轻伤、五人以上轻度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍，或者有其他特别严重危害人体健康情形的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-141)规定的“对人体健康造成特别严重危害”。

**Article 2** : Where, after the use of counterfeit drugs produced or sold, it results in injuries above minor injury, or mild disability, moderate disability, or organ or tissue damage leading to general dysfunction or serious dysfunction, or there are other circumstances causing serious harm to human health, it shall be deemed as "causing serious harm to human health" as provided for in Article 141 of the Criminal Law.  
Where, after the use of counterfeit drugs produced or sold, it results in severe disability, serious injuries to three or more persons, moderate disability to three or more persons, or organ or tissue damage leading to serious dysfunction, minor injuries to ten or more persons, mild disability to five or more persons, or organ or tissue damage leading to general dysfunction, or there are other circumstances causing particularly serious harm to human health, it shall be deemed as "causing particularly serious harm to human health" as provided for in Article 141 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  生产、销售的劣药被使用后，造成轻伤以上伤害，或者轻度残疾、中度残疾，或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍或者严重功能障碍，或者有其他严重危害人体健康情形的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-142)规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”。  
生产、销售的劣药被使用后，致人死亡、重度残疾、三人以上重伤、三人以上中度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍、十人以上轻伤、五人以上轻度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍，或者有其他特别严重危害人体健康情形的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-142)规定的“后果特别严重”。

**Article 3** : Where, after the use of substandard drugs produced or sold, it results in injuries above minor injury, or mild disability, moderate disability, or organ or tissue damage leading to general dysfunction or serious dysfunction, or there are other circumstances causing serious harm to human health, it shall be deemed as "causing serious harm to human health" as provided for in Article 142 of the Criminal Law.  
Where, after the use of substandard drugs produced or sold, it results in death, severe disability, serious injuries to three or more persons, moderate disability to three or more persons, or organ or tissue damage leading to serious dysfunction, minor injuries to ten or more persons, mild disability to five or more persons, or organ or tissue damage leading to general dysfunction, or there are other circumstances causing particularly serious harm to human health, it shall be deemed as "causing particularly serious consequences" as provided for in Article 142 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  医疗机构知道或者应当知道是假药而使用或者销售，符合[本解释第一条](#art-1)或者第二条规定标准的，以销售假药罪追究刑事责任。  
医疗机构知道或者应当知道是劣药而使用或者销售，符合[本解释第三条](#art-3)规定标准的，以销售劣药罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 4** : Where a medical institution, knowing or having reason to know that a drug is a counterfeit drug, uses or sells it, and the circumstances meet the standards set forth in Article 1 or Article 2 of this Interpretation, criminal liability for the crime of selling counterfeit drugs shall be investigated.  
Where a medical institution, knowing or having reason to know that a drug is a substandard drug, uses or sells it, and the circumstances meet the standards set forth in Article 3 of this Interpretation, criminal liability for the crime of selling substandard drugs shall be investigated.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  知道或者应当知道他人生产、销售假药、劣药，而有下列情形之一的，以生产、销售假药罪或者生产、销售劣药罪等犯罪的共犯论处：  
（一）提供资金、贷款、账号、发票、证明、许可证件的；  
（二）提供生产、经营场所、设备或者运输、仓储、保管、邮寄等便利条件的；  
（三）提供生产技术，或者提供原料、辅料、包装材料的；  
（四）提供广告等宣传的。

**Article 5** : Where a person, knowing or having reason to know that another person is producing or selling counterfeit drugs or substandard drugs, commits any of the following acts, such person shall be dealt with as an accomplice in the crime of producing or selling counterfeit drugs or the crime of producing or selling substandard drugs:  
(1) providing funds, loans, accounts, invoices, certificates, or licenses;  
(2) providing production or business premises, equipment, or facilitating conditions such as transportation, storage, custody, or mailing;  
(3) providing production techniques, or providing raw materials, excipients, or packaging materials;  
(4) providing advertising or other publicity.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  实施生产、销售假药、劣药犯罪，同时构成生产、销售伪劣产品、侵犯知识产权、非法经营、非法行医、非法采供血等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** : Where the commission of the crime of producing or selling counterfeit drugs or substandard drugs concurrently constitutes the crimes of producing or selling counterfeit and shoddy products, infringing intellectual property rights, illegal business operations, illegal medical practice, illegal collection and supply of blood, or other crimes, the conviction and punishment shall be in accordance with the provision imposing the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  在自然灾害、事故灾难、公共卫生事件、社会安全事件等突发事件发生时期，生产、销售用于应对突发事件药品的假药、劣药的，依法从重处罚。

**Article 7** : Where, during the period of emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters, public health incidents, or social security incidents, counterfeit drugs or substandard drugs for responding to the emergencies are produced or sold, a severe punishment shall be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  最高人民法院、最高人民检察院以前发布的司法解释、规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 8** : Where any judicial interpretations or normative documents previously issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
