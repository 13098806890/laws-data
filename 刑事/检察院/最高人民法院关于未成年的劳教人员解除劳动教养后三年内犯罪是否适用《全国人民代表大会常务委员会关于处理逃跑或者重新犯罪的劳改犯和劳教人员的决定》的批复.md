### 最高人民法院关于未成年的劳教人员解除劳动教养后三年内犯罪是否适用《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于处理逃跑或者重新犯罪的劳改犯和劳教人员的决定》的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院关于未成年的劳教人员  
  
解除劳动教养后三年内犯罪是否适用  
  
《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于处理  
  
逃跑或者重新犯罪的劳改犯和劳教人员的  
  
决定》的批复  
  
法复〔 1993〕2号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院沪高法研（1992）68号《关于未成年的劳教人员解除劳动教养后三年内犯罪是否适用＜全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于处理逃跑或者重新犯罪的劳改犯和劳教人员的决定＞的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
未成年的劳教人员解除劳动教养后三年内犯罪，如果犯罪时已年满十八岁的，应当适用《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于处理逃跑或者重新犯罪的劳改犯和劳教人员的决定》（以下简称《决定》）；如果犯罪时仍不满十八岁的，应根据[刑法第十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-14)的规定依法从轻或者减轻处罚，不适用《决定》。  
  
1993年3月6日

**Article 1** Reply of the Supreme People's Court on Whether the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Handling Escaped or Recidivist Criminals under Reform Through Labor and Persons Subject to Reeducation Through Labor Shall Apply to Minors Who Commit Crimes within Three Years after the Termination of Reeducation through Labor  
Fafu [1993] No. 2  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
Your court's Shanghai Gaofayan (1992) No. 68 Request for Instructions on Whether the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Handling Escaped or Recidivist Criminals under Reform Through Labor and Persons Subject to Reeducation Through Labor Shall Apply to Minors Who Commit Crimes within Three Years after the Termination of Reeducation through Labor has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
If a minor who is a person subject to reeducation through labor commits a crime within three years after the termination of reeducation through labor and has reached the age of eighteen at the time of the crime, the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on Handling Escaped or Recidivist Criminals under Reform Through Labor and Persons Subject to Reeducation Through Labor (hereinafter referred to as the "Decision") shall apply; if the person is still under the age of eighteen at the time of the crime, a lighter or mitigated punishment shall be imposed according to law in accordance with the provisions of Article 14 of the Criminal Law, and the Decision shall not apply.  
March 6, 1993
