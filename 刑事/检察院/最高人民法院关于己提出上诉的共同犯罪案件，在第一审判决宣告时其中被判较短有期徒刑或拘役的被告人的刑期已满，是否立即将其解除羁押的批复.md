### 最高人民法院关于己提出上诉的共同犯罪案件，在第一审判决宣告时其中被判较短有期徒刑或拘役的被告人的刑期已满，是否立即将其解除羁押的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于己提出上诉的共同犯罪案件，在第一审判决  
  
宣告时其中被判较短有期徒刑或拘役的被告人  
  
的刑期已满，是否立即将其解除羁押的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1990〕6号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于已提出上诉的共同犯罪案件，在第一审判决宣告时，被判较短有期徒刑或拘役的被告人的刑期已满，是否立即将其解除羁押的请示》收悉。  
  
经研究，我们认为，[刑事诉讼法第一百三十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-134)第二款明确规定：第一审判决宣判后，共同犯罪的案件只有部分被告人上诉的，第二审人民法院“应当对全案进行审查，一并处理”。你院请示所述案件的第一审判决尚未发生法律效力，部分被告人提出了上诉，该案正在第二审期间，故不应将另一部分被告人解除羁押。但是，为避免有的被告人的羁押期已达到甚至超过第一审判处的刑期还继续被关押，人民法院可视案件具体情况，对这类被告人依法变更强制措施，即改为监视居住或者取保候审，待第二审人民法院对全案进行审查并作出终审裁判之后，再依法处理。  
  
1990年6月5日

**Article 1** Reply on Whether a Defendant Whose Term Is Shorter Fixed-Term Imprisonment or Criminal Detention Imposed in the First Instance Judgment Has Expired Shall Be Immediately Released from Custody in a Joint Crime Case Where an Appeal Has Been Filed  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1990] No. 6  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions on Whether a Defendant Whose Term Is Shorter Fixed-Term Imprisonment or Criminal Detention Imposed in the First Instance Judgment Has Expired Shall Be Immediately Released from Custody in a Joint Crime Case Where an Appeal Has Been Filed.  
Upon study, we are of the opinion that Article 134, paragraph 2, of the Criminal Procedure Law clearly provides that after the pronouncement of the first instance judgment, if only some of the defendants in a joint crime case have appealed, the second instance People's Court "shall review the entire case and handle it together." The first instance judgment in the case described in your Request has not yet become legally effective, and some of the defendants have filed an appeal; the case is currently pending before the second instance. Therefore, the other defendants shall not be released from custody. However, to avoid the situation where the custody period of certain defendants has reached or even exceeded the term of punishment imposed in the first instance judgment and they continue to be detained, the People's Court may, depending on the specific circumstances of the case, change the compulsory measures for such defendants according to law, i.e., change them to residential surveillance or release on bail pending investigation or trial, and handle them according to law after the second instance People's Court has reviewed the entire case and rendered a final judgment.  
June 5, 1990
