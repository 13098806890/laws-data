# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理环境污染刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕15号  
**公布日期**：2013-06-08  
**生效日期**：2013-06-19

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理环境污染刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年6月8日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1581次会议、2013年6月8日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第7次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年6月19日起施行。 2013年6月17日 最高人民法院   最高人民检察院 关于办理环境污染刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕15号 为依法惩治有关环境污染犯罪，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   实施刑法第三百三十八条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“严重污染环境”： （一）在饮用水水源一级保护区、自然保护区核心区排放、倾倒、处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质的； （二）非法排放、倾倒、处置危险废物三吨以上的； （三）非法排放含重金属、持久性有机污染物等严重危害环境、损害人体健康的污染物超过国家污染物排放标准或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据法律授权制定的污染物排放标准三倍以上的； （四）私设暗管或者利用渗井、渗坑、裂隙、溶洞等排放、倾倒、处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质的； （五）两年内曾因违反国家规定，排放、倾倒、处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质受过两次以上行政处罚，又实施前列行为的； （六）致使乡镇以上集中式饮用水水源取水中断十二小时以上的； （七）致使基本农田、防护林地、特种用途林地五亩以上，其他农用地十亩以上，其他土地二十亩以上基本功能丧失或者遭受永久性破坏的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   实施[刑法第三百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-338)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“严重污染环境”：  
（一）在饮用水水源一级保护区、自然保护区核心区排放、倾倒、处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质的；  
（二）非法排放、倾倒、处置危险废物三吨以上的；  
（三）非法排放含重金属、持久性有机污染物等严重危害环境、损害人体健康的污染物超过国家污染物排放标准或者省、自治区、直辖市人民政府根据法律授权制定的污染物排放标准三倍以上的；  
（四）私设暗管或者利用渗井、渗坑、裂隙、溶洞等排放、倾倒、处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质的；  
（五）两年内曾因违反国家规定，排放、倾倒、处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质受过两次以上行政处罚，又实施前列行为的；  
（六）致使乡镇以上集中式饮用水水源取水中断十二小时以上的；  
（七）致使基本农田、防护林地、特种用途林地五亩以上，其他农用地十亩以上，其他土地二十亩以上基本功能丧失或者遭受永久性破坏的；  
（八）致使森林或者其他林木死亡五十立方米以上，或者幼树死亡二千五百株以上的；  
（九）致使公私财产损失三十万元以上的；  
（十）致使疏散、转移群众五千人以上的；  
（十一）致使三十人以上中毒的；  
（十二）致使三人以上轻伤、轻度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍的；  
（十三）致使一人以上重伤、中度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍的；  
（十四）其他严重污染环境的情形。

**Article 1** Where the conduct as prescribed in Article 338 of the Criminal Law is committed, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be identified as "seriously polluting the environment":  
(1) discharging, dumping, or disposing of radioactive waste, waste containing infectious disease pathogens, or toxic substances in a first-class protection zone of drinking water sources or a core zone of a nature reserve;  
(2) illegally discharging, dumping, or disposing of hazardous waste in an amount of three tons or more;  
(3) illegally discharging pollutants containing heavy metals, persistent organic pollutants, or other pollutants that seriously harm the environment and damage human health, at a concentration three times or more than the national pollutant discharge standard or the pollutant discharge standard formulated by the people's government of a province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government in accordance with legal authorization;  
(4) discharging, dumping, or disposing of radioactive waste, waste containing infectious disease pathogens, or toxic substances through secretly installed pipes or by using seepage wells, seepage pits, fissures, karst caves, or the like;  
(5) having received two or more administrative penalties within the past two years for violating state regulations by discharging, dumping, or disposing of radioactive waste, waste containing infectious disease pathogens, or toxic substances, and again committing the acts listed above;  
(6) causing an interruption of water intake from a centralized drinking water source at or above the township level for twelve hours or more;  
(7) causing the basic functions of five mu or more of basic farmland, shelter forest land, or special-purpose forest land, ten mu or more of other agricultural land, or twenty mu or more of other land to be lost or permanently damaged;  
(8) causing the death of fifty cubic meters or more of forests or other trees, or the death of 2,500 or more saplings;  
(9) causing losses of 300,000 yuan or more to public or private property;  
(10) causing the evacuation or relocation of 5,000 or more people;  
(11) causing thirty or more persons to become poisoned;  
(12) causing three or more persons to suffer minor injuries, minor disabilities, or organ or tissue damage resulting in general dysfunction;  
(13) causing one or more persons to suffer serious injuries, moderate disabilities, or organ or tissue damage resulting in severe dysfunction;  
(14) other circumstances of serious environmental pollution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   实施[刑法第三百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-339)、第四百零八条规定的行为，具有[本解释第一条](#art-1)第六项至第十三项规定情形之一的，应当认定为“致使公私财产遭受重大损失或者严重危害人体健康”或者“致使公私财产遭受重大损失或者造成人身伤亡的严重后果”。

**Article 2** Where the conduct as prescribed in Articles 339 and 408 of the Criminal Law is committed, and any of the circumstances specified in items (6) to (13) of Article 1 of this Interpretation exists, it shall be identified as "causing heavy losses to public or private property or seriously endangering human health" or "causing heavy losses to public or private property or causing serious consequences of personal injury or death."

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   实施[刑法第三百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-338)、第三百三十九条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“后果特别严重”：  
（一）致使县级以上城区集中式饮用水水源取水中断十二个小时以上的；  
（二）致使基本农田、防护林地、特种用途林地十五亩以上，其他农用地三十亩以上，其他土地六十亩以上基本功能丧失或者遭受永久性破坏的；  
（三）致使森林或者其他林木死亡一百五十立方米以上，或者幼树死亡七千五百株以上的；  
（四）致使公私财产损失一百万元以上的；  
（五）致使疏散、转移群众一万五千人以上的；  
（六）致使一百人以上中毒的；  
（七）致使十人以上轻伤、轻度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍的；  
（八）致使三人以上重伤、中度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍的；  
（九）致使一人以上重伤、中度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍，并致使五人以上轻伤、轻度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍的；  
（十）致使一人以上死亡或者重度残疾的；  
（十一）其他后果特别严重的情形。

**Article 3** Where the conduct as prescribed in Articles 338 and 339 of the Criminal Law is committed, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be identified as "particularly serious consequences":  
(1) causing an interruption of water intake from a centralized drinking water source in an urban area at or above the county level for twelve hours or more;  
(2) causing the basic functions of fifteen mu or more of basic farmland, shelter forest land, or special-purpose forest land, thirty mu or more of other agricultural land, or sixty mu or more of other land to be lost or permanently damaged;  
(3) causing the death of 150 cubic meters or more of forests or other trees, or the death of 7,500 or more saplings;  
(4) causing losses of 1,000,000 yuan or more to public or private property;  
(5) causing the evacuation or relocation of 15,000 or more people;  
(6) causing 100 or more persons to become poisoned;  
(7) causing ten or more persons to suffer minor injuries, minor disabilities, or organ or tissue damage resulting in general dysfunction;  
(8) causing three or more persons to suffer serious injuries, moderate disabilities, or organ or tissue damage resulting in severe dysfunction;  
(9) causing one or more persons to suffer serious injuries, moderate disabilities, or organ or tissue damage resulting in severe dysfunction, and causing five or more persons to suffer minor injuries, minor disabilities, or organ or tissue damage resulting in general dysfunction;  
(10) causing the death or severe disability of one or more persons;  
(11) other circumstances of particularly serious consequences.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   实施[刑法第三百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-338)、第三百三十九条规定的犯罪行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当酌情从重处罚：  
（一）阻挠环境监督检查或者突发环境事件调查的；  
（二）闲置、拆除污染防治设施或者使污染防治设施不正常运行的；  
（三）在医院、学校、居民区等人口集中地区及其附近，违反国家规定排放、倾倒、处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质或者其他有害物质的；  
（四）在限期整改期间，违反国家规定排放、倾倒、处置有放射性的废物、含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质或者其他有害物质的。  
实施前款第一项规定的行为，构成妨害公务罪的，以污染环境罪与妨害公务罪数罪并罚。

**Article 4** Where a criminal offense as prescribed in Articles 338 and 339 of the Criminal Law is committed, and any of the following circumstances exists, a heavier punishment shall be given at discretion:  
(1) obstructing environmental supervision and inspection or investigation into an environmental emergency;  
(2) leaving pollution prevention facilities idle, dismantling them, or causing them to operate abnormally;  
(3) in densely populated areas such as hospitals, schools, residential areas or their vicinity, violating state regulations by discharging, dumping, or disposing of radioactive waste, waste containing infectious disease pathogens, toxic substances, or other harmful substances;  
(4) violating state regulations by discharging, dumping, or disposing of radioactive waste, waste containing infectious disease pathogens, toxic substances, or other harmful substances during the period of rectification within a specified time limit.  
Where the conduct specified in item (1) of the preceding paragraph constitutes the crime of obstructing official duties, the offender shall be punished for both the crime of environmental pollution and the crime of obstructing official duties in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   实施[刑法第三百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-338)、第三百三十九条规定的犯罪行为，但及时采取措施，防止损失扩大、消除污染，积极赔偿损失的，可以酌情从宽处罚。

**Article 5** Where a criminal offense as prescribed in Articles 338 and 339 of the Criminal Law is committed, but where timely measures are taken to prevent the expansion of losses and eliminate pollution, and compensation for losses is actively made, a lighter punishment may be given at discretion.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   单位犯[刑法第三百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-338)、第三百三十九条规定之罪的，依照本解释规定的相应个人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。

**Article 6** Where a unit commits a crime as prescribed in Articles 338 and 339 of the Criminal Law, the persons directly in charge and other persons directly liable shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards for the corresponding individual crime provided in this Interpretation, and a fine shall be imposed on the unit.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   行为人明知他人无经营许可证或者超出经营许可范围，向其提供或者委托其收集、贮存、利用、处置危险废物，严重污染环境的，以污染环境罪的共同犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Where an actor knowingly provides to or entrusts another person who has no business license or exceeds the scope of business license to collect, store, utilize, or dispose of hazardous waste, thereby seriously polluting the environment, the actor shall be punished as a joint offender of the crime of environmental pollution.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   违反国家规定，排放、倾倒、处置含有毒害性、放射性、传染病病原体等物质的污染物，同时构成污染环境罪、非法处置进口的固体废物罪、投放危险物质罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的犯罪定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Where a person, in violation of state regulations, discharges, dumps, or disposes of pollutants containing toxic, radioactive, or infectious disease pathogens and other substances, and such conduct simultaneously constitutes the crime of environmental pollution, the crime of illegally disposing of imported solid waste, the crime of throwing hazardous substances, or other crimes, the person shall be convicted and punished according to the crime that carries a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   本解释所称“公私财产损失”，包括污染环境行为直接造成财产损毁、减少的实际价值，以及为防止污染扩大、消除污染而采取必要合理措施所产生的费用。

**Article 9** The term "losses to public or private property" in this Interpretation includes the actual value of property directly destroyed or reduced due to the act of environmental pollution, as well as the expenses incurred for taking necessary and reasonable measures to prevent the expansion of pollution and eliminate pollution.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   下列物质应当认定为“有毒物质”：  
（一）危险废物，包括列入国家危险废物名录的废物，以及根据国家规定的危险废物鉴别标准和鉴别方法认定的具有危险特性的废物；  
（二）剧毒化学品、列入重点环境管理危险化学品名录的化学品，以及含有上述化学品的物质；  
（三）含有铅、汞、镉、铬等重金属的物质；  
（四）《关于持久性有机污染物的斯德哥尔摩公约》附件所列物质；  
（五）其他具有毒性，可能污染环境的物质。

**Article 10** The following substances shall be identified as "toxic substances":  
(1) hazardous waste, including waste listed in the national hazardous waste list and waste with hazardous characteristics identified in accordance with the national standards and methods for identifying hazardous waste;  
(2) highly toxic chemicals, chemicals listed in the list of hazardous chemicals for key environmental management, and substances containing the above chemicals;  
(3) substances containing heavy metals such as lead, mercury, cadmium, chromium;  
(4) substances listed in the annexes to the Stockholm Convention on Persistent Organic Pollutants;  
(5) other substances that are toxic and may pollute the environment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   对案件所涉的环境污染专门性问题难以确定的，由司法鉴定机构出具鉴定意见，或者由国务院环境保护部门指定的机构出具检验报告。  
县级以上环境保护部门及其所属监测机构出具的监测数据，经省级以上环境保护部门认可的，可以作为证据使用。

**Article 11** Where the specialized issue concerning environmental pollution involved in a case is difficult to determine, an expert opinion shall be issued by a judicial appraisal institution, or an inspection report shall be issued by an institution designated by the environmental protection department of the State Council.  
Monitoring data issued by the environmental protection department at or above the county level and its subordinate monitoring institutions may be used as evidence if recognized by the environmental protection department at or above the provincial level.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   本解释发布实施后，最高人民法院《关于审理环境污染刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2006〕4号）同时废止；之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 12** After the promulgation and implementation of this Interpretation, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Environmental Pollution (Fa Shi [2006] No. 4) shall be abolished simultaneously; where any judicial interpretation or normative document previously issued is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
