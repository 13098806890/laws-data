### 最高人民法院关于农村合作基金会从业人员犯罪如何定性问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于农村合作基金会从业人员犯罪  
  
如何定性问题的批复  
  
（2000年4月26日最高人民法院审判委员会第1111次会议通过，自2000年5月12日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2000〕10号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法〔1999〕376号《关于农村合作基金会从业人员犯罪如何定性的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
农村合作基金会从业人员，除具有金融机构现职工作人员身份的以外，不属于金融机构工作人员。对其实施的犯罪行为，应当依照刑法的有关规定定罪处罚。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Judicial Interpretation No. 10 [2000] of the Supreme People's Court  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your Petition No. 376 [1999] of Chuan Gao Fa, titled "Request for Instructions on How to Characterize Crimes Committed by Employees of Rural Cooperative Foundations", has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Employees of rural cooperative foundations, except those who have the status of current employees of financial institutions, shall not be regarded as staff members of financial institutions. The criminal acts committed by them shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
This is the reply.
