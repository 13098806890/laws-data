# 最高人民法院关于审理破坏森林资源刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕36号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-17  
**生效日期**：2000-12-11

> （2000年11月17日最高人民法院审判委员会第1141次会议通过，自2000年12月11日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕36号 为依法惩处破坏森林资源的犯罪活动，根据刑法的有关规定，现就审理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 刑法第三百四十四条规定的“珍贵树木”，包括由省级以上林业主管部门或者其他部门确定的具有重大历史纪念意义、科学研究价值或者年代久远的古树名木，国家禁止、限制出口的珍贵树木以及列入国家重点保护野生植物名录的树木。 第二条 具有下列情形之一的，属于非法采伐、毁坏珍贵树木行为“情节严重”： （一）非法采伐珍贵树木二株以上或者毁坏珍贵树木致使珍贵树木死亡三株以上的； （二）非法采伐珍贵树木二立方米以上的； （三）为首组织、策划、指挥非法采伐或者毁坏珍贵树木的； （四）其他情节严重的情形。 第三条 以非法占有为目的，具有下列情形之一，数量较大的，依照刑法第三百四十五条第一款的规定，以盗伐林木罪定罪处罚： （一）擅自砍伐国家、集体、他人所有或者他人承包经营管理的森林或者其他林木的； （二）擅自砍伐本单位或者本人承包经营管理的森林或者其他林木的； （三）在林木采伐许可证规定的地点以外采伐国家、集体、他人所有或者他人承包经营管理的森林或者其他林木的。 第四条 盗伐林木“数量较大”，以二至五立方米或者幼树一百至二百株为起点；盗伐林木“数量巨大”，以二十至五十立方米或者幼树一千至二千株为起点；盗伐林木“数量特别巨大”，以一百至二百立方米或者幼树五千至一万株为起点。 第五条 违反森林法的规定，具有下列情形之一，数量较大的，依照刑法第三百四十五条第二款的规定，以滥伐林木罪定罪处罚：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 [刑法第三百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-344)规定的“珍贵树木”，包括由省级以上林业主管部门或者其他部门确定的具有重大历史纪念意义、科学研究价值或者年代久远的古树名木，国家禁止、限制出口的珍贵树木以及列入国家重点保护野生植物名录的树木。

**Article 1** The "precious trees" as provided for in Article 344 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall include old and famous trees of great historical commemorative significance, scientific research value, or long standing, as designated by the competent forestry department at or above the provincial level or other departments, precious trees whose export is prohibited or restricted by the State, and trees included in the list of wild plants under special State protection.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 具有下列情形之一的，属于非法采伐、毁坏珍贵树木行为“情节严重”：  
（一）非法采伐珍贵树木二株以上或者毁坏珍贵树木致使珍贵树木死亡三株以上的；  
（二）非法采伐珍贵树木二立方米以上的；  
（三）为首组织、策划、指挥非法采伐或者毁坏珍贵树木的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute "serious circumstances" of illegal felling or destroying precious trees:  
(1) illegally felling two or more precious trees, or destroying precious trees resulting in the death of three or more precious trees;  
(2) illegally felling two cubic meters or more of precious trees;  
(3) being the chief organizer, planner, or commander of illegal felling or destroying precious trees;  
(4) other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 以非法占有为目的，具有下列情形之一，数量较大的，依照[刑法第三百四十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-345)第一款的规定，以盗伐林木罪定罪处罚：  
（一）擅自砍伐国家、集体、他人所有或者他人承包经营管理的森林或者其他林木的；  
（二）擅自砍伐本单位或者本人承包经营管理的森林或者其他林木的；  
（三）在林木采伐许可证规定的地点以外采伐国家、集体、他人所有或者他人承包经营管理的森林或者其他林木的。

**Article 3** Where a person, for the purpose of illegal possession, commits any of the following acts, and the amount is relatively large, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally felling trees in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 345 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) unauthorized felling of forests or other trees owned by the State, a collective, or others, or contract-managed by others;  
(2) unauthorized felling of forests or other trees contract-managed by the person's own unit or by the person himself;  
(3) felling of forests or other trees owned by the State, a collective, or others, or contract-managed by others, beyond the location specified in the tree felling permit.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 盗伐林木“数量较大”，以二至五立方米或者幼树一百至二百株为起点；盗伐林木“数量巨大”，以二十至五十立方米或者幼树一千至二千株为起点；盗伐林木“数量特别巨大”，以一百至二百立方米或者幼树五千至一万株为起点。

**Article 4** The starting point for "relatively large amount" of illegally felled trees shall be two to five cubic meters or 100 to 200 young trees; the starting point for "large amount" shall be 20 to 50 cubic meters or 1,000 to 2,000 young trees; the starting point for "especially large amount" shall be 100 to 200 cubic meters or 5,000 to 10,000 young trees.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 违反森林法的规定，具有下列情形之一，数量较大的，依照[刑法第三百四十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-345)第二款的规定，以滥伐林木罪定罪处罚：  
（一）未经林业行政主管部门及法律规定的其他主管部门批准并核发林木采伐许可证，或者虽持有林木采伐许可证，但违反林木采伐许可证规定的时间、数量、树种或者方式，任意采伐本单位所有或者本人所有的森林或者其他林木的；  
（二）超过林木采伐许可证规定的数量采伐他人所有的森林或者其他林木的。  
林木权属争议一方在林木权属确权之前，擅自砍伐森林或者其他林木，数量较大的，以滥伐林木罪论处。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于在林木采伐许可证规定的地点以外采伐本单位或者本人所有的森林或者其他林木的行为如何适用法律问题的批复.md#art-1" title="被《最高人民法院关于在林木采伐许可证规定的地点以外采伐本单位或者本人所有的森林或者其他林木的行为如何适用法律问题的批复》第1条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 5** Where a person, in violation of the provisions of the Forest Law, commits any of the following acts, and the amount is relatively large, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of indiscriminately felling trees in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 345 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) felling, without authorization, forests or other trees owned by the person's unit or by the person himself, without approval and issuance of a tree felling permit by the competent forestry administrative department or other competent departments as provided by law, or although holding a tree felling permit, violating the time, quantity, tree species, or method specified in the permit;  
(2) felling forests or other trees owned by others beyond the quantity specified in the tree felling permit.  
If one party to a dispute over ownership of trees, before the ownership is confirmed, fells forests or other trees without authorization, and the amount is relatively large, the party shall be punished as the crime of indiscriminately felling trees.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 滥伐林木“数量较大”，以十至二十立方米或者幼树五百至一千株为起点；滥伐林木“数量巨大”，以五十至一百立方米或者幼树二千五百至五千株为起点。

**Article 6** The starting point for "relatively large amount" of indiscriminately felled trees shall be 10 to 20 cubic meters or 500 to 1,000 young trees; the starting point for "large amount" shall be 50 to 100 cubic meters or 2,500 to 5,000 young trees.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 对于一年内多次盗伐、滥伐少量林木未经处罚的，累计其盗伐、滥伐林木的数量，构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。

**Article 7** For persons who, within one year, have repeatedly felled a small amount of trees illegally or indiscriminately without being punished, the amounts of trees illegally or indiscriminately felled shall be accumulated, and if a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 盗伐、滥伐珍贵树木，同时触犯刑法弟三百四十四条、第三百四十五条规定的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Where illegal felling or indiscriminate felling of precious trees simultaneously violates the provisions of Article 344 and Article 345 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision that imposes a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 将国家、集体、他人所有并已经伐倒的树木窃为己有，以及偷砍他人房前屋后、自留地种植的零星树木，数额较大的，依照[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)的规定，以盗窃罪定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Where a person steals trees that have already been felled and owned by the State, a collective, or others, or secretly cuts scattered trees planted around houses or on private plots of others, and the amount is relatively large, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of theft in accordance with Article 264 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 [刑法第三百四十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-345)规定的“非法收购明知是盗伐、滥伐的林木”中的“明知”，是指知道或者应当知道。具有下列情形之一的，可以视为应当知道，但是有证据证明确属被蒙骗的除外：  
（一）在非法的木材交易场所或者销售单位收购木材的；  
（二）收购以明显低于市场价格出售的木材的；  
（三）收购违反规定出售的木材的。

**Article 10** The term "knowingly" in "illegally purchasing trees knowingly felled illegally or indiscriminately" as provided for in Article 345 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China means knowing or should have known. Any of the following circumstances may be deemed as should have known, unless there is evidence proving that the person was actually deceived:  
(1) purchasing timber at an illegal timber trading site or from an illegal sales unit;  
(2) purchasing timber sold at a price obviously lower than the market price;  
(3) purchasing timber sold in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 具有下列情形之一的，属于在林区非法收购盗伐、滥伐的林木“情节严重”：  
（一）非法收购盗伐、滥伐的林木二十立方米以上或者幼树一千株以上的；  
（二）非法收购盗伐、滥伐的珍贵树木二立方米以上或者五株以上的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于在林区非法收购盗伐、滥伐的林木“情节特别严重”：  
（一）非法收购盗伐、滥伐的林木一百立方米以上或者幼树五千株以上的；  
（二）非法收购盗伐、滥伐的珍贵树木五立方米以上或者十株以上的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 11** Any of the following circumstances shall constitute "serious circumstances" of illegally purchasing trees felled illegally or indiscriminately in forest areas:  
(1) illegally purchasing 20 cubic meters or more of trees felled illegally or indiscriminately, or 1,000 or more young trees;  
(2) illegally purchasing 2 cubic meters or more, or 5 or more, of precious trees felled illegally or indiscriminately;  
(3) other serious circumstances.  
Any of the following circumstances shall constitute "especially serious circumstances" of illegally purchasing trees felled illegally or indiscriminately in forest areas:  
(1) illegally purchasing 100 cubic meters or more of trees felled illegally or indiscriminately, or 5,000 or more young trees;  
(2) illegally purchasing 5 cubic meters or more, or 10 or more, of precious trees felled illegally or indiscriminately;  
(3) other especially serious circumstances.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 林业主管部门的工作人员违反森林法的规定，超过批准的年采伐限额发放林木采伐许可证或者违反规定滥发林木采伐许可证，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第四百零七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-407)规定的“情节严重，致使森林遭受严重破坏”，以违法发放林木采伐许可证罪定罪处罚：  
（一）发放林木采伐许可证允许采伐数量累计超过批准的年采伐限额，导致林木被采伐数量在十立方米以上的；  
（二）滥发林木采伐许可证，导致林木被滥伐二十立方米以上的；  
（三）滥发林木采伐许可证，导致珍贵树木被滥伐的；  
（四）批准采伐国家禁止采伐的林木，情节恶劣的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 12** Where a staff member of the competent forestry department, in violation of the provisions of the Forest Law, issues tree felling permits exceeding the approved annual felling quota or issues excessive tree felling permits in violation of regulations, and has any of the following circumstances, which shall fall under the "serious circumstances that cause serious damage to forests" as provided for in Article 407 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally issuing tree felling permits:  
(1) the cumulative amount of felling allowed by the issued permits exceeds the approved annual felling quota, resulting in felling of 10 cubic meters or more of trees;  
(2) issuing excessive permits, resulting in indiscriminate felling of 20 cubic meters or more of trees;  
(3) issuing excessive permits, resulting in indiscriminate felling of precious trees;  
(4) approving felling of trees that the State prohibits from being felled, with serious circumstances;  
(5) other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 对于伪造、变造、买卖林木采伐许可证、木材运输证件，森林、林木、林地权属证书，占用或者征用林地审核同意书、育林基金等缴费收据以及其他国家机关批准的林业证件构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)第一款的规定，以伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件罪定罪处罚。  
对于买卖允许进出口证明书等经营许可证明，同时触犯[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)、第二百八十条规定之罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 13** Where a person forges, alters, buys, or sells tree felling permits, timber transport certificates, certificates of ownership of forests, trees, or forest land, approval letters for occupation or requisition of forest land, payment receipts for forest cultivation funds, or other forestry documents approved by State organs, and a crime is constituted, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of forging, altering, buying, or selling official documents or certificates of State organs in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 280 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where a person buys or sells business licenses such as permits for import and export certificates, and simultaneously violates the provisions of Article 225 and Article 280 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision that imposes a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 聚众哄抢林木五立方米以上的，属于聚众哄抢“数额较大”；聚众哄抢林木二十立方米以上的，属于聚众哄抢“数额巨大”，对首要分子和积极参加的，依照[刑法第二百六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-268)的规定，以聚众哄抢罪定罪处罚。

**Article 14** Where a crowd seizes five cubic meters or more of trees, that shall constitute a "relatively large amount" of crowd seizure; where a crowd seizes 20 cubic meters or more of trees, that shall constitute a "large amount" of crowd seizure; the principal offenders and active participants shall be convicted and punished for the crime of crowd seizure in accordance with Article 268 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 非法实施采种、采脂、挖笋、掘根、剥树皮等行为，牟取经济利益数额较大的，依照[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)的规定，以盗窃罪定罪处罚。同时构成其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 15** Where a person illegally performs acts such as collecting seeds, collecting resin, digging bamboo shoots, digging roots, stripping bark, and obtains economic benefits in a relatively large amount, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of theft in accordance with Article 264 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China. If the act also constitutes another crime, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision that imposes a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 单位犯[刑法第三百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-344)、第三百四十五条规定之罪，定罪量刑标准按照本解释的规定执行。

**Article 16** Where a unit commits a crime provided for in Article 344 or Article 345 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the standards for conviction and sentencing shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 本解释规定的林木数量以立木蓄积计算，计算方法为：原木材积除以该树种的出材率。  
本解释所称“幼树”，是指胸径五厘米以下的树木。  
滥伐林木的数量，应在伐区调查设计允许的误差额以上计算。

**Article 17** The quantity of trees as specified in this Interpretation shall be calculated based on standing volume, and the calculation method shall be: the volume of round timber divided by the yield rate of the tree species.  
The term "young trees" as used in this Interpretation shall refer to trees with a diameter at breast height of less than five centimeters.  
The quantity of indiscriminately felled trees shall be calculated based on the amount exceeding the allowable error in the felling area survey design.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 盗伐、滥伐以生产竹材为主要目的的竹林的定罪量刑问题，有关省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以参照上述规定的精神，规定本地区的具体标准，并报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 18** For the issue of conviction and sentencing for illegal felling or indiscriminate felling of bamboo forests mainly for bamboo timber production, the Higher People's Courts of the relevant provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, by referring to the spirit of the above provisions, formulate specific standards for their respective regions and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court for recordation.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以根据本地区的实际情况，在[本解释第四条](#art-4)、第六条规定的数量幅度内，确定本地区执行的具体数量标准，并报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 19** The Higher People's Courts of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the actual circumstances of their respective regions, determine the specific quantity standards for implementation within the scope of quantity provided in Articles 4 and 6 of this Interpretation, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court for recordation.
