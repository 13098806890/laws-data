### 最高人民法院关于审判人员在审理民事、经济纠纷案件中徇私舞弊枉法裁判构成犯罪的应当依照刑法第一百八十八条规定追究刑事责任的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最 高 人 民 法 院 批 复  
  
关于审判人员在审理民事、经济纠纷案件中徇私舞弊  
  
枉法裁判构成犯罪的应当依照[刑法第一百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-188)  
  
规定追究刑事责任的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1991〕3号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院粤法纪（1990）18号《关于[刑法第一百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-188)是否包括民事、经济案件中的枉法裁判行为的请示》收悉。经研究并征求有关部门意见，现答复如下：  
  
[《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》第四十四条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-44)第三款规定：“审判人员有贪污受贿，徇私舞弊，枉法裁判行为的，应当追究法律责任；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。”据此，审判人员在审判民事案件、经济纠纷案件过程中，徇私舞弊，故意颠倒黑白做枉法裁判，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第一百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-188)的规定追究刑事责任。  
  
1991年7月17日

**Article 1** OFFICIAL REPLY OF THE SUPREME PEOPLE'S COURT  
Regarding the Issue of Investigating Criminal Liability in Accordance with Article 188 of the Criminal Law for Judicial Officers Who, in the Course of Adjudicating Civil and Economic Disputes, Engage in Malpractices for Personal Gain and Bend the Law in Rendering Judgments Constituting a Crime  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1991] No. 3  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions (Yue Fa Ji [1990] No. 18) concerning whether Article 188 of the Criminal Law includes the act of bending the law in rendering judgments in civil and economic cases. After studying the matter and soliciting opinions from relevant departments, we hereby reply as follows:  
Article 44, paragraph 3, of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China provides: "Judicial officers who engage in embezzlement or bribery, malpractices for personal gain, or bend the law in rendering judgments shall be subject to legal liability; if the act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law." Accordingly, if a judicial officer, in the process of adjudicating civil cases or economic disputes, engages in malpractices for personal gain, intentionally confuses right and wrong, and bends the law in rendering a judgment, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 188 of the Criminal Law.  
July 17, 1991
