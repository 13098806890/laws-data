# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理组织和利用邪教组织犯罪案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1999〕18号  
**公布日期**：1999-10-09  
**生效日期**：1999-10-30

> 1999年10月8日最高人民检察院检察委员会第九届第47次会议通过，自1999年10月30日起施行。） 为依法惩处组织和利用邪教组织进行犯罪活动，根据刑法的有关规定，现就办理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 刑法第三百条中的“邪教组织”是指冒用宗教、气功或者其他名义建立，神化首要分子，利用制造、散布迷信邪说等手段蛊惑、蒙骗他人，发展、控制成员，危害社会的非法组织。 第二条 组织和利用邪教组织并具有下列情形之一的，依照刑法第三百条第一款的规定定罪处罚： （一）聚众围攻、冲击国家机关、企业事业单位，扰乱国家机关、企业事业单位的工作、生产、经营、教学和科研秩序的； （二）非法举行集会、游行、示威，煽动、欺骗、组织其成员或者其他人聚众围攻、冲击、强占、哄闹公共场所及宗教活动场所，扰乱社会秩序的； （三）抗拒有关部门取缔或者已经被有关部门取缔，又恢复或者另行建立邪教组织，或者继续进行邪教活动的； （四）煽动、欺骗、组织其成员或者其他人不履行法定义务，情节严重的； （五）出版、印刷、复制、发行宣扬邪教内容出版物，以及印制邪教组织标识的； （六）其他破坏国家法律、行政法规实施行为的。实施前款所列行为，并具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”： （一）跨省、自治区、直辖市建立组织机构或者发展成员的； （二）勾结境外机构、组织、人员进行邪教活动的； （三）出版、印刷、复制、发行宣扬邪教内容出版物以及印制邪教组织标识，数量或者数额巨大的； （四）煽动、欺骗、组织其成员或者其他人破坏国家法律、行政法规实施，造成严重后果的。 第三条 刑法第三百条第二款规定的组织和利用邪教组织蒙骗他人，致人死亡，是指组织和利用邪教组织制造、散布迷信邪说，蒙骗其成员或者其他人实施绝食、自残、自虐等行为，或者阻止病人进行正常治疗，致人死亡的情形。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 [刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)中的“邪教组织”是指冒用宗教、气功或者其他名义建立，神化首要分子，利用制造、散布迷信邪说等手段蛊惑、蒙骗他人，发展、控制成员，危害社会的非法组织。

**Article 1** The term 'cult organization' in Article 300 of the Criminal Law shall refer to an illegal organization that, by usurping the name of religion, Qigong or other names, deifies its principal members, uses methods such as creating and spreading superstitious fallacies to delude and deceive others, and develops and controls members to endanger society.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 组织和利用邪教组织并具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）聚众围攻、冲击国家机关、企业事业单位，扰乱国家机关、企业事业单位的工作、生产、经营、教学和科研秩序的；  
（二）非法举行集会、游行、示威，煽动、欺骗、组织其成员或者其他人聚众围攻、冲击、强占、哄闹公共场所及宗教活动场所，扰乱社会秩序的；  
（三）抗拒有关部门取缔或者已经被有关部门取缔，又恢复或者另行建立邪教组织，或者继续进行邪教活动的；  
（四）煽动、欺骗、组织其成员或者其他人不履行法定义务，情节严重的；  
（五）出版、印刷、复制、发行宣扬邪教内容出版物，以及印制邪教组织标识的；  
（六）其他破坏国家法律、行政法规实施行为的。实施前款所列行为，并具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”：  
（一）跨省、自治区、直辖市建立组织机构或者发展成员的；  
（二）勾结境外机构、组织、人员进行邪教活动的；  
（三）出版、印刷、复制、发行宣扬邪教内容出版物以及印制邪教组织标识，数量或者数额巨大的；  
（四）煽动、欺骗、组织其成员或者其他人破坏国家法律、行政法规实施，造成严重后果的。

**Article 2** Any person who organizes or uses a cult organization under any of the following circumstances shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 300 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) gathering a crowd to besiege or assault State organs, enterprises or institutions, thereby disrupting the order of work, production, operation, teaching or scientific research of such organs, enterprises or institutions;  
(2) holding illegal assemblies, processions or demonstrations, or inciting, deceiving or organizing its members or other persons to gather a crowd to besiege, assault, forcibly occupy or make a clamor in public places or places for religious activities, thereby disrupting public order;  
(3) resisting the banning by the relevant department, or restoring or establishing a new cult organization after being banned by the relevant department, or continuing to engage in cult activities;  
(4) inciting, deceiving or organizing its members or other persons to fail to perform statutory obligations, if the circumstances are serious;  
(5) publishing, printing, reproducing or distributing publications that propagate cult content, or printing logos of cult organizations; or  
(6) other acts that impair the implementation of laws or administrative regulations.  
Where any person commits any of the acts listed in the preceding paragraph under any of the following circumstances, it shall be considered 'especially serious circumstances':  
(1) establishing an organizational structure or developing members across provinces, autonomous regions or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(2) colluding with foreign institutions, organizations or persons to engage in cult activities;  
(3) publishing, printing, reproducing or distributing publications that propagate cult content, or printing logos of cult organizations, in huge quantities or involving huge amounts of money; or  
(4) inciting, deceiving or organizing its members or other persons to impair the implementation of laws or administrative regulations, thereby causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 [刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第二款规定的组织和利用邪教组织蒙骗他人，致人死亡，是指组织和利用邪教组织制造、散布迷信邪说，蒙骗其成员或者其他人实施绝食、自残、自虐等行为，或者阻止病人进行正常治疗，致人死亡的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”：  
（一）造成3人以上死亡的；  
（二）造成死亡人数不满3人，但造成多人重伤的；  
（三）曾因邪教活动受过刑事或者行政处罚，又组织和利用邪教组织蒙骗他人，致人死亡的；  
（四）造成其他特别严重后果的。

**Article 3** The act of organizing or using a cult organization to deceive others, causing death, as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 300 of the Criminal Law, shall refer to the circumstances where a person organizes or uses a cult organization to create and spread superstitious fallacies, deceiving its members or other persons into acts such as fasting, self-injury or self-abuse, or preventing patients from receiving normal medical treatment, thereby causing death.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be considered 'especially serious circumstances':  
(1) causing death of three or more persons;  
(2) causing death of fewer than three persons but causing serious injury to many persons;  
(3) having previously received criminal or administrative penalties for cult activities, and again organizing or using a cult organization to deceive others, causing death; or  
(4) causing other especially serious consequences.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 组织和利用邪教组织制造、散布迷信邪说，指使、胁迫其成员或者其他人实施自杀、自伤行为的，分别依照[刑法第二百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-232)、第二百三十四条的规定，以故意杀人罪或者故意伤害罪定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Any person who, by organizing or using a cult organization, creates or spreads superstitious fallacies, and instructs or coerces its members or other persons to commit suicide or self-injury, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of intentional homicide or the crime of intentional injury in accordance with the provisions of Articles 232 and 234 of the Criminal Law respectively.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 组织和利用邪教组织，以迷信邪说引诱、胁迫、欺骗或者其他手段，奸淫妇女、幼女的，依照[刑法第二百三十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-236)的规定，以强奸罪或者奸淫幼女罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Any person who, by organizing or using a cult organization, uses superstitious fallacies to lure, coerce, deceive or resort to other means to rape a woman or have sexual relations with a young girl, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of rape or the crime of sexual assault on a minor female in accordance with the provisions of Article 236 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 组织和利用邪教组织以各种欺骗手段，收取他人财物的，依照[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)的规定，以诈骗罪定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Any person who, by organizing or using a cult organization, uses various deceptive means to collect money or property from others, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fraud in accordance with the provisions of Article 266 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 组织和利用邪教组织，组织、策划、实施、煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一或者颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度的，分别依照[刑法第一百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-103)、第一百零五条、第一百一十三条的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Any person who, by organizing or using a cult organization, organizes, plans, carries out or incites acts of splitting the State, undermining national unity, or subverting the State power and overthrowing the socialist system, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Articles 103, 105 and 113 of the Criminal Law respectively.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 对于邪教组织和组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施的犯罪分子，以各种手段非法聚敛的财物，用于犯罪的工具、宣传品等，应当依法追缴、没收。

**Article 8** With respect to cult organizations and criminals who organize or use cult organizations to impair the implementation of law, any property illegally accumulated by various means, and instruments, propaganda materials, etc., used for crimes shall be recovered and confiscated according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 对组织和利用邪教组织进行犯罪活动的组织、策划、指挥者和屡教不改的积极参加者，依照刑法和本解释的规定追究刑事责任；对有自首、立功表现的，可以依法从轻、减轻或者免除处罚。  
对于受蒙蔽、胁迫参加邪教组织并已退出和不再参加邪教组织活动的人员，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 9** Organizers, planners, commanders who organize or use a cult organization to engage in criminal activities, and active participants who refuse to reform despite repeated education, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law and this Interpretation. Those who voluntarily surrender or perform meritorious services may be given a lighter, mitigated or exempted punishment according to law.  
Any person who participated in a cult organization through deception or coercion and has withdrawn from and no longer participates in cult organization activities shall not be treated as a crime.
