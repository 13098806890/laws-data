# 最高人民法院关于审理破坏野生动物资源刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕37号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-17  
**生效日期**：2000-12-11

> （2000年11月17日最高人民法院审判委员会第1141次会议通过，自2000年12月11日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕37号 为依法惩处破坏野生动物资源的犯罪活动，根据刑法的有关规定，现就审理这类案件具体应用法律的若于问题解释如下： 第一条 刑法第三百四十一条第一款规定的“珍贵、濒危野生动物”，包括列入国家重点保护野生动物名录的国家一、二级保护野生动物、列入《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》附录一、附录二的野生动物以及驯养繁殖的上述物种。 第二条 刑法第三百四十一条第一款规定的“收购”，包括以营利、自用等为目的的购买行为；“运输”，包括采用携带、邮寄、利用他人、使用交通工具等方法进行运送的行为；“出售”，包括出卖和以营利为目的的加工利用行为。 第三条 非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节严重”： （一）达到本解释附表所列相应数量标准的； （二）非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售不同种类的珍贵、濒危野生动物，其中两种以上分别达到附表所列“情节严重”数量标准一半以上的。 非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”： （一）达到本解释附表所列相应数量标准的； （二）非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售不同种类的珍贵、濒危野生动物，其中两种以上分别达到附表所列“情节特别严重”数量标准一半以上的。 第四条 非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物构成犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为“情节严重”；非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物符合本解释第三条第一款的规定，并具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为“情节特别严重”： （一）犯罪集团的首要分子； （二）严重影响对野生动物的科研、养殖等工作顺利进行的； （三）以武装掩护方法实施犯罪的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 [刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第一款规定的“珍贵、濒危野生动物”，包括列入国家重点保护野生动物名录的国家一、二级保护野生动物、列入《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》附录一、附录二的野生动物以及驯养繁殖的上述物种。

**Article 1** . "Rare and endangered wild animals" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall include state-first-class and second-class protected wild animals listed in the List of Key Protected Wild Animals of the State, wild animals listed in Appendix I and Appendix II of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora, and the above-mentioned species that are domesticated and bred.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 [刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第一款规定的“收购”，包括以营利、自用等为目的的购买行为；“运输”，包括采用携带、邮寄、利用他人、使用交通工具等方法进行运送的行为；“出售”，包括出卖和以营利为目的的加工利用行为。

**Article 2** . "Purchase" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall include the act of buying for purposes such as profit-making or self-use; "transportation" shall include the act of conveying by means such as carrying, mailing, using others, using transportation vehicles; "sale" shall include selling and the act of processing and utilizing for profit-making purposes.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节严重”：  
（一）达到本解释附表所列相应数量标准的；  
（二）非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售不同种类的珍贵、濒危野生动物，其中两种以上分别达到附表所列“情节严重”数量标准一半以上的。  
非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”：  
（一）达到本解释附表所列相应数量标准的；  
（二）非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售不同种类的珍贵、濒危野生动物，其中两种以上分别达到附表所列“情节特别严重”数量标准一半以上的。

**Article 3** . Illegally hunting, killing, purchasing, transporting, or selling rare and endangered wild animals under any of the following circumstances shall constitute "serious circumstances":  
(1) reaching the corresponding quantity standard set forth in the appendix to this Interpretation;  
(2) illegally hunting, killing, purchasing, transporting, or selling different species of rare and endangered wild animals, among which two or more species respectively reach more than half of the quantity standard for "serious circumstances" set forth in the appendix.  
Illegally hunting, killing, purchasing, transporting, or selling rare and endangered wild animals under any of the following circumstances shall constitute "particularly serious circumstances":  
(1) reaching the corresponding quantity standard set forth in the appendix to this Interpretation;  
(2) illegally hunting, killing, purchasing, transporting, or selling different species of rare and endangered wild animals, among which two or more species respectively reach more than half of the quantity standard for "particularly serious circumstances" set forth in the appendix.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物构成犯罪，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为“情节严重”；非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物符合[本解释第三条](#art-3)第一款的规定，并具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为“情节特别严重”：  
（一）犯罪集团的首要分子；  
（二）严重影响对野生动物的科研、养殖等工作顺利进行的；  
（三）以武装掩护方法实施犯罪的；  
（四）使用特种车、军用车等交通工具实施犯罪的；  
（五）造成其他重大损失的。

**Article 4** . Where the illegal hunting, killing, purchasing, transporting, or selling of rare and endangered wild animals constitutes a crime, and any of the following circumstances exists, it may be determined as "serious circumstances"; where the illegal hunting, killing, purchasing, transporting, or selling of rare and endangered wild animals meets the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 3 of this Interpretation, and any of the following circumstances also exists, it may be determined as "particularly serious circumstances":  
(1) being the ringleader of a criminal group;  
(2) seriously affecting the smooth progress of scientific research, breeding, or other work on wild animals;  
(3) committing the crime by armed cover;  
(4) using special vehicles, military vehicles, or other means of transport to commit the crime;  
(5) causing other major losses.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 非法收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物制品具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节严重”：  
（一）价值在十万元以上的；  
（二）非法获利五万元以上的；  
（三）具有其他严重情节的。  
非法收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物制品具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”：  
（一）价值在二十万元以上的；  
（二）非法获利十万元以上的；  
（三）具有其他特别严重情节的。

**Article 5** . Illegally purchasing, transporting, or selling products of rare and endangered wild animals under any of the following circumstances shall constitute "serious circumstances":  
(1) the value being over 100,000 yuan;  
(2) the illegal gains being over 50,000 yuan;  
(3) having other serious circumstances.  
Illegally purchasing, transporting, or selling products of rare and endangered wild animals under any of the following circumstances shall constitute "particularly serious circumstances":  
(1) the value being over 200,000 yuan;  
(2) the illegal gains being over 100,000 yuan;  
(3) having other particularly serious circumstances.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 违反狩猎法规，在禁猎区、禁猎期或者使用禁用的工具、方法狩猎，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法狩猎“情节严重”：  
（一）非法狩猎野生动物二十只以上的；  
（二）违反狩猎法规，在禁猎区或者禁猎期使用禁用的工具、方法狩猎的；  
（三）具有其他严重情节的。

**Article 6** . Where anyone violates hunting regulations and hunts in a prohibited hunting area, during a prohibited hunting period, or using prohibited tools or methods, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall constitute "serious circumstances" of illegal hunting:  
(1) illegally hunting more than twenty wild animals;  
(2) violating hunting regulations and hunting in a prohibited hunting area or during a prohibited hunting period using prohibited tools or methods;  
(3) having other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 使用爆炸、投毒、设置电网等危险方法破坏野生动物资源，构成非法猎捕、杀害珍贵、濒危野生动物罪或者非法狩猎罪，同时构成[刑法第一百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-114)或者第一百一十五条规定之罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 7** . Where anyone uses dangerous methods such as explosion, poisoning, or setting up electric nets to destroy wildlife resources, constituting the crime of illegally hunting, killing rare and endangered wild animals or the crime of illegal hunting, and also constitutes the crimes prescribed in Article 114 or Article 115 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the offense shall be determined and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 实施[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)规定的犯罪，又以暴力、威胁方法抗拒查处，构成其他犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。

**Article 8** . Where anyone commits a crime prescribed in Article 341 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and additionally resists investigation and punishment by violence or threats, thus constituting another crime, the punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions on concurrent punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 伪造、变造、买卖国家机关颁发的野生动物允许进出口证明书、特许猎捕证、狩猎证、驯养繁殖许可证等公文、证件构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)第一款的规定以伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件罪定罪处罚。  
实施上述行为构成犯罪，同时构成[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第二项规定的非法经营罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 9** . Where anyone forges, alters, or buys or sells official documents or certificates such as wildlife import/export permits, special hunting permits, hunting permits, domestication and breeding permits issued by state organs, constituting a crime, the offense shall be determined and punished as the crime of forging, altering, or buying or selling official documents and certificates of state organs in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 280 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Where the above act constitutes a crime and also constitutes the crime of illegal business operation prescribed in subparagraph (2) of Article 225 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the offense shall be determined and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》附录一、附录二所列的非原产于我国的野生动物“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”的认定标准，参照[本解释第三条](#art-3)、第四条以及附表所列与其同属的国家一、二级保护野生动物的认定标准执行；没有与其同属的国家一、二级保护野生动物的，参照与其同科的国家一、二级保护野生动物的认定标准执行。

**Article 10** . The standards for determining "serious circumstances" and "particularly serious circumstances" for illegally hunting, killing, purchasing, transporting, or selling wild animals not originally produced in China listed in Appendix I and Appendix II of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora shall be implemented by reference to the determination standards for state-first-class and second-class protected wild animals of the same genus as set forth in Articles 3 and 4 of this Interpretation and the appendix; if there are no state-first-class or second-class protected wild animals of the same genus, the determination standards for state-first-class or second-class protected wild animals of the same family shall be applied by reference.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 珍贵、濒危野生动物制品的价值，依照国家野生动物保护主管部门的规定核定；核定价值低于实际交易价格的，以实际交易价格认定。

**Article 11** . The value of products of rare and endangered wild animals shall be assessed in accordance with the provisions of the competent department for wildlife protection of the State; if the assessed value is lower than the actual transaction price, the actual transaction price shall be taken as the value.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 单位犯[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)规定之罪，定罪量刑标准依照本解释的有关规定执行。  
附：非法猎捕、杀害、收购、运输、出售珍贵、濒危野生动物刑事案件“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”数量认定标准表（注：2000年第六期公报刊登的《关于审理走私刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2000〕30号），所附表与本解释所附表内容一致。法释〔2000〕30号表中（一）、（二）栏内所列的数量，即分别为本解释附表中“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”的数量认定标准。为节省篇幅，本解释的附表不再刊登，读者可查询法释〔2000〕30号司法解释。）

**Article 12** . Where a unit commits a crime prescribed in Article 341 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the conviction and sentencing standards shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.
