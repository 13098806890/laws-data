# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理偷税、抗税刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：号高检会〔1992〕5号  
**公布日期**：1992-03-16

---

### 正文

一、负有纳税义务的单位和个人（简称纳税人），违反税收法律、法规，采用欺骗、隐瞒等手段，少缴或者不缴应纳税款，逃避履行纳税义务，情节严重的，以偷税罪对直接责任人员追究刑事责任。偷税通常采用的手段有：伪造、涂改、隐匿、销毁帐册、票据、凭证；转移资金、财产、帐户；不报或者谎报应税项目、数量、所得额、收入额；虚增成本、多报费用、减少利润；虚构事实骗取减税、免税等。

**Article None** 1. Where entities or individuals with tax payment obligations (hereinafter referred to as taxpayers) violate tax laws and regulations by means of deception or concealment, pay less or no tax due, evade the performance of tax obligations, and if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of tax evasion. The means commonly used for tax evasion include: forging, altering, concealing, or destroying account books, bills, receipts, or vouchers; transferring funds, property, or accounts; failing to report or falsely reporting taxable items, quantities, income amounts, or revenue amounts; falsely increasing costs, overstating expenses, or reducing profits; fabricating facts to defraud tax reductions or exemptions, etc.

二、纳税人违反税收法律、法规，采取公开对抗或者其他手段，抗拒履行纳税义务，情节严重的，以抗税罪对直接责任人员追究刑事责任。抗税通常采用的手段有：拒绝按照税收法律、法规缴纳税款、滞纳金；以各种借口拖延不缴或者抵制缴纳税款；拒绝按照法定手续办理税务登记、纳税申报和提供纳税资料；拒绝接受税务机关依法进行的税务检查；冲击、打砸税务机关，殴打、污辱税务人员（包括税务助征员、代征员）等。

**Article None** 2. Where taxpayers violate tax laws and regulations, adopt open confrontation or other means to resist performing tax obligations, and if the circumstances are serious, the persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of tax resistance. The means commonly used for tax resistance include: refusing to pay taxes or overdue fines in accordance with tax laws and regulations; delaying or resisting the payment of taxes on various pretexts; refusing to handle tax registration, tax declaration, or provide tax materials in accordance with statutory procedures; refusing to accept tax inspections lawfully conducted by tax authorities; storming, smashing, or vandalizing tax authorities, assaulting or insulting tax officials (including tax assistant collectors and deputy collectors), etc.

三、负有代征代缴税款义务的单位和个人（简称代征人）、负有代扣代缴、代收代缴税款义务的单位和个人（简称扣缴义务人），有上述第一、二条所列行为之一的，以偷税罪、抗税罪追究刑事责任。

**Article None** 3. Where entities or individuals with the obligation to collect and pay taxes on behalf of the tax authority (hereinafter referred to as collection agents) or entities or individuals with the obligation to withhold and remit or collect and remit taxes (hereinafter referred to as withholding agents) commit any of the acts listed in Articles 1 and 2 above, they shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of tax evasion or the crime of tax resistance.

四、偷税罪、抗税罪的“直接责任人员”，是指偷税、抗税单位中对该罪负有直接责任的法定代表人、主管人员和其他直接参与人员，以及偷税、抗税的个人。

**Article None** 4. The term "persons directly responsible" for the crime of tax evasion or the crime of tax resistance refers to the legal representative, persons in charge, and other persons directly involved who bear direct responsibility for the crime in an entity that commits tax evasion or tax resistance, as well as individuals who commit tax evasion or tax resistance.

五、各类企业、事业和社会团体等纳税单位以及负有代征、扣缴义务的单位，偷税数额达到下列标准之一的，属于偷税情节严重：  
（一）偷税五千元以上不满一万元，且达到该单位同期应纳该税种税款总额的百分之四十的  
（二）偷税一万元以上不满五万元，且达到该单位同期应纳该税种税款总额的百分之三十的；  
（三）偷税五万元以上不满十万元，且达到该单位同期应纳该税种税款总额的百分之二十的；  
（四）偷税十万元以上不满三十万元，且达到该单位同期应纳该税种税款总额的百分之十的；  
（五）偷税总额达三十万元以上的。  
对个体工商户、个人承包户、租赁经营户、个人合伙或者其他纳税个人以及负有代征、扣缴义务的个人，认定偷税情节严重的数额起点为二千至五千元。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以结合本地区情况，在本条第二款规定的数额幅度内制定本地区的执行标准，并报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院备案。

**Article None** 5. Where taxpaying entities such as various enterprises, institutions, and social organizations, as well as entities with obligations to collect or withhold taxes, commit tax evasion in amounts reaching any of the following standards, the circumstances shall be considered serious:  
(a) tax evasion of not less than 5,000 yuan but less than 10,000 yuan, and reaching 40% of the total tax amount payable by the entity for the same tax type in the same period;  
(b) tax evasion of not less than 10,000 yuan but less than 50,000 yuan, and reaching 30% of the total tax amount payable by the entity for the same tax type in the same period;  
(c) tax evasion of not less than 50,000 yuan but less than 100,000 yuan, and reaching 20% of the total tax amount payable by the entity for the same tax type in the same period;  
(d) tax evasion of not less than 100,000 yuan but less than 300,000 yuan, and reaching 10% of the total tax amount payable by the entity for the same tax type in the same period;  
(e) total tax evasion reaching 300,000 yuan or more.  
For individual industrial and commercial households, individual contractors, lease operators, individual partnerships, or other individual taxpayers, as well as individuals with obligations to collect or withhold taxes, the starting amount for deeming the circumstances of tax evasion as serious shall be from 2,000 to 5,000 yuan.  
The Higher People's Courts and People's Procuratorates of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of local conditions, formulate local enforcement standards within the range of amounts provided in the second paragraph of this Article, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate for record.

六、偷税数额虽未达到但接近前条所定数额标准，并有下列情形之一，尚不构成其他犯罪的，也属于偷税情节严重：  
（一）偷税三次以上经教育不改的；  
（二）为逃避追查而有意毁坏、伪造计税凭证或者其他纳税资料的；  
（三）阻碍税务机关依法检查的；  
（四）向税务人员行贿的；  
（五）其他偷税情节严重的。

**Article None** 6. Where the amount of tax evasion has not reached but is close to the amount standards specified in the preceding Article, and any of the following circumstances exists, and does not constitute another crime, the circumstances shall also be considered serious:  
(a) having committed tax evasion three or more times and failing to correct after education;  
(b) intentionally destroying or forging tax calculation vouchers or other tax materials in order to evade investigation;  
(c) obstructing tax authorities from conducting lawful inspections;  
(d) bribing tax officials;  
(e) other circumstances where tax evasion is serious.

七、抗税数额达到第五条所定数额标准的百分之五十以上的，应当认定为抗税情节严重。

**Article None** 7. Where the amount of tax resistance reaches 50% or more of the amount standards specified in Article 5, the circumstances shall be deemed serious for tax resistance.

八、抗税数额虽未达到前条所定数额标准，但具有下列情形之一，尚不构成其他犯罪的，也属于抗税情节严重：  
（一）以暴力或者暴力威胁抗税的；  
（二）抗缴税款、滞纳金三次以上的；  
（三）抗拒税务机关依法检查的；  
（四）以各种借口拖延不缴或者抵制缴纳税款，时间超过六个月的；  
（五）冲击、打砸税务机关，污辱、殴打、报复税务人员或者采取其他恶劣手段妨碍税务机关工作秩序的；  
（六）其他抗税情节严重的。

**Article None** 8. Where the amount of tax resistance has not reached the amount standard specified in the preceding Article, but any of the following circumstances exists, and does not constitute another crime, the circumstances shall also be considered serious for tax resistance:  
(a) using violence or threats of violence to resist tax;  
(b) refusing to pay taxes or overdue fines three or more times;  
(c) resisting lawful inspections by tax authorities;  
(d) delaying or resisting the payment of taxes on various pretexts for a period exceeding six months;  
(e) storming or smashing tax authorities, insulting, assaulting, or retaliating against tax officials, or adopting other vile means to disrupt the working order of tax authorities;  
(f) other circumstances where tax resistance is serious.

九、同一纳税人同时偷、抗二种以上税的，只要其中一种达到上列构成犯罪标准的，所偷、抗其他税种的数额应当一并计入偷税、抗税的总额。

**Article None** 9. Where the same taxpayer simultaneously evades or resists two or more types of taxes, as long as one type reaches the above standard for constituting a crime, the amounts evaded or resisted for other tax types shall be included in the total amount of tax evasion or tax resistance.

十、与纳税人、代征人、扣缴义务人勾结，为偷税犯罪提供帐号、发票、证明，或者以其他手段共同实施偷税罪的，以偷税共犯论处。  
唆使、煽动纳税人、代征人、扣缴义务人抗税，或者以其他手段共同实施抗税罪的，以抗税共犯论处。  
税务人员犯前两款罪的，从重处罚。

**Article None** 10. Whoever colludes with taxpayers, collection agents, or withholding agents to provide accounts, invoices, or certificates for tax evasion crimes, or jointly commits the crime of tax evasion by other means, shall be punished as an accomplice to tax evasion.  
Whoever instigates or incites taxpayers, collection agents, or withholding agents to resist tax, or jointly commits the crime of tax resistance by other means, shall be punished as an accomplice to tax resistance.  
Tax officials who commit the crimes in the preceding two paragraphs shall be given severe punishment.

十一、对同一税款，既犯偷税罪，又犯抗税罪的，实行数罪并罚。  
因暴力抗税实施伤害、杀人行为的，按伤害罪、杀人罪定罪处罚，或者根据案情实行数罪并罚。

**Article None** 11. Where a person commits both the crime of tax evasion and the crime of tax resistance with respect to the same tax payment, the person shall be punished for multiple crimes.  
Where injury or homicide is committed through violent tax resistance, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of injury or the crime of homicide, or shall be punished for multiple crimes according to the circumstances of the case.

十二、人民法院、人民检察院在办理偷税、抗税案件中，如果发现纳税人登记的经济性质（包括所有制性质和分配形式）与实际不符的，应当根据查明的情况，按其实际的经济性质依法处理。

**Article None** 12. Where, in handling cases of tax evasion or tax resistance, the People's Courts or People's Procuratorates discover that the registered economic nature (including the nature of ownership and the form of distribution) of a taxpayer is inconsistent with the actual situation, they shall, based on the facts found, handle the case according to law in accordance with the actual economic nature.

十三、以营利为目的，倒卖发票，情节严重的，以投机倒把罪论处。

**Article None** 13. Whoever, for the purpose of profit, resells invoices, and if the circumstances are serious, shall be punished as the crime of speculation and profiteering.

十四、一九九二年五月一日后处理的偷税、抗税刑事案件，适用本解释。  
1992年3月16日

**Article None** 14. This Interpretation shall apply to criminal cases of tax evasion and tax resistance handled after May 1, 1992.  
March 16, 1992
