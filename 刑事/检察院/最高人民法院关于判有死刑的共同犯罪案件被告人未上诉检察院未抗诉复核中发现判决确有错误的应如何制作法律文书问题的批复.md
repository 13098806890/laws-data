### 最高人民法院关于判有死刑的共同犯罪案件被告人未上诉检察院未抗诉复核中发现判决确有错误的应如何制作法律文书问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于判有死刑的共同犯罪案件  
  
被告人未上诉检察院未抗诉复核中发现  
  
判决确有错误的应如何制作法律文书的请示  
  
法复〔1994〕9号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于判有死刑的共同犯罪案件，被告人未上诉、检察院未抗诉、复核中发现判决确有错误的应如何制作法律文书的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
对中级人民法院报送核准的判有死刑的共同犯罪案件，全案各被告人均未提出上诉，检察院也未提出抗诉，高级人民法院在复核中同意原审判决对被告人判处死刑的，应制作刑事裁定书核准判处死刑；如果认为对被告人不应判处死刑的，可以用判决书直接改判。在复核中，如果认为原审判决对同案被判处无期徒刑以下刑罚的被告人量刑不当，应当在核准判处死刑或者改变死刑判决的同时，依照[《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第一百四十九条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-149)第二款的规定，由高级人民法院提审或者指令下级人民法院再审，并另行制作刑事判决书或者刑事裁定书。  
  
1994年5月16日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Request for Instructions on How to Draft Legal Documents When, in a Joint Crime Case Involving the Death Penalty, the Defendant Has Not Appealed and the Procuratorate Has Not Protested, Yet During Review It Is Found That the Judgment Is Indeed Erroneous  
Fa Fu [1994] No. 9  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
Your court's "Request for Instructions on How to Draft Legal Documents When, in a Joint Crime Case Involving the Death Penalty, the Defendant Has Not Appealed and the Procuratorate Has Not Protested, Yet During Review It Is Found That the Judgment Is Indeed Erroneous" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
For a joint crime case involving the death penalty submitted by an Intermediate People's Court for approval, where none of the defendants in the entire case have filed an appeal and the procuratorate has not filed a protest, and the Higher People's Court during review agrees with the original judgment imposing the death penalty on a defendant, it shall issue a criminal ruling to approve the death penalty; if it considers that the defendant should not be sentenced to death, it may directly alter the judgment by issuing a judgment. During review, if it is found that the original judgment's sentencing of a co-defendant sentenced to life imprisonment or a lighter punishment is improper, while approving the death penalty or altering the death sentence, the Higher People's Court shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 149, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, bring the case up for trial itself or instruct a lower people's court to conduct a retrial, and separately issue a criminal judgment or a criminal ruling.  
May 16, 1994
