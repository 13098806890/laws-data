# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理非法生产、销售烟草专卖品等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2010〕7号  
**公布日期**：2009-12-28  
**生效日期**：2010-03-26

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理非法生产、销售烟草专卖品等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2009年12月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1481次会议、2010年2月4日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第29次会议通过，现予公布，自2010年3月26日起施行。 2010年3月2日 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院 关于办理非法生产、销售烟草专卖品等 刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2010〕7号 为维护社会主义市场经济秩序,依法惩治非法生产、销售烟草专卖品等犯罪，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理这类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  生产、销售伪劣卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品，销售金额在五万元以上的，依照刑法第一百四十条的规定，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪定罪处罚。 未经卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品注册商标所有人许可，在卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品上使用与其注册商标相同的商标，情节严重的，依照刑法第二百一十三条的规定，以假冒注册商标罪定罪处罚。 销售明知是假冒他人注册商标的卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品，销售金额较大的，依照刑法第二百一十四条的规定，以销售假冒注册商标的商品罪定罪处罚。 伪造、擅自制造他人卷烟、雪茄烟注册商标标识或者销售伪造、擅自制造的卷烟、雪茄烟注册商标标识，情节严重的，依照刑法第二百一十五条的规定，以非法制造、销售非法制造的注册商标标识罪定罪处罚。 违反国家烟草专卖管理法律法规，未经烟草专卖行政主管部门许可，无烟草专卖生产企业许可证、烟草专卖批发企业许可证、特种烟草专卖经营企业许可证、烟草专卖零售许可证等许可证明，非法经营烟草专卖品，情节严重的，依照刑法第二百二十五条的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。 第二条  伪劣卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品尚未销售，货值金额达到刑法第一百四十条规定的销售金额定罪起点数额标准的三倍以上的，或者销售金额未达到五万元，但与未销售货值金额合计达到十五万元以上的，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪（未遂）定罪处罚。 销售金额和未销售货值金额分别达到不同的法定刑幅度或者均达到同一法定刑幅度的，在处罚较重的法定刑幅度内酌情从重处罚。 查获的未销售的伪劣卷烟、雪茄烟，能够查清销售价格的，按照实际销售价格计算。无法查清实际销售价格，有品牌的，按照该品牌卷烟、雪茄烟的查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的零售价格计算；无品牌的，按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度卷烟平均零售价格计算。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  生产、销售伪劣卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品，销售金额在五万元以上的，依照[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)的规定，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪定罪处罚。  
未经卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品注册商标所有人许可，在卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品上使用与其注册商标相同的商标，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百一十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-213)的规定，以假冒注册商标罪定罪处罚。  
销售明知是假冒他人注册商标的卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品，销售金额较大的，依照[刑法第二百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-214)的规定，以销售假冒注册商标的商品罪定罪处罚。  
伪造、擅自制造他人卷烟、雪茄烟注册商标标识或者销售伪造、擅自制造的卷烟、雪茄烟注册商标标识，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百一十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-215)的规定，以非法制造、销售非法制造的注册商标标识罪定罪处罚。  
违反国家烟草专卖管理法律法规，未经烟草专卖行政主管部门许可，无烟草专卖生产企业许可证、烟草专卖批发企业许可证、特种烟草专卖经营企业许可证、烟草专卖零售许可证等许可证明，非法经营烟草专卖品，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Where any person produces or sells counterfeit or substandard tobacco monopoly commodities such as cigarettes and cigars, and the sales amount is 50,000 RMB or more, such person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard products in accordance with Article 140 of the Criminal Law.  
Where, without permission from the owner of a registered trademark for cigarettes, cigars or other tobacco monopoly commodities, any person uses a trademark identical to the registered trademark on such commodities, and the circumstances are serious, such person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of counterfeiting a registered trademark in accordance with Article 213 of the Criminal Law.  
Where any person sells cigarettes, cigars or other tobacco monopoly commodities that he or she clearly knows bear a counterfeit registered trademark of another person, and the sales amount is relatively large, such person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of selling goods bearing a counterfeit registered trademark in accordance with Article 214 of the Criminal Law.  
Where any person forges or without authorization manufactures representations of another person's registered trademark for cigarettes or cigars, or sells such forged or without-authorization manufactured representations, and the circumstances are serious, such person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally manufacturing or selling illegally manufactured representations of a registered trademark in accordance with Article 215 of the Criminal Law.  
Where any person, in violation of the State laws and regulations on tobacco monopoly administration, and without permission from the competent tobacco monopoly administrative department, and without a license such as a tobacco monopoly production enterprise license, a tobacco monopoly wholesale enterprise license, a special tobacco monopoly operation enterprise license, or a tobacco monopoly retail license, illegally deals in tobacco monopoly commodities, and the circumstances are serious, such person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operation in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  伪劣卷烟、雪茄烟等烟草专卖品尚未销售，货值金额达到[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)规定的销售金额定罪起点数额标准的三倍以上的，或者销售金额未达到五万元，但与未销售货值金额合计达到十五万元以上的，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪（未遂）定罪处罚。  
销售金额和未销售货值金额分别达到不同的法定刑幅度或者均达到同一法定刑幅度的，在处罚较重的法定刑幅度内酌情从重处罚。  
查获的未销售的伪劣卷烟、雪茄烟，能够查清销售价格的，按照实际销售价格计算。无法查清实际销售价格，有品牌的，按照该品牌卷烟、雪茄烟的查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的零售价格计算；无品牌的，按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度卷烟平均零售价格计算。

**Article 2** Where counterfeit or substandard cigarettes, cigars or other tobacco monopoly commodities have not yet been sold, and the value of the goods reaches or exceeds three times the threshold amount for conviction on the basis of sales amount as prescribed in Article 140 of the Criminal Law, or the sales amount is less than 50,000 RMB but the total of the sales amount and the value of the unsold goods reaches or exceeds 150,000 RMB, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard products (attempted).  
Where the sales amount and the value of the unsold goods respectively fall under different statutory sentencing ranges or both fall under the same statutory sentencing range, a heavier punishment shall be imposed within the heavier statutory sentencing range as the circumstances may warrant.  
For the seized unsold counterfeit or substandard cigarettes and cigars, if the sales price can be ascertained, the calculation shall be based on the actual sales price. If the actual sales price cannot be ascertained, for those with a brand, the calculation shall be based on the retail price issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they were seized; for those without a brand, the calculation shall be based on the average retail price of cigarettes in the previous year issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they were seized.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  非法经营烟草专卖品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）非法经营数额在五万元以上的，或者违法所得数额在二万元以上的；  
（二）非法经营卷烟二十万支以上的；  
（三）曾因非法经营烟草专卖品三年内受过二次以上行政处罚，又非法经营烟草专卖品且数额在三万元以上的。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）非法经营数额在二十五万元以上，或者违法所得数额在十万元以上的；  
（二）非法经营卷烟一百万支以上的。

**Article 3** Any person who illegally deals in tobacco monopoly commodities under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to have committed the crime with "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 225 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of illegal business operation is 50,000 RMB or more, or the amount of illegal income is 20,000 RMB or more;  
(2) the number of cigarettes illegally dealt in is 200,000 cigarettes or more;  
(3) having been subjected to administrative penalties twice or more within three years for illegally dealing in tobacco monopoly commodities, the person again illegally deals in tobacco monopoly commodities with an amount of 30,000 RMB or more.  
Any person who is under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed to have committed the crime with "especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 225 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount of illegal business operation is 250,000 RMB or more, or the amount of illegal income is 100,000 RMB or more;  
(2) the number of cigarettes illegally dealt in is one million cigarettes or more.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  非法经营烟草专卖品，能够查清销售或者购买价格的，按照其销售或者购买的价格计算非法经营数额。无法查清销售或者购买价格的，按照下列方法计算非法经营数额：  
（一）查获的卷烟、雪茄烟的价格，有品牌的，按照该品牌卷烟、雪茄烟的查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的零售价格计算；无品牌的，按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度卷烟平均零售价格计算；  
（二）查获的复烤烟叶、烟叶的价格按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度烤烟调拨平均基准价格计算；  
（三）烟丝的价格按照第（二）项规定价格计算标准的一点五倍计算；  
（四）卷烟辅料的价格，有品牌的，按照该品牌辅料的查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的价格计算；无品牌的，按照查获地省级烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的上年度烟草行业生产卷烟所需该类卷烟辅料的平均价格计算；  
（五）非法生产、销售、购买烟草专用机械的价格按照国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门下发的全国烟草专用机械产品指导价格目录进行计算；目录中没有该烟草专用机械的，按照省级以上烟草专卖行政主管部门出具的目录中同类烟草专用机械的平均价格计算。

**Article 4** Where any person illegally deals in tobacco monopoly commodities and the sales or purchase price can be ascertained, the amount of illegal business operation shall be calculated on the basis of the sales or purchase price. Where the sales or purchase price cannot be ascertained, the amount of illegal business operation shall be calculated in accordance with the following methods:  
(1) For seized cigarettes and cigars, if they have a brand, the calculation shall be based on the retail price issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they were seized; if they have no brand, the calculation shall be based on the average retail price of cigarettes in the previous year issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they were seized;  
(2) For seized redried tobacco leaves and tobacco leaves, the calculation shall be based on the average benchmark price for allocation of flue-cured tobacco in the previous year issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they were seized;  
(3) For cut tobacco, the calculation shall be based on 1.5 times the price calculation standard specified in subparagraph (2);  
(4) For cigarette auxiliary materials, if they have a brand, the calculation shall be based on the price of the auxiliary materials of that brand issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they were seized; if they have no brand, the calculation shall be based on the average price of that type of cigarette auxiliary materials required for the production of cigarettes in the tobacco industry in the previous year issued by the provincial tobacco monopoly administrative department of the place where they were seized;  
(5) For tobacco-specific machinery that has been illegally produced, sold, or purchased, the calculation shall be based on the National Guidance Price Catalog for Tobacco-Specific Machinery Products issued by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council; if the machinery is not listed in the catalog, the calculation shall be based on the average price of similar tobacco-specific machinery in the catalog issued by the provincial or higher-level tobacco monopoly administrative department.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  行为人实施非法生产、销售烟草专卖品犯罪，同时构成生产、销售伪劣产品罪、侵犯知识产权犯罪、非法经营罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where a person commits a crime of illegally producing or selling tobacco monopoly commodities, and such act also constitutes the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard products, the crime of infringing intellectual property rights, or the crime of illegal business operation, such person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision providing for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  明知他人实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)所列犯罪，而为其提供贷款、资金、账号、发票、证明、许可证件，或者提供生产、经营场所、设备、运输、仓储、保管、邮寄、代理进出口等便利条件，或者提供生产技术、卷烟配方的，应当按照共犯追究刑事责任。

**Article 6** Where any person, knowing that another person is committing any of the crimes listed in Article 1 of this Interpretation, provides the other person with loans, funds, accounts, invoices, certificates, licenses, or provides convenience such as production or business premises, equipment, transportation, storage, custody, mailing, or agency for import or export, or provides production technology or cigarette formulas, such person shall be investigated for criminal liability as an accomplice.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  办理非法生产、销售烟草专卖品等刑事案件，需要对伪劣烟草专卖品鉴定的，应当委托国务院产品质量监督管理部门和省、自治区、直辖市人民政府产品质量监督管理部门指定的烟草质量检测机构进行。

**Article 7** Where, in handling a criminal case concerning illegal production or sale of tobacco monopoly commodities, an expert evaluation of counterfeit or substandard tobacco monopoly commodities is necessary, such evaluation shall be entrusted to a tobacco quality testing institution designated by the product quality supervision and administration department of the State Council and the product quality supervision and administration departments of the people's governments of provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  以暴力、威胁方法阻碍烟草专卖执法人员依法执行职务，构成犯罪的，以妨害公务罪追究刑事责任。  
煽动群众暴力抗拒烟草专卖法律实施，构成犯罪的，以煽动暴力抗拒法律实施罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 8** Where any person obstructs a tobacco monopoly law enforcement officer from performing duties according to law by means of violence or threat, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of obstructing public service.  
Where any person incites the masses to violently resist the implementation of tobacco monopoly laws, and such act constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of inciting violent resistance to the implementation of laws.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  本解释所称“烟草专卖品”，是指卷烟、雪茄烟、烟丝、复烤烟叶、烟叶、卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束、烟草专用机械。  
本解释所称“卷烟辅料”，是指卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束。  
本解释所称“烟草专用机械”，是指由国务院烟草专卖行政主管部门烟草专用机械名录所公布的，在卷烟、雪茄烟、烟丝、复烤烟叶、烟叶、卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束的生产加工过程中，能够完成一项或者多项特定加工工序，可以独立操作的机械设备。  
本解释所称“同类烟草专用机械”，是指在卷烟、雪茄烟、烟丝、复烤烟叶、烟叶、卷烟纸、滤嘴棒、烟用丝束的生产加工过程中，能够完成相同加工工序的机械设备。

**Article 9** The term "tobacco monopoly commodities" as used in this Interpretation means cigarettes, cigars, cut tobacco, redried tobacco leaves, tobacco leaves, cigarette paper, filter rods, tobacco tow, and tobacco-specific machinery.  
The term "cigarette auxiliary materials" as used in this Interpretation means cigarette paper, filter rods, and tobacco tow.  
The term "tobacco-specific machinery" as used in this Interpretation means mechanical equipment that is listed in the tobacco-specific machinery catalog published by the tobacco monopoly administrative department of the State Council and that can independently perform one or more specific processing procedures in the production and processing of cigarettes, cigars, cut tobacco, redried tobacco leaves, tobacco leaves, cigarette paper, filter rods, and tobacco tow.  
The term "similar tobacco-specific machinery" as used in this Interpretation means mechanical equipment that can perform the same processing procedures in the production and processing of cigarettes, cigars, cut tobacco, redried tobacco leaves, tobacco leaves, cigarette paper, filter rods, and tobacco tow.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  以前发布的有关规定与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 10** Where any relevant provisions previously issued are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
