# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害食品安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2021〕24号  
**公布日期**：2021-12-13  
**生效日期**：2022-01-01

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害食品安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2021年12月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1856次会议、2021年12月29日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第八十四次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年1月1日起施行。 2021年12月30日 最高人民法院　最高人民检察院 关于办理危害食品安全刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2021〕24号 为依法惩治危害食品安全犯罪，保障人民群众身体健康、生命安全，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，对办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百四十三条规定的“足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病”： （一）含有严重超出标准限量的致病性微生物、农药残留、兽药残留、生物毒素、重金属等污染物质以及其他严重危害人体健康的物质的； （二）属于病死、死因不明或者检验检疫不合格的畜、禽、兽、水产动物肉类及其制品的； （三）属于国家为防控疾病等特殊需要明令禁止生产、销售的； （四）特殊医学用途配方食品、专供婴幼儿的主辅食品营养成分严重不符合食品安全标准的； （五）其他足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者严重食源性疾病的情形。 第二条　生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百四十三条规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”： （一）造成轻伤以上伤害的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病”：  
（一）含有严重超出标准限量的致病性微生物、农药残留、兽药残留、生物毒素、重金属等污染物质以及其他严重危害人体健康的物质的；  
（二）属于病死、死因不明或者检验检疫不合格的畜、禽、兽、水产动物肉类及其制品的；  
（三）属于国家为防控疾病等特殊需要明令禁止生产、销售的；  
（四）特殊医学用途配方食品、专供婴幼儿的主辅食品营养成分严重不符合食品安全标准的；  
（五）其他足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者严重食源性疾病的情形。

**Article 1** . Production and sale of food that does not meet food safety standards, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease" as stipulated in Article 143 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Containing pollutants such as pathogenic microorganisms, pesticide residues, veterinary drug residues, biotoxins, heavy metals, and other substances that seriously exceed the standard limits and seriously harm human health;  
(2) Being meat and meat products of livestock, poultry, animals, aquatic animals that are dead or have unknown cause of death, or that fail inspection and quarantine;  
(3) Being products that the state has explicitly prohibited production and sale for special needs such as disease prevention and control;  
(4) Being foods for special medical purposes, or staple and supplementary foods exclusively for infants and young children, whose nutritional components seriously fail to meet food safety standards;  
(5) Other circumstances sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”：  
（一）造成轻伤以上伤害的；  
（二）造成轻度残疾或者中度残疾的；  
（三）造成器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍或者严重功能障碍的；  
（四）造成十人以上严重食物中毒或者其他严重食源性疾病的；  
（五）其他对人体健康造成严重危害的情形。

**Article 2** . Production and sale of food that does not meet food safety standards, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "causing serious harm to human health" as stipulated in Article 143 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing injury of minor injury or above;  
(2) Causing mild or moderate disability;  
(3) Causing organ or tissue damage resulting in general dysfunction or severe dysfunction;  
(4) Causing serious food poisoning or other serious foodborne diseases in ten or more persons;  
(5) Other circumstances causing serious harm to human health.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）生产、销售金额二十万元以上的；  
（二）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，不符合食品安全标准的食品数量较大或者生产、销售持续时间六个月以上的；  
（三）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，属于特殊医学用途配方食品、专供婴幼儿的主辅食品的；  
（四）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，且在中小学校园、托幼机构、养老机构及周边面向未成年人、老年人销售的；  
（五）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，曾因危害食品安全犯罪受过刑事处罚或者二年内因危害食品安全违法行为受过行政处罚的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 3** . Production and sale of food that does not meet food safety standards, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 143 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The production or sale amount is 200,000 yuan or more;  
(2) The production or sale amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the quantity of food not meeting food safety standards is relatively large, or the production or sale duration is six months or more;  
(3) The production or sale amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the food is a food for special medical purposes or a staple or supplementary food exclusively for infants and young children;  
(4) The production or sale amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the food is sold on or near the campuses of primary and secondary schools, childcare institutions, and elderly care institutions, targeting minors or the elderly;  
(5) The production or sale amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the person has previously received criminal punishment for a crime of endangering food safety or has received an administrative penalty for an illegal act endangering food safety within the previous two years;  
(6) Other circumstances with serious circumstances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“后果特别严重”：  
（一）致人死亡的；  
（二）造成重度残疾以上的；  
（三）造成三人以上重伤、中度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍的；  
（四）造成十人以上轻伤、五人以上轻度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍的；  
（五）造成三十人以上严重食物中毒或者其他严重食源性疾病的；  
（六）其他特别严重的后果。

**Article 4** . Production and sale of food that does not meet food safety standards, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "particularly serious consequences" as stipulated in Article 143 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing death;  
(2) Causing severe disability or above;  
(3) Causing serious injury, moderate disability, or organ or tissue damage resulting in severe dysfunction to three or more persons;  
(4) Causing minor injury to ten or more persons, mild disability to five or more persons, or organ or tissue damage resulting in general dysfunction;  
(5) Causing serious food poisoning or other serious foodborne diseases to thirty or more persons;  
(6) Other particularly serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在食品生产、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，违反食品安全标准，超限量或者超范围滥用食品添加剂，足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病的，依照[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)的规定以生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪定罪处罚。  
在食用农产品种植、养殖、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，违反食品安全标准，超限量或者超范围滥用添加剂、农药、兽药等，足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病的，适用前款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 5** . Where, in the process of production, sale, transportation, storage, etc. of food, in violation of food safety standards, food additives are abused beyond the limit or scope, sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards in accordance with Article 143 of the Criminal Law.  
Where, in the process of planting, breeding, sale, transportation, storage, etc. of edible agricultural products, in violation of food safety standards, additives, pesticides, veterinary drugs, etc. are abused beyond the limit or scope, sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply for conviction and punishment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　生产、销售有毒、有害食品，具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”。

**Article 6** . Production and sale of toxic or harmful food, in any of the circumstances specified in Article 2 of this Interpretation, shall be deemed as "causing serious harm to human health" as stipulated in Article 144 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　生产、销售有毒、有害食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）生产、销售金额二十万元以上不满五十万元的；  
（二）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，有毒、有害食品数量较大或者生产、销售持续时间六个月以上的；  
（三）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，属于特殊医学用途配方食品、专供婴幼儿的主辅食品的；  
（四）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，且在中小学校园、托幼机构、养老机构及周边面向未成年人、老年人销售的；  
（五）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，曾因危害食品安全犯罪受过刑事处罚或者二年内因危害食品安全违法行为受过行政处罚的；  
（六）有毒、有害的非食品原料毒害性强或者含量高的；  
（七）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 7** . Production and sale of toxic or harmful food, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "other serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 144 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The production or sale amount is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 500,000 yuan;  
(2) The production or sale amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the quantity of toxic or harmful food is relatively large, or the production or sale duration is six months or more;  
(3) The production or sale amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the food is a food for special medical purposes or a staple or supplementary food exclusively for infants and young children;  
(4) The production or sale amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the food is sold on or near the campuses of primary and secondary schools, childcare institutions, and elderly care institutions, targeting minors or the elderly;  
(5) The production or sale amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the person has previously received criminal punishment for a crime of endangering food safety or has received an administrative penalty for an illegal act endangering food safety within the previous two years;  
(6) The toxic or harmful non-food raw material is highly toxic or has a high content;  
(7) Other circumstances with serious circumstances.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　生产、销售有毒、有害食品，生产、销售金额五十万元以上，或者具有[本解释第四条](#art-4)第二项至第六项规定的情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 8** . Production and sale of toxic or harmful food, where the production or sale amount is 500,000 yuan or more, or where any of the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (2) to (6) of Article 4 of this Interpretation exist, shall be deemed as "other particularly serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 144 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　下列物质应当认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“有毒、有害的非食品原料”：  
（一）因危害人体健康，被法律、法规禁止在食品生产经营活动中添加、使用的物质；  
（二）因危害人体健康，被国务院有关部门列入《食品中可能违法添加的非食用物质名单》《保健食品中可能非法添加的物质名单》和国务院有关部门公告的禁用农药、《食品动物中禁止使用的药品及其他化合物清单》等名单上的物质；  
（三）其他有毒、有害的物质。

**Article 9** . The following substances shall be deemed as "toxic or harmful non-food raw materials" as stipulated in Article 144 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Substances that are prohibited by laws and regulations from being added or used in food production and business operations because they endanger human health;  
(2) Substances that are listed on the "List of Non-Edible Substances that May Be Illegally Added to Food", the "List of Substances that May Be Illegally Added to Health Foods", and the prohibited pesticides announced by relevant departments of the State Council, and the "List of Drugs and Other Compounds Prohibited for Use in Food Animals", and other similar lists, by relevant departments of the State Council because they endanger human health;  
(3) Other toxic or harmful substances.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“明知”，应当综合行为人的认知能力、食品质量、进货或者销售的渠道及价格等主、客观因素进行认定。  
具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“明知”，但存在相反证据并经查证属实的除外：  
（一）长期从事相关食品、食用农产品生产、种植、养殖、销售、运输、贮存行业，不依法履行保障食品安全义务的；  
（二）没有合法有效的购货凭证，且不能提供或者拒不提供销售的相关食品来源的；  
（三）以明显低于市场价格进货或者销售且无合理原因的；  
（四）在有关部门发出禁令或者食品安全预警的情况下继续销售的；  
（五）因实施危害食品安全行为受过行政处罚或者刑事处罚，又实施同种行为的；  
（六）其他足以认定行为人明知的情形。

**Article 10** . The "knowledge" as stipulated in Article 144 of the Criminal Law shall be determined by comprehensively considering subjective and objective factors such as the actor's cognitive ability, food quality, channels and prices of purchase or sale, etc.  
In any of the following circumstances, the actor may be deemed to have "knowledge" as stipulated in Article 144 of the Criminal Law, unless there is contrary evidence that has been verified as true:  
(1) The actor has been engaged in the relevant food or edible agricultural product production, planting, breeding, sale, transportation, or storage industry for a long time and fails to perform the obligation of ensuring food safety according to law;  
(2) The actor has no lawful and valid purchase vouchers and cannot provide or refuses to provide the source of the relevant food sold;  
(3) The actor purchases or sells at a price significantly lower than the market price without a reasonable reason;  
(4) The actor continues to sell after the relevant authorities issue a ban or a food safety warning;  
(5) The actor has received an administrative penalty or criminal punishment for endangering food safety and subsequently commits a similar act;  
(6) Other circumstances sufficient to establish the actor's knowledge.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　在食品生产、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，掺入有毒、有害的非食品原料，或者使用有毒、有害的非食品原料生产食品的，依照[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)的规定以生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪定罪处罚。  
在食用农产品种植、养殖、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，使用禁用农药、食品动物中禁止使用的药品及其他化合物等有毒、有害的非食品原料，适用前款的规定定罪处罚。  
在保健食品或者其他食品中非法添加国家禁用药物等有毒、有害的非食品原料的，适用第一款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 11** . Where, in the process of production, sale, transportation, storage, etc. of food, toxic or harmful non-food raw materials are adulterated, or toxic or harmful non-food raw materials are used to produce food, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling toxic or harmful food in accordance with Article 144 of the Criminal Law.  
Where, in the process of planting, breeding, sale, transportation, storage, etc. of edible agricultural products, prohibited pesticides, drugs and other compounds prohibited for use in food animals, and other toxic or harmful non-food raw materials are used, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply for conviction and punishment.  
Where toxic or harmful non-food raw materials such as nationally prohibited drugs are illegally added to health foods or other foods, the provisions of the first paragraph shall apply for conviction and punishment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　在食品生产、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，使用不符合食品安全标准的食品包装材料、容器、洗涤剂、消毒剂，或者用于食品生产经营的工具、设备等，造成食品被污染，符合[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)、第一百四十四条规定的，以生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪或者生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 12** . Where, in the process of production, sale, transportation, storage, etc. of food, food packaging materials, containers, detergents, disinfectants, or tools, equipment, etc. used for food production and business operations that do not meet food safety standards are used, causing contamination of the food, and the act conforms to the provisions of Articles 143 and 144 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing and selling toxic or harmful food.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，有毒、有害食品，符合[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)、第一百四十四条规定的，以生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪或者生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪定罪处罚。同时构成其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，无证据证明足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病，不构成生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪，但构成生产、销售伪劣产品罪，妨害动植物防疫、检疫罪等其他犯罪的，依照该其他犯罪定罪处罚。

**Article 13** . Where production and sale of food that does not meet food safety standards or toxic or harmful food conforms to the provisions of Articles 143 and 144 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing and selling toxic or harmful food. If the act also constitutes another crime, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that provide for a heavier punishment.  
Where there is no evidence that production and sale of food that does not meet food safety standards is sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease, and the act does not constitute the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards but does constitute other crimes such as the crime of producing and selling counterfeit and shoddy products or the crime of hindering animal and plant epidemic prevention and quarantine, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with such other crime.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　明知他人生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，有毒、有害食品，具有下列情形之一的，以生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪或者生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪的共犯论处：  
（一）提供资金、贷款、账号、发票、证明、许可证件的；  
（二）提供生产、经营场所或者运输、贮存、保管、邮寄、销售渠道等便利条件的；  
（三）提供生产技术或者食品原料、食品添加剂、食品相关产品或者有毒、有害的非食品原料的；  
（四）提供广告宣传的；  
（五）提供其他帮助行为的。

**Article 14** . Where a person, knowing that another person produces or sells food that does not meet food safety standards or toxic or harmful food, falls under any of the following circumstances, the person shall be punished as an accomplice to the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing and selling toxic or harmful food:  
(1) Providing funds, loans, accounts, invoices, certificates, or licenses;  
(2) Providing production or business premises, or facilitating conditions such as transportation, storage, custody, mailing, or sales channels;  
(3) Providing production technology, or food raw materials, food additives, food-related products, or toxic or harmful non-food raw materials;  
(4) Providing advertising or publicity;  
(5) Providing other assistance.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品添加剂，用于食品的包装材料、容器、洗涤剂、消毒剂，或者用于食品生产经营的工具、设备等，符合[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)规定的，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪定罪处罚。  
生产、销售用超过保质期的食品原料、超过保质期的食品、回收食品作为原料的食品，或者以更改生产日期、保质期、改换包装等方式销售超过保质期的食品、回收食品，适用前款的规定定罪处罚。  
实施前两款行为，同时构成生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪，生产、销售不符合安全标准的产品罪等其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 15** . Where production and sale of food additives that do not meet food safety standards, packaging materials, containers, detergents, disinfectants for food, or tools, equipment, etc. used for food production and business operations conform to the provisions of Article 140 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling counterfeit and shoddy products.  
Where a person produces or sells food using food raw materials that have exceeded their shelf life, food that has exceeded its shelf life, or recycled food as raw materials, or sells food that has exceeded its shelf life or recycled food by altering the production date, shelf life, or repackaging, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply for conviction and punishment.  
Where the acts in the preceding two paragraphs also constitute other crimes such as the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing and selling products that do not meet safety standards, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that provide for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　以提供给他人生产、销售食品为目的，违反国家规定，生产、销售国家禁止用于食品生产、销售的非食品原料，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定以非法经营罪定罪处罚。  
以提供给他人生产、销售食用农产品为目的，违反国家规定，生产、销售国家禁用农药、食品动物中禁止使用的药品及其他化合物等有毒、有害的非食品原料，或者生产、销售添加上述有毒、有害的非食品原料的农药、兽药、饲料、饲料添加剂、饲料原料，情节严重的，依照前款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 16** . Where, for the purpose of providing another person with the production or sale of food, a person violates state regulations by producing or selling non-food raw materials that are prohibited by the state for use in food production or sale, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law.  
Where, for the purpose of providing another person with the production or sale of edible agricultural products, a person violates state regulations by producing or selling nationally prohibited pesticides, drugs and other compounds prohibited for use in food animals, and other toxic or harmful non-food raw materials, or by producing or selling pesticides, veterinary drugs, feed, feed additives, or feed raw materials to which the aforesaid toxic or harmful non-food raw materials have been added, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　违反国家规定，私设生猪屠宰厂（场），从事生猪屠宰、销售等经营活动，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定以非法经营罪定罪处罚。  
在畜禽屠宰相关环节，对畜禽使用食品动物中禁止使用的药品及其他化合物等有毒、有害的非食品原料，依照[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)的规定以生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪定罪处罚，对畜禽注水或者注入其他物质，足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病的，依照[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)的规定以生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪定罪处罚，虽不足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病，但符合[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)规定的，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 17** . Where a person violates state regulations by setting up a pig slaughterhouse (site) without authorization and engaging in business activities such as slaughtering and selling pigs, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law.  
Where, in the relevant link of livestock and poultry slaughter, toxic or harmful non-food raw materials such as drugs and other compounds prohibited for use in food animals are used on livestock and poultry, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling toxic or harmful food in accordance with Article 144 of the Criminal Law; where water or other substances are injected into livestock and poultry, sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards in accordance with Article 143 of the Criminal Law; where the act is not sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease but conforms to the provisions of Article 140 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing and selling counterfeit and shoddy products.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　实施本解释规定的非法经营行为，非法经营数额在十万元以上，或者违法所得数额在五万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节严重”；非法经营数额在五十万元以上，或者违法所得数额在二十五万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节特别严重”。  
实施本解释规定的非法经营行为，同时构成生产、销售伪劣产品罪，生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪，生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪，生产、销售伪劣农药、兽药罪等其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 18** . Where a person commits an illegal business operation as stipulated in this Interpretation, if the illegal business amount is 100,000 yuan or more, or the illegal income amount is 50,000 yuan or more, the act shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 225 of the Criminal Law; if the illegal business amount is 500,000 yuan or more, or the illegal income amount is 250,000 yuan or more, the act shall be deemed as "particularly serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 225 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person commits an illegal business operation as stipulated in this Interpretation, and the act also constitutes other crimes such as the crime of producing and selling counterfeit and shoddy products, the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards, the crime of producing and selling toxic or harmful food, or the crime of producing and selling counterfeit and shoddy pesticides or veterinary drugs, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that provide for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　违反国家规定，利用广告对保健食品或者其他食品作虚假宣传，符合[刑法第二百二十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-222)规定的，以虚假广告罪定罪处罚；以非法占有为目的，利用销售保健食品或者其他食品诈骗财物，符合[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)规定的，以诈骗罪定罪处罚。同时构成生产、销售伪劣产品罪等其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 19** . Where a person violates state regulations by using advertisements to make false promotions about health foods or other foods, and the act conforms to the provisions of Article 222 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of false advertising; where a person, for the purpose of illegal possession, uses the sale of health foods or other foods to defraud property, and the act conforms to the provisions of Article 266 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fraud. If the act also constitutes other crimes such as the crime of producing and selling counterfeit and shoddy products, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that provide for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　负有食品安全监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，构成食品监管渎职罪，同时构成徇私舞弊不移交刑事案件罪、商检徇私舞弊罪、动植物检疫徇私舞弊罪、放纵制售伪劣商品犯罪行为罪等其他渎职犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
负有食品安全监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，不构成食品监管渎职罪，但构成前款规定的其他渎职犯罪的，依照该其他犯罪定罪处罚。  
负有食品安全监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员与他人共谋，利用其职务行为帮助他人实施危害食品安全犯罪行为，同时构成渎职犯罪和危害食品安全犯罪共犯的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪从重处罚。

**Article 20** . Where a state functionary with the responsibility for food safety supervision and administration abuses his authority or neglects his duty, constituting the crime of dereliction of duty in food supervision, and also constitutes other crimes of dereliction of duty such as the crime of engaging in malpractices for personal gain by failing to transfer a criminal case, the crime of engaging in malpractices for personal gain in commodity inspection, the crime of engaging in malpractices for personal gain in animal and plant quarantine, or the crime of conniving at the production and sale of counterfeit and shoddy goods, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that provide for a heavier punishment.  
Where a state functionary with the responsibility for food safety supervision and administration abuses his authority or neglects his duty, not constituting the crime of dereliction of duty in food supervision but constituting other crimes of dereliction of duty as specified in the preceding paragraph, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with such other crime.  
Where a state functionary with the responsibility for food safety supervision and administration conspires with another person and uses his official acts to help the other person commit a crime of endangering food safety, and the act simultaneously constitutes a crime of dereliction of duty and being an accomplice to a crime of endangering food safety, the person shall be convicted and given a heavier punishment in accordance with the provisions that provide for a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　犯生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪，生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪，一般应当依法判处生产、销售金额二倍以上的罚金。  
共同犯罪的，对各共同犯罪人合计判处的罚金一般应当在生产、销售金额的二倍以上。

**Article 21** . For the crime of producing and selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing and selling toxic or harmful food, a fine of not less than twice the production or sale amount shall generally be imposed according to law.  
In the case of joint crimes, the total fine imposed on all joint offenders shall generally be not less than twice the production or sale amount.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　对实施本解释规定之犯罪的犯罪分子，应当依照刑法规定的条件，严格适用缓刑、免予刑事处罚。对于依法适用缓刑的，可以根据犯罪情况，同时宣告禁止令。  
对于被不起诉或者免予刑事处罚的行为人，需要给予行政处罚、政务处分或者其他处分的，依法移送有关主管机关处理。

**Article 22** . For criminals who commit the crimes stipulated in this Interpretation, probation and exemption from criminal punishment shall be strictly applied in accordance with the conditions prescribed by the Criminal Law. Where probation is applied according to law, a prohibition order may be issued at the same time based on the circumstances of the crime.  
Where a person is not prosecuted or is exempted from criminal punishment, and administrative penalty, disciplinary sanction, or other sanction is necessary, the person shall be transferred to the relevant competent authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　单位实施本解释规定的犯罪的，对单位判处罚金，并对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照本解释规定的定罪量刑标准处罚。

**Article 23** . Where a unit commits a crime stipulated in this Interpretation, a fine shall be imposed on the unit, and the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be punished in accordance with the standards for conviction and sentencing stipulated in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　“足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病”“有毒、有害的非食品原料”等专门性问题难以确定的，司法机关可以依据鉴定意见、检验报告、地市级以上相关行政主管部门组织出具的书面意见，结合其他证据作出认定。必要时，专门性问题由省级以上相关行政主管部门组织出具书面意见。

**Article 24** . Where specialized issues such as "sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease" or "toxic or harmful non-food raw materials" are difficult to determine, the judicial authority may make a determination based on expert evaluation opinions, inspection reports, written opinions issued by the relevant administrative department at or above the prefectural level, in combination with other evidence. Where necessary, a written opinion on the specialized issue shall be issued by the relevant administrative department at or above the provincial level.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本解释所称“二年内”，以第一次违法行为受到行政处罚的生效之日与又实施相应行为之日的时间间隔计算确定。

**Article 25** . The term "within the previous two years" as used in this Interpretation shall be determined by calculating the time interval between the date on which the first illegal act is subject to an administrative penalty that takes effect and the date on which the corresponding act is subsequently committed.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条　本解释自2022年1月1日起施行。本解释公布实施后，《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害食品安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2013〕12号）同时废止；之前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 26** . This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2022. After this Interpretation is promulgated and implemented, the "Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Handling of Criminal Cases of Endangering Food Safety" (Fa Shi [2013] No. 12) shall be abolished simultaneously; where previously issued judicial interpretations are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
