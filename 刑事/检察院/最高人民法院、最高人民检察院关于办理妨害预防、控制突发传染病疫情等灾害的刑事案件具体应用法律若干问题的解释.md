# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理妨害预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2003〕8号  
**公布日期**：2003-05-13  
**生效日期**：2003-05-15

> （2003年5月13日最高人民法院审判委员会第1269次会议、2003年5月13日最高人民检察院第十届检察委员会第3次会议通过，自2003年5月15日起施行。） 法释〔2003〕8号 为依法惩治妨害预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的犯罪活动，保障预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害工作的顺利进行，切实维护人民群众的身体健康和生命安全，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》等有关法律规定，现就办理相关刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 故意传播突发传染病病原体，危害公共安全的，依照刑法第一百一十四条、第一百一十五条第一款的规定，按照以危险方法危害公共安全罪定罪处罚。 患有突发传染病或者疑似突发传染病而拒绝接受检疫、强制隔离或者治疗，过失造成传染病传播，情节严重，危害公共安全的，依照刑法第一百一十五条第二款的规定，按照过失以危险方法危害公共安全罪定罪处罚。 第二条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，生产、销售伪劣的防治、防护产品、物资，或者生产、销售用于防治传染病的假药、劣药，构成犯罪的，分别依照刑法第一百四十条、第一百四十一条、第一百四十二条的规定，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪，生产、销售假药罪或者生产、销售劣药罪定罪，依法从重处罚。 第三条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，生产用于防治传染病的不符合保障人体健康的国家标准、行业标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料，或者销售明知是用于防治传染病的不符合保障人体健康的国家标准、行业标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料，不具有防护、救治功能，足以严重危害人体健康的，依照刑法第一百四十五条的规定，以生产、销售不符合标准的医用器材罪定罪，依法从重处罚。 医疗机构或者个人，知道或者应当知道系前款规定的不符合保障人体健康的国家标准、行业标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料而购买并有偿使用的，以销售不符合标准的医用器材罪定罪，依法从重处罚。 第四条 国有公司、企业、事业单位的工作人员，在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的工作中，由于严重不负责任或者滥用职权，造成国有公司、企业破产或者严重损失，致使国家利益遭受重大损失的，依照刑法第一百六十八条的规定，以国有公司、企业、事业单位人员失职罪或者国有公司、企业、事业单位人员滥用职权罪定罪处罚。 第五条 广告主、广告经营者、广告发布者违反国家规定，假借预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的名义，利用广告对所推销的商品或者服务作虚假宣传，致使多人上当受骗，违法所得数额较大或者有其他严重情节的，依照刑法第二百二十二条的规定，以虚假广告罪定罪处罚。 第六条 违反国家在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间有关市场经营、价格管理等规定，哄抬物价、牟取暴利，严重扰乱市场秩序，违法所得数额较大或者有其他严重情节的，依照刑法第二百二十五条第（四）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪，依法从重处罚。 第七条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，假借研制、生产或者销售用于预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害用品的名义，诈骗公私财物数额较大的，依照刑法有关诈骗罪的规定定罪，依法从重处罚。 第八条 以暴力、威胁方法阻碍国家机关工作人员、红十字会工作人员依法履行为防治突发传染病疫情等灾害而采取的防疫、检疫、强制隔离、隔离治疗等预防、控制措施的，依照刑法第二百七十七条第一款、第三款的规定，以妨害公务罪定罪处罚。 第九条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，聚众“打砸抢”，致人伤残、死亡的，依照刑法第二百八十九条、第二百三十四条、第二百三十二条的规定，以故意伤害罪或者故意杀人罪定罪，依法从重处罚。对毁坏或者抢走公私财物的首要分子，依照刑法第二百八十九条、第二百六十三条的规定，以抢劫罪定罪，依法从重处罚。 第十条 编造与突发传染病疫情等灾害有关的恐怖信息，或者明知是编造的此类恐怖信息而故意传播，严重扰乱社会秩序的，依照刑法第二百九十一条之一的规定，以编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息罪定罪处罚。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 故意传播突发传染病病原体，危害公共安全的，依照[刑法第一百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-114)、第一百一十五条第一款的规定，按照以危险方法危害公共安全罪定罪处罚。  
患有突发传染病或者疑似突发传染病而拒绝接受检疫、强制隔离或者治疗，过失造成传染病传播，情节严重，危害公共安全的，依照[刑法第一百一十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-115)第二款的规定，按照过失以危险方法危害公共安全罪定罪处罚。

**Article 1** : Whoever intentionally spreads the pathogen of a sudden infectious disease, endangering public security, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 114 and paragraph 1 of Article 115 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of endangering public security by dangerous means.  
Whoever, suffering from a sudden infectious disease or being suspected of such disease, refuses to accept quarantine, compulsory isolation or treatment, and negligently causes the spread of the disease, if the circumstances are serious and public security is endangered, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 115 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of negligently endangering public security by dangerous means.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，生产、销售伪劣的防治、防护产品、物资，或者生产、销售用于防治传染病的假药、劣药，构成犯罪的，分别依照[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)、第一百四十一条、第一百四十二条的规定，以生产、销售伪劣产品罪，生产、销售假药罪或者生产、销售劣药罪定罪，依法从重处罚。

**Article 2** : During the period of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, whoever produces or sells counterfeit or substandard prevention and protection products or materials, or produces or sells fake or inferior drugs for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, which constitutes a crime, shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with Articles 140, 141 and 142 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China respectively for the crime of producing or selling counterfeit products, the crime of producing or selling fake drugs, or the crime of producing or selling inferior drugs.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，生产用于防治传染病的不符合保障人体健康的国家标准、行业标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料，或者销售明知是用于防治传染病的不符合保障人体健康的国家标准、行业标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料，不具有防护、救治功能，足以严重危害人体健康的，依照[刑法第一百四十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-145)的规定，以生产、销售不符合标准的医用器材罪定罪，依法从重处罚。  
医疗机构或者个人，知道或者应当知道系前款规定的不符合保障人体健康的国家标准、行业标准的医疗器械、医用卫生材料而购买并有偿使用的，以销售不符合标准的医用器材罪定罪，依法从重处罚。

**Article 3** : During the period of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, whoever produces medical instruments or medical hygiene materials for the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases that do not meet the national or industrial standards for ensuring human health, or sells such medical instruments or materials knowing that they are for such purpose and do not meet the standards, which lack protection or treatment functions and are sufficient to seriously endanger human health, shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with Article 145 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of producing or selling medical equipment not meeting the standards.  
Medical institutions or individuals who know or should know that the medical instruments or medical hygiene materials specified in the preceding paragraph do not meet the national or industrial standards for ensuring human health and purchase and use them for compensation shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law for the crime of selling medical equipment not meeting the standards.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 国有公司、企业、事业单位的工作人员，在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的工作中，由于严重不负责任或者滥用职权，造成国有公司、企业破产或者严重损失，致使国家利益遭受重大损失的，依照[刑法第一百六十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-168)的规定，以国有公司、企业、事业单位人员失职罪或者国有公司、企业、事业单位人员滥用职权罪定罪处罚。

**Article 4** : Where any staff member of a State-owned company, enterprise or institution, in the work of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, causes bankruptcy of or serious losses to the State-owned company, enterprise or institution, and causes major damage to the interests of the State as a result of gross irresponsibility or abuse of power, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 168 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of dereliction of duty by personnel of State-owned companies, enterprises or institutions or the crime of abuse of power by personnel of State-owned companies, enterprises or institutions.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 广告主、广告经营者、广告发布者违反国家规定，假借预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的名义，利用广告对所推销的商品或者服务作虚假宣传，致使多人上当受骗，违法所得数额较大或者有其他严重情节的，依照[刑法第二百二十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-222)的规定，以虚假广告罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** : Where any advertiser, advertising agent or advertising publisher, in violation of State regulations, under the pretext of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, makes false propaganda through advertisements for the goods or services they promote, causing many people to be deceived, if the amount of illegal gains is relatively large or there are other serious circumstances, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 222 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of false advertising.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 违反国家在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间有关市场经营、价格管理等规定，哄抬物价、牟取暴利，严重扰乱市场秩序，违法所得数额较大或者有其他严重情节的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（四）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪，依法从重处罚。

**Article 6** : Where, in violation of the State regulations on market operation and price management during the period of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, anyone rigs prices and reaps huge profits, seriously disrupting market order, if the amount of illegal gains is relatively large or there are other serious circumstances, he shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with Article 225(4) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of illegal business operation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，假借研制、生产或者销售用于预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害用品的名义，诈骗公私财物数额较大的，依照刑法有关诈骗罪的规定定罪，依法从重处罚。

**Article 7** : During the period of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, whoever, under the pretext of developing, producing or selling supplies for the prevention and control of such disasters, defrauds others of a relatively large amount of public or private property shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China on the crime of fraud.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 以暴力、威胁方法阻碍国家机关工作人员、红十字会工作人员依法履行为防治突发传染病疫情等灾害而采取的防疫、检疫、强制隔离、隔离治疗等预防、控制措施的，依照[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第一款、第三款的规定，以妨害公务罪定罪处罚。

**Article 8** : Whoever, by means of violence or threat, obstructs the performance of duties by personnel of State organs or the Red Cross Society in taking prevention and control measures such as epidemic prevention, quarantine, compulsory isolation and isolation treatment for the prevention and treatment of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters according to law shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 and paragraph 3 of Article 277 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of obstructing public service.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，聚众“打砸抢”，致人伤残、死亡的，依照[刑法第二百八十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-289)、第二百三十四条、第二百三十二条的规定，以故意伤害罪或者故意杀人罪定罪，依法从重处罚。对毁坏或者抢走公私财物的首要分子，依照[刑法第二百八十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-289)、第二百六十三条的规定，以抢劫罪定罪，依法从重处罚。

**Article 9** : During the period of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, whoever gathers a crowd to engage in "smashing, looting and robbing", causing injury, disability or death to others, shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with Articles 289, 234 and 232 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of intentional injury or intentional homicide. The ringleaders who destroy or rob public or private property shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with Articles 289 and 263 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of robbery.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 编造与突发传染病疫情等灾害有关的恐怖信息，或者明知是编造的此类恐怖信息而故意传播，严重扰乱社会秩序的，依照[刑法第二百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-291)之一的规定，以编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息罪定罪处罚。  
利用突发传染病疫情等灾害，制造、传播谣言，煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一，或者煽动颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度的，依照[刑法第一百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-103)第二款、第一百零五条第二款的规定，以煽动分裂国家罪或者煽动颠覆国家政权罪定罪处罚。

**Article 10** : Whoever fabricates terror information related to sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, or knowingly disseminates such fabricated terror information, if it seriously disrupts social order, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 291 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of fabricating and intentionally disseminating false terror information.  
Whoever, by taking advantage of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, fabricates and spreads rumors to incite secession of the State, undermine national unity, or incite subversion of the State power or overthrow of the socialist system shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 103 and paragraph 2 of Article 105 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of inciting secession or the crime of inciting subversion of the State power.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，强拿硬要或者任意损毁、占用公私财物情节严重，或者在公共场所起哄闹事，造成公共场所秩序严重混乱的，依照[刑法第二百九十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-293)的规定，以寻衅滋事罪定罪，依法从重处罚。

**Article 11** : During the period of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, whoever forcibly takes or demands, or arbitrarily damages or occupies public or private property under serious circumstances, or creates disturbances in public places, causing serious disorder in public places, shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with Article 293 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of picking quarrels and provoking trouble.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 未取得医师执业资格非法行医，具有造成突发传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似突发传染病病人贻误诊治或者造成交叉感染等严重情节的，依照[刑法第三百三十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-336)第一款的规定，以非法行医罪定罪，依法从重处罚。

**Article 12** : Whoever engages in illegal medical practice without obtaining the qualification for medical practice, if there are serious circumstances such as causing delay in diagnosis and treatment of patients with sudden infectious diseases, pathogen carriers, or persons suspected of having sudden infectious diseases, or causing cross-infection, shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 336 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of illegal medical practice.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 违反传染病防治法等国家有关规定，向土地、水体、大气排放、倾倒或者处置含传染病病原体的废物、有毒物质或者其他危险废物，造成突发传染病传播等重大环境污染事故，致使公私财产遭受重大损失或者人身伤亡的严重后果的，依照[刑法第三百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-338)的规定，以重大环境污染事故罪定罪处罚。

**Article 13** : Whoever, in violation of the Law on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and other relevant State regulations, discharges, dumps or disposes of wastes containing infectious disease pathogens, toxic substances or other hazardous wastes into land, water bodies or the atmosphere, causing major environmental pollution accidents such as the spread of sudden infectious diseases, resulting in heavy losses to public or private property or serious consequences of personal injury or death, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 338 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of major environmental pollution accident.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 贪污、侵占用于预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的款物或者挪用归个人使用，构成犯罪的，分别依照[刑法第三百八十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-382)、第三百八十三条、第二百七十一条、第三百八十四条、第二百七十二条的规定，以贪污罪、职务侵占罪、挪用公款罪、挪用资金罪定罪，依法从重处罚。  
挪用用于预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的救灾、优抚、救济等款物，构成犯罪的，对直接责任人员，依照[刑法第二百七十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-273)的规定，以挪用特定款物罪定罪处罚。

**Article 14** : Whoever embezzles, misappropriates, or converts for personal use the funds or materials used for the prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, if it constitutes a crime, shall be convicted and given a severe punishment according to law in accordance with Articles 382, 383, 271, 384 and 272 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China respectively for the crime of embezzlement, the crime of duty-related embezzlement, the crime of misappropriation of public funds, or the crime of misappropriation of funds.  
Whoever misappropriates the relief funds or materials such as disaster relief, social relief, and pension funds used for the prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, if it constitutes a crime, the directly responsible personnel shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 273 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of misappropriation of specific funds and materials.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的工作中，负有组织、协调、指挥、灾害调查、控制、医疗救治、信息传递、交通运输、物资保障等职责的国家机关工作人员，滥用职权或者玩忽职守，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)的规定，以滥用职权罪或者玩忽职守罪定罪处罚。

**Article 15** : Where any staff member of a State organ who is responsible for organization, coordination, command, disaster investigation, control, medical treatment, information transmission, transportation, material support, etc., in the work of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, abuses his power or neglects his duty, causing major damage to public property or the interests of the State and the people, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 397 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of abuse of power or the crime of dereliction of duty.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 在预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害期间，从事传染病防治的政府卫生行政部门的工作人员，或者在受政府卫生行政部门委托代表政府卫生行政部门行使职权的组织中从事公务的人员，或者虽未列入政府卫生行政部门人员编制但在政府卫生行政部门从事公务的人员，在代表政府卫生行政部门行使职权时，严重不负责任，导致传染病传播或者流行，情节严重的，依照[刑法第四百零九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-409)的规定，以传染病防治失职罪定罪处罚。  
在国家对突发传染病疫情等灾害采取预防、控制措施后，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第四百零九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-409)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）对发生突发传染病疫情等灾害的地区或者突发传染病病人、病原携带者、疑似突发传染病病人，未按照预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害工作规范的要求做好防疫、检疫、隔离、防护、救治等工作，或者采取的预防、控制措施不当，造成传染范围扩大或者疫情、灾情加重的；  
（二）隐瞒、缓报、谎报或者授意、指使、强令他人隐瞒、缓报、谎报疫情、灾情，造成传染范围扩大或者疫情、灾情加重的；  
（三）拒不执行突发传染病疫情等灾害应急处理指挥机构的决定、命令，造成传染范围扩大或者疫情、灾情加重的；  
（四）具有其他严重情节的。

**Article 16** : During the period of prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, any staff member of the government health administration department engaged in the prevention and treatment of infectious diseases, or any person engaged in official duties in an organization entrusted by the government health administration department to exercise its powers, or any person who, though not included in the establishment of the government health administration department, engages in official duties in that department, who, when exercising powers on behalf of the government health administration department, is seriously irresponsible, resulting in the spread or prevalence of infectious diseases, if the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 409 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of dereliction of duty in infectious disease prevention and treatment.  
After the State takes prevention and control measures against sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 409 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) failing to properly carry out epidemic prevention, quarantine, isolation, protection, treatment and other work in accordance with the work specifications for prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters in the affected areas or for patients with sudden infectious diseases, pathogen carriers, or persons suspected of such diseases, or taking improper prevention and control measures, resulting in the expansion of the scope of infection or aggravation of the epidemic or disaster;  
(2) concealing, delaying reporting, or falsely reporting the epidemic or disaster, or instructing, inciting or compelling others to do so, resulting in the expansion of the scope of infection or aggravation of the epidemic or disaster;  
(3) refusing to implement the decisions or orders of the emergency response command for sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, resulting in the expansion of the scope of infection or aggravation of the epidemic or disaster;  
(4) having other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 人民法院、人民检察院办理有关妨害预防、控制突发传染病疫情等灾害的刑事案件，对于有自首、立功等悔罪表现的，依法从轻、减轻、免除处罚或者依法作出不起诉决定。

**Article 17** : When handling criminal cases involving hindrance to the prevention and control of sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters, the People's Court and the People's Procuratorate shall, for those who show repentance such as voluntary surrender or meritorious service, give a lighter or mitigated punishment or exempt them from punishment according to law, or make a decision not to prosecute according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 本解释所称“突发传染病疫情等灾害”，是指突然发生，造成或者可能造成社会公众健康严重损害的重大传染病疫情、群体性不明原因疾病以及其他严重影响公众健康的灾害。  
2003年5月14日

**Article 18** : For the purposes of this Interpretation, "sudden infectious disease outbreaks and other disasters" refers to major infectious disease outbreaks, clusters of unknown causes of disease, and other disasters that seriously affect public health, which occur suddenly and cause or may cause serious damage to the health of the public.
