# 最高人民法院关于刑事裁判涉财产部分执行的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2014〕13号  
**公布日期**：2014-09-01  
**生效日期**：2014-11-06

> 最高人民法院《关于刑事裁判涉财产部分执行的若干规定》已于2014年9月1日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1625次会议通过，现予公布，自2014年11月6日起施行。 2014年10月30日 最高人民法院 关于刑事裁判涉财产部分执行的若干规定 法释〔2014〕13号 为进一步规范刑事裁判涉财产部分的执行，维护当事人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》等法律规定，结合人民法院执行工作实际，制定本规定。 第一条　本规定所称刑事裁判涉财产部分的执行，是指发生法律效力的刑事裁判主文确定的下列事项的执行： （一）罚金、没收财产； （二）责令退赔； （三）处置随案移送的赃款赃物； （四）没收随案移送的供犯罪所用本人财物； （五）其他应当由人民法院执行的相关事项。 刑事附带民事裁判的执行，适用民事执行的有关规定。 第二条　刑事裁判涉财产部分，由第一审人民法院执行。第一审人民法院可以委托财产所在地的同级人民法院执行。 第三条　人民法院办理刑事裁判涉财产部分执行案件的期限为六个月。有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　本规定所称刑事裁判涉财产部分的执行，是指发生法律效力的刑事裁判主文确定的下列事项的执行：  
（一）罚金、没收财产；  
（二）责令退赔；  
（三）处置随案移送的赃款赃物；  
（四）没收随案移送的供犯罪所用本人财物；  
（五）其他应当由人民法院执行的相关事项。  
刑事附带民事裁判的执行，适用民事执行的有关规定。

**Article 1** The enforcement of the part involving property in a criminal judgment as referred to in these Provisions means the enforcement of the following matters determined in the main text of a criminal judgment that has taken legal effect:  
(1) Fine and confiscation of property;  
(2) Order for restitution or compensation;  
(3) Disposal of illicit money and property transferred with the case;  
(4) Confiscation of the defendant's own property used for the crime and transferred with the case;  
(5) Other relevant matters that shall be enforced by the People's Court.  
The enforcement of a criminal judgment with a civil action attached shall be governed by the relevant provisions on civil enforcement.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　刑事裁判涉财产部分，由第一审人民法院执行。第一审人民法院可以委托财产所在地的同级人民法院执行。

**Article 2** The part involving property in a criminal judgment shall be enforced by the People's Court of first instance. The People's Court of first instance may entrust the enforcement to the People's Court at the same level where the property is located.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　人民法院办理刑事裁判涉财产部分执行案件的期限为六个月。有特殊情况需要延长的，经本院院长批准，可以延长。

**Article 3** The time limit for a People's Court to handle an enforcement case concerning the part involving property in a criminal judgment shall be six months. Where extension is necessary due to special circumstances, it may be extended with the approval of the president of the court.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　人民法院刑事审判中可能判处被告人财产刑、责令退赔的，刑事审判部门应当依法对被告人的财产状况进行调查；发现可能隐匿、转移财产的，应当及时查封、扣押、冻结其相应财产。

**Article 4** Where a People's Court may impose a property penalty on a defendant or order restitution or compensation in criminal adjudication, the criminal adjudication department shall investigate the defendant's property status according to law; if it discovers that property may be concealed or transferred, it shall promptly seal up, seize, or freeze the corresponding property.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　刑事审判或者执行中，对于侦查机关已经采取的查封、扣押、冻结，人民法院应当在期限届满前及时续行查封、扣押、冻结。人民法院续行查封、扣押、冻结的顺位与侦查机关查封、扣押、冻结的顺位相同。  
对侦查机关查封、扣押、冻结的财产，人民法院执行中可以直接裁定处置，无需侦查机关出具解除手续，但裁定中应当指明侦查机关查封、扣押、冻结的事实。

**Article 5** During criminal adjudication or enforcement, with respect to the seizure, impoundment, or freezing already taken by the investigative authority, the People's Court shall promptly renew the seizure, impoundment, or freezing before the expiration of the time limit. The priority order of the renewed seizure, impoundment, or freezing by the People's Court shall be the same as that of the seizure, impoundment, or freezing by the investigative authority.  
With respect to the property sealed up, seized, or frozen by the investigative authority, the People's Court may directly order its disposal by ruling during enforcement, without requiring the investigative authority to issue a release procedure; however, the ruling shall specify the fact of the seizure, impoundment, or freezing by the investigative authority.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　刑事裁判涉财产部分的裁判内容，应当明确、具体。涉案财物或者被害人人数较多，不宜在判决主文中详细列明的，可以概括叙明并另附清单。  
判处没收部分财产的，应当明确没收的具体财物或者金额。  
判处追缴或者责令退赔的，应当明确追缴或者退赔的金额或财物的名称、数量等相关情况。

**Article 6** The adjudicatory content of the part involving property in a criminal judgment shall be clear and specific. Where the number of items of property involved or victims is large and it is not appropriate to list them in detail in the main text of the judgment, a general description may be given with a separate list attached.  
Where partial confiscation of property is imposed, the specific property or amount to be confiscated shall be clearly stated.  
Where recovery or restitution or compensation is ordered, the amount to be recovered or compensated, or the name, quantity, and other relevant circumstances of the property shall be clearly stated.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　由人民法院执行机构负责执行的刑事裁判涉财产部分，刑事审判部门应当及时移送立案部门审查立案。  
移送立案应当提交生效裁判文书及其附件和其他相关材料，并填写《移送执行表》。《移送执行表》应当载明以下内容：  
（一）被执行人、被害人的基本信息；  
（二）已查明的财产状况或者财产线索；  
（三）随案移送的财产和已经处置财产的情况；  
（四）查封、扣押、冻结财产的情况；  
（五）移送执行的时间；  
（六）其他需要说明的情况。  
人民法院立案部门经审查，认为属于移送范围且移送材料齐全的，应当在七日内立案，并移送执行机构。

**Article 7** For the part involving property in a criminal judgment to be enforced by the enforcement division of the People's Court, the criminal adjudication department shall promptly transfer the case to the case-filing department for review and case filing.  
For case filing upon transfer, the effective judgment document and its attachments and other relevant materials shall be submitted, and the "Form for Transfer of Enforcement" shall be completed. The "Form for Transfer of Enforcement" shall contain the following:  
(1) Basic information of the person subject to enforcement and the victim;  
(2) The property status or property clues already ascertained;  
(3) The situation of property transferred with the case and property already disposed of;  
(4) The situation of sealed up, seized, or frozen property;  
(5) The time of transfer for enforcement;  
(6) Other matters that need to be explained.  
Where the case-filing department of the People's Court, upon review, finds that the case falls within the scope of transfer and the transferred materials are complete, it shall file the case within seven days and transfer it to the enforcement division.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民法院可以向刑罚执行机关、社区矫正机构等有关单位调查被执行人的财产状况，并可以根据不同情形要求有关单位协助采取查封、扣押、冻结、划拨等执行措施。

**Article 8** The People's Court may investigate the property status of the person subject to enforcement from relevant units such as the penal enforcement authority and community correction institutions, and may, according to different circumstances, request relevant units to assist in taking enforcement measures such as sealing up, seizing, freezing, or transferring.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　判处没收财产的，应当执行刑事裁判生效时被执行人合法所有的财产。  
执行没收财产或罚金刑，应当参照被扶养人住所地政府公布的上年度当地居民最低生活费标准，保留被执行人及其所扶养家属的生活必需费用。

**Article 9** Where confiscation of property is imposed, the property lawfully owned by the person subject to enforcement at the time the criminal judgment takes effect shall be enforced.  
When enforcing the penalty of confiscation of property or a fine, the necessary living expenses of the person subject to enforcement and the family members he or she supports shall be retained, with reference to the local minimum living standard for residents in the previous year published by the government of the place where the supported persons reside.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　对赃款赃物及其收益，人民法院应当一并追缴。  
被执行人将赃款赃物投资或者置业，对因此形成的财产及其收益，人民法院应予追缴。  
被执行人将赃款赃物与其他合法财产共同投资或者置业，对因此形成的财产中与赃款赃物对应的份额及其收益，人民法院应予追缴。  
对于被害人的损失，应当按照刑事裁判认定的实际损失予以发还或者赔偿。

**Article 10** The People's Court shall recover illicit money and property and the proceeds thereof together.  
Where the person subject to enforcement invests or purchases property with illicit money or property, the People's Court shall recover the property thus formed and the proceeds thereof.  
Where the person subject to enforcement invests or purchases property jointly with illicit money or property and other lawful property, the People's Court shall recover the portion corresponding to the illicit money or property in the property thus formed and the proceeds thereof.  
For the losses of the victim, restitution or compensation shall be made in accordance with the actual losses determined in the criminal judgment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　被执行人将刑事裁判认定为赃款赃物的涉案财物用于清偿债务、转让或者设置其他权利负担，具有下列情形之一的，人民法院应予追缴：  
（一）第三人明知是涉案财物而接受的；  
（二）第三人无偿或者以明显低于市场的价格取得涉案财物的；  
（三）第三人通过非法债务清偿或者违法犯罪活动取得涉案财物的；  
（四）第三人通过其他恶意方式取得涉案财物的。  
第三人善意取得涉案财物的，执行程序中不予追缴。作为原所有人的被害人对该涉案财物主张权利的，人民法院应当告知其通过诉讼程序处理。

**Article 11** Where the person subject to enforcement uses the property involved that is determined as illicit money or property in the criminal judgment to discharge debts, transfer, or create other encumbrances, under any of the following circumstances, the People's Court shall recover it:  
(1) The third party knowingly accepted the property involved;  
(2) The third party obtained the property involved gratuitously or at a price obviously lower than the market price;  
(3) The third party obtained the property involved through illegal debt discharge or illegal or criminal activities;  
(4) The third party obtained the property involved through other malicious means.  
Where a third party acquires the property involved in good faith, it shall not be recovered in the enforcement proceedings. If the victim, as the original owner, claims rights over such property involved, the People's Court shall inform the victim to handle the matter through litigation procedures.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　被执行财产需要变价的，人民法院执行机构应当依法采取拍卖、变卖等变价措施。  
涉案财物最后一次拍卖未能成交，需要上缴国库的，人民法院应当通知有关财政机关以该次拍卖保留价予以接收；有关财政机关要求继续变价的，可以进行无保留价拍卖。需要退赔被害人的，以该次拍卖保留价以物退赔；被害人不同意以物退赔的，可以进行无保留价拍卖。

**Article 12** Where the property to be enforced needs to be liquidated, the enforcement division of the People's Court shall take measures such as auction or sale according to law.  
If the property involved fails to be sold at the last auction and needs to be turned over to the State Treasury, the People's Court shall notify the relevant finance authority to accept it at the reserve price of that auction; if the relevant finance authority requests continued liquidation, an auction without a reserve price may be conducted. If restitution or compensation to the victim is required, the property shall be returned in kind at the reserve price of that auction; if the victim does not agree to restitution in kind, an auction without a reserve price may be conducted.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　被执行人在执行中同时承担刑事责任、民事责任，其财产不足以支付的，按照下列顺序执行：  
（一）人身损害赔偿中的医疗费用；  
（二）退赔被害人的损失；  
（三）其他民事债务；  
（四）罚金；  
（五）没收财产。  
债权人对执行标的依法享有优先受偿权，其主张优先受偿的，人民法院应当在前款第（一）项规定的医疗费用受偿后，予以支持。

**Article 13** Where the person subject to enforcement concurrently bears criminal liability and civil liability during enforcement, and his or her property is insufficient to satisfy all, enforcement shall be carried out in the following order:  
(1) Medical expenses in compensation for personal injury;  
(2) Restitution or compensation for the victim's losses;  
(3) Other civil debts;  
(4) Fine;  
(5) Confiscation of property.  
Where a creditor has a statutory priority right to be satisfied from the subject matter of enforcement and claims priority, the People's Court shall uphold such claim after the medical expenses specified in item (1) of the preceding paragraph have been satisfied.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　执行过程中，当事人、利害关系人认为执行行为违反法律规定，或者案外人对执行标的主张足以阻止执行的实体权利，向执行法院提出书面异议的，执行法院应当依照[民事诉讼法第二百二十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/民事诉讼/中华人民共和国民事诉讼法.md#art-225)的规定处理。  
人民法院审查案外人异议、复议，应当公开听证。

**Article 14** During the enforcement process, if a party or an interested person considers that an enforcement act violates legal provisions, or if a person not involved in the case claims a substantive right sufficient to prevent enforcement over the subject matter of enforcement, and submits a written objection to the enforcement court, the enforcement court shall handle it in accordance with Article 225 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
When a People's Court reviews an objection or reconsideration by a person not involved in the case, it shall hold a public hearing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　执行过程中，案外人或被害人认为刑事裁判中对涉案财物是否属于赃款赃物认定错误或者应予认定而未认定，向执行法院提出书面异议，可以通过裁定补正的，执行机构应当将异议材料移送刑事审判部门处理；无法通过裁定补正的，应当告知异议人通过审判监督程序处理。

**Article 15** During the enforcement process, if a person not involved in the case or a victim believes that the criminal judgment erroneously determined whether the property involved constitutes illicit money or property, or should have been determined as such but was not, and submits a written objection to the enforcement court, if the error can be corrected by a ruling, the enforcement division shall transfer the objection materials to the criminal adjudication department for handling; if it cannot be corrected by a ruling, the objector shall be informed to handle it through the trial supervision procedure.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院办理刑事裁判涉财产部分执行案件，刑法、刑事诉讼法及有关司法解释没有相应规定的，参照适用民事执行的有关规定。

**Article 16** Where the Criminal Law, the Criminal Procedure Law, and relevant judicial interpretations have no corresponding provisions for a People's Court handling an enforcement case concerning the part involving property in a criminal judgment, the relevant provisions on civil enforcement shall be applied mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　最高人民法院此前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 17** Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court is inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
