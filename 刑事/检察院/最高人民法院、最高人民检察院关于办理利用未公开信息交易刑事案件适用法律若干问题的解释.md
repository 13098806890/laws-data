# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理利用未公开信息交易刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院

> 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院《关于办理利用未公开信息交易刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2018 年9 月10 日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1748 次会议、2018 年11 月30 日最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第十次会议通过，现予公布，自2019 年7 月1 日起施行。 2019 年6 月27 日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理利用未公开信息交易刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2019〕10 号 为依法惩治证券、期货犯罪，维护证券、期货市场管理秩序，促进证券、期货市场稳定健康发展，保护投资者合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定，现就办理利用未公开信息交易刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  刑法第一百八十条第四款规定的“ 内幕信息以外的其他未公开的信息”，包括下列信息： （一）证券、期货的投资决策、交易执行信息； （二）证券持仓数量及变化、资金数量及变化、交易动向信息； （三）其他可能影响证券、期货交易活动的信息。 第二条  内幕信息以外的其他未公开的信息难以认定的，司法机关可以在有关行政主（监）管部门的认定意见的基础上， 根据案件事实和法律规定作出认定。 第三条  刑法第一百八十条第四款规定的“ 违反规定”，是指违反法律、行政法规、部门规章、全国性行业规范有关证券、期货未公开信息保护的规定，以及行为人所在的金融机构有关信息保密、禁止交易、禁止利益输送等规定。 第四条  刑法第一百八十条第四款规定的行为人“明示、暗示他人从事相关交易活动”，应当综合以下方面进行认定： （一）行为人具有获取未公开信息的职务便利；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  [刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第四款规定的“ 内幕信息以外的其他未公开的信息”，包括下列信息：  
（一）证券、期货的投资决策、交易执行信息；  
（二）证券持仓数量及变化、资金数量及变化、交易动向信息；  
（三）其他可能影响证券、期货交易活动的信息。

**Article 1** The “other non-public information other than inside information” as prescribed in paragraph 4 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China includes the following information:  
(1) Investment decision and transaction execution information on securities and futures;  
(2) Information on the quantity and changes of securities holdings, the quantity and changes of funds, and transaction trends;  
(3) Other information that may affect securities and futures trading activities.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  内幕信息以外的其他未公开的信息难以认定的，司法机关可以在有关行政主（监）管部门的认定意见的基础上， 根据案件事实和法律规定作出认定。

**Article 2** Where it is difficult to identify other non-public information other than inside information, the judicial organs may, on the basis of the identification opinions of the relevant administrative competent (supervisory) departments, make an identification based on the facts of the case and the provisions of law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  [刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第四款规定的“ 违反规定”，是指违反法律、行政法规、部门规章、全国性行业规范有关证券、期货未公开信息保护的规定，以及行为人所在的金融机构有关信息保密、禁止交易、禁止利益输送等规定。

**Article 3** The “violation of regulations” as prescribed in paragraph 4 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China refers to violating the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, and national industry norms concerning the protection of non-public information on securities and futures, as well as the provisions of the financial institution where the actor works on information confidentiality, prohibition of trading, prohibition of interest transfer, and the like.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  [刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第四款规定的行为人“明示、暗示他人从事相关交易活动”，应当综合以下方面进行认定：  
（一）行为人具有获取未公开信息的职务便利；  
（二）行为人获取未公开信息的初始时间与他人从事相关交易活动的初始时间具有关联性；  
（三）行为人与他人之间具有亲友关系、利益关联、交易终端关联等关联关系；  
（四）他人从事相关交易的证券、期货品种、交易时间与未公开信息所涉证券、期货品种、交易时间等方面基本一致；  
（五）他人从事的相关交易活动明显不具有符合交易习惯、专业判断等正当理由；  
（六）行为人对明示、暗示他人从事相关交易活动没有合理解释。

**Article 4** The actor's “expressly or implicitly instructing others to engage in relevant trading activities” as prescribed in paragraph 4 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be determined by comprehensively considering the following aspects:  
(1) The actor has the convenience of his position to obtain non-public information;  
(2) The initial time when the actor obtained the non-public information is correlated with the initial time when others engaged in the relevant trading activities;  
(3) There are relationships between the actor and others such as family or friendship, interest connection, trading terminal connection, etc.;  
(4) The securities or futures varieties and trading times of the relevant transactions engaged in by others are substantially consistent with the securities or futures varieties and trading times involved in the non-public information;  
(5) The relevant trading activities engaged in by others obviously lack legitimate reasons such as conforming to trading habits or professional judgment;  
(6) The actor has no reasonable explanation for expressly or implicitly instructing others to engage in the relevant trading activities.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  利用未公开信息交易，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第四款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）违法所得数额在一百万元以上的；  
（二）二年内三次以上利用未公开信息交易的；  
（三）明示、暗示三人以上从事相关交易活动的。

**Article 5** Where any of the following circumstances exists in trading using non-public information, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as prescribed in paragraph 4 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) The amount of illegal gains is over one million yuan;  
(2) Trading using non-public information three times or more within two years;  
(3) Expressly or implicitly instructing three or more persons to engage in relevant trading activities.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  利用未公开信息交易，违法所得数额在五十万元以上，或者证券交易成交额在五百万元以上，或者期货交易占用保证金数额在一百万元以上，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第四款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）以出售或者变相出售未公开信息等方式，明示、暗示他人从事相关交易活动的；  
（二）因证券、期货犯罪行为受过刑事追究的；  
（三）二年内因证券、期货违法行为受过行政处罚的；  
（四）造成恶劣社会影响或者其他严重后果的。

**Article 6** Where in trading using non-public information, the amount of illegal gains is over 500,000 yuan, or the turnover of securities transactions is over 5 million yuan, or the amount of margin occupied in futures transactions is over 1 million yuan, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as prescribed in paragraph 4 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Expressly or implicitly instructing others to engage in relevant trading activities by means of selling or selling in disguised form the non-public information, etc.;  
(2) Having been subject to criminal prosecution for securities or futures crimes;  
(3) Having been subject to administrative penalties for securities or futures illegal acts within two years;  
(4) Causing adverse social impact or other serious consequences.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  [刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第四款规定的“依照第一款的规定处罚”，包括该条第一款关于“情节特别严重”的规定。  
利用未公开信息交易，违法所得数额在一千万元以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”。  
违法所得数额在五百万元以上，或者证券交易成交额在五千万元以上，或者期货交易占用保证金数额在一千万元以上， 具有[本解释第六条](#art-6)规定的四种情形之一的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”。

**Article 7** The “shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1” as prescribed in paragraph 4 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China includes the provisions of paragraph 1 of that Article on “especially serious circumstances”.  
Where in trading using non-public information, the amount of illegal gains is over 10 million yuan, it shall be deemed as “especially serious circumstances”.  
Where the amount of illegal gains is over 5 million yuan, or the turnover of securities transactions is over 50 million yuan, or the amount of margin occupied in futures transactions is over 10 million yuan, and any of the four circumstances provided in Article 6 of this Interpretation exists, it shall be deemed as “especially serious circumstances”.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  二次以上利用未公开信息交易，依法应予行政处理或者刑事处理而未经处理的，相关交易数额或者违法所得数额累计计算。

**Article 8** Where an actor trades using non-public information two or more times and such trading shall be subject to administrative sanctions or criminal sanctions according to law but has not yet been handled, the relevant trading amounts or amounts of illegal gains shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 本解释所称“违法所得”，是指行为人利用未公开信息从事与该信息相关的证券、期货交易活动所获利益或者避免的损失。  
行为人明示、暗示他人利用未公开信息从事相关交易活动，被明示、暗示人员从事相关交易活动所获利益或者避免的损失，应当认定为“违法所得”。

**Article 9** For the purposes of this Interpretation, “illegal gains” refers to the benefits obtained or losses avoided by the actor from using non-public information to engage in securities or futures trading activities related to such information.  
Where the actor expressly or implicitly instructs others to use non-public information to engage in relevant trading activities, the benefits obtained or losses avoided by the expressly or implicitly instructed persons from engaging in the relevant trading activities shall be deemed as “illegal gains”.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  行为人未实际从事与未公开信息相关的证券、期货交易活动的，其罚金数额按照被明示、暗示人员从事相关交易活动的违法所得计算。

**Article 10** Where the actor has not actually engaged in securities or futures trading activities related to non-public information, the amount of his fine shall be calculated according to the illegal gains of the expressly or implicitly instructed persons from engaging in the relevant trading activities.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  符合[本解释第五条](#art-5)、第六条规定的标准，行为人如实供述犯罪事实， 认罪悔罪，并积极配合调查，退缴违法所得的，可以从轻处罚；其中犯罪情节轻微的， 可以依法不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。  
符合刑事诉讼法规定的认罪认罚从宽适用范围和条件的，依照刑事诉讼法的规定处理。

**Article 11** Where an actor meets the criteria set forth in Article 5 or Article 6 of this Interpretation, and truthfully confesses the criminal facts, admits guilt and shows repentance, and actively cooperates with the investigation and returns the illegal gains, he may be given a light punishment; if the criminal circumstances are minor, he may, according to law, not be prosecuted or be exempted from criminal punishment.  
Where the scope and conditions for leniency on admission of guilt and acceptance of punishment as provided in the Criminal Procedure Law are met, the case shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  本解释自 2019 年 7 月 1 日起施行。

**Article 12** This Interpretation shall come into force as of July 1, 2019.
