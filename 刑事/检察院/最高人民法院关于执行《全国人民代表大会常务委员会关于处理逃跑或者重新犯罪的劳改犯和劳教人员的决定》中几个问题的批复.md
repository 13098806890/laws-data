# 最高人民法院关于执行《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于处理逃跑或者重新犯罪的劳改犯和劳教人员的决定》中几个问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1990〕8号  
**公布日期**：1990-06-11

---

### 正文

一、关于罪犯在劳改期间又重新犯罪的，能否适用《决定》从重或者加重处罚的问题。《决定》的第二条第二款只规定，劳改犯逃跑后又犯罪的，是法定从重或者加重处罚的情节。因此，对于在劳改期间重新犯罪而不是在劳改期间逃跑后又犯罪的罪犯，不适用《决定》。但是，应将劳改期间重新犯罪作为酌定从重处罚的情节，在量刑时予以考虑。

**Article None** I. Regarding the question of whether the "Decision" may apply to impose severe or heavier punishment on a criminal who commits another crime during the period of reform through labor. Paragraph 2 of Article 2 of the "Decision" only provides that a reform-through-labor offender who commits another crime after escaping constitutes a statutory circumstance for severe or heavier punishment. Therefore, the "Decision" shall not apply to a criminal who commits another crime during the period of reform through labor but does not commit a crime after escaping during that period. However, the commission of another crime during the period of reform through labor shall be considered as a discretionary circumstance for severe punishment and shall be taken into account when imposing the sentence.

二、关于劳改期满后留场就业人员又犯罪的，能否适用《决定》从重处罚的问题。《决定》第二条第二款规定：刑满释放后又犯罪的，从重处罚。劳改期满后留场就业人员又犯罪，属于“刑满释放后又犯罪的”。因此，对这种犯罪行为应当适用《决定》，从重处罚。

**Article None** II. Regarding the question of whether the "Decision" may apply to impose severe punishment on a person who, after completing the term of reform through labor and remaining at the venue for employment, commits another crime. Paragraph 2 of Article 2 of the "Decision" provides that a person who commits another crime after being released upon completion of the sentence shall be subject to severe punishment. A person who, after completing the term of reform through labor and remaining at the venue for employment, commits another crime falls within the scope of "committing another crime after being released upon completion of the sentence." Therefore, the "Decision" shall apply to such criminal conduct, and severe punishment shall be imposed.

三、关于劳动教养人员在劳动教养期间犯罪的，能否从重或者加重处罚的问题。同意你院意见，即：劳教人员在劳教期间犯罪的，除符合《决定》第三条规定，应当从重或者加重处罚的以外，对劳教期间的其他犯罪行为，则不适用《决定》，但可作为酌定从重处罚的情节，在量刑时予以考虑。  
1990年6月11日

**Article None** III. Regarding the question of whether a person subject to re-education through labor who commits a crime during the period of re-education through labor may be subject to severe or heavier punishment. We agree with the opinion of your court: for a person subject to re-education through labor who commits a crime during the period of re-education through labor, unless the circumstances fall within the provisions of Article 3 of the "Decision" and shall be subject to severe or heavier punishment, the "Decision" shall not apply to other criminal acts committed during the period of re-education through labor. However, such acts may be considered as discretionary circumstances for severe punishment and shall be taken into account when imposing the sentence.  
June 11, 1990
