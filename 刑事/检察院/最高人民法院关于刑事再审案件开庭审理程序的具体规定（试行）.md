# 最高人民法院关于刑事再审案件开庭审理程序的具体规定（试行）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕31号  
**公布日期**：2001-10-18  
**生效日期**：2002-01-01

> （2001年10月18日最高人民法院审判委员会第1196次会议通过，自2002年1月1日起施行） 法释〔2001〕31号 为了深化刑事庭审方式的改革，进一步提高审理刑事再审案件的效率，确保审判质量，规范案件开庭审理的程序，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》、最高人民法院《关于执行〈中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法〉若干问题的解释》的规定，制定本规定。 第一条 本规定适用依照第一审程序或第二审程序开庭审理的刑事再审案件。 第二条 人民法院在收到人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的刑事抗诉书后，应当根据不同情况，分别处理； （一）不属于本院管辖的，决定退回人民检察院； （二）按照抗诉书提供的原审被告人（原审上诉人）住址无法找到原审被告人（原审上诉人）的，人民法院应当要求提出抗诉的人民检察院协助查找；经协助查找仍无法找到的，决定退回人民检察院； （三）抗诉书没有写明原审被告人（原审上诉人）准确住址的，应当要求人民检察院在七日内补充，经补充后仍不明确或逾期不补的，裁定维持原判； （四）以有新的证据证明原判决、裁定认定的事实确有错误为由提出抗诉，但抗诉书未附有新的证据目录、证人名单和主要证据复印件或者照片的，人民检察院应当在七日内补充；经补充后仍不完备或逾期不补的，裁定维持原判。 第三条 以有新的证据证明原判决、裁定认定的事实确有错误为由提出申诉的，应当同时附有新的证据目录、证人名单和主要证据复印件或者照片。需要申请人民法院调取证据的，应当附有证据线索。未附有的，应当在七日内补充；经补充后仍不完备或逾期不补的，应当决定不予受理。 第四条 参与过本案第一审、第二审、复核程序审判的合议庭组成人员，不得参与本案的再审程序的审判。 第五条 人民法院审理下列再审案件，应当依法开庭审理： （一）依照第一审程序审理的； （二）依照第二审程序需要对事实或者证据进行审理的； （三）人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 本规定适用依照第一审程序或第二审程序开庭审理的刑事再审案件。

**Article 1** These Provisions shall apply to criminal retrial cases that are tried in open court in accordance with the procedure of first instance or the procedure of second instance.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 人民法院在收到人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的刑事抗诉书后，应当根据不同情况，分别处理；  
（一）不属于本院管辖的，决定退回人民检察院；  
（二）按照抗诉书提供的原审被告人（原审上诉人）住址无法找到原审被告人（原审上诉人）的，人民法院应当要求提出抗诉的人民检察院协助查找；经协助查找仍无法找到的，决定退回人民检察院；  
（三）抗诉书没有写明原审被告人（原审上诉人）准确住址的，应当要求人民检察院在七日内补充，经补充后仍不明确或逾期不补的，裁定维持原判；  
（四）以有新的证据证明原判决、裁定认定的事实确有错误为由提出抗诉，但抗诉书未附有新的证据目录、证人名单和主要证据复印件或者照片的，人民检察院应当在七日内补充；经补充后仍不完备或逾期不补的，裁定维持原判。

**Article 2** After receiving the written protest lodged by the People's Procuratorate in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, the People's Court shall handle it separately according to different circumstances:  
(1) Where the case is not within its jurisdiction, it shall decide to return it to the People's Procuratorate;  
(2) Where the original defendant (original appellant) cannot be found at the address provided in the written protest, the People's Court shall request the People's Procuratorate that lodged the protest to assist in the search; if the search still fails after such assistance, it shall decide to return the written protest to the People's Procuratorate;  
(3) Where the written protest does not specify the exact address of the original defendant (original appellant), the People's Court shall request the People's Procuratorate to supplement it within seven days; if it remains unclear after supplementation or if supplementation is not made within the time limit, it shall render a ruling to uphold the original judgment;  
(4) Where the protest is lodged on the ground that new evidence proves that the facts found in the original judgment or ruling are indeed erroneous, but the written protest is not accompanied by a list of new evidence, a list of witnesses, and copies or photos of the main evidence, the People's Procuratorate shall supplement them within seven days; if after supplementation they remain incomplete or if supplementation is not made within the time limit, it shall render a ruling to uphold the original judgment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 以有新的证据证明原判决、裁定认定的事实确有错误为由提出申诉的，应当同时附有新的证据目录、证人名单和主要证据复印件或者照片。需要申请人民法院调取证据的，应当附有证据线索。未附有的，应当在七日内补充；经补充后仍不完备或逾期不补的，应当决定不予受理。

**Article 3** Where a petition is filed on the ground that there is new evidence proving that the facts found in the original judgment or ruling are indeed erroneous, the petition shall be accompanied by a list of new evidence, a list of witnesses, and copies or photos of the main evidence. Where an application for the People's Court to obtain evidence is required, clues to evidence shall be attached. If such documents are not attached, supplementation shall be made within seven days; if after supplementation they remain incomplete or if supplementation is not made within the time limit, a decision shall be made not to accept the case.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 参与过本案第一审、第二审、复核程序审判的合议庭组成人员，不得参与本案的再审程序的审判。

**Article 4** Members of the collegial panel who participated in the adjudication of the first instance, second instance, or review procedure of the case shall not participate in the adjudication of the retrial procedure of the case.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 人民法院审理下列再审案件，应当依法开庭审理：  
（一）依照第一审程序审理的；  
（二）依照第二审程序需要对事实或者证据进行审理的；  
（三）人民检察院按照审判监督程序提出抗诉的；  
（四）可能对原审被告人（原审上诉人）加重刑罚的；  
（五）有其他应当开庭审理情形的。

**Article 5** The People's Court shall try the following retrial cases in open court according to law:  
(1) Cases tried in accordance with the procedure of first instance;  
(2) Cases where it is necessary to try facts or evidence in accordance with the procedure of second instance;  
(3) Cases where the People's Procuratorate has lodged a protest in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision;  
(4) Cases where a heavier punishment may be imposed on the original defendant (original appellant);  
(5) Other cases where there are circumstances that shall be tried in open court.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 下列再审案件可以不开庭审理：  
（一）原判决、裁定认定事实清楚，证据确实、充分，但适用法律错误，量刑畸重的；  
（二）1979年《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》施行以前裁判的；  
（三）原审被告人（原审上诉人）、原审自诉人已经死亡、或者丧失刑事责任能力的；  
（四）原审被告人（原审上诉人）在交通十分不便的边远地区监狱服刑，提押到庭确有困难的；但人民检察院提出抗诉的，人民法院应征得人民检察院的同意；  
（五）人民法院按照审判监督程序决定再审，按[本规定第九条](#art-9)第（五）项规定，经两次通知，人民检察院不派员出庭的。

**Article 6** The following retrial cases may be tried without open court session:  
(1) Cases where the facts found in the original judgment or ruling are clear, the evidence is reliable and sufficient, but the application of law is erroneous, resulting in an excessively heavy sentence;  
(2) Cases adjudicated before the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (1979) came into force;  
(3) Cases where the original defendant (original appellant) or the original private prosecutor is deceased or has lost criminal capacity;  
(4) Cases where the original defendant (original appellant) is serving his sentence in a prison in a remote area with extremely inconvenient transportation, and it is indeed difficult to produce him in court; however, if the People's Procuratorate has lodged a protest, the People's Court shall obtain the consent of the People's Procuratorate;  
(5) Cases where the People's Court decides to retry the case in accordance with the procedure for trial supervision, and after two notices under Article 9(5) of these Provisions, the People's Procuratorate fails to send a representative to appear in court.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 人民法院审理共同犯罪再审案件，如果人民法院再审决定书或者人民检察院抗诉书只对部分同案原审被告人（同案原审上诉人）提起再审，其他未涉及的同案原审被告人（同案原审上诉人）不出庭不影响案件审理的，可以不出庭参与诉讼；  
部分同案原审被告人（同案原审上诉人）具有[本规定第六条](#art-6)第（三）、（四）项规定情形不能出庭的，不影响案件的开庭审理。

**Article 7** Where the People's Court tries a retrial case of joint crime, if the People's Court's retrial decision or the People's Procuratorate's written protest only initiates retrial against some of the co-defendants (co-appellants) and the absence of other co-defendants (co-appellants) who are not involved does not affect the trial of the case, such co-defendants (co-appellants) may not appear in court to participate in the proceedings;  
Where some of the co-defendants (co-appellants) are unable to appear in court due to the circumstances specified in Article 6(3) and (4) of these Provisions, it shall not affect the trial of the case in open court.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 除人民检察院抗诉的以外，再审一般不得加重原审被告人（原审上诉人）的刑罚。  
根据[本规定第六条](#art-6)第（二）、（三）、（四）、（五）项、第七条的规定，不具备开庭条件可以不开庭审理的，或者可以不出庭参加诉讼的，不得加重未出庭原审被告人（原审上诉人）、同案原审被告人（同案原审上诉人）的刑罚。

**Article 8** Except for cases where the People's Procuratorate has lodged a protest, retrial shall generally not impose a heavier punishment on the original defendant (original appellant).  
Where, according to Articles 6(2), (3), (4), (5) and Article 7 of these Provisions, a case can be tried without open court session because the conditions for open court session are not met, or where a person may not appear in court to participate in the proceedings, a heavier punishment shall not be imposed on the original defendant (original appellant) or co-defendant (co-appellant) who does not appear in court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 人民法院在开庭审理前，应当进行下列工作：  
（一）确定合议庭的组成人员；  
（二）将再审决定书，申诉书副本至迟在开庭三十日前，重大、疑难案件至迟在开庭六十日前送达同级人民检察院，并通知其查阅案卷和准备出庭；  
（三）将再审决定书或抗诉书副本至迟在开庭三十日以前送达原审被告人（原审上诉人），告知其可以委托辩护人，或者依法为其指定承担法律援助义务的律师担任辩护人；  
（四）至迟在开庭十五日前，重大、疑难案件至迟在开庭六十日前，通知辩护人查阅案卷和准备出庭；  
（五）将开庭的时间、地点在开庭七日以前通知人民检察院；  
（六）传唤当事人，通知辩护人、诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人和翻译人员，传票和通知书至迟在开庭七日以前送达；  
（七）公开审判的案件，在开庭七日以前先期公布案由、原审被告人（原审上诉人）姓名、开庭时间和地点。

**Article 9** Before trial in open court, the People's Court shall carry out the following work:  
(1) Determine the members of the collegial panel;  
(2) Serve the retrial decision and a copy of the petition on the People's Procuratorate at the same level no later than thirty days before the trial, or no later than sixty days before the trial for major or difficult cases, and notify it to review the case files and prepare for appearance in court;  
(3) Serve a copy of the retrial decision or the written protest on the original defendant (original appellant) no later than thirty days before the trial, and inform him that he may entrust a defender, or appoint a lawyer who undertakes legal aid obligations as his defender according to law;  
(4) Notify the defender to review the case files and prepare for appearance in court no later than fifteen days before the trial, or no later than sixty days before the trial for major or difficult cases;  
(5) Notify the People's Procuratorate of the time and place of the trial seven days before the trial;  
(6) Summon the parties, and notify the defenders, litigation agents, witnesses, expert evaluators, and interpreters; the summons and notices shall be served no later than seven days before the trial;  
(7) For cases to be tried in public, announce the cause of action, the name of the original defendant (original appellant), and the time and place of the trial in advance seven days before the trial.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 人民法院审理人民检察院提出抗诉的再审案件，对人民检察院接到出庭通知后未出庭的，应当裁定按人民检察院撤回抗诉处理，并通知诉讼参与人。

**Article 10** Where the People's Court tries a retrial case in which the People's Procuratorate has lodged a protest, and the People's Procuratorate fails to appear in court after receiving the notice to appear, the People's Court shall render a ruling to treat the protest as withdrawn by the People's Procuratorate, and shall notify the litigation participants.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民法院决定再审或者受理抗诉书后，原审被告人（原审上诉人）正在服刑的，人民法院依据再审决定书或者抗诉书及提押票等文书办理提押；  
原审被告人（原审上诉人）在押，再审可能改判宣告无罪的，人民法院裁定中止执行原裁决后，可以取保候审；  
原审被告人（原审上诉人）不在押，确有必要采取强制措施并符合法律规定采取强制措施条件的，人民法院裁定中止执行原裁决后，依法采取强制措施。

**Article 11** After the People's Court decides to retry or accepts the written protest, if the original defendant (original appellant) is currently serving a sentence, the People's Court shall arrange for the production of the defendant in court based on the retrial decision or the written protest and documents such as the production warrant;  
If the original defendant (original appellant) is in custody and the retrial may result in an acquittal by amendment of the judgment, the People's Court may, after rendering a ruling to suspend enforcement of the original ruling, release him on bail pending investigation or trial;  
If the original defendant (original appellant) is not in custody and it is truly necessary to take compulsory measures and the conditions for taking compulsory measures as provided by law are met, the People's Court shall, after rendering a ruling to suspend enforcement of the original ruling, take compulsory measures according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 原审被告人（原审上诉人）收到再审决定书或者抗诉书后下落不明或者收到抗诉书后未到庭的，人民法院应当中止审理；原审被告人（原审上诉人）到案后，恢复审理；如果超过二年仍查无下落的，应当裁定终止审理。

**Article 12** Where the original defendant (original appellant) is missing after receiving the retrial decision or the written protest, or fails to appear in court after receiving the written protest, the People's Court shall suspend the trial; after the original defendant (original appellant) appears, the trial shall be resumed; if after more than two years the whereabouts are still unknown, the People's Court shall render a ruling to terminate the trial.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 人民法院应当在开庭三十日前通知人民检察院、当事人或者辩护人查阅、复制双方提交的新证据目录及新证据复印件、照片。  
人民法院应当在开庭十五日前通知控辩双方查阅、复制人民法院调取的新证据目录及新证据复印件、照片等证据。

**Article 13** The People's Court shall, within thirty days before the trial, notify the People's Procuratorate, the parties, or the defenders to review and copy the lists of new evidence and copies and photos of new evidence submitted by both sides.  
The People's Court shall, within fifteen days before the trial, notify the prosecution and the defense to review and copy the lists of new evidence obtained by the People's Court and copies and photos of such evidence.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 控辩双方收到再审决定书或抗诉书后，人民法院通知开庭之日前，可以提交新的证据。开庭后，除对原审被告人（原审上诉人）有利的外，人民法院不再接纳新证据。

**Article 14** After receiving the retrial decision or the written protest, the prosecution and the defense may submit new evidence before the date on which the People's Court notifies the trial. After the trial begins, the People's Court shall not accept new evidence unless it is favorable to the original defendant (original appellant).

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 开庭审理前，合议庭应当核实原审被告人（原审上诉人）何时因何案被人民法院依法裁判，在服刑中有无重新犯罪，有无减刑、假释，何时刑满释放等情形。

**Article 15** Before trial in open court, the collegial panel shall verify when and for what case the original defendant (original appellant) was adjudicated by the People's Court according to law, whether he committed any new crime during the service of sentence, whether he has been granted commutation of punishment or parole, and when the sentence expires or has been completed.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 开庭审理前，原审被告人（原审上诉人）到达开庭地点后，合议庭应当查明原审被告人（原审上诉人）基本情况，告知原审被告人（原审上诉人）享有辩护权和最后陈述权，制作笔录后，分别由该合议庭成员和书记员签名。

**Article 16** Before trial in open court, after the original defendant (original appellant) arrives at the place of trial, the collegial panel shall ascertain the basic information of the original defendant (original appellant), inform him of his right to defense and the right to make a final statement, make a record, and then have it signed by the members of the collegial panel and the court clerk respectively.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 开庭审理时，审判长宣布合议庭组成人员及书记员，公诉人、辩护人、鉴定人和翻译人员的名单，并告知当事人、法定代理人享有申请回避的权利。

**Article 17** At the commencement of the trial in open court, the presiding judge shall announce the members of the collegial panel and the court clerk, the list of public prosecutors, defenders, expert evaluators, and interpreters, and inform the parties and their legal representatives of their right to apply for withdrawal.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 人民法院决定再审的，由合议庭组成人员宣读再审决定书。  
根据人民检察院提出抗诉进行再审的，由公诉人宣读抗诉书。  
当事人及其法定代理人、近亲属提出申诉的，由原审被告人（原审上诉人）及其辩护人陈述申诉理由。

**Article 18** Where the People's Court decides to retry, a member of the collegial panel shall read the retrial decision.  
Where the retrial is conducted based on a protest lodged by the People's Procuratorate, the public prosecutor shall read the written protest.  
Where the parties, their legal representatives, or close relatives have filed a petition, the original defendant (original appellant) and his defender shall state the grounds of the petition.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条 在审判长主持下，控辩双方应就案件的事实、证据和适用法律等问题分别进行陈述。合议庭对控辩双方无争议和有争议的事实、证据及适用法律问题进行归纳，予以确认。

**Article 19** Under the direction of the presiding judge, the prosecution and the defense shall respectively state their positions on the facts, evidence, and application of law of the case. The collegial panel shall summarize and confirm the facts, evidence, and issues of application of law on which the prosecution and the defense agree or disagree.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条 在审判长主持下，就控辩双方有争议的问题，进行法庭调查和辩论。

**Article 20** Under the direction of the presiding judge, court investigation and debate shall be conducted on the issues in dispute between the prosecution and the defense.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条 在审判长主持下，控辩双方对提出的新证据或者有异议的原审据以定罪量刑的证据进行质证。

**Article 21** Under the direction of the presiding judge, the prosecution and the defense shall examine the new evidence presented or the evidence from the original trial which is challenged and on which the conviction and sentencing were based.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条 进入辩论阶段，原审被告人（原审上诉人）及其法定代理人、近亲属提出申诉的，先由原审被告人（原审上诉人）及其辩护人发表辩护意见，然后由公诉人发言，被害人及其代理人发言。  
被害人及其法定代理人、近亲属提出申诉的，先由被害人及其代理人发言，公诉人发言，然后由原审被告人（原审上诉人）及其辩护人发表辩护意见。  
人民检察院提出抗诉的，先由公诉人发言，被害人及其代理人发言，然后由原审被告人（原审上诉人）及其辩护人发表辩护意见。  
既有申诉又有抗诉的，先由公诉人发言，后由申诉方当事人及其代理人或者辩护人发言或者发表辩护意见，然后由对方当事人及其代理人或辩护人发言或者发表辩护意见。  
公诉人、当事人和辩护人、诉讼代理人经审判长许可，可以互相辩论。

**Article 22** When entering the debate stage, if the petition is filed by the original defendant (original appellant) or his legal representative or close relative, the original defendant (original appellant) and his defender shall first present their defense opinions, followed by the public prosecutor's statement, and then the victim and his agent's statement.  
If the petition is filed by the victim or his legal representative or close relative, the victim and his agent shall first make a statement, then the public prosecutor shall make a statement, and then the original defendant (original appellant) and his defender shall present their defense opinions.  
If the protest is lodged by the People's Procuratorate, the public prosecutor shall first make a statement, followed by the victim and his agent's statement, and then the original defendant (original appellant) and his defender shall present their defense opinions.  
If there is both a petition and a protest, the public prosecutor shall first make a statement, followed by the petitioner party and his agent or defender making a statement or presenting defense opinions, and then the opposing party and his agent or defender making a statement or presenting defense opinions.  
The public prosecutor, parties, defenders, and litigation agents may engage in mutual debate with the permission of the presiding judge.

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条 合议庭根据控辩双方举证、质证和辩论情况，可以当庭宣布认证结果。

**Article 23** Based on the evidence presented, examination, and debate by the prosecution and the defense, the collegial panel may announce the results of the certification of evidence in court.

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条 再审改判宣告无罪并依法享有申请国家赔偿权利的当事人，宣判时合议庭应当告知其该判决发生法律效力后即有申请国家赔偿的权利。

**Article 24** Where the retrial modifies the judgment and acquits the party who is entitled to apply for state compensation according to law, the collegial panel shall, when pronouncing the judgment, inform him that after the judgment becomes legally effective, he shall have the right to apply for state compensation.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条 人民法院审理再审案件，应当在作出再审决定之日起三个月内审结。需要延长期限的，经本院院长批准，可以延长三个月。  
自接到阅卷通知后的第二日起，人民检察院查阅案卷超过七日后的期限，不计入再审审理期限。

**Article 25** The People's Court shall conclude the retrial case within three months from the date of the decision to retry. Where an extension of the time limit is necessary, it may be extended by three months with the approval of the president of the court.  
The period during which the People's Procuratorate reviews the case files exceeding seven days from the day after receiving the notice to review the files shall not be counted in the time limit for retrial.

<a id="art-26"></a>第二十六条 依照第一、二审程序审理的刑事自诉再审案件开庭审理程序，参照本规定执行。

**Article 26** The procedures for trial in open court of criminal private prosecution retrial cases tried in accordance with the procedures of first instance or second instance shall be conducted by reference to these Provisions.

<a id="art-27"></a>第二十七条 本规定发布前最高人民法院有关再审案件开庭审理程序的规定，与本规定相抵触的，以本规定为准。

**Article 27** Where any provisions of the Supreme People's Court on procedures for trial in open court of retrial cases issued before these Provisions come into force conflict with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.

<a id="art-28"></a>第二十八条 本规定自2002年1月1日起执行。

**Article 28** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 1, 2002.
