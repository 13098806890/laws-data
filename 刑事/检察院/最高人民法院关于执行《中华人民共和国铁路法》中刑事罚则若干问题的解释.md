# 最高人民法院关于执行《中华人民共和国铁路法》中刑事罚则若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法发〔1993〕28号  
**公布日期**：1993-10-11

---

### 正文

一、怎样理解《中华人民共和国铁路法》第六十条第一款的有关规定？  
《中华人民共和国铁路法》（以下简称《铁路法》）第六十条第一款规定：“违反本法规定，携带危险品进站上车或者以非危险品品名托运危险品，导致发生重大事故的，依照刑法第一百一十五条的规定追究刑事责任。企业事业单位、国家机关、社会团体犯本款罪的，处以罚金，对其主管人员和直接责任人员依法追究刑事责任。”  
（一）本款规定所称的“危险品”，是指具有爆炸、易燃、放射、毒害、腐蚀等性质，在运输、装卸和储存、保管过程中，容易造成人身伤亡和财产毁损而需要特别防护的物品，其具体范围，按国务院及国务院主管部门的规定认定。  
（二）本款规定所称的“重大事故”，是指因非法携带上述危险品而发生爆炸、燃烧、泄露事件，致人重伤一人以上；致人轻伤三人以上；造成直接经济损失一万元以上；或者造成暂时中断铁路行车等严重后果的。行为人实施本款规定的犯罪，致人死亡或者其它特别严重后果的，属于刑法第一百一十五条所规定的“后果特别严重”，从重处罚。

**Article None** I. How to Understand the Relevant Provisions of Article 60, Paragraph 1 of the Railway Law of the People's Republic of China?  
Article 60, Paragraph 1 of the Railway Law of the People's Republic of China (hereinafter referred to as the "Railway Law") stipulates: "Whoever, in violation of the provisions of this Law, carries dangerous goods into a station or aboard a train, or consigns dangerous goods under the name of non-dangerous goods, thereby causing a major accident, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 115 of the Criminal Law. If an enterprise, institution, state organ, or social organization commits the crime under this paragraph, it shall be fined, and the persons in charge and the persons directly responsible shall be investigated for criminal liability according to law."  
(1) The term "dangerous goods" as used in this paragraph refers to articles that are explosive, flammable, radioactive, poisonous, corrosive, or otherwise dangerous by nature, which are liable to cause personal injury or death and property damage during transportation, loading and unloading, storage, and custody, and therefore require special protection. The specific scope shall be determined in accordance with the regulations of the State Council and the competent departments under the State Council.  
(2) The term "major accident" as used in this paragraph refers to an incident of explosion, combustion, or leakage caused by the illegal carrying of the above-mentioned dangerous goods, resulting in serious injury to one or more persons; minor injury to three or more persons; direct economic losses of 10,000 yuan or more; or serious consequences such as temporary interruption of railway operations. If the actor commits the crime under this paragraph and causes death or other particularly serious consequences, it shall constitute "particularly serious consequences" as provided for in Article 115 of the Criminal Law, and the actor shall be given a severe punishment.

二、对携带炸药、雷管或者非法携带枪支子弹、管制刀具进站上车的行为，如何追究刑事责任？  
《铁路法》第关十条第二款规定：“携带炸药、雷管或者非法携带枪支子弹、管制刀具进站上车的，比照刑法第一百六十三条的规定追究刑事责任。”  
（一）携带炸药、雷管或者非法携带枪支子弹、管制刀具进站上车构成犯罪的，应当定非法携带炸药、雷管、枪支子弹、管制刀具进站上车罪，依照刑法第一百六十三条规定适用刑罚。本罪为选择性罪名。分别实施携带炸药、雷管、枪支子弹或者管制刀具进站上车行为的，不实行数罪并罚。行为人携带炸药、雷管进站上车，导致发生重大事故的，适用《铁路法》第六十条第一款规定，依照刑法第一百一十五条的规定追究刑事责任。  
（二）非法携带炸药、雷管、枪支子弹、管制刀具进站上车，具有下列情形之一的，即可构成本罪：  
1、携带炸药、雷管、子弹，在车站、列车上发生爆炸、燃烧，尚未造成严重后果的；  
2、同时携带炸药、雷管，或者携带爆炸装置的；  
3、携带炸药一千克以上的；  
4、携带雷管五十枚以上的；  
5、非法携带枪支并子弹的；  
6、非法携带管制刀具二十把以上或者虽未达到规定的数量标准，但在车站或者列车上进行违法活动时使用，尚未构成其他犯罪的。  
具体认定是否构成本罪，应当对行为人非法携带上列物品的数量、危害后果等情节综合分析。行为人携带炸药、雷管或者非法携带枪支子弹、管制刀具虽未达到规定的数量标准，但拒不交出的，也可以追究刑事责任；如果数量刚已达到规定的数量标准，但行为人进站上车后，能主动、全部交出的，也可不以犯罪论处。  
（三）《铁路法》中所称“进站上车”是指进入铁路车站或者乘上客货列车。  
（四）行为人非法制造、收买枪支、弹药或者盗窃、抢夺枪支、弹药后，携带进站上车的，应当按非法制  
造、买卖枪支、弹药罪或者盗窃、抢夺枪支、弹药罪与非法携带炸药、雷管、枪支子弹进站上车罪实行数罪并罚；行为人非法运输枪支、弹药并携带枪支、弹药进站上车的，应当以非法运输枪支、弹药罪定罪处罚。  
（五）非法携带管制刀具进站上车罪中“管制刀具”的范围，应当依照1983年公安部颁发的《对部分刀具实行管制的暂行规定》认定。

**Article None** II. How to Investigate Criminal Liability for the Act of Carrying Explosives or Detonators, or Illegally Carrying Firearms, Ammunition, or Controlled Knives into a Station or Aboard a Train?  
Article 60, Paragraph 2 of the Railway Law stipulates: "Whoever carries explosives or detonators, or illegally carries firearms, ammunition, or controlled knives into a station or aboard a train shall be investigated for criminal liability by reference to the provisions of Article 163 of the Criminal Law."  
(1) If the act of carrying explosives or detonators, or illegally carrying firearms, ammunition, or controlled knives into a station or aboard a train constitutes a crime, the crime shall be determined as the crime of illegally carrying explosives, detonators, firearms, ammunition, or controlled knives into a station or aboard a train, and punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 163 of the Criminal Law. This crime is a selective charge. If the actor separately commits acts of carrying explosives, detonators, firearms, ammunition, or controlled knives into a station or aboard a train, the punishments shall not be imposed cumulatively for several crimes. If the actor carries explosives or detonators into a station or aboard a train, thereby causing a major accident, the provisions of Article 60, Paragraph 1 of the Railway Law shall apply, and criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with Article 115 of the Criminal Law.  
(2) Illegally carrying explosives, detonators, firearms, ammunition, or controlled knives into a station or aboard a train, in any of the following circumstances, may constitute this crime:  
1. Carrying explosives, detonators, or ammunition which explode or combust at the station or on the train, without causing serious consequences;  
2. Carrying both explosives and detonators, or carrying an explosive device;  
3. Carrying 1 kilogram or more of explosives;  
4. Carrying 50 or more detonators;  
5. Illegally carrying a firearm with ammunition;  
6. Illegally carrying 20 or more controlled knives, or carrying fewer than the prescribed quantity but using them at the station or on the train for illegal activities without constituting another crime.  
In determining whether the crime is constituted, a comprehensive analysis shall be made of the quantity of the above-mentioned articles illegally carried by the actor, the harmful consequences, and other circumstances. If the actor carries explosives or detonators, or illegally carries firearms, ammunition, or controlled knives, but the quantity does not reach the prescribed standard, and the actor refuses to hand them over, criminal liability may also be investigated; if the quantity just reaches the prescribed standard, but the actor voluntarily and completely hands them over after entering the station or boarding the train, the act may not be treated as a crime.  
(3) The term "entering a station or boarding a train" as used in the Railway Law means entering a railway station or boarding a passenger or freight train.  
(4) If the actor illegally manufactures or purchases firearms or ammunition, or steals or seizes firearms or ammunition, and then carries them into a station or aboard a train, the actor shall be punished for the crimes of illegally manufacturing or dealing in firearms or ammunition, or stealing or seizing firearms or ammunition, and the crime of illegally carrying explosives, detonators, firearms, or ammunition into a station or aboard a train, with cumulative punishments; if the actor illegally transports firearms or ammunition and carries them into a station or aboard a train, the crime shall be determined as the crime of illegally transporting firearms or ammunition.  
(5) The scope of "controlled knives" in the crime of illegally carrying controlled knives into a station or aboard a train shall be determined in accordance with the "Provisional Regulations on the Control of Certain Knives" issued by the Ministry of Public Security in 1983.

三、怎样理解《铁路法》第六十一条、第六十二条的有关规定？  
《铁路法》第六十一条规定：“故意损毁、移动铁路行车信号装置或者在铁路线路上放量足以使列车倾覆的障碍物，尚未造成严重后果的，依照刑法第一百零八条的规定追究刑事责任；造成严重后果的，依照刑法第一百一十条的规定追究刑事责任。”《铁路法》第六十二条规定：“盗窃铁路线路上行车设施的零件、部件或者铁路线路上的器材，危及行车安全，尚未造成严重后果的，依照刑法第一百零八条破坏交通设施罪的规定追究刑事责任；造成严重后果的，依照刑法第一百一十条破坏交通设施罪的规定追究刑事责任。”  
（一）《铁路法》第六十一条、第六十二条规定所称的“严重后果”，是指因行为人故意毁损、移动铁路行车信号装置或者在铁路线路上放置足以使列车倾覆的障碍物，或者盗窃铁路线路上行车设施的零件、部件、铁路线路上的器材，造成人身伤亡、重大财产毁损、中断铁路行车等严重后果的。  
（二）行为人实施上述行为，虽未造成上述严重后果，但经铁路有关部门鉴定，足以危及行车安全的，应当依照刑法第一百零八条的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article None** III. How to Understand the Relevant Provisions of Articles 61 and 62 of the Railway Law?  
Article 61 of the Railway Law stipulates: "Whoever intentionally damages or moves railway signaling devices, or places obstacles on railway lines sufficient to cause train derailment, if no serious consequences have been caused, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 108 of the Criminal Law; if serious consequences have been caused, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 110 of the Criminal Law." Article 62 of the Railway Law stipulates: "Whoever steals parts or components of operating facilities on railway lines or equipment on railway lines, thereby endangering traffic safety, if no serious consequences have been caused, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 108 of the Criminal Law concerning the crime of sabotaging transportation facilities; if serious consequences have been caused, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 110 of the Criminal Law concerning the crime of sabotaging transportation facilities."  
(1) The term "serious consequences" as used in Articles 61 and 62 of the Railway Law refers to situations where the actor intentionally damages or moves railway signaling devices, or places obstacles on railway lines sufficient to cause train derailment, or steals parts or components of operating facilities on railway lines or equipment on railway lines, thereby causing personal injury or death, major property damage, interruption of railway operations, or other serious consequences.  
(2) If the actor commits the above acts, although not causing the above serious consequences, but after appraisal by the relevant railway department, the acts are deemed sufficient to endanger traffic safety, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 108 of the Criminal Law.

四、对聚众哄抢铁路运输物资的犯罪分子，如何追究刑事责任？  
《铁路法》第六十四条第一款规定：“聚众哄抢铁路运输物资的，对首要分子和骨干分子依照刑法第一百五十一条或者第一百五十二条的规定追究刑事责任”。  
（一）聚众哄抢铁路运输物资的，对首要分子和骨干分子应当以抢夺罪，依照刑法第一百五十一条或者第一百五十二条的规定追究刑事责任，一般应从重处罚。犯罪分子如果在哄抢铁路运输物资过程中使用暴力或者以暴力相威胁，或者为窝藏赃物、抗拒逮捕、毁灭罪证而当场使用暴力或者以暴力相威胁的，应当以抢劫罪论处，从重处罚。  
（二）上述犯罪中的“首要分子”，是指在聚众哄抢铁路运输物资的犯罪中，起组织、策划、指挥作用的犯罪分子；“骨干分子”，是指在聚众哄抢铁路运输物资的犯罪中，除首要分子之外，其他起主要作用的犯罪分子，如带头哄抢铁路运输物资的，哄抢铁路运输物资数量较大的犯罪分子等。

**Article None** IV. How to Investigate Criminal Liability for Criminals Who Gather a Crowd to Forcibly Seize Railway Transport Goods?  
Article 64, Paragraph 1 of the Railway Law stipulates: "For those who gather a crowd to forcibly seize railway transport goods, the ringleaders and key members shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 151 or Article 152 of the Criminal Law."  
(1) For those who gather a crowd to forcibly seize railway transport goods, the ringleaders and key members shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of forcible seizure in accordance with the provisions of Article 151 or Article 152 of the Criminal Law, and generally shall be given a severe punishment. If the criminal uses violence or threatens violence in the process of forcibly seizing railway transport goods, or uses violence or threatens violence on the spot to conceal stolen goods, resist arrest, or destroy evidence, the act shall be treated as robbery, and a severe punishment shall be imposed.  
(2) The term "ringleader" in the above crime refers to the criminal who organizes, plans, or directs the crime of gathering a crowd to forcibly seize railway transport goods; the term "key member" refers to, apart from the ringleader, other criminals who play a major role in the crime of gathering a crowd to forcibly seize railway transport goods, such as those who take the lead in forcibly seizing railway transport goods or those who forcibly seize a relatively large quantity of railway transport goods.

五、如何认定和处理在列车内实施抢劫的犯罪行为？  
《铁路法》第六十五条规定：在列车内抢劫旅客财物的，“依照刑法有关规定从重处罚”。  
（一）在列车内抢劫旅客财物，是抢劫罪中一种严重的犯罪形式。具体认定时，应当根据刑法有关抢劫罪的规定，结合铁路运输的特殊性，综合考虑。凡在列车内，对旅客使用暴力、胁迫手段，如以语言威胁、暴露或者暗示携带有凶器或者依仗人多势众，对被害人施加精神压力等，强拿旅客财物或者以“借钱借物”为名，索取财物的，以及对旅客实行强买强卖，侵犯旅客财产权益的，均应以抢劫罪论处。  
（二）在列车内抢劫旅客财物的，一般视为刑法第一百五十条第二款中规定的“情节严重”，适用该款的规定从重处罚；但情节较轻的，或者是从犯等，可以适用刑法第一百五十条第一款的规定处罚。

**Article None** V. How to Determine and Handle the Criminal Act of Robbery Committed on a Train?  
Article 65 of the Railway Law stipulates: Robbery of passengers' property on a train shall "be given a severe punishment in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law."  
(1) Robbery of passengers' property on a train is a serious form of the crime of robbery. In specific determination, a comprehensive consideration shall be made in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law on robbery, taking into account the particularities of railway transportation. All acts on a train that use violence or coercion against passengers, such as verbal threats, exposing or implying the carrying of a weapon, or taking advantage of numbers to exert mental pressure on the victim, forcibly taking passengers' property or demanding property under the pretext of "borrowing money or things," as well as forcing passengers to buy or sell goods, infringing upon passengers' property rights, shall be treated as robbery.  
(2) Robbery of passengers' property on a train shall generally be regarded as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 150, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law, and a severe punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of that paragraph; however, if the circumstances are relatively minor, or if the offender is an accomplice, etc., punishment may be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 150, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law.

六、对倒卖旅客车票的行为，如何追究刑事责任？  
《铁路法》第六十六条规定：“倒卖旅客车票数额较大的，依照刑法第一百一十七条的规定追究刑事责任。以倒卖旅客车票为常业的，倒卖数额巨大或者倒卖集团的首要分子，依照刑法第一百一十八条的规定追究刑事责任。铁路职工倒卖旅客车票或者与其他人员勾结倒卖旅客车票的，依照刑法第一百一十九条的规定追究刑事责任。”  
（一）倒卖旅客车票，包括高价、变相加价倒卖车票以及倒卖坐席、卧铺签字号及订购票凭证，非法经营数额在一千元以上，或者非法获利数额在五百元以上的，一般可视为“数额较大”；非法经营数额在一万元以上，或者非法获利数额在五千元以上的，一般可视为“数额巨大”。  
倒卖旅客车票虽未达到“数额较大”的起点标准，但曾因倒卖旅客车票受治安处罚二次以上、或者被劳动教养一次以上，两年内又倒卖旅客车票的，也应追究其刑事责任。  
（二）“以倒卖旅客车票为常业”，是指连续多次倒卖旅客车票，以倒卖车票所得为其挥霍或者生活主要来源的。  
（三）铁路职工倒卖旅客车票或者与其他人员勾结倒卖旅客车票的，依照刑法第一百一十九条的规定及本条第（一）、（二）项的解释，从重处罚。其他利用职务便利倒卖旅客车票的，也应从重处罚。

**Article None** VI. How to Investigate Criminal Liability for the Act of Scalping Passenger Tickets?  
Article 66 of the Railway Law stipulates: "Whoever scalps passenger tickets in a relatively large amount shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 117 of the Criminal Law. Whoever makes scalping passenger tickets a regular occupation, scalps in a huge amount, or is a ringleader of a scalping group shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of Article 118 of the Criminal Law. If a railway employee scalps passenger tickets or colludes with others to scalp passenger tickets, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 119 of the Criminal Law."  
(1) Scalping passenger tickets includes selling tickets at high prices or at markups in disguised form, as well as selling seat reservation slips, sleeper berth reservation slips, and ticket ordering vouchers. If the illegal business turnover is 1,000 yuan or more, or the illegal profit is 500 yuan or more, it may generally be regarded as a "relatively large amount"; if the illegal business turnover is 10,000 yuan or more, or the illegal profit is 5,000 yuan or more, it may generally be regarded as a "huge amount."  
Even if the amount of scalping passenger tickets does not reach the threshold for "relatively large amount," if the person has been subjected to public security penalties twice or more for scalping passenger tickets, or has been subjected to re-education through labor once or more, and within two years scalps passenger tickets again, criminal liability shall also be investigated.  
(2) "Making scalping passenger tickets a regular occupation" refers to continuously and repeatedly scalping passenger tickets, with the proceeds from scalping as the main source of expenditure or livelihood.  
(3) If a railway employee scalps passenger tickets or colludes with others to scalp passenger tickets, a severe punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Article 119 of the Criminal Law and the interpretations in items (1) and (2) of this Article. Other persons who use their job convenience to scalp passenger tickets shall also be given a severe punishment.

七、对违反《铁路法》的犯罪行为追究刑事责任时，在司法文书中如何引用《铁路法》和刑法的有关条款？  
（一）所定罪行属于《铁路法》新增加的罪名，或者《铁路法》对刑法所规定的犯罪构成要件、处罚作了修改、补充规定的，应当同时引用刑法和《铁路法》的有关条款。  
（二）《铁路法》中涉及刑事处罚的规定没有超出刑法有关规定的，不必引用《铁路法》。

**Article None** VII. When Investigating Criminal Liability for Criminal Acts Violating the Railway Law, How to Cite the Relevant Provisions of the Railway Law and the Criminal Law in Judicial Documents?  
(1) If the crime determined is a new crime added by the Railway Law, or if the Railway Law has amended or supplemented the constitutive elements or penalties of a crime provided in the Criminal Law, the relevant provisions of both the Criminal Law and the Railway Law shall be cited simultaneously.  
(2) If the provisions concerning criminal punishment in the Railway Law do not go beyond the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law, it is not necessary to cite the Railway Law.

八、如何执行本解释？  
本解释自发布之日起执行。解释发布前已处理的案件，不再变动；解释发布后，应当依照《铁路法》办理的案件，尚未办理或者正在办理的，一律适用本解释。

**Article None** VIII. How to Implement this Interpretation?  
This Interpretation shall come into force on the date of its promulgation. Cases that have already been handled before the promulgation of this Interpretation shall not be altered; for cases that shall be handled in accordance with the Railway Law after the promulgation of this Interpretation, which have not yet been handled or are being handled, this Interpretation shall apply uniformly.
