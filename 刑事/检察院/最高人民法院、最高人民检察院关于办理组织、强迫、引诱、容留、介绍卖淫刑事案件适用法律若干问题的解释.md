# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍卖淫刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕13号  
**公布日期**：2017-05-08  
**生效日期**：2017-07-25

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍卖淫刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2017年5月8日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1716次会议、2017年7月4日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第66次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年7月25日起施行。 2017年7月21日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍卖淫 刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2017〕13号 为依法惩治组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍卖淫犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，结合司法工作实际，现就办理这类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　以招募、雇佣、纠集等手段，管理或者控制他人卖淫，卖淫人员在三人以上的，应当认定为刑法第三百五十八条规定的“组织他人卖淫”。 组织卖淫者是否设置固定的卖淫场所、组织卖淫者人数多少、规模大小，不影响组织卖淫行为的认定。 第二条　组织他人卖淫，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百五十八条第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）卖淫人员累计达十人以上的； （二）卖淫人员中未成年人、孕妇、智障人员、患有严重性病的人累计达五人以上的； （三）组织境外人员在境内卖淫或者组织境内人员出境卖淫的； （四）非法获利人民币一百万元以上的； （五）造成被组织卖淫的人自残、自杀或者其他严重后果的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　以招募、雇佣、纠集等手段，管理或者控制他人卖淫，卖淫人员在三人以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-358)规定的“组织他人卖淫”。  
组织卖淫者是否设置固定的卖淫场所、组织卖淫者人数多少、规模大小，不影响组织卖淫行为的认定。

**Article 1** : Where a person manages or controls others to engage in prostitution by means such as recruitment, employment, or assembly, and the number of prostitutes is three or more, it shall be identified as "organizing others to engage in prostitution" as provided in Article 358 of the Criminal Law.  
Whether the organizer sets up a fixed place for prostitution, the number of organizers, or the scale thereof shall not affect the determination of the act of organizing prostitution.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　组织他人卖淫，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-358)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）卖淫人员累计达十人以上的；  
（二）卖淫人员中未成年人、孕妇、智障人员、患有严重性病的人累计达五人以上的；  
（三）组织境外人员在境内卖淫或者组织境内人员出境卖淫的；  
（四）非法获利人民币一百万元以上的；  
（五）造成被组织卖淫的人自残、自杀或者其他严重后果的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** : Where organizing others to engage in prostitution falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be identified as "serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 358 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The cumulative number of prostitutes reaches ten or more;  
(2) The cumulative number of minors, pregnant women, mentally disabled persons, or persons suffering from severe venereal diseases among the prostitutes reaches five or more;  
(3) Organizing persons from outside the territory to engage in prostitution within the territory or organizing persons within the territory to engage in prostitution outside the territory;  
(4) Illegally obtaining profits of RMB 1 million or more;  
(5) Causing self-harm, suicide, or other serious consequences to the persons being organized to engage in prostitution;  
(6) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在组织卖淫犯罪活动中，对被组织卖淫的人有引诱、容留、介绍卖淫行为的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。但是，对被组织卖淫的人以外的其他人有引诱、容留、介绍卖淫行为的，应当分别定罪，实行数罪并罚。

**Article 3** : Where, in the course of criminal activities of organizing prostitution, a person induces, shelters, or introduces prostitution to the persons being organized to engage in prostitution, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions providing for a heavier punishment. However, where a person induces, shelters, or introduces prostitution to persons other than those being organized to engage in prostitution, the person shall be convicted separately and cumulative punishment shall be imposed for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　明知他人实施组织卖淫犯罪活动而为其招募、运送人员或者充当保镖、打手、管账人等的，依照[刑法第三百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-358)第四款的规定，以协助组织卖淫罪定罪处罚，不以组织卖淫罪的从犯论处。  
在具有营业执照的会所、洗浴中心等经营场所担任保洁员、收银员、保安员等，从事一般服务性、劳务性工作，仅领取正常薪酬，且无前款所列协助组织卖淫行为的，不认定为协助组织卖淫罪。

**Article 4** : Where a person, knowing that another person is carrying out criminal activities of organizing prostitution, recruits or transports personnel for that person, or acts as a bodyguard, thug, accountant, or the like, the person shall be convicted and punished as the crime of assisting in organizing prostitution in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 4 of Article 358 of the Criminal Law, and shall not be treated as an accomplice to the crime of organizing prostitution.  
Where a person working in a licensed club, bathing center, or other business premises as a cleaner, cashier, security guard, or the like, performing general service or labor work, only receiving normal remuneration, and does not commit any of the acts of assisting in organizing prostitution listed in the preceding paragraph, the person shall not be identified as committing the crime of assisting in organizing prostitution.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　协助组织他人卖淫，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-358)第四款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）招募、运送卖淫人员累计达十人以上的；  
（二）招募、运送的卖淫人员中未成年人、孕妇、智障人员、患有严重性病的人累计达五人以上的；  
（三）协助组织境外人员在境内卖淫或者协助组织境内人员出境卖淫的；  
（四）非法获利人民币五十万元以上的；  
（五）造成被招募、运送或者被组织卖淫的人自残、自杀或者其他严重后果的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 5** : Where assisting in organizing others to engage in prostitution falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be identified as "serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 4 of Article 358 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The cumulative number of prostitutes recruited or transported reaches ten or more;  
(2) The cumulative number of minors, pregnant women, mentally disabled persons, or persons suffering from severe venereal diseases among the prostitutes recruited or transported reaches five or more;  
(3) Assisting in organizing persons from outside the territory to engage in prostitution within the territory or assisting in organizing persons within the territory to engage in prostitution outside the territory;  
(4) Illegally obtaining profits of RMB 500,000 or more;  
(5) Causing self-harm, suicide, or other serious consequences to the persons recruited, transported, or being organized to engage in prostitution;  
(6) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　强迫他人卖淫，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-358)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）卖淫人员累计达五人以上的；  
（二）卖淫人员中未成年人、孕妇、智障人员、患有严重性病的人累计达三人以上的；  
（三）强迫不满十四周岁的幼女卖淫的；  
（四）造成被强迫卖淫的人自残、自杀或者其他严重后果的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
行为人既有组织卖淫犯罪行为，又有强迫卖淫犯罪行为，且具有下列情形之一的，以组织、强迫卖淫“情节严重”论处：  
（一）组织卖淫、强迫卖淫行为中具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)、本条前款规定的“情节严重”情形之一的；  
（二）卖淫人员累计达到[本解释第二条](#art-2)第一、二项规定的组织卖淫“情节严重”人数标准的；  
（三）非法获利数额相加达到[本解释第二条](#art-2)第四项规定的组织卖淫“情节严重”数额标准的。

**Article 6** : Where forcing another person to engage in prostitution falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be identified as "serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 358 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The cumulative number of prostitutes reaches five or more;  
(2) The cumulative number of minors, pregnant women, mentally disabled persons, or persons suffering from severe venereal diseases among the prostitutes reaches three or more;  
(3) Forcing a female minor under the age of 14 to engage in prostitution;  
(4) Causing self-harm, suicide, or other serious consequences to the person being forced into prostitution;  
(5) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.  
Where the actor commits both the crime of organizing prostitution and the crime of forcing prostitution, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the actor shall be punished as "serious circumstances" for organizing and forcing prostitution:  
(1) Any of the circumstances of "serious circumstances" provided in Article 2 of this Interpretation or the preceding paragraph of this Article exists in the acts of organizing prostitution or forcing prostitution;  
(2) The cumulative number of prostitutes reaches the numerical standard for "serious circumstances" of organizing prostitution provided in subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 2 of this Interpretation;  
(3) The total amount of illegal profits reaches the monetary standard for "serious circumstances" of organizing prostitution provided in subparagraph (4) of Article 2 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　根据[刑法第三百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-358)第三款的规定，犯组织、强迫卖淫罪，并有杀害、伤害、强奸、绑架等犯罪行为的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。协助组织卖淫行为人参与实施上述行为的，以共同犯罪论处。  
根据[刑法第三百五十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-358)第二款的规定，组织、强迫未成年人卖淫的，应当从重处罚。

**Article 7** : According to the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 358 of the Criminal Law, where a person commits the crime of organizing or forcing prostitution and also commits criminal acts such as homicide, injury, rape, or kidnapping, the person shall be punished in accordance with the provisions on cumulative punishment for multiple crimes. Where a person who assists in organizing prostitution participates in committing the above acts, the person shall be punished as a joint crime.  
According to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 358 of the Criminal Law, where a person organizes or forces a minor to engage in prostitution, a severe punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百五十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-359)第一款的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）引诱他人卖淫的；  
（二）容留、介绍二人以上卖淫的；  
（三）容留、介绍未成年人、孕妇、智障人员、患有严重性病的人卖淫的；  
（四）一年内曾因引诱、容留、介绍卖淫行为被行政处罚，又实施容留、介绍卖淫行为的；  
（五）非法获利人民币一万元以上的。  
利用信息网络发布招嫖违法信息，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一的规定，以非法利用信息网络罪定罪处罚。同时构成介绍卖淫罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫是否以营利为目的，不影响犯罪的成立。  
引诱不满十四周岁的幼女卖淫的，依照[刑法第三百五十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-359)第二款的规定，以引诱幼女卖淫罪定罪处罚。  
被引诱卖淫的人员中既有不满十四周岁的幼女，又有其他人员的，分别以引诱幼女卖淫罪和引诱卖淫罪定罪，实行并罚。

**Article 8** : Where inducing, sheltering, or introducing another person to engage in prostitution falls under any of the following circumstances, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 359 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Inducing another person to engage in prostitution;  
(2) Sheltering or introducing two or more persons to engage in prostitution;  
(3) Sheltering or introducing minors, pregnant women, mentally disabled persons, or persons suffering from severe venereal diseases to engage in prostitution;  
(4) Having previously been subject to an administrative penalty for inducing, sheltering, or introducing prostitution within one year, and again committing an act of sheltering or introducing prostitution;  
(5) Illegally obtaining profits of RMB 10,000 or more.  
Where a person uses information networks to publish illegal information soliciting prostitution, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished as the crime of illegally using information networks in accordance with Article 287-1 of the Criminal Law. Where the act also constitutes the crime of introducing prostitution, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions providing for a heavier punishment.  
Whether the inducement, sheltering, or introduction of others to engage in prostitution is for profit shall not affect the establishment of the crime.  
Where a person induces a female minor under the age of 14 to engage in prostitution, the person shall be convicted and punished as the crime of inducing a female minor to engage in prostitution in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 359 of the Criminal Law.  
Where among the persons induced to engage in prostitution, there are both female minors under the age of 14 and other persons, the person shall be convicted separately for the crime of inducing a female minor to engage in prostitution and the crime of inducing prostitution, and cumulative punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百五十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-359)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）引诱五人以上或者引诱、容留、介绍十人以上卖淫的；  
（二）引诱三人以上的未成年人、孕妇、智障人员、患有严重性病的人卖淫，或者引诱、容留、介绍五人以上该类人员卖淫的；  
（三）非法获利人民币五万元以上的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 9** : Where inducing, sheltering, or introducing another person to engage in prostitution falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be identified as "serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 359 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Inducing five or more persons, or inducing, sheltering, or introducing ten or more persons to engage in prostitution;  
(2) Inducing three or more minors, pregnant women, mentally disabled persons, or persons suffering from severe venereal diseases to engage in prostitution, or inducing, sheltering, or introducing five or more such persons to engage in prostitution;  
(3) Illegally obtaining profits of RMB 50,000 or more;  
(4) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍他人卖淫的次数，作为酌定情节在量刑时考虑。

**Article 10** : The number of times a person organizes, forces, induces, shelters, or introduces others to engage in prostitution shall be considered as a discretionary circumstance at sentencing.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百六十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-360)规定的“明知”：  
（一）有证据证明曾到医院或者其他医疗机构就医或者检查，被诊断为患有严重性病的；  
（二）根据本人的知识和经验，能够知道自己患有严重性病的；  
（三）通过其他方法能够证明行为人是“明知”的。  
传播性病行为是否实际造成他人患上严重性病的后果，不影响本罪的成立。  
[刑法第三百六十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-360)规定所称的“严重性病”，包括梅毒、淋病等。其他性病是否认定为“严重性病”，应当根据《中华人民共和国传染病防治法》《性病防治管理办法》的规定，在国家卫生与计划生育委员会规定实行性病监测的性病范围内，依照其危害、特点与梅毒、淋病相当的原则，从严掌握。

**Article 11** : Any of the following circumstances shall be identified as "knowing" as provided in Article 360 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) There is evidence proving that the person has previously visited a hospital or other medical institution for medical treatment or examination and was diagnosed as suffering from a severe venereal disease;  
(2) Based on the person's own knowledge and experience, the person is able to know that he or she suffers from a severe venereal disease;  
(3) Other methods can prove that the actor "knows".  
Whether the act of transmitting venereal diseases actually causes another person to suffer from a severe venereal disease shall not affect the establishment of this crime.  
The term "severe venereal diseases" as provided in Article 360 of the Criminal Law includes syphilis, gonorrhea, and the like. Whether other venereal diseases are identified as "severe venereal diseases" shall be strictly determined in accordance with the provisions of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Prevention and Treatment of Infectious Diseases and the Measures for the Administration of Prevention and Treatment of Venereal Diseases, within the scope of venereal diseases subject to surveillance as specified by the National Health and Family Planning Commission, following the principle that their harm and characteristics are equivalent to those of syphilis and gonorrhea.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　明知自己患有艾滋病或者感染艾滋病病毒而卖淫、嫖娼的，依照[刑法第三百六十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-360)的规定，以传播性病罪定罪，从重处罚。  
具有下列情形之一，致使他人感染艾滋病病毒的，认定为[刑法第九十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-95)第三项“其他对于人身健康有重大伤害”所指的“重伤”，依照[刑法第二百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-234)第二款的规定，以故意伤害罪定罪处罚：  
（一）明知自己感染艾滋病病毒而卖淫、嫖娼的；  
（二）明知自己感染艾滋病病毒，故意不采取防范措施而与他人发生性关系的。

**Article 12** : Where a person, knowing that he or she suffers from AIDS or is infected with HIV, engages in prostitution or patronizes prostitution, the person shall be convicted as the crime of transmitting venereal diseases in accordance with Article 360 of the Criminal Law, and a severe punishment shall be imposed.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, causing another person to be infected with HIV, it shall be identified as "serious harm to personal health" as referred to in subparagraph (3) of Article 95 of the Criminal Law, i.e., "serious injury", and the person shall be convicted and punished as the crime of intentional injury in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 234 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Knowing that one is infected with HIV and engaging in prostitution or patronizing prostitution;  
(2) Knowing that one is infected with HIV and intentionally failing to take preventive measures while having sexual relations with another person.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　犯组织、强迫、引诱、容留、介绍卖淫罪的，应当依法判处犯罪所得二倍以上的罚金。共同犯罪的，对各共同犯罪人合计判处的罚金应当在犯罪所得的二倍以上。  
对犯组织、强迫卖淫罪被判处无期徒刑的，应当并处没收财产。

**Article 13** : Where a person commits the crime of organizing, forcing, inducing, sheltering, or introducing prostitution, a fine of not less than twice the illegal gains shall be imposed according to law. In cases of joint crime, the total fine imposed on each joint offender shall be not less than twice the illegal gains.  
Where a person is sentenced to life imprisonment for committing the crime of organizing or forcing prostitution, confiscation of property shall also be imposed.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　根据[刑法第三百六十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-362)、第三百一十条的规定，旅馆业、饮食服务业、文化娱乐业、出租汽车业等单位的人员，在公安机关查处卖淫、嫖娼活动时，为违法犯罪分子通风报信，情节严重的，以包庇罪定罪处罚。事前与犯罪分子通谋的，以共同犯罪论处。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百六十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-362)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）向组织、强迫卖淫犯罪集团通风报信的；  
（二）二年内通风报信三次以上的；  
（三）一年内因通风报信被行政处罚，又实施通风报信行为的；  
（四）致使犯罪集团的首要分子或者其他共同犯罪的主犯未能及时归案的；  
（五）造成卖淫嫖娼人员逃跑，致使公安机关查处犯罪行为因取证困难而撤销刑事案件的；  
（六）非法获利人民币一万元以上的；  
（七）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 14** : According to the provisions of Articles 362 and 310 of the Criminal Law, where personnel of units such as the hotel industry, catering service industry, cultural and entertainment industry, and taxi industry, tip off lawbreakers or criminals during the investigation of prostitution and patronizing prostitution by public security organs, and the circumstances are serious, the personnel shall be convicted and punished as the crime of harboring. Where they conspire with the criminals in advance, they shall be punished as joint crime.  
Any of the following circumstances shall be identified as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 362 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Tipping off a criminal group organizing or forcing prostitution;  
(2) Tipping off three or more times within two years;  
(3) Having been subject to an administrative penalty for tipping off within one year, and again committing the act of tipping off;  
(4) Causing the principal offender of the criminal group or other principal offenders of the joint crime to fail to be brought to justice in a timely manner;  
(5) Causing persons involved in prostitution or patronizing prostitution to escape, and resulting in the public security organ withdrawing the criminal case due to difficulty in obtaining evidence for the investigation of the criminal act;  
(6) Illegally obtaining profits of RMB 10,000 or more;  
(7) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　本解释自2017年7月25日起施行。

**Article 15** : This Interpretation shall come into force as of July 25, 2017.
