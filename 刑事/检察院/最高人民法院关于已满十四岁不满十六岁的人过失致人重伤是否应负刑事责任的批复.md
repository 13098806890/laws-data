### 最高人民法院关于已满十四岁不满十六岁的人过失致人重伤是否应负刑事责任的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于已满十四岁不满十六岁的人过失致人重伤  
  
是否应负刑事责任的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1990〕5号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于十五岁的未成年人，因玩弄汽枪过失致人重伤（眼瞎），是否应负刑事责任的请示》收悉。  
  
经研院，我们认为，[刑法第十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-14)第二款规定：“已满十四岁不满十六岁的人，犯杀人、重伤、抢劫、放火、惯窃罪或者其他严重破坏社会秩序罪，应当负刑事责任。”这里说的“重伤”，是指故意伤害他人身体造成重伤，不包括过失致人重伤。十五岁的未成年人过失致人重伤的行为，不应当负刑事责任，但应责令他的家长或者监护人加以管教；在必要的时候，也可以由政府收容教养。涉及民事赔偿的问题，按有关民事法律规定处理。  
  
1990年6月4日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether a Person Who Has Reached the Age of 14 but Not the Age of 16 Shall Bear Criminal Liability for Negligently Causing Serious Injury to Another Person  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1990] No. 5  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
We have received your court's Request for Instructions on Whether a 15-Year-Old Minor, Who Negligently Causes Serious Injury (Blindness) to Another Person by Playing with an Air Gun, Shall Bear Criminal Liability.  
After study, we are of the opinion that Article 14, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law provides: "A person who has reached the age of 14 but not the age of 16, who commits the crimes of homicide, causing serious injury, robbery, arson, habitual theft or any other crime seriously undermining social order, shall bear criminal liability." The term "serious injury" as used herein refers to intentionally causing serious injury to another person's body, and does not include negligently causing serious injury to another person. The act of a 15-year-old minor negligently causing serious injury to another person shall not bear criminal liability, but the minor's parents or guardian shall be ordered to subject him to discipline; when necessary, the minor may also be taken over by the government for rehabilitation. Issues involving civil compensation shall be handled in accordance with relevant civil law provisions.  
June 4, 1990
