# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕19号  
**公布日期**：2011-06-20  
**生效日期**：2011-09-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2011年6月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1524次会议、2011年7月11日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第63次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年9月1日起施行。 2011年8月1日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理危害计算机信息系统安全刑事案件 应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2011〕19号 为依法惩治危害计算机信息系统安全的犯罪活动，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于维护互联网安全的决定》的规定，现就办理这类刑事案件应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  非法获取计算机信息系统数据或者非法控制计算机信息系统，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百八十五条第二款规定的“情节严重”： （一）获取支付结算、证券交易、期货交易等网络金融服务的身份认证信息十组以上的； （二）获取第（一）项以外的身份认证信息五百组以上的； （三）非法控制计算机信息系统二十台以上的； （四）违法所得五千元以上或者造成经济损失一万元以上的； （五）其他情节严重的情形。 实施前款规定行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百八十五条第二款规定的“情节特别严重”： （一）数量或者数额达到前款第（一）项至第（四）项规定标准五倍以上的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  非法获取计算机信息系统数据或者非法控制计算机信息系统，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)第二款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）获取支付结算、证券交易、期货交易等网络金融服务的身份认证信息十组以上的；  
（二）获取第（一）项以外的身份认证信息五百组以上的；  
（三）非法控制计算机信息系统二十台以上的；  
（四）违法所得五千元以上或者造成经济损失一万元以上的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)第二款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）数量或者数额达到前款第（一）项至第（四）项规定标准五倍以上的；  
（二）其他情节特别严重的情形。  
明知是他人非法控制的计算机信息系统，而对该计算机信息系统的控制权加以利用的，依照前两款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Illegally obtaining data from a computer information system or illegally controlling a computer information system shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 285(2) of the Criminal Law under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) obtaining ten or more sets of identity authentication information for network financial services such as payment settlement, securities trading, and futures trading;  
(2) obtaining 500 or more sets of identity authentication information other than those specified in item (1);  
(3) illegally controlling 20 or more computer information systems;  
(4) illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more, or causing economic losses of 10,000 yuan or more;  
(5) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph is committed under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 285(2) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the quantity or amount reaches five times the standards prescribed in items (1) to (4) of the preceding paragraph;  
(2) other circumstances of an especially serious nature.  
Whoever knowingly takes advantage of the control over a computer information system that is illegally controlled by another person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding two paragraphs.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  具有下列情形之一的程序、工具，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)第三款规定的“专门用于侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的程序、工具”：  
（一）具有避开或者突破计算机信息系统安全保护措施，未经授权或者超越授权获取计算机信息系统数据的功能的；  
（二）具有避开或者突破计算机信息系统安全保护措施，未经授权或者超越授权对计算机信息系统实施控制的功能的；  
（三）其他专门设计用于侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统、非法获取计算机信息系统数据的程序、工具。

**Article 2** Programs or tools under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "programs or tools specially used for intruding into or illegally controlling a computer information system" as provided for in Article 285(3) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) having the function of circumventing or breaking through the security protection measures of a computer information system to obtain data from the computer information system without authorization or beyond authorization;  
(2) having the function of circumventing or breaking through the security protection measures of a computer information system to control the computer information system without authorization or beyond authorization;  
(3) other programs or tools specially designed for intruding into or illegally controlling a computer information system, or illegally obtaining data from a computer information system.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  提供侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的程序、工具，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)第三款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）提供能够用于非法获取支付结算、证券交易、期货交易等网络金融服务身份认证信息的专门性程序、工具五人次以上的；  
（二）提供第（一）项以外的专门用于侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的程序、工具二十人次以上的；  
（三）明知他人实施非法获取支付结算、证券交易、期货交易等网络金融服务身份认证信息的违法犯罪行为而为其提供程序、工具五人次以上的；  
（四）明知他人实施第（三）项以外的侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的违法犯罪行为而为其提供程序、工具二十人次以上的；  
（五）违法所得五千元以上或者造成经济损失一万元以上的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为提供侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的程序、工具“情节特别严重”：  
（一）数量或者数额达到前款第（一）项至第（五）项规定标准五倍以上的；  
（二）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 3** Providing programs or tools for intruding into or illegally controlling a computer information system shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 285(3) of the Criminal Law under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) providing specialized programs or tools capable of illegally obtaining identity authentication information for network financial services such as payment settlement, securities trading, and futures trading to five person-times or more;  
(2) providing programs or tools other than those specified in item (1) that are specially used for intruding into or illegally controlling a computer information system to 20 person-times or more;  
(3) knowingly providing programs or tools to five person-times or more for another person's illegal or criminal act of illegally obtaining identity authentication information for network financial services such as payment settlement, securities trading, and futures trading;  
(4) knowingly providing programs or tools to 20 person-times or more for another person's illegal or criminal act of intruding into or illegally controlling a computer information system other than that specified in item (3);  
(5) illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more, or causing economic losses of 10,000 yuan or more;  
(6) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph is committed under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" for providing programs or tools for intruding into or illegally controlling a computer information system:  
(1) the quantity or amount reaches five times the standards prescribed in items (1) to (5) of the preceding paragraph;  
(2) other circumstances of an especially serious nature.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  破坏计算机信息系统功能、数据或者应用程序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)第一款和第二款规定的“后果严重”：  
（一）造成十台以上计算机信息系统的主要软件或者硬件不能正常运行的；  
（二）对二十台以上计算机信息系统中存储、处理或者传输的数据进行删除、修改、增加操作的；  
（三）违法所得五千元以上或者造成经济损失一万元以上的；  
（四）造成为一百台以上计算机信息系统提供域名解析、身份认证、计费等基础服务或者为一万以上用户提供服务的计算机信息系统不能正常运行累计一小时以上的；  
（五）造成其他严重后果的。  
实施前款规定行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为破坏计算机信息系统“后果特别严重”：  
（一）数量或者数额达到前款第（一）项至第（三）项规定标准五倍以上的；  
（二）造成为五百台以上计算机信息系统提供域名解析、身份认证、计费等基础服务或者为五万以上用户提供服务的计算机信息系统不能正常运行累计一小时以上的；  
（三）破坏国家机关或者金融、电信、交通、教育、医疗、能源等领域提供公共服务的计算机信息系统的功能、数据或者应用程序，致使生产、生活受到严重影响或者造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（四）造成其他特别严重后果的。

**Article 4** Destroying the functions, data, or application programs of a computer information system shall be deemed as "serious consequences" as provided for in Article 286(1) and (2) of the Criminal Law under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) causing the main software or hardware of ten or more computer information systems to be unable to operate normally;  
(2) deleting, modifying, or adding data stored, processed, or transmitted in 20 or more computer information systems;  
(3) illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more, or causing economic losses of 10,000 yuan or more;  
(4) causing a computer information system that provides basic services such as domain name resolution, identity authentication, and billing for 100 or more computer information systems, or provides services for 10,000 or more users, to be unable to operate normally for a cumulative period of one hour or more;  
(5) causing other serious consequences.  
Where the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph is committed under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "especially serious consequences" for destroying a computer information system:  
(1) the quantity or amount reaches five times the standards prescribed in items (1) to (3) of the preceding paragraph;  
(2) causing a computer information system that provides basic services such as domain name resolution, identity authentication, and billing for 500 or more computer information systems, or provides services for 50,000 or more users, to be unable to operate normally for a cumulative period of one hour or more;  
(3) destroying the functions, data, or application programs of a computer information system of a state organ or in fields such as finance, telecommunications, transportation, education, medical care, and energy that provides public services, thereby seriously affecting production or daily life, or causing a vile social impact;  
(4) causing other especially serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  具有下列情形之一的程序，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)第三款规定的“计算机病毒等破坏性程序”：  
（一）能够通过网络、存储介质、文件等媒介，将自身的部分、全部或者变种进行复制、传播，并破坏计算机系统功能、数据或者应用程序的；  
（二）能够在预先设定条件下自动触发，并破坏计算机系统功能、数据或者应用程序的；  
（三）其他专门设计用于破坏计算机系统功能、数据或者应用程序的程序。

**Article 5** Programs under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as "destructive programs such as computer viruses" as provided for in Article 286(3) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) being capable of copying or disseminating part, all, or variants of themselves through media such as networks, storage media, and files, and destroying the functions, data, or application programs of a computer system;  
(2) being capable of automatic activation under preset conditions and destroying the functions, data, or application programs of a computer system;  
(3) other programs specially designed for destroying the functions, data, or application programs of a computer system.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  故意制作、传播计算机病毒等破坏性程序，影响计算机系统正常运行，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)第三款规定的“后果严重”：  
（一）制作、提供、传输第五条第（一）项规定的程序，导致该程序通过网络、存储介质、文件等媒介传播的；  
（二）造成二十台以上计算机系统被植入第五条第（二）、（三）项规定的程序的；  
（三）提供计算机病毒等破坏性程序十人次以上的；  
（四）违法所得五千元以上或者造成经济损失一万元以上的；  
（五）造成其他严重后果的。  
实施前款规定行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为破坏计算机信息系统“后果特别严重”：  
（一）制作、提供、传输第五条第（一）项规定的程序，导致该程序通过网络、存储介质、文件等媒介传播，致使生产、生活受到严重影响或者造成恶劣社会影响的；  
（二）数量或者数额达到前款第（二）项至第（四）项规定标准五倍以上的；  
（三）造成其他特别严重后果的。

**Article 6** Intentionally creating or disseminating destructive programs such as computer viruses, thereby affecting the normal operation of a computer system, shall be deemed as "serious consequences" as provided for in Article 286(3) of the Criminal Law under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) creating, providing, or transmitting a program as prescribed in Article 5(1), resulting in the dissemination of such program through media such as networks, storage media, and files;  
(2) causing 20 or more computer systems to be implanted with programs as prescribed in Article 5(2) or (3);  
(3) providing destructive programs such as computer viruses to ten or more person-times;  
(4) illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more, or causing economic losses of 10,000 yuan or more;  
(5) causing other serious consequences.  
Where the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph is committed under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "especially serious consequences" for destroying a computer information system:  
(1) creating, providing, or transmitting a program as prescribed in Article 5(1), resulting in the dissemination of such program through media such as networks, storage media, and files, thereby seriously affecting production or daily life, or causing a vile social impact;  
(2) the quantity or amount reaches five times the standards prescribed in items (2) to (4) of the preceding paragraph;  
(3) causing other especially serious consequences.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  明知是非法获取计算机信息系统数据犯罪所获取的数据、非法控制计算机信息系统犯罪所获取的计算机信息系统控制权，而予以转移、收购、代为销售或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒，违法所得五千元以上的，应当依照[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)第一款的规定，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得罪定罪处罚。  
实施前款规定行为，违法所得五万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)第一款规定的“情节严重”。  
单位实施第一款规定行为的，定罪量刑标准依照第一款、第二款的规定执行。

**Article 7** Whoever knowingly transfers, purchases, sells on commission, or disguises or conceals by other means data obtained from the crime of illegally obtaining computer information system data or the control right over a computer information system obtained from the crime of illegally controlling a computer information system, with illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 312(1) of the Criminal Law for the crime of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime.  
Where the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph is committed with illegal gains of 50,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 312(1) of the Criminal Law.  
Where a unit commits the act prescribed in the first paragraph, the standards for conviction and sentencing shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the first and second paragraphs.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  以单位名义或者单位形式实施危害计算机信息系统安全犯罪，达到本解释规定的定罪量刑标准的，应当依照[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)、第二百八十六条的规定追究直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员的刑事责任。

**Article 8** Where a crime endangering the security of a computer information system is committed in the name of a unit or in the form of a unit, and the standards for conviction and sentencing prescribed in this Interpretation are met, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with the provisions of Articles 285 and 286 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  明知他人实施[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)、第二百八十六条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为共同犯罪，依照[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)、第二百八十六条的规定处罚：  
（一）为其提供用于破坏计算机信息系统功能、数据或者应用程序的程序、工具，违法所得五千元以上或者提供十人次以上的；  
（二）为其提供互联网接入、服务器托管、网络存储空间、通讯传输通道、费用结算、交易服务、广告服务、技术培训、技术支持等帮助，违法所得五千元以上的；  
（三）通过委托推广软件、投放广告等方式向其提供资金五千元以上的。  
实施前款规定行为，数量或者数额达到前款规定标准五倍以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)、第二百八十六条规定的“情节特别严重”或者“后果特别严重”。

**Article 9** Whoever knowingly commits an act provided for in Article 285 or 286 of the Criminal Law for another person under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as a joint crime and punished in accordance with the provisions of Articles 285 and 286 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) providing programs or tools for destroying the functions, data, or application programs of a computer information system, with illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more or providing them to ten or more person-times;  
(2) providing assistance such as Internet access, server hosting, network storage space, communication transmission channels, fee settlement, transaction services, advertising services, technical training, and technical support, with illegal gains of 5,000 yuan or more;  
(3) providing funds of 5,000 yuan or more through means such as commissioning software promotion or placing advertisements.  
Where the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph is committed and the quantity or amount reaches five times the standards prescribed in the preceding paragraph, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" or "especially serious consequences" as provided for in Articles 285 and 286 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  对于是否属于[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)、第二百八十六条规定的“国家事务、国防建设、尖端科学技术领域的计算机信息系统”、“专门用于侵入、非法控制计算机信息系统的程序、工具”、“计算机病毒等破坏性程序”难以确定的，应当委托省级以上负责计算机信息系统安全保护管理工作的部门检验。司法机关根据检验结论，并结合案件具体情况认定。

**Article 10** Where it is difficult to determine whether a computer information system falls under "a computer information system in the fields of state affairs, national defense construction, or advanced science and technology," a program or tool is "specially used for intruding into or illegally controlling a computer information system," or a program is a "destructive program such as a computer virus" as provided for in Articles 285 and 286 of the Criminal Law, the matter shall be referred for inspection to a department responsible for computer information system security protection management at the provincial level or above. The judicial organ shall make a determination based on the inspection conclusion and in light of the specific circumstances of the case.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  本解释所称“计算机信息系统”和“计算机系统”，是指具备自动处理数据功能的系统，包括计算机、网络设备、通信设备、自动化控制设备等。  
本解释所称“身份认证信息”，是指用于确认用户在计算机信息系统上操作权限的数据，包括账号、口令、密码、数字证书等。  
本解释所称“经济损失”，包括危害计算机信息系统犯罪行为给用户直接造成的经济损失，以及用户为恢复数据、功能而支出的必要费用。

**Article 11** For the purposes of this Interpretation, "computer information system" and "computer system" mean a system with the function of automatically processing data, including computers, network equipment, communication equipment, automatic control equipment, and the like.  
For the purposes of this Interpretation, "identity authentication information" means data used to confirm a user's right to operate in a computer information system, including accounts, passwords, cryptographic keys, digital certificates, and the like.  
For the purposes of this Interpretation, "economic losses" include the economic losses directly caused to the user by the criminal act endangering the computer information system, as well as the necessary expenses incurred by the user for restoring data or functions.
