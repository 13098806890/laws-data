# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于修改《关于办理妨害信用卡管理刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》的决定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2018〕19号  
**公布日期**：2018-07-30  
**生效日期**：2018-12-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于修改〈关于办理妨害信用卡管理刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释〉的决定》已于2018年7月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1745次会议、2018年10月19日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第七次会议通过，现予公布，自2018年12月1日起施行。 2018年11月28日 最高人民法院   最高人民检察院 关于修改《关于办理妨害信用卡管理刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释》的决定 法释〔2018〕19号 根据司法实践情况，现决定对《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理妨害信用卡管理刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2009〕19号，以下简称《解释》）作如下修改： 一、将《解释》原第六条修改为：“持卡人以非法占有为目的，超过规定限额或者规定期限透支，经发卡银行两次有效催收后超过三个月仍不归还的，应当认定为刑法第一百九十六条规定的‘恶意透支’。 “对于是否以非法占有为目的，应当综合持卡人信用记录、还款能力和意愿、申领和透支信用卡的状况、透支资金的用途、透支后的表现、未按规定还款的原因等情节作出判断。不得单纯依据持卡人未按规定还款的事实认定非法占有目的。 “具有以下情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百九十六条第二款规定的‘以非法占有为目的’，但有证据证明持卡人确实不具有非法占有目的的除外： “（一）明知没有还款能力而大量透支，无法归还的； “（二）使用虚假资信证明申领信用卡后透支，无法归还的； “（三）透支后通过逃匿、改变联系方式等手段，逃避银行催收的； “（四）抽逃、转移资金，隐匿财产，逃避还款的； “（五）使用透支的资金进行犯罪活动的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　复制他人信用卡、将他人信用卡信息资料写入磁条介质、芯片或者以其他方法伪造信用卡一张以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)第一款第四项规定的“伪造信用卡”，以伪造金融票证罪定罪处罚。  
伪造空白信用卡十张以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)第一款第四项规定的“伪造信用卡”，以伪造金融票证罪定罪处罚。  
伪造信用卡，有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）伪造信用卡五张以上不满二十五张的；  
（二）伪造的信用卡内存款余额、透支额度单独或者合计数额在二十万元以上不满一百万元的；  
（三）伪造空白信用卡五十张以上不满二百五十张的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
伪造信用卡，有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）伪造信用卡二十五张以上的；  
（二）伪造的信用卡内存款余额、透支额度单独或者合计数额在一百万元以上的；  
（三）伪造空白信用卡二百五十张以上的；  
（四）其他情节特别严重的情形。  
本条所称“信用卡内存款余额、透支额度”，以信用卡被伪造后发卡行记录的最高存款余额、可透支额度计算。

**Article 1** Where a person reproduces another person's credit card, writes another person's credit card information data into a magnetic stripe medium, chip, or forges a credit card by other means, involving one or more credit cards, it shall be determined as "forging a credit card" as stipulated in Item (4) of Paragraph 1 of Article 177 of the Criminal Law, and shall be convicted and punished for the crime of forging financial instruments.  
Where a person forges ten or more blank credit cards, it shall be determined as "forging a credit card" as stipulated in Item (4) of Paragraph 1 of Article 177 of the Criminal Law, and shall be convicted and punished for the crime of forging financial instruments.  
Where a person forges a credit card and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 177 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Forging five or more but less than twenty-five credit cards;  
(2) The deposit balance or overdraft limit in the forged credit card, individually or in aggregate, reaches RMB 200,000 or more but less than RMB 1,000,000;  
(3) Forging fifty or more but less than two hundred and fifty blank credit cards;  
(4) Other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a person forges a credit card and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "especially serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 177 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Forging twenty-five or more credit cards;  
(2) The deposit balance or overdraft limit in the forged credit card, individually or in aggregate, reaches RMB 1,000,000 or more;  
(3) Forging two hundred and fifty or more blank credit cards;  
(4) Other circumstances of an especially serious nature.  
The "deposit balance and overdraft limit in a credit card" referred to in this Article shall be calculated based on the highest deposit balance and maximum overdraft limit recorded by the issuing bank after the credit card is forged.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　明知是伪造的空白信用卡而持有、运输十张以上不满一百张的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款第一项规定的“数量较大”；非法持有他人信用卡五张以上不满五十张的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款第二项规定的“数量较大”。  
有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款规定的“数量巨大”：  
（一）明知是伪造的信用卡而持有、运输十张以上的；  
（二）明知是伪造的空白信用卡而持有、运输一百张以上的；  
（三）非法持有他人信用卡五十张以上的；  
（四）使用虚假的身份证明骗领信用卡十张以上的；  
（五）出售、购买、为他人提供伪造的信用卡或者以虚假的身份证明骗领的信用卡十张以上的。  
违背他人意愿，使用其居民身份证、军官证、士兵证、港澳居民往来内地通行证、台湾居民来往大陆通行证、护照等身份证明申领信用卡的，或者使用伪造、变造的身份证明申领信用卡的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款第三项规定的“使用虚假的身份证明骗领信用卡”。

**Article 2** Where a person, knowing that a blank credit card is forged, possesses or transports ten or more but less than one hundred such cards, it shall be determined as "a relatively large quantity" as stipulated in Item (1) of Paragraph 1 of Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law; where a person illegally possesses five or more but less than fifty credit cards of another person, it shall be determined as "a relatively large quantity" as stipulated in Item (2) of Paragraph 1 of Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "a huge quantity" as stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Possessing or transporting ten or more forged credit cards while knowing them to be forged;  
(2) Possessing or transporting one hundred or more blank credit cards while knowing them to be forged;  
(3) Illegally possessing fifty or more credit cards of another person;  
(4) Obtaining credit cards by fraud using false identity certificates, involving ten or more cards;  
(5) Selling, purchasing, or providing to another person forged credit cards or credit cards obtained by fraud with false identity certificates, involving ten or more cards.  
Where a person, contrary to the will of another person, uses that person's resident identity card, military officer's card, soldier's card, Hong Kong and Macao Residents Entry Permit, Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents, passport or other identity certificates to apply for a credit card, or uses forged or altered identity certificates to apply for a credit card, it shall be determined as "obtaining a credit card by fraud using false identity certificates" as stipulated in Item (3) of Paragraph 1 of Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　窃取、收买、非法提供他人信用卡信息资料，足以伪造可进行交易的信用卡，或者足以使他人以信用卡持卡人名义进行交易，涉及信用卡一张以上不满五张的，依照[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第二款的规定，以窃取、收买、非法提供信用卡信息罪定罪处罚；涉及信用卡五张以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款规定的“数量巨大”。

**Article 3** Where a person steals, purchases, or illegally provides another person's credit card information data, which is sufficient to forge a credit card capable of conducting transactions, or sufficient to enable another person to conduct transactions in the name of the credit card holder, involving one or more but less than five credit cards, the person shall, in accordance with Paragraph 2 of Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of stealing, purchasing, or illegally providing credit card information; where the crime involves five or more credit cards, it shall be determined as "a huge quantity" as stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　为信用卡申请人制作、提供虚假的财产状况、收入、职务等资信证明材料，涉及伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章，或者涉及伪造公司、企业、事业单位、人民团体印章，应当追究刑事责任的，依照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)的规定，分别以伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章罪和伪造公司、企业、事业单位、人民团体印章罪定罪处罚。  
承担资产评估、验资、验证、会计、审计、法律服务等职责的中介组织或其人员，为信用卡申请人提供虚假的财产状况、收入、职务等资信证明材料，应当追究刑事责任的，依照[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)的规定，分别以提供虚假证明文件罪和出具证明文件重大失实罪定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where a person produces or provides false financial status, income, position, or other credit certification materials for a credit card applicant, involving the forgery, alteration, or sale of official documents, certificates, or seals of state organs, or involving the forgery of seals of companies, enterprises, institutions, or people's organizations, and criminal liability shall be investigated, the person shall, in accordance with Article 280 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of forging, altering, or selling official documents, certificates, or seals of state organs, and for the crime of forging seals of companies, enterprises, institutions, or people's organizations, as the case may be.  
Where an intermediary organization or its personnel responsible for asset appraisal, capital verification, auditing, accounting, legal services, or other similar duties, provides false financial status, income, position, or other credit certification materials for a credit card applicant, and criminal liability shall be investigated, the person shall, in accordance with Article 229 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of providing false certification documents and for the crime of issuing grossly inaccurate certification documents, as the case may be.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　使用伪造的信用卡、以虚假的身份证明骗领的信用卡、作废的信用卡或者冒用他人信用卡，进行信用卡诈骗活动，数额在五千元以上不满五万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额较大”；数额在五万元以上不满五十万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额巨大”；数额在五十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额特别巨大”。  
[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)第一款第三项所称“冒用他人信用卡”，包括以下情形：  
（一）拾得他人信用卡并使用的；  
（二）骗取他人信用卡并使用的；  
（三）窃取、收买、骗取或者以其他非法方式获取他人信用卡信息资料，并通过互联网、通讯终端等使用的；  
（四）其他冒用他人信用卡的情形。

**Article 5** Where a person uses a forged credit card, a credit card obtained by fraud with false identity certificates, an invalid credit card, or another person's credit card without authorization, to engage in credit card fraud activities, if the amount is RMB 5,000 or more but less than RMB 50,000, it shall be determined as "a relatively large amount" as stipulated in Article 196 of the Criminal Law; if the amount is RMB 50,000 or more but less than RMB 500,000, it shall be determined as "a huge amount" as stipulated in Article 196 of the Criminal Law; if the amount is RMB 500,000 or more, it shall be determined as "an especially huge amount" as stipulated in Article 196 of the Criminal Law.  
The term "using another person's credit card without authorization" in Item (3) of Paragraph 1 of Article 196 of the Criminal Law includes the following circumstances:  
(1) Picking up another person's credit card and using it;  
(2) Obtaining another person's credit card by deception and using it;  
(3) Stealing, purchasing, obtaining by deception, or acquiring another person's credit card information data by other illegal means, and using it through the Internet, communication terminals, or other means;  
(4) Other circumstances of using another person's credit card without authorization.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　持卡人以非法占有为目的，超过规定限额或者规定期限透支，经发卡银行两次有效催收后超过三个月仍不归还的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“恶意透支”。  
对于是否以非法占有为目的，应当综合持卡人信用记录、还款能力和意愿、申领和透支信用卡的状况、透支资金的用途、透支后的表现、未按规定还款的原因等情节作出判断。不得单纯依据持卡人未按规定还款的事实认定非法占有目的。  
具有以下情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)第二款规定的“以非法占有为目的”，但有证据证明持卡人确实不具有非法占有目的的除外：  
（一）明知没有还款能力而大量透支，无法归还的；  
（二）使用虚假资信证明申领信用卡后透支，无法归还的；  
（三）透支后通过逃匿、改变联系方式等手段，逃避银行催收的；  
（四）抽逃、转移资金，隐匿财产，逃避还款的；  
（五）使用透支的资金进行犯罪活动的；  
（六）其他非法占有资金，拒不归还的情形。

**Article 6** Where a credit card holder, for the purpose of illegal possession, overdrafts beyond the prescribed limit or beyond the prescribed period, and fails to repay within more than three months after two valid reminders from the issuing bank, it shall be determined as "malicious overdraft" as stipulated in Article 196 of the Criminal Law.  
Whether there is a purpose of illegal possession shall be determined by comprehensively considering such circumstances as the credit card holder's credit record, ability and willingness to repay, the circumstances of applying for and overdrafting the credit card, the use of overdraft funds, the conduct after overdraft, and the reasons for failing to repay as prescribed. The purpose of illegal possession shall not be determined solely based on the fact that the card holder fails to repay as prescribed.  
Where a person falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "for the purpose of illegal possession" as stipulated in Paragraph 2 of Article 196 of the Criminal Law, unless there is evidence proving that the card holder indeed does not have the purpose of illegal possession:  
(1) Knowingly having no ability to repay, overdrafts a large amount and is unable to repay;  
(2) After applying for a credit card using false credit certification materials, overdrafts and is unable to repay;  
(3) After overdraft, evades the bank's reminders by absconding, changing contact information, or other means;  
(4) Withdraws or transfers funds, conceals assets, or evades repayment;  
(5) Uses the overdraft funds to engage in criminal activities;  
(6) Other circumstances of illegally possessing funds and refusing to repay.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　催收同时符合下列条件的，应当认定为[本解释第六条](#art-6)规定的“有效催收”：  
（一）在透支超过规定限额或者规定期限后进行；  
（二）催收应当采用能够确认持卡人收悉的方式，但持卡人故意逃避催收的除外；  
（三）两次催收至少间隔三十日；  
（四）符合催收的有关规定或者约定。  
对于是否属于有效催收，应当根据发卡银行提供的电话录音、信息送达记录、信函送达回执、电子邮件送达记录、持卡人或者其家属签字以及其他催收原始证据材料作出判断。  
发卡银行提供的相关证据材料，应当有银行工作人员签名和银行公章。

**Article 7** A reminder that simultaneously meets the following conditions shall be determined as a "valid reminder" as stipulated in Article 6 of this Interpretation:  
(1) It is made after the overdraft exceeds the prescribed limit or the prescribed period;  
(2) The reminder shall be made in a manner that can confirm receipt by the card holder, except where the card holder intentionally evades the reminder;  
(3) The interval between two reminders shall be at least thirty days;  
(4) It complies with the relevant provisions or agreements on reminders.  
Whether a reminder is valid shall be determined based on the telephone recordings, records of information delivery, receipts of letters, records of email delivery, signatures of the card holder or his/her family members, and other original evidence materials of reminders provided by the issuing bank.  
The relevant evidence materials provided by the issuing bank shall bear the signature of a bank staff member and the bank's official seal.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　恶意透支，数额在五万元以上不满五十万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额较大”；数额在五十万元以上不满五百万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额巨大”；数额在五百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额特别巨大”。

**Article 8** For a malicious overdraft, if the amount is RMB 50,000 or more but less than RMB 500,000, it shall be determined as "a relatively large amount" as stipulated in Article 196 of the Criminal Law; if the amount is RMB 500,000 or more but less than RMB 5,000,000, it shall be determined as "a huge amount" as stipulated in Article 196 of the Criminal Law; if the amount is RMB 5,000,000 or more, it shall be determined as "an especially huge amount" as stipulated in Article 196 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　恶意透支的数额，是指公安机关刑事立案时尚未归还的实际透支的本金数额，不包括利息、复利、滞纳金、手续费等发卡银行收取的费用。归还或者支付的数额，应当认定为归还实际透支的本金。  
检察机关在审查起诉、提起公诉时，应当根据发卡银行提供的交易明细、分类账单（透支账单、还款账单）等证据材料，结合犯罪嫌疑人、被告人及其辩护人所提辩解、辩护意见及相关证据材料，审查认定恶意透支的数额；恶意透支的数额难以确定的，应当依据司法会计、审计报告，结合其他证据材料审查认定。人民法院在审判过程中，应当在对上述证据材料查证属实的基础上，对恶意透支的数额作出认定。  
发卡银行提供的相关证据材料，应当有银行工作人员签名和银行公章。

**Article 9** The amount of a malicious overdraft refers to the actual principal amount overdrawn that has not been repaid at the time of criminal case filing by the public security organ, excluding interest, compound interest, late payment fees, handling fees, and other charges collected by the issuing bank. The amount repaid or paid shall be determined as repayment of the actual principal overdrawn.  
When a people's procuratorate examines a case for prosecution and initiates a public prosecution, it shall, based on the transaction details, classified statements (overdraft statements, repayment statements), and other evidence materials provided by the issuing bank, and in conjunction with the explanations, defense opinions, and relevant evidence materials submitted by the criminal suspect, the defendant, and their defenders, examine and determine the amount of the malicious overdraft; if the amount of the malicious overdraft is difficult to determine, it shall be examined and determined based on forensic accounting or audit reports, in conjunction with other evidence materials. In the course of adjudication, the People's Court shall, based on the verification of the aforesaid evidence materials, make a determination of the amount of the malicious overdraft.  
The relevant evidence materials provided by the issuing bank shall bear the signature of a bank staff member and the bank's official seal.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　恶意透支数额较大，在提起公诉前全部归还或者具有其他情节轻微情形的，可以不起诉；在一审判决前全部归还或者具有其他情节轻微情形的，可以免予刑事处罚。但是，曾因信用卡诈骗受过两次以上处罚的除外。

**Article 10** Where the amount of a malicious overdraft is relatively large, if the full amount is repaid before a public prosecution is initiated, or if there are other circumstances of minor seriousness, a decision not to prosecute may be made; if the full amount is repaid before a judgment at first instance is rendered, or if there are other circumstances of minor seriousness, exemption from criminal punishment may be granted. However, this shall not apply where the person has been punished twice or more for credit card fraud.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　发卡银行违规以信用卡透支形式变相发放贷款，持卡人未按规定归还的，不适用[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)“恶意透支”的规定。构成其他犯罪的，以其他犯罪论处。

**Article 11** Where an issuing bank, in violation of regulations, grants loans in disguised form through credit card overdrafts, and the card holder fails to repay as prescribed, the provisions on "malicious overdraft" in Article 196 of the Criminal Law shall not apply. If the conduct constitutes another crime, it shall be dealt with as that other crime.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　违反国家规定，使用销售点终端机具（POS机）等方法，以虚构交易、虚开价格、现金退货等方式向信用卡持卡人直接支付现金，情节严重的，应当依据[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。  
实施前款行为，数额在一百万元以上的，或者造成金融机构资金二十万元以上逾期未还的，或者造成金融机构经济损失十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节严重”；数额在五百万元以上的，或者造成金融机构资金一百万元以上逾期未还的，或者造成金融机构经济损失五十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节特别严重”。  
持卡人以非法占有为目的，采用上述方式恶意透支，应当追究刑事责任的，依照[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)的规定，以信用卡诈骗罪定罪处罚。

**Article 12** Where a person, in violation of state regulations, uses methods such as point-of-sale (POS) terminals to directly pay cash to a credit card holder by means of fictitious transactions, inflated prices, cash refunds, or the like, if the circumstances are serious, the person shall, pursuant to Article 225 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations.  
Where a person commits the conduct in the preceding paragraph, if the amount is RMB 1,000,000 or more, or if it causes funds of a financial institution amounting to RMB 200,000 or more to become overdue and unpaid, or if it causes economic losses to a financial institution of RMB 100,000 or more, it shall be determined as "serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 225 of the Criminal Law; if the amount is RMB 5,000,000 or more, or if it causes funds of a financial institution amounting to RMB 1,000,000 or more to become overdue and unpaid, or if it causes economic losses to a financial institution of RMB 500,000 or more, it shall be determined as "especially serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 225 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a credit card holder, for the purpose of illegal possession, maliciously overdrafts by the methods described above, and criminal liability shall be investigated, the person shall, in accordance with Article 196 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of credit card fraud.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　单位实施本解释规定的行为，适用本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准。

**Article 13** Where a unit commits an act stipulated in this Interpretation, the standards for conviction and sentencing of the corresponding natural person crime as provided in this Interpretation shall apply.
