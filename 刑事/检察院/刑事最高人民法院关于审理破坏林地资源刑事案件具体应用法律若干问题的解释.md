# 刑事最高人民法院关于审理破坏林地资源刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2005〕15号  
**公布日期**：2005-12-19  
**生效日期**：2005-12-30

> 《最高人民法院关于审理破坏林地资源刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2005年12月19日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1374次会议通过，现予公布，自2005年12月30日起施行。 2005年12月26日 最高人民法院 关于审理破坏林地资源刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2005〕15号 为依法惩治破坏林地资源犯罪活动，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》及全国人民代表大会常务委员会《关于〈中华人民共和国刑法〉第二百二十八条、第三百四十二条、第四百一十条的解释》的有关规定，现就人民法院审理这类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  违反土地管理法规，非法占用林地，改变被占用林地用途，在非法占用的林地上实施建窑、建坟、建房、挖沙、采石、采矿、取土、种植农作物、堆放或排泄废弃物等行为或者进行其他非林业生产、建设，造成林地的原有植被或林业种植条件严重毁坏或者严重污染，并具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第三百四十二条规定的犯罪行为，应当以非法占用农用地罪判处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金： （一）非法占用并毁坏防护林地、特种用途林地数量分别或者合计达到五亩以上； （二）非法占用并毁坏其他林地数量达到十亩以上； （三）非法占用并毁坏本条第（一）项、第（二）项规定的林地，数量分别达到相应规定的数量标准的百分之五十以上； （四）非法占用并毁坏本条第（一）项、第（二）项规定的林地，其中一项数量达到相应规定的数量标准的百分之五十以上，且两项数量合计达到该项规定的数量标准。 第二条  国家机关工作人员徇私舞弊，违反土地管理法规，滥用职权，非法批准征用、占用林地，具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第四百一十条规定的“情节严重”，应当以非法批准征用、占用土地罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役： （一）非法批准征用、占用防护林地、特种用途林地数量分别或者合计达到十亩以上； （二）非法批准征用、占用其他林地数量达到二十亩以上；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  违反土地管理法规，非法占用林地，改变被占用林地用途，在非法占用的林地上实施建窑、建坟、建房、挖沙、采石、采矿、取土、种植农作物、堆放或排泄废弃物等行为或者进行其他非林业生产、建设，造成林地的原有植被或林业种植条件严重毁坏或者严重污染，并具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第三百四十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-342)规定的犯罪行为，应当以非法占用农用地罪判处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）非法占用并毁坏防护林地、特种用途林地数量分别或者合计达到五亩以上；  
（二）非法占用并毁坏其他林地数量达到十亩以上；  
（三）非法占用并毁坏本条第（一）项、第（二）项规定的林地，数量分别达到相应规定的数量标准的百分之五十以上；  
（四）非法占用并毁坏本条第（一）项、第（二）项规定的林地，其中一项数量达到相应规定的数量标准的百分之五十以上，且两项数量合计达到该项规定的数量标准。

**Article 1** Anyone who, in violation of land administration laws and regulations, unlawfully occupies forestland, changes the purpose of the occupied forestland, and carries out acts such as constructing kilns, building graves, building houses, digging sand, quarrying stone, mining, extracting soil, planting crops, piling or discharging waste, or engages in other non-forestry production or construction on the unlawfully occupied forestland, thereby causing severe destruction of or serious pollution to the original vegetation or forestry planting conditions of the forestland, and falls under any of the following circumstances, shall constitute a criminal act as provided in Article 342 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention, and/or a fine, for the crime of unlawful occupation of agricultural land:  
(1) The area of protection forestland or special-purpose forestland unlawfully occupied and destroyed reaches five mu or more respectively or in total;  
(2) The area of other forestland unlawfully occupied and destroyed reaches ten mu or more;  
(3) The area of forestland specified in items (1) and (2) of this Article, which is unlawfully occupied and destroyed, reaches 50% or more of the respective quantity standards;  
(4) The forestland specified in items (1) and (2) of this Article is unlawfully occupied and destroyed, where the quantity of one item reaches 50% or more of the corresponding quantity standard, and the total quantity of both items reaches the quantity standard of that item.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  国家机关工作人员徇私舞弊，违反土地管理法规，滥用职权，非法批准征用、占用林地，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第四百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-410)规定的“情节严重”，应当以非法批准征用、占用土地罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役：  
（一）非法批准征用、占用防护林地、特种用途林地数量分别或者合计达到十亩以上；  
（二）非法批准征用、占用其他林地数量达到二十亩以上；  
（三）非法批准征用、占用林地造成直接经济损失数额达到三十万元以上，或者造成本条第（一）项规定的林地数量分别或者合计达到五亩以上或者本条第（二）项规定的林地数量达到十亩以上毁坏。

**Article 2** Where functionaries of State organs, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, violate land administration laws and regulations, abuse their powers, and unlawfully approve the requisition or occupation of forestland, and fall under any of the following circumstances, such circumstances shall be deemed as the 'circumstances being serious' as provided in Article 410 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention for the crime of unlawful approval of requisition or occupation of land:  
(1) The area of protection forestland or special-purpose forestland unlawfully approved for requisition or occupation reaches ten mu or more respectively or in total;  
(2) The area of other forestland unlawfully approved for requisition or occupation reaches twenty mu or more;  
(3) The amount of direct economic losses caused by the unlawful approval of requisition or occupation of forestland reaches 300,000 yuan or more, or causes destruction of the forestland specified in item (1) of this Article reaching an area of five mu or more respectively or in total, or of the forestland specified in item (2) of this Article reaching an area of ten mu or more.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第四百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-410)规定的“致使国家或者集体利益遭受特别重大损失”，应当以非法批准征用、占用土地罪判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：  
（一）非法批准征用、占用防护林地、特种用途林地数量分别或者合计达到二十亩以上；  
（二）非法批准征用、占用其他林地数量达到四十亩以上；  
（三）非法批准征用、占用林地造成直接经济损失数额达到六十万元以上，或者造成本条第（一）项规定的林地数量分别或者合计达到十亩以上或者本条第（二）项规定的林地数量达到二十亩以上毁坏。

**Article 3** Where a person commits an act as prescribed in Article 2 of this Interpretation, and falls under any of the following circumstances, such circumstances shall be deemed as the 'causing particularly heavy losses to the interests of the State or the collective' as provided in Article 410 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years for the crime of unlawful approval of requisition or occupation of land:  
(1) The area of protection forestland or special-purpose forestland unlawfully approved for requisition or occupation reaches twenty mu or more respectively or in total;  
(2) The area of other forestland unlawfully approved for requisition or occupation reaches forty mu or more;  
(3) The amount of direct economic losses caused by the unlawful approval of requisition or occupation of forestland reaches 600,000 yuan or more, or causes destruction of the forestland specified in item (1) of this Article reaching an area of ten mu or more respectively or in total, or of the forestland specified in item (2) of this Article reaching an area of twenty mu or more.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  国家机关工作人员徇私舞弊，违反土地管理法规，非法低价出让国有林地使用权，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第四百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-410)规定的“情节严重”，应当以非法低价出让国有土地使用权罪判处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役：  
（一）林地数量合计达到三十亩以上，并且出让价额低于国家规定的最低价额标准的百分之六十；  
（二）造成国有资产流失价额达到三十万元以上。

**Article 4** Where functionaries of State organs, engaging in malpractices for personal gain, violate land administration laws and regulations, and illegally transfer the right to use State-owned forestland at a low price, and fall under any of the following circumstances, such circumstances shall be deemed as the 'circumstances being serious' as provided in Article 410 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention for the crime of illegally transferring the right to use State-owned land at a low price:  
(1) The total area of forestland reaches thirty mu or more, and the transfer price is less than 60% of the minimum price standard prescribed by the State;  
(2) The amount of loss of State-owned assets caused reaches 300,000 yuan or more.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  实施[本解释第四条](#art-4)规定的行为，造成国有资产流失价额达到六十万元以上的，属于[刑法第四百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-410)规定的“致使国家和集体利益遭受特别重大损失”，应当以非法低价出让国有土地使用权罪判处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑。

**Article 5** Where a person commits an act as prescribed in Article 4 of this Interpretation, causing the amount of loss of State-owned assets to reach 600,000 yuan or more, such circumstances shall be deemed as the 'causing particularly heavy losses to the interests of the State and the collective' as provided in Article 410 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years for the crime of illegally transferring the right to use State-owned land at a low price.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  单位实施破坏林地资源犯罪的，依照本解释规定的相关定罪量刑标准执行。

**Article 6** Where a unit commits a crime of destroying forestland resources, the relevant standards for conviction and sentencing prescribed in this Interpretation shall be applied.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  多次实施本解释规定的行为依法应当追诉且未经处理的，应当按照累计的数量、数额处罚。

**Article 7** Where a person repeatedly commits acts prescribed in this Interpretation that shall be prosecuted according to law and have not been dealt with, the punishment shall be imposed based on the cumulative quantity or amount.
