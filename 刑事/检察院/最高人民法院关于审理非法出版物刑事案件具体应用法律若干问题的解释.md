# 最高人民法院关于审理非法出版物刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔1998〕30号  
**公布日期**：1998-12-11  
**生效日期**：1998-12-23

> （1998年12月11日最高人民法院审判委员会第1032次会议通过，自1998年12月23日起施行。） 为依法惩治非法出版物犯罪活动，根据刑法的有关规定，现对审理非法出版物刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 明知出版物中载有煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一或者煽动颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度的内容，而予以出版、印刷、复制、发行、传播的，依照刑法第一百零三条第二款或者第一百零五条第二款的规定，以煽动分裂国家罪或者煽动颠覆国家政权罪定罪处罚。 第二条 以营利为目的，实施刑法第二百一十七条所列侵犯著作权行为之一，个人违法所得数额在五万元以上，单位违法所得数额在二十万元以上的，属于“违法所得数额较大”；具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他严重情节”： （一）因侵犯著作权曾经两次以上被追究行政责任或者民事责任，两年内又实施刑法第二百一十七条所列侵犯著作权行为之一的； （二）个人非法经营数额在二十万元以上，单位非法经营数额在一百万元以上的； （三）造成其他严重后果的。 以营利为目的，实施刑法第二百一十七条所列侵犯著作权行为之一，个人违法所得数额在二十万元以上，单位违法所得数额在一百万元以上的，属于“违法所得数额巨大”；具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他特别严重情节”： （一）个人非法经营数额在一百万元以上，单位非法经营数额在五百万元以上的； （二）造成其他特别严重后果的。 第三条 刑法第二百一十七条第（一）项中规定的“复制发行”，是指行为人以营利为目的，未经著作权人许可而实施的复制、发行或者既复制又发行其文字作品、音乐、电影、电视、录像作品、计算机软件及其他作品的行为。 第四条 以营利为目的，实施刑法第二百一十八条规定的行为，个人违法所得数额在十万元以上，单位违法所得数额在五十万元以上的，依照刑法第二百一十八条的规定，以销售侵权复制品罪定罪处罚。 第五条 实施刑法第二百一十七条规定的侵犯著作权行为，又销售该侵权复制品，违法所得数额巨大的，只定侵犯著作权罪，不实行数罪并罚。 实施刑法第二百一十七条规定的侵犯著作权的犯罪行为，又明知是他人的侵权复制品而予以销售，构成犯罪的，应当实行数罪并罚。 第六条 在出版物中公然侮辱他人或者捏造事实诽谤他人，情节严重的，依照刑法第二百四十六条的规定，分别以侮辱罪或者诽谤罪定罪处罚。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 明知出版物中载有煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一或者煽动颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度的内容，而予以出版、印刷、复制、发行、传播的，依照[刑法第一百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-103)第二款或者第一百零五条第二款的规定，以煽动分裂国家罪或者煽动颠覆国家政权罪定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Any person who, knowing that a publication contains content inciting the splitting of the State, undermining national unity, or inciting the subversion of the State power or the overthrow of the socialist system, nevertheless publishes, prints, reproduces, distributes, or disseminates such publication, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of inciting the splitting of the State or the crime of inciting the subversion of the State power in accordance with the provisions of Article 103, Paragraph 2 or Article 105, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 以营利为目的，实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)所列侵犯著作权行为之一，个人违法所得数额在五万元以上，单位违法所得数额在二十万元以上的，属于“违法所得数额较大”；具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他严重情节”：  
（一）因侵犯著作权曾经两次以上被追究行政责任或者民事责任，两年内又实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)所列侵犯著作权行为之一的；  
（二）个人非法经营数额在二十万元以上，单位非法经营数额在一百万元以上的；  
（三）造成其他严重后果的。  
以营利为目的，实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)所列侵犯著作权行为之一，个人违法所得数额在二十万元以上，单位违法所得数额在一百万元以上的，属于“违法所得数额巨大”；具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他特别严重情节”：  
（一）个人非法经营数额在一百万元以上，单位非法经营数额在五百万元以上的；  
（二）造成其他特别严重后果的。

**Article 2** Where any person, for the purpose of profit, commits one of the acts of copyright infringement listed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law, and the amount of illegal gains obtained by an individual is over RMB 50,000 yuan or by a unit is over RMB 200,000 yuan, such act falls within the scope of 'the amount of illegal gains is relatively large'; where any of the following circumstances exists, such act falls within the scope of 'there are other serious circumstances':  
(1) The person has been subjected to administrative liability or civil liability twice or more for copyright infringement, and within two years commits again one of the acts of copyright infringement listed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law;  
(2) The amount of illegal business operations by an individual is over RMB 200,000 yuan or by a unit is over RMB 1,000,000 yuan;  
(3) Other serious consequences are caused.  
Where any person, for the purpose of profit, commits one of the acts of copyright infringement listed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law, and the amount of illegal gains obtained by an individual is over RMB 200,000 yuan or by a unit is over RMB 1,000,000 yuan, such act falls within the scope of 'the amount of illegal gains is huge'; where any of the following circumstances exists, such act falls within the scope of 'there are other especially serious circumstances':  
(1) The amount of illegal business operations by an individual is over RMB 1,000,000 yuan or by a unit is over RMB 5,000,000 yuan;  
(2) Other especially serious consequences are caused.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 [刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)第（一）项中规定的“复制发行”，是指行为人以营利为目的，未经著作权人许可而实施的复制、发行或者既复制又发行其文字作品、音乐、电影、电视、录像作品、计算机软件及其他作品的行为。

**Article 3** The term 'reproduce and distribute' as prescribed in Item (1) of Article 217 of the Criminal Law means the act of a person who, for the purpose of profit and without the permission of the copyright owner, reproduces, distributes, or both reproduces and distributes the copyright owner's literary works, musical works, cinematographic works, television works, video works, computer software, or other works.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 以营利为目的，实施[刑法第二百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-218)规定的行为，个人违法所得数额在十万元以上，单位违法所得数额在五十万元以上的，依照[刑法第二百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-218)的规定，以销售侵权复制品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Any person who, for the purpose of profit, commits the act prescribed in Article 218 of the Criminal Law, and the amount of illegal gains obtained by an individual is over RMB 100,000 yuan or by a unit is over RMB 500,000 yuan, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of selling infringing reproductions in accordance with the provisions of Article 218 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的侵犯著作权行为，又销售该侵权复制品，违法所得数额巨大的，只定侵犯著作权罪，不实行数罪并罚。  
实施[刑法第二百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-217)规定的侵犯著作权的犯罪行为，又明知是他人的侵权复制品而予以销售，构成犯罪的，应当实行数罪并罚。

**Article 5** Where a person commits the copyright infringement act prescribed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law and also sells such infringing reproductions, and the amount of illegal gains is huge, the person shall only be convicted of the crime of copyright infringement and shall not be subjected to combined punishment for multiple crimes.  
Where a person commits the criminal act of copyright infringement prescribed in Article 217 of the Criminal Law and, knowing that they are infringing reproductions of another person, sells them, which constitutes a crime, the person shall be subjected to combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 在出版物中公然侮辱他人或者捏造事实诽谤他人，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百四十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-246)的规定，分别以侮辱罪或者诽谤罪定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Any person who publicly insults another person or fabricates facts to defame another person in a publication, where the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of insult or the crime of defamation, respectively, in accordance with the provisions of Article 246 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 出版刊载歧视、侮辱少数民族内容的作品，情节恶劣，造成严重后果的，依照[刑法第二百五十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-250)的规定，以出版歧视、侮辱少数民族作品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Any person who publishes a work containing content that discriminates against or insults minority nationalities, where the circumstances are flagrant and cause serious consequences, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of publishing works that discriminate against or insult minority nationalities in accordance with the provisions of Article 250 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 以牟利为目的，实施[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，以制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚：  
（一）制作、复制、出版淫秽影碟、软件、录像带五十至一百张（盒）以上，淫秽音碟、录音带一百至二百张（盒）以上，淫秽扑克、书刊、画册一百至二百副（册）以上，淫秽照片、画片五百至一千张以上的；  
（二）贩卖淫秽影碟、软件、录像带一百至二百张（盒）以上，淫秽音碟、录音带二百至四百张（盒）以上，淫秽扑克、书刊、画册二百至四百副（册）以上，淫秽照片、画片一千至二千张以上的；  
（三）向他人传播淫秽物品达二百至五百人次以上，或者组织播放淫秽影、像达十至二十场次以上的；  
（四）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品，获利五千至一万元以上的。  
以牟利为目的，实施[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品牟利罪“情节严重”：  
（一）制作、复制、出版淫秽影碟、软件、录像带二百五十至五百张（盒）以上，淫秽音碟、录音带五百至一千张（盒）上，淫秽扑克、书刊、画册五百至一千副（册）以上，淫秽照片、画片二千五百至五千张以上的；  
（二）贩卖淫秽影碟、软件、录像带五百至一千张（盒）以上，淫秽音碟、录音带一千至二千张（盒）以上，淫秽扑克、书刊、画册一千至二千副（册）以上，淫秽照片、画片五千至一万张以上的；  
（三）向他人传播淫秽物品达一千至二千人次以上，或者组织播放淫秽影、像达五十至一百场次以上的；  
（四）制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品，获利三万至五万元以上的。  
以牟利为目的，实施[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款规定的行为，其数量（数额）达到前款规定的数量（数额）五倍以上的，应当认定为制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽物品牟利罪“情节特别严重”。

**Article 8** Any person who, for the purpose of seeking profits, commits an act prescribed in Article 363, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials for profit:  
(1) Producing, reproducing, or publishing fifty to one hundred or more pornographic video discs, software, or videotapes (boxes), one hundred to two hundred or more pornographic audio discs or audio tapes (boxes), one hundred to two hundred or more decks (volumes) of pornographic playing cards, books, or picture albums, or five hundred to one thousand or more pornographic photos or pictures;  
(2) Selling one hundred to two hundred or more pornographic video discs, software, or videotapes (boxes), two hundred to four hundred or more pornographic audio discs or audio tapes (boxes), two hundred to four hundred or more decks (volumes) of pornographic playing cards, books, or picture albums, or one thousand to two thousand or more pornographic photos or pictures;  
(3) Disseminating pornographic materials to two hundred to five hundred or more persons, or organizing the showing of pornographic films or videos for ten to twenty or more sessions;  
(4) Earning profits of five thousand to ten thousand yuan or more from producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials.  
Where any person, for the purpose of seeking profits, commits an act prescribed in Article 363, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, under any of the following circumstances, such act shall be deemed as having 'serious circumstances' for the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials for profit:  
(1) Producing, reproducing, or publishing two hundred fifty to five hundred or more pornographic video discs, software, or videotapes (boxes), five hundred to one thousand or more pornographic audio discs or audio tapes (boxes), five hundred to one thousand or more decks (volumes) of pornographic playing cards, books, or picture albums, or two thousand five hundred to five thousand or more pornographic photos or pictures;  
(2) Selling five hundred to one thousand or more pornographic video discs, software, or videotapes (boxes), one thousand to two thousand or more pornographic audio discs or audio tapes (boxes), one thousand to two thousand or more decks (volumes) of pornographic playing cards, books, or picture albums, or five thousand to ten thousand or more pornographic photos or pictures;  
(3) Disseminating pornographic materials to one thousand to two thousand or more persons, or organizing the showing of pornographic films or videos for fifty to one hundred or more sessions;  
(4) Earning profits of thirty thousand to fifty thousand yuan or more from producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials.  
Where any person, for the purpose of seeking profits, commits an act prescribed in Article 363, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, and the quantity (amount) reaches five times or more of the quantity (amount) specified in the preceding paragraph, such act shall be deemed as having 'especially serious circumstances' for the crime of producing, reproducing, publishing, selling, or disseminating pornographic materials for profit.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 为他人提供书号、刊号，出版淫秽书刊的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第二款的规定，以为他人提供书号出版淫秽书刊罪定罪处罚。  
为他人提供版号，出版淫秽音像制品的，依照前款规定定罪处罚。  
明知他人用于出版淫秽书刊而提供书号、刊号的，依照[刑法第三百六十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-363)第一款的规定，以出版淫秽物品牟利罪定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Any person who provides another person with a book number or periodical number for publishing pornographic books or periodicals shall be convicted and punished for the crime of providing a book number to another person for publishing pornographic books or periodicals in accordance with the provisions of Article 363, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law.  
Any person who provides another person with a plate number for publishing pornographic audio-visual products shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
Any person who, knowing that another person will use the book number or periodical number to publish pornographic books or periodicals, provides such number, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of publishing pornographic materials for profit in accordance with the provisions of Article 363, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 向他人传播淫秽的书刊、影片、音像、图片等出版物达三百至六百人次以上或者造成恶劣社会影响的，属于“情节严重”，依照[刑法第三百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-364)第一款的规定，以传播淫秽物品罪定罪处罚。  
组织播放淫秽的电影、录像等音像制品达十五至三十场次以上或者造成恶劣社会影响的，依照[刑法第三百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-364)第二款的规定，以组织播放淫秽音像制品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 10** Any person who disseminates pornographic publications such as books, periodicals, films, audio-visual materials, or pictures to three hundred to six hundred or more persons, or causes adverse social impact, such act falls within the scope of 'serious circumstances' and shall be convicted and punished for the crime of disseminating pornographic materials in accordance with the provisions of Article 364, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law.  
Any person who organizes the showing of pornographic films, videos, or other audio-visual products for fifteen to thirty or more sessions, or causes adverse social impact, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of organizing the showing of pornographic audio-visual products in accordance with the provisions of Article 364, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 违反国家规定，出版、印刷、复制、发行[本解释第一条](#art-1)至第十条规定以外的其他严重危害社会秩序和扰乱市场秩序的非法出版物，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（三）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。

**Article 11** Any person who, in violation of State regulations, publishes, prints, reproduces, or distributes illegal publications other than those specified in Articles 1 through 10 of this Interpretation that seriously endanger social order and disrupt market order, where the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with the provisions of Item (3) of Article 225 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 个人实施[本解释第十一条](#art-11)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节严重”：  
（一）经营数额在五万元至十万元以上的；  
（二）违法所得数额在二万元至三万元以上的；  
（三）经营报纸五千份或者期刊五千本或者图书二千册或者音像制品、电子出版物五百张（盒）以上的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节特别严重”：  
（一）经营数额在十五万元至三十万元以上的；  
（二）违法所得数额在五万元至十万元以上的；  
（三）经营报纸一万五千份或者期刊一万五千本或者图书五千册或者音像制品、电子出版物一千五百张（盒）以上的。

**Article 12** Where an individual commits the act prescribed in Article 11 of this Interpretation, and any of the following circumstances exists, such act falls within the scope of 'serious circumstances' for illegal business operations:  
(1) The amount of business operations is between RMB 50,000 yuan and RMB 100,000 yuan or more;  
(2) The amount of illegal gains is between RMB 20,000 yuan and RMB 30,000 yuan or more;  
(3) The business involves 5,000 or more copies of newspapers, 5,000 or more copies of periodicals, 2,000 or more copies of books, or 500 or more discs (boxes) of audio-visual products or electronic publications.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, such act falls within the scope of 'especially serious circumstances' for illegal business operations:  
(1) The amount of business operations is between RMB 150,000 yuan and RMB 300,000 yuan or more;  
(2) The amount of illegal gains is between RMB 50,000 yuan and RMB 100,000 yuan or more;  
(3) The business involves 15,000 or more copies of newspapers, 15,000 or more copies of periodicals, 5,000 or more copies of books, or 1,500 or more discs (boxes) of audio-visual products or electronic publications.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 单位实施[本解释第十一条](#art-11)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节严重”：  
（一）经营数额在十五万元至三十万元以上的；  
（二）违法所得数额在五万元至十万元以上的；  
（三）经营报纸一万五千份或者期刊一万五千本或者图书五千册或者音像制品、电子出版物一千五百张（盒）以上的。  
具有下列情形之一的，属于非法经营行为“情节特别严重”：  
（一）经营数额在五十万元至一百万元以上的；  
（二）违法所得数额在十五万元至三十万元以上的；  
（三）经营报纸五万份或者期刊五万本或者图书一万五千册或者音像制品、电子出版物五千张（盒）以上的。

**Article 13** Where a unit commits the act prescribed in Article 11 of this Interpretation, and any of the following circumstances exists, such act falls within the scope of 'serious circumstances' for illegal business operations:  
(1) The amount of business operations is between RMB 150,000 yuan and RMB 300,000 yuan or more;  
(2) The amount of illegal gains is between RMB 50,000 yuan and RMB 100,000 yuan or more;  
(3) The business involves 15,000 or more copies of newspapers, 15,000 or more copies of periodicals, 5,000 or more copies of books, or 1,500 or more discs (boxes) of audio-visual products or electronic publications.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, such act falls within the scope of 'especially serious circumstances' for illegal business operations:  
(1) The amount of business operations is between RMB 500,000 yuan and RMB 1,000,000 yuan or more;  
(2) The amount of illegal gains is between RMB 150,000 yuan and RMB 300,000 yuan or more;  
(3) The business involves 50,000 or more copies of newspapers, 50,000 or more copies of periodicals, 15,000 or more copies of books, or 5,000 or more discs (boxes) of audio-visual products or electronic publications.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条 实施[本解释第十一条](#art-11)规定的行为，经营数额、违法所得数额或者经营数量接近非法经营行为“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”的数额、数量起点标准，并具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为非法经营行为“情节严重”、“情节特别严重”：  
（一）两年内因出版、印刷、复制、发行非法出版物受过行政处罚两次以上的；  
（二）因出版、印刷、复制、发行非法出版物造成恶劣社会影响或者其他严重后果的。

**Article 14** Where a person commits the act prescribed in Article 11 of this Interpretation, and the amount of business operations, the amount of illegal gains, or the quantity of business operations is close to the threshold amount or quantity for 'serious circumstances' or 'especially serious circumstances' of illegal business operations, and any of the following circumstances exists, such act may be deemed as falling within the scope of 'serious circumstances' or 'especially serious circumstances' for illegal business operations:  
(1) The person has been subjected to administrative penalty twice or more within two years for publishing, printing, reproducing, or distributing illegal publications;  
(2) The person causes adverse social impact or other serious consequences due to publishing, printing, reproducing, or distributing illegal publications.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条 非法从事出版物的出版、印刷、复制、发行业务，严重扰乱市场秩序，情节特别严重，构成犯罪的，可以依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)第（三）项的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。

**Article 15** Any person who illegally engages in the business of publishing, printing, reproducing, or distributing publications, seriously disrupting market order, where the circumstances are especially serious and constitute a crime, may be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with the provisions of Item (3) of Article 225 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条 出版单位与他人事前通谋，向其出售、出租或者以其他形式转让该出版单位的名称、书号、刊号、版号，他人实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)、第四条、第八条、第九条、第十条、第十一条规定的行为，构成犯罪的，对该出版单位应当以共犯论处。

**Article 16** Where a publishing unit, in prior conspiracy with another person, sells, leases, or otherwise transfers the publishing unit's name, book number, periodical number, or plate number to such person, and such person commits the acts prescribed in Articles 2, 4, 8, 9, 10, or 11 of this Interpretation, which constitute a crime, the publishing unit shall be treated as an accomplice.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条 本解释所称“经营数额”，是指以非法出版物的定价数额乘以行为人经营的非法出版物数量所得的数额。  
本解释所称“违法所得数额”，是指获利数额。  
非法出版物没有定价或者以境外货币定价的，其单价数额应当按照行为人实际出售的价格认定。

**Article 17** The term 'amount of business operations' as used in this Interpretation means the amount obtained by multiplying the listed price of the illegal publications by the quantity of illegal publications operated by the actor.  
The term 'amount of illegal gains' as used in this Interpretation means the amount of profit obtained.  
Where an illegal publication has no listed price or is priced in foreign currency, the unit price shall be determined in accordance with the price at which the actor actually sells it.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以根据本地的情况和社会治安状况，在[本解释第八条](#art-8)、第十条、第十二条、第十三条规定的有关数额、数量标准的幅度内，确定本地执行的具体标准，并报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 18** The Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of local conditions and the public security situation, determine the specific standards for implementation within the range of the relevant amounts and quantities prescribed in Articles 8, 10, 12, and 13 of this Interpretation, and shall report such standards to the Supreme People's Court for the record.
