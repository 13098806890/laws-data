# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理破坏野生动物资源刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕12号  
**公布日期**：2021-12-13  
**生效日期**：2022-04-09

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理破坏野生动物资源刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2021年12月13日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1856次会议、2022年2月9日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第八十九次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年4月9日起施行。 2022年4月6日 最高人民法院　最高人民检察院 关于办理破坏野生动物资源刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2022〕12号 为依法惩治破坏野生动物资源犯罪，保护生态环境，维护生物多样性和生态平衡，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国野生动物保护法》等法律的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百五十一条第二款规定的走私国家禁止进出口的珍贵动物及其制品： （一）未经批准擅自进出口列入经国家濒危物种进出口管理机构公布的《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》附录一、附录二的野生动物及其制品； （二）未经批准擅自出口列入《国家重点保护野生动物名录》的野生动物及其制品。 第二条　走私国家禁止进出口的珍贵动物及其制品，价值二十万元以上不满二百万元的，应当依照刑法第一百五十一条第二款的规定，以走私珍贵动物、珍贵动物制品罪处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；价值二百万元以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处没收财产；价值二万元以上不满二十万元的，应当认定为“情节较轻”，处五年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。 实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚： （一）属于犯罪集团的首要分子的； （二）为逃避监管，使用特种交通工具实施的； （三）二年内曾因破坏野生动物资源受过行政处罚的。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的走私国家禁止进出口的珍贵动物及其制品：  
（一）未经批准擅自进出口列入经国家濒危物种进出口管理机构公布的《濒危野生动植物种国际贸易公约》附录一、附录二的野生动物及其制品；  
（二）未经批准擅自出口列入《国家重点保护野生动物名录》的野生动物及其制品。

**Article 1** : Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be determined as smuggling of precious animals and their products whose import or export is prohibited by the state as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) importing or exporting without approval wildlife and their products listed in Appendix I or Appendix II of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora published by the national endangered species import and export management authority;  
(2) exporting without approval wildlife and their products listed in the List of Wildlife under State Priority Protection.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　走私国家禁止进出口的珍贵动物及其制品，价值二十万元以上不满二百万元的，应当依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款的规定，以走私珍贵动物、珍贵动物制品罪处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；价值二百万元以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”，处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处没收财产；价值二万元以上不满二十万元的，应当认定为“情节较轻”，处五年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）属于犯罪集团的首要分子的；  
（二）为逃避监管，使用特种交通工具实施的；  
（三）二年内曾因破坏野生动物资源受过行政处罚的。  
实施第一款规定的行为，不具有第二款规定的情形，且未造成动物死亡或者动物、动物制品无法追回，行为人全部退赃退赔，确有悔罪表现的，按照下列规定处理：  
（一）珍贵动物及其制品价值二百万元以上的，可以处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；  
（二）珍贵动物及其制品价值二十万元以上不满二百万元的，可以认定为“情节较轻”，处五年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；  
（三）珍贵动物及其制品价值二万元以上不满二十万元的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 2** : Where the value of smuggled precious animals or their products whose import or export is prohibited by the state is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 2,000,000 yuan, the offender shall, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years and shall also be fined for the crime of smuggling precious animals or precious animal products; where the value is 2,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be determined as “especially serious circumstances,” and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and shall also be sentenced to confiscation of property; where the value is 20,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, it shall be determined as “minor circumstances,” and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years and shall also be fined.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph and has any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment shall be imposed:  
(1) being a principal of a criminal group;  
(2) using special means of transport to evade regulation;  
(3) having previously received an administrative penalty for damaging wildlife resources within two years.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the first paragraph and does not have the circumstances prescribed in the second paragraph, and no death of animals or irrecoverable loss of animals or animal products is caused, and the offender has made full restitution and compensation and truly shows repentance, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) where the value of precious animals or their products is 2,000,000 yuan or more, a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years and a fine may be imposed;  
(2) where the value of precious animals or their products is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 2,000,000 yuan, it may be determined as “minor circumstances,” and a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years and a fine may be imposed;  
(3) where the value of precious animals or their products is 20,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, it may be determined as minor criminal circumstances, and no prosecution shall be instituted or the offender shall be exempted from criminal punishment; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm done is not serious, the act shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　在内陆水域，违反保护水产资源法规，在禁渔区、禁渔期或者使用禁用的工具、方法捕捞水产品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-340)规定的“情节严重”，以非法捕捞水产品罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法捕捞水产品五百公斤以上或者价值一万元以上的；  
（二）非法捕捞有重要经济价值的水生动物苗种、怀卵亲体或者在水产种质资源保护区内捕捞水产品五十公斤以上或者价值一千元以上的；  
（三）在禁渔区使用电鱼、毒鱼、炸鱼等严重破坏渔业资源的禁用方法或者禁用工具捕捞的；  
（四）在禁渔期使用电鱼、毒鱼、炸鱼等严重破坏渔业资源的禁用方法或者禁用工具捕捞的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）暴力抗拒、阻碍国家机关工作人员依法履行职务，尚未构成妨害公务罪、袭警罪的；  
（二）二年内曾因破坏野生动物资源受过行政处罚的；  
（三）对水生生物资源或者水域生态造成严重损害的；  
（四）纠集多条船只非法捕捞的；  
（五）以非法捕捞为业的。  
实施第一款规定的行为，根据渔获物的数量、价值和捕捞方法、工具等，认为对水生生物资源危害明显较轻的，综合考虑行为人自愿接受行政处罚、积极修复生态环境等情节，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 3** : In inland waters, where a person, in violation of laws and regulations on the protection of aquatic resources, catches aquatic products in a no-fishing area, during a no-fishing season, or by using prohibited tools or methods, and has any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as “serious circumstances” as provided in Article 340 of the Criminal Law, and the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal catching of aquatic products:  
(1) illegally catching aquatic products of 500 kilograms or more, or with a value of 10,000 yuan or more;  
(2) illegally catching fry or spawn-bearing broodstock of aquatic animals with important economic value, or catching aquatic products in a aquatic germplasm resources conservation area of 50 kilograms or more, or with a value of 1,000 yuan or more;  
(3) using prohibited methods or tools such as electric fishing, poisoning, or explosives, which seriously damage fishery resources, in a no-fishing area;  
(4) using prohibited methods or tools such as electric fishing, poisoning, or explosives, which seriously damage fishery resources, during a no-fishing season;  
(5) other circumstances that are serious.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph and has any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment shall be imposed:  
(1) violently resisting or obstructing a state functionary in lawfully performing his duties, where the act does not yet constitute the crime of obstructing official duties or the crime of assaulting a police officer;  
(2) having previously received an administrative penalty for damaging wildlife resources within two years;  
(3) causing serious damage to aquatic living resources or the aquatic ecological environment;  
(4) gathering multiple vessels to engage in illegal catching;  
(5) making a living by illegal catching.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the first paragraph, and based on the quantity and value of the catch and the methods and tools used, the harm to aquatic living resources is obviously minor, and taking into account the circumstances such as the actor voluntarily accepting administrative penalties and actively restoring the ecological environment, the act may be determined as minor criminal circumstances, and no prosecution shall be instituted or the offender shall be exempted from criminal punishment; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm done is not serious, the act shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第一款规定的“国家重点保护的珍贵、濒危野生动物”包括：  
（一）列入《国家重点保护野生动物名录》的野生动物；  
（二）经国务院野生动物保护主管部门核准按照国家重点保护的野生动物管理的野生动物。

**Article 4** : The “precious and endangered wildlife under state priority protection” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law includes:  
(1) wildlife listed in the List of Wildlife under State Priority Protection;  
(2) wildlife that has been approved by the wildlife protection authority of the State Council to be managed as wildlife under state priority protection.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第一款规定的“收购”包括以营利、自用等为目的的购买行为；“运输”包括采用携带、邮寄、利用他人、使用交通工具等方法进行运送的行为；“出售”包括出卖和以营利为目的的加工利用行为。  
[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第三款规定的“收购”“运输”“出售”，是指以食用为目的，实施前款规定的相应行为。

**Article 5** : The term “purchasing” as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law includes the act of buying for the purpose of profit, self-use, or other purposes; the term “transporting” includes the act of carrying by means such as carrying, mailing, using another person, or using means of transport; the term “selling” includes the acts of selling and processing for use for the purpose of profit.  
The terms “purchasing,” “transporting,” and “selling” as provided in paragraph 3 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law refer to the corresponding acts prescribed in the preceding paragraph for the purpose of consumption.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　非法猎捕、杀害国家重点保护的珍贵、濒危野生动物，或者非法收购、运输、出售国家重点保护的珍贵、濒危野生动物及其制品，价值二万元以上不满二十万元的，应当依照[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第一款的规定，以危害珍贵、濒危野生动物罪处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金；价值二十万元以上不满二百万元的，应当认定为“情节严重”，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；价值二百万元以上的，应当认定为“情节特别严重”，处十年以上有期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）属于犯罪集团的首要分子的；  
（二）为逃避监管，使用特种交通工具实施的；  
（三）严重影响野生动物科研工作的；  
（四）二年内曾因破坏野生动物资源受过行政处罚的。  
实施第一款规定的行为，不具有第二款规定的情形，且未造成动物死亡或者动物、动物制品无法追回，行为人全部退赃退赔，确有悔罪表现的，按照下列规定处理：  
（一）珍贵、濒危野生动物及其制品价值二百万元以上的，可以认定为“情节严重”，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；  
（二）珍贵、濒危野生动物及其制品价值二十万元以上不满二百万元的，可以处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金；  
（三）珍贵、濒危野生动物及其制品价值二万元以上不满二十万元的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 6** : Where a person illegally hunts or kills precious or endangered wildlife under state priority protection, or illegally purchases, transports, or sells such precious or endangered wildlife or their products, and the value is 20,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, the offender shall, in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention and shall also be fined for the crime of endangering precious or endangered wildlife; where the value is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 2,000,000 yuan, it shall be determined as “serious circumstances,” and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years and shall also be fined; where the value is 2,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be determined as “especially serious circumstances,” and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years and shall also be fined or have property confiscated.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph and has any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment shall be imposed:  
(1) being a principal of a criminal group;  
(2) using special means of transport to evade regulation;  
(3) seriously affecting scientific research on wildlife;  
(4) having previously received an administrative penalty for damaging wildlife resources within two years.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the first paragraph and does not have the circumstances prescribed in the second paragraph, and no death of animals or irrecoverable loss of animals or animal products is caused, and the offender has made full restitution and compensation and truly shows repentance, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
(1) where the value of precious or endangered wildlife or their products is 2,000,000 yuan or more, it may be determined as “serious circumstances,” and a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years and a fine may be imposed;  
(2) where the value of precious or endangered wildlife or their products is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 2,000,000 yuan, a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention and a fine may be imposed;  
(3) where the value of precious or endangered wildlife or their products is 20,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, it may be determined as minor criminal circumstances, and no prosecution shall be instituted or the offender shall be exempted from criminal punishment; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm done is not serious, the act shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　违反狩猎法规，在禁猎区、禁猎期或者使用禁用的工具、方法进行狩猎，破坏野生动物资源，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第二款规定的“情节严重”，以非法狩猎罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法猎捕野生动物价值一万元以上的；  
（二）在禁猎区使用禁用的工具或者方法狩猎的；  
（三）在禁猎期使用禁用的工具或者方法狩猎的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）暴力抗拒、阻碍国家机关工作人员依法履行职务，尚未构成妨害公务罪、袭警罪的；  
（二）对野生动物资源或者栖息地生态造成严重损害的；  
（三）二年内曾因破坏野生动物资源受过行政处罚的。  
实施第一款规定的行为，根据猎获物的数量、价值和狩猎方法、工具等，认为对野生动物资源危害明显较轻的，综合考虑猎捕的动机、目的、行为人自愿接受行政处罚、积极修复生态环境等情节，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 7** : Where a person, in violation of hunting laws and regulations, hunts in a no-hunting area, during a no-hunting season, or by using prohibited tools or methods, thereby damaging wildlife resources, and has any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as “serious circumstances” as provided in paragraph 2 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law, and the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal hunting:  
(1) illegally hunting wildlife with a value of 10,000 yuan or more;  
(2) hunting in a no-hunting area using prohibited tools or methods;  
(3) hunting during a no-hunting season using prohibited tools or methods;  
(4) other circumstances that are serious.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph and has any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment shall be imposed:  
(1) violently resisting or obstructing a state functionary in lawfully performing his duties, where the act does not yet constitute the crime of obstructing official duties or the crime of assaulting a police officer;  
(2) causing serious damage to wildlife resources or the ecological environment of their habitats;  
(3) having previously received an administrative penalty for damaging wildlife resources within two years.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the first paragraph, and based on the quantity and value of the catch and the hunting methods and tools used, the harm to wildlife resources is obviously minor, and taking into account the circumstances such as the motive and purpose of the hunt, the actor voluntarily accepting administrative penalties, and actively restoring the ecological environment, the act may be determined as minor criminal circumstances, and no prosecution shall be instituted or the offender shall be exempted from criminal punishment; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm done is not serious, the act shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　违反野生动物保护管理法规，以食用为目的，非法猎捕、收购、运输、出售[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第一款规定以外的在野外环境自然生长繁殖的陆生野生动物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第三款规定的“情节严重”，以非法猎捕、收购、运输、出售陆生野生动物罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法猎捕、收购、运输、出售有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物或者地方重点保护陆生野生动物价值一万元以上的；  
（二）非法猎捕、收购、运输、出售第一项规定以外的其他陆生野生动物价值五万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，同时构成非法狩猎罪的，应当依照[刑法第三百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-341)第三款的规定，以非法猎捕陆生野生动物罪定罪处罚。

**Article 8** : Where a person, in violation of laws and regulations on the protection and management of wildlife, for the purpose of consumption, illegally hunts, purchases, transports, or sells terrestrial wildlife that naturally grows and breeds in the wild environment outside the scope of paragraph 1 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law, and has any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as “serious circumstances” as provided in paragraph 3 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law, and the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally hunting, purchasing, transporting, or selling terrestrial wildlife:  
(1) illegally hunting, purchasing, transporting, or selling terrestrial wildlife with important ecological, scientific, or social value, or locally key protected terrestrial wildlife, with a value of 10,000 yuan or more;  
(2) illegally hunting, purchasing, transporting, or selling other terrestrial wildlife beyond those specified in item (1) with a value of 50,000 yuan or more;  
(3) other circumstances that are serious.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph which also constitutes the crime of illegal hunting, the person shall, in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 341 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally hunting terrestrial wildlife.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　明知是非法捕捞犯罪所得的水产品、非法狩猎犯罪所得的猎获物而收购、贩卖或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒，符合[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得罪定罪处罚。

**Article 9** : Where a person, knowing that aquatic products are proceeds from illegal catching crimes or that hunted animals are proceeds from illegal hunting crimes, purchases, sells, or otherwise disguises or conceals them, and such act meets the provisions of Article 312 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of disguising or concealing the proceeds of crime.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　负有野生动物保护和进出口监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员，滥用职权或者玩忽职守，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，应当依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)的规定，以滥用职权罪或者玩忽职守罪追究刑事责任。  
负有查禁破坏野生动物资源犯罪活动职责的国家机关工作人员，向犯罪分子通风报信、提供便利，帮助犯罪分子逃避处罚的，应当依照[刑法第四百一十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-417)的规定，以帮助犯罪分子逃避处罚罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 10** : Where a state functionary with the responsibility for wildlife protection and import-export supervision and management abuses his authority or neglects his duties, causing major losses to public property or the interests of the state or the people, he shall, in accordance with Article 397 of the Criminal Law, be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of abuse of authority or the crime of dereliction of duty.  
Where a state functionary with the responsibility for investigating and prohibiting criminal activities that damage wildlife resources tips off or provides convenience to criminals, thus helping them evade punishment, he shall, in accordance with Article 417 of the Criminal Law, be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of helping criminals evade punishment.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对于“以食用为目的”，应当综合涉案动物及其制品的特征，被查获的地点，加工、包装情况，以及可以证明来源、用途的标识、证明等证据作出认定。  
实施本解释规定的相关行为，具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为“以食用为目的”：  
（一）将相关野生动物及其制品在餐饮单位、饮食摊点、超市等场所作为食品销售或者运往上述场所的；  
（二）通过包装、说明书、广告等介绍相关野生动物及其制品的食用价值或者方法的；  
（三）其他足以认定以食用为目的的情形。

**Article 11** : The determination of “for the purpose of consumption” shall be made by comprehensively considering the characteristics of the involved animals and their products, the place where they were seized, the processing and packaging conditions, as well as evidence such as labels and certificates that can prove their source and use.  
Where a person commits the relevant acts prescribed in this Interpretation and has any of the following circumstances, it may be determined as “for the purpose of consumption”:  
(1) selling the relevant wildlife or their products as food in catering units, food stalls, supermarkets, or other places, or transporting them to the aforesaid places;  
(2) introducing the edible value or methods of the relevant wildlife or their products through packaging, instructions, advertisements, or the like;  
(3) other circumstances sufficient to establish the purpose of consumption.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　二次以上实施本解释规定的行为构成犯罪，依法应当追诉的，或者二年内实施本解释规定的行为未经处理的，数量、数额累计计算。

**Article 12** : Where a person commits the acts prescribed in this Interpretation two or more times, and they constitute a crime and shall be prosecuted according to law, or where the acts prescribed in this Interpretation are committed within two years and have not been dealt with, the quantity or amount shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　实施本解释规定的相关行为，在认定是否构成犯罪以及裁量刑罚时，应当考虑涉案动物是否系人工繁育、物种的濒危程度、野外存活状况、人工繁育情况、是否列入人工繁育国家重点保护野生动物名录，行为手段、对野生动物资源的损害程度，以及对野生动物及其制品的认知程度等情节，综合评估社会危害性，准确认定是否构成犯罪，妥当裁量刑罚，确保罪责刑相适应；根据本解释的规定定罪量刑明显过重的，可以根据案件的事实、情节和社会危害程度，依法作出妥当处理。  
涉案动物系人工繁育，具有下列情形之一的，对所涉案件一般不作为犯罪处理；需要追究刑事责任的，应当依法从宽处理：  
（一）列入人工繁育国家重点保护野生动物名录的；  
（二）人工繁育技术成熟、已成规模，作为宠物买卖、运输的。

**Article 13** : When determining whether the relevant acts prescribed in this Interpretation constitute a crime and when imposing a penalty, the following circumstances shall be considered: whether the involved animals are artificially bred, the degree of endangerment of the species, the survival status in the wild, the status of artificial breeding, whether they are included in the list of artificially bred wildlife under state priority protection, the means of the act, the degree of damage to wildlife resources, and the actor's awareness of wildlife and their products. The social harmfulness shall be comprehensively assessed, the determination of whether a crime is constituted shall be accurate, the penalty shall be appropriately imposed, ensuring that the crime, liability, and punishment are in proportion; where the conviction and sentencing under this Interpretation are obviously too heavy, a proper handling may be carried out according to law based on the facts, circumstances, and degree of social harmfulness of the case.  
Where the involved animals are artificially bred and have any of the following circumstances, the case shall generally not be treated as a crime; where criminal liability needs to be investigated, a lenient handling shall be carried out according to law:  
(1) being included in the list of artificially bred wildlife under state priority protection;  
(2) where the artificial breeding technology is mature and has become large-scale, and the animals are bought, sold, or transported as pets.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对于实施本解释规定的相关行为被不起诉或者免予刑事处罚的行为人，依法应当给予行政处罚、政务处分或者其他处分的，依法移送有关主管机关处理。

**Article 14** : Where a person who commits the relevant acts prescribed in this Interpretation is not prosecuted or is exempted from criminal punishment, and an administrative penalty, a disciplinary sanction, or other sanction shall be imposed according to law, the person shall be transferred to the relevant competent authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对于涉案动物及其制品的价值，应当根据下列方法确定：  
（一）对于国家禁止进出口的珍贵动物及其制品、国家重点保护的珍贵、濒危野生动物及其制品的价值，根据国务院野生动物保护主管部门制定的评估标准和方法核算；  
（二）对于有重要生态、科学、社会价值的陆生野生动物、地方重点保护野生动物、其他野生动物及其制品的价值，根据销赃数额认定；无销赃数额、销赃数额难以查证或者根据销赃数额认定明显偏低的，根据市场价格核算，必要时，也可以参照相关评估标准和方法核算。

**Article 15** : The value of the involved animals and their products shall be determined according to the following methods:  
(1) For the value of precious animals or their products whose import or export is prohibited by the state, and the value of precious or endangered wildlife under state priority protection or their products, it shall be calculated based on the evaluation standards and methods formulated by the wildlife protection authority of the State Council;  
(2) For the value of terrestrial wildlife with important ecological, scientific, or social value, locally key protected wildlife, other wildlife, or their products, it shall be determined based on the proceeds from the sale of stolen goods; where there are no proceeds from the sale of stolen goods, the proceeds are difficult to verify, or the determination based on the proceeds is obviously too low, it shall be calculated based on the market price, and where necessary, the relevant evaluation standards and methods may also be referenced.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　根据[本解释第十五条](#art-15)规定难以确定涉案动物及其制品价值的，依据司法鉴定机构出具的鉴定意见，或者下列机构出具的报告，结合其他证据作出认定：  
（一）价格认证机构出具的报告；  
（二）国务院野生动物保护主管部门、国家濒危物种进出口管理机构或者海关总署等指定的机构出具的报告；  
（三）地、市级以上人民政府野生动物保护主管部门、国家濒危物种进出口管理机构的派出机构或者直属海关等出具的报告。

**Article 16** : Where the value of the involved animals or their products is difficult to determine under Article 15 of this Interpretation, the determination shall be made based on the expert opinion issued by a judicial appraisal institution, or the reports issued by the following institutions, and in combination with other evidence:  
(1) reports issued by a price certification institution;  
(2) reports issued by institutions designated by the wildlife protection authority of the State Council, the national endangered species import and export management authority, or the General Administration of Customs;  
(3) reports issued by the wildlife protection authorities of the people's governments at or above the prefecture or city level, the dispatched agencies of the national endangered species import and export management authority, or the directly subordinate customs offices.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　对于涉案动物的种属类别、是否系人工繁育，非法捕捞、狩猎的工具、方法，以及对野生动物资源的损害程度等专门性问题，可以由野生动物保护主管部门、侦查机关依据现场勘验、检查笔录等出具认定意见；难以确定的，依据司法鉴定机构出具的鉴定意见、[本解释第十六条](#art-16)所列机构出具的报告，被告人及其辩护人提供的证据材料，结合其他证据材料综合审查，依法作出认定。

**Article 17** : For specialized issues such as the species or category of the involved animals, whether they are artificially bred, the tools and methods used for illegal catching or hunting, and the degree of damage to wildlife resources, the competent wildlife protection authority or the investigation authority may issue an opinion based on records of on-site inspection or examination; where it is difficult to determine, the determination shall be made based on the expert opinion issued by a judicial appraisal institution, the reports issued by the institutions listed in Article 16 of this Interpretation, the evidence materials provided by the defendant and his defender, and in combination with a comprehensive review of other evidence materials, and in accordance with law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　餐饮公司、渔业公司等单位实施破坏野生动物资源犯罪的，依照本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。

**Article 18** : Where a unit such as a catering company or a fishery company commits a crime of damaging wildlife resources, the directly responsible supervisor and other directly responsible persons shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards for natural persons committing the corresponding crimes under this Interpretation, and a fine shall be imposed on the unit.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　在海洋水域，非法捕捞水产品，非法采捕珊瑚、砗磲或者其他珍贵、濒危水生野生动物，或者非法收购、运输、出售珊瑚、砗磲或者其他珍贵、濒危水生野生动物及其制品的，定罪量刑标准适用《最高人民法院关于审理发生在我国管辖海域相关案件若干问题的规定（二）》（法释〔2016〕17号）的相关规定。

**Article 19** : For illegal catching of aquatic products, illegal harvesting of coral, giant clams, or other precious or endangered aquatic wildlife, or illegal purchasing, transporting, or selling of coral, giant clams, or other precious or endangered aquatic wildlife or their products in marine waters, the conviction and sentencing standards shall apply the relevant provisions of the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Trial of Cases Related to the Sea Areas under the Jurisdiction of China (II) (Fa Shi [2016] No. 17).

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　本解释自2022年4月9日起施行。本解释公布施行后，《最高人民法院关于审理破坏野生动物资源刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2000〕37号）同时废止；之前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 20** : This Interpretation shall come into force on April 9, 2022. After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Damaging Wildlife Resources (Fa Shi [2000] No. 37) shall be abolished simultaneously; where previously issued judicial interpretations are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
