### 最高人民法院关于原告向某人民法院起诉后撤诉又向另一个人民法院起诉该法院是否受理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于原告向某人民法院起诉后撤诉  
  
又向另一个人民法院起诉该法院是否受理的批复  
  
法（经）复〔 1985〕 58号  
  
辽宁省高级人民法院：  
  
你院辽法（经）请〔 1985〕 6号请示报告收悉。关于沈阳市化工设备厂为与乌鲁木齐市化工厂加工承揽合同纠纷一案，向乌鲁木齐市中级人民法院起诉后撤诉，又以同一诉讼请求和同一被告向沈阳市中级人民法院起诉，沈阳市中级人民法院是否应当受理的问题，经研究答复如下：     （一）乌鲁木齐市中级人民法院作出的准予撤诉的裁定，根据民事诉讼法（试行）第一百二十三条的规定，是发生法律效力的裁定；（二）根据民事诉讼法（试行）第八十四条第三项的规定，对裁定已经发生法律效力的案件，当事人不得再行起诉。因此，沈阳市中级人民法院对沈阳市化工设备厂的起诉应不予受理。沈阳市化工设备厂可向乌鲁木齐市中级人民法院申诉。  
  
一九八五年十二月十四日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
  
Reply on Whether a People's Court Shall Accept a Case Where a Plaintiff Withdraws Prosecution from One People's Court and Then Files Prosecution with Another People's Court  
  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1985] No. 58  
  
Higher People's Court of Liaoning Province:  
  
We have received your request report Liao Fa (Jing) Qing [1985] No. 6. With respect to the issue of whether, after Shenyang Chemical Equipment Factory withdrew its prosecution from the Intermediate People's Court of Urumqi City for the dispute over a processing and ordering contract case with Urumqi Chemical Plant and then filed prosecution with the Intermediate People's Court of Shenyang City on the same claim and against the same defendant, the Intermediate People's Court of Shenyang City shall accept the case, upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
(1) The ruling by the Intermediate People's Court of Urumqi City permitting the withdrawal of prosecution is a legally effective ruling in accordance with Article 123 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation);  
  
(2) In accordance with Article 84, Item 3 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), a party shall not file another prosecution for a case for which a legally effective ruling has been made. Therefore, the Intermediate People's Court of Shenyang City shall not accept the prosecution filed by Shenyang Chemical Equipment Factory. Shenyang Chemical Equipment Factory may file a petition with the Intermediate People's Court of Urumqi City.  
  
December 14, 1985
