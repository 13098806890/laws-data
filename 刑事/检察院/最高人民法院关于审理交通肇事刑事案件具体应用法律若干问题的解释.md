# 最高人民法院关于审理交通肇事刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕33号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-10  
**生效日期**：2000-11-21

> （2000年11月10日最高人民法院审判委员会第1136次会议通过，自2000年11月21日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕33号 为依法惩处交通肇事犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现将审理交通肇事刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 从事交通运输人员或者非交通运输人员，违反交通运输管理法规发生重大交通事故，在分清事故责任的基础上，对于构成犯罪的，依照刑法第一百三十三条的规定定罪处罚。 第二条 交通肇事具有下列情形之一的，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役： （一）死亡一人或者重伤三人以上，负事故全部或者主要责任的； （二）死亡三人以上，负事故同等责任的； （三）造成公共财产或者他人财产直接损失，负事故全部或者主要责任，无能力赔偿数额在三十万元以上的。 交通肇事致一人以上重伤，负事故全部或者主要责任，并具有下列情形之一的，以交通肇事罪定罪处罚： （一）酒后、吸食毒品后驾驶机动车辆的； （二）无驾驶资格驾驶机动车辆的； （三）明知是安全装置不全或者安全机件失灵的机动车辆而驾驶的； （四）明知是无牌证或者已报废的机动车辆而驾驶的； （五）严重超载驾驶的； （六）为逃避法律追究逃离事故现场的。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 从事交通运输人员或者非交通运输人员，违反交通运输管理法规发生重大交通事故，在分清事故责任的基础上，对于构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百三十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-133)的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Where a person engaged in transportation or a person not engaged in transportation violates the regulations on the administration of transportation and causes a major traffic accident, on the basis of ascertaining the liability for the accident, if the act constitutes a crime, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 133 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 交通肇事具有下列情形之一的，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役：  
（一）死亡一人或者重伤三人以上，负事故全部或者主要责任的；  
（二）死亡三人以上，负事故同等责任的；  
（三）造成公共财产或者他人财产直接损失，负事故全部或者主要责任，无能力赔偿数额在三十万元以上的。  
交通肇事致一人以上重伤，负事故全部或者主要责任，并具有下列情形之一的，以交通肇事罪定罪处罚：  
（一）酒后、吸食毒品后驾驶机动车辆的；  
（二）无驾驶资格驾驶机动车辆的；  
（三）明知是安全装置不全或者安全机件失灵的机动车辆而驾驶的；  
（四）明知是无牌证或者已报废的机动车辆而驾驶的；  
（五）严重超载驾驶的；  
（六）为逃避法律追究逃离事故现场的。

**Article 2** A person who causes a traffic accident under any of the following circumstances shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention:  
(1) causing the death of one person or seriously injuring three or more persons, and bearing full or primary liability for the accident;  
(2) causing the death of three or more persons, and bearing equal liability for the accident;  
(3) causing direct losses to public property or the property of another person, and bearing full or primary liability for the accident, and being unable to pay compensation in an amount of 300,000 yuan or more.  
A person who causes a traffic accident resulting in serious injury to one or more persons, and bearing full or primary liability for the accident, and under any of the following circumstances, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of causing a traffic accident:  
(1) driving a motor vehicle after consuming alcohol or drugs;  
(2) driving a motor vehicle without a driving license;  
(3) driving a motor vehicle while knowing that the safety devices are incomplete or the safety parts are malfunctioning;  
(4) driving a motor vehicle while knowing that it has no license plate or has been scrapped;  
(5) driving a motor vehicle that is severely overloaded;  
(6) fleeing the scene of the accident to evade legal investigation.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 “交通运输肇事后逃逸”，是指行为人具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第一款规定和第二款第（一）至（五）项规定的情形之一，在发生交通事故后，为逃避法律追究而逃跑的行为。

**Article 3** "Fleeing after causing a traffic accident" means the conduct of a person who, after causing a traffic accident, flees to evade legal investigation, provided that the person is under any of the circumstances specified in Paragraph 1 and Subparagraphs (1) to (5) of Paragraph 2 of Article 2 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 交通肇事具有下列情形之一的，属于“有其他特别恶劣情节”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：  
（一）死亡二人以上或者重伤五人以上，负事故全部或者主要责任的；  
（二）死亡六人以上，负事故同等责任的；  
（三）造成公共财产或者他人财产直接损失，负事故全部或者主要责任，无能力赔偿数额在六十万元以上的。

**Article 4** A person who causes a traffic accident under any of the following circumstances shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, which is deemed to be "other especially odious circumstances":  
(1) causing the death of two or more persons or seriously injuring five or more persons, and bearing full or primary liability for the accident;  
(2) causing the death of six or more persons, and bearing equal liability for the accident;  
(3) causing direct losses to public property or the property of another person, and bearing full or primary liability for the accident, and being unable to pay compensation in an amount of 600,000 yuan or more.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 “因逃逸致人死亡”，是指行为人在交通肇事后为逃避法律追究而逃跑，致使被害人因得不到救助而死亡的情形。  
交通肇事后，单位主管人员、机动车辆所有人、承包人或者乘车人指使肇事人逃逸，致使被害人因得不到救助而死亡的，以交通肇事罪的共犯论处。

**Article 5** "Causing death by fleeing" means the circumstance where, after causing a traffic accident, the actor flees to evade legal investigation, thereby causing the victim to die due to lack of assistance.  
After a traffic accident occurs, if the person in charge of the unit, the owner of the motor vehicle, the contractor, or the passenger directs the person who caused the accident to flee, resulting in the victim's death due to lack of assistance, the person shall be punished as an accomplice in the crime of causing a traffic accident.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 行为人在交通肇事后为逃避法律追究，将被害人带离事故现场后隐藏或者遗弃，致使被害人无法得到救助而死亡或者严重残疾的，应当分别依照[刑法第二百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-232)、第二百三十四条第二款的规定，以故意杀人罪或者故意伤害罪定罪处罚。

**Article 6** If, after causing a traffic accident, the actor, for the purpose of evading legal investigation, takes the victim away from the scene of the accident and then hides or abandons the victim, thereby causing the victim to die or suffer severe disability due to lack of assistance, the actor shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 232 and Paragraph 2 of Article 234 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China as the crime of intentional homicide or the crime of intentional injury respectively.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 单位主管人员、机动车辆所有人或者机动车辆承包人指使、强令他人违章驾驶造成重大交通事故，具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定情形之一的，以交通肇事罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** If the person in charge of the unit, the owner of the motor vehicle, or the contractor of the motor vehicle directs or compels another person to drive in violation of traffic rules, thereby causing a major traffic accident, and if any of the circumstances specified in Article 2 of this Interpretation exists, the person shall be convicted and punished as the crime of causing a traffic accident.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 在实行公共交通管理的范围内发生重大交通事故的，依照[刑法第一百三十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-133)和本解释的有关规定办理。  
在公共交通管理的范围外，驾驶机动车辆或者使用其他交通工具致人伤亡或者致使公共财产或者他人财产遭受重大损失，构成犯罪的，分别依照[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)、第一百三十五条、第二百三十三条等规定定罪处罚。

**Article 8** If a major traffic accident occurs within the scope of public traffic administration, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 133 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China and the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.  
If, outside the scope of public traffic administration, driving a motor vehicle or using other means of transportation causes injury or death to a person or causes major losses to public property or the property of another person, and if the act constitutes a crime, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 134, Article 135, Article 233, etc. of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China respectively.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以根据本地实际情况，在三十万元至六十万元、六十万元至一百万元的幅度内，确定本地区执行[本解释第二条](#art-2)第一款第（三）项、第四条第（三）项的起点数额标准，并报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 9** The Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the actual circumstances of their respective localities, determine the starting amount standards for implementing Subparagraph (3) of Paragraph 1 of Article 2 and Subparagraph (3) of Article 4 of this Interpretation within the ranges of 300,000 yuan to 600,000 yuan and 600,000 yuan to 1,000,000 yuan, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court for record.
