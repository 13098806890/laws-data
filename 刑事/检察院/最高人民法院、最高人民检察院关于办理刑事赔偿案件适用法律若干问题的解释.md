# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕24号  
**公布日期**：2015-12-14  
**生效日期**：2016-01-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2015年12月14日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1671次会议、2015年12月21日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第46次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年1月1日起施行。 2015年12月28日 最高人民法院   最高人民检察院 关于办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2015〕24号 根据国家赔偿法以及有关法律的规定，结合刑事赔偿工作实际，对办理刑事赔偿案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　赔偿请求人因行使侦查、检察、审判职权的机关以及看守所、监狱管理机关及其工作人员行使职权的行为侵犯其人身权、财产权而申请国家赔偿，具备国家赔偿法第十七条、第十八条规定情形的，属于本解释规定的刑事赔偿范围。 第二条　解除、撤销拘留或者逮捕措施后虽尚未撤销案件、作出不起诉决定或者判决宣告无罪，但是符合下列情形之一的，属于国家赔偿法第十七条第一项、第二项规定的终止追究刑事责任： （一）办案机关决定对犯罪嫌疑人终止侦查的； （二）解除、撤销取保候审、监视居住、拘留、逮捕措施后，办案机关超过一年未移送起诉、作出不起诉决定或者撤销案件的； （三）取保候审、监视居住法定期限届满后，办案机关超过一年未移送起诉、作出不起诉决定或者撤销案件的； （四）人民检察院撤回起诉超过三十日未作出不起诉决定的； （五）人民法院决定按撤诉处理后超过三十日，人民检察院未作出不起诉决定的； （六）人民法院准许刑事自诉案件自诉人撤诉的，或者人民法院决定对刑事自诉案件按撤诉处理的。 赔偿义务机关有证据证明尚未终止追究刑事责任，且经人民法院赔偿委员会审查属实的，应当决定驳回赔偿请求人的赔偿申请。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　赔偿请求人因行使侦查、检察、审判职权的机关以及看守所、监狱管理机关及其工作人员行使职权的行为侵犯其人身权、财产权而申请国家赔偿，具备[国家赔偿法第十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-17)、第十八条规定情形的，属于本解释规定的刑事赔偿范围。

**Article 1** . A compensation claimant who applies for state compensation due to the infringement of his personal rights or property rights by the acts of the organs exercising the powers of investigation, prosecution, and adjudication, as well as the administrative organs of detention houses and prisons and their staff members in exercising their functions, and who meets the circumstances specified in Articles 17 and 18 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China, shall fall within the scope of criminal compensation prescribed in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　解除、撤销拘留或者逮捕措施后虽尚未撤销案件、作出不起诉决定或者判决宣告无罪，但是符合下列情形之一的，属于[国家赔偿法第十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-17)第一项、第二项规定的终止追究刑事责任：  
（一）办案机关决定对犯罪嫌疑人终止侦查的；  
（二）解除、撤销取保候审、监视居住、拘留、逮捕措施后，办案机关超过一年未移送起诉、作出不起诉决定或者撤销案件的；  
（三）取保候审、监视居住法定期限届满后，办案机关超过一年未移送起诉、作出不起诉决定或者撤销案件的；  
（四）人民检察院撤回起诉超过三十日未作出不起诉决定的；  
（五）人民法院决定按撤诉处理后超过三十日，人民检察院未作出不起诉决定的；  
（六）人民法院准许刑事自诉案件自诉人撤诉的，或者人民法院决定对刑事自诉案件按撤诉处理的。  
赔偿义务机关有证据证明尚未终止追究刑事责任，且经人民法院赔偿委员会审查属实的，应当决定驳回赔偿请求人的赔偿申请。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理司法赔偿案件适用请求时效制度若干问题的解释.md#art-2" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理司法赔偿案件适用请求时效制度若干问题的解释》第2条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 2** . Where, after the rescission or revocation of detention or arrest measures, the case has not yet been withdrawn, a decision not to prosecute has not been made, or a judgment of acquittal has not been rendered, but any of the following circumstances exists, it shall constitute the termination of criminal liability specified in subparagraphs (1) and (2) of Article 17 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) The case-handling organ decides to terminate the investigation of the criminal suspect;  
(2) After the rescission or revocation of measures of release on bail pending investigation or trial, residential surveillance, detention, or arrest, the case-handling organ fails to transfer the case for prosecution, make a decision not to prosecute, or withdraw the case for more than one year;  
(3) After the expiration of the statutory period for release on bail pending investigation or trial or residential surveillance, the case-handling organ fails to transfer the case for prosecution, make a decision not to prosecute, or withdraw the case for more than one year;  
(4) The People's Procuratorate withdraws the prosecution but fails to make a decision not to prosecute within more than thirty days;  
(5) After the People's Court decides to treat the case as withdrawn, the People's Procuratorate fails to make a decision not to prosecute within more than thirty days;  
(6) The People's Court permits the private prosecutor in a criminal private prosecution to withdraw the prosecution, or the People's Court decides to treat a criminal private prosecution as withdrawn.  
If the organ liable for compensation has evidence proving that the criminal liability has not yet been terminated, and upon examination by the Compensation Committee of the People's Court such evidence is found to be true, it shall decide to reject the compensation application of the compensation claimant.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　对财产采取查封、扣押、冻结、追缴等措施后，有下列情形之一，且办案机关未依法解除查封、扣押、冻结等措施或者返还财产的，属于[国家赔偿法第十八条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-18)规定的侵犯财产权：  
（一）赔偿请求人有证据证明财产与尚未终结的刑事案件无关，经审查属实的；  
（二）终止侦查、撤销案件、不起诉、判决宣告无罪终止追究刑事责任的；  
（三）采取取保候审、监视居住、拘留或者逮捕措施，在解除、撤销强制措施或者强制措施法定期限届满后超过一年未移送起诉、作出不起诉决定或者撤销案件的；  
（四）未采取取保候审、监视居住、拘留或者逮捕措施，立案后超过两年未移送起诉、作出不起诉决定或者撤销案件的；  
（五）人民检察院撤回起诉超过三十日未作出不起诉决定的；  
（六）人民法院决定按撤诉处理后超过三十日，人民检察院未作出不起诉决定的；  
（七）对生效裁决没有处理的财产或者对该财产违法进行其他处理的。  
有前款第三项至六项规定情形之一，赔偿义务机关有证据证明尚未终止追究刑事责任，且经人民法院赔偿委员会审查属实的，应当决定驳回赔偿请求人的赔偿申请。&thinsp;<sup><a href="../../诉讼与司法程序/文书、送达与废止/最高人民法院关于审理司法赔偿案件适用请求时效制度若干问题的解释.md#art-4" title="被《最高人民法院关于审理司法赔偿案件适用请求时效制度若干问题的解释》第4条引用">[1]</a></sup>

**Article 3** . Where, after measures such as sealing, seizing, freezing, or recovering property, any of the following circumstances exists, and the case-handling organ fails to release the sealing, seizure, freezing, or other measures or return the property according to law, it shall constitute the infringement of property rights specified in Article 18 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) The compensation claimant has evidence proving that the property is unrelated to the criminal case that has not yet been concluded, and upon examination such evidence is found to be true;  
(2) The investigation is terminated, the case is withdrawn, a decision not to prosecute is made, or a judgment of acquittal is rendered, terminating the criminal liability;  
(3) After measures of release on bail pending investigation or trial, residential surveillance, detention, or arrest have been taken, and upon the rescission or revocation of the compulsory measures or the expiration of the statutory period of the compulsory measures, the organ fails to transfer the case for prosecution, make a decision not to prosecute, or withdraw the case for more than one year;  
(4) Where no measures of release on bail pending investigation or trial, residential surveillance, detention, or arrest have been taken, and after the case is docketed, the organ fails to transfer the case for prosecution, make a decision not to prosecute, or withdraw the case for more than two years;  
(5) The People's Procuratorate withdraws the prosecution but fails to make a decision not to prosecute within more than thirty days;  
(6) After the People's Court decides to treat the case as withdrawn, the People's Procuratorate fails to make a decision not to prosecute within more than thirty days;  
(7) Property that has not been dealt with in an effective decision is disposed of, or other illegal disposal of such property occurs.  
If any of the circumstances provided in items (3) through (6) of the preceding paragraph exists, and the organ liable for compensation has evidence proving that the criminal liability has not yet been terminated, and upon examination by the Compensation Committee of the People's Court such evidence is found to be true, it shall decide to reject the compensation application of the compensation claimant.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　赔偿义务机关作出赔偿决定，应当依法告知赔偿请求人有权在三十日内向赔偿义务机关的上一级机关申请复议。赔偿义务机关未依法告知，赔偿请求人收到赔偿决定之日起两年内提出复议申请的，复议机关应当受理。  
人民法院赔偿委员会处理赔偿申请，适用前款规定。

**Article 4** . Where the organ liable for compensation makes a compensation decision, it shall, according to law, inform the compensation claimant of the right to apply for reconsideration to the organ at the next higher level within thirty days. If the organ liable for compensation fails to inform according to law, and the compensation claimant submits an application for reconsideration within two years from the date of receipt of the compensation decision, the reconsideration organ shall accept the application.  
The Compensation Committee of the People's Court, when handling a compensation application, shall apply the provisions of the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　对公民采取刑事拘留措施后终止追究刑事责任，具有下列情形之一的，属于[国家赔偿法第十七条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-17)第一项规定的违法刑事拘留：  
（一）违反刑事诉讼法规定的条件采取拘留措施的；  
（二）违反刑事诉讼法规定的程序采取拘留措施的；  
（三）依照刑事诉讼法规定的条件和程序对公民采取拘留措施，但是拘留时间超过刑事诉讼法规定的时限。  
违法刑事拘留的人身自由赔偿金自拘留之日起计算。

**Article 5** . Where, after criminal detention measures are taken against a citizen, the criminal liability is terminated, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall constitute illegal criminal detention as provided in subparagraph (1) of Article 17 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) The detention measures are taken in violation of the conditions prescribed in the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) The detention measures are taken in violation of the procedures prescribed in the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) Detention measures are taken against a citizen in accordance with the conditions and procedures prescribed in the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, but the detention period exceeds the time limit prescribed in the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The compensation for personal freedom for illegal criminal detention shall be calculated from the date of detention.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　数罪并罚的案件经再审改判部分罪名不成立，监禁期限超出再审判决确定的刑期，公民对超期监禁申请国家赔偿的，应当决定予以赔偿。

**Article 6** . In a case involving combined punishment for several crimes, where a retrial modifies the judgment and finds some charges not established, and the period of imprisonment exceeds the sentence determined in the retrial judgment, and the citizen applies for state compensation for the period of excessive imprisonment, it shall be decided to grant compensation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　根据[国家赔偿法第十九条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-19)第二项、第三项的规定，依照[刑法第十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-17)、第十八条规定不负刑事责任的人和依照[刑事诉讼法第十五条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-15)、第一百七十三条第二款规定不追究刑事责任的人被羁押，国家不承担赔偿责任。但是，对起诉后经人民法院错判拘役、有期徒刑、无期徒刑并已执行的，人民法院应当对该判决确定后继续监禁期间侵犯公民人身自由权的情形予以赔偿。

**Article 7** . In accordance with the provisions of subparagraphs (2) and (3) of Article 19 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China, where a person who bears no criminal liability according to Articles 17 and 18 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, or a person whose criminal liability is not pursued according to Article 15 and paragraph 2 of Article 173 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China, is placed in custody, the state shall not bear compensation liability. However, if, after prosecution, the People's Court erroneously sentences the person to criminal detention, fixed-term imprisonment, or life imprisonment, and such sentence has been executed, the People's Court shall compensate for the period of continued imprisonment after the determination of such sentence during which the citizen's personal right to freedom is infringed upon.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　赔偿义务机关主张依据[国家赔偿法第十九条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-19)第一项、第五项规定的情形免除赔偿责任的，应当就该免责事由的成立承担举证责任。

**Article 8** . If the organ liable for compensation claims that the compensation liability is exempted pursuant to the circumstances provided in subparagraphs (1) and (5) of Article 19 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence regarding the establishment of such ground for exemption.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　受害的公民死亡，其继承人和其他有扶养关系的亲属有权申请国家赔偿。  
依法享有继承权的同一顺序继承人有数人时，其中一人或者部分人作为赔偿请求人申请国家赔偿的，申请效力及于全体。  
赔偿请求人为数人时，其中一人或者部分赔偿请求人非经全体同意，申请撤回或者放弃赔偿请求，效力不及于未明确表示撤回申请或者放弃赔偿请求的其他赔偿请求人。

**Article 9** . If the injured citizen dies, his heirs and other relatives who have a dependent relationship with him shall have the right to apply for state compensation.  
Where there are several successors in the same order who enjoy the right of succession according to law, if one or some of them file an application for state compensation as compensation claimants, the effect of the application shall extend to all of them.  
Where there are several compensation claimants, if one or some of them apply to withdraw or waive the compensation claim without the consent of all, the effect shall not extend to the other compensation claimants who have not clearly expressed the intention to withdraw the application or waive the compensation claim.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　看守所及其工作人员在行使职权时侵犯公民合法权益造成损害的，看守所的主管机关为赔偿义务机关。

**Article 10** . Where a detention house or its staff member, in exercising their functions, infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of a citizen and causes damage, the competent authority of the detention house shall be the organ liable for compensation.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　对公民采取拘留措施后又采取逮捕措施，国家承担赔偿责任的，作出逮捕决定的机关为赔偿义务机关。

**Article 11** . Where a citizen is first subjected to detention measures and then to arrest measures, and the state bears compensation liability, the organ that made the decision to arrest shall be the organ liable for compensation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　一审判决有罪，二审发回重审后具有下列情形之一的，属于[国家赔偿法第二十一条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-21)第四款规定的重审无罪赔偿，作出一审有罪判决的人民法院为赔偿义务机关：  
（一）原审人民法院改判无罪并已发生法律效力的；  
（二）重审期间人民检察院作出不起诉决定的；  
（三）人民检察院在重审期间撤回起诉超过三十日或者人民法院决定按撤诉处理超过三十日未作出不起诉决定的。  
依照审判监督程序再审后作无罪处理的，作出原生效判决的人民法院为赔偿义务机关。

**Article 12** . Where, after a first-instance judgment of guilty, the case is remanded for retrial upon appeal and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall constitute compensation for innocence upon retrial as provided in paragraph 4 of Article 21 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China, and the People's Court that rendered the first-instance guilty judgment shall be the organ liable for compensation:  
(1) The original trial People's Court modifies the judgment to acquittal, and such judgment has become legally effective;  
(2) During the retrial, the People's Procuratorate makes a decision not to prosecute;  
(3) During the retrial, the People's Procuratorate withdraws the prosecution for more than thirty days, or the People's Court decides to treat the case as withdrawn for more than thirty days, without a decision not to prosecute being made.  
Where, after a retrial in accordance with the trial supervision procedure, the case is treated as innocence, the People's Court that rendered the original effective judgment shall be the organ liable for compensation.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　医疗费赔偿根据医疗机构出具的医药费、治疗费、住院费等收款凭证，结合病历和诊断证明等相关证据确定。赔偿义务机关对治疗的必要性和合理性提出异议的，应当承担举证责任。

**Article 13** . Compensation for medical expenses shall be determined based on the receipts for medical expenses, treatment expenses, hospitalization expenses, etc., issued by medical institutions, in conjunction with relevant evidence such as medical records and diagnostic certificates. If the organ liable for compensation raises objections regarding the necessity and reasonableness of the treatment, it shall bear the burden of adducing evidence.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　护理费赔偿参照当地护工从事同等级别护理的劳务报酬标准计算，原则上按照一名护理人员的标准计算护理费；但医疗机构或者司法鉴定人有明确意见的，可以参照确定护理人数并赔偿相应的护理费。  
护理期限应当计算至公民恢复生活自理能力时止。公民因残疾不能恢复生活自理能力的，可以根据其年龄、健康状况等因素确定合理的护理期限，一般不超过二十年。

**Article 14** . Compensation for nursing expenses shall be calculated by reference to the standard of labor remuneration for local nursing workers at the same level of care, and in principle, the nursing expenses shall be calculated based on the standard for one nursing worker; however, if a medical institution or a judicial expert has a clear opinion, the number of nursing personnel may be determined by reference thereto and corresponding nursing expenses shall be compensated.  
The period of nursing shall be calculated until the citizen recovers the ability to take care of himself in daily life. If the citizen is unable to recover the ability to take care of himself due to disability, a reasonable nursing period may be determined based on factors such as his age and health condition, generally not exceeding twenty years.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　残疾生活辅助器具费赔偿按照普通适用器具的合理费用标准计算。伤情有特殊需要的，可以参照辅助器具配制机构的意见确定。  
辅助器具的更换周期和赔偿期限参照配制机构的意见确定。

**Article 15** . Compensation for expenses for assistive devices for disabled living shall be calculated according to the reasonable cost standard for ordinary and suitable devices. If the injury has special needs, the compensation may be determined by reference to the opinion of the assistive device fitting institution.  
The replacement cycle and compensation period for assistive devices shall be determined by reference to the opinion of the fitting institution.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　误工减少收入的赔偿根据受害公民的误工时间和国家上年度职工日平均工资确定，最高为国家上年度职工年平均工资的五倍。  
误工时间根据公民接受治疗的医疗机构出具的证明确定。公民因伤致残持续误工的，误工时间可以计算至作为赔偿依据的伤残等级鉴定确定前一日。

**Article 16** . Compensation for reduced income due to missed work shall be determined according to the number of days the injured citizen missed work and the average daily wage of employees in the previous year as published by the state, with the maximum amount being five times the average annual wage of employees in the previous year as published by the state.  
The number of days of missed work shall be determined according to the certificate issued by the medical institution where the citizen received treatment. If the citizen continues to miss work due to disability caused by injury, the number of days of missed work may be calculated up to the day before the disability grade evaluation serving as the basis for compensation is determined.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　造成公民身体伤残的赔偿，应当根据司法鉴定人的伤残等级鉴定确定公民丧失劳动能力的程度，并参照以下标准确定残疾赔偿金：  
（一）按照国家规定的伤残等级确定公民为一级至四级伤残的，视为全部丧失劳动能力，残疾赔偿金幅度为国家上年度职工年平均工资的十倍至二十倍；  
（二）按照国家规定的伤残等级确定公民为五级至十级伤残的，视为部分丧失劳动能力。五至六级的，残疾赔偿金幅度为国家上年度职工年平均工资的五倍至十倍；七至十级的，残疾赔偿金幅度为国家上年度职工年平均工资的五倍以下。  
有扶养义务的公民部分丧失劳动能力的，残疾赔偿金可以根据伤残等级并参考被扶养人生活来源丧失的情况进行确定，最高不超过国家上年度职工年平均工资的二十倍。

**Article 17** . Compensation for physical injury or disability of a citizen shall be determined by the degree of loss of working ability assessed by a judicial expert based on the disability grade evaluation, and the disability compensation shall be determined by reference to the following standards:  
(1) Where a citizen is determined to be at disability grades 1 to 4 according to the state-prescribed disability grades, it shall be deemed as total loss of working ability, and the range of disability compensation shall be ten to twenty times the average annual wage of employees in the previous year as published by the state;  
(2) Where a citizen is determined to be at disability grades 5 to 10 according to the state-prescribed disability grades, it shall be deemed as partial loss of working ability. For grades 5 to 6, the range of disability compensation shall be five to ten times the average annual wage of employees in the previous year as published by the state; for grades 7 to 10, the range of disability compensation shall be less than five times the average annual wage of employees in the previous year as published by the state.  
For a citizen with a duty to support others who has partially lost his working ability, the disability compensation may be determined based on the disability grade and by reference to the extent of loss of living resources of the supported persons, provided that the maximum amount shall not exceed twenty times the average annual wage of employees in the previous year as published by the state.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　受害的公民全部丧失劳动能力的，对其扶养的无劳动能力人的生活费发放标准，参照作出赔偿决定时被扶养人住所地所属省级人民政府确定的最低生活保障标准执行。  
能够确定扶养年限的，生活费可协商确定并一次性支付。不能确定扶养年限的，可按照二十年上限确定扶养年限并一次性支付生活费，被扶养人超过六十周岁的，年龄每增加一岁，扶养年限减少一年；被扶养人年龄超过确定扶养年限的，被扶养人可逐年领取生活费至死亡时止。

**Article 18** . Where the injured citizen has totally lost the ability to work, the standard for the disbursement of living expenses to the dependent who has no ability to work shall be implemented by reference to the minimum living security standard determined by the provincial people's government of the place where the dependent resides at the time the compensation decision is made.  
If the period of support can be determined, the living expenses may be determined through negotiation and paid in a lump sum. If the period of support cannot be determined, the period of support may be determined with a maximum of twenty years and the living expenses may be paid in a lump sum; if the dependent is over sixty years old, for each additional year of age, the period of support shall be reduced by one year; if the dependent's age exceeds the determined period of support, the dependent may receive living expenses annually until death.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　侵犯公民、法人和其他组织的财产权造成损害的，应当依照[国家赔偿法第三十六条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-36)的规定承担赔偿责任。  
财产不能恢复原状或者灭失的，财产损失按照损失发生时的市场价格或者其他合理方式计算。

**Article 19** . Where damage is caused by infringement of the property rights of citizens, legal persons, or other organizations, compensation liability shall be borne in accordance with Article 36 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If the property cannot be restored to its original state or is lost, the property loss shall be calculated according to the market price at the time of loss or by other reasonable methods.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　返还执行的罚款或者罚金、追缴或者没收的金钱，解除冻结的汇款的，应当支付银行同期存款利息，利率参照赔偿义务机关作出赔偿决定时中国人民银行公布的人民币整存整取定期存款一年期基准利率确定，不计算复利。  
复议机关或者人民法院赔偿委员会改变原赔偿决定，利率参照新作出决定时中国人民银行公布的人民币整存整取定期存款一年期基准利率确定。  
计息期间自侵权行为发生时起算，至作出生效赔偿决定时止；但在生效赔偿决定作出前侵权行为停止的，计算至侵权行为停止时止。  
被罚没、追缴的资金属于赔偿请求人在金融机构合法存款的，在存款合同存续期间，按照合同约定的利率计算利息。

**Article 20** . Where a fine or penalty that has been executed, or money that has been recovered or confiscated, is returned, or where frozen remittances are unfrozen, the bank deposit interest for the same period shall be paid, and the interest rate shall be determined by reference to the one-year benchmark interest rate for lump-sum deposit and withdrawal of Renminbi as published by the People's Bank of China at the time the organ liable for compensation makes the compensation decision; no compound interest shall be calculated.  
If the reconsideration organ or the Compensation Committee of the People's Court alters the original compensation decision, the interest rate shall be determined by reference to the one-year benchmark interest rate for lump-sum deposit and withdrawal of Renminbi as published by the People's Bank of China at the time the new decision is made.  
The interest calculation period shall commence from the date of the infringing act and end on the date of the effective compensation decision; however, if the infringing act ceases before the effective compensation decision is made, it shall be calculated until the cessation of the infringing act.  
If the confiscated or recovered funds constitute lawful deposits of the compensation claimant in a financial institution, during the existence of the deposit contract, interest shall be calculated at the interest rate agreed in the contract.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　[国家赔偿法第三十三条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-33)、第三十四条规定的上年度，是指赔偿义务机关作出赔偿决定时的上一年度；复议机关或者人民法院赔偿委员会改变原赔偿决定，按照新作出决定时的上一年度国家职工平均工资标准计算人身自由赔偿金。  
作出赔偿决定、复议决定时国家上一年度职工平均工资尚未公布的，以已经公布的最近年度职工平均工资为准。

**Article 21** . The term 'previous year' as used in Articles 33 and 34 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China shall refer to the year immediately preceding the year in which the organ liable for compensation makes the compensation decision; if the reconsideration organ or the Compensation Committee of the People's Court alters the original compensation decision, the compensation for personal freedom shall be calculated based on the average wage of employees in the previous year as published by the state at the time the new decision is made.  
If the national average wage of employees for the previous year has not yet been published at the time the compensation decision or reconsideration decision is made, the most recently published average annual wage of employees for the latest year shall prevail.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　下列赔偿决定、复议决定是发生法律效力的决定：  
（一）超过[国家赔偿法第二十四条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-24)规定的期限没有申请复议或者向上一级人民法院赔偿委员会申请国家赔偿的赔偿义务机关的决定；  
（二）超过[国家赔偿法第二十五条](../../行政与公法/税务财政/中华人民共和国国家赔偿法.md#art-25)规定的期限没有向人民法院赔偿委员会申请国家赔偿的复议决定；  
（三）人民法院赔偿委员会作出的赔偿决定。  
发生法律效力的赔偿义务机关的决定和复议决定，与发生法律效力的赔偿委员会的赔偿决定具有同等法律效力，依法必须执行。

**Article 22** . The following compensation decisions and reconsideration decisions shall be decisions that have become legally effective:  
(1) A decision of the organ liable for compensation for which no application for reconsideration or application for state compensation to the Compensation Committee of the People's Court at the next higher level has been made beyond the time limit prescribed in Article 24 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(2) A reconsideration decision for which no application for state compensation to the Compensation Committee of the People's Court has been made beyond the time limit prescribed in Article 25 of the State Compensation Law of the People's Republic of China;  
(3) A compensation decision made by the Compensation Committee of the People's Court.  
Decisions of the organ liable for compensation and reconsideration decisions that have become legally effective shall have the same legal effect as compensation decisions of the Compensation Committee that have become legally effective, and shall be executed as required by law.

**Article 23** . This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2016. If any judicial interpretation issued by the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate before the coming into force of this Interpretation is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
