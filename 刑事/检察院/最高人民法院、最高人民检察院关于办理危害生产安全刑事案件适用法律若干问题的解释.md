# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害生产安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕22号  
**公布日期**：2015-11-09  
**生效日期**：2015-12-16

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理危害生产安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2015年11月9日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1665次会议、2015年12月9日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第44次会议通过，现予公布，自2015年12月16日起施行。 2015年12月14日 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院 关于办理危害生产安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2015〕22号 为依法惩治危害生产安全犯罪，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条    刑法第一百三十四条第一款规定的犯罪主体，包括对生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员，以及直接从事生产、作业的人员。 第二条     刑法第一百三十四条第二款规定的犯罪主体，包括对生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员。 第三条   刑法第一百三十五条规定的“直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员”，是指对安全生产设施或者安全生产条件不符合国家规定负有直接责任的生产经营单位负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人，以及其他对安全生产设施或者安全生产条件负有管理、维护职责的人员。 第四条   刑法第一百三十九条之一规定的“负有报告职责的人员”，是指负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人，以及其他负有报告职责的人员。 第五条   明知存在事故隐患、继续作业存在危险，仍然违反有关安全管理的规定，实施下列行为之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百三十四条第二款规定的“强令他人违章冒险作业”： （一）利用组织、指挥、管理职权，强制他人违章作业的； （二）采取威逼、胁迫、恐吓等手段，强制他人违章作业的； （三）故意掩盖事故隐患，组织他人违章作业的； （四）其他强令他人违章作业的行为。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条    [刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第一款规定的犯罪主体，包括对生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员，以及直接从事生产、作业的人员。

**Article 1** The subjects of the crime stipulated in paragraph 1 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law shall include persons such as persons in charge, management personnel, actual controllers, and investors who bear the responsibility of organization, command or management over production and operations, as well as persons who directly engage in production and operations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条     [刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第二款规定的犯罪主体，包括对生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员。

**Article 2** The subjects of the crime stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law shall include persons such as persons in charge, management personnel, actual controllers, and investors who bear the responsibility of organization, command or management over production and operations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   [刑法第一百三十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-135)规定的“直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员”，是指对安全生产设施或者安全生产条件不符合国家规定负有直接责任的生产经营单位负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人，以及其他对安全生产设施或者安全生产条件负有管理、维护职责的人员。

**Article 3** The "directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons" stipulated in Article 135 of the Criminal Law shall refer to the persons in charge, management personnel, actual controllers, and investors of production and business units who bear direct responsibility for the failure of safety production facilities or safety production conditions to meet national regulations, as well as other persons who bear the duty of management and maintenance over safety production facilities or safety production conditions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   [刑法第一百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-139)之一规定的“负有报告职责的人员”，是指负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人，以及其他负有报告职责的人员。

**Article 4** The "persons who have the duty to report" stipulated in Article 139a of the Criminal Law shall refer to persons in charge, management personnel, actual controllers, and investors who bear the responsibility of organization, command or management, as well as other persons who have the duty to report.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   明知存在事故隐患、继续作业存在危险，仍然违反有关安全管理的规定，实施下列行为之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第二款规定的“强令他人违章冒险作业”：  
（一）利用组织、指挥、管理职权，强制他人违章作业的；  
（二）采取威逼、胁迫、恐吓等手段，强制他人违章作业的；  
（三）故意掩盖事故隐患，组织他人违章作业的；  
（四）其他强令他人违章作业的行为。

**Article 5** Where a person, knowing that there are hidden dangers of accidents and that continued operations are dangerous, still violates the provisions on safety management and commits any of the following acts, it shall be deemed as "forcibly ordering others to operate in violation of regulations and at risk" as stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Using the authority of organization, command or management to compel others to operate in violation of regulations;  
(2) Using means such as coercion, duress or intimidation to compel others to operate in violation of regulations;  
(3) Deliberately concealing hidden dangers of accidents and organizing others to operate in violation of regulations;  
(4) Other acts of forcibly ordering others to operate in violation of regulations.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   实施[刑法第一百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-132)、第一百三十四条第一款、第一百三十五条、第一百三十五条之一、第一百三十六条、第一百三十九条规定的行为，因而发生安全事故，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为“造成严重后果”或者“发生重大伤亡事故或者造成其他严重后果”，对相关责任人员，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役：  
（一）造成死亡一人以上，或者重伤三人以上的；  
（二）造成直接经济损失一百万元以上的；  
（三）其他造成严重后果或者重大安全事故的情形。  
实施[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第二款规定的行为，因而发生安全事故，具有本条第一款规定情形的，应当认定为“发生重大伤亡事故或者造成其他严重后果”，对相关责任人员，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役。  
实施[刑法第一百三十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-137)规定的行为，因而发生安全事故，具有本条第一款规定情形的，应当认定为“造成重大安全事故”，对直接责任人员，处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金。  
实施[刑法第一百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-138)规定的行为，因而发生安全事故，具有本条第一款第一项规定情形的，应当认定为“发生重大伤亡事故”，对直接责任人员，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役。

**Article 6** Where a person commits any of the acts stipulated in Articles 132, 134(1), 135, 135a, 136 and 139 of the Criminal Law, thereby causing a safety accident, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "causing serious consequences" or "causing major casualties or other serious consequences", and the relevant liable person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention:  
(1) Causing one or more deaths, or three or more serious injuries;  
(2) Causing direct economic losses of one million yuan or more;  
(3) Other circumstances that cause serious consequences or major safety accidents.  
Where a person commits the act stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law, thereby causing a safety accident, and falls under the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of this Article, it shall be deemed as "causing major casualties or other serious consequences", and the relevant liable person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention.  
Where a person commits the act stipulated in Article 137 of the Criminal Law, thereby causing a safety accident, and falls under the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of this Article, it shall be deemed as "causing a major safety accident", and the directly liable person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention, and shall also be fined.  
Where a person commits the act stipulated in Article 138 of the Criminal Law, thereby causing a safety accident, and falls under the circumstances specified in item (1) of paragraph 1 of this Article, it shall be deemed as "causing major casualties", and the directly liable person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   实施[刑法第一百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-132)、第一百三十四条第一款、第一百三十五条、第一百三十五条之一、第一百三十六条、第一百三十九条规定的行为，因而发生安全事故，具有下列情形之一的，对相关责任人员，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：  
（一）造成死亡三人以上或者重伤十人以上，负事故主要责任的；  
（二）造成直接经济损失五百万元以上，负事故主要责任的；  
（三）其他造成特别严重后果、情节特别恶劣或者后果特别严重的情形。  
实施[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第二款规定的行为，因而发生安全事故，具有本条第一款规定情形的，对相关责任人员，处五年以上有期徒刑。  
实施[刑法第一百三十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-137)规定的行为，因而发生安全事故，具有本条第一款规定情形的，对直接责任人员，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
实施[刑法第一百三十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-138)规定的行为，因而发生安全事故，具有下列情形之一的，对直接责任人员，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：  
（一）造成死亡三人以上或者重伤十人以上，负事故主要责任的；  
（二）具有[本解释第六条](#art-6)第一款第一项规定情形，同时造成直接经济损失五百万元以上并负事故主要责任的，或者同时造成恶劣社会影响的。

**Article 7** Where a person commits any of the acts stipulated in Articles 132, 134(1), 135, 135a, 136 and 139 of the Criminal Law, thereby causing a safety accident, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the relevant liable person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years:  
(1) Causing three or more deaths or ten or more serious injuries, and bearing primary liability for the accident;  
(2) Causing direct economic losses of five million yuan or more, and bearing primary liability for the accident;  
(3) Other circumstances that cause particularly serious consequences, are of particularly vile nature, or result in particularly serious outcomes.  
Where a person commits the act stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law, thereby causing a safety accident, and falls under the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of this Article, the relevant liable person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years.  
Where a person commits the act stipulated in Article 137 of the Criminal Law, thereby causing a safety accident, and falls under the circumstances specified in paragraph 1 of this Article, the directly liable person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years, and shall also be fined.  
Where a person commits the act stipulated in Article 138 of the Criminal Law, thereby causing a safety accident, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the directly liable person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years:  
(1) Causing three or more deaths or ten or more serious injuries, and bearing primary liability for the accident;  
(2) Falling under the circumstances specified in item (1) of paragraph 1 of Article 6 of this Interpretation, and simultaneously causing direct economic losses of five million yuan or more and bearing primary liability for the accident, or simultaneously causing a severe adverse social impact.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   在安全事故发生后，负有报告职责的人员不报或者谎报事故情况，贻误事故抢救，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-139)之一规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）导致事故后果扩大，增加死亡一人以上，或者增加重伤三人以上，或者增加直接经济损失一百万元以上的；  
（二）实施下列行为之一，致使不能及时有效开展事故抢救的：  
1. 决定不报、迟报、谎报事故情况或者指使、串通有关人员不报、迟报、谎报事故情况的；  
2. 在事故抢救期间擅离职守或者逃匿的；  
3. 伪造、破坏事故现场，或者转移、藏匿、毁灭遇难人员尸体，或者转移、藏匿受伤人员的；  
4. 毁灭、伪造、隐匿与事故有关的图纸、记录、计算机数据等资料以及其他证据的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-139)之一规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）导致事故后果扩大，增加死亡三人以上，或者增加重伤十人以上，或者增加直接经济损失五百万元以上的；  
（二）采用暴力、胁迫、命令等方式阻止他人报告事故情况，导致事故后果扩大的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 8** Where, after a safety accident occurs, a person who has the duty to report fails to report or makes a false report on the accident situation, thereby delaying the rescue of the accident, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 139a of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing an expansion of the accident consequences, increasing deaths by one or more, or increasing serious injuries by three or more, or increasing direct economic losses by one million yuan or more;  
(2) Committing any of the following acts, thereby making it impossible to carry out timely and effective accident rescue:  
1. Deciding not to report, delaying reporting, or making a false report on the accident situation, or instructing or colluding with relevant persons not to report, delay reporting, or make false reports on the accident situation;  
2. Deserting one's post or fleeing during the accident rescue period;  
3. Forging or damaging the accident scene, or transferring, concealing, or destroying the bodies of the deceased, or transferring or concealing injured persons;  
4. Destroying, forging, or concealing drawings, records, computer data, and other materials and evidence related to the accident;  
(3) Other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a person falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "particularly serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 139a of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing an expansion of the accident consequences, increasing deaths by three or more, or increasing serious injuries by ten or more, or increasing direct economic losses by five million yuan or more;  
(2) Using violence, coercion, orders, or other means to prevent others from reporting the accident situation, thereby causing an expansion of the accident consequences;  
(3) Other circumstances of a particularly serious nature.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   在安全事故发生后，与负有报告职责的人员串通，不报或者谎报事故情况，贻误事故抢救，情节严重的，依照[刑法第一百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-139)之一的规定，以共犯论处。

**Article 9** Where, after a safety accident occurs, a person colludes with a person who has the duty to report, fails to report or makes a false report on the accident situation, thereby delaying the rescue of the accident, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be dealt with as an accomplice in accordance with the provisions of Article 139a of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   在安全事故发生后，直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员故意阻挠开展抢救，导致人员死亡或者重伤，或者为了逃避法律追究，对被害人进行隐藏、遗弃，致使被害人因无法得到救助而死亡或者重度残疾的，分别依照[刑法第二百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-232)、第二百三十四条的规定，以故意杀人罪或者故意伤害罪定罪处罚。

**Article 10** Where, after a safety accident occurs, the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons intentionally obstruct the carrying out of rescue, causing death or serious injury to persons, or, in order to evade legal investigation, conceal or abandon the victim, thereby causing the victim to die or become severely disabled due to lack of rescue, they shall be convicted and punished for intentional homicide or intentional injury respectively in accordance with the provisions of Articles 232 and 234 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   生产不符合保障人身、财产安全的国家标准、行业标准的安全设备，或者明知安全设备不符合保障人身、财产安全的国家标准、行业标准而进行销售，致使发生安全事故，造成严重后果的，依照[刑法第一百四十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-146)的规定，以生产、销售不符合安全标准的产品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 11** Where a person produces safety equipment that does not meet national standards or industry standards for ensuring personal and property safety, or sells safety equipment knowing that it does not meet such standards, thereby causing a safety accident with serious consequences, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing or selling products that do not meet safety standards in accordance with the provisions of Article 146 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   实施[刑法第一百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-132)、第一百三十四条至第一百三十九条之一规定的犯罪行为，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）未依法取得安全许可证件或者安全许可证件过期、被暂扣、吊销、注销后从事生产经营活动的；  
（二）关闭、破坏必要的安全监控和报警设备的；  
（三）已经发现事故隐患，经有关部门或者个人提出后，仍不采取措施的；  
（四）一年内曾因危害生产安全违法犯罪活动受过行政处罚或者刑事处罚的；  
（五）采取弄虚作假、行贿等手段，故意逃避、阻挠负有安全监督管理职责的部门实施监督检查的；  
（六）安全事故发生后转移财产意图逃避承担责任的；  
（七）其他从重处罚的情形。  
实施前款第五项规定的行为，同时构成[刑法第三百八十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-389)规定的犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。

**Article 12** Where a person commits any of the criminal acts stipulated in Articles 132, 134 through 139a of the Criminal Law, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the person shall be given a severe punishment:  
(1) Engaging in production and business activities without lawfully obtaining a safety permit, or after the safety permit has expired, been temporarily withheld, revoked, or cancelled;  
(2) Shutting down or destroying necessary safety monitoring and alarm equipment;  
(3) Having already discovered hidden dangers of accidents, but still failing to take measures after being alerted by relevant departments or individuals;  
(4) Having previously received an administrative penalty or criminal punishment for illegal or criminal activities endangering production safety within one year;  
(5) Using fraudulent means, bribery, or other methods to intentionally evade or obstruct the supervisory inspection carried out by departments with safety supervision and management responsibilities;  
(6) Transferring property after a safety accident occurs with the intent to evade liability;  
(7) Other circumstances warranting severe punishment.  
Where a person commits the act specified in item (5) of the preceding paragraph and such act also constitutes a crime under Article 389 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be punished in accordance with the provisions on concurrent punishment for several offenses.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条   实施[刑法第一百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-132)、第一百三十四条至第一百三十九条之一规定的犯罪行为，在安全事故发生后积极组织、参与事故抢救，或者积极配合调查、主动赔偿损失的，可以酌情从轻处罚。

**Article 13** Where a person commits any of the criminal acts stipulated in Articles 132, 134 through 139a of the Criminal Law, but actively organizes or participates in accident rescue after the safety accident occurs, or actively cooperates with the investigation and voluntarily compensates for losses, the person may, as the circumstances warrant, be given a light punishment.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条   国家工作人员违反规定投资入股生产经营，构成本解释规定的有关犯罪的，或者国家工作人员的贪污、受贿犯罪行为与安全事故发生存在关联性的，从重处罚；同时构成贪污、受贿犯罪和危害生产安全犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。

**Article 14** Where a state functionary invests in or holds shares in production and business operations in violation of regulations, thereby constituting a relevant crime stipulated in this Interpretation, or where the state functionary's crime of embezzlement or bribery is related to the occurrence of a safety accident, the state functionary shall be given a severe punishment; where the state functionary simultaneously constitutes the crime of embezzlement or bribery and a crime endangering production safety, the state functionary shall be punished in accordance with the provisions on concurrent punishment for several offenses.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条   国家机关工作人员在履行安全监督管理职责时滥用职权、玩忽职守，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，或者徇私舞弊，对发现的刑事案件依法应当移交司法机关追究刑事责任而不移交，情节严重的，分别依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)、第四百零二条的规定，以滥用职权罪、玩忽职守罪或者徇私舞弊不移交刑事案件罪定罪处罚。  
公司、企业、事业单位的工作人员在依法或者受委托行使安全监督管理职责时滥用职权或者玩忽职守，构成犯罪的，应当依照《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于〈中华人民共和国刑法〉第九章渎职罪主体适用问题的解释》的规定，适用渎职罪的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 15** Where a functionary of a state organ abuses power or neglects duties while performing safety supervision and management responsibilities, thereby causing heavy losses to public property, the state, and the interests of the people, or engages in malpractice for personal gain and, with respect to a discovered criminal case that should by law be transferred to the judicial organ for investigation of criminal liability, fails to transfer it, if the circumstances are serious, the functionary shall be convicted and punished for the crime of abuse of power, the crime of neglect of duties, or the crime of malpractice for personal gain in failing to transfer a criminal case, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 397 and 402 of the Criminal Law respectively.  
Where a staff member of a company, enterprise, or institution abuses power or neglects duties while exercising safety supervision and management responsibilities according to law or upon entrustment, thereby constituting a crime, the criminal liability shall be investigated by applying the provisions on dereliction of duty in accordance with the provisions of the "Interpretation of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress on the Application of the Subject of the Crime of Dereliction of Duty in Chapter IX of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China".

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条   对于实施危害生产安全犯罪适用缓刑的犯罪分子，可以根据犯罪情况，禁止其在缓刑考验期限内从事与安全生产相关联的特定活动；对于被判处刑罚的犯罪分子，可以根据犯罪情况和预防再犯罪的需要，禁止其自刑罚执行完毕之日或者假释之日起三年至五年内从事与安全生产相关的职业。

**Article 16** For a criminal who is sentenced to probation for committing a crime endangering production safety, the criminal may, based on the circumstances of the crime, be prohibited from engaging in specific activities related to production safety during the probation period; for a criminal who is sentenced to a punishment, the criminal may, based on the circumstances of the crime and the need to prevent recidivism, be prohibited from engaging in occupations related to production safety for a period of three to five years from the date of completion of the execution of the punishment or from the date of parole.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条   本解释自2015年12月16日起施行。本解释施行后，最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理危害矿山生产安全刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2007〕5号）同时废止。最高人民法院、最高人民检察院此前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 17** This Interpretation shall come into force on December 16, 2015. After this Interpretation comes into force, the "Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Endangering Mine Production Safety" (Judicial Interpretation No. 5 [2007]) shall be simultaneously abolished. Where any judicial interpretation or normative document previously issued by the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
