# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理侵犯公民个人信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕10号  
**公布日期**：2017-03-20  
**生效日期**：2017-06-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理侵犯公民个人信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2017年3月20日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1712次会议、2017年4月26日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第63次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年6月1日起施行。 2017年5月8日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理侵犯公民个人信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2017〕10号 为依法惩治侵犯公民个人信息犯罪活动，保护公民个人信息安全和合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　刑法第二百五十三条之一规定的“公民个人信息”,是指以电子或者其他方式记录的能够单独或者与其他信息结合识别特定自然人身份或者反映特定自然人活动情况的各种信息，包括姓名、身份证件号码、通信通讯联系方式、住址、账号密码、财产状况、行踪轨迹等。 第二条　违反法律、行政法规、部门规章有关公民个人信息保护的规定的，应当认定为刑法第二百五十三条之一规定的“违反国家有关规定”。 第三条　向特定人提供公民个人信息，以及通过信息网络或者其他途径发布公民个人信息的，应当认定为刑法第二百五十三条之一规定的“提供公民个人信息”。 未经被收集者同意，将合法收集的公民个人信息向他人提供的，属于刑法第二百五十三条之一规定的“提供公民个人信息”，但是经过处理无法识别特定个人且不能复原的除外。 第四条　违反国家有关规定，通过购买、收受、交换等方式获取公民个人信息，或者在履行职责、提供服务过程中收集公民个人信息的，属于刑法第二百五十三条之一第三款规定的“以其他方法非法获取公民个人信息”。 第五条　非法获取、出售或者提供公民个人信息，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百五十三条之一规定的“情节严重”： （一）出售或者提供行踪轨迹信息，被他人用于犯罪的； （二）知道或者应当知道他人利用公民个人信息实施犯罪，向其出售或者提供的； （三）非法获取、出售或者提供行踪轨迹信息、通信内容、征信信息、财产信息五十条以上的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一规定的“公民个人信息”,是指以电子或者其他方式记录的能够单独或者与其他信息结合识别特定自然人身份或者反映特定自然人活动情况的各种信息，包括姓名、身份证件号码、通信通讯联系方式、住址、账号密码、财产状况、行踪轨迹等。

**Article 1** "Citizens' personal information" as provided in Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China refers to all kinds of information recorded electronically or by other means that can alone or in combination with other information identify the identity of a specific natural person or reflect the activities of a specific natural person, including name, identity document number, communication contact information, address, account password, property status, whereabouts, etc.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　违反法律、行政法规、部门规章有关公民个人信息保护的规定的，应当认定为[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一规定的“违反国家有关规定”。

**Article 2** Violating the provisions of laws, administrative regulations, and departmental rules concerning the protection of citizens' personal information shall be deemed as "violating the relevant provisions of the state" as provided in Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　向特定人提供公民个人信息，以及通过信息网络或者其他途径发布公民个人信息的，应当认定为[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一规定的“提供公民个人信息”。  
未经被收集者同意，将合法收集的公民个人信息向他人提供的，属于[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一规定的“提供公民个人信息”，但是经过处理无法识别特定个人且不能复原的除外。

**Article 3** Providing citizens' personal information to specific persons, and publishing citizens' personal information through information networks or other channels, shall be deemed as "providing citizens' personal information" as provided in Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Providing to others the citizens' personal information lawfully collected without the consent of the person from whom it was collected shall constitute "providing citizens' personal information" as provided in Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, except where the information has been processed so that it cannot identify a specific person and cannot be restored.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　违反国家有关规定，通过购买、收受、交换等方式获取公民个人信息，或者在履行职责、提供服务过程中收集公民个人信息的，属于[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一第三款规定的“以其他方法非法获取公民个人信息”。

**Article 4** Obtaining citizens' personal information by means of purchase, receipt, exchange, etc., in violation of the relevant provisions of the state, or collecting citizens' personal information in the course of performing duties or providing services, shall constitute "illegally obtaining citizens' personal information by other means" as provided in paragraph 3 of Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　非法获取、出售或者提供公民个人信息，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）出售或者提供行踪轨迹信息，被他人用于犯罪的；  
（二）知道或者应当知道他人利用公民个人信息实施犯罪，向其出售或者提供的；  
（三）非法获取、出售或者提供行踪轨迹信息、通信内容、征信信息、财产信息五十条以上的；  
（四）非法获取、出售或者提供住宿信息、通信记录、健康生理信息、交易信息等其他可能影响人身、财产安全的公民个人信息五百条以上的；  
（五）非法获取、出售或者提供第三项、第四项规定以外的公民个人信息五千条以上的；  
（六）数量未达到第三项至第五项规定标准，但是按相应比例合计达到有关数量标准的；  
（七）违法所得五千元以上的；  
（八）将在履行职责或者提供服务过程中获得的公民个人信息出售或者提供给他人，数量或者数额达到第三项至第七项规定标准一半以上的；  
（九）曾因侵犯公民个人信息受过刑事处罚或者二年内受过行政处罚，又非法获取、出售或者提供公民个人信息的；  
（十）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）造成被害人死亡、重伤、精神失常或者被绑架等严重后果的；  
（二）造成重大经济损失或者恶劣社会影响的；  
（三）数量或者数额达到前款第三项至第八项规定标准十倍以上的；  
（四）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 5** Illegally obtaining, selling, or providing citizens' personal information, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Selling or providing whereabouts information that is used by others for committing crimes;  
(2) Knowing or should have known that others use citizens' personal information to commit crimes, and selling or providing such information to them;  
(3) Illegally obtaining, selling, or providing more than 50 items of whereabouts information, communication content, credit information, or property information;  
(4) Illegally obtaining, selling, or providing more than 500 items of citizens' personal information such as accommodation information, communication records, health and physiological information, transaction information, and other information that may affect personal or property safety;  
(5) Illegally obtaining, selling, or providing more than 5,000 items of citizens' personal information other than those specified in subparagraphs (3) and (4);  
(6) The quantity does not meet the standards specified in subparagraphs (3) to (5), but the combined quantity in corresponding proportions reaches the relevant quantity standard;  
(7) The illegal gains are more than 5,000 yuan;  
(8) Selling or providing to others citizens' personal information obtained during the performance of duties or provision of services, where the quantity or amount reaches more than half of the standards specified in subparagraphs (3) to (7);  
(9) Having previously received criminal punishment for infringing citizens' personal information, or having received an administrative penalty within two years, and then again illegally obtaining, selling, or providing citizens' personal information;  
(10) Other circumstances that are serious.  
Where the conduct described in the preceding paragraph falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "particularly serious circumstances" as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causing serious consequences such as death, serious injury, mental disorder, or kidnapping of the victim;  
(2) Causing significant economic losses or adverse social impact;  
(3) The quantity or amount reaches more than ten times the standards specified in subparagraphs (3) to (8) of the preceding paragraph;  
(4) Other circumstances that are particularly serious.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　为合法经营活动而非法购买、收受[本解释第五条](#art-5)第一款第三项、第四项规定以外的公民个人信息，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）利用非法购买、收受的公民个人信息获利五万元以上的；  
（二）曾因侵犯公民个人信息受过刑事处罚或者二年内受过行政处罚，又非法购买、收受公民个人信息的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，将购买、收受的公民个人信息非法出售或者提供的，定罪量刑标准适用[本解释第五条](#art-5)的规定。

**Article 6** Illegally purchasing or receiving citizens' personal information other than that specified in subparagraphs (3) and (4) of paragraph 1 of Article 5 of this Interpretation for the purpose of lawful business activities, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Making profits of more than 50,000 yuan by using the illegally purchased or received citizens' personal information;  
(2) Having previously received criminal punishment for infringing citizens' personal information, or having received an administrative penalty within two years, and then again illegally purchasing or receiving citizens' personal information;  
(3) Other circumstances that are serious.  
Where the conduct described in the preceding paragraph involves illegally selling or providing the purchased or received citizens' personal information, the conviction and sentencing standards shall apply the provisions of Article 5 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　单位犯[刑法第二百五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-253)之一规定之罪的，依照本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。

**Article 7** Where a unit commits a crime as provided in Article 253-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible persons shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards for natural persons committing the corresponding crimes as provided in this Interpretation, and the unit shall be fined.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　设立用于实施非法获取、出售或者提供公民个人信息违法犯罪活动的网站、通讯群组，情节严重的，应当依照[刑法第二百八十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-287)之一的规定，以非法利用信息网络罪定罪处罚；同时构成侵犯公民个人信息罪的，依照侵犯公民个人信息罪定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Establishing websites or communication groups for the purpose of carrying out illegal and criminal activities of illegally obtaining, selling, or providing citizens' personal information, if the circumstances are serious, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of illegally using information networks in accordance with Article 287-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China; if it also constitutes the crime of infringing citizens' personal information, it shall be convicted and punished as the crime of infringing citizens' personal information.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　网络服务提供者拒不履行法律、行政法规规定的信息网络安全管理义务，经监管部门责令采取改正措施而拒不改正，致使用户的公民个人信息泄露，造成严重后果的，应当依照[刑法第二百八十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-286)之一的规定，以拒不履行信息网络安全管理义务罪定罪处罚。

**Article 9** Where a network service provider refuses to perform the information network security management obligations prescribed by laws and administrative regulations, and after being ordered by the regulatory authority to take corrective measures, refuses to correct, resulting in the leakage of users' citizens' personal information and causing serious consequences, it shall be convicted and punished as the crime of refusing to perform information network security management obligations in accordance with Article 286-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　实施侵犯公民个人信息犯罪，不属于“情节特别严重”，行为人系初犯，全部退赃，并确有悔罪表现的，可以认定为情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；确有必要判处刑罚的，应当从宽处罚。

**Article 10** Where a person commits a crime of infringing citizens' personal information that does not fall within "particularly serious circumstances", and is a first offender, has fully returned the illegal gains, and truly shows repentance, the circumstances may be deemed as minor, and the person may be subject to non-prosecution or exemption from criminal punishment; if it is truly necessary to impose a penalty, a lenient punishment shall be imposed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　非法获取公民个人信息后又出售或者提供的，公民个人信息的条数不重复计算。  
向不同单位或者个人分别出售、提供同一公民个人信息的，公民个人信息的条数累计计算。  
对批量公民个人信息的条数，根据查获的数量直接认定，但是有证据证明信息不真实或者重复的除外。

**Article 11** Where citizens' personal information is sold or provided after being illegally obtained, the number of items of citizens' personal information shall not be counted repeatedly.  
Where the same citizens' personal information is sold or provided separately to different units or individuals, the number of items of citizens' personal information shall be counted cumulatively.  
The number of items of batch citizens' personal information shall be directly determined based on the quantity seized, except where there is evidence proving that the information is untrue or duplicated.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　对于侵犯公民个人信息犯罪，应当综合考虑犯罪的危害程度、犯罪的违法所得数额以及被告人的前科情况、认罪悔罪态度等，依法判处罚金。罚金数额一般在违法所得的一倍以上五倍以下。

**Article 12** For crimes of infringing citizens' personal information, the fine shall be imposed according to law by comprehensively considering the extent of harm of the crime, the amount of illegal gains from the crime, and the defendant's criminal record, admission of guilt and repentance attitude, etc. The amount of the fine shall generally be not less than one time and not more than five times the illegal gains.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　本解释自2017年6月1日起施行。

**Article 13** This Interpretation shall come into force as of June 1, 2017.
