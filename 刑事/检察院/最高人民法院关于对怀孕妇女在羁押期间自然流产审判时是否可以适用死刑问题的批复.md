### 最高人民法院关于对怀孕妇女在羁押期间自然流产审判时是否可以适用死刑问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对怀孕妇女在羁押期间自然流产  
  
审判时是否可以适用死刑问题的批复  
  
法释〔1998〕18号  
  
（1998年8月4日最高人民法院审判委员会第1010次会议通过，自1998年8月13日起施行）  
  
河北省高级人民法院：  
  
你院冀高法〔1998〕40号《关于审判时对怀孕妇女在公安预审羁押期间自然流产，是否适用死刑的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
怀孕妇女因涉嫌犯罪在羁押期间自然流产后，又因同一事实被起诉、交付审判的，应当视为“审判的时候怀孕的妇女”，依法不适用死刑。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Higher People's Court of Hebei Province:  
Your Court's Request No. 40 (1998) titled "Regarding the Applicability of the Death Penalty to Pregnant Women Who Suffer a Miscarriage During Custody for Public Security Preliminary Investigation at the Time of Adjudication" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Pregnant women who suffer a miscarriage during custody due to suspected criminal activity and are subsequently prosecuted and subjected to adjudication for the same facts shall be deemed "pregnant women at the time of adjudication" and shall not be subject to the death penalty according to law.  
This is hereby replied.
