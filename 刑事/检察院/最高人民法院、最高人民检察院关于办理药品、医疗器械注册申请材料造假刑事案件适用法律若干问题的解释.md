# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理药品、医疗器械注册申请材料造假刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕15号  
**公布日期**：2017-04-10  
**生效日期**：2017-09-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理药品、医疗器械注册申请材料造假刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2017年4月10日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1714次会议、2017年6月8日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第65次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年9月1日起施行。 2017年8月14日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理药品、医疗器械注册申请材料 造假刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2017〕15号 为依法惩治药品、医疗器械注册申请材料造假的犯罪行为，维护人民群众生命健康权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　药物非临床研究机构、药物临床试验机构、合同研究组织的工作人员，故意提供虚假的药物非临床研究报告、药物临床试验报告及相关材料的，应当认定为刑法第二百二十九条规定的“故意提供虚假证明文件”。 实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百二十九条规定的“情节严重”，以提供虚假证明文件罪处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金： （一）在药物非临床研究或者药物临床试验过程中故意使用虚假试验用药品的； （二）瞒报与药物临床试验用药品相关的严重不良事件的； （三）故意损毁原始药物非临床研究数据或者药物临床试验数据的； （四）编造受试动物信息、受试者信息、主要试验过程记录、研究数据、检测数据等药物非临床研究数据或者药物临床试验数据，影响药品安全性、有效性评价结果的； （五）曾因在申请药品、医疗器械注册过程中提供虚假证明材料受过刑事处罚或者二年内受过行政处罚，又提供虚假证明材料的； （六）其他情节严重的情形。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　药物非临床研究机构、药物临床试验机构、合同研究组织的工作人员，故意提供虚假的药物非临床研究报告、药物临床试验报告及相关材料的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)规定的“故意提供虚假证明文件”。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)规定的“情节严重”，以提供虚假证明文件罪处五年以下有期徒刑或者拘役，并处罚金：  
（一）在药物非临床研究或者药物临床试验过程中故意使用虚假试验用药品的；  
（二）瞒报与药物临床试验用药品相关的严重不良事件的；  
（三）故意损毁原始药物非临床研究数据或者药物临床试验数据的；  
（四）编造受试动物信息、受试者信息、主要试验过程记录、研究数据、检测数据等药物非临床研究数据或者药物临床试验数据，影响药品安全性、有效性评价结果的；  
（五）曾因在申请药品、医疗器械注册过程中提供虚假证明材料受过刑事处罚或者二年内受过行政处罚，又提供虚假证明材料的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 1** : Where a staff member of a drug non-clinical study institution, a drug clinical trial institution, or a contract research organization intentionally provides false drug non-clinical study reports, drug clinical trial reports, and related materials, it shall be deemed as "intentionally providing false certification documents" as stipulated in Article 229 of the Criminal Law.  
Where the conduct set forth in the preceding paragraph falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as stipulated in Article 229 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years or criminal detention and shall also be fined for the crime of providing false certification documents:  
(1) intentionally using counterfeit trial drugs during the drug non-clinical study or drug clinical trial process;  
(2) concealing or failing to report serious adverse events related to drugs used in clinical trials;  
(3) intentionally damaging original drug non-clinical study data or drug clinical trial data;  
(4) fabricating information on test animals, test subjects, records of main trial processes, research data, test data, or other drug non-clinical study data or drug clinical trial data, thereby affecting the evaluation results of drug safety and efficacy;  
(5) having previously received criminal punishment for providing false certification materials in the process of applying for drug or medical device registration, or having received an administrative penalty within the preceding two years, and again providing false certification materials;  
(6) other circumstances of a serious nature.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的行为，索取或者非法收受他人财物的，应当依照[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第二款规定，以提供虚假证明文件罪处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；同时构成提供虚假证明文件罪和受贿罪、非国家工作人员受贿罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 2** : Where a person who commits the conduct set forth in Article 1 of this Interpretation demands or illegally accepts property from others, he or she shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years and shall also be fined for the crime of providing false certification documents; where the conduct concurrently constitutes the crime of providing false certification documents and the crime of accepting bribes or the crime of accepting bribes by a non-state functionary, the conviction and sentencing shall be in accordance with the provisions on the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　药品注册申请单位的工作人员，故意使用符合[本解释第一条](#art-1)第二款规定的虚假药物非临床研究报告、药物临床试验报告及相关材料，骗取药品批准证明文件生产、销售药品的，应当依照[刑法第一百四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-141)规定，以生产、销售假药罪定罪处罚。

**Article 3** : Where a staff member of a drug registration applicant unit intentionally uses false drug non-clinical study reports, drug clinical trial reports, and related materials that meet the conditions set forth in paragraph 2 of Article 1 of this Interpretation to fraudulently obtain drug approval certificates for manufacturing and selling drugs, he or she shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 141 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of manufacturing and selling counterfeit drugs.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　药品注册申请单位的工作人员指使药物非临床研究机构、药物临床试验机构、合同研究组织的工作人员提供[本解释第一条](#art-1)第二款规定的虚假药物非临床研究报告、药物临床试验报告及相关材料的，以提供虚假证明文件罪的共同犯罪论处。  
具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为前款规定的“指使”，但有相反证据的除外：  
（一）明知有关机构、组织不具备相应条件或者能力，仍委托其进行药物非临床研究、药物临床试验的；  
（二）支付的价款明显异于正常费用的。  
药品注册申请单位的工作人员和药物非临床研究机构、药物临床试验机构、合同研究组织的工作人员共同实施第一款规定的行为，骗取药品批准证明文件生产、销售药品，同时构成提供虚假证明文件罪和生产、销售假药罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 4** : Where a staff member of a drug registration applicant unit instructs a staff member of a drug non-clinical study institution, a drug clinical trial institution, or a contract research organization to provide false drug non-clinical study reports, drug clinical trial reports, and related materials that meet the conditions set forth in paragraph 2 of Article 1 of this Interpretation, he or she shall be dealt with as a joint offender of the crime of providing false certification documents.  
Under any of the following circumstances, it may be deemed as "instruction" as set forth in the preceding paragraph, unless there is evidence to the contrary:  
(1) where the relevant institution or organization is known to lack the corresponding conditions or capabilities, but the drug non-clinical study or drug clinical trial is still entrusted to it;  
(2) where the price paid is obviously different from the normal cost.  
Where a staff member of a drug registration applicant unit and a staff member of a drug non-clinical study institution, a drug clinical trial institution, or a contract research organization jointly commit the conduct set forth in the first paragraph to fraudulently obtain drug approval certificates for manufacturing and selling drugs, and the conduct concurrently constitutes the crime of providing false certification documents and the crime of manufacturing and selling counterfeit drugs, conviction and sentencing shall be in accordance with the provisions on the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在医疗器械注册申请中，故意提供、使用虚假的医疗器械临床试验报告及相关材料的，参照适用[本解释第一条](#art-1)至第四条规定。

**Article 5** : Where, in the application for registration of medical devices, false medical device clinical trial reports and related materials are intentionally provided or used, the provisions of Articles 1 to 4 of this Interpretation shall apply mutatis mutandis.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　单位犯[本解释第一条](#art-1)至第五条规定之罪的，对单位判处罚金，并依照本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚。

**Article 6** : Where a unit commits the crimes set forth in Articles 1 to 5 of this Interpretation, a fine shall be imposed on the unit, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards for the corresponding crimes committed by natural persons as set forth in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　对药品、医疗器械注册申请负有核查职责的国家机关工作人员，滥用职权或者玩忽职守，导致使用虚假证明材料的药品、医疗器械获得注册，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，应当依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)规定，以滥用职权罪或者玩忽职守罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 7** : Where a state functionary responsible for the examination and verification of drug or medical device registration applications abuses his or her power or neglects his or her duties, causing drugs or medical devices for which false certification materials have been used to obtain registration, thereby causing heavy losses to public property, the state, or the people's interests, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 397 of the Criminal Law for the crime of abuse of power or the crime of dereliction of duty.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对是否属于虚假的药物非临床研究报告、药物或者医疗器械临床试验报告及相关材料，是否影响药品或者医疗器械安全性、有效性评价结果，以及是否属于严重不良事件等专门性问题难以确定的，可以根据国家药品监督管理部门设置或者指定的药品、医疗器械审评等机构出具的意见，结合其他证据作出认定。

**Article 8** : Where it is difficult to determine specialized issues such as whether the drug non-clinical study reports, drug or medical device clinical trial reports, and related materials are false, whether they affect the evaluation results of the safety and efficacy of the drug or medical device, or whether they constitute serious adverse events, a determination may be made based on the opinions issued by the drug or medical device review institutions established or designated by the national drug regulatory authority, in conjunction with other evidence.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本解释所称“合同研究组织”，是指受药品或者医疗器械注册申请单位、药物非临床研究机构、药物或者医疗器械临床试验机构的委托，从事试验方案设计、数据统计、分析测试、监查稽查等与非临床研究或者临床试验相关活动的单位。

**Article 9** : The term "contract research organization" as used in this Interpretation refers to an organization that is entrusted by a drug or medical device registration applicant unit, a drug non-clinical study institution, or a drug or medical device clinical trial institution to carry out activities related to non-clinical studies or clinical trials, such as trial protocol design, data statistics, analysis and testing, monitoring and auditing.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　本解释自2017年9月1日起施行。

**Article 10** : This Interpretation shall come into force as of September 1, 2017.
