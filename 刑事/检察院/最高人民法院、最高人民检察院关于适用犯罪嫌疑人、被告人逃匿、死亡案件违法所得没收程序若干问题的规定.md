# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于适用犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、死亡案件违法所得没收程序若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕1号  
**公布日期**：2016-12-26  
**生效日期**：2017-01-05

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于适用犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、死亡案件违法所得没收程序若干问题的规定》已于2016年12月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1705次会议、最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第59次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年1月5日起施行。 2017年1月4日 最高人民法院最高人民检察院 关于适用犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、死亡案件违法所得没收程序若干问题的规定 法释〔2017〕1号 为依法适用犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、死亡案件违法所得没收程序，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法》等法律规定，现就办理相关案件具体适用法律若干问题规定如下： 第一条　下列犯罪案件，应当认定为刑事诉讼法第二百八十条第一款规定的“犯罪案件”： （一）贪污、挪用公款、巨额财产来源不明、隐瞒境外存款、私分国有资产、私分罚没财物犯罪案件； （二）受贿、单位受贿、利用影响力受贿、行贿、对有影响力的人行贿、对单位行贿、介绍贿赂、单位行贿犯罪案件； （三）组织、领导、参加恐怖组织，帮助恐怖活动，准备实施恐怖活动，宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义、煽动实施恐怖活动，利用极端主义破坏法律实施，强制穿戴宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义服饰、标志，非法持有宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义物品犯罪案件； （四）危害国家安全、走私、洗钱、金融诈骗、黑社会性质的组织、毒品犯罪案件。 电信诈骗、网络诈骗犯罪案件，依照前款规定的犯罪案件处理。 第二条　在省、自治区、直辖市或者全国范围内具有较大影响，或者犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿境外的，应当认定为刑事诉讼法第二百八十条第一款规定的“重大”。 第三条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人为逃避侦查和刑事追究潜逃、隐匿，或者在刑事诉讼过程中脱逃的，应当认定为刑事诉讼法第二百八十条第一款规定的“逃匿”。 犯罪嫌疑人、被告人因意外事故下落不明满二年，或者因意外事故下落不明，经有关机关证明其不可能生存的，依照前款规定处理。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　下列犯罪案件，应当认定为[刑事诉讼法第二百八十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-280)第一款规定的“犯罪案件”：  
（一）贪污、挪用公款、巨额财产来源不明、隐瞒境外存款、私分国有资产、私分罚没财物犯罪案件；  
（二）受贿、单位受贿、利用影响力受贿、行贿、对有影响力的人行贿、对单位行贿、介绍贿赂、单位行贿犯罪案件；  
（三）组织、领导、参加恐怖组织，帮助恐怖活动，准备实施恐怖活动，宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义、煽动实施恐怖活动，利用极端主义破坏法律实施，强制穿戴宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义服饰、标志，非法持有宣扬恐怖主义、极端主义物品犯罪案件；  
（四）危害国家安全、走私、洗钱、金融诈骗、黑社会性质的组织、毒品犯罪案件。  
电信诈骗、网络诈骗犯罪案件，依照前款规定的犯罪案件处理。

**Article 1** The following criminal cases shall be determined as "criminal cases" as specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 280 of the Criminal Procedure Law:  
(1) criminal cases of embezzlement, misappropriation of public funds, holding a huge amount of property with unidentified sources, concealing deposits overseas, illegally distributing state-owned assets, and illegally distributing confiscated property;  
(2) criminal cases of acceptance of bribes, acceptance of bribes by a unit, acceptance of bribes by using influence, offering bribes, offering bribes to persons with influence, offering bribes to a unit, introduction of bribes, and offering bribes by a unit;  
(3) criminal cases of organizing, leading, or participating in a terrorist organization, assisting terrorist activities, preparing to commit terrorist activities, advocating terrorism or extremism, inciting the commission of terrorist activities, using extremism to sabotage the implementation of laws, coercing others to wear clothing or symbols that advocate terrorism or extremism, and illegally possessing articles that advocate terrorism or extremism;  
(4) criminal cases of endangering national security, smuggling, money laundering, financial fraud, organizing mafia-type organizations, and drug-related crimes.  
Telecommunications fraud and cyber fraud criminal cases shall be handled in accordance with the criminal cases specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　在省、自治区、直辖市或者全国范围内具有较大影响，或者犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿境外的，应当认定为[刑事诉讼法第二百八十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-280)第一款规定的“重大”。

**Article 2** Where a case has relatively significant influence within a province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the Central Government, or the whole country, or where a criminal suspect or defendant flees outside the territory, it shall be determined as "major" as specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 280 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人为逃避侦查和刑事追究潜逃、隐匿，或者在刑事诉讼过程中脱逃的，应当认定为[刑事诉讼法第二百八十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-280)第一款规定的“逃匿”。  
犯罪嫌疑人、被告人因意外事故下落不明满二年，或者因意外事故下落不明，经有关机关证明其不可能生存的，依照前款规定处理。

**Article 3** Where a criminal suspect or defendant goes into hiding or conceals himself to evade investigation and criminal prosecution, or escapes during criminal proceedings, it shall be determined as "fleeing" as specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 280 of the Criminal Procedure Law.  
Where a criminal suspect or defendant has been missing for two years due to an accident, or is missing due to an accident and relevant authorities have proven it impossible for him to survive, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人死亡，依照刑法规定应当追缴其违法所得及其他涉案财产的，人民检察院可以向人民法院提出没收违法所得的申请。

**Article 4** Where a criminal suspect or defendant dies and, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Law, his illegal gains and other property involved in the case shall be recovered, the People's Procuratorate may file an application with the People's Court for confiscation of illegal gains.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　公安机关发布通缉令或者公安部通过国际刑警组织发布红色国际通报，应当认定为[刑事诉讼法第二百八十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-280)第一款规定的“通缉”。

**Article 5** Where a public security organ issues a wanted order or the Ministry of Public Security issues a Red Notice through INTERPOL, it shall be determined as "wanted" as specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 280 of the Criminal Procedure Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　通过实施犯罪直接或者间接产生、获得的任何财产，应当认定为[刑事诉讼法第二百八十条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-280)第一款规定的“违法所得”。  
违法所得已经部分或者全部转变、转化为其他财产的，转变、转化后的财产应当视为前款规定的“违法所得”。  
来自违法所得转变、转化后的财产收益，或者来自已经与违法所得相混合财产中违法所得相应部分的收益，应当视为第一款规定的“违法所得”。

**Article 6** Any property directly or indirectly derived or obtained through the commission of a crime shall be determined as "illegal gains" as specified in Paragraph 1 of Article 280 of the Criminal Procedure Law.  
Where illegal gains have been partially or wholly converted or transformed into other property, such property after conversion or transformation shall be regarded as "illegal gains" as specified in the preceding paragraph.  
Income from property after conversion or transformation of illegal gains, or income from the corresponding portion of illegal gains in property that has been mixed with illegal gains, shall be regarded as "illegal gains" as specified in Paragraph 1.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[刑事诉讼法第二百八十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-281)第三款规定的“利害关系人”包括犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的近亲属和其他对申请没收的财产主张权利的自然人和单位。  
[刑事诉讼法第二百八十一条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-281)第二款、第二百八十二条第二款规定的“其他利害关系人”是指前款规定的“其他对申请没收的财产主张权利的自然人和单位”。

**Article 7** The "interested parties" as specified in Paragraph 3 of Article 281 of the Criminal Procedure Law include the close relatives of the criminal suspect or defendant, and other natural persons and units that claim rights over the property sought to be confiscated.  
The "other interested parties" as specified in Paragraph 2 of Article 281 and Paragraph 2 of Article 282 of the Criminal Procedure Law refer to "other natural persons and units that claim rights over the property sought to be confiscated" as specified in the preceding paragraph.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　人民检察院向人民法院提出没收违法所得的申请，应当制作没收违法所得申请书。  
没收违法所得申请书应当载明以下内容：  
（一）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的基本情况；  
（二）案由及案件来源；  
（三）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人涉嫌犯罪的事实及相关证据材料；  
（四）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、被通缉、脱逃、下落不明、死亡的情况；  
（五）申请没收的财产的种类、数量、价值、所在地以及已查封、扣押、冻结财产清单和相关法律手续；  
（六）申请没收的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产的相关事实及证据材料；  
（七）提出没收违法所得申请的理由和法律依据；  
（八）有无利害关系人以及利害关系人的姓名、身份、住址、联系方式；  
（九）其他应当载明的内容。  
上述材料需要翻译件的，人民检察院应当将翻译件随没收违法所得申请书一并移送人民法院。

**Article 8** When filing an application with the People's Court for confiscation of illegal gains, the People's Procuratorate shall prepare a written application for confiscation of illegal gains.  
The application for confiscation of illegal gains shall include the following:  
(1) basic information of the criminal suspect or defendant;  
(2) cause of action and source of the case;  
(3) facts of the crime suspected of being committed by the criminal suspect or defendant and relevant evidence materials;  
(4) circumstances of the criminal suspect or defendant fleeing, being wanted, escaping, missing, or dying;  
(5) types, quantity, value, and location of the property sought to be confiscated, and the list of property that has been sealed up, seized, or frozen, along with relevant legal formalities;  
(6) relevant facts and evidence materials showing that the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case;  
(7) reasons and legal basis for filing the application for confiscation of illegal gains;  
(8) whether there are interested parties, and the names, identities, addresses, and contact information of such interested parties;  
(9) other matters that shall be included.  
If the above materials require translation, the People's Procuratorate shall submit the translation together with the application for confiscation of illegal gains to the People's Court.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　对于没收违法所得的申请，人民法院应当在三十日内审查完毕，并根据以下情形分别处理：  
（一）属于没收违法所得申请受案范围和本院管辖，且材料齐全、有证据证明有犯罪事实的，应当受理；  
（二）不属于没收违法所得申请受案范围或者本院管辖的，应当退回人民检察院；  
（三）对于没收违法所得申请不符合“有证据证明有犯罪事实”标准要求的，应当通知人民检察院撤回申请，人民检察院应当撤回；  
（四）材料不全的，应当通知人民检察院在七日内补送，七日内不能补送的，应当退回人民检察院。

**Article 9** Upon receipt of an application for confiscation of illegal gains, the People's Court shall complete its review within thirty days and handle the matter respectively according to the following circumstances:  
(1) if the application falls within the scope of acceptance for confiscation of illegal gains applications and the jurisdiction of the court, and the materials are complete and there is evidence proving the criminal facts, the court shall accept the application;  
(2) if the application does not fall within the scope of acceptance for confiscation of illegal gains applications or the jurisdiction of the court, the court shall return the application to the People's Procuratorate;  
(3) if the application for confiscation of illegal gains does not meet the standard of "there is evidence proving the criminal facts," the court shall notify the People's Procuratorate to withdraw the application, and the People's Procuratorate shall withdraw it;  
(4) if the materials are incomplete, the court shall notify the People's Procuratorate to submit supplementary materials within seven days; if the materials cannot be submitted within seven days, the court shall return the application to the People's Procuratorate.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　同时具备以下情形的，应当认定为[本规定第九条](#art-9)规定的“有证据证明有犯罪事实”：  
（一）有证据证明发生了犯罪事实；  
（二）有证据证明该犯罪事实是犯罪嫌疑人、被告人实施的；  
（三）证明犯罪嫌疑人、被告人实施犯罪行为的证据真实、合法。

**Article 10** Where all of the following circumstances are present, it shall be determined that there is "evidence proving the criminal facts" as specified in Article 9 of these Provisions:  
(1) there is evidence proving that a criminal fact has occurred;  
(2) there is evidence proving that the criminal fact was committed by the criminal suspect or defendant;  
(3) the evidence proving that the criminal suspect or defendant committed the criminal act is authentic and lawful.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　人民法院受理没收违法所得的申请后，应当在十五日内发布公告，公告期为六个月。公告期间不适用中止、中断、延长的规定。  
公告应当载明以下内容：  
（一）案由、案件来源以及属于本院管辖；  
（二）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的基本情况；  
（三）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人涉嫌犯罪的事实；  
（四）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、被通缉、脱逃、下落不明、死亡的情况；  
（五）申请没收的财产的种类、数量、价值、所在地以及已查封、扣押、冻结财产的清单和相关法律手续；  
（六）申请没收的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产的相关事实；  
（七）申请没收的理由和法律依据；  
（八）利害关系人申请参加诉讼的期限、方式以及未按照该期限、方式申请参加诉讼可能承担的不利法律后果；  
（九）其他应当公告的情况。

**Article 11** After accepting an application for confiscation of illegal gains, the People's Court shall issue a public notice within fifteen days, and the notice period shall be six months. The provisions on suspension, interruption, and extension shall not apply to the notice period.  
The public notice shall include the following:  
(1) cause of action, source of the case, and that the case falls within the jurisdiction of the court;  
(2) basic information of the criminal suspect or defendant;  
(3) facts of the crime suspected of being committed by the criminal suspect or defendant;  
(4) circumstances of the criminal suspect or defendant fleeing, being wanted, escaping, missing, or dying;  
(5) types, quantity, value, and location of the property sought to be confiscated, and the list of property that has been sealed up, seized, or frozen, along with relevant legal formalities;  
(6) relevant facts showing that the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case;  
(7) reasons and legal basis for filing the application for confiscation;  
(8) time limit and method for interested parties to apply for participation in the proceedings, and the adverse legal consequences that may arise from failing to apply within such time limit or by such method;  
(9) other circumstances that shall be announced.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　公告应当在全国公开发行的报纸、信息网络等媒体和最高人民法院的官方网站刊登、发布，并在人民法院公告栏张贴。必要时，公告可以在犯罪地、犯罪嫌疑人、被告人居住地或者被申请没收财产所在地张贴。公告最后被刊登、发布、张贴日期为公告日期。人民法院张贴公告的，应当采取拍照、录像等方式记录张贴过程。  
人民法院已经掌握境内利害关系人联系方式的，应当直接送达含有公告内容的通知；直接送达有困难的，可以委托代为送达、邮寄送达。经受送达人同意的，可以采用传真、电子邮件等能够确认其收悉的方式告知其公告内容，并记录在案；人民法院已经掌握境外犯罪嫌疑人、被告人、利害关系人联系方式，经受送达人同意的，可以采用传真、电子邮件等能够确认其收悉的方式告知其公告内容，并记录在案；受送达人未作出同意意思表示，或者人民法院未掌握境外犯罪嫌疑人、被告人、利害关系人联系方式，其所在地国（区）主管机关明确提出应当向受送达人送达含有公告内容的通知的，受理没收违法所得申请案件的人民法院可以决定是否送达。决定送达的，应当将公告内容层报最高人民法院，由最高人民法院依照刑事司法协助条约、多边公约，或者按照对等互惠原则，请求受送达人所在地国（区）的主管机关协助送达。

**Article 12** The public notice shall be published or posted in newspapers with nationwide circulation, information networks, and other media, as well as on the official website of the Supreme People's Court, and shall be posted on the bulletin board of the People's Court. Where necessary, the notice may be posted at the place where the crime was committed, the place of residence of the criminal suspect or defendant, or the place where the property sought to be confiscated is located. The date of the last publication, posting, or release shall be the date of the notice. Where the People's Court posts the notice, it shall record the posting process by taking photographs or video recordings.  
If the People's Court is already aware of the contact information of an interested party within the territory, it shall directly serve a notice containing the content of the public notice; if direct service is difficult, service may be entrusted or made by mail. With the consent of the person to be served, the content of the notice may be notified by fax, email, or other means through which receipt can be confirmed, and such notification shall be recorded in the case file; if the People's Court is already aware of the contact information of an overseas criminal suspect, defendant, or interested party, with the consent of the person to be served, the content of the notice may be notified by fax, email, or other means through which receipt can be confirmed, and such notification shall be recorded in the case file; if the person to be served has not indicated consent, or if the People's Court is not aware of the contact information of the overseas criminal suspect, defendant, or interested party, and the competent authority of the country (region) where the person is located explicitly states that a notice containing the content of the public notice shall be served on the person, the People's Court accepting the application for confiscation of illegal gains may decide whether to serve the notice. If it decides to serve, it shall report the content of the public notice level by level to the Supreme People's Court, and the Supreme People's Court shall, in accordance with criminal judicial assistance treaties, multilateral conventions, or on the principle of reciprocity, request the competent authority of the country (region) where the person to be served is located to assist in service.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　利害关系人申请参加诉讼的，应当在公告期间内提出，并提供与犯罪嫌疑人、被告人关系的证明材料或者证明其可以对违法所得及其他涉案财产主张权利的证据材料。  
利害关系人可以委托诉讼代理人参加诉讼。利害关系人在境外委托的，应当委托具有中华人民共和国律师资格并依法取得执业证书的律师，依照《最高人民法院关于适用〈中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法〉的解释》第四百零三条的规定对授权委托进行公证、认证。  
利害关系人在公告期满后申请参加诉讼，能够合理说明理由的，人民法院应当准许。

**Article 13** An interested party that applies to participate in the proceedings shall do so within the notice period, and shall provide materials proving the relationship with the criminal suspect or defendant, or evidence materials proving that it may claim rights over the illegal gains or other property involved in the case.  
An interested party may entrust a litigation agent to participate in the proceedings. If the interested party entrusts an agent from outside the territory, it shall entrust a lawyer who holds a lawyer's qualification of the People's Republic of China and has lawfully obtained a practicing certificate, and shall notarize and authenticate the power of attorney in accordance with the provisions of Article 403 of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on the Application of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If an interested party applies to participate in the proceedings after the expiration of the notice period and can reasonably justify the reason, the People's Court shall permit it.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　人民法院在公告期满后由合议庭对没收违法所得申请案件进行审理。  
利害关系人申请参加及委托诉讼代理人参加诉讼的，人民法院应当开庭审理。利害关系人及其诉讼代理人无正当理由拒不到庭，且无其他利害关系人和其他诉讼代理人参加诉讼的，人民法院可以不开庭审理。  
人民法院对没收违法所得申请案件开庭审理的，人民检察院应当派员出席。  
人民法院确定开庭日期后，应当将开庭的时间、地点通知人民检察院、利害关系人及其诉讼代理人、证人、鉴定人员、翻译人员。通知书应当依照[本规定第十二条](#art-12)第二款规定的方式至迟在开庭审理三日前送达；受送达人在境外的，至迟在开庭审理三十日前送达。

**Article 14** After the expiration of the notice period, the People's Court shall have a collegial panel hear the case of the application for confiscation of illegal gains.  
If an interested party applies to participate and entrusts a litigation agent to participate in the proceedings, the People's Court shall hold a hearing in court. If the interested party and its litigation agent refuse to appear in court without justifiable reasons, and no other interested party or litigation agent participates in the proceedings, the People's Court may not hold a hearing in court.  
If the People's Court holds a hearing in court for a case concerning an application for confiscation of illegal gains, the People's Procuratorate shall send a representative to attend.  
After determining the hearing date, the People's Court shall notify the People's Procuratorate, the interested parties and their litigation agents, witnesses, expert evaluators, and interpreters of the time and place of the hearing. The notice shall be served at least three days before the hearing in accordance with the method specified in Paragraph 2 of Article 12 of these Provisions; if the person to be served is outside the territory, the notice shall be served at least thirty days before the hearing.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　出庭的检察人员应当宣读没收违法所得申请书，并在法庭调查阶段就申请没收的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产等相关事实出示、宣读证据。  
对于确有必要出示但可能妨碍正在或者即将进行的刑事侦查的证据，针对该证据的法庭调查不公开进行。  
利害关系人及其诉讼代理人对申请没收的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产等相关事实及证据有异议的，可以提出意见；对申请没收的财产主张权利的，应当出示相关证据。

**Article 15** The procurator appearing in court shall read out the application for confiscation of illegal gains and, during the court investigation stage, present and read out evidence regarding the facts that the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case.  
For evidence that is necessary to present but may impede an ongoing or impending criminal investigation, the court investigation of such evidence shall be conducted in camera.  
If the interested party or its litigation agent has objections to the facts and evidence that the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case, it may raise opinions; if it claims rights over the property sought to be confiscated, it shall present relevant evidence.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　人民法院经审理认为，申请没收的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产的，除依法应当返还被害人的以外，应当予以没收；申请没收的财产不属于违法所得或者其他涉案财产的，应当裁定驳回申请，解除查封、扣押、冻结措施。

**Article 16** If, after trial, the People's Court finds that the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case, it shall order confiscation, except for property that shall be returned to the victim according to law; if the property sought to be confiscated does not constitute illegal gains or other property involved in the case, it shall render a ruling to dismiss the application and lift the measures of sealing up, seizure, or freezing.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条　申请没收的财产具有高度可能属于违法所得及其他涉案财产的，应当认定为[本规定第十六条](#art-16)规定的“申请没收的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产”。  
巨额财产来源不明犯罪案件中，没有利害关系人对违法所得及其他涉案财产主张权利，或者利害关系人对违法所得及其他涉案财产虽然主张权利但提供的相关证据没有达到相应证明标准的，应当视为[本规定第十六条](#art-16)规定的“申请没收的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产”。

**Article 17** If there is a high probability that the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case, it shall be determined that "the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case" as specified in Article 16 of these Provisions.  
In a criminal case involving holding a huge amount of property with unidentified sources, if no interested party claims rights over the illegal gains or other property involved in the case, or if an interested party claims rights but the relevant evidence provided does not meet the corresponding standard of proof, it shall be deemed that "the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case" as specified in Article 16 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条　利害关系人非因故意或者重大过失在第一审期间未参加诉讼，在第二审期间申请参加诉讼的，人民法院应当准许，并发回原审人民法院重新审判。

**Article 18** If an interested party, without intentional fault or gross negligence, fails to participate in the proceedings during the first instance and applies to participate in the proceedings during the second instance, the People's Court shall permit it and remand the case to the original People's Court for retrial.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条　犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿境外，委托诉讼代理人申请参加诉讼，且违法所得或者其他涉案财产所在地国（区）主管机关明确提出意见予以支持的，人民法院可以准许。  
人民法院准许参加诉讼的，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的诉讼代理人依照本规定关于利害关系人的诉讼代理人的规定行使诉讼权利。

**Article 19** If a criminal suspect or defendant flees outside the territory, entrusts a litigation agent to apply for participation in the proceedings, and the competent authority of the country (region) where the illegal gains or other property involved in the case is located explicitly expresses support, the People's Court may permit the participation.  
If the People's Court permits participation, the litigation agent of the criminal suspect or defendant shall exercise litigation rights in accordance with the provisions of these Provisions concerning litigation agents of interested parties.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条　人民检察院、利害关系人对第一审裁定认定的事实、证据没有争议的，第二审人民法院可以不开庭审理。  
第二审人民法院决定开庭审理的，应当将开庭的时间、地点书面通知同级人民检察院和利害关系人。  
第二审人民法院应当就上诉、抗诉请求的有关事实和适用法律进行审查。

**Article 20** If the People's Procuratorate and the interested parties have no dispute over the facts and evidence determined in the ruling of the first instance, the People's Court of second instance may not hold a hearing in court.  
If the People's Court of second instance decides to hold a hearing in court, it shall notify the People's Procuratorate at the same level and the interested parties in writing of the time and place of the hearing.  
The People's Court of second instance shall review the relevant facts and application of law in relation to the requests of appeal or protest.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条　第二审人民法院对不服第一审裁定的上诉、抗诉案件，经审理，应当按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）第一审裁定认定事实清楚和适用法律正确的，应当驳回上诉或者抗诉，维持原裁定；  
（二）第一审裁定认定事实清楚，但适用法律有错误的，应当改变原裁定；  
（三）第一审裁定认定事实不清的，可以在查清事实后改变原裁定，也可以撤销原裁定，发回原审人民法院重新审判；  
（四）第一审裁定违反法定诉讼程序，可能影响公正审判的，应当撤销原裁定，发回原审人民法院重新审判。  
第一审人民法院对于依照前款第三项规定发回重新审判的案件作出裁定后，第二审人民法院对不服第一审人民法院裁定的上诉、抗诉，应当依法作出裁定，不得再发回原审人民法院重新审判。

**Article 21** After trial, the People's Court of second instance shall handle an appeal or protest case against a ruling of the first instance according to the following circumstances:  
(1) if the ruling of the first instance clearly determines facts and correctly applies law, the appeal or protest shall be dismissed and the original ruling shall be affirmed;  
(2) if the ruling of the first instance clearly determines facts but incorrectly applies law, the original ruling shall be modified;  
(3) if the ruling of the first instance does not clearly determine facts, the original ruling may be modified after the facts are clarified, or the original ruling may be revoked and the case remanded to the original People's Court for retrial;  
(4) if the ruling of the first instance violates statutory procedure and may affect a fair trial, the original ruling shall be revoked and the case remanded to the original People's Court for retrial.  
After the People's Court of first instance renders a ruling on a case remanded for retrial pursuant to item (3) of the preceding paragraph, the People's Court of second instance shall render a ruling according to law on any appeal or protest against the ruling of the People's Court of first instance, and shall not remand the case again to the original People's Court for retrial.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条　违法所得或者其他涉案财产在境外的，负责立案侦查的公安机关、人民检察院等侦查机关应当制作查封、扣押、冻结的法律文书以及协助执行查封、扣押、冻结的请求函，层报公安、检察院等各系统最高上级机关后，由公安、检察院等各系统最高上级机关依照刑事司法协助条约、多边公约，或者按照对等互惠原则，向违法所得或者其他涉案财产所在地国（区）的主管机关请求协助执行。  
被请求国（区）的主管机关提出，查封、扣押、冻结法律文书的制发主体必须是法院的，负责立案侦查的公安机关、人民检察院等侦查机关可以向同级人民法院提出查封、扣押、冻结的申请，人民法院经审查同意后制作查封、扣押、冻结令以及协助执行查封、扣押、冻结令的请求函，层报最高人民法院后，由最高人民法院依照刑事司法协助条约、多边公约，或者按照对等互惠原则，向违法所得或者其他涉案财产所在地国（区）的主管机关请求协助执行。  
请求函应当载明以下内容：  
（一）案由以及查封、扣押、冻结法律文书的发布主体是否具有管辖权；  
（二）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人涉嫌犯罪的事实及相关证据，但可能妨碍正在或者即将进行的刑事侦查的证据除外；  
（三）已发布公告的，发布公告情况、通知利害关系人参加诉讼以及保障诉讼参与人依法行使诉讼权利等情况；  
（四）请求查封、扣押、冻结的财产的种类、数量、价值、所在地等情况以及相关法律手续；  
（五）请求查封、扣押、冻结的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产的相关事实及证据材料；  
（六）请求查封、扣押、冻结财产的理由和法律依据；  
（七）被请求国（区）要求载明的其他内容。

**Article 22** If illegal gains or other property involved in the case is located outside the territory, the public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or other investigation authority responsible for filing and investigating the case shall prepare legal instruments for sealing up, seizure, and freezing, as well as a request letter for assistance in executing the sealing up, seizure, and freezing, and after reporting level by level to the highest authority of the respective system (e.g., public security, procuratorate), the highest authority shall request assistance from the competent authority of the country (region) where the illegal gains or other property involved in the case is located, in accordance with criminal judicial assistance treaties, multilateral conventions, or on the principle of reciprocity.  
If the competent authority of the requested country (region) states that the legal instruments for sealing up, seizure, and freezing must be issued by a court, the public security organ, People's Procuratorate, or other investigation authority responsible for filing and investigating may file an application with the People's Court at the same level for sealing up, seizure, and freezing. Upon review and approval, the People's Court shall prepare an order for sealing up, seizure, and freezing, as well as a request letter for assistance in executing the order, and after reporting level by level to the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Court shall request assistance from the competent authority of the country (region) where the illegal gains or other property involved in the case is located, in accordance with criminal judicial assistance treaties, multilateral conventions, or on the principle of reciprocity.  
The request letter shall include the following contents:  
(1) the cause of action and whether the issuer of the legal instruments for sealing up, seizure, and freezing has jurisdiction;  
(2) the facts of the crime suspected of being committed by the criminal suspect or defendant and relevant evidence, except for evidence that may impede an ongoing or impending criminal investigation;  
(3) if a public notice has been issued, the circumstances of issuing the public notice, notifying interested parties to participate in the proceedings, and ensuring that participants in the proceedings exercise their litigation rights according to law;  
(4) the types, quantity, value, location, and other information of the property sought to be sealed up, seized, or frozen, as well as relevant legal formalities;  
(5) relevant facts and evidence materials showing that the property sought to be sealed up, seized, or frozen constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case;  
(6) reasons and legal basis for requesting the sealing up, seizure, or freezing;  
(7) other contents required to be included by the requested country (region).

<a id="art-23"></a>第二十三条　违法所得或者其他涉案财产在境外，受理没收违法所得申请案件的人民法院经审理裁定没收的，应当制作没收令以及协助执行没收令的请求函，层报最高人民法院后，由最高人民法院依照刑事司法协助条约、多边公约，或者按照对等互惠原则，向违法所得或者其他涉案财产所在地国（区）的主管机关请求协助执行。  
请求函应当载明以下内容：  
（一）案由以及没收令发布主体具有管辖权；  
（二）属于生效裁定；  
（三）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人涉嫌犯罪的事实及相关证据，但可能妨碍正在或者即将进行的刑事侦查的证据除外；  
（四）犯罪嫌疑人、被告人逃匿、被通缉、脱逃、死亡的基本情况；  
（五）发布公告情况、通知利害关系人参加诉讼以及保障诉讼参与人依法行使诉讼权利等情况；  
（六）请求没收违法所得及其他涉案财产的种类、数量、价值、所在地等情况以及查封、扣押、冻结相关法律手续；  
（七）请求没收的财产属于违法所得及其他涉案财产的相关事实及证据材料；  
（八）请求没收财产的理由和法律依据；  
（九）被请求国（区）要求载明的其他内容。

**Article 23** If illegal gains or other property involved in the case is located outside the territory, and the People's Court accepting the application for confiscation of illegal gains, after trial, orders confiscation by ruling, it shall prepare a confiscation order and a request letter for assistance in executing the confiscation order, and after reporting level by level to the Supreme People's Court, the Supreme People's Court shall request assistance from the competent authority of the country (region) where the illegal gains or other property involved in the case is located, in accordance with criminal judicial assistance treaties, multilateral conventions, or on the principle of reciprocity.  
The request letter shall include the following contents:  
(1) the cause of action and that the issuer of the confiscation order has jurisdiction;  
(2) that the ruling is a legally effective ruling;  
(3) the facts of the crime suspected of being committed by the criminal suspect or defendant and relevant evidence, except for evidence that may impede an ongoing or impending criminal investigation;  
(4) basic information on the fleeing, being wanted, escaping, or death of the criminal suspect or defendant;  
(5) circumstances of issuing the public notice, notifying interested parties to participate in the proceedings, and ensuring that participants in the proceedings exercise their litigation rights according to law;  
(6) the types, quantity, value, location, and other information of the illegal gains or other property involved in the case sought to be confiscated, as well as relevant legal formalities for sealing up, seizure, and freezing;  
(7) relevant facts and evidence materials showing that the property sought to be confiscated constitutes illegal gains or other property involved in the case;  
(8) reasons and legal basis for requesting confiscation of property;  
(9) other contents required to be included by the requested country (region).

<a id="art-24"></a>第二十四条　单位实施[本规定第一条](#art-1)规定的犯罪后被撤销、注销，单位直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员逃匿、死亡，导致案件无法适用刑事诉讼普通程序进行审理的，依照[本规定第四条](#art-4)的规定处理。

**Article 24** If, after a unit commits a crime as specified in Article 1 of these Provisions, the unit is dissolved or cancelled, and the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons of the unit flee or die, rendering the case impossible to adjudicate under the ordinary criminal procedure, the matter shall be handled in accordance with Article 4 of these Provisions.

<a id="art-25"></a>第二十五条　本规定自2017年1月5日起施行。之前发布的司法解释与本规定不一致的，以本规定为准。

**Article 25** These Provisions shall come into force as of January 5, 2017. Where any previously issued judicial interpretations are inconsistent with these Provisions, these Provisions shall prevail.
