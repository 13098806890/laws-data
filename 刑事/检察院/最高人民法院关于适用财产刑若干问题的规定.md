# 最高人民法院关于适用财产刑若干问题的规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕45号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-15  
**生效日期**：2000-12-19

> （2000年11月15日最高人民法院审判委员会第1139次会议通过，自2000年12月19日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕45号 为正确理解和执行刑法有关财产刑的规定，现就适用财产刑的若干问题规定如下： 第一条　刑法规定“并处”没收财产或者罚金的犯罪，人民法院在对犯罪分子判处主刑的同时，必须依法判处相应的财产刑；刑法规定“可以并处”没收财产或者罚金的犯罪，人民法院应当根据案件具体情况及犯罪分子的财产状况，决定是否适用财产刑。 第二条　人民法院应当根据犯罪情节，如违法所得数额、造成损失的大小等，并综合考虑犯罪分子缴纳罚金的能力，依法判处罚金。刑法没有明确规定罚金数额标准的，罚金的最低数额不能少于一千元。 对未成年人犯罪应当从轻或者减轻判处罚金，但罚金的最低数额不能少于五百元。 第三条　依法对犯罪分子所犯数罪分别判处罚金的，应当实行并罚，将所判处的罚金数额相加，执行总和数额。 一人犯数罪依法同时并处罚金和没收财产的，应当合并执行；但并处没收全部财产的，只执行没收财产刑。 第四条　犯罪情节较轻，适用单处罚金不致再危害社会并具有下列情形之一的，可以依法单处罚金： （一）偶犯或者初犯； （二）自首或者有立功表现的； （三）犯罪时不满十八周岁的； （四）犯罪预备、中止或者未遂的； （五）被胁迫参加犯罪的； （六）全部退赃并有悔罪表现的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　刑法规定“并处”没收财产或者罚金的犯罪，人民法院在对犯罪分子判处主刑的同时，必须依法判处相应的财产刑；刑法规定“可以并处”没收财产或者罚金的犯罪，人民法院应当根据案件具体情况及犯罪分子的财产状况，决定是否适用财产刑。

**Article 1** If the Criminal Law provides that confiscation of property or a fine shall be imposed concurrently for a crime, the People's Court shall, at the same time as sentencing the criminal to a principal punishment, must impose the corresponding property punishment according to law. If the Criminal Law provides that confiscation of property or a fine may be imposed concurrently for a crime, the People's Court shall, based on the specific circumstances of the case and the property status of the criminal, decide whether to apply the property punishment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　人民法院应当根据犯罪情节，如违法所得数额、造成损失的大小等，并综合考虑犯罪分子缴纳罚金的能力，依法判处罚金。刑法没有明确规定罚金数额标准的，罚金的最低数额不能少于一千元。  
对未成年人犯罪应当从轻或者减轻判处罚金，但罚金的最低数额不能少于五百元。

**Article 2** The People's Court shall, based on the circumstances of the crime, such as the amount of illegal gains and the extent of losses caused, and after comprehensively considering the criminal's ability to pay the fine, impose a fine according to law. If the Criminal Law does not explicitly prescribe the standard for the amount of a fine, the minimum amount of the fine shall not be less than 1,000 yuan.  
For a crime committed by a minor, the fine shall be imposed leniently or mitigated, but the minimum amount of the fine shall not be less than 500 yuan.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　依法对犯罪分子所犯数罪分别判处罚金的，应当实行并罚，将所判处的罚金数额相加，执行总和数额。  
一人犯数罪依法同时并处罚金和没收财产的，应当合并执行；但并处没收全部财产的，只执行没收财产刑。

**Article 3** Where a criminal is sentenced to a fine separately for each of several crimes according to law, cumulative punishment shall be imposed, and the amounts of the fines imposed shall be added together, with the total amount executed.  
Where a person has committed several crimes and is concurrently sentenced to a fine and confiscation of property according to law, they shall be executed concurrently; however, if confiscation of all property is imposed concurrently, only the punishment of confiscation of property shall be executed.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　犯罪情节较轻，适用单处罚金不致再危害社会并具有下列情形之一的，可以依法单处罚金：  
（一）偶犯或者初犯；  
（二）自首或者有立功表现的；  
（三）犯罪时不满十八周岁的；  
（四）犯罪预备、中止或者未遂的；  
（五）被胁迫参加犯罪的；  
（六）全部退赃并有悔罪表现的；  
（七）其他可以依法单处罚金的情形。

**Article 4** Where the circumstances of a crime are relatively minor, imposing a fine alone would not cause further harm to society, and any of the following circumstances exists, a fine alone may be imposed according to law:  
(1) The offender is an occasional or first-time offender;  
(2) The offender has voluntarily surrendered or rendered meritorious service;  
(3) The offender was under the age of 18 at the time of the crime;  
(4) The offense was in preparation, suspension, or attempt;  
(5) The offender participated in the crime under duress;  
(6) The offender has fully returned the illicit gains and shown repentance;  
(7) Other circumstances where a fine alone may be imposed according to law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　[刑法第五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-53)规定的“判决指定的期限”应当在判决书中予以确定；“判决指定的期限”应为从判决发生法律效力第二日起最长不超过三个月。

**Article 5** The 'period specified in the judgment' as provided in Article 53 of the Criminal Law shall be determined in the judgment; the 'period specified in the judgment' shall be no longer than three months from the second day after the judgment takes legal effect.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　[刑法第五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-53)规定的“由于遭遇不能抗拒的灾祸缴纳确实有困难的”，主要是指因遭受火灾、水灾、地震等灾祸而丧失财产；罪犯因重病、伤残等而丧失劳动能力，或者需要罪犯抚养的近亲属患有重病，需支付巨额医药费等，确实没有财产可供执行的情形。  
具有[刑法第五十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-53)规定“可以酌情减少或者免除”事由的，由罪犯本人、亲属或者犯罪单位向负责执行的人民法院提出书面申请，并提供相应的证明材料。人民法院审查以后，根据实际情况，裁定减少或者免除应当缴纳的罚金数额。

**Article 6** The phrase 'having genuine difficulty in paying the fine due to an irresistible disaster' as provided in Article 53 of the Criminal Law mainly refers to circumstances where the criminal has lost property due to disasters such as fire, flood, or earthquake; the criminal has lost the ability to work due to serious illness, disability, or the like; or a close relative whom the criminal is obliged to support suffers from a serious illness and requires huge medical expenses, and there is genuinely no property available for execution.  
Where the circumstances prescribed in Article 53 of the Criminal Law for 'may be reduced or waived as appropriate' exist, the criminal, his or her relatives, or the criminal's unit shall submit a written application to the People's Court responsible for execution, and provide corresponding supporting materials. After examination, the People's Court shall, based on the actual circumstances, rule to reduce or waive the amount of the fine that shall be paid.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　[刑法第六十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-60)规定的“没收财产以前犯罪分子所负的正当债务”，是指犯罪分子在判决生效前所负他人的合法债务。

**Article 7** The 'legitimate debts incurred by the criminal before confiscation of property' as provided in Article 60 of the Criminal Law refers to lawful debts owed by the criminal to others before the judgment takes effect.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　罚金刑的数额应当以人民币为计算单位。

**Article 8** The amount of a fine shall be calculated in RMB.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　人民法院认为依法应当判处被告人财产刑的，可以在案件审理过程中，决定扣押或者冻结被告人的财产。

**Article 9** If a People's Court deems that a property punishment shall be imposed on the defendant according to law, it may decide to seize or freeze the defendant's property during the case trial process.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　财产刑由第一审人民法院执行。  
犯罪分子的财产在异地的，第一审人民法院可以委托财产所在地人民法院代为执行。

**Article 10** Property punishments shall be executed by the People's Court of first instance.  
If the criminal's property is located in another place, the People's Court of first instance may entrust the People's Court in the place where the property is located with execution on its behalf.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　自判决指定的期限届满第二日起，人民法院对于没有法定减免事由不缴纳罚金的，应当强制其缴纳。  
对于隐藏、转移、变卖、损毁已被扣押、冻结财产情节严重的，依照[刑法第三百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-314)的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 11** From the second day after the expiration of the period specified in the judgment, if a criminal does not pay the fine without a statutory reason for reduction or waiver, the People's Court shall enforce payment.  
If the circumstances are serious where the criminal conceals, transfers, sells, or damages property that has been seized or frozen, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with Article 314 of the Criminal Law.
