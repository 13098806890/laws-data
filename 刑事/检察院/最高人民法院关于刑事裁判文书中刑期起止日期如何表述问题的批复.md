### 最高人民法院关于刑事裁判文书中刑期起止日期如何表述问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于刑事裁判文书中刑期起止日期  
  
如何表述问题的批复  
  
（2000年2月13日最高人民法院审判委员会第1099次会议通过，自2000年3月4日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2000〕7号  
  
江西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院赣高法〔1999〕第151号《关于裁判文书中刑期起止时间如何表述的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据[刑法第四十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-41)、第四十四条、第四十七条和《法院刑事诉讼文书样式》（样本）的规定，判处管制、拘役、有期徒刑的，应当在刑事裁判文书中写明刑种、刑期和主刑刑期的起止日期及折抵办法。刑期从判决执行之日起计算。判决执行以前先行羁押的，羁押一日折抵刑期一日（判处管制刑的，羁押一日折抵刑期二日），即自××××年××月××日（羁押之日）起至××××年××月××日止。羁押期间取保候审的，刑期的终止日顺延。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Judicial Interpretation [2000] No. 7  
Jiangxi Provincial Higher People's Court:  
We have received your court's request (Gan Gao Fa [1999] No. 151) titled 'Request for Instructions on How to Express the Commencement and Termination Time of the Term of Sentence in Adjudicatory Documents'. Upon study, we reply as follows:  
According to the provisions of Article 41, Article 44, Article 47 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China and the 'Style for Criminal Litigation Documents of the People's Court (Sample)', where a sentence of public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment is imposed, the type of penalty, term of sentence, and the commencement and termination dates of the principal punishment sentence, as well as the method of deduction, shall be clearly stated in the criminal adjudicatory document. The term of sentence shall be calculated from the date of execution of the judgment. Where custody has been undergone prior to the execution of the judgment, one day of custody shall be deducted as one day of the term of sentence (for a sentence of public surveillance, one day of custody shall be deducted as two days of the term of sentence), that is, from [YYYY] [MM] [DD] (the date of custody) to [YYYY] [MM] [DD]. If release on bail pending investigation or trial is granted during custody, the termination date of the term of sentence shall be postponed accordingly.  
This is our reply.
