# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理妨害武装部队制式服装、车辆号牌管理秩序等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕16号  
**公布日期**：2011-03-28  
**生效日期**：2011-08-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理妨害武装部队制式服装、车辆号牌管理秩序等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2011年3月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1516次会议、2011年4月13日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第60次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年8月1日起施行。 2011年7月20日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理妨害武装部队制式服装、 车辆号牌管理秩序等刑事案件具体应用 法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2011〕16号 为依法惩治妨害武装部队制式服装、车辆号牌管理秩序等犯罪活动，维护国防利益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》有关规定，现就办理非法生产、买卖武装部队制式服装，伪造、盗窃、买卖武装部队车辆号牌等刑事案件的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队公文、证件、印章，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照刑法第三百七十五条第一款的规定，以伪造、变造、买卖武装部队公文、证件、印章罪或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队公文、证件、印章罪定罪处罚： （一）伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队公文一件以上的； （二）伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队军官证、士兵证、车辆行驶证、车辆驾驶证或者其他证件二本以上的； （三）伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队机关印章、车辆牌证印章或者其他印章一枚以上的。 实施前款规定的行为，数量达到第（一）至（三）项规定标准五倍以上或者造成严重后果的，应当认定为刑法第三百七十五条第一款规定的“情节严重”。 第二条  非法生产、买卖武装部队现行装备的制式服装，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百七十五条第二款规定的“情节严重”，以非法生产、买卖武装部队制式服装罪定罪处罚： （一）非法生产、买卖成套制式服装三十套以上，或者非成套制式服装一百件以上的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队公文、证件、印章，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-375)第一款的规定，以伪造、变造、买卖武装部队公文、证件、印章罪或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队公文、证件、印章罪定罪处罚：  
（一）伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队公文一件以上的；  
（二）伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队军官证、士兵证、车辆行驶证、车辆驾驶证或者其他证件二本以上的；  
（三）伪造、变造、买卖或者盗窃、抢夺武装部队机关印章、车辆牌证印章或者其他印章一枚以上的。  
实施前款规定的行为，数量达到第（一）至（三）项规定标准五倍以上或者造成严重后果的，应当认定为[刑法第三百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-375)第一款规定的“情节严重”。

**Article 1** Where any person forges, alters, buys, sells, steals, or seizes official documents, certificates, or seals of the armed forces under any of the following circumstances, he or she shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 375 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for the crime of forging, altering, buying, or selling official documents, certificates, or seals of the armed forces or the crime of stealing or seizing official documents, certificates, or seals of the armed forces:  
(1) forging, altering, buying, selling, stealing, or seizing one or more official documents of the armed forces;  
(2) forging, altering, buying, selling, stealing, or seizing two or more officer's certificates, soldier's certificates, vehicle driving permits, vehicle driver's licenses, or other certificates of the armed forces;  
(3) forging, altering, buying, selling, stealing, or seizing one or more seals of the organs of the armed forces, vehicle license plate seals, or other seals.  
Where any person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph, and the quantity reaches five times or more the standards specified in items (1) to (3) or causes serious consequences, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 375 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  非法生产、买卖武装部队现行装备的制式服装，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-375)第二款规定的“情节严重”，以非法生产、买卖武装部队制式服装罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法生产、买卖成套制式服装三十套以上，或者非成套制式服装一百件以上的；  
（二）非法生产、买卖帽徽、领花、臂章等标志服饰合计一百件（副）以上的；  
（三）非法经营数额二万元以上的；  
（四）违法所得数额五千元以上的；  
（五）具有其他严重情节的。

**Article 2** Where any person illegally produces or sells current standard uniforms of the armed forces under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 375 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegally producing or selling standard uniforms of the armed forces:  
(1) illegally producing or selling thirty or more complete sets of standard uniforms, or one hundred or more pieces of non-complete sets of standard uniforms;  
(2) illegally producing or selling a total of one hundred or more pieces or pairs of cap badges, collar insignia, armbands, or other insignia and accessories;  
(3) the illegal business turnover is 20,000 yuan or more;  
(4) the illegal gains are 5,000 yuan or more;  
(5) having other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  伪造、盗窃、买卖或者非法提供、使用武装部队车辆号牌等专用标志，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-375)第三款规定的“情节严重”，以伪造、盗窃、买卖、非法提供、非法使用武装部队专用标志罪定罪处罚：  
（一）伪造、盗窃、买卖或者非法提供、使用武装部队军以上领导机关车辆号牌一副以上或者其他车辆号牌三副以上的；  
（二）非法提供、使用军以上领导机关车辆号牌之外的其他车辆号牌累计六个月以上的；  
（三）伪造、盗窃、买卖或者非法提供、使用军徽、军旗、军种符号或者其他军用标志合计一百件（副）以上的；  
（四）造成严重后果或者恶劣影响的。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-375)第三款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）数量达到前款第（一）、（三）项规定标准五倍以上的；  
（二）非法提供、使用军以上领导机关车辆号牌累计六个月以上或者其他车辆号牌累计一年以上的；  
（三）造成特别严重后果或者特别恶劣影响的。

**Article 3** Where any person forges, steals, buys, sells, or illegally provides or uses special signs such as vehicle license plates of the armed forces under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 3 of Article 375 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of forging, stealing, buying, selling, illegally providing, or illegally using special signs of the armed forces:  
(1) forging, stealing, buying, selling, or illegally providing or using one or more sets of vehicle license plates of leading organs at or above the army level of the armed forces, or three or more sets of other vehicle license plates;  
(2) illegally providing or using vehicle license plates other than those of leading organs at or above the army level for an accumulated period of six months or more;  
(3) forging, stealing, buying, selling, or illegally providing or using a total of one hundred or more pieces or pairs of military emblems, military flags, service symbols, or other military signs;  
(4) causing serious consequences or adverse effects.  
Where any person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as prescribed in paragraph 3 of Article 375 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) the quantity reaches five times or more the standards specified in items (1) and (3) of the preceding paragraph;  
(2) illegally providing or using vehicle license plates of leading organs at or above the army level for an accumulated period of six months or more, or other vehicle license plates for an accumulated period of one year or more;  
(3) causing especially serious consequences or especially adverse effects.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  买卖、盗窃、抢夺伪造、变造的武装部队公文、证件、印章的，买卖仿制的现行装备的武装部队制式服装情节严重的，盗窃、买卖、提供、使用伪造、变造的武装部队车辆号牌等专用标志情节严重的，应当追究刑事责任。定罪量刑标准适用本解释第一至第三条的规定。

**Article 4** Where any person buys, sells, steals, or seizes forged or altered official documents, certificates, or seals of the armed forces, or buys or sells counterfeit current standard uniforms of the armed forces under serious circumstances, or steals, buys, sells, provides, or uses forged or altered special signs such as vehicle license plates of the armed forces under serious circumstances, criminal liability shall be investigated. The standards for conviction and sentencing shall be in accordance with the provisions of Articles 1 to 3 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  明知他人实施[刑法第三百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-375)规定的犯罪行为，而为其生产、提供专用材料或者提供资金、账号、技术、生产经营场所等帮助的，以共犯论处。

**Article 5** Where any person knowingly produces or provides special materials for another person who commits a criminal act as prescribed in Article 375 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, or provides assistance such as funds, accounts, technology, or production and business premises, the person shall be punished as an accomplice.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  实施[刑法第三百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-375)规定的犯罪行为，同时又构成逃税、诈骗、冒充军人招摇撞骗等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where any person commits a criminal act as prescribed in Article 375 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and also constitutes crimes such as tax evasion, fraud, or impersonating a military officer to swindle and cheat, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  单位实施[刑法第三百七十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-375)第二款、第三款规定的犯罪行为，对单位判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，分别依照本解释的有关规定处罚。

**Article 7** Where a unit commits a criminal act as prescribed in paragraph 2 or paragraph 3 of Article 375 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the unit shall be fined, and the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be punished respectively in accordance with the relevant provisions of this Interpretation.
