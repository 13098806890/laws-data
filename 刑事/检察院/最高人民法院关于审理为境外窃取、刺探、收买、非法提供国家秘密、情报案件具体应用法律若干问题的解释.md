# 最高人民法院关于审理为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密、情报案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕4号  
**公布日期**：2000-11-20  
**生效日期**：2001-01-22

> （2000年11月20日最高人民法院审判委员会1142次会议通过，自2001年1月22日起施行。） 法释〔2001〕4号 为依法惩治为境外的机构、组织、人员窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密、情报犯罪活动，维护国家安全和利益，根据刑法有关规定，现就审理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　刑法第一百一十一条规定的“国家秘密”，是指《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》第二条、第八条以及《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法实施办法》第四条确定的事项。 刑法第一百一十一条规定的“情报”，是指关系国家安全和利益、尚未公开或者依照有关规定不应公开的事项。 对为境外机构、组织、人员窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密之外的情报的行为，以为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供情报罪定罪处罚。 第二条　为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密或者情报，具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”，处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑，可以并处没收财产： （一）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供绝密级国家秘密的； （二）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供三项以上机密级国家秘密的； （三）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密或者情报，对国家安全和利益造成其他特别严重损害的。 实施前款行为，对国家和人民危害特别严重、情节特别恶劣的，可以判处死刑，并处没收财产。 第三条　为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密或者情报，具有下列情形之一的，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，可以并处没收财产： （一）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供机密级国家秘密的； （二）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供三项以上秘密级国家秘密的； （三）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密或者情报，对国家安全和利益造成其他严重损害的。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　[刑法第一百一十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-111)规定的“国家秘密”，是指[《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法》第二条](../../行政与公法/公安司法/中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法.md#art-2)、第八条以及《中华人民共和国保守国家秘密法实施办法》第四条确定的事项。  
[刑法第一百一十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-111)规定的“情报”，是指关系国家安全和利益、尚未公开或者依照有关规定不应公开的事项。  
对为境外机构、组织、人员窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密之外的情报的行为，以为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供情报罪定罪处罚。

**Article 1** : "State secrets" as referred to in Article 111 of the Criminal Law means the matters determined in Article 2 and Article 8 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets and Article 4 of the Implementation Rules of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Guarding State Secrets.  
"Intelligence" as referred to in Article 111 of the Criminal Law means matters that relate to national security and interests, which have not been disclosed or shall not be disclosed in accordance with relevant provisions.  
The act of stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing any intelligence other than state secrets to an overseas institution, organization, or individual shall be convicted and punished as the crime of stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing intelligence for an overseas entity.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密或者情报，具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节特别严重”，处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑，可以并处没收财产：  
（一）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供绝密级国家秘密的；  
（二）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供三项以上机密级国家秘密的；  
（三）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密或者情报，对国家安全和利益造成其他特别严重损害的。  
实施前款行为，对国家和人民危害特别严重、情节特别恶劣的，可以判处死刑，并处没收财产。

**Article 2** : Where any of the following circumstances exist in stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing state secrets or intelligence for an overseas entity, it shall be considered "especially serious circumstances" and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and may also be sentenced to confiscation of property:  
(1) Stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing top-secret state secrets for an overseas entity;  
(2) Stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing three or more secret-level state secrets for an overseas entity;  
(3) Stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing state secrets or intelligence for an overseas entity, causing other especially serious damage to national security and interests.  
If a person commits the act in the preceding paragraph, causing especially serious harm to the state and the people with especially vile circumstances, the offender may be sentenced to the death penalty and confiscation of property.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密或者情报，具有下列情形之一的，处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，可以并处没收财产：  
（一）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供机密级国家秘密的；  
（二）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供三项以上秘密级国家秘密的；  
（三）为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密或者情报，对国家安全和利益造成其他严重损害的。

**Article 3** : Where any of the following circumstances exist in stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing state secrets or intelligence for an overseas entity, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years, and may also be sentenced to confiscation of property:  
(1) Stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing secret-level state secrets for an overseas entity;  
(2) Stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing three or more confidential-level state secrets for an overseas entity;  
(3) Stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing state secrets or intelligence for an overseas entity, causing other serious damage to national security and interests.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供秘密级国家秘密或者情报，属于“情节较轻”，处五年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，可以并处没收财产。

**Article 4** : Stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing confidential-level state secrets or intelligence for an overseas entity, if it falls under "minor circumstances", the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights, and may also be sentenced to confiscation of property.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　行为人知道或者应当知道没有标明密级的事项关系国家安全和利益，而为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供的，依照[刑法第一百一十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-111)的规定以为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** : Where a person knows or should know that matters not marked with a classification level relate to national security and interests, and steals, spies, buys, or illegally provides them for an overseas entity, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 111 of the Criminal Law as the crime of stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing state secrets for an overseas entity.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　通过互联网将国家秘密或者情报非法发送给境外的机构、组织、个人的，依照[刑法第一百一十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-111)的规定定罪处罚；将国家秘密通过互联网予以发布，情节严重的，依照[刑法第三百九十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-398)的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** : Where a person illegally sends state secrets or intelligence to an overseas institution, organization, or individual through the Internet, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 111 of the Criminal Law; where a person publishes state secrets through the Internet, if the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 398 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　审理为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密案件，需要对有关事项是否属于国家秘密以及属于何种密级进行鉴定的，由国家保密工作部门或者省、自治区、直辖市保密工作部门鉴定。

**Article 7** : Where in the trial of a case of stealing, spying, buying, or illegally providing state secrets for an overseas entity, an expert evaluation is needed to determine whether a relevant matter constitutes a state secret and what classification level it falls under, the evaluation shall be conducted by the state secret-guarding department or the secret-guarding department of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government.
