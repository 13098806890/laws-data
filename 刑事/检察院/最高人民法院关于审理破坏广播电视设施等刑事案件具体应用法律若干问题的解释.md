# 最高人民法院关于审理破坏广播电视设施等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕13号  
**公布日期**：2011-05-23  
**生效日期**：2011-06-13

> 最高人民法院《关于审理破坏广播电视设施等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2011年5月23日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1523次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年6月13日起施行。 2011年6月7日 最高人民法院 关于审理破坏广播电视设施等刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2011〕13号 为依法惩治破坏广播电视设施等犯罪活动，维护广播电视设施运行安全，根据刑法有关规定，现就审理这类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  采取拆卸、毁坏设备，剪割缆线，删除、修改、增加广播电视设备系统中存储、处理、传输的数据和应用程序，非法占用频率等手段，破坏正在使用的广播电视设施，具有下列情形之一的，依照刑法第一百二十四条第一款的规定，以破坏广播电视设施罪处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑： （一）造成救灾、抢险、防汛和灾害预警等重大公共信息无法发布的； （二）造成县级、地市（设区的市）级广播电视台中直接关系节目播出的设施无法使用，信号无法播出的； （三）造成省级以上广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用，地市（设区的市）级广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用三小时以上，县级广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用十二小时以上，信号无法传输的； （四）其他危害公共安全的情形。 第二条  实施本解释第一条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百二十四条第一款规定的“造成严重后果”，以破坏广播电视设施罪处七年以上有期徒刑： （一）造成救灾、抢险、防汛和灾害预警等重大公共信息无法发布，因此贻误排除险情或者疏导群众，致使一人以上死亡、三人以上重伤或者财产损失五十万元以上，或者引起严重社会恐慌、社会秩序混乱的； （二）造成省级以上广播电视台中直接关系节目播出的设施无法使用，信号无法播出的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  采取拆卸、毁坏设备，剪割缆线，删除、修改、增加广播电视设备系统中存储、处理、传输的数据和应用程序，非法占用频率等手段，破坏正在使用的广播电视设施，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)第一款的规定，以破坏广播电视设施罪处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：  
（一）造成救灾、抢险、防汛和灾害预警等重大公共信息无法发布的；  
（二）造成县级、地市（设区的市）级广播电视台中直接关系节目播出的设施无法使用，信号无法播出的；  
（三）造成省级以上广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用，地市（设区的市）级广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用三小时以上，县级广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用十二小时以上，信号无法传输的；  
（四）其他危害公共安全的情形。

**Article 1** Where a person, by means of dismantling or damaging equipment, cutting cables, deleting, modifying, or adding data or application programs stored, processed, or transmitted in a radio and television facility system, or illegally occupying frequencies, destroys a radio and television facility in use, and the circumstances fall under any of the following, the person shall, in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 124 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years for the crime of destroying radio and television facilities:  
(1) causing that major public information such as disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, and disaster warnings cannot be released;  
(2) causing that the facilities directly related to program broadcasting at a county-level or prefecture-level (city divided into districts) radio and television station cannot be used and signals cannot be broadcast;  
(3) causing that facilities within a radio and television transmission network at or above the provincial level cannot be used, facilities within a prefecture-level (city divided into districts) radio and television transmission network cannot be used for more than three hours, or facilities within a county-level radio and television transmission network cannot be used for more than twelve hours, and signals cannot be transmitted;  
(4) other circumstances endangering public security.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)第一款规定的“造成严重后果”，以破坏广播电视设施罪处七年以上有期徒刑：  
（一）造成救灾、抢险、防汛和灾害预警等重大公共信息无法发布，因此贻误排除险情或者疏导群众，致使一人以上死亡、三人以上重伤或者财产损失五十万元以上，或者引起严重社会恐慌、社会秩序混乱的；  
（二）造成省级以上广播电视台中直接关系节目播出的设施无法使用，信号无法播出的；  
（三）造成省级以上广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用三小时以上，地市（设区的市）级广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用十二小时以上，县级广播电视传输网内的设施无法使用四十八小时以上，信号无法传输的；  
（四）造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 2** Where a person commits the act specified in Article 1 of this Interpretation and the circumstances fall under any of the following, it shall be determined as 'causing serious consequences' as stipulated in the first paragraph of Article 124 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years for the crime of destroying radio and television facilities:  
(1) causing that major public information such as disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, and disaster warnings cannot be released, thereby delaying the elimination of dangers or evacuation of the masses, resulting in the death of one or more persons, serious injury to three or more persons, or property losses of not less than 500,000 yuan, or causing serious social panic or social disorder;  
(2) causing that the facilities directly related to program broadcasting at a provincial-level or higher radio and television station cannot be used and signals cannot be broadcast;  
(3) causing that facilities within a radio and television transmission network at or above the provincial level cannot be used for more than three hours, facilities within a prefecture-level (city divided into districts) radio and television transmission network cannot be used for more than twelve hours, or facilities within a county-level radio and television transmission network cannot be used for more than forty-eight hours, and signals cannot be transmitted;  
(4) causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  过失损坏正在使用的广播电视设施，造成[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定的严重后果的，依照[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)第二款的规定，以过失损坏广播电视设施罪处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑；情节较轻的，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役。  
过失损坏广播电视设施构成犯罪，但能主动向有关部门报告，积极赔偿损失或者修复被损坏设施的，可以酌情从宽处罚。

**Article 3** Where a person negligently damages a radio and television facility in use, causing the serious consequences specified in Article 2 of this Interpretation, the person shall, in accordance with the provisions of the second paragraph of Article 124 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years for the crime of negligently damaging radio and television facilities; if the circumstances are relatively minor, the person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention.  
Where a person negligently damages a radio and television facility and the act constitutes a crime, but the person voluntarily reports to the relevant authorities, actively compensates for losses, or repairs the damaged facility, the punishment may be mitigated or reduced as appropriate.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  建设、施工单位的管理人员、施工人员，在建设、施工过程中，违反广播电视设施保护规定，故意或者过失损毁正在使用的广播电视设施，构成犯罪的，以破坏广播电视设施罪或者过失损坏广播电视设施罪定罪处罚。其定罪量刑标准适用本解释第一至三条的规定。

**Article 4** Where managerial personnel or construction workers of a construction or work unit, during the construction process, violate the provisions on protection of radio and television facilities and intentionally or negligently damage a radio and television facility in use, if the act constitutes a crime, they shall be convicted and punished for the crime of destroying radio and television facilities or the crime of negligently damaging radio and television facilities. The standards for conviction and sentencing shall be governed by the provisions of Articles 1 to 3 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  盗窃正在使用的广播电视设施，尚未构成盗窃罪，但具有[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条规定情形的，以破坏广播电视设施罪定罪处罚；同时构成盗窃罪和破坏广播电视设施罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where a person steals a radio and television facility in use, which does not yet constitute the crime of theft, but the circumstances fall under the provisions of Article 1 or Article 2 of this Interpretation, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of destroying radio and television facilities; if the act simultaneously constitutes both the crime of theft and the crime of destroying radio and television facilities, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  破坏正在使用的广播电视设施未危及公共安全，或者故意毁坏尚未投入使用的广播电视设施，造成财物损失数额较大或者有其他严重情节的，以故意毁坏财物罪定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where a person destroys a radio and television facility in use without endangering public safety, or intentionally destroys a radio and television facility not yet put into use, resulting in relatively large property losses or other serious circumstances, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of intentionally destroying property.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  实施破坏广播电视设施犯罪，并利用广播电视设施实施煽动分裂国家、煽动颠覆国家政权、煽动民族仇恨、民族歧视或者宣扬邪教等行为，同时构成其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Where a person commits the crime of destroying radio and television facilities and, by using such facilities, commits acts such as inciting secession of the state, inciting subversion of state power, inciting ethnic hatred or ethnic discrimination, or propagating cults, and such acts simultaneously constitute other crimes, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  本解释所称广播电视台中直接关系节目播出的设施、广播电视传输网内的设施，参照国家广播电视行政主管部门和其他相关部门的有关规定确定。

**Article 8** As used in this Interpretation, the facilities directly related to program broadcasting in a radio and television station and the facilities within a radio and television transmission network shall be determined by reference to the relevant provisions of the competent administrative department of radio and television of the state and other relevant departments.
