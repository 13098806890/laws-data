### 最高人民法院关于工商行政管理机关对无效经济合同引起的财产争议处理后当事人向人民法院起诉是否受理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>关于工商行政管理机关对无效经济合同引起的财产  
  
争议处理后当事人向人民法院起诉是否受理的批复  
  
法（经）复〔1990〕16号  
  
江苏省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（ 1990）经请字第 1号《关于无效经济合同经工商行政管理部门确认并处理后，当事人向人民法院起诉，人民法院是否可以受理的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据《中华人民共和国经济合同法》第七条第三款的规定，工商行政管理机关对无效经济合同具有确认权。但无效经济合同引起的财产争议属于平等主体之间的经济纠纷，如当事人就无效经济合同引起的财产争议向人民法院起诉，凡符合《中华人民共和国民事诉讼法（试行）》第八十一条规定的，人民法院应予受理。  
  
1990年11月3日

**Article 1** Reply on Whether the People's Court Shall Accept a Prosecution Filed by a Party after the Administrative Authority for Industry and Commerce Has Handled the Property Disputes Arising from Invalid Economic Contracts  
Fa (Jing) Fu [1990] No. 16  
Higher People's Court of Jiangsu Province:  
Your court's (1990) Jing Qing Zi No. 1 "Request for Instructions on Whether the People's Court May Accept a Prosecution Filed by a Party after an Invalid Economic Contract Has Been Confirmed and Handled by the Administrative Department for Industry and Commerce" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
According to the provisions of Article 7, Paragraph 3 of the Economic Contract Law of the People's Republic of China, the administrative authority for industry and commerce has the right to confirm invalid economic contracts. However, property disputes arising from invalid economic contracts are economic disputes between equal subjects. If a party initiates a prosecution in the People's Court regarding property disputes arising from an invalid economic contract, provided that the requirements of Article 81 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China (for Trial Implementation) are met, the People's Court shall accept the case.  
November 3, 1990
