# 最高人民法院关于审理偷税抗税刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕33号  
**公布日期**：2002-11-04  
**生效日期**：2002-11-07

> （2002年11月4日最高人民法院审判委员会第1254次会议通过，自2002年11月7日起施行。） 法释〔2002〕33号 为依法惩处偷税、抗税犯罪活动，根据刑法的有关规定，现就审理偷税、抗税刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 纳税人实施下列行为之一不缴或者少缴应纳税款，偷税数额占应纳税额的百分之十以上且偷税数额在一万元以上的，依照刑法第二百零一条第一款的规定定罪处罚： （一）伪造、变造、隐匿、擅自销毁帐簿、记帐凭证； （二）在帐簿上多列支出或者不列、少列收入； （三）经税务机关通知申报而拒不申报纳税； （四）进行虚假纳税申报； （五）缴纳税款后，以假报出口或者其他欺骗手段，骗取所缴纳的税款。 扣缴义务人实施前款行为之一，不缴或者少缴已扣、已收税款，数额在一万元以上且占应缴税额百分之十以上的，依照刑法第二百零一条第一款的规定定罪处罚。 扣缴义务人书面承诺代纳税人支付税款的，应当认定扣缴义务人“已扣、已收税款”。 实施本条第一款、第二款规定的行为，偷税数额在五万元以下，纳税人或者扣缴义务人在公安机关立案侦查以前已经足额补缴应纳税款和滞纳金，犯罪情节轻微，不需要判处刑罚的，可以免予刑事处罚。 第二条 纳税人伪造、变造、隐匿、擅自销毁用于记帐的发票等原始凭证的行为，应当认定为刑法第二百零一条第一款规定的伪造、变造、隐匿、擅自销毁记帐凭证的行为。 具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百零一条第一款规定的“经税务机关通知申报”： （一）纳税人、扣缴义务人已经依法办理税务登记或者扣缴税款登记的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 纳税人实施下列行为之一不缴或者少缴应纳税款，偷税数额占应纳税额的百分之十以上且偷税数额在一万元以上的，依照[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）伪造、变造、隐匿、擅自销毁帐簿、记帐凭证；  
（二）在帐簿上多列支出或者不列、少列收入；  
（三）经税务机关通知申报而拒不申报纳税；  
（四）进行虚假纳税申报；  
（五）缴纳税款后，以假报出口或者其他欺骗手段，骗取所缴纳的税款。  
扣缴义务人实施前款行为之一，不缴或者少缴已扣、已收税款，数额在一万元以上且占应缴税额百分之十以上的，依照[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款的规定定罪处罚。  
扣缴义务人书面承诺代纳税人支付税款的，应当认定扣缴义务人“已扣、已收税款”。  
实施本条第一款、第二款规定的行为，偷税数额在五万元以下，纳税人或者扣缴义务人在公安机关立案侦查以前已经足额补缴应纳税款和滞纳金，犯罪情节轻微，不需要判处刑罚的，可以免予刑事处罚。

**Article 1** Where a taxpayer commits any of the following acts resulting in non-payment or underpayment of tax payable, and the amount of tax evaded accounts for more than 10% of the tax payable and exceeds RMB 10,000, the taxpayer shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 201, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) forging, altering, concealing, or destroying without authorization account books or accounting vouchers;  
(2) overstating expenditures or understating or omitting income in the account books;  
(3) refusing to file a tax return after being notified by the tax authorities to do so;  
(4) filing false tax returns;  
(5) after paying taxes, obtaining a refund of the taxes paid by means of false export declarations or other fraudulent means.  
Where a withholding agent commits any of the acts specified in the preceding paragraph, resulting in non-payment or underpayment of taxes that have been withheld or collected, and the amount exceeds RMB 10,000 and accounts for more than 10% of the taxes payable, the withholding agent shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 201, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a withholding agent makes a written acceptance to pay taxes on behalf of the taxpayer, the withholding agent shall be deemed to have “withheld or collected” the taxes.  
Where a taxpayer or withholding agent commits the acts specified in Paragraph 1 or Paragraph 2 of this Article, the amount of tax evaded is less than RMB 50,000, and the taxpayer or withholding agent has fully paid the tax payable and the surcharge for overdue payment prior to the public security organ's filing of the case for investigation, if the circumstances of the crime are minor and no criminal punishment is necessary, the taxpayer or withholding agent may be exempted from criminal punishment.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 纳税人伪造、变造、隐匿、擅自销毁用于记帐的发票等原始凭证的行为，应当认定为[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的伪造、变造、隐匿、擅自销毁记帐凭证的行为。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的“经税务机关通知申报”：  
（一）纳税人、扣缴义务人已经依法办理税务登记或者扣缴税款登记的；  
（二）依法不需要办理税务登记的纳税人，经税务机关依法书面通知其申报的；  
（三）尚未依法办理税务登记、扣缴税款登记的纳税人、扣缴义务人，经税务机关依法书面通知其申报的。  
[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的“虚假的纳税申报”，是指纳税人或者扣缴义务人向税务机关报送虚假的纳税申报表、财务报表、代扣代缴、代收代缴税款报告表或者其他纳税申报资料，如提供虚假申请，编造减税、免税、抵税、先征收后退还税款等虚假资料等。  
[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第三款规定的“未经处理”，是指纳税人或者扣缴义务人在五年内多次实施偷税行为，但每次偷税数额均未达到[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)规定的构成犯罪的数额标准，且未受行政处罚的情形。  
纳税人、扣缴义务人因同一偷税犯罪行为受到行政处罚，又被移送起诉的，人民法院应当依法受理。  
依法定罪并判处罚金的，行政罚款折抵罚金。

**Article 2** Any act by a taxpayer of forging, altering, concealing, or destroying without authorization original vouchers such as invoices used for accounting shall be deemed as a act of forging, altering, concealing, or destroying without authorization accounting vouchers as specified in Article 201, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law.  
Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “being notified by the tax authorities to file a tax return” as specified in Article 201, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the taxpayer or withholding agent has already undergone tax registration or withholding tax registration according to law;  
(2) a taxpayer who is not required to undergo tax registration according to law is notified in writing by the tax authorities according to law to file a tax return;  
(3) a taxpayer or withholding agent who has not undergone tax registration or withholding tax registration according to law is notified in writing by the tax authorities according to law to file a tax return.  
“False tax return” as specified in Article 201, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law means that a taxpayer or withholding agent submits to the tax authorities false tax returns, financial statements, reports on tax withheld and collected, or other tax filing materials, such as providing false applications, fabricating false materials concerning tax reduction, tax exemption, tax credit, tax collection first and refund later, etc.  
“Without having been dealt with” as specified in Article 201, Paragraph 3 of the Criminal Law means that a taxpayer or withholding agent has committed tax evasion multiple times within five years, but the amount of each tax evasion has not reached the amount standard for constituting a crime as specified in Article 201 of the Criminal Law, and no administrative penalty has been imposed.  
Where a taxpayer or withholding agent has received an administrative penalty for the same tax evasion crime and is subsequently transferred for prosecution, the People's Court shall accept the case according to law.  
If a conviction is made and a fine is imposed according to law, the administrative fine shall be offset against the fine.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 偷税数额，是指在确定的纳税期间，不缴或者少缴各税种税款的总额。  
偷税数额占应纳税额的百分比，是指一个纳税年度中的各税种偷税总额与该纳税年度应纳税总额的比例。  
不按纳税年度确定纳税期的其他纳税人，偷税数额占应纳税额的百分比，按照行为人最后一次偷税行为发生之日前一年中各税种偷税总额与该年纳税总额的比例确定。  
纳税义务存续期间不足一个纳税年度的，偷税数额占应纳税额的百分比，按照各税种偷税总额与实际发生纳税义务期间应当缴纳税款总额的比例确定。  
偷税行为跨越若干个纳税年度，只要其中一个纳税年度的偷税数额及百分比达到[刑法第二百零一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-201)第一款规定的标准，即构成偷税罪。  
各纳税年度的偷税数额应当累计计算，偷税百分比应当按照最高的百分比确定。

**Article 3** The amount of tax evaded means the total amount of taxes of various categories not paid or underpaid within a definite tax payment period.  
The percentage of the amount of tax evaded in the tax payable means the ratio of the total amount of tax evaded in various categories within a tax year to the total amount of tax payable in that tax year.  
For other taxpayers whose tax payment period is not determined by tax year, the percentage of the amount of tax evaded in the tax payable shall be determined according to the ratio of the total amount of tax evaded in various categories in the one year prior to the occurrence of the taxpayer's last act of tax evasion to the total amount of tax payable in that year.  
Where the duration of the tax obligation is less than one tax year, the percentage of the amount of tax evaded in the tax payable shall be determined according to the ratio of the total amount of tax evaded in various categories to the total amount of tax that should have been paid during the period in which the tax obligation actually existed.  
Where a tax evasion act spans several tax years, as long as the amount and percentage of tax evaded in any one of those tax years meet the standards specified in Article 201, Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, the crime of tax evasion shall be constituted.  
The amounts of tax evaded in each tax year shall be calculated cumulatively, and the percentage of tax evasion shall be determined according to the highest percentage.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 两年内因偷税受过二次行政处罚，又偷税且数额在一万元以上的，应当以偷税罪定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where a person has received two administrative penalties for tax evasion within two years and subsequently commits tax evasion with an amount exceeding RMB 10,000, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of tax evasion.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 实施抗税行为具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百零二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-202)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）聚众抗税的首要分子；  
（二）抗税数额在十万元以上的；  
（三）多次抗税的；  
（四）故意伤害致人轻伤的；  
（五）具有其他严重情节。

**Article 5** Where an act of tax resistance falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute “serious circumstances” as specified in Article 202 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) being the principal instigator of an assembled crowd resisting taxes;  
(2) the amount of tax resistance exceeds RMB 100,000;  
(3) resisting taxes on multiple occasions;  
(4) intentionally causing injury to another person resulting in minor injury;  
(5) having other serious circumstances.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 实施抗税行为致人重伤、死亡，构成故意伤害罪、故意杀人罪的，分别依照[刑法第二百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-234)第二款、第二百三十二条的规定定罪处罚。  
与纳税人或者扣缴义务人共同实施抗税行为的，以抗税罪的共犯依法处罚。

**Article 6** Where an act of tax resistance causes serious injury or death to another person, constituting the crime of intentional injury or intentional homicide, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 234, Paragraph 2 and Article 232 of the Criminal Law, respectively.  
Where a person jointly commits an act of tax resistance with a taxpayer or withholding agent, the person shall be punished according to law as an accomplice in the crime of tax resistance.
