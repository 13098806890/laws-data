### 最高人民法院关于人民法院应否受理当事人不服治安管理处罚而提起的刑事自诉问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于人民法院应否受理当事人不服治安管理  
  
处罚而提起的刑事自诉问题的批复  
  
法复〔1993〕8号  
  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院，解放军军事法院：  
  
近年来，一些高级法院请示，对于公安机关的治安管理处罚决定生效后，当事人在法定期间内未提起行政诉讼，而向人民法院提起刑事自诉的，人民法院是否应当受理的问题。经研究，现批复如下：  
  
治安管理处罚决定生效后，当事人在法定期间内未就治安管理处罚决定提起行政诉讼，而就同一事实向人民法院提起刑事自诉的，只要符合刑事诉讼法的有关规定，并且被告人的行为是在追诉时效期限内的，人民法院均应受理。经审理，如果认为被告人的行为构成犯罪，应当依法追究刑事责任。被告人被判处管制、拘役或者有期徒刑的，如果其在原治安管理处罚决定中已受过拘留处罚，应当将拘留处罚天数折抵刑期。对于自诉人提起附带民事诉讼，人民法院经调解或判决被告人赔偿损失的，应当将原治安管理处罚决定中的赔偿部分一并考虑。人民法院审理这类自诉案件所制作的调解书、裁定书或判决书，一经生效即送达作出原治安管理处罚决定的公安机关。  
  
1993年9月3日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Issue of Whether the People's Court Shall Accept a Private Criminal Prosecution  
Filed by a Party Dissatisfied with a Public Security Administrative Penalty  
Fa Fu [1993] No. 8  
To the Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and the Military Court of the People's Liberation Army:  
In recent years, some Higher People's Courts have requested instructions on the issue of whether the People's Court shall accept a private criminal prosecution filed by a party who, after the decision on public security administrative penalty by the public security organ has taken effect, fails to file an administrative action within the statutory period but files a private criminal prosecution with the People's Court. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
After the decision on public security administrative penalty takes effect, if a party fails to file an administrative action against the decision within the statutory period, but files a private criminal prosecution with the People's Court based on the same facts, the People's Court shall accept the case as long as it complies with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law and the act of the defendant is within the statute of limitations for prosecution. Upon trial, if the People's Court finds that the defendant's act constitutes a crime, it shall investigate criminal liability according to law. If the defendant is sentenced to public surveillance, criminal detention, or fixed-term imprisonment, and has previously been subject to detention in the original decision on public security administrative penalty, the number of days of detention shall be offset against the term of the sentence. For the incidental civil action filed by the private prosecutor, if the People's Court orders the defendant to pay compensation through mediation or judgment, it shall take into account the compensation portion in the original decision on public security administrative penalty. Any mediation document, ruling, or judgment produced by the People's Court in hearing such private prosecution cases shall be served on the public security organ that made the original decision on public security administrative penalty upon taking effect.  
September 3, 1993
