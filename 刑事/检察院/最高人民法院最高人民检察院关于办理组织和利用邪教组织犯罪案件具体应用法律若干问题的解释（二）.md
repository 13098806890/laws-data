# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理组织和利用邪教组织犯罪案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2001〕19号  
**公布日期**：2001-05-10  
**生效日期**：2001-06-11

> 2001年4月29日最高人民检察院检察委员会第九届第87次会议通过，自2001年6月11日起施行。） 法释〔2001〕19号 为依法办理邪教组织犯罪案件，根据刑法的有关规定，现对办理邪教组织犯罪案件具体应用法律的若干问题，作如下解释： 第一条 制作、传播邪教宣传品，宣扬邪教，破坏法律、行政法规实施，具有下列情形之一的，依照刑法第三百条第一款的规定，以组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施罪定罪处罚： （一）制作、传播邪教传单、图片、标语、报纸300份以上，书刊100册以上，光盘100张以上，录音、录像带100盒以上的； （二）制作、传播宣扬邪教的DVD、VCD、CD母盘的； （三）利用互联网制作、传播邪教组织信息的； （四）在公共场所悬挂横幅、条幅，或者以书写、喷涂标语等方式宣扬邪教，造成严重社会影响的； （五）因制作、传播邪教宣传品受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚又制作、传播的； （六）其他制作、传播邪教宣传品，情节严重的。 制作、传播邪教宣传品数量达到前款第（一）项规定的标准五倍以上，或者虽未达到五倍，但造成特别严重社会危害的，属于刑法第三百条第一款规定的“情节特别严重”。 第二条 制作、传播邪教宣传品，煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一，或者煽动颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度的，依照刑法第一百零三条第二款、第一百零五条第二款的规定，以煽动分裂国家罪或者煽动颠覆国家政权罪定罪处罚。 第三条 制作、传播邪教宣传品，公然侮辱他人或者捏造事实诽谤他人的，依照刑法第二百四十六条的规定，以侮辱罪或者诽谤罪定罪处罚。 第四条 制作、传播的邪教宣传品具有煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一，煽动颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度，侮辱、诽谤他人，严重危害社会秩序和国家利益，或者破坏国家法律、行政法规实施等内容，其行为同时触犯刑法第一百零三条第二款、第一百零五条第二款、第二百四十六条、第三百条第一款等规定的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。 第五条 邪教组织被取缔后，仍聚集滋事、公开进行邪教活动，或者聚众冲击国家机关、新闻机构等单位，人数达到20人以上的，或者虽未达到20人，但具有其他严重情节的，对于组织者、策划者、指挥者和屡教不改的积极参加者，依照刑法第三百条第一款的规定，以组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施罪定罪处罚。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 制作、传播邪教宣传品，宣扬邪教，破坏法律、行政法规实施，具有下列情形之一的，依照[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款的规定，以组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施罪定罪处罚：  
（一）制作、传播邪教传单、图片、标语、报纸300份以上，书刊100册以上，光盘100张以上，录音、录像带100盒以上的；  
（二）制作、传播宣扬邪教的DVD、VCD、CD母盘的；  
（三）利用互联网制作、传播邪教组织信息的；  
（四）在公共场所悬挂横幅、条幅，或者以书写、喷涂标语等方式宣扬邪教，造成严重社会影响的；  
（五）因制作、传播邪教宣传品受过刑事处罚或者行政处罚又制作、传播的；  
（六）其他制作、传播邪教宣传品，情节严重的。  
制作、传播邪教宣传品数量达到前款第（一）项规定的标准五倍以上，或者虽未达到五倍，但造成特别严重社会危害的，属于[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”。

**Article 1** : Where any person produces or disseminates cult propaganda materials, advocates cults, or undermines the implementation of laws and administrative regulations, under any of the following circumstances, he or she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of organizing or using a cult organization to undermine the implementation of law in accordance with Article 300, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) producing or disseminating 300 or more copies of cult leaflets, pictures, slogans, or newspapers, 100 or more copies of books or periodicals, 100 or more discs, or 100 or more boxes of audio or video tapes;  
(2) producing or disseminating master discs of DVD, VCD, or CD that advocate cults;  
(3) using the Internet to produce or disseminate cult organization information;  
(4) hanging banners or streamers in public places, or advocating cults by writing or spraying slogans, thereby causing serious social impact;  
(5) having previously received criminal punishment or administrative penalty for producing or disseminating cult propaganda materials, and subsequently producing or disseminating them again;  
(6) other circumstances where the production or dissemination of cult propaganda materials is serious.  
Where the quantity of cult propaganda materials produced or disseminated reaches five times or more the standard specified in subparagraph (1) of the preceding paragraph, or although not reaching five times, it causes particularly serious social harm, it shall constitute the "particularly serious circumstances" as prescribed in Article 300, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 制作、传播邪教宣传品，煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一，或者煽动颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度的，依照[刑法第一百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-103)第二款、第一百零五条第二款的规定，以煽动分裂国家罪或者煽动颠覆国家政权罪定罪处罚。

**Article 2** : Where any person produces or disseminates cult propaganda materials to incite secession of the State or undermine national unity, or to incite subversion of State power or overthrow of the socialist system, he or she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of inciting secession of the State or the crime of inciting subversion of State power in accordance with Article 103, paragraph 2 and Article 105, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 制作、传播邪教宣传品，公然侮辱他人或者捏造事实诽谤他人的，依照[刑法第二百四十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-246)的规定，以侮辱罪或者诽谤罪定罪处罚。

**Article 3** : Where any person produces or disseminates cult propaganda materials to publicly humiliate others or to fabricate facts to defame others, he or she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of insult or the crime of defamation in accordance with Article 246 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 制作、传播的邪教宣传品具有煽动分裂国家、破坏国家统一，煽动颠覆国家政权、推翻社会主义制度，侮辱、诽谤他人，严重危害社会秩序和国家利益，或者破坏国家法律、行政法规实施等内容，其行为同时触犯[刑法第一百零三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-103)第二款、第一百零五条第二款、第二百四十六条、第三百条第一款等规定的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 4** : Where the cult propaganda materials produced or disseminated contain content inciting secession of the State or undermining national unity, inciting subversion of State power or overthrow of the socialist system, insulting or defaming others, seriously endangering social order or State interests, or undermining the implementation of national laws or administrative regulations, and the act simultaneously violates the provisions of Article 103, paragraph 2, Article 105, paragraph 2, Article 246, or Article 300, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision subject to the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 邪教组织被取缔后，仍聚集滋事、公开进行邪教活动，或者聚众冲击国家机关、新闻机构等单位，人数达到20人以上的，或者虽未达到20人，但具有其他严重情节的，对于组织者、策划者、指挥者和屡教不改的积极参加者，依照[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款的规定，以组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** : Where, after a cult organization is banned, persons still gather to create disturbances or openly engage in cult activities, or gather a crowd to assault State organs, news agencies, or other units, with the number of persons reaching 20 or more, or although not reaching 20, have other serious circumstances, the organizers, planners, commanders, and active participants who refuse to reform despite repeated education shall be convicted and punished for the crime of organizing or using a cult organization to undermine the implementation of law in accordance with Article 300, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 为组织、策划邪教组织人员聚集滋事、公开进行邪教活动而进行聚会、串联等活动，对于组织者、策划者、指挥者和屡教不改的积极参加者，依照[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** : Where any person engages in gatherings, networking, or other activities for the purpose of organizing or planning for cult organization personnel to gather to create disturbances or engage in open cult activities, the organizers, planners, commanders, and active participants who refuse to reform despite repeated education shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 300, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 邪教组织人员以暴力、威胁方法阻碍国家机关工作人员依法执行职务的，依照[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)第一款的规定，以妨害公务罪定罪处罚。其行为同时触犯刑法其他规定的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 7** : Where a cult organization personnel obstruct a State functionary from performing his or her duties according to law by means of violence or threat, he or she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of obstructing public service in accordance with Article 277, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China. Where the act simultaneously violates other provisions of the Criminal Law, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision subject to the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 邪教组织人员为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密、情报的，以窃取、刺探、收买方法非法获取国家秘密的，非法持有国家绝密、机密文件、资料、物品拒不说明来源与用途的，或者泄露国家秘密情节严重的，分别依照[刑法第一百一十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-111)为境外窃取、刺探、收买、非法提供国家秘密、情报罪，第二百八十二条第一款非法获取国家秘密罪，第二百八十二条第二款非法持有国家绝密、机密文件、资料、物品罪，第三百九十八条故意泄露国家秘密罪、过失泄露国家秘密罪的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 8** : Where a cult organization personnel steals, spies on, purchases, or illegally provides State secrets or intelligence for an overseas entity, illegally obtains State secrets by means of theft, espionage, or purchase, illegally holds top-secret or confidential documents, materials, or articles of the State and refuses to explain their source or purpose, or leaks State secrets with serious circumstances, he or she shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 111 (crime of stealing, spying on, purchasing, or illegally providing State secrets or intelligence for an overseas entity), Article 282, paragraph 1 (crime of illegally obtaining State secrets), Article 282, paragraph 2 (crime of illegally holding top-secret or confidential documents, materials, or articles of the State), or Article 398 (crime of intentionally or negligently leaking State secrets) of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, as the case may be.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 组织、策划、煽动、教唆、帮助邪教组织人员自杀、自残的，依照[刑法第二百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-232)、第二百三十四条的规定，以故意杀人罪、故意伤害罪定罪处罚。

**Article 9** : Where any person organizes, plans, incites, instigates, or assists a cult organization personnel to commit suicide or self-injury, he or she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of intentional homicide or intentional injury in accordance with Articles 232 and 234 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条 邪教组织人员以自焚、自爆或者其他危险方法危害公共安全的，分别依照[刑法第一百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-114)、第一百一十五条第一款以危险方法危害公共安全罪等规定定罪处罚。

**Article 10** : Where a cult organization personnel endangers public security by means of self-immolation, self-detonation, or other dangerous methods, he or she shall be convicted and punished for the crime of endangering public security by dangerous means in accordance with Articles 114 and 115, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, respectively.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条 人民检察院审查起诉邪教案件，对于犯罪情节轻微，有悔罪表现，确实不致再危害社会的犯罪嫌疑人，根据[刑事诉讼法第一百四十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-142)第二款的规定，可以作出不起诉决定。

**Article 11** : Where a People's Procuratorate examines and prosecutes a cult case, for a criminal suspect whose criminal circumstances are minor, who shows repentance, and who truly will not endanger society again, it may decide not to prosecute in accordance with Article 142, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条 人民法院审理邪教案件，对于有悔罪表现，不致再危害社会的被告人，可以依法从轻处罚；依法可以判处管制、拘役或者符合适用缓刑条件的，可以判处管制、拘役或者适用缓刑；对于犯罪情节轻微不需要判处刑罚的，可以免予刑事处罚。

**Article 12** : Where a People's Court tries a cult case, for a defendant who shows repentance and will not endanger society again, it may impose a light punishment according to law; where public surveillance, criminal detention may be imposed according to law, or the conditions for applying probation are satisfied, public surveillance, criminal detention may be imposed, or probation may be applied; where the criminal circumstances are minor and no penalty needs to be imposed, the defendant may be exempted from criminal punishment.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条 本规定下列用语的含义是：  
（一）“宣传品”，是指传单、标语、喷图、图片、书籍、报刊、录音带、录像带、光盘及其母盘或者其他有宣传作用的物品。  
（二）“制作”，是指编写、印制、复制、绘画、出版、录制、摄制、洗印等行为。  
（三）“传播”，是指散发、张贴、邮寄、上载、播放以及发送电子信息等行为。

**Article 13** : For the purpose of these Provisions, the following terms shall have the meanings specified:  
(1) "Propaganda materials" means leaflets, slogans, spray paintings, pictures, books, newspapers, audio tapes, video tapes, discs and their master discs, or other articles with a propaganda function.  
(2) "Production" means the acts of writing, printing, reproducing, drawing, publishing, recording, filming, developing, and the like.  
(3) "Dissemination" means the acts of distributing, posting, mailing, uploading, playing, sending electronic information, and the like.
