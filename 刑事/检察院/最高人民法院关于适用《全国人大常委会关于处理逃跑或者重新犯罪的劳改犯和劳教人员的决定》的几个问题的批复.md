# 最高人民法院关于适用《全国人大常委会关于处理逃跑或者重新犯罪的劳改犯和劳教人员的决定》的几个问题的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法复〔1993〕4号  
**公布日期**：1993-07-24

---

### 正文

一、关于不满十六岁的未成年人因实施危害行为被政府收容教养，在解除教养后三年内犯罪的，是否适用《决定》的问题，同意你院的意见，即：依照刑法第十四条第四款的规定由政府收容教养的人，不属于劳教人员；解除教养后又犯罪的，不适用《决定》。

**Article None** 1. With respect to the issue of whether the Decision shall apply to minors under the age of 16 who have been taken into custody by the government for education due to committing harmful acts and who commit crimes within three years after release from such education, this Court agrees with the opinion of your court, namely: Persons taken into custody by the government for education in accordance with Article 14, Paragraph 4 of the Criminal Law shall not be considered as reeducation-through-labor personnel; where they commit crimes again after release from education, the Decision shall not apply.

二、关于被判处拘役的罪犯，逃跑后或者刑满释放后又犯罪的，是否适用《决定》的问题，我们认为，按照刑法第三十八条、第四十一条的规定，被判处拘役的罪犯不属于劳改犯。因此，其逃跑后或者刑满释放后又犯罪的，不适用《决定》。

**Article None** 2. With respect to the issue of whether the Decision shall apply to offenders sentenced to criminal detention who commit crimes after escape or after release upon completion of the sentence, we are of the opinion that, in accordance with Articles 38 and 41 of the Criminal Law, offenders sentenced to criminal detention shall not be considered as reform-through-labor offenders. Therefore, where they commit crimes after escape or after release upon completion of the sentence, the Decision shall not apply.

三、关于被判处管制或者有期徒刑宣告缓刑的罪犯，管制期满后或者缓刑考验期满后又犯罪的，是否适用《决定》的问题，同意你院的意见，即：被判处管制或者有期徒刑宣告缓刑的罪犯不属于劳改犯，因此，其管制期满后或者缓刑考验期满后又犯罪的，不适用《决定》。  
1993年7月24日

**Article None** 3. With respect to the issue of whether the Decision shall apply to offenders sentenced to public surveillance or to fixed-term imprisonment with probation who commit crimes after expiration of public surveillance or after expiration of the probation period, this Court agrees with the opinion of your court, namely: Offenders sentenced to public surveillance or to fixed-term imprisonment with probation shall not be considered as reform-through-labor offenders. Therefore, where they commit crimes after expiration of public surveillance or after expiration of the probation period, the Decision shall not apply.  
July 24, 1993
