### 最高人民法院关于刑事案件取保候审的被告人在法院审理期间潜逃应宣告中止审理的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于刑事案件取保候审的被告人  
  
在法院审理期间潜逃应宣告中止审理的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1988〕29号  
  
浙江省高级人民法院：  
  
你院浙法研字（87）57号《关于如何处理在法院审理期间刑事案件被告人畏罪潜逃的问题的请示》已收悉。经研究，答复如下：刑事案件取保候审的被告人在法院审理期间潜逃，人民法院应决定中止审理，俟被告人追捕归案后，再恢复审理。  
  
在人民检察院提起公诉时，取保候审的被告人已潜逃的，人民法院应将案件退回人民检察院，在被告人追捕归案后，再提起公诉；在人民检察院提起公诉后，即使在起诉书副本尚未送达取保候审的被告人之前，被告人已潜逃的，  
  
人民法院仍应决定中止审理，俊被告人追捕归案后，再恢复审理。  
  
中止审理的时问，不计入案件审理期限内。  
  
此复  
  
1988年7月6日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply Concerning That a Defendant Released on Bail Pending Investigation or Trial Who Absconds During Court Trial Shall Be Declared to Suspend the Trial  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1988] No. 29  
Zhejiang Higher People's Court:  
Your court's Zhe Fa Yan Zi (87) No. 57 "Request for Instructions on How to Handle Cases Where the Defendant in Criminal Cases Absconds Out of Fear during Court Trial" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows: If a defendant released on bail pending investigation or trial absconds during court trial, the People's Court shall decide to suspend the trial, and after the defendant is arrested and brought back, the trial shall resume.  
Where the defendant released on bail pending investigation or trial has already absconded at the time the People's Procuratorate initiates a public prosecution, the People's Court shall return the case to the People's Procuratorate; after the defendant is arrested and brought back, a public prosecution shall be initiated again. After the People's Procuratorate initiates a public prosecution, even if the defendant has absconded before the copy of the indictment is served on the defendant released on bail pending investigation or trial,  
the People's Court shall still decide to suspend the trial, and after the defendant is arrested and brought back, the trial shall resume.  
The period of suspension of trial shall not be counted within the case trial period.  
This is issued in reply.  
July 6, 1988
