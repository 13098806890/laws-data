# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理妨害信用卡管理刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释

**发布机关**：最高人民法院

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理妨害信用卡管理刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2009年10月12日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1475次会议、2009年11月12日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第22次会议通过，现予公布，自2009年12月16日起施行。 2009年12月3日 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院 关于办理妨害信用卡管理刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2009〕19号 为依法惩治妨害信用卡管理犯罪活动，维护信用卡管理秩序和持卡人合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》规定，现就办理这类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 复制他人信用卡、将他人信用卡信息资料写入磁条介质、芯片或者以其他方法伪造信用卡1张以上的，应当认定为刑法第一百七十七条第一款第（四）项规定的“伪造信用卡”，以伪造金融票证罪定罪处罚。 伪造空白信用卡10张以上的，应当认定为刑法第一百七十七条第一款第（四）项规定的“伪造信用卡”，以伪造金融票证罪定罪处罚。 伪造信用卡，有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百七十七条规定的“情节严重”： （一）伪造信用卡5张以上不满25张的； （二）伪造的信用卡内存款余额、透支额度单独或者合计数额在20万元以上不满100万元的； （三）伪造空白信用卡50张以上不满250张的； （四）其他情节严重的情形。 伪造信用卡，有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百七十七条规定的“情节特别严重”：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 复制他人信用卡、将他人信用卡信息资料写入磁条介质、芯片或者以其他方法伪造信用卡1张以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)第一款第（四）项规定的“伪造信用卡”，以伪造金融票证罪定罪处罚。  
伪造空白信用卡10张以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)第一款第（四）项规定的“伪造信用卡”，以伪造金融票证罪定罪处罚。  
伪造信用卡，有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）伪造信用卡5张以上不满25张的；  
（二）伪造的信用卡内存款余额、透支额度单独或者合计数额在20万元以上不满100万元的；  
（三）伪造空白信用卡50张以上不满250张的；  
（四）其他情节严重的情形。  
伪造信用卡，有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）伪造信用卡25张以上的；  
（二）伪造的信用卡内存款余额、透支额度单独或者合计数额在100万元以上的；  
（三）伪造空白信用卡250张以上的；  
（四）其他情节特别严重的情形。  
本条所称“信用卡内存款余额、透支额度”，以信用卡被伪造后发卡行记录的最高存款余额、可透支额度计算。

**Article 1** The reproduction of another person's credit card, writing the credit card information data of another person into a magnetic stripe medium or chip, or counterfeiting a credit card by other means involving one or more cards shall be determined as "counterfeiting a credit card" as provided in subparagraph (4), paragraph 1, Article 177 of the Criminal Law, and shall be convicted and punished as the crime of counterfeiting financial instruments.  
Counterfeiting ten or more blank credit cards shall be determined as "counterfeiting a credit card" as provided in subparagraph (4), paragraph 1, Article 177 of the Criminal Law, and shall be convicted and punished as the crime of counterfeiting financial instruments.  
Where a credit card is counterfeited under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 177 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Counterfeiting five or more but less than 25 credit cards;  
(2) The deposit balance or overdraft limit in the counterfeited credit card, individually or in aggregate, is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 1,000,000 yuan;  
(3) Counterfeiting 50 or more but less than 250 blank credit cards;  
(4) Other circumstances that are serious.  
Where a credit card is counterfeited under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "exceptionally serious circumstances" as provided in Article 177 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Counterfeiting 25 or more credit cards;  
(2) The deposit balance or overdraft limit in the counterfeited credit card, individually or in aggregate, is 1,000,000 yuan or more;  
(3) Counterfeiting 250 or more blank credit cards;  
(4) Other circumstances that are exceptionally serious.  
The "deposit balance or overdraft limit in the credit card" as mentioned in this Article shall be calculated based on the highest deposit balance or available overdraft limit recorded by the issuing bank after the credit card is counterfeited.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 明知是伪造的空白信用卡而持有、运输10张以上不满100张的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款第（一）项规定的“数量较大”；非法持有他人信用卡5张以上不满50张的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款第（二）项规定的“数量较大”。  
有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款规定的“数量巨大”：  
（一）明知是伪造的信用卡而持有、运输10张以上的；  
（二）明知是伪造的空白信用卡而持有、运输100张以上的；  
（三）非法持有他人信用卡50张以上的；  
（四）使用虚假的身份证明骗领信用卡10张以上的；  
（五）出售、购买、为他人提供伪造的信用卡或者以虚假的身份证明骗领的信用卡10张以上的。  
违背他人意愿，使用其居民身份证、军官证、士兵证、港澳居民往来内地通行证、台湾居民来往大陆通行证、护照等身份证明申领信用卡的，或者使用伪造、变造的身份证明申领信用卡的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款第（三）项规定的“使用虚假的身份证明骗领信用卡”。

**Article 2** The possession or transportation of ten or more but less than 100 blank credit cards known to be counterfeited shall be determined as "relatively large quantity" as provided in subparagraph (1), paragraph 1, Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law; the illegal possession of five or more but less than 50 credit cards of another person shall be determined as "relatively large quantity" as provided in subparagraph (2), paragraph 1, Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be determined as "large quantity" as provided in paragraph 1, Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Possession or transportation of ten or more credit cards known to be counterfeited;  
(2) Possession or transportation of 100 or more blank credit cards known to be counterfeited;  
(3) Illegal possession of 50 or more credit cards of another person;  
(4) Obtaining credit cards by fraudulent means using false identity certificates, involving ten or more cards;  
(5) Selling, purchasing, or providing to others counterfeited credit cards or credit cards obtained by fraudulent means using false identity certificates, involving ten or more cards.  
The use, against another person's will, of their resident identity card, military officer card, soldier card, Hong Kong and Macao residents' travel permit to the Mainland, Taiwan residents' travel permit to the Mainland, passport, or other identity certificates to apply for a credit card, or the use of forged or altered identity certificates to apply for a credit card, shall be determined as "obtaining a credit card by fraudulent means using false identity certificates" as provided in subparagraph (3), paragraph 1, Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 窃取、收买、非法提供他人信用卡信息资料，足以伪造可进行交易的信用卡，或者足以使他人以信用卡持卡人名义进行交易，涉及信用卡1张以上不满5张的，依照[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第二款的规定，以窃取、收买、非法提供信用卡信息罪定罪处罚；涉及信用卡5张以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-177)之一第一款规定的“数量巨大”。

**Article 3** The stealing, purchasing, or illegal provision of credit card information data of another person, sufficient to counterfeit a credit card that can be used for transactions, or sufficient to enable another person to conduct transactions in the name of a credit card holder, involving one or more but less than five credit cards, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 2, Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law as the crime of stealing, purchasing, or illegally providing credit card information; where it involves five or more credit cards, it shall be determined as "large quantity" as provided in paragraph 1, Article 177(A) of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 为信用卡申请人制作、提供虚假的财产状况、收入、职务等资信证明材料，涉及伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章，或者涉及伪造公司、企业、事业单位、人民团体印章，应当追究刑事责任的，依照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)的规定，分别以伪造、变造、买卖国家机关公文、证件、印章罪和伪造公司、企业、事业单位、人民团体印章罪定罪处罚。  
承担资产评估、验资、验证、会计、审计、法律服务等职责的中介组织或其人员，为信用卡申请人提供虚假的财产状况、收入、职务等资信证明材料，应当追究刑事责任的，依照[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)的规定，分别以提供虚假证明文件罪和出具证明文件重大失实罪定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where a person produces or provides false materials on property status, income, position, or other credit information for a credit card applicant, involving the forgery, alteration, or trading of official documents, certificates, or seals of state organs, or involving the forgery of seals of companies, enterprises, institutions, or people's organizations, and criminal liability shall be investigated, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 280 of the Criminal Law as the crime of forging, altering, or trading official documents, certificates, or seals of state organs and the crime of forging seals of companies, enterprises, institutions, or people's organizations, respectively.  
Where an intermediary organization or its personnel that undertakes duties such as asset appraisal, capital verification, validation, accounting, auditing, or legal services provides false materials on property status, income, position, or other credit information for a credit card applicant, and criminal liability shall be investigated, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 229 of the Criminal Law as the crime of providing false certification documents and the crime of issuing grossly inaccurate certification documents, respectively.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 使用伪造的信用卡、以虚假的身份证明骗领的信用卡、作废的信用卡或者冒用他人信用卡，进行信用卡诈骗活动，数额在5000元以上不满5万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额较大”；数额在5万元以上不满50万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额巨大”；数额在50万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额特别巨大”。  
[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)第一款第（三）项所称“冒用他人信用卡”，包括以下情形：  
（一）拾得他人信用卡并使用的；  
（二）骗取他人信用卡并使用的；  
（三）窃取、收买、骗取或者以其他非法方式获取他人信用卡信息资料，并通过互联网、通讯终端等使用的；  
（四）其他冒用他人信用卡的情形。

**Article 5** Where a person uses a counterfeited credit card, a credit card obtained by fraudulent means using false identity certificates, an invalidated credit card, or uses another person's credit card without authorization, to engage in credit card fraud, and the amount is 5,000 yuan or more but less than 50,000 yuan, it shall be determined as "relatively large amount" as provided in Article 196 of the Criminal Law; where the amount is 50,000 yuan or more but less than 500,000 yuan, it shall be determined as "large amount" as provided in Article 196 of the Criminal Law; where the amount is 500,000 yuan or more, it shall be determined as "exceptionally large amount" as provided in Article 196 of the Criminal Law.  
"Using another person's credit card without authorization" as mentioned in subparagraph (3), paragraph 1, Article 196 of the Criminal Law includes the following circumstances:  
(1) Picking up another person's credit card and using it;  
(2) Obtaining another person's credit card by fraud and using it;  
(3) Stealing, purchasing, obtaining by fraud, or obtaining by other illegal means the credit card information data of another person, and using it through the Internet, communication terminals, or other means;  
(4) Other circumstances of using another person's credit card without authorization.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 持卡人以非法占有为目的，超过规定限额或者规定期限透支，并且经发卡银行两次催收后超过3个月仍不归还的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“恶意透支”。  
有以下情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)第二款规定的“以非法占有为目的”：  
（一）明知没有还款能力而大量透支，无法归还的；  
（二）肆意挥霍透支的资金，无法归还的；  
（三）透支后逃匿、改变联系方式，逃避银行催收的；  
（四）抽逃、转移资金，隐匿财产，逃避还款的；  
（五）使用透支的资金进行违法犯罪活动的；  
（六）其他非法占有资金，拒不归还的行为。  
恶意透支，数额在1万元以上不满10万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额较大”；数额在10万元以上不满100万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额巨大”；数额在100万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)规定的“数额特别巨大”。  
恶意透支的数额，是指在第一款规定的条件下持卡人拒不归还的数额或者尚未归还的数额。不包括复利、滞纳金、手续费等发卡银行收取的费用。  
恶意透支应当追究刑事责任，但在公安机关立案后人民法院判决宣告前已偿还全部透支款息的，可以从轻处罚，情节轻微的，可以免除处罚。恶意透支数额较大，在公安机关立案前已偿还全部透支款息，情节显著轻微的，可以依法不追究刑事责任。

**Article 6** Where a cardholder, for the purpose of illegal possession, overdrafts beyond the prescribed limit or beyond the prescribed time limit and fails to repay within three months after two collections by the issuing bank, it shall be determined as "malicious overdraft" as provided in Article 196 of the Criminal Law.  
Where any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be determined as "for the purpose of illegal possession" as provided in paragraph 2, Article 196 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Knowingly having no repayment ability but making a large amount of overdraft and being unable to repay;  
(2) Wantonly squandering the overdraft funds and being unable to repay;  
(3) Absconding, changing contact information, or evading bank collection after the overdraft;  
(4) Illegally transferring or moving funds, concealing property, or evading repayment;  
(5) Using the overdraft funds to engage in illegal or criminal activities;  
(6) Other acts of illegally possessing funds and refusing to repay.  
For malicious overdraft, where the amount is 10,000 yuan or more but less than 100,000 yuan, it shall be determined as "relatively large amount" as provided in Article 196 of the Criminal Law; where the amount is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 1,000,000 yuan, it shall be determined as "large amount" as provided in Article 196 of the Criminal Law; where the amount is 1,000,000 yuan or more, it shall be determined as "exceptionally large amount" as provided in Article 196 of the Criminal Law.  
The amount of malicious overdraft refers to the amount that the cardholder refuses to repay or has not yet repaid under the conditions specified in paragraph 1. It shall not include compound interest, late fees, handling fees, or other fees charged by the issuing bank.  
Where malicious overdraft shall be investigated for criminal liability, but the full amount of the overdraft and interest has been repaid before a judgment is pronounced by the People's Court after the case is filed by the public security organ, a light punishment may be imposed; if the circumstances are minor, punishment may be waived. Where the amount of malicious overdraft is relatively large but the full amount of overdraft and interest has been repaid before the case is filed by the public security organ, and the circumstances are clearly minor, criminal liability may not be investigated according to law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 违反国家规定，使用销售点终端机具（POS机）等方法，以虚构交易、虚开价格、现金退货等方式向信用卡持卡人直接支付现金，情节严重的，应当依据[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定，以非法经营罪定罪处罚。  
实施前款行为，数额在100万元以上的，或者造成金融机构资金20万元以上逾期未还的，或者造成金融机构经济损失10万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节严重”；数额在500万元以上的，或者造成金融机构资金100万元以上逾期未还的，或者造成金融机构经济损失50万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)规定的“情节特别严重”。  
持卡人以非法占有为目的，采用上述方式恶意透支，应当追究刑事责任的，依照[刑法第一百九十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-196)的规定，以信用卡诈骗罪定罪处罚。

**Article 7** Where a person, in violation of state regulations, uses point-of-sale (POS) terminals or other methods to directly pay cash to a credit card holder by means of fictitious transactions, falsely invoiced prices, cash refunds, or other means, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law as the crime of illegal business operations.  
Where the amount involved in the conduct in the preceding paragraph is 1,000,000 yuan or more, or causes overdue non-repayment of 200,000 yuan or more of financial institution funds, or causes financial institution economic losses of 100,000 yuan or more, it shall be determined as "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 225 of the Criminal Law; where the amount involved is 5,000,000 yuan or more, or causes overdue non-repayment of 1,000,000 yuan or more of financial institution funds, or causes financial institution economic losses of 500,000 yuan or more, it shall be determined as "exceptionally serious circumstances" as provided in Article 225 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a cardholder, for the purpose of illegal possession, uses the above methods to maliciously overdraft and criminal liability shall be investigated, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 196 of the Criminal Law as the crime of credit card fraud.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 单位犯[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第七条规定的犯罪的，定罪量刑标准依照各该条的规定执行。

**Article 8** Where a unit commits the crimes specified in Articles 1 and 7 of this Interpretation, the standards for conviction and sentencing shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of those respective Articles.
