# 最高人民法院关于审理拒不支付劳动报酬刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕3号  
**公布日期**：2013-01-14  
**生效日期**：2013-01-23

> 最高人民法院《关于审理拒不支付劳动报酬刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年1月14日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1567次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年1月23日起施行。 2013年1月16日 最高人民法院 关于审理拒不支付劳动报酬刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕3号 为依法惩治拒不支付劳动报酬犯罪，维护劳动者的合法权益，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   劳动者依照《中华人民共和国劳动法》和《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》等法律的规定应得的劳动报酬，包括工资、奖金、津贴、补贴、延长工作时间的工资报酬及特殊情况下支付的工资等，应当认定为刑法第二百七十六条之一第一款规定的“劳动者的劳动报酬”。 第二条   以逃避支付劳动者的劳动报酬为目的，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百七十六条之一第一款规定的“以转移财产、逃匿等方法逃避支付劳动者的劳动报酬”： （一）隐匿财产、恶意清偿、虚构债务、虚假破产、虚假倒闭或者以其他方法转移、处分财产的； （二）逃跑、藏匿的； （三）隐匿、销毁或者篡改账目、职工名册、工资支付记录、考勤记录等与劳动报酬相关的材料的； （四）以其他方法逃避支付劳动报酬的。 第三条   具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百七十六条之一第一款规定的“数额较大”： （一）拒不支付一名劳动者三个月以上的劳动报酬且数额在五千元至二万元以上的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   劳动者依照《中华人民共和国劳动法》和《中华人民共和国劳动合同法》等法律的规定应得的劳动报酬，包括工资、奖金、津贴、补贴、延长工作时间的工资报酬及特殊情况下支付的工资等，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-276)之一第一款规定的“劳动者的劳动报酬”。

**Article 1** The labor remuneration that a worker is entitled to according to the provisions of the Labor Law of the People's Republic of China, the Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China and other laws, including wages, bonuses, allowances, subsidies, wages for extended working hours and wages paid under special circumstances, shall be determined as the "labor remuneration of workers" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 276A of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   以逃避支付劳动者的劳动报酬为目的，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-276)之一第一款规定的“以转移财产、逃匿等方法逃避支付劳动者的劳动报酬”：  
（一）隐匿财产、恶意清偿、虚构债务、虚假破产、虚假倒闭或者以其他方法转移、处分财产的；  
（二）逃跑、藏匿的；  
（三）隐匿、销毁或者篡改账目、职工名册、工资支付记录、考勤记录等与劳动报酬相关的材料的；  
（四）以其他方法逃避支付劳动报酬的。

**Article 2** For the purpose of evading payment of workers' labor remuneration, any of the following circumstances shall be determined as "evading payment of workers' labor remuneration by means such as transferring property, absconding, etc." as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 276A of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Concealing property, maliciously settling debts, fabricating debts, falsely declaring bankruptcy, falsely closing down, or transferring or disposing of property by other means;  
(2) Fleeing or hiding;  
(3) Concealing, destroying or altering accounts, employee rosters, wage payment records, attendance records and other materials related to labor remuneration;  
(4) Evading payment of labor remuneration by other means.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-276)之一第一款规定的“数额较大”：  
（一）拒不支付一名劳动者三个月以上的劳动报酬且数额在五千元至二万元以上的；  
（二）拒不支付十名以上劳动者的劳动报酬且数额累计在三万元至十万元以上的。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以根据本地区经济社会发展状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，研究确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 3** Any of the following circumstances shall be determined as "relatively large amount" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 276A of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Refusing to pay the labor remuneration of one worker for more than three months and the amount is from 5,000 yuan to 20,000 yuan or more;  
(2) Refusing to pay the labor remuneration of ten or more workers and the cumulative amount is from 30,000 yuan to 100,000 yuan or more.  
The Higher People's Courts of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, in light of the economic and social development of their respective regions, study and determine the specific amount standards for enforcement within the range of amounts stipulated in the preceding paragraph, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court for recordation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   经人力资源社会保障部门或者政府其他有关部门依法以限期整改指令书、行政处理决定书等文书责令支付劳动者的劳动报酬后，在指定的期限内仍不支付的，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-276)之一第一款规定的“经政府有关部门责令支付仍不支付”，但有证据证明行为人有正当理由未知悉责令支付或者未及时支付劳动报酬的除外。  
行为人逃匿，无法将责令支付文书送交其本人、同住成年家属或者所在单位负责收件的人的，如果有关部门已通过在行为人的住所地、生产经营场所等地张贴责令支付文书等方式责令支付，并采用拍照、录像等方式记录的，应当视为“经政府有关部门责令支付”。

**Article 4** After the human resources and social security department or other relevant government departments have, in accordance with law, ordered payment of workers' labor remuneration by means of documents such as a letter of order for rectification within a time limit or an administrative decision on treatment, if payment is still not made within the specified time limit, it shall be determined as "having been ordered to pay by the relevant government department but still failing to pay" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 276A of the Criminal Law, unless there is evidence proving that the actor had a justified reason for being unaware of the order to pay or failing to pay the labor remuneration in a timely manner.  
If the actor absconds and the document ordering payment cannot be served on the actor himself, his adult family member living with him, or the person responsible for receiving documents in his unit, and if the relevant department has ordered payment by posting the order document at the actor's domicile, place of production and operation, etc., and has recorded such actions by means of photographing, video recording, etc., it shall be deemed as "having been ordered to pay by the relevant government department."

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   拒不支付劳动者的劳动报酬，符合[本解释第三条](#art-3)的规定，并具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-276)之一第一款规定的“造成严重后果”：  
（一）造成劳动者或者其被赡养人、被扶养人、被抚养人的基本生活受到严重影响、重大疾病无法及时医治或者失学的；  
（二）对要求支付劳动报酬的劳动者使用暴力或者进行暴力威胁的；  
（三）造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 5** Refusing to pay workers' labor remuneration, which meets the provisions of Article 3 of this Interpretation, and falls under any of the following circumstances, shall be determined as "causing serious consequences" as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 276A of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing the basic livelihood of the worker or his dependent parents, dependent persons or supported persons to be seriously affected, causing a serious disease to be unable to receive timely medical treatment, or causing a child to be deprived of schooling;  
(2) Using violence or threatening violence against a worker who demands payment of labor remuneration;  
(3) Causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   拒不支付劳动者的劳动报酬，尚未造成严重后果，在刑事立案前支付劳动者的劳动报酬，并依法承担相应赔偿责任的，可以认定为情节显著轻微危害不大，不认为是犯罪；在提起公诉前支付劳动者的劳动报酬，并依法承担相应赔偿责任的，可以减轻或者免除刑事处罚；在一审宣判前支付劳动者的劳动报酬，并依法承担相应赔偿责任的，可以从轻处罚。  
对于免除刑事处罚的，可以根据案件的不同情况，予以训诫、责令具结悔过或者赔礼道歉。  
拒不支付劳动者的劳动报酬，造成严重后果，但在宣判前支付劳动者的劳动报酬，并依法承担相应赔偿责任的，可以酌情从宽处罚。

**Article 6** If a person refuses to pay workers' labor remuneration without causing serious consequences, and pays the workers' labor remuneration before criminal case filing and bears the corresponding compensation liability according to law, it may be determined that the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not great, and the act shall not be considered a crime; if the person pays the workers' labor remuneration before public prosecution and bears the corresponding compensation liability according to law, the criminal punishment may be mitigated or exempted; if the person pays the workers' labor remuneration before the judgment of first instance and bears the corresponding compensation liability according to law, a light punishment may be imposed.  
For those exempted from criminal punishment, depending on the circumstances of the case, a reprimand, an order to sign a statement of repentance, or an apology may be imposed.  
If a person refuses to pay workers' labor remuneration, causing serious consequences, but pays the workers' labor remuneration before judgment and bears the corresponding compensation liability according to law, a lenient punishment may be imposed discretionarily.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   不具备用工主体资格的单位或者个人，违法用工且拒不支付劳动者的劳动报酬，数额较大，经政府有关部门责令支付仍不支付的，应当依照[刑法第二百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-276)之一的规定，以拒不支付劳动报酬罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 7** If a unit or individual that does not have the qualification for employing workers illegally employs workers and refuses to pay workers' labor remuneration, with a relatively large amount, and after being ordered to pay by the relevant government department still fails to pay, criminal liability shall be investigated for the crime of refusing to pay labor remuneration in accordance with the provisions of Article 276A of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   用人单位的实际控制人实施拒不支付劳动报酬行为，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第二百七十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-276)之一的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 8** If the actual controller of an employer commits the act of refusing to pay labor remuneration, which constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with the provisions of Article 276A of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   单位拒不支付劳动报酬，构成犯罪的，依照本解释规定的相应个人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。

**Article 9** If a unit refuses to pay labor remuneration, which constitutes a crime, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly responsible personnel shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the standards for conviction and sentencing of the corresponding individual crime as prescribed in this Interpretation, and a fine shall be imposed on the unit.
