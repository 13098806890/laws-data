# 最高人民法院关于审理编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕24号  
**公布日期**：2013-09-16  
**生效日期**：2013-09-30

> 最高人民法院 《关于审理编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年9月16日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1591次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年9月30日起施行。 2013年9月18日 最高人民法院 关于审理编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕24号 为依法惩治编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息犯罪活动，维护社会秩序，维护人民群众生命、财产安全，根据刑法有关规定，现对审理此类案件具体适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   编造恐怖信息，传播或者放任传播，严重扰乱社会秩序的，依照刑法第二百九十一条之一的规定，应认定为编造虚假恐怖信息罪。 明知是他人编造的恐怖信息而故意传播，严重扰乱社会秩序的，依照刑法第二百九十一条之一的规定，应认定为故意传播虚假恐怖信息罪。 第二条   编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第二百九十一条之一的“严重扰乱社会秩序”： （一）致使机场、车站、码头、商场、影剧院、运动场馆等人员密集场所秩序混乱，或者采取紧急疏散措施的； （二）影响航空器、列车、船舶等大型客运交通工具正常运行的； （三）致使国家机关、学校、医院、厂矿企业等单位的工作、生产、经营、教学、科研等活动中断的； （四）造成行政村或者社区居民生活秩序严重混乱的； （五）致使公安、武警、消防、卫生检疫等职能部门采取紧急应对措施的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   编造恐怖信息，传播或者放任传播，严重扰乱社会秩序的，依照[刑法第二百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-291)之一的规定，应认定为编造虚假恐怖信息罪。  
明知是他人编造的恐怖信息而故意传播，严重扰乱社会秩序的，依照[刑法第二百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-291)之一的规定，应认定为故意传播虚假恐怖信息罪。

**Article 1** Whoever fabricates terrorist information, and disseminates or allows the dissemination thereof, thereby seriously disrupting public order, shall be determined to have committed the crime of fabricating false terrorist information in accordance with the provisions of Article 291a of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
Whoever, knowing that the terrorist information is fabricated by another person, intentionally disseminates the information, thereby seriously disrupting public order, shall be determined to have committed the crime of intentionally disseminating false terrorist information in accordance with the provisions of Article 291a of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-291)之一的“严重扰乱社会秩序”：  
（一）致使机场、车站、码头、商场、影剧院、运动场馆等人员密集场所秩序混乱，或者采取紧急疏散措施的；  
（二）影响航空器、列车、船舶等大型客运交通工具正常运行的；  
（三）致使国家机关、学校、医院、厂矿企业等单位的工作、生产、经营、教学、科研等活动中断的；  
（四）造成行政村或者社区居民生活秩序严重混乱的；  
（五）致使公安、武警、消防、卫生检疫等职能部门采取紧急应对措施的；  
（六）其他严重扰乱社会秩序的。

**Article 2** Fabricating or intentionally disseminating false terrorist information, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as “seriously disrupting public order” under Article 291a of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causing chaos in crowded places such as airports, train stations, docks, shopping malls, cinemas, theaters, or sports venues, or necessitating emergency evacuation measures;  
(2) Affecting the normal operation of large passenger transport vehicles such as aircraft, trains, or ships;  
(3) Causing interruption of work, production, operation, teaching, scientific research, or other activities in state organs, schools, hospitals, factories, mines, or enterprises;  
(4) Causing serious disorder in the living order of an administrative village or a residential community;  
(5) Causing functional departments such as public security, armed police, firefighting, or health quarantine to take emergency response measures;  
(6) Other circumstances seriously disrupting public order.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息，严重扰乱社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第二百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-291)之一的规定，在五年以下有期徒刑范围内酌情从重处罚：  
（一）致使航班备降或返航；或者致使列车、船舶等大型客运交通工具中断运行的；  
（二）多次编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息的；  
（三）造成直接经济损失二十万元以上的；  
（四）造成乡镇、街道区域范围居民生活秩序严重混乱的；  
（五）具有其他酌情从重处罚情节的。

**Article 3** Where a person fabricates or intentionally disseminates false terrorist information, seriously disrupting public order, and falls under any of the following circumstances, a severe punishment within the range of fixed-term imprisonment of not more than five years shall be imposed at the court's discretion in accordance with the provisions of Article 291a of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causing an aircraft to make an alternate landing or return, or causing the interruption of the operation of large passenger transport vehicles such as trains or ships;  
(2) Fabricating or intentionally disseminating false terrorist information on multiple occasions;  
(3) Causing direct economic losses of RMB 200,000 yuan or more;  
(4) Causing serious disorder in the living order of residents in the area of a township or sub-district;  
(5) Having other circumstances for a discretionary severe punishment.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息，严重扰乱社会秩序，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百九十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-291)之一的 “造成严重后果”，处五年以上有期徒刑：  
（一）造成三人以上轻伤或者一人以上重伤的；  
（二）造成直接经济损失五十万元以上的；  
（三）造成县级以上区域范围居民生活秩序严重混乱的；  
（四）妨碍国家重大活动进行的；  
（五）造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 4** Where a person fabricates or intentionally disseminates false terrorist information, seriously disrupting public order, and falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as “causing serious consequences” under Article 291a of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the person shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years:  
(1) Causing minor injuries to three or more persons, or serious injuries to one or more persons;  
(2) Causing direct economic losses of RMB 500,000 yuan or more;  
(3) Causing serious disorder in the living order of residents in an area at or above the county level;  
(4) Hindering the conduct of major state activities;  
(5) Causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   编造、故意传播虚假恐怖信息，严重扰乱社会秩序，同时又构成其他犯罪的，择一重罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where the acts of fabricating or intentionally disseminating false terrorist information, which seriously disrupt public order, also constitute other crimes, the punishment shall be imposed for the crime that carries a heavier penalty.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   本解释所称的“虚假恐怖信息”，是指以发生爆炸威胁、生化威胁、放射威胁、劫持航空器威胁、重大灾情、重大疫情等严重威胁公共安全的事件为内容，可能引起社会恐慌或者公共安全危机的不真实信息。

**Article 6** The term “false terrorist information” as used in this Interpretation means untrue information that takes as its content events such as threats of explosion, biological or chemical threats, radiation threats, threats to hijack aircraft, major disasters, major epidemics, or other events that seriously threaten public security, and that may cause social panic or a public security crisis.
