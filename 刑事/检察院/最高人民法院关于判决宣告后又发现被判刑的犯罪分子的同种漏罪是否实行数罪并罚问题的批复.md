### 最高人民法院关于判决宣告后又发现被判刑的犯罪分子的同种漏罪是否实行数罪并罚问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于判决宣告后又发现被判刑  
  
的犯罪分子的同种漏罪是否实行数罪并罚  
  
问题的批复  
  
法复〔1993〕3号  
  
江西省高级人民法院：  
  
你院赣高法〔1992〕39号《关于判决宣告后又发现被判刑的犯罪分子的同种漏罪是否按数罪并罚处理的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
人民法院的判决宣告并已发生法律效力以后，刑罚还没有执行完毕以前，发现被判刑的犯罪分子在判决宣告以前还有其他罪没有判决的，不论新发现的罪与原判决的罪是否属于同种罪，都应当依照[刑法第六十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-65)的规定实行数罪并罚。但如果在第一审人民法院的判决宣告以后，被告人提出上诉或者人民检察院提出抗诉，判决尚未发生法律效力的，第二审人民法院在审理期间，发现原审被告人在第一审判决宣告以前还有同种漏罪没有判决的，第二审人民法院应当依照[刑事诉讼法第一百三十六条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-136)第（三）项的规定，裁定撤销原判，发回原审人民法院重新审判，第一审人民法院重新审判时，不适用刑法关于数罪并罚的规定。  
  
1993年4月16日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply Concerning Whether Concurrent Punishment for Multiple Crimes Shall Be Imposed When an Omitted Crime of the Same Kind Is Discovered After the Judgment Is Pronounced  
Fa Fu [1993] No. 3  
Higher People's Court of Jiangxi Province:  
We have received your court's Gan Gao Fa [1992] No. 39 "Request for Instructions on Whether Concurrent Punishment for Multiple Crimes Shall Be Imposed When an Omitted Crime of the Same Kind Is Discovered After the Judgment Is Pronounced Against the Sentenced Criminal." Upon review, we reply as follows:  
After a judgment of the People's Court is pronounced and has become legally effective, before the execution of the penalty is completed, if it is discovered that the sentenced criminal has other crimes that were not adjudicated before the judgment was pronounced, regardless of whether the newly discovered crime and the crime in the original judgment are of the same kind, concurrent punishment for multiple crimes shall be imposed in accordance with Article 65 of the Criminal Law. However, if after the judgment of the first-instance People's Court is pronounced, the defendant files an appeal or the People's Procuratorate files a protest, and the judgment has not yet become legally effective, if during the trial the second-instance People's Court discovers that the original defendant had an omitted crime of the same kind that was not adjudicated before the first-instance judgment was pronounced, the second-instance People's Court shall, in accordance with Article 136, Item (3) of the Criminal Procedure Law, render a ruling to vacate the original judgment and remand the case to the original-instance People's Court for a new trial. When the first-instance People's Court retries the case, the provisions of the Criminal Law regarding concurrent punishment for multiple crimes shall not apply.  
April 16, 1993
