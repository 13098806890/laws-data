# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理危害食品安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕12号  
**公布日期**：2013-04-28  
**生效日期**：2013-05-04

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 《关于办理危害食品安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年4月28日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1576次会议、2013年4月28日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第5次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年5月4日起施行。 2013年5月2日 最高人民法院   最高人民检察院 关于办理危害食品安全刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕12号 为依法惩治危害食品安全犯罪，保障人民群众身体健康、生命安全，根据刑法有关规定，对办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百四十三条规定的“足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病”： （一）含有严重超出标准限量的致病性微生物、农药残留、兽药残留、重金属、污染物质以及其他危害人体健康的物质的； （二）属于病死、死因不明或者检验检疫不合格的畜、禽、兽、水产动物及其肉类、肉类制品的； （三）属于国家为防控疾病等特殊需要明令禁止生产、销售的； （四）婴幼儿食品中生长发育所需营养成分严重不符合食品安全标准的； （五）其他足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者严重食源性疾病的情形。 第二条   生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百四十三条规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”： （一）造成轻伤以上伤害的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病”：  
（一）含有严重超出标准限量的致病性微生物、农药残留、兽药残留、重金属、污染物质以及其他危害人体健康的物质的；  
（二）属于病死、死因不明或者检验检疫不合格的畜、禽、兽、水产动物及其肉类、肉类制品的；  
（三）属于国家为防控疾病等特殊需要明令禁止生产、销售的；  
（四）婴幼儿食品中生长发育所需营养成分严重不符合食品安全标准的；  
（五）其他足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者严重食源性疾病的情形。

**Article 1** The production or sale of food that does not meet food safety standards shall be determined as "sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease" as provided in Article 143 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) containing pathogenic microorganisms, pesticide residues, veterinary drug residues, heavy metals, contaminants, or other substances harmful to human health that seriously exceed the standard limits;  
(2) being livestock, poultry, animals, aquatic animals, or their meat or meat products that are diseased, dead from unknown causes, or fail inspection and quarantine;  
(3) being products that the state explicitly prohibits production or sale of for special needs such as disease prevention and control;  
(4) the nutritional components necessary for growth and development in infant food seriously do not meet food safety standards;  
(5) other circumstances sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”：  
（一）造成轻伤以上伤害的；  
（二）造成轻度残疾或者中度残疾的；  
（三）造成器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍或者严重功能障碍的；  
（四）造成十人以上严重食物中毒或者其他严重食源性疾病的；  
（五）其他对人体健康造成严重危害的情形。

**Article 2** The production or sale of food that does not meet food safety standards shall be determined as "causing serious harm to human health" as provided in Article 143 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) causing injury of minor injury or above;  
(2) causing mild or moderate disability;  
(3) causing organ or tissue damage resulting in general or severe dysfunction;  
(4) causing serious food poisoning or other serious foodborne diseases in ten or more persons;  
(5) other circumstances causing serious harm to human health.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）生产、销售金额二十万元以上的；  
（二）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，不符合食品安全标准的食品数量较大或者生产、销售持续时间较长的；  
（三）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，属于婴幼儿食品的；  
（四）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，一年内曾因危害食品安全违法犯罪活动受过行政处罚或者刑事处罚的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 3** The production or sale of food that does not meet food safety standards shall be determined as "other serious circumstances" as provided in Article 143 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the amount of production or sale is 200,000 yuan or more;  
(2) the amount of production or sale is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the quantity of food that does not meet food safety standards is relatively large, or the period of production or sale is relatively long;  
(3) the amount of production or sale is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the food is infant food;  
(4) the amount of production or sale is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the person has previously received an administrative penalty or criminal punishment for illegal or criminal activities endangering food safety within one year;  
(5) other circumstances with serious circumstances.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)规定的“后果特别严重”：  
（一）致人死亡或者重度残疾的；  
（二）造成三人以上重伤、中度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致严重功能障碍的；  
（三）造成十人以上轻伤、五人以上轻度残疾或者器官组织损伤导致一般功能障碍的；  
（四）造成三十人以上严重食物中毒或者其他严重食源性疾病的；  
（五）其他特别严重的后果。

**Article 4** The production or sale of food that does not meet food safety standards shall be determined as "particularly serious consequences" as provided in Article 143 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) causing death or severe disability;  
(2) causing serious injury, moderate disability, or organ or tissue damage resulting in severe dysfunction in three or more persons;  
(3) causing minor injury in ten or more persons, mild disability in five or more persons, or organ or tissue damage resulting in general dysfunction;  
(4) causing serious food poisoning or other serious foodborne diseases in thirty or more persons;  
(5) other particularly serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   生产、销售有毒、有害食品，具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“对人体健康造成严重危害”。

**Article 5** The production or sale of toxic or hazardous food shall be determined as "causing serious harm to human health" as provided in Article 144 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China if it falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 2 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   生产、销售有毒、有害食品，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）生产、销售金额二十万元以上不满五十万元的；  
（二）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，有毒、有害食品的数量较大或者生产、销售持续时间较长的；  
（三）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，属于婴幼儿食品的；  
（四）生产、销售金额十万元以上不满二十万元，一年内曾因危害食品安全违法犯罪活动受过行政处罚或者刑事处罚的；  
（五）有毒、有害的非食品原料毒害性强或者含量高的；  
（六）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 6** The production or sale of toxic or hazardous food shall be determined as "other serious circumstances" as provided in Article 144 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China under any of the following circumstances:  
(1) the amount of production or sale is 200,000 yuan or more but less than 500,000 yuan;  
(2) the amount of production or sale is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the quantity of toxic or hazardous food is relatively large, or the period of production or sale is relatively long;  
(3) the amount of production or sale is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the food is infant food;  
(4) the amount of production or sale is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 200,000 yuan, and the person has previously received an administrative penalty or criminal punishment for illegal or criminal activities endangering food safety within one year;  
(5) the toxic or hazardous non-food raw material has high toxicity or high content;  
(6) other circumstances with serious circumstances.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   生产、销售有毒、有害食品，生产、销售金额五十万元以上，或者具有[本解释第四条](#art-4)规定的情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)规定的“致人死亡或者有其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 7** The production or sale of toxic or hazardous food shall be determined as "causing death or having other particularly serious circumstances" as provided in Article 144 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China if the amount of production or sale is 500,000 yuan or more, or if it falls under any of the circumstances specified in Article 4 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   在食品加工、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，违反食品安全标准，超限量或者超范围滥用食品添加剂，足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病的，依照[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)的规定以生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪定罪处罚。  
在食用农产品种植、养殖、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，违反食品安全标准，超限量或者超范围滥用添加剂、农药、兽药等，足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病的，适用前款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 8** If, during the processing, sale, transportation, storage, or other processes of food, a person violates food safety standards by abusing food additives beyond the limit or scope, which is sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing or selling food that does not meet safety standards in accordance with Article 143 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If, during the planting, breeding, sale, transportation, storage, or other processes of edible agricultural products, a person violates food safety standards by abusing additives, pesticides, veterinary drugs, or other substances beyond the limit or scope, which is sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply for conviction and punishment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   在食品加工、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，掺入有毒、有害的非食品原料，或者使用有毒、有害的非食品原料加工食品的，依照[刑法第一百四十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-144)的规定以生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪定罪处罚。  
在食用农产品种植、养殖、销售、运输、贮存等过程中，使用禁用农药、兽药等禁用物质或者其他有毒、有害物质的，适用前款的规定定罪处罚。  
在保健食品或者其他食品中非法添加国家禁用药物等有毒、有害物质的，适用第一款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 9** If, during the processing, sale, transportation, storage, or other processes of food, a person incorporates toxic or hazardous non-food raw materials, or uses toxic or hazardous non-food raw materials to process food, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing or selling toxic or hazardous food in accordance with Article 144 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If, during the planting, breeding, sale, transportation, storage, or other processes of edible agricultural products, a person uses prohibited pesticides, veterinary drugs, or other prohibited substances, or other toxic or hazardous substances, the provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply for conviction and punishment.  
If a person illegally adds toxic or hazardous substances, such as drugs prohibited by the state, to health food or other food, the provisions of paragraph 1 shall apply for conviction and punishment.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品添加剂，用于食品的包装材料、容器、洗涤剂、消毒剂，或者用于食品生产经营的工具、设备等，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第一百四十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-140)的规定以生产、销售伪劣产品罪定罪处罚。

**Article 10** The production or sale of food additives that do not meet food safety standards, packaging materials, containers, detergents, disinfectants used for food, or tools, equipment used in food production or operation, if constituting a crime, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard products in accordance with Article 140 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   以提供给他人生产、销售食品为目的，违反国家规定，生产、销售国家禁止用于食品生产、销售的非食品原料，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定以非法经营罪定罪处罚。  
违反国家规定，生产、销售国家禁止生产、销售、使用的农药、兽药，饲料、饲料添加剂，或者饲料原料、饲料添加剂原料，情节严重的，依照前款的规定定罪处罚。  
实施前两款行为，同时又构成生产、销售伪劣产品罪，生产、销售伪劣农药、兽药罪等其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 11** If, for the purpose of providing to others for the production or sale of food, a person violates state regulations by producing or selling non-food raw materials that the state prohibits for use in food production or sale, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If a person violates state regulations by producing or selling pesticides, veterinary drugs, feed, feed additives, or feed raw materials, feed additive raw materials that the state prohibits from production, sale, or use, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph.  
If the acts in the preceding two paragraphs also constitute other crimes such as the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard products, or the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard pesticides or veterinary drugs, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   违反国家规定，私设生猪屠宰厂（场），从事生猪屠宰、销售等经营活动，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-225)的规定以非法经营罪定罪处罚。  
实施前款行为，同时又构成生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪，生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪等其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 12** If a person violates state regulations by privately establishing a pig slaughterhouse (yard) and engaging in business activities such as pig slaughtering and sale, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal business operations in accordance with Article 225 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If the act in the preceding paragraph also constitutes other crimes such as the crime of producing or selling food that does not meet safety standards, or the crime of producing or selling toxic or hazardous food, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条   生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，有毒、有害食品，符合[刑法第一百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-143)、第一百四十四条规定的，以生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪或者生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪定罪处罚。同时构成其他犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，无证据证明足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病，不构成生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪，但是构成生产、销售伪劣产品罪等其他犯罪的，依照该其他犯罪定罪处罚。

**Article 13** The production or sale of food that does not meet food safety standards, or toxic or hazardous food, if meeting the provisions of Article 143 or Article 144 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of producing or selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing or selling toxic or hazardous food. If it also constitutes other crimes, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.  
If the production or sale of food that does not meet food safety standards lacks evidence sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease, and does not constitute the crime of producing or selling food that does not meet safety standards, but constitutes other crimes such as the crime of producing or selling counterfeit or substandard products, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with that other crime.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条   明知他人生产、销售不符合食品安全标准的食品，有毒、有害食品，具有下列情形之一的，以生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪或者生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪的共犯论处：  
（一）提供资金、贷款、账号、发票、证明、许可证件的；  
（二）提供生产、经营场所或者运输、贮存、保管、邮寄、网络销售渠道等便利条件的；  
（三）提供生产技术或者食品原料、食品添加剂、食品相关产品的；  
（四）提供广告等宣传的。

**Article 14** If a person knowingly provides the following to another person who produces or sells food that does not meet food safety standards or toxic or hazardous food, the person shall be punished as an accomplice to the crime of producing or selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing or selling toxic or hazardous food:  
(1) providing funds, loans, accounts, invoices, certificates, or permits;  
(2) providing production or business premises or convenient conditions such as transportation, storage, custody, mailing, online sales channels;  
(3) providing production technology or food raw materials, food additives, or food-related products;  
(4) providing advertising or other publicity.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条   广告主、广告经营者、广告发布者违反国家规定，利用广告对保健食品或者其他食品作虚假宣传，情节严重的，依照[刑法第二百二十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-222)的规定以虚假广告罪定罪处罚。

**Article 15** If an advertiser, advertising agent, or advertisement publisher violates state regulations by using advertisements to make false publicity about health food or other food, and the circumstances are serious, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of false advertising in accordance with Article 222 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条   负有食品安全监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员，滥用职权或者玩忽职守，导致发生重大食品安全事故或者造成其他严重后果，同时构成食品监管渎职罪和徇私舞弊不移交刑事案件罪、商检徇私舞弊罪、动植物检疫徇私舞弊罪、放纵制售伪劣商品犯罪行为罪等其他渎职犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
负有食品安全监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，不构成食品监管渎职罪，但构成前款规定的其他渎职犯罪的，依照该其他犯罪定罪处罚。  
负有食品安全监督管理职责的国家机关工作人员与他人共谋，利用其职务行为帮助他人实施危害食品安全犯罪行为，同时构成渎职犯罪和危害食品安全犯罪共犯的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 16** If a state organ staff member responsible for food safety supervision and administration abuses his or her power or neglects his or her duties, causing a major food safety accident or other serious consequences, and such act simultaneously constitutes the crime of dereliction of duty in food supervision and the crime of engaging in malpractices for personal gain and not transferring criminal cases, the crime of engaging in malpractices for personal gain in commodity inspection, the crime of engaging in malpractices for personal gain in animal and plant quarantine, the crime of indulging in the production and sale of counterfeit and substandard goods, or other crimes of dereliction of duty, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.  
If a state organ staff member responsible for food safety supervision and administration abuses his or her power or neglects his or her duties, which does not constitute the crime of dereliction of duty in food supervision but constitutes other crimes of dereliction of duty specified in the preceding paragraph, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with that other crime.  
If a state organ staff member responsible for food safety supervision and administration conspires with others and uses his or her official acts to help others commit crimes endangering food safety, and such act simultaneously constitutes a crime of dereliction of duty and an accomplice to a crime endangering food safety, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions that impose a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条   犯生产、销售不符合安全标准的食品罪，生产、销售有毒、有害食品罪，一般应当依法判处生产、销售金额二倍以上的罚金。

**Article 17** A person who commits the crime of producing or selling food that does not meet safety standards or the crime of producing or selling toxic or hazardous food shall generally be sentenced to a fine of not less than two times the amount of production or sale according to law.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条   对实施本解释规定之犯罪的犯罪分子，应当依照刑法规定的条件严格适用缓刑、免予刑事处罚。根据犯罪事实、情节和悔罪表现，对于符合刑法规定的缓刑适用条件的犯罪分子，可以适用缓刑，但是应当同时宣告禁止令，禁止其在缓刑考验期限内从事食品生产、销售及相关活动。

**Article 18** For criminals who commit the crimes specified in this Interpretation, probation and exemption from criminal punishment shall be strictly applied in accordance with the conditions prescribed in the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China. Based on the facts, circumstances, and expression of remorse, for a criminal who meets the conditions for probation prescribed in the Criminal Law, probation may be applied; however, a restraining order shall be imposed at the same time, prohibiting the criminal from engaging in food production, sale, and related activities during the probation period.

<a id="art-19"></a>第十九条   单位实施本解释规定的犯罪的，依照本解释规定的定罪量刑标准处罚。

**Article 19** If a unit commits a crime specified in this Interpretation, the unit shall be punished in accordance with the conviction and sentencing standards specified in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-20"></a>第二十条   下列物质应当认定为“有毒、有害的非食品原料”：  
（一）法律、法规禁止在食品生产经营活动中添加、使用的物质；  
（二）国务院有关部门公布的《食品中可能违法添加的非食用物质名单》、《保健食品中可能非法添加的物质名单》上的物质；  
（三）国务院有关部门公告禁止使用的农药、兽药以及其他有毒、有害物质；  
（四）其他危害人体健康的物质。

**Article 20** The following substances shall be determined as "toxic or hazardous non-food raw materials":  
(1) substances prohibited by laws or regulations from being added or used in food production or operation;  
(2) substances listed in the "List of Non-Food Substances That May Be Illegally Added to Food" or the "List of Substances That May Be Illegally Added to Health Food" published by the relevant departments of the State Council;  
(3) pesticides, veterinary drugs, and other toxic or hazardous substances prohibited from use by announcement of the relevant departments of the State Council;  
(4) other substances harmful to human health.

<a id="art-21"></a>第二十一条  “足以造成严重食物中毒事故或者其他严重食源性疾病”“有毒、有害非食品原料”难以确定的，司法机关可以根据检验报告并结合专家意见等相关材料进行认定。必要时，人民法院可以依法通知有关专家出庭作出说明。

**Article 21** If it is difficult to determine whether it is "sufficient to cause a serious food poisoning accident or other serious foodborne disease" or whether a substance is a "toxic or hazardous non-food raw material," the judicial organ may make a determination based on inspection reports and in conjunction with expert opinions and other relevant materials. When necessary, the People's Court may notify relevant experts to appear in court to provide explanations according to law.

<a id="art-22"></a>第二十二条   最高人民法院、最高人民检察院此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 22** If any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
