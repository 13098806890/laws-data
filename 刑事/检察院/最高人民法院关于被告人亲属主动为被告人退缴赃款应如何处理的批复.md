# 最高人民法院关于被告人亲属主动为被告人退缴赃款应如何处理的批复

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：复〔1987〕32号  
**公布日期**：1987-08-26

---

### 正文

一、被告人是成年人，其违法所得都由自己挥霍，无法追缴的，应责令被告人退赔，其家属没有代为退赔的义务。  
被告人在家庭共同财产中有其个人应有部分的，只能在其个人应有部分的范围内，责令被告人退赔。

**Article None** 1. Where the defendant is an adult and all of his or her illegal gains have been squandered by himself or herself and cannot be recovered, the defendant shall be ordered to make restitution, and the family members of the defendant shall have no obligation to make restitution on his or her behalf.  
Where the defendant has a personal share in the family common property, the defendant may only be ordered to make restitution within the scope of his or her personal share.

二、如果被告人的违法所得有一部分用于家庭日常生活，对这部分违法所得，被告人和家属均有退赔的义务。

**Article None** 2. Where part of the defendant's illegal gains has been used for the daily life of the family, both the defendant and the family members shall have the obligation to make restitution for that part of the illegal gains.

三、如果被告人对责令其本人退赔的违法所得已无实际上的退赔能力，但其亲属应被告人的请求，或者主动提出并征得被告人的同意，自愿代被告人退赔部分或者全部违法所得的，法院也可考虑具体情况，收下其亲属自愿代被告人退赔的款项，并视为被告人主动退赔的款项。

**Article None** 3. Where the defendant has no actual ability to make restitution for the illegal gains that he or she has been ordered to make restitution for, but the defendant's relatives, at the request of the defendant or on their own initiative and with the defendant's consent, voluntarily make restitution for part or all of the illegal gains on behalf of the defendant, the court may also, taking into account the specific circumstances, accept the funds voluntarily paid by the relatives on behalf of the defendant, and shall deem such funds as voluntarily made by the defendant.

四、属于以上三种情况，已作了退赔的，均可视为被告人退赃较好，可以酌情从宽处罚。

**Article None** 4. Where restitution has been made in any of the above three circumstances, it may be deemed that the defendant has made relatively good restitution of the illegal gains, and a lenient penalty may be imposed as deemed appropriate.

五、如果被告人的罪行应当判处死刑，并必须执行，属于以上第一、二两种情况的，法院可以接收退赔的款项；属于以上第三种情况的，其亲属自愿代为退赔的款项，法院不应接收。  
1987年8月26日

**Article None** 5. Where the defendant's crime is punishable by death and the death penalty must be enforced, if the case falls under the first or second circumstance above, the court may accept the funds paid as restitution; if the case falls under the third circumstance above, the court shall not accept the funds voluntarily paid by the relatives on behalf of the defendant.  
August 26, 1987
