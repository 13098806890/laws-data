# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理非法采矿、破坏性采矿刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2016〕25号  
**公布日期**：2016-09-26  
**生效日期**：2016-12-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理非法采矿、破坏性采矿刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2016年9月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1694次会议、2016年11月4日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第57次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年12月1日起施行。 2016年11月28日 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院 关于办理非法采矿、破坏性采矿刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2016〕25号 为依法惩处非法采矿、破坏性采矿犯罪活动，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  违反《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》《中华人民共和国水法》等法律、行政法规有关矿产资源开发、利用、保护和管理的规定的，应当认定为刑法第三百四十三条规定的“违反矿产资源法的规定”。 第二条  具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百四十三条第一款规定的“未取得采矿许可证”： （一）无许可证的； （二）许可证被注销、吊销、撤销的； （三）超越许可证规定的矿区范围或者开采范围的； （四）超出许可证规定的矿种的（共生、伴生矿种除外）； （五）其他未取得许可证的情形。 第三条  实施非法采矿行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百四十三条第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）开采的矿产品价值或者造成矿产资源破坏的价值在十万元至三十万元以上的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  违反《中华人民共和国矿产资源法》《中华人民共和国水法》等法律、行政法规有关矿产资源开发、利用、保护和管理的规定的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)规定的“违反矿产资源法的规定”。

**Article 1** Whoever violates the provisions of the Mineral Resources Law of the People's Republic of China, the Water Law of the People's Republic of China, or other laws or administrative regulations concerning the development, utilization, protection, and administration of mineral resources shall be deemed to have violated the provisions of the Mineral Resources Law as stipulated in Article 343 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款规定的“未取得采矿许可证”：  
（一）无许可证的；  
（二）许可证被注销、吊销、撤销的；  
（三）超越许可证规定的矿区范围或者开采范围的；  
（四）超出许可证规定的矿种的（共生、伴生矿种除外）；  
（五）其他未取得许可证的情形。

**Article 2** Any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “failure to obtain a mining license” as stipulated in the first paragraph of Article 343 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) having no license;  
(2) the license has been canceled, revoked, or withdrawn;  
(3) exceeding the mining area or extraction scope specified in the license;  
(4) mining mineral species beyond those specified in the license (excluding associated and companion mineral species);  
(5) other circumstances where no license has been obtained.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  实施非法采矿行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）开采的矿产品价值或者造成矿产资源破坏的价值在十万元至三十万元以上的；  
（二）在国家规划矿区、对国民经济具有重要价值的矿区采矿，开采国家规定实行保护性开采的特定矿种，或者在禁采区、禁采期内采矿，开采的矿产品价值或者造成矿产资源破坏的价值在五万元至十五万元以上的；  
（三）二年内曾因非法采矿受过两次以上行政处罚，又实施非法采矿行为的；  
（四）造成生态环境严重损害的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施非法采矿行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）数额达到前款第一项、第二项规定标准五倍以上的；  
（二）造成生态环境特别严重损害的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 3** The commission of illegal mining activities under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as stipulated in the first paragraph of Article 343 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the value of the mined mineral products or the value of the damage caused to mineral resources is 100,000 yuan or more but less than 300,000 yuan;  
(2) mining within a state-planned mining area or a mining area of significant value to the national economy, mining specific mineral species subject to protective extraction as prescribed by the state, or mining within a prohibited mining area or during a prohibited mining period, where the value of the mined mineral products or the value of the damage caused to mineral resources is 50,000 yuan or more but less than 150,000 yuan;  
(3) having been subjected to two or more administrative penalties for illegal mining within the previous two years, and again committing illegal mining;  
(4) causing serious damage to the ecological environment;  
(5) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
The commission of illegal mining activities under any of the following circumstances shall be deemed as “particularly serious circumstances” as stipulated in the first paragraph of Article 343 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) the amount reaches more than five times the standards specified in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph;  
(2) causing particularly serious damage to the ecological environment;  
(3) other circumstances of a particularly serious nature.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  在河道管理范围内采砂，具有下列情形之一，符合[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款和[本解释第二条](#art-2)、第三条规定的，以非法采矿罪定罪处罚：  
（一）依据相关规定应当办理河道采砂许可证，未取得河道采砂许可证的；  
（二）依据相关规定应当办理河道采砂许可证和采矿许可证，既未取得河道采砂许可证，又未取得采矿许可证的。  
实施前款规定行为，虽不具有[本解释第三条](#art-3)第一款规定的情形，但严重影响河势稳定，危害防洪安全的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款规定的“情节严重”。

**Article 4** Whoever extracts sand within the river management scope under any of the following circumstances, meeting the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 343 of the Criminal Law and Articles 2 and 3 of this Interpretation, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal mining:  
(1) being required to obtain a river sand extraction license pursuant to relevant provisions but failing to do so;  
(2) being required to obtain both a river sand extraction license and a mining license pursuant to relevant provisions, but failing to obtain either.  
Where the conduct specified in the preceding paragraph, though not falling under the circumstances set forth in the first paragraph of Article 3 of this Interpretation, seriously affects the stability of the river regime and endangers flood control safety, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as stipulated in the first paragraph of Article 343 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  未取得海砂开采海域使用权证，且未取得采矿许可证，采挖海砂，符合[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款和[本解释第二条](#art-2)、第三条规定的，以非法采矿罪定罪处罚。  
实施前款规定行为，虽不具有[本解释第三条](#art-3)第一款规定的情形，但造成海岸线严重破坏的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款规定的“情节严重”。

**Article 5** Whoever, without obtaining a right to use sea areas for marine sand extraction and without obtaining a mining license, excavates marine sand, meeting the provisions of the first paragraph of Article 343 of the Criminal Law and Articles 2 and 3 of this Interpretation, shall be convicted and punished for the crime of illegal mining.  
Where the conduct specified in the preceding paragraph, though not falling under the circumstances set forth in the first paragraph of Article 3 of this Interpretation, causes serious damage to the coastline, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as stipulated in the first paragraph of Article 343 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  造成矿产资源破坏的价值在五十万元至一百万元以上，或者造成国家规划矿区、对国民经济具有重要价值的矿区和国家规定实行保护性开采的特定矿种资源破坏的价值在二十五万元至五十万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第二款规定的“造成矿产资源严重破坏”。

**Article 6** Where the value of damage caused to mineral resources is 500,000 yuan or more but less than 1,000,000 yuan, or the value of damage caused to resources in a state-planned mining area, a mining area of significant value to the national economy, or specific mineral species subject to protective extraction as prescribed by the state is 250,000 yuan or more but less than 500,000 yuan, it shall be deemed as “causing serious damage to mineral resources” as stipulated in the second paragraph of Article 343 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  明知是犯罪所得的矿产品及其产生的收益，而予以窝藏、转移、收购、代为销售或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒的，依照[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)的规定，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪定罪处罚。  
实施前款规定的犯罪行为，事前通谋的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Whoever knowingly harbors, transfers, purchases, sells on behalf, or otherwise disguises or conceals mineral products derived from crime and the proceeds generated therefrom shall, in accordance with Article 312 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of disguising or concealing criminal proceeds and the proceeds generated from criminal proceeds.  
If a person colludes in advance before committing the criminal act specified in the preceding paragraph, such person shall be dealt with as a joint offender.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  多次非法采矿、破坏性采矿构成犯罪，依法应当追诉的，或者二年内多次非法采矿、破坏性采矿未经处理的，价值数额累计计算。

**Article 8** Where illegal mining or destructive mining is committed multiple times and constitutes a crime for which prosecution shall be initiated according to law, or where illegal mining or destructive mining is committed multiple times within two years without being dealt with, the value amounts shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  单位犯[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)规定之罪的，依照本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。

**Article 9** Where a unit commits a crime specified in Article 343 of the Criminal Law, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the standards for conviction and sentencing of the corresponding natural person crime as provided in this Interpretation, and a fine shall be imposed on the unit.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  实施非法采矿犯罪，不属于“情节特别严重”，或者实施破坏性采矿犯罪，行为人系初犯，全部退赃退赔，积极修复环境，并确有悔改表现的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。

**Article 10** Where a person commits the crime of illegal mining not falling under “particularly serious circumstances,” or commits the crime of destructive mining, is a first-time offender, has fully returned the stolen property or made restitution, actively remediates the environment, and demonstrates genuine repentance, such person may be deemed to have minor criminal circumstances, and the decision not to prosecute or to grant exemption from criminal punishment may be made.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  对受雇佣为非法采矿、破坏性采矿犯罪提供劳务的人员，除参与利润分成或者领取高额固定工资的以外，一般不以犯罪论处，但曾因非法采矿、破坏性采矿受过处罚的除外。

**Article 11** Persons employed to provide labor services for the crimes of illegal mining or destructive mining shall generally not be deemed as committing a crime, except where they participate in profit sharing or receive a high fixed wage, unless they have previously been punished for illegal mining or destructive mining.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  对非法采矿、破坏性采矿犯罪的违法所得及其收益，应当依法追缴或者责令退赔。  
对用于非法采矿、破坏性采矿犯罪的专门工具和供犯罪所用的本人财物，应当依法没收。

**Article 12** The illegal gains and the proceeds generated therefrom from the crimes of illegal mining or destructive mining shall be recovered or ordered to be returned or compensated according to law.  
The special tools used in the commission of the crimes of illegal mining or destructive mining and the personal property used for the crime shall be confiscated according to law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条  非法开采的矿产品价值，根据销赃数额认定；无销赃数额，销赃数额难以查证，或者根据销赃数额认定明显不合理的，根据矿产品价格和数量认定。  
矿产品价值难以确定的，依据下列机构出具的报告，结合其他证据作出认定：  
（一）价格认证机构出具的报告；  
（二）省级以上人民政府国土资源、水行政、海洋等主管部门出具的报告；  
（三）国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构出具的报告。

**Article 13** The value of illegally mined mineral products shall be determined on the basis of the amount from the sale of the stolen goods; where there is no such amount, the amount is difficult to verify, or where determination based on such amount is manifestly unreasonable, the value shall be determined on the basis of the price and quantity of the mineral products.  
Where the value of mineral products is difficult to determine, it shall be determined on the basis of reports issued by the following institutions, in conjunction with other evidence:  
(1) reports issued by price certification institutions;  
(2) reports issued by the competent departments of land and resources, water administration, marine administration, etc., of the people's governments at or above the provincial level;  
(3) reports issued by the river basin management agencies established by the competent water resources department of the State Council for the major rivers and lakes designated by the state.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条  对案件所涉的有关专门性问题难以确定的，依据下列机构出具的鉴定意见或者报告，结合其他证据作出认定：  
（一）司法鉴定机构就生态环境损害出具的鉴定意见；  
（二）省级以上人民政府国土资源主管部门就造成矿产资源破坏的价值、是否属于破坏性开采方法出具的报告；  
（三）省级以上人民政府水行政主管部门或者国务院水行政主管部门在国家确定的重要江河、湖泊设立的流域管理机构就是否危害防洪安全出具的报告；  
（四）省级以上人民政府海洋主管部门就是否造成海岸线严重破坏出具的报告。

**Article 14** Where the relevant specialized issues involved in a case are difficult to determine, the determination shall be made on the basis of expert evaluation opinions or reports issued by the following institutions, in conjunction with other evidence:  
(1) expert evaluation opinions issued by judicial expert evaluation institutions regarding ecological and environmental damage;  
(2) reports issued by the competent land and resources departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level regarding the value of damage caused to mineral resources and whether the mining method constitutes destructive mining;  
(3) reports issued by the competent water administration departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level or the river basin management agencies established by the competent water resources department of the State Council for the major rivers and lakes designated by the state regarding whether flood control safety is endangered;  
(4) reports issued by the competent marine departments of the people's governments at or above the provincial level regarding whether serious damage to the coastline has been caused.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条  各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院，可以根据本地区实际情况，在[本解释第三条](#art-3)、第六条规定的数额幅度内，确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院备案。

**Article 15** The Higher People's Courts and People's Procuratorates of the provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the actual circumstances of their respective regions, determine the specific amount standards to be implemented within their regions within the range of amounts provided in Articles 3 and 6 of this Interpretation, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate for recordation.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条  本解释自2016年12月1日起施行。本解释施行后，最高人民法院《关于审理非法采矿、破坏性采矿刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2003〕9号）同时废止。

**Article 16** This Interpretation shall come into force on December 1, 2016. After this Interpretation comes into force, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Illegal Mining and Destructive Mining (Fa Shi [2003] No. 9) shall be abolished simultaneously.
