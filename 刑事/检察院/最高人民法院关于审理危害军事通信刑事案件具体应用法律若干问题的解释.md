# 最高人民法院关于审理危害军事通信刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕13号  
**公布日期**：2007-06-18  
**生效日期**：2007-06-29

> 《最高人民法院关于审理危害军事通信刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2007年6月18日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1430次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年6月29日起施行。 2007年6月26日 最高人民法院 关于审理危害军事通信刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2007〕13号 为依法惩治危害军事通信的犯罪活动，维护国防利益和军事通信安全，根据刑法有关规定，现就审理这类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  故意实施损毁军事通信线路、设备，破坏军事通信计算机信息系统，干扰、侵占军事通信电磁频谱等行为的，依照刑法第三百六十九条第一款的规定，以破坏军事通信罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制；破坏重要军事通信的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑。 第二条  实施破坏军事通信行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第三百六十九条第一款规定的“情节特别严重”，以破坏军事通信罪定罪，处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑或者死刑： （一）造成重要军事通信中断或者严重障碍，严重影响部队完成作战任务或者致使部队在作战中遭受损失的； （二）造成部队执行抢险救灾、军事演习或者处置突发性事件等任务的通信中断或者严重障碍，并因此贻误部队行动，致使死亡3人以上、重伤10人以上或者财产损失100万元以上的； （三）破坏重要军事通信三次以上的； （四）其他情节特别严重的情形。 第三条  过失损坏军事通信，造成重要军事通信中断或者严重障碍的，属于刑法第三百六十九条第二款规定的“造成严重后果”，以过失损坏军事通信罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役。 第四条  过失损坏军事通信，具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第三百六十九条第二款规定的“造成特别严重后果”，以过失损坏军事通信罪定罪，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  故意实施损毁军事通信线路、设备，破坏军事通信计算机信息系统，干扰、侵占军事通信电磁频谱等行为的，依照[刑法第三百六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-369)第一款的规定，以破坏军事通信罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制；破坏重要军事通信的，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑。

**Article 1** Whoever intentionally commits any act that damages military communication lines or equipment, destroys military communication computer information systems, interferes with or encroaches upon the electromagnetic spectrum of military communications, shall, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 369 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of destroying military communications, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention or public surveillance; if the destruction involves important military communications, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  实施破坏军事通信行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第三百六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-369)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”，以破坏军事通信罪定罪，处十年以上有期徒刑、无期徒刑或者死刑：  
（一）造成重要军事通信中断或者严重障碍，严重影响部队完成作战任务或者致使部队在作战中遭受损失的；  
（二）造成部队执行抢险救灾、军事演习或者处置突发性事件等任务的通信中断或者严重障碍，并因此贻误部队行动，致使死亡3人以上、重伤10人以上或者财产损失100万元以上的；  
（三）破坏重要军事通信三次以上的；  
（四）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 2** Where any person commits an act of destroying military communications, and the case falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute the “especially serious circumstances” as stipulated in paragraph 1 of Article 369 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be convicted of the crime of destroying military communications and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years, life imprisonment or death penalty:  
(1) causing interruption or serious obstruction of important military communications, which seriously affects the troops’ completion of combat missions or causes losses to the troops in combat;  
(2) causing interruption or serious obstruction of communications for tasks such as emergency rescue and disaster relief, military exercises, or handling of sudden incidents, thereby delaying the troops’ operations and resulting in death of not less than three persons, serious injury of not less than ten persons, or property losses of not less than RMB 1 million;  
(3) destroying important military communications on not less than three occasions;  
(4) other especially serious circumstances.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  过失损坏军事通信，造成重要军事通信中断或者严重障碍的，属于[刑法第三百六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-369)第二款规定的“造成严重后果”，以过失损坏军事通信罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役。

**Article 3** Where any person negligently damages military communications, causing interruption or serious obstruction of important military communications, the case shall fall under the “causing serious consequences” as stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 369 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be convicted of the crime of negligently damaging military communications and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  过失损坏军事通信，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第三百六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-369)第二款规定的“造成特别严重后果”，以过失损坏军事通信罪定罪，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑：  
（一）造成重要军事通信中断或者严重障碍，严重影响部队完成作战任务或者致使部队在作战中遭受损失的；  
（二）造成部队执行抢险救灾、军事演习或者处置突发性事件等任务的通信中断或者严重障碍，并因此贻误部队行动，致使死亡3人以上、重伤10人以上或者财产损失100万元以上的；  
（三）其他后果特别严重的情形。

**Article 4** Where any person negligently damages military communications, and the case falls under any of the following circumstances, it shall constitute the “causing especially serious consequences” as stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 369 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be convicted of the crime of negligently damaging military communications and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years:  
(1) causing interruption or serious obstruction of important military communications, which seriously affects the troops’ completion of combat missions or causes losses to the troops in combat;  
(2) causing interruption or serious obstruction of communications for tasks such as emergency rescue and disaster relief, military exercises, or handling of sudden incidents, thereby delaying the troops’ operations and resulting in death of not less than three persons, serious injury of not less than ten persons, or property losses of not less than RMB 1 million;  
(3) other especially serious consequences.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  建设、施工单位直接负责的主管人员、施工管理人员，明知是军事通信线路、设备而指使、强令、纵容他人予以损毁的，或者不听管护人员劝阻，指使、强令、纵容他人违章作业，造成军事通信线路、设备损毁的，以破坏军事通信罪定罪处罚。  
建设、施工单位直接负责的主管人员、施工管理人员，忽视军事通信线路、设备保护标志，指使、纵容他人违章作业，致使军事通信线路、设备损毁，构成犯罪的，以过失损坏军事通信罪定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Where the person directly in charge or the construction manager of a construction or project entity, knowing that a military communication line or equipment exists, instructs, compels, or connives at others to damage it, or, disregarding the dissuasion of the maintenance personnel, instructs, compels, or connives at others to operate in violation of rules, thereby causing damage to the military communication line or equipment, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of destroying military communications.  
Where the person directly in charge or the construction manager of a construction or project entity, ignoring the protection signs of military communication lines or equipment, instructs or connives at others to operate in violation of rules, thereby causing damage to the military communication line or equipment, which constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of negligently damaging military communications.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  破坏、过失损坏军事通信，并造成公用电信设施损毁，危害公共安全，同时构成[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)和第三百六十九条规定的犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
盗窃军事通信线路、设备，不构成盗窃罪，但破坏军事通信的，依照[刑法第三百六十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-369)第一款的规定定罪处罚；同时构成[刑法第一百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-124)、第二百六十四条和第三百六十九条第一款规定的犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
违反国家规定，侵入国防建设、尖端科学技术领域的军事通信计算机信息系统，尚未对军事通信造成破坏的，依照[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)的规定定罪处罚；对军事通信造成破坏，同时构成[刑法第二百八十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-285)、第二百八十六条、第三百六十九条第一款规定的犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。  
违反国家规定，擅自设置、使用无线电台、站，或者擅自占用频率，经责令停止使用后拒不停止使用，干扰无线电通讯正常进行，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第二百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-288)的规定定罪处罚；造成军事通信中断或者严重障碍，同时构成[刑法第二百八十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-288)、第三百六十九条第一款规定的犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where any person destroys or negligently damages military communications and also causes damage to public telecommunications facilities, endangering public security, and the act concurrently constitutes the crimes stipulated in Article 124 and Article 369 of the Criminal Law, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.  
Where any person steals military communication lines or equipment, which does not constitute the crime of theft, but destroys military communications, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 369 of the Criminal Law; if the act concurrently constitutes the crimes stipulated in Article 124, Article 264, and paragraph 1 of Article 369 of the Criminal Law, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.  
Where any person, in violation of state regulations, intrudes into a military communication computer information system in the fields of national defense construction or advanced science and technology, but has not yet caused damage to military communications, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 285 of the Criminal Law; if the act causes damage to military communications and concurrently constitutes the crimes stipulated in Article 285, Article 286, and paragraph 1 of Article 369 of the Criminal Law, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.  
Where any person, in violation of state regulations, establishes or uses a radio station without authorization, or occupies a frequency without authorization, and after being ordered to cease use, refuses to do so, thereby interfering with the normal progress of radio communications, which constitutes a crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 288 of the Criminal Law; if the act causes interruption or serious obstruction of military communications and concurrently constitutes the crimes stipulated in Article 288 and paragraph 1 of Article 369 of the Criminal Law, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  本解释所称“重要军事通信”，是指军事首脑机关及重要指挥中心的通信，部队作战中的通信，等级战备通信，飞行航行训练、抢险救灾、军事演习或者处置突发性事件中的通信，以及执行试飞试航、武器装备科研试验或者远洋航行等重要军事任务中的通信。  
本解释所称军事通信的具体范围、通信中断和严重障碍的标准，参照中国人民解放军通信主管部门的有关规定确定。

**Article 7** The term “important military communications” as used in this Interpretation refers to communications of military leading organs and important command centers, communications during troops’ combat, communications at graded combat readiness, communications during flight or navigation training, emergency rescue and disaster relief, military exercises, or handling of sudden incidents, as well as communications during the execution of important military missions such as test flights, test voyages, scientific research and testing of weapons and equipment, or ocean voyages.  
The specific scope of military communications and the criteria for communication interruption and serious obstruction as used in this Interpretation shall be determined by reference to the relevant provisions of the competent department of communication of the People's Liberation Army of China.
