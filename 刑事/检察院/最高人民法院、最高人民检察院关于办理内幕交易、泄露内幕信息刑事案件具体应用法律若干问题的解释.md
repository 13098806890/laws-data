# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理内幕交易、泄露内幕信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2012〕6号  
**公布日期**：2011-10-31  
**生效日期**：2012-06-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理内幕交易、泄露内幕信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2011年10月31日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1529次会议、2012年2月27日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第72次会议通过，现予公布，自2012年6月1日起施行。 2012年3月29日 最高人民法院 最高人民检察院 关于办理内幕交易、泄露内幕信息刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2012〕6号 为维护证券、期货市场管理秩序，依法惩治证券、期货犯罪，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理内幕交易、泄露内幕信息刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下: 第一条  下列人员应当认定为刑法第一百八十条第一款规定的“证券、期货交易内幕信息的知情人员”: （一）证券法第七十四条规定的人员； （二）期货交易管理条例第八十五条第十二项规定的人员。 第二条  具有下列行为的人员应当认定为刑法第一百八十条第一款规定的“非法获取证券、期货交易内幕信息的人员”: （一）利用窃取、骗取、套取、窃听、利诱、刺探或者私下交易等手段获取内幕信息的； （二）内幕信息知情人员的近亲属或者其他与内幕信息知情人员关系密切的人员，在内幕信息敏感期内，从事或者明示、暗示他人从事，或者泄露内幕信息导致他人从事与该内幕信息有关的证券、期货交易，相关交易行为明显异常，且无正当理由或者正当信息来源的； （三）在内幕信息敏感期内，与内幕信息知情人员联络、接触，从事或者明示、暗示他人从事，或者泄露内幕信息导致他人从事与该内幕信息有关的证券、期货交易，相关交易行为明显异常，且无正当理由或者正当信息来源的。 第三条  本解释第二条第二项、第三项规定的“相关交易行为明显异常”，要综合以下情形，从时间吻合程度、交易背离程度和利益关联程度等方面予以认定:

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  下列人员应当认定为[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第一款规定的“证券、期货交易内幕信息的知情人员”:  
（一）[证券法第七十四条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-74)规定的人员；  
（二）[期货交易管理条例第八十五条](../../行政与公法/行政法规/金融证券/期货交易管理条例.md#art-85)第十二项规定的人员。

**Article 1** : The following persons shall be deemed the "personnel with knowledge of inside information on securities and futures trading" as stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Persons as stipulated in Article 74 of the Securities Law;  
(2) Persons as stipulated in Item 12 of Article 85 of the Regulations on the Administration of Futures Trading.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  具有下列行为的人员应当认定为[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第一款规定的“非法获取证券、期货交易内幕信息的人员”:  
（一）利用窃取、骗取、套取、窃听、利诱、刺探或者私下交易等手段获取内幕信息的；  
（二）内幕信息知情人员的近亲属或者其他与内幕信息知情人员关系密切的人员，在内幕信息敏感期内，从事或者明示、暗示他人从事，或者泄露内幕信息导致他人从事与该内幕信息有关的证券、期货交易，相关交易行为明显异常，且无正当理由或者正当信息来源的；  
（三）在内幕信息敏感期内，与内幕信息知情人员联络、接触，从事或者明示、暗示他人从事，或者泄露内幕信息导致他人从事与该内幕信息有关的证券、期货交易，相关交易行为明显异常，且无正当理由或者正当信息来源的。

**Article 2** : Persons with the following conduct shall be deemed the "persons who illegally obtain inside information on securities and futures trading" as stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Those who obtain inside information by means of theft, fraud, extraction, eavesdropping, inducement, espionage, or private transactions;  
(2) Where a close relative of a person with knowledge of inside information or another person having a close relationship with such a person, during the sensitive period of inside information, engages in, or explicitly or implicitly instructs another to engage in, or discloses inside information causing another to engage in, securities or futures trading related to such inside information, and the relevant trading conduct is manifestly abnormal and there is no justifiable reason or legitimate source of information;  
(3) Where, during the sensitive period of inside information, a person contacts or communicates with a person with knowledge of inside information, engages in, or explicitly or implicitly instructs another to engage in, or discloses inside information causing another to engage in, securities or futures trading related to such inside information, and the relevant trading conduct is manifestly abnormal and there is no justifiable reason or legitimate source of information.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  [本解释第二条](#art-2)第二项、第三项规定的“相关交易行为明显异常”，要综合以下情形，从时间吻合程度、交易背离程度和利益关联程度等方面予以认定:  
（一）开户、销户、激活资金账户或者指定交易（托管）、撤销指定交易（转托管）的时间与该内幕信息形成、变化、公开时间基本一致的；  
（二）资金变化与该内幕信息形成、变化、公开时间基本一致的；  
（三）买入或者卖出与内幕信息有关的证券、期货合约时间与内幕信息的形成、变化和公开时间基本一致的；  
（四）买入或者卖出与内幕信息有关的证券、期货合约时间与获悉内幕信息的时间基本一致的；  
（五）买入或者卖出证券、期货合约行为明显与平时交易习惯不同的；  
（六）买入或者卖出证券、期货合约行为，或者集中持有证券、期货合约行为与该证券、期货公开信息反映的基本面明显背离的；  
（七）账户交易资金进出与该内幕信息知情人员或者非法获取人员有关联或者利害关系的；  
（八）其他交易行为明显异常情形。

**Article 3** : The "manifestly abnormal relevant trading conduct" as stipulated in Items 2 and 3 of Article 2 of this Interpretation shall be determined by comprehensively considering the following circumstances in terms of the degree of temporal consistency, the degree of trading divergence, and the degree of interest linkage:  
(1) The time of opening an account, cancelling an account, activating a capital account, or designating a transaction (custody) or cancelling a designated transaction (transfer of custody) is essentially consistent with the time of the formation, change, or disclosure of the inside information;  
(2) Capital changes are essentially consistent with the time of the formation, change, or disclosure of the inside information;  
(3) The time of buying or selling securities or futures contracts related to the inside information is essentially consistent with the time of the formation, change, or disclosure of the inside information;  
(4) The time of buying or selling securities or futures contracts related to the inside information is essentially consistent with the time of learning of the inside information;  
(5) The conduct of buying or selling securities or futures contracts is manifestly different from the usual trading habits;  
(6) The conduct of buying or selling securities or futures contracts, or the conduct of holding concentrated securities or futures contracts, is manifestly inconsistent with the fundamentals reflected by the public information on such securities or futures;  
(7) The inflow or outflow of trading funds in the account is associated or connected with a person with knowledge of inside information or a person who illegally obtained such information;  
(8) Other circumstances where the trading conduct is manifestly abnormal.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  具有下列情形之一的，不属于[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第一款规定的从事与内幕信息有关的证券、期货交易:  
（一）持有或者通过协议、其他安排与他人共同持有上市公司百分之五以上股份的自然人、法人或者其他组织收购该上市公司股份的；  
（二）按照事先订立的书面合同、指令、计划从事相关证券、期货交易的；  
（三）依据已被他人披露的信息而交易的；  
（四）交易具有其他正当理由或者正当信息来源的。

**Article 4** : The following circumstances shall not constitute the engagement in securities or futures trading related to inside information as stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) A natural person, legal person, or other organization that holds or jointly holds through an agreement or other arrangement five percent or more of the shares of a listed company acquires shares of such listed company;  
(2) Trading in relevant securities or futures is conducted in accordance with a written contract, instruction, or plan established in advance;  
(3) Trading is conducted on the basis of information already disclosed by another person;  
(4) The trading has other justifiable reasons or legitimate sources of information.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  本解释所称“内幕信息敏感期”是指内幕信息自形成至公开的期间。  
[证券法第六十七条](../../民事与商事/证券与期货/中华人民共和国证券法.md#art-67)第二款所列“重大事件”的发生时间，第七十五条规定的“计划”、“方案”以及[期货交易管理条例第八十五条](../../行政与公法/行政法规/金融证券/期货交易管理条例.md#art-85)第十一项规定的“政策”、“决定”等的形成时间，应当认定为内幕信息的形成之时。  
影响内幕信息形成的动议、筹划、决策或者执行人员，其动议、筹划、决策或者执行初始时间，应当认定为内幕信息的形成之时。  
内幕信息的公开，是指内幕信息在国务院证券、期货监督管理机构指定的报刊、网站等媒体披露。

**Article 5** : The "sensitive period of inside information" as referred to in this Interpretation means the period from the formation of inside information to its disclosure.  
The time of occurrence of a "major event" listed in Paragraph 2 of Article 67 of the Securities Law, the time of formation of a "plan" or "scheme" as stipulated in Article 75 thereof, and the time of formation of a "policy" or "decision" as stipulated in Item 11 of Article 85 of the Regulations on the Administration of Futures Trading shall be deemed the time of formation of inside information.  
For persons whose motions, plans, decisions, or executions affect the formation of inside information, the initial time of such motion, plan, decision, or execution shall be deemed the time of formation of inside information.  
The disclosure of inside information means that the inside information is disclosed in newspapers, websites, or other media designated by the securities and futures regulatory authority of the State Council.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  在内幕信息敏感期内从事或者明示、暗示他人从事或者泄露内幕信息导致他人从事与该内幕信息有关的证券、期货交易，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第一款规定的“情节严重”:  
（一）证券交易成交额在五十万元以上的；  
（二）期货交易占用保证金数额在三十万元以上的；  
（三）获利或者避免损失数额在十五万元以上的；  
（四）三次以上的；  
（五）具有其他严重情节的。

**Article 6** : Where, during the sensitive period of inside information, a person engages in, or explicitly or implicitly instructs another to engage in, or discloses inside information causing another to engage in, securities or futures trading related to such inside information, and the following circumstances are present, it shall be deemed "serious circumstances" as stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The turnover of securities transactions is RMB 500,000 or more;  
(2) The amount of margin occupied in futures trading is RMB 300,000 or more;  
(3) The amount of profit gained or loss avoided is RMB 150,000 or more;  
(4) The conduct occurs on three or more occasions;  
(5) Other serious circumstances exist.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  在内幕信息敏感期内从事或者明示、暗示他人从事或者泄露内幕信息导致他人从事与该内幕信息有关的证券、期货交易，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”:  
（一）证券交易成交额在二百五十万元以上的；  
（二）期货交易占用保证金数额在一百五十万元以上的；  
（三）获利或者避免损失数额在七十五万元以上的；  
（四）具有其他特别严重情节的。

**Article 7** : Where, during the sensitive period of inside information, a person engages in, or explicitly or implicitly instructs another to engage in, or discloses inside information causing another to engage in, securities or futures trading related to such inside information, and the following circumstances are present, it shall be deemed "especially serious circumstances" as stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) The turnover of securities transactions is RMB 2.5 million or more;  
(2) The amount of margin occupied in futures trading is RMB 1.5 million or more;  
(3) The amount of profit gained or loss avoided is RMB 750,000 or more;  
(4) Other especially serious circumstances exist.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  二次以上实施内幕交易或者泄露内幕信息行为，未经行政处理或者刑事处理的，应当对相关交易数额依法累计计算。

**Article 8** : Where insider trading or disclosure of inside information is committed on two or more occasions and has not been subject to administrative processing or criminal punishment, the relevant transaction amounts shall be cumulatively calculated according to law.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  同一案件中，成交额、占用保证金额、获利或者避免损失额分别构成情节严重、情节特别严重的，按照处罚较重的数额定罪处罚。  
构成共同犯罪的，按照共同犯罪行为人的成交总额、占用保证金总额、获利或者避免损失总额定罪处罚，但判处各被告人罚金的总额应掌握在获利或者避免损失总额的一倍以上五倍以下。

**Article 9** : Where, in the same case, the turnover, the amount of margin occupied, and the amount of profit gained or loss avoided respectively constitute serious circumstances and especially serious circumstances, a conviction and punishment shall be imposed according to the amount subject to the heavier penalty.  
Where a joint offense is constituted, a conviction and punishment shall be imposed according to the total turnover, the total amount of margin occupied, and the total amount of profit gained or loss avoided of all joint offenders; however, the total amount of fines imposed on each defendant shall be controlled within the range of not less than one time and not more than five times the total amount of profit gained or loss avoided.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  [刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第一款规定的“违法所得”，是指通过内幕交易行为所获利益或者避免的损失。  
内幕信息的泄露人员或者内幕交易的明示、暗示人员未实际从事内幕交易的，其罚金数额按照因泄露而获悉内幕信息人员或者被明示、暗示人员从事内幕交易的违法所得计算。

**Article 10** : The "illegal income" as stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law means the profit gained or loss avoided through insider trading.  
Where the person who discloses inside information or the person who explicitly or implicitly instructs others in insider trading does not actually engage in insider trading, the amount of the fine shall be calculated based on the illegal income of the person who learns of the inside information as a result of the disclosure or of the person who is explicitly or implicitly instructed to engage in insider trading.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  单位实施[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第一款规定的行为，具有[本解释第六条](#art-6)规定情形之一的，按照[刑法第一百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-180)第二款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 11** : Where a unit commits the conduct stipulated in Paragraph 1 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law and has any of the circumstances stipulated in Article 6 of this Interpretation, a conviction and punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 2 of Article 180 of the Criminal Law.
