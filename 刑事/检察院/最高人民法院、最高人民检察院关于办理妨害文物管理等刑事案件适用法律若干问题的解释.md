# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理妨害文物管理等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2015〕23号  
**公布日期**：2015-10-12  
**生效日期**：2016-01-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理妨害文物管理等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2015年10月12日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1663次会议、2015年11月18日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第43次会议通过，现予公布，自2016年1月1日起施行。 2015年12月30日 最高人民法院   最高人民检察院 关于办理妨害文物管理等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2015〕23号 为依法惩治文物犯罪，保护文物，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》《中华人民共和国文物保护法》的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条    刑法第一百五十一条规定的“国家禁止出口的文物”，依照《中华人民共和国文物保护法》规定的“国家禁止出境的文物”的范围认定。 走私国家禁止出口的二级文物的，应当依照刑法第一百五十一条第二款的规定，以走私文物罪处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；走私国家禁止出口的一级文物的，应当认定为刑法第一百五十一条第二款规定的“情节特别严重”；走私国家禁止出口的三级文物的，应当认定为刑法第一百五十一条第二款规定的“情节较轻”。 走私国家禁止出口的文物，无法确定文物等级，或者按照文物等级定罪量刑明显过轻或者过重的，可以按照走私的文物价值定罪量刑。走私的文物价值在二十万元以上不满一百万元的，应当依照刑法第一百五十一条第二款的规定，以走私文物罪处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；文物价值在一百万元以上的，应当认定为刑法第一百五十一条第二款规定的“情节特别严重”；文物价值在五万元以上不满二十万元的，应当认定为刑法第一百五十一条第二款规定的“情节较轻”。 第二条    盗窃一般文物、三级文物、二级以上文物的，应当分别认定为刑法第二百六十四条规定的“数额较大”“数额巨大”“数额特别巨大”。 盗窃文物，无法确定文物等级，或者按照文物等级定罪量刑明显过轻或者过重的，按照盗窃的文物价值定罪量刑。 第三条    全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的本体，应当认定为刑法第三百二十四条第一款规定的“被确定为全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的文物”。 故意损毁国家保护的珍贵文物或者被确定为全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的文物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第三百二十四条第一款规定的“情节严重”： （一）造成五件以上三级文物损毁的； （二）造成二级以上文物损毁的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条    [刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)规定的“国家禁止出口的文物”，依照《中华人民共和国文物保护法》规定的“国家禁止出境的文物”的范围认定。  
走私国家禁止出口的二级文物的，应当依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款的规定，以走私文物罪处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；走私国家禁止出口的一级文物的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“情节特别严重”；走私国家禁止出口的三级文物的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“情节较轻”。  
走私国家禁止出口的文物，无法确定文物等级，或者按照文物等级定罪量刑明显过轻或者过重的，可以按照走私的文物价值定罪量刑。走私的文物价值在二十万元以上不满一百万元的，应当依照[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款的规定，以走私文物罪处五年以上十年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金；文物价值在一百万元以上的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“情节特别严重”；文物价值在五万元以上不满二十万元的，应当认定为[刑法第一百五十一条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-151)第二款规定的“情节较轻”。

**Article 1** "Cultural relics prohibited from export by the State" as provided for in Article 151 of the Criminal Law shall be determined in accordance with the scope of "cultural relics prohibited from leaving the country by the State" as provided for in the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Cultural Relics.  
Whoever smuggles Grade Two cultural relics prohibited from export by the State shall, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years and shall also be fined for the crime of smuggling cultural relics; whoever smuggles Grade One cultural relics prohibited from export by the State shall be deemed to have committed "especially serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law; whoever smuggles Grade Three cultural relics prohibited from export by the State shall be deemed to have committed "relatively minor circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law.  
Where it is impossible to determine the grade of cultural relics smuggled that are prohibited from export by the State, or where conviction and sentencing according to the grade of cultural relics is obviously too lenient or too severe, conviction and sentencing may be based on the value of the smuggled cultural relics. Where the value of the smuggled cultural relics is not less than 200,000 yuan but less than 1,000,000 yuan, the offender shall, in accordance with paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than five years but not more than ten years and shall also be fined for the crime of smuggling cultural relics; where the value of the cultural relics is not less than 1,000,000 yuan, the offender shall be deemed to have committed "especially serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law; where the value of the cultural relics is not less than 50,000 yuan but less than 200,000 yuan, the offender shall be deemed to have committed "relatively minor circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 151 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条    盗窃一般文物、三级文物、二级以上文物的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)规定的“数额较大”“数额巨大”“数额特别巨大”。  
盗窃文物，无法确定文物等级，或者按照文物等级定罪量刑明显过轻或者过重的，按照盗窃的文物价值定罪量刑。

**Article 2** Whoever steals ordinary cultural relics, Grade Three cultural relics, or Grade Two or above cultural relics shall be deemed to have committed "a relatively large amount", "a huge amount" or "an especially huge amount" as provided for in Article 264 of the Criminal Law, respectively.  
Where it is impossible to determine the grade of stolen cultural relics, or where conviction and sentencing according to the grade of cultural relics is obviously too lenient or too severe, conviction and sentencing shall be based on the value of the stolen cultural relics.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条    全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的本体，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-324)第一款规定的“被确定为全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的文物”。  
故意损毁国家保护的珍贵文物或者被确定为全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的文物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-324)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）造成五件以上三级文物损毁的；  
（二）造成二级以上文物损毁的；  
（三）致使全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的本体严重损毁或者灭失的；  
（四）多次损毁或者损毁多处全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的本体的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，拒不执行国家行政主管部门作出的停止侵害文物的行政决定或者命令的，酌情从重处罚。

**Article 3** The main structures of major historic sites protected at the national level and historic sites protected at the provincial level shall be deemed to be "cultural relics designated as major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 324 of the Criminal Law.  
Whoever intentionally damages precious cultural relics protected by the State or cultural relics designated as major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level, under any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed to have committed "serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 324 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing damage to five or more Grade Three cultural relics;  
(2) causing damage to Grade Two or above cultural relics;  
(3) causing serious damage to or loss of the main structures of major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level;  
(4) repeatedly damaging or damaging multiple main structures of major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level;  
(5) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a person who commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph refuses to execute the administrative decision or order to cease infringing upon cultural relics issued by the competent administrative department of the State, a severe punishment shall be imposed as the circumstances warrant.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条    风景名胜区的核心景区以及未被确定为全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的古文化遗址、古墓葬、古建筑、石窟寺、石刻、壁画、近代现代重要史迹和代表性建筑等不可移动文物的本体，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-324)第二款规定的“国家保护的名胜古迹”。  
故意损毁国家保护的名胜古迹，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-324)第二款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）致使名胜古迹严重损毁或者灭失的；  
（二）多次损毁或者损毁多处名胜古迹的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，拒不执行国家行政主管部门作出的停止侵害文物的行政决定或者命令的，酌情从重处罚。  
故意损毁风景名胜区内被确定为全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的文物的，依照[刑法第三百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-324)第一款和[本解释第三条](#art-3)的规定定罪量刑。

**Article 4** The core scenic areas of scenic spots and historic sites, and the main structures of immovable cultural relics such as ancient cultural sites, ancient tombs, ancient buildings, cave temples, stone carvings, murals, and important modern and contemporary historic sites and representative buildings that have not been designated as major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level shall be deemed to be "scenic spots and historic sites protected by the State" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 324 of the Criminal Law.  
Whoever intentionally damages scenic spots and historic sites protected by the State, under any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed to have committed "serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 324 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing serious damage to or loss of the scenic spots or historic sites;  
(2) repeatedly damaging or damaging multiple scenic spots or historic sites;  
(3) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a person who commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph refuses to execute the administrative decision or order to cease infringing upon cultural relics issued by the competent administrative department of the State, a severe punishment shall be imposed as the circumstances warrant.  
Whoever intentionally damages cultural relics in scenic spots and historic sites that are designated as major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level shall be convicted and sentenced in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 324 of the Criminal Law and Article 3 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条    过失损毁国家保护的珍贵文物或者被确定为全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的文物，具有[本解释第三条](#art-3)第二款第一项至第三项规定情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-324)第三款规定的“造成严重后果”。

**Article 5** Whoever negligently damages precious cultural relics protected by the State or cultural relics designated as major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level, under any of the circumstances specified in subparagraphs (1) through (3) of paragraph 2 of Article 3 of this Interpretation, shall be deemed to have committed "causing serious consequences" as provided for in paragraph 3 of Article 324 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条    出售或者为出售而收购、运输、储存《中华人民共和国文物保护法》规定的“国家禁止买卖的文物”的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-326)规定的“倒卖国家禁止经营的文物”。  
倒卖国家禁止经营的文物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-326)规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）倒卖三级文物的；  
（二）交易数额在五万元以上的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
实施前款规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百二十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-326)规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）倒卖二级以上文物的；  
（二）倒卖三级文物五件以上的；  
（三）交易数额在二十五万元以上的；  
（四）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 6** Whoever sells, or purchases, transports, or stores for the purpose of selling, "cultural relics prohibited from being traded by the State" as provided for in the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Cultural Relics shall be deemed to have committed "trafficking in cultural relics prohibited from being dealt in by the State" as provided for in Article 326 of the Criminal Law.  
Whoever traffics in cultural relics prohibited from being dealt in by the State, under any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed to have committed "serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 326 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) trafficking in Grade Three cultural relics;  
(2) the transaction amount is not less than 50,000 yuan;  
(3) other circumstances of a serious nature.  
Where a person commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph, under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "especially serious circumstances" as provided for in Article 326 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) trafficking in Grade Two or above cultural relics;  
(2) trafficking in five or more Grade Three cultural relics;  
(3) the transaction amount is not less than 250,000 yuan;  
(4) other circumstances of an especially serious nature.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条    国有博物馆、图书馆以及其他国有单位，违反文物保护法规，将收藏或者管理的国家保护的文物藏品出售或者私自送给非国有单位或者个人的，依照[刑法第三百二十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-327)的规定，以非法出售、私赠文物藏品罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 7** Where a State-owned museum, library, or other State-owned unit, in violation of the regulations on the protection of cultural relics, sells or privately gives cultural relics in its collection or under its management that are protected by the State to a non-State-owned unit or individual, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with Article 327 of the Criminal Law for the crime of illegally selling or privately giving cultural relics in custody.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条    [刑法第三百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-328)第一款规定的“古文化遗址、古墓葬”包括水下古文化遗址、古墓葬。“古文化遗址、古墓葬”不以公布为不可移动文物的古文化遗址、古墓葬为限。  
实施盗掘行为，已损害古文化遗址、古墓葬的历史、艺术、科学价值的，应当认定为盗掘古文化遗址、古墓葬罪既遂。  
采用破坏性手段盗窃古文化遗址、古墓葬以外的古建筑、石窟寺、石刻、壁画、近代现代重要史迹和代表性建筑等其他不可移动文物的，依照[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)的规定，以盗窃罪追究刑事责任。

**Article 8** "Ancient cultural sites and ancient tombs" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 328 of the Criminal Law include underwater ancient cultural sites and ancient tombs. "Ancient cultural sites and ancient tombs" shall not be limited to those published as immovable cultural relics.  
Where an act of illegal excavation has already damaged the historical, artistic, or scientific value of ancient cultural sites or ancient tombs, it shall be deemed as consummation of the crime of illegally excavating ancient cultural sites or ancient tombs.  
Whoever, by destructive means, steals other immovable cultural relics other than ancient cultural sites and ancient tombs, such as ancient buildings, cave temples, stone carvings, murals, important modern and contemporary historic sites and representative buildings, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with Article 264 of the Criminal Law for the crime of theft.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条    明知是盗窃文物、盗掘古文化遗址、古墓葬等犯罪所获取的三级以上文物，而予以窝藏、转移、收购、加工、代为销售或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒的，依照[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)的规定，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得罪追究刑事责任。  
实施前款规定的行为，事先通谋的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 9** Whoever knowingly harbors, transfers, purchases, processes, sells on behalf of, or otherwise conceals or disguises Grade Three or above cultural relics obtained from crimes such as theft of cultural relics, illegal excavation of ancient cultural sites or ancient tombs, shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with Article 312 of the Criminal Law for the crime of concealing or disguising the proceeds of crime.  
Where a person who commits the act prescribed in the preceding paragraph has prior collusion with others, he or she shall be dealt with as a joint offender.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条    国家机关工作人员严重不负责任，造成珍贵文物损毁或者流失，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第四百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-419)规定的“后果严重”：  
（一）导致二级以上文物或者五件以上三级文物损毁或者流失的；  
（二）导致全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位的本体严重损毁或者灭失的；  
（三）其他后果严重的情形。

**Article 10** Where a functionary of a State organ, through gross neglect of duty, causes damage to or loss of precious cultural relics, under any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as "serious consequences" as provided for in Article 419 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing damage to or loss of Grade Two or above cultural relics, or five or more Grade Three cultural relics;  
(2) causing serious damage to or loss of the main structures of major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level;  
(3) other circumstances of serious consequences.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条    单位实施走私文物、倒卖文物等行为，构成犯罪的，依照本解释规定的相应自然人犯罪的定罪量刑标准，对直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员定罪处罚，并对单位判处罚金。  
公司、企业、事业单位、机关、团体等单位实施盗窃文物，故意损毁文物、名胜古迹，过失损毁文物，盗掘古文化遗址、古墓葬等行为的，依照本解释规定的相应定罪量刑标准，追究组织者、策划者、实施者的刑事责任。

**Article 11** Where a unit commits acts such as smuggling cultural relics or trafficking in cultural relics, which constitute a crime, the directly responsible supervisors and other directly liable persons shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the standards for conviction and sentencing of the corresponding crimes committed by natural persons as prescribed in this Interpretation, and a fine shall be imposed on the unit.  
Where a company, enterprise, institution, organ, or organization commits acts such as theft of cultural relics, intentional damage to cultural relics or scenic spots and historic sites, negligent damage to cultural relics, illegal excavation of ancient cultural sites or ancient tombs, the criminal liability of the organizers, planners, and executors shall be investigated in accordance with the corresponding standards for conviction and sentencing prescribed in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条    针对不可移动文物整体实施走私、盗窃、倒卖等行为的，根据所属不可移动文物的等级，依照[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条、第六条的规定定罪量刑：  
（一）尚未被确定为文物保护单位的不可移动文物，适用一般文物的定罪量刑标准；  
（二）市、县级文物保护单位，适用三级文物的定罪量刑标准；  
（三）全国重点文物保护单位、省级文物保护单位，适用二级以上文物的定罪量刑标准。  
针对不可移动文物中的建筑构件、壁画、雕塑、石刻等实施走私、盗窃、倒卖等行为的，根据建筑构件、壁画、雕塑、石刻等文物本身的等级或者价值，依照[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条、第六条的规定定罪量刑。建筑构件、壁画、雕塑、石刻等所属不可移动文物的等级，应当作为量刑情节予以考虑。

**Article 12** Where acts such as smuggling, theft, or trafficking are committed against an immovable cultural relic as a whole, conviction and sentencing shall be based on the grade of the immovable cultural relic to which it belongs, in accordance with Articles 1, 2, and 6 of this Interpretation:  
(1) For immovable cultural relics that have not yet been designated as cultural relics protection units, the standards for conviction and sentencing of ordinary cultural relics shall apply;  
(2) For municipal or county-level cultural relics protection units, the standards for conviction and sentencing of Grade Three cultural relics shall apply;  
(3) For major historic sites protected at the national level or historic sites protected at the provincial level, the standards for conviction and sentencing of Grade Two or above cultural relics shall apply.  
Where acts such as smuggling, theft, or trafficking are committed against building components, murals, sculptures, stone carvings, or other components of immovable cultural relics, conviction and sentencing shall be based on the grade or value of the cultural relic itself, such as the building component, mural, sculpture, or stone carving, in accordance with Articles 1, 2, and 6 of this Interpretation. The grade of the immovable cultural relic to which the building component, mural, sculpture, or stone carving belongs shall be considered as a sentencing circumstance.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条    案件涉及不同等级的文物的，按照高级别文物的量刑幅度量刑；有多件同级文物的，五件同级文物视为一件高一级文物，但是价值明显不相当的除外。

**Article 13** Where a case involves cultural relics of different grades, sentencing shall be imposed according to the sentencing range for the higher-grade cultural relics; where there are multiple cultural relics of the same grade, five cultural relics of the same grade shall be deemed as one cultural relic of the next higher grade, unless the values are clearly disproportionate.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条    依照文物价值定罪量刑的，根据涉案文物的有效价格证明认定文物价值；无有效价格证明，或者根据价格证明认定明显不合理的，根据销赃数额认定，或者结合[本解释第十五条](#art-15)规定的鉴定意见、报告认定。

**Article 14** Where conviction and sentencing are based on the value of cultural relics, the value shall be determined according to valid price certificates for the cultural relics involved; where there are no valid price certificates, or where the determination based on price certificates is clearly unreasonable, the value shall be determined according to the amount from disposing of stolen goods, or in combination with the expert evaluation opinions or reports prescribed in Article 15 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条    在行为人实施有关行为前，文物行政部门已对涉案文物及其等级作出认定的，可以直接对有关案件事实作出认定。  
对案件涉及的有关文物鉴定、价值认定等专门性问题难以确定的，由司法鉴定机构出具鉴定意见，或者由国务院文物行政部门指定的机构出具报告。其中，对于文物价值，也可以由有关价格认证机构作出价格认证并出具报告。

**Article 15** Where, before the perpetrator commits the relevant act, the administrative department for cultural relics has already made a determination on the cultural relics involved and their grade, the relevant facts of the case may be directly determined.  
Where the specialized issues such as the identification and value determination of the cultural relics involved in the case are difficult to determine, an expert evaluation opinion shall be issued by a judicial appraisal institution, or a report shall be issued by an institution designated by the administrative department for cultural relics of the State Council. For the value of cultural relics, a price certification may also be made by a relevant price certification institution and a report shall be issued.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条    实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条、第六条至第九条规定的行为，虽已达到应当追究刑事责任的标准，但行为人系初犯，积极退回或者协助追回文物，未造成文物损毁，并确有悔罪表现的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。  
实施[本解释第三条](#art-3)至第五条规定的行为，虽已达到应当追究刑事责任的标准，但行为人系初犯，积极赔偿损失，并确有悔罪表现的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。

**Article 16** Where a person commits the acts prescribed in Articles 1, 2, and 6 through 9 of this Interpretation, although such acts have reached the standard for investigating criminal liability, if the person is a first-time offender, actively returns or assists in recovering the cultural relics, does not cause damage to the cultural relics, and genuinely shows repentance, the circumstances may be deemed as minor, and the person may be subject to non-prosecution or exemption from criminal punishment.  
Where a person commits the acts prescribed in Articles 3 through 5 of this Interpretation, although such acts have reached the standard for investigating criminal liability, if the person is a first-time offender, actively compensates for the losses, and genuinely shows repentance, the circumstances may be deemed as minor, and the person may be subject to non-prosecution or exemption from criminal punishment.

<a id="art-17"></a>第十七条    走私、盗窃、损毁、倒卖、盗掘或者非法转让具有科学价值的古脊椎动物化石、古人类化石的，依照刑法和本解释的有关规定定罪量刑。

**Article 17** Whoever smuggles, steals, damages, traffics in, illegally excavates, or illegally transfers ancient vertebrate fossils or ancient human fossils of scientific value shall be convicted and sentenced in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law and this Interpretation.

<a id="art-18"></a>第十八条    本解释自2016年1月1日起施行。本解释公布施行后，最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理盗窃、盗掘、非法经营和走私文物的案件具体应用法律的若干问题的解释》（法（研）发〔1987〕32号）同时废止；之前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 18** This Interpretation shall come into force as of January 1, 2016. Upon the promulgation and implementation of this Interpretation, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Handling of Cases of Theft, Illegal Excavation, Illegal Business and Smuggling of Cultural Relics (Fa (Yan) Fa [1987] No. 32) shall be simultaneously abolished; where any judicial interpretation previously issued is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
