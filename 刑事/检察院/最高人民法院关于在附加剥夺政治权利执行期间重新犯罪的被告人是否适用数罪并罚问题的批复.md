### 最高人民法院关于在附加剥夺政治权利执行期间重新犯罪的被告人是否适用数罪并罚问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于在附加剥夺政治权利执行期间重新犯罪的  
  
被告人是否适用数罪并罚问题的批复  
  
法复〔1994〕8号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于对在附加剥夺政治权利期间重新犯罪的被告人是否适用数罪并罚问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
对被判处有期徒刑的罪犯，主刑已执行完毕，在执行附加刑剥夺政治权利期间又重新犯罪，如果所犯新罪无须判处附加刑剥夺政治权利的，应当按照[《中华人民共和国刑法》第六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-64)第二款、第六十六条的规定，在对被告人所犯新罪作出判决时，将新罪所判处的刑罚和前罪没有执行完毕的附加刑剥夺政治权利，按照数罪并罚原则，决定执行的刑罚，即在新罪所判处的刑罚执行完毕以后，继续执行前罪没有执行完毕的附加刑剥夺政治权利。  
  
1994年5月16日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on the Question of Whether Combined Punishment for Multiple Crimes Shall Apply to a Defendant Who Commits a New Crime During the Enforcement Period of Deprivation of Political Rights as a Supplementary Punishment  
Supreme People's Court Reply [1994] No. 8  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
Your Request for Instructions on Whether Combined Punishment for Multiple Crimes Shall Apply to a Defendant Who Commits a New Crime During the Period of Deprivation of Political Rights as a Supplementary Punishment has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
For a criminal sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment, if the principal punishment has been fully enforced and he commits a new crime again during the enforcement period of the supplementary punishment of deprivation of political rights, and if the new crime committed does not require the supplementary punishment of deprivation of political rights to be imposed, it shall, in accordance with the provisions of Article 64, paragraph 2, and Article 66 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, when rendering a judgment for the new crime committed by the defendant, combine the punishment imposed for the new crime with the supplementary punishment of deprivation of political rights that has not been fully enforced for the previous crime, and in accordance with the principle of combined punishment for multiple crimes, decide the punishment to be executed, that is, after the punishment imposed for the new crime has been fully enforced, the supplementary punishment of deprivation of political rights that has not been fully enforced for the previous crime shall continue to be enforced.  
May 16, 1994
