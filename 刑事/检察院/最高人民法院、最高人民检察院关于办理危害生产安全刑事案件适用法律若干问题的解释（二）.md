# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害生产安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（二）

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2022〕19号  
**公布日期**：2022-09-19  
**生效日期**：2022-12-19

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害生产安全刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释（二）》已于2022年9月19日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1875次会议、2022年10月25日由最高人民检察院第十三届检察委员会第一百零六次会议通过，现予公布，自2022年12月19日起施行。 2022年12月15日 最高人民法院　最高人民检察院 关于办理危害生产安全刑事案件 适用法律若干问题的解释（二）

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　明知存在事故隐患，继续作业存在危险，仍然违反有关安全管理的规定，有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第二款规定的“强令他人违章冒险作业”：  
（一）以威逼、胁迫、恐吓等手段，强制他人违章作业的；  
（二）利用组织、指挥、管理职权，强制他人违章作业的；  
（三）其他强令他人违章冒险作业的情形。  
明知存在重大事故隐患，仍然违反有关安全管理的规定，不排除或者故意掩盖重大事故隐患，组织他人作业的，属于[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第二款规定的“冒险组织作业”。

**Article 1** If a person, knowing of the existence of a safety hazard and the danger of continuing operations, nevertheless violates the provisions on safety management, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "forcibly ordering others to operate in violation of rules and take risks" as stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Compelling others to operate in violation of rules by means of intimidation, coercion, threats, or the like;  
(2) Compelling others to operate in violation of rules by using the authority of organization, command, or management;  
(3) Other circumstances of forcibly ordering others to operate in violation of rules and take risks.  
If a person, knowing of the existence of a major safety hazard, nevertheless violates the provisions on safety management, and fails to eliminate or deliberately conceals the major safety hazard while organizing others to operate, it shall be deemed as "organizing operations at risk" as stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)之一规定的犯罪主体，包括对生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员，以及直接从事生产、作业的人员。

**Article 2** The criminal subjects stipulated in Article 134-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall include persons such as responsible persons, management personnel, actual controllers, investors, and other persons who bear the responsibility of organization, command, or management over production and operations, as well as persons directly engaged in production and operations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　因存在重大事故隐患被依法责令停产停业、停止施工、停止使用有关设备、设施、场所或者立即采取排除危险的整改措施，有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)之一第二项规定的“拒不执行”：  
（一）无正当理由故意不执行各级人民政府或者负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法作出的上述行政决定、命令的；  
（二）虚构重大事故隐患已经排除的事实，规避、干扰执行各级人民政府或者负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法作出的上述行政决定、命令的；  
（三）以行贿等不正当手段，规避、干扰执行各级人民政府或者负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门依法作出的上述行政决定、命令的。  
有前款第三项行为，同时构成[刑法第三百八十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-389)行贿罪、第三百九十三条单位行贿罪等犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。  
认定是否属于“拒不执行”，应当综合考虑行政决定、命令是否具有法律、行政法规等依据，行政决定、命令的内容和期限要求是否明确、合理，行为人是否具有按照要求执行的能力等因素进行判断。

**Article 3** If, due to the existence of a major safety hazard, a person is ordered according to law to suspend production or business, stop construction, cease using relevant equipment, facilities, or premises, or immediately take corrective measures to eliminate the hazard, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "refusing to execute" as stipulated in item (2) of Article 134-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Intentionally failing to execute, without justifiable reason, the aforementioned administrative decisions or orders made according to law by people's governments at all levels or departments with responsibility for workplace safety supervision and administration;  
(2) Fabricating facts that the major safety hazard has been eliminated to evade or interfere with the execution of the aforementioned administrative decisions or orders made according to law by people's governments at all levels or departments with responsibility for workplace safety supervision and administration;  
(3) Using improper means such as bribery to evade or interfere with the execution of the aforementioned administrative decisions or orders made according to law by people's governments at all levels or departments with responsibility for workplace safety supervision and administration.  
If a person commits the act under item (3) of the preceding paragraph and it concurrently constitutes the crime of bribery under Article 389, the crime of unit bribery under Article 393, or other crimes, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes.  
To determine whether it constitutes "refusing to execute", comprehensive consideration shall be given to factors such as whether the administrative decisions or orders have a basis in laws, administrative regulations, etc., whether the content and time limit requirements of the administrative decisions or orders are clear and reasonable, and whether the actor has the ability to execute as required.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第二款和第一百三十四条之一第二项规定的“重大事故隐患”，依照法律、行政法规、部门规章、强制性标准以及有关行政规范性文件进行认定。  
[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)之一第三项规定的“危险物品”，依照[安全生产法第一百一十七条](../../劳动与社会保障/劳动就业/中华人民共和国安全生产法.md#art-117)的规定确定。  
对于是否属于“重大事故隐患”或者“危险物品”难以确定的，可以依据司法鉴定机构出具的鉴定意见、地市级以上负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门或者其指定的机构出具的意见，结合其他证据综合审查，依法作出认定。

**Article 4** The "major safety hazard" as stipulated in paragraph 2 of Article 134 and item (2) of Article 134-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be determined in accordance with laws, administrative regulations, departmental rules, mandatory standards, and relevant administrative normative documents.  
The "dangerous articles" as stipulated in item (3) of Article 134-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of Article 117 of the Workplace Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If it is difficult to determine whether something constitutes a "major safety hazard" or "dangerous articles", the determination may be made according to law based on expert evaluation opinions issued by judicial expert evaluation institutions, opinions issued by departments at or above the prefecture level with responsibility for workplace safety supervision and administration or institutions designated by them, in conjunction with comprehensive review of other evidence.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　在生产、作业中违反有关安全管理的规定，有[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)之一规定情形之一，因而发生重大伤亡事故或者造成其他严重后果，构成[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)、第一百三十五条至第一百三十九条等规定的重大责任事故罪、重大劳动安全事故罪、危险物品肇事罪、工程重大安全事故罪等犯罪的，依照该规定定罪处罚。

**Article 5** If a person violates the provisions on safety management in production or operations, has any of the circumstances stipulated in Article 134-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and thereby causes a major accident involving casualties or other serious consequences, which constitutes the crimes such as the crime of major liability accident, the crime of major labor safety accident, the crime of causing an accident with dangerous articles, the crime of major engineering safety accident, etc., as stipulated in Articles 134, 135 to 139, etc., of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with those provisions.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　承担安全评价职责的中介组织的人员提供的证明文件有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第一款规定的“虚假证明文件”：  
（一）故意伪造的；  
（二）在周边环境、主要建（构）筑物、工艺、装置、设备设施等重要内容上弄虚作假，导致与评价期间实际情况不符，影响评价结论的；  
（三）隐瞒生产经营单位重大事故隐患及整改落实情况、主要灾害等级等情况，影响评价结论的；  
（四）伪造、篡改生产经营单位相关信息、数据、技术报告或者结论等内容，影响评价结论的；  
（五）故意采用存疑的第三方证明材料、监测检验报告，影响评价结论的；  
（六）有其他弄虚作假行为，影响评价结论的情形。  
生产经营单位提供虚假材料、影响评价结论，承担安全评价职责的中介组织的人员对评价结论与实际情况不符无主观故意的，不属于[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第一款规定的“故意提供虚假证明文件”。  
有本条第二款情形，承担安全评价职责的中介组织的人员严重不负责任，导致出具的证明文件有重大失实，造成严重后果的，依照[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第三款的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 6** If a certificate document provided by a person of an intermediary organization with responsibility for safety assessment has any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as a "false certificate document" as stipulated in paragraph 1 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) It is intentionally forged;  
(2) Fraud is committed in important content such as the surrounding environment, main buildings (structures), processes, devices, equipment and facilities, etc., resulting in inconsistency with the actual situation during the assessment period and affecting the assessment conclusion;  
(3) Circumstances such as major safety hazards of the production or business operation entity and their rectification status, and main disaster grades are concealed, affecting the assessment conclusion;  
(4) Relevant information, data, technical reports, or conclusions of the production or business operation entity are forged or tampered with, affecting the assessment conclusion;  
(5) Dubious third-party certification materials or monitoring inspection reports are intentionally used, affecting the assessment conclusion;  
(6) Other circumstances of fraud affecting the assessment conclusion.  
If the production or business operation entity provides false materials affecting the assessment conclusion, and the person of the intermediary organization with responsibility for safety assessment has no subjective intent regarding the inconsistency between the assessment conclusion and the actual situation, it shall not be deemed as "intentionally providing false certificate documents" as stipulated in paragraph 1 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
If there is a circumstance under paragraph 2 of this Article, and the person of the intermediary organization with responsibility for safety assessment is grossly irresponsible, resulting in a major inaccuracy in the issued certificate document and causing serious consequences, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　承担安全评价职责的中介组织的人员故意提供虚假证明文件，有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第一款规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）造成死亡一人以上或者重伤三人以上安全事故的；  
（二）造成直接经济损失五十万元以上安全事故的；  
（三）违法所得数额十万元以上的；  
（四）两年内因故意提供虚假证明文件受过两次以上行政处罚，又故意提供虚假证明文件的；  
（五）其他情节严重的情形。  
在涉及公共安全的重大工程、项目中提供虚假的安全评价文件，有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第一款第三项规定的“致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受特别重大损失”：  
（一）造成死亡三人以上或者重伤十人以上安全事故的；  
（二）造成直接经济损失五百万元以上安全事故的；  
（三）其他致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受特别重大损失的情形。  
承担安全评价职责的中介组织的人员有[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第一款行为，在裁量刑罚时，应当考虑其行为手段、主观过错程度、对安全事故的发生所起作用大小及其获利情况、一贯表现等因素，综合评估社会危害性，依法裁量刑罚，确保罪责刑相适应。

**Article 7** If a person of an intermediary organization with responsibility for safety assessment intentionally provides false certificate documents, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "serious circumstances" as stipulated in paragraph 1 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causing a safety accident resulting in one or more deaths or three or more serious injuries;  
(2) Causing a safety accident with direct economic losses of 500,000 RMB or more;  
(3) The amount of illegal gains is 100,000 RMB or more;  
(4) Having been subject to administrative penalties two or more times within two years for intentionally providing false certificate documents, and again intentionally providing false certificate documents;  
(5) Other serious circumstances.  
If a false safety assessment document is provided in a major project or project involving public safety, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "causing particularly heavy losses to public property, the state, and the interests of the people" as stipulated in item (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causing a safety accident resulting in three or more deaths or ten or more serious injuries;  
(2) Causing a safety accident with direct economic losses of 5,000,000 RMB or more;  
(3) Other circumstances causing particularly heavy losses to public property, the state, and the interests of the people.  
If a person of an intermediary organization with responsibility for safety assessment commits the act under paragraph 1 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, when determining the penalty, factors such as the means of the act, the degree of subjective fault, the role played in the occurrence of the safety accident, the gains obtained, and the person's consistent conduct shall be considered to comprehensively assess social harmfulness, and the penalty shall be determined according to law to ensure that the crime, responsibility, and punishment are commensurate.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　承担安全评价职责的中介组织的人员，严重不负责任，出具的证明文件有重大失实，有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)第三款规定的“造成严重后果”：  
（一）造成死亡一人以上或者重伤三人以上安全事故的；  
（二）造成直接经济损失一百万元以上安全事故的；  
（三）其他造成严重后果的情形。

**Article 8** If a person of an intermediary organization with responsibility for safety assessment is grossly irresponsible, resulting in a major inaccuracy in the issued certificate document, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as "causing serious consequences" as stipulated in paragraph 3 of Article 229 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causing a safety accident resulting in one or more deaths or three or more serious injuries;  
(2) Causing a safety accident with direct economic losses of 1,000,000 RMB or more;  
(3) Other circumstances causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　承担安全评价职责的中介组织犯[刑法第二百二十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-229)规定之罪的，对该中介组织判处罚金，并对其直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员，依照[本解释第七条](#art-7)、第八条的规定处罚。

**Article 9** If an intermediary organization with responsibility for safety assessment commits the crime stipulated in Article 229 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, the intermediary organization shall be sentenced to a fine, and the directly responsible supervisor and other directly liable persons shall be punished in accordance with Articles 7 and 8 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　有[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)之一行为，积极配合公安机关或者负有安全生产监督管理职责的部门采取措施排除事故隐患，确有悔改表现，认罪认罚的，可以依法从宽处罚；犯罪情节轻微不需要判处刑罚的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不作为犯罪处理。

**Article 10** If a person commits an act under Article 134-1 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, actively cooperates with the public security organ or the department with responsibility for workplace safety supervision and administration in taking measures to eliminate the accident hazard, genuinely demonstrates repentance, and admits guilt and accepts punishment, the person may be given a lighter or mitigated punishment according to law; if the circumstances of the crime are minor and no penalty is necessary, the person may be exempted from prosecution or from criminal punishment; if the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not significant, the act shall not be treated as a crime.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　有本解释规定的行为，被不起诉或者免予刑事处罚，需要给予行政处罚、政务处分或者其他处分的，依法移送有关主管机关处理。

**Article 11** If a person commits an act stipulated in this Interpretation and is exempted from prosecution or from criminal punishment, and it is necessary to impose an administrative penalty, a disciplinary sanction, or other sanction, the case shall be transferred to the relevant competent authority for handling according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　本解释自2022年12月19日起施行。最高人民法院、最高人民检察院此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 12** This Interpretation shall come into force as of December 19, 2022. Where any judicial interpretation previously issued by the Supreme People's Court or the Supreme People's Procuratorate is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
