# 最高人民法院关于审理破坏土地资源刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2000〕14号  
**公布日期**：2000-06-16  
**生效日期**：2000-06-22

> （2000年6月16日最高人民法院审判委员会第1119次会议通过，自2000年6月22日起施行。） 法释〔2000〕14号 为依法惩处破坏土地资源犯罪活动，根据刑法的有关规定，现就审理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 以牟利为目的，违反土地管理法规，非法转让、倒卖土地使用权，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法转让、倒卖土地使用权“情节严重”，依照刑法第二百二十八条的规定，以非法转让、倒卖土地使用权罪定罪处罚： （一）非法转让、倒卖基本农田五亩以上的； （二）非法转让、倒卖基本农田以外的耕地十亩以上的； （三）非法转让、倒卖其他土地二十亩以上的； （四）非法获利五十万元以上的； （五）非法转让、倒卖土地接近上述数量标准并具有其他恶劣情节的，如曾因非法转让、倒卖土地使用权受过行政处罚或者造成严重后果等。 第二条 实施第一条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法转让、倒卖土地使用权“情节特别严重”： （一）非法转让、倒卖基本农田十亩以上的； （二）非法转让、倒卖基本农田以外的耕地二十亩以上的； （三）非法转让、倒卖其他土地四十亩以上的； （四）非法获利一百万元以上的； （五）非法转让、倒卖土地接近上述数量标准并具有其他恶劣情节，如造成严重后果等。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 以牟利为目的，违反土地管理法规，非法转让、倒卖土地使用权，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法转让、倒卖土地使用权“情节严重”，依照[刑法第二百二十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-228)的规定，以非法转让、倒卖土地使用权罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法转让、倒卖基本农田五亩以上的；  
（二）非法转让、倒卖基本农田以外的耕地十亩以上的；  
（三）非法转让、倒卖其他土地二十亩以上的；  
（四）非法获利五十万元以上的；  
（五）非法转让、倒卖土地接近上述数量标准并具有其他恶劣情节的，如曾因非法转让、倒卖土地使用权受过行政处罚或者造成严重后果等。

**Article 1** For the purpose of profit, violating the land management regulations, illegally transferring or reselling the right to use land, in any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” of illegally transferring or reselling the right to use land, and shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 228 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China as the crime of illegally transferring or reselling the right to use land:  
(1) illegally transferring or reselling five mu or more of basic farmland;  
(2) illegally transferring or reselling ten mu or more of arable land other than basic farmland;  
(3) illegally transferring or reselling twenty mu or more of other land;  
(4) illegally gaining 500,000 yuan or more;  
(5) illegally transferring or reselling land close to the above quantity standards and having other vile circumstances, such as having previously received administrative penalties for illegally transferring or reselling the right to use land or causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 实施第一条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法转让、倒卖土地使用权“情节特别严重”：  
（一）非法转让、倒卖基本农田十亩以上的；  
（二）非法转让、倒卖基本农田以外的耕地二十亩以上的；  
（三）非法转让、倒卖其他土地四十亩以上的；  
（四）非法获利一百万元以上的；  
（五）非法转让、倒卖土地接近上述数量标准并具有其他恶劣情节，如造成严重后果等。

**Article 2** Where the acts prescribed in Article 1 are committed and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as “especially serious circumstances” of illegally transferring or reselling the right to use land:  
(1) illegally transferring or reselling ten mu or more of basic farmland;  
(2) illegally transferring or reselling twenty mu or more of arable land other than basic farmland;  
(3) illegally transferring or reselling forty mu or more of other land;  
(4) illegally gaining one million yuan or more;  
(5) illegally transferring or reselling land close to the above quantity standards and having other vile circumstances, such as causing serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 违反土地管理法规，非法占用耕地改作他用，数量较大，造成耕地大量毁坏的，依照[刑法第三百四十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-342)的规定，以非法占用耕地罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法占用耕地“数量较大”，是指非法占用基本农田五亩以上或者非法占用基本农田以外的耕地十亩以上。  
（二）非法占用耕地“造成耕地大量毁坏”，是指行为人非法占用耕地建窑、建坟、建房、挖沙、采石、采矿、取土、堆放固体废弃物或者进行其他非农业建设，造成基本农田五亩以上或者基本农田以外的耕地十亩以上种植条件严重毁坏或者严重污染。

**Article 3** Where a person violates the land management regulations by illegally occupying arable land and converting it to other uses in a large quantity, causing large-scale destruction of arable land, he or she shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 342 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China as the crime of illegally occupying arable land:  
(1) “Large quantity” of illegally occupied arable land refers to illegally occupying five mu or more of basic farmland or illegally occupying ten mu or more of arable land other than basic farmland.  
(2) “Causing large-scale destruction of arable land” means the actor illegally occupies arable land to build kilns, graves, houses, or to dig sand, quarry, mine, extract soil, pile solid waste, or engage in other non-agricultural construction, causing severe destruction or serious pollution of the planting conditions on five mu or more of basic farmland or ten mu or more of arable land other than basic farmland.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 国家机关工作人员徇私舞弊，违反土地管理法规，滥用职权，非法批准征用、占用土地，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法批准征用、占用土地“情节严重”，依照[刑法第四百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-410)的规定，以非法批准征用、占用土地罪定罪处罚：  
（一）非法批准征用、占用基本农田十亩以上的；  
（二）非法批准征用、占用基本农田以外的耕地三十亩以上的；  
（三）非法批准征用、占用其他土地五十亩以上的；  
（四）虽未达到上述数量标准，但非法批准征用、占用土地造成直接经济损失三十万元以上；造成耕地大量毁坏等恶劣情节的。

**Article 4** Where a state organ staff member engages in malpractices for personal gain, violates the land management regulations, abuses his or her authority, and illegally approves the requisition or occupation of land, in any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” of illegally approving the requisition or occupation of land, and shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 410 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China as the crime of illegally approving the requisition or occupation of land:  
(1) illegally approving the requisition or occupation of ten mu or more of basic farmland;  
(2) illegally approving the requisition or occupation of thirty mu or more of arable land other than basic farmland;  
(3) illegally approving the requisition or occupation of fifty mu or more of other land;  
(4) although the above quantity standards are not met, the illegal approval of the requisition or occupation of land causes direct economic losses of 300,000 yuan or more; or causing large-scale destruction of arable land or other vile circumstances.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 实施第四条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法批准征用、占用土地“致使国家或者集体利益遭受特别重大损失”：  
（一）非法批准征用、占用基本农田二十亩以上的；  
（二）非法批准征用、占用基本农田以外的耕地六十亩以上的；  
（三）非法批准征用、占用其他土地一百亩以上的；  
（四）非法批准征用、占用土地，造成基本农田五亩以上，其他耕地十亩以上严重毁坏的；  
（五）非法批准征用、占用土地造成直接经济损失五十万元以上等恶劣情节的。

**Article 5** Where the acts prescribed in Article 4 are committed and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as “causing especially heavy losses to the interests of the state or the collective” for illegally approving the requisition or occupation of land:  
(1) illegally approving the requisition or occupation of twenty mu or more of basic farmland;  
(2) illegally approving the requisition or occupation of sixty mu or more of arable land other than basic farmland;  
(3) illegally approving the requisition or occupation of one hundred mu or more of other land;  
(4) illegally approving the requisition or occupation of land, causing severe destruction of five mu or more of basic farmland or ten mu or more of other arable land;  
(5) illegally approving the requisition or occupation of land, causing direct economic losses of 500,000 yuan or more, or other vile circumstances.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 国家机关工作人员徇私舞弊，违反土地管理法规，非法低价出让国有土地使用权，具有下列情形之一的，属于“情节严重”，依照[刑法第四百一十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-410)的规定，以非法低价出让国有土地使用权罪定罪处罚：  
（一）出让国有土地使用权面积在三十亩以上，并且出让价额低于国家规定的最低价额标准的百分之六十的；  
（二）造成国有土地资产流失价额在三十万元以上的。

**Article 6** Where a state organ staff member engages in malpractices for personal gain, violates the land management regulations, and illegally transfers the right to use state-owned land at a low price, in any of the following circumstances, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances”, and shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 410 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China as the crime of illegally transferring the right to use state-owned land at a low price:  
(1) the area of the transferred right to use state-owned land is thirty mu or more, and the transfer price is lower than sixty percent of the minimum price standard prescribed by the state;  
(2) causing a loss of state-owned land asset value of 300,000 yuan or more.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 实施第六条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于非法低价出让国有土地使用权，“致使国家和集体利益遭受特别重大损失”：  
（一）非法低价出让国有土地使用权面积在六十亩以上，并且出让价额低于国家规定的最低价额标准的百分之四十的；  
（二）造成国有土地资产流失价额在五十万元以上的。

**Article 7** Where the acts prescribed in Article 6 are committed and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as “causing especially heavy losses to the interests of the state and the collective” for illegally transferring the right to use state-owned land at a low price:  
(1) the area of the illegally transferred right to use state-owned land at a low price is sixty mu or more, and the transfer price is lower than forty percent of the minimum price standard prescribed by the state;  
(2) causing a loss of state-owned land asset value of 500,000 yuan or more.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 单位犯非法转让、倒卖土地使用权罪、非法占有耕地罪的定罪量刑标准，依照[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条、第三条的规定执行。

**Article 8** The standards for conviction and sentencing of a unit that commits the crime of illegally transferring or reselling the right to use land or the crime of illegally occupying arable land shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Articles 1, 2 and 3 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条 多次实施本解释规定的行为依法应当追诉的，或者一年内多次实施本解释规定的行为未经处理的，按照累计的数量、数额处罚。

**Article 9** Where a person commits multiple acts specified in this Interpretation which shall be prosecuted according to law, or commits multiple acts specified in this Interpretation within one year and those acts have not been dealt with, the punishment shall be imposed based on the cumulative quantity and amount.
