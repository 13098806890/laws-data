# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2017〕3号  
**公布日期**：2017-01-04  
**生效日期**：2017-02-01

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2017年1月4日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1706次会议、2016年12月8日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第58次会议通过，现予公布，自2017年2月1日起施行。 2017年1月25日 最高人民法院最高人民检察院 关于办理组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施等刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2017〕3号 为依法惩治组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施等犯罪活动，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　冒用宗教、气功或者以其他名义建立，神化、鼓吹首要分子，利用制造、散布迷信邪说等手段蛊惑、蒙骗他人，发展、控制成员，危害社会的非法组织，应当认定为刑法第三百条规定的“邪教组织”。 第二条　组织、利用邪教组织，破坏国家法律、行政法规实施，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照刑法第三百条第一款的规定，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金： （一）建立邪教组织，或者邪教组织被取缔后又恢复、另行建立邪教组织的； （二）聚众包围、冲击、强占、哄闹国家机关、企业事业单位或者公共场所、宗教活动场所，扰乱社会秩序的； （三）非法举行集会、游行、示威，扰乱社会秩序的； （四）使用暴力、胁迫或者以其他方法强迫他人加入或者阻止他人退出邪教组织的； （五）组织、煽动、蒙骗成员或者他人不履行法定义务的； （六）使用“伪基站”“黑广播”等无线电台（站）或者无线电频率宣扬邪教的； （七）曾因从事邪教活动被追究刑事责任或者二年内受过行政处罚，又从事邪教活动的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　冒用宗教、气功或者以其他名义建立，神化、鼓吹首要分子，利用制造、散布迷信邪说等手段蛊惑、蒙骗他人，发展、控制成员，危害社会的非法组织，应当认定为[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)规定的“邪教组织”。

**Article 1** : An illegal organization that is established under the guise of religion, qigong, or other names, that deifies and promotes its principal members, uses methods such as creating and disseminating superstitious and heretical teachings to delude and deceive others, develop and control members, and endanger society, shall be determined to be a "cult organization" as provided for in Article 300 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　组织、利用邪教组织，破坏国家法律、行政法规实施，具有下列情形之一的，应当依照[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款的规定，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金：  
（一）建立邪教组织，或者邪教组织被取缔后又恢复、另行建立邪教组织的；  
（二）聚众包围、冲击、强占、哄闹国家机关、企业事业单位或者公共场所、宗教活动场所，扰乱社会秩序的；  
（三）非法举行集会、游行、示威，扰乱社会秩序的；  
（四）使用暴力、胁迫或者以其他方法强迫他人加入或者阻止他人退出邪教组织的；  
（五）组织、煽动、蒙骗成员或者他人不履行法定义务的；  
（六）使用“伪基站”“黑广播”等无线电台（站）或者无线电频率宣扬邪教的；  
（七）曾因从事邪教活动被追究刑事责任或者二年内受过行政处罚，又从事邪教活动的；  
（八）发展邪教组织成员五十人以上的；  
（九）敛取钱财或者造成经济损失一百万元以上的；  
（十）以货币为载体宣扬邪教，数量在五百张（枚）以上的；  
（十一）制作、传播邪教宣传品，达到下列数量标准之一的：  
1.传单、喷图、图片、标语、报纸一千份（张）以上的；  
2.书籍、刊物二百五十册以上的；  
3.录音带、录像带等音像制品二百五十盒（张）以上的；  
4.标识、标志物二百五十件以上的；  
5.光盘、U盘、储存卡、移动硬盘等移动存储介质一百个以上的；  
6.横幅、条幅五十条(个)以上的。  
（十二）利用通讯信息网络宣扬邪教，具有下列情形之一的：  
1.制作、传播宣扬邪教的电子图片、文章二百张（篇）以上，电子书籍、刊物、音视频五十册（个）以上，或者电子文档五百万字符以上、电子音视频二百五十分钟以上的；  
2.编发信息、拨打电话一千条（次）以上的；  
3.利用在线人数累计达到一千以上的聊天室，或者利用群组成员、关注人员等账号数累计一千以上的通讯群组、微信、微博等社交网络宣扬邪教的；  
4.邪教信息实际被点击、浏览数达到五千次以上的。  
（十三）其他情节严重的情形。

**Article 2** : Whoever organizes or uses a cult organization to undermine the implementation of national laws and administrative regulations, in any of the following circumstances, shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 300 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Establishing a cult organization, or restoring or re-establishing a cult organization after it has been banned;  
(2) Gathering a crowd to surround, storm, forcibly occupy, or make a disturbance at state organs, enterprises and institutions, or public places or venues for religious activities, thereby disrupting social order;  
(3) Illegally holding assemblies, processions, or demonstrations, thereby disrupting social order;  
(4) Using violence, coercion, or other methods to compel others to join or prevent others from leaving a cult organization;  
(5) Organizing, inciting, or deceiving members or others to fail to perform legal obligations;  
(6) Using "pseudo base stations", "illegal radio stations", or other radio stations (stations) or radio frequencies to propagate cult teachings;  
(7) Having been previously investigated for criminal liability or having received an administrative penalty within two years for engaging in cult activities, and then engaging in cult activities again;  
(8) Recruiting fifty or more members for a cult organization;  
(9) Extorting money or causing economic losses of one million yuan or more;  
(10) Using currency as a medium to propagate cult teachings, with a quantity of five hundred or more pieces (coins);  
(11) Producing or disseminating cult propaganda materials, reaching any of the following quantity standards:  
1. One thousand or more copies (sheets) of leaflets, spray paintings, pictures, slogans, or newspapers;  
2. Two hundred and fifty or more copies of books or periodicals;  
3. Two hundred and fifty or more boxes (discs) of audio or video recordings such as audio tapes and videotapes;  
4. Two hundred and fifty or more pieces of identification or markers;  
5. One hundred or more pieces of mobile storage media such as CDs, USB flash drives, memory cards, or portable hard drives;  
6. Fifty or more banners or streamers.  
(12) Using communication information networks to propagate cult teachings, in any of the following circumstances:  
1. Producing or disseminating two hundred or more electronic pictures or articles propagating cult teachings, fifty or more electronic books, periodicals, or audio/video files, or electronic documents of five million or more characters, or electronic audio/video of two hundred and fifty minutes or more;  
2. Sending one thousand or more messages or making one thousand or more phone calls;  
3. Using a chat room with a cumulative online user count of one thousand or more, or using communication groups, WeChat, Weibo, or other social networks with a cumulative account count (group members, followers, etc.) of one thousand or more, to propagate cult teachings;  
4. The actual number of clicks or views of cult information reaches five thousand or more.  
(13) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　组织、利用邪教组织，破坏国家法律、行政法规实施，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”，处七年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)第一项至第七项规定的行为，社会危害特别严重的；  
（二）实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)第八项至第十二项规定的行为，数量或者数额达到第二条规定相应标准五倍以上的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。

**Article 3** : Whoever organizes or uses a cult organization to undermine the implementation of national laws and administrative regulations, in any of the following circumstances, shall be determined to have "extremely serious circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 300 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years or life imprisonment, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated:  
(1) Committing the acts specified in items (1) through (7) of Article 2 of this Interpretation, where the social harm is particularly serious;  
(2) Committing the acts specified in items (8) through (12) of Article 2 of this Interpretation, where the quantity or amount reaches five times or more the corresponding standards specified in Article 2;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are extremely serious.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　组织、利用邪教组织，破坏国家法律、行政法规实施，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款规定的“情节较轻”，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)第一项至第七项规定的行为，社会危害较轻的；  
（二）实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)第八项至第十二项规定的行为，数量或者数额达到相应标准五分之一以上的；  
（三）其他情节较轻的情形。

**Article 4** : Whoever organizes or uses a cult organization to undermine the implementation of national laws and administrative regulations, in any of the following circumstances, shall be determined to have "relatively minor circumstances" as provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 300 of the Criminal Law, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights, and shall also or solely be fined:  
(1) Committing the acts specified in items (1) through (7) of Article 2 of this Interpretation, where the social harm is relatively minor;  
(2) Committing the acts specified in items (8) through (12) of Article 2 of this Interpretation, where the quantity or amount reaches one-fifth or more of the corresponding standards;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are relatively minor.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　为了传播而持有、携带，或者传播过程中被当场查获，邪教宣传品数量达到[本解释第二条](#art-2)至第四条规定的有关标准的，按照下列情形分别处理：  
（一）邪教宣传品是行为人制作的，以犯罪既遂处理；  
（二）邪教宣传品不是行为人制作，尚未传播的，以犯罪预备处理；  
（三）邪教宣传品不是行为人制作，传播过程中被查获的，以犯罪未遂处理；  
（四）邪教宣传品不是行为人制作，部分已经传播出去的，以犯罪既遂处理，对于没有传播的部分，可以在量刑时酌情考虑。

**Article 5** : Where cult propaganda materials are held or carried for the purpose of dissemination, or are seized on the spot during dissemination, and the quantity reaches the relevant standards specified in Articles 2 through 4 of this Interpretation, the matter shall be handled separately according to the following circumstances:  
(1) If the cult propaganda materials were produced by the actor, the case shall be dealt with as a completed crime;  
(2) If the cult propaganda materials were not produced by the actor and have not yet been disseminated, the case shall be dealt with as preparation for a crime;  
(3) If the cult propaganda materials were not produced by the actor and are seized during the process of dissemination, the case shall be dealt with as an attempted crime;  
(4) If the cult propaganda materials were not produced by the actor and part of them has already been disseminated, the case shall be dealt with as a completed crime, and for the part that has not been disseminated, discretion may be exercised during sentencing.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　多次制作、传播邪教宣传品或者利用通讯信息网络宣扬邪教，未经处理的，数量或者数额累计计算。  
制作、传播邪教宣传品，或者利用通讯信息网络宣扬邪教，涉及不同种类或者形式的，可以根据本解释规定的不同数量标准的相应比例折算后累计计算。

**Article 6** : Where an actor has repeatedly produced or disseminated cult propaganda materials or used communication information networks to propagate cult teachings, and those acts have not been dealt with, the quantity or amount shall be calculated cumulatively.  
Where the production or dissemination of cult propaganda materials, or the use of communication information networks to propagate cult teachings, involves different categories or forms, the cumulative calculation may be made by converting them according to the corresponding ratios of the different quantity standards specified in this Interpretation.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　组织、利用邪教组织，制造、散布迷信邪说，蒙骗成员或者他人绝食、自虐等，或者蒙骗病人不接受正常治疗，致人重伤、死亡的，应当认定为[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第二款规定的组织、利用邪教组织“蒙骗他人，致人重伤、死亡”。  
组织、利用邪教组织蒙骗他人，致一人以上死亡或者三人以上重伤的，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。  
组织、利用邪教组织蒙骗他人，具有下列情形之一的，处七年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，并处罚金或者没收财产：  
（一）造成三人以上死亡的；  
（二）造成九人以上重伤的；  
（三）其他情节特别严重的情形。  
组织、利用邪教组织蒙骗他人，致人重伤的，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利，并处或者单处罚金。

**Article 7** : Where an organization or use of a cult organization creates and disseminates superstitious and heretical teachings, deceives members or others into fasting, self-mutilation, etc., or deceives patients into refusing normal treatment, thereby causing serious injury or death to a person, it shall be determined to be the organization or use of a cult organization that "deceives others, causing serious injury or death" as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 300 of the Criminal Law.  
Where an organization or use of a cult organization deceives others, causing the death of one or more persons or serious injury to three or more persons, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined.  
Where an organization or use of a cult organization deceives others, in any of the following circumstances, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than seven years or life imprisonment, and shall also be fined or have property confiscated:  
(1) Causing the death of three or more persons;  
(2) Causing serious injury to nine or more persons;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are extremely serious.  
Where an organization or use of a cult organization deceives others, causing serious injury to a person, the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights, and shall also or solely be fined.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　实施[本解释第二条](#art-2)至第五条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，从重处罚：  
（一）与境外机构、组织、人员勾结，从事邪教活动的；  
（二）跨省、自治区、直辖市建立邪教组织机构、发展成员或者组织邪教活动的；  
（三）在重要公共场所、监管场所或者国家重大节日、重大活动期间聚集滋事，公开进行邪教活动的；  
（四）邪教组织被取缔后，或者被认定为邪教组织后，仍然聚集滋事，公开进行邪教活动的；  
（五）国家工作人员从事邪教活动的；  
（六）向未成年人宣扬邪教的；  
（七）在学校或者其他教育培训机构宣扬邪教的。

**Article 8** : Where any of the acts specified in Articles 2 through 5 of this Interpretation are committed, and the following circumstances exist, a severe punishment shall be imposed:  
(1) Colluding with overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals to engage in cult activities;  
(2) Establishing a cult organization structure, recruiting members, or organizing cult activities across provinces, autonomous regions, or municipalities directly under the Central Government;  
(3) Gathering to create disturbances in important public places, supervision places, or during national major festivals or major events, and publicly conducting cult activities;  
(4) After a cult organization has been banned or determined to be a cult organization, still gathering to create disturbances and publicly conducting cult activities;  
(5) State functionaries engaging in cult activities;  
(6) Propagating cult teachings to minors;  
(7) Propagating cult teachings in schools or other educational training institutions.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　组织、利用邪教组织破坏国家法律、行政法规实施，符合[本解释第四条](#art-4)规定情形，但行为人能够真诚悔罪，明确表示退出邪教组织、不再从事邪教活动的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚。其中，行为人系受蒙蔽、胁迫参加邪教组织的，可以不作为犯罪处理。  
组织、利用邪教组织破坏国家法律、行政法规实施，行为人在一审判决前能够真诚悔罪，明确表示退出邪教组织、不再从事邪教活动的，分别依照下列规定处理：  
（一）符合[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定情形的，可以认定为[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款规定的“情节较轻”；  
（二）符合[本解释第三条](#art-3)规定情形的，可以不认定为[刑法第三百条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-300)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。

**Article 9** : Where an organization or use of a cult organization undermines the implementation of national laws and administrative regulations, and the circumstances conform to the provisions of Article 4 of this Interpretation, but the actor sincerely repents and clearly expresses withdrawal from the cult organization and cessation of cult activities, the actor may be exempted from prosecution or criminal punishment. Where the actor was deceived or coerced into joining the cult organization, the act may not be treated as a crime.  
Where an organization or use of a cult organization undermines the implementation of national laws and administrative regulations, and the actor sincerely repents and clearly expresses withdrawal from the cult organization and cessation of cult activities before the first-instance judgment, the matter shall be handled separately according to the following provisions:  
(1) If the circumstances conform to the provisions of Article 2 of this Interpretation, it may be determined as "relatively minor circumstances" under paragraph 1 of Article 300 of the Criminal Law;  
(2) If the circumstances conform to the provisions of Article 3 of this Interpretation, it may not be determined as "extremely serious circumstances" under paragraph 1 of Article 300 of the Criminal Law, and the actor shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条　组织、利用邪教组织破坏国家法律、行政法规实施过程中，又有煽动分裂国家、煽动颠覆国家政权或者侮辱、诽谤他人等犯罪行为的，依照数罪并罚的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 10** : Where, in the course of organizing or using a cult organization to undermine the implementation of national laws and administrative regulations, criminal acts such as inciting secession of the state, inciting subversion of state power, or insulting or defaming others are also committed, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条　组织、利用邪教组织，制造、散布迷信邪说，组织、策划、煽动、胁迫、教唆、帮助其成员或者他人实施自杀、自伤的，依照[刑法第二百三十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-232)、第二百三十四条的规定，以故意杀人罪或者故意伤害罪定罪处罚。

**Article 11** : Where an organization or use of a cult organization creates and disseminates superstitious and heretical teachings, and organizes, plans, incites, coerces, instigates, or assists its members or others to commit suicide or self-inflicted injury, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of intentional homicide or the crime of intentional injury in accordance with the provisions of Articles 232 and 234 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条　邪教组织人员以自焚、自爆或者其他危险方法危害公共安全的，依照[刑法第一百一十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-114)、第一百一十五条的规定，以放火罪、爆炸罪、以危险方法危害公共安全罪等定罪处罚。

**Article 12** : Where a member of a cult organization endangers public security by self-immolation, self-detonation, or other dangerous methods, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of arson, the crime of explosion, the crime of endangering public security by dangerous means, etc., in accordance with the provisions of Articles 114 and 115 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条　明知他人组织、利用邪教组织实施犯罪，而为其提供经费、场地、技术、工具、食宿、接送等便利条件或者帮助的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 13** : Whoever knowingly provides funding, venues, technology, tools, accommodation, transportation, or other facilitating conditions or assistance to another person who organizes or uses a cult organization to commit a crime shall be dealt with as a joint crime.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条　对于犯组织、利用邪教组织破坏法律实施罪、组织、利用邪教组织致人重伤、死亡罪，严重破坏社会秩序的犯罪分子，根据[刑法第五十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-56)的规定，可以附加剥夺政治权利。

**Article 14** : For a criminal who commits the crime of organizing or using a cult organization to undermine the implementation of laws, or the crime of organizing or using a cult organization to cause serious injury or death to a person, and seriously disrupts social order, deprivation of political rights may be imposed as a supplementary punishment in accordance with the provisions of Article 56 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条　对涉案物品是否属于邪教宣传品难以确定的，可以委托地市级以上公安机关出具认定意见。

**Article 15** : Where it is difficult to determine whether an item involved in a case is a cult propaganda material, a public security organ at or above the prefecture level may be commissioned to issue an opinion of identification.

<a id="art-16"></a>第十六条　本解释自2017年2月1日起施行。最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理组织和利用邪教组织犯罪案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔1999〕18号），最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理组织和利用邪教组织犯罪案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）》（法释〔2001〕19号），以及最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理组织和利用邪教组织犯罪案件具体应用法律若干问题的解答》（法发〔2002〕7号）同时废止。

**Article 16** : This Interpretation shall come into force on February 1, 2017. The Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Organizing and Utilizing Cults (Fa Shi [1999] No. 18), the Interpretation (II) of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Organizing and Utilizing Cults (Fa Shi [2001] No. 19), and the Answers of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Criminal Cases of Organizing and Utilizing Cults (Fa Fa [2002] No. 7) shall be abolished simultaneously.
