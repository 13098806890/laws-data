### 最高人民法院关于地方人民政府规定可向人民法院起诉的行政案件法院应否受理问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于地方人民政府规定可向人民法院起诉的  
  
行政案件法院应否受理问题的批复  
  
法（研）复〔1987〕40号  
  
广东省高级人民法院：  
  
你院（87）粤法经行字第215号《关于深圳特区人民政府规定可向法院起诉的经济行政案件法院应否受理的请示》收悉。经征求全国人民代表大会常务委员会法制工作委员会等部门的意见，现答复如下：  
  
按照民事诉讼法（试行）第三条第二款的规定，人民法院受理的行政案件，必须是法律规定由人民法院审理的行政案件。根据这一规定，凡是全国人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定的法律、国务院制定的行政法规、省和直辖市的人民代表大会及其常务委员会制定的地方性法规、民族自治地方的人民代表大会制定的自治条例和单行条例中规定向人民法院起诉的行政案件，如果当事人向人民法院起诉，人民法院应予受理。你院请示的问题不属于上述情况，不应受理。  
  
此复  
  
1987年10月9日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply on Whether the People's Court Shall Accept Administrative Cases That Local People's Governments Prescribe as Actionable in the People's Court  
Fa (Yan) Fu [1987] No. 40  
Higher People's Court of Guangdong Province:  
We have received your petition, (87) Yue Fa Jing Xing Zi No. 215, "Request for Instructions on Whether the People's Court Shall Accept Economic Administrative Cases That the Shenzhen Special Zone People's Government Prescribes as Actionable in the Court." After soliciting opinions from the Legislative Affairs Committee of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and other departments, we now reply as follows:  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 3, Paragraph 2 of the Civil Procedure Law (for Trial Implementation), the administrative cases accepted by the People's Court shall be those that are prescribed by law to be tried by the People's Court. Pursuant to this provision, for any administrative case that is prescribed as actionable in the People's Court by a law enacted by the National People's Congress or its Standing Committee, an administrative regulation formulated by the State Council, a local regulation formulated by the People's Congress or its Standing Committee of a province or municipality directly under the Central Government, or an autonomous regulation or separate regulation formulated by the People's Congress of a national autonomous area, if a party brings a prosecution in the People's Court, the People's Court shall accept the case. The issue raised in your petition does not fall within the above circumstances and shall not be accepted.  
Hereby reply.  
October 9, 1987
