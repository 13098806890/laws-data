# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理危害矿山生产安全刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕5号  
**公布日期**：2007-02-26  
**生效日期**：2007-03-01

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理危害矿山生产安全刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2007年2月26日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1419次会议、2007年2月27日由最高人民检察院第十届检察委员会第72次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年3月1日起施行。 2007年2月28日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理危害矿山生产安全刑事案件具体 应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2007〕5号 为依法惩治危害矿山生产安全犯罪，保障矿山生产安全，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  刑法第一百三十四条第一款规定的犯罪主体，包括对矿山生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员，以及直接从事矿山生产、作业的人员。 第二条  刑法第一百三十四条第二款规定的犯罪主体，包括对矿山生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员。 第三条  刑法第一百三十五条规定的“直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员”，是指对矿山安全生产设施或者安全生产条件不符合国家规定负有直接责任的矿山生产经营单位负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人,以及对安全生产设施或者安全生产条件负有管理、维护职责的电工、瓦斯检查工等人员。 第四条  发生矿山生产安全事故，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百三十四条、第一百三十五条规定的“重大伤亡事故或者其他严重后果”： （一）造成死亡一人以上，或者重伤三人以上的； （二）造成直接经济损失一百万元以上的； （三）造成其他严重后果的情形。 具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为刑法第一百三十四条、第一百三十五条规定的“情节特别恶劣”：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  [刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第一款规定的犯罪主体，包括对矿山生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员，以及直接从事矿山生产、作业的人员。

**Article 1** The subject of the crime prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law shall include persons such as responsible persons, management personnel, actual controllers, and investors who bear the duty of organization, command, or management over mine production and operations, as well as persons directly engaged in mine production and operations.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  [刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)第二款规定的犯罪主体，包括对矿山生产、作业负有组织、指挥或者管理职责的负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人等人员。

**Article 2** The subject of the crime prescribed in paragraph 2 of Article 134 of the Criminal Law shall include persons such as responsible persons, management personnel, actual controllers, and investors who bear the duty of organization, command, or management over mine production and operations.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  [刑法第一百三十五条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-135)规定的“直接负责的主管人员和其他直接责任人员”，是指对矿山安全生产设施或者安全生产条件不符合国家规定负有直接责任的矿山生产经营单位负责人、管理人员、实际控制人、投资人,以及对安全生产设施或者安全生产条件负有管理、维护职责的电工、瓦斯检查工等人员。

**Article 3** The “directly responsible supervisory personnel and other directly responsible personnel” as prescribed in Article 135 of the Criminal Law shall refer to the responsible persons, management personnel, actual controllers, and investors of mine production and operation units who bear direct responsibility for mine safety production facilities or safety production conditions not conforming to state provisions, as well as persons such as electricians and gas inspectors who bear the duty of management and maintenance of safety production facilities or safety production conditions.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  发生矿山生产安全事故，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)、第一百三十五条规定的“重大伤亡事故或者其他严重后果”：  
（一）造成死亡一人以上，或者重伤三人以上的；  
（二）造成直接经济损失一百万元以上的；  
（三）造成其他严重后果的情形。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)、第一百三十五条规定的“情节特别恶劣”：  
（一）造成死亡三人以上，或者重伤十人以上的；  
（二）造成直接经济损失三百万元以上的；  
（三）其他特别恶劣的情节。

**Article 4** Where a mine production safety accident occurs, if any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as a “major casualty accident or other serious consequences” as prescribed in Articles 134 and 135 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing the death of one or more persons, or causing serious injury to three or more persons;  
(2) causing direct economic losses of one million yuan or more;  
(3) causing other serious consequences.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as “particularly flagrant circumstances” as prescribed in Articles 134 and 135 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing the death of three or more persons, or causing serious injury to ten or more persons;  
(2) causing direct economic losses of three million yuan or more;  
(3) other particularly flagrant circumstances.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  [刑法第一百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-139)之一规定的“负有报告职责的人员”，是指矿山生产经营单位的负责人、实际控制人、负责生产经营管理的投资人以及其他负有报告职责的人员。

**Article 5** The “persons who bear the duty to report” as prescribed in Article 139a of the Criminal Law shall refer to the responsible persons, actual controllers, investors responsible for production and operation management, and other persons who bear the duty to report in mine production and operation units.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  在矿山生产安全事故发生后，负有报告职责的人员不报或者谎报事故情况，贻误事故抢救，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-139)之一规定的“情节严重”：  
（一）导致事故后果扩大，增加死亡一人以上，或者增加重伤三人以上，或者增加直接经济损失一百万元以上的；  
（二）实施下列行为之一，致使不能及时有效开展事故抢救的：  
1.决定不报、谎报事故情况或者指使、串通有关人员不报、谎报事故情况的；  
2.在事故抢救期间擅离职守或者逃匿的；  
3.伪造、破坏事故现场，或者转移、藏匿、毁灭遇难人员尸体，或者转移、藏匿受伤人员的；  
4.毁灭、伪造、隐匿与事故有关的图纸、记录、计算机数据等资料以及其他证据的；  
（三）其他严重的情节。  
具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第一百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-139)之一规定的“情节特别严重”：  
（一）导致事故后果扩大，增加死亡三人以上，或者增加重伤十人以上，或者增加直接经济损失三百万元以上的；  
（二）采用暴力、胁迫、命令等方式阻止他人报告事故情况导致事故后果扩大的；  
（三）其他特别严重的情节。

**Article 6** After the occurrence of a mine production safety accident, if a person who bears the duty to report fails to report or makes a false report on the accident situation, thereby delaying the rescue operations, and if any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as “serious circumstances” as prescribed in Article 139a of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing the consequences of the accident to expand, resulting in an increase of one or more deaths, or an increase of three or more persons with serious injuries, or an increase of one million yuan or more in direct economic losses;  
(2) committing any of the following acts, thereby rendering timely and effective rescue operations impossible:  
1. deciding not to report or to make a false report on the accident situation, or instructing or colluding with relevant persons not to report or to make a false report;  
2. abandoning post or fleeing during the rescue operations;  
3. forging or destroying the accident scene, or transferring, hiding, or destroying the bodies of the deceased, or transferring or hiding injured persons;  
4. destroying, forging, or concealing drawings, records, computer data, or other materials and evidence related to the accident;  
(3) other serious circumstances.  
If any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be deemed as “particularly serious circumstances” as prescribed in Article 139a of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing the consequences of the accident to expand, resulting in an increase of three or more deaths, or an increase of ten or more persons with serious injuries, or an increase of three million yuan or more in direct economic losses;  
(2) preventing others from reporting the accident situation by means of violence, coercion, orders, or the like, thereby causing the consequences of the accident to expand;  
(3) other particularly serious circumstances.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  在矿山生产安全事故发生后，实施[本解释第六条](#art-6)规定的相关行为，帮助负有报告职责的人员不报或者谎报事故情况，贻误事故抢救的，对组织者或者积极参加者，依照[刑法第一百三十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-139)之一的规定，以共犯论处。

**Article 7** After the occurrence of a mine production safety accident, if a person commits the relevant acts as prescribed in Article 6 of this Interpretation to assist a person who bears the duty to report in failing to report or making a false report on the accident situation, thereby delaying the rescue operations, the organizer or active participant shall be punished as an accomplice in accordance with the provisions of Article 139a of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  在采矿许可证被依法暂扣期间擅自开采的，视为[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)第一款规定的“未取得采矿许可证擅自采矿”。  
违反矿产资源法的规定，非法采矿或者采取破坏性的开采方法开采矿产资源，造成重大伤亡事故或者其他严重后果，同时构成[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)规定的犯罪和[刑法第一百三十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-134)或者第一百三十五条规定的犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。

**Article 8** If a person engages in mining without authorization during the period when the mining license is lawfully suspended, it shall be deemed as “mining without obtaining a mining license” as prescribed in paragraph 1 of Article 343 of the Criminal Law.  
If a person, in violation of the provisions of the Mineral Resources Law, illegally mines or exploits mineral resources by destructive mining methods, causing a major casualty accident or other serious consequences, and such act concurrently constitutes the crime prescribed in Article 343 of the Criminal Law and the crime prescribed in Article 134 or Article 135 of the Criminal Law, the person shall be punished in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，危害矿山生产安全，具有下列情形之一，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）对不符合矿山法定安全生产条件的事项予以批准或者验收通过的；  
（二）对于未依法取得批准、验收的矿山生产经营单位擅自从事生产经营活动不依法予以处理的；  
（三）对于已经依法取得批准的矿山生产经营单位不再具备安全生产条件而不撤销原批准或者发现违反安全生产法律法规的行为不予查处的；  
（四）强令审核、验收部门及其工作人员实施本条第(一)项行为，或者实施其他阻碍下级部门及其工作人员依法履行矿山安全生产监督管理职责行为的；  
（五）在矿山生产安全事故发生后，负有报告职责的国家机关工作人员不报或者谎报事故情况，贻误事故抢救的；  
（六）其他滥用职权或者玩忽职守的行为。

**Article 9** If a functionary of a state organ abuses his power or neglects his duty, thereby endangering mine production safety, and if any of the following circumstances exists, causing heavy losses to public property or the interests of the state and the people, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 397 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) approving or passing acceptance inspection for matters that do not meet the statutory safety production conditions for mines;  
(2) failing to deal with according to law the unauthorized production and operation activities of a mine production and operation unit that has not obtained approval or passed acceptance inspection according to law;  
(3) failing to revoke the original approval for a mine production and operation unit that has obtained approval according to law but no longer meets the safety production conditions, or failing to investigate and punish acts that violate safety production laws and regulations discovered;  
(4) coercing the examination and acceptance inspection departments and their staff members to commit the acts specified in subparagraph (1) of this Article, or committing other acts that obstruct subordinate departments and their staff members from performing their duties of mine production safety supervision and management according to law;  
(5) after the occurrence of a mine production safety accident, a functionary of a state organ who bears the duty to report fails to report or makes a false report on the accident situation, thereby delaying the rescue operations;  
(6) other acts of abuse of power or neglect of duty.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  以暴力、威胁方法阻碍矿山安全生产监督管理的，依照[刑法第二百七十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-277)的规定，以妨害公务罪定罪处罚。

**Article 10** If a person obstructs the supervision and management of mine production safety by means of violence or threat, he shall be convicted and punished for the crime of obstructing public service in accordance with the provisions of Article 277 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  国家工作人员违反规定投资入股矿山生产经营，构成本解释涉及的有关犯罪的，作为从重情节依法处罚。

**Article 11** If a state functionary, in violation of regulations, invests in or purchases shares in mine production and operation, and thereby constitutes the relevant crimes covered by this Interpretation, such fact shall be treated as a circumstance for heavier punishment according to law.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条  危害矿山生产安全构成犯罪的人，在矿山生产安全事故发生后，积极组织、参与事故抢救的，可以酌情从轻处罚。

**Article 12** If a person who commits a crime endangering mine production safety actively organizes or participates in the rescue operations after the occurrence of a mine production safety accident, he may be given a light punishment as appropriate.
