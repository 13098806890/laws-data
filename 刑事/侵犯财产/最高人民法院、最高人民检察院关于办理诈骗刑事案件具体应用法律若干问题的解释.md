# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理诈骗刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2011〕7号  
**公布日期**：2011-02-21  
**生效日期**：2011-04-08

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理诈骗刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2011年2月21日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1512次会议、2010年11月24日由最高人民检察院第十一届检察委员会第49次会议通过，现予公布，自2011年4月8日起施行。 2011年3月1日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理诈骗刑事案件具体应用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2011〕7号 为依法惩治诈骗犯罪活动，保护公私财产所有权，根据刑法、刑事诉讼法有关规定，结合司法实践的需要，现就办理诈骗刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  诈骗公私财物价值三千元至一万元以上、三万元至十万元以上、五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为刑法第二百六十六条规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以结合本地区经济社会发展状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，共同研究确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院备案。 第二条  诈骗公私财物达到本解释第一条规定的数额标准，具有下列情形之一的，可以依照刑法第二百六十六条的规定酌情从严惩处： （一）通过发送短信、拨打电话或者利用互联网、广播电视、报刊杂志等发布虚假信息，对不特定多数人实施诈骗的； （二）诈骗救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济、医疗款物的； （三）以赈灾募捐名义实施诈骗的； （四）诈骗残疾人、老年人或者丧失劳动能力人的财物的； （五）造成被害人自杀、精神失常或者其他严重后果的。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  诈骗公私财物价值三千元至一万元以上、三万元至十万元以上、五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以结合本地区经济社会发展状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，共同研究确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院备案。

**Article 1** Where the value of fraudulently obtained public or private property is between 3,000 yuan and 10,000 yuan, between 30,000 yuan and 100,000 yuan, or more than 500,000 yuan, it shall be respectively determined as 'relatively large amount', 'huge amount', or 'especially huge amount' as provided in Article 266 of the Criminal Law.  
The higher people's courts and people's procuratorates of each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, in light of the local economic and social development, jointly study and determine the specific amount standards for enforcement in their respective regions within the range of the amounts provided in the preceding paragraph, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate for recordation.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  诈骗公私财物达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的数额标准，具有下列情形之一的，可以依照[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)的规定酌情从严惩处：  
（一）通过发送短信、拨打电话或者利用互联网、广播电视、报刊杂志等发布虚假信息，对不特定多数人实施诈骗的；  
（二）诈骗救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济、医疗款物的；  
（三）以赈灾募捐名义实施诈骗的；  
（四）诈骗残疾人、老年人或者丧失劳动能力人的财物的；  
（五）造成被害人自杀、精神失常或者其他严重后果的。  
诈骗数额接近[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”的标准，并具有前款规定的情形之一或者属于诈骗集团首要分子的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)规定的“其他严重情节”、“其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 2** Where the value of fraudulently obtained public or private property reaches the amount standards provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation, and any of the following circumstances exists, the offender may be given a heavier punishment at discretion in accordance with the provisions of Article 266 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) committing fraud against an unspecified majority by sending text messages, making phone calls, or publishing false information through the internet, radio, television, newspapers, magazines, etc.;  
(2) fraudulently obtaining materials allocated for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for entitled groups, poverty alleviation, resettlement, relief, or medical treatment;  
(3) committing fraud under the pretext of disaster relief fundraising;  
(4) fraudulently obtaining property from disabled persons, elderly persons, or persons who have lost the ability to work;  
(5) causing the victim to commit suicide, suffer from mental disorders, or other serious consequences.  
Where the amount of fraud is close to the standards of 'huge amount' or 'especially huge amount' provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation, and any of the circumstances provided in the preceding paragraph exists or the offender is a principal offender of a fraud group, it shall be respectively determined as 'other serious circumstances' or 'other especially serious circumstances' as provided in Article 266 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  诈骗公私财物虽已达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额较大”的标准，但具有下列情形之一，且行为人认罪、悔罪的，可以根据[刑法第三十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-37)、[刑事诉讼法第一百四十二条](../../诉讼与司法程序/刑事诉讼/中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法.md#art-142)的规定不起诉或者免予刑事处罚：  
（一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的；  
（二）一审宣判前全部退赃、退赔的；  
（三）没有参与分赃或者获赃较少且不是主犯的；  
（四）被害人谅解的；  
（五）其他情节轻微、危害不大的。

**Article 3** Where the value of fraudulently obtained public or private property has reached the standard of 'relatively large amount' provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation, but any of the following circumstances exists and the actor confesses guilt and shows repentance, a decision may be made not to prosecute or to grant exemption from criminal punishment in accordance with the provisions of Article 37 of the Criminal Law and Article 142 of the Criminal Procedure Law:  
(1) having statutory circumstances for lenient punishment;  
(2) having fully returned the stolen goods or made restitution before the pronouncement of the judgment at the first instance;  
(3) not having participated in the distribution of the spoils or having obtained little spoils and not being a principal offender;  
(4) obtaining forgiveness from the victim;  
(5) other minor circumstances that cause little harm.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  诈骗近亲属的财物，近亲属谅解的，一般可不按犯罪处理。  
诈骗近亲属的财物，确有追究刑事责任必要的，具体处理也应酌情从宽。

**Article 4** Where the property of a close relative is fraudulently obtained, and the close relative grants forgiveness, generally it may not be dealt with as a crime.  
Where it is truly necessary to investigate criminal liability for fraudulently obtaining property of a close relative, the specific handling shall also be lenient at discretion.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  诈骗未遂，以数额巨大的财物为诈骗目标的，或者具有其他严重情节的，应当定罪处罚。  
利用发送短信、拨打电话、互联网等电信技术手段对不特定多数人实施诈骗，诈骗数额难以查证，但具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)规定的“其他严重情节”，以诈骗罪（未遂）定罪处罚：  
（一）发送诈骗信息五千条以上的；  
（二）拨打诈骗电话五百人次以上的；  
（三）诈骗手段恶劣、危害严重的。  
实施前款规定行为，数量达到前款第（一）、（二）项规定标准十倍以上的，或者诈骗手段特别恶劣、危害特别严重的，应当认定为[刑法第二百六十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-266)规定的“其他特别严重情节”，以诈骗罪（未遂）定罪处罚。

**Article 5** An attempted fraud, where the target property is of a huge amount, or where there are other serious circumstances, shall be convicted and punished.  
Where fraud is committed against an unspecified majority by means of telecommunication technologies such as sending text messages, making phone calls, or the internet, and the amount of fraud is difficult to verify, but any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be determined as 'other serious circumstances' as provided in Article 266 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fraud (attempted):  
(1) having sent more than 5,000 fraudulent messages;  
(2) having made more than 500 fraudulent phone calls;  
(3) the fraudulent means being vile and the harm being serious.  
Where the conduct specified in the preceding paragraph is carried out, and the quantity reaches ten times or more of the standards provided in items (1) and (2) of the preceding paragraph, or the fraudulent means are especially vile and the harm is especially serious, it shall be determined as 'other especially serious circumstances' as provided in Article 266 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of fraud (attempted).

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  诈骗既有既遂，又有未遂，分别达到不同量刑幅度的，依照处罚较重的规定处罚；达到同一量刑幅度的，以诈骗罪既遂处罚。

**Article 6** Where fraud includes both completed and attempted acts, and each reaches a different sentencing range, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment; where they reach the same sentencing range, the offender shall be punished for the completed fraud.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  明知他人实施诈骗犯罪，为其提供信用卡、手机卡、通讯工具、通讯传输通道、网络技术支持、费用结算等帮助的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Whoever knowingly provides assistance such as credit cards, SIM cards, communication tools, communication transmission channels, internet technical support, or expense settlement to another person committing the crime of fraud shall be treated as a joint offender.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  冒充国家机关工作人员进行诈骗，同时构成诈骗罪和招摇撞骗罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Whoever impersonates a functionary of a state organ to commit fraud, and such conduct concurrently constitutes the crime of fraud and the crime of impersonating a functionary to swindle, shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条  案发后查封、扣押、冻结在案的诈骗财物及其孳息，权属明确的，应当发还被害人；权属不明确的，可按被骗款物占查封、扣押、冻结在案的财物及其孳息总额的比例发还被害人，但已获退赔的应予扣除。

**Article 9** Where after the case is discovered, the fraudulently obtained property and its fruits are sealed up, seized, or frozen, and the ownership is clear, they shall be returned to the victim; where the ownership is unclear, they may be returned to the victim in proportion to the amount of the defrauded property as a share of the total amount of the sealed-up, seized, or frozen property and its fruits, but the amount already recovered through restitution shall be deducted.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条  行为人已将诈骗财物用于清偿债务或者转让给他人，具有下列情形之一的，应当依法追缴：  
（一）对方明知是诈骗财物而收取的；  
（二）对方无偿取得诈骗财物的；  
（三）对方以明显低于市场的价格取得诈骗财物的；  
（四）对方取得诈骗财物系源于非法债务或者违法犯罪活动的。  
他人善意取得诈骗财物的，不予追缴。

**Article 10** Where the actor has used the fraudulently obtained property to repay a debt or transferred it to another person, and any of the following circumstances exists, it shall be recovered according to law:  
(1) the receiving party knowingly accepts the fraudulently obtained property;  
(2) the receiving party obtains the fraudulently obtained property without payment;  
(3) the receiving party obtains the fraudulently obtained property at a price obviously lower than the market price;  
(4) the receiving party's acquisition of the fraudulently obtained property is derived from an illegal debt or an illegal or criminal act.  
Where another person acquires the fraudulently obtained property in good faith, it shall not be recovered.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条  以前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 11** Where any judicial interpretations previously issued are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
