# 最高人民法院关于对贪污、受贿、挪用公款犯罪分子依法正确适用缓刑的若干规定

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1996-06-26

---

### 正文

一、国家工作人员贪污、受贿数额在二千元以上不满一万元，犯罪情节较轻，能主动坦白，积极退赃，确有悔改表现的，可以适用缓刑。

**Article None** I. Where a state functionary engages in embezzlement or bribery in an amount of not less than 2,000 yuan but less than 10,000 yuan, and the circumstances of the crime are relatively minor, and if the offender voluntarily confesses, actively returns the illicit gains, and demonstrates genuine repentance, probation may be applied.

二、国家工作人员贪污、受贿一万元以上，除具有投案自首或者立功表现等法定减轻情节的之外，一般不适用缓刑。  
国家工作人员贪污、受贿数额一万元以上不满五万元，根据案件具体情况，适用刑法第五十九条第二款减轻处罚在有期徒刑三年以下量刑的，一般不适用缓刑。对其中犯罪情节较轻，积极退赃的，且在重大生产、科研项目中起关键性作用，有特殊需要，或者有其他特殊情况的，可以适用缓刑，但必须从严掌握。

**Article None** II. Where a state functionary engages in embezzlement or bribery in an amount of 10,000 yuan or more, probation shall generally not be applied, except where statutory mitigating circumstances such as voluntary surrender or meritorious service exist.  
Where a state functionary engages in embezzlement or bribery in an amount of not less than 10,000 yuan but less than 50,000 yuan, and based on the specific circumstances of the case, a mitigated punishment is imposed under Article 59, Paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law, resulting in a sentence of fixed-term imprisonment of less than three years, probation shall generally not be applied. However, for those whose crime circumstances are relatively minor, who actively return the illicit gains, and who play a crucial role in major production or scientific research projects, or have special needs, or have other special circumstances, probation may be applied, but such application must be strictly controlled.

三、对下列贪污、受贿、挪用公款犯罪分子不适用缓刑：  
（一）犯罪行为使国家、集体和人民利益遭受重大损失的；  
（二）没有退赃，无悔改表现的；  
（三）犯罪动机、手段等情节恶劣，或者将赃款用于投机倒把、走私、赌博等非法活动的；  
（四）属于共同犯罪中情节严重的主犯，或者犯有数罪的；  
（五）曾因经济违法犯罪行为受过行政处分或刑事处罚的；  
（六）犯罪涉及的财物属于国家救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、救济款项和物资，情节严重的。  
1996年6月26日

**Article None** III. Probation shall not be applied to the following criminals who commit embezzlement, bribery, or misappropriation of public funds:  
(1) whose criminal acts cause significant losses to the state, the collective, or the interests of the people;  
(2) who fail to return the illicit gains and show no signs of repentance;  
(3) whose criminal motives, means, or other circumstances are flagrant, or who use the illicit funds for illegal activities such as speculation, smuggling, or gambling;  
(4) who are principals in joint crimes with serious circumstances, or who commit multiple crimes;  
(5) who have previously received administrative sanctions or criminal penalties for economic illegal or criminal acts;  
(6) where the property involved in the crime consists of state funds or materials for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care, or relief, and the circumstances are serious.  
June 26, 1996
