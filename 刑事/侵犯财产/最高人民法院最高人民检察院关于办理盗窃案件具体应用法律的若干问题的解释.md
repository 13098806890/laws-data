# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理盗窃案件具体应用法律的若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：发〔1992〕43号  
**公布日期**：1992-12-11

> 7、外币，按照被盗当日国家外汇管理局公布的外汇卖出价计算。 8、不属于馆藏三级以上的一般文物（包括古玩、古书画等），按照国有文物商店的一般零售价计算，或者按国家文物主管部门核定的价格计算。 （二）有价支付凭证、有价证券、有价票证，按下列方法计算： 1、不记名、不挂失的有价支付凭证、有价证券、有价票证，不论能否随即兑现，均按票面数额和案发时应得的利息、奖金或者奖品等一并计算。股票应按照被盗当日证券交易所公布的该种股票成交的平均价格计算。 2、记名的有价支付凭证、有价证券、有价票证，如果是票面价值已定并能随即兑现的，如活期存折、已到期的定期存折和已填上金额的支票，以及不需证明手续即可提取货物的提货单等，应按票面数额（有利息的应包括案发时应得的利息）或者可提货物的价值计算。如果是票面价值未定，但能随即兑现的（如已盖好印章的空白支票等），则以实际兑现的财物价值计算。 不能随即兑现的记名有价支付凭证、有价证券、有价票证，或者将能随即兑现的有价支付凭证、有价证券、有价票证销毁或丢弃，而失主可以通过挂失、补领、补办手续等方式避免实际损失的，不按票面数额计算，可作为量刑考虑的情节。 （三）同种类的大宗被盗物品，失主以多种价格购进，能够分清的，应分别计算；难以区分的，可按此类物品的中等价格计算。 （四）被盗物品在被盗后损坏的，仍按物品被盗时的原值计算。 被盗物品已被销赃、挥霍，无法追缴或者已被丢弃、毁灭的，或者几经转手，最初形态被破坏的，一般应当根据被害人、证人的陈述、证言和提供的有效凭证以及犯罪分子本人的供述，按照本条第（一）项规定的核价原则，确定原被盗物品价值。 （五）对已陈旧、残损或者使用过的被盗物品，应结合作案当时、当地同类物品的价格和被盗时的残旧程度，由有关部门作价。 （六）残次品，按主管部门核定的价格计算；废品，按物资回收利用部门的收购价格计算；假货、劣货，按假、劣货的实际价值计算。 （七）失主以明显低于被盗当时、当地市场零售价购进的物品，应按照本条第（一）项规定的核价原则计算。 （八）盗窃后的销赃数额，作为量刑情节考虑，但销赃数额高于按本解释计算的盗窃数额的，则盗窃数额应按销赃数额计算。 （九）盗窃违禁品，如毒品、淫秽物品等，按盗窃罪处理的，不计数额，根据情节轻重量刑。 （十）被盗物品价格不明或者难以确定的，应委托有关主管部门或者有关专业人员核定。受委托对被盗物品负责核价的单位，核价后应出具鉴定结论，加盖作价部门的印章，并且由作价人或者估价人签名或者盖章。

### 正文

四、如何看待盗窃案件的情节？  
（一）盗窃数额是构成盗窃罪的重要标准，但不是定罪量刑的唯一标准。除根据盗窃财物数额外，还应当根据犯罪的其他具体情节和犯罪分子的认罪态度、退赃表现等，进行全面分析，正确定罪量刑。  
个人盗窃公私财物虽未达到“数额较大”的起点标准，但具有下列情节之一的，也可追究其刑事责任：  
1、多次扒窃作案的；  
2、以破坏性手段盗窃并造成公私财产损失严重的；  
3、入户盗窃多次的；  
4、教唆未成年人盗窃的；  
5、劳改、劳教人员在劳改、劳教期间盗窃的；  
6、在缓刑、假释考验期限内或者管制、监外执行期间盗窃的；  
7、曾因盗窃被治安处罚三次以上或者被劳动教养二次以上，二年内又进行盗窃的；  
8、曾因盗窃被免诉、免刑后二年内，或者因盗窃受过刑罚处罚后三年内又进行盗窃的；  
9、盗窃盲、聋、哑等残疾人、孤老或者丧失劳动能力的人的财物的；  
10、因盗窃造成严重后果或者具有其他恶劣情节的。  
个人盗窃公私财物虽已达到“数额较大”的起点标准，但具有下列情节之一的，可不作为犯罪处理：  
1、初犯、偶犯、已满十六岁不满十八岁的未成年人作案情节轻微的；  
2、情节轻微并主动坦白或者积极退赔的；  
3、自首或者有立功表现的；  
4、被胁迫参加盗窃活动，没有分赃或者获赃甚微的；  
5、盗窃未遂情节轻微的；  
6、其他情节显著轻微危害不大的。  
（二）在共同盗窃犯罪中，各共犯基于共同的故意，实施了共同的犯罪行为，应对共同盗窃犯罪行为所造成的危害后果负责。  
1、对盗窃集团的首要分子，应按照集团盗窃的总数额依法处罚；  
2、对其他共同盗窃犯罪中的主犯，应按照参与共同盗窃的总数额依法处罚；  
3、对共同盗窃犯罪中的从犯，应按照参与共同盗窃的总数额适用刑法。数额较大的，适用刑法第一百五十一条；数额巨大的，适用刑法第一百五十二条。具体量刑时，应根据犯罪分子在共同盗窃中的地位、作用和非法所得数额等情节，依照刑法第二十四条第二款的规定，比照主犯从轻、减轻处罚或者免除处罚。  
4、共同盗窃犯罪后，犯罪分子具有自首、立功、未成年等法定从轻、减轻或者免除处罚情节的，可以或者应当依法从轻、减轻处罚或者免除处罚；具有坦白或者积极退赃等情节的，也可以酌情从轻处罚。

**Article None** IV. How to assess the circumstances of a theft case?  
(A) The amount stolen is an important criterion for establishing the crime of theft, but it is not the sole criterion for conviction and sentencing. In addition to the amount of property stolen, a comprehensive analysis shall be conducted based on other specific circumstances of the crime, the offender's attitude toward confession, and the performance in returning stolen goods, so as to correctly convict and sentence.  
Where an individual steals public or private property that does not meet the threshold of “relatively large amount,” but has one of the following circumstances, criminal liability may also be pursued:  
1. Repeatedly committing pickpocketing;  
2. Stealing by destructive means, causing serious loss to public or private property;  
3. Repeatedly committing burglary;  
4. Instigating a minor to commit theft;  
5. A person undergoing reeducation through labor or reform through labor commits theft during the period of such labor;  
6. Committing theft during the probation period for probation or parole, or during the period of public surveillance or serving a sentence outside prison;  
7. Having been subject to public security punishment for theft three times or more, or having been subjected to reeducation through labor twice or more, and committing theft again within two years;  
8. Having been exempted from prosecution or punishment for theft, and committing theft again within two years; or having been criminally punished for theft, and committing theft again within three years;  
9. Stealing property of blind, deaf, mute or other disabled persons, solitary elderly persons, or persons who have lost the ability to work;  
10. Causing serious consequences or having other vile circumstances due to theft.  
Where an individual steals public or private property that has reached the threshold of “relatively large amount,” but has one of the following circumstances, it may not be treated as a crime:  
1. First offense, occasional offense, or a minor between 16 and 18 years old with minor circumstances in the act;  
2. Minor circumstances with voluntary confession or active restitution;  
3. Voluntary surrender or meritorious service;  
4. Participating in theft under coercion, with no share of the stolen goods or receiving a very small share;  
5. Attempted theft with minor circumstances;  
6. Other circumstances where the circumstances are clearly minor and the harm is not significant.  
(B) In joint theft crimes, each co-offender, based on a common intent and having committed a common criminal act, shall be liable for the harmful consequences caused by the common theft.  
1. The principal ringleader of a theft group shall be punished according to law on the basis of the total amount stolen by the group;  
2. Other principal offenders in joint theft crimes shall be punished according to law on the basis of the total amount they participated in stealing;  
3. Accessories in joint theft crimes shall be subject to the Criminal Law on the basis of the total amount they participated in stealing. If the amount is relatively large, Article 151 of the Criminal Law shall apply; if the amount is huge, Article 152 of the Criminal Law shall apply. In specific sentencing, the offender's status, role, and the amount of illegal gains in the joint theft shall be considered, and in accordance with Article 24, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law, a lighter, mitigated, or exempted punishment shall be imposed compared with the principal offender.  
4. After a joint theft crime, if the offender has statutory mitigating circumstances such as voluntary surrender, meritorious service, or being a minor, a lighter, mitigated, or exempted punishment may or shall be imposed according to law; if the offender has circumstances such as confession or active restitution of stolen goods, a lighter punishment may also be imposed at discretion.

五、如何认定惯窃罪？  
（一）惯窃罪，是指盗窃已成习性，并以盗窃所得为其挥霍或者生活的主要来源的犯罪行为。  
惯窃罪犯应同时具有盗窃恶习深、连续作案时间长、犯罪次数多、盗窃数额较大等基本特征。  
（二）具体认定惯窃罪，要以前项规定的基本特征为前提，结合考虑行为人是否因盗窃罪被处罚过和其他情节。因盗窃罪被判过有期徒刑，又犯盗窃罪，符合累犯条件的，按累犯从重处罚；对于曾因盗窃罪或者惯窃罪被判过刑，时隔三年以上，偶尔又犯盗窃罪的，不应按惯犯或者累犯处理。

**Article None** V. How to determine the crime of habitual theft?  
(A) The crime of habitual theft refers to a criminal act where a person has developed a habit of theft and uses the proceeds of theft as the main source of his or her squandering or livelihood.  
A habitual thief shall simultaneously possess the basic characteristics of a deep-rooted habit of theft, a long duration of continuous criminal acts, a high frequency of criminal acts, and a relatively large amount stolen.  
(B) In specifically determining the crime of habitual theft, the basic characteristics stipulated in the preceding paragraph shall be taken as the premise, and consideration shall be given to whether the actor has been previously punished for theft and other circumstances. If a person has been sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment for theft and commits theft again, meeting the conditions for recidivism, the person shall be given a severe punishment as a recidivist. If a person has been previously sentenced for theft or habitual theft and, after an interval of more than three years, occasionally commits theft again, the person shall not be treated as a habitual offender or recidivist.

六、如何认定盗窃案件的“情节特别严重”以及如何适用刑罚？  
（一）具有以下情形之一的，属于盗窃犯罪“情节特别严重”：1、盗窃财物数额特别巨大；2、盗窃数额接近特别巨大并具有其他特别严重的情节。  
“其他特别严重的情节”，一般是指，盗窃集团的首要分子或者共同盗窃犯罪中情节严重的主犯；盗窃银行金库、国家珍贵文物、救灾、救济款物、重要军用物资的；盗窃他人急需的生产资料，严重妨害生产建设或者造成其他严重损失的；盗窃他人生活、医疗急需款物，造成严重后果的；因已挥霍等原因，未能退赃，造成失主重大损失的；破坏性盗窃，后果严重的；引起被害人死亡、精神失常或者其他严重后果的；累犯、惯犯或者多次流窜作案危害严重的，等等。  
（二）盗窃公私财物数额特别巨大的，或者盗窃数额接近特别巨大并具有其他特别严重情节的，依法判处十年以上有期徒刑或者无期徒刑，可以并处没收财产。盗窃公私财物数额特别巨大，同时具有其他特别严重情节的，依法判处无期徒刑或者死刑，可以并处没收财产。

**Article None** VI. How to determine “particularly serious circumstances” in theft cases and how to apply penalties?  
(A) Where one of the following circumstances exists, it shall be considered as “particularly serious circumstances” of theft: 1. The amount stolen is especially huge; 2. The amount stolen is close to especially huge and there are other particularly serious circumstances.  
“Other particularly serious circumstances” generally refer to: the principal ringleader of a theft group or the principal offender with serious circumstances in a joint theft crime; stealing from a bank vault, valuable national cultural relics, disaster relief funds or materials, relief funds or materials, or important military supplies; stealing means of production urgently needed by others, seriously impeding production and construction or causing other serious losses; stealing funds or materials urgently needed for the livelihood or medical treatment of others, causing serious consequences; failing to return stolen goods due to squandering or other reasons, causing significant losses to the victim; destructive theft with serious consequences; causing the death, mental disorder, or other serious consequences of the victim; recidivism, habitual theft, or multiple itinerant crimes causing serious harm, etc.  
(B) Where the amount of public or private property stolen is especially huge, or the amount is close to especially huge and there are other particularly serious circumstances, the offender shall, according to law, be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than ten years or life imprisonment, and may also be sentenced to confiscation of property. Where the amount of public or private property stolen is especially huge and there are other particularly serious circumstances at the same time, the offender shall, according to law, be sentenced to life imprisonment or the death penalty, and may also be sentenced to confiscation of property.

七、如何区分盗窃罪与其他犯罪的界限？  
（一）盗窃通讯设备价值数额不大，但危害公共安全已构成破坏通讯设备罪的，或者盗窃通讯设备造成严重后果的，依照刑法第一百一十一条的规定定罪处罚。盗窃通讯设备情节特别严重，罪该判处无期徒刑或者死刑的，依照刑法第一百五十二条或者《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于严惩严重破坏经济的罪犯的决定》第一条第（一）项的规定，以盗窃罪处罚。  
（二）盗掘具有历史、艺术、科学价值的古文化遗址、古墓葬的，依照《全国人民代表大会常务委员会关于惩治盗掘古文化遗址古墓葬犯罪的补充规定》处理。盗掘其他墓葬，窃取财物数额较大的，以盗窃罪论处。盗掘其他墓葬情节严重，即使未盗得财物或者窃取了少量财物的，也应以盗窃罪论处。情节显著轻微危害不大的，不以犯罪论处。  
（三）对偷开汽车的，以非法占有为目的，变卖或者留用的，应定为盗窃罪；为进行其他犯罪活动，偷开汽车当犯罪工具使用的，可以按其实施的犯罪从重处罚；在偷开汽车中因过失撞死、撞伤他人或者撞坏了车辆，又构成其他罪的，应按交通肇事罪与他罪并罚；为游乐，多次偷开汽车，并将汽车遗弃，严重扰乱工作、生产秩序，造成严重损失的，可以按扰乱社会秩序罪论处；为游乐，偶尔偷开汽车，情节轻微，可以不认为是犯罪，应当责令赔偿损失。  
（四）实施盗窃犯罪，造成公私财物严重毁损的，以盗窃罪从重处罚；同时构成其他犯罪的，择一重罪从重处罚；为掩盖盗窃罪行或者出于报复等动机故意破坏公私财物的，应以盗窃罪和其他罪实行并罚。

**Article None** VII. How to distinguish the crime of theft from other crimes?  
(A) Where the value of stolen communication equipment is not large but it endangers public safety and constitutes the crime of damaging communication equipment, or where stealing communication equipment causes serious consequences, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 111 of the Criminal Law. Where the circumstances of stealing communication equipment are particularly serious, and the crime warrants a sentence of life imprisonment or the death penalty, the offender shall be punished for the crime of theft in accordance with Article 152 of the Criminal Law or Article 1, paragraph (1) of the Decision of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress Regarding the Severe Punishment of Criminals Who Seriously Undermine the Economy.  
(B) Where a person illegally excavates an ancient cultural site or ancient tomb of historical, artistic, or scientific value, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the Supplementary Provisions of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress Regarding the Punishment of the Crime of Illegally Excavating Ancient Cultural Sites and Ancient Tombs. Where a person illegally excavates other tombs and steals property of a relatively large amount, the person shall be convicted and punished for the crime of theft. Where a person illegally excavates other tombs with serious circumstances, even if no property is stolen or only a small amount of property is stolen, the person shall still be convicted and punished for the crime of theft. Where the circumstances are clearly minor and the harm is not significant, the person shall not be treated as a crime.  
(C) Where a person steals a car with the purpose of illegal possession, selling it or keeping it for personal use, the person shall be convicted of the crime of theft. Where a person steals a car for use as a tool for other criminal activities, the person may be given a heavier punishment for the crime actually committed. Where, in the course of stealing a car, a person negligently causes death or injury to another person or damages the vehicle, and this also constitutes another crime, the person shall be punished for the crime of causing a traffic accident and concurrently for the other crime. Where a person, for amusement, repeatedly steals cars and abandons them, seriously disrupting work or production order and causing serious losses, the person may be convicted of the crime of disrupting social order. Where a person occasionally steals a car for amusement with minor circumstances, the act may not be considered a crime, and the person shall be ordered to compensate for the losses.  
(D) Where a person commits a theft crime causing serious damage to public or private property, the person shall be given a heavier punishment for the crime of theft. Where the act also constitutes another crime, the heavier crime shall be selected and given a heavier punishment. Where, for the purpose of concealing the theft or out of revenge or other motives, a person intentionally damages public or private property, the person shall be punished concurrently for the crime of theft and the other crime(s).

八、如何认定窝赃、销赃罪？  
窝赃、销赃罪，是指明知是犯罪所得的赃物而予以窝藏或者代为销售的行为。  
（一）认定窝赃、销赃罪的“明知”，不能仅凭被告人的口供，应当根据案件的客观事实予以分析。只要证明被告人知道或者应当知道是犯罪所得的赃物而予以窝藏或者代为销售的，就可以认定。  
（二）窝藏，既包括提供藏匿赃物的场所，也包括为罪犯转移赃物；代为销售，既包括把赃物卖给他人，也包括以低价买进、高价卖出的行为。买赃自用，情节严重的，也应按销赃罪定罪处罚。  
（三）与盗窃犯罪分子事前通谋，事后对赃物予以窝藏或者代为销售或者收买的，应以盗窃共犯论处。

**Article None** VIII. How to determine the crime of concealing stolen goods or selling stolen goods?  
The crime of concealing stolen goods or selling stolen goods refers to the act of knowingly concealing or selling on behalf of another person goods that are known to be proceeds of crime.  
(A) In determining “knowingly” for the crime of concealing or selling stolen goods, the defendant's confession alone shall not be relied upon; instead, analysis shall be conducted based on the objective facts of the case. As long as it is proved that the defendant knew or should have known that the goods were proceeds of crime and concealed them or sold them on behalf of another person, the determination may be made.  
(B) “Concealing” includes both providing a place to hide the stolen goods and transporting the stolen goods for the offender. “Selling on behalf of another person” includes both selling the stolen goods to others and buying them at a low price and selling them at a high price. Purchasing stolen goods for personal use, if the circumstances are serious, shall also be convicted and punished as the crime of selling stolen goods.  
(C) Where a person conspires with a theft offender beforehand and thereafter conceals, sells on behalf of, or purchases the stolen goods, the person shall be treated as an accomplice in the theft.

九、如何执行本解释？  
（一）本解释自发布之日起执行。本解释发布前已处理的案件，不适用本解释；尚未处理或者正在处理的案件，一律适用本解释。  
（二）本解释发布前有关办理盗窃案件的司法解释，与本解释重复或者抵触的，以本解释为准。

**Article None** IX. How to implement this Interpretation?  
(A) This Interpretation shall come into force as of the date of its issuance. Cases already disposed of before the issuance of this Interpretation shall not be subject to this Interpretation; cases that have not yet been disposed of or are currently being handled shall all be subject to this Interpretation.  
(B) Any judicial interpretations on handling theft cases issued before the issuance of this Interpretation that are inconsistent with or contradictory to this Interpretation shall be superseded by this Interpretation.
