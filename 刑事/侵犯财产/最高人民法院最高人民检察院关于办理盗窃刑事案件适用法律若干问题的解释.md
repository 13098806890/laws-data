# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理盗窃刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕8号  
**公布日期**：2013-03-08  
**生效日期**：2013-04-04

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理盗窃刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年3月8日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1571次会议、2013年3月18日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第1次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年4月4日起施行。 2013年4月2日 最高人民法院　最高人民检察院 关于办理盗窃刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕8号 为依法惩治盗窃犯罪活动，保护公私财产，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理盗窃刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   盗窃公私财物价值一千元至三千元以上、三万元至十万元以上、三十万元至五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为刑法第二百六十四条规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。 在跨地区运行的公共交通工具上盗窃，盗窃地点无法查证的，盗窃数额是否达到“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”，应当根据受理案件所在地省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院确定的有关数额标准认定。 盗窃毒品等违禁品，应当按照盗窃罪处理的，根据情节轻重量刑。 第二条   盗窃公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准可以按照前条规定标准的百分之五十确定： （一）曾因盗窃受过刑事处罚的； （二）一年内曾因盗窃受过行政处罚的； （三）组织、控制未成年人盗窃的；

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   盗窃公私财物价值一千元至三千元以上、三万元至十万元以上、三十万元至五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。  
在跨地区运行的公共交通工具上盗窃，盗窃地点无法查证的，盗窃数额是否达到“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”，应当根据受理案件所在地省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院确定的有关数额标准认定。  
盗窃毒品等违禁品，应当按照盗窃罪处理的，根据情节轻重量刑。

**Article 1** : Where the value of stolen public or private property is from 1,000 yuan to over 3,000 yuan, from 30,000 yuan to over 100,000 yuan, or from 300,000 yuan to over 500,000 yuan, it shall be respectively deemed as "relatively large amount", "huge amount", or "extraordinarily huge amount" as provided in Article 264 of the Criminal Law.  
  
The Higher People's Courts and People's Procuratorates of each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, based on the economic development of their respective regions and taking into account public security conditions, determine the specific numerical standards to be enforced within the numerical range provided in the preceding paragraph, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate for approval.  
  
Where theft is committed on a public transportation vehicle operating across regions and the location of the theft cannot be ascertained, whether the amount of theft reaches the "relatively large amount", "huge amount", or "extraordinarily huge amount" shall be determined in accordance with the relevant numerical standards established by the Higher People's Court and People's Procuratorate of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government where the case is accepted.  
  
Where the theft involves narcotic drugs or other prohibited articles and shall be handled as the crime of theft, the sentence shall be imposed according to the gravity of the circumstances.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   盗窃公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准可以按照前条规定标准的百分之五十确定：  
（一）曾因盗窃受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）一年内曾因盗窃受过行政处罚的；  
（三）组织、控制未成年人盗窃的；  
（四）自然灾害、事故灾害、社会安全事件等突发事件期间，在事件发生地盗窃的；  
（五）盗窃残疾人、孤寡老人、丧失劳动能力人的财物的；  
（六）在医院盗窃病人或者其亲友财物的；  
（七）盗窃救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济款物的；  
（八）因盗窃造成严重后果的。

**Article 2** : Where theft of public or private property falls under any of the following circumstances, the standard for "relatively large amount" may be determined at fifty percent of the standard provided in the preceding article:  
  
(1) Having previously been subjected to criminal punishment for theft;  
(2) Having been subjected to an administrative penalty for theft within the previous year;  
(3) Organizing or controlling minors to commit theft;  
(4) Committing theft in a place where an emergency such as a natural disaster, accident disaster, or social security incident occurs, during the period of such incident;  
(5) Committing theft of property of a disabled person, an elderly person living alone, or a person who has lost the ability to work;  
(6) Committing theft of property of patients or their relatives and friends in a hospital;  
(7) Committing theft of materials or funds for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for entitled groups, poverty alleviation, resettlement, or relief;  
(8) Committing theft that causes serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   二年内盗窃三次以上的，应当认定为“多次盗窃”。  
非法进入供他人家庭生活，与外界相对隔离的住所盗窃的，应当认定为“入户盗窃”。  
携带枪支、爆炸物、管制刀具等国家禁止个人携带的器械盗窃，或者为了实施违法犯罪携带其他足以危害他人人身安全的器械盗窃的，应当认定为“携带凶器盗窃”。  
在公共场所或者公共交通工具上盗窃他人随身携带的财物的，应当认定为“扒窃”。

**Article 3** : Committing theft three or more times within two years shall be deemed as "multiple thefts".  
  
Illegally entering a residence that is used for family life and relatively isolated from the outside to commit theft shall be deemed as "burglary".  
  
Carrying firearms, explosives, controlled knives, or other instruments that the state prohibits individuals from carrying to commit theft, or carrying other instruments sufficient to endanger another person's personal safety for the purpose of committing an illegal or criminal act to commit theft, shall be deemed as "theft with a deadly weapon".  
  
Committing theft of property carried by another person on their person in a public place or on a public transportation vehicle shall be deemed as "pickpocketing".

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   盗窃的数额，按照下列方法认定：  
（一）被盗财物有有效价格证明的，根据有效价格证明认定；无有效价格证明，或者根据价格证明认定盗窃数额明显不合理的，应当按照有关规定委托估价机构估价；  
（二）盗窃外币的，按照盗窃时中国外汇交易中心或者中国人民银行授权机构公布的人民币对该货币的中间价折合成人民币计算；中国外汇交易中心或者中国人民银行授权机构未公布汇率中间价的外币，按照盗窃时境内银行人民币对该货币的中间价折算成人民币，或者该货币在境内银行、国际外汇市场对美元汇率，与人民币对美元汇率中间价进行套算；  
（三）盗窃电力、燃气、自来水等财物，盗窃数量能够查实的，按照查实的数量计算盗窃数额；盗窃数量无法查实的，以盗窃前六个月月均正常用量减去盗窃后计量仪表显示的月均用量推算盗窃数额；盗窃前正常使用不足六个月的，按照正常使用期间的月均用量减去盗窃后计量仪表显示的月均用量推算盗窃数额；  
（四）明知是盗接他人通信线路、复制他人电信码号的电信设备、设施而使用的，按照合法用户为其支付的费用认定盗窃数额；无法直接确认的，以合法用户的电信设备、设施被盗接、复制后的月缴费额减去被盗接、复制前六个月的月均电话费推算盗窃数额；合法用户使用电信设备、设施不足六个月的，按照实际使用的月均电话费推算盗窃数额；  
（五）盗接他人通信线路、复制他人电信码号出售的，按照销赃数额认定盗窃数额。  
盗窃行为给失主造成的损失大于盗窃数额的，损失数额可以作为量刑情节考虑。

**Article 4** : The amount of theft shall be determined in accordance with the following methods:  
  
(1) Where the stolen property has a valid price certificate, the amount shall be determined based on the valid price certificate; if there is no valid price certificate, or if the amount determined according to the price certificate is obviously unreasonable, an appraisal institution shall be entrusted to appraise the property in accordance with the relevant provisions;  
  
(2) Where foreign currency is stolen, the amount shall be calculated by converting the foreign currency into Renminbi at the mid-point price of the Renminbi against that currency published by the China Foreign Exchange Trade System or an institution authorized by the People's Bank of China at the time of the theft; for foreign currency for which neither the China Foreign Exchange Trade System nor an institution authorized by the People's Bank of China publishes a mid-point exchange rate, the conversion shall be made at the mid-point price of the Renminbi against that currency published by domestic banks at the time of the theft, or by cross-rate calculation using the exchange rate of that currency against the US dollar in domestic banks or the international foreign exchange market and the mid-point exchange rate of the Renminbi against the US dollar;  
  
(3) Where property such as electricity, gas, or tap water is stolen, if the quantity stolen can be ascertained, the theft amount shall be calculated based on the ascertained quantity; if the quantity stolen cannot be ascertained, the theft amount shall be estimated by subtracting the average monthly consumption shown on the meter after the theft from the average monthly normal consumption during the six months prior to the theft; if normal use prior to the theft was less than six months, the theft amount shall be estimated by subtracting the average monthly consumption shown on the meter after the theft from the average monthly consumption during the period of normal use;  
  
(4) Where telecommunication equipment or facilities that have been illegally connected to another person's communication line or that replicate another person's telecommunication number are knowingly used, the theft amount shall be determined based on the fees paid by the legitimate user; if direct confirmation is not possible, the theft amount shall be estimated by subtracting the average monthly telephone charges for the six months prior to the illegal connection or replication from the monthly telephone charges after the illegal connection or replication; if the legitimate user has used the telecommunication equipment or facilities for less than six months, the theft amount shall be estimated based on the average monthly telephone charges during the actual period of use;  
  
(5) Where a communication line is illegally connected or a telecommunication number is replicated for sale, the theft amount shall be determined based on the amount of the stolen goods sold.  
  
If the loss caused to the victim by the theft is greater than the amount of the theft, the amount of the loss may be considered as a circumstance for sentencing.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   盗窃有价支付凭证、有价证券、有价票证的，按照下列方法认定盗窃数额：  
（一）盗窃不记名、不挂失的有价支付凭证、有价证券、有价票证的，应当按票面数额和盗窃时应得的孳息、奖金或者奖品等可得收益一并计算盗窃数额；  
（二）盗窃记名的有价支付凭证、有价证券、有价票证，已经兑现的，按照兑现部分的财物价值计算盗窃数额；没有兑现，但失主无法通过挂失、补领、补办手续等方式避免损失的，按照给失主造成的实际损失计算盗窃数额。

**Article 5** : Where the stolen items are valuable payment instruments, valuable securities, or valuable tickets, the theft amount shall be determined in accordance with the following methods:  
  
(1) If the stolen valuable payment instruments, valuable securities, or valuable tickets are not registered or not reportable for loss, the theft amount shall be calculated based on the face value plus any interest, bonuses, prizes, or other proceeds obtainable at the time of the theft;  
  
(2) If the stolen registered valuable payment instruments, valuable securities, or valuable tickets have been cashed, the theft amount shall be calculated based on the value of the property that has been cashed; if they have not been cashed, but the victim cannot avoid the loss through procedures such as reporting the loss, reissuing, or re-applying, the theft amount shall be calculated based on the actual loss caused to the victim.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   盗窃公私财物，具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第三项至第八项规定情形之一，或者入户盗窃、携带凶器盗窃，数额达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”百分之五十的，可以分别认定为[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)规定的“其他严重情节”或者“其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 6** : Where the theft of public or private property falls under any of the circumstances provided in Items 3 to 8 of Article 2 of this Interpretation, or constitutes burglary or theft with a deadly weapon, and the amount reaches fifty percent of the "huge amount" or "extraordinarily huge amount" provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation, such circumstances may be respectively deemed as "other serious circumstances" or "other particularly serious circumstances" as provided in Article 264 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   盗窃公私财物数额较大，行为人认罪、悔罪，退赃、退赔，且具有下列情形之一，情节轻微的，可以不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；必要时，由有关部门予以行政处罚：  
（一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的；  
（二）没有参与分赃或者获赃较少且不是主犯的；  
（三）被害人谅解的；  
（四）其他情节轻微、危害不大的。

**Article 7** : Where the amount of theft of public or private property is relatively large, the offender confesses guilt, shows remorse, returns the stolen property and makes compensation, and falls under any of the following circumstances, if the circumstances are minor, the offender may be exempted from prosecution or from criminal punishment; where necessary, the relevant departments shall impose an administrative penalty:  
  
(1) Having statutory circumstances for lenient punishment;  
(2) Not having participated in the division of stolen goods or having received a relatively small amount of stolen goods and not being a principal offender;  
(3) Having obtained forgiveness from the victim;  
(4) Other circumstances where the circumstances are minor and the harm is not significant.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   偷拿家庭成员或者近亲属的财物，获得谅解的，一般可以不认为是犯罪；追究刑事责任的，应当酌情从宽。

**Article 8** : Where an offender steals property from a family member or close relative and obtains forgiveness, it may generally not be deemed a crime; if criminal liability is investigated, a lenient punishment shall be given as appropriate.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   盗窃国有馆藏一般文物、三级文物、二级以上文物的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。  
盗窃多件不同等级国有馆藏文物的，三件同级文物可以视为一件高一级文物。  
盗窃民间收藏的文物的，根据[本解释第四条](#art-4)第一款第一项的规定认定盗窃数额。

**Article 9** : Where the stolen property is ordinary cultural relics, third-grade cultural relics, or second-grade or above cultural relics in state-owned collections, such theft shall be respectively deemed as "relatively large amount", "huge amount", or "extraordinarily huge amount" as provided in Article 264 of the Criminal Law.  
  
Where multiple cultural relics of different grades in state-owned collections are stolen, three cultural relics of the same grade may be deemed as one cultural relic of a higher grade.  
  
Where the stolen property is cultural relics in private collections, the theft amount shall be determined in accordance with Item 1 of the first paragraph of Article 4 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-10"></a>第十条   偷开他人机动车的，按照下列规定处理：  
（一）偷开机动车，导致车辆丢失的，以盗窃罪定罪处罚；  
（二）为盗窃其他财物，偷开机动车作为犯罪工具使用后非法占有车辆，或者将车辆遗弃导致丢失的，被盗车辆的价值计入盗窃数额；  
（三）为实施其他犯罪，偷开机动车作为犯罪工具使用后非法占有车辆，或者将车辆遗弃导致丢失的，以盗窃罪和其他犯罪数罪并罚；将车辆送回未造成丢失的，按照其所实施的其他犯罪从重处罚。

**Article 10** : Where an offender takes another person's motor vehicle without permission, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
  
(1) If taking the motor vehicle without permission causes the vehicle to be lost, the offender shall be convicted and punished for the crime of theft;  
(2) If the motor vehicle is taken without permission for the purpose of stealing other property, used as a tool of crime, and then unlawfully possessed by the offender, or if the vehicle is abandoned causing it to be lost, the value of the stolen vehicle shall be included in the theft amount;  
(3) If the motor vehicle is taken without permission for the purpose of committing another crime, used as a tool of crime, and then unlawfully possessed by the offender, or if the vehicle is abandoned causing it to be lost, the offender shall be punished for the crime of theft and the other crime in a combined punishment for multiple crimes; if the vehicle is returned without causing loss, the offender shall be given a heavier punishment for the other crime committed.

<a id="art-11"></a>第十一条   盗窃公私财物并造成财物损毁的，按照下列规定处理：  
（一）采用破坏性手段盗窃公私财物，造成其他财物损毁的，以盗窃罪从重处罚；同时构成盗窃罪和其他犯罪的，择一重罪从重处罚；  
（二）实施盗窃犯罪后，为掩盖罪行或者报复等，故意毁坏其他财物构成犯罪的，以盗窃罪和构成的其他犯罪数罪并罚；  
（三）盗窃行为未构成犯罪，但损毁财物构成其他犯罪的，以其他犯罪定罪处罚。

**Article 11** : Where public or private property is stolen and damage is caused to property, the matter shall be handled in accordance with the following provisions:  
  
(1) If destructive means are used to steal public or private property, causing damage to other property, the offender shall be given a heavier punishment for the crime of theft; if the act simultaneously constitutes the crime of theft and another crime, the offender shall be punished for the offense carrying the heavier penalty and given a heavier punishment;  
(2) If, after committing the crime of theft, the offender intentionally destroys other property to cover up the crime or for retaliation, and such destruction constitutes a crime, the offender shall be punished for the crime of theft and the other crime committed in a combined punishment for multiple crimes;  
(3) If the act of theft does not constitute a crime, but the destruction of property constitutes another crime, the offender shall be convicted and punished for that other crime.

<a id="art-12"></a>第十二条   盗窃未遂，具有下列情形之一的，应当依法追究刑事责任：  
（一）以数额巨大的财物为盗窃目标的；  
（二）以珍贵文物为盗窃目标的；  
（三）其他情节严重的情形。  
盗窃既有既遂，又有未遂，分别达到不同量刑幅度的，依照处罚较重的规定处罚；达到同一量刑幅度的，以盗窃罪既遂处罚。

**Article 12** : Where an attempted theft falls under any of the following circumstances, criminal liability shall be investigated according to law:  
  
(1) The target of the theft is property of a huge amount;  
(2) The target of the theft is precious cultural relics;  
(3) Other circumstances where the circumstances are serious.  
  
Where there are both completed and attempted acts of theft, and they respectively reach different sentencing ranges, the punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provision that imposes a heavier penalty; if they reach the same sentencing range, the offender shall be punished for the completed crime of theft.

<a id="art-13"></a>第十三条   单位组织、指使盗窃，符合[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)及本解释有关规定的，以盗窃罪追究组织者、指使者、直接实施者的刑事责任。

**Article 13** : Where a unit organizes or instigates theft, and the act complies with the relevant provisions of Article 264 of the Criminal Law and this Interpretation, the organizer, instigator, and direct perpetrator shall be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of theft.

<a id="art-14"></a>第十四条   因犯盗窃罪，依法判处罚金刑的，应当在一千元以上盗窃数额的二倍以下判处罚金；没有盗窃数额或者盗窃数额无法计算的，应当在一千元以上十万元以下判处罚金。

**Article 14** : Where a fine is imposed for the crime of theft according to law, the fine shall be not less than 1,000 yuan and not more than twice the amount of the theft; if there is no theft amount or the theft amount cannot be calculated, the fine shall be not less than 1,000 yuan and not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-15"></a>第十五条   本解释发布实施后，最高人民法院《关于审理盗窃案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔1998〕4号）同时废止；之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 15** : After this Interpretation is promulgated and implemented, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Theft Cases (Fa Shi [1998] No. 4) shall be abolished simultaneously; if any judicial interpretation or normative document issued previously is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
