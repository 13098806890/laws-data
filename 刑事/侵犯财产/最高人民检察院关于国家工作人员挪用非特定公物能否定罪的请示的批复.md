最高人民检察院关于国家工作人员  
挪用非特定公物能否定罪的请示的批复  
（2000年3月6日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会  
第五十七次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔2000〕1号  
山东省人民检察院：  
你院鲁检发研字〔1999〕第3号《关于国家工作人员挪用非特定公物能否定罪的请示》收悉。经研究认为，刑法第384条规定的挪用公款罪中未包括挪用非特定公物归个人使用的行为，对该行为不以挪用公款罪论处。如构成其他犯罪的，依照刑法的相关规定定罪处罚。  
此复。  
最高人民检察院  
2000年3月15日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
March 15, 2000  
Reply of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on the Request for Instructions as to Whether State Functionaries May Be Convicted for Misappropriating Non-Specific Public Property  
(Adopted at the 57th Meeting of the Ninth Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on March 6, 2000)  
SPP Interpretation Release No. 1 [2000]  
People's Procuratorate of Shandong Province:  
We have received your Request for Instructions on Whether State Functionaries May Be Convicted for Misappropriating Non-Specific Public Property (Lu Jian Fa Yan Zi [1999] No. 3). Upon examination, it is held that the crime of misappropriating public funds as provided for in Article 384 of the Criminal Law does not include the act of misappropriating non-specific public property for personal use, and such act shall not be punished as the crime of misappropriating public funds. If it constitutes any other crime, conviction and punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Criminal Law.  
This is hereby replied.  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
March 15, 2000
