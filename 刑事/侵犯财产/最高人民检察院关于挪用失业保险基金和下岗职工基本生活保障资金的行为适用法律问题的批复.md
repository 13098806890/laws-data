# 最高人民检察院关于挪用失业保险基金和下岗职工基本生活保障资金的行为适用法律问题的批复

2003年1月28日  
最高人民检察院关于挪用失业保险基金和  
下岗职工基本生活保障资金的行为  
适用法律问题的批复  
（2003年1月13日最高人民检察院第九届检察委员会  
第一百一十八次会议通过）  
高检发释字〔2003〕1号  
辽宁省人民检察院：  
你院辽检发研字〔2002〕9号《关于挪用职工失业保险金和下岗职工生活保障金是否属于挪用特定款物的请示》收悉。经研究，批复如下：  
挪用失业保险基金和下岗职工基本生活保障资金属于挪用救济款物。挪用失业保险基金和下岗职工基本生活保障资金，情节严重，致使国家和人民群众利益遭受重大损害的，对直接责任人员，应当依照[刑法第二百七十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-273)的规定，以挪用特定款物罪追究刑事责任；国家工作人员利用职务上的便利，挪用失业保险基金和下岗职工基本生活保障资金归个人使用，构成犯罪的，应当依照[刑法第三百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-384)的规定，以挪用公款罪追究刑事责任。  
此复。  
最高人民检察院  
2003年1月28日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Procuratorate  
January 28, 2003  
Reply of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on Issues concerning the Application of Law to the Acts of Misappropriating Unemployment Insurance Funds and Basic Living Security Funds for Laid-off Workers  
(Adopted at the 118th Session of the Ninth Procuratorial Committee of the Supreme People's Procuratorate on January 13, 2003)  
Gao Jian Fa Shi Zi [2003] No. 1  
The People's Procuratorate of Liaoning Province:  
Your Petition, Liao Jian Fa Yan Zi [2002] No. 9, titled "Request for Instructions on Whether Misappropriating Unemployment Insurance Funds for Workers and Living Security Funds for Laid-off Workers Constitutes Misappropriation of Specific Goods and Materials," has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
Misappropriating unemployment insurance funds and basic living security funds for laid-off workers shall constitute misappropriation of relief goods and materials. Where the misappropriation of unemployment insurance funds and basic living security funds for laid-off workers is of a serious nature and causes significant damage to the interests of the state and the people, the person directly responsible shall, in accordance with Article 273 of the Criminal Law, be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of misappropriating specific goods and materials; where a state functionary, taking advantage of his position, misappropriates unemployment insurance funds and basic living security funds for laid-off workers for personal use, which constitutes a crime, he shall, in accordance with Article 384 of the Criminal Law, be investigated for criminal liability for the crime of misappropriating public funds.  
This is a reply.  
Supreme People's Procuratorate  
January 28, 2003
