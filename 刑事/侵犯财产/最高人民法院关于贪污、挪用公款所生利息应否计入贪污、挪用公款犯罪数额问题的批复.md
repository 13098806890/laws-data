### 最高人民法院关于贪污、挪用公款所生利息应否计入贪污、挪用公款犯罪数额问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于贪污、挪用公款所生利息应否计入  
  
贪污、挪用公款犯罪数额问题的批复  
  
法复〔1993〕11号  
  
四川省高级人民法院：  
  
你院川高法明传〔93〕112号《关于贪污挪用银行库存款其所生利息是否计入贪污挪用公款犯罪数额的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
贪污、挪用公款（包括银行库存款）后至案发前，被贪污、挪用的公款所生利息，不应作为贪污、挪用公款的犯罪数额计算。但该利息是贪污、挪用公款行为给被害单位造成实际经济损失的一部分，应作为被告人的非法所得，连同其贪污、挪用的公款一并依法追缴。  
  
1993年12月15日

**Article 1** Supreme People's Court  
Reply Concerning Whether the Interest Accrued from Embezzlement or Misappropriation of Public Funds Shall Be Included  
in the Amount of Crime of Embezzlement or Misappropriation of Public Funds  
Fa Fu [1993] No. 11  
Higher People's Court of Sichuan Province:  
We have received your High Court's Chuan Gao Fa Ming Chuan [93] No. 112 "Request for Instructions on Whether the Interest Accrued from Embezzlement or Misappropriation of Bank Deposits Shall Be Included in the Amount of Crime of Embezzlement or Misappropriation of Public Funds". Upon study, we reply as follows:  
The interest accrued from the embezzled or misappropriated public funds (including bank deposits) after the act of embezzlement or misappropriation and before the case is discovered shall not be calculated as part of the amount of crime of embezzlement or misappropriation of public funds. However, such interest constitutes part of the actual economic losses caused to the victimized unit by the act of embezzlement or misappropriation, and shall be recovered according to law together with the embezzled or misappropriated public funds as the illegal gains of the defendant.  
December 15, 1993
