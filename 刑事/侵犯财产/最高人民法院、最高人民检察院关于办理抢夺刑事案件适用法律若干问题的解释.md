### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　抢夺公私财物价值一千元至三千元以上、三万元至八万元以上、二十万元至四十万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-267)规定的“数额较大”“数额巨大”“数额特别巨大”。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。

**Article 1** Where the value of public or private property forcibly seized reaches or exceeds 1,000 yuan but less than 3,000 yuan, or reaches or exceeds 30,000 yuan but less than 80,000 yuan, or reaches or exceeds 200,000 yuan but less than 400,000 yuan, such amounts shall be respectively determined as "relatively large amount", "large amount", and "especially large amount" as provided in Article 267 of the Criminal Law.  
The Higher People's Courts and People's Procuratorates of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the economic development of their respective regions and taking into account the social security situation, determine the specific amount standards for enforcement within the amount range as provided in the preceding paragraph, and shall report to the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate for approval.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　抢夺公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准按照前条规定标准的百分之五十确定：  
（一）曾因抢劫、抢夺或者聚众哄抢受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）一年内曾因抢夺或者哄抢受过行政处罚的；  
（三）一年内抢夺三次以上的；  
（四）驾驶机动车、非机动车抢夺的；  
（五）组织、控制未成年人抢夺的；  
（六）抢夺老年人、未成年人、孕妇、携带婴幼儿的人、残疾人、丧失劳动能力人的财物的；  
（七）在医院抢夺病人或者其亲友财物的；  
（八）抢夺救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济款物的；  
（九）自然灾害、事故灾害、社会安全事件等突发事件期间，在事件发生地抢夺的；  
（十）导致他人轻伤或者精神失常等严重后果的。

**Article 2** Where a person forcibly seizes public or private property under any of the following circumstances, the standard for "relatively large amount" shall be determined at fifty percent of the standard provided in the preceding article:  
(1) having previously been subject to criminal punishment for robbery, forcible seizure, or mass seizure;  
(2) having been subject to administrative penalty for forcible seizure or mass seizure within the past year;  
(3) having committed forcible seizure three times or more within the past year;  
(4) committing forcible seizure by driving a motor vehicle or non-motor vehicle;  
(5) organizing or controlling minors to commit forcible seizure;  
(6) forcibly seizing property of elderly persons, minors, pregnant women, persons carrying infants, disabled persons, or persons losing the ability to work;  
(7) forcibly seizing property of patients or their relatives and friends in a hospital;  
(8) forcibly seizing relief funds or materials for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for disabled servicemen and family of revolutionary martyrs, poverty alleviation, resettlement of migrants, or social relief;  
(9) committing forcible seizure during emergencies such as natural disasters, accident disasters, or social security incidents at the place of the incident;  
(10) causing serious consequences such as minor injury or mental disorder to another person.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　抢夺公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-267)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）导致他人重伤的；  
（二）导致他人自杀的；  
（三）具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第三项至第十项规定的情形之一，数额达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额巨大”百分之五十的。

**Article 3** Where a person forcibly seizes public or private property under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "other serious circumstances" as provided in Article 267 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing serious injury to another person;  
(2) causing another person to commit suicide;  
(3) having any of the circumstances provided in subparagraphs (3) to (10) of Article 2 of this Interpretation, and the amount reaches fifty percent of the "large amount" provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　抢夺公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-267)规定的“其他特别严重情节”：  
（一）导致他人死亡的；  
（二）具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第三项至第十项规定的情形之一，数额达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额特别巨大”百分之五十的。

**Article 4** Where a person forcibly seizes public or private property under any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "other especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 267 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) causing the death of another person;  
(2) having any of the circumstances provided in subparagraphs (3) to (10) of Article 2 of this Interpretation, and the amount reaches fifty percent of the "especially large amount" provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　抢夺公私财物数额较大，但未造成他人轻伤以上伤害，行为人系初犯，认罪、悔罪，退赃、退赔，且具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；必要时，由有关部门依法予以行政处罚：  
（一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的；  
（二）没有参与分赃或者获赃较少，且不是主犯的；  
（三）被害人谅解的；  
（四）其他情节轻微、危害不大的。

**Article 5** Where the amount of public or private property forcibly seized is relatively large but does not cause injury of light injury or above to another person, the perpetrator is a first-time offender, pleads guilty, shows repentance, returns the stolen property or makes restitution, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the offense may be deemed as minor in nature, and a decision not to prosecute or exemption from criminal punishment may be imposed; where necessary, the relevant department shall impose an administrative penalty according to law:  
(1) having statutory circumstances for lenient punishment;  
(2) not having participated in dividing the stolen property or having obtained only a small amount of stolen property, and not being a principal offender;  
(3) having obtained forgiveness from the victim;  
(4) other circumstances where the offense is minor and the harm is not significant.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　驾驶机动车、非机动车夺取他人财物，具有下列情形之一的，应当以抢劫罪定罪处罚：  
（一）夺取他人财物时因被害人不放手而强行夺取的；  
（二）驾驶车辆逼挤、撞击或者强行逼倒他人夺取财物的；  
（三）明知会致人伤亡仍然强行夺取并放任造成财物持有人轻伤以上后果的。

**Article 6** Where a person drives a motor vehicle or non-motor vehicle to snatch property from another person under any of the following circumstances, the offense shall be determined and punished as the crime of robbery:  
(1) forcibly taking the property when the victim does not let go;  
(2) driving a vehicle to squeeze, ram, or forcefully knock down another person to take the property;  
(3) knowing that it would cause injury or death, still forcibly taking the property and being indifferent to causing consequences of minor injury or above to the property holder.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　本解释公布施行后，《最高人民法院关于审理抢夺刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2002〕18号）同时废止；之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 7** After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Forcible Seizure (Fa Shi [2002] No. 18) shall be abolished simultaneously; where any previously issued judicial interpretation or normative document is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
