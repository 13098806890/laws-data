# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理敲诈勒索刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕10号  
**公布日期**：2013-04-15  
**生效日期**：2013-04-27

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理敲诈勒索刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年4月15日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1575次会议、2013年4月1日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第2次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年4月27日起施行。 2013年4月23日 最高人民法院　最高人民检察院 关于办理敲诈勒索刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕10号 为依法惩治敲诈勒索犯罪，保护公私财产权利，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理敲诈勒索刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   敲诈勒索公私财物价值二千元至五千元以上、三万元至十万元以上、三十万元至五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为刑法第二百七十四条规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况和社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，共同研究确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。 第二条   敲诈勒索公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准可以按照本解释第一条规定标准的百分之五十确定： （一）曾因敲诈勒索受过刑事处罚的； （二）一年内曾因敲诈勒索受过行政处罚的； （三）对未成年人、残疾人、老年人或者丧失劳动能力人敲诈勒索的； （四）以将要实施放火、爆炸等危害公共安全犯罪或者故意杀人、绑架等严重侵犯公民人身权利犯罪相威胁敲诈勒索的； （五）以黑恶势力名义敲诈勒索的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   敲诈勒索公私财物价值二千元至五千元以上、三万元至十万元以上、三十万元至五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百七十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-274)规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况和社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，共同研究确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。

**Article 1** Extortion of public or private property valued at 2,000 yuan to 5,000 yuan or more, 30,000 yuan to 100,000 yuan or more, or 300,000 yuan to 500,000 yuan or more shall be respectively determined as "a relatively large amount", "a large amount", and "an especially large amount" as provided in Article 274 of the Criminal Law.  
The higher people's courts and people's procuratorates of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, based on the local economic development and public security conditions, jointly determine the specific amount standards for local implementation within the range provided in the preceding paragraph, and shall report to the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate for approval.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   敲诈勒索公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准可以按照[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定标准的百分之五十确定：  
（一）曾因敲诈勒索受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）一年内曾因敲诈勒索受过行政处罚的；  
（三）对未成年人、残疾人、老年人或者丧失劳动能力人敲诈勒索的；  
（四）以将要实施放火、爆炸等危害公共安全犯罪或者故意杀人、绑架等严重侵犯公民人身权利犯罪相威胁敲诈勒索的；  
（五）以黑恶势力名义敲诈勒索的；  
（六）利用或者冒充国家机关工作人员、军人、新闻工作者等特殊身份敲诈勒索的；  
（七）造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 2** Where extortion of public or private property is committed under any of the following circumstances, the standard for "a relatively large amount" may be determined at 50% of the standard provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation:  
(1) having been subject to criminal punishment for extortion;  
(2) having been subject to an administrative penalty for extortion within one year;  
(3) extorting minors, disabled persons, elderly persons, or persons who have lost the ability to work;  
(4) extorting by threatening to commit crimes endangering public security such as arson or explosion, or serious crimes infringing upon citizens' personal rights such as intentional homicide or kidnapping;  
(5) extorting in the name of mafia-like organizations;  
(6) extorting by using or impersonating special identities such as state functionaries, military personnel, or journalists;  
(7) causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   二年内敲诈勒索三次以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-274)规定的“多次敲诈勒索”。

**Article 3** Extortion committed three or more times within two years shall be determined as "multiple extortions" as provided in Article 274 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   敲诈勒索公私财物，具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第三项至第七项规定的情形之一，数额达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”百分之八十的，可以分别认定为[刑法第二百七十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-274)规定的“其他严重情节”、“其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 4** Where extortion of public or private property is committed under any of the circumstances provided in items (3) through (7) of Article 2 of this Interpretation, and the amount reaches 80% of the "large amount" or "especially large amount" provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation, such circumstances may be respectively determined as "other serious circumstances" or "other especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 274 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   敲诈勒索数额较大，行为人认罪、悔罪，退赃、退赔，并具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚，由有关部门依法予以行政处罚：  
（一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的；  
（二）没有参与分赃或者获赃较少且不是主犯的；  
（三）被害人谅解的；  
（四）其他情节轻微、危害不大的。

**Article 5** Where the amount extorted is relatively large, the actor confesses guilt and shows repentance, returns the illicit gains and makes restitution, and if any of the following circumstances exist, the offense may be determined as minor in nature, and no prosecution shall be initiated or the actor shall be exempted from criminal punishment, and an administrative penalty shall be imposed by the relevant authority according to law:  
(1) having statutory circumstances for lenient punishment;  
(2) not having participated in sharing the illicit proceeds or having obtained little illicit proceeds and not being a principal offender;  
(3) having obtained forgiveness from the victim;  
(4) other circumstances where the offense is minor and causes little harm.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   敲诈勒索近亲属的财物，获得谅解的，一般不认为是犯罪；认定为犯罪的，应当酌情从宽处理。  
被害人对敲诈勒索的发生存在过错的，根据被害人过错程度和案件其他情况，可以对行为人酌情从宽处理；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不认为是犯罪。

**Article 6** Extortion of property from close relatives, where forgiveness is obtained, shall generally not be deemed a crime; if it is deemed a crime, a lenient punishment shall be imposed as appropriate.  
Where the victim is at fault for the occurrence of the extortion, the actor may be given a lenient punishment as appropriate based on the degree of the victim's fault and other circumstances of the case; where the circumstances are obviously minor and cause little harm, it shall not be deemed a crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   明知他人实施敲诈勒索犯罪，为其提供信用卡、手机卡、通讯工具、通讯传输通道、网络技术支持等帮助的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 7** Whoever knowingly provides assistance such as credit cards, SIM cards, communication tools, communication transmission channels, or network technical support to another person committing the crime of extortion shall be deemed a joint offender.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条   对犯敲诈勒索罪的被告人，应当在二千元以上、敲诈勒索数额的二倍以下判处罚金；被告人没有获得财物的，应当在二千元以上十万元以下判处罚金。

**Article 8** A defendant convicted of the crime of extortion shall be sentenced to a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than twice the amount extorted; where the defendant has not obtained any property, a fine of not less than 2,000 yuan but not more than 100,000 yuan shall be imposed.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条   本解释公布施行后，最高人民法院《关于敲诈勒索罪数额认定标准问题的规定》（法释〔2000〕11号）同时废止；此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 9** After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, the Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Standards for Determining the Amount of Extortion (Fa Shi [2000] No. 11) shall be abolished simultaneously; where any judicial interpretation previously issued is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
