# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理敲诈勒索刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕10号  
**公布日期**：2013-04-23  
**生效日期**：2013-04-27

> （2013年4月15日最高人民法院审判委员会第1575次会议、2013年4月1日最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第2次会议通过　2013年4月23日最高人民法院、最高人民检察院公告公布　自2013年4月27日起施行） 为依法惩治敲诈勒索犯罪，保护公私财产权利，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》、《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的有关规定，现就办理敲诈勒索刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条　敲诈勒索公私财物价值二千元至五千元以上、三万元至十万元以上、三十万元至五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为刑法第二百七十四条规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况和社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，共同研究确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。 第二条　敲诈勒索公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准可以按照本解释第一条规定标准的百分之五十确定： （一）曾因敲诈勒索受过刑事处罚的； （二）一年内曾因敲诈勒索受过行政处罚的； （三）对未成年人、残疾人、老年人或者丧失劳动能力人敲诈勒索的； （四）以将要实施放火、爆炸等危害公共安全犯罪或者故意杀人、绑架等严重侵犯公民人身权利犯罪相威胁敲诈勒索的； （五）以黑恶势力名义敲诈勒索的； （六）利用或者冒充国家机关工作人员、军人、新闻工作者等特殊身份敲诈勒索的； （七）造成其他严重后果的。 第三条　二年内敲诈勒索三次以上的，应当认定为刑法第二百七十四条规定的“多次敲诈勒索”。 第四条　敲诈勒索公私财物，具有本解释第二条第三项至第七项规定的情形之一，数额达到本解释第一条规定的“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”百分之八十的，可以分别认定为刑法第二百七十四条规定的“其他严重情节”、“其他特别严重情节”。 第五条　敲诈勒索数额较大，行为人认罪、悔罪，退赃、退赔，并具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚，由有关部门依法予以行政处罚：

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条　敲诈勒索公私财物价值二千元至五千元以上、三万元至十万元以上、三十万元至五十万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百七十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-274)规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况和社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，共同研究确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。

**Article 1** : Where the value of public or private property extorted is from 2,000 yuan to 5,000 yuan or more, from 30,000 yuan to 100,000 yuan or more, or from 300,000 yuan to 500,000 yuan or more, such shall be respectively determined as "relatively large amount", "large amount", and "extremely large amount" as provided in Article 274 of the Criminal Law.  
The Higher People's Courts and People's Procuratorates of each province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the Central Government may, based on the local economic development and public security situation, jointly study and determine the specific amount standards for enforcement within the scope of the amounts provided in the preceding paragraph, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate for approval.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条　敲诈勒索公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准可以按照[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定标准的百分之五十确定：  
（一）曾因敲诈勒索受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）一年内曾因敲诈勒索受过行政处罚的；  
（三）对未成年人、残疾人、老年人或者丧失劳动能力人敲诈勒索的；  
（四）以将要实施放火、爆炸等危害公共安全犯罪或者故意杀人、绑架等严重侵犯公民人身权利犯罪相威胁敲诈勒索的；  
（五）以黑恶势力名义敲诈勒索的；  
（六）利用或者冒充国家机关工作人员、军人、新闻工作者等特殊身份敲诈勒索的；  
（七）造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 2** : Where extortion of public or private property falls under any of the following circumstances, the standard for "relatively large amount" may be determined at 50% of the standard provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation:  
(1) Having previously received criminal punishment for extortion;  
(2) Having received administrative penalty for extortion within the past year;  
(3) Extorting from minors, persons with disabilities, elderly persons, or persons who have lost the ability to work;  
(4) Extorting by threatening to commit arson, explosion, or other crimes endangering public security, or intentional homicide, kidnapping, or other serious crimes infringing upon citizens' personal rights;  
(5) Extorting in the name of a criminal gang;  
(6) Extorting by using or impersonating the special status of state functionaries, military personnel, journalists, or the like;  
(7) Causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条　二年内敲诈勒索三次以上的，应当认定为[刑法第二百七十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-274)规定的“多次敲诈勒索”。

**Article 3** : Where extortion is committed three or more times within two years, such shall be determined as "multiple extortions" as provided in Article 274 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条　敲诈勒索公私财物，具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第三项至第七项规定的情形之一，数额达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”百分之八十的，可以分别认定为[刑法第二百七十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-274)规定的“其他严重情节”、“其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 4** : Where extortion of public or private property falls under any of the circumstances provided in subparagraphs (3) to (7) of Article 2 of this Interpretation, and the amount reaches 80% of the "large amount" or "extremely large amount" provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation, such may be respectively determined as "other serious circumstances" or "other especially serious circumstances" as provided in Article 274 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条　敲诈勒索数额较大，行为人认罪、悔罪，退赃、退赔，并具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚，由有关部门依法予以行政处罚：  
（一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的；  
（二）没有参与分赃或者获赃较少且不是主犯的；  
（三）被害人谅解的；  
（四）其他情节轻微、危害不大的。

**Article 5** : Where the amount extorted is relatively large, and the actor confesses guilt, repents, returns the illicit proceeds, and pays compensation, and falls under any of the following circumstances, the circumstances of the crime may be determined as minor, and the actor may be exempted from prosecution or criminal punishment, and shall be subject to administrative penalty according to law by the relevant authorities:  
(1) Having statutory circumstances for lenient punishment;  
(2) Not having participated in the division of the booty or having obtained a small amount of booty and not being a principal offender;  
(3) Having obtained the victim's forgiveness;  
(4) Other circumstances where the circumstances are minor and the harm is not great.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条　敲诈勒索近亲属的财物，获得谅解的，一般不认为是犯罪；认定为犯罪的，应当酌情从宽处理。  
被害人对敲诈勒索的发生存在过错的，根据被害人过错程度和案件其他情况，可以对行为人酌情从宽处理；情节显著轻微危害不大的，不认为是犯罪。

**Article 6** : Where the property of a close relative is extorted and forgiveness is obtained, such generally shall not be considered a crime; if it is determined to be a crime, the punishment shall be lenient as circumstances permit.  
Where the victim is at fault for the occurrence of the extortion, the actor may be given lenient treatment as circumstances permit based on the degree of the victim's fault and other circumstances of the case; where the circumstances are obviously minor and the harm is not great, the act shall not be considered a crime.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条　明知他人实施敲诈勒索犯罪，为其提供信用卡、手机卡、通讯工具、通讯传输通道、网络技术支持等帮助的，以共同犯罪论处。

**Article 7** : Where an actor, knowing that another person is committing the crime of extortion, provides assistance such as credit cards, mobile phone cards, communication tools, communication transmission channels, or network technical support, such actor shall be treated as an accomplice to the crime.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条　对犯敲诈勒索罪的被告人，应当在二千元以上、敲诈勒索数额的二倍以下判处罚金；被告人没有获得财物的，应当在二千元以上十万元以下判处罚金。

**Article 8** : For a defendant convicted of the crime of extortion, a fine shall be imposed in an amount not less than 2,000 yuan and not more than twice the amount extorted; if the defendant has not obtained any property, a fine shall be imposed in an amount not less than 2,000 yuan and not more than 100,000 yuan.

<a id="art-9"></a>第九条　本解释公布施行后，《最高人民法院关于敲诈勒索罪数额认定标准问题的规定》（法释〔2000〕11号）同时废止；此前发布的司法解释与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 9** : After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, the "Provisions of the Supreme People's Court on the Standards for Determining the Amount of the Crime of Extortion" (Fa Shi [2000] No. 11) shall be abolished simultaneously; where any previously issued judicial interpretation is inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
