### 最高人民法院关于对故意伤害、盗窃等严重破坏社会秩序的犯罪分子能否附加剥夺政治权利问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于对故意伤害、盗窃等严重破坏社会秩序的  
  
犯罪分子能否附加剥夺政治权利问题的批复  
  
法释〔1997〕11号  
  
（1997年12月23最高人民法院审判委员会第952次会议通过，自1998年1月13日起施行）  
  
福建省高级人民法院：  
  
你院《关于对故意伤害、盗窃（重大）等犯罪分子被判处有期徒刑的，能否附加剥夺政治权利的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据[刑法第五十六条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-56)规定，对于故意杀人、强奸、放火、爆炸、投毒、抢劫等严重破坏社会秩序的犯罪分子，可以附加剥夺政治权利。对故意伤害、盗窃等其他严重破坏社会秩序的犯罪，犯罪分子主观恶性较深、犯罪情节恶劣、罪行严重的，也可以依法附加剥夺政治权利。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Fujian Higher People's Court:  
Your Request regarding whether criminals sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment for intentional injury, theft (major) and other crimes may be additionally sentenced to deprivation of political rights has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
According to Article 56 of the Criminal Law, criminals who commit intentional homicide, rape, arson, explosion, poisoning, robbery and other crimes seriously undermining social order may be additionally sentenced to deprivation of political rights. For intentional injury, theft and other crimes seriously undermining social order, if the criminal has a deep subjective malignancy, vile criminal circumstances, and serious crimes, deprivation of political rights may also be additionally imposed according to law.  
This is hereby replied.
