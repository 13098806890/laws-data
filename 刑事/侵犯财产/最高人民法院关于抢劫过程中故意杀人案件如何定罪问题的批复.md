### 最高人民法院关于抢劫过程中故意杀人案件如何定罪问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于抢劫过程中故意杀人案件如何定罪问题的批复  
  
（2001年5月22日最高人民法院审判委员会第1176次会议通过，自2001年5月26日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2001〕16号  
  
上海市高级人民法院：  
  
你院沪高法〔2000〕117号《关于抢劫过程中故意杀人案件定性间题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
行为人为劫取财物而预谋故意杀人，或者在劫劫罪和故意杀人罪定罪，实行数罪并罚。  
  
行为人实施抢劫后，为灭口而故意杀人的，以抢劫罪和故意杀人罪定罪，实行数罪并罚。  
  
此复

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2001] No. 16  
  
Shanghai Higher People's Court:  
  
Your court's Shanghai High Law [2000] No. 117 "Request for Instructions on the Characterization of Cases of Intentional Homicide during Robbery" has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
Where an actor premeditates intentional homicide for the purpose of taking property by robbery, or is convicted of the crime of robbery and the crime of intentional homicide and subject to combined punishment for multiple crimes.  
  
Where an actor, after committing robbery, intentionally kills for the purpose of silencing the victim, he/she shall be convicted of the crime of robbery and the crime of intentional homicide and subject to combined punishment for multiple crimes.  
  
It is hereby replied.
