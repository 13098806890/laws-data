### 最高人民法院关于挪用公款犯罪如何计算追诉期限问题的批复

<a id="art-1"></a>最高人民法院  
  
关于挪用公款犯罪如何计算追诉  
  
期限问题的批复  
  
（2003年9月18日最高人民法院审判委员会第1290次会议通过，自2003年10月10日起施行。）  
  
法释〔2003〕16号  
  
天津市高级人民法院：  
  
你院津高法〔2002〕4号《关于挪用公款犯罪如何计算追诉期限问题的请示》收悉。经研究，答复如下：  
  
根据[刑法第八十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-89)、第三百八十四条的规定，挪用公款归个人使用，进行非法活动的，或者挪用公款数额较大、进行营利活动的，犯罪的追诉期限从挪用行为实施完毕之日起计算；挪用公款数额较大、超过三个月未还的，犯罪的追诉期限从挪用公款罪成立之日起计算。挪用公款行为有连续状态的，犯罪的追诉期限应当从最后一次挪用行为实施完毕之日或者犯罪成立之日起计算。  
  
2003年9月22日

**Article 1** Fa Shi [2003] No. 16  
  
The Higher People's Court of Tianjin:  
  
Your request titled 'Regarding the Issue of How to Calculate the Limitation Period for Prosecution of the Crime of Embezzlement of Public Funds' (Jin Gao Fa [2002] No. 4) has been received. Upon study, the reply is as follows:  
  
According to the provisions of Articles 89 and 384 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, where public funds are misappropriated for personal use to engage in illegal activities, or where the amount of misappropriated public funds is relatively large for profit-making activities, the limitation period for prosecution of the crime shall be calculated from the date on which the misappropriation act is completed; where the amount of misappropriated public funds is relatively large and has not been repaid for more than three months, the limitation period for prosecution of the crime shall be calculated from the date on which the crime of embezzlement of public funds is established. If the act of misappropriating public funds is continuous, the limitation period for prosecution of the crime shall be calculated from the date on which the last act of misappropriation is completed or the date on which the crime is established.  
  
September 22, 2003
