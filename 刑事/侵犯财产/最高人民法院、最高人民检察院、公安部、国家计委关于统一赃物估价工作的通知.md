# 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家计委关于统一赃物估价工作的通知

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发文字号**：法发〔1994〕9号  
**公布日期**：1994-04-22  
**生效日期**：1994-04-22

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>一、人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关在办理刑事案件过程中，对于价格不明或者价格难以确定的赃物应当估价。案件移送时，应附《赃物估价鉴定结论书》。

**Article 1** 1. When handling criminal cases, the People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, and the public security organ shall appraise stolen goods whose prices are unknown or difficult to determine. When a case is transferred, the "Appraisal Conclusion of Stolen Goods Valuation" shall be attached.

<a id="art-2"></a>二、国家计委及地方各级政府物价管理部门是赃物估价的主管部门，其设立的价格事务所是指定的赃物估价机构。

**Article 2** 2. The State Planning Commission and the price administration departments of local governments at all levels shall be the competent authorities for the valuation of stolen goods, and the price appraisal offices established by them shall be the designated appraisal institutions for stolen goods.

<a id="art-3"></a>三、人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关在办案中需要对赃物估价时，应当出具估价委托书，委托案件管辖地的同级物价管理部门设立的价格事务所进行估价。估价委托书一般应当载明赃物的品名、牌号、规格、数量、来源、购置时间、以及违法犯罪获得赃物的时间、地点等有关情况。

**Article 3** 3. When the People's Court, the People's Procuratorate, or the public security organ needs to appraise stolen goods in handling a case, it shall issue a letter of entrustment for valuation, entrusting the price appraisal office established by the price administration department at the same level as the jurisdiction of the case to conduct the valuation. The letter of entrustment for valuation shall generally include the name, brand, specification, quantity, source, time of purchase of the stolen goods, and the time, place, and other relevant circumstances under which the stolen goods were obtained through illegal or criminal acts.

<a id="art-4"></a>四、价格事务所应当参照最高人民法院、最高人民检察院1992年12月11日《关于办理盗窃案件具体应用法律的若干问题的解释》第三条的规定估价。价格事务所应当在接受估价委托后七日内作出估价鉴定结论，但另有约定的除外。

**Article 4** 4. The price appraisal office shall appraise the goods by reference to the provisions of Article 3 of the "Interpretation of Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in Handling Theft Cases" issued by the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate on December 11, 1992. The price appraisal office shall make an appraisal conclusion within seven days after accepting the entrustment for valuation, unless otherwise agreed.

<a id="art-5"></a>五、价格事务所对赃物估价后，应当出具统一制作的《赃物估价鉴定结论书》，由估价工作人员签名并加盖价格事务所印章。

**Article 5** 5. After appraising the stolen goods, the price appraisal office shall issue a uniformly produced "Appraisal Conclusion of Stolen Goods Valuation", which shall be signed by the appraisal staff and affixed with the seal of the price appraisal office.

<a id="art-6"></a>六、委托估价的机关应当对《赃物估价鉴定结论书》进行审查。如果对同级价格事务所出具的《赃物估价鉴定结论书》提出异议，可退回价格事务所重新鉴定或者委托上一级价格事务所复核。经审查，确认无误的赃物估价鉴定结论，才能作为定案的根据。国家计委指定的直属价格事务所是赃物估价的最终复核裁定机构。

**Article 6** 6. The agency that entrusts the valuation shall review the "Appraisal Conclusion of Stolen Goods Valuation". If it raises an objection to the "Appraisal Conclusion of Stolen Goods Valuation" issued by the price appraisal office at the same level, it may return it to the price appraisal office for re-appraisal or entrust a price appraisal office at the next higher level for review. Only after review and confirmation that the appraisal conclusion of stolen goods is correct may it be used as the basis for deciding a case. The directly affiliated price appraisal office designated by the State Planning Commission shall be the final review and decision-making body for the valuation of stolen goods.

<a id="art-7"></a>七、赃物估价是一项严肃的工作。各级政府价格主管部门及其价格事务所应积极配合人民法院、人民检察院、公安机关认真做好这项工作。一些尚未组建价格事务所的地区，赃物估价工作暂由物价管理部门承担。

**Article 7** 7. The valuation of stolen goods is a serious task. Price administration departments at all levels of government and their price appraisal offices shall actively cooperate with the People's Courts, the People's Procuratorates, and the public security organs to conscientiously carry out this task. In areas where price appraisal offices have not yet been established, the valuation of stolen goods shall be temporarily undertaken by the price administration departments.

<a id="art-8"></a>八、关于赃物估价的具体规定和办法，另行制定。  
本通知自下达之日起执行。

**Article 8** 8. Specific provisions and measures concerning the valuation of stolen goods shall be formulated separately.  
This Notice shall come into effect as of the date of issuance.
