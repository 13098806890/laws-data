# 最高人民法院关于审理挪用公款案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**公布日期**：1998-04-06  
**生效日期**：1998-05-09

> （1998年4月6日最高人民法院审判委员会第972次会议通过，自1998年5月9日起施行） 为依法惩处挪用公款犯罪，根据刑法的有关规定，现对办理挪用公款案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 刑法第三百八十四条规定的“挪用公款归个人使用”，包括挪用者本人使用或者给他人使用。挪用公款给私有公司、私有企业使用的，属于挪用公款归个人使用。 第二条 对挪用公款罪，应区分三种不同情况予以认定： （一）挪用公款归个人使用，数额较大、超过三个月未还的，构成挪用公款罪。挪用正在生息或者需要支付利息的公款归个人使用，数额较大，超过三个月但在案发前全部归还本金的，可以从轻处罚或者免除处罚。给国家、集体造成的利息损失应予追缴。挪用公款数额巨大，超过三个月，案发前全部归还的，可以酌情从轻处罚。 （二）挪用公款数额较大，归个人进行营利活动的，构成挪用公款罪，不受挪用时间和是否归还的限制。在案发前部分或者全部归还本息的，可以从轻处罚；情节轻微的，可以免除处罚。挪用公款存入银行、用于集资、购买股票、国债等，属于挪用公款进行营利活动。所获取的利息、收益等违法所得，应当追缴，但不计入挪用公款的数额。 （三）挪用公款归个人使用，进行赌博、走私等非法活动的，构成挪用公款罪，不受“数额较大”和挪用时间的限制。 挪用公款给他人使用，不知道使用人用公款进行营利活动或者用于非法活动，数额较大、超过三个月未还的，构成挪用公款罪；明知使用人用于营利活动或者非法活动的，应当认定为挪用人挪用公款进行营利活动或者非法活动。 第三条 挪用公款归个人使用，“数额较大、进行营利活动的”，或者“数额较大、超过三个月未还的”，以挪用公款一万元至三万元为“数额较大”的起点，以挪用公款十五万元至二十万元为“数额巨大”的起点。挪用公款“情节严重”，是指挪用公款数额巨大，或者数额虽未达到巨大，但挪用公款手段恶劣；多次挪用公款；因挪用公款严重影响生产、经营，造成严重损失等情形。 “挪用公款归个人使用，进行非法活动的”，以挪用公款五千元至一万元为追究刑事责任的数额起点。挪用公款五万元至十万元以上的，属于挪用公款归个人使用，进行非法活动“情节严重”的情形之一。挪用公款归个人使用，进行非法活动，情节严重的其他情形，按照本条第一款的规定执行。 各高级人民法院可以根据本地实际情况，按照本解释规定的数额幅度，确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，并报最高人民法院备案。 挪用救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济款物归个人使用的数额标准，参照挪用公款归个人使用进行非法活动的数额标准。 第四条 多次挪用公款不还，挪用公款数额累计计算；多次挪用公款、并以后次挪用的公款归还前次挪用的公款，挪用公款数额以案发时未还的实际数额认定。 第五条 “挪用公款数额巨大不退还的”，是指挪用公款数额巨大，因客观原因在一审宣判前不能退还的。 第六条 携带挪用的公款潜逃的，依照刑法第三百八十二条、第三百八十三条的规定定罪处罚。

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 [刑法第三百八十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-384)规定的“挪用公款归个人使用”，包括挪用者本人使用或者给他人使用。挪用公款给私有公司、私有企业使用的，属于挪用公款归个人使用。

**Article 1** The "misappropriation of public funds for personal use" as provided for in Article 384 of the Criminal Law shall include the misappropriator's own use or use by others. Where public funds are misappropriated for use by private companies or private enterprises, it shall be deemed as misappropriation of public funds for personal use.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 对挪用公款罪，应区分三种不同情况予以认定：  
（一）挪用公款归个人使用，数额较大、超过三个月未还的，构成挪用公款罪。挪用正在生息或者需要支付利息的公款归个人使用，数额较大，超过三个月但在案发前全部归还本金的，可以从轻处罚或者免除处罚。给国家、集体造成的利息损失应予追缴。挪用公款数额巨大，超过三个月，案发前全部归还的，可以酌情从轻处罚。  
（二）挪用公款数额较大，归个人进行营利活动的，构成挪用公款罪，不受挪用时间和是否归还的限制。在案发前部分或者全部归还本息的，可以从轻处罚；情节轻微的，可以免除处罚。挪用公款存入银行、用于集资、购买股票、国债等，属于挪用公款进行营利活动。所获取的利息、收益等违法所得，应当追缴，但不计入挪用公款的数额。  
（三）挪用公款归个人使用，进行赌博、走私等非法活动的，构成挪用公款罪，不受“数额较大”和挪用时间的限制。  
挪用公款给他人使用，不知道使用人用公款进行营利活动或者用于非法活动，数额较大、超过三个月未还的，构成挪用公款罪；明知使用人用于营利活动或者非法活动的，应当认定为挪用人挪用公款进行营利活动或者非法活动。

**Article 2** Concerning the crime of misappropriation of public funds, the following three different circumstances shall be distinguished for determination:  
(1) Where public funds are misappropriated for personal use, the amount is relatively large and has not been returned after more than three months, the crime of misappropriation of public funds is constituted. Where public funds that are accruing interest or require payment of interest are misappropriated for personal use, the amount is relatively large, has exceeded three months but the full principal has been returned before the case is discovered, a lighter punishment may be imposed or the offender may be exempted from punishment. The interest losses caused to the state or the collective shall be recovered. Where the amount of misappropriated public funds is huge, has exceeded three months, and the full amount has been returned before the case is discovered, a lighter punishment may be imposed as appropriate.  
(2) Where public funds are misappropriated in a relatively large amount for personal profit-making activities, the crime of misappropriation of public funds is constituted, regardless of the time limit and whether the funds have been returned. Where part or all of the principal and interest has been returned before the case is discovered, a lighter punishment may be imposed; where the circumstances are minor, the offender may be exempted from punishment. Depositing misappropriated public funds in a bank, using them for fundraising, purchasing stocks, treasury bonds, etc., shall be deemed as misappropriation of public funds for profit-making activities. The illegal gains such as interest and proceeds obtained shall be recovered, but shall not be included in the amount of misappropriated public funds.  
(3) Where public funds are misappropriated for personal use for illegal activities such as gambling or smuggling, the crime of misappropriation of public funds is constituted, regardless of the "relatively large amount" and the time limit.  
Where public funds are misappropriated for use by others, and the misappropriator does not know that the user uses the public funds for profit-making activities or illegal activities, and the amount is relatively large and has not been returned after more than three months, the crime of misappropriation of public funds is constituted; where the misappropriator clearly knows that the user uses them for profit-making or illegal activities, it shall be deemed that the misappropriator misappropriated public funds for profit-making or illegal activities.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 挪用公款归个人使用，“数额较大、进行营利活动的”，或者“数额较大、超过三个月未还的”，以挪用公款一万元至三万元为“数额较大”的起点，以挪用公款十五万元至二十万元为“数额巨大”的起点。挪用公款“情节严重”，是指挪用公款数额巨大，或者数额虽未达到巨大，但挪用公款手段恶劣；多次挪用公款；因挪用公款严重影响生产、经营，造成严重损失等情形。  
“挪用公款归个人使用，进行非法活动的”，以挪用公款五千元至一万元为追究刑事责任的数额起点。挪用公款五万元至十万元以上的，属于挪用公款归个人使用，进行非法活动“情节严重”的情形之一。挪用公款归个人使用，进行非法活动，情节严重的其他情形，按照本条第一款的规定执行。  
各高级人民法院可以根据本地实际情况，按照本解释规定的数额幅度，确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，并报最高人民法院备案。  
挪用救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济款物归个人使用的数额标准，参照挪用公款归个人使用进行非法活动的数额标准。

**Article 3** For misappropriation of public funds for personal use, where "the amount is relatively large and used for profit-making activities" or "the amount is relatively large and has not been returned after more than three months", the starting point for "relatively large amount" shall be 10,000 yuan to 30,000 yuan of misappropriated public funds, and the starting point for "huge amount" shall be 150,000 yuan to 200,000 yuan of misappropriated public funds. "Serious circumstances" in misappropriation of public funds shall mean that the amount of misappropriated public funds is huge, or although the amount does not reach the huge level, the means of misappropriation are despicable; the public funds are misappropriated multiple times; the misappropriation seriously affects production and operation, causing severe losses, and other circumstances.  
"Where public funds are misappropriated for personal use for illegal activities", the starting point for investigating criminal liability shall be 5,000 yuan to 10,000 yuan. Where the amount of misappropriated public funds is 50,000 yuan to 100,000 yuan or more, it shall be one of the circumstances of "serious circumstances" in misappropriation of public funds for personal use for illegal activities. Other circumstances of serious circumstances in misappropriation of public funds for personal use for illegal activities shall be handled in accordance with the provisions of the first paragraph of this Article.  
Each Higher People's Court may, based on the actual local conditions, determine the specific amount standards for enforcement in its region within the amount range provided for in this Interpretation, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court for record.  
The amount standards for misappropriation of funds or materials allocated for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for disabled servicemen and family members of revolutionary martyrs, poverty alleviation, resettlement, and relief for personal use shall refer to the amount standards for misappropriation of public funds for personal use for illegal activities.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 多次挪用公款不还，挪用公款数额累计计算；多次挪用公款、并以后次挪用的公款归还前次挪用的公款，挪用公款数额以案发时未还的实际数额认定。

**Article 4** Where public funds are misappropriated multiple times without being returned, the amount of misappropriated public funds shall be calculated cumulatively; where public funds are misappropriated multiple times and the later misappropriation is used to repay the previous misappropriation, the amount of misappropriated public funds shall be determined as the actual amount not yet returned at the time the case is discovered.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 “挪用公款数额巨大不退还的”，是指挪用公款数额巨大，因客观原因在一审宣判前不能退还的。

**Article 5** "Failure to return a huge amount of misappropriated public funds" shall mean that the amount of misappropriated public funds is huge and cannot be returned due to objective reasons before the pronouncement of judgment at first instance.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 携带挪用的公款潜逃的，依照[刑法第三百八十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-382)、第三百八十三条的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 6** Where a person absconds with the misappropriated public funds, the crime shall be determined and punished in accordance with Articles 382 and 383 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条 因挪用公款索取、收受贿赂构成犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。  
挪用公款进行非法活动构成其他犯罪的，依照数罪并罚的规定处罚。

**Article 7** Where a crime is constituted by soliciting or accepting bribes in connection with misappropriation of public funds, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes.  
Where a crime is constituted by using misappropriated public funds to engage in illegal activities that constitute another crime, punishment shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions on combined punishment for multiple crimes.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条 挪用公款给他人使用，使用人与挪用人共谋，指使或者参与策划取得挪用款的，以挪用公款罪的共犯定罪处罚。

**Article 8** Where public funds are misappropriated for use by others, and the user colludes with the misappropriator, instigates or participates in planning to obtain the misappropriated funds, the user shall be convicted and punished as an accomplice to the crime of misappropriation of public funds.
