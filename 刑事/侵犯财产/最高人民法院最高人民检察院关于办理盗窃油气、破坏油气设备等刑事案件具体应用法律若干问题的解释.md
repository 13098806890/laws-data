# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕3号  
**公布日期**：2006-12-11  
**生效日期**：2007-01-19

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理盗窃油气 、破坏油气设备等刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2006年 11 月 20 日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1406次会议、2006年12月11日由最高人民检察院第十届检察委员会第66次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年1月19日起施行 。 2007年1月15日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等刑事案件 具体应用法律若干问题的解释 法释〔2007〕3号 为维护油气的生产、运输安全，依法惩治盗窃油气、破坏油气设备等犯罪，根据刑法有关规定，现就办理这类刑事案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  在实施盗窃油气等行为过程中，采用切割、打孔、撬砸、拆卸、开关等手段破坏正在使用的油气设备的，属于刑法第一百一十八条规定的“破坏燃气或者其他易燃易爆设备”的行为；危害公共安全，尚未造成严重后果的，依照刑法第一百一十八条的规定定罪处罚。 第二条  实施本解释第一条规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于刑法第一百一十九条第一款规定的“造成严重后果”，依照刑法第一百一十九条第一款的规定定罪处罚： （一）造成一人以上死亡、三人以上重伤或者十人以上轻伤的； （二）造成井喷或者重大环境污染事故的； （三）造成直接经济损失数额在五十万元以上的； （四）造成其他严重后果的。 第三条  盗窃油气或者正在使用的油气设备，构成犯罪，但未危害公共安全的，依照刑法第二百六十四条的规定，以盗窃罪定罪处罚。 盗窃油气，数额巨大但尚未运离现场的，以盗窃未遂定罪处罚。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  在实施盗窃油气等行为过程中，采用切割、打孔、撬砸、拆卸、开关等手段破坏正在使用的油气设备的，属于[刑法第一百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-118)规定的“破坏燃气或者其他易燃易爆设备”的行为；危害公共安全，尚未造成严重后果的，依照[刑法第一百一十八条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-118)的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 1** Any person who, in the process of stealing oil, gas, or other substances, uses means such as cutting, drilling, prying, smashing, disassembling, or switching to damage oil or gas equipment in use shall be deemed to have committed the act of 'destroying gas or other flammable and explosive equipment' as stipulated in Article 118 of the Criminal Law; if such act endangers public security but has not caused serious consequences, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 118 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的行为，具有下列情形之一的，属于[刑法第一百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-119)第一款规定的“造成严重后果”，依照[刑法第一百一十九条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-119)第一款的规定定罪处罚：  
（一）造成一人以上死亡、三人以上重伤或者十人以上轻伤的；  
（二）造成井喷或者重大环境污染事故的；  
（三）造成直接经济损失数额在五十万元以上的；  
（四）造成其他严重后果的。

**Article 2** Any person who commits any of the acts specified in Article 1 of this Interpretation, under any of the following circumstances, shall be deemed to have caused 'serious consequences' as stipulated in Article 119, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, and shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 119, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Causing the death of one or more persons, serious injury to three or more persons, or minor injury to ten or more persons;  
(2) Causing a well blowout or a major environmental pollution accident;  
(3) Causing direct economic losses of 500,000 yuan or more;  
(4) Causing other serious consequences.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  盗窃油气或者正在使用的油气设备，构成犯罪，但未危害公共安全的，依照[刑法第二百六十四条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-264)的规定，以盗窃罪定罪处罚。  
盗窃油气，数额巨大但尚未运离现场的，以盗窃未遂定罪处罚。  
为他人盗窃油气而偷开油气井、油气管道等油气设备阀门排放油气或者提供其他帮助的，以盗窃罪的共犯定罪处罚。

**Article 3** Any person who steals oil, gas, or oil or gas equipment in use, which constitutes a crime but does not endanger public security, shall be convicted and punished as a crime of theft in accordance with Article 264 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person steals oil or gas in a huge amount but has not yet transported it away from the scene, the person shall be convicted and punished as attempted theft.  
Where a person secretly opens valves of oil or gas wells, pipelines, or other oil or gas equipment to discharge oil or gas or provides other assistance for another person to steal oil or gas, the person shall be convicted and punished as an accomplice to the crime of theft.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  盗窃油气同时构成盗窃罪和破坏易燃易爆设备罪的，依照刑法处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 4** Where the theft of oil or gas simultaneously constitutes the crime of theft and the crime of destroying flammable and explosive equipment, the person shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provision of the Criminal Law that imposes a heavier punishment.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  明知是盗窃犯罪所得的油气或者油气设备，而予以窝藏、转移、收购、加工、代为销售或者以其他方法掩饰、隐瞒的，依照[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)的规定定罪处罚。  
实施前款规定的犯罪行为，事前通谋的，以盗窃犯罪的共犯定罪处罚。

**Article 5** Any person who knowingly harbors, transfers, purchases, processes, sells on behalf of, or otherwise conceals or covers up oil, gas, or oil or gas equipment obtained through theft shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 312 of the Criminal Law.  
Where a person commits the criminal act as prescribed in the preceding paragraph and has colluded in advance with the thief, the person shall be convicted and punished as an accomplice to the crime of theft.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  违反矿产资源法的规定，非法开采或者破坏性开采石油、天然气资源的，依照[刑法第三百四十三条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-343)以及《最高人民法院关于审理非法采矿、破坏性采矿刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》的规定追究刑事责任。

**Article 6** Any person who, in violation of the provisions of the Mineral Resources Law, illegally mines or destructively mines petroleum or natural gas resources shall be investigated for criminal liability in accordance with Article 343 of the Criminal Law and the provisions of the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Illegal Mining and Destructive Mining.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条  国家机关工作人员滥用职权或者玩忽职守，实施下列行为之一，致使公共财产、国家和人民利益遭受重大损失的，依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)的规定，以滥用职权罪或者玩忽职守罪定罪处罚：  
（一）超越职权范围，批准发放石油、天然气勘查、开采、加工、经营等许可证的；  
（二）违反国家规定，给不符合法定条件的单位、个人发放石油、天然气勘查、开采、加工、经营等许可证的；  
（三）违反《石油天然气管道保护条例》等国家规定，在油气设备安全保护范围内批准建设项目的；  
（四）对发现或者经举报查实的未经依法批准、许可擅自从事石油、天然气勘查、开采、加工、经营等违法活动不予查封、取缔的。

**Article 7** Any functionary of a state organ who abuses his or her power or neglects his or her duty, commits any of the following acts, thereby causing heavy losses to public property, the state, and the interests of the people, shall be convicted and punished as the crime of abuse of power or the crime of dereliction of duty in accordance with Article 397 of the Criminal Law:  
(1) Exceeding the scope of authority to approve and issue licenses for petroleum or natural gas exploration, exploitation, processing, or operation;  
(2) Violating state regulations to issue licenses for petroleum or natural gas exploration, exploitation, processing, or operation to units or individuals that do not meet the statutory conditions;  
(3) Violating state regulations such as the Regulations on the Protection of Oil and Gas Pipelines to approve construction projects within the safety protection scope of oil or gas equipment;  
(4) Failing to seal up or ban illegal activities of petroleum or natural gas exploration, exploitation, processing, or operation conducted without approval or permission according to law that have been discovered or reported and verified.

<a id="art-8"></a>第八条  本解释所称的“油气”，是指石油、天然气。其中，石油包括原油、成品油；天然气包括煤层气。  
本解释所称“油气设备”，是指用于石油、天然气生产、储存、运输等易燃易爆设备。

**Article 8** The term 'oil or gas' as used in this Interpretation means petroleum and natural gas. Among them, petroleum includes crude oil and refined oil; natural gas includes coalbed methane.  
The term 'oil or gas equipment' as used in this Interpretation means flammable and explosive equipment used for the production, storage, transportation, etc., of petroleum and natural gas.
