# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理抢夺刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2013〕25号  
**公布日期**：2013-09-30  
**生效日期**：2013-11-18

> 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院《关于办理抢夺刑事案件适用法律若干问题的解释》已于2013年9月30日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1592次会议、2013年10月22日由最高人民检察院第十二届检察委员会第12次会议通过，现予公布，自2013年11月18日起施行。 2013年11月11日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理抢夺刑事案件适用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2013〕25号 为依法惩治抢夺犯罪，保护公私财产，根据《中华人民共和国刑法》的有关规定，现就办理此类刑事案件适用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条   抢夺公私财物价值一千元至三千元以上、三万元至八万元以上、二十万元至四十万元以上的，应当分别认定为刑法第二百六十七条规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。 第二条   抢夺公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准按照前条规定标准的百分之五十确定： （一）曾因抢劫、抢夺或者聚众哄抢受过刑事处罚的； （二）一年内曾因抢夺或者哄抢受过行政处罚的； （三）一年内抢夺三次以上的； （四）驾驶机动车、非机动车抢夺的； （五）组织、控制未成年人抢夺的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条   抢夺公私财物价值一千元至三千元以上、三万元至八万元以上、二十万元至四十万元以上的，应当分别认定为[刑法第二百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-267)规定的“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”。  
各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院、人民检察院可以根据本地区经济发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在前款规定的数额幅度内，确定本地区执行的具体数额标准，报最高人民法院、最高人民检察院批准。

**Article 1** : The public or private property forcibly seized with a value from 1,000 yuan to 3,000 yuan, from 30,000 yuan to 80,000 yuan, or from 200,000 yuan to 400,000 yuan shall be respectively determined as "relatively large amount", "large amount", and "extremely large amount" as provided in Article 267 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.  
The Higher People's Courts and People's Procuratorates of all provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the Central Government may, according to the economic development of the region and taking into consideration the public security situation, determine the specific amount standards for enforcement within the region within the range of amounts provided in the preceding paragraph, and report them to the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate for approval.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条   抢夺公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，“数额较大”的标准按照前条规定标准的百分之五十确定：  
（一）曾因抢劫、抢夺或者聚众哄抢受过刑事处罚的；  
（二）一年内曾因抢夺或者哄抢受过行政处罚的；  
（三）一年内抢夺三次以上的；  
（四）驾驶机动车、非机动车抢夺的；  
（五）组织、控制未成年人抢夺的；  
（六）抢夺老年人、未成年人、孕妇、携带婴幼儿的人、残疾人、丧失劳动能力人的财物的；  
（七）在医院抢夺病人或者其亲友财物的；  
（八）抢夺救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济款物的；  
（九）自然灾害、事故灾害、社会安全事件等突发事件期间，在事件发生地抢夺的；  
（十）导致他人轻伤或者精神失常等严重后果的。

**Article 2** : If a person forcibly seizes public or private property and has any of the following circumstances, the standard for "relatively large amount" shall be determined at 50% of the standard provided in the preceding article:  
(1) Has previously been subject to criminal punishment for robbery, forcible seizure, or gathering a crowd to forcibly seize property;  
(2) Has been subject to administrative penalty for forcible seizure or gathering to forcibly seize property within one year;  
(3) Has forcibly seized property three or more times within one year;  
(4) Drives a motor vehicle or non-motor vehicle to forcibly seize property;  
(5) Organizes or controls a minor to forcibly seize property;  
(6) Forcibly seizes property of an elderly person, a minor, a pregnant woman, a person carrying an infant, a disabled person, or a person who has lost the ability to work;  
(7) Forcibly seizes property of a patient or his/her relatives or friends in a hospital;  
(8) Forcibly seizes funds or materials for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for disabled servicemen and families of revolutionary martyrs, poverty alleviation, immigration, or relief;  
(9) During the period of natural disasters, accidents, social security incidents, or other emergencies, forcibly seizes property at the place where the incident occurs;  
(10) Causes consequences such as minor injury to another person or mental disorder.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条   抢夺公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-267)规定的“其他严重情节”：  
（一）导致他人重伤的；  
（二）导致他人自杀的；  
（三）具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第三项至第十项规定的情形之一，数额达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额巨大”百分之五十的。

**Article 3** : If a person forcibly seizes public or private property and has any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "other serious circumstances" as provided in Article 267 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causes serious injury to another person;  
(2) Causes another person to commit suicide;  
(3) Has any of the circumstances provided in items (3) to (10) of Article 2 of this Interpretation and the amount reaches 50% of the "large amount" provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条   抢夺公私财物，具有下列情形之一的，应当认定为[刑法第二百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-267)规定的“其他特别严重情节”：  
（一）导致他人死亡的；  
（二）具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)第三项至第十项规定的情形之一，数额达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的“数额特别巨大”百分之五十的。

**Article 4** : If a person forcibly seizes public or private property and has any of the following circumstances, it shall be determined as "other particularly serious circumstances" as provided in Article 267 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China:  
(1) Causes the death of another person;  
(2) Has any of the circumstances provided in items (3) to (10) of Article 2 of this Interpretation and the amount reaches 50% of the "extremely large amount" provided in Article 1 of this Interpretation.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条   抢夺公私财物数额较大，但未造成他人轻伤以上伤害，行为人系初犯，认罪、悔罪，退赃、退赔，且具有下列情形之一的，可以认定为犯罪情节轻微，不起诉或者免予刑事处罚；必要时，由有关部门依法予以行政处罚：  
（一）具有法定从宽处罚情节的；  
（二）没有参与分赃或者获赃较少，且不是主犯的；  
（三）被害人谅解的；  
（四）其他情节轻微、危害不大的。

**Article 5** : If the amount of public or private property forcibly seized is relatively large, but does not cause injury of minor injury or above to another person, the actor is a first-time offender, confesses the crime, shows remorse, returns the stolen property and compensates for the losses, and has any of the following circumstances, the criminal conduct may be determined as minor, and no prosecution shall be initiated or the actor may be exempted from criminal punishment; if necessary, the relevant departments shall impose administrative penalties according to law:  
(1) Has statutory circumstances for lenient punishment;  
(2) Did not participate in the distribution of stolen property or obtained few stolen goods, and is not a principal offender;  
(3) The victim's understanding is obtained;  
(4) Other circumstances where the conduct is minor and the harm is not great.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条   驾驶机动车、非机动车夺取他人财物，具有下列情形之一的，应当以抢劫罪定罪处罚：  
（一）夺取他人财物时因被害人不放手而强行夺取的；  
（二）驾驶车辆逼挤、撞击或者强行逼倒他人夺取财物的；  
（三）明知会致人伤亡仍然强行夺取并放任造成财物持有人轻伤以上后果的。

**Article 6** : If a person drives a motor vehicle or non-motor vehicle to forcibly take another person's property, and has any of the following circumstances, the conduct shall be convicted and punished as the crime of robbery:  
(1) Forcibly takes the property when the victim does not let go;  
(2) Drives the vehicle to crowd, collide, or forcibly push down another person to take the property;  
(3) Knowing that it will cause casualties, still forcibly takes the property and is indifferent to causing the property holder to suffer minor injury or above.

<a id="art-7"></a>第七条   本解释公布施行后，最高人民法院《关于审理抢夺刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》（法释〔2002〕18号）同时废止；之前发布的司法解释和规范性文件与本解释不一致的，以本解释为准。

**Article 7** : After this Interpretation is promulgated and comes into force, the Interpretation of the Supreme People's Court on Several Issues Concerning the Specific Application of Law in the Trial of Criminal Cases of Forcible Seizure (Fa Shi [2002] No. 18) shall be abolished simultaneously; if any previously issued judicial interpretations or normative documents are inconsistent with this Interpretation, this Interpretation shall prevail.
