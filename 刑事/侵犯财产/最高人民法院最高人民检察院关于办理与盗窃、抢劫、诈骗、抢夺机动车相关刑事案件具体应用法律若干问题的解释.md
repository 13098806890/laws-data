# 最高人民法院最高人民检察院关于办理与盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺机动车相关刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2007〕11号  
**公布日期**：2006-12-25  
**生效日期**：2007-05-11

> 《最高人民法院、最高人民检察院关于办理与盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺机动车相关刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释》已于2006年12月25日由最高人民法院审判委员会第1411次会议、2007年2月14日由最高人民检察院第十届检察委员会第71次会议通过，现予公布，自2007年5月11日起施行。 2007年5月9日 最高人民法院  最高人民检察院 关于办理与盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺机动车 相关刑事案件具体应用法律 若干问题的解释 法释〔2007〕11号 为依法惩治与盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺机动车相关的犯罪活动，根据刑法、刑事诉讼法等有关法律的规定，现对办理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条  明知是盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺的机动车，实施下列行为之一的，依照刑法第三百一十二条的规定，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制，并处或者单处罚金： （一）买卖、介绍买卖、典当、拍卖、抵押或者用其抵债的； （二）拆解、拼装或者组装的； （三）修改发动机号、车辆识别代号的； （四）更改车身颜色或者车辆外形的； （五）提供或者出售机动车来历凭证、整车合格证、号牌以及有关机动车的其他证明和凭证的； （六）提供或者出售伪造、变造的机动车来历凭证、整车合格证、号牌以及有关机动车的其他证明和凭证的。

---

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条  明知是盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺的机动车，实施下列行为之一的，依照[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)的规定，以掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役或者管制，并处或者单处罚金：  
（一）买卖、介绍买卖、典当、拍卖、抵押或者用其抵债的；  
（二）拆解、拼装或者组装的；  
（三）修改发动机号、车辆识别代号的；  
（四）更改车身颜色或者车辆外形的；  
（五）提供或者出售机动车来历凭证、整车合格证、号牌以及有关机动车的其他证明和凭证的；  
（六）提供或者出售伪造、变造的机动车来历凭证、整车合格证、号牌以及有关机动车的其他证明和凭证的。  
实施第一款规定的行为涉及盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺的机动车五辆以上或者价值总额达到五十万元以上的，属于[刑法第三百一十二条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-312)规定的“情节严重”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑，并处罚金。

**Article 1** Whoever knowingly commits any of the following acts with respect to a motor vehicle obtained through theft, robbery, fraud, or forcible seizure shall, in accordance with Article 312 of the Criminal Law, be convicted and punished for the crime of concealing or concealing the proceeds of crime, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, or public surveillance, and/or a fine:  
(1) buying, selling, introducing the sale, pawning, auctioning, mortgaging, or using it to offset debts;  
(2) dismantling, reassembling, or assembling;  
(3) altering the engine number or vehicle identification number;  
(4) changing the body color or vehicle appearance;  
(5) providing or selling the certificate of origin, certificate of conformity, license plate, and other certificates and documents concerning the motor vehicle;  
(6) providing or selling forged or altered certificates of origin, certificates of conformity, license plates, and other certificates and documents concerning the motor vehicle.  
If the act committed under the first paragraph involves five or more motor vehicles obtained through theft, robbery, fraud, or forcible seizure, or the total value reaches RMB 500,000 or more, it shall fall under the "serious circumstances" as provided in Article 312 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years, and shall also be fined.

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条  伪造、变造、买卖机动车行驶证、登记证书，累计三本以上的，依照[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)第一款的规定，以伪造、变造、买卖国家机关证件罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑、拘役、管制或者剥夺政治权利。  
伪造、变造、买卖机动车行驶证、登记证书，累计达到第一款规定数量标准五倍以上的，属于[刑法第二百八十条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-280)第一款规定中的“情节严重”，处三年以上十年以下有期徒刑。

**Article 2** Whoever forges, alters, or buys or sells motor vehicle driving permits or registration certificates, accumulating three or more, shall, in accordance with Article 280, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, be convicted of the crime of forging, altering, or buying or selling official documents of state organs, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years, criminal detention, public surveillance, or deprivation of political rights.  
If the forgery, alteration, or buying or selling of motor vehicle driving permits or registration certificates accumulates to five times the quantity standard provided in the first paragraph, it shall fall under the "serious circumstances" provided in Article 280, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than ten years.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条  国家机关工作人员滥用职权，有下列情形之一，致使盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺的机动车被办理登记手续，数量达到三辆以上或者价值总额达到三十万元以上的，依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)第一款的规定，以滥用职权罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役：  
（一）明知是登记手续不全或者不符合规定的机动车而办理登记手续的；  
（二）指使他人为明知是登记手续不全或者不符合规定的机动车办理登记手续的；  
（三）违规或者指使他人违规更改、调换车辆档案的；  
（四）其他滥用职权的行为。  
国家机关工作人员疏于审查或者审查不严，致使盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺的机动车被办理登记手续，数量达到五辆以上或者价值总额达到五十万元以上的，依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)第一款的规定，以玩忽职守罪定罪，处三年以下有期徒刑或者拘役。  
国家机关工作人员实施前两款规定的行为，致使盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺的机动车被办理登记手续，分别达到前两款规定数量、数额标准五倍以上的，或者明知是盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺的机动车而办理登记手续的，属于[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)第一款规定的“情节特别严重”，处三年以上七年以下有期徒刑。  
国家机关工作人员徇私舞弊，实施上述行为，构成犯罪的，依照[刑法第三百九十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-397)第二款的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 3** Any functionary of a state organ who abuses his power, under any of the following circumstances, causing registration procedures to be processed for a motor vehicle obtained through theft, robbery, fraud, or forcible seizure, where the number reaches three or more or the total value reaches RMB 300,000 or more, shall, in accordance with Article 397, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, be convicted of the crime of abuse of power, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention:  
(1) knowingly processing registration procedures for a motor vehicle with incomplete or non-compliant registration documents;  
(2) directing another person to process registration procedures for a motor vehicle known to have incomplete or non-compliant registration documents;  
(3) illegally or directing another person to illegally alter or exchange vehicle archives;  
(4) other acts of abuse of power.  
If a functionary of a state organ is negligent in examination or fails to conduct a thorough examination, causing registration procedures to be processed for a motor vehicle obtained through theft, robbery, fraud, or forcible seizure, where the number reaches five or more or the total value reaches RMB 500,000 or more, he shall, in accordance with Article 397, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, be convicted of the crime of dereliction of duty, and shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not more than three years or criminal detention.  
If a functionary of a state organ commits the acts provided in the preceding two paragraphs, causing registration procedures to be processed for motor vehicles obtained through theft, robbery, fraud, or forcible seizure, where the number or value respectively reaches five times the standards provided in the preceding two paragraphs, or knowingly processes registration procedures for a motor vehicle obtained through theft, robbery, fraud, or forcible seizure, it shall fall under the "particularly serious circumstances" provided in Article 397, paragraph 1 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of not less than three years but not more than seven years.  
If a functionary of a state organ engages in malpractices for personal gain and commits the acts mentioned above, and a crime is constituted, he shall be convicted and punished in accordance with Article 397, paragraph 2 of the Criminal Law.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条  实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第二条、第三条第一款或者第三款规定的行为，事前与盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺机动车的犯罪分子通谋的，以盗窃罪、抢劫罪、诈骗罪、抢夺罪的共犯论处。

**Article 4** If a perpetrator commits the acts provided in Article 1, Article 2, Article 3, paragraph 1, or Article 3, paragraph 3 of this Interpretation, and conspires with the criminal who commits theft, robbery, fraud, or forcible seizure of a motor vehicle prior to the act, he shall be deemed an accomplice to the crime of theft, robbery, fraud, or forcible seizure.

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条  对跨地区实施的涉及同一机动车的盗窃、抢劫、诈骗、抢夺以及掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益行为，有关公安机关可以依照法律和有关规定一并立案侦查，需要提请批准逮捕、移送审查起诉、提起公诉的，由该公安机关所在地的同级人民检察院、人民法院受理。

**Article 5** For acts involving the same motor vehicle that are committed across regions and involve theft, robbery, fraud, forcible seizure, and the act of concealing or concealing the proceeds of crime or the benefits derived therefrom, the relevant public security organs may, in accordance with the law and relevant provisions, file and investigate the cases jointly. If it is necessary to submit a request for approval of arrest, transfer for review and prosecution, or initiate a public prosecution, the matter shall be accepted by the People's Procuratorate and the People's Court at the same level as the location of the public security organ.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条  行为人实施[本解释第一条](#art-1)、第三条第三款规定的行为，涉及的机动车有下列情形之一的，应当认定行为人主观上属于上述条款所称“明知”：  
（一）没有合法有效的来历凭证；  
（二）发动机号、车辆识别代号有明显更改痕迹，没有合法证明的。

**Article 6** Where a perpetrator commits an act as stipulated in Article 1 or Article 3, paragraph 3 of this Interpretation, and the motor vehicle involved falls under any of the following circumstances, the perpetrator shall be deemed to have the subjective element of "knowingly" as referred to in the aforesaid provisions:  
(1) there is no lawful and valid certificate of origin;  
(2) the engine number or vehicle identification number shows clear signs of alteration, and there is no lawful explanation.
