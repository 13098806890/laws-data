# 最高人民法院关于审理抢夺刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释

**分类**：司法解释  
**法律部门**：刑法  
**发布机关**：最高人民法院  
**发文字号**：法释〔2002〕18号  
**公布日期**：2002-07-15  
**生效日期**：2002-07-20

> （2002年7月15日最高人民法院审判判委员会第1231次会议通过，自2002年7月20日起施行。） 法释〔2002〕18号 为依法惩治抢夺犯罪活动，根据刑法有关规定，现就审理这类案件具体应用法律的若干问题解释如下： 第一条 抢夺公私财物“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”的标准如下： （一）抢夺公私财物价值人民币五百元至二千元以上的，为“数额较大”； （二）抢夺公私财物价值人民币五千元至二万元以上的，为“数额巨大”； （三）抢夺公私财物价值人民币三万元至十万元以上的，为“数额特别巨大”。 第二条 抢夺公私财物达到本解释第一条第（一）项规定的“数额较大”的标准，具有下列情形之一的，可以依照刑法第二百六十七条第一款的规定，以抢夺罪从重处罚： （一）抢夺残疾人、老年人、不满十四周岁未成年人的财物的； （二）抢夺救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济等款物的； （三）一年内抢夺三次以上的； （四）利用行驶的机动车辆抢夺的。 抢夺公私财物，未经行政处罚处理，依法应当追诉的，抢夺数额累计计算。 第三条 抢夺公私财物虽然达到本解释第一条第（一）项规定的“数额较大”的标准，但具有下列情形之一的，可以视为刑法第三十七条规定的“犯罪情节轻微不需要判处刑罚”，免予刑事处罚： （一）已满十六周岁不满十八周岁的未成年人作案，属于初犯或者被教唆犯罪的；

### 正文

<a id="art-1"></a>第一条 抢夺公私财物“数额较大”、“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”的标准如下：  
（一）抢夺公私财物价值人民币五百元至二千元以上的，为“数额较大”；  
（二）抢夺公私财物价值人民币五千元至二万元以上的，为“数额巨大”；  
（三）抢夺公私财物价值人民币三万元至十万元以上的，为“数额特别巨大”。

**Article 1** : The standards for "relatively large amount", "large amount", and "extremely large amount" in the crime of seizing public or private property by force are as follows:  
(1) If the value of the public or private property seized by force is 500 yuan to 2,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as a "relatively large amount";  
(2) If the value of the public or private property seized by force is 5,000 yuan to 20,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as a "large amount";  
(3) If the value of the public or private property seized by force is 30,000 yuan to 100,000 yuan or more, it shall be deemed as an "extremely large amount".

<a id="art-2"></a>第二条 抢夺公私财物达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)第（一）项规定的“数额较大”的标准，具有下列情形之一的，可以依照[刑法第二百六十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-267)第一款的规定，以抢夺罪从重处罚：  
（一）抢夺残疾人、老年人、不满十四周岁未成年人的财物的；  
（二）抢夺救灾、抢险、防汛、优抚、扶贫、移民、救济等款物的；  
（三）一年内抢夺三次以上的；  
（四）利用行驶的机动车辆抢夺的。  
抢夺公私财物，未经行政处罚处理，依法应当追诉的，抢夺数额累计计算。

**Article 2** : Where the seizing of public or private property by force reaches the standard of "relatively large amount" as provided in Paragraph (1) of Article 1 of this Interpretation, and any of the following circumstances exists, the offender may, in accordance with the provisions of Paragraph 1 of Article 267 of the Criminal Law, be given a severe punishment for the crime of forcible seizure:  
(1) seizing by force the property of disabled persons, elderly persons, or minors under the age of 14;  
(2) seizing by force funds or materials allocated for disaster relief, emergency rescue, flood control, special care for disabled servicemen and family members of revolutionary martyrs, poverty alleviation, resettlement of migrants, or social relief;  
(3) seizing by force three times or more within one year;  
(4) seizing by force using a moving motor vehicle.  
Where the forcible seizure of public or private property has not been subject to administrative penalty and shall be prosecuted according to law, the amounts of the seizures shall be calculated cumulatively.

<a id="art-3"></a>第三条 抢夺公私财物虽然达到[本解释第一条](#art-1)第（一）项规定的“数额较大”的标准，但具有下列情形之一的，可以视为[刑法第三十七条](../../刑事/刑法及修正案/中华人民共和国刑法.md#art-37)规定的“犯罪情节轻微不需要判处刑罚”，免予刑事处罚：  
（一）已满十六周岁不满十八周岁的未成年人作案，属于初犯或者被教唆犯罪的；  
（二）主动投案、全部退赃或者退赔的；  
（三）被胁迫参加抢夺，没有分赃或者获赃较少的；  
（四）其他情节轻微，危害不大的。

**Article 3** : Where the forcible seizure of public or private property, though reaching the standard of "relatively large amount" as provided in Paragraph (1) of Article 1 of this Interpretation, has any of the following circumstances, it may be considered as "the circumstances of the crime are minor, and no punishment is necessary" as prescribed in Article 37 of the Criminal Law, and the offender shall be exempted from criminal punishment:  
(1) a minor who has attained the age of 16 but not the age of 18 commits the crime, and it is a first offense or the minor was instigated to commit the crime;  
(2) voluntarily surrenders, and returns all stolen goods or makes full restitution;  
(3) participates in the forcible seizure under duress, and receives no share of stolen goods or receives a small share;  
(4) other circumstances where the circumstances are minor and the harm is not great.

<a id="art-4"></a>第四条 抢夺公私财物，数额接近[本解释第一条](#art-1)第（二）项、第（三）项规定的“数额巨大”、“数额特别巨大”的标准，并具有[本解释第二条](#art-2)规定的情形之一的，可以分别认定为“其他严重情节”或者“其他特别严重情节”。

**Article 4** : Where the amount of public or private property seized by force approaches the standards of "large amount" or "extremely large amount" as provided in Paragraphs (2) and (3) of Article 1 of this Interpretation, and the seizure falls under any of the circumstances provided in Article 2 of this Interpretation, it may be respectively determined as "other serious circumstances" or "other especially serious circumstances".

<a id="art-5"></a>第五条 实施抢夺公私财物行为，构成抢夺罪，同时造成被害人重伤、死亡等后果，构成过失致人重伤罪、过失致人死亡罪等犯罪的，依照处罚较重的规定定罪处罚。

**Article 5** : Where an act of seizing public or private property by force constitutes the crime of forcible seizure, and at the same time causes serious injury, death or other consequences to the victim, constituting the crime of negligently causing serious injury, negligently causing death or other crimes, the offender shall be convicted and punished in accordance with the provisions for the heavier punishment.

<a id="art-6"></a>第六条 各省、自治区、直辖市高级人民法院可以根据本地区经济发展状况，并考虑社会治安状况，在[本解释第一条](#art-1)规定的数额幅度内，分别确定本地区执行的具体标准，并报最高人民法院备案。

**Article 6** : The Higher People's Courts of each province, autonomous region, and municipality directly under the Central Government may, based on the economic development conditions of their respective regions and taking into account the public security situation, determine the specific standards for enforcement within the ranges of amounts prescribed in Article 1 of this Interpretation, and shall report them to the Supreme People's Court for recordation.  
July 16, 2002
